🧬 About Mega Aggron in Pokémon GO

Mega Aggron is a Steel-type Pokémon. When Mega Evolved, it loses its Rock typing and becomes a pure Steel-type Pokémon, which gives it huge defense but also clear weaknesses.

Mega Aggron is useful because:

  • It boosts Steel-type attackers
  • It is extremely tanky and survives a long time in battles
  • It is useful in some niche PvP and raid situations

🌀 Mega Aggron Moveset

Aggron common fast moves are Smack Down or Iron Tail or Dragon Tail. Charged moves include Stone Edge or Rock Tomb or Thunder or Meteor Beam and Heavy Slam

Pro tip: While it doesn’t hit extremely fast, its high defense and solid damage output can slowly drain your team’s resources if you’re not doing enough DPS.

Mega Aggron’s combination of Rock and Steel moves means many counters take neutral or resisted damage, so focus on durability and dodge charged attacks when possible.

🎯 Rewards & Catch CP

In Mega Raids, you catch a normal Aggron and also earn Mega Energy.

  • Perfect (L20): 2,284-2,378 CP
  • Weather Boost (L25): 2,856-2,973 CP
  • Boosted by: Snowy

Use Pinap on poor IVs; switch to Golden Razz on 96%+ or Shiny.

💡 Best Strategy to Defeat Mega Aggron

  1. Lead with your highest DPS counters to quickly break through Mega Aggron’s HP.
  2. Dodge charged moves like Stone Edge and Heavy Slam when possible to preserve your team’s health.
  3. Rotate in bulkier Fire or Ground Pokémon when fighting against Rock moves.
  4. Rejoin the raid quickly if your Pokémon faint — every bit of extra time helps.

Yes, mainly for Mega Energy and Pokédex completion. While it is not the best attacker, it is a very bulky Mega Pokémon and can be useful in certain situations.

FAQ

Can Mega Aggron be shiny in Pokémon GO?

Yes, shiny Mega Aggron is available.

What are best moveset of Aggron?

Best moveset of Aggron are Iron Tail or Dragon Tail which does damage of 14 under Fast Attack and Meteor Beam of 140 under Charged Attack.

Are Shadow Pokémon better?

Yes—Shadow forms have +20% attack, making them excellent choices if available.

What is the best weather to raid Mega Aggron?

Sunny weather boosts Aggron fire and ground type moves, boosts Ground attackers. Rainy weather boosts Water type moves. Partly Cloudy weather boosts Rock moves.

Does Mega Aggron keep its Mega form after the battle?

Mega Evolution lasts only during battles where Mega is enabled; it does not persist outside combat.

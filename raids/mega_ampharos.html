Mega Ampharos Introduction

Mega Ampharos returns to Mega Raids as a bulky Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon that can be a fun addition to your Mega collection. While it may not be the strongest Electric attacker in the game, it offers unique typing, a distinctive appearance, and the chance to earn Mega Energy for future evolutions. Trainers looking to expand their Mega roster or hunt for a shiny version will find Mega Ampharos raids worth checking out during its rotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mega Ampharos worth raiding in Pokémon GO?

Mega Ampharos is worth raiding if you need Mega Energy, want to hunt for a shiny Ampharos, or are looking for a high-IV specimen. While it isn't among the strongest Mega attackers in the game, its unique Electric and Dragon typing makes it an interesting addition to any collection.

Can Mega Ampharos be shiny?

Yes. If shiny Ampharos is available during the raid rotation, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Ampharos after successfully defeating Mega Ampharos. The shiny version is easily recognizable thanks to its pink coloration.

What are Mega Ampharos's weaknesses?

Mega Ampharos is weak to Ground-, Dragon-, Fairy-, and Ice-type attacks. Ground-type Pokémon are often considered the safest and most effective counters because they resist Electric-type moves while dealing super-effective damage.

How many trainers are needed to defeat Mega Ampharos?

Most players can comfortably defeat Mega Ampharos with three to five trainers using proper counters. Highly experienced trainers with strong teams may be able to complete the raid with only two players under favorable conditions.

What weather boosts Mega Ampharos?

Rainy weather boosts Mega Ampharos's Electric-type attacks, while Windy weather boosts its Dragon-type attacks and increases the level of the Ampharos encounter after the raid.

Which Pokémon are the best counters to Mega Ampharos?

Some of the strongest counters include Primal Groudon, Mega Garchomp, Garchomp, Rayquaza, Origin Forme Dialga, Mega Gardevoir, and Excadrill. Ground-type attackers are usually the most reliable option.

How much Mega Energy do I get from a Mega Ampharos raid?

The amount of Mega Energy awarded depends on how quickly the raid is completed. Faster completion times generally result in higher Mega Energy rewards.

What is the best moveset for Mega Ampharos?

For raid battles, Volt Switch paired with Zap Cannon is generally considered Mega Ampharos's strongest Electric-type moveset. This combination provides solid damage output and benefits from STAB (Same Type Attack Bonus).

Is Mega Ampharos good in PvP?

Mega Ampharos has limited use in standard PvP formats because Mega Evolutions are not usually allowed. However, it can be a fun and powerful option in special formats or events that permit Mega Pokémon.

Should I power up Mega Ampharos?

If you enjoy collecting Mega Evolutions or want an Electric-type Mega Pokémon for raid support, powering up Mega Ampharos can be worthwhile. Trainers focused solely on maximizing raid damage may prefer investing resources into stronger Electric-type attackers first.

Can Mega Ampharos be soloed?

For most trainers, Mega Ampharos is not a realistic solo raid target due to its bulk and raid timer constraints. A duo or small group is generally recommended for a comfortable victory.

Does Mega Ampharos have any special advantages?

Electric and Dragon typing is relatively uncommon in Pokémon GO. This unique combination allows Mega Ampharos to provide useful Mega boosts for both Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon during raids, making it a versatile support Mega Evolution.

Is Mega Ampharos Worth Raiding?

Mega Ampharos is not considered a top-tier raid attacker compared to some other Mega Evolutions, but it still has value. Its Electric and Dragon typing gives it a unique role, and having access to Mega Ampharos can be useful when boosting Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon during raids.

For newer trainers, Mega Ampharos is a solid Mega Evolution to unlock because it can help provide bonus Candy and damage boosts in group raids. Veteran players may be more interested in collecting Mega Energy, improving IVs, or hunting for a shiny.

Mega Ampharos Weaknesses

Mega Ampharos is an Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon, which changes the weaknesses found on regular Ampharos. It takes super-effective damage from Ground, Dragon, Fairy, and Ice-type attacks.

Ground-type attackers are often the safest option because they resist Electric moves while dealing strong damage. Dragon-types can also perform extremely well, but they must be careful because Mega Ampharos may use Dragon-type attacks in return. Fairy-types are another reliable choice thanks to their resistance to Dragon-type moves.

Understanding these weaknesses is the key to building an efficient raid team and finishing the battle more quickly.

Best Counters for Mega Ampharos

Some of the strongest counters against Mega Ampharos include powerful Ground-, Dragon-, and Fairy-type Pokémon.

Primal Groudon is usually the standout option thanks to its incredible Ground-type damage output. Other excellent choices include Garchomp, Mega Garchomp, Shadow Garchomp, Rayquaza, Origin Forme Dialga, and Mega Gardevoir.

If you don't have legendary or shadow Pokémon, strong budget options such as Excadrill, Mamoswine, and Togekiss can still contribute meaningful damage. The most important thing is to focus on Pokémon that can exploit Mega Ampharos's weaknesses rather than simply using your highest-CP Pokémon.

Can Mega Ampharos Be Soloed?

Mega Ampharos is generally too bulky for most trainers to defeat alone under normal conditions. Even with top-tier counters, weather boosts, and high-level Pokémon, a solo victory is extremely difficult and not realistic for the average player.

A duo is achievable for experienced trainers using optimized teams and powered-up counters. For most players, bringing three to five trainers will make the raid comfortable and significantly reduce the risk of running out of time.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can have a noticeable impact on Mega Ampharos raids.

Rainy weather boosts Mega Ampharos's Electric-type attacks, making the raid boss slightly more dangerous. Windy weather boosts its Dragon-type attacks and also increases the level of Ampharos encountered after the raid.

On the attacking side, Sunny weather benefits Ground-type counters, while Snow boosts Ice-type attackers. Cloudy weather can help Fairy-type Pokémon perform better.

Planning raids around favorable weather conditions can make battles easier and more efficient.

Mega Ampharos CP

After defeating Mega Ampharos, the CP of the Ampharos encounter depends on weather conditions.

Without a weather boost, trainers can expect lower CP values, while Windy weather increases the encounter level and results in higher CP. Players searching for perfect IV Ampharos should pay close attention to CP values because they can provide clues about the Pokémon's quality before appraisal.

Checking CP ranges before raiding can help identify potential high-IV catches more quickly.

Personal Raid Experience

In my experience, Mega Ampharos raids tend to be straightforward when at least three trainers are using proper counters. Ground-type Pokémon consistently perform well, especially when Mega Ampharos is using Electric-type moves. The raid can feel surprisingly bulky compared to some other Mega bosses, but a coordinated group rarely has trouble finishing with plenty of time remaining.

One of the most enjoyable aspects of these raids is the opportunity to collect Mega Energy while also hunting for a shiny Ampharos. Even if it isn't the strongest Mega Evolution available, it remains a fun raid target and a useful addition to many trainers' collections.

Shiny Mega Ampharos Information

Yes, shiny Ampharos can be encountered after successfully defeating Mega Ampharos in a raid, provided the shiny form is currently available.

Shiny Ampharos is easy to recognize thanks to its bright pink coloration, making it one of the more distinctive shiny Pokémon in the game. Many trainers continue raiding Mega Ampharos specifically for a chance at obtaining a high-IV shiny version.

As with other raid bosses, shiny encounters are determined randomly, so multiple raids may be required before finding one.

Stats Overview

Mega Ampharos stands out because of its unusual Electric and Dragon typing. It has impressive bulk compared to many Mega Pokémon, allowing it to stay on the battlefield for a long time. While its attack stat is respectable, its durability is often what players notice most during raids.

Typing Analysis

The Dragon typing gained through Mega Evolution changes how Ampharos performs in battles. It loses the simple Electric-type profile of its regular form and gains additional strengths and weaknesses. This unique combination allows it to resist several common attack types while also creating opportunities for Dragon-type strategies.

Best Mega Evolutions to Use

If you're raiding with a group, bringing the right Mega Evolution can help everyone deal more damage.

Good choices include:

  • Mega Garchomp for Ground-type boosts.
  • Mega Rayquaza for Dragon-type teams.
  • Mega Gardevoir for Fairy-type teams.
  • Primal Groudon for maximum Ground-type damage.

Choosing a Mega that matches your team's attackers can improve overall raid performance.

Catch Tips After the Raid

Once Mega Ampharos has been defeated, take your time during the catch encounter. Use Golden Razz Berries whenever possible and aim for Excellent Curveball throws. Since raid encounters cannot flee until all Premier Balls are used, patience often leads to better results than rushing throws.

Best Moveset for Mega Ampharos

For trainers planning to use Mega Ampharos after unlocking its Mega Evolution, the strongest Electric-type moveset is generally Volt Switch paired with Zap Cannon. This combination allows it to deal consistent Electric-type damage in raids and gym battles.

PvP

Mega Pokémon are not commonly used in standard PvP formats, but Mega Ampharos can be an interesting choice in special events that allow Mega Evolutions. Its Dragon typing provides unique coverage options that differ from most Electric-type Pokémon.

Fun Facts About Mega Ampharos

  • Mega Ampharos becomes part Dragon-type when it Mega Evolves.
  • Its flowing wool-like mane is inspired by mythical dragons.
  • Ampharos was one of the earliest Electric-type Pokémon introduced in the main series games.
  • Its shiny form changes from yellow to a distinctive pink color.

Should You Power Up Mega Ampharos?

For collectors and Mega Evolution enthusiasts, investing in Mega Ampharos can be worthwhile. However, trainers focused purely on raid efficiency may prioritize stronger Electric attackers first. Mega Ampharos shines most as a versatile support Mega rather than a top damage dealer.

Trivia Section

Many trainers were surprised when Ampharos gained the Dragon type through Mega Evolution, as it had never been associated with dragons before. This unexpected typing quickly made Mega Ampharos one of the most memorable Mega Evolutions introduced in the Pokémon series.

How Much Mega Energy Do You Need?

Unlocking Mega Ampharos for the first time requires a significant amount of Mega Energy. After the initial Mega Evolution, future evolutions become much cheaper, especially as the Pokémon's Mega Level increases.

Participating in multiple Mega Ampharos raids is often the fastest way to gather enough energy for long-term use.

Mega Levels Explained

Once Mega Ampharos has been Mega Evolved, its Mega Level can gradually increase through repeated Mega Evolutions.

Higher Mega Levels provide benefits such as:

  • Increased XP bonuses.
  • Additional Candy from catches.
  • Greater Candy XL chances.
  • Reduced Mega Evolution costs.

For active players, these bonuses can become surprisingly valuable over time.

Team Building Tips

When preparing for Mega Ampharos, focus on building a team around a single weakness whenever possible.

A full Ground-type team is often more effective than mixing Ground, Fairy, Dragon, and Ice attackers together. Specialized teams benefit from Mega boosts and make better use of raid party bonuses.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make

Many trainers unknowingly make raids harder by:

  • Using random high-CP Pokémon instead of proper counters.
  • Ignoring Mega Evolutions.
  • Forgetting weather boosts.
  • Entering raids with fainted Pokémon.
  • Using auto-selected teams.

Taking a few moments to create a custom raid party can noticeably improve performance.

Best Budget Counters

Not every trainer has access to legendary Pokémon or Shadow Pokémon.

Affordable options include:

  • Excadrill
  • Mamoswine
  • Golem
  • Rhyperior
  • Togekiss

These Pokémon are easier to obtain and can still contribute strong damage during Mega Ampharos raids.

How Difficult Is Mega Ampharos Compared to Other Mega Raids?

Mega Ampharos is generally considered a medium-difficulty Mega Raid boss.

It is usually:

  • Easier than Mega Latios.
  • Easier than Mega Latias.
  • Similar to Mega Altaria.
  • Slightly bulkier than some common Mega raid bosses.

Most groups of three to five trainers should have little trouble completing the raid.

Best Friend Bonus Impact

Raiding with Best Friends provides an additional damage bonus that many players overlook.

When combined with:

  • Weather boosts,
  • Mega Evolution boosts,
  • Strong counters,

the Best Friend bonus can significantly reduce completion times.

IV Hunting Guide

Many trainers raid Mega Ampharos specifically to hunt for high-IV Pokémon.

Good IVs can improve:

  • Raid performance.
  • Gym battles.
  • Long-term Mega Evolution investment.

If you plan to use Ampharos regularly, aiming for strong IVs can be worthwhile.

Candy XL Farming Potential

Mega Ampharos can be useful for trainers working toward Level 50 Pokémon.

Using a Mega-Evolved Pokémon that shares typing with wild catches can increase Candy XL opportunities during events, making Mega Ampharos more valuable than its raid performance alone might suggest.

Raid Preparation Checklist

Before joining a Mega Ampharos raid:

  • Revive fainted Pokémon.
  • Create a Ground-type raid team.
  • Check local weather.
  • Mega Evolve a suitable Pokémon.
  • Stock up on Golden Razz Berries.
  • Clear Pokémon storage space.
  • Ensure enough healing items are available.

A little preparation can make raids much smoother.

Collector's Perspective

For collectors, Mega Ampharos offers several reasons to raid:

  • Shiny hunting.
  • High-IV hunting.
  • Mega Energy farming.
  • Pokédex completion.
  • Building a Mega collection.

Even trainers who rarely use Ampharos in battle often enjoy adding it to their collection.

Conclusion

Mega Ampharos may not dominate the raid meta, but it remains a worthwhile Mega Raid boss thanks to its unique typing, shiny availability, and Mega Evolution benefits. By taking advantage of its weaknesses with strong Ground-, Dragon-, Fairy-, or Ice-type attackers, trainers can defeat it efficiently and earn valuable Mega Energy. Whether you're hunting for a shiny, chasing perfect IVs, or simply expanding your Mega collection, Mega Ampharos is a raid boss that offers something for every Pokémon GO trainer.

Mega Alakazam – Raid Boss Overview

What is Mega Alakazam?

Mega Alakazam appears as a Mega Raid Boss in Pokémon GO, where you team up with other players to defeat it and earn Mega Energy.

  • It shows up in Mega Raids
  • You battle it in groups
  • After winning, you can catch Alakazam and collect Mega Energy

Why Mega Alakazam is important

This raid boss is popular mainly because of its offensive strength and Mega utility.

  • One of the strongest Psychic-type attackers in the game
  • Useful in Fighting and Poison-type raids
  • Its Mega form boosts Psychic damage for your entire team
  • Helps clear raids faster due to high damage output

It’s also a good Mega Energy source for building Mega Alakazam long-term.

Battle difficulty

Mega Alakazam sits in the medium-to-hard difficulty range depending on your team.

Mega Alakazam Raid Strategy Guide

Can you solo it?

Short answer: no — Mega Alakazam is not realistically soloable.

It simply has too much HP and hits too hard with moves like Future Sight and Shadow Ball. Even strong players struggle to keep up alone.

Factor Reality
SoloNot possible
Minimum workable setup2 strong players (very hard)
Recommended group3–5 players

Duo raids (2 players)

This is one of the hardest ways to take it down. Only experienced trainers should attempt it.

  • You’ll need high-level counters (Level 40+ helps a lot)
  • Weather boost (Fog or Windy) makes a big difference
  • Dark, Ghost, and Bug types are essential

Even with perfect play, you’ll be burning through revives quickly, so timing and coordination matter a lot.

Aspect Reality
DifficultyVery high
Coordination neededHigh
Success rateMedium

Trio raids (3 players)

This is where the fight starts to feel stable. With decent counters, a trio can comfortably clear it.

  • Bring at least one Mega for team boost
  • Focus on consistent damage instead of risky plays
  • Don’t waste time over-dodging — just avoid major hits
Aspect Reality
DifficultyHard
CoordinationMedium
Success rateHigh

4–6 players (comfortable raids)

At this point, the raid becomes straightforward. Most groups can clear it without stress.

  • Any strong counters will work
  • Mega boost helps but isn’t required
  • Focus on staying in battle rather than perfect play
Aspect Reality
DifficultyEasy
CoordinationLow
Success rateVery high

Skill-based progression

How you approach the raid depends on your experience level:

  • Beginner: just join groups and focus on rejoining after fainting
  • Intermediate: start using proper Dark/Ghost/Bug counters and learn boss moves
  • Advanced: optimize teams, use Megas smartly, and time dodges properly
  • Expert: focus on speed clears, weather boosts, and near-perfect execution

Important things most players miss

  • Shadow Ball can delete Psychic counters instantly
  • Future Sight is the main move you must watch out for
  • Small groups require fast relobbying to avoid time loss

Final takeaway

Mega Alakazam isn’t hard because of mechanics — it’s hard because it punishes mistakes quickly. Once you have the right counters and enough players, it becomes a fast and smooth raid.

  • Impossible solo
  • Hard in duos
  • Comfortable in trios
  • Easy in full groups

  • Psychic-types

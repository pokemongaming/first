Mega Pidgeot Overview

Mega Pidgeot is a Flying-type Mega Evolution known for its strong aerial attacks and high Speed-based pressure in battle. In raids, it doesn’t hit like a tank, but it can still wear down unprepared teams quickly with Flying-type moves.

It is weak to Electric, Rock, and Ice-type attacks, which makes it relatively easy for prepared trainers—but only if you bring the right Pokémon.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mega Pidgeot weak against?

Mega Pidgeot is weak to Rock, Electric, and Ice-type attacks. Rock-type Pokémon are usually the best option because they can deal heavy damage while taking advantage of Mega Pidgeot's Flying typing.

Can Mega Pidgeot be defeated solo?

Yes, Mega Pidgeot can be soloed by experienced trainers with high-level counters, favorable weather, and strong Pokémon. However, most players will find it much easier with at least one or two additional trainers.

How many players are needed to beat Mega Pidgeot?

A group of 2–3 well-prepared trainers can usually defeat Mega Pidgeot without much difficulty. Larger groups make the raid even faster and more comfortable.

Is Mega Pidgeot worth raiding?

Mega Pidgeot may not be the most powerful Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO, but it is still worth raiding for Mega Energy, Pokédex completion, and shiny hunting. Players who enjoy collecting Mega Evolutions will likely want to add it to their roster.

Can Mega Pidgeot be shiny?

Yes. After defeating Mega Pidgeot, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Pidgeot. The shiny version features golden feathers and is highly sought after by collectors.

What weather boosts Mega Pidgeot?

Windy weather boosts Mega Pidgeot's Flying-type attacks and increases its level when encountered after the raid. This can make the battle slightly more challenging.

What weather helps trainers defeat Mega Pidgeot?

Rainy weather boosts Electric-type Pokémon, while Snowy weather boosts Ice-type attackers. Both weather conditions can help trainers deal more damage during the raid.

What are the best counters for Mega Pidgeot?

Some of the strongest counters include Rampardos, Rhyperior, Zekrom, Raikou, Mamoswine, and Mega Diancie. These Pokémon can take advantage of Mega Pidgeot's weaknesses and help finish the raid quickly.

How much Mega Energy do you get from a Mega Pidgeot raid?

The amount of Mega Energy earned depends on how quickly the raid is completed. Faster completion times generally reward more Mega Energy.

Is Mega Pidgeot good in Pokémon GO?

Mega Pidgeot has some niche uses as a Flying-type Mega Evolution and can provide useful Mega boosts. While it is not among the strongest Mega Pokémon in the game, it remains a solid choice for collectors and Flying-type enthusiasts.

What CP does Mega Pidgeot have after the raid?

The CP of the Pidgeot you catch after the raid depends on weather conditions and IVs. A perfect IV Pidgeot will have a higher CP than average encounters, making it a valuable catch for trainers looking for strong specimens.

Can new players beat Mega Pidgeot?

Yes. Mega Pidgeot is considered one of the more approachable Mega Raids. Newer players can successfully complete it by joining a group and using Pokémon that exploit its weaknesses.

Best Counters for Mega Pidgeot

If you want a smooth raid, focus on strong Electric, Rock, and Ice attackers.

Some top choices include:

  • Rampardos / Rhyperior (Rock types) – Extremely high damage, best for fast clears
  • Zekrom / Raikou (Electric types) – Consistent and reliable damage output
  • Mamoswine / Glaceon (Ice types) – Great budget-friendly options

The key is simple: don’t bring Fighting, Grass, or Bug types—they won’t help much here.

Can Mega Pidgeot Be Soloed?

Yes, but it depends on your setup.

  • High-level trainers with top counters → Can solo it under good conditions
  • Average players → Duo is much safer and consistent
  • New players → Trio or full group recommended

If the weather is in your favor, this raid becomes much easier and faster.

Weather Boost Strategy

If you want a smooth raid, focus on strong Electric, Rock, and Ice attackers.

Weather can make a big difference in this raid:

  • Windy Weather → Boosts Mega Pidgeot (be careful, it becomes stronger)
  • Rainy Weather → Boosts Electric types (best for attackers)
  • Snow → Boosts Ice types (also a great advantage)

If the weather is in your favor, this raid becomes much easier and faster.

Raid Tips

  • Always prioritize high DPS over bulk
  • Dodge only charged attacks if your Pokémon are fragile
  • Use Mega Evolution boosts if possible (Mega Manectric, Mega Diancie, etc.)
  • Don’t waste time with low-level Pokémon just for type advantage

Is Mega Pidgeot Worth Raiding?

Honestly, Mega Pidgeot is not the most meta-defining Mega Evolution in the game, but it still has value.

  • Useful for Mega boosts in Flying-type raids
  • Good choice for casual raid grinding
  • Decent for Pokédex completion and Mega energy farming)

If you’re a collector or someone building Mega resources, it’s worth doing a few runs.

Shiny Information

There is always a chance to encounter a shiny Pidgeot after defeating Mega Pidgeot in raids. The shiny version is not guaranteed, but raid events often keep players hunting multiple times just for this reason.

Raid Difficulty Explanation

Mega Pidgeot is generally considered an easy to moderate Mega Raid boss, depending on your team strength. Unlike tanky Mega Evolutions, it doesn’t have extremely high defense, so experienced players can clear it quickly with the right counters.

However, for casual trainers or low-level accounts, it can still feel challenging if they don’t use proper Rock, Electric, or Ice-type Pokémon.

Common Mistakes Players Make

Many trainers lose time or damage in this raid because of simple mistakes:

  • Using wrong typing like Grass or Fighting
  • Ignoring weather boost advantages
  • Using low-level Pokémon just for type advantage
  • Not dodging strong charged attacks when using fragile attackers

Avoiding these mistakes can easily cut your raid time in half.

Recommended Party Setup

A balanced raid team for Mega Pidgeot should look like this:

  • 2–3 strong Rock attackers
  • 2 Electric types for consistency
  • 1 Ice type as backup
  • Optional Mega Pokémon for boost (like Mega Manectric or Mega Aerodactyl)

This setup gives you both speed + safety in the raid.

Mega Energy Tip

If your goal is to farm Mega Energy efficiently:

  • Try to complete raids with minimum fainting Pokémon
  • Faster clears = more Mega energy farming per hour
  • Use friendship bonuses (Best Friends = extra damage boost)

Fun Fact

Pidgeot is one of the original Flying-type Pokémon from Generation 1. Its Mega Evolution symbolizes its peak form, with dramatically increased speed and precision in flight.

Even though it doesn’t dominate PvE meta, it remains a fan-favorite nostalgic Pokémon among long-time trainers.

Raid Experience

In most Mega Pidgeot raids, the battle feels pretty straightforward if your team is prepared. In my experience, Rock-type attackers finish the fight the fastest, while Electric types give more consistency when weather is not ideal. The raid becomes noticeably easier when multiple trainers coordinate instead of going in solo with average counters.

Move Set

Mega Pidgeot usually pressures trainers with a mix of Flying-type fast attacks and strong charged moves that can quickly chip away at weaker counters. This is why low-HP Pokémon often faint faster than expected if you don’t dodge properly.

When to Raid

Mega Pidgeot raids are best done when weather boosts Ice, Rock, or Electric types. Rainy or Snow weather gives a clear advantage to attackers, making raids faster and more efficient for farming Mega Energy.

Group Size Advice

  • Small groups (1–3 players): Requires strong counters and good levels
  • Medium groups (4–6 players): Very comfortable clear
  • Large groups (7+ players): Fast farming runs with minimal effort

Should You Raid It?

If your goal is competitive PvE dominance, Mega Pidgeot is not a top priority. But if you’re focusing on Mega collection, Pokédex completion, or general Mega Energy farming, it is still worth participating in a few raids.

How the Raid Feels in Battle

From experience, Mega Pidgeot doesn’t hit like a heavy tank, but it keeps pressure with fast Flying-type attacks. That’s what makes it slightly tricky for newer players — your Pokémon might faint faster than expected if you’re using weak or off-type counters.

If your team is properly built though, the raid goes down pretty quickly.

Weather Actually Matters

Weather can make this raid either super easy or slightly annoying.

If it’s raining or snowing, you’re in luck because Electric and Ice types get boosted. On the other hand, windy weather makes Mega Pidgeot stronger, so the fight might feel a bit longer.

Best Pokémon to Use

Honestly, you don’t need anything too complicated here.

Rock-type attackers usually do the best job. Pokémon like Rampardos or Rhyperior can melt its HP bar pretty fast. Electric types like Zekrom or Raikou are also very reliable if you don’t have strong Rock attackers built.

Ice types also work fine, especially Mamoswine, which is a great budget option for many players.

How Many Players You Need

If your counters are strong, even two skilled players can handle it without much trouble. Solo is possible, but only if you really know what you’re doing and have high-level Pokémon.

For most players though, 3–5 trainers feels like the sweet spot where the raid is smooth and stress-free.

Is Mega Pidgeot Worth It?

To be honest, Mega Pidgeot isn’t a must-have Mega for battle dominance. You’re not going to use it everywhere.

But it still has value — especially for Mega Pokédex completion and farming Mega Energy. If you’re already doing raids, it’s worth grabbing a few wins just to add it to your collection.

Simple Strategy That Works

Don’t overthink this raid.

Just bring your strongest Rock or Electric Pokémon, avoid weak type matchups, and focus on dealing damage instead of surviving everything.

Dodging isn’t even necessary most of the time unless you’re using fragile attackers.

Conclusion

Mega Pidgeot is more of a relaxed raid compared to many other Mega bosses. It doesn’t require crazy preparation, just the right type advantage and a decent team.

If you’re prepared, it feels quick and smooth. If you’re not, it can drag a bit — but nothing too overwhelming.

Mega Alakazam Overview

Mega Alakazam is one of the strongest Psychic-type Mega Pokémon available in Pokémon GO. It boasts extremely high Attack power and can deal massive damage in raids, especially when using Psychic-type moves. However, like most glass-cannon Pokémon, it doesn't have much bulk and can faint quickly against strong attackers.

When Mega Alakazam appears in Mega Raids, trainers often target it not only for Mega Energy but also because it has useful applications as a Psychic-type attacker and can provide Mega Boosts for Psychic-type teams during raids.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type is Mega Alakazam?

Mega Alakazam is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon. This means it is weak to Bug-, Ghost-, and Dark-type attacks while resisting Fighting- and Psychic-type moves.

Can Mega Alakazam be shiny in Pokémon GO?

Yes. If the shiny form is available during the raid rotation, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Alakazam after defeating Mega Alakazam in raids. The shiny version features a distinctive pink coloration instead of the normal brown appearance.

How many trainers are needed to defeat Mega Alakazam?

The number of trainers required depends on player level, counters, and weather conditions. Highly prepared trainers may be able to complete the raid with just two players, while a group of three or more trainers can usually defeat Mega Alakazam comfortably.

What are the best counters for Mega Alakazam?

Strong Dark- and Ghost-type Pokémon generally perform the best. Popular choices include Mega Tyranitar, Mega Gengar, Dawn Wings Necrozma, Shadow Tyranitar, Hydreigon, and Chandelure.

Is Mega Alakazam worth raiding?

Yes, especially for trainers who enjoy raid battles. Mega Alakazam is one of the strongest Psychic-type attackers in Pokémon GO and provides useful Mega Evolution bonuses for Psychic-type Pokémon.

How do I get Mega Alakazam?

First, defeat Mega Alakazam in Mega Raids to earn Mega Energy. Once you have enough Mega Energy, you can Mega Evolve your own Alakazam.

Does weather affect Mega Alakazam raids?

Yes. Windy weather boosts Mega Alakazam's Psychic-type attacks and increases the level of the Pokémon encountered after the raid. Foggy weather boosts Dark- and Ghost-type counters, making the battle easier for prepared trainers.

What is Mega Energy used for?

Mega Energy is required to Mega Evolve Alakazam. After the initial Mega Evolution, future evolutions become cheaper and can eventually become free after sufficient cooldown periods.

Can Mega Alakazam be soloed?

A solo victory is extremely challenging and usually requires top-tier counters, ideal weather conditions, and advanced raid strategies. Most trainers will find a duo or trio much more realistic.

Is Mega Alakazam good for PvP?

Mega Alakazam is primarily designed for raid battles and PvE content. While it can be powerful in certain PvP situations, its low bulk often limits its effectiveness compared to bulkier alternatives.

What CP should I look for when catching Alakazam after the raid?

Higher CP values generally indicate better IVs. Trainers interested in competitive use or collection goals often aim for the highest possible CP available during normal and weather-boosted encounters.

Can I use Remote Raid Passes for Mega Alakazam?

Yes. As long as Mega Alakazam is available in raids, trainers can participate remotely using a Remote Raid Pass, subject to the current Pokémon GO raid rules.

What happens if my Pokémon faint during the raid?

You can rejoin the battle by using Revives and Potions on your Pokémon or by selecting a new battle party. Re-entering quickly helps maximize your team's damage contribution.

Which Mega Evolution should I use against Mega Alakazam?

Mega Tyranitar is often considered one of the best choices because it deals super-effective Dark-type damage while boosting Dark-type attacks from other trainers. Mega Gengar is another excellent option for maximizing Ghost-type damage.

Should I keep raiding after unlocking Mega Alakazam?

Many trainers continue raiding for additional Mega Energy, better IVs, shiny hunting, XL Candy, and progress toward maximizing Mega Levels. Even after unlocking the Mega Evolution, there are several reasons to keep participating in raids.

Is Mega Alakazam Worth Raiding?

For many trainers, the answer is yes.

Mega Alakazam offers one of the highest Psychic-type attack stats in the game, making it a powerful option for future raids where Psychic-type damage is useful. It is also valuable for players who enjoy collecting Mega Evolutions or building specialized raid teams.

Even if you already own a strong Alakazam, raiding Mega Alakazam allows you to gather Mega Energy, hunt for high IV specimens, and potentially encounter its shiny form.

Casual players may not use Mega Alakazam every day, but dedicated raiders will find it a worthwhile addition to their collection.

Mega Alakazam Weaknesses

Mega Alakazam is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon. Because of this typing, it is weak to:

  • Bug-type attacks
  • Ghost-type attacks
  • Dark-type attacks

Dark and Ghost attackers are generally the best options because many of the strongest raid Pokémon belong to these categories.

One thing trainers should remember is that Mega Alakazam's Defense is relatively low. Even though it is a Mega Raid Boss, powerful counters can deal significant damage very quickly.

Best Counters for Mega Alakazam

Several Pokémon perform exceptionally well against Mega Alakazam.

Mega Tyranitar

Mega Tyranitar combines tremendous Dark-type damage with excellent bulk. It can stay on the battlefield longer than most counters while dealing super-effective damage.

Mega Gengar

Mega Gengar hits incredibly hard with Ghost-type attacks. Although it is somewhat fragile, its damage output can shorten the raid considerably.

Dawn Wings Necrozma

Dawn Wings Necrozma is one of the strongest Ghost-type attackers available. Its combination of power and useful resistances makes it a top-tier counter.

Shadow Tyranitar

Shadow Tyranitar offers impressive Dark-type damage and remains one of the most reliable counters for Psychic-type raid bosses.

Hydreigon

Hydreigon continues to be a favorite Dark-type attacker thanks to its powerful moveset and accessibility for many trainers.

Can Mega Alakazam Be Soloed?

Highly experienced trainers with optimal counters may occasionally attempt a solo challenge under ideal conditions, but Mega Alakazam is generally considered difficult to solo.

Solo Raid

Possible only for top-level trainers with excellent counters, favorable weather, and strong Mega boosts.

Duo Raid

A duo is realistic for well-prepared trainers using strong Dark- or Ghost-type teams.

Trio Raid

Three trainers can defeat Mega Alakazam comfortably in most situations.

Larger Groups

Four or more trainers should have little difficulty defeating the boss before the timer expires.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can significantly influence raid performance.

Windy Weather

Windy weather boosts Psychic-type attacks and increases Mega Alakazam's level when caught. However, it also makes the raid boss more dangerous.

Foggy Weather

Foggy weather boosts Dark- and Ghost-type attacks, making it one of the best conditions for battling Mega Alakazam.

Cloudy Weather

Cloudy weather does not directly impact the raid matchup but may influence some secondary counters.

Many trainers prefer Foggy weather because it strengthens the most effective counters against Mega Alakazam.

Mega Alakazam CP

After successfully defeating Mega Alakazam, the CP of the encounter can help indicate its IV quality.

A higher CP generally means better overall stats. Trainers looking for perfect IVs should pay close attention to the highest possible CP values available during both normal and weather-boosted encounters.

Checking the CP immediately after capture is one of the quickest ways to identify potentially valuable Alakazam.

Personal Raid Experience

In actual raid battles, Mega Alakazam often feels easier than its intimidating Attack stat suggests. Its relatively low bulk means that strong Dark-type teams can burn through its HP surprisingly quickly.

One thing that stands out is how fast your own Pokémon can faint if Mega Alakazam is carrying powerful Psychic attacks. Revives and healing items are worth keeping ready, especially during smaller group raids.

Most experienced raiders find that preparation matters more than group size. A well-built Dark-type team can often outperform a larger group using random counters.

  • Its design emphasizes intelligence and psychic power rather than physical strength.
  • Alakazam's Pokédex entries often describe its extraordinary brain power and memory.

What Makes Mega Alakazam Special?

Mega Alakazam stands out because it focuses almost entirely on offensive power. Unlike some Mega Evolutions that balance attack and defense, Mega Alakazam is built to hit as hard as possible. This makes it an excellent choice when maximum Psychic-type damage is needed, but it also means trainers must be prepared for it to faint more quickly than bulkier Mega Pokémon.

Shiny Mega Alakazam

Yes, Mega Alakazam can be shiny if the shiny form is available during the raid period.

Shiny Alakazam swaps its traditional brown coloration for a distinctive pink appearance. The difference is immediately noticeable and makes it one of the more recognizable Psychic-type shinies in the game.

Many trainers continue raiding even after obtaining enough Mega Energy simply for the chance of finding a shiny version.

Best Mega Evolution to Bring

Choosing the right Mega Evolution can make a noticeable difference during the raid.

Mega Tyranitar is usually the safest choice because it deals super-effective Dark-type damage while boosting other Dark-type attackers in the lobby. Trainers who prefer aggressive strategies may choose Mega Gengar for its exceptional Ghost-type damage output, though it requires more careful play due to its lower survivability.

Recommended Team Composition

A balanced raid team generally performs better than relying on a single Pokémon.

For Mega Alakazam raids, consider using a combination of Dark- and Ghost-type attackers. Having a mix of bulky Pokémon and high-damage attackers helps maintain consistent pressure throughout the battle. If your strongest counters faint, backup options can prevent valuable time from being lost during relobbying.

Best Moveset

Once you have enough Mega Energy, you'll want a strong moveset.

For raid battles, Psychic-type moves generally provide the best performance. Trainers focused on PvE content usually prefer moves that maximize damage output rather than coverage. While move preferences can change with game updates, Psychic-focused sets are typically the most effective.

Mega Alakazam in Raids vs PvP

Mega Alakazam shines primarily in raid content.

Its incredible Attack stat allows it to deal huge damage against raid bosses, but its fragility can make it less practical in trainer battles. Most players use Mega Alakazam as a raid attacker or Mega booster rather than as a staple PvP Pokémon.

Tips Before Starting the Raid

A little preparation can make the battle much smoother.

  • Revive fainted Dark- and Ghost-type Pokémon before joining.
  • Activate a Mega Evolution for bonus damage.
  • Raid with friends whenever possible for extra Premier Balls.
  • Use weather conditions to your advantage.
  • Avoid using Fighting- or Psychic-type attackers, as they generally perform poorly against Mega Alakazam.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make

Many failed raids happen because of poor counter choices rather than a lack of players.

One common mistake is bringing Pokémon based solely on CP instead of type effectiveness. Another is entering the raid with teams that contain Normal- or Psychic-type attackers. Even high-CP Pokémon can struggle if they do not exploit Mega Alakazam's weaknesses.

Mega Alakazam Compared to Other Psychic Megas

Compared with other Psychic-type Mega Evolutions, Mega Alakazam is among the strongest offensive options available.

While some Psychic Megas provide better survivability or additional utility, Mega Alakazam focuses on raw damage output. Trainers looking for maximum Psychic-type DPS often consider it one of the best choices in the game.

Collector's Corner

For collectors, Mega Alakazam offers several goals beyond simply winning the raid.

Many trainers aim to:

  • Obtain a perfect IV Alakazam.
  • Unlock Mega Level Max.
  • Catch a shiny version.
  • Build a dedicated Psychic-type raid team.
  • Collect enough Mega Energy for future Mega Evolutions.

These long-term goals can make Mega Alakazam raids worthwhile even after the initial Pokédex entry is secured.

Who Should Raid Mega Alakazam?

Mega Alakazam is a good target for nearly every type of trainer.

Newer players can use the raid to add a strong Psychic-type Pokémon to their collection and earn useful Mega Energy. Intermediate players may be interested in upgrading an existing Alakazam, while veteran trainers often raid repeatedly in search of better IVs, more Mega Energy, or a shiny encounter.

Even if Psychic-type attackers are not your immediate priority, Mega Alakazam remains a valuable Pokémon to have available for future raid rotations.

How Difficult Is Mega Alakazam Compared to Other Mega Raids?

Compared to many Mega Raid bosses, Mega Alakazam sits somewhere in the middle of the difficulty scale.

It is noticeably easier than bulky Mega bosses that can absorb huge amounts of damage, but it can still be dangerous because of its offensive power. Trainers with proper counters usually find that the raid progresses quickly, whereas players relying on random Pokémon may struggle more than expected.

Its lower durability means battles often feel shorter and more action-packed than other Mega Raids.

What Happens After You Defeat Mega Alakazam?

Winning the raid is only part of the experience.

After the battle, trainers receive Mega Energy that can be used to Mega Evolve their own Alakazam. The amount earned depends on how quickly the boss is defeated. Faster completion times generally result in larger Mega Energy rewards.

The catch encounter is often the most exciting moment because it determines whether you've found a high-IV Pokémon or perhaps even a shiny.

Why Trainers Continue Raiding After Getting Enough Mega Energy

Many players stop raiding once they unlock a Mega Evolution, but Mega Alakazam often encourages trainers to keep going.

Some are searching for perfect IVs. Others want enough energy to Mega Evolve Alakazam whenever needed without worrying about costs. There is also the thrill of chasing a shiny version, which can keep trainers raiding long after their initial goals have been met.

For dedicated collectors, every raid represents another chance at finding that one exceptional Alakazam.

Fun Facts About Mega Alakazam

  • Mega Alakazam carries five spoons instead of the two used by regular Alakazam.
  • According to Pokémon lore, Mega Evolution further amplifies Alakazam's already incredible intelligence.
  • Mega Alakazam's design emphasizes psychic mastery rather than physical strength.
  • It remains one of the most iconic Psychic-type Mega Evolutions introduced in the Pokémon series.
  • Despite its immense power, its defenses remain relatively fragile, reflecting its classic glass-cannon playstyle.

Conclusion

Mega Alakazam is one of those raid bosses that feels rewarding from multiple perspectives. It offers useful Mega Energy, a powerful Psychic-type attacker, and the possibility of obtaining a striking shiny Pokémon.

While it may not be the most universally useful Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO, it excels at what it was designed to do: dealing enormous Psychic-type damage. Trainers who enjoy building specialized raid teams will likely find plenty of value in adding Mega Alakazam to their roster.

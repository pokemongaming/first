What is Mega Banette?

Mega Banette is the Mega Evolution of Banette, a pure Ghost-type Pokémon that appears as a Mega Raid boss in Pokémon GO.

In raids, the process works like this:

  • You battle Mega Banette as the raid boss
  • After defeating it, you catch a regular Banette
  • You also earn Mega Energy to evolve your own Banette later

Why Mega Banette is Worth Paying Attention To

At first glance, Mega Banette may look like just another Ghost-type Mega, but it actually has strong value for raid-focused players.

Strong Ghost-Type Damage

  • Very high Attack stat
  • Performs extremely well against Psychic and Ghost-type raid bosses

Useful Mega Boost for Teams

While active in battle, Mega Banette boosts:

  • Ghost-type attacks
  • Dark-type attacks

This makes it especially useful when your team is built around Ghost or Dark attackers.

Long-Term Investment Value

  • Useful in multiple raid matchups
  • Stays relevant for PvE content over time
  • Good addition if you focus on raid performance

Shiny Chance

You also get a chance to encounter Shiny Banette after completing the raid, which makes it interesting for collectors as well.

How Difficult is Mega Banette?

Overall, Mega Banette sits around a medium difficulty level, but it can feel easier or harder depending on your team size and counters.

It’s not too tanky, so with decent counters, it goes down fairly quickly. However, it can still punish weak teams with strong Ghost-type attacks.

Why it feels manageable:

  • Low defensive bulk
  • Can be defeated with a small group of 3–4 experienced players
  • Has common weaknesses like Dark and Ghost counters available

Where players struggle:

  • High damage output from charged moves
  • Shadow Ball can quickly knock out unprepared Pokémon
  • Can turn into a fast “glass cannon vs glass cannon” fight

Difficulty by Group Size

Players Difficulty

3 Medium
4–6 Easy

Final Takeaway

  • Strong point: High DPS Ghost attacker with useful Mega boost
  • Difficulty: Medium (depends heavily on team quality)

Mega Banette Raid Strategy Guide

Mega Banette is one of those raid bosses that feels easy at first because of its low bulk, but it can still punish teams that ignore its damage output.

With proper Dark or Ghost counters, the raid becomes manageable. Without preparation, Shadow Ball can quickly wipe fragile attackers.

Solo Attempts

Technically, solo clears are possible, but they are considered challenge runs rather than practical raids for most players.

Why solo is difficult

  • The raid timer is extremely tight
  • Mega Banette still has a large HP pool
  • Frequent fainting costs valuable time

What you would need

  • Top-tier counters like Shadow Tyranitar, Mega Gengar, or Hydreigon
  • Very efficient dodging
  • Strong weather conditions such as Fog

Overall: Mostly for experienced challenge players.

Duo Strategy

A duo clear is much more realistic, but it still requires strong preparation and coordination.

Recommended setup

  • High-level Dark and Ghost attackers
  • At least one active Mega Evolution
  • Players around Level 40+ with powered-up teams

Important tips

  • One player can run Mega Banette or Mega Gengar for the team boost
  • Focus more on damage output than tankiness
  • Dodge heavy charged attacks when possible

The biggest problem in duo runs is losing time from repeated fainting and relobbying.

Difficulty: Hard, but achievable with good teams.

Trio Strategy

For most players, a trio is the safest and most comfortable setup.

  • Enough total DPS to beat the timer consistently
  • Less pressure on perfect dodging
  • More forgiving if someone faints

Recommended approach

  • Use strong Dark-type attackers like Tyranitar or Hydreigon
  • Have one Mega Evolution active for team boosts
  • Dodge only major charged attacks instead of every move

Best balance overall: Safety, speed, and consistency.

Beginner Tips

If you are newer to raids, the easiest strategy is simply joining a larger group.

  • 4–6 trainers usually make the raid comfortable
  • Auto-selected teams are acceptable for casual clears
  • Focus on staying in battle rather than perfect dodging

Avoid:

  • Very low CP Pokémon
  • Entering raids without revives or potions ready

In larger groups, Mega Banette is generally an easy raid boss.

Intermediate Strategy

Intermediate players should focus on improving efficiency rather than simply surviving.

  • Build proper Dark and Ghost raid teams
  • Practice dodging charged attacks only
  • Use at least one Mega Evolution for extra team damage

The goal here is balancing damage output with survivability.

Advanced Strategy

At higher levels, the focus shifts toward speed and optimization.

Advanced techniques

  • Use pre-built raid parties
  • Coordinate Mega boosts with teammates
  • Dodge selectively instead of over-dodging
  • Relobby quickly to reduce downtime

Well-organized groups can clear Mega Banette very quickly with optimized Dark-type teams.

Expert-Level Play

Expert players usually approach Mega Banette as a speed-clear or minimum-player challenge.

Typical expert strategies

  • Level 50 optimized counters
  • Perfect dodge timing
  • Instant relobby execution
  • Heavy glass-cannon raid teams

In coordinated groups, Mega boosts are carefully planned to maximize team DPS throughout the raid.

Focus: Maximum efficiency with minimal wasted time.

Final Strategy Notes

Mega Banette rewards aggressive play, but careless teams can still struggle because of its strong Ghost-type damage.

  • High damage helps end raids quickly
  • Low bulk means mistakes are punished hard
  • Smart dodging matters more than pure tankiness

For most players, a coordinated trio with Dark-type counters is the ideal way to handle this raid.

Evolution and Buddy Distance Guide: Mega Banette

Mega Banette follows a simple evolution line, but understanding how Mega Energy and buddy walking work can save a lot of time in Pokémon GO.

Evolution Line

The full evolution path is:

Shuppet → Banette → Mega Banette

Evolution Requirements

Evolution Requirement
Shuppet → Banette 50 Candy
Banette → Mega Banette 100 Mega Energy

How Mega Evolution Works

Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO is temporary rather than permanent.

  • You first evolve Banette normally using candy
  • After that, Mega Energy is used to activate Mega Banette
  • The Mega form lasts for several hours before returning to normal

The first Mega Evolution costs the full amount of Mega Energy, but future Mega Evolutions become cheaper over time because of the cooldown system.

Important Details

  • First Mega Evolution: 100 Mega Energy
  • Later Mega Evolutions: Reduced cost or free after cooldown
  • Mega duration: Around 8 hours

Mega Banette is mainly useful for boosting Ghost- and Dark-type raid teams.

Buddy Distance

Shuppet and Banette both require:

Pokémon Candy Distance
Shuppet / Banette 3 km per candy

This means you earn one candy for every 3 km walked while Banette is your active buddy.

Walking for Mega Energy

After Mega Evolving Banette at least once, you can also start earning Mega Energy by walking it as your buddy.

This is one of the easiest long-term ways to maintain Mega Energy without constantly repeating raids.

Why this matters

  • Helps refill Mega Energy passively
  • Reduces raid dependency
  • Useful for players who regularly walk or play daily

Best Progression Strategy

A simple long-term strategy works best for Mega Banette.

  • Complete Mega Banette raids to collect initial Mega Energy
  • Mega Evolve Banette once
  • Set Banette as your buddy afterward
  • Earn extra candy and Mega Energy while walking

Once unlocked, Mega Banette becomes much easier to maintain over time.

Common Mistakes

Not Mega Evolving It Once

Players sometimes collect Mega Energy but never activate the Mega form.

Without the first Mega Evolution, walking will not generate additional Mega Energy.

Ignoring the Buddy System

Relying only on raids makes progression slower and more resource-heavy.

Using Banette as a buddy is much more efficient in the long run.

Mega Banette Counters Guide

Mega Banette is a pure Ghost-type raid boss, so Dark- and Ghost-type attackers are the best choices. The raid itself is not very bulky, but Banette hits surprisingly hard with moves like Shadow Ball, especially against fragile counters.

If your goal is a safe and consistent clear, Dark-types are usually the better option because they survive longer. Ghost-types can deal more damage, but they also faint much faster if you miss dodges.

Best Overall Counters

Pokémon Best Moveset Why It Works
Mega Gengar Lick + Shadow Ball Incredible damage output and boosts Ghost-type teammates
Shadow Tyranitar Bite + Brutal Swing Excellent mix of damage and survivability
Hydreigon Bite + Brutal Swing Reliable Dark-type attacker with strong overall performance
Darkrai Snarl + Shadow Ball Fast attacks and strong Ghost-type coverage
Chandelure Hex + Shadow Ball Strong Ghost-type damage but needs careful dodging

Dark-Type vs Ghost-Type Counters

Dark-types are generally easier to use in this raid because they resist Ghost-type damage. Pokémon like Tyranitar and Hydreigon can stay on the field much longer, which helps maintain steady DPS during smaller group raids.

Ghost-types are more aggressive options. Mega Gengar and Chandelure can melt Mega Banette quickly, but they are also vulnerable to heavy charged attacks. If Banette has Shadow Ball, fragile Ghost-types may faint before dealing full damage.

Counter Tips

  • If you are raiding with casual groups, prioritize survival over maximum DPS.
  • Mega Gengar is the strongest offensive Mega option, but Mega Tyranitar is much safer.
  • Dodging Shadow Ball can greatly reduce relobby time.
  • In group raids, one Mega Evolution boosting the team is usually enough.

Pokémon to Avoid

Psychic-types perform poorly here because they are weak to Ghost-type attacks. Neutral attackers also slow the raid down noticeably compared to proper Dark- or Ghost-type counters.

Final Thoughts

Mega Banette becomes much easier once you build the right team. Dark-types provide safer and more stable runs, while Ghost-types offer faster clears for players willing to take more risk.

Mega Banette Weakness Guide

Ghost vs Dark Counters

Dark-types are generally safer and easier to use. Tyranitar, Hydreigon, and Darkrai can survive longer while still dealing strong damage. For most players, Dark-types offer the most consistent performance during regular raids.

Normal- and Fighting-type attackers should never be used against Mega Banette because their moves are completely ineffective. This is one of the easiest ways to lose raid efficiency, especially if recommended teams accidentally include poor counters.

Bug- and Poison-type Pokémon can still deal some damage, but their performance is generally too weak compared to proper Ghost- or Dark-type attackers.

The safest and most reliable counters are Dark-types such as Tyranitar or Hydreigon. They resist Ghost-type attacks and usually survive much longer during the raid.

Conclusion: Is Mega Banette Worth Raiding?

Mega Banette is one of the more aggressive Mega Raid bosses in Pokémon GO. It does not rely on bulk or survivability like some other Megas. Instead, the raid revolves around fast damage, smart counters, and avoiding heavy charged attacks such as Shadow Ball.

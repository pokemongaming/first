Mega Pinsir Raid Introduction

Mega Pinsir is one of the strongest Bug-type Mega Evolutions available in Pokémon GO. Originally a pure Bug-type Pokémon, Mega Evolution grants it the Flying type as well, changing both its strengths and weaknesses. While Mega Pinsir may not be the most frequently used Mega Pokémon in raids, it remains a powerful attacker capable of boosting Bug-type damage for your entire team.

Whether you're raiding for Mega Energy, hunting for a shiny, or simply adding another Mega Evolution to your collection, Mega Pinsir is a worthwhile challenge for trainers looking to expand their roster.

Is Mega Pinsir Worth Raiding?

Mega Pinsir offers solid value, especially for trainers who enjoy using Bug-type attackers. Its Attack stat becomes significantly higher after Mega Evolution, allowing it to deal impressive damage against Psychic-, Dark-, and Grass-type raid bosses.

The biggest reason to raid Mega Pinsir is to collect Mega Energy. Once unlocked, you can Mega Evolve Pinsir repeatedly while benefiting from reduced energy costs over time. Even if Bug-type attackers aren't always at the top of the raid meta, having Mega Pinsir available gives you another useful option for specific raid matchups.

For shiny collectors, Mega Pinsir raids are also attractive because defeating the boss gives you a chance to encounter a shiny Pinsir.

History

Mega Pinsir joined Pokémon GO as part of the game's ongoing Mega Evolution rollout. Its arrival gave Bug-type enthusiasts another powerful Mega Evolution option and introduced a new way for trainers to strengthen Bug-focused raid teams.

Although it doesn't receive as much attention as Mega Rayquaza or Mega Garchomp, Mega Pinsir has maintained a niche role among trainers who enjoy optimizing raid counters and collecting Mega Evolutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Mega Pinsir be shiny?

Yes. After defeating Mega Pinsir, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Pinsir during the catch encounter.

How many trainers are needed?

Most groups can comfortably defeat Mega Pinsir with three trainers. Strong duos are also possible using top-tier Rock-type counters.

What is Mega Pinsir's biggest weakness?

Rock-type attacks. Mega Pinsir takes double super-effective damage from Rock moves because of its Bug/Flying typing.

What is Mega Beedrill's biggest weakness?

Psychic-type attacks are generally the most effective option against Mega Beedrill.

Is Mega Pinsir useful in raids?

Yes, particularly against Psychic and Dark-type raid bosses where Bug-type attacks are effective.

What weather is best for raiding Mega Pinsir?

Partly Cloudy weather is ideal because it boosts Rock-type attacks, allowing trainers to deal significantly more damage.

Pokédex

Pinsir is known as the Stag Beetle Pokémon. It originally debuted in the first generation of Pokémon games and quickly became known for its massive pincers and powerful physical attacks.

When it Mega Evolves, large wings emerge from its back, allowing it to fly despite its heavy body. This transformation also changes its typing from pure Bug to Bug/Flying, giving it a completely different set of strengths and weaknesses.

Mega Pinsir's design is often considered one of the most dramatic Mega Evolutions because of how drastically it changes the Pokémon's appearance.

Raid Preparation Checklist

Before entering a Mega Pinsir raid, make sure you:

  • Have at least six strong Rock-type Pokémon.
  • Stock up on Revives and Max Potions.
  • Use Mega Diancie if available.
  • Check local weather conditions.
  • Ensure enough Pokémon storage space for the encounter.
  • Have Golden Razz Berries ready for the catch phase.

A few minutes of preparation can make the raid significantly smoother.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make Against Mega Pinsir

One of the most common mistakes is ignoring Mega Pinsir's double weakness and using random high-CP Pokémon instead of dedicated Rock-type counters.

Another mistake is relying heavily on Grass, Fighting, or Ground Pokémon. While these Pokémon may be powerful in other raids, Mega Pinsir resists many of their attacks, reducing overall damage output.

Some trainers also forget to check the weather. Partly Cloudy weather can significantly improve Rock-type performance and make the raid much easier.

Team Building Strategy

A successful Mega Pinsir raid team should focus on maximizing Rock-type damage. Rather than mixing many different types, trainers often achieve better results by using a full team of Rock attackers.

A balanced team might include Pokémon such as Rampardos, Rhyperior, Tyranitar, Terrakion, and Gigalith. These Pokémon take advantage of Mega Pinsir's biggest weakness and can quickly reduce its HP.

If available, a Mega Diancie in the first slot can provide additional damage boosts for both you and your teammates.

Raid Tips for Beginners

If this is your first time battling Mega Pinsir, don't worry too much about building a perfect team. The most important thing is to bring Pokémon that can take advantage of its double weakness to Rock-type attacks. Even budget-friendly options such as Gigalith or Golem can contribute meaningful damage.

Revive and heal your strongest Rock-type Pokémon before the raid begins, and try joining larger groups if you're still building your collection. Mega Pinsir is generally considered one of the easier Mega Raid bosses when players use the correct counters.

Should You Power Up Pinsir?

If you enjoy Bug-type Pokémon or frequently participate in raids against Psychic and Dark bosses, investing in a strong Pinsir can be worthwhile.

However, if Stardust is limited, many trainers prioritize Pokémon such as Tyranitar, Rhyperior, or legendary attackers before focusing on Mega Pinsir.

A high-IV shiny Pinsir can also be a fun long-term project for collectors.

Catch Tips After the Raid

After defeating Mega Pinsir, take your time during the catch encounter.

  • Use Golden Razz Berries whenever possible.
  • Aim for Excellent Throws to maximize catch chances.
  • Wait for Pinsir's attack animation before throwing.
  • Utilize Curveball Throws for the highest catch bonus.

Even though raid Pokémon have high catch rates compared to wild encounters, consistent Excellent Curveballs can make the process much smoother.

How Much Mega Energy Do You Need?

The first Mega Evolution requires a larger amount of Mega Energy. After unlocking Mega Pinsir, future Mega Evolutions become significantly cheaper.

Participating in multiple Mega Pinsir raids during its raid rotation is usually enough to collect the required energy and build a reserve for future use.

Once Mega Level increases through repeated Mega Evolutions, energy costs become even more manageable.

Recommended Moveset

For trainers planning to use Mega Pinsir after obtaining enough Mega Energy, the strongest Bug-type moveset is generally:

  • Fast Move: Bug Bite
  • Charged Move: X-Scissor

This combination provides reliable Bug-type damage and takes full advantage of Mega Pinsir's high Attack stat.

If using it for Flying-type coverage, Flying-type moves can also be considered, though Bug-type attacks are usually the primary reason to Mega Evolve Pinsir.

Mega Pinsir Compared to Other Bug-Type Megas

When compared with other Bug-type Mega Evolutions, Mega Pinsir remains highly competitive thanks to its massive offensive power.

However, trainers may sometimes prefer Mega Beedrill because it is cheaper to Mega Evolve and easier to maintain. Mega Scizor offers more defensive durability, while Mega Pinsir focuses almost entirely on dealing damage as quickly as possible.

The best choice ultimately depends on the raid boss and your personal collection of Mega Pokémon.

Best Use Cases

Mega Pinsir performs best when facing Pokémon that are weak to Bug-type attacks. Raid bosses with Psychic or Dark typing are where it can truly show its strength.

In addition to dealing damage, Mega Pinsir provides a Bug-type Mega Boost to teammates, increasing the effectiveness of other Bug-type attackers in the raid lobby. This makes it valuable in coordinated group raids where multiple trainers are taking advantage of the boost.

Mega Pinsir Strengths and Weaknesses

Mega Pinsir occupies an interesting place in the Pokémon GO meta. While Bug-type attackers are not always the first choice for raids, Mega Pinsir shines against Psychic and Dark raid bosses where Bug damage is highly effective. Its incredible Attack stat allows it to hit hard, but its defensive weaknesses mean it often functions as a glass cannon rather than a tanky attacker.

For trainers who enjoy maximizing raid efficiency, Mega Pinsir remains a useful Mega Evolution to have unlocked even if it is not used every day.

Trivia

  • Its wings are powerful enough to keep its heavy body airborne despite its large size.
  • Mega Pinsir was introduced in Generation VI alongside several other Mega Evolutions.
  • It is one of the few Bug-type Pokémon capable of reaching extremely high Attack stats after Mega Evolution.

Personal Raid Experience

In my experience, Mega Pinsir tends to be one of the smoother Mega Raids whenever strong Rock-type counters are available. Battles often feel straightforward because its double weakness allows trainers to deal huge amounts of damage quickly.

One thing that stands out is how quickly Pokémon like Rampardos and Rhyperior can melt through Mega Pinsir's health bar. Even smaller raid groups often finish with plenty of time remaining.

The raid is also enjoyable for shiny hunters because each victory provides another chance at obtaining the distinctive blue shiny Pinsir. While Mega Pinsir may not receive as much attention as some Dragon- or Legendary-focused raids, it's generally an easy and rewarding Mega Raid to complete.

Shiny Mega Pinsir Information

Yes, shiny Pinsir can be encountered after successfully completing a Mega Pinsir Raid.

Shiny Pinsir is easy to recognize thanks to its noticeable blue coloration, which replaces the standard brown appearance. Many trainers consider it one of the more visually appealing Bug-type shinies in Pokémon GO.

Like other raid shinies, encountering one is completely random, so multiple raids may be necessary before finding your blue-colored Pinsir.

The shiny remains available after Mega Evolution, allowing you to use a shiny Mega Pinsir in battles and raids.

Mega Pinsir CP

After defeating Mega Pinsir, you'll encounter a regular Pinsir.

A perfect IV Pinsir from the raid encounter has a CP of 1692 at Level 20.

If weather boosted, the perfect IV Pinsir reaches 2115 CP at Level 25.

These CP values are useful for quickly identifying potentially strong catches before conducting a full appraisal.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can significantly influence both the raid battle and the Pokémon you catch afterward.

Rainy weather boosts Mega Pinsir's Bug-type attacks, making the raid boss slightly more dangerous. Windy weather boosts its Flying-type attacks for the same reason.

Partly Cloudy weather is ideal for raiders because it boosts Rock-type attacks, allowing your counters to deal even more damage. Under these conditions, Mega Pinsir can fall surprisingly quickly.

When caught during Rainy or Windy weather, Pinsir will also appear at a higher level, reducing the amount of Stardust and Candy needed for future powering up.

Can Mega Pinsir Be Soloed, Duoed, or Trioed?

Mega Pinsir is one of the more accessible Mega Raids due to its double weakness to Rock-type attacks.

Highly experienced trainers with maxed-out counters, favorable weather, and strong friendship bonuses may be able to complete a solo attempt, though it remains challenging and often depends on movesets and team quality.

A duo is much more realistic and can usually be achieved by trainers with strong Rock-type teams.

For most players, a trio provides a comfortable and reliable victory. Groups of three to five trainers should have little trouble defeating Mega Pinsir within the raid timer.

Best Counters for Mega Pinsir

Rock-type Pokémon dominate this raid thanks to Mega Pinsir's double weakness.

Shadow Rampardos is one of the strongest choices available, capable of dealing incredible damage with Smack Down and Rock Slide. Shadow Rhyperior also performs exceptionally well thanks to its combination of high bulk and strong Rock-type attacks.

For trainers who prefer Mega Evolutions, Mega Diancie is arguably the best counter in the game. Not only does it deal tremendous damage, but it also boosts the attacks of other Rock-type Pokémon on your team.

Other excellent options include Rampardos, Rhyperior, Terrakion, Tyranitar, and Gigalith. Even trainers without top-tier raid teams can contribute effectively by focusing on high-level Rock-type attackers.

Mega Pinsir Weaknesses

Mega Pinsir's Bug/Flying typing creates an interesting defensive profile. While it gains useful resistances to Fighting, Grass, and Ground-type attacks, it also becomes extremely vulnerable to Rock-type moves.

This double weakness to Rock is the key to defeating Mega Pinsir efficiently. Rock-type attackers can deal massive damage, making battles significantly easier and reducing the number of trainers required.

Mega Pinsir is also weak to Electric, Fire, Flying, and Ice-type attacks, though these weaknesses are less severe than its devastating Rock weakness.

Whenever possible, prioritize powerful Rock-type Pokémon, as they consistently deliver the best raid performance.

Conclusion

Mega Pinsir may not generate as much excitement as some Legendary or Dragon-type raid bosses, but it remains a solid Mega Evolution with impressive offensive potential. Its double weakness to Rock makes the raid approachable for most trainers, while the opportunity to earn Mega Energy and hunt for a shiny provides additional incentive to participate.

For players looking to expand their Mega roster, Mega Pinsir is definitely worth completing at least a few raids whenever it returns to Pokémon GO.

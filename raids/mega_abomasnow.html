What is Mega Abomasnow?

Mega Abomasnow is the Mega Evolution of Abomasnow, an Ice/Grass-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region.

When it Mega Evolves, its stats—especially Attack and Defense—get a significant boost, making it much stronger in raids and group battles.

  • Higher Attack and Defense after Mega Evolution
  • Boosts Ice- and Grass-type damage for teammates
  • Useful Mega option for raid support

Role in Mega Raids

In Pokémon GO Mega Raids, Mega Abomasnow appears as a strong boss with high CP and moderate raid difficulty.

  • High CP (around 45,000+)
  • Best tackled with 3–6 trainers depending on counters
  • Becomes much easier with Fire-type teams

Its Ice/Grass typing makes it predictable once you prepare the right counters.

Why Players Raid It

Most trainers do this raid for a few simple reasons:

  • To earn Mega Energy for Abomasnow
  • To catch a strong IV Abomasnow
  • To unlock a useful Ice/Grass Mega for future raids

It’s especially helpful for boosting Ice-type attackers used against Dragon raid bosses.

Quick Summary

  • Ice/Grass Mega Raid Boss
  • Weak to Fire (4× damage)
  • More valuable for its Mega boost than raw difficulty

Why It’s Worth Doing

Mega Abomasnow is mainly useful because of its Mega boost. Once activated, it increases Ice- and Grass-type damage for your entire raid team, which helps a lot in Dragon and Ground-type matchups.

It also fits well into Ice-type raid teams, especially during weather-boosted conditions.

  • Improves overall raid damage output
  • Good support Mega for Ice-focused teams
  • Useful in weather-boosted raid setups

Difficulty Overview

This is a moderate-difficulty Mega Raid, but Fire-types make a huge difference.

  • Strong Fire teams → Easy to Medium
  • Average counters → Medium
  • Weak or unprepared teams → Hard

With proper Fire attackers, even 2–3 players can handle it comfortably. Larger groups make it very easy.

It can feel harder if it uses strong charged moves like Blizzard or if players ignore its Fire weakness.

Quick Verdict

  • Value: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (Very useful Mega)
  • Difficulty: ⭐⭐☆☆☆ (Easy with proper counters)

Performance Guide – PvP, PvE, Raids, Gyms & Cups

Abomasnow is a bit of a mixed Pokémon depending on where you use it. In some modes it feels average, but in PvP it can actually be one of the strongest Ice-type options in the game.

PvE (General Gameplay)

In PvE content like Team Rocket battles or general encounters, Abomasnow does an okay job but isn’t a standout attacker.

  • Good Ice-type damage for Dragons, Flying, and Ground Pokémon
  • Slightly bulkier than many Ice-types
  • Can provide decent coverage with Ice + Grass typing

On the downside, its many weaknesses mean it doesn’t stay on the field for long against strong opponents.

Verdict: Usable when you need Ice coverage, but not something you build a team around.

Raids

As a Raid Attacker

  • Works well as a budget Ice-type attacker
  • Helps against Dragon and Ground raid bosses
  • Benefits a lot from weather boosts

As a Mega

  • Provides a useful Ice and Grass damage boost to the entire team
  • One of the more accessible Mega Ice options
  • Improves overall raid speed for the group

Verdict: More valuable as a Mega support Pokémon than as a main attacker.

Gyms

Abomasnow doesn’t really shine in gym battles on either side.

Defense

  • Gets countered easily by Fire and Fighting types
  • Not durable enough to stall attackers

Offense

  • Can handle some Water, Ground, and Flying defenders
  • Better options are usually available

Verdict: Mostly situational in gyms, not a priority pick.

PvP (Trainer Battles)

This is where Abomasnow becomes genuinely strong, especially in lower leagues.

What makes it good:

  • Powder Snow generates energy very quickly
  • Weather Ball (Ice) applies constant shield pressure
  • Grass + Ice coverage hits a wide range of Pokémon

Great League

  • Fast move pressure forces shields early

Ultra League

  • Not as dominant as in Great League

Master League

  • Outclassed by legendary and pseudo-legendary Pokémon

Special PvP Cups

Limited formats are where Abomasnow often feels strongest again.

  • Weather Cup – very strong due to typing advantage
  • Holiday Cup – consistent top pick
  • Ice/Grass restricted formats – usually central to the meta

Its fast move pressure and flexible typing make it very annoying to face in these cups.

Raid Strategy Guide – Mega Abomasnow

How hard this raid feels depends mostly on your team size and how strong your Fire-types are. Because of the 4× Fire weakness, the raid becomes much easier as soon as you build around the right counters.

Solo Attempt

This one is basically not meant for solo play.

Mega Abomasnow has enough HP that even strong players will struggle to finish it alone under normal conditions.

  • Not recommended for solo attempts
  • Only possible in very specific setups (weather boost + maxed Fire counters)

Duo (2 Players)

Duo is technically possible, but it’s still quite demanding.

You’ll need strong coordination and proper Fire teams on both sides.

  • High-level trainers (preferably Level 40+)
  • Strong Fire attackers like Reshiram or Mega Charizard Y
  • Best Friend bonus and weather boost make a big difference

Most duos succeed mainly because Fire damage is so heavily boosted against it.

Trio (3 Players)

This is where the raid starts to feel comfortable for most groups.

  • Decent Fire-type teams are enough
  • Less pressure on perfect dodging
  • More forgiving if a few Pokémon faint early

For many players, trio is the sweet spot between challenge and reliability.

Group Raids (4–6 Players)

With a full group, this raid becomes very straightforward.

  • No strict coordination needed
  • Even average counters can finish it easily
  • Focus is just on consistent damage output

This is the most common and safest way to clear Mega Abomasnow.

How Players Approach This Raid

Beginner Approach

  • Join larger groups (4+ players)
  • Use recommended teams if unsure
  • Don’t worry too much about dodging

Goal: just complete the raid comfortably

Intermediate Approach

  • Build a proper Fire-type team
  • Start learning when to dodge charged moves
  • Avoid using resisted Pokémon like Grass-types

Goal: better damage and fewer fainted Pokémon

Advanced Approach

  • Use top Fire attackers (Mega/Shadow if available)
  • Focus on DPS instead of over-dodging
  • Time Mega Evolution for maximum team benefit

Goal: faster clears and higher contribution

Expert Approach

  • Attempt duo or fast-clear challenges
  • Use optimized teams with XL-powered Pokémon
  • Coordinate Mega boosts with teammates

At this level, it’s about efficiency and execution rather than just winning.

Raid Difficulty Overview

Team Size Difficulty Experience

Duo Hard Requires strong Fire teams
Trio Medium Balanced and reliable
4–6 Players Easy Very safe clear

Final Takeaway

  • Fire-types make or break this raid
  • Smaller groups need stronger coordination
  • Larger groups can clear it very easily

In raids, good counters are Pokémon that deal strong damage while staying on the field long enough to keep attacking.

  • Deal super-effective damage
  • Stay alive long enough to maintain DPS

Mega Abomasnow is one of those raid bosses where the typing works heavily against it. Because of its Ice / Grass combination, it has a lot of exploitable weaknesses, with Fire standing out as the clear best option.

Fire-type — 4× Damage

Fire is the main reason this raid is considered easy.

  • Even mid-tier Fire attackers perform very well
  • Makes the raid much faster compared to other Megas

Mega Swampert Overview

Mega Swampert is one of the strongest Water-type Mega Evolutions available in Pokémon GO. It combines excellent bulk with impressive attack power, making it a valuable Pokémon for raids, Gym battles, and certain PvP formats. Whenever Mega Swampert returns to Mega Raids, many trainers prioritize farming Mega Energy because it remains useful long after the event ends.

Unlike many raid bosses that have multiple weaknesses, Mega Swampert has only one weakness. However, that weakness is a massive double weakness, which makes the raid surprisingly manageable if you bring the right counters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mega Swampert weak to?

Grass-type attacks.

Is Mega Swampert easy to beat?

Yes, compared to many Mega Raid bosses, it is relatively easy because of its double weakness to Grass.

How many players are needed?

Is Mega Swampert worth Mega Evolving?

Yes, it remains one of the most useful Water-type Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO.

Is Mega Swampert Worth Raiding?

Absolutely. Mega Swampert is considered one of the best Mega Evolutions in the game thanks to its strong Water-type damage output and useful Water/Ground typing.

Reasons to raid Mega Swampert:

  • Powerful Water-type attacker for raids.
  • Useful Mega Evolution for boosting Water, Ground, and Electric-type catches.
  • Relatively easy raid boss when proper Grass-type counters are used.

Even experienced trainers often continue raiding Mega Swampert whenever it returns because its Mega Evolution remains relevant in many raid rotations.

Mega Swampert Weakness

Mega Swampert is a Water and Ground-type Pokémon.

At first glance, this typing seems extremely strong because it removes Electric-type weaknesses and provides useful resistances. However, it comes with one major drawback.

Mega Swampert has a double weakness to Grass-type attacks.

This means Grass-type moves deal significantly more damage than normal, allowing trainers to defeat Mega Swampert much faster than many other Mega Raid bosses. A strong team of Grass attackers can make the battle surprisingly comfortable.

Mega Swampert Resistances

Mega Swampert resists several common attack types:

  • Fire
  • Poison
  • Rock
  • Steel
  • Electric

Because of these resistances, non-Grass teams may struggle to deal efficient damage. Trainers should prioritize Grass attackers whenever possible.

Best Mega Swampert Counters

Grass-type Pokémon dominate this raid because of Mega Swampert's double weakness.

Top counter options include:

Kartana

Kartana remains one of the strongest counters available. Its incredible attack stat combined with Razor Leaf and Leaf Blade allows it to shred Mega Swampert's HP bar at an impressive rate.

Mega Sceptile

Mega Sceptile deals tremendous Grass-type damage while also boosting the attacks of other Grass-type Pokémon in the raid. It is one of the best Mega Evolutions to bring.

Zarude

Zarude combines excellent survivability with strong Grass-type attacks, making it a reliable choice throughout the battle.

Mega Venusaur

Mega Venusaur offers a balance of damage and durability. It may not hit quite as hard as some glass-cannon options, but it stays alive longer and supports other Grass attackers.

Roserade

Roserade remains a budget-friendly powerhouse that many trainers already have built. It performs exceptionally well against Mega Swampert.

Tapu Bulu

Tapu Bulu's powerful Grass-type moves take full advantage of Mega Swampert's double weakness, making it another excellent choice.

Can Mega Swampert Be Soloed?

Under ideal conditions, highly experienced trainers with top-level Grass-type counters may be able to solo Mega Swampert.

Factors that help include:

  • Sunny weather boost
  • Level 40-50 counters
  • Best Friend bonus
  • Strong Mega Evolution support

For most players, a duo is much more realistic and comfortable.

Recommended Group Sizes

  • Solo: Possible for expert trainers with optimized teams.
  • Duo: Easily achievable with strong Grass counters.
  • Trio: Very safe and consistent.
  • 4+ Trainers: Usually an easy victory.

Mega Swampert is generally considered one of the easier Mega Raid bosses due to its double Grass weakness.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can significantly affect the difficulty of the raid.

Rainy Weather

Rain boosts Mega Swampert's Water-type moves, increasing the damage it deals. The raid may become slightly more challenging.

Sunny Weather

Sunny weather boosts Grass-type attacks, making it the ideal condition for raiding Mega Swampert. Trainers can take advantage of increased damage and faster clear times.

If possible, scheduling raids during sunny weather provides the best overall experience.

Shiny Mega Swampert

The shiny version features a distinctive purple coloration that stands out from the standard blue appearance. Many trainers consider it one of the better-looking shiny starter Pokémon.

As with other Mega Raids, defeating Mega Swampert gives you a chance to encounter a shiny Swampert in the catch screen.

Mega Swampert CP Chart

Raid Boss CP

Mega Swampert appears at a high CP during Mega Raids, reflecting its status as a Mega-Evolved Pokémon.

Catch CP Range

After defeating the raid, trainers can encounter a regular Swampert.

The CP range depends on weather conditions:

  • Normal conditions: Lower catch CP range.
  • Weather boosted: Higher catch CP range.

Checking IV charts after each catch can help identify strong candidates for future Mega Evolution use.

Personal Raid Experience

In my experience, Mega Swampert is one of the more enjoyable Mega Raid bosses because the strategy is straightforward. Bringing a team full of Grass-type attackers creates a noticeable difference in battle performance. Health bars disappear quickly, and even small groups can secure comfortable victories.

The biggest mistake trainers make is using strong Water, Ground, or neutral attackers instead of fully committing to Grass-types. Once a team is optimized around its double weakness, Mega Swampert becomes significantly easier than many other Mega Raid bosses.

Why Trainers Love Mega Swampert

Mega Swampert remains popular because it is useful in multiple areas of Pokémon GO. Unlike some Mega Evolutions that only shine during specific events, Mega Swampert consistently provides value through raid battles, Mega bonuses, and strong offensive performance. Many trainers consider it one of the safest investments for Mega Energy.

Mega Swampert Lore and Background

Mega Swampert is the Mega Evolution of Swampert, the final evolution of Mudkip, one of the three starter Pokémon from the Hoenn region. Known for its immense physical strength, Mega Swampert can easily drag large ships and move massive boulders. Its muscular arms become significantly larger after Mega Evolution, allowing it to unleash incredibly powerful attacks.

Best Moveset for Mega Swampert

For raid battles, the most commonly recommended moveset is:

Fast Move

  • Water Gun

Charged Move

  • Hydro Cannon

This combination provides the highest Water-type damage output and makes Mega Swampert a powerful choice against many raid bosses.

For trainers who want Ground-type coverage, Earthquake can also be useful in certain situations.

How to Get Mega Swampert Energy

Mega Energy can primarily be earned by defeating Mega Swampert in Mega Raids. The amount received depends on how quickly the raid boss is defeated.

Once Mega Swampert has been Mega Evolved for the first time, additional Mega Energy can be earned by walking Swampert as your buddy.

Mega Evolution Bonuses

When Mega Swampert is active, trainers receive several benefits:

  • Increased damage from certain Pokémon types in raids.
  • Bonus Candy from matching Pokémon catches.
  • Increased chance of earning XL Candy at higher trainer levels.
  • Useful catch bonuses during Water and Ground-type events.

These bonuses make Mega Swampert valuable even when it is not actively battling.

Common Mistakes During Mega Swampert Raids

Many trainers make the raid harder than necessary by:

  • Using Electric-type attackers.
  • Bringing neutral legendary Pokémon.
  • Ignoring Grass-type Mega Evolutions.
  • Not taking advantage of weather boosts.

A team focused entirely on strong Grass attackers will usually outperform a mixed team.

Preparing Before the Raid

Before joining a Mega Swampert raid, consider:

  • Reviving and healing your Grass-type attackers.
  • Mega Evolving a Grass-type Pokémon.
  • Checking weather conditions.
  • Ensuring enough storage space for rewards and catches.
  • Having Golden Razz Berries ready for the catch encounter.

A few minutes of preparation can make the raid significantly smoother.

Conclusion

Mega Swampert is a raid boss that every Pokémon GO trainer should consider tackling. It offers valuable Mega Energy, strong battle performance, and the chance to obtain a shiny Swampert. While its stats make it look intimidating at first, its double weakness to Grass-type attacks makes the raid far more approachable than many other Mega Evolutions. Build a strong Grass-type team, take advantage of sunny weather when possible, and Mega Swampert can become one of the easiest and most rewarding Mega Raids to complete.

Mega Blaziken Introduction

Mega Blaziken is one of the most powerful Fire-type attackers available in Pokémon GO. Originally introduced in the Hoenn region, this Mega Evolution transforms Blaziken into an even faster and stronger battler, making it a valuable addition for trainers who regularly participate in raids and gym battles.

As a Fire and Fighting-type Pokémon, Mega Blaziken brings impressive offensive power but also has several weaknesses that experienced trainers can take advantage of. Whether you're raiding for the first time or hunting for a high-IV Mega Blaziken, this guide covers everything you need to know before challenging this Mega Raid Boss.

Catch Tips After Raid

After defeating Mega Blaziken, catching Blaziken becomes important.

Tips:

  • Use Golden Razz Berries for higher catch rate
  • Throw Excellent Curveballs for best results
  • Wait for attack animation before throwing
  • Check CP carefully for high IV Pokémon

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Mega Blaziken be soloed?

Only by high-level trainers with top counters and weather boost.

What is Mega Blaziken weak to?

Flying, Ground, Water, and Psychic types.

Is Mega Blaziken good for raids?

Yes, it is one of the best Fire-type attackers.

Can it be shiny?

Yes, shiny Blaziken is available from Mega Raids.

Move Set Threat Analysis

Mega Blaziken can run dangerous moves like:

  • Blaze Kick (Fire)
  • Blast Burn (Fire)
  • Brave Bird (Flying coverage in some cases)
  • Focus Blast (Fighting)

Why this matters:

  • Fire moves punish Grass/Ice counters
  • Fighting moves punish Rock/Steel counters

Beginner vs Advanced Strategy

Beginner Strategy

If you’re new or low-level, focus on survival. Use recommended counters from the game and join larger groups instead of attempting small-team raids.

Advanced Strategy

Experienced players should focus on maximizing DPS using Shadow Pokémon, Mega boosts, and weather synergy. Dodging charged moves can also increase damage uptime.

Recommended Team Setup

A balanced raid team against Mega Blaziken usually includes:

  • 2–3 strong Flying-type attackers
  • 1 Water-type attacker for backup coverage
  • 1 Psychic-type attacker for consistency
  • Optional Mega Pokémon for team boost

Raid Difficulty Breakdown

Mega Blaziken is generally considered a mid-difficulty Mega Raid Boss. It is not as tanky as some Legendary-tier raids, but it can still punish unprepared teams due to its high Attack stat. With proper counters, most groups will find it manageable rather than difficult.

Should You Use Remote Raid Passes?

Mega Blaziken is usually a reasonable target for Remote Raid Passes because:

  • It provides useful Mega Energy.
  • It has strong raid utility.
  • Shiny hunters often target it.
  • Smaller groups can defeat it successfully.

If you need a strong Fire-type Mega Evolution, spending a few Remote Raid Passes can be worthwhile.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make

Many trainers accidentally make these mistakes:

  • Using Grass-type attackers because Blaziken is part Fighting-type.
  • Ignoring Flying-type counters.
  • Forgetting to Mega Evolve before entering the raid.
  • Using low-level Pokémon with poor movesets.
  • Not reviving their strongest attackers before a second raid attempt.

Avoiding these mistakes can noticeably improve raid performance.

Best Moveset for Blaziken

After catching Blaziken, trainers may want to optimize its moves.

For Fire-type damage:

  • Fire Spin
  • Blast Burn

For Fighting-type damage:

  • Counter
  • Focus Blast

Blast Burn remains one of Blaziken's strongest attacks and is usually the preferred choice for PvE content.

Mega Energy Explained

Defeating Mega Blaziken rewards Mega Energy, which is required to Mega Evolve your own Blaziken.

The amount of Mega Energy earned generally depends on how quickly the raid is completed. Faster victories usually result in higher Mega Energy rewards.

Once enough Mega Energy has been collected, trainers can Mega Evolve Blaziken and take advantage of its increased combat power and raid bonuses.

Best Mega Evolutions to Use

If available, consider Mega Evolving one of these Pokémon before the raid:

  • Mega Rayquaza
  • Mega Latios
  • Mega Swampert
  • Mega Alakazam
  • Mega Pidgeot

These Mega Evolutions either deal excellent damage or provide useful raid-wide attack boosts.

Resistances

Understanding what Mega Blaziken resists can help trainers avoid bringing ineffective counters.

Mega Blaziken resists:

  • Fire
  • Grass
  • Ice
  • Dark
  • Steel
  • Bug

Because of these resistances, Pokémon that rely on these attack types may deal noticeably less damage than expected.

Battle Tips

A few simple strategies can make this raid significantly easier:

  • Dodge powerful charged attacks if your Pokémon are fragile.
  • Use Flying-type attackers whenever possible.
  • Mega Evolve a Flying-type Pokémon to boost team damage.
  • Join raids with Best Friends for additional attack bonuses.
  • Activate Party Power if raiding with local friends.

These small advantages can save revives and help finish the raid faster.

Why Trainers Raid Mega Blaziken

Mega Blaziken isn't just another Mega Evolution. Many trainers raid it because it serves multiple purposes at once. It is useful for collecting Mega Energy, strengthening Fire-type raid teams, and hunting for a shiny Blaziken. Since Fire-type attackers are frequently useful in Pokémon GO, investing in Mega Blaziken can provide long-term value for future raids.

Personal Raid Experience

Mega Blaziken is usually a fast-paced raid that rewards strong Flying-type teams. During previous raid rotations, many trainers found that Mega Rayquaza and Shadow Moltres could melt through its health surprisingly quickly.

One of the most enjoyable aspects of this raid is how accessible it feels. Even smaller groups can complete it comfortably with proper counters, making it a good choice for local raid groups and remote raid invitations alike.

If you're looking for Mega Energy, a strong Fire-type attacker, or simply another shiny to add to your collection, Mega Blaziken is a raid that delivers plenty of value.

Trivia

  • Mega Blaziken was the first Mega Evolution revealed for a Hoenn starter Pokémon.
  • It gains the Ability Speed Boost in the main series games.
  • Mega Blaziken's leg flames become even more intense after Mega Evolving.
  • It remains one of the most popular Fire-type Pokémon among trainers worldwide.
  • Mega Blaziken combines both Fire and Fighting types, giving it powerful offensive coverage..

Mega Blaziken CP

After successfully defeating Mega Blaziken, trainers encounter regular Blaziken.

Typical CP values include:

  • Normal encounter (Level 20): around 1500–1600 CP range.
  • Weather boosted encounter (Level 25): around 1900–2000 CP range.

A perfect IV Blaziken will appear at the maximum CP available for the encounter level.

Always check IVs before transferring, especially if you're planning to Mega Evolve the Pokémon later.

Shiny Mega Blaziken Information

Yes, shiny Blaziken can be encountered after winning a Mega Blaziken raid.

The shiny version features a darker body color and a noticeable change in its overall appearance. While shiny odds are not guaranteed, Mega Raids generally provide one of the best opportunities to hunt shiny Pokémon.

If you are a shiny collector, Mega Blaziken raids are definitely worth completing whenever they return to the game.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can have a major impact on this raid.

Sunny weather boosts Fire-type moves used by Mega Blaziken, making the boss more dangerous. However, it also boosts Ground-type attackers that can be used against it.

Rainy weather boosts Water-type attacks, helping trainers deal extra damage.

Windy weather benefits Flying and Psychic-type attacks, making some of the strongest counters even more effective.

For faster raid completions, many trainers prefer battling Mega Blaziken during Windy or Rainy weather conditions.

Best Counters for Mega Blaziken

The easiest way to defeat Mega Blaziken is by using high-level Flying, Ground, Water, or Psychic-type attackers.

Top counter choices include:

  • Mega Rayquaza
  • Rayquaza
  • Shadow Moltres
  • Mega Latios
  • Primal Kyogre
  • Mewtwo
  • Mega Swampert
  • Mega Alakazam
  • Shadow Mewtwo

Flying-type attackers usually perform the best because Mega Blaziken takes significant damage from Flying moves. Trainers with access to Mega Rayquaza will notice extremely fast clear times thanks to its overwhelming damage output.

Water-type attackers such as Primal Kyogre also remain excellent choices, especially during rainy weather.

Mega Blaziken Weaknesses

Mega Blaziken is a Fire and Fighting-type Pokémon. This typing gives it several useful resistances but also leaves it vulnerable to a few powerful attack types.

Mega Blaziken is weak to:

  • Flying
  • Ground
  • Water
  • Psychic

Flying-type attacks are particularly effective because Flying-type moves exploit its Fighting typing while avoiding resistance from its Fire typing.

When preparing for this raid, prioritize powerful Flying-type attackers if possible. Pokémon such as Rayquaza, Mega Rayquaza, and Shadow Moltres can deal tremendous damage throughout the battle.

Can Mega Blaziken Be Soloed?

Highly experienced trainers with top-tier counters may be able to complete the raid solo under ideal conditions, but it is generally considered a difficult challenge.

Recommended group sizes:

  • Solo: Possible for elite trainers with optimal counters.
  • Duo: Comfortable with strong level 40+ teams.
  • Trio: Easy for most experienced players.
  • 4+ Trainers: Very safe and beginner-friendly.

Weather boosts, friendship bonuses, party power, and Mega Evolutions can significantly reduce raid difficulty.

Is Mega Blaziken Worth Raiding?

For most trainers, the answer is yes.

Mega Blaziken is currently among the strongest Fire-type attackers in Pokémon GO and performs exceptionally well against many raid bosses. It also provides useful Mega Boost bonuses during raids, helping teammates deal more damage when using Fire, Fighting, or Flying-type Pokémon.

If you enjoy collecting strong Mega Evolutions, participating in raid events, or building powerful teams for PvE content, Mega Blaziken is definitely worth adding to your collection.

Reasons to raid Mega Blaziken:

  • One of the strongest Fire-type Mega Evolutions.
  • Excellent raid attacker.
  • Useful Mega Boost for teammates.
  • Strong future investment for PvE content.
  • Chance to encounter a shiny Blaziken after the raid.

Conclusion

Mega Blaziken is a rewarding Mega Raid Boss that offers strong battle performance, useful Mega bonuses, and a chance at a shiny encounter. Bring powerful Flying, Water, Ground, or Psychic-type Pokémon, gather a few friends, and you'll have little trouble earning Mega Energy and adding this fiery powerhouse to your collection.

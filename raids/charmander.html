About Charmander

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and one of the original starter Pokémon introduced in Generation I. Recognizable by the flame burning at the tip of its tail, Charmander has become one of the most beloved Pokémon of all time.

In Pokémon GO, Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and eventually into Charizard, one of the strongest and most popular Pokémon in the game. Thanks to its powerful final evolution, Mega Evolutions, and shiny form, Charmander remains a Pokémon that many trainers actively seek.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Charmander good in Pokémon GO?

Charmander itself is mainly valuable because it evolves into Charizard, a strong Fire-type Pokémon.

Can Charmander be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Charmander is available and can evolve into shiny Charizard.

What is Charmander weak to?

Charmander is weak to Ground-, Rock-, and Water-type attacks.

Should I evolve Charmander?

Yes. Evolving Charmander into Charizard is highly recommended because Charizard is useful in raids, PvP, and Mega Evolution battles.

Is shiny Charmander rare?

Outside of special events, shiny Charmander can be difficult to find, making it a desirable shiny Pokémon.

What makes Charizard special?

Charizard has two Mega Evolutions, strong battle performance, exclusive moves, and one of the most popular shiny forms in Pokémon history.

Charmander Stats in Pokémon GO

Charmander serves as the first stage of the Charizard evolution line and is mainly collected for evolution purposes.

  • Attack: 116
  • Defense: 93
  • Stamina: 118
  • Max CP: 1108 (Level 50)

Although Charmander itself isn't used in high-level battles, evolving it unlocks one of the best Fire-type Pokémon in the game.

Charmander in PvP

Charmander has limited PvP usage due to its low CP and stats.

Where It Can Be Used

  • Little Cup

Most competitive players prefer Charizard, which performs significantly better in PvP.

Charmander in Raids

Charmander is not suitable for raid battles because its evolved forms offer much higher damage output.

The main goal is to evolve Charmander into Charizard, which can become a powerful Fire-type attacker.

Why Charmander Is Valuable

Charmander's value comes from its evolution line.

Benefits of Charizard

  • Strong Fire-type attacker
  • Excellent PvP performance
  • Access to exclusive moves
  • Two Mega Evolutions
  • Popular shiny form

Because of these advantages, many trainers continue to collect Charmander Candy long after catching their first one.

Shiny Charmander

Shiny Charmander is available in Pokémon GO.

Shiny Appearance

  • The body changes from orange to golden-yellow.
  • The shiny form becomes even more impressive after evolving.

Many trainers hunt shiny Charmander because shiny Charizard is one of the most famous shiny Pokémon ever released.

Fun Facts About Charmander

  • Charmander is Pokédex #004.
  • It is one of the original Kanto starter Pokémon.
  • The flame on its tail reflects its health and emotions.
  • If the flame goes out, Charmander cannot survive.
  • Charmander evolves into Charizard, one of Pokémon's most popular mascots.
  • Shiny Charizard's black coloration is one of the most sought-after shiny forms in Pokémon GO.

Conclusion

Charmander remains one of the most iconic starter Pokémon in Pokémon GO. While the base form is primarily a stepping stone toward Charizard, it is still a favorite among trainers thanks to its memorable design, shiny form, and powerful evolution line. Whether you're completing your Pokédex, hunting shinies, or building a strong Fire-type team, Charmander is a Pokémon worth catching whenever you see one.

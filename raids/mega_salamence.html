Mega Salamence Raid Guide – Best Counters, Weakness, CP & Strategy

← Back to All Raid Guides

Mega Salamence raids in Pokémon GO always feel like a “final boss” moment for many trainers. It’s one of those encounters where you don’t just jump in blindly—you plan a bit, pick your counters carefully, and hope your team holds up long enough to bring it down.

Salamence in its Mega form becomes a Dragon/Flying powerhouse with huge attack pressure. That means if you’re unprepared, it can wipe raid teams quickly, especially with strong Dragon-type and Flying-type moves. The good part? It also has very clear weaknesses, so smart planning makes a big difference.

Mega Salamence Raid Overview

Mega Salamence is mainly a high-DPS raid boss. It hits hard, but it doesn’t have the tankiness of some other Mega raids, so coordinated lobbies can bring it down fairly smoothly. The key is avoiding bringing Pokémon that are weak to Dragon or Ice-type damage

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Mega Salamence be shiny?

Yes. If the shiny form is available during its raid rotation, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Salamence after winning the raid.

How many trainers are needed?

Most groups can comfortably defeat Mega Salamence with three to five prepared trainers. Larger groups make the raid significantly easier.

What is its biggest weakness?

Ice-type attacks. Mega Salamence takes double damage from Ice moves, making them the best option.

Is it worth using Rare Candy on Salamence?

For players focused on raids, investing resources into a strong Salamence can be worthwhile due to its long-term usefulness.

Best Counters

You don’t need legendary-only teams to succeed, but stronger Ice attackers make a big difference.

Good options include:

  • Mamoswine with Powder Snow + Avalanche (top-tier budget king)
  • Glaceon with Frost Breath + Avalanche (solid and easy to build)
  • Weavile with Ice Shard + Avalanche (high damage, a bit glassy)
  • Baxcalibur (if available) for strong Dragon/Ice pressure
  • Rayquaza with Dragon Tail + Breaking Swipe (useful but not ideal vs Ice weakness focus)

If you coordinate with friends, stacking Ice teams makes the raid noticeably faster and safer.

Can Mega Salamence Be Soloed or Duoed?

Solo: Not realistically. The damage output and bulk make soloing impractical even with maxed counters.

Duo: Possible for very high-level trainers with optimal Ice teams and weather boosts, but it’s still tight.

Trio/4 trainers: Comfortable and recommended for most players.

5–10 trainers: Very easy clear, even with mixed teams.

Should You Keep Raiding After Getting Enough Mega Energy?

For many trainers, the answer is yes.

Additional raids provide opportunities to:

  • Find better IVs
  • Hunt for a shiny
  • Collect Rare Candy
  • Earn extra XP
  • Build a stronger Salamence collection

Once you've secured enough Mega Energy, the decision becomes more about your long-term goals than necessity.

Trainers searching for the best possible Salamence often continue raiding long after their initial Mega Evolution is unlocked.

Weather Boost Tips

Snow weather is your best friend here—it boosts Ice-type attackers and makes raids significantly easier.

Windy weather boosts Salamence’s Dragon moves, which makes it more dangerous, so be a bit more careful in that case.

CP Range

Mega Salamence CP varies depending on raid settings, but expect a high CP encounter after defeat. If you’re hunting a perfect IV, this is one raid you don’t want to rush—check each catch carefully.

Within the Pokémon GO community, Mega Salamence is generally viewed positively.

Raid-focused players appreciate its offensive capabilities, collectors enjoy hunting for shiny and high-IV versions, and long-time Pokémon fans often raid it because of their attachment to Salamence from earlier games.

Whenever Mega Salamence returns to raids, it typically attracts strong participation from both casual and dedicated trainers.

Even trainers who already own stronger Dragon attackers often raid Mega Salamence simply because it is a fan-favorite Pokémon.

For players focused on PvE progression, resources invested into Salamence rarely feel wasted.

For difficult raids or small groups, every damage bonus matters.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make

One of the biggest mistakes is ignoring Mega Salamence's double weakness to Ice-type attacks. Some players bring general Dragon counters and end up dealing far less damage than they could with dedicated Ice attackers.

Another common error is relying entirely on auto-selected battle teams. While Pokémon GO's recommendations have improved over time, manually selecting Ice-type counters usually produces better results.

Weather conditions are also frequently overlooked. Windy weather can strengthen Mega Salamence's Dragon-type attacks, making the raid more dangerous. Paying attention to weather boosts before entering the battle can help trainers choose better counters and avoid unnecessary knockouts.

Finally, many players forget to activate a Mega Evolution before raiding. Using a Mega Pokémon can provide valuable damage bonuses for the entire group and speed up the raid completion time.

Why Trainers Raid Mega Salamence

There are plenty of Dragon-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO, but Mega Salamence remains one of the most attractive raid targets for players who enjoy PvE content. Its Mega Evolution offers excellent offensive power and can boost Dragon-type damage for the entire raid group.

Many trainers choose to raid Mega Salamence not only for the chance at a strong Pokémon but also to collect Mega Energy for future battles. Whether you're preparing for legendary raids, Team GO Rocket encounters, or simply expanding your Mega Evolution collection, Mega Salamence provides long-term value.

For newer players, it can also serve as a powerful Dragon-type attacker without requiring an extensive roster of rare Pokémon.

Personal Raid Experience

During Mega Salamence raids, one thing becomes clear almost immediately: Ice-type attackers make a massive difference. Raids that seem challenging with mixed teams often become much easier when several trainers focus on Ice-type damage.

In larger lobbies, Mega Salamence usually goes down comfortably. Smaller groups, however, may notice how hard its Dragon-type attacks can hit. Pokémon that are weak to Dragon moves can faint quickly, forcing trainers to relobby if they are not careful.

The raid often feels fast-paced because Mega Salamence deals strong damage while also taking heavy damage from its double weakness to Ice. This creates a battle that is exciting without feeling overly difficult for prepared groups.

Best Mega Evolutions to Use Against Mega Salamence

Bringing a Mega Evolution into a raid does more than boost your own damage. It can also help nearby trainers deal extra damage, making the entire group more efficient.

Mega Glalie is often one of the best choices because it boosts Ice-type attacks, which directly target Mega Salamence's biggest weakness. Trainers who have access to strong Ice-type teams can see noticeably faster clear times when Mega Glalie is active.

Mega Abomasnow is another excellent option. While it may not survive as long against certain movesets, it still provides valuable Ice-type support for the raid group.

If Ice-type Mega Evolutions are unavailable, other powerful Mega Pokémon can still contribute meaningful damage, but Ice remains the preferred strategy whenever possible.

Best Budget Counters for New Players

Not everyone has a collection filled with legendary or fully powered Pokémon, and that's perfectly fine. Mega Salamence can still be defeated using several accessible options.

Mamoswine remains one of the best investments a trainer can make. It performs well in numerous raids and continues to be useful long after Mega Salamence leaves rotation.

Glaceon is another reliable choice because Eevee is relatively common and evolving one into Glaceon is much easier than obtaining some rarer Ice-type attackers.

Weavile can also deliver impressive damage if trained properly, though it tends to faint more quickly than bulkier alternatives.

For newer players, focusing on a team of strong Ice-types often provides better results than trying to build a mixed team of random counters.

Shiny Chance

Yes, shiny Salamence is available during Mega raids when Niantic enables it. If you get one, it’s purely luck-based, but the shiny form is definitely one of the more sought-after catches.

Why Ice-Type Pokémon Dominate This Raid

Many raid bosses have multiple weaknesses that perform similarly. Mega Salamence is different.

Because of its Dragon/Flying typing, Ice attacks benefit from a double weakness. This means Ice-type moves deal dramatically more damage than most alternatives.

As a result, even average Ice attackers can outperform stronger Pokémon using neutral attacks.

This double weakness is the reason Mega Salamence is often considered one of the more approachable Dragon-type Mega raids for prepared trainers.

Is Mega Salamence Worth Raiding?

Yes, especially if you need:

  • A strong Mega Evolution for Dragon-type raids
  • A powerful Dragon-type attacker for general PvE use
  • Mega energy farming for long-term value

Even outside raids, Mega Salamence is useful for boosting Dragon-type damage in your team, which makes future raids easier.

Team Composition Recommendations

For the smoothest raid experience, try building a team focused primarily on Ice-type attackers.

A balanced team might include:

  • Mamoswine
  • Glaceon
  • Weavile
  • Baxcalibur
  • Mega Glalie
  • Shadow Mamoswine

If some of these Pokémon are unavailable, simply prioritizing Ice-type damage is usually enough.

The goal is consistency rather than perfection. A full team of decent Ice attackers often performs better than a mixed group of unrelated Pokémon.

Mega Energy Farming Strategy

If your goal is to Mega Evolve Salamence regularly, you'll need a healthy supply of Mega Energy. The fastest way to earn it is by completing multiple Mega Salamence raids while it is available.

Raid completion speed matters. Finishing battles faster generally rewards more Mega Energy, so coordinating with friends or joining larger groups can be beneficial.

Once you've Mega Evolved Salamence for the first time, future Mega Evolutions become cheaper. Walking Salamence as your buddy can also help you earn additional Mega Energy over time, reducing the need to raid repeatedly.

Because Mega Salamence is useful in many raid situations, many trainers consider it worth farming beyond the minimum energy requirement.

Is Mega Salamence Good for PvE?

Mega Salamence shines most in PvE content. Its strong attack stat allows it to perform exceptionally well in raids where Dragon-type damage is valuable.

Against Dragon-type legendary bosses, Mega Salamence can contribute significant damage while simultaneously boosting the attacks of teammates using Dragon-type Pokémon.

This combination of personal damage output and team support is one of the main reasons many experienced trainers invest resources into it.

If your focus is raid battles, Mega Salamence is absolutely a worthwhile addition to your collection.

Is Mega Salamence Good for PvP?

Mega Evolutions are not commonly used in standard GO Battle League formats, so Mega Salamence does not have the same impact in PvP that it does in raids.

However, in formats where Mega Pokémon are permitted, its offensive pressure can be intimidating. It has access to powerful Dragon-type attacks and can threaten opponents that are not prepared with Fairy-type counters.

Even so, most trainers prioritize Mega Salamence for PvE rather than competitive PvP play.

Mega Salamence Weaknesses

Despite its impressive power, Mega Salamence is not without flaws.

Its biggest weakness is its double vulnerability to Ice-type attacks. This weakness can cause it to faint quickly when facing opponents that carry strong Ice moves.

Competition is another factor. Mega Rayquaza and several other Dragon-type Pokémon can challenge Mega Salamence for a spot on a trainer's raid team.

Mega Evolution also requires energy management, meaning trainers must continue collecting Mega Energy if they want regular access to its powered-up form.

Mega Salamence Strengths

Mega Salamence earns its reputation through raw offensive power. Its Dragon-type attacks can deal significant damage against many raid bosses, especially other Dragon Pokémon. Combined with the stat boost provided by Mega Evolution, it becomes one of the strongest attackers available to many trainers.

Another major strength is its ability to boost other trainers' Dragon-type attacks during raids. This team-wide support makes it valuable even when several players already have powerful counters.

Unlike some specialized Pokémon that only shine in certain situations, Mega Salamence remains useful across a wide range of PvE content.

Hundo Hunting

Whenever Mega Salamence appears in raids, many trainers begin searching for a perfect IV, often called a "hundo."

A hundo has maximum IV values in Attack, Defense, and HP. These Pokémon are highly sought after because they represent the strongest possible version of that species.

While the difference between a perfect Salamence and a strong non-perfect Salamence may not always be noticeable in everyday gameplay, collecting hundos has become a popular goal within the Pokémon GO community.

If you're planning to invest large amounts of Stardust and Candy, finding a high-IV Salamence can make that investment feel even more rewarding.

Catching Tips After the Raid

Defeating Mega Salamence is only half the challenge. Successfully catching it is equally important.

Take your time between throws instead of rushing through Premier Balls. Waiting for attack animations and aiming for consistent Excellent Throws can improve your chances of success.

Using Golden Razz Berries whenever possible is recommended, especially if the Salamence has strong IV indicators.

Patience often leads to better results than throwing quickly and hoping for the best.

Mega Salamence Compared to Other Dragon Megas

Mega Salamence often finds itself compared with other powerful Dragon-type Mega Evolutions.

Mega Rayquaza generally receives more attention due to its incredible power and versatility, while Mega Garchomp offers a different combination of typing and utility.

Even with such strong competition, Mega Salamence remains a valuable option. Not every trainer owns every Mega Evolution, and having multiple powerful Dragon-type Megas available provides flexibility for future raid lineups.

Rather than replacing other Dragon Megas, Mega Salamence complements them and expands your options.

Future Value in Pokémon GO

One reason trainers continue raiding Mega Salamence is its long-term usefulness. Dragon-type attackers rarely become completely obsolete because Dragon-type raid bosses appear regularly.

As new legendary Pokémon, Ultra Beasts, and special events arrive, powerful Dragon attackers continue to find opportunities to shine.

A well-built Mega Salamence can remain relevant for years, making it one of the safer investments among Mega Evolutions.

Trivia

Mega Salamence's Mega Evolution design is one of the most unique among Dragon-type Pokémon. Its wings transform into a large crescent-shaped structure that resembles a flying blade.

In the main series games, Mega Salamence became known for its incredible offensive power and quickly gained a reputation as one of the strongest Mega Evolutions available.

Its popularity carried over into Pokémon GO, where it remains a favorite target whenever Mega raids return.

Conclusion

Mega Salamence is the kind of raid boss that rewards preparation. Trainers who bring proper Ice-type counters can clear it efficiently, earn valuable Mega Energy, and potentially walk away with a powerful Dragon-type Pokémon for future battles.

Whether you're collecting Mega Evolutions, hunting for a shiny, searching for a hundo, or simply strengthening your raid roster, Mega Salamence offers enough value to justify multiple raid attempts during its availability.

About Charmeleon

Charmeleon is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the middle evolution of the Charmander family. It evolves from Charmander and later evolves into Charizard, one of the most popular and powerful Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

Compared to Charmander, Charmeleon is more aggressive and battle-focused. The flame on its tail burns hotter as it becomes stronger, and it is known for its fierce personality. While many trainers view Charmeleon as a stepping stone to Charizard, it can still be a useful Pokémon in certain situations and is an important part of one of Pokémon's most famous evolution lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Charmeleon good in Pokémon GO?

Charmeleon is decent in some limited PvP formats, but most trainers evolve it into Charizard.

Can Charmeleon be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Charmeleon is available and evolves into shiny Charizard.

What is Charmeleon weak to?

Charmeleon is weak to Ground-, Rock-, and Water-type attacks.

Should I evolve Charmeleon?

Yes. Charizard offers significantly stronger performance in raids, PvP, and Mega Evolution battles.

Is Charmeleon useful in PvP?

It can be used in Evolution Cup and certain themed formats, but it is rarely seen in open leagues.

How much Candy does it take to evolve Charmeleon?

Charmeleon requires 100 Charmander Candy to evolve into Charizard.

Charmeleon Stats in Pokémon GO

Charmeleon receives a significant stat boost compared to Charmander and becomes much more capable in battles.

  • Attack: 158
  • Defense: 126
  • Stamina: 151
  • Max CP: 1868 (Level 50)

While stronger than Charmander, Charmeleon is still usually outclassed by its final evolution.

Charmeleon in Raids

Charmeleon is rarely used in raids because its damage output is significantly lower than Charizard's.

For raid-focused trainers, evolving Charmeleon is almost always the better investment.

Why Evolve Charmeleon?

The main reason to keep Charmeleon is access to Charizard and its Mega Evolutions.

Benefits of Charizard

  • Powerful Fire-type attacker
  • Strong Flying-type coverage
  • Excellent PvP performance
  • Community Day exclusive moves
  • Popular shiny form

Benefits of Mega Charizard

  • Mega Charizard X gains Fire/Dragon typing.
  • Mega Charizard Y becomes one of the strongest Fire-type attackers in the game.
  • Provides raid damage boosts to teammates.

Shiny Charmeleon

Shiny Charmeleon is available in Pokémon GO.

Shiny Appearance

  • The body becomes golden-yellow.
  • The flame remains bright and vibrant.

Many trainers collect shiny Charmeleon while working toward the iconic shiny Charizard.

How to Get Charmeleon

You can obtain Charmeleon by:

  • Evolving Charmander with 25 Candy
  • Special event encounters
  • Research rewards
  • Kanto-themed events
  • Limited-time wild spawns

Evolving Charmander remains the most common method..

Fun Facts About Charmeleon

  • Charmeleon is Pokédex #005.
  • It was introduced in Generation I.
  • Its tail flame burns hotter when it becomes angry.
  • Charmeleon is much more aggressive than Charmander.
  • It evolves into Charizard, one of the most popular Pokémon ever created.
  • Shiny Charmeleon keeps the golden coloration introduced by shiny Charmander.

Conclusion

Charmeleon may not receive as much attention as Charmander or Charizard, but it remains an important part of one of Pokémon's most iconic evolution lines. Its improved stats, fiery personality, and role as the gateway to Charizard make it a memorable Pokémon for trainers. Whether you're completing your Pokédex, collecting shinies, or preparing to evolve a powerful Charizard, Charmeleon is a valuable addition to your collection.

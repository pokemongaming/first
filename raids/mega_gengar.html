Mega Gengar Overview

Mega Gengar is one of the strongest Ghost-type attackers in Pokémon GO and remains a popular Mega Raid boss whenever it returns. With its massive Attack stat and powerful Ghost- and Poison-type moves, Mega Gengar can deal heavy damage to unprepared trainers. The good news is that it has several common weaknesses, making it manageable with the right team.

Whether you're raiding for Mega Energy, hunting for a shiny, or building a top-tier Ghost attacker, Mega Gengar is usually a raid worth doing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Mega Gengar be shiny?

Yes, shiny Gengar can be encountered from Mega Gengar raids when the shiny form is available.

How many trainers are needed?

A strong duo is often enough, while three or more trainers make the raid very comfortable.

Is Mega Gengar good after Mega Evolution?

Absolutely. It remains one of the strongest Ghost-type attackers available.

Should I use Rare Candy on Gengar?

Only if you lack Gastly Candy and have a particularly strong Gengar worth powering up.

Community Opinion

Mega Gengar is consistently ranked among the most useful Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO. Most veteran players consider it a worthwhile long-term investment because of its offensive power, usefulness in raids, and highly desirable shiny Mega form.

Raid Rewards Overview

Besides the encounter itself, defeating Mega Gengar can reward trainers with:

  • Mega Energy
  • Rare Candy
  • Golden Razz Berries
  • Revives
  • Potions
  • XP bonuses

For active raiders, these rewards can be just as valuable as the Pokémon itself.

What Makes Mega Gengar Unique?

Unlike many Mega Evolutions that rely on bulk, Mega Gengar focuses almost entirely on offense. It is designed to deal damage quickly rather than absorb attacks. This high-risk, high-reward playstyle is one reason many experienced raiders enjoy using it.

How Mega Gengar Helps Future Raids

Many players view Mega Gengar as an investment.

Once Mega Evolved, it can help against numerous future bosses that are weak to Ghost-type attacks, including Psychic- and Ghost-type raid bosses. This means one strong Mega Gengar can remain useful for years.

High IV Hunting Tips

When hunting for a strong Gengar:

  • Prioritize higher CP catches.
  • Check IVs immediately after the raid.
  • Save rare high-IV catches for Mega Evolution.
  • Consider lucky trades with friends if you already own multiple Gengar.

A lucky Gengar can significantly reduce future Stardust costs.

Best Weather for Catching Gengar

Cloudy weather does not boost Gengar after the raid. Instead, look for:

  • Foggy weather for Ghost-type boosts.
  • Cloudy weather for Poison-type boosts.

Weather-boosted catches appear at higher levels, reducing the amount of Stardust needed later.

Mega Energy Farming Strategy

If your goal is simply Mega Energy, focus on:

  • Completing raids quickly.
  • Raiding with larger groups.
  • Participating during raid events.
  • Using daily free raid passes whenever possible.

Faster clears generally result in higher Mega Energy rewards.

Best Friend Bonus Impact

Raiding with friends provides several advantages:

  • Extra Premier Balls after victory.
  • Increased damage through friendship bonuses.
  • Faster raid completion times.
  • Better chances of earning additional rewards.

A strong friendship bonus can sometimes make the difference between a close win and a failed raid.

Resource Investment

If you catch a high-IV Gengar after the raid, investing Stardust and Candy can be worthwhile.

A powered-up Gengar can contribute to:

  • Legendary Raids
  • Mega Raids
  • Team GO Rocket battles
  • Certain PvP formats

Because Gastly appears fairly often during events, many trainers already have the Candy needed for upgrades.

Who Should Raid Mega Gengar?

Mega Gengar raids are valuable for:

  • New trainers building a Ghost-type team.
  • Shiny hunters searching for white Mega Gengar.
  • Players collecting Mega Energy.
  • Raid-focused trainers looking for top-tier attackers.
  • Trainers preparing for future Psychic-type legendary raids.

How Difficult Is Mega Gengar Compared to Other Mega Raids?

Mega Gengar is generally considered easier than bulky Mega Raid bosses such as Mega Latias and Mega Latios because its defenses are lower. However, it can still hit extremely hard, especially with Ghost-type charged attacks. Most trainers find it easier to defeat but more punishing if they bring the wrong counters.

Fun Fact

Mega Gengar's shiny form is dramatically different from its regular coloration. While normal shiny Gengar has only subtle differences from regular Gengar, Mega Shiny Gengar turns bright white, making it one of the most sought-after shiny Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO.

Raid Preparation Checklist

Before joining a Mega Gengar raid:

  • Revives and Potions stocked
  • Dark- or Ghost-type counters prepared
  • Mega Evolution activated
  • Enough Remote Raid Passes (if raiding remotely)
  • Friends invited for faster completion

A quick preparation check can save time and improve raid success rates.

Mega Gengar for PvE

Mega Gengar shines most in PvE content.

Its exceptional Attack stat allows it to deal huge amounts of damage against raid bosses weak to Ghost-type attacks. When Mega Evolved, it also boosts the damage of teammates using Ghost- and Poison-type Pokémon.

Mega Gengar Moveset Analysis

Mega Gengar can be more dangerous depending on its charged move.

  • Shadow Ball can heavily damage Psychic- and Ghost-type attackers.
  • Sludge Bomb threatens Fairy-type Pokémon.
  • Focus Blast can surprise Dark-type counters such as Tyranitar.

Checking the boss's moves during the raid can help explain why certain teams perform better than expected.

Best Mega Evolution to Bring

If available, Mega Tyranitar is often one of the safest Mega Evolutions to bring into a Mega Gengar raid. It deals super-effective Dark-type damage while also surviving much longer than many Ghost-type attackers.

Other strong choices include Mega Houndoom and Mega Gyarados.

Common Mistakes Against Mega Gengar

Many trainers make a few mistakes when battling Mega Gengar:

  • Using Fighting-type Pokémon because Gengar looks fragile.
  • Ignoring Dark-type counters in favor of stronger-looking neutral attackers.
  • Entering raids with low-level Ghost-types that faint too quickly.
  • Forgetting to Mega Evolve a Pokémon before the raid begins.
  • Not taking advantage of weather boosts.

Avoiding these mistakes can make raids much easier.

Why Trainers Raid Mega Gengar

Mega Gengar attracts different types of players for different reasons. Some trainers raid it for Mega Energy, while others are interested in its shiny form. Competitive players often target Mega Gengar because it remains one of the strongest Ghost-type attackers available. Newer trainers may simply want a powerful Mega Evolution to help in future raids.

Trivia

  • Mega Gengar first appeared in Generation VI.
  • It is known as the Shadow Pokémon.
  • Mega Gengar gains the ability Shadow Tag in the main series games
  • Its body partially merges with another dimension when Mega Evolved.
  • Mega Gengar's shiny form changes from white to a bright pink shade.
  • It is widely considered one of the strongest Ghost-type Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO.

Personal Raid Experience

In my experience, Mega Gengar raids usually feel faster than many other Mega Raids because of Mega Gengar's relatively low defense. Battles often turn into a race between dealing damage and surviving its powerful attacks.

Dark-type teams led by Pokémon such as Tyranitar, Hydreigon, and Darkrai tend to provide the most consistent results. Ghost-type attackers can finish battles even faster, but they are often less durable and may faint more frequently.

For players looking to farm Mega Energy efficiently, Mega Gengar is generally one of the more enjoyable Mega Raid bosses to battle.

Shiny Mega Gengar Information

Yes, shiny Gengar can be encountered after winning a Mega Gengar raid if the shiny form is available during the event.

Shiny Gengar is known for having a subtle appearance in its regular form. However, its Mega Evolution is much more noticeable, featuring a striking white coloration that many trainers consider one of the best Mega shiny designs in Pokémon GO.

Because of this, shiny hunters often prioritize Mega Gengar raids whenever they return

Mega Gengar CP Chart

Raid Boss CP

Mega Gengar appears with a very high Raid Boss CP, reflecting its status as a Mega Raid boss.

Catch CP Range

After defeating Mega Gengar, trainers can encounter Gengar within a specific CP range based on weather conditions.

  • Normal weather: Lower CP range
  • Weather boosted: Higher CP range

Higher CP catches can save Stardust and Candy when powering up your Gengar later.

Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can significantly impact Mega Gengar raids.

Fog

Fog boosts Ghost-type moves. This benefits both Mega Gengar and your Ghost-type counters, often resulting in very fast battles.

Windy

Windy weather boosts Psychic-type attackers, allowing Pokémon such as Mewtwo to deal increased damage.

Sunny

Sunny weather boosts Ground-type moves, making Pokémon like Primal Groudon stronger raid choices.

When weather favors your counters, raid completion becomes noticeably easier.

Can Mega Gengar Be Soloed?

Mega Gengar is one of the few Mega Raid bosses that highly experienced trainers can sometimes solo under ideal conditions.

However, successful solo attempts generally require:

  • High-level counters
  • Weather boost
  • Optimal movesets
  • Strong Mega Evolutions
  • Excellent dodging

For most trainers, a duo is far more realistic and comfortable.

Recommended Group Size

  • Solo: Possible for expert players
  • Duo: Easy with strong counters
  • Trio: Very comfortable
  • 4+ Trainers: Quick and stress-free victory

Best Counters for Mega Gengar

Several Pokémon stand out as excellent counters.

Mega Tyranitar

Mega Tyranitar combines high durability with powerful Dark-type attacks. It can survive Mega Gengar's attacks while continuously dealing strong super-effective damage.

Dawn Wings Necrozma

One of the strongest Ghost-type attackers available, Dawn Wings Necrozma can shred Mega Gengar's HP bar quickly when using Ghost-type moves.

Mega Rayquaza

Although it does not hit Mega Gengar for super-effective damage with Dragon attacks, its incredible stats allow it to remain a strong option in many raid teams.

Hydreigon

Hydreigon with Brutal Swing remains one of the most reliable Dark-type counters and is accessible to many trainers.

Darkrai

Darkrai combines excellent Attack power with strong Dark-type moves, making it a consistent raid performer.

Chandelure

Chandelure's Ghost-type attacks allow it to hit Mega Gengar hard while providing a budget-friendly option for many players.

Mega Gengar Weaknesses

Mega Gengar is a Ghost and Poison-type Pokémon.

Because of this typing, it is weak to:

  • Ghost-type attacks
  • Dark-type attacks
  • Ground-type attacks
  • Psychic-type attacks

Ghost and Dark Pokémon are generally the safest choices because many powerful attackers in these categories can deal super-effective damage while surviving long enough to contribute throughout the battle.

Ground-type attackers can also perform well, although Mega Gengar's moveset sometimes makes them less consistent.

Is Mega Gengar Worth Raiding?

For most trainers, the answer is yes.

Mega Gengar is among the best Ghost-type attackers in the game and performs exceptionally well against Psychic- and Ghost-type raid bosses. It also boosts Ghost- and Poison-type Pokémon during raids while Mega Evolved, making it valuable in both solo play and group raids.

Even if you already have a strong Ghost team, collecting extra Mega Energy can help you use Mega Gengar more frequently during future events and raid rotations.

Conclusion

Mega Gengar remains one of the most useful Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO thanks to its incredible offensive power and relevance in raids. Whether you're collecting Mega Energy, searching for a shiny, or building a top-tier Ghost-type attacker, Mega Gengar is usually a raid boss worth your time. Bring strong Dark-, Ghost-, Ground-, or Psychic-type counters, gather a few trainers if needed, and you'll have little trouble taking down this spooky powerhouse.

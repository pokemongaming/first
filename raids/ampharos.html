Ampharos Overview

Ampharos is an Electric-type Pokémon introduced in Generation II. It evolves from Flaaffy and serves as the final evolution of Mareep. Known for the bright light at the tip of its tail, Ampharos has become one of the most recognizable Electric-type Pokémon in the franchise.

In Pokémon GO, Ampharos is appreciated for its Mega Evolution and solid Electric-type moveset. While it may not be the strongest Electric attacker available, it remains a popular choice among trainers thanks to its unique design, accessibility, and useful Mega Evolution.

Basic Information

  • Pokédex Number: #181
  • Type: Electric
  • Category: Light Pokémon
  • Generation: Johto

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ampharos good in Pokémon GO?

Yes. While it is not the strongest Electric-type attacker, Ampharos remains useful thanks to its balanced stats and Mega Evolution.

Can Ampharos be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Ampharos is available and can be identified by its distinctive pink coloration.

What is Ampharos weak to?

Ampharos is weak only to Ground-type attacks.

Is Mega Ampharos worth using?

Mega Ampharos is useful for trainers looking to boost Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon during raids.

How do I get Ampharos?

Ampharos can be obtained by evolving Flaaffy, participating in events, or completing activities that feature the Mareep evolutionary line.

Ampharos Stats

Ampharos possesses balanced stats that allow it to perform reasonably well in raids, gyms, and certain PvP formats.

Its strengths lie in its respectable Attack stat and decent bulk, making it more durable than many Electric-type Pokémon. While it doesn't compete with the strongest legendary Electric attackers, it remains a reliable option for many trainers.

Type Effectiveness

As a pure Electric-type Pokémon, Ampharos has a straightforward set of strengths and weaknesses.

Resistances

  • Electric
  • Flying
  • Steel

Weaknesses

  • Ground

Because Ground is its only weakness, Ampharos can sometimes surprise opponents with its durability.

Evolution Line

Mareep

Mareep is a sheep-like Electric-type Pokémon that can often be found during special events and Community Days.

Flaaffy

After evolving from Mareep, Flaaffy gains improved stats and acts as the middle stage of the evolutionary line.

Ampharos

The final evolution offers significantly improved battle performance and access to Mega Evolution.

Best Movese

For raid battles, Ampharos performs best when using Electric-type attacks that benefit from STAB (Same Type Attack Bonus).

The most effective Electric-type moveset generally focuses on maximizing damage output against Water- and Flying-type raid bosses.

When using Ampharos, choosing Electric-type moves is usually the most reliable option.

Ampharos in Raids

Ampharos can contribute effectively against raid bosses that are weak to Electric attacks.

It performs particularly well against:

  • Water-type Pokémon
  • Flying-type Pokémon

Although stronger Electric attackers exist, Ampharos remains a practical choice for trainers who may not have access to legendary or shadow Pokémon.

Ampharos in Gyms

When defending gyms, Ampharos benefits from having only one weakness. Opponents often need Ground-type attackers to defeat it efficiently.

While it is not among the top gym defenders, it can still provide reasonable resistance against casual attackers.

Ampharos in PvP

Ampharos has found success in certain PvP formats thanks to its access to strong charged attacks and decent overall bulk.

In Ultra League especially, Ampharos can pressure opponents with powerful Electric-type damage while threatening several common Water- and Flying-type Pokémon.

Its performance often depends on team composition and move selection.

Mega Ampharos

Mega Ampharos is one of the most unique Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO.

Unlike regular Ampharos, Mega Ampharos gains the Dragon typing, becoming an Electric- and Dragon-type Pokémon.

This transformation provides additional strengths and weaknesses while significantly increasing its battle power.

Mega Ampharos can also boost Electric- and Dragon-type attacks used by other trainers in raids.

Shiny Ampharos

Shiny Ampharos is one of the easiest shiny forms to recognize.

Instead of its normal yellow appearance, shiny Ampharos features a bright pink body that immediately stands out.

Many trainers consider it one of the more attractive shiny Pokémon available in Pokémon GO.

Is Ampharos Worth Powering Up?

For casual players, Ampharos can be a worthwhile investment due to its versatility and Mega Evolution.

For highly competitive raid-focused players, there may be stronger Electric-type alternatives available. However, Ampharos remains valuable because of its Mega Evolution benefits and unique typing when Mega Evolved.

Fun Facts About Ampharos

  • The light on its tail can reportedly be seen from great distances.
  • Use Flying-type attackers whenever possible.
  • Mega Ampharos gains the Dragon type.
  • Its shiny form changes from yellow to pink.
  • Ampharos is one of the most popular Electric-type Pokémon from Generation II.

Conclusion

Ampharos remains one of the most beloved Electric-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its simple typing, recognizable design, and powerful Mega Evolution make it a valuable addition to many collections. Whether you're powering one up for battles, hunting for a shiny, or working toward Mega Ampharos, this Johto Pokémon continues to be a fan favorite years after its introduction.

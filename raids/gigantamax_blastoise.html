Gigantamax Blastoise is one of those raids that feels more like a “collection + experience” boss rather than something you rush repeatedly for meta value.

Gigantamax Mechanics — How It Works

Gigantamax is basically the most powerful version of Dynamax in Pokémon GO raids. It doesn’t just make Pokémon bigger—it changes how battles play out by adding special moves, higher durability, and longer fight dynamics.

What Gigantamax Actually Changes

  • Pokémon become significantly larger than standard Dynamax forms
  • Their appearance changes completely, making them visually distinct
  • They gain access to a unique G-Max move instead of a normal Max Move
  • Gigantamax replaces regular Dynamax for that Pokémon

Why These Raids Feel Different

  • HP is much higher than normal raids, so fights last longer
  • Damage pressure builds up gradually instead of quick knockouts
  • Coordination matters more than individual performance

G-Max Cannonade (Blastoise Signature Move)

This is what defines Gigantamax Blastoise in raids.

  • Deals Water-type damage with sustained pressure over time
  • Feels less like a single hit and more like continuous strain on your team
  • Encourages healing and rotation instead of pure rushing

Gigantamax Battle Flow

  • Gigantamax form is temporary and lasts only for a limited time in battle
  • Once it ends, Pokémon return to their normal state
  • This creates a “power window” where timing matters a lot

Shield Mechanic in Max Raids

  • Raid bosses can activate shields during battle
  • These shields temporarily reduce incoming damage
  • Teams need to break through them before full damage resumes

Common Raid Mistakes — Gigantamax Blastoise

Most failed Gigantamax Blastoise raids don’t come down to lack of power. They usually happen because teams are built without a clear Electric core, or because players underestimate how quickly pressure builds over time.

Weak Type Choices That Slow Everything Down

One of the biggest issues is simply bringing the wrong damage types.

  • Fire-types perform poorly because their damage is heavily resisted
  • Water-types don’t help much due to mirror resistance
  • Ice and Steel attackers contribute very little overall pressure

When too many of these appear in a lobby, the raid doesn’t fail instantly—it just becomes slow enough for pressure mechanics to take over.

Not Building Around Electric Damage

Electric attackers are what actually keep this raid under control.

  • Without Electric Pokémon, fights last much longer than they should
  • Longer fights mean more faint cycles and more healing breaks
  • This is one of the most common reasons raids feel “close but fail”

Over-Relying on Grass Teams

Grass Pokémon are strong here, but they’re not risk-free.

  • Ice Beam can heavily punish Grass-heavy lineups
  • Pure Grass teams are inconsistent in longer fights
  • Without Electric backup, damage drops too much in bad situations

Not Adapting to Weather Conditions

Weather has a bigger impact on this raid than most players realize.

  • In Rain, Blastoise becomes noticeably stronger, but many players still use unoptimized teams
  • In Sunny weather, Grass becomes much more effective, but players don’t always adjust accordingly

Slow Damage Teams Getting Overwhelmed

Teams built only for survival often struggle here.

  • Tank-heavy setups don’t reduce raid pressure fast enough
  • The longer the fight goes on, the more G-Max Cannonade matters
  • Eventually, small chip damage adds up across the entire team

Not Reacting to Key Moves

Even strong teams can struggle if they ignore what the boss is actually doing.

  • Hydro Pump can remove key attackers instantly if not respected
  • Ice Beam can shift momentum against Grass-focused teams
  • G-Max Cannonade slowly drains momentum if fights drag on

Lack of Coordination in Groups

Another common issue is team imbalance across players.

  • Everyone bringing the same type reduces flexibility
  • No balance between Electric and Grass lowers overall efficiency
  • Without role distribution, raids become less stable

Slow Re-entry After Fainting

Small delays matter more than most players expect.

  • Waiting too long after fainting reduces total damage output
  • Even a few seconds of downtime affects tight raids
  • Efficient re-entry keeps pressure consistent on the boss

Evolution & Mega Potential — Gigantamax Blastoise

Gigantamax Blastoise comes from the classic Squirtle evolution line, but its Gigantamax form exists separately from Mega Evolution. Even though both are tied to Blastoise, they work in completely different systems and don’t overlap in battles.

Evolution Line

Everything starts from the same base Pokémon:

  • Squirtle → Wartortle → Blastoise

Once you reach Blastoise, it becomes the final evolution and can branch into different forms depending on the system being used.

Two Different Power Systems

Blastoise has two separate advanced forms, but they serve completely different purposes in gameplay.

Gigantamax Blastoise is a temporary transformation used only in specific Max Raid battles. It changes appearance and unlocks the signature G-Max Cannonade, but it does not permanently alter stats.

Mega Blastoise, on the other hand, is a battle-time power boost that increases its offensive output during Mega Evolution windows, making it more relevant for raid attacking roles.

Can You Use Both?

Yes, but never together.

  • Gigantamax is restricted to Max Battles and raid boss scenarios
  • Mega Evolution is used in standard battles and raids as a damage boost form
  • Each system operates independently, so you choose based on the battle type

How Mega Blastoise Performs

In practice, Mega Blastoise is more focused on damage output than Gigantamax form.

  • Improves Water-type raid damage significantly
  • Works as a solid team support attacker in raids
  • Performs better in standard PvE content than Gigantamax form

However, it still competes with stronger modern Water-type attackers in raw DPS.

Where Each Form Fits Best

Form Best Use
Gigantamax Blastoise Max Raid battles and special Gigantamax events
Mega Blastoise Raid attacker and damage support in PvE

Which One Is More Useful?

The answer depends on the content you’re playing.

  • For raids and general PvE damage → Mega Blastoise is usually more practical
  • For Gigantamax or Max Battle content → Gigantamax Blastoise is required

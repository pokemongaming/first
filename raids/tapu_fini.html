Tapu Fini Raid Overview

Tapu Fini is a Tier 5 Legendary Raid Boss in Pokémon GO with a Water/Fairy typing. While it does not hit as hard as some other legendary bosses, its high defenses can make raids feel longer than expected if players bring weak counters.

Most trainers raid Tapu Fini for PvP rather than raid damage. It remains one of the most reliable defensive Pokémon in both Great League and Ultra League.

Why Players Raid Tapu Fini

  • Excellent performance in Great League and Ultra League
  • Very bulky in PvP battles
  • Useful Water/Fairy typing
  • Strong against Dragon, Dark, and Fighting Pokémon
  • Popular choice in competitive PvP teams

Tapu Fini is not considered a top-tier raid attacker, so many players focus mainly on PvP IVs, shiny hunting, or adding it to their collection.

Raid Difficulty

Tapu Fini is usually considered a medium-difficulty Tier 5 raid. The main challenge comes from its bulk rather than extremely dangerous attacks.

  • Beginners: Around 5–8 players are recommended
  • Intermediate players: 4–5 strong trainers can clear it comfortably
  • Advanced groups: 3–4 optimized players are usually enough
  • Experts: Small groups of 2–3 trainers can win with top counters and favorable conditions

Using proper counters makes a huge difference. Random auto-selected teams often struggle against Tapu Fini because the battle lasts much longer than most legendary raids.

What Makes Tapu Fini Difficult?

Tapu Fini survives for a long time thanks to its strong defensive stats, which can quickly expose weak raid teams.

Its Water/Fairy typing also gives it several useful resistances, including Fighting, Dark, Bug, and Dragon attacks.

Another reason the raid can feel challenging is its limited weaknesses. Tapu Fini mainly takes super effective damage from Steel and Poison-type attacks, so bringing the right counters is important.

Tapu Fini Raid Strategy Guide

Tapu Fini is usually considered a medium-difficulty Tier 5 raid, although the battle can feel slower than most legendary raids because of its high defenses.

It does not deal overwhelming damage most of the time, but its bulk and awkward typing can punish weak raid teams very quickly.

Can You Solo Tapu Fini?

A solo clear is technically possible only under extremely favorable conditions.

  • Maxed-out counters
  • Shadow Pokémon
  • Mega support
  • Weather boost
  • Best Friend bonus

For most players, Tapu Fini is not considered a realistic solo raid.

Duo Strategy

A duo is possible, but both players usually need optimized teams and strong friendship bonuses.

Electric attackers generally perform the most consistently, while Grass-types can produce higher damage if they survive long enough.

Recommended Duo Setup

  • Player 1: Mega Electric or Mega Grass support
  • Player 2: Shadow and Legendary damage dealers

Strong Duo Counters

Type Recommended Pokémon
Electric Zekrom, Xurkitree, Thundurus
Grass Kartana, Mega Sceptile
Mega Support Mega Venusaur

The biggest challenge in duos is Tapu Fini’s survivability. Moonblast can quickly remove Dragon attackers, while Hydro Pump heavily punishes fragile counters.

Trio Strategy

For smaller groups, three players is usually the safest and most comfortable setup.

  • More room for mistakes
  • Safer relobbies
  • Less pressure on perfect IVs and shadows

Recommended Team Balance

  • Player 1: Mega Grass support
  • Player 2: Electric-type damage dealers
  • Player 3: Mixed Electric and Grass attackers

Keeping Mega boosts active throughout the raid helps a lot, especially for Kartana, Zekrom, and other top counters.

Beginner Tips

Newer players should generally aim for groups of at least 5–8 trainers, especially if counters are underleveled.

Mega Sharpedo Raid Strategy Guide

Mega Sharpedo is a glass cannon raid boss:

  • Very high damage output
  • Extremely low bulk
  • Fast battles (win or wipe quickly)

Strategy is all about maximizing damage before it faints you

1. Solo Strategy

Is Solo Possible?

  • Not realistically soloable
  • Requires:
    • Perfect counters
    • Maxed Level 50 teams
    • Ideal weather

Even then, extremely inconsistent

Solo Attempt Strategy

If attempting:

  • Use Electric / Grass / Fighting / Fairy / Bug counters
  • Focus on:
    • High DPS over bulk
    • Fast relobby

Solo Verdict

Factor Rating
Difficulty Extreme
Success Rate Very Low

2. Duo Strategy

Is Duo Possible?

YES (with strong players)

Key Requirements

  • Level 35–50 counters
  • Proper Mega boost
  • Weather advantage

Best Approach

Strategy Plan

  • Lead with a Mega Pokémon for boost
  • Use Electric/Grass attackers
  • Dodge Hydro Pump

Duo Difficulty

Moveset Difficulty
Bite + Poison Fang Easy
Bite + Crunch Medium
Waterfall + Crunch Hard
Waterfall + Hydro Pump Very Hard

Duo Tip

If boss has Hydro Pump, dodging becomes mandatory

3. Trio Strategy

Is Trio Possible?

YES (easy for most players)

Why Trio is Safe

  • Mega Sharpedo has low defense
  • Damage stacks quickly with 3 players

Trio Plan

  • No need for perfect counters
  • Mixed teams still work
  • Less pressure on dodging

Trio Difficulty

Player Level Difficulty

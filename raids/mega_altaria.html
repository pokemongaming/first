What is Mega Altaria in Pokémon GO?

Mega Altaria appears in Mega Raids and rewards players with Mega Energy after defeat. Winning the raid also gives you a chance to catch Altaria along with bonus rewards such as Rare Candy, Golden Razz Berries, and TMs.

Unlike many Dragon-type raid bosses, Mega Altaria uses the unusual Dragon/Fairy typing. That combination changes the battle completely because Dragon attackers become far less reliable here.

Why Players Raid Mega Altaria

Mega Altaria is not the strongest Mega Pokémon in raw damage output, but it still has value because of its typing, PvP usefulness, and Mega bonuses.

  • Dragon/Fairy typing gives it several useful resistances
  • Provides Dragon and Fairy Mega boosts during raids
  • Useful for Mega Pokédex progress and Mega Energy farming
  • Shiny Altaria is popular among collectors

Mega Altaria in PvP

Altaria has always been a solid PvP Pokémon because of its bulk and consistent Dragon Breath pressure. Its Mega form cannot be used in standard GO Battle League formats, but regular Altaria still performs well in limited cups like:

  • Fantasy Cup
  • Love Cup
  • Dragon-themed formats

Even experienced players respect Altaria because it can slowly wear teams down if left unchecked.

Raid Difficulty

Mega Altaria is usually considered a moderate Mega Raid. The fight is more about endurance than heavy burst damage.

  • Steel-type counters perform extremely well here
  • Its moveset is fairly predictable
  • The raid becomes much easier with powered-up counters

The biggest issue is Mega Altaria’s bulk. It survives longer than many players expect, especially when your group lacks strong Steel attackers like Metagross.

Moonblast can also punish Dragon teams very quickly, while Dragon Breath applies constant pressure throughout the battle.

Mega Altaria PvP, PvE, Raid & Gym Analysis

Mega Altaria is one of the more unusual Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO because it focuses more on survivability and team support than raw damage output. Its Dragon/Fairy typing gives it several useful resistances and allows it to perform well in certain PvP formats.

Mega Altaria in PvP

Regular Altaria has been a longtime PvP favorite thanks to its bulk, typing, and Dragon Breath pressure. While Mega Altaria is not allowed in most GO Battle League formats, its overall battle style remains very similar.

It performs best as a bulky pressure Pokémon that slowly wears opponents down while resisting many common attack types.

Great League Performance

Altaria remains one of the strongest Dragon-type options in Great League because Dragon/Fairy coverage is difficult to handle without Steel or Poison counters.

It performs especially well against:

  • Dragon-type Pokémon
  • Dark-type Pokémon
  • Fighting-type Pokémon

Its defensive typing also gives it useful resistances against Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Dark, and Fighting attacks.

Steel-types are still the biggest problem for Altaria. Pokémon like Metagross can completely shut it down if shields are unavailable.

Ice-type fast attacks can also become dangerous because Altaria takes super effective damage while relying heavily on bulk to survive.

Master League

Altaria is less common in Master League because stronger Dragon Pokémon usually dominate the format. However, its typing still allows it to function as a niche anti-Dragon pick in some matchups.

It can pressure Pokémon that rely too heavily on Dragon damage while also threatening Fighting-type teams.

Best PvP Cups for Altaria

Dragon Cup

Altaria becomes extremely dangerous in Dragon-focused formats because Fairy typing gives it a massive advantage over most opposing Dragons.

Fantasy Cup

Its resistances and steady Dragon Breath pressure make it one of the safer picks in Fantasy Cup teams.

Love Cup & Limited Formats

Altaria usually stays relevant whenever Fighting-types or Dragon-types are common in the meta.

PvE Performance

Mega Altaria is not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its lower Attack stat makes it noticeably slower than stronger Dragon or Fairy Megas.

Pokémon like Mega Rayquaza, Mega Salamence, and Mega Gardevoir generally outperform it in raw raid damage.

Because of this, Mega Altaria is used more for utility and Mega bonuses rather than fast raid clears.

Raid Utility

Mega Altaria works best as a support Mega during group raids.

  • Boosts Dragon-type attacks for teammates
  • Boosts Fairy-type attacks for teammates
  • Provides decent survivability during longer battles

It becomes more useful when your raid group is heavily focused on Dragon or Fairy attackers.

Gym Performance

As a Gym Defender

Altaria can be annoying to remove from gyms because of its bulk and defensive typing.

It performs well against:

  • Dragon attackers
  • Fighting attackers

Steel-types remain its biggest weakness, especially strong attackers like Metagross.

Ice attackers can also remove Altaria fairly quickly if left unchecked.

As a Gym Attacker

Mega Altaria is usually not the best choice for attacking gyms because its damage output is relatively low compared to more offensive Mega Pokémon.

Stat Breakdown

  • Attack: Average
  • Defense: High
  • Stamina: High

Its stats are designed more around consistency and survivability rather than burst damage.

How Mega Altaria Compares to Other Megas

Mega Altaria stands out because of its defensive utility and support value.

However, it falls behind stronger offensive Megas when pure raid damage is the priority.

  • Mega Rayquaza offers much higher Dragon-type damage
  • Mega Salamence is stronger offensively
  • Mega Gardevoir performs better as a Fairy attacker

Final Thoughts

Mega Altaria is more of a utility Mega than a raid damage monster. It shines in PvP-related formats, defensive situations, and coordinated raid groups that benefit from Fairy or Dragon Mega boosts.

If you mainly care about speed-clearing raids, there are stronger options available. But for PvP players, collectors, and support-focused raid teams, Mega Altaria still has solid value.

Mega Altaria Raid Strategy Guide

Mega Altaria is more of an endurance raid than a high-pressure damage race. Its bulk can make the fight feel slower than expected, especially for smaller groups without strong Steel-type attackers.

The raid becomes much easier once players start coordinating Mega boosts and using proper counters instead of relying on random Dragon teams.

Can Mega Altaria Be Soloed?

For most players, soloing Mega Altaria is not realistic.

Its high defensive stats combined with the raid timer make solo attempts extremely difficult, even with top-tier counters.

A successful solo attempt would usually require:

  • Level 50 counters
  • Optimized IVs and movesets
  • Strong weather boost conditions
  • Perfect Mega Evolution support
  • Minimal relobby time

Even experienced challenge raiders generally prefer duo or trio runs instead.

Duo Strategy

A duo clear is possible with strong preparation and coordinated teams.

Both players should ideally use high-level Steel or Ice attackers backed by Mega boosts.

  • Steel-types are the safest overall option
  • Ice-types deal heavy damage but faint faster
  • Moonblast becomes dangerous for Dragon attackers

Dodging charged moves can make a huge difference in close duo attempts, especially against Moonblast sets.

Trio Strategy

Three players is usually the most comfortable small-group setup for Mega Altaria.

At this point, the raid becomes much more consistent and less dependent on perfect dodging.

A balanced trio team often works best:

  • One player runs a Mega support Pokémon
  • Other players focus on maximum damage output
  • Steel attackers remain the most reliable choice

Most organized trios should finish comfortably if counters are properly powered up.

4–6 Player Groups

This is the easiest and most common raid setup.

With four or more trainers using decent counters, Mega Altaria usually goes down without much trouble.

  • Steel-types provide stable damage and survivability
  • Ice-types help speed up the clear time
  • Mega boosts improve the entire group’s performance

At this group size, perfect optimization is not really necessary.

Beginner Raid Tips

If you are newer to raids, focus more on surviving than maximizing damage.

  • Auto-recommended teams are acceptable early on
  • Steel Pokémon are generally safer than Dragons
  • Try dodging charged moves when possible

Many beginners make the mistake of bringing Dragon attackers against Mega Altaria. Because of its Fairy typing, those Pokémon can faint very quickly.

Intermediate Player Strategy

Once you start building stronger raid teams, proper counter selection becomes more important.

  • Prioritize Steel attackers first
  • Use Ice attackers for higher DPS
  • Learn to dodge Moonblast consistently
  • Bring a Mega Pokémon whenever possible

This is usually the stage where raid completion times improve noticeably.

Advanced & Expert Strategies

Experienced players often focus on efficiency, speed clears, and short-man challenges.

Advanced raid groups typically:

  • Coordinate Mega Evolutions carefully
  • Use pre-built battle parties
  • Relobby quickly after fainting
  • Optimize weather boosts and friendship bonuses

Expert players also tend to dodge only charged attacks instead of every incoming move, which helps maintain higher overall damage output.

Glass-cannon Ice attackers can produce very fast clear times, although they require better timing and team coordination.

General Raid Advice

Moonblast is usually the most threatening charged move because it heavily punishes Dragon-type counters.

Dragon Breath can also wear teams down surprisingly fast during longer battles.

For most players, Steel-type Pokémon remain the safest overall strategy because they resist Fairy attacks while still dealing strong damage.

Ice attackers are excellent for raw DPS, but they are noticeably less durable during extended fights.

Mega Altaria Counters Guide

Mega Altaria can be surprisingly awkward to counter because of its Dragon/Fairy typing. Many players instinctively bring Dragon attackers into Dragon raids, but that strategy usually backfires here because Fairy moves can hit Dragons extremely hard.

Steel-types are generally the safest and most consistent counters, while Ice-types offer faster clear times at the cost of survivability.

Best Overall Counters

These Pokémon combine strong damage output with reliable performance during longer raid battles:

  • Metagross (Bullet Punch + Meteor Mash)
  • Dialga (Metal Claw + Iron Head)
  • Mamoswine (Powder Snow + Avalanche)
  • Galarian Darmanitan (Ice Fang + Avalanche)
  • Excadrill (Metal Claw + Iron Head)
  • Gengar (Lick + Sludge Bomb)

Metagross is usually considered the safest overall counter because it resists Fairy attacks while still dealing heavy Steel-type damage back.

Dialga performs well for similar reasons and tends to survive comfortably during most Mega Altaria movesets.

Ice attackers like Mamoswine and Galarian Darmanitan can produce very high DPS, although they faint much faster if Mega Altaria is using strong charged moves.

Gengar works surprisingly well with Sludge Bomb, but its low bulk makes dodging more important.

Best Mega Counters

Using a Mega Evolution helps the entire raid group by boosting teammate damage.

  • Mega Gardevoir – strong Fairy support
  • Mega Beedrill – boosts Poison attackers
  • Mega Glalie – powerful Ice support option
  • Mega Aggron – durable Steel-type Mega

Even one active Mega Evolution can noticeably speed up raid completion times in smaller groups.

Ice-Type Counters

Ice-types usually produce the fastest clear times against Mega Altaria.

  • Mamoswine
  • Weavile
  • Glaceon

Because Mega Altaria is still part Dragon-type, strong Ice attacks hit extremely hard.

The downside is survivability. Most Ice attackers are fairly fragile and can disappear quickly against repeated charged moves.

Steel-Type Counters

Steel-types are widely considered the safest option for this raid.

  • Metagross
  • Excadrill
  • Dialga

Steel Pokémon resist Fairy attacks like Moonblast while maintaining strong offensive pressure throughout the battle.

If you are unsure which counters to build, Steel-types are usually the best long-term investment for Mega Altaria raids.

Poison-Type Counters

Poison attackers can work well because Fairy is weak to Poison damage.

  • Gengar
  • Roserade

These counters are more aggressive but noticeably less durable than Steel-types.

Dodging becomes much more important when using glass-cannon Poison attackers.

Fairy-Type Counters

Fairy attackers are usable, although they are generally less efficient than Steel or Ice teams.

  • Togekiss
  • Gardevoir

Fairy damage still helps against Mega Altaria’s Dragon typing, but the Fairy side of its typing reduces overall effectiveness compared to stronger counter types.

Mega Altaria Evolution Line & Buddy Distance

Altaria has one of the more expensive evolutions in Pokémon GO because evolving Swablu requires a huge amount of candy. Because of that, many players rely heavily on the buddy system and event bonuses while preparing for Mega Altaria.

Evolution Line

Swablu

Swablu is the first stage in the evolution line.

  • Type: Normal / Flying
  • Frequently featured during weather and event spawns
  • Known for its very high evolution cost

Altaria

Altaria evolves from Swablu and becomes much more useful in PvP formats thanks to its bulk and Dragon typing.

  • Type: Dragon / Flying
  • Strong Great League PvP option
  • Required before Mega Evolution becomes available

Evolution requirement:

  • 400 Swablu Candy

Mega Altaria

Mega Altaria is a temporary Mega Evolution rather than a permanent form.

  • Type changes to Dragon / Fairy
  • Provides Dragon and Fairy Mega bonuses during raids
  • Activated using Mega Energy

Buddy Distance

Swablu and Altaria only require 1 km per candy as a buddy Pokémon, which makes them one of the easier long-term candy farms in the game.

  • Buddy distance: 1 km
  • Applies to both Swablu and Altaria

That short distance helps offset the massive 400-candy evolution requirement over time.

Why Walking Swablu Helps So Much

Many trainers underestimate how useful the buddy system is for this evolution line.

  • Free candy while walking normally
  • Makes the 400-candy grind much easier
  • Helpful during events with increased Swablu spawns

Even casual walking can add up surprisingly quickly because of the low 1 km requirement.

Using Mega Evolutions for Extra Candy

Running an active Mega Pokémon with matching types can increase candy gains during events and catch sessions.

Useful examples include:

  • Mega Gardevoir for Fairy-type bonuses
  • Mega Salamence for Dragon-type bonuses

This becomes especially effective during Community Days or boosted spawn events.

Best Candy Farming Strategy

The fastest way to build enough candy for Altaria usually combines multiple methods together:

  • Use Pinap Berries during catches
  • Keep Swablu as your active buddy
  • Use matching Mega Evolutions during events
  • Take advantage of weather boosts and spotlight hours

Players who combine all of these methods can reach the 400-candy requirement much faster than expected.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring the buddy system and relying only on catches
  • Forgetting to use Pinap Berries during events
  • Using random buddies instead of efficient 1 km candy Pokémon

Because Altaria is so candy-expensive, small efficiency mistakes add up over time.

Best Mega-Evolved Pokémon Against Mega Altaria

Choosing the right Mega Evolution during a Mega Altaria raid is not only about your personal damage output. A good Mega also improves the entire raid group by boosting matching attack types for teammates.

Because Mega Altaria is both Dragon and Fairy-type, the best Mega counters usually come from Steel, Ice, or Poison categories.

What Makes a Good Mega Counter?

The strongest Mega choices generally do three things well:

  • Deal super effective damage consistently
  • Stay alive long enough to maintain the Mega boost
  • Support teammates using similar attack types

Steel-Type Megas

Steel Megas are usually the safest and most reliable option against Mega Altaria.

  • Mega Metagross
  • Mega Aggron

Steel-types resist both Dragon Breath and Fairy attacks like Moonblast, which helps them remain active on the field longer than many other counters.

Mega Aggron is especially useful for players who prefer stability and survivability over risky high-DPS setups.

Ice-type Megas can produce extremely fast raid clears because Dragon Pokémon are naturally weak to Ice damage.

  • Mega Glalie
  • Mega Abomasnow

These Megas are ideal for aggressive raid groups trying to maximize damage output quickly.

The main drawback is survivability. Ice-types tend to faint much faster during longer fights, especially against stronger charged moves.

Poison-Type Megas

  • Mega Beedrill

Mega Beedrill can deal strong damage, although its low defensive stats make dodging much more important.

This option is usually better suited for experienced players comfortable with glass-cannon raid teams.

Fairy-Type Megas

Fairy Megas are less focused on raw counter damage and more useful for supporting teammates running Fairy attackers.

  • Mega Gardevoir

However, it does not pressure Mega Altaria as efficiently as Steel or Ice-focused Megas.

Mega Altaria Resistances & Defensive Strengths

Mega Altaria has a very unusual defensive profile thanks to its Dragon and Fairy typing combination.

This gives it several useful resistances and makes it especially strong against many common Dragon-focused teams in both raids and PvP.

Typing Overview

  • Type: Dragon / Fairy

The Fairy typing removes Mega Altaria’s normal weakness to Dragon attacks, which is one of the biggest reasons it performs so well in Dragon-heavy matchups.

Why Dragon Resistance Matters

Most Dragon Pokémon struggle against other Dragons because Dragon moves usually hit each other very hard.

Mega Altaria changes that completely.

Its Fairy typing allows it to absorb Dragon-type pressure much more comfortably than most Dragon Pokémon, which is why it performs so well in formats like Dragon Cup.

This also makes Dragon attackers much less effective during raids compared to Steel-type counters.

What Mega Altaria Struggles Against

Despite its strong defensive profile, Mega Altaria still has some important weaknesses:

  • Steel-types are the biggest threat overall
  • Poison-types can pressure it effectively
  • Ice attackers still deal strong raid damage because of high DPS potential

Ice damage ends up being neutral overall because Dragon is weak to Ice while Fairy resists it.

However, powerful Ice attackers like Mamoswine can still clear raids quickly due to raw damage output.

Raid Impact

Steel-types are usually considered the safest raid counters because they combine strong damage with excellent survivability.

Many Steel Pokémon also resist Mega Altaria’s common Fairy and Dragon attacks, making raids much more consistent.

Ice-types are more aggressive options for faster clears, although they tend to faint more often.

PvP Matchups

Mega Altaria performs especially well against:

  • Dragon-types

Weather Boost Effects for Mega Altaria Raids

Weather plays a bigger role in Pokémon GO raids than many players expect.

Depending on the in-game weather, Mega Altaria can become stronger, certain counters may perform better, and the overall raid difficulty can change quite a bit.

How Weather Boost Works

When the current in-game weather matches a Pokémon’s typing, that Pokémon receives a temporary boost.

For raid bosses, this usually means:

  • Higher catch CP after the raid
  • Higher encounter level
  • Stronger move damage during battle

Weather can also strengthen your own raid counters if their typing matches the active conditions.

Important Weather Conditions for Mega Altaria

Cloudy Weather

Cloudy weather boosts Fairy-type moves, which directly benefits Mega Altaria.

  • Moonblast becomes more threatening
  • Fairy damage hits harder overall
  • Longer raids can feel slightly more punishing

Poison-type counters also gain a boost in Cloudy weather, although Steel-types are still usually the safest overall option.

Windy Weather

Windy weather boosts Dragon-type attacks.

  • Dragon Breath becomes noticeably stronger
  • Mega Altaria deals more consistent pressure
  • Dragon-type counters become even riskier to use

This weather also increases the CP of the catch encounter after the raid.

Snow Weather

Snow is generally the best weather condition for fighting Mega Altaria.

  • Ice-type attackers gain a major damage boost
  • Steel-type counters become even stronger
  • Raid clear times become much faster

Strong Ice attackers like Mamoswine can perform especially well during Snow weather, although they still remain fairly fragile.

Other Weather Conditions

Weather like Rainy or Sunny conditions does not directly favor Mega Altaria itself.

However, some individual counters may still benefit depending on their move types and team setup.

How Weather Changes Raid Difficulty

One important thing many players overlook is that weather affects both sides of the raid battle.

  • A weather-boosted boss becomes harder to defeat
  • Weather-boosted counters can make raids significantly easier

Because of this, the same raid can feel completely different depending on the current weather conditions.

How to Catch Mega Altaria Easily

Catching Altaria after a Mega raid is mostly about consistency rather than difficulty. Compared to legendary raid bosses, its catch rate is relatively forgiving, but poor timing or missed bonuses can still make it frustrating.

Use Golden Razz Berries

Golden Razz Berries are the most important part of the catch process. They significantly increase your chances and should be used for every throw until the Pokémon is caught.

  • Best berry available for raid catches
  • Stacked with other bonuses for maximum success rate

Aim for Excellent Throws

The key to consistent catches is timing your throws for the smallest target circle. Altaria has a moderately sized catch circle, making Excellent throws easier compared to many other raid bosses.

  • Wait for the circle to shrink to Excellent range
  • Aim for center hits instead of rushing throws

Use Curveball Throws

Curveball throws provide an additional catch bonus and should always be used in combination with berries and Excellent throws.

  • Spin the ball before throwing
  • Combine with Golden Razz + Excellent for best results

Time Your Throws Carefully

One of the most common mistakes is throwing while Altaria is in its attack animation. This often leads to missed throws.

  • Wait for the attack animation to finish
  • Throw immediately after it ends for better accuracy

Use the Circle Lock Technique

Circle locking helps improve consistency, especially for players aiming for Excellent throws.

  • Hold the Poké Ball until the circle reaches ideal size
  • Wait for the attack animation
  • Release the throw with controlled timing

Weather conditions can affect both CP and difficulty. Cloudy or Windy weather increases Altaria’s CP, which slightly raises catch difficulty, but does not change the core strategy.

  • Windy weather: higher CP, slightly tougher catches
  • Cloudy weather: boosts Fairy typing, similar effect on difficulty
  • Strategy remains the same: berries + Excellent throws

Don’t Rush Premier Balls

After a raid, you will receive a limited number of Premier Balls based on performance. Taking your time is more effective than rushing throws.

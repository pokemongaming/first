Introduction

Mega Audino returns to Pokémon GO as a Mega Raid Boss, giving trainers another opportunity to earn Mega Energy and expand their collection of Mega Evolutions. Unlike many Mega Pokémon that focus on overwhelming offensive power, Mega Audino stands out for its unique Normal and Fairy typing, impressive durability, and supportive role in the Pokémon universe.

While Mega Audino may not be the first choice for trainers seeking the strongest raid attackers, it remains an appealing target for collectors, shiny hunters, and players working toward completing their Mega Pokédex. Its manageable raid difficulty also makes it accessible to smaller groups, especially when equipped with powerful Steel-type counters.

In this guide, you'll find everything you need to know about Mega Audino, including its weaknesses, best counters, weather boosts, catch CP ranges, shiny availability, and whether it's worth spending your Raid Passes on this Mega Evolution.

Mega Audino Weaknesses

Mega Audino is a unique Normal- and Fairy-type Pokémon, which gives it a different set of strengths and weaknesses compared to many other Mega Raid Bosses. The addition of the Fairy type removes the Fighting-type weakness normally associated with pure Normal-type Pokémon, making Mega Audino more resilient against attacks that would typically be effective against Normal Pokémon.

As a result of its Normal/Fairy typing, Mega Audino is weak to only two attack types:

  • Steel-type attacks
  • Poison-type attacks

Among these options, Steel-type Pokémon are generally the best choice for raids because they offer excellent damage output while also resisting many of Mega Audino's possible Fairy-type moves. Powerful Steel-type attackers such as Metagross, Dusk Mane Necrozma, Dialga Origin Forme, and Excadrill can significantly reduce the time needed to complete the raid.

Poison-type Pokémon can also deal super-effective damage, but they are usually less effective than top-tier Steel-type counters due to lower overall damage output and survivability in raid battles.

Since Mega Audino has only two weaknesses, building a team focused on strong Steel-type attackers is often the most efficient strategy. Trainers who use well-powered Steel-type Pokémon will find this raid considerably easier than relying on neutral damage attackers.

Is Mega Audino Worth Raiding?

Whether Mega Audino is worth raiding depends largely on your goals in Pokémon GO. For collectors and completionists, the answer is generally yes. Mega Audino provides an opportunity to earn Mega Energy, register another Mega Evolution in your Pokédex, and potentially encounter a shiny Audino after the raid.

From a battle perspective, Mega Audino is not considered one of the strongest Mega Evolutions currently available. Trainers focused on maximizing raid damage may find greater value in Mega Evolutions such as Mega Gardevoir, Mega Diancie, or Mega Rayquaza. As a result, competitive players may choose to prioritize other Mega Raids when multiple options are available.

However, Mega Audino raids still have several advantages. The raid is typically easier than many Legendary or Mega Legendary encounters, making it accessible to smaller groups of trainers. Newer players can participate without needing a large raid team, while experienced trainers can often complete the raid quickly using powerful Steel-type Pokémon.

If you don't already own Mega Audino or need additional Mega Energy to Mega Evolve it more frequently, completing a few raids is worthwhile. On the other hand, trainers who already have plenty of Mega Energy and are focused solely on building the strongest raid teams may find limited long-term value in repeatedly raiding Mega Audino.

Overall, Mega Audino is best viewed as a collection-focused Mega Raid rather than a must-have competitive powerhouse. It is a solid target for Pokédex completion, Mega Energy farming, and shiny hunting, even if it isn't among the highest-priority Mega Evolutions in Pokémon GO.

Best Mega Audino Counters

Mega Audino's Normal and Fairy typing leaves it vulnerable to Steel- and Poison-type attacks, but Steel-type Pokémon are generally the most effective choice due to their superior damage output and useful resistances. Building a team around powerful Steel-type attackers will allow trainers to defeat Mega Audino more quickly and efficiently.

Mega Metagross

Mega Metagross is one of the strongest counters available against Mega Audino. Its Steel-type attacks deal super-effective damage while benefiting from Mega Evolution bonuses, making it an excellent option for both personal damage and boosting other Steel-type Pokémon on the team.

Dusk Mane Necrozma

Dusk Mane Necrozma combines exceptional Attack stats with powerful Steel-type moves. Its impressive bulk allows it to stay in battle longer while consistently dealing heavy damage throughout the raid.

Shadow Metagross

Shadow Metagross remains one of the best non-Mega options thanks to its massive offensive power. Although Shadow Pokémon sacrifice some defense, the increased attack makes it a top-tier choice for experienced trainers looking to maximize raid damage.

Dialga (Origin Forme)

Dialga Origin Forme offers a strong balance between offense and survivability. Its Steel-type attacks hit Mega Audino for super-effective damage while its Dragon/Steel typing provides useful resistances during the battle.

Excadrill

Excadrill is a reliable and widely available counter for many trainers. Its Steel-type moveset allows it to take advantage of Mega Audino's weakness while delivering consistent damage throughout the encounter.

Nihilego

Although Poison-type attackers are generally less common than Steel-types in raids, Nihilego stands out as one of the strongest Poison-type options. Its high Attack stat allows it to deal significant super-effective damage against Mega Audino.

Why Steel-Type Pokémon Perform Best

While both Steel- and Poison-type attacks are effective, Steel-type Pokémon typically dominate this raid because many of them possess higher overall damage output and greater durability. In addition, Steel Pokémon often resist Fairy-type moves that Mega Audino may use, helping them stay on the battlefield longer. For most trainers, a team built primarily around Steel-type attackers will provide the fastest and most reliable path to victory.

Can Mega Audino Be Soloed, Duoed, or Trioed?

Mega Audino is generally considered one of the easier Mega Raids in Pokémon GO, but the number of trainers required depends on team strength, Pokémon levels, weather conditions, and friendship bonuses.

Can Mega Audino Be Soloed?

For most trainers, soloing Mega Audino is not realistic. Although it has fewer weaknesses than many raid bosses and isn't known for extremely high damage output, Mega Raid bosses have a large amount of HP, making a solo victory very difficult within the raid timer. Only highly optimized teams with top-tier Steel-type attackers and favorable conditions might come close, but a successful solo is generally not expected.

Can Mega Audino Be Duoed?

Yes, Mega Audino can usually be defeated by two high-level trainers. A duo becomes much easier when both players use powerful Steel-type Pokémon such as Mega Metagross, Dusk Mane Necrozma, Shadow Metagross, and Dialga Origin Forme. Friendship bonuses, weather boosts, and properly powered-up counters can significantly improve the chances of a comfortable duo victory.

Can Mega Audino Be Trioed?

A trio is considered the safest and most accessible option for most trainers. Three players using reasonably strong Steel-type teams should have little trouble defeating Mega Audino within the time limit. Even if some trainers lack fully optimized counters, a group of three can generally complete the raid without major difficulty.

Recommended Group Size

  • 1 Trainer: Not recommended for most players.
  • 2 Trainers: Possible with strong counters and high-level teams.
  • 3 Trainers: Comfortable and reliable.
  • 4–5 Trainers: Easy victory for casual groups.

Overall, Mega Audino is among the more approachable Mega Raid Bosses, and most trainers will find that a duo or trio is more than enough to secure a successful raid clear.

Mega Audino Weather Boost Analysis

Weather can have a noticeable impact on both the raid battle and the Pokémon you encounter after defeating Mega Audino. Understanding these effects can help trainers choose the best time to raid and maximize their chances of success.

Cloudy Weather

Cloudy weather boosts Fairy-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO, which means Mega Audino will appear at a higher level after the raid and have a higher CP when caught. However, this weather also strengthens any Fairy-type attacks Mega Audino may use during the battle, potentially making the encounter slightly more challenging.

Trainers looking for stronger catches may prefer raiding during Cloudy weather since weather-boosted Mega Audino can save Stardust and Candy that would otherwise be needed for powering up.

Snow Weather

Snowy weather is particularly favorable for trainers because it boosts Steel-type Pokémon and Steel-type attacks. Since Steel is Mega Audino's most important weakness, top counters such as Metagross, Dusk Mane Necrozma, and Dialga can deal even more damage under snowy conditions.

If Snow weather is active, raids may require fewer revives, finish faster, and become easier for smaller groups.

Best Weather for Raiding

From a battle perspective, Snow weather is the most beneficial condition because it strengthens Mega Audino's strongest counters. Trainers using Steel-type teams will notice significantly improved performance and faster raid completion times.

Best Weather for Catching

If your goal is obtaining the highest-level Audino possible, Cloudy weather is ideal because it boosts the Pokémon you encounter after the raid. Weather-boosted catches start at a higher level, reducing the amount of resources needed for future power-ups.

Summary

  • Cloudy Weather: Boosts Mega Audino and increases catch CP.
  • Snow Weather: Boosts Steel-type counters and makes the raid easier.
  • Best for Battling: Snow Weather.
  • Best for Catching: Cloudy Weather.

Checking the in-game weather before raiding can help you decide whether you want an easier battle or a stronger Audino encounter after the raid.

Common Mistakes Trainers Make Against Mega Audino

Mega Audino isn't a particularly difficult Mega Raid, but there are still a few mistakes that can make the battle slower than it needs to be.

One of the most common errors is bringing Fighting-type Pokémon simply because Audino is known as a Normal-type Pokémon. While that would work against regular Audino, Mega Audino gains the Fairy type, which removes its Fighting weakness entirely. Trainers who overlook this often end up dealing much less damage than expected.

Another mistake is relying on strong neutral attackers instead of building a team around Steel-type Pokémon. Even average Steel-type counters can outperform many powerful neutral attackers because they take advantage of Mega Audino's limited weaknesses.

Some players also underestimate the value of weather and Mega boosts. A well-timed Mega Evolution that boosts Steel-type damage can noticeably speed up the raid, especially in smaller groups.

Finally, many trainers assume every Mega Raid requires a large team. Mega Audino is actually one of the more approachable Mega Raid bosses, and well-prepared groups of two or three trainers can usually handle it comfortably.

Who Should Raid Mega Audino?

Mega Audino isn't the kind of raid that every trainer needs to grind endlessly, but it definitely has its audience.

If you're a Pokédex completionist, Mega Audino is an easy recommendation. Adding another Mega Evolution to your collection is always satisfying, especially if you missed previous appearances.

Shiny hunters may also find this raid worthwhile. Every completed raid gives you another chance to encounter a shiny Audino, which remains one of the more distinctive shiny forms thanks to its purple coloration.

Newer trainers can benefit as well. Mega Audino is generally more forgiving than many Mega Raids, making it a good opportunity to learn raid mechanics, earn rewards, and collect Mega Energy without facing an extremely challenging boss.

On the other hand, trainers focused purely on building the strongest raid attackers may not consider Mega Audino a top priority. It has its uses, but there are other Mega Evolutions that provide more value in competitive raid scenarios.

For most players, Mega Audino sits comfortably in the "worth doing a few times" category rather than the "farm as much as possible" category.

What Makes Mega Audino Different From Other Mega Raids?

Mega Audino stands out from many other Mega Raid bosses because the battle doesn't revolve around surviving huge amounts of damage. Instead, the challenge comes from dealing enough damage efficiently. While raid bosses like Mega Gengar or Mega Lopunny can quickly knock out unprepared teams, Mega Audino tends to feel much more relaxed and forgiving.

Its Normal and Fairy typing also makes it somewhat unusual. Many trainers see Audino and immediately think about Fighting-type counters, only to realize that the Fairy typing completely changes the matchup. Because of this, Mega Audino rewards trainers who pay attention to typing rather than relying on assumptions.

Another thing that makes Mega Audino unique is its role in Pokémon GO. It isn't considered a must-have Mega Evolution for competitive raiding, yet it remains popular among collectors, shiny hunters, and players who enjoy completing their Mega Pokédex. In many ways, Mega Audino is a collection-focused raid rather than a meta-defining one.

Should You Use Premium Raid Passes on Mega Audino?

The answer depends on what you're hoping to get from the raid.

If you don't already have Mega Audino registered, using a few Premium Raid Passes can be a reasonable investment. You'll earn Mega Energy, work toward unlocking another Mega Evolution, and have a chance to encounter a shiny Audino after each successful raid.

However, if you're a trainer who mainly spends Raid Passes on the strongest raid attackers in the game, Mega Audino may not be your highest priority. Once you've collected enough Mega Energy and secured a good Audino, there usually isn't a strong reason to continue farming the raid heavily.

For most players, the sweet spot is somewhere in the middle. Doing a handful of raids for Mega Energy and shiny chances makes sense, but Mega Audino is rarely the kind of raid that demands dozens of passes unless you're specifically hunting for a perfect IV or shiny specimen.

Best Mega Evolution to Bring and Tips for First-Time Raiders

If you're joining a Mega Audino raid, bringing a Steel-type Mega Evolution is usually the best choice. Not only will it deal super-effective damage, but it can also provide a valuable damage boost to teammates using Steel-type Pokémon. In group raids, those team-wide bonuses can make a noticeable difference in how quickly the boss goes down.

For first-time raiders, the battle is generally less intimidating than many other Mega Raids. A small group of prepared trainers can often complete it without much trouble. The biggest piece of advice is to focus on Steel-type attackers and avoid bringing Fighting-type Pokémon simply because Audino is normally a Normal-type Pokémon.

Don't worry if your entire team faints during the battle. Rejoining is a normal part of raiding, especially for newer players. What matters most is using the right counters and keeping damage output consistent.

After the raid, take a moment to check the CP of your Audino encounter before catching it. High CP values can indicate strong IVs, and you may end up with a Pokémon that's worth investing in later. Whether you're hunting for Mega Energy, a shiny, or a high-IV catch, a little attention after the battle can be just as important as the raid itself.

Why Is Audino So Popular Among Pokémon Fans?

Long-time Pokémon players often recognize Audino for a reason that has nothing to do with Mega Evolution. In several main-series Pokémon games, Audino became famous for providing large amounts of experience points when defeated.

Many trainers spent hours battling Audino to level up their teams quickly, which helped the Pokémon earn a reputation as one of the best experience-grinding targets in the franchise. Because of this history, Audino has a special place in the memories of many veteran Pokémon fans.

While Pokémon GO doesn't use Audino in quite the same way, that legacy still contributes to its popularity whenever it appears in events, raids, or shiny hunts.

Is Mega Audino a Good Raid for Beginners?

Mega Audino is one of the more beginner-friendly Mega Raids that appears in Pokémon GO. Unlike some Mega Raid bosses that require large groups and highly optimized counters, Mega Audino can often be defeated by smaller teams using accessible Steel-type Pokémon.

The raid also serves as a good learning experience for newer trainers. It teaches the importance of type matchups, team building, and Mega Evolution bonuses without being overwhelmingly difficult.

Because the battle is relatively forgiving, beginners have more room to experiment and learn raid mechanics. Even if your team isn't perfectly optimized, joining a group raid can still be a rewarding experience and a good source of valuable items and Mega Energy.

Mega Audino Raid Rewards

Defeating Mega Audino isn't just about earning Mega Energy. Like other Mega Raids, trainers also receive a variety of rewards that can make each battle worthwhile.

Depending on your performance and raid conditions, you can earn Rare Candy, Golden Razz Berries, Revives, Potions, and experience points. Trainers who complete raids quickly and battle with friends often receive additional reward bonuses.

Of course, the biggest reward is usually Mega Energy. This allows you to Mega Evolve your own Audino and gradually reduce future Mega Evolution costs as you continue using it.

For many players, the combination of Mega Energy, item rewards, and a potential shiny encounter makes Mega Audino a solid raid target even if it isn't one of the strongest Mega Evolutions in the game.

Mega Audino CP Chart Explained

The Mega Audino CP chart helps trainers determine how strong the Audino encounter is after successfully completing a Mega Raid. Since you do not catch Mega Audino directly, the Pokémon encounter after the raid will be a regular Audino. The CP of that Audino can indicate whether it has strong IVs and how much investment it may need in the future.

Mega Audino Catch CP Range

What Do These Numbers Mean?

When Mega Audino is not weather boosted, the highest possible CP encounter is 963 CP, which corresponds to a perfect 15/15/15 IV Audino at Level 20. Trainers looking for a hundo should pay close attention to this value when catching their reward encounter.

During Cloudy weather, Audino receives a weather boost and is encountered at Level 25 instead of Level 20. In this case, the highest possible CP increases to 1204 CP, which indicates a perfect IV Audino.

Why Is Weather-Boosted CP Important?

Weather-boosted Pokémon start at a higher level, meaning they require fewer Stardust and Candy investments if you plan to power them up later. Even if you are primarily raiding for Mega Energy, catching a high-CP Audino can save valuable resources in the long run.

What CP Should You Look For?

  • 963 CP: Perfect IV Audino (Level 20)
  • 1204 CP: Perfect IV Weather-Boosted Audino (Level 25)
  • CP values close to these maximums often indicate strong IV combinations.
  • Lower CP values can still have good IVs, but they are less likely to be perfect.

Summary

The Mega Audino CP chart is useful for quickly evaluating your raid reward encounter. Trainers hunting for a hundo should watch for 963 CP in normal weather or 1204 CP in Cloudy weather, as these values represent the best possible Audino available from Mega Audino raids.

What is Mega Audino weak against?

Mega Audino is weak to Steel-type and Poison-type attacks. Steel-type Pokémon are generally the most effective counters due to their high damage output and useful resistances.

Can Mega Audino be shiny?

Yes. After defeating Mega Audino in a Mega Raid, trainers have a chance to encounter a shiny Audino during the catch encounter, provided its shiny form is available in Pokémon GO.

Tapu Lele Raid Guide Overview

Tapu Lele is a Legendary Pokémon from the Alola region and one of the four Island Guardians in Pokémon lore.

  • Type: Psychic / Fairy
  • Role: Offensive Legendary attacker
  • Known for strong Psychic-type damage and fast pressure

Why Tapu Lele Matters

Strong Psychic-Type Attacker

Tapu Lele performs best as a Psychic-type attacker thanks to its high Attack stat and powerful charged moves.

Its strongest raid movesets usually include:

  • Confusion
  • Future Sight

These moves are especially useful against:

  • Fighting-type raid bosses
  • Poison-type Pokémon

Fairy-Type Coverage

Its Fairy typing also gives it useful coverage against several popular Pokémon types.

  • Strong against Dragon-types
  • Useful against Dark-types
  • Can pressure Fighting Pokémon in PvP

A Unique Role

Tapu Lele is not always the strongest option in every category, but the Psychic/Fairy combination gives it a unique place in Pokémon GO.

It becomes more valuable if:

  • You do not have top-tier Psychic attackers yet
  • You want more type variety in raid teams
  • You play PvP formats where Fairy coverage matters

Shiny & Collection Value

Tapu Lele is also popular with collectors because of its rare Legendary status and shiny form.

  • Shiny Tapu Lele is event-exclusive at times
  • Part of the Tapu guardian quartet
  • One of the more visually unique Legendary Pokémon

Tapu Lele Raid Strategy Guide

Can You Solo Tapu Lele?

For most players, soloing Tapu Lele is not realistic.

Its combination of strong bulk, heavy charged move pressure, and the raid timer makes a solo clear extremely difficult, even with top-tier counters.

Pokémon such as:

  • Mega Gengar
  • Metagross

can deal excellent damage, but surviving long enough while maintaining consistent DPS is the main challenge.

Duo Strategy

Difficulty: Very Hard

A duo is possible, but only with optimized teams and experienced trainers.

Recommended setup:

  • Level 40+ trainers
  • Maxed counters with strong IVs
  • Mega Evolution coordination
  • Good friendship bonuses

Strong duo counters include:

  • Mega Gengar
  • Mega Banette
  • Shadow Metagross

Dodging charged moves like Future Sight becomes very important during smaller raids.

Trio Strategy

Difficulty: Challenging but reliable

Three strong trainers can usually clear Tapu Lele comfortably with proper counters.

Recommended approach:

  • At least one Mega Evolution active
  • Focus on Steel and Ghost attackers
  • Dodge only major charged moves
  • Maintain steady damage output

This is often the best balance between difficulty and consistency.

Beginner Raid Strategy

Recommended Group Size: 5–7 trainers

For casual groups, Tapu Lele becomes much safer with a larger lobby.

Beginners should focus on:

  • Using their strongest Steel or Ghost Pokémon
  • Rejoining quickly after fainting
  • Avoiding Dragon and Fighting types

Perfect movesets are helpful, but team size matters more for newer players.

Intermediate Strategy

Recommended Group Size: 3–5 trainers

At this level, team building starts to matter more.

Reliable counters include:

  • Chandelure
  • Metagross
  • Gengar

Important improvements:

  • Use proper raid movesets
  • Learn basic dodging timing
  • Include at least one Mega Evolution for team boosts

Advanced Strategy

Recommended Group Size: 2–3 trainers

Advanced raids focus heavily on efficiency and minimizing downtime.

Key goals:

  • Use fully powered counters
  • Build type-focused teams
  • Relobby as quickly as possible
  • Maintain high DPS uptime

Smaller mistakes become much more noticeable at this level.

Expert-Level Challenge Runs

Recommended Group Size: 2 trainers

Expert duos require near-perfect preparation.

Typical requirements:

  • Level 45–50 trainers
  • Optimized Shadow Pokémon
  • Mega rotations
  • Strong friendship bonuses
  • Fast relobby timing

Popular Mega choices:

  • Mega Gengar
  • Mega Banette

Future Sight is usually the most dangerous move during expert attempts.

Dangerous Moves to Watch

Move Type Threat Level
Psychic Psychic High
Moonblast Fairy High
Future Sight Psychic Very High

Tip: If Tapu Lele has Future Sight, dodging becomes much more valuable, especially for Ghost-type attackers.

Tapu Lele – Performance in Different Game Modes

PvE Performance

Tapu Lele performs best as an offensive Psychic or Fairy attacker, especially against Dragon, Fighting, and Dark-type Pokémon.

Strengths:

  • High Attack stat allows strong charged move pressure
  • Useful typing against Fighting and Dragon raid bosses
  • Psychic-type coverage helps against Poison and Fighting Pokémon

Weaknesses:

  • Low durability compared to bulky Legendaries
  • Faints quickly in longer raids
  • Outperformed by stronger Fairy and Psychic attackers

Raid Performance

For raid battles, Tapu Lele works best with a Fairy-focused moveset.

Recommended moveset:

  • Charm (Fast Move)
  • Dazzling Gleam (Charged Move)

It performs well against:

  • Dragon-type raid bosses
  • Fighting-type raid bosses
  • Dark-type raid bosses

However, several Pokémon generally perform better in the same role:

  • Shadow Gardevoir
  • Togekiss
  • Xerneas

Gym Battles

Gym Attacking

Tapu Lele can clear Dragon-type defenders fairly quickly thanks to Charm damage.

  • Strong against Pokémon like:
    • Dragonite
  • Applies steady Fairy-type pressure

Gym Defense

As a defender, Tapu Lele is less reliable because of its lower bulk.

  • Weak against Steel, Ghost, and Poison attackers
  • Does not stall battles as effectively as tankier defenders

About the Paldea Region

The Paldea region was introduced in Pokémon Scarlet and Pokémon Violet as the ninth main Pokémon generation. Inspired by the Iberian Peninsula, Paldea features a large open-world environment and introduces dozens of new Pokémon species. Many Paldean Pokémon have already begun appearing in Pokémon GO and are expected to become increasingly important in raids, PvP, and special events.

Shiny Paldean Pokémon

As Pokémon GO gradually releases more Paldean Pokémon, shiny forms continue to be introduced through events, Community Days, raids, and special celebrations. Collectors often prioritize shiny hunting whenever a new Paldean Pokémon becomes available.

Interesting Facts About Paldea

  • Paldea introduced over 100 new Pokémon species.
  • The region features the Terastal phenomenon in the main series games.
  • Koraidon and Miraidon serve as ride Pokémon throughout their adventures.
  • Paldea is the first fully open-world region in the Pokémon franchise.
  • The region introduced several convergent species and Paradox Pokémon.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Pokémon are in the Paldea Pokédex?

The Paldea Pokédex contains more than 400 Pokémon including regional and returning species.

Who are the Paldea starters?

Sprigatito, Fuecoco, and Quaxly are the starter Pokémon of the Paldea region.

Which legendary Pokémon belong to Paldea?

Koraidon, Miraidon, and the Treasures of Ruin are among Paldea's most notable legendary Pokémon.

What is the strongest Paldean Pokémon?

Baxcalibur, Annihilape, and Palafin are often considered among the strongest Paldean Pokémon.

Conclusion

The Paldea region introduced a fresh generation of Pokémon with creative designs, powerful battle potential, and memorable legendary Pokémon. Whether you're collecting new species, preparing for future Pokémon GO releases, or exploring Generation 9 for the first time, Paldean Pokémon offer plenty of exciting additions for trainers of all experience levels.

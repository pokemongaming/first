About the Johto Region

The Johto region was introduced in Pokémon Gold and Silver and brought 100 new Pokémon to the series. Many of these Pokémon remain popular today thanks to their strong battle performance, unique designs, and important roles in Pokémon GO.

Johto introduced fan-favorites such as Ampharos, Tyranitar, Scizor, Heracross, Umbreon, Espeon, Lugia, and Ho-Oh. Whether you enjoy raids, PvP battles, shiny hunting, or Pokédex completion, Johto offers something for every trainer.

Interesting Facts About Johto

  • Johto introduced 100 new Pokémon.
  • The region debuted in Pokémon Gold and Silver.
  • Lugia and Ho-Oh are Johto's mascot Legendary Pokémon.
  • Johto introduced baby Pokémon such as Pichu and Togepi.
  • Many Johto Pokémon later received Mega Evolutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Pokémon are in Johto?

There are 100 Pokémon in the Johto Pokédex.

Which is the strongest Johto Pokémon?

Tyranitar is widely considered one of the strongest Johto Pokémon due to its usefulness in raids and PvP.

Who are the Johto legendary Pokémon?

Lugia, Ho-Oh, Raikou, Entei, and Suicune are the primary legendary Pokémon associated with the Johto region.

What makes Johto special?

Johto introduced many beloved Pokémon and remains one of the most popular regions among Pokémon fans.

Conclusion

The Johto region introduced some of the most iconic Pokémon in the franchise. From legendary Pokémon such as Lugia and Ho-Oh to fan favorites like Ampharos and Tyranitar, Johto continues to play a major role in Pokémon GO. Whether you're building raid teams, competing in PvP battles, or hunting shinies, Johto Pokémon offer plenty of options for trainers of all experience levels.

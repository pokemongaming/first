Normal-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Normal-type Pokémon are a diverse group in Pokémon GO. Although Normal is not usually the first type Trainers think of for raid damage, individual Normal Pokémon can have important PvP, gym, evolution, collection and specialized battle roles.

How to use this hub: Use the generation and database sections to discover Pokémon. Then compare typing, stats and moves before opening the individual guide for detailed investment advice.

⭐ Normal Type

Normal is a distinct Pokémon type with a straightforward offensive interaction but a more interesting defensive profile.

🥊 Main Weakness

Fighting-type attacks are the primary offensive threat against a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

👻 Ghost Interaction

Normal's relationship with Ghost attacks is one of its most important distinguishing mechanics.

Normal Type Explained

The Normal typing should be analyzed separately from the individual Pokémon. A dual-type Pokémon can behave very differently from a pure Normal Pokémon.

⭐ Pure Normal

Pure Normal Pokémon have the straightforward Normal defensive profile. Their individual stats and moves determine their practical usefulness.

🔀 Dual Typing

Normal can be combined with Flying, Fighting, Fairy, Ghost and other types, producing substantially different matchup profiles.

🎯 Battle Role

Normal Pokémon can have very different purposes, including PvP, gyms, raids, evolution tasks, collection and specialist roles.

Normal-Type Weaknesses & Resistances

The pure Normal matchup is simple, but dual typing can change the practical result. Always check the complete Pokémon rather than relying only on the primary type.

⚠️ Normal Weakness

Fighting

Fighting-type attacks are super effective against a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

🛡️ Normal Defensive Interactions

Ghost: Immune None: Other Resistances

Pure Normal typing has no conventional resistance to most attacking types, but Ghost-type attacks do not damage it normally.


Dual-type warning: A Normal/Flying Pokémon, Normal/Fighting Pokémon and Normal/Ghost Pokémon can have very different weaknesses and resistances. Individual guides should always provide their own matchup chart.

How to Choose a Normal Pokémon

1. Start With the Role

Decide whether you need the Pokémon for raids, PvP, gyms, collection, evolution tasks or another purpose.

2. Check the Full Typing

Dual typing can dramatically change weaknesses, resistances and battle performance.

3. Check the Moves

A Pokémon's move combination can matter as much as its headline stats.

4. Consider Investment

Candy, Stardust, XL Candy, Elite TMs and availability should all be considered before spending resources.

Normal-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What is Normal weak against in Pokémon GO?

A pure Normal-type Pokémon is weak to Fighting-type attacks. Dual typing can add or modify weaknesses.

Is Normal good for raids?

Normal-type Pokémon generally have more specialized value than universal raid value. The individual Pokémon's stats, moves and matchup should determine the recommendation.

Can Normal Pokémon be useful in PvP?

Yes. Some Normal Pokémon can have useful PvP roles because of their moves, bulk, typing or access to particular charged attacks.

What is the best Normal Pokémon?

There is no single best Normal Pokémon for every purpose. The answer changes depending on raids, PvP, gyms, collection and other objectives.

Is Snorlax worth powering up?

That depends on the intended format and the available moveset. Its individual guide should compare the investment against other options.

Does every Normal Pokémon have a Mega form?

No. Mega Evolution is species-specific. Only include Mega information when the relevant Pokémon actually has a Mega form available in Pokémon GO.

Should every Pokémon have Shadow, Dynamax and Gigantamax sections?

No. These should be conditional sections. Showing an "available" form that does not exist creates inaccurate content and makes the page look automatically generated.

Fairy-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Fairy-type Pokémon have a particularly interesting role in Pokémon GO because Fairy attacks are valuable against several important Dragon, Dark and Fighting matchups.

However, not every Fairy Pokémon performs the same role. Some are better suited to raids, some are more interesting in PvP, and others are primarily valuable for collection, evolution or special forms.

This hub separates those roles so Trainers can understand not only which Pokémon are Fairy-type, but also which ones are worth investing resources into.

Fairy-Type Matchups

The Fairy typing has a distinctive defensive and offensive profile. Individual Pokémon can have very different matchups because of their secondary typing.

Fairy-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What are Fairy Pokémon weak against in Pokémon GO?

Pure Fairy-type Pokémon are weak to Poison and Steel attacks. A Pokémon's secondary typing can change its complete weakness profile.

What are Fairy Pokémon good against?

Fairy attacks are particularly useful against Dragon, Dark and Fighting targets.

What is the best Fairy Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on the specific raid boss and current movesets. Strong Fairy attackers can be valuable against Dragon, Dark and Fighting raid bosses.

Is Gardevoir a good Fairy Pokémon?

Gardevoir can be an important Fairy attacker and also has a Mega form. Its exact value depends on its current moves, forms and the battle matchup being considered.

Is Togekiss useful in Pokémon GO?

Togekiss can have value in both battle and collection contexts. Its Fairy/Flying typing gives it a different matchup profile from pure Fairy Pokémon.

Are Shadow Fairy Pokémon worth powering up?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with regular and special forms while considering Stardust, Candy, moves and your intended battle role.

Does Max CP determine the best Fairy Pokémon?

No. Max CP is only one data point. Moves, stats, typing, battle mechanics and matchup all influence practical usefulness.

Should every Fairy Pokémon page have the same content?

The core data structure can be consistent, but the actual analysis should be genuinely different for each Pokémon. Avoid simply replacing the Pokémon name inside an identical article.

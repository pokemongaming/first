19. Ground-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What are Ground-type Pokémon weak against?

Ground typing has important weaknesses, but a dual-type Pokémon must always be evaluated using its complete typing.

Are Ground Pokémon good raid attackers?

Some are excellent raid attackers, while others are more useful in PvP, collection or specific matchups. Stats, moves and availability all matter.

Should I power up Ground Pokémon for raids?

If you frequently encounter raid bosses vulnerable to Ground attacks, having strong Ground attackers can be valuable.

Is a Shadow Ground Pokémon always better?

Not automatically. Shadow Pokémon can have stronger offensive performance, but the resource cost and survivability should also be considered.

Are all Ground Pokémon available in the wild?

No. Availability can involve wild encounters, raids, research, eggs, events, trading and other methods.

Should CP be the main factor when choosing a Ground Pokémon?

No. CP is only one metric. Moves, stats, typing, battle format and availability can be more important.

Poison-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Poison-type Pokémon have a distinctive role in Pokémon GO. Their usefulness depends heavily on their individual stats, movesets and secondary typing. A Pokémon should therefore be evaluated as a complete package rather than by its type alone.

Why this hub exists: Use this page for broad Poison-type information and then move into individual Pokémon guides when you need detailed evolution, moveset, raid, Shadow, shiny or availability information.

☠️ Offensive Role

Poison-type attacks have specific offensive matchups. The practical value of an attacker depends on its available moves and the opponent it is fighting.

🛡️ Defensive Role

Poison typing provides several useful resistances, but individual dual typings can dramatically change how a Pokémon handles incoming attacks.

⚔️ Raid Role

Some Poison Pokémon are specialist raid attackers, while others are more valuable in PvP, collection, evolution families or specific type matchups.

How to Choose a Poison Pokémon

1. Identify the Opponent

Start with the raid boss or battle opponent. The best Poison Pokémon depends on the matchup.

2. Compare Moves

Fast-move energy generation and charged-move performance can matter more than simply comparing CP.

3. Check the Form

Regular and Shadow versions can have different practical roles. Mega and other special forms should be evaluated separately.

4. Consider Your Resources

Candy, Stardust, Elite TMs and availability should influence whether powering up a Pokémon makes sense for your account.

Poison-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What is Poison weak against in Pokémon GO?

Pure Poison typing is vulnerable to Ground- and Psychic-type attacks. A dual-type Pokémon can have a different final matchup depending on its second type.

What is the best Poison Pokémon in Pokémon GO?

There is no universal winner. The best choice depends on the raid boss, moveset, stats, form, weather and intended battle format.

Are Poison Pokémon good for raids?

Some Poison Pokémon can be useful raid attackers, especially when their moves and stats match the boss's weaknesses. However, their raid value should be compared against other available types.

Are Shadow Poison Pokémon worth powering up?

The answer depends on the individual Pokémon. Compare its Shadow performance, moves, resource requirements and the alternative attackers available to you.

Should every Poison Pokémon have an individual guide?

Important Pokémon should receive dedicated guides when you can provide genuinely useful information. Avoid publishing hundreds of pages containing nearly identical text.

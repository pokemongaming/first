01 · Type Overview

🔥 What Is the Fire Type?

Fire Pokémon specialize in powerful offensive pressure and are particularly useful against Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel opponents. In Pokémon GO, Fire attackers are especially valuable for raids against bosses vulnerable to Fire damage.

🔥 Offensive Role

Fire attackers are commonly used against Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel-type raid bosses. Their value depends on the Pokémon's Attack stat, moveset, availability and the current raid metagame.

🛡️ Defensive Profile

Pure Fire Pokémon resist Bug, Fairy, Fire, Grass, Ice and Steel attacks in Pokémon GO. Dual typing can significantly change the final matchup.

⚔️ Raid Role

Strong Fire attackers can fill dedicated raid teams, while Mega and Primal forms can additionally provide powerful party-wide type bonuses.

07 · Raid Intelligence

🏆 Fire Pokémon Raid Usefulness

For raid teams, the strongest Fire Pokémon are not always those with the highest CP. Attack stat, move quality, resistances, Mega bonuses, survivability and the target boss's weakness all matter.

🥇 Elite Fire Attackers

Reshiram, Mega Charizard Y, Mega Blaziken, Chandelure and other top-performing Fire attackers can form the core of a specialized Fire raid team.

🔥 Mega Strategy

Mega Evolution can improve the Mega Pokémon's own performance while also boosting compatible attacks used by other Trainers during the raid.

🌦️ Weather Strategy

Sunny or clear weather can increase the effectiveness of Fire attacks in Pokémon GO. Weather should be considered when planning difficult raids.

14 · Trainer Strategy

🧠 How to Build a Fire Raid Team

1. Check the Raid Boss

Identify the boss's weaknesses before choosing your Fire team. Never choose a Pokémon simply because it has a high CP.

2. Compare Attackers

Compare Attack, moveset, survivability and actual raid performance rather than relying exclusively on CP.

3. Consider Mega Support

A suitable Mega or Primal Pokémon can provide additional value to the entire raid party.

4. Check Weather

Weather can alter move effectiveness and therefore change the most efficient raid counters.

5. Don't Ignore Shadows

Shadow attackers can offer greater offensive pressure but usually sacrifice durability.

6. Build a Backup Team

A second Fire team helps when a difficult raid knocks out your first group before the battle is finished.

15 · Data Interpretation

📊 CP Is Not Everything

Why? A Pokémon's CP is a combined value influenced by its stats and level. It should not be treated as a direct ranking of raid damage.

For raid analysis, consider Attack, moveset, typing, weather, Mega/Primal bonuses, Shadow mechanics and the specific raid boss matchup.

This is why a lower-CP Pokémon can sometimes outperform a higher-CP Pokémon when it has a substantially better offensive moveset or typing.

16 · Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Fire-Type Pokémon FAQ

What is Fire strong against in Pokémon GO?

Fire attacks are especially useful against Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel-type opponents.

What is Fire weak to?

Pure Fire Pokémon are weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks. Dual typing can change the exact matchup.

What is the best Fire Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on the raid boss, available moves, weather and whether Mega, Primal or Shadow Pokémon are allowed or useful. Strong candidates include Mega Charizard Y, Mega Blaziken, Reshiram and Chandelure.

Is Primal Groudon a Fire attacker?

Yes. Primal Groudon has Ground/Fire typing and can contribute to Fire-focused strategies in addition to its major Ground role.

Is CP enough to choose a Fire raid attacker?

No. CP is only one metric. Attack, moveset, typing, survivability and the specific raid matchup are also important.

Should Mega and Primal Pokémon have separate database pages?

Yes. For a serious Pokémon GO database, special forms should have their own entries so users can compare their stats, typing, moves and raid roles accurately.

Should Shadow Pokémon have separate pages?

Yes. Shadow mechanics can significantly change offensive performance, so Shadow forms deserve dedicated information.

Are all Fire Pokémon available in Pokémon GO?

No. Availability varies by species, form, event, raid rotation, research, region and other game mechanics.

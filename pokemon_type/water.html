01 • TYPE OVERVIEW

What Is the Water Type?

Water is one of the most common Pokémon types and has played an important role in Pokémon battles since Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, Water-type Pokémon are particularly useful against Fire, Ground and Rock opponents.

💧 Water-Type Strengths

Water-type attacks are super effective against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon. This makes Water attackers useful in many raids where the boss has one of these weaknesses.

The Water category also contains several powerful Legendary, Mega and Primal Pokémon that can perform important roles in raid teams.

🌊 Why Water Pokémon Matter in Pokémon GO

A strong Water roster gives Trainers answers to many Fire-, Ground- and Rock-type raid bosses. Individual Pokémon can also gain additional value through Mega Evolution, Shadow bonuses, special moves and high Attack stats.

05 • POKÉMON GO STATS

CP & Battle Stats

CP is only one part of a Pokémon's performance. For raid attackers, Attack, move quality, typing, available moves and special mechanics can be more important than raw maximum CP.

⚔️ Attack

Attack strongly influences the damage a Pokémon can deal in Pokémon GO. High-Attack Water Pokémon are generally more attractive for raid attacking roles.

🛡️ Defense

Defense affects how much damage the Pokémon can withstand. A high Defense stat can improve survival, although raid attackers generally prioritize damage output.

❤️ HP

HP represents the Pokémon's stamina. Higher HP allows a Pokémon to remain in battle longer before fainting.

16 • EXPERT TIPS

Water-Type Investment Tips

  • Do not choose a raid attacker based only on maximum CP.
  • Compare Attack, moveset, typing and battle role.
  • Keep strong legacy or event-exclusive moves when they provide meaningful battle value.
  • Consider Shadow Pokémon separately from normal Pokémon.
  • Mega and Primal forms can provide team-wide benefits, not simply personal damage.
  • Check whether a Pokémon is more valuable in raids, PvP or both before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • Use Candy XL carefully on Pokémon that you actually intend to use at high levels.
17 • FAQ

Water Pokémon FAQ

What are Water Pokémon weak against?

Pure Water Pokémon are weak to Electric and Grass attacks. Dual typing can add or remove weaknesses.

What is the best Water Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on the current Pokémon GO movesets and available forms. Primal Kyogre, Mega Swampert and powerful Shadow or Legendary Water attackers are among the most important categories to evaluate.

Is CP the most important Water Pokémon statistic?

No. CP is useful for comparing overall progression, but raid performance also depends heavily on Attack, moves, typing, survivability and special mechanics.

Should I evolve every Water Pokémon?

Not necessarily. Before evolving a Pokémon, check its IVs, moves, PvP potential, raid usefulness and whether it has a special event or Community Day move.

Are Shadow Water Pokémon better?

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive benefit but also have reduced defensive durability. Whether a Shadow Pokémon is better depends on the battle and the specific comparison.

Are Mega and Primal Pokémon included in this database?

Yes. Special forms should be displayed separately because they can have different stats, typing, moves, CP and battle roles.

01 • TYPE OVERVIEW

What Is the Grass Type?

Grass is one of the original Pokémon types and remains important in Pokémon GO because Grass attacks are super effective against Water, Ground and Rock opponents. Grass Pokémon also have access to a wide range of secondary types that create very different battle roles.

🌿 Grass-Type Strengths

Grass-type attacks are super effective against Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon. This makes Grass attackers particularly useful when a raid boss has one of these types as a weakness.

Grass Pokémon can also gain additional value through secondary typing, Community Day moves, Shadow bonuses and Mega Evolution.

🌱 Why Grass Pokémon Matter

A well-built Grass team gives Trainers a strong answer to many Water, Ground and Rock raid bosses. Some Grass Pokémon also have excellent utility in PvP because of their combination of fast energy generation, bulk and charged-move pressure.

05 • MAX CP & STATS

Max CP, Attack, Defense & HP

Maximum CP provides a quick indication of overall combat potential, but it should not be used as the only measurement of a Pokémon's usefulness.

⚔️ Attack

Attack is particularly important for raid attackers because it contributes heavily to the damage a Pokémon can produce.

🛡️ Defense

Defense helps determine how much damage a Pokémon can withstand before fainting. Its importance varies depending on the battle format.

❤️ HP

HP represents stamina. A larger HP pool can help a Pokémon survive longer, although damage output and moves remain critical for raid performance.

08 • MEGA & PRIMAL

Mega & Primal Grass Pokémon

🌿 Mega Venusaur

Mega Venusaur is a Grass/Poison Pokémon with strong raid utility. Its Mega status also gives the party the relevant Mega attack-type bonuses.

  • Grass / Poison
  • Strong Grass role
  • Mega raid utility
  • Useful defensive typing

🌿 Mega Sceptile

Mega Sceptile combines Grass and Dragon typing and has a strong offensive profile. It is one of the most important Mega options to track in a Grass-type database.

  • Grass / Dragon
  • High offensive potential
  • Mega raid utility
  • Excellent Grass role

There is no standard Grass-type Primal Reversion equivalent to Primal Kyogre or Primal Groudon. Your database should therefore distinguish between Mega Evolution and Primal Reversion rather than displaying a generic “Mega/Primal” label for every type.

16 • INVESTMENT GUIDE

Should You Invest in a Grass Pokémon?

  • Check the Pokémon's Attack and overall stat distribution.
  • Check whether it has a competitive Grass moveset.
  • Check whether it has a legacy or event-exclusive move.
  • Compare the normal, Shadow and Mega versions.
  • Consider whether you need the Pokémon for raids or PvP.
  • Avoid spending large amounts of Stardust solely because a Pokémon has a high maximum CP.
  • Consider Candy XL investment only when the Pokémon has a clear long-term role.
17 • FAQ

Grass Pokémon FAQ

What are Grass Pokémon weak against?

Pure Grass Pokémon are weak to Fire, Flying, Bug, Ice and Poison attacks. Secondary typing can change this matchup.

What are Grass Pokémon strong against?

Grass attacks are super effective against Water, Ground and Rock opponents.

What is the best Grass Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on current Pokémon GO movesets and available forms. Mega Sceptile, Mega Venusaur, strong Shadow Pokémon and high-performing regular Grass attackers are important options to compare.

Is Mega Sceptile better than normal Sceptile?

Mega Sceptile has a different battle role and additional Mega mechanics. It should be evaluated separately from normal Sceptile.

Are Shadow Grass Pokémon worth powering up?

They can be valuable raid attackers because of their increased offensive potential. However, they also take increased damage and cost more resources to power up.

Is maximum CP the best way to rank Grass Pokémon?

No. Raid rankings should consider Attack, moves, typing, battle mechanics and actual damage output rather than CP alone.

4. Ghost Type — Typing & Battle Identity

Ghost is both an offensive and defensive typing with a number of distinctive interactions. The secondary typing of an individual Pokémon can significantly change its actual matchup profile.

For example, a pure Ghost Pokémon should not be treated the same way as a Ghost/Poison, Ghost/Dark, Ghost/Fairy or Ghost/Dragon Pokémon.

Pure Ghost

Use the basic Ghost type profile when evaluating pure Ghost Pokémon.

Dual-Type Ghost

Secondary typing can add resistances, weaknesses and different offensive opportunities.

STAB

19. Ghost Pokémon for Beginners

A beginner doesn't need a perfect six-Pokémon team on day one. The most useful approach is to build a small collection of reliable attackers and gradually improve them.

Start With Useful Encounters

Prioritize Ghost Pokémon that are readily obtainable and have useful moves rather than spending all resources on a rare Pokémon simply because its CP is high.

Check the Moves

A Pokémon with excellent stats can still perform poorly if its available moves don't suit the battle you are preparing for.

Don't Overspend Stardust

New players should generally avoid powering every Pokémon to the maximum level. Save expensive resources for Pokémon with a clear purpose.

21. Ghost-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What are Ghost-type Pokémon weak against?

Ghost typing has important interactions with Ghost and Dark attacks. Always check the complete typing of a dual-type Pokémon before selecting counters.

Are Ghost Pokémon good in raids?

Some Ghost Pokémon are excellent raid attackers, while others are more valuable in PvP or as collection targets. Moves and typing are important factors.

Which Ghost Pokémon should I power up?

The best choice depends on whether your goal is raids, PvP, Mega utility, collection or another specific purpose.

Is Shadow always better than regular?

No. Shadow Pokémon can provide stronger offensive output, but their overall value depends on the battle, resources and availability.

Are all Ghost Pokémon available in the wild?

No. Different species may appear through wild encounters, raids, research, eggs, events, trading or other methods.

Is Max CP enough to determine the best Ghost Pokémon?

No. CP is only one part of the picture. Moves, typing, stats, battle format and availability can be more important.

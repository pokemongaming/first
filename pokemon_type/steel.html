Steel-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Steel is one of the most useful defensive typings in Pokémon GO. However, Steel Pokémon vary substantially in their offensive stats, movesets, secondary typing and battle roles. This hub separates those factors so Trainers can make more informed choices.

How to use this page: Start with the type information and generation sections, then compare stats and moves. For investment decisions, open the individual Pokémon guide for detailed analysis.

⚙️ Defensive Strength

Steel has a broad collection of useful resistances. The exact defensive profile changes when Steel is paired with another type.

⚔️ Offensive Options

Steel attackers can be particularly valuable when their movesets provide the right matchup against bosses weak to Steel-type damage.

🏆 Battle Versatility

Different Steel Pokémon can specialize in raids, PvP, defensive play, Mega support, Shadow performance or other specific situations.

Steel Type Explained

Steel Pokémon are especially interesting because many of their most important characteristics come from their secondary typing. Always examine the full type combination before deciding whether a Pokémon is suitable for a specific battle.

⚙️ Primary Type

Steel-type Pokémon use Steel typing as either their primary or secondary type.

🔀 Dual Typing

Steel may be combined with types such as Psychic, Rock, Electric, Ground, Fairy, Fighting, Ghost and others.

🏆 Practical Role

A Steel Pokémon may function as a raid attacker, PvP specialist, Mega attacker, Shadow attacker or defensive specialist.

How to Choose a Steel Pokémon

1. Check the Full Typing

Never judge a dual-type Steel Pokémon using only the Steel type chart. Its second type can completely change the matchup.

2. Check the Moves

A Pokémon with excellent stats may still have limited value if its available moves are poorly suited to the intended battle.

3. Check the Form

Regular, Shadow and Mega forms can have significantly different practical roles.

4. Consider Resources

Candy, Stardust, Elite TMs, XL Candy and availability all matter when deciding whether to invest.

Steel-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What is Steel weak against in Pokémon GO?

Pure Steel typing is weak to Fire-, Fighting- and Ground-type attacks. Dual typing can change the final damage relationship.

Why is Steel considered a strong defensive type?

Steel has a large number of useful resistances, making Steel Pokémon valuable in situations where their typing and stats allow them to withstand incoming attacks.

What is the best Steel Pokémon for raids?

There is no universal answer. The best choice depends on the raid boss, moveset, weather, available forms and the other attackers available to the Trainer.

Is Metagross good in Pokémon GO?

Metagross can be highly useful depending on its moveset, form and battle situation. Its individual guide should compare its Steel and Psychic applications separately.

Are Shadow Steel Pokémon worth powering up?

It depends on the individual species. Compare the Shadow form's offensive benefit against its resource cost and available alternatives.

Should every Steel Pokémon have a separate page?

Important Pokémon should have dedicated pages when you can provide substantial, original information. Avoid creating large numbers of pages that contain almost identical text.

1. Psychic-Type Pokémon GO Hub

Psychic-type Pokémon occupy an important role across Pokémon GO's raid, gym and PvP systems. Some are built around powerful Psychic-type damage, while others gain value from their secondary typing, movepool or unique battle role.

This hub is designed to help trainers quickly answer practical questions such as which Psychic Pokémon are worth powering up, which ones are useful attackers, which Pokémon have Mega or Shadow forms and which evolution families deserve Candy and Stardust investment.

20. Psychic Pokémon GO FAQ

What are Psychic-type Pokémon weak to?

Psychic-type Pokémon have weaknesses that should be considered when selecting raid counters. Always check the complete typing of the individual Pokémon.

Is every Psychic Pokémon good for raids?

No. Typing alone does not determine raid performance. Stats, moves, availability and battle mechanics all matter.

Should I power up a Shadow Psychic Pokémon?

It depends on the Pokémon, its moveset and your available resources. Shadow and regular versions should be compared individually.

Are shiny Psychic Pokémon always available?

No. Shiny availability can depend on releases, events and other game-specific circumstances.

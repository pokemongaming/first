1. Dark Type Hub

Dark is an important offensive type in Pokémon GO, especially against Psychic- and Ghost-type raid bosses. A Pokémon's actual value depends on its moves, stats, secondary typing, forms and the current PvE/PvP meta.

⚠️ Weaknesses

Dark-type Pokémon take increased damage from Bug, Fairy and Fighting-type attacks. Dual typing can add further weaknesses or modify the matchup.

🛡️ Resistances

Dark typing reduces damage from Dark, Ghost and Psychic-type attacks. Secondary typing can create additional resistances or new vulnerabilities.

How to use this hub: start with the database, then open the individual guide for a Pokémon when you need its exact moveset, CP chart, evolution requirements, PvP details, raid counters or availability history.

5. Type & Dual-Type Breakdown

Pure Dark Pokémon have a predictable matchup profile, while dual-type Pokémon can be dramatically different. Always check the full typing before selecting a raid counter.

Pure Dark

Examples include Umbreon, Absol, Darkrai and several single-type families. They retain the standard Dark weakness and resistance profile.

Dark + Dragon

Hydreigon and Guzzlord gain Dragon interactions alongside Dark. Fairy attacks are especially important to consider because of the combined typing.

Dark + Fighting

Scrafty and Pangoro combine Dark's offensive profile with Fighting. Their defensive matchups differ substantially from pure Dark Pokémon.

Dark + Steel / Fairy / Flying

Bisharp, Kingambit, Grimmsnarl, Honchkrow and other dual types should be evaluated individually rather than using a generic Dark weakness list.

7. Weaknesses & Resistances

Bug — Weak

Bug attacks deal super-effective damage to Dark typing.

Fairy — Weak

Fairy is one of the most important counters to Dark Pokémon, particularly when secondary typing creates additional Fairy vulnerability.

Fighting — Weak

Fighting attackers are reliable answers to many Dark-type raid bosses.

Psychic — Resistant

Dark typing reduces Psychic damage, making Dark Pokémon valuable against Psychic raid bosses.

Ghost — Resistant

Dark Pokémon resist Ghost attacks and can turn Ghost-focused encounters into favorable matchups.

Dark — Resistant

Dark attacks are resisted by Dark typing, so mirror-type matchups require careful move selection.

8. Best Dark-Type Moves

Moveset recommendations change as Pokémon GO adds or rebalances moves. The strongest Dark attackers generally benefit from a Dark fast move paired with a strong Dark charged move when the goal is pure Dark-type PvE damage.

9. Dark Raid Tier & PvE Usefulness

Raid usefulness should be evaluated using damage output, survivability, move quality and role-specific rankings—not Max CP alone.

ELITE

Mega Tyranitar

One of the premier Dark-type PvE options. Its high Attack and strong Dark moves make it a standout choice when Dark damage is needed.

ELITE

Shadow Tyranitar

A powerful Dark attacker when equipped with an appropriate Dark moveset. Shadow mechanics increase offensive pressure while reducing durability.

STRONG

Hydreigon

A highly useful Dark attacker when it has its preferred Dark moveset, with strong PvE performance.

STRONG

Darkrai / Yveltal

Legendary and Mythical Dark attackers that can fill strong raid roles depending on moves and the current meta.

For a serious raid guide, add DPS/TDO, party size recommendations, weather effects, best counters, dodge strategy, revive planning and catch CP after the Pokémon's individual page is opened.

10. Mega / Primal Forms

Mega Evolution can substantially change a Pokémon's stats and raid utility. Dark-focused Mega options should be evaluated separately from their regular forms.

Mega Tyranitar

Major Dark PvE option with exceptionally high Attack and CP potential.

Mega Houndoom

Useful Dark/Fire Mega with value in both Dark and Fire contexts.

Mega Gyarados

Water/Dark Mega with useful raid flexibility.

Mega Absol

Dark Mega with high offensive stats but comparatively lower durability.

Primal note: Primal forms are a separate mechanic and should only be listed when the species actually has a Primal form. Do not invent a Primal Dark form for a Pokémon that does not have one.

11. Shadow / Dynamax / Gigantamax

👤 Shadow

Shadow Pokémon trade durability for greater offensive pressure. For Dark attackers, Shadow Tyranitar and other Shadow candidates can be highly relevant. Availability and move restrictions should be checked before recommending a specific Shadow build.

💠 Dynamax

Dynamax is a separate battle system. A Pokémon should only be listed here when its Dynamax form is actually supported in Pokémon GO.

🏰 Gigantamax

Gigantamax is also distinct from ordinary Dynamax. Keep a separate form record so readers do not confuse the two mechanics.

⚠️ Form verification

Availability changes over time. Individual Pokémon pages should show a clear status such as Available, Event-only, Previously available or Not currently available.

12. Shiny Dark Pokémon

Shiny availability varies by species and can differ from the availability of the regular form. A useful guide should distinguish “Shiny available” from “Shiny currently obtainable.”

✨ Shiny available

Mark species where the Shiny form has been released in Pokémon GO.

🔒 Not released

Do not imply that an unreleased Shiny can currently be obtained.

📅 Event Shiny

Explain when a Shiny was introduced and whether its current availability is event dependent.

📸 Comparison

16. Dark Type FAQ

What are Dark Pokémon weak against in Pokémon GO?

Dark-type Pokémon are weak to Bug, Fairy and Fighting-type attacks. Dual typing can add additional weaknesses.

What types does Dark resist?

Dark typing reduces damage from Dark, Ghost and Psychic attacks.

What is the best Dark attacker?

The answer depends on the current moves and form. Mega Tyranitar and strong Shadow options are among the major Dark PvE choices, while Hydreigon, Darkrai and Yveltal can also be important.

Is Darkrai good for raids?

Darkrai can be a strong Dark-type PvE attacker when equipped with a suitable Dark moveset. Its individual page should be used for the current moveset and ranking.

Is Umbreon good for raids?

Umbreon is much more notable for PvP and defensive bulk than for raid damage.

Should I power up every Dark Pokémon?

No. Prioritize Pokémon with strong moves, useful stats, relevant forms and a role you actually need.

TRAINER'S FIELD GUIDE

Why Electric Pokémon Matter in Pokémon GO

Electric-type Pokémon occupy an important role in Pokémon GO because their attacks are super effective against Water- and Flying-type opponents. This makes strong Electric attackers particularly useful for certain raids, Gym battles and other PvE situations.

However, not every Electric Pokémon performs the same role. Some are powerful raid attackers, while others are better suited for PvP, collecting, evolution progression or specialized teams.

This hub is designed to help Trainers understand those differences instead of simply listing Pokémon names and statistics.

ABOUT THIS DATABASE

How We Organize Electric Pokémon Data

Pokémon GO information can change as the game receives new moves, balance adjustments, events, raid rotations and new Pokémon releases. For that reason, this hub separates permanent reference information from time-sensitive availability information.

Battle recommendations should also be interpreted in context. A Pokémon that looks impressive on paper may not always be the best choice for a particular raid, PvP league or moveset.

Whenever possible, individual guides should explain why a Pokémon is recommended rather than simply assigning it a ranking.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Electric Type Pokémon FAQ

What is Electric type weak against?

Electric-type Pokémon are primarily vulnerable to Ground-type attacks. Secondary typing can introduce additional weaknesses or resistances.

What are Electric Pokémon strong against?

Electric-type attacks are particularly useful against Water- and Flying-type opponents.

Is the highest CP Electric Pokémon always the best?

No. Max CP is only one part of Pokémon GO performance. Attack, Defense, HP, moveset, typing, role and the specific battle matchup all matter.

Should I evolve every Electric Pokémon?

Not necessarily. Before evolving a Pokémon, consider its IVs, moves, intended role, evolution cost and whether the evolved form has meaningful value for your account.

Are Shadow Electric Pokémon worth powering up?

Some can be excellent investments, but the answer depends on the individual Pokémon, moveset, available resources and the battles you intend to use it for.

Where can I find current Electric Pokémon events?

Check the site's current event calendar and individual Pokémon availability pages because encounters can change with events and seasons.

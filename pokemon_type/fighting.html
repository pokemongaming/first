Fighting-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Fighting-type Pokémon are one of the most useful offensive categories in Pokémon GO. Fighting attacks are particularly valuable against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark opponents. This makes strong Fighting attackers useful in a wide range of raid encounters.

The important distinction is that a Pokémon being Fighting-type does not automatically make it a good Fighting attacker. Individual stats, moves, secondary typing, availability and battle mechanics determine its actual usefulness.

This hub therefore separates type information from practical battle recommendations. A Pokémon can be excellent in raids, useful in PvP, valuable because of a Mega form, or primarily interesting to collectors.

Fighting-Type Matchups

Fighting attacks are super effective against five Pokémon types: Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark. Fighting Pokémon themselves have a different defensive profile, so offensive and defensive type charts should not be confused. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

⚔️ Fighting Attacks Are Strong Against

Normal Rock Steel Ice Dark

These matchups are one of the main reasons Fighting attackers are valuable in raids.

⚠️ Fighting Attacks Are Weak Against

Bug Fairy Flying Ghost Poison Psychic

These are important defensive considerations when selecting a Fighting attacker or choosing counters against a Fighting raid boss.

Fighting Pokémon Weaknesses

A pure Fighting-type Pokémon is vulnerable to Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks in Pokémon GO. Dual-type Pokémon can have additional weaknesses or altered damage relationships. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Flying Psychic Fairy
Important Dual-Type Rule Never copy the pure Fighting weakness chart onto a dual-type Pokémon. For example, Fighting/Steel, Fighting/Dark and Fighting/Poison Pokémon have different defensive profiles.

Fighting Pokémon Resistances

Pure Fighting Pokémon resist Bug, Dark and Rock attacks in Pokémon GO. Dual typing can add further resistances or modify the final damage multiplier. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Fighting-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What is Fighting super effective against in Pokémon GO?

Fighting attacks are super effective against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark Pokémon. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

What is Fighting weak against in Pokémon GO?

Fighting Pokémon are vulnerable to Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks when considering a pure Fighting typing. Dual typing can change the final matchup.

What are the best Fighting Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on the current moves and battle environment. Strong examples to investigate include Terrakion, Lucario, Conkeldurr and Machamp. Official Pokémon GO raid material has specifically listed several of these as Fighting-type raid options. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Is Machamp still useful?

Machamp remains a useful Fighting option, particularly for Trainers who already have good Machamp or Shadow Machamp resources. Compare its current performance with newer Fighting attackers before making a large investment.

Is Lucario a good Fighting Pokémon?

Lucario has Fighting/Steel typing and has access to important Fighting-oriented moves. It also has a Mega form, making it worth treating regular Lucario and Mega Lucario as separate analysis targets. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Are Shadow Fighting Pokémon better?

Not automatically. Shadow Pokémon can offer greater offensive potential but also require more careful resource planning. Compare the Shadow and regular versions directly.

Is high Max CP enough to determine the best Fighting Pokémon?

No. Max CP is only one metric. Attack, Defense, HP, moves, typing, availability and the actual raid matchup all matter.

Should Mega and regular Pokémon have separate guides?

Yes. A Mega form can have different stats, battle roles, resource requirements and availability considerations. Treating it as simply another paragraph on the regular page loses useful information.

Does every Fighting Pokémon need a Mega or Primal section?

No. Only species with applicable forms should receive detailed form analysis. For others, clearly state that no applicable Mega or Primal form exists rather than creating filler.

Fighting-Type Data & Editorial Methodology

A comprehensive type hub should tell readers how its data is maintained. This is especially important for Pokémon GO because moves, availability and battle mechanics can change.

📊 Stats

Verify Max CP, Attack, Defense and HP before publication. Do not reuse unverified values between species.

⚔️ Raid Rankings

Explain why a Pokémon receives its raid tier instead of providing an unexplained rating.

✨ Shiny

Verify shiny availability independently for each Pokémon and special form.

📅 Availability

Review encounter information after major events, raid rotations and game updates.

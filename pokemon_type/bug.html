🐛 Bug Type Hub

Bug-type Pokémon have an important place in the Pokémon GO battle system. Bug attacks can be especially useful against certain Psychic-, Dark- and Grass-type opponents, while individual Bug Pokémon can gain additional roles from secondary typings such as Steel, Flying, Poison, Fire or Rock.

This hub is designed to separate simple Pokédex information from practical Pokémon GO analysis. A Bug Pokémon is not automatically a strong Bug attacker. Its Attack stat, moveset, survivability, typing, availability and current battle environment all matter.

📈 Max CP, Attack, Defense & HP

Max CP is useful for understanding a Pokémon's overall progression, but it should not be treated as a complete measure of raid performance. Pokémon GO battle performance also depends on offensive stats, durability and moves.

Attack Important for raid damage and offensive pressure.
Defense Helps determine how much damage a Pokémon can withstand.
HP Determines the Pokémon's base endurance.

Why Max CP should not be your only metric

Two Pokémon can have similar CP but perform very differently in raids because their Attack values, movesets and typing are different. The database should therefore display CP alongside the underlying battle information.

🧠 How to Choose a Bug Pokémon

1. Start with the raid boss

First determine whether Bug damage is actually effective against the raid boss. Do not build a Bug team simply because a Pokémon has Bug typing.

2. Compare Attack

Attack is an important part of raid damage, but it should always be evaluated alongside the Pokémon's moves.

3. Check the moveset

A Pokémon can have excellent Attack but still be a poor Bug attacker if its available Bug moves are inefficient for the intended role.

4. Consider survivability

A Pokémon that faints quickly may require additional relobbying, which can reduce practical raid performance.

5. Consider Mega bonuses

Mega Pokémon can provide additional team value through their battle effects, making them valuable even when another Pokémon has stronger individual statistics.

❓ Bug-Type Pokémon FAQ

What are Bug-type Pokémon weak to?

Pure Bug-type Pokémon are commonly weak to Fire, Flying and Rock attacks. Dual typing can change the final matchup.

What are Bug-type Pokémon resistant to?

Pure Bug Pokémon have useful resistances including Grass, Fighting and Ground. Individual dual-type Pokémon can have additional resistances or weaknesses.

What is the best Bug-type Pokémon for raids?

Raid usefulness depends on the current Pokémon GO moveset, stats, forms and matchup. Dedicated Mega and high-performing Bug attackers should be compared using current battle data rather than CP alone.

Is Mega Beedrill important for Bug raids?

Mega Beedrill is particularly relevant to a Bug-focused database because Mega Evolution gives it a specialized role as a Bug/Poison attacker and can provide Mega raid bonuses.

Is a high-CP Bug Pokémon automatically better?

No. CP is only one part of the picture. Moves, Attack, Defense, HP, typing and the specific battle matchup all affect practical usefulness.

Should Shadow Bug Pokémon have separate entries?

Yes. If the Shadow form changes the practical battle role, it deserves separate analysis rather than being hidden inside the standard Pokémon entry.

How often should this page be updated?

Update the database when Pokémon GO releases new Pokémon, forms, moves, events or mechanics that change the information displayed here.

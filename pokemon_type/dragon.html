Dragon-Type Pokémon GO Overview

Dragon-type Pokémon occupy an important place in Pokémon GO. Many Dragon species have high offensive stats, strong CP potential or useful secondary typings. Dragon-type attacks can also be valuable when facing opponents vulnerable to Dragon damage.

However, being a Dragon-type Pokémon does not automatically make a species a top attacker. Raid performance depends on the Pokémon's stats, moveset, form, game mechanics and the specific opponent being fought.

This hub therefore separates simple Pokédex information from battle usefulness. A Pokémon can be valuable for raids, PvP, collecting, Mega Evolution, special forms or simply completing the Pokédex.

Dragon-Type Pokémon GO FAQ

What are Dragon Pokémon weak against in Pokémon GO?

Pure Dragon-type Pokémon are weak to Dragon, Fairy and Ice attacks. Secondary typing can change the complete weakness profile.

What types do Dragon attacks work well against?

Dragon attacks are particularly useful against Dragon-type targets. Their usefulness should always be considered in the context of the specific raid boss and available attacker movesets.

What is the best Dragon Pokémon for raids?

The answer depends on the exact matchup and moveset. Rayquaza, Garchomp, Salamence and other strong Dragon attackers can have important raid roles, while special forms may perform differently from their regular versions.

Is Dragonite still useful in Pokémon GO?

Dragonite can remain useful for Trainers because of its combination of stats, typing and access to different moves. Its value should be evaluated against the current raid and PvP environment.

Are Shadow Dragon Pokémon better than regular Pokémon?

Not automatically. Shadow Pokémon can provide stronger offensive performance, but they also require different resource considerations. Compare the Shadow and regular versions for the specific battle role.

Should I power up every Dragon Pokémon I catch?

No. Prioritize Pokémon with strong movesets, useful stats, relevant forms and a clear role in the battles you actually play.

Does high Max CP mean a Dragon Pokémon is a good raid attacker?

No. Max CP alone does not determine raid performance. Moves, stats, typing, battle mechanics and the specific raid matchup all matter.

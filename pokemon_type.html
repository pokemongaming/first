12. Pokémon GO Types FAQ

How many Pokémon types are there in Pokémon GO?

Pokémon GO uses 18 Pokémon types. Individual Pokémon can have one or two types.

What is the best Pokémon type?

There is no single best type for every situation. Different types have different offensive and defensive matchups, and their usefulness depends on raids, PvP and the specific opponent.

Why do some Pokémon have two types?

Dual-type Pokémon combine two type profiles. This can provide additional offensive opportunities and defensive resistances, but can also introduce additional weaknesses.

Are type matchups the same for raids and PvP?

The underlying type relationships are important in both formats, but raids and PvP use different battle systems, so a Pokémon's overall performance can differ significantly.

Which type is best for raids?

It depends on the raid boss. The best attacking type is normally the one that exploits the boss's weaknesses while providing strong practical damage.

Should I choose Pokémon only by type?

No. Type is only one part of Pokémon GO battle performance. Moves, stats, forms, availability and the battle format also matter.

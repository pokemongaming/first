Lampent Type

Why Lampent's Typing Matters

Lampent's Ghost/Fire combination gives it an unusual offensive and defensive profile. Its STAB attacks can cover both Ghost and Fire targets, while its typing also provides several useful resistances.

Defensive Type Profile

Lampent takes increased damage from Water, Ground, Rock, Ghost and Dark attacks. It has strong resistances to Bug, Fighting and several other types, while Normal and Fighting attacks are especially ineffective against its Ghost typing.

Should You Power Up Lampent for Raids?

For most players, no. Lampent's main value is its evolutionary role, collection value and limited PvP niche rather than high-level raid performance.

Better investment:

If your goal is Fire or Ghost raid performance, evolving Lampent into Chandelure is generally much more valuable than investing heavily in Lampent itself.

08 • IV GUIDE

Lampent IV Guide

The best IV spread depends on what you intend to do with Lampent. For general collection purposes, a 15/15/15 specimen is the perfect IV combination. For Great League, however, lower Attack IVs can often be more useful because of CP limits.

15 Perfect Attack IV
15 Perfect Defense IV
15 Perfect HP IV

Perfect IV Lampent

A perfect Lampent has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Its Level 50 maximum CP is 1,931.

Great League IV Consideration

Because Lampent has a relatively low CP ceiling, Great League is its most realistic PvP environment. If you intend to use Lampent competitively, evaluate its actual PvP IV rank instead of automatically choosing 15/15/15.

IV Investment Advice

Keep high-quality Lampent specimens if you plan to evolve them into Chandelure. For PvP, use a dedicated IV checker before spending Stardust.

09 • PVP

Lampent PvP Guide

Lampent's relatively low CP makes Great League the only standard league where it can realistically fit. It can function as a niche pick because of its unique Ghost/Fire typing and access to coverage moves.

Great League

Lampent can use Ember, Heat Wave and Energy Ball in Great League. Ember provides useful energy generation, while Heat Wave and Energy Ball give it offensive coverage.

Ultra League

Lampent's 1,931 maximum CP makes it unsuitable for standard Ultra League competition because it cannot reach the league's CP ceiling.

Master League

Lampent is not a Master League Pokémon. Its maximum CP is far too low to compete with the high-stat Pokémon that dominate the format.

Great League Moveset

Ember + Heat Wave + Energy Ball

PvP note:

PvP rankings change as moves, balance updates and metagame trends change. Treat Lampent as a niche Great League option rather than a general-purpose PvP investment.

13 • EVOLUTION

Litwick → Lampent → Chandelure

Lampent is the middle evolution in the Litwick family. It evolves from Litwick using 25 Litwick Candy and then evolves into Chandelure using 100 Litwick Candy and a Unova Stone.

Should You Evolve Lampent?

For most players, yes. Chandelure is substantially more useful for battle than Lampent, making evolution the natural long-term goal for strong Litwick and Lampent specimens.

Evolution tip:

Before evolving, check the Pokémon's IVs, appraisal, Shiny status and whether it is a Shadow Pokémon. A strong specimen can be worth keeping for the final Chandelure stage.

14 • RESOURCES

Lampent Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Lampent itself generally does not justify heavy Stardust investment, but Litwick Candy is valuable because the evolutionary family leads to Chandelure.

  • Save Litwick Candy for strong Chandelure candidates.
  • Keep high-IV and useful PvP specimens before evolving.
  • Collect Candy XL if you plan to build Chandelure beyond Level 40.
  • Check the moveset after evolution.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust on Lampent without a specific PvP goal.
  • Compare Shadow and regular specimens before purifying.
  • Keep Shiny Lampent if you want a complete Shiny evolutionary family.

Buddy Distance

Lampent has a 5 km Buddy distance. Walking with it can help generate additional Candy when you need resources for evolution or powering up.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Lampent

Before Investing

  • Check whether the Pokémon is intended for PvP or evolution.
  • Review its IV spread.
  • Check whether it is Shiny or Shadow.
  • Consider saving Candy for Chandelure.
  • Avoid unnecessary Stardust investment.

For Great League

If you specifically want to experiment with Lampent in Great League, examine PvP IV rankings and the current moveset before powering it up.

For Raids

Lampent is not a recommended high-level raid investment. Its evolutionary successor, Chandelure, is considerably more appropriate for serious Fire and Ghost PvE teams.

For Collectors

Shiny Lampent, Shadow Lampent and high-IV specimens can have collection value even when their battle performance is limited.

Common Lampent Investment Mistakes

01 • DATABASE

Aron Overview

Aron is a small Steel/Rock-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is the first stage of a three-stage evolutionary family that leads to Lairon and Aggron. Because Aron is an early evolution-stage Pokémon, its biggest long-term value usually comes from collecting Candy and finding strong specimens to evolve.

Shiny Aron is a desirable collector variant, while Shadow Aron can be interesting for players who enjoy Shadow collections or want to evaluate its eventual evolution into Shadow Lairon and Shadow Aggron.

Why Aron's Typing Matters

Aron's Steel/Rock combination gives it a very different defensive profile from many Rock-type Pokémon. Steel provides numerous resistances, while Rock adds useful offensive coverage. The combination also creates important weaknesses that matter when fighting Aron as a raid or Team GO Rocket encounter.

09 • PVP

Aron PvP Guide

Aron can be considered in specialty formats where its CP and typing fit the rules, but it is not normally a broad PvP investment. Its evolutionary forms are much more relevant for players looking for competitive builds.

Great League

Aron can fit under the Great League CP limit, but its performance is specialized. Its Steel/Rock typing gives it useful resistances, while its low HP and limited overall stats restrict its consistency.

Ultra League

Aron is generally not an efficient Ultra League investment because of its low CP ceiling and early evolution stage.

Master League

Aron is not suitable for standard Master League play because its maximum CP is far below the league's typical power level.

PvP Advice

If you are building Aron for PvP, check the current cup rules and IV rankings first. A specialized low-CP build can have a very different ideal IV spread from a raid or evolution candidate.

10 • SHADOW FORM

Shadow Aron

Shadow Aron is the Team GO Rocket variant of Aron. It retains its Steel/Rock typing, while Shadow mechanics increase outgoing damage and also increase damage received. Shadow Aron can be worth keeping because it can be evolved through the family while retaining Shadow status.

Evolution Strategy

If you are collecting the family, save enough Aron Candy to evolve through the line. A strong Aron can become a long-term Aggron project, while a Shiny or Shadow Aron can preserve its special status as it evolves.

Important:

Shiny status and Shadow status are retained when an eligible Pokémon evolves. That makes good Shiny and Shadow Aron specimens worth evaluating before transferring them.

12 • SHINY

Shiny Aron

✨
Shiny Aron

Shiny Aron has an alternate coloration and retains the same species battle stats as regular Aron.

Shiny Aron Collection Value

Shiny Aron is especially useful for completing the Shiny Hoenn collection and for creating a Shiny Lairon or Shiny Aggron. A Shiny Aron with strong IVs can also become a long-term evolution project.

Can Shiny Aron Evolve?

Yes. Shiny status is retained through evolution, so a Shiny Aron can evolve into Shiny Lairon and then Shiny Aggron.

Availability note:

Shiny encounter availability can change with events and encounter rotations. For a live website, update the availability text when Pokémon GO introduces new encounter sources.

18 • FAQ

Aron FAQ

Is Aron a Generation 3 Pokémon?

Yes. Aron was introduced in Generation 3 and is Pokédex #304.

What type is Aron?

Aron is a Steel/Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Aron weak to?

Aron is weak to Fire, Fighting, Ground and Water attacks.

What does Aron evolve into?

Aron evolves into Lairon and then Aggron.

How much Candy does Aron need to evolve?

Aron requires 25 Candy to evolve into Lairon, while Lairon requires 100 Candy to evolve into Aggron.

What is Aron's Max CP?

Aron can reach approximately 1,297 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Aron good for raids?

Aron is generally not a strong direct raid attacker. Its greater value is as the first stage of the Lairon and Aggron evolutionary family.

Is Aron good in PvP?

Aron is mainly a niche or specialty-format option. Check the current cup rules and rankings before investing.

Can Aron be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Aron is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters, with availability depending on the current encounter pool and events.

Does Shiny Aron evolve into Shiny Lairon?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Aron evolves.

Is Shadow Aron available?

Shadow Aron can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and events. Its availability can change over time.

Does Shadow Aron stay Shadow when it evolves?

Yes. Shadow status is retained when a Shadow Pokémon evolves through its normal evolutionary family.

Should I purify Shadow Aron?

Do not purify automatically. Consider its Shadow value, IVs, evolution plans and collection goals first.

Is a hundo Aron worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Aron is a strong collection and evolution candidate.

Should I power up Aron?

Usually not. Save Stardust for a stronger evolutionary stage unless you have a specific low-CP or specialty-format reason.

What is Aron's best use?

Aron's primary value is collecting Candy, evolving into Lairon and Aggron, completing the Hoenn Pokédex and collecting Shiny or Shadow variants.

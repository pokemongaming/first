01 · OVERVIEW

Aerodactyl Overview

Aerodactyl is a Rock / Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. It is associated with ancient fossil Pokémon and does not evolve through a normal evolutionary chain.

Pokémon GO also features Mega Aerodactyl, a temporary Mega Evolution that significantly improves Aerodactyl's offensive potential and makes it much more relevant for Rock-focused raid teams.

Why Aerodactyl's Typing Matters

Aerodactyl's dual typing gives it several useful resistances but also creates a broader weakness profile than a pure Rock-type Pokémon. Its Rock typing makes it relevant for Rock damage, while its Flying typing changes how it interacts with Electric, Ice and other attacking types.

20 · FAQ

Aerodactyl FAQ

What type is Aerodactyl?

Aerodactyl is a Rock / Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Aerodactyl weak to?

Aerodactyl is vulnerable to Water, Electric, Ice, Rock and Steel attacks.

Does Aerodactyl evolve?

Aerodactyl does not have a normal evolution. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Aerodactyl.

Is Shiny Aerodactyl available?

Yes. Shiny Aerodactyl is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Mega Aerodactyl good for raids?

Mega Aerodactyl can be a strong Rock-focused raid option and also provides Mega-related team utility while active.

Is Shadow Aerodactyl good?

Shadow Aerodactyl has the increased offensive profile associated with Shadow Pokémon, but also reduced durability. Its value depends on its moveset and your battle goals.

Should I purify Shadow Aerodactyl?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, particularly if you value Shadow battle performance.

Can Shiny Aerodactyl Mega Evolve?

Yes. An eligible Shiny Aerodactyl can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

What is Mega Aerodactyl's type?

Mega Aerodactyl remains a Rock / Flying-type Pokémon.

Is Aerodactyl a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Aerodactyl is not a Legendary Pokémon. It is a Fossil Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1.

Is Aerodactyl good for raids?

Regular Aerodactyl can contribute Rock-type damage, while Mega Aerodactyl is the more significant raid form for players building around Rock attacks.

What is Aerodactyl's Pokédex number?

Aerodactyl is National Pokédex number #142.

Staravia

Staravia is a Normal and Flying-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it sits between Starly and Staraptor in the evolutionary family. Staravia can be found in regular, Shiny and Shadow forms, giving collectors and trainers several variants to hunt.

06 • PVE

Staravia Raid & PvE Performance

Staravia is not considered a competitive raid attacker. Its relatively low CP and stats compared with stronger Flying-type Pokémon make it more useful as an evolutionary milestone and collection Pokémon than as a serious raid investment.

Should You Power Up Staravia for Raids?

Generally, no. If your goal is raid performance, resources are usually better spent on stronger Flying or other appropriate attackers. Staravia is more interesting for collectors, evolution projects, special cups or personal challenges.

Investment advice:

Before spending large amounts of Stardust or Candy, compare Staravia with its final evolution, Staraptor, and with other Pokémon available for the same battle role.

09 • PVP

Staravia PvP Guide

Staravia's low maximum CP prevents it from being a natural Master League Pokémon. Its main PvP interest comes from lower CP formats and special cups where its Normal/Flying typing and moveset may have a role.

Great League

Staravia can fit under the 1,500 CP Great League limit, although it is generally a niche choice rather than a universally strong meta Pokémon.

Ultra League

Staravia can technically reach the Ultra League CP range, but its overall stats make it difficult to recommend as a standard Ultra League investment.

Master League

Staravia is not suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for competitive Master League play.

PvP Moves to Consider

Quick Attack + Fly + Brave Bird

PvP note:

PvP rankings change as move stats, cups and the competitive metagame change. Treat Staravia as a specialist or personal-choice pick rather than a general PvP recommendation.

Should You Evolve Staravia?

If your objective is to complete the evolutionary family or build a stronger Flying-type Pokémon, continuing to Staraptor is the natural next step. Staravia itself is generally more useful as an intermediate evolution than as a long-term combat investment.

Evolution note:

15 • RESOURCES

Staravia Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Staravia requires Candy for both its evolution from Starly and its evolution into Staraptor. Because Staravia itself has limited battle utility, think carefully before spending large amounts of Stardust or Candy XL on it.

  • Save Candy if your goal is to evolve Staravia into Staraptor.
  • Keep strong IV Staravia for collection or evolution projects.
  • Keep interesting Shiny Staravia if you want every family stage.
  • Compare Shadow Staravia before purifying it.
  • Check the current moveset before using valuable TMs.
  • Consider whether Staraptor provides greater value before powering up.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Staravia

Before Investing

  • Decide whether Staravia is being kept for PvP, collection or evolution.
  • Check its IVs and CP.
  • Compare it with Staraptor before spending Stardust.
  • Check whether it is Shiny or Shadow.
  • Consider whether purification would actually benefit the Pokémon.
  • Review the current move pool before using TMs.

During PvP Team Building

Staravia should be considered a niche option rather than a default team member. Its Normal/Flying typing gives it useful resistances, but its relatively modest overall stats limit its competitive ceiling.

Long-Term Investment

For most players, the most sensible long-term use of Staravia Candy is evolution into Staraptor rather than building a heavily powered Staravia. However, collectors and players interested in special cups may have different priorities.

Wartortle

Wartortle is the second stage of the Squirtle evolution family. This pure Water-type Pokémon is best known as the middle evolution between Squirtle and Blastoise. In Pokémon GO, Wartortle can be useful for collectors, Great League specialists and players building a complete Kanto Pokédex, although Blastoise is generally the stronger battle investment.

01 • DATABASE

Wartortle Overview

Wartortle is a Water-type Pokémon from Generation 1 and the Kanto region. It evolves from Squirtle and can later evolve into Blastoise. Unlike many final-stage Pokémon, Wartortle is primarily useful as a collection and specialist PvP Pokémon rather than a top-tier raid attacker.

Why Wartortle's Typing Matters

Wartortle's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward battle profile. Water attacks are super effective against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Wartortle's defensive typing allows it to resist Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

06 • PVE

Wartortle Raid & PvE Performance

Wartortle is not a competitive top-tier raid attacker. Its relatively low Attack stat and CP ceiling make Blastoise a much more practical investment for players looking for a strong Water attacker.

Should You Use Wartortle in Raids?

Usually no. If you have access to Blastoise or stronger Water attackers, those Pokémon should generally take priority. Wartortle is better kept for evolution, collection, specialized PvP situations or personal challenge teams.

08 • IV GUIDE

Wartortle IV Guide

The best Wartortle IV spread depends on what you want to do with it. For general collection and evolution, a high-IV Wartortle is desirable. For Great League, low Attack and higher Defense/HP can be more useful because of CP-based league restrictions.

Perfect IV Wartortle

A 15/15/15 Wartortle is the perfect IV specimen. However, perfect IVs do not automatically make Wartortle a top-tier battle Pokémon.

09 • PVP

Wartortle PvP Guide

Wartortle is much more interesting in PvP than it is in raids. Its defensive profile and access to Aqua Jet and Ice Beam can make it a specialist choice in Great League formats.

Great League

Great League is the most natural CP-limited league for Wartortle. Its preferred PvP moveset is commonly built around Water Gun, Aqua Jet and Ice Beam.

Great League Recommended Moveset

Water Gun + Aqua Jet + Ice Beam

Ultra League

Wartortle's CP ceiling limits its ability to compete at the same level as Pokémon designed for Ultra League. It can still appear in special formats, but it is not normally a first-choice Ultra League investment.

Master League

Wartortle is not a practical Master League Pokémon. Its maximum CP is far below the level required to compete effectively with fully evolved Master League Pokémon.

Does Shiny Wartortle Have Better Stats?

No. Shiny Pokémon do not receive a special stat bonus just because they are Shiny. A Shiny Wartortle and regular Wartortle with identical IVs have the same battle statistics.

Shiny Wartortle for Collectors

  • Keep high-IV Shiny Wartortle for collection value.
  • Consider evolving a Shiny Squirtle for a Shiny Blastoise.
  • Check IVs before transferring duplicate Shinies.
  • Keep event-costume Shinies separately when applicable.
  • A Shiny Wartortle can be kept unevolved for collection.
11 • SHADOW FORM

Shadow Wartortle

Shadow Wartortle is the Shadow version of Wartortle. It remains a pure Water-type Pokémon, but the Shadow mechanic changes its offensive and defensive performance.

16 • RESOURCES

Wartortle Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Wartortle itself usually does not justify a large Stardust investment unless you specifically want it for PvP, collection purposes or a personal challenge. Candy is often more valuable for completing the Squirtle family and evolving strong specimens into Blastoise.

  • Save Squirtle Candy for strong evolution candidates.
  • Keep good IV Wartortle specimens before transferring duplicates.
  • Use Candy XL only when you have a specific high-level project.
  • Check PvP IVs before powering up a Great League Wartortle.
  • Do not spend Stardust simply because a Wartortle has high IVs.
  • Compare Wartortle with Blastoise before making a major investment.
17 • AVAILABILITY

Wartortle Availability

Wartortle can be obtained through the Squirtle evolution family when players have sufficient Squirtle Candy. Its availability as a direct encounter can vary with events, research and special Pokémon GO rotations.

Availability Strategy

If Wartortle is not currently appearing as an encounter, collect Squirtle and evolve it when you have enough Candy.

Shadow Wartortle Availability

Shadow Wartortle is connected to Shadow Squirtle and Team GO Rocket availability. Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Rocket events and game updates.

Shiny Wartortle Availability

Shiny Wartortle is associated with the Shiny Squirtle evolution family. A Shiny Squirtle can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Wartortle

Before a Battle

  • Check whether the battle is Great League or another format.
  • Check Wartortle's CP and IV distribution.
  • Consider Water Gun for consistent Water pressure.
  • Use Aqua Jet for faster Charged Move pressure.
  • Consider Ice Beam for additional coverage.
  • Do not use Wartortle as a default raid Water attacker.

During Team Building

Wartortle should normally be considered a specialist rather than a universal Water-type attacker. Its lower Attack and CP compared with Blastoise make it less useful for PvE, while its moves and defensive profile give it more potential in CP-limited PvP formats.

Long-Term Investment

For most players, the strongest long-term investment is usually a high-quality Squirtle that can eventually become Blastoise. A Wartortle should receive significant investment mainly when you have a specific PvP, collection or challenge goal.

20 • FAQ

Wartortle FAQ

What type is Wartortle?

Wartortle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wartortle weak to?

Wartortle is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

What is Wartortle's Max CP?

Wartortle reaches 1,682 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Wartortle's base stats?

Wartortle has 126 Attack, 155 Defense and 153 HP.

What is Wartortle's best raid moveset?

Water Gun and Hydro Pump provide its strongest Water-focused PvE combination, although Wartortle is not a top-tier raid attacker.

What is Wartortle's best Great League moveset?

Water Gun with Aqua Jet and Ice Beam is a strong specialist Great League moveset.

Is Wartortle good for raids?

Generally no. Wartortle's lower Attack and CP make Blastoise and other stronger Water attackers better choices for most raids.

Is Wartortle good in Great League?

Wartortle can be useful as a specialist Great League Pokémon when built with an appropriate IV spread and moveset.

Is Wartortle good in Master League?

No. Its maximum CP is too low to compete effectively with typical Master League Pokémon.

How do you evolve Squirtle into Wartortle?

Squirtle evolves into Wartortle using 25 Squirtle Candy.

How do you evolve Wartortle into Blastoise?

Wartortle evolves into Blastoise using 100 Squirtle Candy.

Can Shiny Squirtle evolve into Shiny Wartortle?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Squirtle evolves into Wartortle.

Can Shiny Wartortle evolve into Shiny Blastoise?

Yes. Shiny Wartortle can evolve into Shiny Blastoise while retaining its Shiny status.

Does Shiny Wartortle have better stats?

No. Shiny Pokémon do not receive a special statistical bonus simply for being Shiny.

Is Shadow Wartortle Water type?

Yes. Shadow Wartortle remains a pure Water-type Pokémon.

How do you get Shadow Wartortle?

Shadow Wartortle can be obtained by evolving a Shadow Squirtle when the applicable Shadow Pokémon is available.

Does Shadow Wartortle have higher attack?

The Shadow mechanic increases damage dealt and damage received; the displayed species base stats remain the same.

Can Shadow Wartortle have Frustration?

Yes. Shadow Wartortle can have Frustration as its Charged Move until it is removed under applicable Team GO Rocket event rules.

Should I purify Shadow Wartortle?

Not automatically. Compare its Shadow value, IVs, moves and your personal goals before purifying it.

Should I evolve Shadow Wartortle into Shadow Blastoise?

If your goal is battle performance or completing the Shadow Squirtle family, evolving it into Shadow Blastoise is generally more useful than keeping it unevolved.

Is Wartortle worth powering up?

Usually only for a specific Great League, collection, Shadow or personal challenge project. Most players will get more battle value by investing in Blastoise or another stronger Water Pokémon.

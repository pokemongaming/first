Diggersby

Diggersby is a Normal and Ground-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. It is the evolved form of Bunnelby and is particularly notable in Pokémon GO for its PvP potential, unusual typing and flexible moveset options.

Diggersby PvP Guide

Diggersby is primarily known for PvP rather than high-level raid damage. Its defensive stat profile, typing and move options can make it valuable in specific league formats.

Great League

Great League is generally the format most associated with Diggersby. A properly built Diggersby can provide bulk, Ground-type pressure and useful coverage.

Ultra League

Diggersby may be considered in certain Ultra League situations, although resource requirements and alternative Pokémon should be compared carefully.

Master League

Diggersby's relatively limited maximum CP generally makes Master League a less natural role.

Purified Diggersby

Purified Diggersby is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Diggersby. Purification removes the Shadow battle modifier and changes the Pokémon into its Purified state.

  • Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status.
  • IVs may improve through Purification mechanics.
  • Shadow battle bonuses are removed.
  • Compare PvP performance before purifying.
  • Purification is a permanent decision.

Diggersby FAQ

What type is Diggersby?

Diggersby is a Normal and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Diggersby weak to?

Diggersby is vulnerable to Water, Grass, Ice and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Diggersby good for PvP?

Diggersby can be particularly useful in PvP because of its bulk, typing and moveset options.

Can Diggersby be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and current game availability.

Is Shadow Diggersby stronger?

Shadow Diggersby has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability compared with the regular version.

Should I purify Shadow Diggersby?

Not automatically. Compare the potential PvP and collection value of the Shadow Pokémon before purifying.

What is a 100% Diggersby?

XXL size classification does not normally change standard battle stats or moveset performance.

No. XXS size classification is generally separate from standard battle stats.

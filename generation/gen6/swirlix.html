Swirlix Overview

Why Swirlix's Typing Matters

Fairy is one of Pokémon GO's important defensive and offensive typings. Swirlix benefits from resistance to Dragon, Fighting, Bug and Dark attacks, while Poison and Steel attacks deal super-effective damage.

Should You Power Up Swirlix?

For most players, Swirlix is not a priority for expensive Level 50 investment. If you enjoy the Pokémon, PvP, collecting or its evolution into Slurpuff, however, a strong specimen can still be worth keeping.

Swirlix PvP Guide

Swirlix can be considered for lower-CP formats, although its exact PvP value depends on the current move pool, cups and metagame.

Great League

Swirlix's low maximum CP makes Great League the most natural standard league to investigate. However, players should compare it with other Fairy Pokémon before investing Stardust and Candy.

Ultra League

Swirlix is less naturally suited to Ultra League because its maximum CP is relatively low.

Master League

Swirlix is not designed as a Master League Pokémon. Its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for competitive Master League play.

XXL Swirlix

XXL Swirlix is a size variant that can be encountered independently of its IV quality. A large Swirlix is not automatically a high-IV Swirlix.

📏

XXL Swirlix

XXL identifies a particularly large specimen. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Swirlix can have any IV combination.

XXL Swirlix Collection Tips

  • Keep an XXL Swirlix if you collect size records.
  • Check its IVs separately from its size.
  • Consider keeping XXL Shiny Swirlix as a special collection piece.
  • Record XXL specimens separately from Hundo specimens.

XXS Swirlix

XXS Swirlix represents a particularly small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is independent of IV quality.

🔍

XXS Swirlix

A tiny Swirlix can be valuable to size-focused collectors. Its size does not determine its Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

XXS Swirlix Collection Tips

  • Keep an XXS specimen if you collect size variants.
  • Check IVs independently from size.
  • Shiny + XXS can be a particularly interesting collection combination.
  • Lucky + XXS is another possible collection combination.

Swirlix FAQ

What type is Swirlix?

Swirlix is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Swirlix weak to?

Swirlix is weak to Poison- and Steel-type attacks.

What is Swirlix's max CP?

Swirlix has a Level 50 maximum CP of 1,333 with perfect IVs.

What are Swirlix's stats?

Swirlix has 109 Attack, 119 Defense and 158 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Swirlix available?

Yes. Shiny Swirlix is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Swirlix available?

Shadow Swirlix is not currently listed as an available Shadow Pokémon.

Is Purified Swirlix available?

No. Because Shadow Swirlix is not currently available, Purified Swirlix cannot currently be obtained.

Can Swirlix be Lucky?

Yes. Swirlix can become Lucky when obtained through a successful Lucky Trade.

What is a 100% Swirlix?

A 100% Swirlix has 15 Attack IV, 15 Defense IV and 15 HP IV.

What CP is a hundo Swirlix?

A perfect Swirlix has 505 CP at Level 15, 673 CP at Level 20, 842 CP at Level 25 and 1,333 CP at Level 50.

Can Swirlix be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification and does not determine a Swirlix's IVs.

Can Swirlix be XXS?

Yes. XXS is a size classification for particularly small Pokémon and is separate from IV quality.

Is XXL Swirlix better than normal Swirlix?

Not automatically. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. XXL is mainly useful for collection and size-related features.

Is Swirlix good for raids?

Swirlix is generally not a high-priority raid attacker. Its main value is as a collection, evolution and specialty Pokémon.

Is Swirlix good for PvP?

Its PvP value depends on the current move pool and format. Great League and limited cups are more relevant than Master League because of Swirlix's low maximum CP.

Does Swirlix evolve?

Yes. Swirlix evolves into Slurpuff.

Can Shiny Swirlix be Lucky?

Yes. A Shiny Swirlix can also become Lucky if the trade results in Lucky status.

Can Shiny Swirlix be XXL or XXS?

Yes. Shiny status and size classifications are separate properties, so a Shiny Swirlix can also qualify as XXL or XXS.

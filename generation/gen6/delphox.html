10 — PURIFIED

✨ Purified Delphox

Purified Delphox is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Delphox. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered carefully before committing.

Purification decision:

A higher IV result is not automatically better than keeping a useful Shadow Pokémon. Compare the purpose of the Pokémon before purifying.

17 — FAQ

Delphox FAQ

What type is Delphox?

Delphox is a Fire and Psychic-type Pokémon.

Can Delphox be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Delphox is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution methods.

Is Shadow Delphox good?

Shadow Delphox can be useful for players focused on increased offensive pressure.

Should I purify Shadow Delphox?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow benefits with the potential Purified result before deciding.

Is Lucky Delphox useful?

Lucky Delphox can be valuable because of reduced Stardust costs for powering it up.

What is a 100% Delphox?

A perfect Delphox has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Delphox have higher combat stats?

Size classification does not automatically make an XXL Delphox stronger in battle.

Does Delphox have a Mega Evolution?

Yes. Delphox has Mega Delphox as a temporary Mega Evolution form.

Is Mega Delphox good for raids?

Mega Delphox can be an extremely powerful option for appropriate Fire and Psychic raid matchups.

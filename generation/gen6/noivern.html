22 · FAQ

Noivern FAQ

What type is Noivern?

Noivern is a Dragon and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Noivern weak to?

Noivern is weak to Ice, Dragon, Fairy and Rock-type attacks.

What Pokémon evolves into Noivern?

Noivern evolves from Noibat.

Is Shiny Noivern available?

Shiny Noivern can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Noibat when its Shiny form is available.

Is Noivern a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Noivern is a standard Pokémon from Generation 6 and is the final evolution of Noibat.

Is 100% Noivern rare?

A 100% Noivern has perfect 15/15/15 IVs and is naturally desirable to collectors and players who enjoy high-IV Pokémon.

What is Lucky Noivern?

Lucky Noivern is a Noivern that has become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is XXL Noivern?

XXL Noivern is an extra-large specimen. Size is separate from IVs and does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

What is XXS Noivern?

XXS Noivern is an extra-small specimen. Like XXL, its size is separate from its IV combination.

Should I power up Noivern?

It depends on your goal. Noivern can be useful for collection and selected battle situations, but players focused purely on raid efficiency should compare it with stronger current attackers.

Is Shadow Noivern stronger than regular Noivern?

Shadow Pokémon have different offensive and defensive characteristics. Whether a Shadow Noivern is preferable depends on its current availability, moveset and your intended use.

Should I purify Shadow Noivern?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions and consider your collection and battle goals before making the decision.

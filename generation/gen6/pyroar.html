Pyroar

Pyroar is a Fire and Normal-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. It evolves from Litleo and has distinct male and female visual forms. In Pokémon GO, Pyroar can also appear in special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Pyroar Overview

Pyroar is the evolved form of Litleo and is known for its distinctive lion-inspired appearance. Unlike many Pokémon with purely cosmetic gender differences, Pyroar has noticeably different male and female appearances.

Pokédex #668

Male and Female Pyroar

Male Pyroar has a much larger mane, while Female Pyroar has a smaller and more streamlined appearance. Both forms represent Pyroar and should be considered separate collection targets for players interested in form completion.

Pyroar Type

Pyroar has a dual Fire and Normal typing.

🔥 Fire ⚪ Normal

Why Pyroar's Typing Matters

Fire gives Pyroar access to strong offensive pressure against several Pokémon types, while its Normal typing changes its defensive profile. Understanding both types is important when choosing Pyroar for raids or PvP.

Shadow Pyroar

Shadow Pyroar should be evaluated separately from Regular Pyroar because the Shadow mechanic changes its offensive and defensive battle profile.

🌑🔥

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally provide increased damage potential while taking more damage themselves.

Shadow Pyroar Investment Checklist

  • Check its IVs before investing heavily.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare its performance with other Fire attackers.
  • Consider the increased resource cost.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Pyroar

Purified Pyroar originates from a Shadow Pyroar that has been purified. Purification changes its status and removes the Shadow battle profile.

✨🔥

Purified Form

Consider purification carefully because it is a permanent decision.

Lucky Pyroar

Lucky Pyroar is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when being powered up.

🍀✨

Why Lucky Pyroar Is Useful

  • Lower Stardust investment.
  • Useful for collectors.
  • Can be a good high-level project.
  • Lucky status does not change typing.

Pyroar FAQ

What type is Pyroar?

Pyroar is a Fire and Normal-type Pokémon.

Does Pyroar have male and female forms?

Yes. Male and Female Pyroar have visibly different appearances.

Is Shiny Pyroar available?

Shiny availability depends on the Pokémon GO encounter method and current game availability.

Can Pyroar be Lucky?

Yes, an eligible Pyroar can become Lucky through Pokémon trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Pyroar?

A 100% Pyroar has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Pyroar?

XXL refers to an unusually large size variant.

What is XXS Pyroar?

XXS refers to an unusually small size variant.

Should I purify Shadow Pyroar?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow battle profile with the Purified result before making a permanent decision.

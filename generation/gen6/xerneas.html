Xerneas PvP Guide

Master League

Master League is the most natural PvP environment for Xerneas because its high CP ceiling allows it to use its Legendary stats without being restricted by the lower CP caps.

Ultra League

Xerneas can be used in Ultra League, although investing a Legendary Pokémon for a lower CP format requires careful consideration of IVs and resources.

Great League

Standard Xerneas is not naturally suited to the Great League because of its high CP and Legendary investment requirements.

Purified Xerneas Status

Purified Pokémon Mechanics

When a Shadow Pokémon is purified, its Shadow status is removed and it receives the standard purification benefits. Purification also replaces Frustration with Return.

🍀 Lucky Xerneas

Lucky Xerneas is a traded Xerneas that receives the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Xerneas attractive for long-term investment.

Lucky Xerneas

Xerneas Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Xerneas requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Xerneas is a Legendary Pokémon, resource planning is especially important for players building multiple high-level specimens.

  • Save Xerneas Candy for strong specimens.
  • Collect Xerneas Candy XL for Level 40+ investment.
  • Prioritize strong IVs for expensive projects.
  • A Lucky Xerneas can reduce Stardust investment.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite resources.

Xerneas FAQ

What type is Xerneas?

Xerneas is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Xerneas weak to?

Xerneas is weak to Poison and Steel-type attacks.

What is Xerneas's max CP?

Xerneas has a Level 50 maximum CP of 4,275.

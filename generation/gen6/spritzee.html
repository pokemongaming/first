Spritzee Overview

Spritzee FAQ

What type is Spritzee?

Spritzee is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Spritzee weak to?

Spritzee is weak to Poison- and Steel-type attacks.

What does Spritzee evolve into?

Spritzee evolves into Aromatisse.

Is Shiny Spritzee available?

Yes. Shiny Spritzee is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Lucky Spritzee different from regular Spritzee?

What is a 100% Spritzee?

A 100% Spritzee has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Spritzee?

Does XXL Spritzee have different base stats?

Does XXS Spritzee have different base stats?

Is Shadow Spritzee available?

Shadow Pokémon availability changes with Team GO Rocket rotations. The page should only identify Shadow Spritzee as currently obtainable when current game data confirms it.

Can Spritzee be Purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, Purified Spritzee depends on Shadow Spritzee being available.

Is Spritzee good for raids?

Spritzee is generally not a high-priority raid attacker. Its main value is as an evolution, collection and potential PvP target.

Is Spritzee good for PvP?

Spritzee's low maximum CP makes lower leagues more relevant than Master League, but its actual PvP value depends on its current moveset and the metagame.

Can a Shiny Spritzee be Lucky?

Yes. A Pokémon can combine multiple characteristics, so a Shiny Spritzee can also have Lucky status if obtained through an eligible trade.

Can a 100% Spritzee be Lucky?

A Lucky Pokémon's IVs are determined when the trade result is generated. Therefore, a Lucky Spritzee can have very strong IVs, including a perfect IV result.

Can a 100% Spritzee be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Spritzee can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

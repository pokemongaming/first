Why Sylveon's Fairy Typing Matters

Sylveon's Fairy typing gives it useful defensive matchups against Fighting, Bug and Dark attacks while making it especially valuable against Dragon-type opponents. Its main weaknesses are Poison and Steel.

Eevee to Sylveon Evolution

Sylveon is one of Eevee's special evolutions. The normal evolution requirement and the one-time nickname method should be kept separate on your database so trainers understand how the evolution works.

🦊

Eevee

Base Pokémon used to evolve into Sylveon.

🎀

Sylveon

Fairy-type Eeveelution obtained through the Sylveon-specific evolution requirement.

Buddy Evolution

Sylveon's standard evolution method involves Eevee earning Buddy Hearts before evolution. The nickname method can be used for the first eligible evolution, while subsequent evolutions should follow the normal in-game requirement.

Evolution Cost

25 Eevee Candy is required for the evolution. Make sure the Sylveon evolution silhouette is shown before confirming the evolution.

Sylveon FAQ

What type is Sylveon?

Sylveon is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Sylveon weak to?

Sylveon is weak to Poison and Steel-type attacks.

What is Sylveon's maximum CP?

Sylveon's maximum CP is 3,470 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Sylveon's base stats?

Sylveon has 203 Attack, 205 Defense and 216 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Sylveon available?

Yes. Shiny Sylveon is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Sylveon an Eeveelution?

Yes. Sylveon is one of Eevee's evolutions and was introduced in Generation 6.

How do I evolve Eevee into Sylveon?

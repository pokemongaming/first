Goomy

Goomy is a Dragon-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6 from the Kalos region. It evolves into Sliggoo and eventually Goodra, giving players access to a powerful Dragon-type evolution family.

Goomy Overview

Goomy is the first Pokémon in the Goomy → Sliggoo → Goodra evolution family. While Goomy itself is not designed as a major raid attacker, evolving a strong specimen can provide a useful Dragon-type Pokémon.

Regular Goomy

Regular Goomy is the standard form of the species. It is primarily collected for evolution into Sliggoo and Goodra.

  • Keep strong IV specimens for future evolution.
  • Consider evolution requirements before spending Candy.
  • Compare multiple Goomy before evolving.
  • Keep at least one for Pokédex and collection purposes.

Shiny Goomy

✨ Shiny Goomy

Shiny Goomy has alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny status remains associated with the Pokémon as it evolves.

Should You Evolve Shiny Goomy?

Many collectors choose to evolve at least one Shiny Goomy through its full evolution family. If you only have one, you may want to consider whether you prefer keeping the base form or obtaining Shiny Sliggoo and Shiny Goodra.

Shadow Goomy

🌑 Shadow Goomy

Shadow Goomy is the Shadow version of Goomy. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile and should be evaluated separately from regular and Purified versions.

Shadow Goomy Investment

Before purifying or evolving a Shadow Goomy, compare its IVs, evolution potential and your available resources. Shadow Pokémon can have increased offensive pressure at the cost of reduced durability.

Availability note:

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Update this section whenever Goomy's Shadow availability changes.

Lucky Goomy

🍀 Lucky Goomy

Lucky Goomy is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are particularly valuable for players planning to power up and evolve a Pokémon because of their Stardust investment advantages.

Is Lucky Goomy Worth Keeping?

A Lucky Goomy can be an attractive long-term investment, especially if it has strong IVs and you plan to evolve it into Goodra.

100% Goomy

A 100% IV Goomy has the perfect IV combination of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

💯 Perfect IV Goomy

A 15/15/15 Goomy is a strong collector and long-term evolution candidate. However, non-perfect IV Goomy can also evolve into highly useful Pokémon.

XXL Goomy

📏 XXL Goomy

XXL Goomy is an unusually large size variant. Size variants are mainly relevant for collectors, Pokédex records and game features that track Pokémon size.

XXL status does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has better battle IVs or higher attack strength. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

XXS Goomy

🔍 XXS Goomy

XXS Goomy is an unusually small size variant. It is primarily a collection variant and can be useful for players interested in unusual size records.

Like XXL Goomy, XXS status does not determine IV quality. A tiny Goomy can still have excellent IVs.

Goomy FAQ

What type is Goomy?

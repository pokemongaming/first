09 — PVP

Goodra PvP Guide

Master League

Goodra's high overall stats and Dragon typing give it potential in Master League. Its effectiveness depends heavily on its current moveset and the opponents appearing in the metagame.

Ultra League

Goodra can also be considered in Ultra League depending on the specific moveset and IV spread. CP limits should always be checked when building a league-specific Goodra.

Great League

Goodra is much less straightforward to build for Great League because of its evolution requirements and CP limitations. League-specific IV optimization should be considered separately from a perfect-IV collection Goodra.

Should You Power Up Shadow Goodra?

  • Compare Shadow Goodra with your existing Dragon attackers.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Goodra

Lucky Goodra is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Goodra particularly attractive for long-term investment.

🍀
Lucky Goodra

A Lucky Goodra can be a useful Stardust-efficient option for players planning a long-term power-up project.

Why Keep a Lucky Goodra?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term Candy XL investments.
  • Attractive collector variant.
  • Lucky status can make a high-IV Goodra especially desirable.
Lucky IV reminder:

Lucky Pokémon receive a guaranteed minimum IV floor from the trade process, but a Lucky Pokémon is not automatically a 100% IV Pokémon.

23 — FAQ

Goodra FAQ

What type is Goodra?

Goodra is a pure Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Goodra weak against?

Goodra is weak to Dragon, Fairy and Ice attacks.

What does Goodra evolve from?

Goodra evolves from Sliggoo and is the final evolution of the Goomy family.

Is Goodra good in Pokémon GO?

Goodra can be useful in Pokémon GO, particularly for players interested in Dragon-type Pokémon and PvP. Its value depends on the current moveset and battle format.

Is Shiny Goodra available?

Yes. Shiny Goodra is an alternate-color variant of Goodra.

Is Shadow Goodra better than regular Goodra?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on your battle and investment goals.

Should I purify Shadow Goodra?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

What is a 100% Goodra?

A 100% Goodra has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Goodra?

Lucky Goodra is obtained through trading and benefits from the reduced Stardust cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

Is Lucky Goodra automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor from trading, but they are not automatically perfect IV.

What is an XXL Goodra?

XXL Goodra is an unusually large Goodra. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is an XXS Goodra?

XXS Goodra is an unusually small Goodra. Its size does not determine its IV percentage.

Can Goodra be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Goodra can be both XXL and 15/15/15.

Can Goodra be both XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. An XXS Goodra can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Goodra valuable?

Shiny Goodra can be particularly desirable to collectors because its alternate coloration makes it distinct from standard Goodra.

What is the best Goodra to keep?

The best Goodra depends on your goal. A high-IV Goodra can be useful for investment, while Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, XXL and XXS Goodra can be valuable for collectors.

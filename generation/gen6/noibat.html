Noibat IV Guide

Should You Keep a High-IV Noibat?

22 • FAQ

Noibat FAQ

What type is Noibat?

Noibat is a Dragon and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Noibat weak to?

Noibat is weak to Fairy, Ice, Dragon and Rock attacks.

Does Noibat evolve?

Yes. Noibat evolves directly into Noivern.

Is Shiny Noibat available?

Yes. Shiny Noibat is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Noibat available?

If Shadow Noibat is available through the current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket rotation, it can be obtained through the relevant Shadow encounter. Current availability should always be checked.

Can Shadow Noibat evolve?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can evolve while retaining their Shadow status, so a Shadow Noibat can be evolved into Shadow Noivern when the relevant mechanics allow it.

Can Shadow Noibat be purified?

An eligible Shadow Noibat can be purified. The resulting Pokémon is a Purified Noibat and no longer has Shadow status.

What is a 100% Noibat?

A 100% Noibat has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Noibat?

Lucky Noibat is a Noibat obtained through a trade that becomes Lucky. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Noibat?

XXL Noibat is an unusually large Noibat and can be valuable for collectors and size-focused goals.

What is XXS Noibat?

XXS Noibat is an unusually small Noibat and can be valuable to collectors who track Pokémon size variants.

Is Noibat good for raids?

Noibat is generally not considered a priority raid attacker because of its relatively low battle stats. Its evolution into Noivern is the more natural long-term investment target.

Should I evolve my 100% Noibat?

If your goal is to own a perfect Noivern, a 100% Noibat is an excellent evolution candidate. You should still consider your collection and resource goals before evolving.

Should I keep a Shiny Noibat unevolved?

That depends on your collection goals. You can keep one Shiny Noibat unevolved and evolve another Shiny Noibat if you want both forms in your collection.

Can a Lucky Noibat evolve into Lucky Noivern?

Yes. Lucky status is retained when the Pokémon evolves.

Can a Purified Noibat evolve?

Yes. A Purified Noibat can evolve while retaining its Purified status.

Can an XXL Noibat evolve?

Size characteristics can be relevant to collection tracking, and the evolution should be evaluated according to the current Pokémon GO size mechanics.

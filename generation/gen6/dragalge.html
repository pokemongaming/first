02 • TYPE

Dragalge Type

Dragalge is a dual-type Poison and Dragon Pokémon. This combination gives it a distinctive defensive profile compared with many other Dragon-type Pokémon.

Why Dragalge's Typing Matters

Dragalge's Poison/Dragon typing gives it several useful resistances, including a particularly strong resistance to Grass attacks. It also takes super-effective damage from Dragon, Ground, Ice and Psychic attacks.

07 • PVP

Dragalge PvP Guide

Dragalge's defensive stats and unusual Poison/Dragon typing make it more interesting in PvP than its relatively modest maximum CP might suggest.

Great League

Dragalge can be useful in Great League when its typing and moveset provide favorable matchups. IV selection should be evaluated specifically for the Great League CP limit rather than simply choosing the highest percentage IV Pokémon.

Ultra League

Ultra League is another important format for Dragalge. Its defensive profile can allow it to pressure certain opponents while its Poison and Dragon attacks provide useful coverage.

Master League

Dragalge's relatively low maximum CP makes Master League a much less natural environment for it compared with higher-CP Dragon Pokémon.

What Is 100% Dragalge?

A 100% Dragalge has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is also called a hundo. A hundo Dragalge reaches the maximum CP possible for its level.

10 • SHINY

Shiny Dragalge

Shiny Dragalge is available in Pokémon GO. Its Shiny form has a different coloration from the standard Dragalge.

✨

Shiny Dragalge

Shiny Dragalge can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Skrelp. Shiny status is retained when Skrelp evolves into Dragalge.

Shiny Dragalge IVs

Shiny status does not automatically mean high IVs. A Shiny Dragalge can have any eligible IV combination. For collectors, a Shiny hundo is an especially rare combination.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Dragalge

Lucky Dragalge is a special collection state rather than a separate species or typing. Dragalge can be Lucky when obtained through an eligible Lucky Trade after obtaining the appropriate Skrelp or Dragalge.

How to Use Dragalge

Dragalge Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Dragalge?

Zygarde

02 • FORMES

Zygarde 10%, 50% & Complete Forme

Zygarde has three main Formes in Pokémon GO. These are not ordinary cosmetic changes: the different Formes have different combat statistics and maximum CP values.

🐕
Zygarde 10% Forme Starting Forme • Max CP 2,685

The smaller canine-like Forme. It is the Forme associated with the initial Zygarde encounter.

🐍
Zygarde 50% Forme 50 Cells • Max CP 3,855

The standard serpentine Forme. It requires 50 Zygarde Cells to change from 10% Forme.

🦎
Zygarde Complete Forme 200 Additional Cells • Max CP 4,208

The most powerful Forme, requiring another 200 Cells after reaching the 50% Forme.

Forme reminder:

Zygarde Forme changes are not the same as a traditional Pokémon evolution. The Zygarde Cell system controls these Formes.

07 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Zygarde PvP Guide

Zygarde is particularly interesting in GO Battle League because its 50% and Complete Formes have substantially different bulk and CP profiles.

Great League

Standard Zygarde investment is not naturally suited to Great League because of its high CP profile. League-specific availability should always be checked before building a restricted-format Zygarde.

Ultra League

50% Zygarde can be relevant in Ultra League depending on the season, moveset and available IV combination. The Dragon/Ground typing gives it useful matchup coverage.

Master League

Zygarde becomes much more interesting in Master League. Complete Forme's enormous HP stat gives it exceptional bulk, while its typing and charged moves provide useful pressure.

08 • ZYGARDE CELLS

Zygarde Cells & Routes

Zygarde Cells are the key resource used to change Zygarde's Forme. Cells can be encountered while exploring Routes after obtaining the Zygarde Cube.

Forme Change Requirements
0 Starting 10% Forme
50 10% → 50%
250 Total for Complete

How to Collect Zygarde Cells

  • Obtain Zygarde and the Zygarde Cube through the relevant Special Research.
  • Open the Route feature in Pokémon GO.
  • Follow eligible Routes.
  • Watch for the green Zygarde Cell glow.
  • Tap the Cell before completing the Route.
  • Continue collecting Cells until you have enough for the desired Forme.
Complete Forme target:

Zygarde requires 50 Cells to change from 10% Forme to 50% Forme, followed by another 200 Cells to reach Complete Forme.

14 • XXL

XXL Zygarde

XXL is a size classification rather than a separate species or Forme. A Zygarde can have a size category associated with its individual Pokémon data.

XXL Zygarde

Large Size

XXL identifies a particularly large individual. It does not change Zygarde's species, IVs or Dragon/Ground typing.

What XXL Does Not Mean

Not a New Forme

XXL should not be confused with 50% Forme or Complete Forme. Forme and size are separate Pokémon attributes.

Collection tip:

If you are building a complete Zygarde collection, track XXL and XXS separately from Forme, IV and Shiny status.

15 • XXS

XXS Zygarde

XXS identifies a particularly small individual Zygarde. Like XXL, it is a size category rather than a separate Pokémon Forme.

XXS Zygarde

Small Size

An XXS Zygarde is an unusually small individual. Size status does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger or weaker in battle.

XXS vs 10% Forme

Different Attributes

XXS refers to size. 10% refers to Zygarde's Forme. These should be stored and displayed independently in a Pokémon database.

22 • FAQ

Zygarde Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Zygarde?

Zygarde is a Dragon and Ground-type Legendary Pokémon.

What is Zygarde weak to?

Zygarde is especially weak to Ice-type attacks and is also weak to Dragon and Fairy attacks.

How many Zygarde Forms are in Pokémon GO?

Pokémon GO features 10% Forme, 50% Forme and Complete Forme.

How do I get Zygarde 50% Forme?

Collect 50 Zygarde Cells and use them to change Zygarde from 10% Forme to 50% Forme.

How do I get Complete Forme Zygarde?

After reaching 50% Forme, collect another 200 Zygarde Cells. That makes 250 Cells required in total from the original 10% Forme.

Is 100% Zygarde the same as Complete Forme?

No. "100% Zygarde" can mean a 15/15/15 perfect-IV Zygarde, while Complete Forme refers to Zygarde's Forme. They are separate concepts.

Is Shiny Zygarde available?

No. Shiny Zygarde is currently not released in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Zygarde available?

Shadow Zygarde is not currently listed as an obtainable Shadow Pokémon. Future availability should be checked against official game updates.

Can Zygarde be purified?

A Purified Zygarde would require an eligible Shadow Zygarde first. Since Shadow Zygarde is not currently obtainable, Purified Zygarde is not currently obtainable either.

Can Zygarde be Lucky?

Zygarde is currently not eligible for trading, so it cannot currently become Lucky through a Lucky Trade.

Is XXL Zygarde a different Forme?

No. XXL is a size classification. It is separate from Zygarde's 10%, 50% and Complete Formes.

Is XXS Zygarde a different Forme?

No. XXS describes size, not Zygarde's Forme.

Is Zygarde good in Master League?

Zygarde can be a strong Master League option, particularly in its higher Formes where its durability becomes a major part of its value.

What is Zygarde's best PvP moveset?

Dragon Tail with Crunch and Earthquake is a commonly recommended starting point for Zygarde PvP builds. Rankings can change as the GO Battle League meta and move balance change.

What is Zygarde's maximum CP?

At Level 50 with perfect IVs, Zygarde's current maximum CP is approximately 2,685 for 10% Forme, 3,855 for 50% Forme and 4,208 for Complete Forme.

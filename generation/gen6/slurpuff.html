Slurpuff Overview

Slurpuff is a Fairy-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6. It evolves from Swirlix and is known for its dessert-inspired appearance and unusually strong sense of smell in the main Pokémon series. In Pokémon GO, Slurpuff is primarily interesting to collectors, Pokédex completionists and players who enjoy building unusual Fairy-type teams.

Why Slurpuff's Typing Matters

Slurpuff's pure Fairy typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. Fairy Pokémon are especially useful against Dragon-type opponents and can also resist several common attack categories. Because Slurpuff has only one type, there are no dual-type interactions to consider when evaluating its basic weaknesses and resistances.

What Is a 100% Slurpuff?

A 100% Slurpuff has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a perfect-IV or hundo Slurpuff. A perfect IV specimen is especially appealing to collectors, although a 100% Pokémon is not automatically the strongest choice for every PvP format.

Slurpuff FAQ

What type is Slurpuff?

Slurpuff is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Slurpuff weak to?

Slurpuff is weak to Poison and Steel-type attacks.

Is Slurpuff a good raid attacker?

Slurpuff is generally not a priority Fairy-type raid attacker compared with stronger specialized options.

Is Shiny Slurpuff available?

Shiny Slurpuff can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events. Current availability should always be checked against the live game rotation.

What is a 100% Slurpuff?

A 100% Slurpuff has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Slurpuff?

A Lucky Slurpuff is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic and receives a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is Shadow Slurpuff?

Shadow Slurpuff is the Shadow variant of Slurpuff. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from regular Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Slurpuff?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision, especially if you care about battle performance.

What is XXL Slurpuff?

XXL Slurpuff is an unusually large individual. XXL status is a size characteristic and is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Slurpuff?

XXS Slurpuff is an unusually small individual. Like XXL, XXS status is separate from IVs and Shiny status.

Is a 100% Slurpuff rare?

Perfect-IV Pokémon are desirable because all three appraisal IVs are 15. Their availability depends on encounter sources and the applicable IV distribution.

Does Slurpuff evolve?

Yes. Slurpuff evolves from Swirlix and represents the final stage of that evolution family.

Does Slurpuff have a Mega Evolution?

Slurpuff does not have a Mega Evolution. It also does not have a Primal Reversion form.

Can Slurpuff be Shiny, Lucky and XXL at the same time?

Different Pokémon characteristics can overlap when the game allows those statuses to coexist. A Pokémon's Shiny, Lucky, size and IV characteristics should be treated as separate attributes.

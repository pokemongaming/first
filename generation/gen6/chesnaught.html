Chesnaught FAQ

What type is Chesnaught?

Chesnaught is a Grass/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Chesnaught weak to?

Chesnaught is especially weak to Flying and is also weak to Fairy, Fire, Ice, Poison and Psychic.

What is Chesnaught's max CP?

Regular Chesnaught has a Level 50 maximum CP of 3,340 with perfect IVs.

Is Chesnaught good for raids?

Chesnaught can be useful as a Grass attacker, particularly with an appropriate Grass moveset, although its ranking depends on the current Pokémon GO PvE meta.

Is Shiny Chesnaught available?

Yes. Shiny Chesnaught is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Chesnaught available?

Yes. Shadow Chesnaught is available in Pokémon GO.

Should I purify Shadow Chesnaught?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your goals before spending resources on purification.

Is Lucky Chesnaught worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Chesnaught can be attractive because Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Chesnaught?

A 100% Chesnaught has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is XXL Chesnaught?

XXL Chesnaught is an exceptionally large individual of the species. XXL is a size classification rather than a separate species.

What is XXS Chesnaught?

XXS Chesnaught is an exceptionally small individual. Size classification is separate from IV appraisal.

Is Mega Chesnaught released?

Yes. Mega Chesnaught is a released Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO.

What is Mega Chesnaught's max CP?

Mega Chesnaught can reach 4,628 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Mega Chesnaught's best moveset?

The current recommended Grass-oriented Mega build is Vine Whip with Seed Bomb+, although moves and rankings can change with game updates.

Can Shiny Chesnaught Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Chesnaught can be used for Mega Evolution and retains its Shiny identity.

Can a Lucky Chesnaught Mega Evolve?

Mega Evolution is performed on an eligible Chesnaught; Lucky status is an attribute of the Pokémon and does not create a separate species.

Does Mega Evolution permanently change Chesnaught?

No. Mega Evolution is temporary and the underlying Pokémon remains Chesnaught.

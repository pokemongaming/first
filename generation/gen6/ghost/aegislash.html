Aegislash Overview

Aegislash is Pokédex #681, a Steel/Ghost Pokémon from the Kalos region. Pokémon GO features Shield Forme and Blade Forme, with dramatically different stat profiles. Shield Forme has 97 Attack and 272 Defense, while Blade Forme flips that balance with 291 Attack and 97 Defense.

Why the Typing Matters

Steel/Ghost gives Aegislash an unusually large resistance profile. It takes reduced damage from many common attacking types and is especially resistant to Normal and Poison. Its four weaknesses are Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground.

09 • PVP

Aegislash PvP Guide

Aegislash is primarily a PvP-focused Pokémon. Shield Forme is the bulkier profile and has been listed as a strong Great League specialist, while Blade Forme trades durability for much higher Attack.

Great League

Shield Forme is the natural focus for Great League because its 1,746 Level 50 CP ceiling allows it to fit under the 1,500 CP limit. Psycho Cut + Shadow Ball is a strong starting moveset, with Gyro Ball as an alternative charged move.

Ultra League

Shield Forme can be tested in Ultra League, but its CP ceiling and current ranking make it less compelling than in Great League.

Blade Forme

Blade Forme has much higher Attack and a lower Defense profile. It should be evaluated separately rather than assuming Shield Forme performance carries over.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Aegislash in Battle

Use Shield Forme for Bulk

Shield Forme's 272 Defense is the defining feature of Aegislash. Its high resistance count can create favorable switch and shield-management opportunities.

Use Blade Forme Carefully

Blade Forme dramatically increases offensive pressure but drops to 97 Defense. Treat it as an offensive mode rather than expecting Shield Forme durability.

Watch the Four Weaknesses

Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground attacks are the main threats. Successful Aegislash play depends heavily on anticipating these moves and managing shields.

Doublade Overview

Doublade is Pokédex #680 and a dual Steel/Ghost-type Pokémon. Its 135 Attack, 139 Defense and 128 HP give it a balanced but modest Pokémon GO profile, while its 1,571 Level 50 maximum CP keeps it outside the normal Master League range. Its unusual typing gives it several valuable resistances but also leaves it vulnerable to Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground attacks.

Why Doublade's Typing Matters

Steel/Ghost is one of Doublade's defining features. It provides strong defensive coverage against Normal and Poison attacks and useful resistances to many other common types. The trade-off is a four-way weakness to Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground attacks.

09 • PVP

Doublade PvP Guide

Doublade's maximum CP makes Great League the most natural standard league to consider. However, its current ranking and practical performance should be checked against the live moveset and cup rules before investing.

Great League

Doublade can be considered for Great League experimentation because its CP remains below the 1,500 CP threshold at Level 50. Its Steel/Ghost typing can create interesting defensive matchups, but its current battle performance is not that of a premier open-meta pick.

Ultra League

Doublade cannot naturally reach the normal 2,500 CP ceiling, so it is not a conventional Ultra League investment.

Master League

Doublade is not suitable for Master League because its maximum CP is far below the league's normal competitive range.

PvP Investment Advice

Before spending Stardust, check the current cup format, rankings and exact IV spread. Doublade is better treated as a specialist or collection project than an automatic PvP build.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Doublade in Battle

Use Its Typing, Not Its CP

Doublade's best practical feature is its Steel/Ghost typing. Look for situations where its resistances matter rather than trying to force it into a general-purpose attacker role.

Against Weaknesses

Be especially careful around Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground moves. These matchups can remove Doublade quickly despite its useful defensive typing.

For Collection Battles

Doublade is a good candidate for players who enjoy unusual Pokémon, evolution families and specialist PvP cups. It is not a mandatory investment for the average player.

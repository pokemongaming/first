01 • OVERVIEW

Skiddo Pokémon GO Overview

Skiddo is a pure Grass-type Pokémon from Generation 6 and the pre-evolution of Gogoat. It has a modest maximum CP compared with many evolved Pokémon, so its biggest appeal is generally collection, evolution, IV hunting, Shiny hunting and size variants rather than high-end raid performance.

What Does Skiddo's Grass Typing Mean?

Skiddo's pure Grass typing gives it useful resistances against Water, Electric, Grass and Ground attacks. However, it is vulnerable to several common offensive types including Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug.

13 • XXS

XXS Skiddo

XXS Skiddo is the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size system. It represents an unusually small Skiddo and can be valuable to players collecting size variants.

XXS
XXS Skiddo Approximately 0.44 m to 0.45 m according to current size reference data.

Why Keep an XXS Skiddo?

XXS Pokémon are useful for collectors who want a complete size collection. A Shiny XXS or Hundo XXS combination can be considerably more interesting from a collection perspective than a normal-size Skiddo.

21 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Skiddo

For Collectors

Skiddo is particularly fun for collectors because one species can be evaluated across multiple dimensions: Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, XXL, XXS, high level, low level and evolution status.

For Evolution Hunters

If your goal is a complete Skiddo family, prioritize Candy collection and consider keeping strong IV and Shiny specimens before evolving them into Gogoat.

For Raid Players

Skiddo is not a priority high-end raid attacker. Resources are generally better spent on stronger Grass-type attackers if your primary goal is raid performance.

For PvP Players

Check the current PvP rules and moves before committing resources. Skiddo's relatively low CP ceiling limits its overall relevance compared with many specialized PvP Pokémon.

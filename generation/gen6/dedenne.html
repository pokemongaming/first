Dedenne Overview

Dedenne is a dual Electric and Fairy-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kalos region. Unlike many small Electric-type Pokémon, Dedenne has the additional Fairy typing, giving it a distinctive weakness and resistance profile.

Why Dedenne's Typing Matters

The Electric and Fairy combination gives Dedenne a very different battle identity from a standard Electric Pokémon. Electric typing provides useful offensive coverage, while Fairy typing adds additional defensive resistances and matchups.

Dedenne PvP Guide

Great League

Dedenne's low maximum CP makes Great League its most natural PvP environment. Its Electric and Fairy typing can create useful matchups against certain Pokémon.

Ultra League

Dedenne's maximum CP limits its ability to compete at higher CP levels, making it generally less naturally suited to Ultra League compared with Pokémon capable of reaching closer to the league's CP limit.

Master League

Dedenne's comparatively low maximum CP makes it a niche choice rather than a standard Master League investment.

🌑 Shadow Dedenne

Shadow Dedenne is the Shadow version of Dedenne. Shadow status changes its battle characteristics and resource requirements compared with a regular Dedenne.

🌑⚡

Shadow Dedenne

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. The usefulness of Shadow Dedenne depends on its available moveset and the battle format being considered.

Should You Purify Shadow Dedenne?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Pokémon simply because purification improves IVs. Compare the Shadow battle profile, resource cost and your collection goals before making a permanent decision.

✨ Purified Dedenne

Purified Dedenne is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Dedenne. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource costs.

💎✨

🍀 Lucky Dedenne

Lucky Dedenne is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky status is particularly useful for players planning to power up a Pokémon because Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up requirements.

🍀✨

Lucky Dedenne

Lucky status does not change Dedenne's typing or species moves. Its main advantage is reduced Stardust investment for powering up the Pokémon.

  • Useful for collectors building a complete Dedenne collection.
  • Requires less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Retains Electric and Fairy typing.
  • Can also have high IVs.

💯 100% IV Dedenne

A 100% IV Dedenne has the IV combination 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

📏 XXL Dedenne & XXS Dedenne

XXL and XXS Pokémon are size variants. These size categories are primarily important for collection, showcases and size-related gameplay features.

📏⬆️

XXL Dedenne

XXL Dedenne is a larger-than-standard size specimen. It remains the same Pokémon species with the same basic typing and gameplay identity.

📏⬇️

XXS Dedenne

XXS Dedenne is a smaller-than-standard size specimen. It is primarily a collector variation.

Do XXL and XXS Change Battle Stats?

Size categories should not automatically be treated as different battle forms. Their main significance is generally connected to collecting and size-based features rather than creating a completely separate Pokémon species.

Dedenne FAQ

What type is Dedenne?

Dedenne is an Electric and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Dedenne weak to?

Dedenne is weak to Ground and Poison-type attacks.

Does Dedenne evolve?

Dedenne does not have a standard evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Dedenne different in battle?

Shiny status primarily changes appearance. A Shiny Dedenne uses the same species mechanics as a regular Dedenne.

What is a 100% IV Dedenne?

A 100% IV Dedenne has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Dedenne useful?

Lucky Dedenne can be useful because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when being powered up.

Does XXL Dedenne have different stats?

XXL status is primarily a size variation and should not automatically be treated as a separate battle form.

Does XXS Dedenne evolve differently?

No. XXS is a size variation rather than a separate evolution.

Should I purify Shadow Dedenne?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of the Shadow battle profile with your collection and investment goals before purifying.

Is Dedenne good for PvP?

Dedenne's most natural PvP environment is generally lower CP formats where its Electric and Fairy typing can provide useful matchup coverage.

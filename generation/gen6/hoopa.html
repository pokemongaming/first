Hoopa

Hoopa is a Mythical Pokémon from the Kalos region with two major forms in Pokémon GO: Hoopa Confined and Hoopa Unbound. Confined Hoopa is Psychic/Ghost, while Unbound Hoopa becomes Psychic/Dark and has significantly higher Attack and Max CP.

Hoopa Overview

Hoopa is a Mythical Pokémon introduced in Generation VI. In Pokémon GO, its primary forms are Hoopa Confined and Hoopa Unbound. The two forms have different typings, stats, movesets and maximum CP.

Form Change

Hoopa's form can be changed between Confined and Unbound. The current Pokémon GO mechanic requires Hoopa Candy and Stardust for the transformation.

CONFined → UNBOUND 50 Candy + 10,000 Stardust
UNBOUND → CONFINED 10 Candy + 2,000 Stardust

Hoopa IV Guide

10 — PVP

Hoopa PvP Guide

Hoopa's extremely high CP and unusual typing make it more interesting in Master League than in Great League or Ultra League, although its actual ranking depends on the current moveset and metagame.

Great League

Hoopa's high base stats make it poorly suited to standard Great League CP restrictions.

Ultra League

The same CP limitations make standard Hoopa difficult to fit naturally into Ultra League teams.

Master League

Master League is the most natural traditional PvP format for a high-CP Hoopa. Its unusual typing and high Attack can create interesting matchup opportunities, but it also has exploitable weaknesses.

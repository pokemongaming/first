Greninja PvP Guide

Greninja's fast energy generation and charged move pressure can make it an interesting PvP Pokémon. Its relatively low defensive bulk, however, means shields and matchup management are important.

Great League

Greninja can function as an aggressive Water-type option when its moveset and team composition support its offensive style.

Ultra League

Greninja can also have Ultra League relevance, although its performance depends heavily on the current meta and available Pokémon.

Master League

Greninja's maximum CP is lower than many Legendary Pokémon commonly used in Master League, making it a more specialized choice.

Lucky Greninja

Lucky Greninja is obtained when an eligible Froakie, Frogadier or Greninja becomes Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for long-term investments because they require less Stardust for power-ups.

Lucky Investment Benefit

A Lucky Greninja can be an excellent choice for players planning to build a high-level Water-type attacker while saving Stardust.

Greninja FAQ

What type is Greninja?

Greninja is a Water and Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Greninja weak to?

Greninja is weak to Electric, Grass, Bug, Fairy and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Greninja good for raids?

Greninja can be a strong Water-type raid attacker when Water damage is useful and it has an appropriate moveset.

Is Shiny Greninja different in battle?

No. Shiny status changes appearance but does not provide different battle stats.

What is a 100% Greninja?

A 100% Greninja has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Shadow Greninja stronger?

Shadow Greninja has a different offensive and defensive battle profile. It may be attractive for damage-focused players but requires more investment.

Should I purify Shadow Greninja?

Not automatically. Consider your goals before removing the Shadow status.

Is Lucky Greninja good?

Lucky Greninja can be particularly useful for players who want to save Stardust while powering it up.

What is Mega Greninja?

Mega Greninja is Greninja's enhanced Mega Evolution form.

Does XXL Greninja have higher stats?

XXL and XXS size classifications do not normally change Greninja's standard battle stats.

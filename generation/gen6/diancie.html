Diancie Overview

Diancie is a Mythical Pokémon introduced in Generation 6. It is associated with the Kalos region and has the unusual Rock / Fairy typing. Unlike normal evolution families, Diancie does not evolve through Candy. Its major special form is Mega Diancie.

Diancie FAQ

Is Diancie a Mythical Pokémon?

Yes. Diancie is classified as a Mythical Pokémon.

What type is Diancie?

Diancie is a Rock and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Diancie weak to?

Important weaknesses include Water, Grass, Ground and Steel attacks.

Does Diancie evolve?

Diancie does not evolve through a normal evolution chain. It has a Mega Evolution.

Can Diancie Mega Evolve?

Yes. Diancie can access Mega Diancie through Pokémon GO's Mega Evolution mechanics.

What is a 100% IV Diancie?

A perfect Diancie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Diancie have stronger stats?

Size categories should not automatically be treated as a combat-stat advantage.

Is Shadow Diancie available?

Check current Pokémon GO live data before claiming that Shadow Diancie is obtainable.

Can Diancie be Lucky?

Lucky status depends on Pokémon GO's current trade eligibility and game rules.

Is Mega Diancie good for raids?

Mega Diancie can be a highly valuable option when its Rock or Fairy typing is effective against the raid boss.

Gourgeist

Gourgeist is a Ghost and Grass-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. It is known for its pumpkin-inspired design, multiple size forms and unusual dual typing. In Pokémon GO, Gourgeist can be collected in different variants and may have value for collectors, PvP players and players building special Ghost or Grass-type teams.

Gourgeist Overview

Gourgeist is the evolved form of Pumpkaboo. Its Ghost and Grass typing gives it a distinctive battle profile with different offensive and defensive matchups compared with pure Ghost or pure Grass Pokémon.

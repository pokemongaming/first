Vivillon Overview

Vivillon is the final evolution of the Scatterbug family. It evolves from Spewpa and has no evolution after Vivillon. Its most distinctive feature in Pokémon GO is its collection of regional wing patterns.

Why Vivillon's Typing Matters

Vivillon's Bug/Flying combination gives it useful resistances to Ground, Fighting, Grass and Bug damage. However, the combination also creates a particularly severe weakness to Rock-type attacks.

Should You Power Up Vivillon?

For most players, Vivillon does not need to be a high-priority Stardust investment for raids. Building one can make sense for collection purposes, PvP experimentation or personal attachment to a particular pattern.

Vivillon PvP Guide

Vivillon can be used in Trainer Battles, but its relatively low Defense and overall CP profile make it a specialized choice rather than a universally strong PvP Pokémon.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to experiment with Vivillon because the 1,500 CP limit keeps its maximum CP from immediately excluding it.

Ultra League

Vivillon can technically be used in Ultra League, although its overall performance is more limited compared with many established Ultra League options.

Master League

Vivillon's maximum CP is too low to make it a natural Master League investment. Master League generally favours Pokémon capable of reaching substantially higher CP values.

PvP Tip

If you enjoy unusual Pokémon in PvP, evaluate Vivillon as a specialist rather than assuming that its many patterns make it a competitive powerhouse.

How to Collect Vivillon Patterns

The Vivillon Collector system is linked to Postcards. Pinning Postcards from different regions progresses the corresponding Vivillon Collector medal. Completing the required progress can lead to a Scatterbug encounter, which evolves into Vivillon with the pattern associated with that region.

Collector Tip

If you are trying to complete all Vivillon patterns, organize your Postcard collection by region instead of simply transferring every Scatterbug immediately.

Should You Keep a Lucky Vivillon?

For collectors, a Lucky Vivillon can be a fun addition, particularly when paired with a rare regional pattern. Because Vivillon is not generally a top raid attacker, the main value of a Lucky Vivillon is often collection and reduced investment cost.

XXL Vivillon

XXL Vivillon is a size classification rather than a separate species or form. Vivillon can be encountered with different size measurements.

XXL Vivillon Very large size classification

A large Vivillon can receive the XXL size classification when its measured height falls within the appropriate Pokémon GO size range.

Does XXL Change Vivillon's Base Stats?

No. Size classification should not be confused with Attack, Defense or HP IVs. XXL status is primarily interesting for collection, showcases and size-related features.

Database recommendation:

Store XXL as a separate size/status attribute instead of creating a completely separate Pokémon entry.

XXS Vivillon

XXS Vivillon is the opposite end of the size spectrum. It represents a particularly small Vivillon rather than a different Pokémon species.

XXS Vivillon Very small size classification

XXS status is determined by the Pokémon's measured size and is separate from its IV combination.

XXS vs 100% IV

An XXS Vivillon can also theoretically have perfect IVs. Size and IVs represent different attributes, so an XXS Pokémon is not automatically weaker or stronger because of its size classification.

Vivillon IV Guide

IVs determine additional Attack, Defense and HP points. For a collector, a 15/15/15 Vivillon is the perfect IV combination.

Vivillon FAQ

What type is Vivillon?

Vivillon is a Bug and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Vivillon weak to?

Vivillon is especially weak to Rock-type attacks. It is also weak to Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice.

What is Vivillon's max CP?

Vivillon's maximum CP is 2,097 at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Vivillon available?

Shiny Vivillon is available in Pokémon GO. Availability of individual pattern variants should be checked separately.

Does Vivillon have different patterns?

Yes. Pokémon GO features multiple regional Vivillon patterns associated with the Postcard and Scatterbug collection system.

How do I get different Vivillon patterns?

Collect and pin Postcards from different regions to progress the corresponding Vivillon Collector medal and encounter Scatterbug associated with that region.

Is Shadow Vivillon available?

Shadow Vivillon is not currently available as a normal Shadow Pokémon form.

Is Purified Vivillon available?

Because Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon, there is currently no normal Purified Vivillon path while Shadow Vivillon is unavailable.

What is Lucky Vivillon?

Lucky Vivillon is a Vivillon that has received the Lucky status through trading. Lucky is a status, not a separate species.

What is a 100% IV Vivillon?

A 100% IV Vivillon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Vivillon?

XXL Vivillon is a large size classification. It does not represent a separate Pokémon species.

What is XXS Vivillon?

XXS Vivillon is a very small size classification and is separate from IV quality.

Is Vivillon good for raids?

Vivillon is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its strongest appeal is its collection and regional pattern mechanics.

Is Vivillon good in PvP?

Vivillon can be used in PvP, especially as a specialized Great League option, but it is not generally considered a dominant all-purpose PvP Pokémon.

Does Vivillon evolve?

Vivillon is the final evolution of the Scatterbug family. Scatterbug evolves into Spewpa, and Spewpa evolves into Vivillon.

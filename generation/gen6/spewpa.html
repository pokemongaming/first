What Does Spewpa's Bug Typing Mean?

Spewpa's pure Bug typing determines both its offensive STAB opportunities and its defensive matchups. It takes increased damage from Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

Perfect IV Spewpa

A 15/15/15 Spewpa is commonly called a "hundo." Perfect IVs are especially attractive to collectors and players building a complete Pokédex collection.

Shiny Spewpa

Shiny Spewpa is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny versions of Scatterbug, Spewpa and Vivillon made their Pokémon GO debut during the Scattered to the Winds event in February 2025.

✨ AVAILABLE

Shiny Spewpa

A Shiny Spewpa has the alternate coloration associated with its Shiny form. Shiny status does not change the Pokémon's base typing.

Shiny Spewpa Collection Tips

  • Keep strong-IV Shiny Spewpa if you are a collector.
  • A Shiny Spewpa can be evolved into Shiny Vivillon.
  • Consider keeping different Vivillon patterns.
  • Avoid transferring a rare Shiny specimen accidentally.

Shadow Spewpa

Shadow Pokémon are associated with Team GO Rocket encounters. Shadow availability is species-specific, so a database page should not automatically label every Pokémon as an obtainable Shadow Pokémon.

VERIFY CURRENT AVAILABILITY

Shadow Spewpa Status

Do not present Shadow Spewpa as currently obtainable unless your live Pokémon GO data confirms an active Shadow release.

If Shadow Spewpa Becomes Available

Shadow Pokémon receive increased damage output but also take increased damage. They also require additional resources for powering up and can initially know Frustration.

Important:

Shadow status should be driven by live game data. Do not create a permanent "Shadow available" claim unless Niantic has released the species through the appropriate Team GO Rocket mechanics.

Purified Spewpa

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification improves each IV by 2 points, up to 15, and replaces Frustration with Return.

DEPENDS ON SHADOW RELEASE

Purified Spewpa

A Purified Spewpa would require an eligible Shadow Spewpa to exist first. Therefore, your database should connect this section to the current Shadow availability system rather than assuming it is always obtainable.

Lucky Spewpa

Lucky Pokémon are created through trading. Spewpa is listed as tradeable, making a Lucky Spewpa a possible trade outcome.

★ TRADE-BASED VARIANT

Lucky Spewpa

A traded Spewpa can become Lucky as part of the Pokémon GO trade mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

Why Keep a Lucky Spewpa?

  • It is a useful collectible variant.
  • Lucky Pokémon are cheaper to power up with Stardust.
  • It can be evolved into Vivillon if desired.
  • Lucky status is permanent after the trade.
Trade reminder:

Pokémon GO rerolls IVs during a trade, so a Pokémon's IVs before trading do not guarantee the same IVs afterward.

100% Spewpa

A 100% Spewpa has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a hundo.

Perfect Spewpa IVs

Perfect IV Spewpa is primarily valuable for collection, evolution and Pokédex completion. It is not normally a Pokémon that requires expensive combat investment.

How to Find 100% Spewpa

  • Check appraisal after every worthwhile encounter.
  • Use Pokémon GO search filters to narrow down high-IV Pokémon.
  • Keep a dedicated perfect-IV tag.
  • Consider evolving a hundo if you want a perfect-IV Vivillon.

XXL Spewpa

XXL is a size classification rather than an IV rating. An XXL Spewpa can therefore have any combination of IVs.

XXL Spewpa

XXL Spewpa represents an unusually large individual within the Pokémon GO size system.

Does XXL Mean Better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Spewpa can be a 0%, 50%, 90% or 100% IV Pokémon.

Why Keep an XXL Spewpa?

  • XXL Pokémon are useful for size-based collection goals.
  • Keep rare XXL Shinies if you are a collector.
  • An XXL Pokémon can also have excellent IVs.
  • Size does not automatically increase combat stats.

XXS Spewpa

XXS is the opposite size classification to XXL. It represents an unusually small individual.

XXS Spewpa

An XXS Spewpa is a collectible size variant that can be kept independently of its IV quality.

XXS Spewpa and IVs

Size does not determine IVs. Therefore, an XXS Spewpa can also be a perfect 15/15/15 Pokémon.

Should You Keep XXS Spewpa?

If you collect unusual sizes, a first XXS Spewpa is worth keeping. A particularly desirable collector combination would be an XXS Shiny, XXS hundo or XXS Lucky specimen.

Froakie Overview

Froakie is the first stage of the Froakie evolution family. It evolves into Frogadier and then Greninja. While Froakie itself is mainly collected and evolved rather than powered up for competitive battles, its final evolution makes this family particularly valuable.

Why Froakie's Water Typing Matters

As a pure Water-type Pokémon, Froakie is weak to Electric and Grass attacks while resisting Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks. These matchups remain important throughout the early stages of the Froakie evolution family.

Froakie Forms & Special Variants

Froakie can be collected in several special variants. These variants may differ in appearance, background, collection value or game mechanics.

✨ Shiny Froakie

Shiny Froakie has alternate coloration and is primarily a collector variant. A Shiny Froakie can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

🌑 Shadow Froakie

Shadow Froakie has Shadow Pokémon battle characteristics. Players should consider its long-term evolution potential before deciding whether to purify it.

✨ Purified Froakie

Purified Froakie is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Froakie. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its future investment path.

🍀 Lucky Froakie

Lucky Froakie is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust for power-ups, making future evolutions cheaper to invest in.

💯 100% IV Froakie

A 100% Froakie has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. It is especially valuable as a long-term evolution candidate and collection target.

📏 XXL Froakie

XXL Froakie is a larger size variant and can be valuable for collection and PokéStop Showcase participation.

🔹 XXS Froakie

XXS Froakie is an unusually small size variant. Its primary value is collection rarity and size-based collecting.

💧 Regular Froakie

The standard Froakie form is the base version used for normal collection and evolution.

Should You Purify Shadow Froakie?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Froakie simply because purification may improve its IVs. Consider whether you want to preserve its Shadow status for future evolution and battle use.

  • Check the Froakie's IVs before making a decision.
  • Consider the potential value of future evolutions.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified investment paths.
  • Do not purify only because the displayed IVs are imperfect.

Purified Froakie

Purified Froakie is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Froakie. It becomes a normal non-Shadow Pokémon with Purified status.

Purified Investment Idea

A Purified Froakie may be a useful evolution candidate, particularly if it has IVs you want to keep for your future Frogadier or Greninja.

Lucky Froakie

Lucky Froakie can be valuable for players planning a long-term evolution project because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

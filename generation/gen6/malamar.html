Malamar Overview

Malamar is the evolved form of Inkay and is classified as a Dark / Psychic-type Pokémon. It is known for its unusual upside-down evolution concept and its distinctive squid-like appearance.

Why Malamar's Typing Matters

Malamar's unusual Dark / Psychic combination gives it a very different defensive profile from many Psychic-type Pokémon. The Dark typing removes the usual Psychic-type weakness to Ghost and Dark attacks while creating important weaknesses of its own.

Why Volcanion's Typing Matters

Fire / Water is a rare defensive combination. Volcanion takes reduced damage from several common attack types, including Fire, Ice and Steel, while Electric, Ground and Rock attacks are its main weaknesses.

13 • SIZE VARIANT

XXS Volcanion

XXS is a size category rather than a separate Volcanion species or permanent form. Your database can therefore track an XXS Volcanion as a size variant.

🔹

XXS Volcanion

SIZE VARIANT

An XXS Volcanion represents a particularly small specimen. Its size classification should be stored separately from IVs, CP and the Pokémon's base species.

XXS vs IVs

XXS size and IV percentage are different properties. A 100% IV Volcanion does not automatically have to be XXS, and an XXS Volcanion does not automatically have perfect IVs.

14 • SIZE VARIANT

XXL Volcanion

XXL is the large-size classification. Like XXS, it should be treated as a size attribute rather than a different Pokémon form.

🔶

XXL Volcanion

SIZE VARIANT

An XXL Volcanion represents a particularly large specimen. It can be tracked separately for collection purposes without changing the Pokémon's species, typing or IV statistics.

XXL vs 100% IV

Size and IVs are independent. An XXL Volcanion can have ordinary IVs, while a perfect 15/15/15 Volcanion can have another size classification.

Espurr Overview

Espurr is a Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6. It evolves into Meowstic after collecting the required Candy. Espurr itself is not normally used as a top-tier raid attacker, but it remains useful for Pokédex completion, evolution collecting, Shiny hunting and special variant collecting.

Why Espurr's Typing Matters

Espurr's pure Psychic typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. Psychic-type Pokémon take super-effective damage from Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks while resisting Fighting and Psychic attacks.

What Is a 100% Espurr?

A 100% Espurr has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Players commonly call this a Hundo or perfect Espurr. A perfect specimen can be especially attractive to collectors and players planning to evolve it.

100% Espurr — Hundo Espurr

A Hundo Espurr is one of the most desirable standard IV combinations for collectors.

💯

Perfect IV

Attack 15 / Defense 15 / HP 15.

⭐

Collector Value

A perfect Espurr can be saved for evolution or collection purposes.

Should You Keep a Hundo Espurr?

  • Keep it if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon.
  • Consider evolving it into Meowstic.
  • Compare its future battle usefulness before spending Stardust.
  • Mark it as a favorite to prevent accidental transfer.

Shadow Espurr & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can affect when Frustration can be replaced. Always check current event rules before using resources on a Shadow Pokémon.

Do not purify automatically.

If you are interested in the Shadow Pokémon mechanic or collecting Shadow species, compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making your decision.

Purified Espurr

Purified Espurr is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Espurr. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the standard benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

💎

Lucky Espurr

Why Keep a Lucky Espurr?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky Espurr can be useful for collectors.
  • Check IVs after a trade because traded Pokémon receive new IVs.
  • Consider evolving a Lucky Espurr if you want a Lucky Meowstic.

XXL Espurr & XXS Espurr

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon according to their size. XXL and XXS are special size categories, not separate species or forms.

📏 XXL Espurr

An XXL Espurr is an unusually large specimen. Size can be especially interesting for collectors and medal-related goals.

🔹 XXS Espurr

An XXS Espurr is an unusually small specimen. It can be saved as a size-collection entry.

Should You Keep XXL or XXS Espurr?

If you collect size records, XXL and XXS Espurr can be worth keeping even when their IVs are not perfect. For battle-focused players, size is generally less important than IVs, moves and overall battle role.

Espurr FAQ

What type is Espurr?

Espurr is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

Espurr is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

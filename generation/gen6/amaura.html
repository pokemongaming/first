Shadow Amaura

Shadow Amaura should be treated separately from regular Amaura because Shadow Pokémon use different battle mechanics and investment considerations.

🌑 Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade greater offensive pressure for reduced durability. The exact value of Shadow Amaura depends on its current availability, evolution potential and battle role.

Shadow Amaura Considerations

  • Check the current availability of Shadow Amaura.
  • Evaluate IVs without assuming only 100% IVs are useful.
  • Consider the additional cost of powering up Shadow Pokémon.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare the Shadow evolution with regular Aurorus.

Gogoat FAQ

What type is Gogoat?

Gogoat is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Gogoat weak to?

Gogoat is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

Does Gogoat evolve?

Yes. Gogoat evolves from Skiddo and is the final stage of that evolutionary family.

Is Shiny Gogoat available?

Shiny Gogoat availability depends on the current Pokémon GO encounter and event system.

What is a 100% Gogoat?

A 100% Gogoat has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Gogoat?

Lucky Gogoat is a Gogoat that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

Does XXL Gogoat have better IVs?

No. XXL size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Gogoat can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Gogoat have worse IVs?

No. XXS size does not determine IV quality.

Is Gogoat good for raids?

Gogoat is generally more interesting for collection and PvP purposes than as a premier Grass-type raid attacker.

Is Gogoat good in PvP?

Gogoat can have PvP potential depending on its moveset, IVs and the current Pokémon GO meta.

Can Gogoat be Shiny and Lucky?

A Pokémon can have multiple special attributes. For example, a Shiny Pokémon can also become Lucky through an eligible trade.

Can Gogoat be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are independent attributes, so an XXL Gogoat can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Gogoat be XXS and Shiny?

Yes, when the relevant size and Shiny characteristics are available through Pokémon GO's current mechanics.

Is Shadow Gogoat available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Team GO Rocket and Pokémon GO data rather than assumed from the main-series games.

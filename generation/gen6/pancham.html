Why Pancham's Typing Matters

Pancham's pure Fighting typing gives it access to Fighting-type attacks and determines its main weaknesses and resistances. Its Fighting typing is especially relevant when comparing Pancham with other Fighting-type Pokémon and when preparing it for evolution into Pangoro.

A 15/15/15 Pancham is a perfect-IV specimen. Because Pancham evolves into Pangoro, a perfect Pancham can also be a desirable evolution and collection target.

100% IV Tip

Do not judge IV quality from CP alone. Use the Pokémon GO appraisal system or an appropriate IV checker to confirm the exact IV combination.

XXL & XXS Pancham

Pokémon GO can feature size variations such as XXL and XXS. These variants are separate collection targets and can be especially interesting for Pokédex completion.

📏 XXL Pancham

XXL Pancham is an unusually large Pancham. Its size classification is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon is not automatically a high-IV Pokémon.

📐 XXS Pancham

XXS Pancham is an unusually small Pancham. Like XXL Pokémon, size and IVs are independent characteristics.

Should You Keep XXL or XXS Pancham?

If you enjoy collecting unique Pokémon, keeping both XXL and XXS Pancham can be worthwhile. Players interested in showcases may also want to pay attention to size classifications.

Pancham FAQ

What type is Pancham?

Pancham is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Pancham weak to?

Pancham is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic attacks.

What does Pancham evolve into?

Pancham evolves into Pangoro.

Is Shiny Pancham available?

Shiny Pancham is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Pancham available?

Shadow Pancham can be available through Pokémon GO's Shadow-related mechanics and rotations. Current availability should be checked before publication.

Can Shadow Pancham be purified?

An eligible Shadow Pancham can be purified, which removes its Shadow status and changes its associated mechanics.

What is a 100% Pancham?

A 100% Pancham has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Pancham?

XXL Pancham is a Pancham classified as unusually large. Size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Pancham?

XXS Pancham is a Pancham classified as unusually small.

What is Lucky Pancham?

Lucky Pancham is a Lucky Pokémon that generally requires less Stardust for power-ups.

Is Pancham good for raids?

Pancham is generally more valuable as an evolution and collection target than as a top-tier raid attacker in its unevolved form.

Is Pancham good for PvP?

Pancham can have niche PvP applications depending on the format and current moveset, but its evolution Pangoro is generally the more important battle form to evaluate.

Should I evolve a 100% Pancham?

A 100% Pancham is a strong collection and evolution candidate. Before evolving, consider whether you want to keep the Pancham itself for your collection or build Pangoro.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Pancham?

Collectors may want to keep both size variants, especially if they participate in Pokémon GO Showcases or want a complete collection.

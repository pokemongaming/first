01 • OVERVIEW

Avalugg Overview

Avalugg is an Ice-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. It evolves from Bergmite after spending 50 Bergmite Candy. In Pokémon GO, Avalugg has a particularly high Defense stat and can function as a bulky Ice-type Pokémon. Its strongest listed Ice-focused moveset is Ice Fang with Avalanche.

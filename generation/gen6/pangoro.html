Why Pangoro's Typing Matters

Pangoro's Fighting / Dark typing gives it access to two useful offensive identities. Fighting attacks are valuable against several common defensive types, while Dark attacks can target Psychic- and Ghost-type opponents.

Pangoro PvP Guide

Pangoro's Fighting / Dark typing gives it a distinctive matchup profile in Pokémon GO PvP.

Ultra League

Pangoro can be considered for Ultra League teams where its typing and charged-move pressure fit the team's strategy. Its Fairy vulnerability must be respected carefully.

Great League

Pangoro may be considered when eligible through CP-limited formats, but its exact usefulness depends on the current moveset and metagame.

Pangoro FAQ

What type is Pangoro?

Pangoro is a Fighting / Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Pangoro weak to?

Pangoro is weak to Fairy, Fighting, Flying and Bug attacks. Fairy is especially dangerous because it deals double-super-effective damage.

Is Pangoro good in Pokémon GO?

Pangoro can be useful in certain PvP and battle situations, but its overall value depends on its moveset, league and the current metagame.

Is Shiny Pangoro available?

Shiny Pangoro can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or evolution from an eligible Shiny Pancham.

What is a 100% Pangoro?

A 100% Pangoro has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Pangoro?

Lucky Pangoro is a Pangoro obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

What is Shadow Pangoro?

Shadow Pangoro is the Shadow version of Pangoro. It retains the Fighting / Dark typing while using the Shadow battle mechanic.

Should I purify Shadow Pangoro?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your PvP, PvE and collection goals before purifying.

What is XXL Pangoro?

XXL Pangoro is an exceptionally large size variant. Its size is separate from its IV statistics.

What is XXS Pangoro?

XXS Pangoro is an exceptionally small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status does not automatically indicate better IVs.

Is a Hundo Pangoro worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Pangoro is a perfect-IV specimen and can be valuable for collection, PvP or battle purposes depending on the current meta.

Does Pangoro evolve?

Yes. Pangoro evolves from Pancham. Check the current Pokémon GO evolution requirements before publishing exact requirements.

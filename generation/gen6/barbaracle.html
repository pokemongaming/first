01 • OVERVIEW

Barbaracle Overview

Barbaracle is the final evolution of Binacle and the #689 Pokémon in the National Pokédex. It was introduced with Generation 6 and belongs to the Rock and Water categories.

Unlike many Pokémon that specialize strongly in one battle role, Barbaracle has access to several move types, allowing it to attack with Rock, Water, Fighting and Grass coverage.

02 • TYPE

Barbaracle Type

Barbaracle has a dual Rock/Water typing. This combination is important because the two types create both useful resistances and a major weakness to Grass attacks.

🌊
Rock / Water Dual Type Pokémon
ROCK WATER

Why Barbaracle's Typing Matters

The Rock typing gives Barbaracle access to useful resistances and Rock-type STAB attacks, while the Water typing adds additional defensive interactions and gives it access to Water-type attacks.

The biggest drawback is its severe vulnerability to Grass. A Grass-type attack can exploit both of Barbaracle's defensive weaknesses simultaneously.

08 • PVP

Barbaracle PvP Guide

Barbaracle's dual typing and access to several charged moves give it interesting coverage. Its effectiveness depends heavily on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Great League

A properly optimized Barbaracle can be considered for Great League experimentation where its typing and charged-move coverage fit the team.

Ultra League

Barbaracle can also be built for Ultra League, although its overall ranking should be checked against the current PvP meta before investing large amounts of Stardust or Candy XL.

Master League

Barbaracle's maximum CP is considerably lower than the stat ceiling of many dedicated Master League Pokémon, making it a less natural Master League choice.

PvP Investment Tip

For capped leagues, do not judge a Barbaracle only by its percentage IV. League-specific stat product and the actual CP at the desired level are more important.

09 • IV GUIDE

Barbaracle IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to the Pokémon's base stats. Each IV ranges from 0 to 15.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is a Perfect Barbaracle?

A Barbaracle with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP has 100% IVs and is commonly called a hundo.

Should You Keep Lower-IV Barbaracle?

Absolutely. IV perfection is not the only reason to keep a Barbaracle. A Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS or PvP-optimized specimen may have value even without perfect IVs.

10 • 100% BARBARACLE

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Barbaracle

Lucky Barbaracle is a trading outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. A Barbaracle can become Lucky when obtained through a Lucky Trade.

✨ LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Barbaracle

Lucky status provides reduced Stardust costs for powering up the Pokémon and comes with a guaranteed minimum IV floor from the trade.

Lucky Barbaracle Collection

  • Lucky Barbaracle can be powered up more cheaply.
  • Lucky status does not change Barbaracle's typing.
  • Lucky Barbaracle can also be Shiny.
  • Lucky Barbaracle can also become a hundo.
  • Lucky status is independent of XXL and XXS size.

Lucky Hundo Barbaracle

A Lucky Barbaracle that receives 15/15/15 IVs after the trade is both Lucky and 100% IV. This is a particularly nice combination for collectors and players who want a lower-cost power-up project.

23 • FAQ

Barbaracle FAQ

What type is Barbaracle?

Barbaracle is a Rock/Water-type Pokémon.

What is Barbaracle weak to?

Barbaracle is weak to Grass, Electric, Fighting and Ground attacks. Grass is especially dangerous because the Rock/Water typing creates a double weakness.

What is Barbaracle's max CP?

A perfect IV Barbaracle reaches approximately 3,025 CP at Level 50. Its Level 40 perfect-IV CP is approximately 2,675.

Is Shiny Barbaracle available?

Yes. Shiny Barbaracle is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Barbaracle available?

Shadow Barbaracle is not currently listed as obtainable. Its status should be updated if Pokémon GO introduces the Shadow form.

Can Barbaracle be purified?

Purification applies to eligible Shadow Pokémon. Because Shadow Barbaracle is not currently available, there is no current normal route to obtain a Purified Barbaracle.

Can Barbaracle be Lucky?

Yes. Barbaracle can become Lucky through trading when the trade results in a Lucky Pokémon.

What is a 100% Barbaracle?

A 100% Barbaracle, commonly called a hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Shiny Barbaracle be 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate attributes, so a Shiny Barbaracle can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Barbaracle be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification and is separate from IV percentage and CP.

Can Barbaracle be XXS?

Yes. XXS is a size classification and is separate from IV percentage and CP.

Can Barbaracle be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so one Barbaracle can have both statuses.

Can a Lucky Barbaracle be a hundo?

Yes. If the post-trade IVs are 15/15/15, the Pokémon is both Lucky and 100% IV.

Is Barbaracle good for raids?

Barbaracle is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its unusual typing and move coverage can be more interesting for specific battles and collection purposes.

Is Barbaracle good in PvP?

Barbaracle can be used in PvP, particularly in capped leagues, but its ranking depends on the current moveset and metagame.

How do you evolve Binacle into Barbaracle?

Binacle evolves into Barbaracle using 50 Binacle Candy.

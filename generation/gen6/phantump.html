XXL Phantump

XXL Phantump is a size classification rather than a different species or evolution. Size can be important for collectors and for trainers who enjoy collecting unusual Pokémon measurements.

📏
XXL Phantump SIZE VARIANT

An XXL Phantump is a particularly large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

🏆
Collector Value

XXL Pokémon can be worth keeping for size collections, medals and personal collecting goals.

XXL vs IVs

XXL size and IV quality are separate characteristics. An XXL Phantump does not automatically have perfect IVs, and a 100% Phantump does not automatically have XXL size.

XXS Phantump

XXS Phantump is the opposite size extreme from XXL. It represents a particularly small specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

📐

Finding both XXL and XXS Phantump can be a fun collection goal alongside Shiny and perfect-IV specimens.

Phantump FAQ

What type is Phantump?

Phantump is a Ghost and Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Phantump weak to?

Phantump is vulnerable to several attack types including Fire, Ice, Flying, Ghost and Dark.

What is Phantump's max CP?

Phantump's maximum CP is approximately 1,668 at level 50 with perfect IVs.

Does Phantump evolve?

Yes. Phantump evolves into Trevenant.

Is Shiny Phantump available?

Shiny Phantump can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny version is enabled.

What is a 100% Phantump?

A 100% Phantump has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Can Phantump be Lucky?

Yes. A Phantump can have Lucky status as a result of eligible Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

Can Phantump be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification and is separate from Phantump's IVs.

Can Phantump be XXS?

Yes. XXS is a size classification representing an unusually small specimen.

Is Shadow Phantump available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket Pokémon rotation. Check the current game data before listing Shadow Phantump as obtainable.

Is Purified Phantump available?

Purified status depends on the availability of Shadow Phantump. It should not be presented as an independent normal encounter form.

Should I keep a 100% Phantump?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Phantump is a perfect-IV specimen and can be kept as a collector piece or evolved into a perfect Trevenant.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Phantump?

If you collect size variants, keeping both XXL and XXS Phantump can be worthwhile. Their size classification is separate from IV quality.

Should I evolve my best Phantump?

If your goal is Trevenant, compare IVs, league requirements, Shiny status, Lucky status and your collection goals before evolving.

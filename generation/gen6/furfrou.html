Furfrou FAQ

What type is Furfrou?

Furfrou is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Furfrou weak to?

Furfrou is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Does Furfrou evolve?

No. Furfrou does not have a normal evolution. It instead has multiple Trims.

How many Furfrou Trims are there?

Pokémon GO has featured multiple Furfrou Trims, including Heart, Star, Diamond, Debutante, Matron, Dandy, La Reine, Kabuki and Pharaoh Trims, alongside its Natural Form. Availability can vary by region and event.

Is Shiny Furfrou available?

Shiny Furfrou is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and events.

Is Furfrou good for raids?

Furfrou is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary appeal is its form and collection value.

What is 100% IV Furfrou?

A 100% IV Furfrou has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Furfrou?

XXL Furfrou is an unusually large Furfrou classified as XXL by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Furfrou?

XXS Furfrou is an unusually small Furfrou classified as XXS by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is Lucky Furfrou?

Lucky Furfrou is obtained through a Lucky Trade and has the benefits associated with the Lucky Pokémon mechanic.

Is Shadow Furfrou available?

Shadow availability should be checked against current Pokémon GO releases. Do not assume that every Pokémon species has an obtainable Shadow form.

Can Furfrou be Shiny and have a Trim?

Shiny status and Trim are separate form attributes. Where the game permits the combination, a Shiny Furfrou can therefore be particularly valuable to collectors.

Are Furfrou Trims different Pokémon?

No. Furfrou Trims represent different forms or appearances of Furfrou rather than separate Pokédex species.

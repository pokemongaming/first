01 • OVERVIEW

Talonflame Overview

Talonflame is the final evolution of Fletchling and Fletchinder. It is a dual Fire/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6.

Unlike many Pokémon that are primarily evaluated for raids, Talonflame's biggest Pokémon GO strength is its Trainer Battle performance. It can be a strong choice in both Great League and Ultra League when built around its high-pressure charged moves.

Talonflame Type

FIRE FLYING

Why Talonflame's Typing Matters

Fire/Flying gives Talonflame useful resistances against several common attack types, but it also creates a major vulnerability to Rock-type attacks.

In Pokémon GO's damage system, Rock attacks deal especially heavy damage to Talonflame, while Electric and Water attacks are also super effective.

08 • IV GUIDE

Talonflame IV Guide

Talonflame has different IV priorities depending on whether you are building it for PvP, collection or general use.

Perfect 100% Talonflame

💯 100% Talonflame

A 100% Talonflame has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. At Level 50, this produces the maximum possible 2,493 CP.

A perfect Talonflame can be particularly appealing to collectors and for Ultra League because Talonflame can reach the 2,500 CP range.

PvP IVs

PvP IVs are different from the traditional 15/15/15 target. For Great League, Talonflame often prefers a low Attack IV combined with high Defense and HP so it can fit more total stat product under the CP limit.

Example Great League IV Target

0 Attack / 13 Defense / 15 HP is a strong example of a Great League Talonflame spread.

IV reminder:

The best PvP IV spread can vary slightly depending on exact rank, level and current league mechanics. Use a current PvP IV calculator when building your final specimen.

09 • PVP

Talonflame PvP Guide

Talonflame is considerably more interesting in PvP than in raids. Its Fire/Flying typing and charged-move pressure allow it to threaten several common Pokémon.

Great League

Talonflame can be a strong Great League pick when built with the appropriate moves. Incinerate generates pressure while Fly provides a relatively efficient Flying-type charged attack. Brave Bird gives Talonflame a powerful finishing option.

Great League Recommended Build

Incinerate + Fly + Brave Bird

Ultra League

Talonflame can also perform well in Ultra League, where a Level 50 build can reach the league's CP ceiling very effectively.

Ultra League Recommended Build

Incinerate + Fly + Brave Bird

Master League

Talonflame's maximum CP of 2,493 makes it much less natural for open Master League play than Pokémon with substantially higher CP ceilings.

Talonflame's PvP rankings can change as moves, cups and the overall GO Battle League meta change.

21 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Talonflame

Great League

  • Prioritize a PvP-appropriate IV spread.
  • Use Incinerate for strong fast-move pressure.
  • Use Fly as the efficient charged-move option.
  • Keep Brave Bird available as a high-damage finisher.
  • Manage Rock, Water and Electric threats carefully.

Ultra League

Ultra League is another strong environment for Talonflame. A Level 50 build can sit close to the 2,500 CP limit, making Candy XL investment particularly important.

Raid Use

Talonflame can contribute Fire or Flying damage, but specialized attackers generally provide better raid value. For most players, Talonflame's PvP potential is a stronger reason to invest.

23 • FAQ

Talonflame FAQ

What type is Talonflame?

Talonflame is a Fire/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Talonflame weak to?

Talonflame is especially vulnerable to Rock-type attacks and is also weak to Electric and Water.

What is Talonflame's max CP?

Talonflame's maximum CP is 2,493 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Talonflame good for raids?

Talonflame is not considered a top raid attacker. Its biggest strength is generally in PvP rather than PvE raids.

Is Talonflame good in Great League?

Yes. Talonflame can be a strong Great League Pokémon when equipped with an appropriate Incinerate, Fly and Brave Bird moveset.

Is Talonflame good in Ultra League?

Yes. Talonflame can also perform strongly in Ultra League and benefits from high-level investment.

Is Shiny Talonflame available?

Yes. Shiny Talonflame is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Shadow Talonflame exist?

Yes. Shadow Talonflame is represented as a Shadow form and retains its Fire/Flying typing.

Should I purify Shadow Talonflame?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, moves, PvP usefulness and your personal goals before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Talonflame?

Lucky Talonflame is a Talonflame obtained through Pokémon trading that has Lucky status and receives the associated Stardust power-up benefits.

What is a 100% Talonflame?

A 100% Talonflame has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a perfect Talonflame at Level 50?

A perfect Level 50 Talonflame reaches 2,493 CP.

Can Talonflame be XXL?

Yes. Talonflame can occur in the XXL size category.

Can Talonflame be XXS?

Yes. Talonflame can also occur in the XXS size category.

Does XXL Talonflame have better stats?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. XXL status does not automatically make Talonflame stronger.

What are the best moves for Talonflame?

For PvP, Incinerate with Fly and Brave Bird is a commonly recommended build. Move availability should always be checked against current game data.

What are the best Talonflame IVs for Great League?

Talonflame generally prefers low Attack and high Defense and HP for Great League. A commonly cited example is 0/13/15, but exact rank should be checked with a current PvP IV calculator.

Is 15/15/15 Talonflame good for Ultra League?

Yes. Because Talonflame's maximum CP is just under 2,500, a high-level 15/15/15 Talonflame can be a particularly useful Ultra League investment.

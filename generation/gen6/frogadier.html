Shiny Frogadier

Shiny Frogadier is the alternate-colour version of Frogadier. A Shiny Froakie can remain Shiny after evolving into Frogadier.

✨ Shiny Frogadier

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not automatically improve stats, IVs, battle performance or moves. A Shiny Frogadier can still be an excellent collector Pokémon and can evolve into Shiny Greninja.

  • Shiny status does not change Frogadier's typing.
  • Shiny status does not guarantee high IVs.
  • A Shiny Frogadier can evolve into Shiny Greninja.
  • Keep strong Shiny specimens for collection or evolution.
```

Shadow Frogadier

Shadow Frogadier is the Shadow version of Frogadier. Shadow status gives the Pokémon a different battle profile and should be considered separately from regular Frogadier.

🌑 Shadow Frogadier

Shadow Frogadier retains Frogadier's Water typing. Shadow status does not create a new species or change its evolution family.

Purified Frogadier

Purified Frogadier is obtained when an eligible Shadow member of the evolution family is purified.

✨ Purified Frogadier

Purification removes Shadow status and creates a Purified version of the Pokémon. Whether purification is the best decision depends on the Pokémon's IVs, future evolution plans and your preferred gameplay goals.

Shadow vs Purified

Shadow

Keeps Shadow status and its associated battle mechanics.

Purified

Removes Shadow status and becomes a Purified Pokémon.

Evolution

Lucky Frogadier

Lucky Frogadier is a special trade result with Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon are particularly useful for players planning Stardust-heavy investments.

🍀 Lucky Frogadier

Lucky Frogadier is visually identified by Lucky status and can be an attractive evolution candidate for players planning to continue the evolution line toward Greninja.

  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny status.
  • Lucky Pokémon can be valuable investment candidates.
  • A Lucky Frogadier can continue through the normal evolution path.
  • Evaluate IVs before choosing a long-term evolution candidate.

100% Frogadier

A 100% Frogadier has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

💯 Perfect IV Frogadier

15 / 15 / 15 is the perfect IV combination. A perfect Frogadier can be an excellent candidate for collectors or players planning to evolve it into Greninja.

XXL Frogadier

XXL Frogadier is an unusually large size variant. Size classification is separate from IVs, Shiny status and battle stats.

📏 XXL Frogadier

XXL Pokémon are primarily collector variants based on size. They may also be relevant to features or competitions that evaluate Pokémon size.

  • XXL does not automatically mean higher IVs.
  • XXL does not change Frogadier's Water typing.
  • XXL status is separate from Shiny and Lucky status.
  • Keep unusually large specimens for collection goals.
```

XXS Frogadier

XXS Frogadier is an unusually small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, its size category is primarily a collection characteristic.

🔍 XXS Frogadier

XXS status represents a particularly small Frogadier. It does not automatically change typing, IVs or battle performance.

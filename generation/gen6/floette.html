Floette

Floette is a Fairy-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. It evolves from Flabébé and can later evolve into Florges. In Pokémon GO, Floette can be collected in different flower color forms and may also exist in special status variants.

Floette Overview

Floette is the middle stage of the Flabébé evolution family. It evolves from Flabébé and can eventually become Florges. Its Fairy typing gives it useful matchups against Dragon, Fighting and Dark Pokémon.

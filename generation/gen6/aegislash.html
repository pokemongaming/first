Aegislash

Aegislash is a Ghost and Steel-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. Known for its unique sword-and-shield identity, Aegislash is one of the most distinctive Pokémon in the entire franchise because of its connection with offensive Blade Forme and defensive Shield Forme concepts.

Aegislash Type

Aegislash combines Ghost and Steel typing. This unusual combination gives it a distinctive defensive profile with several useful resistances and immunities in the wider Pokémon battle system.

👻 Ghost ⚙️ Steel

Why Ghost / Steel Typing Matters

Ghost and Steel is one of the most interesting type combinations available to Pokémon. It combines Ghost's offensive identity with Steel's large resistance profile.

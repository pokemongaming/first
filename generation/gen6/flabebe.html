Flabébé Overview

Flabébé is a Fairy-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6. It evolves into Floette and later into Florges. One of its most distinctive features is the flower it carries.

Why Fairy Typing Matters

Flabébé's Fairy typing gives it a useful set of matchups against several Pokémon types. However, because Flabébé has relatively low CP compared with fully evolved Pokémon, its main long-term value is generally connected to its evolution into Floette and Florges.

Purified Flabébé

A Purified Flabébé is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Flabébé. Purification changes the Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource costs.

💧 Purified Flabébé

Purified Flabébé is mainly a separate collection variant and should be evaluated carefully before purification.

Should You Purify Flabébé?

Do not purify solely because the IV numbers will improve. Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow version for collection purposes or future battle use.

Lucky Flabébé

Lucky Flabébé is obtained through Lucky Trades. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up, making them attractive long-term investment candidates.

🍀 Lucky Flabébé

A Lucky Flabébé can be particularly useful if you plan to evolve it into Floette and eventually Florges.

Lucky Collection Goal

Flabébé FAQ

What type is Flabébé?

Does Flabébé evolve?

Yes. Flabébé evolves into Floette, which can later evolve into Florges.

Can Flabébé be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on Pokémon GO's current release status and should be checked against current game data.

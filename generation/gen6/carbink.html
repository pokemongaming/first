Carbink

07 • PVP

Carbink PvP Guide

Great League

Carbink's high Defense and relatively low Attack make it well suited to CP-limited formats. In Great League, its Rock/Fairy typing can create useful matchups against specific Pokémon while its bulk allows it to remain in battle for extended periods.

Ultra League

Carbink's CP profile makes Great League the more natural environment for evaluating it. League-specific rules and current rankings should always be checked before building a team.

Master League

Carbink is generally not designed around the high CP requirements of Master League. Its low Attack and maximum CP make it considerably less natural for unrestricted Master League competition.

PvP Investment Tip

Do not judge Carbink only by its percentage IV score. For CP-limited PvP, the best IV distribution can differ from a conventional 15/15/15 specimen.

Is 100% Carbink Best for PvP?

Not necessarily. For CP-limited PvP, a perfect IV specimen can have a higher Attack stat than an optimized low-Attack PvP specimen. The best choice depends on the league and specific CP limit.

Collector Recommendation

Keep at least one 15/15/15 Carbink if you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon, even if another Carbink has a more suitable PvP IV spread.

20 • FAQ

Carbink FAQ

What type is Carbink?

Carbink is a Rock/Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Carbink weak to?

Carbink's Rock/Fairy typing makes it vulnerable to several attack types, including Water, Grass, Ground, Steel and Fighting attacks.

Is Carbink good for raids?

Carbink is generally more notable for PvP and defensive utility than for high raid damage because of its low Attack.

Is Carbink good in Great League?

Carbink can be highly interesting in Great League because of its high Defense and Rock/Fairy typing. The best build depends on the current PvP meta and IV spread.

Is 100% Carbink good for PvP?

A 100% Carbink is excellent as a perfect-IV specimen, but a 15/15/15 spread is not automatically the optimal Great League IV distribution.

Does Carbink evolve?

No. Carbink does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Carbink available?

Shiny availability depends on Pokémon GO's current encounter and event systems. Check the current game schedule before claiming an active encounter method.

What is Lucky Carbink?

Lucky Carbink is a Carbink obtained through trading that has the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Is Lucky Carbink automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, but Lucky status does not automatically mean 15/15/15.

What is XXL Carbink?

XXL Carbink is an extra-large size variant. Its size is separate from its IVs and does not automatically make it stronger in battle.

What is XXS Carbink?

XXS Carbink is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL, its size is separate from its IV statistics.

Is Shadow Carbink available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotations. Do not permanently claim availability without current data.

Should I purify Shadow Carbink?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your PvP, collection and investment goals.

What is the best Carbink IV?

The best IV depends on your goal. A 15/15/15 Carbink is perfect, but a Great League Carbink may benefit from a different IV distribution designed around the CP limit.

Is Carbink related to Diancie?

Carbink and Diancie are separate Pokémon. They share lore connections, but Carbink does not evolve into Diancie.

Yveltal

Yveltal is a Legendary Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon from the Kalos region. In Pokémon GO, it is valuable in both raids and the GO Battle League, with particularly strong Flying-type performance when equipped with Oblivion Wing.

10 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Yveltal PvP Guide

Yveltal has significant value in higher CP formats, especially Master League, where its Dark/Flying typing and charged-move coverage can create difficult matchups.

Master League

Master League is Yveltal's most natural PvP environment. Its Dark typing gives it useful pressure against Psychic- and Ghost-type threats, while Flying attacks add additional coverage.

Ultra League

Yveltal can be used in Ultra League when available within the relevant CP constraints, although its high maximum CP and Legendary investment are more naturally suited to Master League.

Great League

Yveltal's high CP requirements make Great League an unusual environment for the species. League availability and special formats should always be checked before investing specifically for this cup.

PvP Starting Point

Sucker Punch + Dark Pulse + Oblivion Wing

15 • XXL

XXL Yveltal

XXL Yveltal is an unusually large Yveltal. Size is a separate characteristic from IVs, CP and battle stats.

XXL Yveltal

8.70–8.99 m

The documented XXL size range for Yveltal reaches approximately 8.99 meters.

Standard Height

5.80 m

Yveltal's standard Pokédex height is approximately 5.8 meters.

Does XXL Yveltal Have Higher Stats?

No. XXL status is a size characteristic rather than an automatic increase to Attack, Defense or HP. A large Yveltal can have excellent or poor IVs just like any other Yveltal.

Collector Tip

A 100% IV XXL Yveltal is significantly more interesting to collectors than a large Yveltal with ordinary IVs, because the size and IV characteristics can coexist.

16 • XXS

24 • FAQ

Yveltal FAQ

What type is Yveltal?

Yveltal is a Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Yveltal weak to?

Yveltal is weak to Electric, Fairy, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What is Yveltal's max CP?

Yveltal's maximum standard CP is 4,275.

What is the best Yveltal moveset for raids?

Gust and Oblivion Wing are the recommended Flying-focused raid combination. Snarl and Dark Pulse are useful for its Dark role.

Is Shiny Yveltal available?

Yes. Shiny Yveltal is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Is Yveltal good in raids?

Yes. Yveltal is particularly strong as a Flying-type attacker when equipped with Gust and Oblivion Wing.

Is Yveltal good in Master League?

Yveltal has strong Master League relevance because of its high CP ceiling, Dark/Flying typing and useful charged-move coverage.

What is a 100% Yveltal?

A 100% Yveltal has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Yveltal?

A Lucky Yveltal is a Yveltal that has obtained the Lucky status through trading and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Is XXL Yveltal stronger?

No. XXL is a size characteristic and does not automatically increase Yveltal's Attack, Defense or HP.

Is XXS Yveltal rare?

XXS Yveltal can be an attractive collector specimen because of its unusually small size.

Can Yveltal be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Yveltal can potentially have both.

Can Yveltal be 100% and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so a Yveltal can potentially be both perfect IV and XXL.

Does Yveltal evolve?

No. Yveltal has no normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Can Shadow Yveltal be purified?

If Shadow Yveltal is available through current Pokémon GO mechanics, an eligible Shadow Pokémon can be evaluated for purification. Always check the current in-game availability and mechanics.

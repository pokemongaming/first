Why Aurorus' Typing Matters

12 — PERFECT IV

100% IV Aurorus

A 100% IV Aurorus has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a perfect-IV or hundo Aurorus.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
15 / 15 / 15 = 100% IV

A perfect Aurorus can be an excellent collector target. Whether it deserves maximum investment depends on your preferred battle format and the current Pokémon GO meta.

How to Identify a 100% Aurorus

Use the in-game Appraisal system or an IV calculator to determine the Attack, Defense and HP IVs. The displayed CP alone does not prove that a Pokémon is 100% IV.

16 — PVP

Aurorus PvP Guide

Aurorus is particularly interesting in PvP because its typing, bulk and coverage can create unusual matchups.

Great League

When eligible under the current CP rules, Aurorus can offer useful offensive coverage and significant charged-move pressure. Its weaknesses must still be carefully managed.

Ultra League

Aurorus can also be considered for Ultra League teams depending on its CP, IV distribution and available moves.

Master League

Aurorus is less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League competition because of the stat requirements of the format. Always check the current meta before investing specifically for Master League.

PvP Tip

Aurorus' unusual Rock / Ice typing means that team composition matters. Pair it with Pokémon capable of covering its major Fighting, Ground, Steel, Water, Grass and Rock weaknesses.

Aurorus FAQ

What type is Aurorus?

Aurorus is a Rock / Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Aurorus weak to?

Aurorus has major weaknesses associated with Fighting, Ground, Steel, Water, Grass and Rock attacks.

Does Aurorus evolve?

Yes. Aurorus evolves from Amaura.

What generation is Aurorus from?

Aurorus was introduced in Generation 6 and is associated with the Kalos region.

Is Shiny Aurorus available?

Shiny availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO encounter and event rules.

What is 100% Aurorus?

A 100% Aurorus has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Aurorus?

Lucky Aurorus is an Aurorus with Lucky status, obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

Is XXL Aurorus a different Pokémon?

No. XXL is a size classification rather than a separate species.

Is XXS Aurorus a different Pokémon?

No. XXS is a size classification for an unusually small Aurorus.

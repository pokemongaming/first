Why Chespin's Typing Matters

Chespin's pure Grass typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. It performs well against Water, Ground and Rock-type opponents when using appropriate Grass attacks, but it must be careful around several common offensive types.

Should You Purify Shadow Chespin?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Pokémon simply because purification can increase its appraisal. Consider the Shadow bonus, your collection goals, Stardust requirements and whether you want the Shadow or Purified version.

Lucky Chespin

Lucky Chespin is a special traded Pokémon with the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making the Lucky tag useful for trainers who want to build or collect resource-efficient Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Chespin

Chespin can become Lucky through trading when the Lucky mechanic is triggered. Lucky status does not change Chespin's species or Grass typing.

Lucky Chespin Features

  • Obtained through trading
  • Has Lucky status
  • Requires less Stardust to power up
  • IVs are rerolled through the trade
  • Can also be Shiny if the traded Pokémon is Shiny
  • Can potentially be a Lucky 100% Chespin

Chespin PvP Guide

Chespin's relatively low CP and unevolved status limit its role in serious PvP compared with its evolved form. It can nevertheless be used for personal challenges and special formats where applicable.

Great League

Chespin is more naturally suited to lower-CP formats than Master League simply because of its modest maximum CP. Whether it is viable depends on the specific cup rules and current move pool.

Ultra League

Chespin is generally not a primary Ultra League investment because it is an unevolved Pokémon with relatively limited battle stats.

Master League

Chespin's low maximum CP makes standard Master League play impractical. Trainers interested in the species competitively will generally want to examine its evolved forms instead.

Chespin FAQ

What type is Chespin?

Chespin is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Chespin weak to?

Chespin is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

Is Shiny Chespin available?

Yes. Shiny Chespin is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Chespin a good raid attacker?

Chespin itself is not considered a major end-game raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolution line and collection potential.

What does Chespin evolve into?

Chespin evolves into Quilladin and eventually Chesnaught.

What is a 100% Chespin?

A 100% Chespin has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Can Chespin be Lucky?

Yes. Chespin can become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is Lucky Chespin?

Lucky Chespin is a traded Chespin that receives Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Chespin?

XXL Chespin is an individual Chespin classified as an extra-large Pokémon based on its size.

What is XXS Chespin?

XXS Chespin is an individual Chespin classified as an extra-small Pokémon.

Can a Chespin be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a traded Shiny Chespin can become Lucky.

Can Chespin be both 100% and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics. An XXL Chespin can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Chespin be Shadow and Shiny?

Whether a particular Shadow species can also be encountered as Shiny depends on Pokémon GO's current encounter and game rules. Check current game data before making a permanent availability claim.

Should I evolve a 100% Chespin?

If you want the strongest possible IV specimen from that individual evolutionary line, evolving a 100% Chespin into its later forms preserves its perfect IV combination.

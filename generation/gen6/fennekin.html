Fennekin

Fennekin is a Fire-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 6 from the Kalos region. In Pokémon GO, it can evolve into Braixen and eventually Delphox. Collectors can also obtain special versions such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Fennekin.

Fennekin Overview

Fennekin is the first stage of the Kalos Fire-type starter evolution family. It evolves into Braixen and then Delphox. While Fennekin itself is primarily a collection and evolution-stage Pokémon, its evolution line can provide more significant battle utility.

Why Fennekin's Fire Typing Matters

Fire-type Pokémon are generally effective against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel Pokémon. Fennekin's typing remains Fire throughout its first two evolutionary stages before Delphox gains an additional Psychic type.

Fennekin Forms & Special Variants

The following versions represent different collectible or gameplay states of Fennekin in Pokémon GO.

🔥 Regular Fennekin

The standard version of Fennekin. It can evolve into Braixen and eventually Delphox when the required Candy and evolution conditions are met.

✨ Shiny Fennekin

Shiny Fennekin has alternate coloration and is mainly valuable as a collector variant. It can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

🌑 Shadow Fennekin

Shadow Fennekin is a Team GO Rocket variant. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile and may require additional resources to power up.

💎 Purified Fennekin

Purified Fennekin is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Fennekin. Purification changes its status and can affect its IVs and resource costs.

🍀 Lucky Fennekin

Lucky Fennekin can be obtained through eligible trades. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

💯 100% Fennekin

A 100% Fennekin has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. It can be a valuable long-term evolution and collection target.

📏 XXL Fennekin

XXL Fennekin is an unusually large size variant and can be useful for size-based collection challenges.

🔹 XXS Fennekin

XXS Fennekin is an unusually small size variant and is primarily a collector form.

Shiny Fennekin

Shiny Fennekin is the alternate-color version of Fennekin. Shiny status is retained when Fennekin evolves into Braixen and Delphox.

Should You Evolve Shadow Fennekin?

Players may choose to keep a Shadow Fennekin for their collection or evolve it toward a Shadow Delphox project. The value of doing so depends on current move availability and the role you want the Pokémon to fill.

Purified Fennekin

Purified Fennekin is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Fennekin. Purification removes Shadow status and can increase each IV by a fixed amount within the game's purification rules.

Purification Decision

Do not purify automatically. Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow version for collection or battle purposes before committing.

Lucky Fennekin

Lucky Fennekin is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Its primary advantage is reduced Stardust cost when powering up compared with a normal Pokémon.

  • Useful for long-term evolution projects.
  • Costs less Stardust to power up.
  • Can be a valuable collector variant.
  • Can evolve into Lucky Braixen and Lucky Delphox.

XXL Fennekin

XXL Fennekin is a larger-than-usual size variant. XXL Pokémon are primarily valuable for collectors and size-related Pokémon GO challenges.

XXL Collection Value

XXS Fennekin

XXS Fennekin is an unusually small version of Fennekin. Like XXL Pokémon, its primary value is collection and size-based gameplay.

XXS Collection Value

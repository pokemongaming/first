Why Honedge's Typing Matters

Honedge's Steel/Ghost combination gives it a large collection of resistances. It takes particularly reduced damage from Normal and Poison attacks and also resists several common offensive types.

Why Purification Matters

Purification changes an eligible Shadow Pokémon's status and improves its appraisal while reducing certain resource costs. Purified Pokémon also forget Frustration and learn Return when purified.

19 • PVP

Honedge PvP Guide

Honedge's Steel/Ghost typing gives it an interesting set of resistances, but its low maximum CP limits its role in higher CP leagues.

Great League

Honedge can have niche value in lower CP formats, but it is not generally considered a top Great League option. Its typing can create useful matchups, although its limited moveset and overall performance must be considered.

Ultra League

Honedge's maximum CP is too low to make it a natural Ultra League Pokémon.

Master League

Honedge is not suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the requirements for competing with the format's strongest Pokémon.

PvP Takeaway

Keep Honedge primarily as an evolution and collection project unless you have a specific lower-league strategy.

20 • RESOURCES

Honedge Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Honedge requires Candy for evolution and additional resources if you want to power up an individual specimen.

  • 25 Honedge Candy evolves Honedge into Doublade.
  • 100 additional Honedge Candy evolves Doublade into Aegislash.
  • Candy XL becomes important for high-level investment.
  • Lucky Honedge requires less Stardust to power up.
  • Check IVs before committing large amounts of Stardust.
21 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Honedge

Before Powering Up

  • Check the IV appraisal.
  • Decide whether the Pokémon will be evolved.
  • Check whether it is Shiny, Lucky, XXL or XXS.
  • Consider saving Candy for Doublade and Aegislash.
  • Avoid spending excessive Stardust on a low-IV specimen unless it has collector value.

Best Long-Term Strategy

For most trainers, Honedge is best treated as an evolution and collection project. A high-IV or Shiny Honedge can be saved until you have enough Candy to build the evolved family.

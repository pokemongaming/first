08 · IV GUIDE

Aromatisse IV Guide

IVs add individual points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Aromatisse is the perfect IV combination and is commonly called a Hundo.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is 100% Aromatisse?

A 100% Aromatisse has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is a perfect-IV or Hundo Aromatisse.

💯 Hundo Aromatisse

15 / 15 / 15 = 100% IV

Is a Hundo Always the Best?

Not necessarily. The ideal IV spread can depend on the battle format. For CP-limited PvP leagues, lower Attack with higher Defense and HP can sometimes be preferable to a perfect 15/15/15 specimen.

09 · PVP

Aromatisse PvP Guide

Aromatisse's Fairy typing gives it useful theoretical matchups against Dragon, Fighting and Dark Pokémon, although its practical PvP value depends on the current moveset and league meta.

Great League

Aromatisse can be considered for Great League when its current moveset and available cup rules make it relevant. IV optimization is particularly important because of the CP limit.

Ultra League

Aromatisse can be evaluated in Ultra League with appropriately powered builds. Always check current rankings and move updates before spending significant resources.

Master League

Aromatisse's maximum CP is substantially lower than that of many Legendary and pseudo-Legendary Pokémon, making Master League a less natural environment for it.

Aromatisse FAQ

What type is Aromatisse?

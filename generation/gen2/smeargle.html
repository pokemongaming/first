01 • OVERVIEW

Smeargle Overview

Smeargle is a Normal-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. It is best known in Pokémon GO for its unusual interaction with GO Snapshot rather than for its battle performance.

Smeargle does not have a normal evolution chain. Its most distinctive feature is Sketch, which gives it a unique relationship with the moves used by Pokémon appearing in GO Snapshot.

05 • MOVES

Smeargle Moves & Sketch

Smeargle is unusual because its signature mechanic revolves around Sketch. In Pokémon GO, its moveset can reflect moves associated with the Pokémon appearing in the Snapshot encounter.

Smeargle's Signature Mechanic

Sketch allows Smeargle to copy certain moves from the Pokémon involved in the relevant Photo Bomb encounter.

Why Smeargle Moves Are Different

Unlike ordinary Pokémon where trainers simply select from a fixed move pool, Smeargle's Pokémon GO identity is closely connected with its Photo Bomb mechanic. This makes its individual moveset more interesting to collectors than to competitive battlers.

16 • PHOTO BOMB

How to Get Smeargle in Pokémon GO

Smeargle's most recognizable Pokémon GO mechanic is its interaction with GO Snapshot. When Smeargle Photo Bombs an eligible Snapshot, the trainer can encounter Smeargle afterward.

General Photo Bomb Flow

  1. Open a Pokémon that can be photographed.
  2. Enter GO Snapshot.
  3. Take photographs.
  4. Check whether Smeargle appears in the Snapshot.
  5. Exit the Snapshot sequence.
  6. Encounter Smeargle when the Photo Bomb encounter is triggered.
Important Availability Note

Smeargle encounter frequency and special Shiny opportunities can change during events. Your site should display current event-specific rules rather than treating every historical encounter rule as permanent.

13 — PERFECT IV

100% IV Miltank

A 100% IV Miltank has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a perfect IV or hundo Miltank.

15 Defense

💯 Perfect Miltank

If all three IVs are 15, the Pokémon is a 100% IV Miltank. A perfect specimen can be valuable for collectors even when it is not a top raid attacker.

100% Miltank vs PvP Miltank

Do not automatically transfer a Miltank simply because it is not close to 15/15/15. PvP leagues can favor different IV spreads, particularly when a Pokémon is being optimized under a CP limit.

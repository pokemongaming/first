07 • PVP

Ariados PvP Guide

Ariados can have niche value in lower CP-limited formats where its typing and charged-move pressure can matter more than its raw maximum CP.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to investigate Ariados because of the 1,500 CP limit. Its Bug/Poison typing and fast energy generation can create interesting matchups.

Ultra League

Ariados can be considered in specialized Ultra League formats, although trainers should compare it with stronger alternatives before investing significant Stardust and Candy XL.

Master League

Ariados's relatively low maximum CP makes it a poor natural fit for standard Master League competition.

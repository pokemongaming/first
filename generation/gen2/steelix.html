09 • IV GUIDE

Steelix IV Guide

A 15/15/15 Steelix is a perfect IV or "hundo" Steelix. However, the best IV spread depends on the battle league or role for which you are building the Pokémon.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV

What Is a 100% Steelix?

A 100% Steelix has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a hundo. At Level 50, a perfect Steelix reaches 2,729 CP.

Hundo Collector Tip

If you find a 15/15/15 Steelix, consider keeping it even if you do not immediately plan to power it up. A perfect specimen can also be useful as a future Mega Steelix candidate.

10 • PVP

Steelix PvP Guide

Steelix's enormous Defense and Steel typing make it considerably more interesting in PvP than its relatively low Attack would suggest.

Great League

Steelix can be useful in CP-limited formats where its defensive profile allows it to absorb damage and apply pressure with efficient charged moves.

Ultra League

Ultra League can also provide opportunities for Steelix builds. IV selection, move timing and the current meta should be considered before choosing a specific Steelix.

Master League

Steelix's lower Attack and overall CP ceiling make Master League a less natural home for standard Steelix compared with CP-limited formats.

12 • SHADOW STEELIX

Shadow Steelix

Shadow Steelix is a separate Shadow variant of Steelix. The Shadow mechanic changes its battle profile, making it important to evaluate Shadow Steelix separately from the regular version.

SPECIES Steelix
TYPE Steel / Ground

MAX CP 2,729

Shadow Steelix Mechanics

Shadow Steelix keeps the same underlying species and Steel/Ground typing. Its Shadow status changes the damage and durability relationship, so Shadow Steelix should be judged according to its intended battle role rather than simply compared by IV percentage.

Should You Power Up Shadow Steelix?

23 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Steelix

Before Investing

  • Check the Pokémon's IVs.
  • Decide whether you want PvP, Mega or collection value.
  • Check whether the Steelix is Shadow, Lucky or Purified.
  • Consider whether its size makes it valuable as a collection piece.
  • Check current moves before spending Elite TMs.

PvP Investment

For PvP, do not automatically choose the highest Attack IV specimen. League-specific CP limits can make lower-Attack, higher-Defense spreads attractive depending on the league and current meta.

Mega Investment

For a Mega Steelix candidate, consider overall IV quality, level, Shiny status and your collection goals. A perfect Steelix is particularly appealing if you want a long-term Mega candidate.

25 • FAQ

Steelix FAQ

What type is Steelix?

Steelix is a Steel- and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Steelix weak to?

Steelix is weak to Fighting, Fire, Ground and Water-type attacks.

What is Steelix's max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Steelix reaches 2,729 CP.

What are Steelix's stats?

Steelix has 148 Attack, 272 Defense and 181 HP.

Does Steelix evolve?

Steelix is the final evolution of Onix and does not normally evolve further. It can Mega Evolve.

How do you evolve Onix into Steelix?

Onix evolves into Steelix using 50 Onix Candy and a Metal Coat.

Is Shiny Steelix available?

Yes. Shiny Steelix is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Steelix available?

Yes. Shadow Steelix exists as a Shadow variant, although its current availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

What is Purified Steelix?

Purified Steelix is created by purifying Shadow Steelix. Purification removes the Shadow status and replaces Frustration with Return.

What is Lucky Steelix?

Lucky Steelix is obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Steelix?

A 100% Steelix has 15 Attack IV, 15 Defense IV and 15 HP IV.

What is the max CP of a hundo Steelix?

A Level 50 15/15/15 Steelix has a maximum CP of 2,729.

Can Steelix be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size attribute and is separate from IVs, Shiny status and Lucky status.

Can Steelix be XXS?

Yes. XXS Steelix is a small-size variant and is primarily interesting to collectors.

Is Mega Steelix available?

Yes. Steelix can Mega Evolve into Mega Steelix using Mega Energy.

What is Mega Steelix's max CP?

Mega Steelix reaches 4,149 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Steelix useful for Mega Evolution?

Yes. A Shiny Steelix can be used for Mega Evolution while retaining its Shiny identity.

Is Steelix good for raids?

Standard Steelix is primarily a defensive Pokémon rather than a premier raid attacker. Its Mega form provides additional utility and should be evaluated separately.

Is Steelix good for PvP?

Steelix can be useful in CP-limited PvP formats, particularly because of its high Defense and broad resistance profile.

Should I purify Shadow Steelix?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first and consider your PvP, collection and investment goals.

Should I Mega Evolve a 100% Steelix?

A 100% Steelix is an excellent candidate for Mega Evolution if you want a high-IV long-term Mega investment.

A Shiny Sunflora with strong IVs can be an attractive long-term collection piece.

💠 Purified Sunflora

Purified Sunflora is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon in the Sunflora evolutionary line. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's investment path.

Purified Sunflora Investment

Purification may improve IVs, but players should consider the loss of Shadow battle characteristics before making a permanent decision.

  • Check the Shadow Pokémon's IVs before purifying.
  • Compare regular and Shadow performance.
  • Consider whether the Pokémon is valuable for raids.
  • Consider collector value.
  • Do not purify automatically.

🍀 Lucky Sunflora

Lucky Sunflora is a Sunflora obtained through a Lucky trade. Its major advantage is the reduced Stardust cost required for powering it up.

Why Lucky Sunflora Is Useful

Lucky status can make Sunflora cheaper to power up, making it useful for collectors who want a high-level Sunflora without spending as much Stardust.

Is a 100% Sunflora Worth Keeping?

Yes, especially for collectors and players building complete Pokédex collections. Whether it should be powered to a high level depends on your available Stardust and Grass-type team.

01 — OVERVIEW

Wobbuffet Overview

Wobbuffet is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. Unlike many Pokémon that rely on high Attack for battle performance, Wobbuffet is famous for its enormous HP stat and extremely low Attack.

That unusual stat distribution makes Wobbuffet particularly interesting for Pokémon GO PvP. Its value is not primarily based on raid damage; instead, it can function as a bulky specialist in CP-limited formats.

Why Wobbuffet's Typing Matters

Wobbuffet's pure Psychic typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. It does not gain the extra defensive interactions that come with a dual typing.

Its battle identity is therefore driven more by its exceptional HP and unusual moveset than by defensive typing alone.

07 — PVP

Wobbuffet PvP Guide

Wobbuffet's biggest competitive advantage is its enormous HP combined with very low Attack. This makes it a niche Great League Pokémon that can absorb considerable damage.

Great League

Great League is the most natural environment for Wobbuffet. Its unusual stat distribution allows it to function as a bulky specialist under the 1,500 CP limit.

Wobbuffet is especially interesting when you want a Pokémon that can remain on the field for a long time and force your opponent to spend resources dealing with its bulk.

Ultra League

Wobbuffet's low overall CP makes Ultra League a much less natural environment. Even with significant investment, its stat profile does not provide the same role it has under the Great League CP limit.

Master League

Wobbuffet is generally unsuitable for Master League because its maximum CP and offensive stats are far below the level normally expected in that format.

PvP Investment Tip

Do not judge Wobbuffet solely by Attack IVs. Because of its unusual stat distribution, PvP IV optimization can look very different from the simple 15/15/15 rule used for many raid Pokémon.

08 — IV GUIDE

Wobbuffet IV Guide

The best Wobbuffet IV spread depends on the intended role. A perfect 15/15/15 Wobbuffet is excellent for collection purposes, but Great League PvP often rewards different IV distributions.

100% IV Wobbuffet

A 15/15/15 Wobbuffet is a perfect-IV specimen. It is a valuable collector's Pokémon and can also be useful depending on the format and level at which you plan to use it.

PvP IV reminder:

A 100% IV Pokémon is not automatically the best PvP specimen. For CP-limited leagues, the optimal IV spread can prioritize Defense and HP while keeping Attack lower.

12 — LUCKY

Lucky Wobbuffet

TRADING

How to Get Lucky Wobbuffet

Lucky status is obtained through Pokémon GO trading rather than through a normal wild encounter.

Lucky Wobbuffet Benefits

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky trades guarantee a special Lucky outcome under certain game conditions.
  • Lucky Wobbuffet can be useful for collectors.
  • Check IVs after the trade because traded Pokémon receive new IV values.
  • A Lucky status does not automatically make Wobbuffet better in every PvP situation.
Important:

Lucky status and perfect IV status are separate properties. A Lucky Wobbuffet is not automatically a 100% IV Wobbuffet.

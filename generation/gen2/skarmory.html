01 • OVERVIEW

Skarmory Overview

Skarmory is a dual Steel/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. Unlike many Pokémon, Skarmory has no normal evolution chain. Its combination of high Defense, useful resistances and access to Flying-type attacks gives it a distinct role in Pokémon GO.

02 • TYPE

Skarmory Type

🛡️
Steel / Flying Dual Type

Why Skarmory's Typing Matters

Steel/Flying is one of Skarmory's biggest advantages. The combination gives Skarmory a large collection of resistances and helps explain why it can be difficult to remove in PvP.

The Flying typing also removes the normal Ground weakness that Steel Pokémon would otherwise have. However, the combination leaves Skarmory especially vulnerable to Fire and Electric attacks.

09 • PVP

Skarmory PvP Guide

PvP is one of Skarmory's most important roles in Pokémon GO. Its defensive stats, Steel/Flying typing and access to strong Flying attacks make it particularly interesting around Great League formats.

Great League

Skarmory is naturally suited to Great League because its maximum CP is close to the format's CP ceiling. It can pressure Grass, Bug and many other targets while benefiting from its extensive resistance profile.

Ultra League

Skarmory can be used in Ultra League, although its overall value depends heavily on the current metagame and available alternatives.

Master League

Skarmory's maximum CP is relatively low compared with Legendary and other high-stat Master League Pokémon, making Master League a much less natural environment for it.

  • Build around Skarmory's defensive typing.
  • Consider current Great League rankings before investing.
  • Check the exact CP and IV combination.
  • Use shields strategically around charged-move pressure.

    25 • FAQ

    Skarmory FAQ

    What type is Skarmory?

    Skarmory is a Steel/Flying-type Pokémon.

    What is Skarmory weak to?

    Skarmory is weak to Fire- and Electric-type attacks.

    Is Skarmory good in Pokémon GO?

    Skarmory's strongest practical role is generally PvP, where its defensive typing and moveset can make it a useful Great League Pokémon.

    Is Skarmory good for raids?

    Skarmory is not generally considered a top raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack stat.

    Is Shiny Skarmory available?

    Yes. Shiny Skarmory is available through eligible encounters.

    Is Shadow Skarmory available?

    Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current encounter pool.

    Should I purify Shadow Skarmory?

    Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions for your intended PvP, collection and resource goals before purifying.

    What is a Lucky Skarmory?

    A Lucky Skarmory is a Pokémon that has obtained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon receive a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

    What is a 100% Skarmory?

    A 100% Skarmory has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

    What is XXL Skarmory?

    XXL Skarmory is a Skarmory with a large recorded size attribute. XXL status is independent of IVs.

    What is XXS Skarmory?

    XXS Skarmory is a Skarmory with a small recorded size attribute. XXS status is independent of IVs.

    Does Skarmory evolve?

    No. Skarmory is a standalone Pokémon with no normal evolution.

    Does Skarmory have a Mega Evolution?

    Skarmory does not currently have an official Mega Evolution available in Pokémon GO. Any future Mega Skarmory information should be added only after an official game release.

    Can Skarmory be Shiny and Lucky?

    Yes. Shiny appearance and Lucky status are separate attributes, so a Skarmory can potentially have both.

    Can Skarmory be XXL and 100% IV?

    Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXL Skarmory can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

    Can Skarmory be XXS and 100% IV?

    Yes. An XXS Skarmory can also have perfect IVs.

Why Ledian's Typing Matters

Ledian's Bug and Flying combination gives it useful resistances but also leaves it vulnerable to several important attacking types. Its Flying typing also means Ground-type attacks do not normally damage it.

21 • FAQ

Ledian FAQ

What type is Ledian?

Ledian is a Bug and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Ledian weak to?

Ledian is vulnerable to several attacking types, including Fire, Electric, Ice, Flying and Rock.

Is Shiny Ledian available?

Shiny Ledian can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny form is available.

Is Shadow Ledian available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotations.

What is a 100% Ledian?

A 100% Ledian has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Ledian?

Lucky Ledian is a Ledian that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

What is an XXL Ledian?

An XXL Ledian is an extra-large size specimen. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is an XXS Ledian?

An XXS Ledian is an extra-small size specimen. It does not automatically have high or perfect IVs.

Is Ledian good for raids?

Ledian is generally not considered a priority raid attacker. Its strongest appeal is usually collection, variant hunting and Pokédex completion.

Should I purify Shadow Ledian?

Do not purify automatically. Consider the Shadow version's value to your collection and battle goals before making the decision.

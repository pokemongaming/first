Feraligatr PvP Guide

Feraligatr can be an interesting PvP Pokémon because its performance depends strongly on its fast move, charged move access and the current league metagame.

Great League

Feraligatr can be considered for Great League teams when built around the appropriate CP range and moveset.

Ultra League

A higher-level Feraligatr can also be evaluated for Ultra League depending on IV distribution and current meta matchups.

Master League

Feraligatr's maximum CP is lower than many traditional Master League Pokémon, making other leagues generally more natural places to evaluate its PvP role.

Shiny Feraligatr

Shiny Feraligatr is an alternate-color version of the regular Pokémon. Shiny status does not independently change its base battle stats or IV system.

Collector Value

A Shiny Feraligatr with strong IVs, useful PvP IVs, Lucky status or an unusual size can be particularly desirable for collectors.

Shadow Feraligatr for Raids

Shadow Feraligatr can be considered by players who prioritize damage output. Its usefulness should be compared against other available Water attackers and the resources required to build it.

  • Evaluate its current moveset before investing.
  • Consider the higher resource cost of Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not automatically purify a useful Shadow specimen.
  • Compare it with your existing Water attackers.

Purified Feraligatr

Purified Feraligatr is created when an eligible Shadow member of the Totodile evolution family is purified.

Purified Investment

Purification should be considered carefully. A higher IV after purification is not automatically more valuable than keeping a strong Shadow Pokémon for a damage-focused role.

Lucky Feraligatr

Lucky Feraligatr is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon.

Why Lucky Feraligatr Is Valuable

A Lucky Feraligatr can be a good long-term investment when you want to power it up for raids, PvP or collection purposes while reducing Stardust requirements.

  • Useful for Stardust-efficient power-ups.
  • Can have useful raid IVs.
  • Can be collected as a special trade variant.
  • Useful for players building multiple Water Pokémon.

💯 Perfect Feraligatr

A 100% IV Feraligatr can be an excellent collector target and a strong option for high-level investment. However, a lower-IV Feraligatr may be more suitable for specific CP-capped PvP leagues.

XXL Feraligatr

XXL Feraligatr is a larger-than-normal size variant. Size classification is separate from IV quality and does not mean the Pokémon automatically has better battle statistics.

XXL Collector Value

XXL Feraligatr is primarily useful for collectors and size-based showcases. A rare combination such as Shiny XXL, Lucky XXL or 100% XXL can be especially interesting.

XXS Feraligatr

XXS Feraligatr is a smaller-than-normal size variant. Its size classification is independent of IVs, Shiny status and Lucky status.

XXS Collector Value

An XXS Feraligatr can be a fun collection target, particularly when combined with other rare characteristics such as Shiny, Lucky or high IVs.

Feraligatr FAQ

What type is Feraligatr?

Feraligatr is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Feraligatr weak to?

Feraligatr is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Is Shiny Feraligatr available?

Shiny Feraligatr can be obtained when eligible Shiny Totodile encounters are available.

Is Shadow Feraligatr good?

Shadow Feraligatr can be useful for damage-focused builds, depending on its moveset and available Water-type alternatives.

Should I purify Shadow Feraligatr?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version and the purified result before making a permanent decision.

What is a 100% Feraligatr?

A 100% Feraligatr has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Feraligatr good?

Lucky Feraligatr can be valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Feraligatr?

XXL Feraligatr is a larger size variant and is mainly relevant for collecting and size-based features.

What is XXS Feraligatr?

XXS Feraligatr is a smaller size variant that is independent of IV quality and battle strength.

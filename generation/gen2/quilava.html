Quilava FAQ

What type is Quilava?

Quilava is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

Does Quilava evolve?

Yes. Quilava evolves into Typhlosion.

Can Quilava be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Quilava can exist through eligible Pokémon GO evolution or encounter methods.

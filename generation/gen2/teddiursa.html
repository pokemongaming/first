01 • OVERVIEW

Teddiursa Overview

Teddiursa is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Ursaring and is later connected to Ursaluna through the evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Teddiursa can be collected in several different forms and classifications, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky specimens.

Why Teddiursa's Typing Matters

Teddiursa's pure Normal typing gives it a simple defensive profile. It has relatively few weaknesses, but its overall battle performance is limited by its lower stats compared with its evolved forms.

05 • EVOLUTION

Teddiursa Evolution Family

Teddiursa is the first stage of its evolution family. It evolves into Ursaring, while Ursaring can later evolve into Ursaluna when the required Pokémon GO conditions are met.

Should You Evolve Teddiursa?

If you have a strong Teddiursa, evolving it can be more useful than investing heavily in the first-stage Pokémon. High-IV specimens can also be saved for future evolution projects.

Evolution Tip

Before evolving a rare Teddiursa, check its IVs, appraisal, level and whether it is Shiny, Shadow, Purified or Lucky.

08 • PVP

Teddiursa PvP Guide

Teddiursa's relatively low maximum CP means that it can technically fit within lower CP formats, but its overall battle performance is generally overshadowed by stronger Pokémon.

Great League

Teddiursa can fall within Great League CP ranges after investment, but its performance should be evaluated against the current cup rules and available moves.

Ultra League

Teddiursa's limited maximum CP makes it an unusual choice for Ultra League. Its evolved forms are generally more relevant for competitive experimentation.

Master League

Teddiursa is not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP and overall stats are too low compared with Pokémon designed for that environment.

09 • SHINY

Shiny Teddiursa

Shiny Teddiursa is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny specimens are particularly desirable because Teddiursa is part of a recognizable evolution family and can be evolved while retaining its Shiny status.

✨

Shiny Teddiursa

Shiny Teddiursa has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny status is retained when Teddiursa evolves.

Shiny Teddiursa Collector Tips

13 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Teddiursa

A 100% Teddiursa has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Does 100% Teddiursa Mean?

A 100% Teddiursa has a total IV combination of 15/15/15. It is commonly referred to as a hundo by Pokémon GO players.

Hundo Teddiursa

A perfect Teddiursa is particularly useful for collectors and evolution projects. Whether it deserves Stardust investment depends on your goals.

100% Teddiursa After Evolution

IVs do not change when you evolve a Pokémon. Therefore, a 15/15/15 Teddiursa will remain a 15/15/15 Pokémon after evolving.

14 • XXL

XXL Teddiursa

XXL Teddiursa is a size classification. XXL status is separate from IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

🐻

XXL Teddiursa

An XXL Teddiursa is larger than the normal size range and is useful for collectors and size-based Pokédex goals.

📏

Size Classification

XXL status does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has better IVs or higher battle performance.

Can XXL Teddiursa Be 100% IV?

Yes. Size classification and IVs are separate properties. Therefore, an XXL Teddiursa can also be a 100% IV specimen.

Collector Combination

An XXL + Shiny + 100% Teddiursa would be an exceptionally desirable collector combination.

15 • XXS

XXS Teddiursa

XXS Teddiursa represents the opposite end of the size spectrum. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of the Pokémon's IV combination.

🧸

XXS Teddiursa

An XXS Teddiursa is classified as exceptionally small and can be valuable for players completing size-based collections.

📐

Independent of IVs

A tiny Teddiursa can have excellent IVs, poor IVs, Shiny status, Lucky status or Shadow status.

Can XXS Teddiursa Be a Hundo?

Yes. XXS and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXS Teddiursa can theoretically also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Teddiursa FAQ

What type is Teddiursa?

Teddiursa is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Teddiursa weak to?

Teddiursa is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Shiny Teddiursa available?

Yes. Shiny Teddiursa is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Can Teddiursa be Shadow?

Shadow Teddiursa has been available through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting.

Can Shadow Teddiursa be purified?

An eligible Shadow Teddiursa can be purified using Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

What is a Lucky Teddiursa?

Lucky Teddiursa is a Teddiursa that has received the Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Teddiursa?

A 100% Teddiursa has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Can Teddiursa be XXL?

Yes. Teddiursa can receive a size classification such as XXL when the relevant Pokémon GO size mechanics apply.

Can Teddiursa be XXS?

Yes. XXS is the extra-small size classification.

Can XXL Teddiursa be 100% IV?

Yes. Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics.

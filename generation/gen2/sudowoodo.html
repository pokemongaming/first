Sudowoodo

Sudowoodo is a Rock-type Pokémon from the Johto region. Despite its appearance resembling a tree, Sudowoodo is not a Grass-type Pokémon. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily known as a Rock-type Pokémon with access to different special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Sudowoodo Overview

Why Sudowoodo Is Not a Grass Type

Sudowoodo is designed to resemble a tree, but its Pokédex identity and Pokémon GO battle typing are Rock. This is important when selecting counters because Water, Grass, Ground, Fighting and Steel attacks are effective against Rock-type Pokémon.

Azumarill

Azumarill is a Water/Fairy-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is especially well known for its role in Great League PvP, where its combination of bulk, typing and charged-move coverage makes it a long-standing defensive and strategic option.

09 — PVP

Azumarill PvP Guide

PvP is where Azumarill becomes particularly interesting. Its high bulk, Water/Fairy typing and broad charged-move coverage allow it to fit several Great League team structures.

Great League

Great League has traditionally been Azumarill's most recognizable PvP environment. Its bulk allows it to survive extended battles while its charged moves can pressure a wide variety of opponents.

Common PvP Concept

Bubble + Ice Beam + Play Rough gives Azumarill Water pressure together with Ice and Fairy coverage.

Ultra League

Azumarill's low maximum CP makes Ultra League a much less natural environment for it than Great League. Always check the current league rules before investing resources for a specific format.

Master League

Azumarill's maximum CP is far below the level normally required for competitive Master League play, making it primarily a CP-limited PvP Pokémon.

Is Lucky Azumarill Good for PvP?

Lucky status itself does not guarantee the best PvP IV spread. Because Lucky Pokémon have minimum IV floors, players interested in Great League optimization should evaluate the resulting CP and IV distribution rather than assuming every Lucky Azumarill is PvP-perfect.

Is Lucky Azumarill Worth Keeping?

Yes, especially if you enjoy collecting special forms, want a lower Stardust investment or obtain a particularly useful IV combination through a trade.

14 — 100% IV

100% Azumarill — Hundo Guide

A 100% Azumarill has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo or perfect-IV Pokémon.

15 / 15 / 15
Perfect IV Azumarill

Attack 15 • Defense 15 • HP 15

Is Hundo Azumarill Best for Great League?

Not necessarily. Great League uses a CP limit, so a lower Attack IV can sometimes allow a Pokémon to reach a higher level while staying under the limit. This means the traditional 15/15/15 Hundo is not automatically the highest-ranked PvP specimen.

Why Keep a Hundo Azumarill?

  • Excellent collector milestone.
  • Useful for building a complete 100% IV collection.
  • Can be valuable for formats without the same CP optimization constraints.
  • Easy to identify as a perfect IV specimen.
15 — XXL

XXL Azumarill

XXL Azumarill is a size variant. XXL status does not automatically mean that Azumarill has better battle stats or higher IVs.

Should You Keep an XXL Azumarill?

XXL Pokémon are particularly interesting for collectors and size-focused Pokédex completion. If you enjoy collecting unusual variants, an XXL Azumarill is worth keeping even if its IVs are not perfect.

Important

XXL classification is separate from IV quality. An XXL Azumarill can have excellent, average or poor IVs.

Crobat

Crobat is a Poison- and Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region and the final stage of the Zubat evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Crobat is more notable for its flexible PvP moveset and collection variants than for top-tier raid damage.

09 • PVP

Crobat PvP Guide

Crobat can be used in multiple PvP formats, although its performance varies considerably between leagues.

Great League

Crobat can use Air Slash, Poison Fang and Shadow Ball in Great League. A lower-Attack, higher-Defense and higher-HP IV spread can be preferred for CP-limited PvP builds.

Great League Recommendation

Air Slash + Poison Fang + Shadow Ball

Ultra League

Crobat has more natural room to reach its level potential in Ultra League. Its Poison Fang pressure and Shadow Ball coverage can make it a situational option.

Ultra League Recommendation

Air Slash + Poison Fang + Shadow Ball

Master League

Crobat's maximum CP is much less competitive in Master League than many Legendary and high-stat Pokémon, making it a lower-priority choice for unrestricted Master League battles.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Crobat

Lucky Crobat is a special trade result rather than a separate species or permanent alternate form.

🍀

Lucky Crobat

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon. Lucky Crobat can therefore be attractive when you want to build a high-level Crobat while reducing Stardust costs.

Lucky Crobat IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor. However, a Lucky Crobat is not automatically a 100% Crobat. Always check the appraisal after a trade.

Can Crobat Be Lucky and Shiny?

Yes. A Crobat can combine special characteristics, meaning a Pokémon can be both Shiny and Lucky if obtained through the appropriate game mechanics.

16 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Crobat

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by their size. XXL and XXS are size characteristics, not different species or alternate typings.

📏 XXL Crobat Extra-large size classification

XXL Crobat is an unusually large individual. It can be useful for collection, showcases and size-related achievements.

🔬 XXS Crobat Extra-small size classification

XXS Crobat is an unusually small individual and can also be valuable to collectors and Showcase-focused players.

XXL / XXS Does Not Mean Better IVs

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Crobat is not automatically stronger than a normal Crobat, and an XXS Crobat is not automatically weaker.

Can Crobat Be XXL and Hundo?

Special characteristics can coexist. Therefore, a Crobat can potentially be valuable because of both its size and its IV appraisal.

Spinarak

Spinarak is a Bug and Poison-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily useful as a Pokédex, evolution and collection Pokémon, with additional interest for Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXS, XXL and high-IV collectors.

01 — OVERVIEW

Spinarak Overview

Spinarak is a small Bug and Poison-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Ariados after collecting the required Candy.

Spinarak is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker, but it remains useful for Pokédex completion, evolution goals, Shiny hunting, Lucky trading and size-variant collecting.

02 — TYPE

Spinarak Type

Spinarak has a dual Bug and Poison typing. This combination determines its offensive options as well as the types of attacks that are effective against it.

08 — IV GUIDE

Spinarak IV Guide

Individual Values add points to a Pokémon's Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Spinarak is considered a perfect IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect 100% Spinarak

A 15/15/15 Spinarak is known by Pokémon GO players as a 100% or hundo Spinarak. Because Spinarak has limited combat value, the main reason to keep a hundo is usually collection, evolution or personal preference.

09 — PVP

Spinarak PvP Guide

Spinarak is not a standard high-priority PvP investment. Its low stat product and CP ceiling make it much less attractive than many other Pokémon available for competitive formats.

Great League

Spinarak can theoretically participate in CP-limited formats when eligible, but players should compare its actual battle performance with stronger Bug- and Poison-type options before investing resources.

Ultra League

Its relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment particularly unattractive for a standard Spinarak.

Master League

Spinarak is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP and overall stats are far below the level normally required for the format.

Should You Power Up Shadow Spinarak?

For most players, Shadow Spinarak is more interesting as a collection or evolution project than as a serious combat investment. Consider your existing Pokémon before spending large amounts of Stardust.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Attack Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration is tied to specific Pokémon GO events and mechanics.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Spinarak

Lucky Spinarak is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

✨ LUCKY SPINARAK

Why Keep a Lucky Spinarak?

Lucky Spinarak can be a fun collection piece, especially for players collecting Lucky Pokémon from different generations.

Lucky Spinarak Benefits

  • Requires less Stardust to power up.
  • Has a special Lucky background in the game.
  • Can be combined with other collection goals.
  • Can be evolved into a Lucky Ariados.
  • Can be valuable as a trading collection piece.
15 — 100% IV

100% Spinarak — Hundo Guide

A 100% Spinarak has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Players commonly call this combination a "hundo."

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP

Is a Hundo Spinarak Worth Keeping?

Absolutely, if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon. A 100% Spinarak can also be evolved into a 100% Ariados. Whether it deserves Stardust investment is a separate question and depends on your personal goals.

Collector Tip

If you have limited storage space, compare a hundo Spinarak against your other perfect-IV Pokémon before transferring it.

22 — FAQ

Spinarak FAQ

What type is Spinarak?

Spinarak is a Bug and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Spinarak weak to?

Spinarak is vulnerable to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What is Spinarak's max CP?

Spinarak has a relatively low maximum CP compared with fully evolved and Legendary Pokémon. Your exact displayed CP depends on level and IVs.

Is Shiny Spinarak available?

Shiny Spinarak is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Spinarak available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current in-game schedule for availability.

What is a 100% Spinarak?

A 100% Spinarak has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a hundo Spinarak?

"Hundo" is the community term commonly used for a Pokémon with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is Lucky Spinarak?

Lucky Spinarak is a Spinarak that has received the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

Is Lucky Spinarak cheaper to power up?

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up compared with ordinary Pokémon.

What is XXL Spinarak?

XXL Spinarak is an unusually large Spinarak that belongs to the XXL size category.

What is XXS Spinarak?

XXS Spinarak is an unusually small Spinarak that belongs to the XXS size category.

Is XXL Spinarak stronger?

XXL status is primarily a size classification. It should not be treated as a guarantee of better battle performance.

Is XXS Spinarak weaker?

XXS status is a size classification and does not automatically mean the Pokémon is weaker.

Does Spinarak evolve?

Yes. Spinarak evolves into Ariados.

Is Spinarak good for raids?

Spinarak is generally not a recommended high-level raid attacker because of its relatively low stats.

Is Spinarak good for PvP?

Spinarak is generally a low-priority PvP Pokémon. Always check current cup rules and rankings before investing resources.

Should I evolve my 100% Spinarak?

If your goal is to have a perfect Ariados, evolving a 100% Spinarak makes sense. If you prefer collecting perfect Pokémon in their original forms, you may choose to keep it unevolved.

Should I purify Shadow Spinarak?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the value of keeping the Shadow Pokémon with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

Can Shiny Spinarak be Lucky?

A Shiny Pokémon can also have the Lucky status when obtained through a qualifying trade.

Can Spinarak be both XXL and Shiny?

Different Pokémon characteristics can coexist, so collectors may encounter combinations of appearance, size and other status attributes.

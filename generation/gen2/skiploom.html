Skiploom

Skiploom is a Grass/Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region and the evolved form of Hoppip. In Pokémon GO, Skiploom is primarily a Pokédex, collection and evolution Pokémon rather than a top-tier raid attacker.

01 • OVERVIEW

Skiploom Overview

Skiploom is the second stage of the Hoppip family. It evolves from Hoppip and can later evolve into Jumpluff. Its relatively low CP and offensive stats mean that Skiploom is not normally chosen as a primary raid attacker, but it remains useful for Pokédex completion, collecting, evolution projects and certain limited PvP situations.

Cleffa

01 • OVERVIEW

Cleffa Pokémon GO Overview

Cleffa is a Fairy-type Baby Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Clefairy using Cleffa Candy.

Unlike many fully evolved Pokémon, Cleffa is not generally chosen for high-level raid damage. Its main appeal comes from collecting, evolution, Shiny hunting, Lucky trades, IV hunting and size variants.

09 • PVP

Cleffa PvP Guide

Great League

Cleffa's low CP ceiling means it is not a conventional Great League choice. Its pre-evolution status and limited battle statistics make it substantially less practical than Clefairy or Clefable.

Ultra League

Master League

Cleffa is not suitable for conventional Master League competition because of its very low CP ceiling.

12 • SHADOW

Shadow Cleffa

Shadow Cleffa is the Shadow variant of Cleffa. Shadow Pokémon have a distinct battle profile and should be evaluated separately from ordinary Cleffa.

Shadow Cleffa

Shadow status increases the Pokémon's offensive potential while reducing its defensive durability. Cleffa's low base performance means this is primarily a collector and evolution consideration.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Cleffa

Lucky Cleffa is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special visual treatment and require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Cleffa

A Lucky Cleffa can be an excellent collection piece, particularly when combined with strong IVs, Shiny status or rare size characteristics.

Why Keep a Lucky Cleffa?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky status is useful for collection goals.
  • A Lucky Shiny Cleffa can be particularly desirable.
  • Check IVs after receiving a traded Cleffa.
  • Consider keeping rare combinations such as Lucky + Shiny + high IV.

23 • FAQ

Cleffa FAQ

What type is Cleffa?

Cleffa is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Cleffa weak to?

Cleffa is weak to Poison and Steel attacks.

Does Cleffa evolve?

Yes. Cleffa evolves into Clefairy and Clefairy can evolve into Clefable.

Is Shiny Cleffa available?

Shiny Cleffa is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Cleffa?

A 100% Cleffa has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Cleffa?

Lucky Cleffa is a traded Cleffa that receives Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is Shadow Cleffa?

Shadow Cleffa is the Shadow variant of Cleffa and has the battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Can Shadow Cleffa be purified?

An eligible Shadow Cleffa can be purified, subject to the game's current purification mechanics.

What is XXL Cleffa?

XXL Cleffa is a Cleffa classified as an extra-large specimen by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Cleffa?

XXS Cleffa is a Cleffa classified as an extra-small specimen by Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Cleffa good for raids?

Cleffa is not considered a high-level raid attacker. Its primary value is collection, evolution and special variants.

Should I evolve my 100% Cleffa?

That depends on your goals. A perfect Cleffa can be kept unevolved as a collection piece or evolved through its evolutionary family.

Can a Shiny Cleffa be evolved?

Yes. Its Shiny status remains through evolution.

Can Cleffa be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon can combine multiple attributes, so a traded Shiny Cleffa can become a Lucky Pokémon.

Can Cleffa be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Cleffa can also have perfect IVs.

Can Cleffa be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size classification and Shiny status are separate attributes.

01 / OVERVIEW

Corsola Overview

Corsola is a dual Water/Rock-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. Unlike many Pokémon, Corsola does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Its relatively modest CP ceiling means that Corsola is generally more interesting as a Pokédex, Shiny, Lucky, regional and collection target than as a top-tier raid attacker.

13 / LUCKY

Lucky Corsola

Lucky Corsola is created through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon are particularly appealing to collectors because they have a special Lucky status and can be cheaper to power up.

🍀
Lucky Corsola

A Lucky Corsola is a great collection target, especially when combined with Shiny, high-IV or regional collection goals.

Why Keep a Lucky Corsola?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky trades can produce useful IV combinations.
  • Lucky Corsola is valuable for collection-focused players.
  • A Lucky Pokémon can also be Shiny if the traded Pokémon is Shiny.
  • A Lucky Corsola with strong IVs can be a long-term collector piece.

Lucky Corsola IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. A Lucky Corsola is therefore particularly interesting when you are searching for a strong specimen through trading.

23 / FAQ

Corsola FAQ

What type is Corsola?

Corsola is a Water/Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Corsola weak to?

Corsola is vulnerable to Electric, Grass, Fighting and Ground attacks.

Is Shiny Corsola available?

Yes. Shiny Corsola is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Corsola a good raid attacker?

Corsola is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack stat.

Is Corsola good for PvP?

Corsola can have value in specific PvP formats, but its usefulness depends on the current rules, moveset and meta.

What is a 100% Corsola?

A 100% Corsola has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Corsola?

XXL Corsola is an extra-large size variant. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is XXS Corsola?

XXS Corsola is an extra-small size variant. An XXS Corsola can have any IV combination.

Can Corsola be Lucky?

Yes. Corsola can become Lucky through eligible Pokémon GO trades.

Is Lucky Corsola worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky status is valuable for collectors and can reduce the Stardust cost of powering up the Pokémon.

Is Shadow Corsola available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket content and rotations. Check current game data before claiming it is obtainable.

Should I purify Shadow Corsola?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions and consider your collection and battle goals first.

Can Corsola be Shiny and Lucky at the same time?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate Pokémon characteristics, so a traded Shiny Corsola can potentially become Lucky.

Can Corsola be 100% and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so a Corsola can have perfect IVs while also being XXL.

Can Corsola be 100% and XXS?

Yes. A perfect 15/15/15 Corsola can also have XXS size status.

Does Corsola evolve?

Corsola does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

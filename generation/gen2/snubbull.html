Snubbull FAQ

What type is Snubbull?

Snubbull is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Snubbull weak to?

Snubbull is weak to Poison- and Steel-type attacks.

What does Snubbull evolve into?

Snubbull evolves into Granbull using 50 Candy.

What is Snubbull's max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Snubbull reaches 1,399 CP.

Is Shiny Snubbull available?

Yes. Shiny Snubbull is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Snubbull available?

Yes. Shadow Snubbull exists as a special Shadow variant associated with Pokémon GO's Shadow encounter system.

Can Shadow Snubbull be purified?

Yes. A Shadow Snubbull can be purified when eligible.

What is a 100% Snubbull?

A 100% Snubbull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

What CP is a hundo Snubbull?

A Level 50 15/15/15 Snubbull has 1,399 CP. A hundo from a Level 20 raid or egg is 707 CP, while a weather-boosted Level 25 hundo is 884 CP.

What is Lucky Snubbull?

Lucky Snubbull is obtained through trading and has a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is XXS Snubbull?

XXS Snubbull is a Snubbull whose measured size falls within Pokémon GO's XXS size category.

What is XXL Snubbull?

XXL Snubbull is a Snubbull whose measured size falls within Pokémon GO's largest size category.

Does size affect Snubbull IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Snubbull can have any IV combination.

Is Snubbull good for raids?

Snubbull is generally not a top raid attacker. Its main value is evolution, collection and special-form hunting.

Is Snubbull useful in PvP?

Snubbull has limited PvP relevance compared with stronger Pokémon, although special limited formats can change its usefulness.

Furret Overview

Furret is the evolution of Sentret and is known for its long, flexible body. It was introduced in Generation 2 alongside the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, Furret is more relevant as a collection target, evolution entry and occasional PvP curiosity than as a high-end raid attacker.

Why Furret's Typing Matters

Furret has a pure Normal typing. This gives it only one type weakness in Pokémon GO: Fighting. Its Normal typing also makes it useful as a simple, straightforward Pokémon for collection and certain lower-level battle situations.

Furret Weaknesses & Resistances

Weakness

🥊
Fighting

Fighting-type attacks deal super-effective damage to Furret.

Resistance

👻
Ghost

Furret's Normal typing gives it immunity-level protection against Ghost-type attacks in Pokémon GO.

Normal-type Pokémon have a simple matchup profile: Fighting attacks are the main threat, while Ghost attacks are especially ineffective against Furret.

Furret Attack, Defense & HP

Furret's stat distribution gives it a relatively high HP value compared with its Attack and Defense. However, its overall CP ceiling keeps it well below the strongest Pokémon used for high-level PvE content.

What Counters Furret?

Because Furret is a Normal-type Pokémon, Fighting-type attackers are the natural counter category.

100% IV Furret

A perfect Furret has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A 15/15/15 Furret is commonly described as a 100% IV or hundo Furret.

Is 100% IV Furret Worth Keeping?

A perfect Furret can be an excellent collection piece, especially for players who collect hundos from every Pokémon species. Its practical battle value is more limited than that of stronger Normal-type Pokémon.

Shadow Furret IVs

Shadow Furret does not need to have perfect IVs to be worth keeping. Players interested in Shadow collections may value the Shadow status itself.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace it.

Purified Furret

Purified Furret is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Furret. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and other mechanics.

🌑

Shadow Furret

Retains the Shadow state and its associated battle characteristics.

✨

Purified Furret

Purification removes the Shadow status and provides the benefits associated with purification.

Should You Purify Furret?

There is no universal reason to purify every Shadow Furret. Collectors may prefer keeping the original Shadow form, while other players may prefer the purified version. Compare the two before committing to purification.

Lucky Furret

Lucky Furret is a Furret obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have a special appearance in the Pokémon storage system and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Furret

A Lucky Furret is particularly attractive to collectors who enjoy building complete Lucky Pokédex collections.

💫

Lucky IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor, making Lucky Trades useful when searching for stronger specimens.

Is Lucky Furret Worth Trading?

If you are completing a Lucky Pokédex, Furret can be a fun target for a Lucky Trade. For practical battle investment, however, stronger Pokémon generally provide more value.

XXL Furret

XXL Furret is a Furret that qualifies for the game's extra-large size category. Size variants are independent of IV quality.

📏 XXL Furret

An extra-large Furret that can be valuable to collectors who track size records and showcase Pokémon.

🏆 Collection Value

An XXL Furret can be kept as a size-focused collectible, regardless of whether its IVs are perfect.

XXL status and IV percentage are separate attributes. An XXL Furret can have low, average or perfect IVs.

XXS Furret

XXS Furret is the extra-small size category of Furret. Like XXL Pokémon, size is separate from IVs.

📐 XXS Furret

An extra-small Furret can be an interesting collectible for players who enjoy size variants.

🎯 Size Collection

Collectors may choose to keep both XXL and XXS Furret to complete their size collection.

Furret PvP Guide

Furret can be experimented with in lower CP formats, although it is not generally regarded as a premier PvP Pokémon.

Great League

Furret's CP ceiling makes Great League the most natural place to experiment with it. Its actual effectiveness depends heavily on its current moveset and the evolving PvP metagame.

Ultra League

Furret can be tested in Ultra League-style formats, but its overall stats make it difficult to compete with many specialized Pokémon.

Master League

Furret's maximum CP is far too low for it to be considered a serious Master League investment.

Furret FAQ

What type is Furret?

Furret is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Furret weak to?

Furret is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Furret resistant to?

Furret has immunity-level protection against Ghost-type attacks because it is a Normal-type Pokémon.

Does Furret evolve?

Furret is the final evolution of Sentret and does not evolve further.

Is Shiny Furret available?

Shiny Furret is an available alternate-color form when obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Furret available?

A 100% Furret has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

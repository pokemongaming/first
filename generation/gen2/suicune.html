Suicune is a Legendary Water-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its excellent bulk, Legendary status and several collectible forms including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

Suicune Overview

Suicune is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It is a pure Water-type Pokémon and is one of the three Legendary beasts of the Johto region alongside Raikou and Entei.

✨ Shiny Suicune

Shiny Suicune is an alternate-colour version of regular Suicune. The Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not change Suicune's base species stats.

Shiny Suicune Collection Guide

A Shiny Suicune can still have different IVs, levels and size classifications. Shiny status and battle IV quality are separate characteristics.

  • Shiny status does not automatically mean perfect IVs.
  • A Shiny Suicune can have different size variants.
  • A high-IV Shiny can be an excellent collector project.
  • Shiny availability depends on eligible encounters.

🌑 Shadow Suicune

Shadow Suicune is the Shadow version of Suicune. It retains the Water typing of the base Pokémon while using Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade durability for greater offensive pressure. This means Shadow Suicune should be evaluated separately from a regular Suicune.

✨ Purified Suicune

Purified Suicune is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Suicune. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and follows Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

Purified vs Shadow

Purification should be considered carefully. A player focused on Shadow battle performance may prefer to keep the Shadow version, while collectors or other players may prefer a Purified specimen.

  • Check IV changes before deciding.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified goals.
  • Consider raid and PvP usage.
  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Check current Pokémon GO mechanics.

🍀 Lucky Suicune

Lucky Suicune is obtained when an eligible Pokémon trade results in Lucky status.

Why Lucky Suicune Is Valuable

Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for long-term investment because the Lucky status can reduce the Stardust required for powering up.

Lucky Suicune Investment Checklist

  • Check the IV combination after the trade.
  • Compare the Lucky Suicune with your existing one.
  • Consider Stardust savings.
  • Choose the moveset before major investment.
  • Keep valuable Legendary Lucky Pokémon organised.

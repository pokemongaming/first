13 • LUCKY

Lucky Misdreavous

Lucky Misdreavous is obtained through Pokémon trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up.

Is Lucky Misdreavous Worth Keeping?

A Lucky Misdreavous can be an attractive collector Pokémon and a useful low-cost candidate for evolution. If its IVs are high, it becomes even more desirable.

Lucky Trading Tip

Check the IVs immediately after a trade. A Lucky Pokémon is guaranteed to meet the Lucky minimum IV floor, but it is not automatically a 100% IV Pokémon.

14 • SIZE

XXL Misdreavous

XXL Misdreavous is an unusually large specimen. Size classifications are separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

📏
XXL Misdreavous A very large Misdreavous that can be kept as a size-based collection target.

XXL vs IVs

Being XXL does not mean that Misdreavous has perfect IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. A player can therefore encounter an XXL Misdreavous that is low IV, high IV or even 100% IV.

15 • SIZE

XXS Misdreavous

XXS Misdreavous is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, its size is independent of its IV percentage.

🔎
XXS Misdreavous A very small Misdreavous suitable for players completing size-based collection goals.

XXS Misdreavous Collection Tip

Keep unusual size specimens if you enjoy completing Pokédex and collection milestones. An XXS Misdreavous can also be combined with other desirable traits such as Shiny or high IVs.

16 • PVP

Misdreavous PvP Guide

Misdreavous can be considered for specialized PvP situations, although its overall usefulness should be judged against the current Pokémon GO move pool and available alternatives.

Great League

Misdreavous fits naturally within the CP range of Great League, making this the most accessible league for testing it. However, moveset quality, IV distribution and the current meta should all be considered before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Misdreavous can be evaluated in Ultra League, but its relatively low maximum CP means it may require substantial level investment to approach the format's upper limits.

Master League

Misdreavous is generally not a natural Master League investment because of its lower CP ceiling compared with fully evolved and Legendary Pokémon.

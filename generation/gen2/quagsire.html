JOHTO WATER / GROUND POKÉMON

Quagsire

Quagsire is a Water and Ground-type Pokémon from the Johto region. Its unusual typing gives it only one major type weakness while also making it immune to Electric-type attacks. In Pokémon GO, Quagsire is especially known for its PvP potential and its access to powerful Ground-type charged moves.

Quagsire Overview

Quagsire evolves from Wooper and is one of the most recognizable Water / Ground-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its typing removes the usual Electric weakness associated with many Water Pokémon.

Although Quagsire does not have extremely high raid-oriented stats, its bulk, typing and moveset can make it particularly useful in certain PvP formats.

Why Quagsire's Typing Is Important

Water / Ground is one of Quagsire's defining advantages. The Ground typing makes Quagsire immune to Electric-type damage while Water helps it resist several other attack categories.

Important Typing Advantage

Quagsire has no weakness to Electric attacks and instead has a single major double weakness to Grass-type damage.

Quagsire Forms & Special Versions

The following versions represent different collection, battle or cosmetic variations of Quagsire in Pokémon GO.

💧 Regular Quagsire

The standard version of Quagsire. It is the base form used for most PvP and collection builds.

✨ Shiny Quagsire

Shiny Quagsire has alternate coloration while retaining the same underlying battle mechanics and stats as regular Quagsire.

🌑 Shadow Quagsire

Shadow Quagsire has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability compared with its regular version.

✨ Purified Quagsire

Purified Quagsire is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Quagsire.

🍀 Lucky Quagsire

Lucky Quagsire is valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

💯 100% Quagsire

A 100% Quagsire has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

📏 XXL Quagsire

XXL Quagsire is a size-based collectible version with an unusually large recorded size.

🔹 XXS Quagsire

XXS Quagsire is an unusually small size variant for collectors.

Purified Quagsire

Purified Quagsire is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Quagsire. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can alter its IVs.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Purified Pokémon have different investment considerations.
  • IVs may increase after purification.
  • Compare the result before purifying a strong Shadow specimen.
  • Consider both PvP and collection goals.

Stantler Overview

Stantler is a Normal-type Pokémon from the Johto region. It has a distinctive deer-like appearance and is associated with the Stantler family that includes Wyrdeer. In Pokémon GO, regular Stantler has moderate stats and limited PvE usefulness, but its Shiny, Shadow, Lucky and size variants make it an interesting Pokémon for collectors.

What Does Normal Typing Mean?

Normal-type Pokémon have a relatively simple defensive profile in Pokémon GO. Stantler's main weakness is Fighting-type damage. Normal-type attacks are also useful in neutral situations but do not provide a natural super-effective matchup against most raid bosses.

Battle tip:

When fighting Stantler, prioritize strong Fighting-type attackers. Ghost-type attacks should generally be avoided because of the Normal-type immunity interaction.

For most players, Stantler should be powered up for collection, personal preference or specific limited-use goals rather than because it is a top raid attacker.

Shiny Stantler

Shiny Stantler is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny form has a different coloration from the standard Stantler.

✨

Shiny Stantler

Shiny Stantler is primarily a collector-focused variant. Its Shiny status does not automatically increase its combat stats.

Shiny Stantler IVs

A Shiny Stantler can have any IV combination allowed by its encounter method. A Shiny 15/15/15 Stantler is therefore both a Shiny and a perfect-IV specimen.

  • Shiny status does not change the Pokémon's species.
  • Shiny status does not provide a combat-stat bonus.
  • Shiny Stantler can also be Lucky.
  • Shiny Stantler can also be 100% IV.

    • Shadow Stantler

    Shadow Stantler CP

    Shadow Stantler can reach 2,447 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs. Encounter CP ranges depend on the encounter source and whether the Pokémon is weather boosted.

    Frustration reminder:

    Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Team GO Rocket events can provide special opportunities to replace Frustration, so check current game events before using resources.

    Purified Stantler

    Purified Stantler is created by purifying a Shadow Stantler. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and removes the Shadow mechanics.

    💎

    Purified Stantler

    Purified Stantler is no longer a Shadow Pokémon. It can also have the Purified-exclusive Charged Move Return depending on the current game mechanics.

    Do not purify a Shadow Stantler automatically. If you prefer collecting Shadow Pokémon or want to keep its Shadow battle characteristics, purification may not be the right choice.

    Lucky Stantler

    Lucky Stantler is a special traded version of Stantler. Lucky status is not a separate species or permanent form, but it provides important resource benefits when powering up the Pokémon.

    🍀
    Lucky Stantler

    Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. Lucky status can therefore make a Stantler cheaper to build than a normal Stantler.

    100% IV Stantler

    A 100% IV Stantler has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a Hundo.

    100% Stantler CP

    A Level 50 15/15/15 Stantler reaches 2,447 CP. At Level 40, a perfect Stantler reaches 2,164 CP.

    Is a Hundo Stantler Worth Powering Up?

    For most players, a Hundo Stantler is more valuable as a collector's specimen than as a competitive raid investment. If you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon, however, a 100% Stantler is an excellent addition to your Pokédex collection.

    XXL Stantler

    XXL Stantler is a size category rather than a separate Pokémon form. Large-size Pokémon can be useful for collectors and showcase competitions.

    🦌 XXL Stantler

    2.10 m – 2.45 m

    Current Stantler size data lists XXL Stantler within the upper size range, reaching up to approximately 2.45 metres.

    XXL Stantler and IVs

    Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Stantler can have low IVs, average IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

  • XXL does not automatically mean 100% IV.
  • XXL does not provide a standard combat-stat bonus.
  • XXL Stantler can be Shiny.
  • XXL Stantler can be Lucky.
  • XXL Stantler can also be Shadow where applicable.

    • XXS Stantler

    XXS Stantler is the smallest size category. Like XXL, XXS is an individual size characteristic rather than a different species or form.

    🔹 XXS Stantler

    0.686 m – 0.70 m

    Current Stantler size data places XXS Stantler at approximately 0.686 to 0.70 metres.

    XXS Stantler Collection Value

    XXS Pokémon can be desirable for collectors, especially when combined with other rare characteristics such as Shiny, Lucky or perfect IVs.

    Collector Combination

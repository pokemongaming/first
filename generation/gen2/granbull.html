Granbull

Granbull is a Fairy-type Pokémon from the Johto region. It evolves from Snubbull and is known in Pokémon GO for its strong Attack stat, Fairy-type damage potential and multiple collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Granbull.

Is a 100% Granbull Worth Keeping?

Yes. A perfect Granbull is valuable for collectors and can be a good long-term candidate for a Fairy-type build. However, a lower IV Granbull can still perform well depending on its level, moveset and intended use.

Shadow Granbull

Shadow Granbull is the Shadow variant of Granbull. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile, making them a separate investment decision from regular Granbull.

🌑 Shadow Granbull

Shadow Granbull can be attractive for players focused on offensive damage. The Shadow mechanic increases offensive pressure while reducing durability.

Should You Keep Shadow Granbull?

  • Check the IVs before making a long-term decision.
  • Consider Shadow power-up costs.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare Shadow Granbull with your existing Fairy attackers.
  • Do not purify automatically only for higher IVs.

Purified Granbull

Purified Granbull is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Granbull. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's long-term investment profile.

💠 Purified Granbull

Purification can increase Individual Values within Pokémon GO's purification system and provides a non-Shadow version of the Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Granbull?

The decision depends on your goals. Players focused on maximizing Shadow damage may prefer keeping Shadow Granbull, while collectors or players who want a Purified specimen may choose purification.

Lucky Granbull

Lucky Granbull is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trade mechanic. Lucky Pokémon are particularly useful for players planning to power up a Pokémon because they require less Stardust for powering up.

🍀 Lucky Granbull

A Lucky Granbull can be an excellent long-term investment if you want to build a powered-up Fairy Pokémon while reducing Stardust costs.

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Useful for long-term investment.
  • Obtained through eligible Lucky trades.
  • Can also be Shiny if the traded Granbull is Shiny.

XXL Granbull & XXS Granbull

XXL and XXS Granbull are size variants. Their size classification is primarily a collection feature and does not create a separate battle-stat species.

📏
XXL Granbull

An unusually large Granbull that may be useful for size-related collection goals and showcase competitions when eligible.

🔍
XXS Granbull

An unusually small Granbull collectible with the same species identity and standard gameplay mechanics.

Size variants should not automatically be treated as stronger or weaker in battle. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Granbull FAQ

What type is Granbull?

Granbull is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Granbull weak to?

Granbull is weak to Steel and Poison-type attacks.

Is Shiny Granbull available?

Shiny Granbull can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Granbull stronger?

Shadow Granbull has a different offensive and defensive profile, with increased offensive pressure and reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Granbull?

Not automatically. Compare your Shadow Granbull with the Purified result before deciding.

What is a Lucky Granbull?

A Lucky Granbull is obtained through the Lucky trade system and requires less Stardust for powering up.

What is a 100% Granbull?

A 100% Granbull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Granbull have better stats?

No. XXL is primarily a size classification and does not automatically mean better battle stats.

Does Granbull evolve?

Granbull evolves from Snubbull and does not currently have a further evolution.

    Lucky Sneasel

    🍀 Lucky Sneasel

    Lucky Sneasel can be attractive when you want to build a strong evolution candidate while reducing the Stardust required for powering it up.

    22 — FAQ

    Sneasel FAQ

    What type is Sneasel?

    Sneasel is a Dark and Ice-type Pokémon.

    What is Sneasel weak to?

    Sneasel is vulnerable to Fighting, Bug, Fairy, Fire, Rock and Steel attacks.

    What does Sneasel evolve into?

    Sneasel evolves into Weavile in Pokémon GO when the required evolution conditions are met.

    Is Shiny Sneasel available?

    Yes. Shiny Sneasel is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

    Is Shadow Sneasel available?

    Shadow Sneasel can be available through Team GO Rocket encounters when included in the active Shadow Pokémon rotation.

    What is a 100% Sneasel?

    A 100% Sneasel has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

    What is an XXL Sneasel?

    XXL Sneasel is an unusually large Sneasel based on Pokémon GO's size system.

    What is an XXS Sneasel?

    XXS Sneasel is an unusually small Sneasel based on Pokémon GO's size system.

    Is Lucky Sneasel worth keeping?

    Lucky Sneasel can be useful because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups and can also have collection value.

    Should I purify Shadow Sneasel?

    Not automatically. Consider the Shadow version's potential and your intended use before choosing purification.

    Should I evolve a 100% Sneasel?

    If your goal is to obtain a perfect-IV Weavile, a 100% Sneasel is an excellent evolution candidate. However, always check current Weavile performance and your available resources before investing.

    Can Sneasel be Shiny and Lucky?

    Pokémon GO characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky status can coexist when the game's mechanics allow the Pokémon to be obtained through the relevant combination of encounters or trades.

    Are XXL and XXS related to IVs?

    No. Size categories and IVs represent different characteristics. A large or small Sneasel can have any IV combination.

Why Mantine's Typing Matters

Mantine's Water/Flying combination gives it several useful resistances, but it also creates a major vulnerability to Electric-type attacks. Rock attacks are also particularly dangerous.

Mantine PvP Guide

Mantine's defensive profile and Water/Flying typing make PvP its more interesting battle role. It can pressure certain opponents while taking advantage of its useful resistances.

Great League is the most natural place to consider Mantine because of the league's CP limit. A suitable low-Attack, high-Defense and high-HP IV spread can provide additional bulk depending on the specific PvP build.

Lugia PvP Guide

Lugia's exceptional Defense, high HP and access to powerful charged attacks make it a major consideration for Master League players.

Master League

Master League is where Lugia's defensive profile becomes particularly valuable. Its bulk allows it to withstand many attacks and generate opportunities to reach charged moves.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Lugia is generally more naturally suited to Master League because of its high CP potential. Special cups and restricted formats can change its usefulness.

Shiny Lugia

Shiny Lugia for Collectors

A Shiny Lugia with strong IVs can be particularly valuable to collectors because it combines the rare coloration with a Legendary Pokémon that has strong PvP relevance.

Purified Lugia

Purified Lugia is created by purifying Shadow Lugia. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🌟

Purified Lugia

Purified Lugia no longer has the Shadow battle modifier. It also receives the standard benefits associated with Pokémon purification.

Lucky Lugia

Lucky Lugia is a special Pokémon obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky status is different from Shiny, Shadow and Purified status.

🍀

Lucky Lugia

Lucky Lugia requires less Stardust to power up than a normal Pokémon, making Lucky Legendary Pokémon attractive for long-term investment.

Why Lucky Lugia Matters

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for expensive Legendary investment projects.
  • Can be Shiny and Lucky at the same time.
  • Can be especially appealing for Master League investment.
  • Lucky status is separate from Shadow status.
Lucky Pokémon are obtained through trading and related in-game mechanics. Current Lucky trade rules should be checked before publishing detailed guarantees or thresholds.

XXL Lugia

XXL Lugia is a size variant representing a Lugia that falls into the game's extra-large size category.

📏

XXL Lugia

XXL status is a size characteristic rather than a separate species or evolution. It does not automatically make Lugia stronger in battle.

Why Keep an XXL Lugia?

  • Useful for Pokédex and collection completion.
  • Can be combined with other special characteristics.
  • Size status is independent from IVs.
  • An XXL Lugia can also have Shiny or Lucky status when the game mechanics allow the combination.

XXS Lugia

XXS Lugia is the extra-small size category for Lugia. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than a different Pokémon species.

🔹

XXS Lugia

An XXS Lugia is a particularly small specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system. Its size does not directly increase its combat stats.

Should You Keep an XXS Lugia?

Collectors who enjoy completing size variants may want to keep an XXS Lugia. Trainers focused only on battle performance should prioritize IVs, moves, level and specific battle requirements instead.

Lugia FAQ

What type is Lugia?

Lugia is a Psychic/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Lugia weak to?

Lugia is weak to Electric, Ice, Rock, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Is Lugia a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Lugia is a Legendary Pokémon from Generation 2.

Is Shiny Lugia available?

Yes. Shiny Lugia is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Lugia available?

Shadow Lugia is a special Shadow variant whose availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and event mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Lugia?

Not automatically. Purification removes the Shadow status, so compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent decision.

What is a 100% Lugia?

A 100% or Hundo Lugia has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Lugia good?

Lucky Lugia can be attractive for expensive investments because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Lugia?

XXL Lugia is a Lugia that falls into the game's extra-large size category.

What is XXS Lugia?

XXS Lugia is a Lugia that falls into the game's extra-small size category.

Is Lugia good in Master League?

Yes. Lugia's enormous defensive profile and charged moves make it an important Master League Pokémon, although its exact performance depends on the current moveset and metagame.

Is Lugia good for raids?

Lugia is primarily valued for its bulk and PvP performance rather than being one of the game's strongest pure damage attackers.

Can Lugia be Shiny and Lucky?

Certain special characteristics can coexist, so a Lugia may potentially combine Shiny status with Lucky status when obtained through eligible mechanics.

Does Lugia evolve?

No. Lugia does not have a normal evolution chain.

What Does Phanpy's Ground Typing Mean?

Phanpy's pure Ground typing determines the attacks that deal increased damage to it and the types it can resist. This makes Water, Grass and Ice particularly important when facing Phanpy.

100% IV Phanpy

A 100% IV Phanpy has perfect Individual Values in Attack, Defense and HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
Perfect IV combination: 15 / 15 / 15

A 15/15/15 Phanpy is a perfect-IV specimen. Whether it is worth powering up depends on your goals, because Phanpy's evolution and collection value may be more important than Phanpy's own battle performance.

Should You Keep a 100% Phanpy?

  • Keep it as a perfect-IV Pokédex specimen.
  • Consider evolving it into Donphan.
  • Compare its future battle usefulness before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • Perfect IVs are especially valuable for collectors.

XXL Phanpy

XXL Phanpy is a size variant that can be encountered in Pokémon GO. XXL status is independent of IV percentage and should be treated as a separate collection characteristic.

XXL XXL Phanpy

A particularly large Phanpy that can be valuable to players collecting different size categories.

COLLECTION Why Keep XXL?

XXL Pokémon can be useful for size-based collection goals and related in-game features.

XXL does not mean 100% IV.

A Phanpy can be XXL without having perfect IVs. Size and IV values are separate characteristics.

XXS Phanpy

XXS Phanpy represents the smaller end of the Pokémon's size range. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV quality.

XXS XXS Phanpy

A particularly small Phanpy that can be kept for collection and size-related goals.

COLLECTION XXS vs XXL

XXS and XXL represent different size characteristics and can be useful for completing a size-focused collection.

Phanpy FAQ

What type is Phanpy?

Phanpy is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Phanpy weak to?

Phanpy is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

What does Phanpy evolve into?

Phanpy evolves into Donphan.

Is Shiny Phanpy available?

Shiny Phanpy can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters. Availability should be checked against current game data.

What is a 100% Phanpy?

A 100% Phanpy has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Phanpy?

XXL Phanpy is a Phanpy with an especially large recorded size. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Phanpy?

XXS Phanpy is a particularly small Phanpy. Size is independent of its IV percentage.

Can Phanpy be Lucky?

Yes. Phanpy can become Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system when the trade produces a Lucky Pokémon.

What is Purified Phanpy?

Purified Phanpy is a Phanpy that has been purified from its Shadow state through the game's purification mechanic.

Is Phanpy good for raids?

Phanpy is generally not a top-tier Ground raid attacker. Its evolution into Donphan is more relevant for battle investment.

Should I evolve a 100% Phanpy?

If your goal is to own a perfect-IV Donphan, a 100% Phanpy is an excellent evolution candidate. If you are collecting Phanpy itself, you may prefer to keep it unevolved.

Can a Shiny Phanpy evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Phanpy can retain its Shiny identity when evolved into its eligible evolution.

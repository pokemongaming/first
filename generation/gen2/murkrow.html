Murkrow Overview

Murkrow is a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. It is #198 in the National Pokédex and is associated with the Johto region.

Murkrow can evolve into Honchkrow in Pokémon GO. Unlike many Pokémon that evolve only with Candy, Murkrow requires both Candy and a Sinnoh Stone for its evolution.

  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions first.

  • Check its IVs before making the decision.

Purified Murkrow

Purified Murkrow is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Murkrow. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can improve its IVs.

PURIFIED

✨ Purified Murkrow

XXL Murkrow

XXL Murkrow is an unusually large size variant. Size is separate from IVs, CP and Shiny status, so a large Murkrow does not automatically have better battle stats.

📏 XXL Murkrow

An exceptionally large Murkrow size. XXL classification is based on the Pokémon's size measurements rather than its IV percentage.

XXL + Shiny

A Murkrow can combine special characteristics, meaning a player may encounter a Shiny Murkrow that also has an unusual size.

XXL Murkrow and IVs

XXL status does not mean 100% IV. A large Murkrow can have any valid IV combination.

XXS Murkrow

XXS Murkrow is an unusually small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of IVs and CP.

📐 XXS Murkrow

An exceptionally small Murkrow that can be collected as a size variant.

XXS + Shiny

A Murkrow can have multiple collectible characteristics at the same time, such as Shiny status and an XXS size.

XXS Murkrow and IVs

XXS classification does not indicate perfect IVs. Always inspect the actual Appraisal screen when evaluating IV quality.

Murkrow PvP Guide

Murkrow's relatively low maximum CP means it is not a natural choice for Master League. Its performance should also be distinguished from Honchkrow, which has a different battle profile.

Great League

Murkrow can be considered in limited PvP situations, particularly for players experimenting with unusual or themed teams. Its low Defense makes it vulnerable to heavy incoming damage.

Ultra League

Murkrow is generally less attractive in Ultra League because its overall stat product and CP ceiling make it difficult to compete with stronger Pokémon.

Master League

Standard Murkrow is not generally suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the level of Pokémon normally used in that format.

Murkrow FAQ

What type is Murkrow?

Murkrow is a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Murkrow weak to?

Murkrow is weak to Electric, Fairy, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Does Murkrow evolve?

Yes. Murkrow evolves into Honchkrow using 100 Murkrow Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Murkrow available?

Yes. Shiny Murkrow is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is 100% Murkrow?

A 100% Murkrow has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Murkrow?

Shadow Murkrow is the Shadow variant of Murkrow. It retains its Dark/Flying typing while having the standard Shadow Pokémon battle characteristics.

Should I purify Shadow Murkrow?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow's IVs, moves, collection value and your plans for evolving it before deciding.

What is Lucky Murkrow?

Lucky Murkrow is obtained through trading and has the special Lucky status, including reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Murkrow?

XXL Murkrow is an unusually large size variant. XXL status is independent of IVs.

What is XXS Murkrow?

XXS Murkrow is an unusually small size variant. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

Can Shiny Murkrow evolve?

Yes. Shiny Murkrow can evolve into Shiny Honchkrow.

Can Lucky Murkrow evolve?

Yes. A Lucky Murkrow can evolve into a Lucky Honchkrow.

Can Shadow Murkrow evolve?

Shadow Pokémon that are eligible for evolution can retain their Shadow status after evolving.

Is Murkrow good for raids?

Murkrow is generally more valuable as an evolution and collection Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker.

Is Murkrow good in PvP?

Murkrow has limited PvP applications compared with stronger Pokémon, and its low Defense can make it difficult to use consistently.

Flaaffy Overview

Flaaffy is an Electric-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves from Mareep and can evolve into Ampharos. Flaaffy is primarily an evolution-stage Pokémon, although collectors may want to keep special versions such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified or Lucky Flaaffy.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Hitmontop

Lucky Hitmontop is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background effect and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

🍀 Lucky Hitmontop

A Lucky Hitmontop can be an attractive collection Pokémon because it combines the Lucky status with Hitmontop's distinctive species identity.

Lucky Hitmontop Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Distinctive Lucky status.
  • Guaranteed minimum IV floor from Lucky trades.
  • Useful collector and trade target.
  • Can also be Shiny if the traded Pokémon is Shiny.

Lucky Hitmontop IVs

Lucky Pokémon received from a Lucky Trade have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. A Lucky Hitmontop can therefore be an appealing candidate for collection or investment.

Hitmontop Evolution

Hitmontop evolves from Tyrogue. Unlike normal linear evolution families, Tyrogue can evolve into Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan or Hitmontop depending on its Attack and Defense statistics when it evolves.

Bayleef IV Guide

What Does 100% Bayleef Mean?

A 100% Bayleef means the Pokémon has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. The term "100%" describes IV quality rather than the Pokémon's level.

100% Bayleef

A perfect 15/15/15 Bayleef is a valuable collection target, especially for players who keep perfect-IV entries for every evolutionary family.

09 • PVP

Bayleef PvP Guide

Bayleef is not a standard top-tier PvP choice, but middle-evolution Pokémon can occasionally have niche value in restricted formats.

Great League

Bayleef's lower maximum CP allows it to fit more naturally into Great League than Master League. However, its actual usefulness depends heavily on the current moveset and cup rules.

Ultra League

Bayleef generally has less natural relevance in Ultra League because its overall stats and CP ceiling are not designed around that environment.

Master League

Bayleef is generally not a practical Master League investment because its CP and overall battle profile are substantially below the Pokémon normally used in this format.

PvP rankings change as moves, balance updates and Cup formats change. Always check current PvP data before making a competitive recommendation.

11 • SHINY

Shiny Bayleef

Shiny Bayleef is the alternate-color version of Bayleef. Shiny status is independent of IVs, level and Lucky status.

✨
SHINY BAYLEEF

Shiny Variant

Shiny Bayleef has an alternate coloration compared with standard Bayleef. The Shiny status remains associated with the Pokémon through evolution.

Shiny Bayleef Collection Tips

  • Keep a Shiny Bayleef if you are building a complete Shiny Johto collection.
  • Consider evolving a Shiny Chikorita if you want the entire evolutionary family.

  • A Shiny Pokémon can also have other attributes such as Lucky status after a trade.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Bayleef

Lucky Bayleef is obtained through trading. Lucky status is different from Shiny status and different from IV perfection.

🍀
LUCKY BAYLEEF

Lucky Pokémon

A Lucky Bayleef has the Lucky background/status and benefits from the reduced Stardust cost associated with powering up Lucky Pokémon.

Lucky Bayleef Facts

  • Lucky status comes from trading.
  • Lucky Bayleef receives the Lucky Pokémon Stardust investment benefit.
  • Lucky does not mean 100% IV.
  • A Lucky Bayleef can also be Shiny.
  • A Lucky Bayleef can be a useful collection target.
Lucky + Shiny + 100% are different concepts

Lucky describes the Pokémon's trade-related status, Shiny describes its appearance, and 100% describes its IV combination. One Pokémon can potentially satisfy more than one of these categories.

Is 100% Bayleef Rare?

A perfect-IV Bayleef is less common than an ordinary specimen because all three IVs must reach 15. For collectors, perfect middle-stage Pokémon can be particularly useful when completing Pokédex and IV collections.

Perfect Bayleef = 15 / 15 / 15

"100% IV" does not mean level 100. It means the Pokémon has the maximum possible IV value in all three IV categories.

23 • FAQ

Bayleef FAQ

What type is Bayleef?

Bayleef is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Bayleef weak to?

Bayleef is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

What is Bayleef's Pokédex number?

Bayleef is #153 in the National Pokédex.

What generation is Bayleef from?

Bayleef was introduced in Generation 2 and is associated with the Johto region.

Does Bayleef evolve?

Yes. Bayleef can evolve into Meganium as the final stage of the Chikorita evolutionary family.

Is Shiny Bayleef available?

Shiny Bayleef can be obtained when the relevant Shiny form is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or evolution.

Is Shadow Bayleef available?

Shadow Bayleef can exist when Bayleef is available through the game's Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

Can Shadow Bayleef be purified?

An eligible Shadow Bayleef can be purified, subject to the current Pokémon GO purification mechanics.

What is a Lucky Bayleef?

Lucky Bayleef is a Bayleef that has received Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon receive a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is 100% Bayleef?

A 100% Bayleef has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Bayleef?

XXL Bayleef is an unusually large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size classification.

What is XXS Bayleef?

XXS Bayleef is an unusually small specimen according to Pokémon GO's size classification.

Is Bayleef good for raids?

Bayleef is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main value is more closely associated with evolution, collection and niche gameplay situations.

Is Bayleef good for PvP?

Bayleef can have niche value in certain formats, but it is not generally considered a dominant all-purpose PvP Pokémon.

Can Bayleef be Shiny and Lucky at the same time?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status describe different characteristics, so a Pokémon can potentially have both.

Can Bayleef be 100% and Lucky?

Yes, although the combination is much more specific because Lucky status comes from a trade and the traded Pokémon receives a new IV combination.

Can Bayleef be XXL and Shiny?

Yes. Size classification and Shiny status are independent characteristics.

Can Bayleef be XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IV quality are separate attributes, so an XXS Bayleef can theoretically also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Shadow Girafarig

Shadow Girafarig is the Shadow version of the species. The Shadow mechanic changes its battle characteristics while retaining Girafarig's Normal and Psychic typing.

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade defensive durability for greater offensive pressure. This means Shadow Girafarig should be evaluated separately from a regular Girafarig.

Shadow Girafarig Investment

  • Check IVs before investing Stardust.
  • Consider the higher Shadow power-up cost.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare its performance with your existing Pokémon.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Girafarig

Purified Girafarig is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Girafarig. Purification removes the Shadow status and can improve its IV values.

Shadow

Purified

Purification decision:

Consider your collection and gameplay goals before purifying. A Shadow Pokémon cannot be returned to Shadow status after purification.

Girafarig FAQ

What type is Girafarig?

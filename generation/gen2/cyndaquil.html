Cyndaquil

Cyndaquil is a Fire-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first stage of the Cyndaquil evolution family and as a collectible Pokémon with Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV variants.

Cyndaquil Pokémon GO Overview

Why Cyndaquil's Fire Typing Matters

Cyndaquil's Fire typing gives it access to Fire-type attacks and determines its defensive matchup against other Pokémon types. Its Fire attacks are particularly useful against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel Pokémon.

09 — IV GUIDE

Cyndaquil IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Cyndaquil is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Does 100% Cyndaquil Mean?

A 100% Cyndaquil has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Trainers often call this a "hundo." A perfect Cyndaquil can be particularly desirable if you plan to evolve it into Typhlosion or keep it as part of a complete collection.

Cyndaquil Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Cyndaquil?

Cyndaquil is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

Cyndaquil is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What is Cyndaquil's Pokédex number?

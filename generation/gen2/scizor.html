Scizor Overview

Scizor is the evolution of Scyther and has one of the most distinctive type combinations in Pokémon GO: Bug and Steel. This combination gives Scizor a long list of useful resistances while leaving Fire as its major weakness.

✨ Shiny Scizor

✨
Shiny Scizor

Shiny Scizor is a cosmetic alternate version of Scizor. Its battle statistics and typing remain the same as a regular Scizor with the same level and IV combination.

Does Shiny Scizor Have Different Stats?

No. Shiny status does not automatically change Scizor's base battle statistics. The Pokémon's IVs, level and moveset remain the important factors for performance.

Collector Value

A Shiny Scizor with strong IVs can be particularly valuable for collectors, especially if it is also a candidate for Mega Evolution.

Should You Purify Shadow Scizor?

Do not purify automatically. A Shadow Scizor can have different strategic value from a Purified Scizor, so the decision should depend on IVs, collection goals and the role you want the Pokémon to perform.

Shadow move availability, Frustration mechanics and Team GO Rocket rotations can change. Check current game information before making a permanent investment decision.

✨ Purified Scizor

✨
Purified Scizor

Purified Scizor is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Scyther or Shadow Scizor according to current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Purified Scizor Benefits

  • Purified Pokémon have their Shadow status removed.
  • Purification can change the Pokémon's IV values according to game mechanics.
  • Purified Pokémon can have different resource requirements.
  • Purification removes the Shadow battle profile.
  • Purified Scizor can be useful for collectors and specific investment goals.

Purified vs Shadow Scizor

The main decision is usually between Shadow offensive pressure and the benefits associated with purification. There is no universal answer for every player.

Is a 100% Scizor Always the Best?

📏 XXL Scizor

📏
XXL Scizor

XXL Scizor represents an unusually large size variation. Size is primarily relevant for collection features and eligible Showcase competitions.

An XXL Scizor does not automatically deal more battle damage because of its size. Battle performance remains primarily dependent on stats, level, IVs and moves.

🔍 XXS Scizor

🔍
XXS Scizor

XXS Scizor represents an unusually small size variation and is mainly a collector-focused attribute.

XXS status does not automatically make Scizor weaker or stronger in normal Pokémon GO battles.

Scizor FAQ

What type is Scizor?

Scizor is a Bug and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Scizor weak to?

Is Shiny Scizor available?

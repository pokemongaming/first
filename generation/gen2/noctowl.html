Noctowl PvP Guide

Noctowl's combination of high HP, useful defensive typing and Flying-type pressure makes PvP a more interesting area for Noctowl than high-level raids.

Great League

Noctowl can be considered for Great League teams because its relatively bulky stat profile allows it to remain on the battlefield longer than many fragile Flying attackers.

Ultra League

Noctowl can also be evaluated in higher CP formats, although its effectiveness depends heavily on the current Cup rules, available moves and metagame.

Master League

Noctowl's maximum CP makes Master League a much less natural environment for it compared with Great League and Ultra League.

Noctowl FAQ

What type is Noctowl?

Noctowl is a Normal and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Noctowl weak to?

Noctowl is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Does Noctowl evolve?

Noctowl evolves from Hoothoot. Noctowl does not have another normal evolution.

Is Shiny Noctowl available?

Shiny Noctowl is an available alternate form when obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Noctowl different from regular Noctowl?

Yes. Shadow Noctowl has Shadow mechanics that change its offensive and defensive battle profile.

Should I purify Shadow Noctowl?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, battle use and your collection goals before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Noctowl?

Lucky Noctowl is a Noctowl that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Noctowl?

A 100% Noctowl has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Noctowl?

XXL Noctowl is a Noctowl that falls into the game's extra-large size category.

What is XXS Noctowl?

XXS Noctowl is a Noctowl that falls into the game's extra-small size category.

Is XXL Noctowl automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Noctowl can have any IV combination.

Can Noctowl be useful in PvP?

Yes. Noctowl's bulk and Normal/Flying typing can make it useful in selected PvP formats, particularly when its current moveset fits the format.

Is Noctowl good for raids?

Noctowl is generally not a priority high-end raid attacker because its Attack is relatively low compared with dedicated raid specialists.

Can a Noctowl be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so an individual Pokémon can potentially have both.

Can a Noctowl be Shadow and Shiny?

Special-form combinations should be checked against the current Pokémon GO rules and availability for the species.

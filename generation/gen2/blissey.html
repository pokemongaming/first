Why Blissey's Typing Matters

Normal typing gives Blissey a relatively simple defensive profile. Its main weakness is Fighting, while Ghost-type attacks are especially poor against it.

Why Blissey Is So Difficult to Defeat

Blissey's combination of very high HP and useful defensive stats makes it exceptionally durable. Trainers attacking a Gym usually want to bring powerful Fighting-type attackers rather than trying to wear Blissey down with neutral damage.

What Counters Blissey?

Fighting-type attackers are the natural answer to Blissey because Fighting attacks exploit its single type weakness.

Shiny Blissey

Shiny Blissey is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny Pokémon have an alternate appearance while retaining the same species and basic battle typing.

✨ Shiny Blissey

Shiny status does not change Blissey's species, typing or underlying battle role. The primary difference is its alternate coloration.

Should You Keep Shiny Blissey?

Yes if you enjoy collecting alternate forms. A Shiny Blissey with strong IVs can also be a nice long-term Gym or collection Pokémon.

Shadow Blissey

Shadow Blissey should be considered separately from regular Blissey because the Shadow mechanic changes its battle profile.

😈 Shadow Blissey

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive boost but sacrifice defensive durability. Because Blissey's primary strength is its bulk, this trade-off makes Shadow Blissey an unusual investment compared with regular Blissey.

Purified Blissey

Purified Blissey is a former Shadow Blissey that has been purified using Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

💎 Purified Blissey

Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's appraisal and resource characteristics. It can also affect which moves and power-up costs are relevant.

Lucky Blissey

Lucky Blissey is created through Pokémon GO's Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky status is different from Shiny status and does not change Blissey's species or typing.

🍀 Lucky Blissey

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up compared with ordinary Pokémon, making Lucky Blissey attractive for players who want to build a high-level defender efficiently.

Why Keep a Lucky Blissey?

  • Reduced Stardust investment can make power-ups easier.
  • Lucky status is valuable for collectors.
  • A Lucky Blissey can be useful as a long-term Gym defender.
  • Lucky and Shiny status can coexist on eligible Pokémon.

XXL Blissey

XXL is a size designation rather than a separate Pokémon species. An XXL Blissey is simply a Blissey that has an unusually large size measurement.

🏆 XXL Blissey

XXL Blissey is valuable primarily for collection, size-related achievements and personal goals. XXL status does not automatically mean higher IVs or better battle performance.

XXL vs IV

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Blissey can have excellent IVs, poor IVs or anything in between.

XXS Blissey

XXS is the opposite size category to XXL. An XXS Blissey is unusually small compared with other Blissey.

🔹 XXS Blissey

XXS Blissey can be useful for collection goals, size medals and personal Pokédex completion. XXS status does not directly determine IV quality.

XXL or XXS — Which Is Better?

Neither size is inherently better for normal combat. The main value of XXL and XXS Pokémon comes from collection and size-related gameplay features.

Blissey Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Blissey requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Blissey is primarily valued for its durability, trainers should think carefully before investing large amounts of resources.

  • Keep strong-IV Blissey candidates.
  • Save Candy XL for meaningful high-level projects.
  • Lucky Blissey can reduce Stardust costs.
  • Do not judge Blissey only by CP.
  • Compare IVs before transferring duplicates.

Individual Blissey Guide

Blissey is best understood as a specialist rather than a universal attacker. Its exceptional HP gives it a strong identity as a Gym defender.

Before Powering Up

  • Check Blissey's IVs.
  • Decide whether the Pokémon is intended for Gym defense.
  • Consider whether it is Shiny, Lucky or another valuable status.
  • Compare Stardust and Candy requirements.
  • Keep high-quality special variants for collection goals.

Gym Strategy

Blissey works best when used as part of a diverse Gym lineup. Its huge HP can force attackers to spend significant time and resources defeating it.

Long-Term Investment

A high-level Blissey can remain useful for Gym defense, while Lucky, Shiny, Hundo, XXL and XXS examples can provide additional collection value.

Blissey FAQ

What type is Blissey?

Magcargo

01 • OVERVIEW

Magcargo Overview

Magcargo is the evolved form of Slugma and combines Fire and Rock typing. It is a relatively defensive Pokémon with modest offensive stats, so its main value in Pokémon GO is generally collection, Pokédex completion and niche battle situations rather than high-end raids.

08 • IV GUIDE

Magcargo IV Guide

IVs add up to 15 points each to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Magcargo is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is a 100% Magcargo?

A 100% Magcargo has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. These Pokémon are especially attractive to collectors and players who enjoy completing perfect-IV Pokédex collections.

09 • PVP

Magcargo PvP Guide

Magcargo's dual typing can give it interesting matchups, but its overall PvP value depends heavily on league rules, moves and the current meta.

Great League

Magcargo can fit into some specialized Great League teams because its Fire/Rock typing gives it useful resistances. However, its weaknesses to Water, Ground and Rock attacks can make team building challenging.

Ultra League

Magcargo can reach higher levels in Ultra League, but its overall performance remains niche compared with many established meta Pokémon.

Master League

Magcargo's relatively low maximum CP makes Master League an uncommon environment for it.

Lucky Magcargo

Magcargo FAQ

What type is Magcargo?

Magcargo is a Fire and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Magcargo weak to?

Magcargo is weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

What is Magcargo's max CP?

Magcargo has a maximum CP of approximately 2,362 under standard Pokémon GO stat calculations.

Is Magcargo good for raids?

Magcargo is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its lower Attack stat makes stronger Fire and Rock Pokémon preferable for serious PvE teams.

Can Slugma evolve into Magcargo?

Yes. Slugma evolves into Magcargo using 50 Slugma Candy.

Is Shiny Magcargo available?

Shiny Magcargo is available as a special alternate-color form through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

What is a 100% Magcargo?

A 100% Magcargo has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Magcargo useful?

Lucky Magcargo is especially attractive to collectors and can be cheaper to power up because Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust costs.

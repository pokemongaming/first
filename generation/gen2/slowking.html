Slowking Overview

Slowking is the evolved form of Slowpoke that can be obtained through the King's Rock evolution method. Its Water/Psychic typing gives it several useful resistances, although it also leaves it vulnerable to five attacking types.

Slowking PvP Guide

Slowking can participate in Pokémon GO's GO Battle League, but it is generally not a dominant option. Its effectiveness depends heavily on the current metagame, moveset and league format.

Great League

Slowking can fit within the Great League CP limit when built at an appropriate level, although it competes against many stronger options.

Ultra League

Slowking's higher CP potential allows it to reach the Ultra League environment, but it remains a niche choice rather than a universal meta pick.

Master League

Slowking's 2,877 maximum CP is relatively low for Master League. It generally struggles to compete with the Legendary and high-stat Pokémon commonly used there.

01 • OVERVIEW

Donphan Overview

Donphan is a pure Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves from Phanpy after reaching the required evolution condition in Pokémon GO.

Unlike Legendary Pokémon, Donphan is a regular evolutionary species. Its main collectible variations come from features such as Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky status, IV quality and Pokémon size.

Why Donphan's Ground Typing Matters

Donphan's pure Ground typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. Ground attacks are especially useful against Electric, Fire, Poison, Rock and Steel Pokémon.

Its typing also gives Donphan immunity-style protection from Electric attacks in the Pokémon GO type system, making it useful in appropriate matchups.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Donphan

Lucky Donphan is a special trade outcome. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive appearance in the Pokémon storage and receive useful Stardust benefits when powered up.

🍀 Lucky Donphan

A Lucky Donphan can be an attractive investment because powering it up requires less Stardust than powering up a normal Pokémon at the same level.

Why Keep a Lucky Donphan?

  • Lower Stardust investment when powering up.
  • Useful collector variant.
  • Can combine Lucky status with strong IVs.
  • Can be particularly interesting for high-level builds.
  • A Lucky Donphan can also be Shiny.

Lucky Donphan IVs

Lucky Pokémon receive a guaranteed IV floor, but a Lucky Donphan is not automatically a 100% IV Donphan. Check its individual appraisal to determine its actual IV combination.

16 • XXL

XXL Donphan

XXL Donphan is a size variant. XXL status is independent of IV quality, meaning an XXL Donphan can have low, average or perfect IVs.

📏 XXL Donphan EXTRA-LARGE SIZE

XXL is primarily a size and collection characteristic. It does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Is XXL Donphan Rare?

Large-size Pokémon can be desirable to collectors, especially when combined with other valuable traits such as Shiny, Lucky or 100% IV.

17 • XXS

XXS Donphan

XXS Donphan is the small-size counterpart to XXL. Like other size classifications, XXS status is separate from IV quality.

🔹 XXS Donphan EXTRA-SMALL SIZE

An XXS Donphan can have any IV combination. Its size classification should not be confused with its appraisal.

XXS Donphan Collector Value

XXS Pokémon can be attractive additions to a size-focused collection. A Shiny, Lucky or high-IV XXS Donphan can be particularly interesting to collectors.

Donphan FAQ

What type is Donphan?

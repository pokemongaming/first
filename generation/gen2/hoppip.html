Hoppip

Hoppip is a small Grass and Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, Hoppip is primarily useful as the first stage of the Hoppip → Skiploom → Jumpluff evolution family, while its Shiny, Shadow, Lucky and size variants are popular with collectors.

Why Hoppip's Typing Matters

Hoppip combines Grass and Flying typing. This gives it several useful resistances but also leaves it vulnerable to a number of common attack types, including a particularly important weakness to Ice.

11 — 100% IV

100% IV Hoppip

A 100% IV Hoppip has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is the highest possible IV combination for an individual Hoppip.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Hoppip CP

The maximum CP of a perfect Hoppip at its maximum level is approximately 635. The exact displayed CP at lower levels depends on the Pokémon's level.

100% Hoppip Collector Tip

A perfect Hoppip can be worth keeping even if you do not intend to use Hoppip in battle. You may choose to evolve it later into a perfect Skiploom or Jumpluff.

21 — FAQ

Hoppip FAQ

What type is Hoppip?

Hoppip is a Grass/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Hoppip weak to?

Hoppip is vulnerable to Ice, Fire, Rock and Electric attacks. Ice is an especially important weakness because of its Grass/Flying typing.

What generation is Hoppip from?

Hoppip was introduced in Generation 2 and is part of the Johto Pokédex.

What does Hoppip evolve into?

Hoppip evolves into Skiploom, which can then evolve into Jumpluff.

How much Candy does Hoppip need to evolve?

Hoppip requires 25 Hoppip Candy to evolve into Skiploom. Skiploom then requires 100 Hoppip Candy to evolve into Jumpluff.

Is Shiny Hoppip available?

Yes. Shiny Hoppip is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Hoppip available?

Shadow Hoppip can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations.

Should I purify Shadow Hoppip?

Not automatically. Consider whether you prefer retaining the Shadow version or want the benefits associated with purification.

What is a 100% Hoppip?

A 100% Hoppip has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Hoppip?

XXL Hoppip is a large-size Hoppip identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Hoppip?

XXS Hoppip is a very small Hoppip identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

Can Hoppip be Lucky?

Yes. Hoppip can become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Is Hoppip good for raids?

Hoppip is not normally considered a strong raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolution line, collection potential and special variants.

Is Hoppip good in PvP?

Hoppip itself has limited PvP relevance. Trainers interested in competitive use should generally investigate its evolved forms.

Is a 100% Hoppip worth keeping?

Yes. A perfect Hoppip can be valuable for collectors and can be evolved into a perfect member of its evolution family.

Can Hoppip be both Shiny and XXL?

Yes. Shiny status and size characteristics are separate attributes, so special combinations can exist.

Can Hoppip be both Lucky and 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Pokémon can have perfect IVs, making a Lucky 100% Hoppip an especially interesting collection piece.

Jumpluff

Jumpluff is a Grass/Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is known primarily for its PvP utility, unusual typing, low maximum CP and multiple collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

01 • OVERVIEW

Jumpluff Overview

Jumpluff is the final evolutionary stage of the Hoppip family. It combines Grass and Flying typing, giving it a useful collection of resistances but also several important weaknesses.

07 • PVP

Jumpluff PvP Guide

Great League

Great League is one of the most natural places to consider Jumpluff. Its relatively low Attack and respectable bulk allow it to operate effectively under a 1500 CP limit when built appropriately.

Ultra League

Jumpluff can be used in higher CP formats, but the opportunity cost becomes more significant. Check the current cup rules and metagame before investing heavily.

Master League

Standard Jumpluff is generally not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the levels commonly reached by Master League Pokémon.

PvP IV Tip

A 15/15/15 Jumpluff is not automatically the ideal Great League specimen. PvP builds often prefer different Attack/Defense/HP IV distributions to maximize stat product under a CP limit.

10 • SHADOW

Shadow Jumpluff

Shadow Jumpluff is a Shadow variant with the standard Shadow battle modifier. It should be evaluated separately from regular Jumpluff because Shadow Pokémon trade defensive durability for increased offensive pressure.

11 • PURIFIED

Purified Jumpluff

Purified Jumpluff is created by purifying a Shadow Jumpluff. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can alter its IVs and resource requirements.

💠

Purified Jumpluff

Purified Jumpluff no longer has the Shadow battle modifier and receives the benefits associated with purification.

✨

Purified Aura

Purified Pokémon display a distinct visual treatment and can be useful additions to collections.

Should You Purify Shadow Jumpluff?

Do not purify a Shadow Jumpluff automatically. If the Shadow version has value for your preferred PvP format, compare both versions before making the decision.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Jumpluff

Lucky Jumpluff is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special collection properties and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Jumpluff

A Lucky Jumpluff is guaranteed to meet a minimum IV floor when it becomes Lucky and receives the Lucky Stardust discount for power-ups.

Why Collect a Lucky Jumpluff?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collectors building special-form Pokédex collections.
  • Can be valuable for players who enjoy trading.
  • Lucky status is retained after evolution.
13 • 100% IV

100% IV Jumpluff

A 100% IV Jumpluff has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo or perfect-IV Pokémon.

Perfect Jumpluff — 15 / 15 / 15

The perfect IV combination for a standard Jumpluff.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Is 100% Jumpluff Best for PvP?

Not necessarily. For CP-limited PvP formats, a lower Attack IV can sometimes allow a Pokémon to reach a higher overall level while remaining under the league's CP limit. Always evaluate the individual PvP build rather than assuming 15/15/15 is automatically optimal.

14 • XXL

XXL Jumpluff

XXL Jumpluff is an extra-large size variant. Pokémon size classifications are separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

XXL Extra Large Jumpluff

XXL status is a size characteristic and does not automatically indicate better battle performance.

XXL vs 100% Jumpluff

XXL and perfect IV are completely different attributes. A Jumpluff can be XXL without being 100% IV, and a 100% IV Jumpluff does not have to be XXL.

15 • XXS

XXS Jumpluff

XXS Jumpluff is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL status, XXS classification is independent from IV quality.

XXS Extra Small Jumpluff

XXS status is primarily a size and collection characteristic rather than an indicator of battle strength.

XXS Jumpluff for Collectors

XXS Pokémon can be particularly appealing to collectors who are completing size-based Pokédex goals or building a collection of unusual variants.

Jumpluff FAQ

What type is Jumpluff?

01 · OVERVIEW

Lanturn Overview

Lanturn is a dual Water/Electric-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves from Chinchou after collecting the required Candy.

Unlike many Pokémon that are primarily valued for raid damage, Lanturn is particularly interesting because of its PvP performance. Its combination of Water and Electric typing gives it useful coverage against several common Pokémon.

06 · PVP

Lanturn PvP Guide

PvP is one of Lanturn's most important uses in Pokémon GO. Its high HP, useful typing and access to Water and Electric attacks allow it to pressure several common opponents.

Great League

Great League is one of Lanturn's most natural PvP environments. Trainers can use its Water/Electric typing to apply pressure to Water and Flying Pokémon while Surf provides reliable charged-move pressure.

Great League
Strong

Ultra League

Lanturn can be used in Ultra League, although its overall effectiveness and stat distribution make Great League a more natural environment for many players.

Master League

Lanturn's lower maximum CP makes Master League a considerably less natural format for this Pokémon.

PvP Tip

Do not automatically build every Lanturn with perfect IVs. PvP IV spreads can favor lower Attack and higher Defense/HP depending on the league and CP limit.

19 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Lanturn

Great League

  • Use Lanturn to pressure Water and Flying Pokémon.
  • Take advantage of its useful Water/Electric typing.
  • Use Surf for efficient charged-move pressure.
  • Be careful around Grass and Ground-type attacks.
  • Select IVs based on the specific Great League build.

Team Building

Lanturn works best when paired with Pokémon that can handle Grass and Ground threats. Building a team around its resistances can help compensate for its limited defensive options against its primary weaknesses.

Long-Term Investment

Lanturn is generally a more PvP-focused investment than a raid-focused one. Trainers who enjoy Great League should consider keeping several Lanturn candidates with different IV spreads.

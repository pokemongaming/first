Why Meganium's Typing Matters

Meganium's pure Grass typing gives it access to Grass-type STAB attacks while also defining its defensive matchup profile. It is vulnerable to several common offensive types but can resist Water, Ground, Electric and other Grass-relevant attacks.

09 — PVP

Meganium PvP Guide

Meganium's defensive profile and access to Frenzy Plant make it more interesting in PvP than its raid performance might suggest.

Great League

Meganium can be useful in Great League formats where its Grass typing, bulk and charged-move pressure fit the current matchup environment.

Ultra League

Meganium can also be considered for Ultra League teams, although the ideal IV spread and moveset should be evaluated using current league rules and rankings.

Master League

Meganium's maximum CP is substantially lower than the Pokémon normally associated with Master League, making it less natural for this format.

XXS Meganium is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is a size classification and does not determine IV quality.

XXS vs IVs

23 — FAQ

Meganium FAQ

What type is Meganium?

Meganium is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Meganium weak to?

Meganium is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying, Bug and Poison-type attacks.

What is Meganium's max CP?

Meganium's maximum CP is approximately 2,725 under standard maximum-level conditions.

Is Shiny Meganium available?

Yes. Shiny Meganium is available through eligible Shiny Chikorita evolution and other applicable Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Chikorita evolve into Shiny Meganium?

Yes. A Shiny Chikorita remains Shiny when evolved into Bayleef and then Meganium.

Is Shadow Meganium available?

Shadow Meganium can be created by evolving an eligible Shadow Chikorita through Bayleef.

Is Purified Meganium worth keeping?

It can be. Whether purification is worthwhile depends on your goals, IVs, moveset and whether you prefer the Shadow or purified version.

What is a 100% Meganium?

A 100% Meganium has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Is Lucky Meganium better?

Lucky status does not automatically make Meganium stronger in battle. Its main practical benefit is reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is XXL Meganium?

XXL Meganium is an unusually large Meganium based on its size classification.

What is XXS Meganium?

XXS Meganium is an unusually small Meganium based on its size classification.

Is XXL Meganium automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Meganium can have any IV combination.

Is XXS Meganium automatically 100% IV?

No. XXS status does not determine the Pokémon's IV percentage.

Is Meganium good for raids?

Meganium can use Grass-type attacks, but its relatively low Attack makes it less attractive than dedicated high-damage Grass attackers for optimized raids.

Is Meganium good for PvP?

Meganium can be useful in selected PvP formats, particularly when its bulk and Frenzy Plant pressure fit the current matchup environment.

What is the best Meganium moveset?

Vine Whip paired with Frenzy Plant is a notable Grass-focused combination when those moves are available.

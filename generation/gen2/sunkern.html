Sunkern Overview

Sunkern is a pure Grass-type Pokémon from the Johto region. It evolves into Sunflora using Sunkern Candy and an evolution item when the required evolution conditions are met.

In Pokémon GO, Sunkern is generally more interesting as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a major raid attacker. However, different variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky Sunkern can give collectors additional reasons to keep multiple specimens.

Sunkern Forms & Variants

The following variants are useful for collectors and players building a complete Sunkern collection.

🌱 Regular Sunkern

The standard version of Sunkern. It retains the normal appearance and standard Pokémon GO mechanics.

A rare alternate-color version of Sunkern. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically improve IVs.

Shadow Sunkern 🌑

Shadow Sunkern is the Shadow version of Sunkern. Shadow status does not change its Grass typing, but Shadow Pokémon have different battle and resource mechanics.

Offensive Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade additional offensive pressure for reduced durability.

Resource Cost

Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated carefully before investing Stardust and Candy.

Frustration Reminder

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as a Charged Move. Check current Pokémon GO event rules before planning to replace it.

Sunkern FAQ

What type is Sunkern?

Sunkern is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

Can Sunkern evolve?

Yes. Sunkern evolves into Sunflora when the required Pokémon GO evolution conditions are met.

Is Shiny Sunkern available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Does Shiny Sunkern have better stats?

No. Shiny status is separate from Individual Values.

Is Shadow Sunkern different from regular Sunkern?

Shadow Sunkern retains the same species and Grass typing but uses Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

What is a 100% Sunkern?

A 100% Sunkern has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Sunkern?

XXL Sunkern is a Sunkern belonging to Pokémon GO's extra-large size category.

What is XXS Sunkern?

XXS Sunkern belongs to the extra-small size category.

Can a Lucky Sunkern be 100% IV?

Lucky status and IV quality are separate Pokémon characteristics, so a Lucky Pokémon can potentially have very high IVs.

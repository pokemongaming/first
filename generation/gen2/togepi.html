Togepi Overview

Togepi is Pokédex #175 and belongs to the Generation 2 Johto Pokémon family. Unlike many Pokémon, Togepi begins as a Baby Pokémon and can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters. It is a pure Fairy type and evolves into Togetic before eventually reaching Togekiss.

What Does Togepi's Fairy Typing Mean?

Togepi's pure Fairy typing gives it a compact defensive profile. Its main weaknesses are Poison and Steel, while it has useful resistances against several common attack types. The Fairy typing becomes substantially more important once Togepi evolves into Togetic and eventually Togekiss.

Why Keep Strong Togepi?

The biggest reason to keep a good Togepi is its evolution family. Togepi can become Togetic and eventually Togekiss, which has substantially greater battle utility. A strong Togepi can therefore be a useful long-term investment even if the unevolved Pokémon itself is not a major raid attacker.

Togepi IV Guide

A 15/15/15 Togepi is considered a 100% IV or "hundo" Togepi. IVs are independent Attack, Defense and HP values from 0 to 15.

What Is a Perfect Togepi?

A perfect Togepi has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. When all three values are 15, the Pokémon has 100% IVs. For a collector, a hundo Togepi can be especially valuable because it can be preserved as Togepi or evolved through the Togetic and Togekiss family.

Perfect IV Togepi

A 100% Togepi has maximum Attack, Defense and HP IVs. The perfect IV combination is 15/15/15.

Why Keep a Hundo Togepi?

Shadow Togepi IVs

Shadow Pokémon do not need to have perfect IVs to be worth keeping. A strong Shadow Togepi can still be valuable, especially if you intend to evolve it and use the resulting Pokémon for a specific purpose.

Frustration reminder:

Newly caught Shadow Pokémon know Frustration. Pokémon GO normally restricts removal of Frustration except during eligible events, so check the current Team GO Rocket event rules before using resources.

Should You Purify Shadow Togepi?

Purification is a permanent decision. Purifying removes the Shadow status, improves the Pokémon's appraisal and reduces some future resource costs, but it also removes the Shadow battle bonus. Do not automatically purify a Shadow Togepi simply because the purified appraisal will be higher.

Purified Togepi

Purified Togepi is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Togepi. Purification changes its status and gives it specific gameplay characteristics.

Lucky Togepi

Lucky Togepi is a traded Pokémon that has received the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive to collectors because they have reduced Stardust costs for Powering Up.

🍀 Lucky Togepi

A Lucky Togepi can be a particularly nice collection piece, especially when combined with strong IVs, Shiny status or a rare size.

Why Keep a Lucky Togepi?

  • Can be evolved while retaining Lucky status.
  • Useful for Lucky Pokédex collectors.
  • Can be especially valuable when combined with high IVs.
  • Shiny Lucky Togepi is an especially desirable collection combination.

XXL Togepi

XXL Togepi is a size variant that is larger than the standard size range. Size is separate from IVs and CP, so an XXL Togepi can have any IV combination.

XXL Togepi

Extra Large

An XXL Togepi is a large-size specimen and can be kept primarily for collection purposes.

Size ≠ IV

An XXL Pokémon is not automatically a high-IV Pokémon. Always check the Appraise screen separately for IV quality.

Should You Keep an XXL Togepi?

  • Keep it if you collect size variants.
  • Check whether it is also Shiny.
  • Check its IVs before transferring.
  • Consider keeping rare combinations such as XXL + Lucky.
  • Consider tagging it as an XXL collection Pokémon.

Togepi PvP Guide

Togepi's low maximum CP means that it is generally not the primary Fairy choice for competitive PvP. Its evolved forms are much more relevant to battle-focused players.

Great League

Togepi can technically fit within Great League's CP restriction after investment, but its low offensive pressure generally makes it less attractive than stronger Fairy options.

Ultra League

Togepi is generally not a natural Ultra League investment because its maximum CP is too low to compete efficiently with Pokémon designed for higher CP formats.

Master League

Togepi is not a practical Master League battler. Togekiss is the form of the family that becomes relevant to higher-CP competitive play.

Togepi Candy, Candy XL & Stardust

Building the Togepi family requires Candy and, at higher levels, Candy XL and Stardust.

  • Save Togepi Candy for your best evolution candidates.
  • Keep strong IV Togepi before transferring extras.
  • Collect Candy XL if you plan to build the evolved family at higher levels.
  • Use the Appraise feature before investing Stardust.
  • Consider Lucky Pokémon for Stardust-efficient investment.
Evolution Resource Reminder

Togepi requires 25 Candy to evolve into Togetic. Togetic requires 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone to evolve into Togekiss.

Togepi Collection Guide

If you collect Togepi variants, it can be useful to organize your storage around attributes rather than simply keeping every copy.

  • Keep at least one strong-IV Togepi.

Individual Togepi Investment Guide

For Evolution

Choose a Togepi with strong IVs and a combination of attributes you value before evolving it. This is especially important if your ultimate goal is a high-quality Togekiss.

For Collectors

Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, Shadow, Purified, XXL and XXS Togepi can each serve different collection goals. Avoid transferring a rare variant until you have checked its IVs, size and other attributes.

For Battle Players

Togepi itself is not a high-priority raid attacker. If your goal is battle performance, consider whether evolving the Togepi into Togetic and ultimately Togekiss better fits your team.

Before Transferring Togepi

  • Check the Appraise screen.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow or Purified.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Check whether it is XXL or XXS.
  • Check whether it has a desirable background or event attribute.

Togepi FAQ

What type is Togepi?

Togepi is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Togepi weak to?

Togepi is weak to Poison- and Steel-type attacks.

What is Togepi's maximum CP?

Togepi's maximum CP is 743 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Togepi available?

Yes. Shiny Togepi is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Does Togepi evolve?

Yes. Togepi evolves into Togetic using 25 Togepi Candy. Togetic can then evolve into Togekiss using 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

What is a 100% Togepi?

A 100% Togepi has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Hundo Togepi?

Hundo is the community term commonly used for a 100% IV Pokémon.

Can Togepi be Lucky?

Yes. Togepi can have the Lucky status after an eligible trade.

Can Togepi be Shadow?

Shadow Togepi can exist when Togepi is available through eligible Shadow Pokémon encounters.

Can Shadow Togepi be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes its moves and resource requirements.

Can Togepi be XXL?

Yes. Togepi can appear in the XXL size category.

Can Togepi be XXS?

Yes. Togepi can appear in the XXS size category.

Is XXL Togepi automatically high IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Togepi can have any IV combination.

Is XXS Togepi automatically low IV?

No. XXS is a size characteristic and does not determine the Pokémon's IVs.

Is Togepi good for raids?

Togepi is not considered a high-level raid attacker. Its primary value comes from its evolution family and collector attributes.

Is Togepi good in PvP?

Togepi generally has limited PvP relevance because of its low CP and offensive stats. Its evolved forms are more important for battle-focused players.

Should I evolve a 100% Togepi?

If your goal is a perfect-IV Togekiss, evolving a 100% Togepi is a logical choice. If you are a collector, you may instead prefer keeping a perfect Togepi in its original form.

Should I purify Shadow Togepi?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your collection and battle goals before making a permanent decision.

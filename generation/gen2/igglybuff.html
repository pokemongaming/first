Igglybuff Overview

Igglybuff is a Baby Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Jigglypuff and eventually Wigglytuff. Its low combat stats mean it is not normally considered a top raid attacker, but its various special forms can make it interesting for collectors.

Igglybuff FAQ

What type is Igglybuff?

Igglybuff is a Normal / Fairy-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

What is Igglybuff weak to?

Igglybuff is weak to Steel- and Poison-type attacks.

Does Igglybuff evolve?

Yes. Igglybuff evolves into Jigglypuff, which can then evolve into Wigglytuff.

Is Shiny Igglybuff available?

Shiny Igglybuff is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when featured by the game.

What is 100% Igglybuff?

Do not purify automatically. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, Shadow status, collection value and intended evolution before making the decision.

Should I evolve my perfect Igglybuff?

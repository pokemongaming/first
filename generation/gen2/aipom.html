Aipom Overview

Aipom is a Normal-type Pokémon from the Johto region. It evolves into Ambipom after meeting the appropriate Pokémon GO evolution requirements. Aipom is primarily a collection and evolution Pokémon, although individual variants such as Shadow, Lucky and Purified Aipom can have different long-term investment considerations.

✨ Shiny Aipom

Shiny Aipom is the alternate-color version of regular Aipom. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not change Aipom's underlying battle stats.

Regular Aipom

Standard appearance with the normal Aipom coloration.

Shiny Aipom

Alternate coloration for collectors. A Shiny Aipom can also be evolved while retaining its Shiny status.

Should You Evolve Shiny Aipom?

This is mainly a collection decision. Some players prefer keeping one Shiny Aipom unevolved while evolving another into Shiny Ambipom.

Shadow Aipom

Standard Aipom

Is Aipom Good for Raids?

Aipom is generally not a major raid attacker because it is an unevolved Pokémon with modest combat statistics. Players interested in maximizing combat performance will usually want to consider stronger fully evolved Normal-type or role-specific attackers.

Recommended Use

Aipom is more valuable as a collection, evolution and variant Pokémon than as a dedicated raid attacker.

Aipom FAQ

What type is Aipom?

Aipom is a Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Aipom weak to?

Aipom is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Can Aipom evolve?

Yes. Aipom can evolve into Ambipom when the appropriate Pokémon GO evolution requirements are met.

Can Aipom be Shiny?

Shiny Aipom can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Aipom?

Shadow Aipom is the Shadow variant of Aipom with a different battle profile from the standard version.

Can Shadow Aipom be purified?

Eligible Shadow Aipom can be purified according to Pokémon GO purification mechanics.

What is a Lucky Aipom?

Lucky Aipom is an Aipom with Lucky Pokémon status, primarily valuable for collection and investment purposes.

XXL size does not automatically mean that an Aipom has better IVs or combat performance.

No. XXS size classification is separate from an Aipom's Individual Values.

Is Aipom good for raids?

Aipom is generally more useful as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a dedicated raid attacker.

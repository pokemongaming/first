Totodile Overview

Totodile is a small Water-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Croconaw and eventually Feraligatr. In Pokémon GO, Totodile is primarily useful as an evolution-family Pokémon, collection target and source of Candy for building Feraligatr.

Why Totodile's Typing Matters

Totodile's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. Water-type attacks are useful against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Totodile must be careful around Electric and Grass attacks.

Why Keep Good Totodile?

A strong Totodile can become a useful Feraligatr. For this reason, players interested in building their Water collection should consider IVs, Shiny status, Candy availability and evolution opportunities before transferring Totodile.

Totodile Candy & Candy XL

Totodile Candy is required for evolution and can also be useful when powering up its evolved forms.

  • Catch additional Totodile when Candy is needed.
  • Use Pinap Berries when farming Candy.
  • Keep strong specimens before transferring extras.
  • Consider Candy XL for high-level Feraligatr investment.
  • Save rare or high-IV specimens for long-term projects.

Lucky Totodile

Lucky Totodile is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups compared with standard Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Totodile

Lucky Totodile is particularly attractive to collectors and players who want a lower-Stardust-cost specimen to eventually evolve.

Why Keep a Lucky Totodile?

  • Lucky Pokémon are useful for Stardust-efficient investment.
  • Lucky status remains when Totodile evolves.
  • Lucky Totodile can become Lucky Croconaw and Lucky Feraligatr.
  • Lucky Pokémon are popular collection targets.
Lucky status and IVs are determined through the trading mechanic. Always evaluate the resulting Pokémon after the trade rather than assuming a particular IV spread.

100% IV Totodile

A 100% IV Totodile has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP
100% Totodile = 15/15/15

A perfect Totodile is commonly called a "hundo." Although Totodile itself is not an endgame attacker, a perfect specimen can be valuable to collectors and can be evolved into a perfect Feraligatr.

Should You Evolve a 100% Totodile?

If you want a perfect Feraligatr for your collection, evolving a 100% Totodile is a natural choice. Before evolving, however, consider the current Feraligatr moveset and your intended PvE or PvP use.

XXL & XXS Totodile

Pokémon GO can generate Totodile with different size measurements. XXL and XXS specimens are special size variants that can be valuable to collectors.

XXL Totodile

Extra Large

An XXL Totodile is an unusually large specimen. XXL status is independent of IV quality, so an XXL Totodile can have any IV combination.

XXS Totodile

Extra Small

An XXS Totodile is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS status is separate from IVs.

Totodile PvP Guide

Totodile's low maximum CP means it is generally not the preferred competitive Pokémon for standard GO Battle League formats. Its evolved forms are much more relevant when evaluating PvP potential.

Great League

Totodile itself is generally overshadowed by stronger Water-type choices. However, special cups or Little League-style formats can sometimes change which Pokémon are relevant.

Ultra League

Totodile's CP ceiling makes it an unusual choice for Ultra League. Players should generally evaluate Croconaw or Feraligatr instead.

Master League

Totodile is not a natural Master League investment. Its final evolution is the appropriate Pokémon to evaluate for higher-CP competitive play.

Totodile FAQ

What type is Totodile?

Totodile is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Totodile weak against?

Totodile is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What generation is Totodile from?

Totodile was introduced in Generation 2 and is associated with the Johto region.

What does Totodile evolve into?

Totodile evolves into Croconaw, which can then evolve into Feraligatr.

Is Shiny Totodile available?

Yes. Shiny Totodile is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Shadow Totodile evolve?

Shadow Totodile can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status.

What is a Purified Totodile?

A Purified Totodile is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Totodile.

What is a Lucky Totodile?

Lucky Totodile is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics and has the special Lucky status.

What is a 100% Totodile?

A 100% Totodile has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is an XXL Totodile?

XXL Totodile is an unusually large Totodile identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Totodile?

XXS Totodile is an unusually small Totodile identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

Should I evolve a Shiny Totodile?

If you want a Shiny Croconaw or Shiny Feraligatr, evolving a Shiny Totodile is an appropriate option. Consider your collection goals before evolving.

Should I purify Shadow Totodile?

Not automatically. If you want to preserve the Shadow characteristics for its evolution, compare the benefits before purifying.

Is a 100% Totodile rare?

Perfect-IV Pokémon are much less common than ordinary specimens, making a 100% Totodile a desirable collection target.

Does XXL or XXS affect IVs?

Size classification and Individual Values are separate attributes. An XXL or XXS Totodile can have any IV spread.

16 • XXL

XXL Croconaw

XXL Croconaw is a size variant that represents an exceptionally large specimen. Size is separate from IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

EXTRA LARGE XXL Croconaw

XXL status is primarily a size and collection characteristic. A Pokémon being XXL does not automatically mean it has perfect IVs.

XXL Does Not Mean 100%

A common mistake is assuming that a very large Pokémon must have high IVs. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Croconaw can have low, average or perfect IVs.

23 • FAQ

Croconaw FAQ

What type is Croconaw?

Croconaw is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Croconaw weak to?

Croconaw is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

What generation is Croconaw from?

Croconaw was introduced in Generation 2 and is part of the Johto Pokédex.

Does Croconaw evolve?

Yes. Croconaw is the middle evolution between Totodile and Feraligatr.

Is Shiny Croconaw available?

Shiny Croconaw can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can retain its Shiny status when evolved.

What is a 100% Croconaw?

A 100% Croconaw has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Shadow Croconaw?

Shadow Croconaw is the Shadow variant of Croconaw. It retains the Water typing while having the gameplay characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Croconaw?

Not automatically. Consider your collection goals, battle plans and the characteristics of the Shadow Pokémon before purifying it.

Should I evolve my 100% Croconaw?

Espeon

Espeon is a Psychic-type Pokémon from the Johto region and one of Eevee's possible evolutions. In Pokémon GO, Espeon is known for its high Attack, strong Psychic-type moves and multiple collectible forms including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky variants.

Espeon Overview

Espeon is the Psychic-type evolution of Eevee. It was introduced in Generation 2 and can be obtained in Pokémon GO by evolving Eevee under specific conditions. Its high Attack makes it an interesting Psychic-type attacker, although its overall raid value depends on the current moves available and the alternatives in the Pokémon GO metagame.

Why Espeon's Typing Matters

How to Get Espeon

One method is to make Eevee your Buddy, walk the required distance and evolve it during the daytime while it meets the evolution requirements. The Eevee name trick can also be used once for each evolution name.

Evolution tip:

Before evolving an Eevee into Espeon, check that the desired evolution conditions are satisfied. Do not evolve until the Espeon silhouette appears on the evolution button.

Espeon PvP Guide

Espeon's high Attack and Psychic typing give it an offensive identity in PvP. Its lower bulk means that shield management and matchup selection are important.

Great League

Espeon's high CP and offensive stat distribution can make it difficult to optimize for Great League compared with Pokémon specifically suited to lower CP formats.

Ultra League

Espeon can be considered in Ultra League under suitable conditions, although its performance depends heavily on moveset and the current metagame.

Master League

Espeon's maximum CP is substantially higher than the lower leagues, but it generally faces significant competition from stronger or bulkier Psychic options.

PvP rankings can change whenever Pokémon GO introduces new moves, buffs, nerfs or new Pokémon. Avoid treating a static ranking as permanent.

Espeon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Powering Espeon to higher levels requires Eevee Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Legendary-level resource costs do not apply because Espeon is an evolved non-Legendary Pokémon, but high-level investment can still become expensive.

  • Save Eevee Candy for strong evolution candidates.
  • Collect Eevee Candy XL for high-level Espeon builds.
  • Check IVs before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • Keep useful Shadow, Lucky and Hundo specimens separately.
  • Compare your Espeon with other Psychic attackers before committing to a level 50 project.

Espeon FAQ

What type is Espeon?

Espeon is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

How do you evolve Eevee into Espeon?

Espeon can be obtained by meeting the appropriate Eevee evolution requirements, including the Buddy method during the daytime.

Is Shiny Espeon available?

Yes. Shiny Espeon is available through eligible Shiny Eevee encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Espeon available?

Shadow Espeon can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow Eevee when the required evolution conditions are met.

What is a 100% Espeon?

A 100% Espeon has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Espeon?

Lucky Espeon is an Espeon with the Lucky status, normally obtained through a Lucky Trade.

What is Purified Espeon?

Purified Espeon is obtained by evolving a purified Eevee.

What is XXL Espeon?

XXL Espeon is an unusually large Espeon based on Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Espeon?

XXS Espeon is an unusually small Espeon based on Pokémon GO's size system.

What is Espeon weak to?

Espeon is weak to Ghost, Dark and Bug-type attacks.

Is Espeon good for raids?

Espeon can be useful as a Psychic-type attacker, particularly when Psychic damage is super effective.

Is Espeon good in PvP?

Espeon can be used in PvP, but its performance depends on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Should I keep a Hundo Espeon?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Espeon is a perfect-IV specimen and is worth keeping for collection or potential investment.

Should I purify Shadow Eevee before evolving it?

Think carefully before purifying. Purification removes the Shadow status, so compare your goals before making the change.

Does size affect Espeon's IVs?

Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Espeon does not automatically have better or worse IVs because of its size.

Qwilfish Forms & Special Variants

The following variants represent collectible or gameplay versions of Qwilfish that players may encounter.

Regular Qwilfish

The standard version of Qwilfish. It forms the baseline for stats, moves, PvP use and other comparisons.

✨ Shiny Qwilfish

Shiny Qwilfish has alternate coloration while retaining the same species identity and general gameplay role.

🌑 Shadow Qwilfish

Shadow Qwilfish has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability compared with a regular Qwilfish.

✨ Purified Qwilfish

Purified Qwilfish is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Qwilfish. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its investment profile.

🍀 Lucky Qwilfish

Lucky Qwilfish is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades and can be attractive for players planning Stardust-efficient power-up projects.

💯 100% Qwilfish

A perfect Qwilfish has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

📏 XXL Qwilfish

XXL Qwilfish is a size-based collectible variant that can be useful for showcases and collection goals.

📏 XXS Qwilfish

XXS Qwilfish is the smaller size category and is primarily important for collecting and size-based features.

Shadow Qwilfish

Should You Purify Shadow Qwilfish?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the IVs, intended battle role and resource cost before deciding whether the Shadow or Purified version better fits your collection.

Purified Qwilfish

Purified Qwilfish is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Qwilfish. Players should compare the benefits of purification with the loss of the Shadow battle characteristics.

  • Compare the Shadow and Purified battle roles.
  • Check IV changes before purification.
  • Consider Stardust and Candy investment.
  • Do not purify solely because the Pokémon is Shadow.

Lucky Qwilfish

Lucky Qwilfish is primarily valuable as a trade-based collectible and as a potentially more resource-efficient power-up project.

Lucky Investment Tip

If you receive a Lucky Qwilfish with useful IVs for your intended league or collection, it may be worth keeping as a lower-Stardust investment option.

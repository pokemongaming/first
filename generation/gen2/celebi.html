22 — FAQ

Celebi FAQ

Is Celebi a Legendary Pokémon?

Celebi is classified as a Mythical Pokémon rather than a Legendary Pokémon.

What type is Celebi?

Celebi is a dual Psychic- and Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Celebi weak to?

Celebi is vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Bug, Ghost, Dark and Flying attacks.

Does Celebi evolve?

No. Celebi does not have a conventional evolution line in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Celebi available?

Yes. Shiny Celebi has been available through eligible special research and event opportunities.

What is a 100% Celebi?

A 100% Celebi has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Can Celebi be Lucky?

Celebi is a Mythical Pokémon and normal trading restrictions prevent it from being traded like ordinary Pokémon.

Is Shadow Celebi available?

Shadow Celebi should not be presented as normally obtainable unless Pokémon GO officially introduces it through an eligible mechanic.

Can Celebi be purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. A normal Celebi obtained through Special Research does not simply become Purified.

What is an XXL Celebi?

XXL refers to Celebi's size classification rather than its IV percentage.

What is an XXS Celebi?

XXS refers to an extra-small size classification. It does not mean that Celebi has low IVs.

Is Celebi good for raids?

Celebi can be used in raids, but it is generally not considered one of the strongest specialized Psychic- or Grass-type raid attackers.

Is Celebi good in PvP?

Celebi can have PvP applications, but its many weaknesses and current move options should be evaluated against the active GO Battle League metagame.

Can Shiny Celebi be 100% IV?

Shiny status and IVs are separate properties. Therefore, a Shiny Celebi can theoretically also be a perfect 15/15/15 specimen if the encounter produces those IVs.

Can Celebi be XXL and 100% IV at the same time?

Size and IVs are separate properties, so an XXL specimen and a perfect-IV specimen represent different characteristics and can coexist when the game generates both properties on the same Pokémon.

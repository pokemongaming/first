Why Smoochum's Typing Matters

Smoochum's Ice/Psychic combination gives it access to two offensive identities, but its low overall stats keep it from being a major PvE attacker. Its typing is more important for collection purposes, move coverage and limited PvP formats.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Smoochum

Lucky Smoochum is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species form. A Smoochum received through a Lucky Trade can become Lucky while retaining its normal, Shiny or other applicable identity.

Important:

Lucky status does not mean that the Pokémon automatically has 100% IVs. IVs are rerolled during a trade, so a Lucky Smoochum can have different IVs after the trade.

Great League

Ultra League

Master League

Shiny Dunsparce IVs

A Shiny Pokémon can have any eligible IV combination. Finding a Shiny Dunsparce does not automatically mean that it has high IVs.

🌑 Shadow Dunsparce

Shadow Dunsparce is the Shadow variant of Dunsparce. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately because their battle mechanics and resource requirements differ from regular Pokémon.

🌑 Shadow Dunsparce

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure while trading some durability. Whether a Shadow Pokémon is worth investing in depends on its intended role.

Shadow Dunsparce Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Dunsparce's Normal typing. Its primary type weakness remains Fighting-type attacks.

✨ Purified Dunsparce

Purified Dunsparce is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Dunsparce. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon into its Purified form.

💠 Purified Dunsparce

Purified Pokémon have different resource considerations from Shadow Pokémon and are part of a separate collectible category.

Should You Purify Dunsparce?

  • Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow form.
  • Compare IV changes before purifying.
  • Consider the intended PvP or collection role.
  • Do not purify automatically only for cosmetic reasons.

🍀 Lucky Dunsparce

Lucky Dunsparce is obtained through eligible Pokémon GO trades. Lucky Pokémon are a separate collectible category and can be especially attractive for players planning long-term power-up projects.

🍀 Lucky Dunsparce

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up, making them attractive candidates for resource-efficient investments.

Why Keep a Lucky Dunsparce?

  • Lower Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Special Lucky background and collectible status.
  • Useful for collectors completing different Dunsparce forms.
  • Can be a convenient long-term investment if IVs are useful.

💯 100% IV Dunsparce

A perfect Dunsparce has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly referred to by players as a 100% IV or "hundo" Dunsparce.

Is a 100% Dunsparce Always Best?

Not necessarily. A 100% IV Dunsparce is excellent for collecting and high-level general investment, but PvP formats with CP limits can prefer different IV spreads.

XXL & XXS Dunsparce

🔹 XXS Dunsparce

Does XXL or XXS Change Battle Performance?

Size categories should generally be treated separately from IVs and battle investment. A Pokémon's size and IVs are different collection characteristics.

Dunsparce FAQ

What type is Dunsparce?

Dunsparce is a Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Dunsparce weak to?

Is Shiny Dunsparce available?

Murkrow

Murkrow is a Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. In Pokémon GO, Murkrow is best known as the pre-evolution of Honchkrow and is also a useful collector target for Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

Murkrow Overview

Why Murkrow's Typing Matters

Murkrow's Dark / Flying combination gives it a mixture of useful resistances and several important weaknesses. Its Dark typing makes it resistant to Psychic and Ghost attacks, while Flying typing gives it useful matchups against Ground-type attacks.

Should You Power Up Murkrow?

For most players, Stardust and Candy are better spent on a strong Murkrow intended for evolution. Before powering one up, check its IVs, size, Shiny status and whether you want to keep it as a collector's specimen.

Murkrow Evolution

Murkrow evolves into Honchkrow in Pokémon GO. Unlike a normal Candy-only evolution, the evolution requires a Sinnoh Stone in addition to Murkrow Candy.

Purified Murkrow

Purified Murkrow is created by purifying a Shadow Murkrow. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can increase its individual values.

✨

Lucky Murkrow

Lucky Murkrow is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Murkrow

Lucky Murkrow is especially interesting for collectors and players who want to reduce Stardust costs when powering up a Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Murkrow?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky trades can produce useful IV combinations.
  • Lucky Murkrow can be evolved into Lucky Honchkrow.
  • Lucky status makes the Pokémon visually distinct.

100% IV Murkrow

A 100% IV Murkrow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are the maximum possible IVs for a standard Pokémon GO Pokémon.

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP

Perfect Murkrow

A 15/15/15 Murkrow is commonly called a 4-star or 100% IV Pokémon. For collectors, a perfect Shiny, Lucky or XXL Murkrow can be particularly desirable.

Perfect Collection Targets

100% Shiny Murkrow, 100% Lucky Murkrow, 100% Shadow Murkrow and 100% XXL Murkrow can all be valuable collection milestones.

XXL Murkrow

XXL Murkrow is an exceptionally large specimen compared with other Murkrow. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

Murkrow Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building and evolving Murkrow requires Candy, while powering it up requires Stardust and Candy. XL Candy becomes relevant at higher Pokémon levels.

Murkrow PvP Guide

Murkrow's relatively low overall bulk limits its usefulness in many standard PvP formats. Its evolution, Honchkrow, is generally the more relevant battle option.

Great League

Murkrow can be considered in limited formats where its specific typing and moveset are useful, but it is generally not a default Great League investment.

Ultra League

Murkrow's stat profile makes Ultra League investment less attractive compared with many bulkier Pokémon.

Master League

Murkrow's maximum CP is too low to make it a natural Master League choice.

Collector Strategy

Murkrow is particularly interesting for collectors because one Pokémon can combine several desirable attributes. A Shiny Lucky XXL Murkrow, for example, represents a much different collection goal from an ordinary low-IV Murkrow.

Evolution Strategy

If your main goal is Honchkrow, evaluate Murkrow before evolving it. IVs, Shiny status, Lucky status, Shadow status and size cannot be changed after the fact.

Murkrow FAQ

What type is Murkrow?

Murkrow is a Dark / Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Murkrow weak against?

Murkrow is vulnerable to Electric, Ice, Rock and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Murkrow evolve?

Yes. Murkrow evolves into Honchkrow using Murkrow Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Murkrow available?

Yes. Shiny Murkrow is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Murkrow?

A 100% Murkrow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Murkrow?

Shadow Murkrow is the Shadow version of Murkrow and uses the standard Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Murkrow?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision, particularly if you value Shadow Pokémon.

What is Lucky Murkrow?

Lucky Murkrow is obtained through trading and benefits from the reduced Stardust power-up cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is XXL Murkrow?

XXL Murkrow is a very large Murkrow based on its size attributes.

What is XXS Murkrow?

XXS Murkrow is a very small Murkrow based on its size attributes.

Is Murkrow good for raids?

Murkrow itself is generally not a top raid investment. Its greater value comes from evolving into Honchkrow and from collection purposes.

Is Murkrow good in PvP?

Murkrow is generally a limited PvP option because of its low bulk. Its usefulness can vary in special formats.

Can a Shiny Murkrow be evolved?

Yes. Shiny Murkrow can evolve into Shiny Honchkrow.

Can a Lucky Murkrow be evolved?

Yes. Lucky Murkrow can be evolved into Lucky Honchkrow.

Can XXL and XXS Murkrow evolve?

Yes. Size attributes do not prevent a Murkrow from evolving.

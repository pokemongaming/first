Xatu

Xatu is a Psychic- and Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, Xatu is more relevant as a collectible and Great League option than as a top-tier raid attacker. Its many individual variants, including Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, Hundo, XXL and XXS Xatu, make it an interesting Pokémon for collectors.

01 • OVERVIEW

Xatu Pokémon GO Overview

Xatu is a dual Psychic/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves from Natu and does not have a further evolution. In Pokémon GO, Xatu has a modest overall PvE role but can be useful in selected PvP formats. Its combination of Psychic/Flying typing, Shadow availability and several collectible attributes gives it value beyond raid performance.

Why Xatu's Typing Matters

Xatu's Psychic/Flying combination gives it useful resistances to Fighting and Ground attacks while also creating several important weaknesses. Trainers should pay particular attention to Dark, Electric, Ghost, Ice and Rock attacks when using Xatu in battle.

11 • PVP

Xatu PvP Guide

Xatu has considerably more interest in PvP than it does in raids. Its Flying/Psychic typing and charged-move options can give it useful matchups, although its exact ranking depends on the current moves and PvP metagame.

Great League

Xatu can be considered in Great League teams where its typing and moveset provide useful coverage. However, it should be evaluated against the current meta before committing Stardust and Candy.

Ultra League

Xatu can also be experimented with in Ultra League, especially in formats where its Psychic/Flying typing provides valuable matchup coverage.

Master League

Xatu's CP ceiling is considerably lower than the Pokémon normally associated with open Master League, making it a much less natural choice for that format.

15 • LUCKY XATU

Lucky Xatu

Lucky Xatu is a special trade result rather than a separate Pokémon form. Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Xatu potentially attractive for trainers who want a low-cost investment or collection piece.

🍀

Lucky Xatu

Lucky status is obtained through trading and can make a Xatu cheaper to power up than a normal equivalent.

Lucky Xatu IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary wild catches. A Lucky Xatu can therefore be an interesting target when you want a relatively inexpensive high-IV specimen.

Is Lucky Xatu Worth Keeping?

  • Keep a Lucky Xatu with strong IVs.
  • Consider its lower Stardust power-up cost.
  • Lucky Shiny Xatu is an especially attractive collection combination.
  • A Lucky Hundo Xatu is an extremely desirable collection result.
  • Compare the Pokémon's PvP IV spread before investing.

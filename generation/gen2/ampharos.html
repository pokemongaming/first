Ampharos Overview

Ampharos is the final evolution of the Mareep family. It evolves from Flaaffy and is primarily an Electric-type Pokémon. Ampharos can also Mega Evolve into Mega Ampharos, which gains Dragon typing and significantly improved battle performance.

Why Ampharos's Typing Matters

Electric typing gives Ampharos a clear role against Water and Flying Pokémon. It also provides useful resistances to Electric, Flying and Steel attacks while leaving Ampharos particularly vulnerable to Ground-type attacks.

Ampharos PvP Guide

Ampharos has a different role in PvP compared with its raid performance. Its moveset, energy generation and coverage options can make it more interesting in selected league formats.

Great League

Ampharos can be considered when its CP and IV spread fit the Great League limit. Coverage moves can be particularly useful for unpredictable matchups.

Ultra League

Ultra League can provide Ampharos with a larger CP range, allowing it to use its stat profile differently from Great League builds.

Master League

Regular Ampharos generally faces stronger competition in Master League because of its lower maximum CP compared with many Legendary and Mega-level Pokémon.

Ampharos FAQ

What type is Ampharos?

Regular Ampharos is an Electric-type Pokémon.

What type is Mega Ampharos?

Mega Ampharos is an Electric and Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Ampharos weak to?

Regular Ampharos is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Shiny Ampharos available?

Shiny Ampharos can be obtained through eligible Shiny Mareep family encounters and evolutions.

Is Shadow Ampharos good?

Shadow Ampharos can be useful for players who prioritize increased offensive pressure, although its value depends on the moveset and current attacker pool.

Should I purify Shadow Ampharos?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your investment goal before purifying.

What is a 100% Ampharos?

A 100% Ampharos has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Ampharos have better stats?

Size categories should not be treated as higher combat stats. XXL status is primarily a size and collection characteristic.

Can Shiny Ampharos Mega Evolve?

An eligible Shiny Ampharos can retain its Shiny appearance when Mega Evolved.

Is Mega Ampharos good for raids?

Mega Ampharos can be a strong option in appropriate Electric and Mega-supported raid situations.

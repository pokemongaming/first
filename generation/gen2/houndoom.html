09 • PVP

Houndoom PvP Guide

Great League

Houndoom is generally not a top-tier Great League choice. Its high offensive pressure can be interesting in specific cups, but its relatively fragile Defense makes it difficult to use consistently.

Ultra League

Houndoom can find occasional use in limited formats where its Dark and Fire typing is valuable, but it is not normally a first-choice general Ultra League Pokémon.

Master League

Regular Houndoom's CP ceiling and defensive profile make it a niche option rather than a standard Master League staple.

10 • SHINY

✨ Shiny Houndoom

Shiny Houndoom is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collector's Pokémon.

✨ Shiny Houndoom Available

Shiny status does not provide a direct combat stat advantage. Its value is primarily cosmetic and collectible.

Shiny Houndoom & Mega Evolution

A Shiny Houndoom can also be used as the basis for Mega Houndoom, allowing collectors to build a Shiny Mega Houndoom.

🍀 Lucky Houndoom

Lucky Houndoom is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species or form.

Why Keep a Lucky Houndoom?

Lucky Pokémon are attractive for Stardust-conscious trainers because powering them up requires less Stardust than powering up a standard Pokémon.

  • Can be a good Mega Houndoom candidate.
  • Lucky status does not directly increase combat stats.
  • IVs should still be checked before investing.

15 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Houndoom

XXL and XXS describe size variants. They do not change Houndoom's species, typing or base combat stats.

📏 XXL Houndoom

An XXL Houndoom is an unusually large specimen and can be valuable to collectors and size-focused medal hunters.

🔹 XXS Houndoom

An XXS Houndoom is an unusually small specimen and can be useful for collection and size-related achievements.

Do XXL and XXS Change Houndoom's CP?

Size variants should be treated as collection characteristics rather than alternative combat forms. IVs, level and other gameplay factors determine CP.

Houndoom Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

How to Use Houndoom

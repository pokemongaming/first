Bellossom

Bellossom is a Grass-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. In Pokémon GO, Bellossom is primarily interesting as a collection Pokémon and for its role in certain limited PvP situations. It evolves from Gloom using a Sun Stone and is one of Gloom's two possible evolution paths.

Bellossom Overview

Bellossom is the Grass-type evolution of Gloom. Unlike Vileplume, which evolves from Gloom using a Leaf Stone, Bellossom requires a Sun Stone and Candy. This branching evolution makes Bellossom an important part of the Oddish evolution family in Pokémon GO.

Why Bellossom's Typing Matters

Bellossom is a pure Grass-type Pokémon. Its typing determines both the attacks it can use effectively and the types of attacks it is vulnerable to. Grass Pokémon are especially useful against Water, Ground and Rock opponents.

Bellossom FAQ

What type is Bellossom?

Bellossom is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Bellossom weak to?

Bellossom is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

How do you evolve Gloom into Bellossom?

Gloom can evolve into Bellossom using a Sun Stone and the required Candy.

Is Bellossom a Gen 2 Pokémon?

Yes. Bellossom is Pokédex number 182 and was introduced in Generation 2.

Is Shiny Bellossom available?

Yes. Shiny Bellossom is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution opportunities.

Is Bellossom good for raids?

Bellossom is generally not a top-tier Grass raid attacker. Stronger Grass Pokémon are normally preferred for optimized PvE teams.

Is Bellossom good in PvP?

Bellossom can have situational PvP value, particularly in formats where its Grass typing and available moves match the current meta.

What is a 100% Bellossom?

A 100% Bellossom has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Bellossom?

Lucky Bellossom is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Bellossom?

XXL Bellossom has an unusually large size measurement. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Bellossom?

XXS Bellossom has an unusually small size measurement. XXS status does not automatically improve battle stats.

Can Bellossom be Shiny and Lucky at the same time?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can possess both.

Can Bellossom be both 100% IV and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size measurements are separate attributes, so an XXL Bellossom can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Bellossom be both Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status does not prevent a Pokémon from having perfect IVs.

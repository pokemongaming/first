Piloswine

Piloswine is an Ice and Ground-type Pokémon from Generation 2. In Pokémon GO, it is an important evolution-stage Pokémon because it can evolve into Mamoswine, a much stronger Pokémon for raids and other battle roles.

Piloswine Overview

Piloswine is the evolved form of Swinub and the middle stage of its evolutionary family. It has Ice / Ground typing and can eventually evolve into Mamoswine using Candies and the required evolution conditions in Pokémon GO.

Piloswine Type

Piloswine has a dual Ice and Ground typing. This combination gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates several important weaknesses.

❄️ Ice Primary Type
🌎 Ground Secondary Type

Why Piloswine's Typing Matters

Ice attacks are useful against Dragon, Flying and Ground targets, while Ground attacks give Piloswine coverage against several Electric, Fire, Poison, Rock and Steel targets.

A 15/15/15 Piloswine is a perfect-IV specimen. However, because Piloswine can evolve into Mamoswine, a strong IV Piloswine may be more valuable as an evolution candidate than as a final battle investment.

Shiny Piloswine

Shadow Piloswine IV Guide

Do not automatically discard a Shadow Piloswine because it does not have perfect IVs. Its future evolution and Shadow mechanics should be considered before transferring it.

Frustration warning:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. Check current Team GO Rocket event rules before planning a Shadow evolution or investment.

Purified Piloswine

Purified Piloswine is created by purifying a Shadow Piloswine. Purification changes its status and can increase its IVs.

🌟
Purified Status

Purification removes the Shadow status.

💎
IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by the applicable amount, up to the maximum of 15.

Should You Purify Piloswine?

Purification is not automatically the best choice. If you want to evolve the Pokémon for Shadow battle performance, compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

Lucky Piloswine

Lucky Piloswine is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Pokémon mechanics, usually involving a trade.

✨ Lucky Piloswine

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. Lucky status is retained if Piloswine is evolved into Mamoswine.

Why Keep a Lucky Piloswine?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for players building a long-term Mamoswine.
  • Good collection target for Lucky Pokémon collectors.
  • Can be evolved while retaining Lucky status.

Piloswine FAQ

What type is Piloswine?

Piloswine is an Ice and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Piloswine weak to?

Piloswine is weak to several types including Fire, Water, Grass, Fighting, Rock and Steel.

Does Piloswine evolve?

Yes. Piloswine can evolve into Mamoswine.

Is Shiny Piloswine available?

Shiny Piloswine is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Piloswine good?

Shadow Piloswine can be valuable as an evolution candidate, particularly for players interested in Shadow Mamoswine.

Should I purify Shadow Piloswine?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your intended use before purifying.

What is a 100% Piloswine?

A 100% Piloswine has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Piloswine?

A Lucky Piloswine is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the game's trade mechanics and has a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is an XXL Piloswine?

An XXL Piloswine is an unusually large size variant that can be useful for Pokémon GO collectors.

What is an XXS Piloswine?

An XXS Piloswine is an unusually small size variant that can be valuable to collectors.

Is Piloswine good for raids?

Piloswine can contribute to Ice or Ground teams, but evolving it into Mamoswine generally provides a stronger long-term battle option.

Is Piloswine good in PvP?

Piloswine can have niche PvP applications depending on the league, IVs and current moveset, although Mamoswine is generally the more important battle investment.

Should I keep a 100% Piloswine?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Piloswine is a strong collection and evolution candidate, especially if you want a perfect Mamoswine.

Can Shiny Piloswine evolve into Shiny Mamoswine?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Piloswine evolves.

Can Shadow Piloswine evolve?

Yes. A Shadow Piloswine can evolve while retaining its Shadow status.

Does Lucky Piloswine stay Lucky after evolution?

Yes. Lucky status is retained when Piloswine evolves.

Elekid Overview

Why Elekid's Typing Matters

Elekid's pure Electric typing gives it a straightforward type profile. Electric attacks are especially useful against Water and Flying Pokémon, while Ground-type attacks are its major offensive weakness.

IVs determine the additional Attack, Defense and HP values of an individual Elekid. A 15/15/15 Elekid is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

100% Perfect Elekid

A 100% Elekid has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. For collectors, perfect Baby Pokémon can be especially valuable because they are less commonly kept at the Baby stage instead of being evolved.

Perfect IV

15 Attack / 15 Defense / 15 HP = 100% IV

Elekid PvP Guide

Elekid's low maximum CP and relatively modest stats make it an unusual PvP choice. Its evolved forms are generally more relevant for serious competitive play.

Great League

Elekid can theoretically fit under Great League's CP limit, but its performance should be evaluated against the current Cup rules and metagame before investing resources.

Ultra League

Elekid's stat profile makes Ultra League a particularly difficult environment for it. Players looking for competitive Electric Pokémon will generally have stronger alternatives.

Master League

Elekid is not naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the format's typical power level.

Elekid FAQ

What type is Elekid?

Elekid is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Elekid weak against?

Elekid is weak to Ground-type attacks.

What generation is Elekid from?

Elekid was introduced in Generation 2, the Johto region.

What does Elekid evolve into?

Elekid evolves into Electabuzz and can eventually reach Electivire.

Is Shiny Elekid available?

Yes. Shiny Elekid is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Elekid available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and encounter rotations.

What is a 100% Elekid?

A 100% Elekid has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Elekid?

XXL Elekid is a large-size variant. Its size does not automatically indicate high IVs.

What is XXS Elekid?

XXS Elekid is a very small-size variant. Its size is separate from its IVs.

Is Lucky Elekid worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Elekid is useful for collectors and can be attractive for evolution projects because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Should I purify Shadow Elekid?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your collection and evolution goals before purifying.

Is Elekid good for raids?

Elekid is generally not a top raid attacker. Its evolved forms are much more relevant for serious Electric-type PvE teams.

Is Elekid good in PvP?

Elekid has limited competitive relevance. Its low overall power makes its evolved forms more practical for most competitive situations.

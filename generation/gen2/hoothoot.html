Shadow Hoothoot IV Guide

A Shadow Pokémon does not need perfect IVs to be useful. For Hoothoot, the decision to keep a Shadow specimen will often be more about collection goals, evolution and personal preference than raid performance.

  • Check the Shadow appraisal before transferring.
  • Consider evolving a strong Shadow Hoothoot.
  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Check the current rules for removing Frustration.
    • Frustration reminder:

    Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on specific Team GO Rocket events.

    12 · LUCKY

    Lucky Hoothoot

    Lucky Hoothoot is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

    🍀🦉

    Lucky Hoothoot

    A collectible Hoothoot with the Lucky status, making it especially interesting for trainers who enjoy Lucky Pokémon collections.

    Why Keep a Lucky Hoothoot?

    • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
    • Lucky trades can produce useful IV spreads.
    • Lucky Hoothoot can be evolved into Lucky Noctowl.
    • It can be combined with collection goals such as Shiny, perfect IV and size hunting.
    Collection tip:

    A Lucky Hoothoot with excellent IVs can make a particularly interesting collection piece even when Hoothoot is not a major raid attacker.

    20 · FAQ

    Hoothoot FAQ

    What type is Hoothoot?

    Hoothoot is a Normal and Flying-type Pokémon.

    What is Hoothoot weak to?

    Hoothoot is vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

    Does Hoothoot evolve?

    Yes. Hoothoot evolves into Noctowl.

    Is Shiny Hoothoot available?

    Yes. Shiny Hoothoot is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

    Is Shadow Hoothoot available?

    Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

    What is a 100% Hoothoot?

    A 100% Hoothoot has perfect 15/15/15 IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

    What is XXL Hoothoot?

    XXL Hoothoot is an exceptionally large individual according to Pokémon GO's size classification.

    What is XXS Hoothoot?

    XXS Hoothoot is an exceptionally small individual according to Pokémon GO's size classification.

    Is Lucky Hoothoot worth keeping?

    Yes. Lucky Hoothoot is a useful collection piece and benefits from the Lucky Pokémon power-up Stardust discount.

    Should I purify Shadow Hoothoot?

    Not automatically. Consider keeping the Shadow version if you value its Shadow status or plan to use it for a particular collection or battle purpose.

    Is Hoothoot good for raids?

    Hoothoot is generally not considered a strong high-level raid attacker. Its main value is in collecting and evolving it into Noctowl.

    Can Shiny Hoothoot evolve into Shiny Noctowl?

    Yes. A Shiny Hoothoot retains its Shiny status when evolved.

    Can Shadow Hoothoot evolve?

    Yes. An eligible Shadow Hoothoot can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status.

    Can a Lucky Hoothoot evolve?

    Yes. Lucky status is retained when a Pokémon evolves.

    Are XXL and XXS Hoothoot the same as high-IV Hoothoot?

    No. Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Hoothoot can have any IV combination.

Shuckle Overview

Shuckle is a dual Bug/Rock-type Pokémon introduced in the Johto region. It does not evolve and has one of the most unusual stat distributions in Pokémon GO, featuring extremely high Defense and very low Attack.

Purified Shuckle

Purified Shuckle is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Shuckle. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect IVs, moves and resource requirements.

💎

Purification Note

Purification should be considered carefully. A Shadow Shuckle and a Purified Shuckle can serve different collection and gameplay goals.

Lucky Shuckle

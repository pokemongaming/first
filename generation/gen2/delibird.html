Purified Delibird

Purified Delibird is created when an eligible Shadow Delibird is purified.

✨ PURIFIED FORM

✨ Purified Delibird

Purification changes the Shadow Pokémon into a Purified Pokémon and removes its Shadow status.

  • No longer has Shadow status.
  • Can have IV changes through purification.
  • Has a Purified visual effect.
  • Should be evaluated separately from Shadow Delibird.

Delibird FAQ

What type is Delibird?

Delibird is an Ice and Flying-type Pokémon.

Does Delibird evolve?

No. Delibird does not have a normal evolution.

Can Delibird be Shiny?

Shiny Delibird is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when included in the relevant encounter pool.

What is Shadow Delibird?

Shadow Delibird is the Shadow Pokémon version of Delibird obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters.

Can Shadow Delibird be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified, creating a Purified version.

What is a Lucky Delibird?

Lucky Delibird is a Delibird that becomes Lucky through an eligible Pokémon trade.

What is a 100% Delibird?

A 100% Delibird has perfect 15/15/15 Individual Values.

What is XXL Delibird?

XXL Delibird is an unusually large specimen in Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Delibird?

XXS Delibird is an unusually small specimen in Pokémon GO's size system.

Is XXL or XXS Delibird better in battle?

Size categories and IV quality are separate systems. XXL or XXS status does not automatically make a Delibird stronger in battle.

Why Pichu's Typing Matters

Pichu's pure Electric typing gives it a straightforward weakness profile. Ground-type attacks are super effective against Pichu, while Electric, Flying and Steel attacks are resisted.

What Is a 100% Pichu?

A 100% Pichu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. The perfect-IV label is particularly useful for collectors who want to keep a high-quality specimen before evolving it into Pikachu.

Perfect Pichu

15 Attack / 15 Defense / 15 HP = 100% IV.

Pichu PvP Guide

Pichu's low CP and overall stat profile make it an unusual PvP choice. It is not normally a standard Great League or Ultra League investment.

Great League

Pichu can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but its low overall battle performance means it is usually selected for challenge, special formats or personal preference rather than maximum competitive efficiency.

Ultra League & Master League

Pichu is generally unsuitable for these higher CP formats because its maximum CP and stats are far below what is normally required.

Shadow Pichu

Shadow Pichu is a special Shadow variant with the same Electric typing as regular Pichu. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability.

🌑
Shadow Pichu

Electric-type Shadow variant.

⚡
Same Typing

Shadow status does not change Pichu's Electric typing.

Shadow Pichu Weakness

Shadow Pichu retains its Electric typing, so Ground-type attacks remain super effective.

Should You Keep Shadow Pichu?

  • Keep unusual Shadow Pichu specimens for your collection.
  • Check IVs before transferring.
  • Compare the Shadow form with your regular Pichu.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve the Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not purify automatically if you value the Shadow form.

Purified Pichu

A Shadow Pichu that is purified becomes a Purified Pichu. Purification changes its status and applies the standard purification mechanics available to eligible Shadow Pokémon.

Purification decision:

Do not purify a Shadow Pichu solely because the Purified label looks better. Compare its IVs, collection value and your plans for evolution first.

Pichu Candy & Stardust

Pichu requires Candy for evolution and power-up decisions. Because Pichu has relatively low battle stats, many players may prefer saving resources for Pikachu, Raichu or other Electric attackers.

  • Save high-IV Pichu before transferring duplicates.
  • Keep Shiny Pichu separate from ordinary specimens.
  • Check Shadow, Purified and Lucky status before transferring.
  • Keep interesting XXL and XXS specimens for collection.
  • Consider evolution before spending significant Stardust.

For a collection-focused player, a particularly unusual Pichu could combine several traits, such as Shiny + Lucky, Shiny + 100% IV, or XXS/XXL + Shiny. These combinations can be much more interesting than ordinary specimens.

Pichu FAQ

What type is Pichu?

Pichu is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Pichu weak to?

Pichu is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Does Pichu evolve?

Yes. Pichu evolves into Pikachu after meeting the required friendship condition.

Can Pichu evolve into Raichu directly?

No. Pichu evolves into Pikachu first. Pikachu can then evolve into Raichu.

Is Shiny Pichu available?

Yes. Shiny Pichu has been available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is a 100% Pichu?

A 100% Pichu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Pichu?

Shadow Pichu is the Shadow variant of Pichu and retains its Electric typing.

What is Purified Pichu?

Purified Pichu is a Pichu that has been purified from its Shadow state.

What is Lucky Pichu?

Lucky Pichu is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is XXL Pichu?

XXL Pichu is a Pichu with an exceptionally large size classification.

What is XXS Pichu?

XXS Pichu is a Pichu with an exceptionally small size classification.

Is Pichu good for raids?

Pichu is generally not a strong raid attacker because of its low overall stats.

Should I evolve my 100% Pichu?

That depends on your goals. Collectors may prefer keeping a perfect Pichu unevolved, while other players may prefer evolving it through the Pikachu line.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Pichu?

Yes, if you care about size collections, medals or unusual Pokémon. Size is separate from IV quality.

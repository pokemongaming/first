Why Kingdra's Typing Matters

Kingdra's Water and Dragon combination gives it a different defensive profile from most Water-type Pokémon. The Dragon typing reduces the damage taken from several common elemental attacks while also creating a vulnerability to Dragon and Fairy attacks.

Its dual typing also makes Kingdra an interesting specialist for trainers who want a Water Pokémon with Dragon characteristics rather than a conventional mono-Water attacker.

Is 100% Kingdra Worth Keeping?

Kingdra PvP Guide

Kingdra's most notable battle role is PvP. Its unusual Water/Dragon typing and charged-move pressure can make it an interesting pick in formats where it is eligible.

Great League

Kingdra can be useful in CP-limited formats when its current moveset and matchup profile fit the meta. Its Dragon typing helps it resist several common attacks, while its Water typing provides useful offensive coverage.

Ultra League

Kingdra can also appear in Ultra League discussions, although its actual ranking can change as moves, cups and the PvP metagame evolve.

Master League

Kingdra's maximum CP is considerably lower than the highest-end Master League Pokémon, so it is generally less attractive there than in CP-limited formats.

PvP Tip

Do not judge Kingdra only by its Pokédex CP. Its typing, move pressure and individual matchup performance are important when deciding whether it belongs on a PvP team.

Does XXL Change Kingdra's Battle Stats?

Size classification such as XXL does not function as a replacement for IVs. A large Kingdra should therefore not automatically be considered stronger in battle.

XXS Kingdra

Kingdra FAQ

What type is Kingdra?

Kingdra is a Water and Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Kingdra weak to?

Kingdra is weak to Dragon and Fairy-type attacks.

What is Kingdra's maximum CP?

Kingdra's maximum CP is approximately 2,983 at the standard maximum level under the commonly used Pokémon GO stat calculation.

Is Kingdra good for PvP?

Kingdra can be useful in PvP, particularly in CP-limited formats where its Water/Dragon typing and moveset give it favorable matchups.

Is Kingdra good for raids?

Kingdra is generally not considered one of the game's premier raid attackers, but it can still be used when its moveset and typing fit a particular matchup.

How do you evolve Seadra into Kingdra?

Seadra evolves into Kingdra using 100 Horsea Candy and a Dragon Scale.

Is Shiny Kingdra available?

Yes. Shiny Kingdra can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Horsea-family Pokémon.

Is Shadow Kingdra available?

Shadow Kingdra can be obtained through the Shadow Horsea evolution family when Shadow Horsea or Shadow Seadra is available through Pokémon GO's current Shadow content.

Should I purify Shadow Kingdra?

Not automatically. Purification is permanent, so compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

What is a 100% Kingdra?

A 100% Kingdra has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Kingdra worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Kingdra has reduced Stardust costs for power-ups, making it useful for trainers planning a significant investment.

What is XXL Kingdra?

XXS Kingdra is a particularly small Kingdra according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

Does XXL Kingdra have higher IVs?

No. Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Kingdra can have any IV combination.

Can Shiny Kingdra be Lucky?

Can Shadow Kingdra be Shiny?

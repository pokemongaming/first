XXL & XXS Octillery

Pokémon GO can identify individual Pokémon with unusual size classifications. XXL and XXS Octillery are special size variants rather than different species.

🐙 XXL Octillery Extra-large size classification

XXL Octillery is a larger-than-usual specimen. Its size can make it desirable for collection, showcases and size-related medals.

🔹 XXS Octillery Extra-small size classification

XXS Octillery is a smaller-than-usual specimen and can be particularly interesting for collectors completing size-based collections.

Do XXL and XXS Octillery Have Better IVs?

No. Size classification and IVs are separate properties. An XXL or XXS Octillery can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Politoed's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. It is useful against Fire, Ground and Rock-type opponents, while Electric and Grass attacks are important threats to watch for.

Politoed PvP Guide

Politoed is primarily interesting as a PvP Pokémon because its Water typing, bulk and access to different charged-move options can give it useful matchup coverage.

Ultra League

XXL & XXS Politoed

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Politoed are special size variants that can be valuable for collectors and size-focused medal progress.

📏 XXL Politoed

An XXL Politoed is significantly larger than the standard size range for the species. XXL status is determined by the individual Pokémon's size data.

📐 XXS Politoed

An XXS Politoed is exceptionally small compared with the normal size range. Like XXL status, this is an individual size characteristic.

Do XXL and XXS Change Battle Stats?

XXL and XXS classification should not be confused with IVs. A Pokémon's size category is a separate characteristic from its Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Collector tip:

A Politoed that combines a rare appearance such as Shiny with XXL, XXS, Lucky or perfect IV status can be especially interesting for collectors.

Politoed FAQ

What type is Politoed?

Politoed is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Politoed weak to?

Politoed is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Politoed evolve?

No. Politoed is a final evolution in its branch of the Poliwag evolution family.

How do you evolve Poliwhirl into Politoed?

Poliwhirl can evolve into Politoed using the appropriate evolution item and Candy requirements in Pokémon GO. Check the current in-game evolution screen for the exact requirements.

Is Shiny Politoed available?

Yes. Shiny Politoed is available through the appropriate Pokémon GO evolution or encounter path when the Shiny Poliwag family is obtainable.

Is Shadow Politoed good?

Shadow Politoed should be evaluated based on its current moveset, IVs and intended PvP or collection role. Shadow mechanics increase offensive pressure while reducing durability.

Should I purify Shadow Politoed?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, particularly if you are building it for a specific battle format.

What is a 100% Politoed?

A 100% Politoed has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Politoed?

A Lucky Politoed is obtained through trading and has the Lucky status, including reduced Stardust costs for powering it up.

What is an XXL Politoed?

An XXL Politoed is an individual classified as exceptionally large according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Politoed?

An XXS Politoed is an individual classified as exceptionally small according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Politoed good for PvP?

Politoed can be useful in selected PvP formats, particularly when its current moveset fits the team and league meta.

Is Politoed good for raids?

Politoed is generally not considered a top-tier Water-type raid attacker. Its PvP and collection value are usually more important reasons to invest in one.

Can a Shiny Politoed also be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so an individual Pokémon can potentially have both.

Can Politoed be both 100% IV and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so an individual Politoed can potentially combine perfect IVs with an XXL or XXS size classification.

Gligar Overview

Gligar is a dual Ground/Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Johto region. Unlike many Pokémon, Gligar does not evolve simply by reaching a particular level. In Pokémon GO, it evolves into Gliscor using Gligar Candy and a Sinnoh Stone while meeting the evolution timing requirement.

Gligar PvP Guide

Gligar is much more interesting in PvP than its modest maximum CP might suggest. Its defensive stats, useful typing and charged-move options can make it a valuable Great League Pokémon.

Great League

Great League is the most natural competitive environment for Gligar because its maximum CP sits close to the 1,500 CP limit. IV selection matters significantly, so players should not automatically assume that a 15/15/15 Gligar is the best PvP specimen.

Ultra League

Gligar's relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural for standard Gligar. A specific format, limited cup or special ruleset can change that assessment.

Master League

Standard Gligar is generally unsuitable for Master League because its maximum CP is far below the format's usual stat ceiling.

Lucky Gligar

Lucky Gligar is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Gligar

A Lucky Gligar is a desirable trading target for collectors and players who want to reduce the Stardust burden of powering up a Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Gligar?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky trades can produce useful IV spreads.
  • A Lucky Gligar can be evolved into a Lucky Gliscor.
  • Lucky Shiny Gligar is an especially desirable collection combination.
Lucky + Shiny Combination

A Lucky Shiny Gligar combines the visual rarity of the Shiny form with the Lucky trade status.

XXL Gligar

XXL Gligar is an exceptionally large individual compared with the normal size range for the species. Size is separate from IVs, so an XXL Gligar does not automatically have perfect stats.

📏

XXL Gligar

XXL Pokémon are recognized by their unusually large size. Gligar's XXL height range can extend to approximately 1.91 metres according to current Pokémon GO database data.

XXL Does Not Mean 100%

A Gligar can be XXL without being a hundo. Size, CP and IVs are separate characteristics.

Collector Tip

Consider keeping unusual combinations such as XXL Shiny, XXL Lucky or XXL 100% Gligar if collecting is important to you.

XXS Gligar

XXS Gligar is an exceptionally small individual. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification and does not indicate the Pokémon's IV quality.

🔹

XXS Gligar

Current size data places the lower XXS height range at approximately 0.53 metres for Gligar.

XXS Collector Value

XXS Gligar can be worth keeping for collectors, especially when combined with other rare characteristics such as Shiny, Lucky or perfect IVs.

Gligar FAQ

What type is Gligar?

Gligar is a Ground/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Gligar weak to?

Gligar is especially vulnerable to Ice-type attacks and is also weak to Water-type attacks.

What is Gligar's max CP?

Gligar's Level 50 maximum CP is 2,099 with perfect IVs.

Is Gligar good in Pokémon GO?

Gligar's main competitive value is in PvP, particularly Great League, rather than high-level raids.

Does Gligar evolve?

Yes. Gligar evolves into Gliscor using 100 Gligar Candy and a Sinnoh Stone while meeting the nighttime evolution condition.

Is Shiny Gligar available?

Yes. Shiny Gligar is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Gligar?

A 100% Gligar has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is also commonly called a hundo.

Is Shadow Gligar available?

Shadow Gligar exists as a separate Shadow variant and retains Gligar's Ground/Flying typing.

Should I purify Shadow Gligar?

Not automatically. Consider its PvP potential, IVs, collection value and Shadow mechanics before purifying it.

What is Lucky Gligar?

Lucky Gligar is obtained through trading and has the Lucky Pokémon status, which reduces Stardust costs for powering up.

What is XXL Gligar?

XXL Gligar is an exceptionally large Gligar according to Pokémon GO's size system. Size does not determine IV quality.

What is XXS Gligar?

XXS Gligar is an exceptionally small Gligar according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Is 15/15/15 always the best Gligar for PvP?

No. Great League PvP often favors specific IV spreads that maximize stat product or meet particular matchup requirements. A 15/15/15 Gligar is the perfect-IV collection specimen, not automatically the ideal PvP build.

Can Shiny Gligar evolve into Shiny Gliscor?

Yes. Shiny Gligar retains its Shiny identity when evolved.

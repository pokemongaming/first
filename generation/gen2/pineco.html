BUG-TYPE POKÉMON

Pineco

Pineco is a Bug-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. In Pokémon GO, Pineco is best known as the first stage of the Pineco → Forretress evolution family and can also appear in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms or variants.

Pineco Overview

10 — LUCKY

Lucky Pineco

Lucky Pineco is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀 Lucky Pineco

A Lucky Pineco can be a useful collection piece, especially for players building complete Lucky Pokédex collections.

Lucky Pineco Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Special Lucky visual treatment.
  • Useful for Lucky Pokédex collectors.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor.
  • Can still evolve into Forretress.
11 — PERFECT IV

100% IV Pineco

A 100% IV Pineco has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% Pineco = 15/15/15

A perfect Pineco is particularly attractive to collectors and players who want to evolve a perfect specimen into Forretress.

Should You Keep a 100% Pineco?

Yes, especially if you collect perfect IV Pokémon. Before evolving it, consider whether you want the perfect Pineco itself for your collection or prefer having a perfect Forretress.

12 — XXL

XXL Pineco

XXL Pineco is a larger-than-average size variant. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pineco can have any combination of IVs.

XXL

Extra-Large Pineco

An XXL Pineco has a notably large size measurement compared with the normal range.

COLLECTION

Why Keep XXL Pineco?

XXL Pokémon are valuable to collectors, showcases and size-focused Pokédex projects.

XXL does not mean 100% IV.

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Pineco can have low, average or perfect IVs.

13 — XXS

XXS Pineco

XXS Pineco is a smaller-than-average size variant. Like XXL, the XXS classification is separate from Individual Values.

XXS

Extra-Small Pineco

An XXS Pineco falls into the extra-small size category used by Pokémon GO.

COLLECTION

Why Keep XXS Pineco?

XXS Pokémon can be useful for collectors who want rare size variants in their collection.

XXS and IVs are separate.

An XXS Pineco is not automatically a low-IV or high-IV Pokémon. Check the appraisal separately.

15 — PVP

Pineco PvP Guide

Pineco is generally overshadowed by stronger evolved Pokémon in competitive formats. Its relatively low CP ceiling also limits its role compared with more specialized PvP Pokémon.

Great League

Great League is the most natural CP-limited format to investigate for Pineco. However, its usefulness depends heavily on the current moveset, matchup environment and cup rules.

Ultra League

Pineco is less naturally suited to Ultra League because its maximum CP and overall battle profile make it difficult to compete with many stronger Pokémon.

Master League

Pineco is not a realistic Master League investment target for standard competitive play because of its comparatively low CP ceiling.

22 — FAQ

Pineco FAQ

What type is Pineco?

Pineco is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Pineco weak to?

Pineco is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

Does Pineco evolve?

Yes. Pineco evolves into Forretress.

How much Candy does Pineco need to evolve?

Pineco requires 50 Pineco Candy to evolve into Forretress.

Is Shiny Pineco available?

Yes. Shiny Pineco is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Pineco?

A 100% Pineco has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina.

What is XXL Pineco?

XXL Pineco is an extra-large size variant. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Pineco?

XXS Pineco is an extra-small size variant. It does not automatically indicate anything about the Pokémon's IVs.

What is Lucky Pineco?

Lucky Pineco is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the trading system and receives the benefits associated with Lucky Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Pineco?

Not automatically. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, Shadow status, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

Is Pineco good for raids?

Pineco is generally not considered a high-priority raid attacker. Its main value is usually its evolution and collection characteristics.

Is Pineco good in PvP?

Pineco has limited competitive relevance compared with stronger Pokémon. Its suitability depends on the current moveset and cup rules.

GEN 2 • FAIRY / FLYING

Togetic

Togetic is a Fairy and Flying-type Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it sits between Togepi and Togekiss in the evolution family and is available in regular, Shiny, Shadow and Purified variants. Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL and XXS Togetic are special characteristics rather than separate species forms.

01 • OVERVIEW

Togetic Overview

Togetic is the evolved form of Togepi and the middle stage of the Togepi evolution family. It evolves into Togekiss, making it an important part of the Fairy-type family for collectors and players building a complete Pokédex.

Why Togetic's Typing Matters

Togetic's dual Fairy and Flying typing gives it a different defensive profile from pure Fairy-type Pokémon. Its Flying typing also affects how it interacts with Ground-type attacks and creates additional weaknesses that trainers should consider.

05 • EVOLUTION

Togetic Evolution Family

Togetic is the second stage in the Togepi evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Togepi evolves into Togetic, and Togetic can then evolve into Togekiss.

Should You Evolve Togetic?

If your goal is to build a competitive Fairy-type Pokémon from this family, Togekiss is generally the more important long-term evolution. However, keeping a Togetic can be worthwhile for Pokédex completion, collection purposes, or special variants.

08 • PVP

Togetic PvP Guide

Togetic can appear in lower-CP PvP discussions, but its battle value depends heavily on league restrictions, moves and the current metagame.

Great League

Togetic can be considered for certain limited formats where its Fairy/Flying typing provides useful matchups. It should not, however, be treated as a universal Great League recommendation.

Ultra League

Togetic's lower maximum CP makes it less natural for Ultra League compared with Pokémon specifically suited to the format.

Master League

Standard Togetic is not a typical Master League investment. Togekiss is the substantially more relevant member of this evolution family for high-CP play.

PvP recommendations should be updated when moves, move stats, CP limits and the competitive meta change.
09 • SHINY

Shiny Togetic

Shiny Togetic is the alternate-color version of Togetic. It is a collectible variant rather than a stronger battle form.

✨
Shiny Togetic Alternate coloration • Same species • Same typing

Does Shiny Togetic Have Better Stats?

No. Shiny status does not inherently increase Togetic's Attack, Defense, HP or CP. Its value is primarily cosmetic and collectible.

Shiny Togetic Evolution

A Shiny Togetic can evolve into Shiny Togekiss, preserving its Shiny identity.

10 • SHADOW

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Togetic

A Lucky Togetic is a special Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky status is different from Shiny, Shadow and Purified status.

🍀
Lucky Togetic Special trade status • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups

Why Keep a Lucky Togetic?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky status is valuable for collectors.
  • Trading can produce useful IV combinations.
  • A Lucky Togetic can evolve into a Lucky Togekiss.

Lucky Togetic vs Shiny Togetic

These are separate characteristics. A Togetic can potentially combine multiple desirable traits depending on how it was obtained and the game's mechanics.

Is 100% Togetic Rare?

Perfect IV Pokémon are considerably less common than ordinary specimens. A 100% Togetic can therefore be a desirable collector's Pokémon even when it is not the strongest battle investment in its family.

Should You Evolve a 100% Togetic?

If you want a perfect Togekiss, evolving a 100% Togetic preserves the perfect IV combination. This can make a perfect Togetic a useful long-term evolution candidate.

14 • SIZE

XXL Togetic & XXS Togetic

Pokémon GO can record size characteristics for individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS describe an individual Pokémon's size rather than creating separate species with different base stats.

📏
XXL Togetic Extra-large size classification. Primarily valuable for collection, showcases and size-related features.
📐
XXS Togetic Extra-small size classification. Useful for collectors and size-related game features.

Do XXL and XXS Change Togetic's Base Stats?

No. XXL and XXS should not be treated as alternative species forms with different base Attack, Defense or HP values.

Can XXL or XXS Togetic Be Shiny?

Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics. When the game's mechanics allow the combination, a Pokémon can possess both a size characteristic and Shiny status.

XXL / XXS Collection Value

Size variants can be especially attractive to collectors who maintain themed Pokédex entries or participate in size-related features and showcases.

16 • RESOURCES

Togetic Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a Togetic or evolving one into Togekiss requires Candy, while powering up a Pokémon also requires Stardust and, at higher levels, Candy XL.

  • Save Togepi Candy for important evolution projects.
  • Walk Togetic or the evolution family as a Buddy when appropriate.
  • Use Pinap Berries when Candy farming during eligible encounters.
  • Save high-IV Togetic before transferring duplicates.
  • Consider keeping special Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, Purified, XXL and XXS specimens.
  • Reserve Candy XL for Pokémon you genuinely plan to use at high levels.

21 • FAQ

Togetic FAQ

What type is Togetic?

Togetic is a Fairy and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Togetic weak to?

Togetic is vulnerable to Electric, Ice, Rock, Poison and Steel-type attacks.

What is Togetic resistant to?

Togetic has useful resistances to several types, including Dragon, Fighting, Bug, Dark and Grass, while its Flying typing also makes it immune to Ground-type attacks.

Does Togetic evolve?

Yes. Togetic evolves into Togekiss using 100 Togepi Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Togetic available?

Yes. Shiny Togetic is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can also be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Togepi.

Is Shadow Togetic available?

Shadow Togetic can exist through the Shadow Pokémon system when the relevant Team GO Rocket encounter is available.

Can Shadow Togetic be purified?

An eligible Shadow Togetic can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and applies the normal purified-Pokémon mechanics.

What is a Lucky Togetic?

A Lucky Togetic is a Togetic obtained through trading that has Lucky status and benefits from a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is a 100% Togetic?

A 100% Togetic has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Togetic?

XXL Togetic is an individual Togetic recorded as extra-large. XXL is a size characteristic rather than a separate species form.

What is an XXS Togetic?

XXS Togetic is an individual Togetic recorded as extra-small. Like XXL, XXS is a size characteristic.

Are XXL and XXS Togetic stronger?

No. XXL and XXS should not be treated as alternate base-stat forms.

Is Togetic good for raids?

Togetic is generally not a top raid investment. Its evolution, Togekiss, is usually the more relevant Fairy-type battle choice.

Is Togetic good in PvP?

Togetic can have niche value in certain lower-CP or limited formats, but its usefulness depends on the current rules, moves and metagame.

Should I evolve my 100% Togetic?

If your goal is a perfect Togekiss, a 100% Togetic is an excellent evolution candidate because its perfect IVs are retained.

Can Shiny Togetic evolve into Shiny Togekiss?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Togetic evolves into Togekiss.

Can Lucky Togetic evolve?

Yes. Lucky status is retained through evolution.

Can Shadow Togetic evolve?

Shadow Pokémon can evolve while retaining their Shadow status when the relevant evolution is available.

Can Purified Togetic evolve?

Yes. A Purified Togetic can evolve while retaining its purified status.

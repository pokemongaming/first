Purified Heracross

Purified Heracross is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Heracross. Purification removes Shadow status and creates a separate collection and investment option.

✨ Purified Heracross

A Purified Heracross no longer receives the Shadow battle profile. Players should compare the value of keeping a Shadow Pokémon against the benefits of purification before deciding.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Shadow battle mechanics no longer apply.
  • Purification changes the Pokémon's collection form.
  • Consider your raid and PvP goals before purifying.
  • Do not purify automatically only for a higher IV value.

XXL Heracross

XXL Heracross is an unusually large size variant. Its size classification is primarily a collection and showcase characteristic.

XXS Heracross

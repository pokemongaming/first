Swinub Overview

Swinub is a small Ice and Ground-type Pokémon from the Johto region. Although Swinub itself has limited maximum CP, it is an important Pokémon because it evolves into Piloswine and eventually Mamoswine.

Regular Swinub

🐗

Standard Swinub

Regular Swinub is the standard version obtained through eligible wild encounters, eggs, events and other Pokémon GO encounter methods.

Should You Keep Regular Swinub?

  • Keep high-IV Swinub for future evolution.
  • Save Candy for Piloswine and Mamoswine.
  • Consider multiple Swinub for different future builds.
  • Keep special event or costume versions separately.

Shiny Swinub

✨

Shiny Swinub

Shiny Swinub has alternate coloration compared with regular Swinub. A Shiny Swinub can evolve through the same evolution family and eventually become a Shiny Mamoswine.

Collector Tip

If you obtain a high-IV Shiny Swinub, it can be an especially interesting candidate for evolution into Piloswine and Mamoswine.

Shadow Swinub

👤

Shadow Swinub

Shadow Swinub is obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from their regular counterparts.

Why Shadow Swinub Matters

Shadow Swinub itself is usually not the final investment target. However, its evolution potential is extremely important because Shadow Mamoswine can become a powerful damage-focused attacker.

  • Do not automatically purify a strong Shadow Swinub.
  • Check its IVs before making an investment decision.
  • Consider its future evolution into Shadow Mamoswine.
  • Remember that Shadow Pokémon require additional resources.

Purified Swinub

✨

Purified Swinub

Purified Swinub is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Swinub. Purification changes its Shadow status and can change its IV values.

Lucky Swinub

🍀

Lucky Swinub

Lucky Swinub is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are useful for long-term investments because their power-up requirements are generally more resource efficient.

Is Lucky Swinub Worth Keeping?

Yes, particularly if it has useful IVs and you plan to evolve it into Mamoswine. A Lucky Mamoswine can be an attractive long-term investment for players building Ice and Ground-type teams.

100% IV Swinub

💯

Perfect Swinub

A 100% IV Swinub has the IV combination 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Should You Evolve a 100% Swinub?

A perfect Swinub is an excellent long-term candidate for evolution, especially for players who want a strong Mamoswine for raids, collection purposes or high-level Pokémon investment.

XXL Swinub

📈

XXL Swinub

XXL Swinub is a size variant that is larger than a standard Swinub. Size variants are primarily important for collecting and Pokémon GO Showcase participation.

Does XXL Swinub Have Better Battle Stats?

XXL status should not be treated as a normal IV improvement. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Which Swinub Should You Invest In?

  • High-IV Swinub is useful for a regular Mamoswine investment.
  • Strong Shadow Swinub can be valuable for Shadow Mamoswine.
  • Lucky Swinub can reduce long-term power-up costs.

Swinub FAQ

What type is Swinub?

Swinub is an Ice and Ground-type Pokémon.

Can Swinub evolve?

Yes. Swinub evolves into Piloswine and eventually Mamoswine.

Is Shiny Swinub available?

Shiny Swinub can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

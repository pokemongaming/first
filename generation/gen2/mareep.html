Mareep Type

Why Mareep's Typing Matters

Mareep is a pure Electric-type Pokémon. Electric typing gives it a straightforward offensive identity and makes it useful for learning how type advantages work in Pokémon GO.

Its typing also carries a useful resistance profile, although Ground-type attacks are particularly dangerous because Electric Pokémon are weak to Ground.

12 — LUCKY

Should You Keep a Lucky Mareep?

Yes, especially if you enjoy collecting Lucky Pokémon or want to evolve a Lucky Mareep into the later stages of its evolutionary family.

Lucky Mareep Investment Tip

Before powering up a Lucky Mareep, decide whether you want it primarily as a collection Pokémon or whether you intend to evolve it into Flaaffy or Ampharos.

14 — SIZE

XXL Mareep

XXL Mareep is a size variant identified through Pokémon GO's size system. XXL status is separate from IV quality and does not mean the Pokémon is automatically stronger in battle.

📏

XXL Mareep

An XXL Mareep is larger than the standard size range used for ordinary specimens.

🏆

XXL Collection

XXL Pokémon can be valuable for collectors, showcases and size-focused collections.

XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Mareep can have excellent IVs, poor IVs or anything in between.

Collector tip:

If you find an XXL Mareep with strong IVs, Shiny status, Lucky status or another desirable attribute, consider keeping it before transferring or evolving it.

18 — RESOURCES

Mareep Candy is required to evolve Mareep and can also be used for powering up its evolved forms.

How to Use Mareep

22 — FAQ

Mareep FAQ

What type is Mareep?

Mareep is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Mareep weak to?

Mareep is weak to Ground-type attacks.

What generation is Mareep from?

Mareep was introduced in Generation 2 and is part of the Johto Pokédex.

What is Mareep's Pokédex number?

Mareep is Pokédex number #179.

Does Mareep evolve?

Yes. Mareep evolves into Flaaffy using 25 Mareep Candy. Flaaffy then evolves into Ampharos using 100 Mareep Candy.

Is Shiny Mareep available?

Yes. Shiny Mareep is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Mareep available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check current game data before claiming that Shadow Mareep is currently obtainable.

Can Shadow Mareep be purified?

A Shadow Mareep that is eligible for purification can be purified using Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

What is a 100% Mareep?

A 100% Mareep has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is Lucky Mareep rare?

Lucky Mareep can be desirable for collectors because Lucky Pokémon have special visual characteristics and reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Mareep?

XXL Mareep is a Mareep with a very large size category in Pokémon GO. Size is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Mareep?

XXS Mareep is a very small Mareep according to Pokémon GO's size system. XXS status is separate from IVs.

Is Mareep good for raids?

Mareep itself is generally not a major raid attacker. Players interested in battle performance should consider its evolved forms and their current movesets.

Should I evolve a 100% Mareep?

If your goal is to build a perfect-IV evolution family, evolving a 100% Mareep can be worthwhile. Keep in mind that Mareep itself may also have collection value.

Should I transfer an XXL Mareep?

Check its IVs, Shiny status, Lucky status and other attributes before transferring it. Size-focused collectors may want to keep XXL specimens.

Should I keep an XXS Mareep?

If you collect size variants, an XXS Mareep can be worth keeping. Check its other attributes before transferring it.

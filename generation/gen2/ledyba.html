Ledyba Overview

Ledyba is a Bug/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Ledian after collecting the required Candy. Its relatively low combat stats mean that Ledyba is generally more valuable as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a serious raid attacker.

Why Ledyba's Typing Matters

Ledyba's Bug/Flying typing gives it several useful resistances, but it also creates important weaknesses to Rock, Fire, Electric, Ice and Flying attacks. This typing is more relevant for understanding its battle matchups than for high-level raid use.

Ledyba IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to a Pokémon. A perfect Ledyba has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

⭐ 100% IV Ledyba

A 15/15/15 Ledyba is a perfect IV specimen. A perfect Ledyba can be especially appealing to collectors because it represents the maximum IV combination before evolution.

Should You Keep a 100% Ledyba?

Yes, if you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon. Even though Ledyba is not a top raid attacker, a hundo can be valuable as a collection piece and can also be evolved into a perfect-IV Ledian.

XXL Ledyba

XXL Ledyba is a larger-than-normal size variant. Size attributes are separate from IVs, so a Pokémon can be XXL without having perfect IVs.

SIZE XXL Ledyba

An unusually large Ledyba. Useful for collectors interested in size records and special attributes.

COLLECTION XXL + Shiny

Combining a size attribute with Shiny status can create a particularly desirable collection specimen.

Size and IV are independent attributes. An XXL Ledyba does not automatically have high IVs, and a 100% IV Ledyba does not automatically have XXL size.

XXS Ledyba

XXS Ledyba is a smaller-than-normal size variant. Like XXL status, XXS is a separate size attribute and does not indicate the Pokémon's IV quality.

SIZE XXS Ledyba

An unusually small Ledyba that can be kept as part of a size-focused collection.

COLLECTION XXS + Shiny

A Shiny XXS Ledyba can be an especially interesting collector combination.

XXS and XXL are size attributes rather than IV ratings. Always check the appraisal separately when evaluating a special-size Ledyba.

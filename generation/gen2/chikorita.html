Why Chikorita's Typing Matters

Chikorita is a pure Grass-type Pokémon. Grass attacks are particularly useful against Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Chikorita itself has to be careful around several common offensive types such as Fire, Ice and Flying.

Chikorita PvP Guide

Chikorita can be used in specialized PvP situations, but its lower overall stats make it less common than many fully evolved Pokémon.

Great League

Because Great League uses a CP limit, unevolved Pokémon can sometimes have niche applications. However, Chikorita should be evaluated using the current moveset and league rules rather than assuming that a high IV specimen is automatically optimal.

Ultra League & Master League

Chikorita's limited CP potential makes it much less natural for higher CP leagues. Its evolved forms are generally more relevant when building competitive teams.

Is 100% Chikorita Good?

Perfect IVs do not automatically make an unevolved Pokémon a top raid attacker. Chikorita's overall battle performance remains limited compared with stronger evolved Pokémon. However, a 100% Chikorita can be an excellent collection target and may be evolved into Bayleef or Meganium.

XXL & XXS Chikorita

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Chikorita can therefore become special collection targets.

📏 XXL Chikorita

An XXL Chikorita is a particularly large specimen. Its size classification is separate from its IVs, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

📐 XXS Chikorita

An XXS Chikorita is an especially small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS status is independent from IV percentage.

XXL / XXS vs IVs

Size and IVs measure different characteristics. An XXL Chikorita is not automatically a 100% IV Chikorita, and an XXS Chikorita can also have perfect IVs.

Chikorita FAQ

What type is Chikorita?

Chikorita is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Chikorita weak to?

Chikorita is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying, Bug and Poison-type attacks.

Does Chikorita evolve?

Yes. Chikorita evolves into Bayleef and then Meganium.

Is Shiny Chikorita available?

Yes. Shiny Chikorita is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Chikorita be Shadow?

Yes, Shadow Chikorita can exist when it is available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters.

What is Purified Chikorita?

Purified Chikorita is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Chikorita.

What is Lucky Chikorita?

Lucky Chikorita is a Chikorita that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

What is a 100% Chikorita?

A 100% Chikorita has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

Can Chikorita be XXL?

Yes. Individual Pokémon can receive XXL size classifications when their size meets the game's requirements.

Can Chikorita be XXS?

Yes. Chikorita can also be classified as XXS when its size meets the game's requirements.

Can a Shiny Chikorita also be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate attributes, so an eligible Shiny Pokémon can also be Lucky after trading.

Can a 100% Chikorita be XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics. A Chikorita can have 15/15/15 IVs and also have an XXL classification.

Should I power up Chikorita?

For most players, Chikorita is more useful as an evolution and collection project than as a major raid investment. Compare it with stronger Grass attackers before spending significant Stardust.

Is Chikorita good for raids?

Chikorita is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its evolved forms and other stronger Grass Pokémon are usually more appropriate for serious PvE investment.

What is the best Chikorita IV?

For a perfect-IV collection target, the ideal combination is 15/15/15. For PvP, however, the best IV spread can depend on the specific league and CP limit.

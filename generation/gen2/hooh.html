01 — OVERVIEW

Ho-Oh Overview

Ho-Oh is a Fire/Flying Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 2. It does not evolve and has no normal evolution chain. Its special variants and battle statuses include Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky versions.

02 — TYPE

Ho-Oh Type

Why Ho-Oh's Typing Matters

Ho-Oh's Fire/Flying typing gives it several useful resistances, but it also creates a major vulnerability to Rock-type attacks. This is particularly important in raid battles because Rock attacks can deal heavily increased damage to Ho-Oh.

09 — PVP

Ho-Oh PvP Guide

Master League

Ho-Oh can be particularly valuable in Master League because its high overall stats allow it to operate without the low CP limitations of Great League. Its Fire/Flying typing gives it useful matchups, while its moves can create significant shield pressure.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Ho-Oh is generally much more naturally suited to Master League because of its high CP potential. Special formats can change which Pokémon are eligible, so league rules should always be checked.

12 — PURIFIED

Purified Ho-Oh

Purified Ho-Oh is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Ho-Oh. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can alter its IVs and other mechanics.

💎

Purified Ho-Oh

Purified Ho-Oh no longer has Shadow status. Purification can be useful for certain collection, appraisal or gameplay goals, but players should consider the consequences before purifying a strong Shadow Pokémon.

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Ho-Oh

Lucky Ho-Oh is not a separate species or evolution. Lucky is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Ho-Oh

A Lucky Ho-Oh can be an excellent long-term investment because its power-up Stardust cost is reduced compared with a standard Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Ho-Oh?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for expensive Legendary investments.
  • Excellent collector status.
  • Can be especially attractive for high-level Ho-Oh builds.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean perfect IVs.
Lucky IV note:

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor when obtained through trading, but a Lucky Ho-Oh is not automatically a 100% IV Ho-Oh.

22 — FAQ

Ho-Oh FAQ

Is Ho-Oh a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Ho-Oh is a Legendary Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2 and associated with the Johto region.

What type is Ho-Oh?

Ho-Oh is a Fire- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Ho-Oh weak to?

Ho-Oh is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock is especially dangerous because of Ho-Oh's Fire/Flying combination.

Does Ho-Oh evolve?

No. Ho-Oh does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Ho-Oh available?

Yes. Shiny Ho-Oh is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Ho-Oh good?

Shadow Ho-Oh can be valuable for players who want a more offensive Shadow Legendary build. Its value depends on moveset, IVs and the intended battle role.

Should I purify Shadow Ho-Oh?

Not automatically. Purification cannot be undone, so compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

What is Lucky Ho-Oh?

Lucky Ho-Oh is a Ho-Oh obtained through trading that has the Lucky status and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Ho-Oh?

A 100% Ho-Oh has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Ho-Oh?

XXL Ho-Oh is a large-size Ho-Oh. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

What is XXS Ho-Oh?

XXS Ho-Oh is a small-size Ho-Oh. Like XXL, XXS status does not automatically indicate anything about IV quality.

Is Ho-Oh good for raids?

Ho-Oh can be useful in Fire-focused raid situations when equipped with an appropriate Fire moveset. Its overall usefulness depends on the available alternatives and current move balance.

Is Ho-Oh good in Master League?

Yes. Ho-Oh can be highly relevant in Master League thanks to its high stats, typing and charged-move options.

Is a Lucky Ho-Oh automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky status and perfect IVs are different characteristics. A Lucky Ho-Oh can have a range of IV combinations.

Can a Shiny Ho-Oh also be Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky can coexist when the individual Pokémon meets the requirements for both statuses.

Can Ho-Oh be both 100% and XXL?

Yes. Size classification and IV quality are separate characteristics, so an individual Ho-Oh can be both XXL and 15/15/15.

Can Ho-Oh be both Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status and IV quality are independent, so a Shiny Ho-Oh can have perfect IVs.

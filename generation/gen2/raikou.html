Raikou

Raikou is a powerful Electric-type Legendary Pokémon from the Johto region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its strong Electric-type raid damage and its availability in several collectible forms including Shadow, Shiny, Purified and Lucky Raikou.

Raikou Overview

Raikou is one of the Legendary beasts originally discovered in the Johto region. It is a pure Electric-type Pokémon and does not evolve. Its high Attack stat and Electric-type moves make it an important Pokémon for players building raid teams.

Why Raikou's Typing Matters

Electric is an important offensive type in Pokémon GO. Raikou is particularly useful against raid bosses weak to Electric-type damage, including many Water-type and Flying-type Pokémon.

Raikou PvP Guide

Raikou can be used in PvP, particularly in formats where its Electric typing and Legendary-level stats can create useful matchups.

Master League

Raikou can provide Electric-type pressure against certain Master League opponents. Its weakness to Ground-type attacks must be considered carefully during team building.

Great League & Ultra League

Raikou's high CP means lower leagues require specific investment decisions and may not always be the most natural use for a standard raid-focused Raikou.

Raikou Forms & Variants

Raikou can exist in several different collectible and battle-related variants. Some change its battle mechanics, while others are primarily collector features.

⚡ Regular Raikou

The standard form of Raikou and the baseline version for most comparisons.

✨ Shiny Raikou

A rare alternate-color version of Raikou available through eligible encounters.

🌑 Shadow Raikou

A Shadow variant focused on increased offensive pressure with reduced defensive durability.

💎 Purified Raikou

A Raikou obtained after purifying an eligible Shadow Raikou.

🍀 Lucky Raikou

A special trade result that can have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

💯 100% Raikou

A larger-than-usual Raikou primarily valuable for collection and size-record systems.

📉 XXS Raikou

A smaller-than-usual Raikou that can be valuable for collection purposes.

Shadow Raikou

Shadow Raikou is one of the most interesting variants for players focused on offensive raid performance. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from their regular versions.

Should You Power Up Shadow Raikou?

  • Keep strong Shadow Raikou specimens for Electric raid teams.
  • Check the available moveset before investing resources.
  • Consider the higher Stardust and Candy requirements.
  • Do not purify automatically just to improve IVs.
  • Compare Shadow Raikou with your existing Electric attackers.

Purified Raikou

Purified Raikou is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Raikou. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state into a Purified state.

💎 Purified Raikou

Purification can improve certain aspects of a Pokémon's individual values, but it removes the Shadow battle profile.

Shadow vs Purified Raikou

Shadow

Higher offensive pressure with reduced durability.

Purified

Standard battle profile with purification-related benefits.

Lucky Raikou

Lucky Raikou is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading system. A Lucky Pokémon is particularly useful for players planning to invest Stardust into high-level power-ups.

Lucky Raikou Advantage

Reduced Stardust cost for powering up compared with a standard non-Lucky Raikou.

  • Useful for long-term Level 40 or higher investments.
  • Can save a significant amount of Stardust.
  • Does not automatically mean the Pokémon is 100% IV.
  • Can still be useful for raids or collection purposes.

100% Raikou

Perfect Raikou

A 15/15/15 Raikou is considered a perfect IV specimen and is an excellent long-term investment candidate.

Shiny Raikou

Shiny Raikou is an alternate-color version of regular Raikou. Its battle stats and performance are determined by the individual Pokémon rather than the Shiny status itself.

✨ Shiny Raikou

Shiny status is primarily a visual and collector feature. A Shiny Raikou can still have excellent IVs and be powered up for raid or PvP use.

XXL Raikou

XXL Raikou is larger than a typical Raikou. Size variants are mainly relevant for collectors and Pokémon GO's size-related systems.

📈 XXL Raikou

An XXL Raikou can be an interesting collector target while still functioning as a normal Raikou in battle.

XXS Raikou

XXS Raikou is smaller than a typical Raikou. Like XXL, the size classification is primarily a collection feature.

📉 XXS Raikou

An XXS Raikou can be valuable for players collecting rare size variants.

Raikou FAQ

Is Raikou a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Raikou is a Legendary Pokémon from the Johto region.

What type is Raikou?

Raikou is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Raikou weak to?

Raikou is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Shiny Raikou available?

Shiny Raikou can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Raikou stronger than regular Raikou?

Shadow Raikou has a different battle profile with increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Raikou?

Not automatically. Players focused on maximum raid damage should compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making a decision.

What is a 100% Raikou?

A 100% Raikou has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Raikou useful?

Yes. Lucky Raikou can be useful for players who want reduced Stardust costs when powering it up.

Does XXL Raikou have higher battle stats?

Size classification does not automatically make an XXL Raikou stronger in normal battle performance.

Does XXS Raikou have lower battle stats?

No. XXS size is primarily a size and collection classification.

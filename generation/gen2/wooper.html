Wooper Overview

Wooper is a small Water / Ground-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 2. It evolves into Quagsire after being powered up with Wooper Candy. In Pokémon GO, Wooper is more valuable as an evolution, collection and PvP-related Pokémon than as a conventional raid attacker.

02 — TYPE

Wooper Type

💧
Water / Ground Dual Type Pokémon

Why Wooper's Typing Matters

Wooper's Water / Ground combination gives it a useful defensive profile. Most importantly, Ground typing makes Wooper immune to Electric-type damage in the main Pokémon type system and removes the normal Water-type weakness to Electric attacks. However, Grass-type attacks are especially dangerous.

08 — SHINY

Shiny Wooper

Shiny Wooper has an alternate coloration and is one of the more desirable Wooper variants for collectors.

✨

Shiny Wooper

Shiny Wooper has the same basic species, typing and battle mechanics as regular Wooper. Its primary distinction is its alternate Shiny appearance.

Shiny Wooper Collection Tips

09 — SHADOW

Shadow Wooper

Shadow Wooper is the Shadow version of Wooper obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters. Shadow Pokémon have a distinct battle profile and cannot simply be treated as cosmetic variants.

🌑

Shadow Wooper

Shadow Wooper retains its Water / Ground typing but has the Shadow Pokémon battle modifier. This can make Shadow specimens interesting for specialized collections and evolution projects.

Should You Purify Shadow Wooper?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Wooper. If the individual has strong IVs, useful size characteristics, or collection value, keeping it Shadow may be preferable.

Frustration reminder:

10 — PURIFIED

Purified Wooper

Purified Wooper is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Wooper. Purification changes its status and appraisal while also providing the normal benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

Lucky Wooper

Lucky Wooper is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Wooper

Lucky Wooper can be useful for collectors and players who want a lower Stardust investment cost when powering up the Pokémon.

12 — PERFECT IV

100% IV Wooper

A 100% IV Wooper has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Collectors commonly call this combination a "hundo."

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
💯 Perfect Wooper

A 15/15/15 Wooper is a perfect-IV specimen. Its value comes primarily from collection, evolution and personal goals rather than high-end raid performance.

Should You Keep a 100% Wooper?

Yes, if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon or want a perfect Quagsire evolution. A hundo Wooper can also be a useful long-term collection piece even if you never power it up.

13 — XXL

XXL Wooper

XXL Wooper is an exceptionally large specimen that qualifies for Pokémon GO's XXL size category. Size is independent from IV quality.

📏 XXL Wooper Extra-large size category
🏆 Collection Value Useful for size collections

XXL Wooper vs 100% Wooper

An XXL Wooper is not automatically a 100% IV Wooper. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. You can have an XXL Wooper with low IVs, a hundo Wooper that is not XXL, or a specimen that combines both desirable attributes.

22 — FAQ

Wooper FAQ

What type is Wooper?

Wooper is a Water / Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Wooper weak to?

Wooper is especially vulnerable to Grass-type attacks.

Does Wooper evolve?

Yes. Wooper evolves into Quagsire.

Is Shiny Wooper available?

Yes. Shiny Wooper is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Wooper?

Shadow Wooper is a Shadow variant obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters.

Should I purify Shadow Wooper?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow specimen's IVs, collection value and your personal goals before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Wooper?

Lucky Wooper is a Wooper obtained through a Lucky Trade and has the special Lucky status.

What is a 100% Wooper?

A 100% Wooper has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Wooper?

XXL Wooper is a Wooper that qualifies for the game's extra-large size category.

What is XXS Wooper?

XXS Wooper is a Wooper that qualifies for the game's extra-small size category.

Is XXL Wooper better than normal Wooper?

Not necessarily. XXL is a size characteristic and does not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Is 100% Wooper rare?

Perfect-IV Pokémon are desirable collector targets because they require the maximum 15/15/15 IV combination.

Is Wooper good for raids?

Wooper is generally not a strong raid attacker because of its low offensive stats and CP ceiling.

Should I evolve my Shiny Wooper?

That depends on your collection goals. If you want the Shiny evolution family, evolving one is a natural choice.

Can Shadow Wooper be evolved?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can generally be evolved while retaining their Shadow status.

Can Lucky Wooper be evolved?

Yes. Lucky Wooper can be evolved and its Lucky status remains after evolution.

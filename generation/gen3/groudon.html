Groudon Forms FAQ

How many Groudon variations are included in this guide?

This guide covers Regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, Primal, XXL and XXS Groudon.

Is Shiny Groudon stronger than normal Groudon?

Shiny status changes appearance rather than automatically increasing battle statistics.

Is Shadow Groudon good for raids?

Shadow Groudon can be extremely valuable for damage-focused raid teams, although it requires additional investment and has reduced durability.

What is a 100% Groudon?

A 100% Groudon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Primal Groudon a permanent evolution?

No. Primal Reversion is a temporary special transformation rather than a conventional permanent evolution.

Do XXL and XXS Groudon have different battle stats?

XXL and XXS represent size variations and should not be treated as separate battle forms.

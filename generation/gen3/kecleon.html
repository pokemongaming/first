Kecleon Overview

Kecleon is a single-stage Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. Unlike many Hoenn Pokémon, Kecleon does not evolve into or from another Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its most distinctive feature is its camouflage-inspired design, which is reflected in its special encounter mechanic.

Why Kecleon's Typing Matters

Kecleon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it only one traditional type weakness in Pokémon GO, while several common attack categories are resisted. This makes Kecleon relatively straightforward to evaluate defensively, although its overall battle performance is more important than its typing alone.

Kecleon Weaknesses & Resistances

Weakness

🥊
Fighting

Fighting-type attacks deal super-effective damage to Normal-type Kecleon.

Resistance

👻
Ghost

Normal-type Pokémon resist Ghost attacks and Kecleon takes reduced damage from them.

Battle note:

Kecleon's type effectiveness is simple because it is a pure Normal type. Always consider the actual move being used rather than judging a matchup from species typing alone.

Kecleon Attack, Defense & HP

Kecleon has a defensive-leaning stat distribution, with Defense higher than its Attack and moderate HP. Its maximum CP is substantially lower than that of many Legendary and fully evolved raid-focused Pokémon.

100% IV Kecleon

A 15/15/15 Kecleon is commonly called a 100% IV or hundo Kecleon. For collectors, finding a hundo Kecleon can be particularly desirable because Kecleon encounters can be less common than ordinary wild Pokémon encounters.

Kecleon PvP Guide

Lucky Kecleon

Lucky Kecleon is not a separate species or permanent form. Lucky status is an attribute obtained through Pokémon trading.

🍀

Lucky Kecleon

A Kecleon can become Lucky through a successful trade that results in Lucky Pokémon status.

✨

Lucky Benefits

Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups, making Lucky Kecleon useful for collectors who want a cheaper investment.

Should You Keep a Lucky Kecleon?

Yes, especially if you collect Lucky Pokémon. A Lucky Kecleon can also be an attractive combination when paired with strong IVs or a rare size classification.

XXL Kecleon

XXL Kecleon refers to an individual Kecleon with an exceptionally large recorded size. XXL is an individual size classification rather than a different species.

📏

XXL Kecleon

An XXL Kecleon is a large-size individual that receives the corresponding size designation in Pokémon GO.

🏆

Collector Value

XXL Kecleon can be desirable to collectors, particularly when combined with Shiny, Lucky or high-IV characteristics.

XXL + 100% IV Kecleon

An XXL Kecleon with 15/15/15 IVs can be especially valuable to collectors because it combines a rare size classification with a perfect IV spread.

XXS Kecleon

XXS Kecleon refers to an individual Kecleon with an exceptionally small recorded size. Like XXL, XXS is an individual size classification rather than a separate Pokémon form.

🔬

XXS Kecleon

An XXS Kecleon receives the corresponding small-size classification when its recorded size meets the game's criteria.

🏆

Collector Value

XXS Kecleon can be valuable for size collectors, especially when combined with a Shiny or high-IV specimen.

Kecleon FAQ

What type is Kecleon?

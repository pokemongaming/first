What Does Volbeat's Bug Typing Mean?

Volbeat's pure Bug typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. Bug attacks are particularly useful against Psychic, Dark and Grass Pokémon, although Volbeat itself does not have the offensive stats needed to compete with the strongest Bug attackers.

10 • PVP

Volbeat PvP Guide

Volbeat can be considered for certain limited formats, but it is not generally regarded as a premier Open Great League or Master League Pokémon.

Great League

Volbeat's CP range allows it to be considered around Great League's CP environment, but its overall battle performance is highly dependent on the current move pool and limited cups.

Ultra League

Volbeat's stat ceiling makes Ultra League investment relatively niche. Check the current format before committing substantial Stardust.

Master League

Volbeat is generally not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP is much lower than the Pokémon commonly used in that league.

22 • FAQ

Volbeat FAQ

What type is Volbeat?

Volbeat is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Volbeat weak to?

Volbeat is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock attacks.

Does Volbeat evolve?

No. Volbeat does not have a normal evolution.

Is Shiny Volbeat available?

Shiny Volbeat is an obtainable special variant when it is included in eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Volbeat good for raids?

Volbeat is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker and is more valuable as a collector Pokémon.

What is a 100% Volbeat?

A 100% Volbeat has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Volbeat?

Lucky Volbeat is a Volbeat obtained through trading that has the Lucky Pokémon status.

What is Shadow Volbeat?

Shadow Volbeat is the Shadow version of Volbeat and has the standard Shadow Pokémon battle characteristics.

What is Purified Volbeat?

Purified Volbeat is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Volbeat.

What is XXL Volbeat?

XXL Volbeat is an exceptionally large Volbeat with a size classification that makes it interesting to collectors.

What is XXS Volbeat?

XXS Volbeat is an exceptionally small Volbeat and can be a desirable size-collection Pokémon.

Is a Shiny Hundo Volbeat rare?

Yes. A Shiny 15/15/15 Volbeat combines two separate desirable characteristics, making it much harder to obtain than an ordinary Volbeat.

Is Lucky Hundo Volbeat worth keeping?

Yes. A Lucky Hundo combines perfect IVs with Lucky status and is a strong collector specimen.

Can Volbeat be purified?

A Volbeat can only be purified if you have an eligible Shadow Volbeat.

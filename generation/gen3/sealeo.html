Sealeo

Sealeo is a Water and Ice-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. It is the middle evolution of the Spheal family, evolving from Spheal and eventually evolving into Walrein. In Pokémon GO, Sealeo can be useful as an evolution stage, a collection target, and a potential PvP option depending on its moveset and league eligibility.

Sealeo Overview

Sealeo is the second Pokémon in the Spheal evolutionary family. It evolves from Spheal and can later evolve into Walrein. Its dual Water and Ice typing gives it access to two useful offensive types, while also creating a specific set of weaknesses and resistances.

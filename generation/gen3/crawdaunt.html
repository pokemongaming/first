Crawdaunt Overview

Crawdaunt is the final evolution of Corphish and was introduced in Generation 3. It combines Water and Dark typing, giving it an unusual offensive profile compared with many other Water Pokémon.

Why Crawdaunt's Typing Matters

Crawdaunt's Water/Dark combination gives it access to two distinct offensive identities. Its Water typing provides useful matchups against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Dark attacks can be valuable against Psychic and Ghost targets.

Crawdaunt Special Variants

Crawdaunt can be encountered or evaluated in several special categories. These include Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky status, as well as IV and size classifications.

✨

Shiny Crawdaunt

Shiny Crawdaunt has an alternate coloration and is a popular collection target. Shiny status does not automatically make a Crawdaunt stronger in battle.

🌑

Shadow Crawdaunt

Shadow Crawdaunt has the Shadow battle modifier, giving it a different offensive and defensive profile from a regular Crawdaunt.

💠

Purified Crawdaunt

Purified Crawdaunt is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Crawdaunt. Purification changes its Shadow status and affects its IVs and resource requirements.

🍀

Lucky Crawdaunt

Lucky Crawdaunt is a special trade outcome. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and have a minimum IV floor.

💯

100% Crawdaunt

A 100% Crawdaunt has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is also commonly described as a hundo Crawdaunt.

📏

XXL Crawdaunt

XXL Crawdaunt is a size category. XXL status does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs and should be evaluated separately from its battle statistics.

📐

XXS Crawdaunt

XXS Crawdaunt is a small-size category used by Pokémon GO. Like XXL status, size is independent from IV quality.

Crawdaunt FAQ

What type is Crawdaunt?

Crawdaunt is a Water and Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Crawdaunt weak to?

Crawdaunt is vulnerable to Electric, Grass, Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Crawdaunt good for raids?

Crawdaunt can contribute in Water or Dark roles, but its overall raid value should be compared with stronger available alternatives.

Is Shiny Crawdaunt available?

Shiny Crawdaunt is available when obtained through an eligible Shiny Corphish encounter and evolution.

What is a 100% Crawdaunt?

What is Shadow Crawdaunt?

Should I purify Shadow Crawdaunt?

Can Crawdaunt be 100% and XXL?

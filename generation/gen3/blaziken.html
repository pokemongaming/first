01 • OVERVIEW

Blaziken Overview

Blaziken is the final evolution of Torchic and a dual-type Fire/Fighting Pokémon. Its combination of offensive stats and access to Fire- and Fighting-type attacks gives it multiple uses in Pokémon GO.

Why Blaziken's Typing Matters

Blaziken's Fire typing gives it access to strong Fire-type offensive options, while its Fighting typing provides a second offensive role. The dual typing also determines the attacks it resists and the types that can deal super-effective damage.

09 • PVP

Blaziken PvP Guide

Great League & Ultra League

Blaziken's offensive profile and access to Fighting and Fire attacks can create interesting PvP matchups. However, its relatively fragile defenses mean that shield management and timing are important.

Master League

Standard Blaziken is less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League competition than some bulkier Legendary options. Its value can depend heavily on the current metagame and available moves.

PvP Tip

Do not judge Blaziken solely by its raid performance. Fast charged moves, bait potential, coverage and shield advantage can change its PvP value.

13 • PURIFIED

Purified Blaziken

Purified Blaziken is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Blaziken. Purification changes the Pokémon's mechanics and resource requirements.

💎 Purified Blaziken

Purified Blaziken no longer has Shadow status. Purification also removes Frustration and can change the Pokémon's IVs and resource requirements.

Should You Purify Shadow Blaziken?

Do not purify automatically. If your primary goal is maximum Shadow raid damage, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be more valuable. Consider the individual Pokémon, IVs, moves and your available resources before making the decision.

24 • FAQ

Blaziken FAQ

What type is Blaziken?

Blaziken is a Fire/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Blaziken weak to?

Blaziken is weak to Water, Ground, Flying and Psychic attacks.

Is Shiny Blaziken available?

Yes. Shiny Blaziken is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Blaziken good?

Shadow Blaziken can be valuable for players prioritizing offensive damage, although its lower durability and resource requirements should be considered.

Should I purify Shadow Blaziken?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

What is Lucky Blaziken?

Lucky Blaziken is a Blaziken obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have special trade characteristics and reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Blaziken?

A 100% Blaziken has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Mega Blaziken good for raids?

Mega Blaziken can be an excellent Fire/Fighting raid option and can provide Mega-related team benefits while active.

Can Shiny Blaziken become Mega Blaziken?

Yes. A Shiny Blaziken can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution.

What is XXL Blaziken?

XXL Blaziken is a larger-than-standard size variant. Size does not automatically make it stronger in battle.

What is XXS Blaziken?

XXS Blaziken is an exceptionally small size variant and is primarily relevant to collection and size-related gameplay.

Is Blaziken good for raids?

Blaziken can be useful as a Fire or Fighting attacker, while Mega Blaziken can provide significantly greater raid value.

Is Blaziken good in PvP?

Blaziken can have PvP applications, but its effectiveness depends on the league, moveset, shields and current meta.

09 — 100% MASQUERAIN

100% IV Masquerain

A 100% Masquerain has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These Pokémon are often called “hundos” by Pokémon GO players.

15 / 15 / 15
Perfect IV Masquerain

Perfect IV status is determined by IVs rather than simply by the Pokémon's displayed CP.

Should You Keep a 100% Masquerain?

  • Keep it if you collect perfect IV Pokémon.
  • It can be useful for Pokédex and appraisal collections.
  • Consider evolving a perfect Surskit if you are building a hundo Masquerain.
  • A hundo is especially attractive to collectors even when the Pokémon is not a top raid attacker.

13 — LUCKY MASQUERAIN

Lucky Masquerain

Lucky Masquerain is created through Pokémon trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up than normal Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Masquerain

A Lucky Masquerain is a special trade outcome and is particularly attractive to collectors.

✨

Lucky IVs

Lucky Pokémon receive a guaranteed minimum IV floor, making Lucky trades useful when looking for stronger specimens.

Lucky Masquerain Collection

  • Lucky status is obtained through trading.
  • Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny status.
  • A Pokémon can be both Lucky and Shiny.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.
14 — XXL MASQUERAIN

XXL Masquerain

XXL Masquerain is a size variation representing an especially large individual. XXL status is separate from IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

📏 XXL Masquerain

An exceptionally large Masquerain that can be useful for size-focused collections and showcases.

📊 Size vs IV

A Pokémon's size does not determine whether it has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What Makes an XXL Masquerain Special?

XXL is a size classification rather than a separate Pokémon species or evolutionary form. An XXL Masquerain can also have other properties such as Shiny, Lucky or high IV status.

15 — XXS MASQUERAIN

XXS Masquerain

XXS Masquerain represents an especially small individual. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification rather than a different species.

🔬 XXS Masquerain

An exceptionally small Masquerain that can be valuable for collectors who track Pokémon size variations.

📐 Size Collection

Collecting both XXL and XXS Masquerain can add another layer to a complete Masquerain collection.

Can XXS Masquerain Be Shiny or Lucky?

Yes. Size classification and special statuses are separate attributes. Depending on the individual encounter and game mechanics, a Masquerain can have multiple collectible properties.

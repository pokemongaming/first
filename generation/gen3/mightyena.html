Mightyena Overview

Mightyena is a Dark-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Poochyena after collecting the required Candy. In Pokémon GO, Mightyena is more notable as a Pokédex and collection Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker.

Why Mightyena's Typing Matters

Mightyena's pure Dark typing gives it access to Dark-type offensive options and determines its defensive matchup profile. Dark-type Pokémon are useful against Psychic- and Ghost-type opponents, while Fighting-, Bug- and Fairy-type attacks are important threats to Mightyena.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Mightyena

Lucky Mightyena is a special trade outcome. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and have a guaranteed minimum IV floor determined by the game's Lucky trade mechanics.

🍀
Lucky Mightyena

A Lucky Mightyena can be a useful low-cost collection or investment project, particularly when obtained with strong IVs.

Lucky Mightyena Benefits

  • Requires less Stardust to power up than a normal Pokémon.
  • Can have strong IVs after a Lucky trade.
  • Useful for Lucky Pokédex and collection goals.
  • Can be kept as a special trade milestone.
  • A Lucky status does not change Mightyena's Dark typing.
Important:

Lucky status and IV perfection are separate characteristics. A Lucky Mightyena is not automatically a 100% Mightyena.

Why Collect a 100% Mightyena?

A perfect Mightyena can be valuable to collectors because the 15/15/15 combination is relatively uncommon. It can also serve as a personal trophy Pokémon even when the species is not a top battle performer.

100% IV vs High-Level Mightyena

IV perfection should not be confused with Pokémon level. A 100% Mightyena at a low level will still require Stardust and Candy before reaching its maximum potential level.

22 • FAQ

Mightyena FAQ

What type is Mightyena?

Mightyena is a pure Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Mightyena weak to?

Mightyena is weak to Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Mightyena a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Mightyena is a standard Pokémon from the Hoenn region.

What Pokémon evolves into Mightyena?

Poochyena evolves into Mightyena.

Is Shiny Mightyena available?

Shiny Mightyena is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Mightyena available?

Shadow Mightyena availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotations.

Can Shadow Mightyena be purified?

An eligible Shadow Mightyena can be purified, subject to the game's current purification mechanics.

Is Purified Mightyena better than Shadow Mightyena?

Not automatically. Purification removes the Shadow battle modifier, so the better choice depends on your collection goals and intended use.

Can Mightyena be Lucky?

Yes. Mightyena can become Lucky through the applicable Pokémon GO trade mechanics.

What is a 100% Mightyena?

A 100% Mightyena has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Mightyena be XXL?

Yes. Pokémon GO can classify individual Mightyena as XXL depending on its generated size.

Can Mightyena be XXS?

Yes. Individual Mightyena can also receive the XXS size classification.

Is XXL Mightyena automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Mightyena can have any IV combination.

Is XXS Mightyena automatically low IV?

No. XXS size does not determine Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Is Mightyena good for raids?

Mightyena is generally not among the strongest Dark-type raid attackers, although it can still be used for casual or themed teams.

Is Mightyena good for PvP?

Its PvP value depends on the current moveset, league format and metagame. Check current rankings before committing resources.

What is the best Mightyena moveset?

For a Dark-oriented build, Snarl and Crunch are natural moves to consider. Current move availability should always be checked before publication.

Should I power up a 100% Mightyena?

A 100% Mightyena is an excellent collection specimen, but whether it deserves significant Stardust investment depends on your preferred battle format and collection goals.

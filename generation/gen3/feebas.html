Feebas

Feebas is a Water-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. Despite its intentionally weak appearance, Feebas evolves into the powerful and elegant Milotic. In Pokémon GO, Feebas is especially valuable for players working toward Milotic, collecting special variants and completing their Pokédex.

Feebas Overview

Feebas is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is known for having extremely low offensive stats, but its evolution into Milotic makes it an important Pokémon for collectors and players building Water-type teams.

Why Feebas's Water Typing Matters

Feebas is a pure Water-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it weaknesses to Electric and Grass attacks while allowing it to resist Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

Feebas Evolution

Feebas evolves into Milotic. The exact requirements for evolution should be checked in-game because Pokémon GO evolution requirements can depend on Candy and special Buddy tasks.

Evolution

Feebas → Milotic

Evolution note:

Before evolving a rare or high-IV Feebas, consider whether you want to keep it as a collector's variant or evolve it into Milotic.

Feebas Forms & Special Variants

Feebas can exist in several collectible forms and variants. Some affect appearance, some affect resource costs, and some are connected to IVs or Pokémon size.

🐟

Regular Feebas

The standard version of Feebas and the base form used for evolution into Milotic.

✨

Shiny Feebas

Shiny Feebas has alternate coloration and is primarily valuable as a collector's Pokémon. A Shiny Feebas can evolve into Shiny Milotic.

🌑

Shadow Feebas

Shadow Feebas is a Shadow variant with the standard Shadow Pokémon battle characteristics. Availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

✨

Purified Feebas

Purified Feebas is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Feebas. Purification changes its Shadow status and affects its investment path.

🍀

Lucky Feebas

Lucky Feebas has reduced Stardust costs for power-ups. Lucky status is especially useful when planning a long-term investment.

💯

100% Feebas

A 100% Feebas has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. Many players keep one as a collector's specimen or evolve it into Milotic.

⬆️

XXL Feebas

XXL Feebas is a larger size variant. Size does not automatically increase its battle stats.

⬇️

XXS Feebas

XXS Feebas is a smaller size variant intended primarily for collection and Pokédex tracking.

Shiny Feebas

Shiny Feebas is a collectible alternate-color version of regular Feebas. Players who obtain one can choose to keep it as Feebas or evolve it into Shiny Milotic.

Collector Recommendation

Consider keeping at least one Shiny Feebas before evolving additional Shiny copies.

Shadow Feebas

Shadow Feebas retains the same species and Water typing as regular Feebas, but Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile and investment cost.

Purified Feebas

Purified Feebas is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Feebas. Before purifying, consider whether you want to keep the Shadow version for collection purposes.

Purification Tip

Do not purify automatically. Compare the value of keeping the Shadow Pokémon with the benefits of purification.

Lucky Feebas

Lucky Feebas requires less Stardust to power up than a standard Feebas. Lucky status can make a high-level Milotic project less expensive.

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term Milotic investment.
  • Valuable for Lucky Pokémon collectors.
  • Consider IVs before evolving.

Should You Evolve a 100% Feebas?

A perfect IV Feebas can be an excellent candidate for evolution if you want a high-IV Milotic. However, collectors may prefer to keep a perfect Feebas and evolve another high-IV specimen.

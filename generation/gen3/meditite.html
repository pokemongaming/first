Meditite

Meditite is a Fighting and Psychic-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first stage of the Meditite → Medicham evolution family and as a Pokémon with relevance in specific PvP situations and collection-focused builds.

01 • OVERVIEW

Meditite Overview

Meditite is a dual Fighting/Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves into Medicham after receiving enough Meditite Candy. Unlike Medicham, Meditite has relatively modest combat stats and is usually more interesting as an evolution, collection, PvP-cup or special-form Pokémon than as a major raid attacker.

06 • BATTLE USE

Is Meditite Good for Raids?

Meditite is not normally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its relatively low Attack stat and low maximum CP make it much less suitable for conventional high-level PvE than its evolved form, Medicham, or specialized Fighting-type attackers.

Should You Power Up Meditite?

Most players should first decide whether the Meditite is being kept for PvP, evolution, a special-form collection, a perfect-IV collection or simply as a personal favorite.

07 • PVP

Meditite PvP Guide

Meditite's low CP ceiling makes it a niche PvP Pokémon rather than a general-purpose competitive choice. Its evolution, Medicham, is considerably more relevant when discussing the family's PvP history and investment potential.

Great League

Meditite can fit within Great League's CP limit, but players should evaluate it against the current format and available moves rather than assuming that an unevolved Pokémon is automatically competitive.

Ultra League

Meditite's low maximum CP makes Ultra League an unnatural environment for the species.

Master League

Meditite is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for the format.

08 • EVOLUTION

Meditite Evolution

Meditite evolves into Medicham using Meditite Candy. There is no branching evolution from Meditite.

Evolution Tip

Before evolving a Meditite, check its IVs, CP, appraisal, size, Shiny status and any personal collection goals. Do not automatically evolve every high-CP specimen.

09 • SHINY

Shiny Meditite

Shadow Meditite and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration as their Charged Attack when obtained. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration. Always check the current event rules before spending resources on a Shadow Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Meditite?

Do not purify a Shadow Meditite automatically. Compare its IVs, potential evolution, battle use, collection value and your personal goals before making the decision.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Meditite

Lucky Meditite is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

🍀
Lucky Meditite

A traded Meditite can become Lucky when the trade results in the Lucky Pokémon status.

⭐
Lucky IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, making Lucky trades attractive for collectors and investment projects.

Should You Keep a Lucky Meditite?

A Lucky Meditite can be useful for a collection, evolution project or lower-Stardust investment. Whether it should be evolved depends on its IVs and your goals for Medicham.

13 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Meditite

A 100% IV Meditite has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stat IV. These are the maximum possible IV values for a standard Pokémon.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV
Perfect Meditite

A 15/15/15 Meditite is a perfect-IV specimen. Whether it should be evolved depends on the role you want it to play and the current usefulness of Medicham.

How to Check a 100% Meditite

  • Open the Pokémon appraisal screen.
  • Check Attack IV.
  • Check Defense IV.
  • Check HP IV.
  • Confirm all three IV values are 15.

How to Use Meditite

Before Evolving

22 • FAQ

Meditite FAQ

What type is Meditite?

Meditite is a Fighting and Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Meditite weak to?

Meditite is vulnerable to Flying, Ghost and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Meditite evolve?

Yes. Meditite evolves into Medicham.

Is Shiny Meditite available?

Shiny Meditite is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Meditite available?

Shadow Meditite can be available through Team GO Rocket-related content when included in the current game rotation.

Can Shadow Meditite be purified?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Meditite can be purified.

Can Meditite be Lucky?

Yes. Meditite can become Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is a 100% Meditite?

A 100% Meditite has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Meditite?

An XXL Meditite is a Pokémon classified as extra large by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Meditite?

An XXS Meditite is a Pokémon classified as extra small by Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Meditite good for raids?

Meditite is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its evolved form and other specialized Pokémon are usually more appropriate for serious PvE teams.

Is Meditite good for PvP?

Meditite has niche PvP potential, but its low maximum CP makes it much less broadly useful than its evolved form, Medicham.

Should I evolve my 100% Meditite?

If your goal is to build a perfect Medicham, a 15/15/15 Meditite is an excellent candidate. However, check the current usefulness of Medicham before spending resources.

Should I purify Shadow Meditite?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your battle and collection goals before making purification permanent.

Can Shiny Meditite evolve into Shiny Medicham?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Meditite evolves into Medicham.

Can XXL Meditite evolve?

Yes. An XXL Meditite can evolve, although collectors may choose to keep the original specimen for their size collection.

Can XXS Meditite evolve?

Yes. An XXS Meditite can evolve into Medicham.

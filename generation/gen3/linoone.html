Linoone

Linoone is a Normal-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the evolution of Hoenn Zigzagoon and can appear in regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky variants. Linoone can also be encountered with different IV combinations and size measurements including XXL and XXS.

01 — OVERVIEW

Linoone Overview

Linoone is the evolved form of Hoenn Zigzagoon and was introduced with Generation 3. It is a pure Normal-type Pokémon with a long, striped body and a distinctive running-focused design.

In Pokémon GO, Linoone is more relevant as a collection, evolution and specialty Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker. Trainers may still want to keep Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL and XXS specimens.

Why Linoone's Typing Matters

Linoone has a pure Normal typing, meaning it does not gain an additional elemental type from its standard Hoenn form. This gives it a simple defensive profile but limits its super-effective offensive coverage.

09 — PVP

Linoone PvP Guide

Linoone's low maximum CP means it can theoretically fit within lower-league CP limits, but its practical PvP value depends heavily on its current moveset, stats and the evolving metagame.

Great League

Great League is the most natural CP-limited format to investigate for Linoone. However, trainers should compare its current moveset and performance against other Normal-type and general-purpose Pokémon before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Master League

10 — SHINY

Shiny Linoone

Shiny Linoone is an alternate-color variant of Linoone. Shiny status does not give Linoone a different species identity or automatically improve its battle statistics.

SHINY

Shiny Linoone

Shiny Linoone is primarily valuable for collectors. A Shiny Linoone can also have additional attributes such as high IVs, Lucky status or special size.

Shiny Linoone Collection Goals

  • Keep your first Shiny Linoone if you are building a complete Shiny Pokédex.
  • Look for strong IVs if you want a competitive specimen.
  • Consider trading duplicate Shiny Linoone for Lucky chances.
  • Check the Pokémon's size if you collect XXL and XXS specimens.

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Linoone

Lucky Linoone is not a separate species or form. Lucky is a special status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and have a guaranteed minimum IV floor from the Lucky trade mechanic.

LUCKY

Lucky Linoone

A Lucky Linoone can be useful for collectors and trainers who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Linoone.

Why Keep a Lucky Linoone?

  • Lucky Pokémon cost less Stardust to power up.
  • Trading can produce Lucky Pokémon.
  • A Lucky Linoone can also be Shiny.
  • A Lucky Linoone can also have perfect IVs.
  • Lucky status and size attributes can coexist.
Lucky is a Pokémon status rather than a separate Linoone form. For database purposes, it is better to display Lucky as an attribute attached to the Pokémon.

24 — FAQ

Linoone FAQ

What type is Linoone?

Hoenn Linoone is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Linoone weak to?

Linoone is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Linoone a Gen 3 Pokémon?

Yes. Linoone was introduced in Generation 3 and is part of the Hoenn Pokédex.

What is Linoone's Pokédex number?

Linoone is National Pokédex number 264.

Is Shiny Linoone available?

Yes. Shiny Linoone is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Linoone available?

Shadow Linoone can be available when Pokémon GO includes it in the current Shadow Pokémon rotation.

Can Shadow Linoone be purified?

An eligible Shadow Linoone can be purified. Always check the current in-game availability and mechanics.

Is Lucky Linoone a separate form?

No. Lucky is a special Pokémon status rather than a separate species or evolutionary form.

What is a 100% Linoone?

A 100% Linoone has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Linoone?

XXL Linoone is an individual Linoone that qualifies for the XXL size category.

What is XXS Linoone?

XXS Linoone is an individual Linoone that qualifies for the XXS size category.

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate attributes and can coexist.

Does Linoone evolve into Obstagoon?

Standard Hoenn Linoone does not evolve into Obstagoon. Obstagoon belongs to the Galarian Zigzagoon and Galarian Linoone evolutionary line.

Is Linoone good for raids?

Linoone is generally not considered a high-priority raid attacker because of its comparatively modest offensive stats and CP ceiling.

Is Linoone good in PvP?

Linoone's PvP value depends on its current moveset, league and the metagame. Its relatively low overall stats can limit its usefulness.

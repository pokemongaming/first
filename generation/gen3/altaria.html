Altaria

Altaria is a Dragon and Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Hoenn region. It is especially well known in Pokémon GO for its PvP potential and its Mega Evolution, which becomes Dragon and Fairy type.

Altaria Overview

Altaria is the evolved form of Swablu. Its Dragon and Flying typing gives it an unusual combination of resistances and weaknesses. Altaria is particularly notable in PvP because its lower maximum CP allows it to fit naturally into Great League environments.

Shiny Altaria

Shiny Altaria is a collectible alternate-color version. Its battle statistics remain based on the same underlying Altaria species, so Shiny status does not automatically make it stronger.

Shiny Altaria

Shiny status is primarily cosmetic. IVs, level and moveset remain more important for battle performance.

Should You Evolve a Shiny Swablu?

  • Check the IVs before committing Candy to evolution.
  • Consider whether you want a Shiny Altaria for PvP.
  • A high-IV Shiny can be an excellent collector project.
  • Mega Evolution can preserve the Shiny identity.

Purified Altaria

Purified Altaria is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can alter its investment path.

Purified Status

Shadow status is removed after purification.

IV Adjustment

Purification can improve Individual Values according to Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

Investment Choice

Compare the purified result with the original Shadow Pokémon before making the decision.

Purification is permanent. Players focused on optimized Shadow performance should evaluate the decision carefully before purifying.

Lucky Altaria

Lucky Altaria is especially attractive for players planning a long-term power-up project because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up.

Lucky Altaria Advantage

The primary advantage is reduced Stardust investment, making a high-level Altaria project more economical.

  • Good option for long-term power-up projects.
  • Useful if the Lucky Altaria also has strong IVs.
  • Consider keeping Lucky Pokémon for Mega Evolution projects.
  • Check the intended PvP league before powering up.

Altaria PvP Guide

Altaria is especially associated with Pokémon GO PvP. Its typing, defensive profile and Dragon Breath pressure make it a Pokémon that can perform well in appropriate league environments.

Great League

Great League is one of the formats most commonly associated with Altaria. The ideal IV spread can differ from a standard 100% IV Pokémon, so league-specific IV rankings should be checked before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Altaria can be used in higher CP formats, but its investment requirements and matchups should be evaluated separately from Great League performance.

Master League

Altaria's relatively low maximum CP generally makes it less natural for standard Master League competition than many Legendary or higher-stat alternatives.

Altaria FAQ

What type is Altaria?

Regular Altaria is a Dragon and Flying-type Pokémon.

What type is Mega Altaria?

Mega Altaria is a Dragon and Fairy-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Altaria available?

Shiny Altaria can be obtained through eligible Shiny Swablu or Altaria encounters.

Is Shadow Altaria different from regular Altaria?

Yes. Shadow Altaria has the Shadow battle profile, including increased offensive pressure and reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Altaria?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version and purified result before making a permanent purification decision.

What is a 100% IV Altaria?

A 100% Altaria has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Altaria have stronger stats?

No. XXL status is a size characteristic and does not automatically improve combat IVs or base stats.

Is Lucky Altaria worth powering up?

Lucky Altaria can be an attractive power-up project because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up.

Is Altaria good for PvP?

Altaria has historically been particularly useful in PvP formats where its CP range, typing and moveset fit the current metagame.

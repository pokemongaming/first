How to Use Shiftry in PvP

  • Use fast energy generation to pressure shields.
  • Avoid exposing Shiftry to major Bug-type threats.
  • Use Grass and Dark coverage strategically.
  • Do not rely on Shiftry to absorb large amounts of damage.
  • Consider switching when facing an unfavorable matchup.

All Shiftry Forms & Variants

The following variants can exist as collectible or mechanically different versions of Shiftry in Pokémon GO.

🌿 Regular Shiftry

The standard Grass/Dark-type Shiftry. This is the normal form used as the base for most PvP and collection discussions.

✨ Shiny Shiftry

Shiny Shiftry has alternate coloration. Its battle statistics remain the same as a regular Shiftry with identical level, IVs and moves.

👤 Shadow Shiftry

Shadow Shiftry has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. It requires separate resource planning.

💙 Purified Shiftry

Purified Shiftry is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Shiftry. Purification changes its Shadow status.

🍀 Lucky Shiftry

Lucky Shiftry is obtained through Lucky Trades and generally requires less Stardust for power-ups.

💯 100% IV Shiftry

A perfect Shiftry has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Perfect IVs are valuable for collectors and high-level builds.

📏 XXL Shiftry

XXL Shiftry is a larger size variant primarily associated with collection and size-based showcase features.

🔬 XXS Shiftry

XXS Shiftry is a smaller size variant primarily valued for collecting and size-based features.

Shiny Shiftry

Shiny Shiftry is a collectible alternate-color version of Shiftry. A Shiny Pokémon does not automatically have better battle statistics.

Purified Shiftry

Purified Shiftry is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Shiftry. Purification removes the Shadow status and creates a different long-term investment decision.

💙 Purified Shiftry

Lucky Shiftry

Lucky Shiftry is obtained through the Lucky Trade system. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for players who want to reduce Stardust costs when powering up a Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Shiftry

Lucky status does not change Shiftry's typing or species moves. Its main gameplay advantage is related to the cost of powering up the Pokémon.

  • Useful for Stardust-efficient investments.
  • Can also be Shiny if obtained through an eligible trade.
  • IVs are determined by the resulting Lucky Trade.
  • Can be valuable for collectors.

Shiftry FAQ

What type is Shiftry?

Shiftry is a Grass and Dark-type Pokémon.

Can Shiftry be Shiny?

Yes, Shiftry has a Shiny variant.

Is Shadow Shiftry stronger than regular Shiftry?

Shadow Shiftry has a different offensive and defensive battle profile. The best version depends on your intended use.

Should I purify Shadow Shiftry?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version with the purified result before deciding.

Does Lucky Shiftry have better stats?

Lucky status does not automatically make its base species stats stronger, but Lucky Pokémon are valuable for Stardust-efficient power-ups.

What is a 100% Shiftry?

A 100% IV Shiftry has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Shiftry stronger?

No. XXL size does not automatically provide better combat statistics.

Is XXS Shiftry weaker?

No. XXS size alone does not automatically reduce Shiftry's battle performance.

Is Shiftry good in PvP?

Shiftry can be useful because of its fast energy generation and charged-move pressure, although its defensive weaknesses must be managed carefully.

Manectric Overview

Manectric is a pure Electric-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Electrike and can Mega Evolve into Mega Manectric in Pokémon GO.

Can Shadow Manectric Become Mega Manectric?

Shadow and Mega mechanics should not be treated as interchangeable forms. Pokémon GO's current rules determine which Pokémon can Mega Evolve, so Shadow Manectric should not automatically be assumed to be eligible for Mega Evolution.

15 • SIZE

XXL Manectric

XXL Manectric is an unusually large specimen. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Manectric can have any IV combination.

EXTRA LARGE

XXL Manectric

An XXL Manectric is primarily valuable for collection, size records and personal goals. XXL status does not automatically mean better battle stats.

IV INDEPENDENT

XXL + 100%

A Manectric can be both XXL and 100% IV. This combination is considerably more desirable to collectors than size alone.

16 • SIZE

XXS Manectric

XXS Manectric is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV quality.

EXTRA SMALL

XXS Manectric

XXS Manectric can be a useful addition to a size-focused collection. Its small size does not automatically make it stronger or weaker in battle.

COLLECTION

XXS + 100%

A perfect-IV XXS Manectric combines a rare size characteristic with maximum IVs.

23 • FAQ

Manectric FAQ

What type is Manectric?

Manectric is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Manectric weak to?

Manectric is weak to Ground-type attacks.

What Pokémon evolves into Manectric?

Electrike evolves into Manectric.

Does Manectric have a Mega Evolution?

Yes. Manectric can Mega Evolve into Mega Manectric in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Manectric available?

Shiny Manectric is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Manectric good?

Shadow Manectric can provide increased offensive pressure compared with regular Manectric, although it also has reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Manectric?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before deciding, especially if your goal is maximizing raid damage.

What is Lucky Manectric?

Lucky Manectric is a special trade-related status that reduces the Stardust cost of powering up the Pokémon.

What is a 100% Manectric?

A 100% Manectric has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is XXL Manectric?

XXL Manectric is an unusually large specimen. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Manectric?

XXS Manectric is an unusually small specimen. XXS status is separate from IV quality.

Is Mega Manectric good for raids?

Mega Manectric is a strong Electric-focused Mega option and can be particularly useful when Electric damage is effective against the raid boss.

Can a Lucky Manectric Mega Evolve?

A Manectric that is eligible for Mega Evolution can retain its applicable Pokémon status while being used as a Mega Evolution candidate, subject to the game's current mechanics.

Can a Shiny Manectric Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Manectric can be used as a Mega Evolution candidate when eligible, allowing the Shiny identity to remain part of the form.

Is a 100% Manectric worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Manectric is a perfect-IV specimen and can be especially attractive for collectors and players planning a long-term investment.

Is Manectric good in Pokémon GO?

Manectric can be useful as an Electric attacker, while Mega Manectric provides a substantially more specialized role for Electric-focused teams.

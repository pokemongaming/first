01 • OVERVIEW

Sableye Overview

Sableye is a Dark/Ghost-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. Unlike many Pokémon, Sableye does not have a normal evolution chain. Its main gameplay identities are its Great League PvP role, unusual defensive typing, Shadow and Purified variants, and Mega Sableye.

Sableye Type

Why Sableye's Typing Matters

Sableye's Dark/Ghost combination gives it a highly unusual defensive profile. In Pokémon GO, this results in only one super-effective weakness: Fairy. It also gives Sableye useful resistances to several common attack types, which is one reason the Pokémon can be interesting in Great League battles.

08 • IV GUIDE

Sableye IV Guide

A perfect 15/15/15 Sableye is the traditional 100% IV specimen. However, PvP Sableye can favor different IV distributions because of CP limits.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Sableye

A 15/15/15 Sableye is a perfect IV or "hundo" Sableye. For collection purposes, this is the highest possible IV combination.

PvP IVs Are Different

For Great League, a lower Attack IV combined with higher Defense and HP can sometimes produce a better stat product under the CP limit. Therefore, do not automatically transfer every low-Attack Sableye.

10 • PVP

Sableye PvP Guide

Sableye is much more relevant in PvP than in traditional raid attacking. Its Dark/Ghost typing and efficient moves make it a notable Great League Pokémon.

Great League

Great League is the format where standard Sableye has its strongest identity. Its maximum CP is low enough to fit naturally into the format, while its typing gives it useful defensive matchups.

Ultra League

Sableye can be considered in specialized Ultra League situations, but its traditional strength is much more closely associated with Great League.

Master League

Sableye's relatively low maximum CP makes Master League a poor natural home for standard Sableye.

PvP Investment Tip

Before powering up a Sableye for Great League, check its IV spread, level, moves and current PvP meta rather than relying only on its overall appraisal percentage.

Should You Purify Sableye?

Do not automatically purify every Shadow Sableye. If the Shadow version has useful PvP IVs or personal collection value, keeping it Shadow can be preferable. Purification can be useful when you specifically want a purified Pokémon, need the IV increase, or want to remove Frustration.

Think before purifying:

Purification is permanent. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before committing.

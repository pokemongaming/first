Why Duskull's Typing Matters

Duskull's pure Ghost typing gives it a useful set of offensive and defensive interactions. Ghost-type Pokémon are particularly relevant against Psychic and Ghost opponents, while Duskull itself has to be careful around Dark and Ghost attacks.

09 — PURIFIED

Purified Duskull

Purified Duskull is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Duskull. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and resource requirements.

PURIFIED FORM

Purified Duskull

Purified Duskull no longer has the Shadow status. It can be useful for collection purposes and may provide a lower-cost investment path in appropriate situations.

Should You Purify Shadow Duskull?

  • Check the Shadow Duskull's IVs first.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified versions.
  • Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow.
  • Check the purification resource requirements.
  • Do not purify solely because the CP increases.

Lucky Duskull IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon receive a minimum IV floor determined by Pokémon GO's Lucky trade mechanics. A Lucky Duskull therefore cannot have arbitrarily low IVs after becoming Lucky.

11 — IV GUIDE

100% IV Duskull — Hundo Duskull

A 100% IV Duskull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These three perfect IV values make it a Hundo, one of the most desirable specimens for collectors.

Is a 100% Duskull Worth Keeping?

Yes, especially for collectors and players who want a perfect specimen for the Duskull evolution family. However, the usefulness of the Pokémon in raids or PvP should be evaluated separately from its IV perfection.

12 — SIZE VARIANTS

XXL Duskull & XXS Duskull

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon according to size categories. XXL and XXS Duskull specimens are primarily interesting for collectors, showcases and size-related gameplay features.

📏 XXL Duskull Extra-large size classification

XXL Duskull can be desirable for collectors interested in large-size Pokémon and size-based achievements.

📐 XXS Duskull Extra-small size classification

XXS Duskull represents the opposite end of the size spectrum and can be a useful addition to a dedicated collection.

Important:

XXL and XXS are size classifications rather than alternate Pokémon species. Size does not automatically mean higher IVs or better battle performance.

21 — FAQ

Duskull FAQ

What type is Duskull?

Duskull is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Duskull weak to?

Duskull is weak to Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

Does Duskull evolve?

Yes. Duskull evolves into Dusclops and eventually Dusknoir.

Is Shiny Duskull available?

Yes. Shiny Duskull is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Duskull?

Shadow Duskull is a Shadow variant associated with Team GO Rocket encounters.

Should I purify Shadow Duskull?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your collection and battle goals before purifying.

What is Lucky Duskull?

Lucky Duskull is obtained through trading and receives the Lucky Pokémon benefits, including reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Duskull?

A 100% Duskull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Duskull?

XXL Duskull is an extra-large size classification for an individual Duskull.

What is XXS Duskull?

XXS Duskull is an extra-small size classification for an individual Duskull.

Is Duskull good for raids?

Duskull itself is generally not considered a top-tier Ghost raid attacker. Its evolution family is more relevant when building battle teams.

Is Duskull good for PvP?

Duskull can have situational value in CP-limited formats, but its PvP performance depends on the current moveset and Cup rules.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Duskull?

If you care about size collections, showcases or completing your Pokémon collection, XXL and XXS Duskull can be worth keeping.

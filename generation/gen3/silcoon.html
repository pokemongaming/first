What Does Bug Typing Mean?

Silcoon's Bug typing determines its weaknesses, resistances and battle matchups. As an intermediate evolution Pokémon, Silcoon is generally more useful as a collection and evolution step than as a major raid attacker.

✨ Shiny Silcoon

Shiny Silcoon is the alternate-colour version of Silcoon. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

Shiny Evolution

A Shiny Silcoon retains its Shiny status when evolving into its next evolution where the evolution is available.

Should You Keep Shiny Silcoon?

  • Keep at least one for your Shiny collection.
  • Check its IVs before deciding whether to evolve it.
  • Consider keeping different Shiny family stages.

Shadow Silcoon Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally have increased offensive pressure while taking more damage. Because Silcoon is an intermediate evolution, its long-term value may depend on whether you plan to keep or evolve it.

Frustration Note

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. The ability to remove or change this move can depend on Pokémon GO events and current game mechanics.

💠 Purified Silcoon

🍀 Lucky Silcoon

Lucky Silcoon is obtained through an eligible Pokémon trade. Lucky Pokémon are valuable to collectors and can be attractive for players planning future power-up investments.

Lucky Pokémon Benefit

Lucky Silcoon Collection Value

XXS Silcoon

XXS Silcoon is an unusually small version of Silcoon. Its size status is separate from IVs, Shiny status and most battle characteristics.

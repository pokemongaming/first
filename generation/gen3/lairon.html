Lairon

Lairon is a Steel- and Rock-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it sits between Aron and Aggron in its evolution family and is best known as an evolution, collection and specialty PvP Pokémon rather than a major raid attacker.

Lairon Overview

Lairon is the second stage of the Aron evolutionary family. It evolves from Aron and can later evolve into Aggron. Its Steel/Rock typing gives it several useful resistances, although it also creates important weaknesses to Fighting, Ground and other attack types.

Why Lairon's Typing Matters

Lairon's Steel/Rock combination gives it a defensive profile that differs considerably from many other Rock-type Pokémon. Steel provides numerous resistances, while Rock contributes additional matchup advantages. However, the combination also leaves Lairon vulnerable to several important attacking types.

Lairon PvP Guide

Lairon's relatively low maximum CP can make it more relevant to lower CP formats than many fully evolved Pokémon. However, its actual usefulness depends heavily on the current league rules and moveset.

Great League

A carefully selected Lairon can be considered for specialized Great League builds. IV optimization can matter considerably when trying to maximize bulk under a CP limit.

Ultra League

Lairon is less naturally suited to Ultra League because its maximum CP and overall battle profile limit its ability to compete with many fully evolved Pokémon.

Master League

Lairon's CP ceiling makes it unsuitable for the standard Master League environment.

Lucky Lairon

Lucky Lairon is a special status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Lairon

A Lucky Lairon is particularly attractive for players who want to reduce Stardust costs while building a strong specimen.

Why Keep a Lucky Lairon?

XXL Lairon

XXL Lairon is a size variant that is larger than the standard size range. Size is separate from IV quality and CP, so a large Lairon can have any IV combination.

📏 XXL Lairon

An exceptionally large Lairon. XXL status is primarily a collection and size-related distinction rather than a direct combat-stat upgrade.

Size ≠ IV

An XXL Lairon does not automatically have high IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Should You Keep an XXL Lairon?

Yes if you collect size variants, work toward size-related medals or want a unique Lairon collection. Keep in mind that XXL status should not be confused with higher battle stats.

XXS Lairon

XXS Lairon is a very small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of IVs and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger or weaker in battle.

📐 XXS Lairon

A particularly small Lairon that can be valuable for collectors and players working on size-related achievements.

Lairon FAQ

What type is Lairon?

Lairon is a Steel and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Lairon weak against?

Lairon is vulnerable to several important attacking types, including Fighting, Ground, Water and Fire.

Does Lairon evolve?

Yes. Lairon evolves from Aron and can evolve into Aggron.

Is Shiny Lairon available?

Shiny Lairon can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolutionary lines.

What is a 100% Lairon?

A 100% Lairon has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs: 15/15/15.

What is Shadow Lairon?

Shadow Lairon is the Shadow variant of Lairon. It retains its Steel/Rock typing while having the different battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Lairon?

Not automatically. Consider the purpose of the Pokémon before purifying it, especially if you want to preserve the Shadow variant for collection or battle purposes.

What is Lucky Lairon?

Lucky Lairon is a Lairon that has obtained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Lairon?

XXL Lairon is a particularly large size variant. XXL status is separate from IVs and does not automatically increase its battle stats.

What is XXS Lairon?

XXS Lairon is a particularly small size variant. Like XXL, XXS status is independent of IV quality.

Is Lairon good for raids?

Lairon is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Players looking for stronger PvE performance will usually have better options, including evolved Pokémon.

Is Lairon good for PvP?

Lairon can be considered for specialized lower-CP PvP formats, but its effectiveness depends on its moveset, IVs, league rules and the current metagame.

Should I evolve my Lairon?

If your goal is stronger overall combat performance or completing the evolutionary family, evolving Lairon into Aggron is usually the more natural long-term path.

Lileep Overview

Lileep is a dual-type Rock and Grass Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 3. It is based on an ancient sea organism and can be revived from a Root Fossil in the main Pokémon series. In Pokémon GO, Lileep evolves into Cradily after collecting the required Lileep Candy.

Why Lileep's Typing Matters

Lucky Lileep

Lucky Lileep is obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have a special visual effect and require fewer Stardust resources to power up.

Why Keep a Lucky Lileep?

A Lucky Lileep can be useful for collectors and players who want a lower-cost Pokémon to power up. Lucky status is independent of Shiny status and IV quality.

Lucky + Shiny Lileep

It is possible for a Pokémon to have multiple desirable characteristics, making combinations such as Lucky and Shiny especially valuable to collectors.

100% IV Lileep

A perfect Lileep has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A 15/15/15 Pokémon is commonly called a 100% IV or hundo.

XXL & XXS Lileep

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Lileep are special size variants that can be valuable for collectors and size-related medals.

🏆 XXL Lileep Extra-large size classification. Useful for collectors and size-related achievements.
🔹 XXS Lileep Extra-small size classification. Another collectible size variant.

XXL and XXS vs IVs

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Lileep is not automatically a high-IV Lileep, and an XXS Lileep is not automatically a low-IV Pokémon.

Collector Tip

A particularly rare collection target can be a combination such as Shiny, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL or XXS. These characteristics should be tracked independently in your database.

Lileep FAQ

What type is Lileep?

Lileep is a Rock and Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Lileep weak to?

Lileep has several weaknesses because of its Rock and Grass typing, including Bug, Fighting, Ice, Steel, Fire and Flying attacks.

Does Lileep evolve?

Yes. Lileep evolves into Cradily using Lileep Candy.

Is Shiny Lileep available?

Shiny Lileep is an available alternate-color variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is 100% Lileep?

A 100% Lileep has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Lileep?

Lucky Lileep is obtained through a Lucky Trade and has the reduced Stardust power-up cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is Shadow Lileep?

Shadow Lileep is the Shadow version of Lileep and has the battle mechanics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Lileep?

Not automatically. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

What is XXL Lileep?

XXL Lileep is a Lileep classified as extra large under Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Lileep?

XXS Lileep is a Lileep classified as extra small under Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Lileep good for raids?

Lileep is generally more valuable as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker.

Can Shiny Lileep evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Lileep can evolve into Shiny Cradily.

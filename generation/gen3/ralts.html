```
Ralts Evolution

Ralts evolves into Kirlia. Kirlia can then evolve into Gardevoir, while eligible Kirlia can also evolve into Gallade.
```

```
Shiny Ralts

Shiny Ralts is the alternate-colour version of regular Ralts. A Shiny Ralts can retain its Shiny identity when evolved.

✨ Shiny Ralts

Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle. IVs, level and moves remain important.

  • Shiny Ralts can be evolved.
  • Shiny status is retained through evolution.
  • A Shiny Ralts can also have high IVs.
  • Availability depends on eligible encounters.
```

```
Shadow Ralts

Shadow Ralts is the Shadow variant of the Ralts species. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile and require additional resource planning.

🌑 Shadow Ralts

Shadow Ralts can be an important long-term evolution project because its evolved forms may benefit from Shadow battle mechanics.
```

```
Purified Ralts

Purified Ralts is obtained when an eligible Shadow Ralts is purified.

💎 Purified Ralts

Purification changes the Shadow Pokémon into its Purified version. Players should compare the long-term purpose of the Pokémon before deciding whether to purify.

  • Purified Ralts is no longer a Shadow Pokémon.
  • Purification changes the Pokémon's resource profile.
  • IV values may increase through purification.
  • Consider future evolution plans before purifying.
```

```
Lucky Ralts

Lucky Ralts is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky trade system.

🍀 Lucky Ralts

Lucky Pokémon are particularly interesting for evolution projects because they require less Stardust for powering up compared with standard Pokémon.

Why Lucky Ralts Can Be Valuable

  • Lower Stardust power-up cost.
  • Useful for building Gardevoir or Gallade.
  • Good collector value.
  • Lucky status remains through evolution.
```

```
XXL Ralts

XXL Ralts is an unusually large size variant of Ralts.

📏 XXL Ralts

XXL status is primarily associated with Pokémon size and collection features. It does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better IVs or battle stats.

  • XXL refers to Pokémon size.
  • XXL does not automatically mean 100% IV.
  • XXL Ralts can still be Shiny.
  • XXL Ralts may also be eligible for evolution.
```

```
🔍 XXS Ralts
```

```
Which Ralts Should You Evolve?

The best Ralts depends on your long-term goal.

  • 100% IV Ralts is a strong long-term collector and investment candidate.
  • Lucky Ralts is useful when Stardust efficiency is important.
  • Shadow Ralts can be valuable for future damage-focused evolution projects.
  • Shiny Ralts is an excellent collector evolution.
  • XXL and XXS Ralts are particularly interesting for size collectors.
  • Always consider whether you want Gardevoir or Gallade before evolving.
```

```
Ralts FAQ

What type is Ralts?

Ralts is a Psychic and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What does Ralts evolve into?

Ralts evolves into Kirlia, which can eventually evolve into Gardevoir or Gallade.

Is Shiny Ralts available?

Shiny Ralts can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Ralts different from regular Ralts?
```

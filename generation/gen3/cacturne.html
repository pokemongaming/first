Why Cacturne's Typing Matters

Cacturne's Grass / Dark combination gives it access to two different offensive identities. The Grass typing can be useful against Water, Ground and Rock targets, while Dark attacks are useful against Psychic and Ghost opponents.

21 • FAQ

Cacturne FAQ

What type is Cacturne?

Cacturne is a Grass / Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Cacturne weak to?

Cacturne is vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Bug, Flying and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Cacturne evolve?

Cacturne evolves from Cacnea and is the final stage of that two-stage evolution family.

Is Shiny Cacturne available?

Shiny Cacturne can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can also be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Cacnea.

Is Shadow Cacturne available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current game events.

What is a Purified Cacturne?

A Purified Cacturne is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Cacturne.

What is a Lucky Cacturne?

Lucky Cacturne is a Cacturne that received the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

What is a 100% Cacturne?

A 100% Cacturne has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Cacturne?

XXL Cacturne is a Cacturne with the game's XXL size classification. It is not a separate species.

What is XXS Cacturne?

XXS Cacturne is a Cacturne with the game's XXS size classification. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Is Cacturne good for raids?

Cacturne is generally a niche PvE option rather than a top-priority raid attacker. Its usefulness depends on the matchup and current moveset.

Is Cacturne good in PvP?

Cacturne can have niche PvP applications, but its relatively low durability limits its consistency in many standard formats.

Are Shiny, Lucky, XXL and XXS Cacturne stronger?

These classifications do not automatically make Cacturne stronger. IVs, level, moveset and battle mechanics should be evaluated separately.

Flygon

Flygon is a Ground and Dragon-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, Flygon is an interesting dual-type attacker with access to Ground and Dragon-based battle roles.

Flygon Overview

Flygon is the final evolution of Trapinch. It evolves from Trapinch into Vibrava and finally into Flygon. Its Ground / Dragon typing gives it a distinctive combination of offensive strengths and defensive weaknesses.

Why Flygon's Typing Matters

Flygon's dual Ground and Dragon typing allows it to deal useful damage against several different Pokémon types. However, the same typing also creates an important weakness profile that players should consider when using Flygon in raids or PvP.

Shadow Flygon

Shadow Flygon is the Shadow variant of Flygon. It has the same Ground / Dragon typing as regular Flygon but uses the Shadow battle mechanic, which changes its offensive and defensive profile.

Lucky Flygon

Lucky Flygon is obtained when an eligible Trapinch, Vibrava or Flygon becomes Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Why Lucky Flygon Matters

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than regular Pokémon, making Lucky Flygon an attractive long-term investment for players building Ground or Dragon-type teams.

Is Shiny Flygon Stronger?

No. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not automatically increase Flygon's Attack, Defense, HP or battle power.

XXL Flygon

XXL Flygon represents an unusually large size variant. Size does not automatically change Flygon's standard battle stats.

XXL Flygon

XXL size is primarily relevant for collecting, showcases and size-related Pokémon GO features.

XXS Flygon

XXS Flygon is an unusually small size variant of Flygon. Like XXL Pokémon, its size is primarily a collection characteristic.

XXS Flygon

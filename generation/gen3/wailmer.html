Wailmer

Wailmer is a Water-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a high-Stamina Pokémon with a very high 400-Candy evolution requirement. Wailmer can also appear in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

Why Wailmer's Water Typing Matters

Wailmer's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. It takes super-effective damage from Electric and Grass attacks while resisting Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

XXS Wailmer

XXS Wailmer is an unusually small Wailmer. Like XXL classification, XXS is a size characteristic and should not be confused with IV quality.

26 • FAQ

Wailmer FAQ

What type is Wailmer?

Wailmer is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wailmer weak against?

Wailmer is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What is Wailmer's max CP?

Wailmer's maximum CP is 1,659 at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

How much Candy does Wailmer need to evolve?

Wailmer requires 400 Wailmer Candy to evolve into Wailord.

Is Shiny Wailmer available?

Yes. Shiny Wailmer is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Wailmer be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Wailmer exists as a special Shadow variant.

What is a 100% Wailmer?

A 100% Wailmer has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Wailmer?

A Lucky Wailmer is a Wailmer that has received Lucky status through the trading system.

Can Wailmer be XXL?

Yes. Wailmer can have an XXL size classification.

Can Wailmer be XXS?

Yes. Wailmer can have an XXS size classification.

Is XXL Wailmer better than normal Wailmer?

Can Wailmer be both Shiny and XXL?

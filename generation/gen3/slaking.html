NORMAL-TYPE POKÉMON

Slaking

Slaking is a Normal-type Pokémon from Generation 3 and the final evolution of Slakoth. In Pokémon GO, it is famous for its exceptionally high CP and enormous base Attack and HP stats, although its unique role makes it very different from conventional raid attackers.

01 — OVERVIEW

Slaking Overview

Slaking is the final evolution of Slakoth and Vigoroth. It is a pure Normal-type Pokémon with an unusually high maximum CP and very high Attack and HP stats.

Why Slaking's Typing Matters

Slaking has a single Normal typing. This gives it a simple defensive profile: it is vulnerable to Fighting-type attacks, while several other attack types deal reduced damage.

Why Isn't Slaking a Top Raid Attacker?

Pokémon GO raid performance depends on much more than Attack and CP. Fast Move and Charged Move damage, energy generation, typing and overall battle mechanics all influence real PvE performance. Slaking's unusual moveset prevents its enormous headline stats from translating directly into elite raid damage.

Don't judge Slaking by CP alone.

A Pokémon with a higher maximum CP is not automatically a better raid attacker.

07 — COUNTERS

What Counters Slaking?

Fighting-type Pokémon are the primary offensive counter category against Slaking because Normal-type Pokémon are weak to Fighting damage.

08 — IV GUIDE

Slaking IV Guide

Individual Values add bonus points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Slaking is known as a perfect or hundo Slaking.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is 100% Slaking?

A 100% Slaking, commonly called a hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is the highest-IV Slaking possible.

💯 Hundo Slaking

A perfect Slaking is particularly desirable for collectors because of its exceptionally high maximum CP.

Is 100% Slaking Worth Powering Up?

A hundo Slaking can be an excellent collection and gym investment, but its high CP should not be mistaken for elite raid performance.

09 — PVP

Slaking PvP Guide

Slaking's huge stats and high CP make it interesting to look at, but its practical PvP value is strongly influenced by its available moves.

Master League

Slaking can reach the CP range associated with Master League, but simply reaching a high CP does not guarantee strong competitive performance. Its moveset and battle mechanics are major limitations.

Great League & Ultra League

Slaking's extremely high maximum CP makes lower CP leagues less natural for a standard fully powered build. League-specific rules and current rankings should always be checked before investing specifically for PvP.

PvP note:

Rankings can change when Niantic adjusts moves, damage values or league mechanics. Treat this section as strategic guidance rather than a permanent ranking.

Why Get a Lucky Slaking?

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon. This can make a Lucky Slaking attractive for collectors or players who want to build a high-level specimen while reducing Stardust expenditure.

🍀 Lucky + Hundo Hunting

20 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Slaking

Gym Defense

Slaking's high HP, high CP and imposing appearance make it a common type of Pokémon players may encounter in Gym defense. However, experienced attackers know that its Normal typing gives Fighting Pokémon a clear advantage.

Raid Teams

Avoid placing Slaking into a raid team solely because it has an enormous CP value. Evaluate the Pokémon's actual moveset and damage output against the alternatives available to you.

Collection

Slaking is arguably more interesting as a collection Pokémon than as a conventional raid investment. Hundo, Shiny, Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens can all be worthwhile depending on your collection goals.

Slaking FAQ

What type is Slaking?

Slaking is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

Slaking is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Slaking's Max CP?

Slaking can reach a maximum CP of approximately 5,010 under the standard maximum-level and perfect-IV conditions.

Slaking's raw stats are impressive, but its moveset means it is generally not considered a premier raid attacker.

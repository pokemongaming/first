Why Taillow's Typing Matters

Taillow's Normal/Flying typing gives it useful resistances to several attack categories, while also leaving it vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

07 • RAID PERFORMANCE

Taillow Raid Performance

Taillow is not considered a high-end raid attacker. Its low CP and modest offensive stats mean that players should generally evolve or collect Taillow rather than spend large amounts of Stardust powering it up specifically for raids.

Should You Power Up Taillow?

For most players, Stardust and Candy are better spent on stronger Flying or Normal-type attackers. A Taillow can still be worth keeping for its IVs, Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky status, size, or future evolution into Swellow.

Taillow Special Variants

10 • SHINY TAILLOW

Shiny Taillow

Shiny Taillow is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collection target, particularly for players building complete Hoenn or Shiny Pokédexes.

✨

Shiny Taillow

A Shiny Taillow can be kept as a collector's Pokémon or evolved into Shiny Swellow.

Shiny Taillow Investment

  • Keep high-IV Shiny Taillow if you value collection quality.
  • Consider evolving a duplicate into Shiny Swellow.
  • Keep a Shiny with useful size attributes if you collect variants.
  • Check the appraisal before transferring a rare Shiny.

13 • LUCKY TAILLOW

Lucky Taillow

Lucky Taillow is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive to collectors because they have a guaranteed minimum IV floor and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

Why Keep a Lucky Taillow?

  • Lucky Pokémon have a useful minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • A Lucky Shiny Taillow can be an excellent collector piece.
  • A Lucky Taillow can be evolved into Lucky Swellow.
14 • 100% IV

100% IV Taillow

A 100% IV Taillow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo or perfect-IV Taillow.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
Perfect Taillow

Is 100% Taillow Worth Keeping?

Absolutely, especially if you are completing a perfect-IV collection. Although Taillow is not a top raid attacker, a Hundo can be valuable for collectors and can be evolved into a perfect-IV Swellow.

15 • XXL TAILLOW

XXL Taillow

XXL Taillow is an exceptionally large Taillow. Size is a separate Pokémon attribute and should not be confused with IV quality.

🔷 XXL Taillow Approximate size range: 0.45 m to 0.60 m

XXL Taillow and IVs

An XXL Taillow can have poor IVs, average IVs or perfect IVs. Size and IVs are independent attributes.

Why Collect XXL Taillow?

  • XXL Pokémon are useful for size-based collection goals.
  • An XXL Shiny Taillow is a particularly attractive collector combination.
  • An XXL Hundo Taillow combines maximum IV quality with large size.
  • XXL status can be preserved as part of your Pokémon collection.

What IVs Should You Keep?

19 • PVP

Taillow PvP Guide

Taillow has limited PvP relevance because of its relatively low overall stats and maximum CP. Players interested in PvP should generally evaluate the current league rankings before investing.

Great League

Taillow can fit within the CP range of Great League, but its overall battle performance is limited compared with stronger Pokémon.

Ultra League

Taillow's low maximum CP makes it a poor natural choice for Ultra League.

Master League

Taillow is not a practical Master League Pokémon because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for competitive Master League play.

PvP IV reminder

PvP IV recommendations can differ from the traditional 15/15/15 approach because CP limits favor different combinations of Attack, Defense and HP.

27 • FAQ

Taillow FAQ

What type is Taillow?

Taillow is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Taillow weak to?

Taillow is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What is Taillow's Max CP?

Taillow's maximum CP is 865 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Taillow available?

Yes. Shiny Taillow is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Taillow available?

Yes. Shadow Taillow is available in Pokémon GO, although its availability can depend on current Team GO Rocket rotations.

Can Shadow Taillow be purified?

Yes. Shadow Taillow can be purified.

How much does it cost to purify Taillow?

The current listed purification cost for Shadow Taillow is 3 Taillow Candy and 3,000 Stardust.

Can Taillow be Lucky?

Yes. Taillow can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Taillow?

A 100% Taillow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Hundo Taillow?

Hundo is the community term commonly used for a Pokémon with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is an XXL Taillow?

XXL Taillow is an exceptionally large Taillow. XXL status is a size attribute and is separate from IVs.

What is an XXS Taillow?

XXS Taillow is an exceptionally small Taillow. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has low or high IVs.

What is Taillow's best moveset?

Peck and Aerial Ace are the recommended current moveset listed for Taillow.

Is Taillow good for raids?

Taillow is not a high-end raid attacker. Its low maximum CP and overall stats make it more useful for collection and evolution than serious raid investment.

Does Taillow evolve?

Yes. Taillow evolves into Swellow using 50 Taillow Candy.

Can Shiny Taillow evolve into Shiny Swellow?

Yes. A Shiny Taillow retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Can Shadow Taillow evolve?

Shadow Pokémon can retain their Shadow status through eligible evolutions.

Should I transfer a low-IV Shiny Taillow?

Usually not automatically. Shiny status is a collection attribute, so many players keep even low-IV Shiny Pokémon.

Is a 100% Taillow rare?

A perfect 15/15/15 Taillow is much less common than an ordinary Taillow because all three IV categories must reach their maximum value.

Is XXL Taillow better than normal Taillow?

Not automatically. XXL is a size attribute and does not by itself indicate better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Is XXS Taillow better than normal Taillow?

Not automatically. XXS describes size rather than IV quality.

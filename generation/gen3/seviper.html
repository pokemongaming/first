Seviper

Seviper is a Poison-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. It does not evolve and has no Mega Evolution, but Pokémon GO players may encounter several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Seviper.

Seviper Overview

Seviper is a pure Poison-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is known as the Fang Snake Pokémon and has a long-standing Pokédex rivalry with Zangoose. In Pokémon GO, Seviper is primarily a collector-focused Pokémon, although its usefulness can depend on the current moveset, battle format and available alternatives.

Regular Seviper

STANDARD FORM

Regular Seviper

Regular Seviper is the standard version of the Pokémon. It retains the normal Poison typing, standard appearance and standard resource costs.

Should You Keep Regular Seviper?

  • Keep high-IV specimens for your collection.
  • Compare its moveset before investing Stardust.
  • Keep a strong specimen if you collect every Hoenn Pokémon.
  • Consider PvP IV spreads separately from perfect PvE IVs.

Shiny Seviper

✨ SHINY FORM

Shiny Seviper

Shiny Seviper is a rare alternate-color version of Seviper. Its gameplay typing and base battle role remain the same as regular Seviper.

Shiny Seviper Collection Value

Shiny Pokémon are primarily valuable as collectible variants. A Shiny Seviper with strong IVs, a useful moveset or a special size can be particularly interesting to collectors.

Current Shiny availability should always be checked against the latest Pokémon GO encounter pool and event schedule.

Purified Seviper

PURIFIED FORM

Purified Seviper

Purified Seviper is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Seviper. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and move situation.

Purified Seviper vs Shadow Seviper

Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has Shadow battle characteristics.

IV Change

Purification can change a Pokémon's IV values.

Investment

Evaluate purification before committing because Shadow and Purified forms serve different collection and battle goals.

Lucky Seviper

✨ LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Seviper

Lucky Seviper is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for players planning to invest Stardust into a Pokémon.

Why Lucky Seviper Can Be Valuable

  • Lower Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Special Lucky background in the Pokémon collection.
  • Useful for collectors building variant collections.
  • Can combine Lucky status with Shiny status when obtained through an eligible trade.

XXS Seviper

XXS SIZE

XXS Seviper

XXS Seviper is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS variants are primarily collectible and represent another distinct form of size-based rarity.

Why Keep an XXS Seviper?

  • Useful for size-based collections.
  • Can be paired with Shiny status in a collection.
  • Provides a rare variation from a standard specimen.
  • Worth keeping if you collect both minimum and maximum sized Pokémon.

Seviper FAQ

What type is Seviper?

Seviper is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

Does Seviper evolve?

No. Seviper does not evolve into another Pokémon.

What is Seviper weak to?

Seviper is weak to Ground and Psychic-type attacks.

Can Seviper be Shiny?

Shiny Seviper can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Seviper be a Shadow Pokémon?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation.

Can Shadow Seviper be purified?

An eligible Shadow Seviper can be purified, removing its Shadow status.

What is a Lucky Seviper?

Lucky Seviper is a Seviper obtained through an eligible Pokémon trade that receives Lucky Pokémon status.

What is a 100% IV Seviper?

A perfect Seviper has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Seviper?

XXL Seviper is an unusually large size variant.

What is XXS Seviper?

XXS Seviper is an unusually small size variant.

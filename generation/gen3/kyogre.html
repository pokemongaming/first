09 • PVP

Kyogre PvP Guide

Kyogre's high maximum CP makes it most naturally suited to the Master League rather than the lower CP leagues.

Master League

Kyogre can be useful in Master League teams because of its high stats and Water-type pressure. Its performance depends on the current metagame, moveset, shields and team composition.

Great League & Ultra League

Kyogre's high CP makes the lower leagues less natural for standard Kyogre. Special cups or limited formats may have different rules.

15 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL Kyogre & XXS Kyogre

Pokémon GO can record size variations for Pokémon. XXL and XXS are size classifications and do not mean that Kyogre has different base combat stats.

XXL Kyogre

An XXL Kyogre is a particularly large specimen. Size classification is separate from IV quality.

  • Large-size classification
  • Useful for collectors
  • Can have any IV combination
  • Can be Shiny or Lucky when applicable
XXS Kyogre

An XXS Kyogre is a particularly small specimen. Its size does not automatically make it stronger or weaker.

  • Small-size classification
  • Useful for collectors
  • Can have any IV combination
  • Size and IVs are separate characteristics
Important:

XXL and XXS should not be confused with high or low IVs. A Pokémon can be XXL or XXS regardless of whether its IVs are perfect.

Primal Reversion is a temporary transformation that allows Kyogre to enter its ancient Primal form.

22 • FAQ

Kyogre FAQ

Is Kyogre a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Kyogre is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3.

What type is Kyogre?

Regular Kyogre is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Kyogre weak to?

Kyogre is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

Is Kyogre good for raids?

Yes. Kyogre is a powerful Water-type raid attacker, particularly with an appropriate Water-focused moveset.

Is Shiny Kyogre available?

Yes. Shiny Kyogre is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Kyogre good?

Shadow Kyogre can be valuable for players who prioritize damage output and are willing to manage the additional investment requirements of Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Kyogre?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your collection and battle goals before making the decision.

What is Lucky Kyogre?

Lucky Kyogre is Kyogre with the Lucky Pokémon status. It can be attractive for players looking to reduce the Stardust burden of powering up Pokémon.

What is a 100% Kyogre?

A 100% Kyogre has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Kyogre?

XXL Kyogre is a Kyogre recorded with an exceptionally large size classification. XXL status is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Kyogre?

What is Primal Kyogre?

Kyogre does not have a normal evolution. It can instead undergo Primal Reversion into Primal Kyogre.

Yes. A Shiny Kyogre can retain its Shiny identity when used for Primal Reversion.

09 • IV GUIDE

Ninjask IV Guide

IVs provide additional Attack, Defense and HP points. A perfect Ninjask has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect 100% Ninjask

A 15/15/15 Ninjask is considered a perfect IV Pokémon. Perfect specimens can be useful for collectors, Pokédex completion and personal projects even when the Pokémon is not a top competitive attacker.

10 • PVP

Ninjask PvP Guide

Ninjask's low Defense and overall stat profile make it a more specialized PvP choice than many stronger Bug or Flying Pokémon.

Great League

Ninjask can technically fit within lower CP formats depending on its level and IV combination, but trainers should compare its performance against the current Great League field before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Ninjask's overall stat profile makes Ultra League investment more niche. League rules and the current Pokémon GO metagame should be checked before building around it.

Master League

Ninjask's maximum CP is far below the typical level required for a competitive Master League Pokémon, making it an uncommon choice for that format.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Ninjask

Lucky Ninjask is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and generally require less Stardust to power up.

✨

Lucky Ninjask

A traded Ninjask that has received the Lucky Pokémon status.

💰

Stardust Benefit

Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust costs for power-ups compared with standard Pokémon.

Lucky Ninjask IVs

Lucky Pokémon receive a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. A Lucky Ninjask can still have different IV combinations, so always appraise it after the trade.

Collector Tip

A Lucky Shiny Ninjask can be an especially attractive collection piece because it combines two desirable Pokémon GO attributes.

15 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Ninjask

A 100% IV Ninjask has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs. In Pokémon GO, this means an IV combination of 15/15/15.

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP

Is a 100% Ninjask Rare?

A perfect IV Pokémon is considerably less common than an ordinary IV specimen because all three IV values must reach 15. A 100% Ninjask can therefore be a worthwhile collector's Pokémon even if it is not a top raid attacker.

Perfect IV Tip

Keep a perfect Ninjask if you enjoy collecting hundos, building complete evolution families or maintaining a high-IV Pokédex.

23 • FAQ

Ninjask FAQ

What type is Ninjask?

Ninjask is a Bug / Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Ninjask weak to?

Ninjask is vulnerable to Fire, Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks, with Rock being particularly dangerous against its Bug / Flying typing.

What Pokémon does Ninjask evolve from?

Ninjask evolves from Nincada.

Is Shiny Ninjask available?

Shiny Ninjask is a collectible alternate-color form. Availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO encounter system.

Is Shadow Ninjask available?

Shadow availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations. Check the current in-game encounter pool before claiming that Shadow Ninjask is obtainable.

What is a 100% Ninjask?

A 100% Ninjask has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Ninjask?

Lucky Ninjask is a Ninjask that has obtained the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

What is XXL Ninjask?

XXL Ninjask is an exceptionally large Ninjask. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is XXS Ninjask?

XXS Ninjask is an exceptionally small Ninjask. Like XXL status, XXS size does not determine IV quality.

Is Ninjask good for raids?

Ninjask is generally a niche raid option rather than a top-tier Bug or Flying attacker. Stronger specialized Pokémon are usually better choices for optimized raid teams.

Is Ninjask good in PvP?

Ninjask can have niche PvP applications, but its performance depends heavily on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Does Shiny Ninjask have different stats?

No. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not inherently change Ninjask's base battle stats.

Does XXL Ninjask have higher stats?

No. XXL and XXS status are size characteristics and should not be confused with IVs or base battle stats.

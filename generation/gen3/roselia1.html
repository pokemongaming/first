Lucky Roselia

Lucky Roselia is a trade-based special variant. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when powered up, making them attractive for players planning to invest heavily.

Lucky Investment Advantage

A Lucky Roselia can be particularly useful if you plan to evolve it into Roserade and power the final evolution to a high level.

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term Roserade investment.
  • Can also be a valuable collection variant.

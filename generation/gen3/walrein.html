Walrein

Walrein is a dual Ice and Water-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its strong Ice typing, useful charged moves and relevance in certain PvP formats.

01 • OVERVIEW

Walrein Overview

Walrein is the final evolution of Spheal and Sealeo. It is a dual Ice/Water-type Pokémon with a large HP stat and access to moves that make it useful in both PvP and selected PvE situations.

100% IV Walrein

A Walrein with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP has perfect IVs and is commonly called a 100%, hundo or perfect Walrein by Pokémon GO players.

Are 100% IVs Always Best?

Not necessarily. PvP uses different IV priorities in many CP-limited leagues. A lower-Attack Walrein can sometimes reach a more favorable stat product under a league CP limit.

09 • PVP

Walrein PvP Guide

Walrein's combination of bulk, Ice typing and charged-move pressure makes PvP one of its most important uses.

Great League

Walrein can be a useful Great League option when built around efficient energy generation and strong charged-move pressure. Its matchup spread can change as the PvP meta and move balance change.

Ultra League

Walrein can also operate in Ultra League, where its higher available level and bulk can be valuable. IV selection becomes particularly important because CP limits influence the final build.

Master League

Walrein generally faces much stronger competition in Master League because the format favors Pokémon with higher overall stat ceilings.

PvP Build

Powder Snow + Icicle Spear + Earthquake is a notable PvP-oriented combination when available under the current Pokémon GO moveset rules.

23 • FAQ

Walrein FAQ

What type is Walrein?

Walrein is an Ice/Water-type Pokémon.

What is Walrein weak against?

Walrein is weak to Electric, Grass, Fighting, Rock and Steel-type attacks.

Is Walrein good in Pokémon GO?

Walrein can be useful, particularly in PvP. Its value for raids is more matchup-dependent.

Is Walrein good for PvP?

Yes. Walrein can be a strong PvP option in appropriate formats when equipped with a suitable moveset.

What is the best Walrein moveset?

A notable PvP-oriented combination is Powder Snow with Icicle Spear and Earthquake when those moves are currently available.

Is Shiny Walrein available?

Yes. Shiny Walrein is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Walrein available?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and Pokémon GO's active content.

What is a 100% IV Walrein?

A 100% IV Walrein has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is a Hundo Walrein good for PvP?

A hundo can be useful, but it is not automatically the optimal IV spread for CP-limited PvP leagues.

What is Lucky Walrein?

Lucky Walrein is a Walrein obtained through the Lucky Trade mechanic and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Walrein?

XXL Walrein is an unusually large Walrein. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Walrein?

XXS Walrein is an unusually small Walrein. XXS status does not automatically indicate better or worse battle performance.

Can Shadow Walrein be purified?

An eligible Shadow Walrein can be purified. Players should compare the Shadow and purified versions before making that decision.

Does Walrein evolve?

Yes. Walrein is the final evolution of Spheal and evolves from Sealeo.

What generation is Walrein from?

Walrein was introduced in Generation 3 and is associated with the Hoenn region.

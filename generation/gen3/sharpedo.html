Shiny Sharpedo is the alternate-color version of Sharpedo. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not automatically provide better battle stats.

Shiny Collection Tip

If you have multiple Shiny Sharpedo, compare IVs, level and future investment requirements before deciding which one to power up.

Shadow Sharpedo

Shadow Sharpedo has a more aggressive battle profile than regular Sharpedo. Shadow mechanics increase offensive pressure while reducing effective durability.

Should You Power Up Shadow Sharpedo?

  • Consider it if you want maximum offensive pressure.
  • Check its moveset before investing Stardust.
  • Remember that Shadow power-ups require additional resources.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Sharpedo

Purified Sharpedo is created when an eligible Shadow Sharpedo is purified. Purification removes the Shadow battle mechanic and can change IV values according to Pokémon GO's purification rules.

Purification Decision

Do not assume purification is always the best option. Compare your Shadow Sharpedo's battle value, IVs and resource goals before making the decision.

Lucky Sharpedo

Lucky Sharpedo is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups. This can make a high-IV Lucky Sharpedo a more economical long-term project.

Sharpedo PvP Guide

Sharpedo has a highly offensive stat profile but relatively poor durability. Its PvP value can depend heavily on shields, energy management and the current league environment.

Great League

Sharpedo can be a niche pick because its offensive pressure comes with significant durability limitations.

Ultra League

Sharpedo generally requires careful matchup planning due to its stat profile and CP limitations.

Master League

Sharpedo's maximum CP and durability profile make it less naturally suited to many Master League environments.

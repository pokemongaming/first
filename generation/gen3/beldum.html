01 • OVERVIEW

Beldum Overview

Beldum is a Generation 3 Pokémon first introduced in the Hoenn region. It is a dual Steel/Psychic type and is the first stage of the Beldum evolution family.

Unlike many Pokémon that are primarily evaluated as standalone attackers, Beldum's biggest long-term value comes from evolving it into Metang and then Metagross.

05 • EVOLUTION

Beldum Evolution Family

Beldum evolves twice. You need 25 Beldum Candy to evolve Beldum into Metang and another 100 Candy to evolve Metang into Metagross.

Beldum #374
→
Metang 25 Candy
→
Metagross 100 Candy
Total Candy Requirement

Starting with Beldum, you need 125 Beldum Candy to reach Metagross.

Why Beldum Candy Matters

If your goal is to build a strong Metagross, do not spend Beldum Candy randomly. Save Candy for the Beldum specimens you actually want to evolve and power up.

Perfect Beldum

A 15/15/15 Beldum is a perfect IV or "hundo" specimen. Because Beldum evolves into Metagross, a perfect specimen can be especially attractive for long-term investment.

Should You Keep a 100% Beldum?

  • Keep a 15/15/15 Beldum if you are building a long-term Metagross.
  • Check its level and current CP before transferring.
  • Consider whether it has a useful event or special background before evolving.
  • Save enough Candy for the complete evolution chain.
09 • SHINY BELDUM

✨ Shiny Beldum

Shiny Beldum is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny status is a collectible property and does not automatically make a Beldum stronger in battle.

SHINY

Shiny Beldum

Shiny Beldum has an alternate coloration compared with the standard Beldum. If you evolve a Shiny Beldum, its Shiny identity continues through Metang and Metagross.

Should You Evolve Shiny Beldum?

If you are collecting the full Shiny evolution family, keeping multiple Shiny Beldum can be useful. If you want a Shiny Metagross, choose the Beldum you like most before spending the Candy required for evolution.

Shadow Beldum and Frustration

Newly obtained Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Attack Frustration. Whether and when Frustration can be replaced depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket events and rules.

Do not purify automatically.

If you want a Shadow Metagross, compare the Shadow specimen before choosing purification. Purification changes the Pokémon and removes its Shadow status.

12 • LUCKY BELDUM

🍀 Lucky Beldum

Lucky Beldum is a special traded variant with a distinctive Lucky background. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

LUCKY

Lucky Beldum

A Lucky Beldum can be an attractive long-term investment because its reduced Stardust cost can make the eventual Metagross project cheaper.

Lucky Beldum IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary wild Pokémon. However, Lucky status alone does not guarantee a perfect 15/15/15 Beldum.

Is Lucky Beldum Worth Keeping?

  • Keep Lucky Beldum with strong IVs.
  • Consider it for a future Metagross build.
  • Compare its IVs with your other Beldum.
  • Lucky status can reduce long-term Stardust costs.
13 • 100% BELDUM

💯 100% IV Beldum

A 100% Beldum has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These Pokémon are commonly called "hundos" by the Pokémon GO community.

15 / 15 / 15

Perfect IV Beldum

A perfect Beldum has maximum IVs in all three appraisal categories.

100% Beldum CP

A Level 50 Beldum with 15/15/15 IVs reaches 1,104 CP. Encounter-specific CP values depend on the encounter level and IV floor.

Hundo tip:

If you find a perfect Beldum, consider saving it for Metagross rather than powering up Beldum itself.

XXL Beldum is a size variant determined by the Pokémon's individual height and weight. Size does not automatically mean higher IVs or higher CP.

19 • RESOURCES

Beldum Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a strong Metagross from Beldum requires a significant amount of Candy and Stardust. Because Beldum evolves twice, resource planning is important.

  • 25 Beldum Candy for Beldum → Metang.
  • 100 additional Beldum Candy for Metang → Metagross.
  • Save Candy XL for high-level investment.
  • Keep strong IV Beldum before transferring duplicates.
  • Lucky Beldum can be useful for reducing Stardust costs.
  • Compare the eventual Metagross build before committing major resources.

21 • INVESTMENT

Should You Power Up Beldum?

For Most Players

Usually, no. If you are interested in battle performance, it makes more sense to evolve a strong Beldum and evaluate the resulting Metagross before spending large amounts of Stardust on the first-stage Pokémon.

When Beldum Is Worth Keeping

  • 100% IV Beldum.
  • High-IV Beldum with a useful evolution goal.
  • Shiny Beldum.
  • Shadow Beldum.
  • Lucky Beldum.
  • XXL or XXS Beldum.
  • Special-event Beldum.
  • Dynamax-capable Beldum.
22 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Beldum

Before Transferring

  • Check the appraisal.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check Shadow or Purified status.
  • Check Lucky status.
  • Check whether it is XXS or XXL.
  • Check whether it is Dynamax-capable.

Before Evolving

Decide whether you want a regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky or Dynamax Metagross. Once you evolve a particular Beldum, you cannot reverse the evolution.

Long-Term Strategy

If you regularly participate in raids or collect special variants, maintaining several Beldum candidates can be more useful than immediately transferring every duplicate.

24 • FAQ

Beldum FAQ

What type is Beldum?

Beldum is a Steel/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Beldum weak against?

Beldum is weak to Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

What is Beldum's Max CP?

Beldum's maximum CP at Level 50 is 1,104 for a perfect 15/15/15 specimen.

How much Candy does Beldum need to evolve?

Beldum requires 25 Beldum Candy to evolve into Metang. Metang then requires another 100 Beldum Candy to evolve into Metagross.

Is Shiny Beldum available?

Yes. Shiny Beldum is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Beldum available?

Yes. Shadow Beldum exists as a separate Shadow variant.

Should I purify Shadow Beldum?

Not automatically. If you want a Shadow Metagross, consider keeping the Shadow Beldum rather than purifying it.

What is a Lucky Beldum?

Lucky Beldum is a special traded Pokémon with reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Beldum?

A 100% Beldum has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Beldum?

XXL Beldum is an extra-large size variant. Size is independent of IV quality.

What is an XXS Beldum?

XXS Beldum is an extra-small size variant. It can have any IV combination.

Is Beldum good for raids?

Beldum itself is not a major raid attacker. Its primary value is evolving into Metagross.

Should I power up Beldum?

Generally, it is more efficient to evolve a good Beldum first and invest in the resulting Metagross rather than heavily powering up Beldum itself.

Can Beldum Dynamax?

Yes. Beldum can appear as a Dynamax-capable Pokémon.

What Pokémon does Beldum evolve into?

Beldum evolves into Metang, which then evolves into Metagross.

Mudkip Overview

Mudkip is a Water-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 3. It is one of the Hoenn region's three starter Pokémon.

In Pokémon GO, Mudkip can evolve into Marshtomp and eventually Swampert. Mudkip is also available in several individual variants and attributes, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS.

    Shiny Mudkip is the alternate-color version of Mudkip. Its Shiny status is a collectible attribute and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

    Shiny Mudkip

    Alternate coloration with the same species, typing and base battle identity as regular Mudkip.

    💧
    Regular Mudkip

    Standard-color Mudkip and the normal starting point of the evolution family.

    Shiny Mudkip Evolution

    A Shiny Mudkip can be evolved while retaining its Shiny status. This means Shiny Mudkip can eventually become Shiny Marshtomp and Shiny Swampert.

    Shadow Mudkip

    Shadow Mudkip is a Shadow version of Mudkip. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from their regular counterparts.

    🌑
    Shadow Mudkip

    A Shadow Pokémon that can be evaluated separately from regular Mudkip when planning evolution and investment.

    💧
    Shadow Type

    Shadow Mudkip remains a pure Water-type Pokémon.

    Shadow Mudkip Weaknesses

    Shadow status does not change Mudkip's basic Water typing. Its primary type weaknesses remain Grass and Electric.

    Should You Evolve Shadow Mudkip?

    • Check the Shadow Mudkip's IVs.
    • Consider its future role after evolution.
    • Check whether Frustration is present.
    • Compare the investment with regular Mudkip.
    • Avoid purifying automatically if you want to preserve the Shadow battle bonus.
    Shadow note:

    Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive power and reduced defensive performance compared with their regular counterparts. Their value should therefore be judged according to the intended battle role.

    Lucky Mudkip

    Lucky Mudkip is a special trade-related status. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background in the Pokémon GO storage interface and require fewer Stardust resources to power up.

    A Lucky Mudkip can be useful for players who intend to evolve it into a long-term Swampert investment while reducing Stardust costs.

    100% IV Mudkip

    A 100% IV Mudkip has the maximum possible Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

    15 Attack IV
    15 Defense IV
    15 HP IV

    How to Identify a Hundo Mudkip

    A Mudkip with 15/15/15 IVs is commonly called a "hundo" or 100% IV Pokémon. Use the in-game appraisal system or a reliable IV-checking method to confirm its individual values.

    Important

    100% IV does not mean a Pokémon always has the highest possible CP. CP also depends on Pokémon level.

    XXL Mudkip

    XXL Mudkip is an exceptionally large individual Mudkip. Size is separate from IVs, CP and battle moves.

    XXL Mudkip Large-size Mudkip that can be valuable for collectors and size-focused achievements.
    Does XXL Mean Stronger? No. XXL status does not automatically mean higher Attack, Defense or HP.

    XXL Mudkip Collection Value

    An XXL Mudkip can be especially interesting when combined with other desirable attributes such as Shiny status, strong IVs or Lucky status.

    XXS Mudkip

    XXS Mudkip is an exceptionally small individual Mudkip. Like XXL status, XXS is a size attribute rather than a separate species or evolution.

    XXS Mudkip Very small Mudkip that can be desirable for collectors.
    Size vs IV XXS status does not determine whether Mudkip has perfect IVs.

    XXS Mudkip Collector Value

    A rare combination such as XXS + Shiny + high IVs can make an individual Mudkip particularly interesting to collectors.

    Mudkip FAQ

    What type is Mudkip?

    Mudkip is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

    What is Mudkip weak to?

    Mudkip is weak to Grass- and Electric-type attacks.

    What is Mudkip's Pokédex number?

    Mudkip is Pokédex number #258.

    What does Mudkip evolve into?

    Mudkip evolves into Marshtomp and then Swampert.

    Is Shiny Mudkip available?

    Yes. Shiny Mudkip is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

    Is Shadow Mudkip available?

    Shadow Mudkip can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before publication.

    What is a 100% Mudkip?

    A 100% Mudkip has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

    What is Lucky Mudkip?

    Lucky Mudkip is a Mudkip that has obtained Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

    What is XXL Mudkip?

    XXL Mudkip is an unusually large Mudkip. XXL is a size attribute and does not automatically indicate higher IVs or stronger battle performance.

    What is XXS Mudkip?

    XXS Mudkip is an unusually small Mudkip. XXS is a size attribute rather than a separate Pokémon species.

    Is Mudkip good for raids?

    Mudkip itself is generally more useful as an evolution candidate than as a standalone raid attacker.

    Should I keep a 100% Mudkip?

    A 100% Mudkip is usually worth keeping, especially if you are interested in evolving it or collecting perfect-IV Pokémon.

    Should I purify Shadow Mudkip?

    Not automatically. Consider the Shadow bonus, IVs, future evolution and your personal collection or battle goals before purifying it.

    Can Shiny Mudkip evolve?

    Yes. A Shiny Mudkip retains its Shiny status when evolved.

    Can Lucky Mudkip evolve?

    Yes. Lucky Mudkip can evolve while retaining its Lucky status.

    Does XXL Mudkip have better IVs?

    No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Mudkip can have any IV combination.

    Does XXS Mudkip have worse stats?

    XXS status does not by itself determine the Pokémon's IVs or battle stats.

02 • TYPE

Marshtomp Type

🌊
Water / Ground Dual Type Pokémon

Why Marshtomp's Typing Matters

Marshtomp's Water/Ground combination gives it a very different matchup profile from a pure Water-type Pokémon. Most importantly, the Ground typing makes it immune to Electric-type attacks in Pokémon GO's type system.

The major drawback is its severe weakness to Grass-type attacks. Grass attacks are especially dangerous because both of Marshtomp's types are vulnerable to Grass.

    12 • PURIFIED

    Purified Marshtomp

    Purified Marshtomp is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Marshtomp. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

    🌟

    Purified Marshtomp

    Purified Marshtomp is no longer a Shadow Pokémon and can receive the benefits associated with purification.

    💎

    Purification Bonus

    Purification can increase each IV by 2 points, up to the normal IV maximum of 15.

    Should You Purify Shadow Marshtomp?

    Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically. Consider whether you value the Shadow battle profile, the purified form, lower power-up costs or the collection value of keeping the Pokémon Shadow.

    13 • LUCKY

    Lucky Marshtomp

    Lucky Marshtomp is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

    🍀

    Lucky Marshtomp

    Lucky status does not change Marshtomp's species or Water/Ground typing.

    💰

    Lower Stardust Cost

    Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when powered up, making Lucky Marshtomp useful for players who want to reduce investment costs.

    Lucky IV reminder:

    Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, but a Lucky Marshtomp is not automatically a 100% IV Marshtomp.

    14 • SIZE

    XXL Marshtomp

    XXL Marshtomp is an unusually large individual based on Pokémon GO's size system. XXL status is a size classification, not a different species or evolution.

    📏

    XXL Marshtomp

    An XXL Marshtomp can be kept as a collection specimen and may be useful for size-related medals, showcases or other game features where applicable.

    XXL Does Not Mean Higher IV

    A Pokémon can be XXL without having perfect IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

    15 • SIZE

    XXS Marshtomp

    🔹

    An XXS Marshtomp can be valuable to collectors and may be relevant to size-based showcases or game features when applicable.

    XXS reminder:

    17 • PVP

    Marshtomp PvP Guide

    Marshtomp is generally overshadowed by its evolution, Swampert, in standard PvP. However, Marshtomp can still be interesting in restricted formats where its specific moveset and typing are useful.

    Great League

    Marshtomp's CP range allows it to reach the Great League limit, making it the most natural traditional PvP format to investigate if Marshtomp is allowed.

    Ultra League

    Marshtomp is less naturally suited to Ultra League because of its lower maximum CP compared with Pokémon specifically built for the format.

    Master League

    Marshtomp's maximum CP is far too low for it to be a competitive standard Master League choice.

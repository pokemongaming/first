Latios IV Guide

Individual Values add additional Attack, Defense and HP points. A 15/15/15 Latios is commonly called a perfect or hundo Latios.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

100% / Hundo Latios

A 15/15/15 Latios is a 100% IV or hundo Latios. It is especially desirable for collectors, long-term investments and players who want the highest possible IV combination.

Perfect IV Combination

Attack 15 / Defense 15 / HP 15

A lower-IV Latios can still perform very well. IV perfection should not automatically outweigh level, moveset, form and the role you need the Pokémon to fill.

Latios PvP Guide

Latios has access to strong Dragon and Psychic attacks, but its PvP usefulness depends on the current league rules and metagame.

Master League

Latios's high maximum CP makes Master League the most natural open PvP environment to consider. Its Dragon typing can provide useful offensive pressure, while its weaknesses require careful matchup management.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Latios is generally less natural for the lower CP leagues because of its high base stats. Special cups and restricted formats can change this evaluation.

Latios FAQ

Is Latios a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Latios is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3.

What type is Latios?

Latios is a Dragon and Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Latios weak to?

Latios is weak to Bug, Dark, Dragon, Fairy, Ghost and Ice-type attacks.

Does Latios evolve?

Latios does not have a conventional evolution. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Latios.

Is Shiny Latios available?

Yes. Shiny Latios is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Latios can be useful as a Dragon or Psychic attacker, depending on its moveset and the raid matchup.

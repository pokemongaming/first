Dusclops

Dusclops is a Ghost-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its unusually high Defense, Ghost typing, PvP applications and its evolution into Dusknoir. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Dusclops.

01 • OVERVIEW

Dusclops Overview

Dusclops is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Duskull and can later evolve into Dusknoir when the required evolution conditions are met. Unlike many offensive Ghost Pokémon, Dusclops has a particularly high Defense stat and a much lower Attack stat.

Why Dusclops' Typing Matters

Dusclops' pure Ghost typing gives it useful matchups against several types while leaving it vulnerable to Ghost and Dark attacks. Its typing also provides important defensive advantages against Normal and Fighting moves.

What Does 100% Dusclops Mean?

A 100% Dusclops has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs. This is known as a perfect-IV Pokémon.

Is a 100% Dusclops Automatically the Best?

Not necessarily. IV priorities depend on the battle league and intended role. A perfect IV specimen is excellent for collection, but PvP optimization can favor different IV distributions.

09 • PVP

Dusclops PvP Guide

Dusclops' high Defense makes it much more interesting in PvP than its low Attack might suggest.

Great League

Ultra League

23 • FAQ

Dusclops FAQ

What type is Dusclops?

Dusclops is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Dusclops weak to?

Dusclops is weak to Ghost and Dark attacks.

What is Dusclops' max CP?

Dusclops reaches 1,799 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Dusclops' stats?

Dusclops has 124 Attack, 234 Defense and 120 HP/Stamina in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Dusclops available?

Yes. Shiny Dusclops is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Dusclops?

A 100% Dusclops has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

Is Shadow Dusclops different from regular Dusclops?

Yes. Shadow Dusclops has Shadow mechanics that change its battle characteristics compared with regular Dusclops.

Should I purify Shadow Dusclops?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow version's IVs, moves and intended use before purifying it.

What is Lucky Dusclops?

Lucky Dusclops is a special traded version of Dusclops that receives the Lucky status and reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Can Dusclops be XXL?

Yes. Dusclops can have an XXL size classification.

Can Dusclops be XXS?

Yes. Dusclops can have an XXS size classification.

Does XXL Dusclops mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Dusclops can have any valid IV combination.

Does Shiny Dusclops have different battle stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily an appearance distinction; it does not inherently give Dusclops different species battle stats.

Is Dusclops good for PvP?

Dusclops can have PvP value because of its high Defense and available charged-move coverage. Its usefulness depends on the league and current metagame.

Is Dusclops good for raids?

Dusclops is generally more notable for its defensive profile and PvP applications than for maximizing Ghost-type raid damage.

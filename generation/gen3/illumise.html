What Does Illumise's Bug Typing Mean?

Illumise is a pure Bug-type Pokémon, meaning it receives Same-Type Attack Bonus from Bug moves and has a straightforward defensive profile. Its Bug typing is useful against Grass, Psychic and Dark Pokémon, while its main weaknesses are Fire, Flying and Rock.

Illumise PvP Guide

Illumise can technically participate in lower CP PvP formats, but it is not considered a top-tier Great League or Ultra League choice.

Great League

Illumise can fit under the 1,500 CP Great League limit. However, its moves and overall battle performance make it a niche option rather than a meta-defining Pokémon.

Ultra League

Illumise can technically reach the Ultra League CP range, but its performance remains limited compared with stronger Pokémon available in the format.

Master League

Illumise's maximum CP of 2,002 makes it unsuitable for the traditional Master League environment.

Lucky Illumise

Lucky Illumise is possible through Pokémon GO's trading system. A Lucky Pokémon has special trading-related benefits and requires less Stardust to power up.

🍀 Lucky Illumise

Lucky Illumise is especially attractive to collectors because it combines the Lucky background/status with a relatively uncommon Pokémon species.

Lucky Illumise IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor when received through a trade. A Lucky Illumise can therefore be easier to obtain with solid IVs than an unrestricted wild catch.

  • Keep Lucky Illumise if you collect special forms.
  • Check its IV appraisal after the trade.
  • Consider its reduced Stardust power-up cost.
  • A Lucky Shiny Illumise is an especially desirable collector combination.

Illumise Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Powering Illumise requires Stardust and Illumise Candy, with Candy XL becoming relevant for higher-level investment.

  • Save Illumise Candy for Pokémon you genuinely intend to power up.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for collection goals.
  • Check whether a Lucky Illumise can reduce Stardust investment.
  • Collect Candy XL if you want a Level 40+ Illumise.
  • Keep rare combinations such as Shiny + Lucky, Shiny + Hundo or XXL + Shiny.

Seedot

Seedot is a Grass-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is the first stage of the Seedot evolution family and can evolve into Nuzleaf and then Shiftry. Collectors can also encounter special versions such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Seedot.

07 • SPECIAL VARIANTS

Seedot Forms & Special Versions

Seedot can exist as multiple collectible versions. Some variants affect battle mechanics and resource costs, while others primarily affect appearance, size or collection value.

🌰

Regular Seedot

The standard version of Seedot. Regular Seedot can evolve through the normal Seedot evolution family.

✨

Shiny Seedot

Shiny Seedot has alternate coloration and is primarily valuable as a collector's version. Its battle performance is determined by its level, IVs and moves rather than its Shiny status.

👤

Shadow Seedot

Shadow Seedot is a Team GO Rocket variant. Shadow status changes battle mechanics and creates a separate investment decision from regular Seedot.

✨

Purified Seedot

A Purified Seedot is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Seedot. Purification changes its status and should be considered carefully before evolving.

🍀

Lucky Seedot

Lucky Seedot requires less Stardust to power up than a standard Pokémon. This can make a high-quality Lucky Seedot more attractive for a long-term evolution project.

💯

100% IV Seedot

A 100% IV Seedot has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Many players keep these specimens as collector targets or potential evolution investments.

📏

XXL Seedot

XXL Seedot is an unusually large individual. Size is primarily a collection characteristic and does not directly make the Pokémon stronger in normal battle stats.

🔬

XXS Seedot

XXS Seedot is an unusually small individual. Like XXL Pokémon, its size is primarily relevant to collection and size-based features.

08 • SHINY

Shiny Seedot

Shiny Seedot is an alternate-colour version of regular Seedot. The Shiny status does not automatically improve Attack, Defense, HP or IVs.

Shiny Collection Tip

If you obtain a Shiny Seedot with useful IVs, decide whether you prefer keeping the base form or evolving it into Shiny Nuzleaf and Shiny Shiftry.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Seedot

Purified Seedot is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Seedot. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and may influence its IVs and resource requirements.

👤

Before Purification

Shadow Seedot keeps its Shadow status and Shadow-specific battle mechanics.

✨

After Purification

Purified Seedot becomes a non-Shadow Pokémon with Purified status.

Should You Purify Seedot?

  • Check the Seedot's IVs before purifying.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve the Shadow version.
  • Do not purify automatically for cosmetic reasons.
  • Compare long-term evolution plans before committing.
  • Consider keeping unusual Shadow Pokémon for your collection.
11 • LUCKY

Lucky Seedot

Lucky Seedot is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. A Lucky Seedot can therefore be a resource-efficient candidate for evolution and long-term investment.

Lucky Evolution Strategy

If you receive a strong Lucky Seedot, consider whether you want to evolve it into Nuzleaf and eventually Shiftry before investing Stardust.

16 • PVP

Seedot PvP Guide

Seedot itself is generally not the primary competitive target of its evolution family because of its low stats. Players interested in competitive use will usually evaluate Nuzleaf and especially Shiftry separately.

PvP Evolution Planning

Do not assume a 100% IV Seedot is automatically the best choice for every PvP league. League-specific CP limits can make different IV distributions desirable.

17 • RAID USE

Is Seedot Good for Raids?

Seedot is primarily an early-stage Pokémon and is generally not built as a dedicated high-level raid attacker. Its long-term battle value comes from its evolution family.

  • Use Seedot mainly as an evolution project.
  • Evaluate Nuzleaf separately.
  • Check Shiftry for final PvP and battle roles.
  • Keep high-IV or rare variants for collection purposes.

19 • FAQ

Seedot FAQ

What type is Seedot?

Seedot is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What does Seedot evolve into?

Seedot evolves into Nuzleaf, which can eventually evolve into Shiftry.

Can Seedot be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Seedot is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when included in the game's availability pool.

Can Seedot be Shadow?

Shadow Seedot can be available when it is included in an eligible Team GO Rocket rotation.

Can Shadow Seedot be purified?

Eligible Shadow Seedot can be purified into Purified Seedot.

What is a 100% Seedot?

A 100% Seedot has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is Lucky Seedot useful?

Lucky Seedot can be useful as an evolution project because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Does XXL Seedot have higher stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase Seedot's normal battle stats.

Does XXS Seedot have lower stats?

No. XXS size does not automatically reduce Seedot's normal battle stats.

Should I evolve a Shiny Seedot?

That depends on your collection goals. Some players prefer keeping Shiny base forms, while others evolve them to obtain Shiny versions of the entire evolution family.

Is Seedot good for raids?

Seedot itself is generally not a major raid attacker. Its evolved forms are more relevant for long-term battle use.

Absol

Absol is a Dark-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is available in multiple collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms. Absol can also Mega Evolve into Mega Absol.

Absol Overview

Absol is a pure Dark-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It does not evolve through a normal evolution chain, but it can Mega Evolve into Mega Absol when Mega Energy and the required game mechanics are available.

Why Absol's Typing Matters

Dark-type attacks are particularly valuable against Psychic and Ghost Pokémon. Absol's offensive stat profile makes it capable of dealing strong damage, although its relatively low Defense means it can be fragile in difficult battles.

Should You Power Up Shadow Absol?

  • Check the IVs before investing heavily.
  • Compare Shadow Absol with other Dark attackers.
  • Consider its relatively low survivability.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Avoid purifying automatically without comparing the possible outcomes.

Purified Absol

Purified Absol is created when an eligible Shadow Absol is purified. Purification changes its status and removes the Shadow battle profile.

💎 Purified Absol

Purified Absol becomes a standard non-Shadow Absol with purification-related mechanics and reduced power-up costs compared with Shadow investment.

Absol FAQ

What type is Absol?

Absol is a pure Dark-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Absol available?

Shiny Absol can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Absol stronger than regular Absol?

Shadow Absol has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Absol?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your goals.

What is a Lucky Absol?

Lucky Absol is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trade system and requires less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Absol?

A 100% Absol has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Absol?

What is XXS Absol?

XXS Absol is an unusually small size variant.

Can Absol Mega Evolve?

Yes. Absol can Mega Evolve into Mega Absol when the required Mega Evolution mechanics are available.

Is Mega Absol good for raids?

Mega Absol can be a strong Dark-focused option, particularly when its offensive role and Mega bonuses are useful for the raid team.

ICE-TYPE POKÉMON

Snorunt

Snorunt is an Ice-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as an evolution-family Pokémon that can evolve into Glalie or, under the appropriate conditions, Froslass. Snorunt is also available in several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV forms.

01 • OVERVIEW

Snorunt Overview

Snorunt is a pure Ice-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is one of the more interesting evolution-family Pokémon because its evolution path can lead to two different Pokémon.

Male Snorunt can evolve into Glalie, while a female Snorunt can evolve into Froslass when the required evolution conditions are met.

Why Snorunt's Typing Matters

What Is a 100% Snorunt?

A 100% Snorunt has a perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IV spread. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a hundo.

💯 100% Snorunt

A perfect Snorunt can be particularly valuable for collectors and players planning to evolve it into Glalie or, where eligible, Froslass.

Should You Keep a 100% Snorunt?

  • Keep it if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon.
  • Consider evolving it after deciding between Glalie and Froslass.
  • Check its eventual evolution's battle usefulness before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • A hundo is also useful as a long-term collection milestone.
10 • PVP

Snorunt PvP Guide

Snorunt has limited relevance in standard PvP compared with its evolved forms and stronger Pokémon available for Great and Ultra League.

Great League

Snorunt can technically fit within the Great League CP limit, but its overall stats and battle performance make it a niche choice rather than a broadly preferred competitive Pokémon.

Ultra League

Snorunt's relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League use difficult. Evolved alternatives are generally more practical.

Master League

Snorunt is not naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP and battle statistics are too low for the format's high-power environment.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Snorunt

Lucky Snorunt is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups and guaranteed minimum IV values from the Lucky trade mechanic.

🍀 Lucky Snorunt

A Lucky Snorunt is particularly attractive to collectors and players who want to evolve a special Snorunt while benefiting from the Lucky power-up discount.

Why Keep a Lucky Snorunt?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky trades guarantee a minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky Snorunt can be evolved while retaining its Lucky status.
  • Lucky status makes Snorunt more interesting as a collection Pokémon.

23 • FAQ

Snorunt FAQ

What type is Snorunt?

Snorunt is a pure Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Snorunt weak to?

Snorunt is weak to Fire, Fighting, Rock and Steel-type attacks.

What is Snorunt's max CP?

Snorunt's maximum CP is approximately 888 under standard Pokémon GO CP calculations.

Is Shiny Snorunt available?

Yes. Shiny Snorunt is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Snorunt evolve?

Yes. Snorunt can evolve into Glalie, while eligible female Snorunt can evolve into Froslass.

Can male Snorunt evolve into Froslass?

No. Froslass evolution requires a female Snorunt and the applicable evolution conditions.

Is Shadow Snorunt available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current events.

What is Purified Snorunt?

Purified Snorunt is a Snorunt that was previously Shadow and has been purified.

What is Lucky Snorunt?

Lucky Snorunt is a special trade outcome that receives Lucky Pokémon benefits.

What is a 100% Snorunt?

A 100% Snorunt has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Snorunt?

What is XXS Snorunt?

Is Snorunt good for raids?

Snorunt itself is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolution family and collection potential.

Should I keep a 100% Snorunt?

Yes, especially if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon or want to evolve the specimen into Glalie or Froslass.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Snorunt?

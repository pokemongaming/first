Castform

01 / OVERVIEW

Castform Overview

Castform is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. Its defining characteristic is its ability to change form depending on weather. In Pokémon GO, Castform is represented by several forms, including Normal Castform, Sunny Castform, Rainy Castform and Snowy Castform.

12 / SIZE

XXL & XXS Castform

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS are separate size characteristics and should not be confused with IVs.

⭐

Important:

Size and IVs are different attributes. An XXL Castform is not automatically a high-IV Castform, and an XXS Castform is not automatically a low-IV Pokémon.

15 / PVP

Castform PvP Guide

Castform is generally more of a collection and novelty Pokémon than a top-tier competitive choice. However, different forms can have different typing and move characteristics.

Great League

Castform's relatively low maximum CP makes the Great League the most natural CP-limited environment to evaluate its standard form. Actual usefulness depends heavily on the current moveset and metagame.

Ultra League

Castform can be evaluated in Ultra League through appropriate level and IV combinations, although it is generally not considered a primary competitive target.

Master League

Castform's CP and overall battle profile make Master League investment substantially less attractive than many stronger Pokémon.

Should You Keep Low-IV Castform?

Yes, especially when collecting forms. A low-IV Castform can still be useful as a Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, Purified, XXS, XXL or weather-form collector piece.

Collector rule:

Do not automatically transfer a Castform simply because its IV percentage is low. Form, size, Shiny status, Lucky status and personal collection goals can all matter.

21 / BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Castform

Before Investing Stardust

  • Identify the exact Castform form.
  • Check its IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny, Shadow or Lucky.
  • Check its size classification.
  • Check its current moveset.
  • Consider whether you are building it for PvP or collecting.

For Collectors

Form and rarity can matter much more than battle performance. A low-IV Shiny Snowy Castform, for example, may be more valuable to a collector than a higher-IV ordinary Castform.

For PvP Players

Check current PvP rankings and movesets before spending large amounts of Stardust. A Pokémon's usefulness can change when moves or league metas change.

Castform FAQ

What type is Castform?

Normal Castform is a Normal-type Pokémon. Its weather forms have different types.

How many Castform forms are there in Pokémon GO?

The main Castform forms are Normal, Sunny, Rainy and Snowy Castform.

What type is Sunny Castform?

Sunny Castform is Fire type.

What type is Rainy Castform?

Rainy Castform is Water type.

What type is Snowy Castform?

Snowy Castform is Ice type.

Is Shiny Castform available?

What is a 100% Castform?

A 100% Castform has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Castform be Lucky?

Can Castform be XXL?

Castform can be tracked by Pokémon GO's size classifications when the applicable size mechanics are used.

Is Castform good for raids?

Which Castform form should collectors keep?

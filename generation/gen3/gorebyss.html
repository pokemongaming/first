Gorebyss Overview

Gorebyss is a pure Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is part of the Clamperl evolutionary family and evolves separately from Clamperl into either Gorebyss or Huntail depending on the evolution result available through Pokémon GO's evolution system.

Shadow Gorebyss

Shadow Gorebyss is the Shadow version of Gorebyss. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts, emphasizing increased offensive pressure while sacrificing some durability.

🌑 Shadow Gorebyss

A Shadow Gorebyss retains the Water typing of the Gorebyss species while using Shadow battle mechanics.

Shadow Gorebyss Characteristics

  • Retains Gorebyss's Water typing.
  • Has a different offensive and defensive battle profile.
  • Can require additional resources for power-ups.
  • May have Frustration depending on current Shadow mechanics.
  • Should be evaluated separately from a regular Gorebyss.
Shadow availability note:

Shadow Pokémon availability can change through Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check current game data before claiming that Shadow Gorebyss is currently obtainable.

Purified Gorebyss

Purified Gorebyss is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Gorebyss. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state into a Purified version.

✨ Purified Gorebyss

Purified Gorebyss is visually and mechanically distinct from the Shadow version while remaining the same Gorebyss species.

Spinda's pure Normal typing gives it a simple defensive profile. Its primary weakness is Fighting, while Ghost-type attacks are particularly ineffective against Normal-type Pokémon.

What Is a 100% Spinda?

A 100% Spinda means the Pokémon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is also called a perfect-IV or hundo Spinda.

Perfect Spinda

Absolutely for collectors. A perfect Spinda is especially attractive when combined with a rare pattern, Shiny status or special size.

SPECIAL STATUS

Shadow Spinda

Shadow Spinda should be treated differently from species that have an officially released Shadow form. It is not currently a standard obtainable Shadow Pokémon GO form.

🌑

Shadow Spinda — Not Currently Available

Pokémon GO has not established Shadow Spinda as a currently obtainable standard Shadow species. Do not label a regular Spinda as Shadow simply because it has a dark appearance in artwork.

If Shadow Spinda Is Ever Released

If Niantic introduces Shadow Spinda in a future Team GO Rocket rotation, this section should be updated with its Shadow mechanics, availability, Frustration information, IV considerations and battle performance.

Database recommendation:

Keep unavailable forms marked as unavailable rather than generating fictional Shadow stats or claiming that players can currently obtain Shadow Spinda.

Purified Spinda

Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon. Because Shadow Spinda is not currently an obtainable standard form, there is no normal current route for obtaining a Purified Spinda.

💠

Purified Spinda — Not Currently Available

There is currently no standard Shadow-to-Purified Spinda pathway in Pokémon GO.

Why This Matters

Purified status should not be confused with Lucky status. A Lucky Spinda can exist through trading, while Purified status requires the Pokémon to have originated as an eligible Shadow Pokémon.

XXS & XXL Spinda

Pokémon GO supports size classifications for Pokémon, allowing collectors to find unusually small and unusually large specimens.

XXS Spinda

XXS Spinda is an unusually small Spinda. For collectors, an XXS specimen can become more valuable when combined with a rare pattern, Shiny status or perfect IVs.

XXL Spinda

XXL Spinda is an unusually large specimen. XXL size is independent of IV quality, so an XXL Spinda can also separately be a 100%, Shiny or Lucky Pokémon.

Collector Combination

Shiny + rare pattern + 100% IV + XXL/XXS would represent an especially unusual Spinda collection target.

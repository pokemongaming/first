Pelipper Overview

Pelipper is a dual Water/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Wingull after collecting the required Candy. Unlike many high-stat Pokémon, Pelipper's main Pokémon GO identity is not as a top raid attacker; instead, its typing and move combination can make it useful in PvP formats.

Why Pelipper's Typing Matters

Pelipper's Water/Flying combination gives it useful resistances and a distinctive battle profile. The Flying typing removes the normal Ground weakness associated with many pure Water Pokémon, while also giving Pelipper a strong relationship with Ground-type attacks.

However, the dual typing also creates a major vulnerability to Electric attacks, which deal especially strong damage to Pelipper.

Pelipper PvP Guide

PvP is one of Pelipper's strongest reasons to exist in a Pokémon GO collection. Its Water/Flying typing, useful resistances and charged-move options can give it meaningful Great League utility.

Great League

Pelipper can be particularly useful in Great League because its CP is suitable for the format and its typing gives it interesting matchups. Wing Attack and Water-type charged pressure allow it to threaten several Pokémon while forcing opponents to consider their shield usage.

Ultra League

Pelipper can also be considered in higher CP formats when properly powered up, although its effectiveness depends on the current meta, available moves and team composition.

Master League

Pelipper's relatively low maximum CP makes it a much less natural choice for Master League compared with Pokémon built around very high CP ceilings.

Pelipper FAQ

What type is Pelipper?

Pelipper is a Water/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Pelipper weak to?

Pelipper is weak to Electric, Rock and Ice attacks. Electric attacks are particularly dangerous because of its dual typing.

Does Pelipper evolve?

Pelipper evolves from Wingull and does not evolve further.

How do you evolve Wingull into Pelipper?

Wingull can evolve into Pelipper using the required amount of Wingull Candy.

Is Shiny Pelipper available?

Yes. Shiny Pelipper is available through eligible Pokémon GO Shiny encounters.

Is Pelipper good for raids?

Pelipper is generally not a top raid attacker. Its main strength in Pokémon GO is its PvP utility rather than high-end raid damage.

Is Pelipper good in Great League?

Pelipper can be a useful Great League Pokémon because of its Water/Flying typing and charged-move pressure. Its actual performance depends on the current PvP meta and moveset.

What is a 100% Pelipper?

A 100% Pelipper has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Pelipper?

A Lucky Pelipper is obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics and benefits from a reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

What is Shadow Pelipper?

Shadow Pelipper is the Shadow variant of Pelipper. It retains the Water/Flying typing but has the different battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Pelipper?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, moves, battle usefulness and your collection goals before purifying it.

What is XXL Pelipper?

XXL Pelipper is a particularly large Pelipper size variant. Size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Pelipper?

XXS Pelipper is a particularly small Pelipper size variant. It can be valuable to collectors who collect different Pokémon sizes.

Is a 100% Pelipper always the best PvP Pelipper?

No. For CP-limited PvP formats, a lower-Attack IV combination can sometimes provide a more favorable stat distribution. The ideal IVs depend on the league and specific build.

Can a Shiny Pelipper be Lucky?

Yes. A Pokémon can have multiple special characteristics, so a Pelipper can potentially combine characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky status.

Can a Shadow Pelipper be Shiny?

Special Pokémon characteristics can have different availability rules. Check current Pokémon GO encounter mechanics before claiming that a particular combination is currently obtainable.

Why Solrock's Typing Matters

Solrock's Rock/Psychic combination gives it a distinctive defensive profile. The dual typing creates several weaknesses, but it also provides useful resistances to attacks such as Normal, Flying, Poison, Fire and Psychic.

Solrock PvP Guide

Solrock can be considered in PvP because its dual typing provides useful offensive and defensive interactions. League performance depends heavily on IV spread, moves and the current metagame.

Great League

Solrock's relatively moderate CP ceiling makes Great League evaluation possible. When building for a CP-limited league, do not automatically use a 15/15/15 Solrock. League-specific IV spreads can sometimes provide better stat distribution.

Ultra League

Solrock can also be evaluated in Ultra League, where its higher available level and dual typing may create interesting matchups.

Master League

Solrock's maximum CP makes Master League a much less natural environment for it compared with higher-stat Pokémon.

PvP Reminder

A 100% IV Solrock is excellent for collection, but a PvP Solrock may prefer a different IV distribution depending on the league.

Solrock FAQ

What type is Solrock?

Solrock is a Rock/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Solrock weak to?

Solrock is weak to Bug, Dark, Ghost, Grass, Ground, Steel and Water-type attacks.

What is Solrock's max CP?

Solrock reaches 2,631 CP at Level 50 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What are Solrock's base stats?

Solrock has 178 Attack, 153 Defense and 207 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Solrock available?

Yes. Shiny Solrock is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Solrock evolve?

No. Solrock is a standalone Pokémon and does not have a normal evolution.

What is a 100% Solrock?

A 100% or hundo Solrock has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Solrock rare?

XXL is a size classification rather than an IV category. Collectors may value unusually large Solrock specimens.

What is XXS Solrock?

XXS Solrock is a Solrock in the smallest size category. Size is separate from IVs.

Is Lucky Solrock worth keeping?

Lucky Solrock can be worthwhile for collectors and can be useful when you want to reduce Stardust costs during power-ups.

Is Shadow Solrock available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Team GO Rocket rotation. Do not assume a Shadow variant is currently obtainable without confirming the active game rotation.

Is Purified Solrock available?

A Purified Pokémon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Its availability therefore depends on whether Solrock can currently be obtained as a Shadow Pokémon.

Is Solrock good for raids?

Solrock is generally not considered a top-tier dedicated raid attacker. It is more useful as a collection Pokémon or for players who specifically enjoy using it.

Is Solrock good for PvP?

Solrock can have PvP applications, particularly in CP-limited formats, but its usefulness depends on the league, moveset, IV spread and current metagame.

Is 15/15/15 always the best Solrock IV?

A 15/15/15 Solrock is the best IV combination for maximizing total IVs and is ideal for a hundo collection. PvP can favor different IV distributions depending on the league.

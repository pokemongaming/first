Hariyama

Hariyama is a powerful Fighting-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. Known for its massive HP stat and strong physical attacks, Hariyama can be useful for players looking for a bulky Fighting-type attacker in Pokémon GO.

Hariyama Overview

Hariyama is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Makuhita and is notable for its exceptionally high Stamina compared with many other Fighting-type Pokémon.

Why Hariyama's Fighting Typing Matters

Hariyama's Fighting typing gives it access to useful matchups against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark-type Pokémon. This allows Hariyama to serve as a specialist counter when Fighting damage is effective.

Hariyama for Raids

Hariyama can be a useful Fighting-type raid attacker, particularly for players who need an accessible Pokémon for battles against bosses weak to Fighting damage.

  • Useful against Normal-type raid bosses.
  • Can help against Rock-type opponents.
  • Useful in some Steel-type matchups.
  • Can be considered against Ice-type raid bosses.
  • Move availability should be checked before investment.

Hariyama PvP Guide

Hariyama's combination of high HP and Fighting-type pressure can make it interesting in certain PvP formats. Its weaknesses to Flying, Psychic and Fairy Pokémon must be considered carefully during team building.

Great League

Hariyama can be explored as a bulky Fighting-type option, although the exact usefulness depends heavily on the current PvP meta and moveset.

Ultra League

Hariyama can reach a competitive CP range for Ultra League, but investment should be compared with other available Fighting-type Pokémon.

Master League

Hariyama's maximum CP is relatively limited compared with Legendary and pseudo-Legendary Master League Pokémon, so it is generally more situational in this format.

Shadow Hariyama

Shadow Hariyama is the Shadow version of Hariyama. Shadow Pokémon trade some defensive durability for increased offensive pressure, making Shadow Hariyama a separate investment decision from regular Hariyama.

🌑

Shadow Hariyama

Shadow Hariyama is primarily attractive to players looking for increased Fighting-type damage output.

Shadow Hariyama Weaknesses

Shadow Hariyama remains a pure Fighting-type Pokémon and therefore remains weak to Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks.

Should You Power Up Shadow Hariyama?

  • Consider it for damage-focused Fighting teams.
  • Check its IVs and available moves.
  • Remember Shadow power-ups require additional resources.
  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Compare it with your existing Fighting attackers.
Frustration Note

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration as a Charged Move. The ability to remove Frustration can depend on special Team GO Rocket events.

Purified Hariyama

Purified Hariyama is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Hariyama. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state into a Purified version.

✨

Purified Hariyama

A Purified Hariyama no longer has the Shadow battle bonus. The decision to purify should be based on your collection and battle goals rather than simply increasing IV values.

Lucky Hariyama

Lucky Hariyama is a Hariyama with Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon are especially valuable for players planning to power up their Pokémon because they provide reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Hariyama

A Lucky Hariyama can be an excellent long-term investment if you have a high-IV specimen that you want to power up.

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for high-level investments.
  • Can retain Shiny status if the Hariyama is Shiny.
  • Can be valuable for collectors.

100% Hariyama

A 100% Hariyama has perfect Individual Values of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

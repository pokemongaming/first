Ludicolo

Ludicolo is a Water and Grass-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily known for its unusual dual typing, evolution from Lombre and its collection value through Shiny, Lucky, IV and size variants.

Why Ludicolo's Typing Matters

Ludicolo's Water and Grass combination gives it a different defensive profile from most Water-type Pokémon. The dual typing also influences which attacks are super effective against it and which matchups it can handle comfortably.

Ludicolo FAQ

What type is Ludicolo?

Ludicolo is a Water and Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Ludicolo weak to?

Ludicolo is vulnerable to Bug, Flying and Poison-type attacks.

Is Ludicolo good for raids?

Ludicolo is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main appeal is its unusual typing, PvP potential and collection value.

Is Shiny Ludicolo available?

Shiny Ludicolo can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

What is 100% Ludicolo?

A 100% Ludicolo has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs: 15/15/15.

What is Lucky Ludicolo?

Lucky Ludicolo is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through eligible trades and receives the Lucky Stardust power-up benefit.

What is Shadow Ludicolo?

Shadow Ludicolo refers to a Shadow version of Ludicolo when that species is available through the game's Shadow Pokémon system.

What is Purified Ludicolo?

Purified Ludicolo is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Ludicolo.

What is XXL Ludicolo?

XXL Ludicolo is an extra-large size variant. Its size is separate from its IV rating.

What is XXS Ludicolo?

XXS Ludicolo is an extra-small size variant. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has low IVs.

Does Ludicolo evolve?

Yes. Ludicolo is the final evolution of the Lotad evolutionary family, following Lombre.

Is Ludicolo good in PvP?

Ludicolo can have situational PvP value, particularly because of its unusual Water / Grass typing. Its usefulness depends on the current moveset and metagame.

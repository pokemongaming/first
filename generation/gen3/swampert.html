Why Swampert's Typing Matters

Water and Ground is an important combination because it gives Swampert a broad offensive identity. It also creates a very specific defensive profile, including a major weakness to Grass-type attacks.

Swampert FAQ

What type is Swampert?

Swampert is a Water and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Swampert weak to?

Grass-type attacks are Swampert's major weakness.

Can Swampert Mega Evolve?

Yes. Swampert has Mega Swampert as its Mega Evolution form.

Is Shiny Swampert available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and the current game rotation.

Is Shadow Swampert different from regular Swampert?

Yes. Shadow Pokémon have different offensive and defensive battle characteristics and should be evaluated separately.

Should I purify Shadow Swampert?

Not automatically. Compare your Shadow and Purified options based on your intended battle role and investment goal.

What is a 100% Swampert?

A 100% Swampert has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Swampert?

XXL Swampert is an extra-large size variant with collection and potential Showcase relevance.

What is XXS Swampert?

XXS Swampert is an extra-small size variant primarily valued as a special collection form.

Is Lucky Swampert worth powering up?

Lucky Swampert can be attractive for long-term investment, particularly for players planning to spend significant Stardust.

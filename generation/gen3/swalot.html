Swalot Overview

Regular Swalot

Regular Swalot is the standard form obtained by evolving Gulpin. It is the base version used for most gameplay comparisons.

Regular Form

Standard Poison-type Swalot with no Shadow, Purified, Lucky or size-based modifier.

✨ Shiny Swalot

Shiny Swalot is the alternate-color version of Swalot. It has the same battle statistics as regular Swalot, with the difference being visual appearance and collector value.

✨

Shiny Swalot

Alternate coloration with the same base battle performance as regular Swalot.

🏆

Collector Value

Shiny Pokémon are primarily valuable for collection and appearance.

🌑 Shadow Swalot

Shadow Swalot is the Shadow variant of Swalot. Shadow Pokémon should be considered separately because the Shadow battle modifier changes their offensive and defensive profile.

🌑

Higher Damage

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure.

🛡️

Lower Durability

Shadow Pokémon also have reduced defensive durability.

Shadow Swalot Investment

  • Check its IVs before investing heavily.
  • Consider Shadow power-up costs.
  • Check the current move availability.
  • Do not purify automatically.

🤍 Purified Swalot

Purified Swalot is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Swalot. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered carefully.

🤍

Purified Form

Purified Swalot is no longer a Shadow Pokémon. Compare its final IVs, moveset and intended use before deciding whether purification is worthwhile.

Should You Purify Shadow Swalot?

The decision depends on whether you prefer the Shadow battle profile, improved IVs after purification or the collection value of a Purified Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Swalot

Lucky Swalot is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Trade system when eligible trades become Lucky.

🍀

Lucky Benefits

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making Lucky Swalot a potentially cheaper long-term investment compared with a standard Swalot.

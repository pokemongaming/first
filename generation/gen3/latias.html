Latias Overview

Latias is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is one of the Eon duo alongside Latios and combines Dragon and Psychic typing. Unlike ordinary evolutionary Pokémon, Latias does not evolve through a conventional evolution chain. Pokémon GO can instead feature Mega Latias as its special Mega Evolution.

Shadow Latias Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Latias' underlying Dragon/Psychic typing. Its primary type weaknesses therefore remain Dragon, Dark, Ghost, Ice, Bug and Fairy.

Shadow Investment Tip

Do not judge Shadow Latias only by its IV percentage. Compare its battle role, moveset, Stardust cost and your existing Legendary team before investing heavily.

Frustration note:

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace the move, so check current event rules before spending resources.

Should You Purify Latias?

Purification is a strategic choice rather than an automatic upgrade. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle profile, purification removes that advantage. If you prefer a purified collection entry or the benefits associated with purification, it may be worthwhile.

Always compare the Shadow and Purified versions before committing to purification, especially if the Pokémon has strong IVs or a valuable moveset.

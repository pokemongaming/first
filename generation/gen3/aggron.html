Aggron Overview

Aggron is the final evolution of the Aron family. It evolves from Lairon and combines Steel and Rock typing. In Pokémon GO, Aggron can be used as a bulky option in certain battles, while Mega Aggron provides a separate and much stronger special form.

XXL Aggron & XXS Aggron

XXL and XXS labels describe the Pokémon's size category. They are primarily collectible size variants and should not be confused with a separate battle form.

XXL Aggron

An unusually large Aggron. XXL specimens can be valuable for collectors and size-related Pokédex goals.

XXS Aggron

Size note:

Aggron FAQ

What type is Aggron?

Regular Aggron is a Steel and Rock-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Aggron different in battle?

Shiny Aggron is primarily a cosmetic variant and does not gain different base battle stats because of its Shiny status.

What is Shadow Aggron?

Shadow Aggron is the Shadow version of Aggron and has a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

What is Purified Aggron?

Purified Aggron is an eligible Aggron that has had its Shadow status removed through purification.

What is a 100% Aggron?

A 100% Aggron generally refers to a specimen with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Aggron?

Lucky Aggron is an Aggron with Lucky status, which can provide resource advantages when powering it up.

Does XXL Aggron have better stats?

XXL status is primarily a size category and should not be treated as a separate battle form.

Does XXS Aggron have different typing?

No. XXS Aggron retains the same typing as regular Aggron.

What type is Mega Aggron?

Mega Aggron is a pure Steel-type Pokémon.

Is Mega Aggron permanent?

Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation rather than a normal permanent evolution.

Can Shiny Aggron become Mega Aggron?

An eligible Shiny Aggron can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved according to the game's Mega Evolution mechanics.

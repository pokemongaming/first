16 • SIZE

XXL Poochyena

XXL Poochyena is a larger-than-normal size variant. Size is separate from IV quality, meaning an XXL Pokémon can have any combination of IVs.

📏 XXL Poochyena Extra-large size classification

XXL status is a size attribute and does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

XXL + 100% Possible special combination

A Poochyena can potentially be both XXL and 15/15/15, making the combination particularly interesting to collectors.

XXL Poochyena Collection Tips

  • Check the Pokémon's size classification separately from IVs.
  • Keep unusual XXL specimens if you collect size variants.
  • An XXL Pokémon does not automatically have high IVs.
17 • SIZE

XXS Poochyena

XXS Poochyena Collection Tips

25 • FAQ

Poochyena FAQ

What type is Poochyena?

Poochyena is a pure Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Poochyena weak to?

Poochyena is weak to Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

What is Poochyena's Pokédex number?

Poochyena is #261 in the National Pokédex.

What generation is Poochyena from?

Poochyena was introduced in Generation 3 and originates from the Hoenn region.

Does Poochyena evolve?

Yes. Poochyena evolves into Mightyena using Poochyena Candy.

Is Shiny Poochyena available?

Yes. Shiny Poochyena is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Poochyena be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Poochyena can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters.

Can Shadow Poochyena be purified?

An eligible Shadow Poochyena can be purified. Consider its Shadow value and your collection goals before doing so.

Can Poochyena be Lucky?

Yes. Poochyena can become Lucky through Pokémon trading.

What is a 100% Poochyena?

A 100% Poochyena has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Poochyena be XXL?

Yes. Poochyena can have an XXL size classification when encountered with the appropriate size attribute.

Can Poochyena be XXS?

Yes. Poochyena can also have an XXS size classification.

Is XXL Poochyena automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Poochyena can have many different IV combinations.

Is XXS Poochyena automatically low IV?

No. XXS describes size rather than IV quality.

Is Poochyena good for raids?

Poochyena is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker because of its relatively low stats.

Is Poochyena good for PvP?

Its usefulness is limited and depends heavily on the specific CP-limited format and current Pokémon GO metagame.

Should I evolve a 100% Poochyena?

If your goal is to complete a perfect-IV evolution family, evolving a 100% Poochyena into Mightyena is a natural choice.

Should I keep Shadow Poochyena?

If you collect Shadow Pokémon or may want to experiment with its battle profile, keeping a good Shadow specimen can be worthwhile.

Numel

Numel is a Fire- and Ground-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, Numel is best known as the first stage of the Camerupt evolutionary family and can also appear in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Why Numel's Typing Matters

Numel combines Fire and Ground typing, giving it useful resistances against several common attack types. However, the combination also creates a major vulnerability to Water-type attacks.

Numel Weaknesses & Resistances

Numel's Fire/Ground typing creates a particularly important matchup against Water-type attacks.

Weaknesses

Shiny Numel

Shiny Numel is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny Numel has an alternate coloration that makes it a popular collection target.

Shiny availability and encounter methods can change with events and Pokémon GO rotations.

Shadow Numel

Shadow Numel is the Shadow variant of Numel. It retains its Fire/Ground typing but uses the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

😈 Shadow Numel

Shadow Numel has the Shadow battle modifier and can have Frustration as its Charged Move when obtained.

✨ Purified Numel

Purifying Shadow Numel removes its Shadow status and changes its move and IV situation.

Purified Numel

Purified Numel is created by purifying a Shadow Numel. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the standard benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

✨ Purified Numel

Purified Numel is no longer Shadow and can be evaluated separately from both regular and Shadow Numel.

Should You Purify Numel?

  • Check its IVs before purifying.
  • Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow form.
  • Compare the purified result with your collection goals.
  • Do not purify automatically just because the IVs increase.

Lucky Numel

Lucky Numel is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Numel

A Lucky Numel can be an attractive collection target, particularly when you want to evolve it into Camerupt while reducing future Stardust investment.

Lucky Numel IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon received from trades have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary wild catches. The exact resulting IVs are determined when the trade is completed.

Lucky Numel Investment

  • Useful for reducing Stardust costs when powering up.
  • Check the IVs after the trade.
  • Consider keeping a Lucky Numel for your collection.
  • Compare the Lucky Numel with your existing Camerupt projects.

XXL Numel

XXL Numel is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

📏 XXL Numel

An XXL Numel is larger than the standard size range. XXL Pokémon can be valuable collection targets and may matter for size-related showcases.

🏆 Showcase Potential

Large Numel specimens can be worth keeping if you participate in Pokémon GO showcases where Numel is eligible.

XXL Collection Tip

If you enjoy Pokémon GO showcases, avoid transferring unusually large Numel until you have checked its size and current showcase usefulness.

XXS Numel

XXS Numel is an unusually small Numel identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

📐 XXS Numel

An XXS Numel is smaller than the normal size range and can be particularly desirable for collectors who keep unusual size variants.

🎯 Collector Value

Size variants are separate from IV quality. An XXS Numel can therefore be both small and a high-IV specimen independently.

Numel IV Guide

Numel's IVs range from 0 to 15 for each individual stat in ordinary wild encounters. Perfect Numel has 15/15/15 IVs.

Should You Keep a High-IV Numel?

High-IV Numel can be worth keeping for collection purposes, evolution into Camerupt, showcases or personal goals. Because Numel has relatively low maximum CP, IV quality alone does not make it a major raid investment.

Numel for Raids

Numel is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its relatively low Attack and low maximum CP make it more useful as an early-stage Pokémon or collection target.

🔥
Fire Role

Numel can use Fire-type moves, but its overall stats limit its raid performance.

🌎
Ground Role

Ground moves provide additional coverage, but Numel is not a leading Ground attacker.

⭐
Collection Role

High-IV, Shiny, Lucky, Shadow and size variants can be more interesting for collectors.

Numel PvP Guide

Numel's low maximum CP means it can fit within lower CP formats more naturally than many Legendary Pokémon, but its overall battle performance depends heavily on the current Pokémon GO move and PvP meta.

Great League

Numel can technically fit within the Great League CP limit when appropriately powered, but it should be evaluated against the current meta before being recommended as a serious competitive pick.

Ultra League

Numel's relatively low CP ceiling makes Ultra League investment less natural for the standard Pokémon.

Master League

Numel's maximum CP is far below the level normally required for Master League competition.

Numel FAQ

What type is Numel?

Numel is a Fire- and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Numel weak to?

Numel is weak to Water- and Ground-type attacks, with Water being its most significant weakness.

What is Numel's Max CP?

Numel reaches approximately 1,196 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Numel's best moves?

Numel can use Ember and Tackle as Fast Moves, with Bulldoze, Heat Wave and Stomp as Charged Moves. The best choice depends on the intended battle role and current game balance.

Does Numel evolve?

Yes. Numel evolves into Camerupt using 50 Numel Candy.

Is Shiny Numel available?

Yes. Shiny Numel is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Numel available?

Shadow Numel is available as a Shadow Pokémon. Its availability can depend on Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

What is a 100% Numel?

A 100% Numel has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Numel?

Lucky Numel is obtained through trading and has the Lucky Pokémon status, including reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Numel?

XXL Numel is an unusually large Numel identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Numel?

XXS Numel is an unusually small Numel identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

Should I evolve my 100% Numel?

If you want a perfect Camerupt for your collection, evolving a 100% Numel is a natural choice. Check current Camerupt performance and your own goals before spending resources.

Is Numel good for raids?

Numel is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack and CP ceiling. It is more commonly valuable as an evolution, collection or special-variant Pokémon.

Is Numel good for PvP?

Numel can fit within lower CP formats, but its current PvP usefulness should be evaluated against the live Pokémon GO metagame and available moves.

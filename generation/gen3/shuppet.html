Shiny Shuppet

Shiny Shuppet is the alternate-color version of the species. A Shiny Pokémon does not automatically receive stronger battle stats, but it can have significant collector value.

✨

Shiny Shuppet Collection Guide

Consider checking the IVs before evolving a Shiny Shuppet. Some players prefer to keep one Shiny Shuppet and evolve another into Shiny Banette.

  • Check IVs before evolving.
  • Consider keeping one unevolved Shiny Shuppet.
  • A Shiny Shuppet can evolve into Shiny Banette.
  • Shiny status is separate from IV quality.

Shadow Shuppet

Shadow Shuppet is a Shadow version of the species. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from regular Pokémon, making them separate investment decisions.

🌑

Shadow Shuppet Overview

Shadow Shuppet can be kept as a collector variant or evolved into Shadow Banette. Before evolving or purifying, consider the long-term purpose of the Pokémon.

Shadow Shuppet Investment

  • Check the IV combination before making a long-term decision.
  • Consider the value of Shadow Banette for your collection.
  • Do not purify automatically for IV increases.
  • Consider Stardust and Candy costs before powering up.
  • Check current Shadow move mechanics and availability.
Frustration note

Shadow Pokémon can be affected by Frustration mechanics. The ability to remove or replace certain Shadow moves may depend on Pokémon GO events and current game rules.

Purified Shuppet

Purified Shuppet is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Shuppet. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and should be considered carefully before committing.

Lucky Shuppet

Lucky Shuppet is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Its primary gameplay advantage is the reduced Stardust cost associated with powering up Lucky Pokémon.

🍀

Why Lucky Shuppet Can Be Valuable

A Lucky Shuppet can be a useful candidate if you intend to evolve and power up Banette while saving Stardust.

  • Check IVs after receiving the Lucky Pokémon.

  • Keep rare Lucky variants for collection purposes.

XXL Shuppet

📏 XXL Shuppet

XXL Pokémon are especially useful for players interested in collecting unusual sizes and participating in eligible Pokémon Showcase competitions.

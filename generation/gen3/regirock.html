Regirock Overview

Regirock is a pure Rock-type Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It does not evolve and is one of the three original Legendary Titans alongside Regice and Registeel.

✨ Purified Regirock

Purified Regirock

Purified Regirock is created when an eligible Shadow Regirock is purified. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon back to a non-Shadow battle profile.

Purification

Removes the Shadow status.

IV Adjustment

Purification can increase Individual Values according to Pokémon GO purification mechanics.

Investment

Should You Purify Regirock?

Purification should not automatically be treated as an upgrade. Players should compare IV improvements, resource costs and the loss of the Shadow battle modifier before making a decision.

Size classifications should not be confused with IVs or battle levels. XXL and XXS status primarily concerns the individual Pokémon's size rather than providing a standard combat-stat advantage.

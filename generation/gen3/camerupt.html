01 • OVERVIEW

Camerupt Pokémon GO Overview

Camerupt is a dual Fire/Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Numel and can Mega Evolve into Mega Camerupt.

Regular Camerupt has a relatively modest role in the current Pokémon GO battle meta, while its Mega form provides substantially stronger stats and additional raid utility.

02 • TYPE

Camerupt Type

Camerupt has a dual Fire/Ground typing. This gives it useful resistances but also creates a major double weakness to Water-type attacks.

🔥
Fire Primary Type
🌎
Ground Secondary Type

Why Camerupt's Typing Matters

The combination of Fire and Ground gives Camerupt resistance to several attack types, including Fire, Electric, Bug, Fairy, Poison and Steel.

However, Water attacks are particularly dangerous. Because both of Camerupt's types are vulnerable to Water, Water-type attacks deal greatly increased damage.

09 • SHINY

Shiny Camerupt

Shiny Camerupt is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny Pokémon retain their battle characteristics; the main difference is their alternate coloration.

✨
Shiny Camerupt

A Shiny Camerupt can also be used as the candidate for Mega Evolution when eligible.

Why Keep a Shiny Camerupt?

  • Useful for completing your Shiny Pokédex.
  • Can be kept as a future Mega Evolution candidate.
  • A Shiny hundo is an especially rare collector target.
  • Shiny status does not automatically make Camerupt stronger in battle.

Shadow Camerupt IV Guide

Do not automatically transfer a Shadow Camerupt simply because it does not have perfect IVs. Shadow Pokémon have their own offensive characteristics, so overall usefulness should be considered alongside IVs.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon may know Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace the move, so check the current game schedule before investing Charged TMs.

11 • PURIFIED

Purified Camerupt

Purified Camerupt is created by purifying a Shadow Camerupt. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and affects its IVs, level and resource requirements.

🌟
+2 IVs

Purification increases each IV by 2, up to the maximum of 15.

⚡
No Shadow Bonus

The Shadow battle modifier is removed.

💎
Purified Status

The Pokémon receives the Purified status and associated mechanics.

Should You Purify Camerupt?

Purification should be a deliberate decision rather than an automatic upgrade. If your goal is to retain the Shadow battle characteristics, keeping Camerupt Shadow may be preferable.

If you prefer the Purified status, improved IVs or other purification benefits, purification can make sense for a separate collection or investment.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Camerupt

Lucky Camerupt is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Camerupt

A Lucky Camerupt can be especially useful when you want to reduce the Stardust cost associated with powering up a Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Camerupt?

  • Reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Useful for building a Mega Evolution candidate.
  • Guaranteed Lucky IV floor makes poor-IV trades less risky than ordinary trades.
  • Lucky and Shiny status can make a Camerupt a strong collector piece.

20 • PVP

Camerupt PvP Guide

Camerupt has access to useful Fire- and Ground-type coverage, but its overall PvP performance depends heavily on the current format and available moves.

Great League

Camerupt can technically fit within Great League's CP limit, but it is not generally a first-choice Pokémon for most teams.

Ultra League

Camerupt can reach Ultra League-relevant CP levels, but its matchup profile and defensive limitations should be considered carefully.

Master League

Regular Camerupt's maximum CP is not competitive with the highest-stat Master League Pokémon. It is generally better considered a niche or collection option.

How to Use Camerupt

Before Investing

  • Check Camerupt's IVs.
  • Decide whether it is intended for Mega Evolution.
  • Check its current moveset.

Camerupt FAQ

What type is Camerupt?

Camerupt is a Fire/Ground-type Pokémon.

Camerupt is weak to Water- and Ground-type attacks, with Water being its particularly severe weakness.

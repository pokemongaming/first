Regice Forms & Special Variants

Regice can exist in several collectible and gameplay variants. These versions may differ in appearance, mechanics, IVs, resource costs or size classifications.

The standard Legendary version of Regice. This is the base form used for most normal Regice investments.

✨ Shiny Regice

🌑 Shadow Regice

Shadow Regice has the Shadow battle profile, providing increased offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

🤍 Purified Regice

Purified Regice is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Regice. Purification changes its Shadow status and affects its resource requirements.

🍀 Lucky Regice

Lucky Regice is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

💯 100% Regice

A 100% Regice has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. This is often called a "Hundo" by Pokémon GO players.

📏 XXL Regice

XXL Regice is a larger size variant. Size classifications are primarily important for collection and Showcase participation.

🔹 XXS Regice

XXS Regice is an unusually small size variant. It is mainly a collector-focused version of Regice.

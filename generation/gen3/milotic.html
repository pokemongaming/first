Milotic

Milotic is a Water-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region and the evolved form of Feebas. In Pokémon GO, Milotic has a defensive stat profile and can be used in Gym, PvP and collection-focused builds. Shiny, Shadow and Purified variants are also part of its available form information.

Why Milotic's Typing Matters

Milotic's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. It takes increased damage from Electric and Grass attacks while resisting Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

Milotic PvP Guide

Milotic's combination of high Defense, high HP and Water typing gives it relevance in certain GO Battle League formats.

Great League

Milotic's CP profile makes Great League use possible only when a suitable low-level specimen fits the league's CP limit. Check the exact IV and CP before building a team.

Ultra League

Milotic can be used in Ultra League because its maximum CP comfortably exceeds the 2,500 CP limit. Water-type pressure and its defensive profile can make it useful in specific teams.

Master League

Milotic can reach 3,398 CP at Level 50, so it is capable of entering Master League. However, its actual performance depends heavily on the current meta and moveset.

Shadow Milotic IVs

Shadow Milotic does not need to have perfect 15/15/15 IVs to be useful. Evaluate its IVs, moveset, role and the additional investment cost before deciding whether to power it up.

Shadow warning:

Shadow Pokémon normally know Frustration when caught. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace it with a different Charged Attack.

Purified Milotic

Purified Milotic is created by purifying a Shadow Milotic. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and removes Frustration.

Should You Purify Shadow Milotic?

Purification should be a deliberate decision rather than an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow version with the Purified version and consider your PvP, collection and resource goals before purifying.

Purification note:

Purification removes the Shadow status and gives the Pokémon the Purified characteristics. It also changes Frustration to Return.

Lucky Milotic

A Lucky Milotic is a Milotic obtained through trading that has become Lucky. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making them attractive long-term investment targets.

✨ Lucky Milotic

Lucky status is separate from IV perfection. A Lucky Milotic can have any IV combination within the applicable Lucky-trade IV floor.

Lucky Milotic Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Useful for long-term collection projects.
  • Can be traded into a Lucky status.
  • Can also be Shiny and Lucky simultaneously.
  • Can be evaluated for PvP or Gym investment.

Lucky + Shiny Milotic

A Milotic can have multiple independent characteristics. For example, a Pokémon can be both Shiny and Lucky if it was obtained through an appropriate trade.

Is 100% Milotic Rare?

A perfect-IV Pokémon is less common than most ordinary IV combinations. A 100% Milotic can therefore be a useful collector target, particularly when combined with Shiny, Lucky or other special attributes.

XXL Milotic

XXL is a size classification in Pokémon GO. It is separate from IVs, CP and appraisal, meaning an XXL Milotic does not automatically have better battle stats than a normal-sized Milotic.

📏 XXL Milotic

Extra Large

XXL status represents an unusually large size measurement. It is independent from the Pokémon's IV percentage.

XXL Milotic Collection Value

XXL Pokémon can be desirable for collectors and size-based collection goals. If you find an XXL Milotic with strong IVs, Shiny status or Lucky status, it can become an especially interesting collection piece.

XXS Milotic

XXS is the smallest size classification used for Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Like XXL, it is independent from IV quality and CP.

📐 XXS Milotic

Extra Small

An XXS Milotic has an unusually small size measurement compared with standard Milotic.

XXS Milotic Collection Value

XXS Milotic can be valuable to players who collect unusual Pokémon sizes. Its size does not automatically increase battle performance.

Lucky Plusle

Lucky Plusle

Lucky Plusle is a special status obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a useful Stardust-cost advantage when powering them up.

Why Collect Lucky Plusle?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky Plusle can be useful for completing special collections.
  • Trading provides another route to obtaining the Lucky status.
  • Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have at least a minimum IV floor determined by the game mechanics.

Is 100% Plusle Rare?

A perfect IV Plusle is a desirable collector specimen. Its value is particularly appealing to players who collect perfect Pokédex entries, trophies and high-IV Pokémon.

Important:

Perfect IV does not automatically mean that a Pokémon is one of the strongest attackers in Pokémon GO. Species, moves, level and battle role remain important.

XXL & XXS Plusle

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon into different size categories. XXL and XXS Plusle are particularly interesting for collectors.

XXL Plusle represents an exceptionally large size classification. It can be kept as a size-focused collector specimen.

Should You Keep XXL or XXS Plusle?

If you enjoy collecting unusual Pokémon, keeping at least one XXL and one XXS Plusle can be worthwhile. Size is separate from IV quality, so a size variant can have any combination of IVs.

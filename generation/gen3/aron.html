11 • SHINY

Shiny Aron

Shiny Aron is a collectible alternate coloration of Aron. Shiny status does not change Aron’s basic Pokémon typing.

✨

Shiny Aron

Shiny Aron has an alternate appearance compared with the standard Aron. Its Shiny status remains associated with the Pokémon when evolved.

Shiny Aron Evolution

If a Shiny Aron is evolved, its evolved forms remain Shiny. This makes a Shiny Aron a useful starting point for a Shiny Lairon and Shiny Aggron collection.

12 • SHADOW

Shadow Aron

Shadow Aron is the Shadow variant of Aron. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their standard counterparts and require additional consideration before investing resources.

🌑

Shadow Aron

Shadow Aron retains the Steel/Rock typing of regular Aron. Its Shadow status affects battle characteristics but does not turn it into a different species.

Should You Purify Shadow Aron?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Pokémon. Consider its IVs, evolution plans, collection goals, Stardust costs and the battle role you want it to have.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration depends on applicable Pokémon GO events and mechanics.

23 • FAQ

Aron Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Aron?

Aron is a Steel and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Aron weak to?

Aron is weak to Fighting, Ground and Fire-type attacks.

Does Aron evolve?

Yes. Aron evolves into Lairon and then Aggron.

Is Shiny Aron available?

Shiny Aron is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Aron available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current game schedule.

What is a 100% Aron?

A 100% Aron has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Aron?

XXL Aron is an extra-large size variant. Size is separate from IV percentage.

What is an XXS Aron?

XXS Aron is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL, XXS status does not automatically indicate perfect IVs.

What is Lucky Aron?

Lucky Aron is a special trade-result variant that receives the Lucky Pokémon benefits.

Should I evolve a 100% Aron?

If you want a perfect Aron evolution family, evolving a 100% Aron into Lairon and eventually Aggron can be a good long-term collection project.

Is Aron good for raids?

Aron is generally not a priority raid attacker. Its main value is its evolution family, collection potential and special variants.

Should I purify Shadow Aron?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, evolution plans, collection goals and the benefits of keeping a Shadow Pokémon before purifying.

Can a Shiny Aron be Lucky?

Pokémon can have multiple attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially combine special characteristics depending on how it was obtained and the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Can a Lucky Aron be XXL or XXS?

Size classification and Lucky status are separate attributes. A Pokémon can therefore have multiple collection characteristics.

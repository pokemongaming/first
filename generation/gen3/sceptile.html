Sceptile

Sceptile Overview

07 · SHINY

Shiny Sceptile

✨ Shiny Sceptile

Shiny Sceptile has alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Its Shiny status is cosmetic and does not automatically improve its battle stats.

Battle Stats

Identical species stats to regular Sceptile.

Moves

Uses the same eligible move pool.

Mega Evolution

A Shiny Sceptile can become Shiny Mega Sceptile.

Should You Power Up a Shiny Sceptile?

A Shiny Pokémon should be evaluated using the same practical criteria as a regular Pokémon. IVs, level, moveset and intended use are generally more important than rarity for battle performance.

11 · PERFECT IV

100% IV Sceptile

A perfect or "100%" Sceptile has the maximum possible IV combination of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

A 100% IV Sceptile is an excellent long-term investment candidate, although a slightly lower-IV Sceptile can still be extremely useful in raids.

12 · SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Sceptile

XXL and XXS describe special size variants. Size does not normally change the base battle stats used in standard Pokémon GO battles.

📏
XXL Sceptile

An unusually large Sceptile that may be valuable for collection and Showcase-related gameplay.

🔬
XXS Sceptile

An unusually small Sceptile with collector value and special size classification.

Does XXL or XXS Change Battle Strength?

Players should not assume that an XXL or XXS Sceptile has higher Attack, Defense or HP simply because of its size. Evaluate battle strength using IVs, level and moveset.

17 · FAQ

Sceptile FAQ

What type is Sceptile?

Regular Sceptile is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What type is Mega Sceptile?

Mega Sceptile is a Grass and Dragon-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Sceptile stronger?

Shiny status is primarily cosmetic. Battle strength depends on level, IVs and moveset.

Is Shadow Sceptile good?

Shadow Sceptile can be useful for players prioritizing damage output, although it has different resource requirements and reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Sceptile?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow form with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

What is a Lucky Sceptile?

A Lucky Sceptile is a Sceptile with Lucky status obtained through eligible trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Sceptile?

A 100% Sceptile has perfect 15/15/15 Individual Values.

Does XXL Sceptile have better stats?

XXL size does not automatically mean higher standard battle stats. IVs and level remain important.

Can Shiny Sceptile Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Sceptile can Mega Evolve into its Shiny Mega form when Mega Evolution requirements are met.

Is Mega Sceptile good for raids?

Mega Sceptile is a powerful option for raid teams where its Grass or Dragon damage is useful.

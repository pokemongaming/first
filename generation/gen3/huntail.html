Huntail Overview

Huntail is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is one of the two possible evolutions of Clamperl. Unlike a standard linear evolution family, players can obtain either Huntail or Gorebyss from Clamperl through Pokémon GO's evolution mechanics.

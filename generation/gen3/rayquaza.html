Rayquaza Overview

Rayquaza is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It belongs to the Dragon and Flying types and has no traditional evolution chain. Its most important special transformation in Pokémon GO is Mega Rayquaza, which requires the unique Dragon Ascent mechanic.

How to Mega Evolve Rayquaza

Mega Rayquaza uses a special Mega Evolution mechanic. Rayquaza must know the move Dragon Ascent, which is associated with a Meteorite, before it can Mega Evolve.

Dragon Ascent + Meteorite

Dragon Ascent is the key requirement associated with Mega Rayquaza. Players should check the current in-game requirements and event availability before planning a Mega Rayquaza project.

15 • FAQ

Rayquaza FAQ

Is Rayquaza a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Rayquaza is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3 and is associated with the Hoenn region.

What type is Rayquaza?

Rayquaza is a Dragon- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Rayquaza weak to?

Rayquaza is weak to Ice, Rock, Dragon and Fairy-type attacks. Ice is particularly important when planning a counter team.

Is Shiny Rayquaza available?

Yes. Shiny Rayquaza is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Does Rayquaza evolve?

Rayquaza does not have a traditional evolutionary chain. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Rayquaza through its special Mega Evolution mechanics.

Is Rayquaza good for raids?

Yes. Rayquaza's high Attack makes it an important Dragon-type raid attacker, while Mega Rayquaza provides even greater raid value.

What is Mega Rayquaza?

Mega Rayquaza is Rayquaza's Mega Evolution form. It remains Dragon/Flying type and has substantially increased battle power.

How do I Mega Evolve Rayquaza?

Rayquaza uses a special Mega Evolution mechanic involving Dragon Ascent and the associated Meteorite requirement. Current in-game requirements should be checked before investing resources.

Is 100% Rayquaza good?

A 100% Rayquaza has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It can be an excellent long-term investment, particularly when paired with a useful moveset.

What is Lucky Rayquaza?

Lucky Rayquaza is a Rayquaza that has obtained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is XXL Rayquaza?

XXL Rayquaza is an unusually large Rayquaza according to Pokémon GO's size system. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Rayquaza?

XXS Rayquaza is an unusually small Rayquaza according to Pokémon GO's size system. Its size does not indicate perfect IVs.

Should I purify Shadow Rayquaza?

Do not automatically purify a strong Shadow Rayquaza. Compare its Shadow performance, IVs, resource costs and your personal collection goals first.

Can Shiny Rayquaza become Mega Rayquaza?

Yes. A Shiny Rayquaza can Mega Evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.

Can a 100% Rayquaza become 100% Mega Rayquaza?

Yes. Mega Evolution does not create a new IV roll. A 15/15/15 Rayquaza remains a perfect-IV Pokémon when Mega Evolved.

Is Mega Rayquaza good for raids?

Yes. Mega Rayquaza is an extremely powerful raid option and can contribute significant Dragon or Flying-type damage depending on its moveset.

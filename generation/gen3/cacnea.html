13 — LUCKY

Lucky Cacnea

Lucky Cacnea is a special trade-related status. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background in the Pokémon GO storage interface and require fewer Stardust to power up.

A Lucky Cacnea can be an interesting collection piece, especially when combined with high IVs, Shiny status or an evolution project.

22 — FAQ

Cacnea FAQ

What type is Cacnea?

Cacnea is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Cacnea weak to?

Cacnea's Grass typing makes it vulnerable to Fire, Bug, Flying, Ice and Poison attacks.

Does Cacnea evolve?

Yes. Cacnea evolves into Cacturne.

Is Shiny Cacnea available?

Shiny Cacnea is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Cacnea available?

Shadow Cacnea can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations. Availability can change over time.

Can Shadow Cacnea be purified?

An eligible Shadow Cacnea can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and applies the standard purification mechanics.

What is a 100% Cacnea?

A 100% Cacnea has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Cacnea?

Lucky Cacnea is a Cacnea that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is XXL Cacnea?

XXL Cacnea is a Cacnea with an unusually large size classification in Pokémon GO.

What is XXS Cacnea?

XXS Cacnea is a Cacnea with an unusually small size classification.

Is Cacnea good for raids?

Cacnea is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolution, collection variants and Pokédex role.

Should I keep a 100% Cacnea?

Yes, especially if you enjoy perfect-IV collections or want a perfect Cacturne evolution.

Should I keep a Shiny Cacnea?

For collectors, keeping a Shiny Cacnea is generally worthwhile because Shiny Pokémon are desirable collection pieces.

Can Cacnea be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon statuses such as Shiny and Lucky can overlap, creating a particularly interesting collection specimen.

Can Cacnea be both XXL and 100% IV?

Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Cacnea can have an unusual size while also having excellent or perfect IVs.

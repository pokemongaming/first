Registeel Overview

Registeel is a Steel-type Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It is one of the three Hoenn Legendary titans alongside Regirock and Regice.

Unlike many Legendary Pokémon that are primarily valued for raid damage, Registeel is particularly valuable because of its defensive typing and charged-move pressure in Pokémon GO PvP.

Why Registeel's Typing Matters

Steel is one of the most defensively useful typings in Pokémon GO. Registeel's Steel typing gives it numerous resistances, helping explain why its low Attack stat does not prevent it from being a powerful PvP Pokémon.

Registeel PvP Guide

PvP is one of Registeel's most important roles in Pokémon GO. Its high Defense, useful Steel typing and Lock-On energy generation make it especially interesting in CP-limited formats.

Great League

Registeel can be highly effective in Great League when available within the league's CP restrictions. A carefully selected IV spread can improve its overall performance.

Ultra League

Registeel can also be used in Ultra League with appropriate level and IV investment. XL Candy can become important depending on the desired build.

Master League

Registeel's maximum CP is lower than many traditional Master League staples, making its lower-league roles generally more natural.

Registeel FAQ

What type is Registeel?

Registeel is a pure Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Registeel weak to?

Registeel is weak to Fire, Fighting and Ground attacks.

What is Registeel's max CP?

Registeel has a maximum CP of approximately 2,447 at level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Registeel good in Pokémon GO?

Registeel is particularly valuable in Pokémon GO PvP because of its high Defense, Steel typing and strong charged-move pressure.

Is Shiny Registeel available?

Yes. Shiny Registeel is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is 100% Registeel?

A 100% Registeel has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Registeel?

Shadow Registeel is Registeel with Shadow status. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from their regular forms.

What is Purified Registeel?

Purified Registeel is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Registeel.

Should I purify Shadow Registeel?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified Pokémon based on IVs, moves, PvP goals and collection priorities before purifying.

What is Lucky Registeel?

Lucky Registeel is a Registeel obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky trade mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Registeel?

XXL Registeel is a Registeel that receives the XXL size classification. Size is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Registeel?

XXS Registeel is a Registeel that receives the XXS size classification. It can still have excellent or perfect IVs.

Can Shiny Registeel also be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status describe different characteristics, so a Pokémon can have both statuses.

Can Registeel be 100% IV and Shiny?

Yes. Shiny status and IV quality are independent. A Shiny Registeel can have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Registeel be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size classification and IVs are independent characteristics.

Does Registeel evolve?

No. Registeel does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

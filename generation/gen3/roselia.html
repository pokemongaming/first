01 • OVERVIEW

Roselia Overview

Roselia is a dual Grass/Poison Pokémon from Generation 3. It occupies the middle stage of its family: Budew evolves into Roselia, while Roselia can evolve into Roserade using Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Why Roselia's Typing Matters

Roselia's dual typing gives it useful resistances to Water, Electric, Fighting, Fairy and Grass attacks. At the same time, it is vulnerable to Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic attacks.

10 • PVP

Roselia PvP Guide

Roselia can technically participate in Great League battles, but it is not a mainstream high-ranking Pokémon. Its relatively low bulk and CP ceiling limit its use compared with stronger alternatives.

Great League

Roselia has the most natural PvP opportunity in the Great League because the 1,500 CP limit is closer to its maximum potential. However, it remains a niche choice rather than a general meta staple.

Great League IV Consideration

A low-Attack, high-Defense/high-HP spread can be considered when building Roselia specifically for Great League.

Ultra League

Roselia's 2,114 Level 50 maximum CP leaves it well below the 2,500 CP Ultra League ceiling, making it a poor general choice for this format.

Master League

Roselia does not have the CP or bulk expected for meaningful Master League competition.

Roselia Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

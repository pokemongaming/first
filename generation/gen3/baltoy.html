Baltoy Overview

Baltoy is a dual Psychic/Ground-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. Unlike many Pokémon with a single-stage appearance, Baltoy has a normal evolution into Claydol.

In Pokémon GO, Baltoy is more interesting to collectors and Pokédex completionists than to high-end raid teams. Its different forms and size variants can nevertheless make it a useful addition to a complete Pokémon collection.

Lucky Baltoy

Lucky Baltoy is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon are particularly useful to collectors because they require fewer Stardust resources to power up.

🍀
Lucky Baltoy

A Lucky Baltoy can make an interesting collection entry, especially when combined with Shiny, high IVs or a special size.

Why Keep a Lucky Baltoy?

  • Lucky trades can produce useful IV combinations.

100% IV Baltoy — Hundo Baltoy

Yes, especially if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon. Even when a Pokémon is not a top raid attacker, a hundo can be valuable for your personal Pokédex, evolution collection or future game updates.

Hundo Collector Tip

What Is a Perfect Baltoy?

A perfect Baltoy has a 15/15/15 IV combination. Players commonly refer to this as a 100% or hundo Pokémon.

Should You Keep Low-IV Baltoy?

Yes. IVs are only one part of a Pokémon's value. A low-IV Baltoy may still be worth keeping if it is Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, XXS, XXL or otherwise unusual.

Baltoy Candy, Stardust & Evolution Resources

If your objective is to evolve Baltoy into Claydol, you should plan your Candy collection accordingly. Stardust is more relevant when powering up a specimen for battle.

  • Save enough Baltoy Candy for evolution.
  • Keep high-IV specimens before transferring duplicates.
  • Check for Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS status.
  • Consider using Pinap Berries when farming Candy.
  • Save a strong specimen for Claydol if you want a complete evolution family.

Baltoy FAQ

What type is Baltoy?

Baltoy is a Psychic/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Baltoy weak against?

Baltoy's Psychic/Ground typing makes it vulnerable to several attack types, including Water, Grass, Ice, Bug, Ghost and Dark.

Does Baltoy evolve?

Yes. Baltoy evolves into Claydol.

Is Shiny Baltoy available?

Shiny Baltoy is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Baltoy available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events, so current availability should be checked before hunting for one.

What is a 100% Baltoy?

A 100% Baltoy has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Baltoy?

Lucky Baltoy is a Baltoy obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics that has Lucky status and associated Stardust benefits.

What is XXL Baltoy?

XXL Baltoy is an unusually large size variant. Size and IVs are separate attributes.

What is XXS Baltoy?

XXS Baltoy is an unusually small size variant. It does not automatically have better or worse IVs.

Is Baltoy good for raids?

Baltoy is generally not considered a high-priority raid attacker because of its comparatively modest offensive stats. Its evolved form and stronger Psychic/Ground Pokémon are generally more relevant for PvE investment.

Should I evolve my 100% Baltoy?

If you want a perfect-IV Claydol, evolving a 100% Baltoy is a natural choice. However, collectors may prefer to keep one perfect Baltoy unevolved.

Can Baltoy be Shiny and Lucky?

Special attributes can overlap in Pokémon GO. A Pokémon obtained through trading can retain characteristics such as Shiny status.

Can Baltoy be both XXL and Shiny?

Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so special combinations can be particularly interesting to collectors when available.

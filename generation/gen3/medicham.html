Medicham

Medicham is a Fighting/Psychic-type Pokémon from Hoenn. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its PvP presence, especially its historical role in Great League formats. Medicham also has Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and Mega forms or variants to track separately in your database.

Medicham Overview

Medicham is a dual-type Fighting/Psychic Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Meditite and has no further regular evolution. Its Mega Evolution is represented separately as Mega Medicham.

Medicham has two types: Fighting and Psychic. This combination gives it a different matchup profile from pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

Why Medicham's Typing Matters

The Fighting typing gives Medicham offensive pressure against several common defensive types, while its Psychic typing changes its defensive matchup profile. Trainers should therefore consider the exact opponent and moveset instead of judging Medicham only by its CP.

Medicham PvP Guide

Medicham's low maximum CP and defensive stat profile have historically made it especially relevant to Great League-style PvP rather than high-CP leagues.

Great League

Medicham has historically been a notable Great League Pokémon because its stats fit the CP-limited format well. However, PvP rankings can change substantially after move updates, balance changes and the introduction of new Pokémon.

Ultra League

Medicham's maximum CP makes Ultra League a less natural home for the standard form. Always check the current league rankings and format rules before investing.

Master League

Standard Medicham's 1,618 Level 50 maximum CP means it does not naturally compete with the high-CP Pokémon normally found in Master League.

What Is a 100% Medicham?

A 100% Medicham has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a hundo. For PvP, however, a 100% Pokémon is not automatically the best possible IV spread. League-specific stat product and CP limits can make other IV combinations more desirable.

Shiny Medicham

Shiny Medicham is an alternate-color form that can be collected separately from standard Medicham.

✨

Shiny Medicham

Shiny Medicham retains the same species, typing and core battle mechanics as regular Medicham. The main distinction is its alternate coloration.

Shiny Medicham IVs

Shiny Pokémon can have any legal IV combination. A Shiny Medicham can therefore also be a 100% IV hundo, Lucky Pokémon, XXL or XXS depending on how it was obtained and its individual attributes.

Shadow Medicham and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Attack. Whether Frustration can be removed depends on the current Team GO Rocket event rules.

Do not purify automatically.

Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making a permanent decision. A Shadow Pokémon can have value beyond its IV percentage.

Purified Medicham

Purified Medicham is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Medicham. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and available move situation.

💎

Lucky Medicham

Lucky Medicham is a special trading result with a distinct visual background in the Pokémon storage screen and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Medicham

Lucky status does not create a new species or typing. It is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading.

Lucky Medicham Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Can still be Shiny if the traded Pokémon was Shiny.
  • Can have strong IVs after the trade.
  • Can be used as a long-term collection Pokémon.
  • Can also be Shadow-free because Shadow Pokémon cannot simply be traded while remaining Shadow.

Lucky Hundo Medicham

A Lucky Medicham can also become a 100% IV Pokémon after trading if the resulting IVs are 15/15/15. This combination is particularly attractive to collectors.

Does XXL Change Medicham's Battle Stats?

XXL classification is a size attribute and should not be confused with IVs. A larger Pokémon is not automatically stronger simply because it is XXL.

XXS Medicham

Medicham Candy & Stardust Investment

Building Medicham for PvP or collection goals can require a different investment strategy from building a conventional raid attacker.

  • Save strong PvP candidates before transferring them.
  • Check IVs before evolving Meditite.
  • Keep Shiny Meditite and Medicham separately if you are building a collection.
  • Consider Lucky Medicham when planning Stardust-heavy power-up projects.
  • Do not automatically transfer XXL or XXS specimens.
  • Keep interesting Shadow and Purified candidates before deciding which one to invest in.
  • Compare Mega Medicham with other Mega options before spending Mega Energy.

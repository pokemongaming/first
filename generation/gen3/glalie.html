Purified Glalie

Purified Glalie is created by purifying a Shadow Glalie. Purification changes its status and can provide the normal benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

Purified Glalie

Should You Purify Shadow Glalie?

  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions first.
  • Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow form.
  • Check the IV increase that purification would provide.
  • Consider the Stardust and Candy requirements.
  • Do not purify purely because the purified form has higher visible IVs.

Glalie FAQ

What type is Glalie?

Glalie is a pure Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Glalie weak to?

Glalie is weak to Fire, Fighting, Rock and Steel attacks.

Is Shiny Glalie available?

Yes. Shiny Glalie is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can be valuable to collectors.

Can Glalie Mega Evolve?

Yes. Glalie can Mega Evolve into Mega Glalie when the required Mega Evolution mechanics and resources are available.

Is Mega Glalie good for raids?

Mega Glalie can be a useful Ice-type Mega Evolution for raids where Ice damage is super effective.

Is Shadow Glalie available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current game events.

Should I purify Shadow Glalie?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions and decide based on your collection and battle goals.

What is a 100% Glalie?

A 100% Glalie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs, for a total perfect IV combination of 15/15/15.

What is Lucky Glalie?

Lucky Glalie is obtained through trading and has the special Lucky status, including a reduced Stardust power-up cost.

What is XXL Glalie?

XXL Glalie is an extra-large size variant. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Glalie?

XXS Glalie is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL, its size does not determine its IVs.

Is Glalie good for PvP?

Glalie has niche PvP applications depending on the league, moveset and current Pokémon GO metagame.

Is Glalie good for raids?

Regular Glalie is generally more niche as a raid attacker, while Mega Glalie has considerably more value for Ice-focused raid teams.

Does Glalie evolve?

Glalie evolves from Snorunt. Glalie itself does not normally evolve further, but it can Mega Evolve.

Can Shiny Glalie become Mega Glalie?

A Shiny Glalie can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolution is available for that Pokémon and form.

Spoink Overview

Spoink is a Psychic-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. It evolves into Grumpig after collecting the required Candy.

Spoink is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker, but it can be useful for Pokédex progress, evolution, Shiny hunting, Lucky trades and special size collections.

Spoink PvP Guide

Spoink is not normally a first-choice Pokémon for serious PvP teams. Its evolution, Grumpig, can be considered separately because evolution changes the Pokémon's stats and battle profile.

Great League

Spoink can technically participate when its CP fits the league requirements, but trainers should compare its performance with stronger Psychic and generalist options before spending significant resources.

Ultra League

Spoink generally has limited relevance in Ultra League. Its evolved form may be more interesting depending on the current Pokémon GO moves and metagame.

Master League

Spoink's comparatively low CP ceiling makes it a poor fit for Master League competition.

Spoink FAQ

What type is Spoink?

Spoink is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Spoink weak to?

Spoink is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Does Spoink evolve?

Yes. Spoink evolves into Grumpig.

Is Shiny Spoink available?

Yes. Shiny Spoink is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Spoink available?

Shadow availability can depend on Team GO Rocket rotations and current game events.

What is a 100% Spoink?

A 100% Spoink has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Spoink?

Lucky Spoink is a Spoink that has become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Is XXL Spoink rare?

XXL is a size classification and can be valuable to trainers who collect large Pokémon.

What is XXS Spoink?

XXS Spoink is a particularly small Spoink based on Pokémon GO's size classification system.

Should I evolve my Shiny Spoink?

Can a Spoink be Shiny and Lucky?

Can Spoink be both 100% IV and XXL?

Gardevoir Overview

Gardevoir is the final evolution of Ralts and evolves from Kirlia. It is a Psychic and Fairy-type Pokémon with a strong offensive profile, particularly when using Fairy-type attacks against Dragon, Fighting and Dark Pokémon.

The Psychic and Fairy combination gives Gardevoir an interesting offensive profile. Fairy-type attacks are especially valuable against Dragon-type raid bosses, while Psychic attacks can provide coverage in other matchups.

Gardevoir for Raids

Gardevoir can be a strong Fairy-type attacker, especially against raid bosses vulnerable to Fairy damage. Its Mega Evolution provides an additional high-level option.

  • Strong Fairy-type offensive potential.
  • Useful against Dragon-type raid bosses.
  • Mega Gardevoir provides significantly enhanced battle power.
  • Regular Gardevoir can still be useful without Mega Energy.

Shadow Gardevoir

Shadow Gardevoir Overview

Shadow Gardevoir has the same Psychic and Fairy typing as regular Gardevoir but benefits from the Shadow battle mechanic, increasing offensive pressure while reducing durability.

Shadow Gardevoir for Raids

Shadow Gardevoir can be especially attractive for players focused on high damage output. Its Shadow bonus changes its battle profile compared with regular Gardevoir.

Investment Advice

Do not judge Shadow Gardevoir only by whether it has 15/15/15 IVs. A strong Shadow specimen can still be worth using depending on its IVs, level, moveset and your existing Fairy-type team.

Purified Gardevoir

Purified Gardevoir is created when an eligible Shadow Gardevoir is purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource costs.

Purified vs Shadow Gardevoir

  • Purification removes the Shadow status.
  • Shadow offensive mechanics are no longer retained.
  • Purification can change the Pokémon's IVs.
  • Purified Pokémon may have different power-up economics.
  • Do not purify automatically without comparing your goals.

For raid-focused players, the decision should depend on whether you value Shadow damage performance or prefer the Purified version for collection, IV or resource reasons.

Lucky Gardevoir

Lucky Pokémon Benefits

Lucky Gardevoir is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. This can make a Lucky Gardevoir an attractive long-term investment.

  • Lower Stardust investment for powering up.
  • Useful for high-level raid projects.
  • Can be a good candidate for Mega Evolution.
  • Strong choice for collectors.

Gardevoir FAQ

What type is Gardevoir?

Gardevoir is a Psychic and Fairy-type Pokémon.

Is Gardevoir good in Pokémon GO?

Gardevoir can be a useful Fairy-type attacker and has additional value through Mega Gardevoir.

What is Gardevoir weak to?

Gardevoir is weak to Poison, Ghost and Steel-type attacks.

Can Gardevoir be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Gardevoir is available through eligible encounters and evolution paths.

Is Shadow Gardevoir good?

Shadow Gardevoir can be valuable for players prioritizing high offensive damage.

Should I purify Shadow Gardevoir?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow battle mechanics with the benefits of purification before deciding.

Is Lucky Gardevoir worth powering up?

Lucky Gardevoir can be a useful investment because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% IV Gardevoir?

A perfect Gardevoir has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Gardevoir Mega Evolve?

Yes. Gardevoir can temporarily Mega Evolve into Mega Gardevoir.

Does XXL Gardevoir have better stats?

XXL status is primarily related to size and collection mechanics rather than creating a separate battle species.

Does XXS Gardevoir have different typing?

No. XXS Gardevoir remains Psychic and Fairy type.

Metagross Overview

Why Metagross's Typing Matters

The Steel/Psychic combination gives Metagross a broad resistance profile. Its main weaknesses are Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground attacks.

Metagross PvP Guide

Metagross is particularly relevant in Master League because its high overall stats work naturally with the format's high CP ceiling.

Master League

Metagross can apply significant pressure with Meteor Mash while its Steel typing provides useful defensive matchups. Its effectiveness depends on shields, movesets, IVs and the current Master League metagame.

Great League & Ultra League

✨ Shiny Metagross

Shiny Metagross is available in Pokémon GO and has a distinctive alternate coloration compared with the standard form.

SHINY FORM

Shiny Metagross

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not create a separate species. A Shiny Metagross retains the same Steel/Psychic typing and core battle mechanics.

Is Shiny Metagross Good for Battle?

Yes. A Shiny Metagross has the same fundamental battle characteristics as a regular Metagross. The value of a Shiny specimen is therefore primarily cosmetic and collectible, while its IVs, level and moves determine battle performance.

😈 Shadow Metagross

Shadow Metagross is the Shadow version of Metagross. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately because their battle characteristics and resource requirements differ from regular Pokémon.

SHADOW FORM

Shadow Metagross

Shadow Metagross retains the Steel/Psychic typing but has the Shadow battle modifier.

🍀 Lucky Metagross

Lucky Metagross is a Metagross obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

Why Lucky Metagross Is Valuable

Metagross can require substantial resources when powered to high levels. A Lucky Metagross can therefore be attractive for players planning a long-term investment.

  • Check the IVs before transferring a Lucky Metagross.
  • Consider Lucky Metagross for long-term power-up projects.
  • Confirm the moveset before spending Elite resources.
  • Consider whether the Pokémon will eventually be used for Mega Evolution.

📏 XXL Metagross

XXL Metagross is an unusually large individual of the species. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Metagross can have any combination of IVs.

XXL Extra-large size category
IV Independent Size does not automatically mean 100% IVs.

Should You Keep XXL Metagross?

XXL Metagross can be worth keeping for collection, showcase and personal preference. If the specimen also has excellent IVs, it can become a particularly valuable collector's Pokémon.

Metagross Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Metagross requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Metagross can be useful in both raids and Master League, it can be a significant long-term investment.

  • Save Beldum Candy for important Metagross projects.
  • Collect Candy XL for high-level investment.
  • Confirm Meteor Mash availability before using Elite TMs.
  • Compare regular and Shadow Metagross before investing.
  • Consider a high-IV Metagross for Mega Evolution.
  • Keep interesting XXL and XXS specimens for collection.

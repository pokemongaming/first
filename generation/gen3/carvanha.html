Why Carvanha's Typing Matters

Carvanha's Water/Dark typing gives it a combination of offensive strengths and defensive weaknesses. The Water typing makes it useful against Fire, Ground and Rock targets, while Dark provides useful offensive coverage against Psychic and Ghost opponents.

XXS

XXS Carvanha

XXS Carvanha belongs to the extra-small size category. These Pokémon can be especially interesting to collectors who hunt for unusual size combinations.

  • Extra-small size category.
  • Size does not determine IV quality.
  • Can be combined with other eligible attributes.
Important:

XXS and XXL describe size categories rather than individual IV quality. A small Pokémon can still have perfect IVs, and a large Pokémon can have poor IVs.

21 • FAQ

Carvanha FAQ

What type is Carvanha?

Carvanha is a Water/Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Carvanha weak to?

Carvanha is vulnerable to Electric, Grass, Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Carvanha evolve?

Yes. Carvanha evolves into Sharpedo.

Is Shiny Carvanha available?

Yes. Shiny Carvanha is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Carvanha available?

Shadow Carvanha can be available through Pokémon GO Shadow and Team GO Rocket encounter rotations when it is included in the current game pool.

What is a 100% Carvanha?

A 100% Carvanha has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Carvanha?

Lucky Carvanha is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

What is XXL Carvanha?

XXL Carvanha belongs to the extra-large Pokémon size category.

What is XXS Carvanha?

XXS Carvanha belongs to the extra-small Pokémon size category.

Is Carvanha good for raids?

Carvanha is generally not a major raid investment priority because of its low defensive stats. Its evolution, Sharpedo, is usually more relevant when evaluating the family for battle purposes.

Should I evolve my 100% Carvanha?

If you value the perfect-IV evolution family, evolving a 100% Carvanha into a perfect Sharpedo can be a good collection project.

Should I purify Shadow Carvanha?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow status, IVs, collection goals and the Pokémon's intended future use before purifying it.

Can Shiny Carvanha evolve into Shiny Sharpedo?

Yes. Shiny Carvanha retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Can Lucky Carvanha evolve into Lucky Sharpedo?

Yes. Lucky status is retained when the Pokémon evolves.

Are XXL and XXS Carvanha better than normal Carvanha?

Not automatically. XXL and XXS describe size categories and should not be confused with perfect IVs or superior battle statistics.

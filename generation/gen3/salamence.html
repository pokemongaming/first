Salamence Overview

Salamence is the final evolution of Bagon and evolves through Shelgon. Its Dragon/Flying typing gives it a strong offensive identity, while its high Attack makes it especially valuable for Dragon-focused PvE teams.

11 · SHINY

Shiny Salamence

Shiny Salamence is available in Pokémon GO through eligible Shiny encounters and evolution from Shiny Bagon or Shiny Shelgon.

✨ Shiny Salamence

The Shiny form has an alternate coloration and can be used in the same battle roles as standard Salamence.

Shiny Evolution

A Shiny Bagon can evolve into Shiny Shelgon and then Shiny Salamence.

Is Shiny Salamence Stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not by itself increase Salamence's battle stats.

13 · PURIFIED

Purified Salamence

Purified Salamence is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Salamence. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and affects its resource and move characteristics.

Purified Status

The Pokémon is no longer Shadow after purification.

Purification Benefits

Purification can change appraisal and resource requirements and removes Shadow status.

Purify Carefully

If your goal is maximum Shadow raid performance, do not purify simply because the purified appraisal may improve. Compare the two versions first.

14 · LUCKY

Lucky Salamence

Lucky Salamence is a special trading status rather than a separate species or permanent form. A Salamence can become Lucky through a Lucky Trade.

✨ Lucky Salamence

15 · SIZE

XXL & XXS Salamence

XXL and XXS are size classifications rather than different species. A Salamence can receive a size classification that distinguishes it from standard-sized specimens.

📏 XXL Salamence

An XXL Salamence is an exceptionally large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system. It can be useful for collection and size-related achievements.

📐 XXS Salamence

An XXS Salamence is an exceptionally small specimen. Its size status is separate from IV quality and battle stats.

Do XXL or XXS Salamence Have Better IVs?

Not automatically. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL or XXS Salamence can have low, average or perfect IVs.

23 · FAQ

Salamence FAQ

What type is Salamence?

Salamence is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Salamence weak to?

Salamence is weak to Ice, Dragon, Fairy, Rock and Electric attacks. Ice is especially important because Salamence has a double weakness to Ice.

Is Salamence good for raids?

Yes. Salamence can be a strong Dragon-type raid attacker when equipped with an appropriate Dragon moveset.

Is Shiny Salamence available?

Yes. Shiny Salamence is available through eligible Shiny encounters and evolution.

What is Shadow Salamence?

Shadow Salamence is the Shadow variant of Salamence and has the altered offensive and defensive characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Salamence?

Not automatically. If raid damage is your priority, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before purifying.

What is a 100% Salamence?

A 100% Salamence has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Salamence?

Lucky Salamence is a Salamence that has received Lucky status through trading. Lucky status is not a separate species or evolution.

What is an XXL Salamence?

XXL Salamence is an exceptionally large Salamence according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

What is an XXS Salamence?

XXS Salamence is an exceptionally small Salamence according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

Is Mega Salamence good?

Mega Salamence is a powerful Mega Evolution and can be particularly useful for Dragon and Flying-focused raid teams.

Does Salamence evolve?

Yes. Salamence evolves from Shelgon, which evolves from Bagon.

Can a Shiny Salamence Mega Evolve?

A Shiny Salamence can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution, subject to the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Does XXL Salamence have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Salamence can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Salamence have better stats?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better IVs or battle performance.

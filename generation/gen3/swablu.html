Swablu Overview

Swablu is a small bird-like Pokémon covered in cloud-like feathers. In Pokémon GO, Swablu itself is not generally known for high raid damage, but it is extremely important because it evolves into Altaria.

Why Swablu's Typing Matters

Swablu's Normal and Flying typing creates a unique weakness and resistance profile. Its typing also changes completely after evolving into Altaria, making the evolution especially interesting for players building Pokémon GO PvP teams.

Swablu Forms & Special Variants

Swablu can exist in several collectible and mechanically different forms or variants in Pokémon GO.

STANDARD

Regular Swablu

The standard version of Swablu. It can be evolved into Altaria and may be used as the base for other available Pokémon GO mechanics.

✨ SHINY

Shiny Swablu

Shiny Swablu has alternate coloration while retaining the same underlying species and battle role as regular Swablu.

🌑 SHADOW

Shadow Swablu

Shadow Swablu has the Shadow battle profile, giving increased offensive pressure with reduced defensive durability.

✨ PURIFIED

Purified Swablu

Purified Swablu is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Swablu. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and investment characteristics.

🍀 LUCKY

Lucky Swablu

Lucky Swablu costs less Stardust to power up, making it an attractive long-term investment candidate for evolution into Altaria.

💯 100% IV

100% Swablu

A 100% Swablu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is a popular collector and evolution target.

XXL

XXL Swablu

XXL Swablu is a size variant that may have relevance for collection and PokéStop Showcase competitions.

XXS

XXS Swablu

XXS Swablu is a small size variant primarily valuable for collection purposes and size-based gameplay features.

Swablu & Altaria PvP Value

Swablu itself has limited battle relevance compared with its evolution. Most competitive players catch Swablu primarily to build Altaria for PvP.

PvP investment tip:

Do not automatically evolve the highest CP or highest IV Swablu. Check the IV ranking of the resulting Altaria for your target league first.

09 • PVP

Chimecho PvP Guide

Chimecho can technically participate in trainer battles, but it does not have the consistency or unique role of stronger Psychic-type PvP options.

Great League

Chimecho can fit within the Great League CP limit, but its performance is generally limited by the strength of the current meta and competition from other Psychic-type Pokémon.

Ultra League

Chimecho can be built for Ultra League, although it is generally a specialist or collection-oriented choice rather than a top competitive option.

Master League

Chimecho's 2,554 Level 50 maximum CP makes it poorly suited to the Master League's unrestricted CP environment.

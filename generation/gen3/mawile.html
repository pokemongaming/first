02 • TYPE

Mawile Type

Mawile has the unusual combination of Steel and Fairy typing. This gives it a broad resistance profile while leaving it vulnerable to Fire and Ground attacks.

⚙️
Steel / Fairy Dual Type
Steel Fairy

Why Mawile's Typing Matters

Steel/Fairy is one of Mawile's defining characteristics. The combination gives Mawile useful resistance against many common attacking types, although Fire and Ground attacks remain its major weaknesses.

08 • IV GUIDE

Mawile IV Guide

IVs add individual Attack, Defense and HP points to Mawile's base stats. A 15/15/15 Mawile is known as a 100% or Hundo Mawile.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is a 100% Mawile?

A 100% Mawile has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. This is commonly called a Hundo.

09 • PVP

Mawile PvP Guide

Mawile's relatively low Max CP makes it much more interesting for CP-limited formats than Master League.

Great League

Mawile can be considered for Great League teams, where its Steel/Fairy typing provides useful matchups against several common Pokémon. Team composition, shields and moveset can strongly influence its value.

Ultra League

Mawile's low maximum CP makes Ultra League a less natural environment for standard Mawile compared with Pokémon with higher overall stat products.

Master League

Standard Mawile's 1,848 Level-50 Max CP makes it unsuitable for serious Master League competition.

Purified Mawile

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Mawile

Lucky Mawile is a special trade-related variant. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and are guaranteed to have a higher IV floor than ordinary wild Pokémon.

Useful for collectors and players who want to reduce Stardust investment when powering up Mawile.

⭐ Lucky Hundo Goal

Lucky Mawile Collection Ideas

  • Consider a Lucky Mawile for Mega Evolution.
  • Check IVs before transferring older Lucky Pokémon.
  • Compare Stardust savings before powering up.

15 • XXL

XXL Mawile

XXL Mawile is a size variant that is larger than ordinary Mawile. Size categories are separate from IVs, so an XXL Pokémon is not automatically a high-IV Pokémon.

📏 XXL Mawile

A very large Mawile size variant.

🏆 XXL Collection

Useful for size-based collection goals, medals and special Pokédex records.

Important:

XXL refers to size rather than IV quality. You can have an XXL Mawile with poor IVs or an XXL Mawile with perfect IVs.

16 • XXS

XXS Mawile

XXS Mawile is the opposite size extreme. It represents an unusually small Mawile.

🔹 XXS Mawile

A very small Mawile size variant.

📖 Size Collection

XXS Mawile can be kept alongside regular, XL and XXL specimens for a complete size collection.

Size and IVs are independent. An XXS Mawile can still have excellent IVs, including 15/15/15.

24 • FAQ

Mawile FAQ

What type is Mawile?

Mawile is a Steel/Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Mawile weak to?

Mawile is weak to Fire and Ground attacks.

What is Mawile's Max CP?

Mawile's Level 50 Max CP is 1,848 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Mawile available?

Yes. Shiny Mawile is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Mawile available?

Shadow Mawile is a separate Shadow variant and its availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Shadow and Team GO Rocket rotations.

Should I purify Shadow Mawile?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result and consider your PvP, collection and Mega goals first.

What is a 100% Mawile?

A 100% Mawile has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Hundo Mawile?

Hundo is another name for a Pokémon with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is a Lucky Mawile?

Lucky Mawile is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Mawile?

XXL Mawile is an unusually large size variant. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

What is XXS Mawile?

XXS Mawile is an unusually small size variant. Like XXL status, XXS is independent of IVs.

Does Mawile evolve?

Mawile does not have a normal evolutionary chain. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Mawile.

What is Mega Mawile's Max CP?

Mega Mawile reaches 2,691 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Mega Mawile good?

Mega Mawile is substantially stronger than standard Mawile and can provide Mega bonuses while active. Its practical value depends on the current raid environment and available Fairy/Steel options.

Can Shiny Mawile become Shiny Mega Mawile?

Yes. A Shiny Mawile can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Is Mawile good in Great League?

Mawile can be useful in Great League because its lower CP ceiling allows it to fit naturally into CP-limited formats. Its effectiveness depends on moveset and the current PvP meta.

Is Mawile good in Master League?

Standard Mawile is generally not suited to Master League because its Level 50 Max CP is only 1,848.

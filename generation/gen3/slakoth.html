Why Slakoth's Typing Matters

Slakoth is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it only one direct weakness in Pokémon GO: Fighting-type attacks.

Normal typing also means Slakoth is resistant to Ghost-type attacks. However, its relatively modest battle stats mean that typing alone does not make Slakoth a major raid attacker.

Should You Keep Shadow Slakoth?

23 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Check a Slakoth Before Transferring

Before transferring a Slakoth, especially one with a special appearance or size, check several characteristics rather than judging it only by CP.

  • Check its appraisal and IV percentage.
  • Look for a 15/15/15 perfect IV specimen.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow or Purified.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Look for XXL or XXS size classification.
  • Decide whether you want to preserve the Pokémon before evolving it.
Collector tip:

CP alone does not tell you whether a Slakoth is valuable. Always check IVs, form/status, Shiny status, Lucky status and size before transferring rare specimens.

25 • FAQ

Slakoth FAQ

What type is Slakoth?

Slakoth is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Slakoth weak against?

Slakoth is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Slakoth resistant to?

Slakoth's Normal typing gives it resistance to Ghost-type attacks.

What is Slakoth's max CP?

Slakoth's maximum CP is approximately 1,187, depending on the Pokémon GO calculation and level/IV conditions being referenced.

What are Slakoth's base stats?

Slakoth has 92 Attack, 78 Defense and 120 HP in Pokémon GO's base-stat system.

Does Slakoth evolve?

Yes. Slakoth evolves into Vigoroth and then Slaking.

How much Candy does Slakoth need to evolve?

Slakoth evolves into Vigoroth using 25 Candy. Vigoroth then requires 100 Candy to evolve into Slaking.

Is Shiny Slakoth available?

Shiny Slakoth is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

What is a 100% Slakoth?

A 100% Slakoth has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Slakoth?

Shadow Slakoth is the Shadow variant of Slakoth. It retains the Normal typing while using the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanic.

Can Shadow Slakoth be purified?

An eligible Shadow Slakoth can be purified, subject to the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

What is Lucky Slakoth?

Lucky Slakoth is obtained through trading and has the special Lucky Pokémon characteristics, including a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is XXL Slakoth?

XXL Slakoth is an unusually large Slakoth with an XXL size classification.

What is XXS Slakoth?

XXS Slakoth is an unusually small Slakoth with an XXS size classification.

Does XXL mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Slakoth can have any IV combination.

Is Slakoth good for raids?

Slakoth is generally not considered a major raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolutionary family and collection potential.

Should I evolve a 100% Slakoth?

You can evolve it if you want a perfect-IV Vigoroth or Slaking. If you collect perfect first-stage Pokémon, you may prefer keeping the Slakoth unevolved.

Should I transfer a low-IV Shiny Slakoth?

Most collectors should consider keeping Shiny Pokémon regardless of IVs. Whether to transfer one depends on your storage space and collection goals.

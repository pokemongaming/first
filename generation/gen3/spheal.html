08 • SHINY

Shiny Spheal

Shiny Spheal is a special alternate-color variant. Its Shiny status is independent of IV quality, size and Lucky status.

✨

Shiny Spheal

A Shiny Spheal can be kept as a collector's Pokémon, evolved into Shiny Sealeo and eventually Shiny Walrein when evolution requirements are met.

Shiny Spheal Checklist

  • Keep Shiny Spheal if you are building a complete Shiny evolution family.
  • Check IVs before transferring duplicate Shinies.
  • Consider evolving one Shiny for the complete family.
  • A Shiny Pokémon does not automatically have better IVs.
09 • SHADOW

Shadow Spheal

Shadow Spheal is a Shadow variant that can be obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters when available. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts.

🌑

Shadow Spheal

Shadow status does not change Spheal's Water/Ice typing. The Shadow mechanic instead changes its battle performance and investment considerations.

Should You Keep Shadow Spheal?

  • Compare the Shadow specimen with your regular Spheal.

12 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Spheal

A 100% IV Spheal has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Players often call this a "hundo."

100% IV • HUNDO

Perfect Spheal

A perfect Spheal has maximum IVs across Attack, Defense and HP. Although Spheal itself has limited battle power, a hundo can be valuable for evolving, collecting and completing appraisal goals.

22 • FAQ

Spheal FAQ

What type is Spheal?

Spheal is a Water and Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Spheal weak to?

Spheal is vulnerable to Electric, Grass, Fighting and Rock-type attacks.

What is Spheal's Pokédex number?

Spheal is #363 in the National Pokédex.

What does Spheal evolve into?

Spheal evolves into Sealeo and then Walrein.

Is Shiny Spheal available?

Shiny Spheal is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when its Shiny form is active in the game.

Is Shadow Spheal available?

Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

What is a 100% Spheal?

A 100% Spheal has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Spheal?

A Lucky Spheal is obtained through trading and has special Lucky status, including a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is an XXL Spheal?

An XXL Spheal is an unusually large individual Pokémon. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is an XXS Spheal?

An XXS Spheal is an unusually small individual Pokémon. It does not automatically have high or low IVs.

Should I evolve my 100% Spheal?

If you value perfect-IV evolution families, evolving a 100% Spheal can be a good collection goal. Battle usefulness should be evaluated based on the final evolution and current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Spheal?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified Pokémon before making the decision, especially if you plan to evolve or use the Pokémon for battle.

Is Spheal good for raids?

Spheal itself has limited raid value compared with stronger Pokémon. Its main value is generally its evolution family, collection potential and special variants.

Can Spheal be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. A Shiny Pokémon can become Lucky through a Lucky trade, so combinations such as Shiny + Lucky are possible.

Can Spheal be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Spheal can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

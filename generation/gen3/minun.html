Why Minun's Typing Matters

Minun's pure Electric typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. Electric-type Pokémon are especially useful against Flying- and Water-type opponents, although Minun's relatively modest stats limit its role compared with stronger Electric attackers.

XXL Minun & XXS Minun

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon as different size categories. XXL and XXS Minun are therefore individual size variants rather than separate Pokémon species.

📏 XXL Minun

XXL Minun is a particularly large individual compared with the normal size range. It can be valuable to collectors interested in size records and special Pokémon classifications.

🔎 XXS Minun

XXS Minun is an especially small individual. Like XXL Minun, its appeal is mainly related to collection, size records and special individual characteristics.

Size vs IV:

A Pokémon's size classification and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Minun can have any IV combination.

Minun FAQ

What type is Minun?

Minun is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Minun weak to?

Minun is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Does Minun evolve?

No. Minun does not have an evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Minun available?

Shiny Minun is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Minun available?

Shadow Minun availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket and Pokémon GO encounter rotations.

What is 100% Minun?

A 100% Minun has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Lucky Minun?

Lucky Minun is obtained through trading and receives the special Lucky Pokémon benefits.

What is XXL Minun?

XXL Minun is an individual Minun that receives the XXL size classification.

What is XXS Minun?

XXS Minun is an individual Minun that receives the XXS size classification.

Is Minun good for raids?

Minun can use Electric-type attacks effectively, but its relatively modest stats make it less competitive than stronger Electric attackers.

Is Minun good in PvP?

Minun's PvP value depends on the league, moveset and current metagame. It is generally more suited to lower CP formats than Master League.

Should I keep a perfect Minun?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Minun is a perfect IV specimen and can be particularly valuable for collectors.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Minun?

If you collect size variants, both XXL and XXS Minun are worth considering as collection pieces.

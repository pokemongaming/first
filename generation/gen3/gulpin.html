Shadow Status

IV Improvement

Investment Cost

🍀 Lucky Gulpin

Lucky Gulpin is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players who want to reduce Stardust investment.

Lucky Pokémon Benefit

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon.

  • Useful for Stardust-efficient evolution projects.
  • Can be valuable for Lucky Pokédex collectors.
  • IVs are determined by the trade result.
  • A Lucky Gulpin can still be evolved into Swalot.

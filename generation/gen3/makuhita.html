Makuhita

Makuhita is a Fighting-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as an early-stage Fighting Pokémon that evolves into Hariyama. Makuhita can also appear in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms depending on the game's current features and availability.

14 • SIZE FORMS

XXL Makuhita & XXS Makuhita

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Makuhita are collection variants that can also matter for size-related medals and showcases.

📏 XXL Makuhita

An XXL Makuhita is unusually large for its species. Size is separate from IVs, so an XXL Pokémon is not automatically a high-IV Pokémon.

📐 XXS Makuhita

An XXS Makuhita is unusually small for its species. These specimens can be desirable for collectors and size-focused gameplay features.

Size vs IV:

XXL and XXS classifications do not mean 100% IV. Size and IV appraisal are separate characteristics.

15 • PVP

Makuhita PvP Guide

Makuhita is generally overshadowed by its evolution and other Fighting-type choices in competitive PvP, but it can still be relevant for special cups or restricted formats.

Great League

Makuhita's lower maximum CP allows it to reach Great League CP ranges, although its performance depends heavily on the current cup rules, moveset and matchup environment.

Ultra League

Makuhita is usually less attractive in Ultra League because its overall stats and maximum CP limit its effectiveness against stronger fully developed Pokémon.

Master League

Makuhita is not naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the format's upper level.

PvP IV Reminder

Do not automatically assume 15/15/15 is the best PvP IV spread. CP-limited leagues can favor different IV combinations depending on the Pokémon and league.

21 • FAQ

Makuhita FAQ

What type is Makuhita?

Makuhita is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Makuhita weak against?

Makuhita is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic attacks.

What does Makuhita evolve into?

Makuhita evolves into Hariyama.

How much Candy does Makuhita need to evolve?

Makuhita requires 50 Makuhita Candy to evolve into Hariyama.

Is Shiny Makuhita available?

Yes. Shiny Makuhita is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Makuhita available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current encounter rules.

What is 100% Makuhita?

A 100% Makuhita has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Makuhita?

Lucky Makuhita is a Makuhita obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have favorable power-up Stardust requirements.

What is XXL Makuhita?

XXL Makuhita is an unusually large Makuhita based on its in-game size classification.

What is XXS Makuhita?

XXS Makuhita is an unusually small Makuhita based on its in-game size classification.

Is Makuhita good for raids?

Makuhita itself is generally not a top raid attacker. Its greater practical value is often as a Pokémon that can be evolved into Hariyama.

Should I evolve a 100% Makuhita?

If your goal is to build a perfect Hariyama or keep a perfect evolutionary-line specimen, a 100% Makuhita is an excellent candidate for evolution.

Should I purify Shadow Makuhita?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions and decide based on your battle and collection goals.

Are XXL and XXS Makuhita the same as high-IV Makuhita?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Makuhita can have any IV combination.

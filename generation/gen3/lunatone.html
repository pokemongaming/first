Lucky Lunatone

Lucky Pokémon are special Pokémon obtained through trades that trigger the Lucky mechanic. Lucky Lunatone keeps its Rock/Psychic typing while gaining the benefits associated with its Lucky status.

✨ Lucky Lunatone

A Lucky Lunatone can be an attractive collector's Pokémon because Lucky Pokémon have special trade-related characteristics and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

Why Keep a Lucky Lunatone?

  • Lucky status is permanently retained.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a special visual background.
  • Lucky Pokémon can require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • A Lucky Lunatone can be a fun collection milestone.
  • High-IV Lucky Lunatone can be especially desirable.

XXL Lunatone

XXL Lunatone refers to an unusually large individual Lunatone. Size is a separate characteristic from IVs, CP and battle statistics.

LARGE SIZE XXL Lunatone

An XXL Lunatone is a size-focused collectible that can be useful for players completing different size categories.

IMPORTANT Size ≠ IV

XXL status does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

Is XXL Lunatone Rare?

XXL Pokémon can be desirable to collectors because size classifications are independent from traditional IV rankings. A large Lunatone can therefore be worth keeping even when its IVs are not perfect.

XXS Lunatone

XXS Lunatone refers to an unusually small individual Lunatone. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than an indication of IV quality.

SMALL SIZE XXS Lunatone

An XXS Lunatone can be useful for trainers who collect different size variants.

IMPORTANT Size ≠ CP

XXS status does not automatically indicate low IVs or weak battle performance.

Should You Keep an XXS Lunatone?

Yes, especially if you collect unusual size records. Size, IVs and CP represent different aspects of a Pokémon, so an XXS Lunatone can still be a valuable collection piece.

Lunatone FAQ

What type is Lunatone?

Lunatone is a Rock/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Lunatone weak against?

Lunatone's weaknesses include several categories such as Water, Grass, Bug, Ghost, Dark and Steel.

Does Lunatone evolve?

No. Lunatone does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Lunatone available?

Shiny Lunatone is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Lunatone good for raids?

Lunatone is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main appeal is its unique typing, collection value and availability.

What is a 100% Lunatone?

A 100% Lunatone has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Lunatone?

Lucky Lunatone is a Lunatone that has received Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is XXL Lunatone?

XXL Lunatone is an unusually large Lunatone according to its size classification.

What is XXS Lunatone?

XXS Lunatone is an unusually small Lunatone according to its size classification.

Is Shadow Lunatone available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotation and should be checked against current game data.

Should I purify Shadow Lunatone?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first and consider your collection and battle goals.

Is Shiny Lunatone stronger than regular Lunatone?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and collectible. It does not automatically make Lunatone stronger.

Are XXL and XXS Lunatone the same species?

Yes. XXL and XXS describe size characteristics of individual Lunatone rather than different Pokémon species.

Is a 100% IV Lunatone rare?

Perfect-IV Pokémon are generally much less common than ordinary IV combinations, making a 15/15/15 Lunatone a desirable collection piece.

Wailord

Wailord Overview

Why Wailord's Typing Matters

Wailord's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. Water attacks are useful against Fire-, Ground- and Rock-type Pokémon, while Wailord must be careful around Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

09 • PVP

Wailord PvP Guide

Wailord's unusually high HP can make it interesting to experiment with, but its low Defense and overall moveset limit its competitive relevance compared with stronger Water-type choices.

Great League

Wailord can fit within the Great League CP limit when appropriately powered, but it is not normally regarded as a premier competitive choice. Team composition and the current move meta matter significantly.

Ultra League

Wailord can also be used in Ultra League formats depending on its CP and build. However, players should compare it against other Water Pokémon before spending substantial Stardust or Candy XL.

Master League

Wailord is generally less suited to Master League because its maximum CP is substantially lower than the Pokémon that dominate the format.

24 • FAQ

Wailord FAQ

What type is Wailord?

Wailord is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wailord weak to?

Wailord is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

What is Wailord's Pokédex number?

Wailord is #321 in the National Pokédex.

What Pokémon evolves into Wailord?

Wailmer evolves into Wailord.

How much Candy does Wailmer need to evolve?

Wailmer requires 400 Wailmer Candy to evolve into Wailord.

Is Shiny Wailord available?

Yes. Shiny Wailord is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Wailord?

A 100% Wailord has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is an XXL Wailord?

An XXL Wailord is an individual classified in Pokémon GO as exceptionally large.

What is an XXS Wailord?

An XXS Wailord is an individual classified as exceptionally small within the Pokémon GO size system.

Is XXL Wailord stronger than normal Wailord?

No. Size classification should not be confused with IVs or combat strength.

Is XXS Wailord weaker than normal Wailord?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not automatically mean lower IVs or weaker battle performance.

What is Lucky Wailord?

Lucky Wailord is a Wailord that became Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is Shadow Wailord?

Shadow Wailord is the Shadow version of Wailord and uses the standard Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Wailord?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions and decide based on your battle and collection goals.

Is Wailord good for raids?

Wailord is generally not a priority Water-type raid attacker, although it can still be used by players who enjoy the species or need an additional Water Pokémon.

Is Wailord good in PvP?

Wailord can be used in PvP formats, but its overall competitive value depends on the current moveset and meta. It is not generally considered a premier Water-type option.

Can Wailord be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate attributes, so an individual Pokémon can potentially have both.

Can Wailord be 100% and XXL?

Yes. IV perfection and size classification are separate attributes, so an XXL Wailord can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Wailord be Shiny, Lucky, 100% and XXL?

These attributes are independent. Therefore, such a combination is possible in principle, although obtaining one individual with all of these desirable characteristics would be exceptionally difficult.

06 • EVOLUTION

Clamperl Evolution: Huntail or Gorebyss

Clamperl has a split evolution. Depending on the evolution result, Clamperl can become either Huntail or Gorebyss.

Clamperl #366 • Water

🐊
Huntail

One possible Clamperl evolution. Keep additional Clamperl if you want to complete both branches.

The second possible evolution. Collectors generally want both Huntail and Gorebyss.

Evolution Tip

If your goal is Pokédex completion, do not assume your first Clamperl evolution will give you both branches. Keep enough Candy and Clamperl encounters to complete the two-form family.

10 • SHINY

Shiny Clamperl

Shiny Clamperl is an alternate-color version of Clamperl. It is primarily a collector's variant and does not receive a separate battle-stat bonus simply for being Shiny.

✨
Shiny Clamperl

Shiny status changes the Pokémon's appearance, while its underlying species and typing remain Clamperl and Water.

Why Keep Shiny Clamperl?

  • Keep one for your Shiny Pokédex.
  • Consider evolving another Shiny into Huntail.
  • Keep another Shiny available for Gorebyss.
  • Strong IV Shiny Clamperl can be a valuable collector piece.
  • A Shiny can retain its identity after evolution.

20 • FAQ

Clamperl FAQ

What type is Clamperl?

Clamperl is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Clamperl weak to?

Clamperl is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

What does Clamperl evolve into?

Clamperl can evolve into either Huntail or Gorebyss. The evolution family therefore has two possible final forms.

Is Shiny Clamperl available?

Yes. Shiny Clamperl is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Clamperl available?

Shadow Clamperl can be available through Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket-related encounter rotations. Availability should be checked against the current game rotation.

Can Shadow Clamperl be purified?

Yes. A Shadow Clamperl can be purified when the applicable Pokémon GO mechanics allow purification.

What is a 100% Clamperl?

A 100% Clamperl has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Clamperl better than normal Clamperl?

XXL status refers to size rather than IV quality. An XXL Clamperl is not automatically stronger because it is larger.

Is XXS Clamperl rare?

XXS status represents a particularly small individual and can be desirable for collectors.

What is Lucky Clamperl?

Lucky Clamperl is a Clamperl that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

Is Lucky Clamperl cheaper to power up?

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups, making Lucky Clamperl attractive for players who want to reduce Stardust costs.

Is Clamperl good for raids?

Clamperl itself is generally not considered a top-tier Water raid attacker. Players usually have stronger Water-type choices for serious PvE investment.

Should I evolve my 100% Clamperl?

If your goal is to maximize the value of a perfect specimen, consider whether you want a perfect Huntail or Gorebyss before evolving.

Should I keep multiple Clamperl?

Yes. Because Clamperl has two evolution outcomes and several collectible variants, keeping multiple Clamperl can be useful for completing your collection.

Can Shiny Clamperl evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Clamperl can evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.

Can Lucky Clamperl evolve?

Yes. Lucky status remains associated with the Pokémon when it evolves.

Can XXL Clamperl evolve?

Yes. Size status can be associated with an individual Pokémon and its evolution should be considered separately from IV quality.

Can XXS Clamperl evolve?

Yes. An XXS Clamperl can be evolved like other eligible Clamperl.

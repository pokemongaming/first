What Does Normal Typing Mean?

Zangoose's pure Normal typing gives it a relatively simple defensive profile. It is vulnerable to Fighting-type attacks, while Ghost-type attacks do not damage it normally.

What Is a 100% Zangoose?

A 100% Zangoose has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In the Pokémon GO community, this is commonly called a perfect IV or hundo Zangoose.

100% Zangoose

15 / 15 / 15 — the maximum possible IV combination. A perfect specimen can be especially attractive to collectors even when the Pokémon is not a top raid attacker.

09 • PVP

Zangoose PvP Guide

Zangoose's relatively high Attack and low Defense create a glass-cannon style profile. Its PvP usefulness depends heavily on the current move pool, league format and metagame.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to investigate Zangoose because of the 1,500 CP limit. A Zangoose with the right moves can apply meaningful shield pressure, but its low durability makes matchup selection important.

Ultra League

Zangoose can be tested in Ultra League formats, although its stat profile means that it faces considerable competition from more durable Pokémon.

Master League

Zangoose is generally not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP and overall stat profile do not compete with the Legendary and high-stat Pokémon commonly used in that format.

Zangoose FAQ

What type is Zangoose?

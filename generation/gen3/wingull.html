01 • OVERVIEW

Wingull Pokémon GO Overview

Wingull is a Water/Flying Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves into Pelipper after spending 50 Wingull Candy. Unlike many Legendary or fully evolved Pokémon, Wingull's low CP ceiling makes it more interesting for limited formats than conventional high-level PvE teams.

02 • TYPE

Wingull Type

Water / Flying Dual Type Pokémon

Why Wingull's Typing Matters

Wingull's combination of Water and Flying typing gives it a useful set of resistances. It takes reduced damage from Ground, Fighting, Bug, Fire, Steel and Water attacks. However, Electric attacks are especially dangerous, while Rock attacks also deal super-effective damage.

08 • PVP

Wingull PvP Guide

Wingull's maximum CP makes it naturally suited to CP-restricted formats. Its Water/Flying typing, energy generation and access to Flying and Water attacks can make it useful in Little Cup-style environments.

Little Cup

Wingull can be a useful Little Cup option when it fits the active ruleset. Quick Attack provides strong energy generation, while Air Cutter and Water Pulse provide STAB charged-move pressure.

Suggested Little Cup Build

Quick Attack + Air Cutter + Water Pulse

Great League & Ultra League

Wingull's low CP ceiling makes it a poor fit for conventional Great League and Ultra League play. Its evolution, Pelipper, is much more relevant in broader PvP formats.

Master League

Standard Wingull is not a realistic Master League investment because of its low maximum CP.

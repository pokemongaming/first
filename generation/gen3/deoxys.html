Purified Deoxys

✨🕊️

Purified Form Status

A Purified Pokémon is normally the result of purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, Purified Deoxys availability depends on whether an official Shadow Deoxys mechanic exists.

Purified Mechanics

Purification should never be described as a separate standard Deoxys Forme. It is a special Pokémon status connected to the Shadow system.

XXL Deoxys

📏 XXL Deoxys

XXL Pokémon are size variants and do not gain additional combat stats simply because they are larger.

Why Keep XXL Deoxys?

  • Collector value.
  • Rare size record.
  • Potential showcase relevance.
  • Can also have strong IVs.

XXS Deoxys

📏 XXS Deoxys

XXS Deoxys represents an unusually small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is mainly related to size and collection value.

XXS Deoxys Collection Value

  • Rare small-size collection entry.
  • Does not automatically change IVs.
  • Does not automatically increase battle power.
  • Can be collected across available Deoxys Formes.

Deoxys PvP Guide

Defense Forme

Defense Forme has historically been the most recognizable Deoxys Forme for bulk-oriented PvP discussion because its stat distribution is very different from the other Formes.

Attack Forme

Attack Forme prioritizes offensive pressure but has significantly lower durability. It requires more careful matchup management.

Normal Forme

Normal Forme occupies a more balanced role and should be evaluated separately from the specialized Attack and Defense Formes.

Deoxys FAQ

How many forms does Deoxys have?

Deoxys has four major Formes: Normal, Attack, Defense and Speed Forme.

What type is Deoxys?

Deoxys is a Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Deoxys weak to?

Deoxys is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

Can Deoxys be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on the specific Forme and eligible Pokémon GO encounter history and current availability.

What is a 100% IV Deoxys?

A perfect Deoxys has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Deoxys have stronger stats?

XXL size does not automatically increase Deoxys's combat stats.

Does XXS Deoxys have weaker stats?

XXS size alone does not automatically reduce combat stats.

Which Deoxys Forme is best?

The answer depends on the intended role. Attack Forme focuses on offense, while Defense Forme is known for its exceptional bulk.

Can Deoxys evolve?

No. Deoxys does not evolve.

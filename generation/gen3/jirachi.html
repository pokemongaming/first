Jirachi PvP Guide

Great League & Ultra League

Jirachi FAQ

What type is Jirachi?

Jirachi is a Steel / Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon.

What is Jirachi weak to?

Jirachi is weak to Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Is Jirachi a Legendary Pokémon?

Jirachi is classified as a Mythical Pokémon rather than a Legendary Pokémon.

Does Jirachi evolve?

No. Jirachi does not have a normal evolution or evolution family in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Jirachi available?

Yes. Shiny Jirachi has been available through eligible special Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Can you get a 100% Jirachi?

Yes. A 100% Jirachi is a perfect 15/15/15 IV specimen.

Can Jirachi be Shadow?

Jirachi does not have a standard obtainable Shadow form. This should not be presented as a normal Team GO Rocket encounter.

Can Jirachi be purified?

There is no standard Purified Jirachi because there is no normal Shadow Jirachi encounter to purify.

Can Jirachi be Lucky?

Jirachi is not normally tradeable, so a conventional Lucky Jirachi cannot be obtained through normal trading.

Can Jirachi be XXL?

Do not assume that Jirachi can have an XXL size. Size classifications depend on Pokémon GO's eligibility rules.

Can Jirachi be XXS?

XXS Jirachi should not be listed as an obtainable variant unless Pokémon GO officially makes Jirachi eligible for the size system.

Is Jirachi good for raids?

Jirachi can have specialized battle uses, but it is generally more notable as a Mythical collection Pokémon than as a top-tier general raid attacker.

Is Jirachi good in PvP?

Jirachi can have niche PvP applications depending on its moveset and the current Pokémon GO metagame.

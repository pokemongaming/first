Why Keep Torchic?

A Torchic with good IVs can be worth keeping because it can eventually become Blaziken. A strong Torchic can therefore be the starting point for a much more valuable Fire- and Fighting-type evolution.

Torchic Evolution Family

Torchic evolves into Combusken and then Blaziken. Blaziken can also Mega Evolve when the appropriate Mega Evolution mechanics are available.

Recommended Shadow Moveset

Ember + Flame Charge is a useful listed Shadow combination, while Frustration can appear on newly obtained Shadow Pokémon until it is removed under the appropriate game mechanics.

Shadow Torchic IVs

Shadow Pokémon do not need perfect IVs to be useful. When evaluating Shadow Torchic, consider the IVs, move availability, evolution plans and Stardust cost together.

  • Check Attack IV before transferring.
  • Check whether Frustration is still present.
  • Consider evolving into Shadow Combusken or Shadow Blaziken.
  • Compare the Shadow investment with your regular Torchic evolution project.

Purified Torchic

A Shadow Torchic can be purified when eligible. Purification changes its status and replaces Frustration with Return.

😈
Shadow Torchic

Retains Shadow status and its Shadow battle characteristics.

✨
Purified Torchic

Shadow status is removed through purification.

Purification Considerations

  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions first.
  • Consider whether you want the Shadow battle bonus.
  • Check the Stardust and Candy cost.
  • Do not purify a useful Shadow Pokémon automatically.
Current Shadow and purification mechanics should be checked against the live Pokémon GO rules before publishing exact costs or event-specific advice.

Lucky Torchic

Lucky Torchic is a special traded version of Torchic. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups and are especially attractive for long-term investment projects.

★ Lucky Torchic

A Lucky Torchic can be an excellent candidate for evolution because reduced power-up costs can make the eventual Blaziken investment more economical.

Should You Keep a Lucky Torchic?

  • Keep a Lucky Torchic with strong IVs.
  • Consider evolving it into Blaziken.
  • Compare its IVs with your existing Torchic.
  • Lucky status is particularly valuable for expensive long-term power-up projects.

100% IV Torchic

A 100% IV Torchic has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
Perfect Torchic

A 15/15/15 Torchic is a perfect-IV specimen. Because Torchic evolves into Blaziken, a Hundo Torchic can be an especially satisfying long-term collection and evolution target.

100% Torchic at Level 50

A perfect Level 50 Torchic reaches a maximum CP of 1,236. CP alone should not be used to identify a Hundo because level and IV combinations both affect CP.

XXS Torchic

XXS Torchic is an extremely small size variant. Size does not change Torchic's base battle stats, but it can be valuable for collectors and Pokédex completion.

📏 XXS Torchic

Torchic's listed XXS size range begins around 0.2009 m and extends to approximately 0.205 m. Size categories are independent from IV quality.

XXL Torchic

XXL Torchic is the large size category for the species. Like XXS, XXL status is primarily a collection and size distinction rather than a direct combat-stat bonus.

📏 XXL Torchic

Torchic's listed XXL size range extends from approximately 0.615 m to 0.6355 m.

XXL Torchic Collector Checklist

  • Keep an XXL specimen if you collect size variants.

Does Torchic Have Incarnate or Therian Forms?

No. Torchic does not have Incarnate Forme or Therian Forme variants in Pokémon GO.

⚠️ Incarnate Torchic — Not a real form

Incarnate Forme is used by certain Legendary Pokémon such as Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus. Torchic has no Incarnate Forme.

⚠️ Therian Torchic — Not a real form

Therian Forme is also associated with Pokémon such as Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus. There is no official Therian Torchic.

Torchic PvP Guide

Torchic has limited competitive relevance compared with its evolved forms. Its lower maximum CP makes it more naturally suited to low-CP formats than Master League.

Little Cup

Torchic can be considered in Little Cup-style formats when the species is eligible under the specific cup rules. Always check the current rules because eligible Pokémon and move updates can change.

Great League & Ultra League

Torchic is generally overshadowed by stronger evolved Pokémon in standard Great and Ultra League competition. Combusken and Blaziken are more natural candidates for players looking for an evolved Fire-type battler.

Master League

Torchic's maximum CP is far too low for it to be a natural Master League investment.

Torchic Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Torchic Candy becomes particularly useful when evolving through Combusken into Blaziken.

  • 25 Candy is required to evolve Torchic into Combusken.
  • 100 Candy is required to evolve Combusken into Blaziken.
  • Save Candy if you plan to build multiple Blaziken.
  • Candy XL becomes relevant for high-level investments.
  • Compare IVs before spending Stardust and Candy XL.

How to Use Torchic

Before Evolving

  • Check the IVs.

For Blaziken Projects

Torchic FAQ

What type is Torchic?

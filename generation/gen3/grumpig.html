Grumpig Forms & Variants

Pokémon GO collectors can encounter Grumpig in several different forms and collectible categories.

Regular Grumpig

The standard version of Grumpig. This is the normal form used as the base for comparison with other variants.

✨ Shiny Grumpig

Shiny Grumpig has alternate coloration and is primarily a collector variant.

🌑 Shadow Grumpig

Shadow Grumpig has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability.

✨ Purified Grumpig

A Shadow Grumpig that has been purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and may increase its IV values.

🍀 Lucky Grumpig

Lucky Grumpig requires less Stardust to power up, making it an efficient long-term investment.

💯 100% Grumpig

A 100% IV Grumpig has the perfect 15/15/15 IV combination.

📏 XXL Grumpig

XXL Grumpig is a large-sized collectible variant useful for showcases and collections.

🔹 XXS Grumpig

XXS Grumpig is a very small-sized collectible variant.

Purified Grumpig

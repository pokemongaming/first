Metang

Metang is a Steel- and Psychic-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. It evolves from Beldum and can eventually evolve into Metagross. In Pokémon GO, Metang is primarily important as the middle stage of the Beldum evolution family and as a collectible in its regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified and special-attribute forms.

Metang Overview

Why Metang's Typing Matters

Metang's Steel/Psychic typing gives it a broad set of resistances. It can resist several common attack types, but it is particularly vulnerable to Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark attacks.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Metang

Purified Metang is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Metang. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its appraisal and resource requirements.

✨ Purified Metang

Purified Metang no longer has Shadow status. Purification also provides the standard purification benefits associated with eligible Pokémon.

Should You Purify Metang?

Purification is a strategic choice rather than an automatic upgrade. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle profile or eventually evolve the Shadow Pokémon, compare both options before purifying.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Metang

Lucky Metang is a special-status Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive appearance in storage and benefit from reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Metang

Lucky status is particularly useful when you plan to invest Stardust into a Pokémon. Metang can also be evolved while retaining its Lucky status.

Why Keep a Lucky Metang?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky status remains after evolution.
  • Lucky Metang can become Lucky Metagross.
  • Trading can provide another way to obtain desirable Metang specimens.
  • Lucky Pokémon are popular collection targets.
12 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Metang

A 100% Metang has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV
💯 Perfect Metang

A 15/15/15 Metang is a perfect-IV specimen. Because Metang evolves into Metagross, a perfect Metang can be an appealing long-term evolution candidate.

Should You Evolve a 100% Metang?

If your goal is to build a perfect Metagross, a 100% Metang can be an excellent candidate. Before evolving, check the current evolution requirements and any event-specific move opportunities.

13 • SIZE

XXL Metang

XXL is a size attribute rather than a separate Pokémon species or evolution. An XXL Metang can be identified through its size information in Pokémon GO.

📏 XXL Metang

XXL Metang is larger than the standard size range. XXL Pokémon can be valuable to collectors and may be relevant to size-based collection goals.

XXL Metang Collection Value

  • Keep an XXL Metang if you collect size records.
  • XXL status is an individual Pokémon attribute.
  • Check the in-game size information rather than judging size only from the sprite.
  • An XXL Metang can still be evolved into Metagross.
14 • SIZE

XXS Metang

XXS is another individual size attribute. An XXS Metang is smaller than the standard size range and can be particularly interesting for collectors.

🔹 XXS Metang

XXS Metang represents a very small individual Pokémon. Its XXS status does not create a separate species or change Metang's basic typing.

XXS Metang Collection Value

  • Keep an XXS Metang if you collect size variants.
  • XXS is an individual Pokémon attribute.
  • XXS Metang can be evolved.
  • Size should be confirmed using the in-game Pokémon information.

17 • TRAINER GUIDE

How to Use Metang

Before Powering Up

  • Check Metang's IVs.
  • Decide whether you want to evolve it.
  • Check whether it is Shiny, Shadow or Lucky.
  • Check its size if you collect XXL or XXS Pokémon.
  • Compare the individual with your existing Beldum and Metagross collection.

Before Evolving

If Metang has strong IVs or a special attribute, think carefully before transferring it. Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL and XXS specimens can all have collection value.

Long-Term Investment

Metang itself is usually not the main investment target. Its greatest long-term value is often as the bridge between Beldum and Metagross.

20 • FAQ

Metang FAQ

What type is Metang?

Metang is a dual Steel- and Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Metang weak to?

Metang is vulnerable to Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark attacks.

What is Metang's Pokédex number?

Metang is #375 in the National Pokédex.

What does Metang evolve into?

Metang evolves into Metagross.

Can Beldum evolve into Metang?

Yes. Beldum evolves into Metang using Beldum Candy.

Is Shiny Metang available?

Shiny Metang is available in Pokémon GO through eligible Shiny Beldum or Metang encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Metang available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Can Shadow Metang be purified?

An eligible Shadow Metang can be purified, subject to the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

What is a 100% Metang?

A 100% Metang has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Metang?

Lucky Metang is a Metang with Lucky status, normally obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Metang?

XXL Metang is an individual Metang with an exceptionally large size classification.

What is XXS Metang?

XXS Metang is an individual Metang with an exceptionally small size classification.

Is Metang good for raids?

Metang is generally not a major high-level raid investment. Its greatest value is usually as an evolutionary step toward Metagross.

Should I evolve a 100% Metang?

If your goal is a perfect Metagross, a 15/15/15 Metang is an excellent evolution candidate.

Can a Lucky Metang evolve?

Yes. Lucky status remains when Metang evolves into Metagross.

Can Shiny Metang evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Metang remains Shiny after evolving.

Can XXL Metang evolve?

Yes. XXL is an individual size attribute and does not prevent Metang from evolving.

Can XXS Metang evolve?

Yes. XXS Metang can evolve normally.

Whiscash Overview

Whiscash is the evolved form of Barboach and was introduced in Generation 3. Its Water/Ground typing gives it a distinctive battle profile, especially because Ground typing removes the normal Water-type weakness to Electric attacks.

Whiscash Type

Why Whiscash's Typing Matters

Whiscash combines Water and Ground typing. One of the most important benefits is its immunity-like protection from Electric-type attacks in the Pokémon type chart, while Grass remains a major threat because it is super effective against both of Whiscash's types.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Whiscash

Purified Whiscash is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Whiscash. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its appraisal and resource requirements.

✨
Purified Status

Purified Whiscash no longer has the Shadow status.

💎
Purified Appraisal

Purification increases each IV by a fixed amount, subject to the game's IV cap.

💰
Resource Consideration

Purification can reduce some future power-up and evolution resource requirements compared with Shadow Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Whiscash?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions for your intended PvP, collection and investment goals.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Whiscash

Lucky Whiscash is obtained through a successful Pokémon trade that results in the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups, making them attractive for players who want to build them at higher levels.

✨ Lucky Whiscash

Lucky status is primarily valuable because of the reduced Stardust cost for powering up the Pokémon. It does not give Whiscash a different typing.

Lucky Whiscash Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term investment projects.
  • Excellent collector variant.
  • Can still have a wide range of IV combinations depending on the trade.
  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny and Shadow status.
14 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Whiscash

A 100% IV Whiscash has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs: 15/15/15. In the Pokémon GO community, this is commonly described as a hundo.

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP

A perfect Whiscash is particularly desirable for collection purposes and can be useful for formats where its IV distribution is appropriate. For capped PvP leagues, do not assume that 15/15/15 is automatically the best possible spread.

15 • SIZE

XXL Whiscash

XXL Whiscash is a size variant representing an exceptionally large individual. Size classification is separate from IVs, Shiny status and Lucky status.

XXL Extra-large size classification

A large Whiscash can receive the XXL size designation based on its recorded size measurements.

Collection Value Size-focused collecting

XXL Pokémon can be valuable to collectors and can be useful for size-related Pokédex goals.

Important:

XXL is a size classification. It does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect IVs or higher battle stats simply because it is large.

16 • SIZE

XXS Whiscash

XXS Whiscash is an exceptionally small Whiscash based on its recorded size. Like XXL, XXS status is separate from IV quality and battle stats.

XXS Extra-small size classification

XXS Whiscash is a useful target for collectors completing size-related Pokémon GO records.

Collector Value Rare size variation

An XXS specimen can be worth keeping even when its IVs are not perfect, particularly for collectors.

18 • PVP

Whiscash PvP Guide

Whiscash's Water/Ground typing and access to low-cost charged moves can make it a format-dependent PvP option.

Great League

Whiscash can be considered for Great League teams when its moveset and current meta matchups are favorable. Its Ground typing gives it useful protection against Electric Pokémon, while Grass opponents can be particularly dangerous.

Ultra League

Whiscash can also be evaluated in Ultra League, although its maximum CP and stat distribution mean that trainers should compare it against other Water/Ground options before investing heavily.

Master League

Whiscash generally has less natural relevance in Master League than Pokémon built around substantially higher maximum stats. Its value is much more format-dependent here.

PvP IV Reminder

For CP-limited leagues, use a PvP IV ranking tool rather than automatically selecting a 15/15/15 Whiscash.

21 • TRAINER GUIDE

How to Use Whiscash

Before PvP

  • Confirm the league you want to enter.
  • Check the Whiscash IV spread.
  • Review the current moveset.
  • Check the current PvP meta.
  • Build around Whiscash's Grass weakness.

During Team Building

Whiscash works best when its weaknesses are covered by teammates. Grass-resistant partners and Pokémon capable of handling common Grass attackers can make the overall team more reliable.

Long-Term Investment

Do not power up every Whiscash you catch. Keep candidates that have a clear purpose, particularly strong PvP IV spreads, perfect IVs, useful Shadow variants or valuable collector traits.

23 • FAQ

Whiscash FAQ

What type is Whiscash?

Whiscash is a Water/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Whiscash weak to?

Whiscash has a major weakness to Grass-type attacks because both Water and Ground typing are vulnerable to Grass.

Is Whiscash good for PvP?

Whiscash can be useful in certain PvP formats because of its typing, moves and relatively bulky HP stat. Its exact value depends on the current meta.

Is Whiscash good for raids?

Whiscash is generally more interesting for PvP and collection purposes than as a premier raid attacker.

Does Whiscash evolve?

Whiscash evolves from Barboach and does not have a further evolution.

Is Shiny Whiscash available?

Yes. Shiny Whiscash is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Whiscash?

Shadow Whiscash is a Shadow variant of Whiscash that retains its Water/Ground typing while using the Shadow battle mechanic.

Should I purify Shadow Whiscash?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your PvP, collection and investment goals.

What is Lucky Whiscash?

Lucky Whiscash is obtained through trading and has a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is a 100% Whiscash?

A 100% Whiscash has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Whiscash?

XXL Whiscash is an exceptionally large individual according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

What is XXS Whiscash?

XXS Whiscash is an exceptionally small individual according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

Does XXL Whiscash have higher IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Whiscash can have any IV combination.

Does Shiny Whiscash have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual and collection variant and does not provide a direct stat advantage.

Is 15/15/15 always the best Whiscash for PvP?

No. In CP-limited leagues, a different IV distribution can sometimes provide a better stat product or matchup profile.

07 • PVP

Wynaut PvP Guide

Wynaut's low maximum CP prevents it from being a normal Great League or Ultra League Pokémon, but its high bulk can make it interesting in special low-CP formats.

Little Cup

Wynaut's low Attack and relatively high Defense and HP are particularly relevant when the CP limit is very low. In Little Cup-style formats, its bulk can allow it to stay in battle longer than many Pokémon.

Great League

Wynaut cannot approach the 1,500 CP limit because its Level 50 maximum is only 603 CP. It therefore does not function as a conventional Great League investment.

Ultra League & Master League

Wynaut's maximum CP is far below the CP range normally required for these leagues, making it unsuitable for standard Ultra League and Master League play.

PvP IV Tip

For CP-limited formats, low Attack with higher Defense and HP can often provide more total stats under the CP cap. However, the optimal IV spread depends on the specific format and current meta.

    19 • BATTLE GUIDE

    How to Use Wynaut

    For PvP

    Wynaut is much more interesting in low-CP formats than in raids. Its high HP and Defense relative to its Attack make it suitable for consideration in Little Cup-style formats.

    For Raids

    Wynaut is not a practical raid attacker. Its low Attack and extremely low maximum CP prevent it from competing with dedicated raid Pokémon.

    For Collection

    This is where Wynaut becomes much more interesting. Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, XXL and XXS combinations can create valuable long-term collection targets.

    Wynaut Pokémon GO FAQ

    What type is Wynaut?

    Wynaut is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

    Wynaut is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

    What is Wynaut's maximum CP?

    Wynaut's Level 50 maximum CP is 603.

    Is Shiny Wynaut available?

    Yes. Shiny Wynaut is available in Pokémon GO.

    Yes. XXS is another size attribute that can occur on an individual Wynaut.

    No. Wynaut's low Attack and low maximum CP make it unsuitable as a serious raid attacker.

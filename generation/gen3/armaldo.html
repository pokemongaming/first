XXL & XXS Armaldo

Pokémon GO can generate size variants including XXL and XXS Pokémon. These size classifications are separate from IV quality and do not automatically mean the Pokémon has better battle statistics.

Armaldo FAQ

What type is Armaldo?

Armaldo is a Bug and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Armaldo weak against?

Armaldo has several weaknesses because of its Bug/Rock typing. Water, Rock, Steel, Electric and Ice attacks are useful counter categories.

Does Armaldo evolve?

Armaldo is the final evolution of Anorith and does not have another standard evolution.

Is Shiny Armaldo available?

Yes. Shiny Armaldo can be obtained by evolving a Shiny Anorith when evolution is available.

What is a 100% Armaldo?

A 100% Armaldo has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Is Lucky Armaldo useful?

Lucky Armaldo can be attractive because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Armaldo?

XXL Armaldo is a size variant classified as exceptionally large. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Armaldo?

XXS Armaldo is an exceptionally small size variant. It can be valuable to collectors and for size-related achievements.

Is Shadow Armaldo available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotation and should be checked against current game data.

Should I purify Shadow Armaldo?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your collection and battle goals before making the decision.

Is Armaldo good for raids?

Armaldo can be used in PvE, but players seeking maximum raid efficiency should compare its current performance with stronger Bug and Rock attackers.

Is Armaldo good in PvP?

Armaldo can be evaluated in PvP, but its usefulness depends on its current moveset, league and the evolving Pokémon GO metagame.

Is Shiny Armaldo stronger than normal Armaldo?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not inherently increase Armaldo's battle stats.

Can an XXL Armaldo also be 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Armaldo can theoretically have any IV combination, including 15/15/15.

Can a Lucky Armaldo also be Shiny?

Yes. Pokémon can have multiple applicable collection characteristics, so a traded Shiny Armaldo can become Lucky.

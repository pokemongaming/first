Breloom

Breloom is a dual Grass and Fighting-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its strong Attack stat and its ability to function as an offensive specialist in both Grass and Fighting-type roles.

Breloom Overview

Breloom is the evolved form of Shroomish and is a dual Grass/Fighting-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. Its high Attack stat makes it naturally suited to offensive roles, although its defensive stats and overall durability are more limited than many bulkier Pokémon.

Regular Breloom

Standard Breloom

Regular Breloom is the standard version obtained by evolving Shroomish. It is the base form used for most PvE and PvP builds.

  • Standard Grass/Fighting typing.
  • High Attack-focused stat profile.
  • Can be used for Grass or Fighting coverage.
  • Can evolve from Shroomish.
  • Available in regular and special variants.

Shiny Breloom

✨ Shiny Breloom

Shiny Breloom has alternate coloration while retaining the same species, typing and underlying battle mechanics as regular Breloom.

A Shiny Breloom does not automatically have stronger battle performance. Its value is primarily collectible unless it also has useful IVs, level and moves.

  • Alternate Shiny appearance.
  • Same Grass/Fighting typing.
  • Same species statistics.
  • Can still be powered up for battle use.
  • Useful for collectors and form collections.

Shadow Breloom

🌑 Shadow Breloom

Shadow Breloom has a different battle profile from standard Breloom. Shadow Pokémon are generally evaluated separately because their offensive and defensive mechanics and resource requirements differ from normal Pokémon.

Shadow Breloom for Raids

Because Breloom already has a high Attack stat, the Shadow form can be particularly interesting for damage-focused players. However, reduced durability means it may faint quickly in some raid matchups.

Damage

High offensive potential.

Durability

Lower than regular Breloom.

Investment

More resource-sensitive.

Shadow Breloom Advice

  • Do not judge it only by IV percentage.
  • Check the moveset before investing.
  • Consider its role as a damage-focused attacker.
  • Compare resource costs with regular Breloom.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Breloom

✨ Purified Breloom

Purified Breloom is created when an eligible Shadow Breloom is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered carefully before making the decision.

Purification should not be treated as an automatic upgrade. Consider your raid, collection and long-term investment goals before changing a Shadow Breloom.

Lucky Breloom

🍀 Lucky Breloom

Lucky Breloom is obtained when a trade results in Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon can be attractive long-term investments because their power-up requirements are generally more resource efficient than standard Pokémon.

Why Lucky Breloom Is Useful

  • More efficient Stardust investment.
  • Useful for building a high-level attacker.
  • Can combine collector and battle value.
  • IVs should still be considered.
  • Moveset remains important.

100% IV Breloom

💯 Perfect Breloom

A 100% IV Breloom has 15 Attack IV, 15 Defense IV and 15 HP IV.

A perfect Breloom can be an attractive investment target, especially for players building high-level PvE Pokémon. However, a lower-IV Breloom can still perform extremely well when it has the correct moveset and level.

XXL Breloom

📏 XXL Breloom

XXL Breloom is a larger size variant. Size classification is primarily relevant to collection features and certain size-based competitions.

Battle Performance

XXL status does not automatically make Breloom stronger in standard Pokémon GO battles. Its value is primarily related to size collection and eligible Showcase competitions.

XXS Breloom

🔹 XXS Breloom

XXS Breloom is a smaller size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, its size classification is generally associated with collecting and eligible size-based features rather than direct combat strength.

Battle Performance

XXS status does not automatically change Breloom's standard battle stats or make its attacks weaker.

Breloom FAQ

What type is Breloom?

Breloom is a dual Grass and Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Breloom weak to?

Breloom is vulnerable to Flying, Fire, Psychic, Ice, Poison and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Shiny Breloom available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game availability.

Is Shadow Breloom stronger?

Shadow Breloom has a different battle profile and can be particularly attractive for damage-focused players.

Should I purify Shadow Breloom?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making a long-term decision.

What is a 100% Breloom?

A 100% Breloom has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Breloom good?

Lucky status can make Breloom more efficient to power up, which can be valuable for long-term investment.

Does XXL Breloom have higher battle stats?

No. XXL status does not automatically increase standard battle stats.

Does XXS Breloom have lower battle stats?

No. XXS status does not automatically reduce standard battle stats.

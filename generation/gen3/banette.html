Banette Overview

Banette is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Shuppet and represents the final stage of the Shuppet evolutionary family. In Pokémon GO, Banette has a high Attack stat compared with its Defense, making it more naturally suited to offensive roles than long-duration defensive play.

Why Banette's Typing Matters

Banette's pure Ghost typing gives it a focused offensive identity. Ghost-type attacks are particularly useful against Psychic- and Ghost-type targets, while Banette must be careful around Dark- and Ghost-type attacks.

Banette Weaknesses & Resistances

Understanding Banette's defensive typing helps when building raid counters and PvP teams.

Weaknesses

Important:

Ghost-type Pokémon also have a unique relationship with Normal-type attacks. Normal attacks do not normally damage Banette, while Banette's Ghost attacks do not normally damage Normal-type targets.

Is a 100% Banette Worth Keeping?

Banette PvP Guide

Banette's offensive stats and Ghost typing give it some interesting PvP applications, although its relatively low bulk means it needs careful play.

Great League

Banette can operate within the Great League CP limit, but its lower durability compared with bulkier Ghost options can make shield management especially important.

Ultra League

Banette can be considered for specific Ultra League teams, although players should compare its performance with other Ghost-type choices before committing resources.

Master League

Banette's CP ceiling makes it less naturally suited to Master League compared with Pokémon specifically built around the highest CP environments.

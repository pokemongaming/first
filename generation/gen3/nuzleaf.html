07 — PVP

Nuzleaf PvP Guide

Great League

Nuzleaf can technically be used in Great League because its maximum CP sits below 1,500, but its low Defense limits its overall performance. Shiftry is generally the more interesting evolution-stage choice.

Ultra League

Nuzleaf cannot naturally reach the 2,500 CP threshold, making Ultra League a poor fit for a standard Nuzleaf.

Master League

Nuzleaf's maximum CP is far below the Master League environment, so it is not a practical Master League investment.

PvP IV note:

For CP-limited leagues, PvP IV rankings can differ from traditional 15/15/15 IV rankings. Low Attack with higher Defense and HP can sometimes produce a better stat product under a CP cap.

Shiny Nuzleaf

Shadow Nuzleaf

Shadow Nuzleaf retains its Grass/Dark typing but has the Shadow battle modifier. Shadow Nuzleaf is primarily of interest to collectors and players experimenting with Shadow Pokémon rather than as a top raid investment.

12 — LUCKY

Lucky Nuzleaf

🍀 LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Nuzleaf

Lucky Nuzleaf is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and benefit from reduced Stardust costs when powered up.

Why Keep a Lucky Nuzleaf?

  • Reduced Stardust cost makes powering up cheaper.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed IV floor from the trade mechanic.
  • Lucky Nuzleaf is useful for collectors.
  • A Lucky Nuzleaf can still be evolved into Shiftry.
  • Lucky status remains after evolution.
Lucky Nuzleaf Tip

If you want a Lucky Shiftry for your collection, trading for a Nuzleaf and evolving it can preserve the Lucky status through evolution.

Nuzleaf FAQ

What type is Nuzleaf?

Nuzleaf is a Grass and Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Nuzleaf weak to?

Nuzleaf is especially vulnerable to Bug-type attacks and is also weak to Fairy, Fighting, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison attacks.

What is Nuzleaf's max CP?

Nuzleaf's Level 50 maximum CP is approximately 1,387.

Is Shiny Nuzleaf available?

Yes. Shiny Nuzleaf is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Nuzleaf available?

Shadow Nuzleaf exists as a Shadow variant and retains the Grass/Dark typing.

Can Nuzleaf be Lucky?

Can Nuzleaf be XXL?

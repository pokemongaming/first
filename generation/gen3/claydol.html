Claydol Overview

Claydol is the evolved form of Baltoy and was introduced in Generation 3. It has a distinctive Psychic/Ground typing and is known for its defensive stat profile.

Why Claydol's Typing Matters

Psychic/Ground is an unusual combination that gives Claydol a different matchup profile from many other Psychic-type Pokémon. Its Ground typing also means Electric-type attacks do not deal normal damage.

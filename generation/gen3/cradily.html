Why Cradily's Typing Matters

Cradily's Rock / Grass combination is unusual in Pokémon GO. Grass gives it useful interactions with Water and Ground attacks, while Rock contributes important resistances and offensive coverage.

16 • XXL

XXL Cradily

XXL Cradily is a size variant that represents an unusually large individual Pokémon. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Cradily can have any combination of IVs.

📏 XXL Cradily

XXL status is based on the Pokémon's size rather than its Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

XXL Cradily Facts

  • XXL is a size classification.
  • XXL does not mean 100% IV.
  • An XXL Cradily can have low, average or perfect IVs.
  • Size and IVs should be evaluated separately.

23 • FAQ

Cradily FAQ

What type is Cradily?

Cradily is a Rock / Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Cradily weak to?

Cradily is weak to Steel, Fighting, Bug and Ice attacks, with Steel being especially important because of the interaction between its two types.

What is Cradily's Max CP?

Cradily's maximum CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs is approximately 2,211.

Is Cradily good for raids?

Cradily is generally more situational in raids than dedicated top-tier Grass or Rock attackers.

Is Cradily good in PvP?

Cradily can be more interesting in CP-limited PvP because of its defensive stat profile and unusual typing. Its usefulness depends on the current moveset and format.

Is Shiny Cradily available?

Yes. Shiny Cradily is available through eligible Shiny encounters and can also be obtained by evolving a Shiny Lileep.

Can you get Shadow Cradily?

Shadow Cradily can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow Lileep when that Shadow species is available through Pokémon GO's current Rocket mechanics.

What is Purified Cradily?

Purified Cradily is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Cradily.

What is Lucky Cradily?

Lucky Cradily is a Cradily obtained through Pokémon trading that receives Lucky status and the associated Stardust power-up benefit.

What is a 100% Cradily?

A 100% or Hundo Cradily has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Cradily?

XXL Cradily is an unusually large individual. XXL status is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Cradily?

XXS Cradily is an unusually small individual. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

Is a Hundo Cradily automatically the best PvP Cradily?

No. Perfect IVs are excellent for collection and can be useful in unrestricted formats, but CP-limited PvP often favors different IV combinations.

Does Lucky Cradily mean 100% IV?

No. Lucky status and IV quality are separate characteristics. A Lucky Cradily can have many different IV combinations.

Can a Cradily be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a traded Shiny Cradily can potentially become Lucky.

Can Cradily be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Cradily can also have a perfect 15/15/15 IV spread.

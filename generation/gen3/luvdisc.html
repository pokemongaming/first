Luvdisc Overview

Luvdisc is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3 and originally discovered in the Hoenn region. It does not evolve into another Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

Because of its relatively low maximum CP and limited offensive stats, Luvdisc is generally more interesting to collectors and specialty PvP players than to raid-focused players.

Luvdisc PvP Guide

Luvdisc's low CP ceiling means it naturally fits low-CP formats better than Master League. Its usefulness depends heavily on the current cup rules, available moves and meta.

Great League

Lucky Luvdisc

Lucky Luvdisc is not a separate species or evolution. Lucky is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀
Lucky Luvdisc

A Lucky Luvdisc can be useful for collectors and players who enjoy building special trade collections.

Lucky Luvdisc Benefits

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky status is obtained through trading.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky Luvdisc can be kept as a trade or collection milestone.
Lucky status does not change Luvdisc's species or Water typing.

Luvdisc FAQ

What type is Luvdisc?

Luvdisc is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Luvdisc weak to?

Luvdisc is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Does Luvdisc evolve?

No. Luvdisc does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Luvdisc available?

Yes. Shiny Luvdisc is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Luvdisc good for raids?

Luvdisc is generally not a strong raid attacker because of its low Attack stat and low maximum CP.

Is Luvdisc good for PvP?

Luvdisc has a niche role at best. Its usefulness depends on the current cup, CP limit and moveset.

What is a 100% Luvdisc?

A 100% Luvdisc has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Luvdisc?

Lucky Luvdisc is a Luvdisc that received Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Luvdisc?

XXL Luvdisc is an extra-large size specimen. It remains a normal Luvdisc species.

What is XXS Luvdisc?

XXS Luvdisc is an extra-small size specimen. XXS does not create a separate Pokémon species.

Is Shadow Luvdisc available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations. Check current game data before claiming that Shadow Luvdisc is obtainable.

Can Luvdisc be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a status obtained through trading and is not a separate Luvdisc species.

Can Shiny Luvdisc be Lucky?

Shiny and Lucky are separate characteristics, so a traded Shiny Luvdisc can potentially have Lucky status.

Can Luvdisc be XXL and Shiny?

Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so collectors can look for combinations such as Shiny XXL or Shiny XXS Luvdisc.

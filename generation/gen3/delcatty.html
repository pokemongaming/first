Delcatty Overview

Delcatty is the evolved form of Skitty. It is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 3 and originally discovered in the Hoenn region.

While Delcatty is generally more of a collection and Pokédex Pokémon than a top-tier raid attacker, its multiple special variants can make it interesting for collectors and players completing different Pokémon GO collections.

Why Delcatty's Typing Matters

As a pure Normal-type Pokémon, Delcatty has a simple defensive profile. Normal-type Pokémon are weak to Fighting-type attacks and are immune to Ghost-type damage in Pokémon GO's type-effectiveness system.

Shiny Delcatty

Shiny Delcatty is the alternate-color version of Delcatty. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not automatically increase battle stats.

✨ Collector Variant

A Shiny Delcatty can be especially valuable for players completing a Hoenn Pokédex or Shiny collection.

Shiny Delcatty IVs

Shiny status and IV quality are separate systems. A Shiny Delcatty can have any eligible IV combination, including a perfect 15/15/15 IV spread.

Purified Delcatty

Purified Delcatty is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Delcatty. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can modify its IVs.

Shadow status is removed.

📈

💨
Frustration Removed

Purification removes the Shadow-exclusive Frustration move.

The decision depends on whether you prefer the Shadow version, a Purified collection entry, improved IVs or lower-cost Purified Pokémon investment.

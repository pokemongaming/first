Kirlia Evolution Guide

Kirlia is unusual because it can evolve into two different final forms. The evolution path depends on the requirements for Gardevoir or Gallade.

EVOLUTION 01

Gardevoir

Kirlia can evolve into Gardevoir using the standard evolution path and required Candy.

EVOLUTION 02

Gallade

A male Kirlia can be eligible to evolve into Gallade when the required evolution conditions are met.

Evolution Tip

Before evolving a high-IV Kirlia, decide whether you want Gardevoir or Gallade. This is especially important when you are investing Candy and XL Candy into a specific battle role.

Shiny Kirlia

Shiny Kirlia is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collector target.

SHINY FORM

✨ Shiny Kirlia

Shiny Kirlia has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. A Shiny Kirlia can also be evolved, allowing collectors to continue the Shiny Ralts family.

Should You Evolve Shiny Kirlia?

If you are collecting Shiny Pokémon, keeping at least one Shiny Kirlia can be worthwhile before evolving additional copies. Players who want a Shiny Gardevoir or Shiny Gallade can use eligible Shiny Kirlia for evolution.

Shadow Kirlia

Shadow Kirlia is the Shadow variant of Kirlia. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts and require careful consideration before purification.

SHADOW FORM

🌑 Shadow Kirlia

Shadow Kirlia retains the Psychic/Fairy typing of regular Kirlia. Its Shadow status changes its battle characteristics but does not change its basic Pokémon typing.

Lucky Kirlia

Lucky Kirlia is obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

LUCKY FORM

🍀 Lucky Kirlia

Lucky Kirlia can be an attractive candidate for evolution because powering up Lucky Pokémon requires less Stardust than powering up standard Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Kirlia?

  • Useful for collectors building a Lucky Pokédex.
  • Can reduce Stardust costs for power-ups.
  • Can be evolved into an eligible final evolution.
  • Good candidate for a long-term evolution project.

100% IV Kirlia

A 100% IV Kirlia has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A perfect Kirlia is especially interesting to collectors because it can later become part of the Gardevoir or Gallade evolution family.

XXL Kirlia

XXL Kirlia is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system. XXL Pokémon are primarily useful for collection, showcase and size-focused gameplay.

📏

XXL Kirlia

An XXL Kirlia is larger than the standard size range. If you participate in PokéStop Showcases, keeping strong XXL specimens can be useful when Kirlia is featured.

Why Keep XXL Kirlia?

  • Useful for PokéStop Showcases.
  • Adds a size variant to your collection.
  • Can be kept as an XXL evolution candidate.
  • Useful for collectors completing size variants.

XXS Kirlia

XXS Kirlia is an exceptionally small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS Pokémon can be valuable to collectors and may have relevance in size-based Showcase competitions.

🔹

XXS Kirlia

An XXS Kirlia is smaller than the normal size range. Collectors may choose to keep one rather than immediately evolving it.

Should You Keep an XXS Kirlia?

If you collect size variants, keeping an XXS Kirlia is recommended. It can also be useful when Pokémon GO features size-related Showcase competitions.

Kirlia Candy & Evolution Resources

Because Kirlia sits in the middle of the Ralts evolution family, Candy management is important. Before spending large amounts of Candy, decide whether you want Gardevoir or Gallade.

  • Keep high-IV Kirlia for future evolution.
  • Keep Shiny Kirlia if you collect Shiny evolutions.
  • Compare male Kirlia before choosing Gallade.
  • Save strong Shadow Kirlia before purifying.
  • Keep XXL and XXS specimens for size collection.
  • Lucky Kirlia can be useful for reducing Stardust costs.

Kirlia FAQ

What type is Kirlia?

Kirlia is a Psychic/Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Kirlia weak against?

Kirlia is weak to Ghost, Poison and Steel attacks.

Can Kirlia evolve?

Yes. Kirlia can evolve into Gardevoir, while eligible male Kirlia can evolve into Gallade when the required conditions are met.

Is Shiny Kirlia available?

Yes. Shiny Kirlia is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Kirlia available?

Shadow availability can depend on Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current game rotation.

What is a 100% Kirlia?

A 100% Kirlia has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Kirlia?

Lucky Kirlia is obtained through a Lucky Trade and has the reduced Stardust power-up cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is XXL Kirlia?

XXL Kirlia is an unusually large size variant that can be useful for collection and PokéStop Showcases.

What is XXS Kirlia?

XXS Kirlia is an unusually small size variant and can be valuable for size-focused collectors and Showcases.

Should I evolve my 100% Kirlia?

If you have a perfect-IV Kirlia, consider whether you want Gardevoir or Gallade before evolving it. The best choice depends on your collection and battle goals.

Should I purify Shadow Kirlia?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, evolution potential and your personal goals before purifying it.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Kirlia?

Yes, if you collect size variants or participate in PokéStop Showcases. Size can make these Pokémon more interesting even when their battle stats are ordinary.

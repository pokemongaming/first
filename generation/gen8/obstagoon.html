Why Obstagoon's Typing Matters

Obstagoon's Dark / Normal combination gives it a useful set of resistances and makes it particularly interesting in PvP. However, its dual typing also creates a significant weakness to Fighting-type attacks.

Obstagoon PvP Guide

PvP is one of Obstagoon's most important roles. Its combination of Counter, Dark-type pressure and coverage moves gives it a distinctive battle profile.

Great League

Great League is one of the formats where Obstagoon can be considered because its CP and stat distribution allow it to operate below the league's CP limit. IV selection and the current meta can strongly influence its value.

Ultra League

Obstagoon can also function in Ultra League, where its Dark typing and charged-move pressure can become useful against selected opponents.

Master League

Obstagoon is generally less naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP is substantially lower than that of many Legendary and high-stat Pokémon commonly used there.

Obstagoon Raid & PvE Performance

Obstagoon is generally more notable for PvP than for high-end raid attacking. Its dual typing and moveset give it utility in selected situations, but dedicated raid attackers are usually preferred for maximizing damage output.

Obstagoon FAQ

What type is Obstagoon?

Obstagoon is a Dark / Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Obstagoon weak to?

Obstagoon is vulnerable to Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks, with Fighting being an especially important weakness.

Is Obstagoon good in Pokémon GO?

Obstagoon is particularly interesting for PvP because of its typing, fast energy generation and charged-move coverage.

Is Obstagoon good in Great League?

Obstagoon can be useful in Great League depending on its IVs, moveset, team composition and the current PvP meta.

Is Obstagoon good in Ultra League?

Obstagoon can also have value in Ultra League, where its Dark typing and charged-move options can create useful matchups.

Is Shiny Obstagoon available?

Shiny Obstagoon can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution opportunities.

What is a 100% Obstagoon?

A 100% Obstagoon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Obstagoon?

Lucky Obstagoon is a Pokémon that has obtained the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is Shadow Obstagoon?

Shadow Obstagoon is the Shadow variant of Obstagoon. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from their standard counterparts.

Should I purify Shadow Obstagoon?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen with the purified result and consider whether you value the Shadow battle characteristics or the purified form.

What is XXL Obstagoon?

XXL Obstagoon is a size variant classified toward the larger end of Obstagoon's size range.

What is XXS Obstagoon?

XXS Obstagoon is a size variant classified toward the smaller end of Obstagoon's size range.

Does XXL Obstagoon have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Obstagoon can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Obstagoon have lower stats?

Size classification does not automatically determine the Pokémon's Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Does Obstagoon evolve?

Yes. Obstagoon evolves from Galarian Linoone and is the final evolution of the Galarian Zigzagoon family.

Corviknight

Corviknight is a dual Flying and Steel-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 8. It is the final evolution of Rookidee and Corvisquire and combines a useful defensive typing with access to different roles in Pokémon GO.

Corviknight Overview

Corviknight is the final evolution of the Rookidee evolution family. Its Flying and Steel typing is one of its most important characteristics because it creates a large number of resistances while also leaving Corviknight with a smaller number of important weaknesses.

Regular Corviknight

Regular Corviknight is the standard version of the Pokémon. It is the base reference for comparing Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky and size variants.

Standard Form

The regular form has no Shadow bonus, purification status, Lucky status or size classification affecting its battle identity.

Should You Keep Regular Corviknight?

Purified Corviknight

Purified Corviknight is relevant when a Shadow member of the Rookidee evolution family can be purified and evolved. Purification should be considered carefully because it changes the Pokémon's Shadow status.

💠 Purified Corviknight

A Purified version no longer has Shadow status. The final decision to purify should depend on your IVs, battle goals, resource plans and collection preferences.

Lucky Corviknight

Lucky Corviknight is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon are particularly valuable for long-term power-up projects because their Stardust requirements are reduced compared with a normal Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Corviknight

A Lucky Corviknight with strong IVs can be an excellent candidate for players planning a long-term investment.

Why Lucky Corviknight Is Valuable

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term investment projects.
  • High minimum IV potential from Lucky trades.
  • Can also be Shiny depending on the traded Pokémon.
  • Special collector value.

Corviknight FAQ

What type is Corviknight?

Corviknight is a Flying and Steel-type Pokémon.

What evolves into Corviknight?

Corviknight evolves from Corvisquire, which evolves from Rookidee.

What is Corviknight weak to?

Corviknight's Flying and Steel typing makes Electric and Fire attacks particularly important weaknesses.

Is Shiny Corviknight different in battle?

A Shiny version has the same underlying species battle identity. The main difference is its alternate appearance.

What is a 100% Corviknight?

A 100% IV Corviknight has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Corviknight worth powering up?

Lucky Pokémon can be attractive power-up candidates because they require less Stardust than standard Pokémon.

Does XXL Corviknight have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate Pokémon characteristics.

Does XXS Corviknight have lower stats?

Size classification should not be confused with the Pokémon's individual IV values.

Should I purify Shadow Corviknight?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow version, its IVs and your intended use before making the decision.

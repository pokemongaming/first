Why Morpeko's Typing Matters

The Electric / Dark combination gives Morpeko a different matchup profile from a pure Electric-type Pokémon. Electric attacks are useful against Water and Flying opponents, while Dark attacks can be useful against Psychic and Ghost targets.

Morpeko Forms — Full Belly & Hangry Mode

Morpeko has two recognizable forms associated with its Hunger Switch ability: Full Belly Mode and Hangry Mode. These forms are central to Morpeko's identity and should be represented separately in a Pokémon database.

🐹
FULL BELLY MODE

Full Belly Morpeko

Full Belly Mode represents Morpeko in its well-fed appearance. Its form is associated with its normal coloration and appearance.

😈
HANGRY MODE

Hangry Morpeko

Hangry Mode represents Morpeko after its hunger state changes. It has a darker, more aggressive appearance.

What Is Hunger Switch?

Hunger Switch is Morpeko's signature mechanic. The mechanic is closely associated with Morpeko changing between its Full Belly and Hangry appearances.

Database recommendation

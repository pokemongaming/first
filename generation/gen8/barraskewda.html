01 • OVERVIEW

Barraskewda Overview

Barraskewda is a pure Water-type Pokémon from the Galar region. It evolves from Arrokuda and represents a more offensive stage of the evolution family. Its high Attack and comparatively low Defense mean that Barraskewda is better understood as an aggressive attacker rather than a bulky Pokémon.

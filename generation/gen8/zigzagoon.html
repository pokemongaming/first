What Does Zigzagoon's Typing Mean?

Regular Zigzagoon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile, but its relatively modest battle stats mean it is generally more interesting as an evolution, collection or event Pokémon than as a serious raid attacker.

Zigzagoon Weaknesses & Resistances

Resistance

Normal-type Pokémon are immune to Ghost-type attacks in Pokémon GO's type-effectiveness system.

Battle note:

Zigzagoon's typing is more useful for understanding matchups than for building an optimized raid team. Its low overall battle ceiling limits its PvE role.

Zigzagoon Attack, Defense & HP

Zigzagoon has modest offensive stats but a relatively high HP stat for its size. Its overall CP remains low compared with evolved Pokémon and most raid attackers.

Zigzagoon Evolution

Regular Hoenn Zigzagoon evolves into Linoone. This is separate from the Galarian Zigzagoon evolution family, which leads to Galarian Linoone and Obstagoon.

Evolution Candy

Keep enough Zigzagoon Candy before evolving. If you are collecting a complete Pokédex, consider checking the IVs, size and special status of your Zigzagoon before spending Candy.

When Is Zigzagoon Worth Keeping?

Zigzagoon is worth keeping when it has a useful collection property such as Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky status, strong IVs, XXL or XXS size, or when you need it for evolution and Pokédex completion.

What Is 100% Zigzagoon?

A 100% Zigzagoon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a hundo.

100% Zigzagoon

A perfect Zigzagoon can be a fun collector Pokémon even though its low CP limits its usefulness in high-level battles.

Shadow Zigzagoon Weakness

Shadow Zigzagoon remains a pure Normal-type Pokémon, so Fighting-type attacks remain its primary type weakness.

Should You Purify Shadow Zigzagoon?

Purification should be a deliberate choice. If you like the Shadow appearance or want to preserve a Shadow specimen for your collection, you may prefer to keep it Shadow.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Attack Frustration. The ability to remove Frustration can depend on special events and current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Purified Zigzagoon

Purified Zigzagoon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Zigzagoon. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can improve its IVs while also affecting its resource requirements.

🌟

Purified Zigzagoon

A purified Zigzagoon is no longer Shadow and can be kept as a special collection variant or evolved normally.

Lucky Zigzagoon

Lucky Zigzagoon is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Zigzagoon

A Lucky Zigzagoon is especially attractive to collectors and players who enjoy keeping unusual Pokémon variants.

Why Keep a Lucky Zigzagoon?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive visual background.
  • A Lucky Zigzagoon can be useful for collection goals.
  • Trading provides another way to obtain special variants.
IV note:

Lucky Pokémon receive a guaranteed minimum IV floor when created through a trade, but being Lucky does not automatically mean the Pokémon is 100% IV.

Is a Hundo Zigzagoon Rare?

Perfect IV Pokémon are much less common than ordinary specimens, which makes a 100% Zigzagoon an appealing collector Pokémon.

Hundo Tip

If you find a 15/15/15 Zigzagoon, consider keeping it before transferring it, especially if you are working toward a complete perfect Pokémon collection.

XXL Zigzagoon

XXL Zigzagoon is an unusually large specimen. Size categories are separate from IVs, so an XXL Pokémon does not automatically have perfect Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

XXS Zigzagoon

XXS Zigzagoon is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of the Pokémon's IV percentage.

🔎

XXS Zigzagoon

A particularly small Zigzagoon that can be retained for size collection goals.

Should You Keep XXS Zigzagoon?

If you collect size variants, an XXS Zigzagoon is worth keeping. You can also keep one for comparison with an XXL specimen.

Size Collector Tip

Size and IVs are separate properties. An XXS Zigzagoon can still have excellent IVs, poor IVs or anything in between.

How to Use Zigzagoon

For Raids

Zigzagoon should generally not be selected for optimized raid teams. Its role is far more focused on collecting and evolution.

For Collection

  • Keep a Shiny Zigzagoon if you collect Shiny Pokémon.
  • Keep a strong IV specimen for your perfect-IV collection.
  • Keep interesting Shadow and Purified specimens.
  • Save Lucky Zigzagoon if you collect Lucky Pokémon.
  • Check the size before transferring duplicates.
  • Consider keeping both XXL and XXS specimens.

Before Evolving

Check the Pokémon's IVs, Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky status and size before evolving. Once you evolve a Zigzagoon, the resulting Pokémon keeps the relevant inherited properties, but its species and appearance change.

Zigzagoon Candy & Stardust

Zigzagoon Candy is mainly useful for evolving Zigzagoon and powering up the resulting Pokémon. Because Zigzagoon has limited high-level battle value, consider your goals before investing large amounts of Stardust.

  • Save Candy for evolution if you need the Pokédex entry.
  • Check IVs before spending Stardust.
  • Keep special variants before transferring duplicates.
  • Trade duplicates if you are hunting Lucky Pokémon.
  • Save XXL and XXS specimens for size collection goals.

Zigzagoon FAQ

What type is Zigzagoon?

Regular Hoenn Zigzagoon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

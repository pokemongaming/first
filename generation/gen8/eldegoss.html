Lucky Eldegoss

Lucky Pokémon are obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. A Lucky Eldegoss has a special Lucky status and generally requires fewer Stardust resources to power up.

⭐ Lucky Eldegoss

Lucky status is separate from Shiny status and IV quality. A Lucky Eldegoss can also be Shiny or have excellent IVs, depending on the individual Pokémon.

Lucky Eldegoss Benefits

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collectors building special Pokémon variants.
  • Can have strong IVs after trading.
  • Can also be Shiny if the traded Pokémon was Shiny.
  • Lucky status does not change Eldegoss's Pokémon type.

XXL Eldegoss

XXL Eldegoss is an unusually large individual based on Pokémon GO's size system. XXL is an individual size characteristic rather than a separate species.

📏 XXL Eldegoss

An XXL Eldegoss is larger than the standard size range. XXL status can be useful for collection, medals and size-based features.

XXL ≠ Evolution

XXL status does not create a different Pokémon species and does not change Eldegoss's base typing.

Size classifications are individual characteristics. Do not confuse XXL status with a special battle form.

XXS Eldegoss

XXS Eldegoss is an unusually small individual. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification rather than a different Pokémon species.

📐 XXS Eldegoss

An XXS Eldegoss is smaller than the normal size range and can be valuable for collectors interested in size records.

XXS Collection Value

Size variants can be especially interesting when combined with other characteristics such as Shiny, Lucky or high IVs.

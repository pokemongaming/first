🌑 Shadow Gossifleur

Shadow Gossifleur is a Shadow variant of Gossifleur. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately because the Shadow battle mechanic changes their offensive and defensive characteristics.

🌑 Shadow Gossifleur

Shadow Gossifleur retains its Grass typing while having the battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Shadow Gossifleur Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Gossifleur's basic typing. It therefore remains weak to Fire, Ice, Bug, Flying and Poison attacks.

Should You Keep Shadow Gossifleur?

  • Check its IVs before making a long-term decision.
  • Compare Shadow performance with regular Gossifleur.
  • Consider the higher resource cost of Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Check current Frustration removal opportunities.

💧 Purified Gossifleur

💧 Purified Gossifleur

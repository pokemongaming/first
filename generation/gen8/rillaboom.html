Rillaboom

Why Rillaboom's Typing Matters

Rillaboom's pure Grass typing gives it a clear offensive role against Water, Ground and Rock-type opponents. At the same time, it must be careful around Fire, Flying, Ice, Bug and Poison attacks.

08 • IV GUIDE

Rillaboom IV Guide

IVs add individual points to Attack, Defense and HP. A perfect Rillaboom has 15/15/15 IVs.

Is 15/15/15 Rillaboom Worth Keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Rillaboom is a perfect-IV specimen and makes an excellent collector target. However, IV perfection is not required for Rillaboom to be useful.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Rillaboom

A Lucky Rillaboom is obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics. Lucky status reduces the Stardust required for powering up the Pokémon and gives the traded Pokémon a guaranteed minimum IV floor under the game's Lucky trade rules.

🍀

Lucky Rillaboom

A Lucky Rillaboom can be especially attractive for trainers who want to build a high-level Pokémon while saving Stardust.

LUCKY TRADE VARIANT

Lucky Rillaboom IVs

Lucky status does not mean the Pokémon is automatically 100%. A Lucky Rillaboom can have different IV spreads, but Lucky trades have a special minimum IV floor.

Why Keep a Lucky Rillaboom?

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term investment.
  • Excellent collector variant.
  • A Lucky Shiny Rillaboom can be an especially rare combination.

24 • FAQ

Rillaboom FAQ

What type is Rillaboom?

Rillaboom is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Rillaboom weak to?

Rillaboom is weak to Fire, Flying, Ice, Bug and Poison-type attacks.

What does Rillaboom evolve from?

Rillaboom evolves from Thwackey, which evolves from Grookey.

What is Rillaboom's max CP?

Rillaboom's Level 50 maximum CP is 3,758 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Rillaboom available?

Yes. Shiny Rillaboom is available.

Is Shadow Rillaboom available?

Shadow Rillaboom is not currently listed as an obtainable Shadow form. This should be updated if Pokémon GO introduces it in a future rotation.

Can Rillaboom be purified?

Only a Shadow Pokémon can be purified. Since Shadow Rillaboom is not currently available, Purified Rillaboom is not currently obtainable.

What is a 100% Rillaboom?

A 100% Rillaboom has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Rillaboom be Lucky?

Yes. A Rillaboom can become Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system.

Can Rillaboom be XXL or XXS?

Rillaboom can have size classifications such as XXL or XXS. Size and IVs are separate attributes.

Is Rillaboom good for raids?

Rillaboom can be useful as a Grass-type attacker, especially against bosses weak to Grass damage. Its exact ranking depends on the current Pokémon GO move and attacker meta.

What is Rillaboom's best moveset?

Razor Leaf with Frenzy Plant is a strong Grass-focused combination. Frenzy Plant is an event-related move and may require an Elite Charged TM outside eligible events.

Does Rillaboom have Dynamax and Gigantamax forms?

Yes. Rillaboom has Dynamax and Gigantamax forms associated with Pokémon GO's Max Battle system.

What is Gigantamax Rillaboom's special move?

Gigantamax Rillaboom uses G-Max Drum Solo.

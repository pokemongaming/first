Zamazenta Overview

Zamazenta is the Legendary Pokémon associated with Pokémon Shield and was introduced to Pokémon GO as a powerful Galar Legendary. Its standard Pokémon GO form is Hero of Many Battles, while Crowned Shield is a special transformed form with Fighting/Steel typing.

Why Zamazenta's Typing Matters

Hero of Many Battles Zamazenta is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon. Its primary weaknesses are Fairy, Flying and Psychic attacks. When Zamazenta becomes Crowned Shield, it gains Steel as a secondary type and therefore has a substantially different defensive profile.

Zamazenta PvP Guide

Zamazenta's high stat product and charged-move options make it most interesting in the Master League environment.

Hero of Many Battles

Hero Zamazenta can apply pressure with Close Combat, while its coverage moves can create matchup-specific opportunities. However, its actual performance depends heavily on the current Master League metagame.

Crowned Shield Zamazenta

Crowned Shield is considerably more interesting in Master League because its Fighting/Steel typing gives it a large number of useful resistances. Ice Fang, Close Combat and Behemoth Bash give it flexible offensive coverage.

Zamazenta FAQ

What type is Zamazenta?

Hero of Many Battles Zamazenta is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon. Crowned Shield Zamazenta is Fighting/Steel.

What is Zamazenta weak to?

Hero Zamazenta is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic. Crowned Shield Zamazenta is weak to Fighting, Fire and Ground.

What is Zamazenta's max CP?

Hero of Many Battles Zamazenta reaches 4,329 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs. Crowned Shield Zamazenta reaches 4,717 CP.

Is Zamazenta a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Zamazenta is a Legendary Pokémon from Generation 8.

Does Zamazenta evolve?

Zamazenta does not have a conventional evolution chain in Pokémon GO. It can instead transform into Crowned Shield Zamazenta.

What is Crowned Shield Zamazenta?

Crowned Shield Zamazenta is Zamazenta's special Fighting/Steel form.

How do I get Crowned Shield Zamazenta?

A qualifying Zamazenta that knows Iron Head can be transformed using Crowned Shield Energy and Zamazenta Candy.

What move does Crowned Shield Zamazenta get?

Iron Head changes to the signature Steel-type Charged Attack Behemoth Bash while in Crowned Shield form.

Is Shiny Zamazenta available?

Shiny Zamazenta is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny status is a collectible variant rather than a separate species.

Is Shadow Zamazenta available?

Shadow Zamazenta is not currently an available Pokémon GO form. This section should be updated if Niantic introduces it in the future.

Is Purified Zamazenta available?

Not currently. Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon, and Shadow Zamazenta is not currently available.

What is a Lucky Zamazenta?

Lucky Zamazenta is a traded Zamazenta with Lucky status. It remains the same Pokémon species and form.

What is a 100% Zamazenta?

A 100% Zamazenta has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Zamazenta?

XXL Zamazenta is a size category describing an unusually large specimen. It is not a separate species or battle form.

What is an XXS Zamazenta?

XXS Zamazenta is a size category describing an unusually small specimen.

Is Crowned Shield Zamazenta good for raids?

Crowned Shield Zamazenta is particularly useful as a Steel-focused attacker with Behemoth Bash and also offers strong defensive utility.

Is Zamazenta good in Master League?

Zamazenta can have Master League relevance, with Crowned Shield offering particularly strong defensive typing and useful charged-move coverage.

Can Shiny Zamazenta become Crowned Shield Zamazenta?

Yes. A Shiny Zamazenta can retain its Shiny identity when transformed into Crowned Shield form.

Can a Hundo Zamazenta become Crowned Shield?

Yes. The IVs belong to the underlying Zamazenta, so a perfect 15/15/15 Zamazenta can be used as the basis for Crowned Shield when the transformation requirements are met.

Can a Lucky Zamazenta become Crowned Shield?

Lucky is a status of the underlying Pokémon rather than a separate species, so eligible Lucky Zamazenta can be considered for Crowned Shield transformation.

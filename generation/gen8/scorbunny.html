Why Scorbunny's Typing Matters

Scorbunny is a pure Fire-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it useful resistances against several common attack categories while leaving it vulnerable to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

    Should You Power Up Scorbunny?

    For most players, powering up Scorbunny itself is not a priority. Resources are generally better spent on stronger Fire attackers or on evolving a good Scorbunny into Raboot and Cinderace.

    Scorbunny PvP Guide

    Scorbunny's low overall stats and CP ceiling limit its role in standard Pokémon GO PvP. However, special cups with unusual rules can sometimes create opportunities for unevolved Pokémon.

    Great League

    Scorbunny can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but its low Defense and overall battle statistics make it a niche choice rather than a standard competitive pick.

    Ultra League

    Scorbunny is generally not an attractive Ultra League investment because its maximum CP is far below the format's normal ceiling.

    Master League

    Scorbunny is not suitable for standard Master League competition due to its low maximum CP.

    Shiny Scorbunny

    Shiny Scorbunny is available in Pokémon GO. Its Shiny form provides an alternate coloration and is especially attractive to collectors because Scorbunny is part of a starter evolutionary family.

    ✨
    SHINY FORM

    Shiny Scorbunny

    Shiny Scorbunny can be encountered through eligible Scorbunny encounters. Its Shiny status remains when it evolves into Shiny Raboot and Shiny Cinderace.

    Collector tip:

    If you find a high-IV Shiny Scorbunny, consider keeping it before evolving it. A Shiny starter with strong IVs can be useful as both a collection piece and an evolution project.

    Lucky Scorbunny

    Lucky Scorbunny is a special traded Pokémon with the Lucky background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

    🍀✨

    Lucky Scorbunny

    A Lucky Scorbunny is particularly interesting for collectors and players who want to build its evolutionary family while saving Stardust on power-ups.

    Why Keep a Lucky Scorbunny?

    • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
    • Lucky trades guarantee a Lucky Pokémon.
    • Lucky Pokémon receive a minimum IV floor from the trade process.
    • A Lucky Scorbunny can be evolved into Lucky Raboot and Lucky Cinderace.
    • Lucky Scorbunny can be a useful collection target.

    100% IV Scorbunny

    Is 100% Scorbunny Worth Keeping?

    XXL Scorbunny

    XXL Scorbunny is a size-variant Scorbunny that exceeds the normal size range. Size variants are primarily valuable for collection, showcase and visual purposes rather than conventional battle strength.

    XXL

    XXL Scorbunny

    A Scorbunny recorded at an exceptionally large size. XXL status does not automatically mean higher Attack, Defense or HP.

    SHOWCASE

    Showcase Value

    Large Pokémon can be relevant for PokéStop Showcases and size-focused collection goals.

    XXL size should not be confused with IV quality. An XXL Scorbunny can have poor IVs, excellent IVs, or anything between them.

    XXS Scorbunny

    XXS Scorbunny is a very small Scorbunny recorded within the game's size system. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of IV quality.

    XXS

    XXS Scorbunny

    An exceptionally small Scorbunny that can be valuable to collectors and useful for size-based Showcase participation.

    COLLECTION

    Collector Value

    XXS Pokémon can be worth keeping when building a complete size collection.

    XXS does not mean low IV. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

    Scorbunny FAQ

    What type is Scorbunny?

    Scorbunny is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

    What is Scorbunny weak to?

    Scorbunny is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

    What is Scorbunny's Max CP?

    Scorbunny's Level 50 maximum CP with perfect IVs is approximately 1,240.

    Is Shiny Scorbunny available?

    Yes. Shiny Scorbunny is available in Pokémon GO.

    Does Scorbunny evolve?

    Yes. Scorbunny evolves into Raboot and then Cinderace.

    How much Candy does Scorbunny need to evolve?

    Scorbunny requires 25 Candy to evolve into Raboot. Raboot then requires 100 Candy to evolve into Cinderace.

    Is 100% Scorbunny rare?

    A 100% Scorbunny has perfect 15/15/15 IVs and can be a valuable collection and evolution target.

    What is 100% Scorbunny?

    100% Scorbunny means its Attack, Defense and HP IVs are all 15.

    What is XXL Scorbunny?

    XXL Scorbunny is an exceptionally large size variant recorded by Pokémon GO's size system.

    What is XXS Scorbunny?

    XXS Scorbunny is an exceptionally small size variant.

    Is XXL Scorbunny stronger?

    No. XXL status should not be treated as an automatic increase to Attack, Defense or HP.

    Is XXS Scorbunny weaker?

    No. XXS status is a size characteristic and should not automatically be interpreted as lower IV quality or weaker battle performance.

    Is Lucky Scorbunny worth keeping?

    Yes. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups, making a Lucky Scorbunny useful for collection and potential evolution projects.

    Is Shadow Scorbunny better than regular Scorbunny?

    Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. Whether the Shadow version is preferable depends on your intended use.

    Should I purify Shadow Scorbunny?

    Not automatically. Consider its IVs, future evolution potential, Shadow value and your collection goals before purifying it.

    Is Scorbunny good for raids?

    Scorbunny itself is generally not a top raid attacker because it is unevolved. Its evolution into Cinderace is more relevant for Fire-type battle teams.

    Is Scorbunny good in PvP?

    Scorbunny has limited PvP relevance because of its low stats and CP ceiling, although special cups can sometimes change the value of unevolved Pokémon.

    Did Scorbunny have a Community Day?

    Yes. Scorbunny had its Pokémon GO Community Day debut in March 2026, with Blast Burn associated with evolving to Cinderace during the event window.

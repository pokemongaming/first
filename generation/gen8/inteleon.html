Why Inteleon's Typing Matters

Inteleon's pure Water typing gives it useful defensive matchups against Fire, Ice, Steel and Water attacks. Its main weaknesses are Electric and Grass.

Should You Power Up Inteleon?

Inteleon can be worth powering up if you want a high-Attack Water attacker, particularly if you have a strong IV specimen with an appropriate moveset. Its lower bulk means it should be compared with your existing Water-type raid options before committing large amounts of Stardust and Candy.

Inteleon PvP Guide

Inteleon's high Attack and comparatively low bulk make it a more specialized PvP option than a traditional bulky Water-type.

Great League

Inteleon's high CP and fragile defensive profile make it a difficult choice for many Great League teams. League-specific IV spreads should always be checked against the current PvP meta.

Ultra League

Inteleon can reach the Ultra League CP range, but its low Defense means careful shield and matchup management is important.

Master League

Inteleon's maximum CP of 3,351 allows it to enter Master League, but it generally faces tougher competition from Pokémon with better overall bulk and matchup profiles.

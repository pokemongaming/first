Thievul PvP Guide

Thievul's PvP usefulness depends heavily on its available moves and the current Pokémon GO battle meta.

Great League

Great League is the primary format where Thievul's CP range can be evaluated under the 1,500 CP limit.

Ultra League

Thievul can be powered further for Ultra League, although its performance should be compared against other Dark-type options.

Master League

Thievul's maximum CP is relatively low compared with many traditional Master League Pokémon, making it less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League play.

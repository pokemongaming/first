100% IV Nickit

A 100% Nickit has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
15 / 15 / 15 = 100% IV

A perfect Nickit is commonly called a "hundo" by Pokémon GO players.

Is 100% Nickit Rare?

A perfect-IV Pokémon is less common than an ordinary specimen because all three IV values must reach 15. The rarity of a 100% Nickit depends on the encounter source and its IV distribution.

A 100% IV Nickit is a perfect individual, but perfect IVs do not automatically make every Pokémon highly useful in raids or PvP.

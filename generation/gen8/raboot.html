Raboot Overview

Raboot is the second stage of the Scorbunny family. It evolves from Scorbunny and can eventually evolve into Cinderace. Unlike a Legendary Pokémon, Raboot is primarily a collection and evolution-family Pokémon rather than a top-end raid attacker.

Why Raboot's Fire Typing Matters

Raboot's Fire typing gives it access to Fire-type damage while also defining its defensive matchup. Fire Pokémon are weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks, while resisting several types including Fire, Grass, Ice, Bug, Steel and Fairy.

Dubwool Overview

Dubwool is the evolved form of Wooloo and represents the final stage of its evolution family. As a pure Normal-type Pokémon, Dubwool has a straightforward weakness and resistance profile while gaining access to moves that can make it more interesting in PvP than its typing alone might suggest.

Dubwool is generally more interesting as a PvP Pokémon than as a raid attacker. Its bulk and flexible move options allow it to pressure opponents and use coverage moves against Pokémon that would otherwise expect a straightforward Normal-type matchup.

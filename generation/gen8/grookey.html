Grookey Forms & Special Variants

Grookey can be collected in several special forms and variations. These variants may have different collection value, mechanics or visual characteristics.

✨ Shiny Grookey

Shiny Grookey has alternate coloration and is primarily a collector-focused version of the standard Grookey.

🌑 Shadow Grookey

Shadow Grookey retains the same species and typing but has Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

✨ Purified Grookey

Purified Grookey is created when an eligible Shadow Grookey is purified.

🍀 Lucky Grookey

Lucky Grookey is a special traded version with reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

💯 100% Grookey

A 100% Grookey has perfect 15/15/15 IVs and is often considered a valuable long-term evolution candidate.

⬆️ XXL Grookey

XXL Grookey is a larger size variant that can be particularly interesting for collectors and size-based showcases.

⬇️ XXS Grookey

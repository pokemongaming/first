100% Applin IV Guide

A 100% Applin has the perfect IV combination of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV
100% IV does not always mean best:

For some Pokémon GO leagues, lower Attack IV spreads can be preferred because of CP limits. A perfect 15/15/15 Applin is still a desirable collection and long-term evolution target.

Applin FAQ

What type is Applin?

Applin is a Grass and Dragon-type Pokémon.

What generation is Applin from?

Applin originates from Generation 8 and the Galar region.

Can Applin evolve?

Yes. Applin belongs to a branching evolution family. Check the current Pokémon GO evolution requirements for the available evolution paths.

Is Shiny Applin different in battle?

Shiny status primarily changes appearance. IVs, level and moveset remain separate factors for battle performance.

Is a 100% Applin always the best?

A 15/15/15 Applin is a perfect-IV specimen, but the ideal IV spread can depend on how its evolution is used.

What is Lucky Applin?

Lucky Applin is an Applin with Lucky status obtained through eligible Pokémon trading mechanics.

What is XXL Applin?

XXL Applin is a larger-than-usual size variant.

What is XXS Applin?

XXS Applin is a smaller-than-usual size variant.

Should I evolve my best Applin immediately?

It is usually worth checking all available evolution paths before evolving a rare, Shiny, Lucky or high-IV Applin.

Is Shadow Applin always available?

No. Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket rotations and Pokémon GO events.

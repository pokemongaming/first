Sobble

Sobble is a Water-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Galar region. In Pokémon GO, it is the Water-type starter of the Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble trio and can evolve into Drizzile and eventually Inteleon.

Why Sobble's Water Typing Matters

Sobble is a pure Water-type Pokémon. Its Water typing gives it useful defensive interactions while also determining which attacks are super effective against it. Water is especially vulnerable to Electric and Grass attacks in Pokémon GO.

Should You Evolve Sobble?

If you are collecting the complete Galar Pokédex, evolving Sobble is necessary for obtaining Drizzile and Inteleon. If you have a high-IV or rare Sobble, consider whether you want to evolve it immediately or keep it as an unevolved collector's Pokémon.

What Is a Perfect Sobble?

A perfect Sobble has an IV combination of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. This produces a 100% IV Pokémon.

100% Sobble
15 / 15 / 15

A perfect Sobble is especially desirable for collectors and players who want a perfect specimen to eventually evolve into Drizzile and Inteleon.

Is 100% Sobble Worth Keeping?

Yes. A perfect Sobble is worth keeping even if you do not immediately plan to evolve or power it up. Evolution, future move changes and collection value can all make a perfect starter useful later.

10 — SHINY

Shiny Sobble

Shiny Sobble is the alternate-color version of Sobble. Its Shiny status is separate from its IVs, level and other attributes.

✨
Shiny Sobble Rare Variant

Shiny Sobble has an alternate coloration and can be evolved while retaining its Shiny identity.

💧
Shiny Evolution Retained

Evolving Shiny Sobble produces Shiny versions of Drizzile and Inteleon.

Shiny Sobble IVs

Shiny status does not guarantee good IVs. A Shiny Sobble can have any legal IV combination, so check the appraisal before deciding whether to evolve, trade or transfer it.

Collector tip:

A Shiny Sobble with strong IVs can be an excellent long-term candidate for evolution into Shiny Inteleon.

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Sobble

Lucky Sobble is obtained through trading. Lucky status is not a separate species or form; it is a special property resulting from a Lucky Trade.

21 — FAQ

Sobble FAQ

What type is Sobble?

Sobble is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Sobble weak to?

Sobble is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

What does Sobble evolve into?

Sobble evolves into Drizzile, which can then evolve into Inteleon.

Is Shiny Sobble available?

Shiny availability can depend on Pokémon GO's current release schedule and events. Your live database should display the current status.

What is 100% Sobble?

A 100% Sobble has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Sobble?

Lucky Sobble is a Sobble that has received Lucky status through a Lucky Trade.

Can Sobble be XXL?

XXL is a size classification that can apply to Pokémon when the game assigns an extra-large size.

Can Sobble be XXS?

Yes, XXS is a size classification representing an especially small Pokémon when applicable.

Is XXL Sobble a different Pokémon?

No. XXL is an attribute of Sobble rather than a separate species or form.

Is XXS Sobble a different Pokémon?

No. XXS is a size attribute and does not create a different species.

Is Shadow Sobble different from regular Sobble?

It is still Sobble and retains its Water typing, but Shadow Pokémon have different battle mechanics.

What is Purified Sobble?

Purified Sobble refers to a Shadow Sobble that has undergone the Purification process.

Can a Lucky Sobble be 100% IV?

Yes. Lucky status and IV quality are separate attributes, so a Lucky Pokémon can also have perfect IVs.

Should I evolve a 100% Sobble?

If you want the perfect evolutionary line, evolving a 100% Sobble into Drizzile and eventually Inteleon is a reasonable long-term collection goal.

Should I keep a Shiny Sobble?

Yes, especially if you collect Shiny Pokémon. You can also evolve it while retaining its Shiny identity.

Is Sobble good for raids?

Sobble itself is generally not a top raid attacker. Its main value comes from its starter status and evolutionary family.

Is Sobble good in PvP?

Sobble is generally a niche PvP choice. Check current league rankings before investing resources.

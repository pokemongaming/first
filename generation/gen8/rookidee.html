Why Rookidee's Typing Matters

Rookidee's pure Flying typing determines both its weaknesses and resistances. It takes increased damage from Electric, Ice and Rock attacks while resisting Ground, Fighting, Bug and Grass attacks.

Shiny Rookidee

Purified Rookidee

Purified Rookidee is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Rookidee. Purification removes the Shadow status and gives the Pokémon the Purified characteristics associated with the mechanic.

🌟

Lucky Rookidee

Lucky Rookidee is an individual Rookidee obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system with the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon have a special background/effect and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Rookidee

A Lucky Rookidee can be especially appealing to collectors or players who intend to evolve the Pokémon while taking advantage of its reduced power-up Stardust requirements.

Lucky Rookidee Tips

  • Lucky status is obtained through trading.
  • Check the IVs after the trade.
  • Consider evolving a Lucky Rookidee into Corvisquire or Corviknight.
  • Take advantage of reduced Stardust costs when powering up a Lucky Pokémon.

XXL Rookidee

XXL Rookidee is a size variant rather than a separate Pokémon species. Pokémon GO assigns individual Pokémon different size values, allowing some Rookidee to qualify as XXL.

XXL Rookidee

Rookidee's XXL size range extends from approximately 0.30 m to 0.35 m according to current Pokémon GO database size information.

Why Keep an XXL Rookidee?

  • XXL Pokémon can be valuable for PokéStop Showcases.
  • XXL Rookidee can be kept as a collection trophy.
  • A large Rookidee can still have excellent IVs.
  • Size and IV quality are separate characteristics.
XXL size should not be confused with high IVs. A Pokémon can be XXL without being a Hundo.

XXS Rookidee

XXS Rookidee is the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size spectrum. It represents an unusually small individual Rookidee and can be valuable for collectors and Showcases where smaller Pokémon are relevant.

XXS Rookidee

Current Pokémon GO size data places Rookidee's XXS range at approximately 0.098 m to 0.10 m.

XXS Rookidee Collection Tips

  • Keep unusual XXS specimens if you collect size variants.
  • Compare the Pokémon's size independently from its IV percentage.
  • Check Showcase requirements before transferring unusual-size Pokémon.

Should You Keep a 100% Rookidee?

Absolutely, especially if you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon or intend to evolve Rookidee through the Corvisquire and Corviknight family.

Rookidee Battle Performance

Rookidee is not primarily a top-tier raid attacker. Its relatively low base stats and early evolutionary stage mean that its main gameplay value is usually as an evolution project, collector Pokémon or Dynamax-capable specimen.

Best Reason to Invest

For most players, the best reason to invest in a Rookidee is to evolve a strong specimen into Corvisquire and eventually Corviknight rather than to build Rookidee itself as a competitive attacker.

Rookidee Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Rookidee uses its own Candy for evolution and investment. Because it has a three-stage family, collecting enough Candy is important if you want to reach Corviknight.

  • Collect 25 Rookidee Candy for Corvisquire.
  • Collect another 100 Candy for Corviknight.
  • Save high-IV Rookidee before evolving.
  • Keep special Shiny, Lucky and Hundo specimens.
  • Consider keeping unusual XXS and XXL specimens.

Rookidee FAQ

What type is Rookidee?

Rookidee is a pure Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Rookidee weak to?

Rookidee is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What generation is Rookidee from?

Rookidee was introduced in Generation 8 and is associated with the Galar region.

What does Rookidee evolve into?

Rookidee evolves into Corvisquire using 25 Candy, and Corvisquire evolves into Corviknight using 100 Candy.

What is Rookidee's max CP?

A perfect Rookidee can reach 753 CP at Level 50.

What is a 100% Rookidee?

A 100% Rookidee has perfect 15/15/15 IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

What is a Lucky Rookidee?

A Lucky Rookidee is an individual Rookidee that received the Lucky status through trading.

What is XXL Rookidee?

XXL Rookidee is an unusually large individual Rookidee. It is a size characteristic rather than a separate species.

What is XXS Rookidee?

XXS Rookidee is an unusually small individual Rookidee. Like XXL, it is a size characteristic rather than a separate species.

Is Shadow Rookidee available?

Current Pokémon GO databases list Shadow Rookidee and its associated Shadow mechanics. Availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I evolve my 100% Rookidee?

If your goal is to maximize the value of the perfect specimen within the Rookidee family, evolving it into Corvisquire and eventually Corviknight is a logical long-term option.

Is Rookidee good for raids?

Rookidee is generally not a top raid attacker. Its primary value comes from its evolution family, collection variants and Dynamax-related mechanics.

Can Rookidee Dynamax?

Dynamax Rookidee is represented in Pokémon GO's Dynamax system and can use Max Moves in appropriate battles.

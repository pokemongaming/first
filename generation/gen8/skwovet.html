Skwovet Overview

Skwovet is a Normal-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Galar region. It was introduced to Pokémon GO in 2021 and later received its Shiny release in 2024. Skwovet can evolve into Greedent using 50 Skwovet Candy.

What Does Normal Typing Mean?

Skwovet is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its most important offensive matchup consideration is that Normal-type attacks are super effective against no Pokémon types in the standard Pokémon GO type system. Defensively, Skwovet is vulnerable to Fighting-type attacks and benefits from resistance to Ghost-type damage.

What Is a Perfect Skwovet?

A Skwovet with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs is a 100% or "hundo" Skwovet. Its IV percentage is 100%, making it the maximum-IV specimen.

Is a 100% Skwovet Useful?

A hundo Skwovet is more valuable as a collection achievement than as a conventional raid investment. It can also become a memorable Pokémon for collectors who enjoy perfect IV specimens, special sizes, Shiny combinations or Dynamax collections.

XXS vs 100% IV

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXS Skwovet can have excellent IVs, poor IVs or anything in between. Therefore, do not transfer an XXS Pokémon simply because its CP or IVs are not perfect.

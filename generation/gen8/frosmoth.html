Frosmoth

Frosmoth is an Ice and Bug-type Pokémon from the Galar region. It evolves from Snom and has a distinctive typing that gives it access to Ice and Bug attacks while also creating a very unusual defensive weakness profile.

Frosmoth FAQ

What type is Frosmoth?

Frosmoth is an Ice and Bug-type Pokémon.

What does Snom evolve into?

Snom evolves into Frosmoth.

Is Shiny Frosmoth different in battle?

Shiny status changes appearance rather than giving automatic combat bonuses.

Is 100% Frosmoth 15/15/15?

Yes. A perfect IV Frosmoth has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Frosmoth have higher stats?

XXL size status does not automatically provide higher combat stats.

Is XXS Frosmoth weaker?

XXS status is primarily related to size and does not automatically make a Pokémon weaker in battle.

Is Lucky Frosmoth good?

Lucky status can make a Pokémon attractive for players planning to invest Stardust into power-ups.

Should I purify Shadow Frosmoth?

Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision. Purifying only for higher IVs may not always be the best choice.

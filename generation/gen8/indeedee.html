Does XXL Increase CP?

No. XXL size itself should not be treated as an IV boost or a direct CP multiplier. IVs, level and the Pokémon GO CP calculation determine CP.

XXS Indeedee

XXS Indeedee is an unusually small Indeedee according to Pokémon GO's size categories. Like XXL, XXS status is primarily a size and collection attribute.

Greedent

Greedent is a Normal-type Pokémon from the Galar region. It evolves from Skwovet and is known in Pokémon GO primarily for its bulky stat profile and its potential usefulness in selected PvP formats.

Greedent Overview

Greedent is the evolved form of Skwovet and belongs to Generation 8's Galar region. Its Normal typing gives it a simple weakness profile, while its high HP makes it more durable than many players initially expect.

Regular Greedent

✨ Shiny Greedent

✨ Shiny Greedent

🌑 Shadow Form

💎 Purified Greedent

Purified Greedent is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Greedent.

💎 Purified Form

Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon back to a standard battle profile.

Purified Greedent Considerations

  • Purification changes the Shadow battle profile.
  • IV changes should be considered before purifying.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified performance first.
  • Do not purify only because the Pokémon appears rare.

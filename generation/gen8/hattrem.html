Hattrem

Hattrem is a Psychic-type Pokémon from the Galar region. It is the middle stage of the Hatenna evolution family and evolves into Hatterene. In Pokémon GO, Hattrem can be interesting for collectors because of its regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variations.

Why Hattrem's Psychic Typing Matters

Hattrem's pure Psychic typing gives it a straightforward type profile. Psychic Pokémon are vulnerable to Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks while resisting Fighting and Psychic damage.

Purified Hattrem

Before Purifying

Lucky Hattrem

Lucky Hattrem is a special Lucky Pokémon obtained through eligible Pokémon GO trades.

🍀 Lucky Hattrem

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making Lucky Hattrem potentially attractive for players planning to evolve and invest in the Hatenna family.

XXS Hattrem

01 — OVERVIEW

Zacian Overview

Zacian is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 8 and associated with the Galar region. Its standard Pokémon GO form is Hero of Many Battles, which is a pure Fairy type.

Zacian also has access to Crowned Sword form. Unlike a normal evolution, Crowned Sword is a special form change that gives Zacian Steel typing, different stats and access to Behemoth Blade.

09 — PVP

Zacian PvP Guide

Zacian has particularly strong relevance in Master League because its high CP ceiling allows it to compete at the top level.

Master League is the most natural competitive format for Zacian. Its high stats, Fairy typing and access to coverage moves give it useful matchups against a wide range of opponents.

Great League

Ultra League

Ultra League can also be restrictive for a standard Zacian because of its high maximum CP. League-specific special formats can change that recommendation.

Shadow Zacian

Shadow Zacian should be treated differently from Pokémon that are actually available through Team GO Rocket. A Shadow version is not a standard Zacian form currently listed in the game's regular Shadow system.

🌑
Shadow Zacian — Not Currently Listed as an Available Form

Do not publish Shadow Zacian as an obtainable Pokémon unless Pokémon GO officially introduces it through Team GO Rocket, Shadow Raids or another official mechanic.

Why This Matters

Shadow Pokémon receive a different offensive and defensive battle profile, and Shadow Pokémon normally come through Team GO Rocket encounters or Shadow Raids. Those mechanics should not be automatically applied to every Legendary Pokémon.

12 — PURIFIED STATUS

Purified Zacian

Purified Zacian is not currently a separate obtainable Zacian form because Purification requires a Pokémon to first exist as a Shadow Pokémon.

💠
Purified Zacian — Not Currently Available

There is no normal Shadow Zacian → Purified Zacian path to document at present.

How Purification Normally Works

When a Pokémon is purified, its appraisal improves, its resource costs are reduced, Frustration is removed and it learns Return. These mechanics apply to eligible Shadow Pokémon rather than automatically creating a Purified version of every Legendary Pokémon.

24 — FAQ

Zacian FAQ

Is Zacian a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Zacian is a Legendary Pokémon from Generation 8.

What type is regular Zacian?

Hero of Many Battles Zacian is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon.

What type is Crowned Sword Zacian?

Crowned Sword Zacian is Fairy/Steel type.

What is Zacian weak to?

Regular Zacian is weak to Poison and Steel attacks. Crowned Sword Zacian is weak to Fire and Ground attacks.

What is Zacian's max CP?

Hero of Many Battles Zacian reaches 4,329 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs. Crowned Sword Zacian reaches 5,629 CP.

Is Zacian good in Master League?

Yes. Zacian has strong Master League potential because of its high CP ceiling, stats, typing and charged-move coverage.

Is Crowned Sword Zacian good for raids?

Yes. Crowned Sword Zacian is particularly valuable as a Steel attacker when using Metal Claw and Behemoth Blade.

What is the best move for Crowned Sword Zacian?

Metal Claw and Behemoth Blade are the recommended Steel-focused raid moves.

Does Zacian evolve?

No. Zacian does not have a normal evolutionary chain. Crowned Sword is a special form change.

What is 100% Zacian?

A 100% Zacian has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Zacian be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a trading-related status rather than a separate Zacian species or form.

What is XXL Zacian?

XXL Zacian is an unusually large Zacian classified with the game's size system.

What is XXS Zacian?

XXS Zacian is an unusually small Zacian classified with the game's size system.

Is Shadow Zacian available?

Shadow Zacian should not be presented as currently obtainable unless Pokémon GO officially introduces it.

Is Purified Zacian available?

Purified Zacian is not currently an obtainable Zacian form because Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon.

Can Shiny Zacian become Crowned Sword Zacian?

Yes. Shiny status and Crowned Sword form are separate properties, allowing a Shiny Zacian to retain its Shiny identity when using the Crowned Sword form.

Is Crowned Sword Zacian the same Pokémon?

It is the same species, Zacian, but in a special form with different typing, stats and moves.

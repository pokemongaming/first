13 • SHADOW ZARUDE

Shadow Zarude

Shadow Zarude is not an available Pokémon GO form. Zarude has not been released as a Shadow Pokémon.

NOT RELEASED

💥 Shadow Zarude

There is currently no legitimate Shadow Zarude encounter in Pokémon GO. Do not treat Shadow Zarude as an obtainable variant.

Why This Matters

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive bonus but also take increased damage and require additional resources. Those mechanics apply to Pokémon that are actually available as Shadow Pokémon. Zarude should not be given fictional Shadow stats or a fictional Rocket encounter.

If Shadow Zarude is officially introduced in the future, update this page with its encounter method, moves, Frustration status, IV floor and Shadow-specific analysis.
14 • PURIFIED ZARUDE

Purified Zarude

A Purified Zarude is not currently obtainable because Zarude is not available as a Shadow Pokémon.

NOT AVAILABLE

💎 Purified Zarude

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying Shadow Pokémon. Since Zarude does not have an available Shadow form, a legitimate Purified Zarude cannot currently be created.

General Purification Mechanics

When an eligible Shadow Pokémon is purified, its appraisal improves, its resource costs are reduced and it learns Return. These mechanics should not be applied to regular Zarude because regular Zarude cannot simply be converted into a Purified Pokémon.

Important: Do not list "Purified Zarude" as a currently obtainable collection form unless Pokémon GO officially introduces Shadow Zarude first.
15 • LUCKY ZARUDE

Lucky Zarude

Lucky Pokémon are normally created through trading. However, Zarude is a Mythical Pokémon and Pokémon GO does not allow Zarude to be traded.

IMPOSSIBLE IN CURRENT GAMEPLAY

🍀 Lucky Zarude

A legitimate Lucky Zarude cannot currently exist in Pokémon GO because Zarude cannot be traded.

Why Zarude Cannot Become Lucky

Lucky Pokémon result from trades. Pokémon GO's trading restrictions specifically exclude Mythical Pokémon such as Zarude from normal player-to-player trading.

Therefore, a "Lucky Zarude" should be treated as an unavailable concept rather than a normal collection variant.

Sirfetch'd

01 · OVERVIEW

Sirfetch'd Overview

Sirfetch'd is the evolved form of Galarian Farfetch'd. Unlike Kantonian Farfetch'd, the Galarian form evolves into Sirfetch'd and belongs to the Fighting type. Its defining visual feature is the large leek it carries into battle, which also forms part of its identity in the Pokémon series.

Why Sirfetch'd's Typing Matters

Sirfetch'd is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon. Fighting attacks are particularly valuable against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark-type targets. Its pure typing also means its defensive matchups are relatively straightforward compared with dual-type Fighting Pokémon.

Evolution Tip

17 · PVP

Sirfetch'd PvP Guide

Sirfetch'd can be interesting in PvP because of its Fighting typing, high Attack and access to charged move pressure.

Great League

Great League performance depends heavily on CP, IV distribution, moveset and the current Cup or Open Great League metagame. A lower-Attack IV spread can sometimes be desirable in CP-limited leagues because it can allow a Pokémon to reach a higher level before hitting the CP cap.

Ultra League

Sirfetch'd can also be evaluated for Ultra League, particularly when its moves provide useful pressure against the current meta.

Master League

Sirfetch'd's maximum CP makes Master League a less natural environment than Great or Ultra League. Current rankings should always be checked before recommending it as a Master League investment.

PvP IV Reminder

The best IV spread for PvP is not necessarily 15/15/15. Use a league-specific PvP ranking tool when optimizing a Sirfetch'd for CP-limited formats.

24 · FAQ

Sirfetch'd FAQ

What type is Sirfetch'd?

Sirfetch'd is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Sirfetch'd weak against?

Sirfetch'd is weak to Flying, Psychic and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Sirfetch'd evolve?

Sirfetch'd is the evolution of Galarian Farfetch'd. Sirfetch'd itself does not evolve further.

How do you evolve Galarian Farfetch'd into Sirfetch'd?

Galarian Farfetch'd has a special evolution requirement involving successful Excellent Throws. Check the current in-game evolution requirement before evolving.

Is Shiny Sirfetch'd available?

Shiny Sirfetch'd can exist as the evolved form of Shiny Galarian Farfetch'd when the Shiny species is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is 100% Sirfetch'd?

100% Sirfetch'd has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Sirfetch'd?

Lucky Sirfetch'd is a Sirfetch'd that obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Does Lucky Sirfetch'd have better IVs?

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, but becoming Lucky does not guarantee perfect IVs.

What is XXL Sirfetch'd?

XXL Sirfetch'd is an extra-large Sirfetch'd based on Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Sirfetch'd?

XXS Sirfetch'd is an extra-small Sirfetch'd based on Pokémon GO's size system.

Does XXL Sirfetch'd have higher Attack?

Size should not be confused with IVs or base stats. Evaluate Sirfetch'd's actual battle statistics separately from its size category.

Is Shadow Sirfetch'd available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotation. Check current game data before claiming that Shadow Sirfetch'd is obtainable.

What is Purified Sirfetch'd?

Purified Sirfetch'd would refer to a Sirfetch'd that was previously Shadow and subsequently purified, assuming the relevant Shadow species is eligible under the current game mechanics.

Is Sirfetch'd good for raids?

Sirfetch'd can contribute Fighting-type damage, although its raid value should be compared with the current pool of Fighting attackers and available movesets.

Is Sirfetch'd good in PvP?

Sirfetch'd can be useful in PvP depending on league, moveset, IV distribution and the current metagame.

Should I keep a 100% Sirfetch'd?

Yes, a 100% Sirfetch'd is a perfect-IV specimen and can be valuable for collection or long-term investment.

Can Shiny Sirfetch'd be Lucky?

Pokémon statuses such as Shiny and Lucky can coexist. A Shiny Sirfetch'd obtained through a qualifying trade can therefore be a particularly desirable collection piece.

Can a Lucky Sirfetch'd be 100% IV?

Yes. Lucky Pokémon have an IV floor but are not automatically perfect. A Lucky Sirfetch'd can potentially have 15/15/15 IVs.

What is the best Sirfetch'd IV?

For general maximum-stat purposes, 15/15/15 is the perfect IV combination. PvP can use different IV spreads depending on the league and desired stat product.

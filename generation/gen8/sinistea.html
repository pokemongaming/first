01 • OVERVIEW

Sinistea Overview

Sinistea is a Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 8. It is Pokémon #854 in the National Pokédex and evolves into Polteageist.

Unlike many Pokémon with straightforward alternate appearances, Sinistea is particularly interesting because Pokémon GO includes its Phony and Antique forms. These forms share the same basic battle statistics but have different collection value.

Why Sinistea's Typing Matters

Sinistea's Ghost typing gives it access to useful Ghost-type offensive pressure and several useful resistances. However, its relatively low overall battle stats limit its usefulness in high-level raids and standard open-league PvP.

09 • PVP

Sinistea PvP Guide

Sinistea's low maximum CP means it is not a natural Great, Ultra or Master League investment. Its more interesting PvP possibilities come from special formats such as Little Cup when eligible.

Great League

Sinistea reaches only 1,287 CP at Level 50, leaving it well below the Great League CP ceiling. Its defensive profile also makes it difficult to justify as a standard Great League choice.

Ultra League

Sinistea's maximum CP is far too low for a practical Ultra League build.

Master League

Sinistea is not suitable for standard Master League competition because its maximum CP and stats are significantly below the requirements of the format.

Little Cup

When eligible, Little Cup can be the most interesting competitive environment to investigate for Sinistea. Cup rules, species eligibility and current moves should always be checked before building one specifically for the format.

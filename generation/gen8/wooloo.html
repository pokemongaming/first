Wooloo

Wooloo is a Normal-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Galar region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a collectible early-stage Pokémon, a Dynamax option and a popular target for Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, XXL and XXS collectors.

01 • OVERVIEW

Wooloo Overview

Wooloo is a Normal-type Pokémon from Generation 8 and the Galar region. It evolves into Dubwool using Wooloo Candy, but the unevolved Wooloo itself is also popular with collectors because of its Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV possibilities.

Why Wooloo's Typing Matters

Wooloo is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its biggest type-related disadvantage is its weakness to Fighting-type attacks. In return, Wooloo takes reduced damage from Ghost-type attacks.

09 • PVP

Wooloo PvP Guide

Great League

Wooloo's maximum CP is only 798, so it does not naturally reach the Great League's 1500 CP limit. It is therefore not a standard Great League pick.

Ultra League

Wooloo's maximum CP is far below the Ultra League limit. It is not a conventional Ultra League Pokémon.

Master League

Wooloo is not suitable for the Master League because its maximum CP is substantially below the format's usual competitive range.

Little Cup

Little Cup formats can sometimes create opportunities for unevolved Pokémon, but Wooloo has generally not been a major Little Cup meta choice.

24 • FAQ

Wooloo FAQ

What type is Wooloo?

Wooloo is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Wooloo weak to?

Wooloo is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What does Wooloo evolve into?

Wooloo evolves into Dubwool using 50 Wooloo Candy.

What is Wooloo's max CP?

Wooloo reaches 798 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs and 706 CP at Level 40.

Is Shiny Wooloo available?

Yes. Shiny Wooloo is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Wooloo available?

Current database information lists Shadow Wooloo as unavailable.

Can Wooloo be Purified?

There is currently no normal Purified Wooloo acquisition path because Shadow Wooloo is not currently available.

Can Wooloo be Lucky?

Yes. Wooloo can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Wooloo?

A 100% Wooloo is a Hundo with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Wooloo be XXL?

Yes. Wooloo can have XXL size characteristics.

Can Wooloo be XXS?

Yes. Wooloo can have XXS size characteristics.

Can Wooloo Dynamax?

Yes. Dynamax Wooloo is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Wooloo good for raids?

Wooloo is not a strong conventional raid attacker. Its primary value is collection, evolution and Dynamax-related gameplay.

Is Wooloo good in PvP?

Wooloo's low maximum CP makes it unsuitable for the main open PvP leagues. Limited formats can change the answer depending on their rules.

Should I evolve my Shiny Wooloo?

Only if you want a Shiny Dubwool. If you specifically collect Wooloo, you may prefer keeping at least one Shiny Wooloo unevolved.

Should I keep a Hundo Wooloo?

Yes, especially if you collect perfect IV Pokémon or want a perfect Dubwool later.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Wooloo?

Yes if you care about Pokémon GO's size system, Showcases or collection goals. Rare size combinations can be particularly desirable.

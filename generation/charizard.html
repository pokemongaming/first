Charizard Overview

Charizard is the final evolution of Charmander and Charmeleon. It is a dual Fire/Flying-type Pokémon and has access to two Mega Evolutions: Mega Charizard X and Mega Charizard Y.

10 • PVP

Charizard PvP Guide

Charizard has had a strong PvP identity thanks to its access to efficient charged moves, Dragon Claw and powerful Fire attacks.

Great League

Charizard can be relevant in Great League when available within the appropriate CP range and when its moveset fits the current metagame.

Ultra League

Ultra League can be one of Charizard's most attractive PvP environments because its CP and stat profile fit the league naturally.

Master League

Regular Charizard is less naturally suited to the Master League than many Legendary Pokémon, although special formats can change its usefulness.

PvP Investment

For PvP, look beyond the simple 15/15/15 rule. League-specific IV spreads, moveset, XL Candy and current meta matchups should guide your investment.

15 • MEGA ENERGY

Charizard Mega Energy

Charizard uses Mega Energy to access its Mega forms. Mega Charizard X and Mega Charizard Y are separate Mega forms of the same species.

RESOURCE Charizard Mega Energy

Mega Energy can be earned through eligible Mega Raids, research, walking and other Pokémon GO mechanics when available.

Mega Evolution Strategy

  • Build Mega Energy before an important raid rotation.
  • Choose Mega Charizard X when its Fire/Dragon identity fits your team.
  • Choose Mega Charizard Y for a Fire/Flying Mega role.
  • Use a strong high-IV Charizard as your long-term Mega candidate when practical.
  • Remember that Mega Evolution is temporary.

18 • RESOURCES

Charizard Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building multiple Charizard variants can require a substantial amount of Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Charizard has regular, Shadow and Mega roles, resource planning is particularly important.

  • Save Charmander Candy for evolution and power-ups.
  • Collect Candy XL for high-level PvP and PvE investments.
  • Prioritize Blast Burn when building a Fire attacker.
  • Consider Shadow Charizard separately from regular Charizard.
  • Keep strong IV specimens for Mega Evolution projects.
  • Save Mega Energy for important raid rotations.
  • Do not spend Elite TMs without checking current move value.

20 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Charizard

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Fire.
  • Check whether Charizard should use Fire or Flying attacks.
  • Confirm the Charizard's level and moveset.
  • Check current weather.
  • Compare regular Charizard with Mega Charizard Y.
  • Consider Mega Charizard X if Dragon damage is relevant.

Fire-Focused Strategy

When Fire damage is the objective, Fire Spin and Blast Burn are the most important tools for a conventional Charizard build. Mega Charizard Y is especially valuable when you want maximum Fire-type raid pressure.

PvP Strategy

PvP Charizard is a different project from raid Charizard. Dragon Claw can create shield pressure while Fire-type charged moves punish targets vulnerable to Fire.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Charizard can serve as a flexible long-term Pokémon because the same species can be used for PvP, regular PvE, Shadow investment and Mega Evolution.

23 • FAQ

Charizard FAQ

What type is Charizard?

Charizard is a Fire/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Charizard weak to?

Charizard is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock is especially important because of the Fire/Flying typing.

What is Charizard's Max CP?

Charizard reaches 2,889 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Charizard's best raid moveset?

Fire Spin with Blast Burn is the recommended Fire-focused raid combination.

Is Blast Burn a legacy move?

Blast Burn has been associated with special event availability and may require an Elite Charged TM when not currently obtainable.

Is Charizard good for raids?

Yes. Charizard can be useful as a Fire attacker, while its Mega forms provide substantially greater raid utility.

Is Charizard good in PvP?

Yes. Charizard can be useful in CP-limited PvP formats, particularly when its current moveset fits the meta.

Can Charizard be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Charizard is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolutions.

Can Shiny Charizard Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Charizard can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolving.

Does Charizard have two Mega Evolutions?

Yes. Charizard has Mega Charizard X and Mega Charizard Y.

What type is Mega Charizard X?

Mega Charizard X is Fire/Dragon type.

What type is Mega Charizard Y?

Mega Charizard Y is Fire/Flying type.

What is Mega Charizard X's Max CP?

Mega Charizard X reaches approximately 4,352 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Mega Charizard Y's Max CP?

Mega Charizard Y reaches approximately 5,037 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Which is better: Mega Charizard X or Y?

For a Fire-focused raid role, Mega Charizard Y is generally the more natural choice because of its very high Attack. Mega Charizard X provides a different Fire/Dragon typing and can be useful in situations where that identity matters.

Is Shadow Charizard good?

Shadow Charizard can be a strong damage-focused attacker because Shadow Pokémon deal increased damage while suffering reduced defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Charizard?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow's offensive value with the benefits you would gain from purification before making the permanent decision.

Can Shadow Charizard Mega Evolve?

Mega Evolution and Shadow status have separate mechanics. Check current Pokémon GO rules before treating Shadow Charizard as a Mega Evolution candidate.

How do I evolve Charmander into Charizard?

Charmander evolves into Charmeleon using Candy, and Charmeleon evolves into Charizard using additional Candy.

Is Charizard a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Charizard is a regular Pokémon from the Charmander evolution family.

Which Charizard is best for Fire raids?

Mega Charizard Y is the form to prioritize when you want a high-powered Fire-focused Mega attacker.

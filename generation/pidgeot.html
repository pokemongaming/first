Pidgeot Overview

Pidgeot is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Pidgeotto and represents the final stage of the Pidgey evolutionary family. Pidgeot can also Mega Evolve into Mega Pidgeot.

Why Pidgeot's Typing Matters

Pidgeot's Normal/Flying typing gives it a useful combination of Flying-type offensive options and several defensive resistances. It is especially important to remember that its typing makes it particularly vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

09 • PVP

Pidgeot PvP Guide

PvP is one of Pidgeot's strongest reasons to exist in a Pokémon GO collection. Its Flying typing, debuff-oriented moves and Brave Bird pressure give it a distinct role in eligible leagues.

Great League

Pidgeot can be effective in Great League formats where its Flying typing and debuff pressure are useful. However, exact rankings can change as the move pool and PvP metagame change.

Ultra League

Ultra League can be particularly interesting for Pidgeot because its CP ceiling allows it to reach useful levels while retaining a strong Flying-oriented moveset.

Master League

Regular Pidgeot generally has a much harder time competing in unrestricted Master League because its maximum CP is well below the level of many Legendary and pseudo-Legendary competitors.

PvP Moveset

Gust + Feather Dance + Brave Bird is a strong starting point for PvP-focused Pidgeot builds when those moves are available under current game rules.

16 • RESOURCES

Pidgeot Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Pidgeot can require a meaningful amount of Stardust and Candy XL, particularly for PvP projects. Mega Evolution also introduces Mega Energy requirements.

  • Keep high-quality Pidgey and Pidgeotto candidates before evolving.
  • Evaluate PvP IVs before transferring unusual IV spreads.
  • Save Candy for important evolution and power-up projects.
  • Collect Candy XL for Level 40+ investment.
  • Check moves before spending Elite TMs.
  • Compare regular and Shadow performance before purifying.
  • Save useful Pidgeot Mega Energy for Mega projects.
17 • AVAILABILITY

Pidgeot Availability

Pidgeot itself may be available through evolution from Pidgeotto, while Pidgey and its earlier evolutionary forms can appear through different Pokémon GO encounter sources depending on the current season, events and biome-related mechanics.

Current Availability

Encounter availability can change. Check the current Pokémon GO event and wild encounter rotation before publishing a live availability statement.

Mega Pidgeot Availability

Mega Pidgeot availability depends on Mega Raids, Mega Energy and the current Mega Evolution rotation. Once sufficient Mega Energy has been obtained, players can use eligible Pidgeot according to current Mega Evolution rules.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Pidgeot

Before PvP

  • Check the league CP limit.
  • Evaluate Pidgeot's PvP IV spread.
  • Confirm the current recommended moveset.
  • Consider whether Shadow Pidgeot offers better pressure.
  • Check the current PvP meta before investing Stardust.

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Flying damage is useful against the raid boss.
  • Consider Mega Pidgeot instead of regular Pidgeot.
  • Check the weather bonus.
  • Compare Pidgeot with stronger Flying attackers.

Long-Term Investment

For most players, Pidgeot's strongest long-term appeal comes from PvP and Mega Evolution rather than regular raid damage. A good PvP specimen can justify investment even when a perfect IV Pidgeot is not available.

20 • FAQ

Pidgeot FAQ

What type is Pidgeot?

Pidgeot is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Pidgeot weak to?

Pidgeot is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

What is Pidgeot's Max CP?

Pidgeot reaches 2,405 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Pidgeot good for raids?

Regular Pidgeot is not normally a top-tier raid attacker, but Mega Pidgeot provides considerably more PvE value.

Is Pidgeot good for PvP?

Yes. Pidgeot can be useful in PvP, particularly in formats where its Flying typing and debuff-oriented moveset are effective.

What is Pidgeot's best PvP moveset?

Gust with Feather Dance and Brave Bird is a strong PvP-oriented combination when those moves are available.

Does Pidgeot evolve?

Pidgeot is the final evolution of Pidgey and Pidgeotto. It can also undergo Mega Evolution.

What does Pidgeot evolve from?

Pidgeot evolves from Pidgeotto.

What is Pidgeot's evolution line?

Pidgey evolves into Pidgeotto, which then evolves into Pidgeot.

Can Pidgeot be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Pidgeot is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

Can Shiny Pidgeot Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Pidgeot can Mega Evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.

Is Shadow Pidgeot available?

Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket encounters and Pokémon GO rotations.

Is Shadow Pidgeot stronger than regular Pidgeot?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on your intended use.

Should I purify Shadow Pidgeot?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, moves and intended PvP or battle role first.

What is Mega Pidgeot?

Mega Pidgeot is the temporary Mega Evolution of Pidgeot.

What type is Mega Pidgeot?

Mega Pidgeot remains a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Mega Pidgeot's Max CP?

Mega Pidgeot can reach approximately 3,681 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Mega Pidgeot good for raids?

Yes. Mega Pidgeot can provide useful Flying-type damage and Mega team bonuses in suitable raid battles.

Can Pidgeot Mega Evolve permanently?

No. Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation under the Pokémon GO Mega Evolution system.

Can Shadow Pidgeot Mega Evolve?

Mega Evolution eligibility depends on current Pokémon GO rules. Shadow Pokémon should not automatically be assumed to be eligible for every special transformation.

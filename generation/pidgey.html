08 • IV GUIDE

Pidgey IV Guide

The best Pidgey IV depends on what you intend to do with it. For general collection and evolution, a high-IV specimen is a useful choice. PvP IVs can be different depending on the league and final evolution.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect IV Pidgey

A 15/15/15 Pidgey is the traditional perfect-IV specimen. However, do not automatically transfer lower-IV Pidgey if you plan to use them for research, trades, evolution or special collection goals.

PvP IV note:

For PvP, the ideal IV combination can differ from 15/15/15 because CP limits favor different Attack, Defense and HP distributions.

09 • PVP

Pidgey PvP Guide

Pidgey is not normally a competitive choice in the standard Pokémon GO leagues. Its low stats and low maximum CP make its evolved forms considerably more interesting.

Great League

Pidgey can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but its overall performance is generally limited compared with stronger Pokémon.

Ultra League

Pidgey's low CP ceiling makes it unsuitable for normal Ultra League competition.

Master League

Pidgey does not have the stats or CP necessary to compete meaningfully in Master League.

PvP Investment Advice

If your goal is PvP, consider keeping useful IV Pidgey for evolution into Pidgeot rather than investing heavily in Pidgey itself.

Shadow Pidgey and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can normally have Frustration as their Charged Attack. The ability to replace Frustration depends on Team GO Rocket events and current Pokémon GO rules.

Purification warning:

Purification is a permanent decision. If you are keeping Shadow Pidgey for collection or evolution, compare its Shadow and purified versions before purifying it.

Shadow Pidgey for Raids

Although Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive boost, Pidgey's low base stats mean that Shadow Pidgey is still not a practical high-level raid attacker. For players interested in the evolutionary family, the more important question is whether the Shadow Pidgey should eventually be evolved into Shadow Pidgeot.

Shiny Evolution

Shadow Evolution

A Shadow Pidgey can also be evolved while remaining a Shadow Pokémon. If you want Shadow Pidgeot, avoid purifying the Pidgey first.

14 • RESOURCES

Pidgey Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Pidgey Candy is most useful for evolving the Pidgey family. Because Pidgey itself has limited battle utility, powering it up should generally be a lower priority than evolving it or investing in stronger Pokémon.

  • Save enough Pidgey Candy to complete the evolution family.
  • Check IVs before evolving important specimens.
  • Keep Shiny Pidgey if you are building a Shiny collection.
  • Consider keeping Shadow Pidgey before purification.
  • Use Lucky Pokémon opportunities when making large investments.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust on Pidgey without a specific goal.
15 • AVAILABILITY

Pidgey Availability

Pidgey is a common Generation 1 Pokémon and has appeared in Pokémon GO through normal wild encounters, events and other encounter methods over the game's history. Availability can change with seasons and events.

Shiny Availability

Shiny Pidgey is available when Shiny Pidgey encounters are enabled in Pokémon GO.

Shadow Availability

Shadow Pidgey availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and current game events. Availability should be checked against the current Rocket lineup.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Pidgey

For New Players

  • Catch Pidgey whenever you need Candy for its evolution family.
  • Check IVs before transferring high-quality specimens.
  • Save Shiny Pidgey for collection or evolution.
  • Keep Shadow Pidgey if you want a Shadow Pidgeot later.
  • Do not prioritize Stardust investment in Pidgey.

For Collectors

Pidgey can be useful as a collection target because regular, Shiny and Shadow versions can be collected and evolved through the Pidgey family.

For PvP Players

Players interested in PvP should generally evaluate Pidgey as an evolution candidate rather than as a standalone battler. Pidgeot is substantially more relevant to competitive play.

20 • FAQ

Pidgey FAQ

Is Pidgey a Generation 1 Pokémon?

Yes. Pidgey was introduced in Generation 1 and is Pokédex number #016.

What type is Pidgey?

Pidgey is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Pidgey weak to?

Pidgey is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

What is Pidgey's Max CP?

Pidgey has a relatively low maximum CP compared with its evolved forms.

Is Pidgey good for raids?

No. Pidgey is not considered a practical raid attacker. Its evolved form, Pidgeot, is much more useful.

Is Pidgey good in PvP?

Pidgey has limited PvP usefulness because of its low overall stats and CP ceiling.

How do you evolve Pidgey?

Pidgey evolves into Pidgeotto and can eventually be evolved into Pidgeot using Pidgey Candy.

How much Candy does Pidgey need to evolve?

The first evolution requires Pidgey Candy, while the second evolution requires additional Pidgey Candy. Check the current in-game evolution screen for the exact requirement.

Can Pidgey be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Pidgey is available when enabled through Pokémon GO's encounter system.

Can Shiny Pidgey evolve?

Yes. Shiny Pidgey can evolve into Shiny Pidgeotto and then Shiny Pidgeot.

Is Shadow Pidgey available?

Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Does Shadow Pidgey stay Shadow when evolved?

Yes. A Shadow Pidgey can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status.

Should I purify Shadow Pidgey?

Not automatically. Purification is permanent, so consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow Pokémon before making the decision.

What is Pidgey's evolution family?

Pidgey evolves into Pidgeotto and then Pidgeot.

Is Pidgey useful for collecting?

Yes. Regular, Shiny and Shadow Pidgey can all be valuable collection targets.

Should I transfer low IV Pidgey?

Before transferring, check whether the Pokémon is Shiny, Shadow, useful for a special collection goal, or suitable for evolution and PvP.

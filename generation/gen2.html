FAQ

Generation 2 Pokémon FAQ

How many Pokémon were introduced in Generation 2?

Generation II introduced 100 new Pokémon, numbered #152 through #251 in the National Pokédex.

Which region is Generation 2 from?

Generation II introduced the Johto region.

What are the Gen 2 starter Pokémon?

Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile are the three Johto starters.

Who are the Johto Legendary Pokémon?

Raikou, Entei, Suicune, Lugia and Ho-Oh are the major Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation II. Celebi is the Mythical Pokémon of the generation.

Which Pokémon is #251?

Celebi is National Pokédex #251 and is the final Pokémon introduced in Generation II.

Did Generation 2 introduce new Pokémon types?

Yes. Dark and Steel were introduced in Generation II.

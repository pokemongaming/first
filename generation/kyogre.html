Why Kyogre's Typing Matters

Kyogre's pure Water typing gives it a focused offensive identity. Water attacks are particularly effective against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Kyogre's defensive profile gives it useful resistances to several common attack types.

09 • PVP

Kyogre PvP Guide

Kyogre's high CP ceiling and strong Water typing make it particularly relevant in Master League. Its value depends on the current moveset and metagame.

Master League

Master League is the natural PvP environment for high-CP Kyogre. Its combination of strong stats, Water typing and powerful charged attacks allows it to pressure several important Pokémon.

Great League & Ultra League

Kyogre's very high CP makes it a poor natural fit for the lower CP leagues. League rules and special cups should always be checked before recommending it.

PvP Investment

For Master League, prioritize a Kyogre with suitable IVs, sufficient XL Candy and a current competitive moveset.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Kyogre

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Water.
  • Confirm Kyogre has a Water-focused moveset.
  • Check Kyogre's level and IVs.
  • Consider current weather.
  • Compare Kyogre with your other Water attackers.
  • Consider whether Primal Kyogre would provide more value.

During Team Building

Kyogre should not automatically occupy every Water-type slot. Compare damage, survivability, moveset, weather boosts and Mega or Primal team utility for the specific raid.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Kyogre can remain useful across many raid matchups and can also support Master League projects. That makes a high-quality specimen a potentially valuable long-term investment.

Kyogre FAQ

Is Kyogre a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Kyogre is a Legendary Pokémon from Generation 3.

Regular Kyogre is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

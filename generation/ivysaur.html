Ivysaur

Ivysaur is a Grass- and Poison-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the evolved form of Bulbasaur. In Pokémon GO, Ivysaur is primarily useful as an evolution step toward Venusaur, while Shadow and Shiny variants give collectors and battlers additional reasons to keep strong specimens.

09 • PVP

Ivysaur PvP Guide

Unlike many middle-stage evolutions, Ivysaur can have some limited relevance in lower CP battles because its maximum CP remains below the Ultra and Master League thresholds.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to consider Ivysaur itself. Its Grass/Poison typing and access to Vine Whip, Power Whip and Sludge Bomb can provide useful coverage in suitable cups or limited metas.

Ultra League

Ivysaur can technically reach the Ultra League CP threshold, but it is generally overshadowed by stronger Grass and Poison options.

Master League

Ivysaur's CP ceiling makes Master League unsuitable. Venusaur is considerably more relevant for higher-level battle applications.

PvP Starting Point

Vine Whip + Power Whip + Sludge Bomb

🌑 Shadow Mechanics

Shadow Pokémon deal more damage but also receive more damage. Pokémon GO's official Shadow guidance describes the Shadow damage modifier as 20% more damage dealt and 20% more damage received.

Shadow Ivysaur and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon normally have Frustration as their Charged Attack. During certain Team GO Rocket events, Trainers can use a Charged TM to remove Frustration.

Important:

If you are planning to evolve a Shadow Ivysaur into Shadow Venusaur for an event-exclusive move, pay close attention to the order of removing Frustration and evolving. Exclusive evolution moves can have special requirements.

Should You Purify Shadow Ivysaur?

Do not purify automatically. Purification is permanent, and removing the Shadow status also removes the Shadow damage advantage. If your goal is to eventually build a Shadow Venusaur, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be more appropriate.

Shadow Ivysaur Availability

Shadow Ivysaur has been available in Pokémon GO through Shadow Pokémon mechanics. Availability can rotate with Team GO Rocket content and events, so current Rocket encounters should be checked before publishing a live availability claim.

15 • RESOURCES

Ivysaur Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Ivysaur itself is relatively inexpensive to evolve, but building the resulting Venusaur to a high level can require significant Stardust and Candy XL.

  • Save Bulbasaur-family Candy for important evolution projects.
  • Collect Candy XL if you plan a Level 40+ Venusaur.
  • Check the final Venusaur moveset before investing heavily.
  • Compare regular and Shadow Venusaur before purifying.
  • Keep strong IV specimens for long-term evolution projects.
  • Keep interesting Shiny specimens for collection goals.

What Should You Invest In?

For most players, the better long-term investment is a strong Venusaur rather than Ivysaur. Ivysaur can still be worth keeping for Great League experiments, special cups, Shiny collections or Shadow evolution projects.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Ivysaur

Before Investing

  • Decide whether you want Ivysaur or Venusaur.
  • Check the Pokémon's IVs.
  • Check whether it is Regular, Shiny or Shadow.
  • Check the current PvP meta if using Ivysaur directly.
  • Consider saving Stardust for Venusaur.
  • Check current event moves before evolving.

For Great League

If you are specifically building Ivysaur for Great League, evaluate its IV spread and current moveset rather than automatically evolving it. A lower-CP Pokémon can sometimes have more favorable PvP IVs than a perfect specimen.

For Raids

Ivysaur is generally not the preferred investment for raids. If the objective is Grass-type PvE damage, evolve toward Venusaur or consider stronger Grass attackers.

For Collection

Shiny Ivysaur and Shiny Shadow Ivysaur can be especially valuable to collectors. If you enjoy completing form collections, keep notable specimens before evolving or transferring them.

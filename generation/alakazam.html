Why Alakazam's Typing Matters

Psychic attacks are especially useful against Fighting and Poison-type opponents. Alakazam's pure Psychic typing also gives it useful resistance to Fighting and Psychic attacks, although its relatively low bulk means that avoiding super-effective damage is important.

09 • PVP

Alakazam PvP Guide

Alakazam's very high Attack and comparatively low bulk create a glass-cannon profile. It can apply strong pressure but requires careful shield and switch management.

Great League

Alakazam can be used in special formats or limited cups when eligible, but its low durability makes it a niche choice rather than a universally safe pick.

Ultra League

Its higher CP ceiling allows Alakazam to participate more naturally in Ultra League than Great League, although its fragility remains an important limitation.

Master League

Alakazam is generally more difficult to justify in open Master League because its bulk and overall matchup profile can leave it vulnerable to stronger meta options.

PvP Investment

Before building a PvP Alakazam, check the current moveset, cup rules and PvP rankings rather than relying solely on its high Attack stat.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Alakazam

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Psychic.
  • Confirm Alakazam has a Psychic-focused moveset.
  • Check Alakazam's level and IVs.
  • Consider the raid boss's moves and damage output.
  • Compare Alakazam with your other Psychic attackers.
  • Consider whether Mega Alakazam would provide more team value.

During Team Building

Alakazam should not automatically occupy every Psychic slot. Compare damage, survivability, Mega bonuses, movesets and weather effects for the specific battle.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Alakazam can be useful as a Psychic attacker, while a particularly good specimen can also become a Mega Evolution project. Shadow specimens should be evaluated separately because of their different battle mechanics.

20 • FAQ

Alakazam FAQ

Is Alakazam a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Alakazam is a non-Legendary Pokémon from Generation 1.

What type is Alakazam?

Alakazam is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Alakazam weak to?

Alakazam is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

What is Alakazam's Max CP?

Alakazam reaches 3,627 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Alakazam's best raid moveset?

Confusion with Psychic is a straightforward Psychic-focused moveset for PvE use.

Is Alakazam good for raids?

Yes. Its high Attack makes it a strong Psychic-type offensive option, although its low bulk can limit its durability.

Is Alakazam good in PvP?

Alakazam can be useful in certain PvP formats, but its low durability makes it a relatively niche choice.

What Pokémon evolve into Alakazam?

Abra evolves into Kadabra, and Kadabra evolves into Alakazam.

Can Abra evolve into Shiny Alakazam?

Yes. A Shiny Abra retains its Shiny status through Kadabra and Alakazam.

Is Shiny Alakazam available?

Yes. Shiny Alakazam is available when its Shiny evolution family is obtainable in Pokémon GO.

What is Shadow Alakazam?

Shadow Alakazam is the Shadow variant of Alakazam. It remains a pure Psychic type and receives the standard Shadow battle effects.

Is Shadow Alakazam good for raids?

Shadow Alakazam can provide strong Psychic-type damage, although it sacrifices some durability.

Should I purify Shadow Alakazam?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified version and consider your raid, PvP and collection goals.

What is Mega Alakazam?

Mega Alakazam is Alakazam's temporary Mega Evolution with significantly increased battle stats.

What type is Mega Alakazam?

Mega Alakazam remains a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Mega Alakazam's Max CP?

Mega Alakazam reaches a much higher CP ceiling than regular Alakazam while Mega Evolved.

Is Mega Alakazam good for raids?

Yes. Mega Alakazam is a powerful Psychic-focused Mega attacker and can provide additional team utility.

Can Shiny Alakazam become Mega Alakazam?

Yes. A Shiny Alakazam can retain its Shiny status when Mega Evolved.

Does Alakazam have an evolution?

Alakazam is the final evolution of the Abra family. It can also Mega Evolve when Mega Evolution is available.

How do I get Alakazam in Pokémon GO?

Alakazam can be obtained by evolving Kadabra. Check current Pokémon GO mechanics for the applicable Candy and trade-evolution requirements.

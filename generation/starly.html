08 • IV GUIDE

Starly IV Guide

The ideal Starly IV depends on what you plan to do with it. A perfect 15/15/15 specimen is useful for Pokédex completion and evolution projects, while PvP IV priorities can be different.

15 Attack IV

Perfect Starly

A 15/15/15 Starly is a useful collection target, especially if you want a perfect entry in the Starly family or plan to evolve it into a perfect Staraptor.

PvP IV Considerations

For capped PvP formats, the mathematically best IV spread is not necessarily 15/15/15. Lower Attack and higher Defense/HP combinations can sometimes produce better stat products under a CP limit.

IV advice:

Do not transfer a Shadow, Shiny or high-IV Starly without first checking whether it has collection, evolution or PvP value.

09 • PVP

Starly PvP Guide

Starly is generally not a major competitive PvP Pokémon. Its low overall stats and CP ceiling make Staravia and especially Staraptor more relevant members of the family for battle purposes.

Great League

Starly can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but its overall performance is limited. It is generally chosen for specialty challenges, restricted formats or personal preference rather than as a standard meta pick.

Ultra League

Starly's low CP ceiling prevents it from being a natural Ultra League Pokémon.

Master League

Starly is not suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the level required for the format.

PvP Investment

If your goal is competitive PvP, consider saving resources for a later evolution in the Starly family rather than heavily investing in Starly itself.

13 • EVOLUTION

Starly Evolution — Staravia & Staraptor

Starly is the first stage of a three-stage evolution family. It evolves into Staravia and then into Staraptor.

Starly #396
→
Staravia #397
→
Staraptor #398

Starly to Staravia

Starly evolves into Staravia using Pokémon GO Candy. Staravia is the middle stage and has stronger combat stats than Starly.

Staravia to Staraptor

Staravia can be evolved further into Staraptor with additional Starly Candy. Staraptor is the final evolution and is considerably more relevant for battle than Starly.

Evolution advice:

If you have limited Candy, prioritize evolving a strong IV, Shiny or Shadow Starly that fits your long-term goal rather than spending resources on multiple weak specimens.

Choosing Which Starly to Evolve

Best Regular Starly

For a general evolution project, prioritize a Starly with strong overall IVs, good level and enough Candy to complete the evolution line.

Starly FAQ

Is Starly a Pokémon from Generation 4?

Yes. Starly was introduced in Generation 4 and is associated with the Sinnoh region.

What type is Starly?

Starly is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Starly weak to?

Starly is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

What is Starly's Pokédex number?

Starly is Pokédex number #396.

What is Starly's Max CP?

Starly has a maximum CP of about 719 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Does Starly evolve?

Yes. Starly evolves into Staravia and then Staraptor.

How much Candy does Starly need to evolve?

Starly requires 25 Candy to evolve into Staravia, while Staravia requires additional Candy to evolve into Staraptor.

Is Shiny Starly available?

Yes. Shiny Starly is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Starly available?

Shadow Starly can be available through Team GO Rocket encounters when the relevant game rotation offers it.

Is Shadow Starly stronger than regular Starly?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. However, Starly itself remains a low-CP Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Starly?

Do not purify automatically. Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow form or eventually evolve it before making a permanent decision.

Is Starly good for raids?

Starly is generally not a strong raid attacker. Its main value comes from its evolution family and collection potential.

Is Starly good in PvP?

Starly has limited competitive PvP value. Its later evolution, Staraptor, is more relevant for battle.

What are Starly's best moves?

Starly's move options can change with game updates. For most players, the more important goal is choosing a good Starly to evolve into the later stages.

Should I keep a 100% Starly?

Yes. A perfect Starly can be useful as a collection specimen or as the basis for a perfect evolution project.

Should I evolve Shiny Starly?

That depends on your collection goals. You can keep Shiny Starly itself or evolve it into Shiny Staravia and eventually Shiny Staraptor.

Should I evolve Shadow Starly?

If you like Shadow Pokémon or want to build the evolution line, evolving it can be worthwhile. Compare the specimen's IVs and your goals first.

What is Starly's evolution?

Starly evolves into Staravia, which evolves into Staraptor.

Is Starly a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Starly is a regular Pokémon and is not Legendary, Mythical or Ultra Beast.

Can Shadow Starly become Shadow Staraptor?

A Shadow Starly can be evolved through its evolution line while retaining its Shadow status, subject to current Pokémon GO mechanics.

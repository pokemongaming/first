Salandit

Salandit is a Poison and Fire-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Alola region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a collectible Pokémon with an important gender-based evolution: only female Salandit can evolve into Salazzle.

01 • OVERVIEW

Salandit Overview

Salandit is a dual Poison/Fire-type Pokémon from Generation 7. It has relatively modest combat stats, so its main appeal in Pokémon GO is collection, evolution hunting, PvP experimentation in limited formats and special specimens such as Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV Salandit.

Why Salandit's Typing Matters

Salandit's dual typing gives it several useful resistances, but it also creates a particularly important weakness to Ground-type attacks. Psychic, Rock and Water attacks also deal super-effective damage.

08 • PVP

Salandit PvP Guide

Salandit's low maximum CP prevents it from being a natural choice for Great League and higher formats, but its low CP can make it relevant in restricted formats such as Little Cup.

Little Cup

Salandit can be interesting in Little Cup because Poison and Fire provide useful offensive coverage. Poison Fang can create shield pressure while Flamethrower provides Fire-type coverage.

Great League

Although Salandit can technically be used below 1,500 CP, its overall stat profile makes it a much less natural choice than stronger Great League Pokémon.

Ultra League & Master League

Salandit's 1,268 maximum CP means it cannot reach the CP requirements normally associated with Ultra League or Master League competition.

09 • EVOLUTION

Salandit Evolution

Salandit's evolution mechanic is unusual: only female Salandit can evolve into Salazzle.

Salandit Male or Female
→
Salazzle Female Salandit only
Important evolution requirement

Only female Salandit can evolve into Salazzle. The evolution costs 50 Salandit Candy.

Male Salandit

Male Salandit cannot evolve into Salazzle in Pokémon GO. A male Salandit can still be valuable as a Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS or perfect-IV collection specimen.

Female Salandit

Female Salandit is the evolution candidate. If your goal is to complete the Salandit family, check the gender before transferring a duplicate.

23 • FAQ

Salandit FAQ

What type is Salandit?

Salandit is a Poison and Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Salandit weak to?

Salandit is especially weak to Ground attacks and is also weak to Psychic, Rock and Water attacks.

What is Salandit's Max CP?

Salandit's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,268.

Can Salandit evolve?

Yes. Female Salandit can evolve into Salazzle for 50 Salandit Candy.

Can male Salandit evolve?

No. Only female Salandit can evolve into Salazzle.

Is Shiny Salandit available?

Yes. Shiny Salandit is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Salandit good for raids?

Salandit is not considered a strong raid attacker because of its relatively low combat stats.

Is Salandit good in PvP?

Salandit can be interesting in Little Cup, while its low maximum CP limits its usefulness in higher CP leagues.

What is a 100% Salandit?

A 100% Salandit has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Salandit?

XXL Salandit is an unusually large Salandit belonging to the XXL size category.

What is XXS Salandit?

XXS Salandit is an unusually small Salandit belonging to the XXS size category.

Is Lucky Salandit worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Salandit can be a useful collection specimen, especially when it has high IVs, female gender, Shiny status or unusual size.

Should I transfer male Salandit?

Do not transfer one automatically. Male Salandit cannot evolve, but it can still have value as a Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, XXL or XXS collection specimen.

Should I keep female Salandit?

Yes, particularly if you want Salazzle. Female Salandit is required for the evolution.

Can a Shiny Salandit evolve?

Yes. A female Shiny Salandit can evolve into Shiny Salazzle.

Can XXL Salandit evolve?

If the XXL Salandit is female, it can evolve into Salazzle while retaining its evolution eligibility.

Can XXS Salandit evolve?

If the XXS Salandit is female, it can evolve into Salazzle.

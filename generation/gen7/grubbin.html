Grubbin Overview

Grubbin is the first Pokémon in a three-stage evolution family consisting of Grubbin, Charjabug and Vikavolt. While Grubbin itself is mainly an evolution-stage Pokémon rather than a major raid attacker, obtaining strong Grubbin can be useful for building future Charjabug or Vikavolt projects.

Purified Grubbin

🤍🐛

Purified Grubbin

Purified Grubbin is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Grubbin. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon into its Purified version.

Should You Purify Grubbin?

Purification should depend on your collection and evolution goals. Compare the value of keeping the Shadow version against the benefits of obtaining a Purified specimen.

Grubbin FAQ

What type is Grubbin?

Grubbin is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

Does Grubbin evolve?

Yes. Grubbin evolves into Charjabug and eventually Vikavolt.

Is Shiny Grubbin available?

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO release status and encounter pool.

Can Grubbin be Lucky?

Yes. An eligible Grubbin obtained through a Lucky Trade can become Lucky.

What is a 100% IV Grubbin?

A perfect Grubbin has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Grubbin have better stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically mean better IVs or battle performance.

Should I evolve my best Grubbin?

A strong Grubbin can be a good candidate for Charjabug and Vikavolt, depending on your IV and gameplay goals.

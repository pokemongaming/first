Wimpod Overview

Wimpod is a small Bug/Water-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 7. Its low individual combat stats mean that Wimpod itself is generally more important as an evolution and collection target than as a standalone raid attacker.

Why Wimpod's Typing Matters

Wimpod combines Bug and Water typing, giving it a mixed weakness and resistance profile. Its typing makes it resistant to several common attack categories, while Flying, Rock and Electric attacks can deal increased damage.

Should You Power Up Wimpod?

For most players, investing heavily in Wimpod itself is not a priority. The better long-term reason to collect Wimpod Candy is evolving Wimpod into Golisopod or keeping special specimens such as Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS or high-IV Wimpod.

Wimpod Evolution

Wimpod evolves into Golisopod. Unlike many Pokémon, the evolution requires a substantial amount of Candy.

Is Wimpod Worth Evolving?

Yes, especially if you want to complete the Wimpod family, build a Golisopod, or evolve a high-IV or special Wimpod. Because the Candy requirement is 400, it is worth checking IVs, appraisal, size and special attributes before evolving.

Shadow Wimpod

Shadow Wimpod should be treated separately from regular Wimpod because Shadow Pokémon have different battle mechanics and resource requirements.

Current Shadow Status

Shadow Wimpod is not currently listed as an available Shadow form in the Pokémon GO database data used for this guide. Do not assume that a Shadow Wimpod is obtainable unless a future Team GO Rocket event introduces it.

Purified Wimpod

Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon. Because Wimpod is not currently listed as an obtainable Shadow Pokémon, a normal Wimpod cannot simply be purified.

Purified Wimpod Status

A Purified Wimpod would require Shadow Wimpod to exist and be obtainable first. If Shadow Wimpod is introduced in a future Pokémon GO update, the purification mechanics and resulting moves should be checked before publishing specific claims.

Database rule:

Do not label an ordinary Wimpod as Purified. Purified is a specific Pokémon status obtained through purification of an eligible Shadow Pokémon.

Lucky Pokémon receive a minimum IV floor when traded as Lucky Pokémon. A Lucky Wimpod can therefore be a useful candidate for evolution if its resulting IVs are strong.

XXL Wimpod

XXL Wimpod is a size variant that can be identified from Wimpod's height and size information. Size is separate from IVs and CP, so an XXL Wimpod can have any combination of IVs.

Wimpod Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Because Wimpod requires 400 Candy to evolve, Candy management is one of the most important parts of building the Wimpod family.

  • Save Wimpod Candy before evolving your best specimen.
  • Check IVs before spending 400 Candy.
  • Keep Shiny Wimpod if you are building a complete collection.
  • Consider Lucky Wimpod for lower Stardust power-up costs.
  • Check XXL and XXS status before transferring extras.
  • Collect Candy XL if you eventually plan high-level investment.

Buddy Distance

Wimpod has a 1 km Buddy Candy distance, making it relatively convenient to earn additional Candy while walking with it as your Buddy.

Wimpod Availability

Wimpod has appeared through wild encounters and has also featured in Pokémon GO events and other encounter methods. Availability can change as event rotations change.

Current Availability

Check the current Pokémon GO event schedule and encounter tables before publishing a claim that Wimpod is available through a specific method.

Historical encounter methods should be separated from current availability so that an old event does not accidentally appear to be an active encounter source.

How to Use Wimpod

For Collection

  • Keep your best IV Wimpod.
  • Check for Shiny Wimpod.
  • Check XXL and XXS size markers.
  • Keep unusual Lucky specimens.
  • Consider evolving a high-quality Wimpod into Golisopod.

Before Transferring

Wimpod is a Pokémon where blindly transferring low-CP specimens can be a mistake. CP alone does not reveal IVs, size or special status. Always check appraisal, Shiny status, Lucky status, XXL/XXS markers and evolution potential before transferring.

Wimpod FAQ

What type is Wimpod?

Wimpod is a Bug/Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wimpod weak to?

Wimpod is weak to Flying, Rock and Electric-type attacks.

What does Wimpod evolve into?

Wimpod evolves into Golisopod.

How much Candy does Wimpod need to evolve?

Wimpod requires 400 Wimpod Candy to evolve into Golisopod.

What is Wimpod's max CP?

A perfect Wimpod reaches 567 CP at Level 50.

What is a 100% Wimpod?

A 100% Wimpod has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 Stamina IVs.

Is Shiny Wimpod available?

Yes. Shiny Wimpod is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Wimpod available?

Shadow Wimpod is not currently listed as an available Shadow form in the database data used for this guide. Availability can change through future Team GO Rocket content.

Can Wimpod be Purified?

A normal Wimpod cannot be purified. Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon.

Can Wimpod be Lucky?

Yes. Wimpod can become Lucky through a Lucky Trade.

What is an XXL Wimpod?

An XXL Wimpod is a Wimpod that falls into the game's XXL size category.

What is an XXS Wimpod?

An XXS Wimpod is a Wimpod that falls into the game's smallest size category.

Is XXL Wimpod automatically a Hundo?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Wimpod can have any IV combination.

Is Wimpod good for raids?

Wimpod itself is not considered a high-end raid attacker. Its main value is as an evolution, collection and special variant Pokémon.

Should I evolve a 100% Wimpod?

If you want a perfect Golisopod, a 100% Wimpod is an excellent evolution candidate. Make sure you actually want the evolved form before spending the 400 Candy.

01 • OVERVIEW

Incineroar Overview

Incineroar is the final evolution of Litten, evolving from Torracat. It is a Fire/Dark-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 7.

Unlike many Legendary Pokémon, Incineroar is obtainable through its evolution family. This makes Candy and XL Candy management particularly important for players building a high-level Incineroar.

Why Incineroar's Typing Matters

Incineroar's Fire/Dark typing gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates several important weaknesses. Its Fire typing helps it handle Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel targets, while Dark-type attacks are useful against Psychic and Ghost Pokémon.

09 • PVP

Incineroar PvP Guide

Incineroar can have PvP value because of its Fire/Dark typing and access to charged attacks that create useful matchup pressure.

Great League

A standard Incineroar is generally less natural for Great League because of its stat distribution and available CP range. League-specific formats can change this assessment.

Ultra League

Ultra League can provide more room for Incineroar's CP range. Its Fire/Dark typing can give it interesting matchups, although it must carefully manage its weaknesses.

Master League

Incineroar's maximum CP is considerably lower than many top Master League Pokémon. As a result, it is generally less natural for unrestricted Master League competition.

A Shadow Pokémon does not need to have perfect IVs to be useful. Evaluate the Pokémon's overall battle role, moveset, level and resource requirements before deciding whether to invest.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Incineroar

Lucky Incineroar is not a separate species or evolution. It is an Incineroar with Lucky status, obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

🍀 Lucky Incineroar

Lucky Pokémon have special visual characteristics in storage and require fewer resources to power up than standard Pokémon under the game's Lucky mechanics.

Why Keep a Lucky Incineroar?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Useful for long-term investment projects.
  • Valuable for collectors.
  • Can be especially useful when building multiple battle-ready Pokémon.

Incineroar FAQ

What type is Incineroar?

Incineroar is a Fire/Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Incineroar weak to?

Incineroar is weak to Water, Ground, Rock and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Incineroar a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Incineroar is the final evolution of Litten and is not a Legendary Pokémon.

What is the Incineroar evolution line?

Litten evolves into Torracat, which evolves into Incineroar.

Is Shiny Incineroar available?

Shiny Incineroar can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Litten through its evolution line.

What is a 100% Incineroar?

A 100% Incineroar has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Incineroar?

A Lucky Incineroar is an Incineroar with Lucky status obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

What is XXL Incineroar?

XXL Incineroar is an unusually large individual. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Incineroar?

XXS Incineroar is an unusually small individual. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has low IVs.

Is Shadow Incineroar stronger?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The value of Shadow Incineroar depends on its current moveset, role and investment.

Should I purify Shadow Incineroar?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, especially if you are interested in battle performance.

Is Incineroar good for raids?

Incineroar can be useful in Fire or Dark raid roles, but its ranking depends on its current moveset and the other attackers available in Pokémon GO.

Is Incineroar good in PvP?

Incineroar can have PvP applications depending on the league, moveset and current metagame.

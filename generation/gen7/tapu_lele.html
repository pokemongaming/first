Tapu Lele FAQ

What type is Tapu Lele?

Tapu Lele is a Psychic/Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Tapu Lele weak to?

Tapu Lele is weak to Ghost, Poison and Steel-type attacks.

What is Tapu Lele's max CP?

Tapu Lele's Level 50 maximum CP is 3,950.

Is Shiny Tapu Lele available?

Yes. Shiny Tapu Lele is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Does Tapu Lele evolve?

What is a 100% Tapu Lele?

A 100% Tapu Lele has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Tapu Lele?

What is an XXS Tapu Lele?

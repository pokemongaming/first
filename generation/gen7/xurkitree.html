Why Xurkitree's Typing Matters

Xurkitree's pure Electric typing gives it a focused offensive role. Electric attacks are especially useful against Water- and Flying-type opponents.

Its main defensive drawback is its single weakness: Ground-type attacks deal super-effective damage.

Xurkitree PvP Guide

Xurkitree's very high Attack makes it more naturally suited to offensive pressure than traditional bulky PvP builds.

Master League

Xurkitree can be considered in Master League because its CP ceiling is high enough to enter the format. However, its relatively low Defense means it requires careful matchup selection.

Ultra League

Xurkitree can technically fit within the Ultra League CP limit, but its overall PvP performance should be evaluated against the current metagame before investment.

Great League

A standard Xurkitree is not naturally optimized for Great League because its high offensive stat distribution and Legendary rarity make low-level competitive builds less practical.

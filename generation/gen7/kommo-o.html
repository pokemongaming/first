Kommo-o Overview

Kommo-o is the final evolution of the Jangmo-o family and combines Dragon and Fighting typing. Its high Defense and useful charged-move options give it a distinct role compared with many other Dragon-type Pokémon.

Kommo-o FAQ

What type is Kommo-o?

Kommo-o is a Dragon/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Kommo-o weak to?

Kommo-o is vulnerable to Fairy, Ice, Dragon and Flying-type attacks, with Fairy being especially dangerous because of its dual typing.

Is Kommo-o a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Kommo-o is not a Legendary Pokémon. It is the final evolution of the Jangmo-o family.

Is Shiny Kommo-o available?

Shiny Kommo-o availability depends on Pokémon GO's current encounter and event systems. Check the latest game information for current availability.

What is a 100% Kommo-o?

Shadow Kommo-o is a Shadow version of Kommo-o with the Shadow battle modifier. Its availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket systems.

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your intended raid, PvP or collection goal before purifying.

Why Kartana's Typing Matters

Kartana's Grass and Steel typing gives it a strong collection of resistances while allowing it to function as a powerful Grass-type attacker. Its Steel typing also gives it a very different defensive profile from many other Grass attackers.

Kartana Weaknesses & Resistances

Major Weaknesses

🔥
Fire

Fire is Kartana's biggest weakness. Because of its Grass and Steel combination, Fire attacks deal double super-effective damage.

🥊
Fighting

Fighting-type attacks are also super effective against Kartana.

Important Resistances

🌿
Grass

Kartana strongly resists Grass-type attacks.

⚡
Electric

Kartana resists Electric-type attacks.

🧊
Ice

Its Steel typing helps Kartana resist Ice attacks.

Kartana has a large resistance profile because of its Grass/Steel combination. Its most important offensive threats are Fire and Fighting.

Kartana PvP Guide

Kartana's enormous Attack stat gives it offensive pressure, but its relatively low HP and Defense make it more fragile than many bulky Master League Pokémon.

Master League

Kartana can be considered for Master League experimentation, but it is much more naturally suited to players who want to exploit its offensive strengths rather than rely on heavy bulk.

Great League & Ultra League

Kartana's high CP and Legendary/Ultra Beast availability make the lower leagues less natural environments for a standard investment.

Kartana FAQ

What type is Kartana?

Kartana is a Grass and Steel-type Ultra Beast.

What is Kartana weak to?

Kartana is especially weak to Fire-type attacks and is also weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Kartana good for raids?

Yes. Kartana's exceptionally high Attack stat makes it a strong Grass-type raid attacker when Grass damage is effective.

Is Shiny Kartana available?

Yes. Shiny Kartana is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Kartana available?

No. Shadow Kartana is not currently available. This should be updated if Pokémon GO introduces the Shadow form.

Can you get Purified Kartana?

Not currently. A Purified Kartana would require Kartana to first be obtainable as a Shadow Pokémon.

What is a Lucky Kartana?

A Lucky Kartana is Kartana obtained through the trading system with Lucky status.

What is a 100% Kartana?

A 100% Kartana has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is the max CP of Kartana?

Kartana reaches 4,156 CP at level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is XXL Kartana?

XXL Kartana is a Kartana with the XXL size attribute. It is not a separate species or evolution.

What is XXS Kartana?

XXS Kartana is a Kartana with the XXS size attribute. It is primarily useful as a collector's specimen.

Does Kartana evolve?

Kartana does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

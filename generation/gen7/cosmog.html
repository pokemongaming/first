Cosmog Overview

Cosmog is a Psychic-type Legendary Pokémon introduced with the Alola region. Unlike a typical raid attacker, Cosmog is primarily valuable because of its special evolutionary progression.

Why Cosmog Matters

Cosmog is more of a long-term progression Pokémon than a traditional battle investment. Players should carefully consider its evolution path before making irreversible evolution decisions.

Cosmog Forms & Collection Variants

This section separates different collectible Cosmog variants. Cosmetic forms, Shadow mechanics, Lucky status, IV quality and size categories should not be treated as the same type of variation.

🌌 Regular Cosmog

The standard version of Cosmog obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

✨ Shiny Cosmog

A special alternate-color variant. Shiny availability should always be confirmed against the current Pokémon GO encounter pool.

🌑 Shadow Cosmog

A hypothetical or special Shadow variant. Its availability depends entirely on current Team GO Rocket and game mechanics.

✨ Purified Cosmog

Purified Cosmog would only be relevant when a Shadow Cosmog is officially available and eligible for purification.

🍀 Lucky Cosmog

Lucky status is a separate collectible property connected to Lucky Trade mechanics when trading is permitted for the species.

💯 100% Cosmog

A Cosmog with perfect 15/15/15 Individual Values.

⬆ XXL Cosmog

An unusually large Cosmog that may be valuable for collection and size-record purposes.

⬇ XXS Cosmog

An unusually small Cosmog with collectible size value.

Regular Cosmog

Regular Cosmog is the standard form used as the starting point for its evolution line.

Main Role

Cosmog's primary value is progression rather than direct battle performance.

  • Check IVs before choosing a long-term evolution project.

Lucky Cosmog

🍀 Lucky Investment

Cosmog FAQ

What type is Cosmog?

Cosmog is a Psychic-type Pokémon.

Is Cosmog a Legendary Pokémon?

Cosmog is classified as a Legendary Pokémon.

Does Cosmog evolve?

Cosmog evolves into Cosmoem and can later progress through its special evolution line.

Is Shiny Cosmog available?

Shiny availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO encounter pool before publication.

Is Shadow Cosmog available?

Shadow availability depends on official Pokémon GO releases and Team GO Rocket rotations.

What is a 100% Cosmog?

A 100% Cosmog has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Cosmog have better stats?

XXL size does not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Is Lucky Cosmog useful?

Lucky status can make a Pokémon more attractive for long-term power-up investment because of Lucky Pokémon's Stardust mechanics.

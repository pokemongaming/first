XXL Primarina

XXL Primarina is an unusually large size variant. Size is separate from IVs and CP.

XXL Extra-large Primarina

An XXL Primarina can have ordinary, high or perfect IVs. Its size does not automatically determine its battle stats.

Collector Value Size Collection

XXS Primarina

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. A Purified Cutiefly therefore depends on Shadow Cutiefly being obtainable in Pokémon GO.

Purification Considerations

10 · LUCKY

Lucky Cutiefly

Lucky Cutiefly is created through a Lucky Trade. Its Lucky status is different from Shiny, Shadow, Purified or size attributes.

🍀
Lucky Pokémon

Lucky Cutiefly

Lucky Cutiefly has the special Lucky background and benefits from the reduced Stardust cost associated with powering up Lucky Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Cutiefly?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • A Lucky Cutiefly can be a fun collection milestone.
  • Lucky status can be especially interesting when combined with strong IVs.
  • A Lucky Shiny Cutiefly is a particularly desirable collector combination.
11 · PERFECT IV

100% IV Cutiefly

A 100% IV Cutiefly has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a Hundo.

Should You Evolve a Hundo Cutiefly?

If you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon, a Hundo Cutiefly is an excellent evolution candidate. Before evolving it, check Ribombee's current usefulness and your own collection goals.

16 · PVP

Cutiefly PvP Guide

Cutiefly's relatively low overall stats mean it is not generally treated as a major Master League Pokémon. Its evolution, Ribombee, is the more natural Pokémon to investigate for battle-focused players.

Great League

If Cutiefly is used in a CP-limited format, its low maximum CP can make it more compatible with lower-level formats than with Master League. However, the current Cup rules and moveset should be checked before investing.

Master League

Cutiefly is not naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP and overall stats are far below the Pokémon commonly used in that format.

20 · FAQ

Cutiefly FAQ

What type is Cutiefly?

Cutiefly is a Bug and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Cutiefly weak to?

Cutiefly's Bug/Fairy typing makes it vulnerable to Fire, Flying, Poison, Rock and Steel-type attacks.

What does Cutiefly evolve into?

Cutiefly evolves into Ribombee.

Is Shiny Cutiefly available?

Shiny Cutiefly is a collectible variant when it is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Cutiefly?

A 100% Cutiefly has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs, commonly referred to as a Hundo.

What is an XXL Cutiefly?

XXL Cutiefly is an unusually large Cutiefly according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Cutiefly?

XXS Cutiefly is an unusually small Cutiefly according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is Lucky Cutiefly?

Lucky Cutiefly is a Cutiefly obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

Is Shadow Cutiefly available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket encounters. It should not be listed as currently obtainable without checking the current rotation.

Can Cutiefly be Purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, whether Purified Cutiefly exists depends on Shadow Cutiefly being made available in Pokémon GO.

Is Cutiefly good for raids?

Cutiefly is generally not considered a major raid attacker. Players interested in battle performance should also evaluate its evolution, Ribombee.

Should I keep a Hundo Cutiefly?

Yes, if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon or want a perfect Cutiefly for evolution. A 15/15/15 specimen is a strong collection piece.

Can Cutiefly be Shiny and Lucky?

Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially have both attributes when obtained through the appropriate mechanics.

Can Cutiefly be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXL Cutiefly can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Blacephalon PvP Guide

Blacephalon's extremely high Attack and relatively low bulk create a very different profile from bulky PvP specialists.

Great League

Standard Blacephalon is not naturally suited to the Great League CP environment because of its high Attack and overall stat profile.

Ultra League

Ultra League can provide more room for Blacephalon, but its low defensive bulk remains an important consideration.

Master League

Master League removes the low CP ceiling, allowing Blacephalon to use more of its raw stats. However, players should compare its current performance against the evolving Master League meta before investing heavily.

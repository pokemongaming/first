Dewpider is a dual Water and Bug-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 7. It is part of a two-stage evolutionary family and evolves into Araquanid.

Do not purify automatically. Compare the potential value of keeping the Shadow version with the benefits of obtaining a Purified Dewpider.

Shadow tip:

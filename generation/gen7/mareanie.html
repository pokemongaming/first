08 — IV GUIDE

Mareanie IV Guide

Individual Values determine additional Attack, Defense and HP points. A 15/15/15 Mareanie is considered a perfect-IV Mareanie.

What Does 100% Mareanie Mean?

A 100% Mareanie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are commonly called perfect IVs or a hundo. A perfect Mareanie can be valuable to collectors, especially if it is Shiny, Lucky, XXL or XXS.

100% Mareanie

18 — PVP

Mareanie PvP Guide

Mareanie's low maximum CP means that it can fit naturally within lower CP formats, although players should evaluate the exact league rules and current moveset before investing.

Great League

Mareanie's relatively low CP makes Great League a more natural environment to consider than Master League. However, its actual performance depends heavily on moves, IV distribution and the current PvP metagame.

Ultra League

Mareanie can theoretically be used within Ultra League's CP limit, but its overall performance should be evaluated against current alternatives rather than assumed from typing alone.

Master League

23 — FAQ

Mareanie FAQ

What type is Mareanie?

Mareanie is a Poison and Water-type Pokémon.

What is Mareanie weak to?

Mareanie is weak to Electric, Ground and Psychic attacks.

Does Mareanie evolve?

Yes. Mareanie evolves into Toxapex.

How much Candy does Mareanie need to evolve?

Mareanie requires 50 Mareanie Candy to evolve into Toxapex.

Is Shiny Mareanie available?

Shiny Mareanie is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny is active in the game.

What is 100% Mareanie?

A 100% Mareanie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Mareanie?

Lucky Mareanie is a Mareanie that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is XXL Mareanie?

XXL Mareanie is an exceptionally large Mareanie based on its recorded Pokémon GO size.

What is XXS Mareanie?

XXS Mareanie is an exceptionally small Mareanie based on its recorded Pokémon GO size.

Is Mareanie good for raids?

Mareanie is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary value is more closely associated with evolution, collection and certain battle formats.

Is Mareanie good for PvP?

Mareanie can be considered for lower CP formats, but its actual performance depends on its moves, IVs and the current Pokémon GO PvP metagame.

Is Shadow Mareanie available?

Shadow availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations. Check current Pokémon GO data before claiming that Shadow Mareanie is currently obtainable.

Can Mareanie be purified?

Only an eligible Shadow Mareanie can be purified. A normal Mareanie cannot simply be converted into a Purified Pokémon.

Can Shiny Mareanie be evolved?

Yes. A Shiny Mareanie can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

Can a Mareanie be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Lucky status and Shiny status are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can possess both.

Can Mareanie be both 100% and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so an XXL Mareanie can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

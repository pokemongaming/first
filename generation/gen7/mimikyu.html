Mimikyu Overview

Mimikyu is a Generation 7 Pokémon originally introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon. In Pokémon GO, it is a Ghost/Fairy-type Pokémon with no normal evolution. Its unusual battle mechanic is tied to its disguise. During Trainer Battles, Mimikyu can transition from its Disguised Form to Busted Form after its special protection is triggered.

Why Mimikyu's Typing Matters

The Ghost/Fairy combination gives Mimikyu a distinctive defensive profile. It is especially resistant to Fighting, Bug, Dragon and Normal-type attacks, while Ghost and Steel moves deal super-effective damage.

Mimikyu PvP Guide

Mimikyu's combination of high Defense, Ghost/Fairy typing and its unusual Disguised-to-Busted mechanic makes it much more interesting for Trainer Battles than for raids.

Great League

Mimikyu can be particularly interesting around Great League CP limits. Its typing gives it useful matchups against Fighting and Dragon-oriented opponents, while its special disguise mechanic creates additional shield-management decisions.

Ultra League

Mimikyu can also be relevant in Ultra League formats when its CP and battle rules allow it. The best moveset and viability can change with the current metagame.

Master League

Mimikyu's maximum CP is considerably lower than many Master League specialists, so Great League and Ultra League are generally more natural environments for it.

PvP Tip

Do not treat Mimikyu's disguise protection like a normal third shield. Its interaction with the Busted Form and Defense reduction is part of the Pokémon's unique battle identity.

Mimikyu Disguised & Busted Forms

Mimikyu's most important special mechanic is its transition between Disguised Form and Busted Form during Trainer Battles.

🎭
Disguised Form Mimikyu begins battle with its disguise intact.

Its disguise can absorb the first Charged Attack that hits it, creating a unique defensive mechanic.

👻
Busted Form Battle-only form

After the disguise is broken, Mimikyu enters Busted Form and receives a Defense reduction for the remainder of the battle.

How the Mimikyu Form Mechanic Works

  • Mimikyu starts Trainer Battles in Disguised Form.
  • Its special protection can absorb the first Charged Attack.
  • The disguise then breaks.
  • Mimikyu changes into Busted Form.
  • Its Defense is reduced after the disguise is broken.

```
Shiny Rowlet

Shiny Rowlet is the alternate-color version of regular Rowlet. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

✨🦉

Shiny Rowlet Guide

If you obtain a Shiny Rowlet with strong IVs, you may want to keep it as a collector Pokémon or evolve it into Shiny Dartrix and eventually Shiny Decidueye.

  • Check IVs before evolving.
  • Decide whether you want to preserve the base-stage Shiny.
  • Consider evolving an extra Shiny if you have duplicates.
  • Check for event-exclusive evolution moves.
```

```
Shadow Rowlet

Shadow Rowlet is the Shadow version of Rowlet. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from regular Pokémon.

🌑🦉
```

```
Purified Rowlet

Purified Rowlet is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Rowlet. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and IV situation according to Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

🤍🦉

Should You Purify Shadow Rowlet?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Rowlet just because purification can improve IVs. Consider the future evolution, battle role and collector value before making the decision.

  • Check the Shadow Rowlet's IVs.
  • Consider its future Shadow evolution potential.
  • Compare resource costs.
  • Avoid purifying automatically.
```
```

Lucky Rowlet

Lucky Rowlet is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for long-term power-up projects because Lucky status can reduce Stardust investment costs.

🍀✨

Lucky Rowlet Investment

A Lucky Rowlet can be a useful long-term evolution project if you plan to power up its evolved forms.

Should You Keep Lucky Rowlet?

  • Lucky status can make future power-ups more efficient.
  • Check the IV combination.
  • Consider evolving into Lucky Decidueye.
  • Keep special collector variants when possible.
```

```
Rowlet PvP Guide

Rowlet itself has limited battle potential compared with fully evolved Pokémon. Trainers generally evaluate its final evolution when considering serious PvP investment.

Great League
```

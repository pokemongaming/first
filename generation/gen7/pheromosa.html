Pheromosa Pokémon GO Overview

Pheromosa is National Pokédex #795 and is classified as an Ultra Beast. It originates from the Alola region and has a unique Bug/Fighting typing.

Its biggest defining feature in Pokémon GO is its enormous Attack stat combined with extremely low Defense. This gives Pheromosa a glass-cannon playstyle: it can deal significant damage quickly, but it can also faint much faster than bulkier attackers.

Pheromosa in PvP

Pheromosa's extreme lack of bulk makes PvP use challenging. Although its Attack stat is impressive, it can struggle to survive neutral damage and opponents with strong fast-move pressure.

PvP takeaway: Pheromosa is much more naturally suited to high-damage situations than prolonged battles. If used in PvP, shield management and matchup selection become especially important.

How to Use Pheromosa

1. Use It Against Appropriate Raid Bosses

Pheromosa is most valuable when its Bug-type damage can exploit a raid boss's weakness.

2. Avoid Unnecessary Damage

Its Defense is extremely low, so avoid leaving Pheromosa exposed to powerful super-effective Charged Attacks.

3. Build Around Its Attack

Pheromosa's main strength is offensive pressure. A high-Attack, high-IV specimen is the natural choice for long-term investment.

4. Consider Flying Counters When Fighting It

If Pheromosa appears as a raid boss, prioritize powerful Flying attackers because Flying exploits its strongest weakness.

What Torracat's Typing Means

Because Torracat is a pure Fire type, it has a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. Fire attacks are useful against Bug, Steel, Grass and Ice Pokémon, while Torracat itself takes increased damage from Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

Is a Hundo Torracat Worth Keeping?

Absolutely as a collection and evolution specimen. Its perfect IVs are valuable even though Torracat itself is not a top raid attacker. If you eventually evolve it, the hundo IVs remain the same.

09 • PVP

Torracat PvP Guide

Torracat can be used in CP-limited battles, but it is not a dominant competitive choice. Flame Charge can create pressure in Trainer Battles, while Crunch provides Dark-type coverage.

Great League

Torracat's CP ceiling makes Great League the most natural standard league to investigate. However, its low Defense and overall performance mean it should be treated as a niche option rather than a meta staple.

Ultra League & Master League

Its 1,934 Level 50 maximum CP prevents Torracat from naturally reaching Master League's unrestricted CP environment. League recommendations should always be checked against the current rules and rankings.

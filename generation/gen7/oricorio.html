Oricorio FAQ

How many Oricorio forms are there in Pokémon GO?

There are four main styles: Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u and Sensu.

What type is Baile Oricorio?

Baile Style Oricorio is Fire/Flying type.

What type is Pom-Pom Oricorio?

Pom-Pom Style Oricorio is Electric/Flying type.

What type is Pa'u Oricorio?

Pa'u Style Oricorio is Psychic/Flying type.

What type is Sensu Oricorio?

Sensu Style Oricorio is Ghost/Flying type.

Can Oricorio be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Oricorio is available, including its different styles.

Does Oricorio evolve?

No. Oricorio does not have a conventional evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

What is Oricorio's maximum CP?

Oricorio reaches 2,638 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Oricorio's base stats?

Oricorio has 196 Attack, 145 Defense and 181 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is 100% IV Oricorio worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Oricorio is a perfect-IV specimen and is a strong collection target, particularly when paired with a rare style or special size.

What is XXL Oricorio?

XXL Oricorio is a large-size classification. It is not a different species or evolution.

What is XXS Oricorio?

XXS Oricorio is a small-size classification rather than a separate Pokémon form.

Can Oricorio be Lucky?

A traded Oricorio can become Lucky when the trade results in the Lucky Pokémon state.

Is Oricorio good for raids?

Oricorio is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary value is collection, form variety and personal preference.

02 • EVOLUTION

Salandit → Salazzle Evolution

Salazzle has a two-stage family. The important part is that the Salandit must be female. Male Salandit cannot evolve into Salazzle.

10 • PVP

Salazzle PvP Guide

Salazzle is more interesting in GO Battle League than in raids. Its Fire/Poison typing and Poison Fang debuff give it a useful niche.

Great League

Salazzle can surprise opponents in Great League with Incinerate, Poison Fang and Dragon Pulse. However, its high Attack and relatively low bulk limit its overall consistency.

Ultra League

Ultra League gives Salazzle more room to use its offensive typing. Incinerate provides strong Fire pressure while Poison Fang can lower the opponent's Defense.

Master League

Salazzle is generally not considered a strong Master League choice because its maximum CP and overall bulk are not competitive with the format's strongest Pokémon.

Recommended PvP Moveset

Incinerate + Poison Fang + Dragon Pulse

    24 • FAQ

    Salazzle FAQ

    What type is Salazzle?

    Salazzle is a Poison and Fire-type Pokémon.

    How do you evolve Salandit into Salazzle?

    You need 50 Salandit Candy and the Salandit must be female. Male Salandit cannot evolve into Salazzle.

    What is Salazzle's max CP?

    Salazzle reaches 2,802 CP at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

    What is Salazzle weak to?

    Salazzle is especially weak to Ground attacks, which deal approximately 2.56× damage. It is also weak to Psychic, Rock and Water.

    Is Shiny Salazzle available?

    Yes. Shiny Salazzle is available in Pokémon GO.

    Is Salazzle good for raids?

    Salazzle has usable Fire and Poison offensive options, but it is generally not a top raid attacker because stronger alternatives are available.

    Is Salazzle good in PvP?

    Salazzle has niche value in Great and Ultra League. Incinerate, Poison Fang and Dragon Pulse are its recommended PvP moves.

    What is a 100% Salazzle?

    A 100% Salazzle has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    Can Salazzle be Lucky?

    Yes. Salazzle can be traded, so a traded Salazzle can become Lucky through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic.

    Is Shadow Salazzle available?

    Current Pokémon GO database information does not list Shadow Salazzle as an available form. This should be updated if Niantic releases the form in the future.

    Is Purified Salazzle available?

    A normal Purified Salazzle should not be listed as an obtainable Pokémon GO variant while Shadow Salazzle is unavailable.

    What is XXL Salazzle?

    XXL Salazzle is a size-classified Salazzle. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

    What is XXS Salazzle?

    XXS Salazzle is a particularly small size-classified Salazzle. XXS status is separate from IVs and CP.

    Can a Shiny Salazzle be Lucky?

    Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a traded Shiny Salazzle can be a valuable Lucky Shiny collection piece.

    Can a 100% Salazzle also be XXL?

    Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so a Salazzle can theoretically combine 15/15/15 IVs with an XXL or XXS size classification.

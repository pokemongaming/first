🌴 Gen 7 / Alola Overview

The seventh generation introduced the tropical Alola region, regional forms, Z-Moves and Ultra Beasts.

About Generation 7

Generation 7 introduced 88 new Pokémon, starting with Rowlet at National Pokédex #722 and ending with Melmetal at #809. The generation is associated with Pokémon Sun, Pokémon Moon, Pokémon Ultra Sun and Pokémon Ultra Moon.

01 Rowlet, Litten and Popplio form the Alola starter trio.
02 Regional forms became a major part of the generation.
03 Z-Moves introduced powerful once-per-battle attacks.
04 Ultra Beasts created a new group of unusual Ultra Space Pokémon.
05 Meltan and Melmetal expanded the Gen 7 roster through Pokémon GO connectivity.

🌀 Alolan Forms & Special Forms

Gen 7 expanded the concept of regional forms and introduced several Pokémon with multiple battle forms.

🌺 Oricorio

Oricorio has four styles associated with different islands and elemental themes: Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u and Sensu.

4 STYLES

🐺 Lycanroc

Lycanroc has Midday, Midnight and Dusk forms. The available form can affect its appearance and battle characteristics.

🌌 Minior

💥 Z-Moves

Z-Moves were one of the defining battle mechanics introduced in Generation 7.

How Z-Moves Worked

A Trainer could equip a Z-Crystal and use a compatible move to unleash a powerful Z-Move. In the original Gen 7 battle system, this was generally limited to once per battle.

Database Opportunity

Your individual Pokémon pages can include a dedicated "Z-Move" section explaining compatible crystals, signature Z-Moves and historical relevance.

❓ Gen 7 Pokémon FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Alola generation.

How many Pokémon were introduced in Gen 7?

Generation 7 introduced 88 new Pokémon, covering National Pokédex numbers #722 through #809.

What region is Gen 7 from?

Generation 7 introduced the Alola region.

Who are the Gen 7 starters?

The three Alola starters are Rowlet, Litten and Popplio.

What is Gen 7 famous for?

Generation 7 is particularly associated with Alolan regional forms, Z-Moves, Ultra Beasts and the Alola island-based adventure structure.

What are the Gen 7 Ultra Beasts?

The Gen 7 Ultra Beast group includes Nihilego, Buzzwole, Pheromosa, Xurkitree, Celesteela, Kartana, Guzzlord, Stakataka and Blacephalon.

Who are the Gen 7 Legendary Pokémon?

Important Gen 7 Legendary Pokémon include the four Tapu, Cosmog, Cosmoem, Solgaleo, Lunala and Necrozma.

Which Mythical Pokémon belong to Gen 7?

Magearna, Marshadow, Zeraora, Meltan and Melmetal are among the Mythical Pokémon associated with Generation 7.

What is the final Gen 7 Pokémon?

Melmetal is National Pokédex #809 and is the final species introduced in Generation 7.

Why should Pokémon GO information be updated separately?

Pokémon GO availability, moves, raid roles, forms and other game-specific mechanics can change over time. The individual Pokémon pages should therefore be maintained independently from the static generation overview.

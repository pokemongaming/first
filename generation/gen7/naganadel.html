Why Naganadel's Typing Matters

Naganadel's dual Poison / Dragon typing gives it four major weaknesses: Dragon, Ground, Ice and Psychic. At the same time, it resists several common attack types, including Grass, Water, Fire, Electric, Fighting, Bug and Poison.

01 • OVERVIEW

Sandygast Overview

Sandygast is a Ghost/Ground-type Pokémon from Generation 7. It resembles a small pile of sand with a shovel embedded in its head and evolves into Palossand.

In Pokémon GO, Sandygast is generally more notable as a collection, evolution and Pokédex Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker. Its unusual typing, Shiny hunting potential and size variants make it an interesting Pokémon to collect.

Sandygast Type

Ghost / Ground Dual Type Pokémon

Why Sandygast's Typing Matters

Sandygast's Ghost/Ground combination gives it a very different defensive profile from many other Ghost-type Pokémon. Ground provides useful interactions against Electric attacks, while Ghost contributes several important resistances and immunities.

The same dual typing also creates several weaknesses, so Sandygast is not simply a defensive Pokémon that can switch into every matchup safely.

23 • FAQ

Sandygast FAQ

What type is Sandygast?

Sandygast is a Ghost/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Sandygast weak to?

Sandygast is vulnerable to Water, Grass, Ice, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Does Sandygast evolve?

Yes. Sandygast evolves into Palossand.

What is a 100% Sandygast?

A 100% Sandygast has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shiny Sandygast?

Shiny Sandygast is the alternate-color version of Sandygast. Its availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Shiny release status.

What is Shadow Sandygast?

Shadow Sandygast is Sandygast with the Shadow Pokémon status. Availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Shadow encounter rotations.

What is Purified Sandygast?

Purified Sandygast is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Sandygast.

What is Lucky Sandygast?

Lucky Sandygast is a Sandygast that received the Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Sandygast?

XXL Sandygast is an unusually large Sandygast. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Sandygast?

XXS Sandygast is an unusually small Sandygast. Like XXL status, XXS does not mean perfect IVs.

Can a Shiny Sandygast be 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Sandygast can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Sandygast be Lucky and Shiny?

Lucky and Shiny are separate characteristics. Where the relevant Pokémon GO mechanics allow it, a Pokémon can possess multiple collection characteristics.

Is Sandygast good for raids?

Sandygast is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its primary value is usually its collection, evolution and specialized gameplay potential.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Sandygast?

If you collect size variants or participate in size-based features, keeping XXL and XXS Sandygast can be worthwhile.

Why the typing matters

08 • PVP

Tapu Fini PvP Guide

Tapu Fini's high Defense and useful Water/Fairy typing make PvP its more interesting competitive role. Current rankings place it in B tier for Great League, A tier for Ultra League and A tier for Master League. citeturn0search4

Great League

A strong Great League candidate can use Water Gun, Surf and Nature's Madness. Current database guidance lists a 0/15/12 IV spread around 1,500 CP for this league. citeturn0search4

Ultra League

Tapu Fini performs well in Ultra League with Water Gun, Surf and Nature's Madness. A 0/15/15-style spread is a useful starting point for optimization. citeturn0search4

Master League

Master League favors high IVs and Level 50 investment. Current listings recommend 15/15/15 for the standard Master League build. citeturn0search4

Solgaleo Overview

Solgaleo is the Sunne Pokémon and one of the final evolutions in the Cosmog family. In Pokémon GO, Cosmoem can evolve into Solgaleo during the daytime for 100 Cosmog Candy.

Solgaleo is a dual Psychic/Steel-type Legendary Pokémon with a maximum CP of 4,570 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Why Solgaleo's Typing Matters

Solgaleo's Psychic/Steel combination gives it a distinctive defensive profile. It resists a wide range of attacks, including Psychic and Poison, while also taking reduced damage from several common types.

Its main weaknesses are Dark, Fire, Ghost and Ground.

Solgaleo PvP Guide

Solgaleo's high CP, strong overall stats and Psychic/Steel typing make it much more interesting in Master League than in the lower CP leagues.

Master League

Solgaleo can function as a serious Master League Pokémon when built around its current PvP moveset. Fire Spin, Psychic Fangs and Iron Head give it useful pressure and coverage.

Great League

Standard Solgaleo is not naturally suited to Great League because of its extremely high maximum CP.

Ultra League

Solgaleo's stat profile is generally more naturally suited to Master League than Ultra League. Check the current league rules and rankings before investing specifically for a CP-limited format.

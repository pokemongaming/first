Why Crabominable's Typing Matters

Crabominable's Ice/Fighting combination gives it access to two useful offensive categories. Ice attacks are valuable against several Flying, Ground, Grass and Dragon targets, while Fighting attacks are useful against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark targets.

Decidueye FAQ

What type is Decidueye?

Decidueye is a Grass and Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Decidueye weak to?

Its weaknesses depend on its dual Grass and Ghost typing.

Can Decidueye be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Decidueye?

A 100% Decidueye has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Decidueye stronger?

Is Shadow Decidueye different from regular Decidueye?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of Shadow status with the purified result before making the decision.

Size variants are primarily collection features and should not automatically be considered stronger simply because they are XXL.

Does Decidueye evolve?

Decidueye is the final evolution of Rowlet and Dartrix.

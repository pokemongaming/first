Why Litten's Fire Typing Matters

Fire-type Pokémon are particularly useful against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel opponents. Litten's Fire typing also gives it useful resistances, although its relatively modest stats limit its role compared with stronger Fire-type attackers.

    11 • PURIFIED

    Purified Litten

    Purified Litten is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Litten. Purification changes its status and provides the standard Purified Pokémon mechanics.

    💎

    Purified Litten

    A purified Litten is no longer Shadow and receives the standard purification benefits.

    ⭐

    Purification IV Boost

    Purification increases each IV by 2 points, up to the normal IV maximum of 15.

    Should You Purify Litten?

    Purification is a decision rather than an automatic upgrade. If you want to preserve the Shadow status for collection or future battle use, consider keeping the Pokémon Shadow.

    💎 Purified Litten

    Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before spending Stardust or making a permanent decision.

    14 • SIZE

    XXL & XXS Litten

    Pokémon GO can record size categories such as XXL and XXS. These size records are separate from IVs and CP.

    📏 XXL Litten Extra Large

    An XXL Litten has a notably large recorded size compared with the normal range.

    🔹 XXS Litten Extra Small

    An XXS Litten has a notably small recorded size compared with the normal range.

    XXL and XXS vs IVs

    Size and IVs are different characteristics. A Litten can be XXL or XXS without being a perfect IV Pokémon, and a 15/15/15 Litten does not automatically have to be XXL or XXS.

    21 • FAQ

    Litten FAQ

    What type is Litten?

    Litten is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

    What is Litten weak against?

    Litten is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

    What does Litten evolve into?

    Litten evolves into Torracat and eventually into Incineroar.

    Is Shiny Litten available?

    Shiny Litten can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

    What is a 100% Litten?

    A 100% Litten has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is Shadow Litten?

    Shadow Litten is the Shadow variant of Litten. Shadow Pokémon have different battle mechanics from their regular counterparts.

    Should I purify Shadow Litten?

    Not automatically. Compare your goals for the Shadow and Purified versions before purifying.

    What is Lucky Litten?

    Lucky Litten is a Litten that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

    What is XXL Litten?

    XXL Litten is an extra-large size record. Size is separate from IVs.

    What is XXS Litten?

    XXS Litten is an extra-small size record. It can be collected independently of IV quality.

    Is Litten good for raids?

    Litten is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its evolved forms are more relevant for battle investment.

    Is Litten good for PvP?

    Litten has limited competitive relevance compared with stronger evolved Pokémon and other available Fire-type options.

    Should I keep a perfect Litten?

    Yes. A 15/15/15 Litten can be a valuable collection target and can be evolved into its later forms.

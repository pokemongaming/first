01 • OVERVIEW

Tapu Koko Overview

Tapu Koko is a Legendary Electric/Fairy Pokémon introduced in Generation 7. It is the guardian deity associated with Melemele Island in the Alola region. In Pokémon GO, Tapu Koko does not evolve and does not have a conventional evolution family.

02 • TYPE

Tapu Koko Type

Tapu Koko has a unique Electric/Fairy dual typing. This combination gives it a useful collection of resistances and only two normal weaknesses.

🧚 Fairy Secondary Type

Why Tapu Koko's Typing Matters

Electric/Fairy is one of Tapu Koko's defining features. Electric attacks give it an offensive identity against Water and Flying targets, while Fairy typing provides useful defensive interactions against several common attack types.

11 • PVP

Tapu Koko PvP Guide

Master League

Tapu Koko's high CP ceiling makes Master League the most natural open GO Battle League environment for a fully powered standard Tapu Koko. Its Electric/Fairy typing can create useful matchups, while Ground-type attacks remain an important threat.

Great League & Ultra League

Tapu Koko's Legendary status and high maximum CP make it less naturally suited to traditional Great League and Ultra League investment. League-specific cups can change that evaluation, so always check the current format.

PvP Reminder

IV ranking for PvP is different from simply looking for 15/15/15. League-specific IV spreads, CP limits and the current meta should be considered.

14 • PURIFIED

Purified Tapu Koko

A Purified Tapu Koko would be the result of purifying an eligible Shadow Tapu Koko. Since Shadow Tapu Koko is not currently listed as implemented, there is no normal current acquisition path for a Purified Tapu Koko.

🌟

Purified Tapu Koko

Status: Not currently obtainable through a standard Shadow Tapu Koko route.

General Purified Pokémon Mechanics

When a Shadow Pokémon is purified, its appraisal improves, powering it up becomes cheaper, and it learns Return. Purification also removes its Shadow status.

Important

Purified status should not be confused with Shiny, Lucky, XXL or 100% IV status. These are different Pokémon attributes.

25 • FAQ

Tapu Koko FAQ

What type is Tapu Koko?

Tapu Koko is Electric/Fairy type.

What is Tapu Koko weak to?

Tapu Koko is weak to Ground and Poison attacks.

What is Tapu Koko's max CP?

Tapu Koko reaches 3,582 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are the Tapu Koko raid catch CPs?

Standard raid encounters are approximately 1,730–1,810 CP, while Rainy or Cloudy weather boosted encounters are approximately 2,162–2,263 CP.

Is Shiny Tapu Koko available?

Yes. Shiny Tapu Koko is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Tapu Koko available?

Current database information does not list Shadow Tapu Koko as an implemented standard form. Availability should be updated if Pokémon GO officially introduces it.

Can Tapu Koko be Purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Since Shadow Tapu Koko is not currently listed as implemented, there is no standard current Purified Tapu Koko acquisition route.

What is a Lucky Tapu Koko?

A Lucky Tapu Koko is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Trade mechanic and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Is a Lucky Tapu Koko automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have an IV floor but are not guaranteed to have 15/15/15 IVs.

What is a 100% Tapu Koko?

A 100% Tapu Koko has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Tapu Koko?

XXL Tapu Koko is an exceptionally large size variant. Size is independent from IV quality.

What is an XXS Tapu Koko?

XXS Tapu Koko is an exceptionally small size variant. It can still have excellent or perfect IVs.

Can Tapu Koko be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Tapu Koko can have both statuses.

Can Tapu Koko be Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. A Shiny Tapu Koko can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Tapu Koko be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are independent attributes.

Does Tapu Koko evolve?

No. Tapu Koko does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Tapu Koko good for PvP?

Tapu Koko can have Master League relevance, depending on the current moveset and metagame.

Is Tapu Koko good for raids?

Tapu Koko can be useful in Electric-focused roles, although its ranking depends on current moves and competing Electric attackers.

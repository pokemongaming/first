Perfect Bewear

A 15/15/15 Bewear is considered a perfect-IV specimen. However, a perfect IV combination is not automatically the best possible PvP spread because different leagues have different CP limitations.

100% IV Bewear

A 100% Bewear has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are the maximum possible individual values.

💯 Perfect Bewear

IV combination: 15 / 15 / 15

A perfect Bewear is valuable for collectors and can be useful for high-level investment when its moveset and battle role justify the cost.

How to Identify a 100% Bewear

  • Attack IV is 15.
  • Defense IV is 15.
  • HP IV is 15.
  • Use the in-game Appraisal screen or an IV tool to confirm the exact IV combination.

Bewear PvP Guide

Bewear's dual typing, high HP and Fighting access can make it interesting in PvP. Its usefulness depends heavily on league rules, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Great League Bewear requires careful IV selection because the 1,500 CP limit makes stat distribution particularly important. A low-Attack, high-bulk IV spread can sometimes be preferable to a traditional 15/15/15 specimen.

Ultra League

Bewear can be evaluated as an Ultra League option when its available moveset and current meta matchups support the investment.

Master League

Bewear's maximum CP is considerably lower than the CP ceiling of many Master League specialists, making it less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League competition.

Bewear FAQ

What type is Bewear?

Bewear is a Normal/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Bewear weak to?

Bewear is weak to Psychic, Flying and Fairy-type attacks.

What Pokémon does Bewear evolve from?

Bewear evolves from Stufful.

Is Shiny Bewear available?

Shiny Bewear can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution from Shiny Stufful when the Shiny is obtainable.

Is Shadow Bewear available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket mechanics and rotations. Check current game data before assuming it is obtainable.

What is a 100% Bewear?

A 100% Bewear has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Is Lucky Bewear good?

Lucky Bewear can be attractive because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. Its usefulness still depends on IVs, moveset and battle role.

What is XXL Bewear?

XXL Bewear is a particularly large Bewear. XXL status is independent of IVs and CP.

What is XXS Bewear?

XXS Bewear is a particularly small Bewear. XXS status is independent of IVs and CP.

Is Bewear good for raids?

Bewear can provide Fighting-type utility, but trainers should compare its current performance against other Fighting attackers before investing heavily for raids.

Is Bewear good for PvP?

Bewear can be evaluated for PvP depending on league, moveset and the current metagame. IV selection is especially important in CP-limited leagues.

Is a 100% Bewear always the best PvP Bewear?

No. In CP-limited leagues, a different IV spread may provide a better stat distribution than 15/15/15.

Are Shiny, Lucky, XXL and 100% Bewear the same thing?

No. These are separate characteristics. A single Bewear can potentially combine multiple desirable characteristics depending on how it was obtained.

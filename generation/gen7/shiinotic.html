Shiinotic

Shiinotic is a Grass/Fairy-type Pokémon from Generation 7. In Pokémon GO, it evolves from Morelull and is known for its unusual typing, Fairy-type pressure and access to moves such as Moonblast, Seed Bomb and Sludge Bomb.

Why Shiinotic's Typing Matters

Shiinotic's Grass/Fairy combination gives it several useful resistances, including reduced damage from Water, Grass, Electric, Ground, Fighting and Dark attacks. Its Fairy typing also provides a particularly strong matchup against Dragon-type damage.

10 • PVP

Shiinotic PvP Guide

Shiinotic's maximum CP of 2,104 means it naturally fits much better into Great League and Ultra League considerations than Master League.

Great League

Great League is the most natural CP-limited environment to test Shiinotic. Its Grass/Fairy typing gives it useful matchups against several Water, Ground, Grass, Dark and Fighting threats, while Poison, Fire, Flying, Ice and Steel attacks remain important dangers.

Ultra League

Shiinotic can be evaluated in Ultra League, although reaching the league's upper CP range may require substantial investment.

Master League

Standard Shiinotic's 2,104 maximum CP makes it a poor fit for Master League compared with Pokémon capable of reaching much higher CP values.

PvP Tip

Don't judge a Shiinotic solely by its IV percentage. CP-cap leagues reward specific IV distributions, stat products and matchup performance.

Should You Purify Shiinotic?

Purification should not be treated as an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before spending resources, particularly if the Shadow version has good IVs or collector value.

  • Check the Shadow IVs before purifying.
  • Consider the Pokémon's current and future PvP role.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified resource requirements.
  • Consider whether Return is useful to you.
  • Keep a valuable Shadow specimen unpurified if you specifically want its Shadow status.

16 • XXS

XXS Shiinotic

XXS is the smallest size category available for eligible Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Size is separate from IV quality and does not automatically indicate stronger battle performance.

🔹 XXS Shiinotic

A very small Shiinotic that can be valuable to collectors who are completing size variants.

Does XXS Increase Shiinotic's Stats?

No. XXS status should not be confused with IVs. A Pokémon can be XXS with poor IVs, excellent IVs or perfect IVs.

  • XXS is a size classification.
  • XXS does not mean 100% IV.
  • XXS can be valuable for collectors.
  • An XXS Hundo is especially interesting as a collection specimen.

Stakataka

Stakataka is a Rock and Steel-type Ultra Beast from the Alola region. In Pokémon GO, its huge Defense stat gives it a distinctive defensive profile, although its low HP and double weaknesses to Fighting and Ground limit its overall battle performance.

01 • OVERVIEW

Stakataka Overview

Stakataka is Pokédex #805 and a Generation 7 Rock/Steel Ultra Beast. It has one of the highest Defense stats among Pokémon GO species, but its relatively low HP prevents that Defense from making it as durable in practice as its stat alone suggests.

02 • TYPE

Stakataka Type

🪨
Rock / Steel Dual Type

Why Stakataka's Typing Matters

Rock/Steel gives Stakataka a large collection of resistances, including an immunity to Poison in Pokémon GO's type-effectiveness system. However, the typing also creates major vulnerabilities: Fighting and Ground deal double super-effective damage, while Water is super effective.

03 • TYPE EFFECTIVENESS

Stakataka's Rock/Steel typing gives it several resistances, but Fighting and Ground are especially dangerous because both deal double super-effective damage.

For most players, Stakataka should not be a first-choice investment for a Rock or Steel raid team. Its primary value is more likely to come from collection goals, Ultra Beast completion and personal preference than from maximizing raid damage.

09 • PVP

Stakataka PvP Guide

Stakataka's excellent Defense looks promising for PvP, but its low HP and major weaknesses to Fighting, Ground and Water make it difficult to justify as a competitive staple.

Great League

A standard Stakataka has a high CP requirement and is not naturally positioned as a top Great League option. League-specific CP restrictions should always be checked before investing.

Ultra League

Stakataka can technically be evaluated in Ultra League, but its overall matchup profile remains difficult. Fighting and Ground weaknesses are especially important because both are common attacking categories.

Master League

Stakataka can reach a much more appropriate CP range in Master League, but its current overall battle performance does not make it a priority competitive investment.

PvP Moves to Test

A 100% Stakataka has 15 Attack IV, 15 Defense IV and 15 HP IV. It represents the highest possible IV combination for a standard Stakataka.

A perfect IV encounter is significantly rarer than an ordinary encounter. For collectors, a hundo Stakataka can therefore be worth keeping even when the Pokémon is not a top-tier raid attacker.

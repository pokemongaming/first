Popplio

Popplio is a Water-type Pokémon from the Alola region and one of the three first partner Pokémon introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon. In Pokémon GO, Popplio can evolve into Brionne and eventually Primarina.

Popplio Overview

Popplio is a Water-type starter Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 7. It is part of a three-stage evolution family and can eventually evolve into the powerful Water/Fairy-type Primarina.

Why Popplio's Typing Matters

Popplio's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward battle profile. Water-type attacks are useful against several common Pokémon types, although Popplio itself must watch for Electric and Grass attacks.

Popplio Attack, Defense & HP

Popplio has a comparatively low offensive profile because it is the first stage of a three-stage evolution family. Its long-term value comes largely from evolving it into Brionne and Primarina.

    Lucky Popplio

    Lucky Popplio is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon are useful for players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Pokémon.

    🍀

    Lucky Popplio

    A Popplio with the Lucky status obtained through a trade.

    ✨

    Lucky Background

    The Lucky status provides a distinctive visual indicator in the Pokémon collection.

    Why Keep a Lucky Popplio?

    • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
    • Lucky Popplio can be evolved into Lucky Brionne.
    • A Lucky Popplio can eventually become Lucky Primarina.
    • Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor after a successful trade.

    100% IV Popplio

    A 100% IV Popplio has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. This is commonly called a Hundo.

    15 Attack IV
    15 Defense IV
    15 HP IV
    100% Popplio = 15/15/15

    A perfect Popplio has maximum IVs in all three categories. Because Popplio evolves twice, a perfect specimen can be kept as a long-term evolution project.

    Should You Keep a 100% Popplio?

    • Keep it if you collect perfect IV Pokémon.
    • Consider evolving it into a perfect Brionne.
    • Consider eventually completing the Primarina evolution.
    • Check the evolved Pokémon's current battle usefulness.

    XXL Popplio

    XXL Popplio is a size-variant Popplio that is larger than the standard size range. Size variants are separate from IV quality and do not automatically indicate stronger battle performance.

    📏

    XXL Popplio

    A particularly large Popplio that can be valuable for size-focused collectors.

    🏆

    Collection Value

    An XXL Popplio can be kept as a size-variant collection piece regardless of its IVs.

    Important:

    XXL size and IV quality are separate characteristics. An XXL Popplio is not automatically a 100% IV Pokémon.

    XXS Popplio

    XXS Popplio is a particularly small Popplio. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is a size characteristic rather than an indication of battle IV quality.

    📐

    XXS Popplio

    A very small Popplio that can be valuable for collectors who track Pokémon size variants.

    🔎

    Size Collection

    Collectors can keep both XXL and XXS Popplio as separate size milestones.

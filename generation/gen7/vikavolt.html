Vikavolt

Vikavolt is a Bug/Electric-type Pokémon from the Alola region. In Pokémon GO, its high Attack gives it useful offensive potential as both a Bug-type and Electric-type attacker, while its relatively low Defense and HP make it more fragile than its impressive Attack suggests.

Vikavolt Overview

Why Vikavolt's Typing Matters

Bug/Electric gives Vikavolt a useful combination of offensive options. Bug attacks are useful against Grass, Psychic and Dark targets, while Electric attacks are especially useful against Flying and Water Pokémon.

15 • PVP

Vikavolt PvP Guide

Vikavolt can be used in Trainer Battles, although its performance varies considerably by league and matchup.

Great League

Great League is generally the most natural place to experiment with Vikavolt. Its Bug/Electric typing gives it access to useful offensive coverage, although its low bulk can make shield management important.

Ultra League

Vikavolt can be used in Ultra League, but it faces significant competition from bulkier Bug and Electric Pokémon.

Master League

Vikavolt's maximum CP is high enough to enter Master League, but its limited bulk and competition from stronger options make it a more specialized choice.

PvP starting moveset

Volt Switch + X-Scissor + Fly

16 • RESOURCES

Vikavolt Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Vikavolt requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. The amount required increases significantly as you approach the highest levels.

  • Save Grubbin Candy for evolution and power-ups.

17 • AVAILABILITY

Vikavolt Availability

Vikavolt is obtained through the Grubbin evolutionary family. Availability of Grubbin, Charjabug and Vikavolt encounters can change through events, research and seasonal rotations.

Evolution Route

Grubbin → Charjabug → Vikavolt. Charjabug requires 100 Candy and a Magnetic Lure Module to evolve into Vikavolt.

Shiny Availability

Shiny Vikavolt is available, so collectors can complete both regular and Shiny versions of the Vikavolt family.

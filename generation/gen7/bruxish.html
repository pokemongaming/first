Shadow Bruxish

Shadow Bruxish is the Shadow variation of Bruxish. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately from standard Pokémon because the Shadow mechanic changes their battle profile.

🌑 Shadow Bruxish

Shadow status can increase offensive potential while reducing defensive durability, creating a different investment decision compared with a regular Bruxish.

Shadow Bruxish Considerations

  • Evaluate the Shadow version separately from regular Bruxish.
  • Consider the increased resource cost.
  • Check Charged Move availability.
  • Avoid purifying automatically without comparing the result.

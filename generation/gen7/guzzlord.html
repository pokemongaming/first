Guzzlord Overview

Guzzlord is an Ultra Beast introduced from the Alola region. Unlike a normal Pokémon evolution family, Guzzlord does not evolve into or from another Pokémon.

Its combination of Dark and Dragon typing gives Guzzlord access to several useful offensive and defensive interactions. Its extremely high HP is one of its most distinctive characteristics.

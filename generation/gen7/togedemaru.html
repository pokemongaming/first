Electric / Steel Dual Type Pokémon
⚡ ELECTRIC ⚙ STEEL

Why Togedemaru's Typing Is Important

Electric and Steel is an excellent defensive combination. Togedemaru resists a large number of attack types, including Flying, Poison and Steel attacks at particularly strong resistance levels.

The major weakness players need to remember is Ground. Because both Electric and Steel are weak to Ground, Togedemaru takes especially heavy damage from Ground-type attacks.

07 • PVP

Togedemaru PvP Guide

Togedemaru's Electric and Steel typing gives it interesting defensive matchups in lower CP formats. Its actual usefulness depends heavily on the current cup rules, moveset and meta.

Great League

Great League is generally the most natural PvP format to investigate for Togedemaru because its maximum CP fits comfortably above the league cap, allowing players to optimize IV spreads around the 1,500 CP limit.

Ultra League

Togedemaru can technically be powered toward the Ultra League range, but its maximum CP of 2,442 means it does not reach the full 2,500 CP limit.

Master League

Togedemaru's maximum CP is too low for it to compete comfortably against the highest-stat Pokémon normally used in Master League environments.

PvP Reminder

PvP rankings can change when moves are adjusted, new Pokémon enter the game or limited-format cups rotate. Update live recommendations separately from this permanent species guide.

Shadow Togedemaru Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Togedemaru's typing. Ground remains the most dangerous weakness, followed by Fire and Fighting attacks.

Shadow Togedemaru Investment

  • Check the IVs before deciding on a long-term investment.
  • Consider the increased Stardust and Candy costs.

18 • FAQ

Togedemaru FAQ

What type is Togedemaru?

Togedemaru is an Electric and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Togedemaru weak to?

Togedemaru is weak to Ground, Fire and Fighting attacks, with Ground being its most significant weakness.

Does Togedemaru evolve?

No. Togedemaru does not currently have a normal evolution chain.

What is Togedemaru's maximum CP?

A perfect Togedemaru can reach approximately 2,442 CP at Level 50.

Is Shiny Togedemaru different in battle?

No. Shiny status is visual and does not provide different base battle stats.

What is a 100% Togedemaru?

A perfect Togedemaru has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is the 100% raid CP for Togedemaru?

A perfect Level 20 Togedemaru is 1,234 CP, while a weather-boosted Level 25 perfect specimen is 1,543 CP.

Is Lucky Togedemaru good?

Lucky Togedemaru can be an attractive investment because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Does XXL Togedemaru have more CP?

XXL size should not be confused with IVs, level or CP. Size and battle IVs are separate systems.

Does XXS Togedemaru have weaker stats?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not automatically mean the Pokémon has weaker IVs or lower battle potential.

Is Shadow Togedemaru stronger than regular Togedemaru?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile. The better version depends on whether you prioritize damage, survivability, PvP or collection goals.

Should I purify Shadow Togedemaru?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your intended use before making the decision.

Why Drampa's Typing Matters

Drampa's combination of Normal and Dragon typing gives it an unusual defensive profile. It takes increased damage from Fighting, Ice, Dragon and Fairy attacks, while its typing reduces damage from several other attack types.

22 • FAQ

Drampa FAQ

What type is Drampa?

Drampa is a Normal/Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Drampa weak against?

Drampa is weak to Fighting, Ice, Dragon and Fairy-type attacks.

What is Drampa's max CP?

Drampa reaches 3,294 CP at Level 50 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Drampa available?

Yes. Shiny Drampa is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Drampa available?

Shadow Drampa is not currently available according to current Pokémon GO database information.

Can I get Purified Drampa?

Not currently through the normal Shadow-to-Purified mechanic, because Shadow Drampa is not currently available.

How do I get Lucky Drampa?

A Drampa can become Lucky through a Lucky Trade.

What is a 100% Drampa?

A 100% or Hundo Drampa has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a Hundo Drampa?

A Level 50 Hundo Drampa reaches 3,294 CP.

What is XXL Drampa?

XXL Drampa is an extra-large size variant. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Drampa?

XXS Drampa is an extra-small size variant. It can have any legal IV combination, including 15/15/15.

Is Drampa good for raids?

Drampa is not generally regarded as a top-tier dedicated raid attacker. Its value is more dependent on collection, PvP and personal use.

Does Drampa evolve?

Drampa does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Can a Shiny Drampa be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Drampa can also become Lucky through trading.

Can a Shiny Drampa be 100% IV?

Yes. A Shiny Drampa can have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can an XXL Drampa be a Hundo?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Drampa can theoretically have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can an XXS Drampa be a Hundo?

Yes. An XXS Drampa can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

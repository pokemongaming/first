Why Nihilego's Typing Matters

The combination of Rock and Poison gives Nihilego access to useful same-type attacks while also creating several important weaknesses. Its Poison typing is especially useful when facing Fairy-type opponents.

What Is a 100% Nihilego?

A 100% Nihilego has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO, this combination is displayed as a 4★ appraisal.

100% Nihilego

A perfect Nihilego has maximum IVs in all three appraisal categories.

💯 Perfect Nihilego

A 15/15/15 Nihilego is a perfect-IV specimen. It is a valuable target for collectors and players who want a maximum-IV Ultra Beast.

15 / 15 / 15 IV

Should You Keep a 100% Nihilego?

Purified Nihilego

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. Because Shadow Nihilego is currently not listed as available, a normal Purified Nihilego should not be presented as an obtainable current form.

🤍 Purified Nihilego

A Purified Nihilego would require an eligible Shadow Nihilego first. Since Shadow Nihilego is currently not available, this is a future-status section rather than a current acquisition method.

Not Currently Obtainable

How Purification Normally Works

When an eligible Shadow Pokémon is purified, its IVs increase and it forgets Frustration in favor of Return. Purification also changes its resource requirements and removes the Shadow battle bonus.

Lucky Nihilego

Lucky Nihilego is a special trade-related status rather than a different species or form.

🍀 Lucky Nihilego

A Nihilego can become Lucky through trading when the trade results in the Lucky Pokémon status.

Trade Attribute

Why Keep a Lucky Nihilego?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.
  • A Lucky Nihilego can still be Shiny.
  • Lucky status is separate from IV perfection.
  • Lucky Nihilego can be useful for long-term investment.
Collection note:

Lucky, Shiny and perfect IV are separate attributes. A Pokémon being Lucky does not automatically mean it is 100% IV.

XXL Nihilego

XXL Nihilego is an exceptionally large Nihilego that can be useful for collection and Pokémon GO Showcase-related activities.

📏 XXL Nihilego

XXL is a size attribute rather than a different Nihilego form. An XXL Nihilego remains a normal Nihilego species.

Size Attribute

XXL Nihilego Collection Tips

  • Check its size before transferring duplicates.

  • XXL status does not guarantee perfect IVs.

Nihilego FAQ

What type is Nihilego?

Nihilego is a Rock- and Poison-type Ultra Beast.

What is Nihilego weak to?

Nihilego is weak to Ground, Psychic, Steel and Water attacks, with Ground being its strongest weakness.

What is Nihilego's Max CP?

Nihilego's Level 50 maximum CP is 4,465 under standard conditions.

Is Shiny Nihilego available?

Yes. Shiny Nihilego is available through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Nihilego available?

Current database information lists Shadow Nihilego as not available. Check future Pokémon GO updates for changes.

Can you get Purified Nihilego?

Purified Pokémon require an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Since Shadow Nihilego is currently unavailable, a Purified Nihilego is not currently obtainable.

Can Nihilego be Lucky?

Yes. A Nihilego can receive Lucky status through trading if the trade produces a Lucky Pokémon.

What is a 100% Nihilego?

A 100% Nihilego has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Nihilego be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size attribute rather than a separate Pokémon form.

Can Nihilego be XXS?

Yes. XXS is also a size attribute and does not change Nihilego's species or typing.

Does Nihilego evolve?

No. Nihilego does not have a normal Pokémon GO evolution chain.

Is Nihilego good for raids?

Nihilego can be particularly useful as a Poison-type attacker and has access to Rock-type coverage as well.

Is Nihilego good in Master League?

Nihilego can have relevance in higher CP PvP formats, although its performance depends on the current moves, meta and team composition.

Lycanroc Overview

Lycanroc is a Rock-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 7. In Pokémon GO, Lycanroc has three forms: Midday Form, Midnight Form and Dusk Form. The form you obtain depends on the Rockruff and evolution conditions involved.

What Does Rock Typing Mean?

All three Lycanroc forms are Rock type in Pokémon GO. This gives Lycanroc the same general weakness and resistance profile regardless of whether you have Midday, Midnight or Dusk Form.

Is Lycanroc Good for Raids?

Lycanroc can function as a Rock-type attacker, but it faces significant competition from stronger Rock attackers. Its main appeal is often its different forms and collection value rather than being an essential raid investment.

🪨
Rock Role

Rock Throw and Stone Edge provide a straightforward Rock attacking build.

⚔️
Damage

Lycanroc can deal useful Rock damage but is not generally a top priority compared with elite Rock attackers.

⭐
Collection

Its three forms, Shiny status and size variants make Lycanroc attractive to collectors.

Should You Power Up Lycanroc?

If you already have stronger Rock-type attackers, Lycanroc usually does not need to be a first-priority Stardust project. A high-IV or Shiny specimen can still be worth keeping for collection and personal use.

100% IV Lycanroc

A perfect Lycanroc has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Because Lycanroc has three forms, the resulting CP depends on which form you have.

What Does 100% Lycanroc Mean?

A 100% Lycanroc is a Pokémon with a 15/15/15 IV combination. It is commonly called a hundo by Pokémon GO players. A perfect IV Lycanroc is especially useful for collectors who want the best specimen of each form.

Collector Tip

If you collect all three forms, a 15/15/15 Midday, Midnight and Dusk Lycanroc set can make a particularly nice long-term collection goal.

SHINY AVAILABLE

Shiny Lycanroc

Shiny Lycanroc is available in Pokémon GO. Shiny status is a separate cosmetic attribute and does not create a fourth Lycanroc form.

✨

Shiny Midday

A Shiny Midday Form Lycanroc can be kept as a special collection Pokémon.

✨

Shiny Midnight

Shiny status can be combined with the Midnight Form when an eligible Shiny Rockruff evolves.

✨

Shiny Dusk

Shiny Dusk Lycanroc is a particularly desirable target for collectors.

Important:

Shiny, Lucky, XXS, XXL and 100% IV are attributes or statuses rather than additional species forms.

Rockruff to Lycanroc Evolution

Lycanroc evolves from Rockruff using Candy, but the resulting form depends on the evolution conditions and the Rockruff involved.

☀️

Midday

Rockruff can evolve into Midday Form under the appropriate daytime evolution conditions.

🌙

Midnight

Rockruff can evolve into Midnight Form under the appropriate nighttime evolution conditions.

🌅

Dusk

Dusk Form has special eligibility requirements associated with the Rockruff being evolved.

Evolution Cost

Lycanroc evolution costs 50 Rockruff Candy.

Important:

Lycanroc's form is not something you should assume can simply be switched after evolution. Check the Rockruff's eligibility and the current in-game evolution conditions before evolving.

Lycanroc PvP Guide

Lycanroc's PvP performance varies by form and league. Its Rock typing gives it useful offensive coverage, but its overall PvP value is generally limited compared with stronger meta options.

Great League

Lycanroc can be used in Great League when its CP fits the league requirements, but it is generally a niche choice rather than a first-line Rock-type option.

Ultra League

Some Lycanroc builds can fit Ultra League, although trainers should compare the selected form against the current meta before investing heavily.

Master League

Lycanroc's maximum CP is substantially lower than the Pokémon commonly used in Master League, making it a less natural choice for unrestricted Master League play.

Lycanroc FAQ

What type is Lycanroc?

Lycanroc is a pure Rock-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

How many Lycanroc forms are there?

Pokémon GO has Midday Form, Midnight Form and Dusk Form Lycanroc.

What is Lycanroc's highest Max CP?

Midnight Form has the highest listed Level 50 maximum CP at 3102.

Which Lycanroc has the highest Attack?

Dusk Form has the highest listed Attack stat among the three forms, at 234.

Is Shiny Lycanroc available?

Yes. Shiny Lycanroc is available in Pokémon GO.

What is Lycanroc weak against?

Lycanroc is weak to Fighting, Grass, Ground, Steel and Water-type attacks.

What is the best Lycanroc moveset?

Rock Throw and Stone Edge are the primary Rock-focused moveset to consider for Lycanroc.

Is Lycanroc good for raids?

Lycanroc can be used as a Rock-type attacker, but stronger Rock attackers generally make better long-term raid investments.

What is 100% Lycanroc?

A 100% Lycanroc has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Lycanroc be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a status obtained through trading and is not a separate Lycanroc form.

Can Lycanroc be XXL?

Yes. Lycanroc can have different size classifications, including XXL.

Can Lycanroc be XXS?

Yes. XXS is a size classification and is separate from Lycanroc's three forms.

Is Shadow Lycanroc available?

Shadow should be treated as a separate Pokémon status, not one of Lycanroc's three standard forms. Current Shadow availability should be verified before claiming that Shadow Lycanroc can currently be obtained.

What is Purified Lycanroc?

Purified is a status associated with a Pokémon that was previously Shadow. It is not a fourth Lycanroc form.

Does Lycanroc evolve?

Lycanroc evolves from Rockruff. The resulting Lycanroc form depends on the applicable evolution conditions and Rockruff eligibility.

Can Shiny Lycanroc be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially have both statuses.

Which Lycanroc form is best?

There is no single answer for every player. Dusk has the highest Attack, Midnight has the highest listed Max CP and HP, while Midday sits between them in several stats. Your choice can also depend on collection goals and current move availability.

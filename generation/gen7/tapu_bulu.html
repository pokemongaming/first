Tapu Bulu Overview

Why Tapu Bulu's Typing Matters

Grass/Fairy is a valuable combination because it gives Tapu Bulu several useful resistances while also creating a very clear list of weaknesses. The typing is particularly valuable against Water-, Ground-, Dark-, Fighting- and Dragon-oriented opponents.

08 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Tapu Bulu PvP Guide

Great League

Tapu Bulu is not a natural Great League Pokémon because its Legendary raid acquisition and CP profile make lower CP optimization difficult.

Ultra League

Tapu Bulu can be built for Ultra League with carefully selected IVs, although it faces significant competition from other Grass and Fairy Pokémon.

Master League

Master League is the most natural high-level PvP format for Tapu Bulu. Its Grass/Fairy typing provides useful matchups against several Water-, Ground-, Dark- and Dragon-oriented Pokémon.

Recommended PvP Build

Bullet Seed + Grass Knot + Nature's Madness

Powering a Legendary Pokémon to high levels can require a significant amount of Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Tapu Bulu reaches Level 50 CP of 3,865, serious Master League or collection projects can become expensive.

22 • FAQ

Tapu Bulu FAQ

What type is Tapu Bulu?

Tapu Bulu is a Grass/Fairy-type Legendary Pokémon.

What is Tapu Bulu weak to?

Tapu Bulu is weak to Poison, Fire, Flying, Ice and Steel attacks. Poison is its strongest weakness.

What is Tapu Bulu's max CP?

Tapu Bulu's Level 50 maximum CP is 3,865.

What is a 100% IV Tapu Bulu?

A 100% IV Tapu Bulu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

What CP is a hundo Tapu Bulu from a raid?

A perfect 15/15/15 Tapu Bulu caught from a normal Level 20 raid has 1,953 CP. A weather-boosted Level 25 hundo is 2,442 CP.

Is Shiny Tapu Bulu available?

Yes. Shiny Tapu Bulu is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Lucky Tapu Bulu possible?

Yes. Tapu Bulu can have Lucky status as a result of trading mechanics.

What is the best moveset for Tapu Bulu?

Bullet Seed with Nature's Madness is a strong Fairy build, while Bullet Seed with Grass Knot is a strong Grass-oriented raid build.

Is Tapu Bulu good for raids?

Tapu Bulu can be useful as a Grass- or Fairy-type attacker, although its overall raid value depends on the specific matchup and the alternatives available.

Is Tapu Bulu good in Master League?

Tapu Bulu can have meaningful Master League utility thanks to its Grass/Fairy typing, high CP and access to moves such as Grass Knot and Nature's Madness.

Does Tapu Bulu evolve?

No. Tapu Bulu does not have a normal evolution chain.

Can Tapu Bulu be XXL?

Yes. Tapu Bulu can appear in the XXL size category.

Can Tapu Bulu be XXS?

Yes. Tapu Bulu can appear in the XXS size category.

Does XXL Tapu Bulu have higher Attack?

No. XXL is a size classification and should not be treated as a separate species with higher base Attack.

Does XXS Tapu Bulu have lower Defense?

No. XXS is a size classification rather than a separate battle-stat form.

Is Shadow Tapu Bulu available?

Shadow availability should be verified against the current Pokémon GO game data. This page intentionally does not invent a Shadow encounter method.

Is Purified Tapu Bulu available?

Purified status depends on a corresponding Shadow Pokémon mechanic. This page should be updated if Shadow Tapu Bulu becomes obtainable.

What is the best Tapu Bulu to keep?

For battle, prioritize a strong moveset and useful IVs. For collectors, Shiny, Hundo, Lucky, XXL and XXS Tapu Bulu can each be valuable for different reasons.

Necrozma Fusion Forms

One of Necrozma's most important features in Pokémon GO is its ability to fuse with Solgaleo or Lunala.

SUN FUSION

Dusk Mane Necrozma

Dusk Mane Necrozma is created by fusing Necrozma with Solgaleo. It has Psychic / Steel typing and has a distinct role from regular Necrozma.

MOON FUSION

Dawn Wings Necrozma

Dawn Wings Necrozma is created by fusing Necrozma with Lunala. It has Psychic / Ghost typing and has its own battle profile.

XXL & XXS Necrozma

Pokémon GO can record size information separately from IVs and battle statistics. A Pokémon's size status should not be confused with its IV percentage.

📏 XXL Necrozma

XXL Necrozma refers to an exceptionally large size classification. Size is separate from IVs, Shiny status and Lucky status.

📐 XXS Necrozma

XXS Necrozma refers to an exceptionally small size classification. A XXS Pokémon can have any IV combination.

Important:

XXL or XXS status does not mean the Pokémon is automatically stronger, weaker, or 100% IV.

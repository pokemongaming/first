Why Jangmo-o's Typing Matters

Jangmo-o's pure Dragon typing gives it several useful resistances, although it also leaves it vulnerable to Ice, Dragon and Fairy-type attacks. Its main competitive value comes from its evolutionary potential rather than its first-stage battle performance.

Jangmo-o PvP Guide

Jangmo-o's first-stage stats and relatively low CP ceiling mean that it is generally less important in PvP than its evolved forms.

Great League

Jangmo-o can be considered for niche Great League use, but players should compare its performance with its evolved forms and other Dragon-type options before investing resources.

Ultra League

Jangmo-o's lower maximum CP makes it less naturally suited to Ultra League compared with stronger evolved Pokémon.

Master League

Jangmo-o is not designed for Master League. Its later evolution, Kommo-o, is much more relevant for players building a Dragon-type Pokémon for higher CP formats.

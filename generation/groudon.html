What Is Primal Reversion?

Primal Reversion is a special transformation available to Groudon. Instead of permanently evolving into a new Pokémon, Groudon temporarily enters its Primal form.

When Primal Reversion is active, Groudon gains the characteristics and gameplay benefits associated with Primal Groudon.

Important: Primal Reversion should not be treated as a normal evolution. Your underlying Pokémon remains Groudon.

Primal Groudon Type

Unlike standard Groudon, which is a pure Ground type, Primal Groudon is a Ground / Fire type. This change is important when evaluating its weaknesses and resistances.

Groudon FAQ

Is Groudon a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Groudon is a Legendary Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 3.

What type is Groudon?

Regular Groudon is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Groudon weak to?

Groudon is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Does Groudon evolve?

Groudon does not have a normal evolution. It can instead undergo Primal Reversion into Primal Groudon.

Is Shiny Groudon available?

Yes. Shiny Groudon can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Groudon good for raids?

Groudon can be an excellent Ground-type raid attacker when equipped with an appropriate Ground-focused moveset.

Groudon can have significant Master League relevance, although its effectiveness depends on moveset, IVs, shields and the current metagame.

Why Bulbasaur's Typing Matters

Bulbasaur's Grass and Poison typing gives it a useful combination of offensive and defensive matchups. Its Grass typing allows it to pressure Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while its Poison typing adds additional resistances and affects its weakness profile.

09 • PVP

Bulbasaur PvP Guide

Bulbasaur is rarely the first choice for open Great League play because its evolved forms generally provide more battle value. However, special cups and Little League-style formats can create situations where unevolved Pokémon become relevant.

Great League

Bulbasaur can technically fit within the Great League CP limit, but Ivysaur and Venusaur generally provide stronger overall battle performance. Cup restrictions can change this assessment.

Little Cup & Special Cups

When a cup allows Bulbasaur, its typing and access to Grass and Poison attacks can make it an interesting special-format option.

PvP Investment

Before powering up a PvP Bulbasaur, check the current cup rules, CP cap and recommended IV spread for the specific format.

19 • FAQ

Bulbasaur FAQ

Is Bulbasaur a starter Pokémon?

Yes. Bulbasaur is one of the three original Kanto starter Pokémon.

What type is Bulbasaur?

Bulbasaur is a Grass/Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Bulbasaur weak to?

Bulbasaur is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying and Psychic attacks.

What is Bulbasaur's Max CP?

Bulbasaur has a relatively low CP ceiling compared with its final evolution, Venusaur.

Is Bulbasaur good for raids?

Bulbasaur is not normally considered a strong raid attacker. Its main value comes from evolving it into Ivysaur and Venusaur.

Is Bulbasaur good for PvP?

Bulbasaur can have niche value in certain CP-limited or special formats, but its evolved forms generally offer greater battle utility.

Does Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. Bulbasaur evolves into Ivysaur and then Venusaur.

How much Candy does Bulbasaur need to evolve?

Bulbasaur requires 25 Bulbasaur Candy to evolve into Ivysaur, while Ivysaur requires 100 Bulbasaur Candy to evolve into Venusaur.

Can Bulbasaur be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Bulbasaur is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Shiny Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. Shiny Bulbasaur can evolve into Shiny Ivysaur and Shiny Venusaur.

Is Shadow Bulbasaur available?

Shadow Bulbasaur has been available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters and its availability depends on the current encounter rotation.

Is Shadow Bulbasaur stronger than regular Bulbasaur?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased attack power but reduced defensive durability. The practical value depends on how the Pokémon is being used.

Should I purify Shadow Bulbasaur?

Do not purify automatically. If you want to eventually build Shadow Venusaur, keeping it Shadow may be more valuable.

What is the best Bulbasaur moveset?

Vine Whip with a Grass-type charged attack such as Seed Bomb is a reasonable Grass-focused combination, although the best moveset depends on the format and current move availability.

Should I power up a Hundo Bulbasaur?

A Hundo Bulbasaur is an excellent specimen to keep, but most players will get more practical value by evolving it before making a major Stardust investment.

Is Bulbasaur worth keeping?

Yes. High-IV, Shiny, Shadow and PvP-relevant Bulbasaur can all be worth keeping, especially if you plan to build the Venusaur evolution family.

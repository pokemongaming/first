🏴 Gen 8 Overview — Galar & Hisui

Generation 8 introduced Galar and later expanded the generation through Pokémon Legends: Arceus and Hisui.

What makes Gen 8 different?

Generation 8 introduced 96 new Pokémon. The original Galar games introduced the Galar roster and the Dynamax/Gigantamax battle mechanic. Later, Pokémon Legends: Arceus introduced Hisui and the final seven National Pokédex species #899–#905.

01 Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble begin the Galar Pokédex.
02 Galar introduced Dynamax and Gigantamax.
03 Galarian Forms expanded regional-form design.
04 Zacian, Zamazenta and Eternatus headline Galar's Legendary story.
05 Hisui added seven new species from #899 through #905.

⚡ Dynamax & Gigantamax

One of Generation 8's defining battle systems.

⚡

Dynamax

Dynamax dramatically increases a Pokémon's size and changes its battle presentation and moves for a limited number of turns.

👑

Gigantamax

Gigantamax is a special transformation available to particular Pokémon. It changes the Pokémon's appearance and can provide a unique G-Max Move.

🏟️

Pokémon GO Connection

For your Pokémon GO database, keep Dynamax and Gigantamax information separate from ordinary raid, PvP and evolution information.

🌀 Galarian & Special Forms

Generation 8 expanded regional variants and introduced multiple important alternate forms.

🌑

Galarian Forms

Gen 8 introduced Galarian variants such as Galarian Ponyta, Galarian Corsola, Galarian Farfetch'd, Galarian Zigzagoon and Galarian Darumaka.

🐺

Zacian Forms

Zacian has Hero of Many Battles and Crowned Sword forms, with its Crowned form being especially important to battle-focused databases.

🛡️

Zamazenta Forms

Zamazenta has Hero of Many Battles and Crowned Shield forms.

🐉

Urshifu Styles

Urshifu has Single Strike Style and Rapid Strike Style, giving the species two distinct type combinations.

🌌

Calyrex Riders

Calyrex can combine with Glastrier or Spectrier, creating Ice Rider and Shadow Rider forms.

💫

Eternamax Eternatus

Eternatus has a special Eternamax form associated with the climax of the Galar storyline.

❓ Gen 8 Pokémon FAQ

Common questions about Galar, Hisui and Generation 8.

How many Pokémon were introduced in Gen 8?

Generation 8 introduced 96 new Pokémon, spanning National Pokédex #810 through #905.

What region is Generation 8 associated with?

The primary Generation 8 region is Galar. The generation was later expanded with Hisui through Pokémon Legends: Arceus.

Who are the Gen 8 starters?

The three Galar starters are Grookey, Scorbunny and Sobble.

What is Generation 8 famous for?

Generation 8 is strongly associated with Galar, Dynamax, Gigantamax, Galarian Forms, the Wild Area, and later the Hisui region.

What are the main Gen 8 Legendary Pokémon?

Major Gen 8 Legendary Pokémon include Zacian, Zamazenta, Eternatus, Kubfu, Urshifu, Regieleki, Regidrago, Glastrier, Spectrier and Calyrex.

Is Zarude a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Zarude is classified as a Mythical Pokémon.

Are Wyrdeer and Kleavor Gen 8 Pokémon?

Yes. Wyrdeer, Kleavor, Ursaluna, Basculegion, Sneasler, Overqwil and Enamorus are the seven species numbered #899–#905 and are associated with the Hisui expansion of Generation 8.

What is the difference between Dynamax and Gigantamax?

Dynamax changes a Pokémon's scale and battle mechanics, while Gigantamax is a special form available to specific Pokémon and can change their appearance and provide a unique G-Max Move.

What is the final Pokémon of Gen 8?

Enamorus is National Pokédex #905, the final species introduced in Generation 8.

Should Galar and Hisui be separate sections on a website?

Yes. For a large Pokémon database, separating Galar #810–#898 and Hisui #899–#905 makes navigation clearer, especially for Pokémon GO users.

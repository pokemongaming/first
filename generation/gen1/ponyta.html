100% IV Ponyta

A 100% IV Ponyta has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs. In Pokémon GO, this means a 15/15/15 IV combination.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

How to Identify a Hundo Ponyta

A perfect Ponyta has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. The in-game appraisal system can be used to determine the individual values.

100% Ponyta Collection Tip

Because Ponyta is more commonly valued as a collector and evolution target than as a top-tier attacker, a 100% specimen can be an excellent candidate for evolution and long-term collection.

XXL Ponyta

XXL Ponyta is a size variant that is larger than the standard size range. Size categories are separate from IV quality, meaning an XXL Ponyta can have any combination of IVs.

XXL Ponyta

A particularly large Ponyta that can be valuable for size collectors and showcase-related activities.

XXL + 100% IV

An XXL Ponyta with perfect 15/15/15 IVs is a particularly desirable combination for collectors.

Does XXL Affect Battle Stats?

XXL size is a size characteristic rather than an IV replacement. Do not assume that a larger Ponyta automatically has better Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

XXS Ponyta

XXS Ponyta is an unusually small Ponyta. Like XXL, the XXS classification is independent of IV quality.

XXS Ponyta

A particularly small Ponyta that can be useful for collectors and size-focused showcases.

XXS + Shiny Ponyta

A Shiny XXS Ponyta combines alternate coloration with a rare size characteristic, making it a strong collector target.

Does XXS Mean Low IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXS Ponyta can have low, average or perfect IVs.

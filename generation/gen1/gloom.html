Why Gloom's Typing Matters

Gloom's Grass and Poison typing gives it a useful collection of resistances, but it also creates several weaknesses. Its dual typing is inherited from Oddish and remains important when considering battle matchups.

Lucky Gloom is obtained through Pokémon trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a special background effect and require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups.

Gloom FAQ

What type is Gloom?

Gloom is a Grass- and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Gloom weak to?

Gloom is vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Psychic, Flying and Bug attacks.

Does Gloom evolve?

Yes. Gloom can evolve into Vileplume or Bellossom.

Is Shiny Gloom available?

Zubat Pokémon GO Overview

Zubat is a Generation 1 Pokémon introduced in Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow. In Pokémon GO, Zubat is a Poison/Flying-type Pokémon with a relatively low maximum CP.

Although regular Zubat is not considered a major raid attacker, it can have niche value in Little Cup-style PvP formats and is especially useful for collectors working toward complete Pokémon families and special variants.

Why Zubat's Typing Matters

The combination of Poison and Flying gives Zubat resistance to several attack types, including Fighting, Bug and Grass. It also makes Zubat weak to Electric, Ice, Psychic and Rock attacks.

Purified Zubat

Purified Zubat is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Zubat. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides several standard purification benefits.

✨

Purified Zubat

Purified Zubat is no longer a Shadow Pokémon and can have Return as its Purified Charged Move.

🕊️

Purification Benefits

Purification increases each IV by 2 points, up to 15, and provides the normal purification benefits associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Lucky Zubat

Lucky Zubat is a traded Pokémon that has received the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Zubat useful primarily as a collection piece or as a low-cost investment candidate.

🍀

Lucky Zubat

Lucky Zubat has the distinctive Lucky background and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

🔄

How Zubat Becomes Lucky

Zubat can become Lucky through trading. Lucky status is determined by the trade system and cannot be created using an item.

Lucky Zubat IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary wild Pokémon. A Lucky Zubat is therefore generally more attractive for collectors looking for a high-IV specimen.

Is Lucky Zubat Worth Keeping?

Absolutely as a collector piece. For practical battle investment, however, Zubat's low maximum CP means that most players will get greater value from investing in evolved Pokémon such as Golbat or Crobat.

    Zubat PvP Guide

    Zubat is not designed for the three main open leagues because of its low maximum CP. Its more interesting PvP niche appears in restricted formats such as Little Cup.

    Great League

    Zubat's maximum CP is far below the normal Great League ceiling. As a result, it is generally not a natural Great League choice.

    Ultra League

    Zubat is not realistically competitive in Ultra League because its CP and stats are too low.

    Master League

    Zubat is unsuitable for Master League due to its very low maximum CP.

    Little Cup

    Zubat can become more interesting when the format restricts Pokémon to 500 CP or another specialized ruleset. Its Fast Move and inexpensive Charged Move options can create pressure in certain matchups.

    PvP Tip

    Always check the current Little Cup rules before investing in a specialized Zubat PvP build.

    Zubat Pokémon GO FAQ

    What type is Zubat?

    Zubat is a Poison/Flying-type Pokémon.

    What is Zubat weak to?

    Zubat is weak to Electric, Ice, Psychic and Rock-type attacks.

    What is Zubat's max CP?

    Zubat reaches 754 CP at level 50 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

    Is Shiny Zubat available?

    Yes. Shiny Zubat is available in Pokémon GO.

    Is Shadow Zubat available?

    Shadow Zubat is an obtainable Shadow variant, with availability controlled by Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

    Can Shadow Zubat be purified?

    Yes. An eligible Shadow Zubat can be purified.

    Is Lucky Zubat different from normal Zubat?

    Lucky Zubat is not a separate species. Lucky is a special status obtained through trading that provides reduced Stardust power-up costs.

    What is a 100% Zubat?

    A 100% Zubat has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

    What is the max CP of a hundo Zubat?

    A level 50 15/15/15 Zubat reaches 754 CP.

    Can Zubat be XXL?

    Yes. Zubat can be found in the XXL size category.

    Can Zubat be XXS?

    Yes. Zubat can be found in the XXS size category.

    Does XXL Zubat have better stats?

    No. XXL is a size attribute and does not directly increase Zubat's base combat stats.

    Does XXS Zubat have lower stats?

    XXS is a size category and should not be treated as a direct reduction to Zubat's base combat stats.

    Is Zubat good for raids?

    No. Zubat's low stats make it a poor conventional raid attacker.

    Is Zubat good in PvP?

    Zubat is mainly a niche option in restricted low-CP formats rather than the main Great, Ultra or Master Leagues.

    How much Candy does Zubat need to evolve?

    Zubat requires 25 Candy to evolve into Golbat. Crobat requires another 100 Candy plus its evolution requirement.

    What is the best Zubat moveset?

    Bite and Poison Fang are a strong general moveset recommendation for Zubat, especially when considering its limited PvP applications.

    Can Shiny Zubat become Shiny Crobat?

    Yes. Shiny Zubat retains its Shiny status when evolved through the Zubat family.

    Can Lucky Zubat be Shiny?

    Yes. Lucky and Shiny are separate attributes and can occur together on the same Pokémon.

    Can a 100% Zubat also be XXL?

    Yes. IVs and size are separate attributes, so a Zubat can be both 100% IV and XXL.

01 — OVERVIEW

Chansey Overview

Chansey is a Normal-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. Unlike many Pokémon, Chansey is known less for offensive power and more for its exceptionally high HP and defensive usefulness in Pokémon GO Gyms.

Why Chansey's Typing Matters

Chansey's pure Normal typing gives it one primary weakness: Fighting-type attacks. It also has a useful resistance to Ghost-type attacks, although its overall battle role is shaped much more by its enormous HP than by offensive pressure.

Chansey Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

Chansey has an unusual stat distribution. Its Attack is low, but its HP is exceptionally high. This makes Chansey far more important as a defensive Pokémon than as a traditional raid attacker.

07 — RAID PERFORMANCE

Is Chansey Good for Raids?

Chansey is generally not recommended as a raid attacker. Its enormous HP gives it durability, but its low Attack means it lacks the damage output expected from an optimized PvE attacker.

Chansey's Main Pokémon GO Role

Chansey is best viewed as a high-end Gym defender rather than a raid attacker. Its large HP pool forces attackers to spend additional time dealing with it, particularly when Chansey is placed in a Gym alongside other bulky defenders.

08 — COUNTERS

What Counters Chansey?

Because Chansey is weak to Fighting, Fighting-type Pokémon are the natural counter category.

⏱️
Fast Gym Teams

Players attacking Gyms generally want high-damage Fighting attackers to remove Chansey efficiently.

18 — EVOLUTION

Chansey Evolution — Happiny to Blissey

Chansey belongs to a three-stage family in Pokémon GO: Happiny, Chansey and Blissey. Chansey can evolve into Blissey using Chansey Candy.

→

Should You Evolve a 100% Chansey?

If you have a perfect Chansey, evolving it into Blissey preserves its perfect IV combination. Because Blissey has an important Gym-defense role, a high-IV Chansey can be a useful evolution project for players who regularly interact with Gyms.

19 — PVP

Chansey PvP Guide

Chansey's enormous HP makes it an unusual PvP Pokémon. Its extremely low Attack means that it does not play like a conventional damage-focused Pokémon.

Great League

Chansey can be considered in limited Great League situations, but its effectiveness depends heavily on the current rules, moves and metagame.

Ultra League

Chansey's maximum CP makes Ultra League a difficult environment for standard optimized Chansey builds.

Master League

Chansey's 1,418 maximum CP makes it unsuitable for the Master League's uncapped CP environment.

PvP reminder:

League rankings can change as Pokémon GO introduces new moves, balance updates and cups. Check current PvP data before building a competitive team.

How to Use Chansey

Before Powering Up

  • Check IVs.
  • Decide whether Chansey or Blissey is your final goal.
  • Consider whether the Pokémon is Lucky.
  • Check whether it has Shiny, Shadow or Purified status.

For Collectors

Chansey's huge HP makes it one of the most recognizable Gym-defense Pokémon. If Gym defense is your primary goal, consider Chansey and its evolution Blissey as part of a broader defensive strategy.

26 — FAQ

Chansey FAQ

What type is Chansey?

Chansey is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Chansey weak against?

Chansey is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What does Chansey resist?

Chansey's Normal typing gives it resistance to Ghost-type attacks.

What is Chansey's maximum CP?

Chansey's maximum CP is 1,418 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Chansey's stats?

Chansey has 60 Attack, 128 Defense and 487 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Chansey good for raids?

Chansey is generally not a recommended raid attacker because its Attack is very low.

Is Chansey good for Gym defense?

Yes. Chansey is one of the strongest Gym-defense Pokémon because of its exceptionally high HP.

Is Shiny Chansey available?

Yes. Shiny Chansey is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Chansey?

A 100% Chansey has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Chansey?

Shadow Chansey is the Shadow version of Chansey and retains the Normal typing.

What is Purified Chansey?

Purified Chansey is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Chansey.

What is Lucky Chansey?

Lucky Chansey is obtained through trading and benefits from a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is XXL Chansey?

XXL Chansey is an extra-large size classification. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Chansey?

XXS Chansey is an extra-small size classification. It does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better IVs.

Does Chansey evolve?

Yes. Chansey evolves into Blissey. Chansey is also part of the Happiny → Chansey → Blissey family.

Should I evolve a 100% Chansey?

If your goal is a perfect Blissey, a 100% Chansey is an excellent evolution candidate because its perfect IVs are retained after evolution.

Is Lucky Chansey worth keeping?

Yes, particularly if you want to reduce Stardust costs when powering up Chansey or evolving it into Blissey.

Is a Shiny 100% Chansey rare?

A Shiny Chansey with perfect 15/15/15 IVs combines two desirable collection characteristics and can be particularly valuable to collectors.

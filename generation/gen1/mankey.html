Mankey Overview

Mankey is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It is a relatively low-CP Pokémon by modern Pokémon GO standards, but its importance comes from its evolution family and the availability of special forms such as Shiny and Shadow Mankey.

Mankey Stats & Max CP

Mankey has 148 base Attack, 82 base Defense and 120 base HP in Pokémon GO. Its maximum CP reaches 1,317 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Mankey PvP Guide

Mankey's relatively low maximum CP means it is not normally a natural choice for the major open Great, Ultra or Master League formats. Its evolutionary forms are generally more relevant for competitive play.

Great League

Mankey's maximum CP is low enough to enter Great League, but its overall stats and moveset make it much more niche than its evolved forms.

Ultra League

Mankey can technically be powered beyond Great League limits, but its low maximum CP makes it poorly suited to the Ultra League.

Master League

Mankey is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP is only 1,317.

Mankey FAQ

What type is Mankey?

Mankey is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Mankey's max CP?

Mankey reaches 1,317 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Mankey available?

Yes. Shiny Mankey is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Mankey available?

Shadow Mankey is part of Pokémon GO's Shadow Pokémon system and its availability can depend on current Team GO Rocket content.

What is Mankey weak to?

Mankey is weak to Flying, Psychic and Fairy-type attacks.

What does Mankey evolve into?

Mankey evolves into Primeape using 50 Mankey Candy. Primeape can subsequently evolve into Annihilape after meeting the required evolution condition.

What is a 100% Mankey?

A 100% Mankey has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Lucky Mankey?

Lucky Mankey is a Mankey that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

What is XXL Mankey?

XXL Mankey is an extra-large size variant. Its size does not automatically indicate perfect IVs.

What is XXS Mankey?

XXS Mankey is an extra-small size variant. Its size is separate from its IV percentage.

Should I evolve a 100% Mankey?

For most collectors, a perfect Mankey is worth keeping. Whether you evolve it immediately depends on the value you place on Mankey itself compared with Primeape and Annihilape.

Is Mankey good for raids?

Mankey itself has limited raid value because of its low maximum CP. Its evolutionary family is generally more interesting for battle investment.

Can Shiny Mankey evolve?

Yes. Shiny Mankey can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

Can Shadow Mankey evolve?

Shadow Mankey can be evolved when the relevant evolution is available, while retaining its Shadow status.

Lucky Ekans

Lucky Ekans is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special visual effects and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Ekans

A Lucky Ekans can be an attractive collection target, particularly when paired with high IVs, Shiny status or a favorable trade history.

Lucky Ekans Benefits

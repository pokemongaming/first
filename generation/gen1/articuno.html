01 — OVERVIEW

Articuno Overview

Articuno is one of the Legendary birds originally introduced in Generation 1. Regular Articuno is an Ice/Flying-type Pokémon with high Defense and a more defensive stat profile than many other Legendary attackers.

02 — TYPE

Articuno Type

❄️
Ice / Flying Dual Type

Why Articuno's Typing Matters

Articuno's Ice/Flying typing gives it access to valuable Ice-type offensive coverage while also creating a distinctive defensive profile. It is especially useful when Ice damage is super effective against the raid target.

The Flying secondary type also changes the way Articuno handles Ground, Grass, Bug and other attacking types.

10 — PVP

Articuno PvP Guide

Articuno's high Defense, Ice typing and charged-move utility give it a more interesting PvP role than its raid Attack stat might suggest.

Great League

Standard Articuno is not naturally optimized around Great League because of its Legendary status and CP requirements, although special formats can change its usefulness.

Ultra League

Articuno can have more room to operate in Ultra League formats. Moves such as Icy Wind can provide useful pressure and attack reduction utility.

Master League

Articuno can participate in Master League, but the current metagame and its overall stat profile should be considered before investing heavily for this format.

PvP Tip

Do not judge Articuno solely by its raid performance. Its defensive profile and charged-move utility can create different PvP considerations.

Should You Purify Shadow Articuno?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Articuno simply because purification increases its IVs. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your intended raid, PvP and collection goals.

  • Keep a strong Shadow specimen if you value Shadow performance.
  • Check its current moves before investing Elite resources.
  • Consider the higher Stardust and Candy requirements.
  • Compare it with regular Articuno before purification.
  • Do not assume perfect IVs are mandatory for Shadow use.

Shadow Articuno Availability

20 — FAQ

Articuno FAQ

Is Articuno a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Articuno is one of the Legendary bird Pokémon introduced in Generation 1.

What type is regular Articuno?

Regular Articuno is Ice/Flying type.

What is Articuno weak to?

Articuno is weak to Rock, Electric, Fire and Steel attacks. Rock is especially effective because of its Ice/Flying typing.

What is Articuno's Max CP?

Regular Articuno has a maximum CP of 3,450 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Articuno's best moves?

For an Ice-oriented build, Frost Breath and Triple Axel are a strong current combination. Move availability can change, so verify the live moveset.

Is Shadow Articuno good?

Shadow Articuno can provide greater offensive pressure than regular Articuno, although the Shadow mechanic also reduces durability.

Should I purify Shadow Articuno?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your raid, PvP and collection goals.

Is Shiny Articuno available?

Yes. Shiny Articuno is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What type is Galarian Articuno?

Galarian Articuno is Psychic/Flying type rather than Ice/Flying.

What is Galarian Articuno's Max CP?

Galarian Articuno has a maximum CP of 4,059 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Galarian Articuno's best moves?

Psycho Cut and Fly are a strong current Flying-oriented combination for Galarian Articuno.

Is Galarian Articuno the same typing as regular Articuno?

No. Regular Articuno is Ice/Flying, while Galarian Articuno is Psychic/Flying.

Can Articuno Dynamax in Pokémon GO?

Current Pokémon GO data lists Articuno as capable of Dynamaxing.

Does Articuno evolve?

No. Articuno does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Galarian Articuno a different species?

Galarian Articuno is a regional form of Articuno rather than a separate Pokédex species.

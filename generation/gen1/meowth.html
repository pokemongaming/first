Meowth Overview

Meowth is a small Normal-type Pokémon known for its coin-loving behavior and its evolution into Persian. The standard Meowth is different from its Alolan and Galarian regional forms, which have different typings and gameplay characteristics.

Why Meowth's Typing Matters

Regular Meowth is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Normal-type Pokémon have relatively straightforward offensive and defensive interactions, but Meowth's low overall combat stats limit its role in high-level raids.

Meowth Special Variants

The following variants are useful to track separately in a Pokémon collection. Some are visual or gameplay states rather than separate species.

✨

Shiny Meowth

Shiny Meowth has an alternate coloration and is a collectible variant of the standard Meowth.

😈

Shadow Meowth

Shadow Meowth is a Shadow Pokémon with the Shadow battle mechanic and can have Frustration as a Charged Move when obtained.

💎

Purified Meowth

Purified Meowth is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Meowth. Purification changes its Shadow status and can provide the usual Purified Pokémon benefits.

🍀

Lucky Meowth

Lucky Meowth is a special trading outcome. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

💯

100% IV Meowth

A perfect Meowth has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

📏

XXL Meowth

An XXL Meowth is a size variant that is larger than normal. Size is separate from IV quality.

🔹

XXS Meowth

An XXS Meowth is an unusually small size variant. It can be valuable to collectors even when its IVs are not perfect.

Shiny Meowth

Shiny Meowth is the alternate-color version of regular Meowth. Shiny status is independent of IVs, CP, Lucky status and size.

✨ Shiny Collection Tip

A Shiny Meowth can also be valuable as a collector's Pokémon when combined with other special attributes such as high IVs, Lucky status, XXL or XXS size.

Shadow Meowth

Shadow Meowth is a Shadow version of Meowth. Shadow Pokémon receive a battle-performance modification that makes their offensive and defensive profile different from the regular form.

😈 Shadow Meowth

Shadow Meowth can be useful as a collector's variant and for players who want to preserve unusual Shadow Pokémon. Its low base stats mean it is not normally a major raid investment.

Purification warning:

Purified Meowth

Purified Meowth is produced by purifying an eligible Shadow Meowth. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's available characteristics.

💎

Purified Form

Purified Meowth no longer has the Shadow status and can have Return as its Purified Charged Move when applicable.

⭐

Collector Value

A Purified Meowth can be especially interesting when it also has strong IVs, Lucky status or an unusual size category.

Should You Purify Shadow Meowth?

  • Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first.
  • Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow Pokémon.
  • Check the IVs before making the decision.
  • Consider collector value rather than only CP.
  • Remember that purification is permanent.

Lucky Meowth

Lucky Meowth is created through Pokémon trading. Its Lucky status is separate from Shiny status, IVs and size.

🍀

Lucky Pokémon

Lucky Meowth has a special appearance in the Pokémon storage interface and requires less Stardust to power up.

🔁

Trading

Trading is the normal way to obtain a Lucky Pokémon. The Lucky status is determined by the trade rather than being manually activated.

Lucky Meowth Collection Ideas

  • Lucky + 100% IV Meowth
  • Lucky + Shiny Meowth
  • Lucky + XXL Meowth
  • Lucky + XXS Meowth
  • Lucky Meowth with a legacy or event move

100% IV Meowth

A 100% IV Meowth has the maximum possible IV value in Attack, Defense and HP/Stamina.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP / Stamina IV
💯 Perfect IV

15/15/15 is the standard definition of a perfect IV Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

Is 100% Meowth Rare?

A perfect Meowth is considerably more collectible than an ordinary specimen because all three IV values are at their maximum. Its usefulness in battle is still limited by Meowth's relatively low base stats.

XXL Meowth

XXL Meowth is a size variant that is larger than standard Meowth. Size and IVs are separate properties, so an XXL Pokémon does not automatically have high IVs.

📏

XXL Meowth

An XXL Meowth can be kept for size-based collection goals and visual variety.

💯

XXL + 100%

An XXL Meowth with 15/15/15 IVs is a particularly interesting combination for collectors.

Meowth FAQ

What type is Meowth?

Regular Kanto Meowth is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Meowth weak to?

Regular Meowth is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Meowth's max CP?

Regular Meowth can reach 846 CP at level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Meowth available?

Yes. Shiny Meowth is an available alternate-color variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Meowth be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Meowth can exist as a Shadow Pokémon when made available through the appropriate Pokémon GO mechanics and encounters.

Can Shadow Meowth be purified?

An eligible Shadow Meowth can be purified. Purification is permanent, so collectors should consider the decision before proceeding.

What is a 100% Meowth?

A 100% Meowth has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP/Stamina IVs.

What is XXL Meowth?

XXL Meowth is a size variant that is unusually large. XXL status is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Meowth?

XXS Meowth is an unusually small size variant. Its size does not determine its IVs.

Can Meowth be Lucky?

Yes. Meowth can become Lucky through trading when the trade results in the Lucky Pokémon outcome.

Does Meowth evolve?

Yes. Regular Kanto Meowth evolves into Persian.

Are Alolan and Galarian Meowth the same as regular Meowth?

No. Alolan and Galarian Meowth are regional forms with different typings and characteristics. They should be treated separately in a Pokémon database.

Is Meowth good for raids?

Regular Meowth is generally not a high-priority raid attacker because of its relatively low combat stats.

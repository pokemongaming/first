Why Hitmonlee's Typing Matters

Hitmonlee's pure Fighting typing gives it access to super-effective damage against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark-type opponents. However, Fighting Pokémon must also watch out for Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks.

Hitmonlee FAQ

What type is Hitmonlee?

Hitmonlee is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Hitmonlee weak to?

Hitmonlee is weak to Flying, Psychic and Fairy attacks.

What is Hitmonlee's max CP?

Hitmonlee has a maximum CP of 2,576.

Is Shiny Hitmonlee available?

Yes. Shiny Hitmonlee is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Hitmonlee available?

Shadow Hitmonlee can be available through Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket-related mechanics and rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting for one.

Can Shadow Hitmonlee be purified?

An eligible Shadow Hitmonlee can be purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and associated mechanics.

What is a 100% Hitmonlee?

A 100% Hitmonlee has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Hitmonlee?

Lucky Hitmonlee is obtained through trading and receives the benefits associated with Lucky Pokémon, including reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Hitmonlee?

XXL Hitmonlee is a Hitmonlee with an unusually large size. Size variants can be particularly useful for collection and Showcase purposes.

What is XXS Hitmonlee?

XXS Hitmonlee is a particularly small Hitmonlee and can be valuable to collectors looking for different size variants.

Is Hitmonlee good for raids?

Hitmonlee can deal Fighting-type damage, but it is generally not a first-choice Fighting raid attacker compared with stronger available options.

Is Hitmonlee good for PvP?

Hitmonlee is a more specialized PvP choice. Its usefulness depends on the current moveset, league and PvP meta.

Does Hitmonlee evolve?

Hitmonlee itself does not normally evolve further. It is one of the possible evolutions of Tyrogue.

How do you get Hitmonlee from Tyrogue?

Tyrogue's evolution outcome depends on its Attack and Defense values under Pokémon GO's evolution mechanics.

Should I power up a perfect Hitmonlee?

A perfect Hitmonlee is a great collection target, but its battle usefulness should be considered separately from its IV quality.

Why Cloyster's Typing Matters

Cloyster's Water/Ice typing gives it useful resistances to Water and Ice attacks while creating several important weaknesses. Its Ice typing also gives it access to Ice-type offensive moves, which can be useful against Dragon, Flying and Ground opponents.

17 • PVP

Cloyster PvP Guide

Cloyster's very high Defense can make it interesting in PvP, although its effectiveness depends heavily on league rules, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Cloyster can appear in Great League discussions, particularly when its Ice coverage is useful against relevant opponents. However, team composition and current rankings should be checked before investing.

Ultra League

Its defensive profile can make Cloyster interesting in certain Ultra League teams, but it competes with many specialized Pokémon for a limited team slot.

Master League

Cloyster's maximum CP is relatively low for Master League compared with Legendary and other high-stat options, making it less natural for this format.

20 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Cloyster

Before Powering Up

  • Check the Pokémon's IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny, Shadow, Lucky or Purified.

21 • FAQ

Cloyster FAQ

What type is Cloyster?

Cloyster is a Water and Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Cloyster weak to?

Cloyster is weak to Electric, Fighting, Grass and Rock-type attacks.

What is Cloyster's max CP?

Cloyster's Level 50 maximum CP is 2,880 with 15/15/15 IVs.

What are Cloyster's stats?

Cloyster has 186 Attack, 256 Defense and 137 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Cloyster available?

Yes. Shiny Cloyster is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Cloyster have a Shadow form?

Yes. Shadow Cloyster exists in Pokémon GO when available through the relevant Team GO Rocket encounter system.

What happens when Shadow Cloyster is purified?

Purification removes the Shadow status and normally replaces Frustration with Return while also providing the standard Purified Pokémon benefits.

Can Cloyster be Lucky?

Yes. Cloyster can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Cloyster?

A 100% or Hundo Cloyster has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Cloyster be XXL?

Yes. Cloyster can have the XXL size designation.

Can Cloyster be XXS?

Yes. Cloyster can also have the XXS size designation.

Does XXL Cloyster have higher IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Cloyster can have any valid IV combination.

Does XXS Cloyster have lower CP?

Size does not automatically determine IVs or CP. An XXS Cloyster can still have excellent IVs.

Is Cloyster good for raids?

Cloyster can provide Water or Ice damage, but it is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker compared with specialized alternatives.

Is Cloyster good in PvP?

Cloyster can have niche PvP applications because of its high Defense and Ice coverage, but its usefulness depends on the league and current metagame.

How do you evolve Shellder into Cloyster?

Shellder evolves into Cloyster using 50 Shellder Candy.

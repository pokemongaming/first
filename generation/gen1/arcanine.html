Arcanine

Arcanine is a Fire-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it evolves from Growlithe and can be used in raids, Gym battles and selected PvP situations. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL and XXS Arcanine.

Arcanine Overview

Arcanine is the evolved form of Growlithe and is a pure Fire-type Pokémon. Its high Attack and respectable stamina make it a recognizable Gen 1 Pokémon, although its usefulness depends heavily on the current Pokémon GO moveset and the role you want it to fill.

Shadow Arcanine

Shadow Arcanine is the Shadow version of Arcanine. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts and require additional consideration before powering up.

🌑 Shadow Arcanine

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive boost while also taking more damage. This makes Shadow Arcanine a different investment option rather than simply a cosmetic variant.

Shadow Arcanine & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon may have the Charged Move Frustration. Rocket-related events can provide opportunities to replace it, so check the current event rules before investing Charged TMs.

Should You Purify Shadow Arcanine?

Purification should be considered carefully. Purifying removes the Shadow status, so compare your goals for the Pokémon before making the decision.

Purified Arcanine

Purified Arcanine is created by purifying a Shadow Growlithe or Shadow Arcanine when purification is available.

💠 Purified Arcanine

Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can also increase its individual values. It is therefore important to compare the original Shadow Pokémon with the purified result before committing.

Lucky Arcanine

Lucky Arcanine is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for players who want to build Pokémon while reducing Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Arcanine

Lucky status does not change Arcanine's species or typing. Its main attraction is the reduced Stardust cost for powering up the Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Arcanine?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collectors building a Lucky Pokédex.
  • Good candidate for a long-term investment project.
  • Trading can provide another way to obtain desirable IVs.

Arcanine FAQ

What type is Arcanine?

Arcanine is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Arcanine weak to?

Arcanine is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Is Arcanine good for raids?

Arcanine can deal Fire-type damage, but its raid value should be compared with stronger Fire attackers available in the current Pokémon GO metagame.

Is Shiny Arcanine available?

Yes. Shiny Arcanine is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution from Shiny Growlithe.

What is 100% Arcanine?

A 100% Arcanine has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Arcanine?

Shadow Arcanine is the Shadow variant of Arcanine and has the different battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Arcanine?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's battle potential, IVs, resources and your personal goals before purifying it.

What is Lucky Arcanine?

Lucky Arcanine is an Arcanine obtained through Lucky Trade mechanics and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Arcanine?

XXL Arcanine is an unusually large Arcanine based on the game's size mechanics.

What is XXS Arcanine?

XXS Arcanine is an unusually small Arcanine based on the game's size mechanics.

Does XXL Arcanine have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Arcanine can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Arcanine have better IVs?

No. XXS status does not automatically mean better IVs.

Can Shiny Arcanine be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can have both properties.

Can Shadow Arcanine be 100% IV?

Yes. A Shadow Arcanine can have a 15/15/15 IV combination.

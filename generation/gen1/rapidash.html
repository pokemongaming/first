Shiny Rapidash

Shiny Rapidash is an alternate-color version of the standard Pokémon. Its battle statistics remain based on the same species mechanics, making its main difference visual and collectible.

✨ Shiny Status

A Shiny Ponyta can retain its Shiny status when evolved into Rapidash.

Should You Power Up Shiny Rapidash?

Powering up a Shiny Rapidash should be based on its IVs, moveset and intended use. A Shiny status alone does not improve its battle statistics.

Purified Rapidash

Purified Rapidash is created by purifying an eligible Shadow version. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered carefully before committing.

Purification Decision

Do not purify automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow damage bonus with the advantages of a purified Pokémon for your own collection and investment goals.

Rapidash FAQ

What type is Rapidash?

Standard Kanto Rapidash is a Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Rapidash weak to?

Rapidash is weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

Can Rapidash be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution opportunities.

Is Shadow Rapidash stronger?

Shadow Rapidash has a different offensive and defensive battle profile because of the Shadow mechanic.

Should I purify Shadow Rapidash?

Not automatically. Consider the value of the Shadow battle bonus before purifying.

What is a Lucky Rapidash?

Lucky Rapidash is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics and costs less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Rapidash?

A 100% IV Rapidash has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Rapidash do more damage?

Size variants are primarily collection-related and do not normally provide standard battle-stat advantages.

Does Rapidash evolve?

Rapidash evolves from Ponyta and is the final evolution in the standard Kanto evolution line.

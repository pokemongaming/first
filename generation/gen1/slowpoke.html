01 — OVERVIEW

Slowpoke Overview

Slowpoke is a dual Water/Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Its low offensive stats mean that regular Slowpoke is not normally built as a top-tier raid attacker, but it remains useful as an evolution-family Pokémon, collector target and source of Slowbro and Slowking.

11 — SHINY

Shiny Slowpoke

Shiny Slowpoke is an alternate-color variant of Slowpoke. It does not receive a separate species number and retains the same underlying species identity.

SHINY AVAILABLE

✨ Shiny Slowpoke

Shiny Slowpoke has an alternate coloration compared with regular Slowpoke. Its Shiny status is especially relevant for collectors and players who want to evolve a Shiny Slowpoke into Slowbro or Slowking.

Shiny Slowpoke Collection Tips

  • Keep a Shiny Slowpoke if you want both evolution outcomes.
  • Check IVs before transferring duplicate Shinies.
  • Consider keeping a Shiny with useful PvP IVs.
  • Save special event Shinies when they have unique value.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Slowpoke

Lucky Slowpoke is a special trade outcome with a distinct Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust to power up than normal Pokémon.

LUCKY POKÉMON

🍀 Lucky Slowpoke

Lucky Slowpoke can be particularly attractive to collectors and players who want a cheaper Stardust investment. It can also make an interesting candidate for evolving into a Lucky Slowbro or Lucky Slowking.

Why Keep a Lucky Slowpoke?

  • Reduced Stardust cost can make powering it up cheaper.
  • Lucky status remains useful for collection purposes.
  • A Lucky Slowpoke can be evolved into its available forms.
  • Check IVs after the trade because Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor.

16 — XXL

XXL Slowpoke

XXL Slowpoke is a size variant associated with Pokémon GO's size system. XXL status is separate from IV quality, CP and Shiny status.

LARGE SIZE

📏 XXL Slowpoke

An XXL Slowpoke is larger than the standard size range. Size is independently tracked, so an XXL Slowpoke can also be Shiny, Lucky, Shadow or even 100% IV.

XXL Slowpoke Collection Value

  • XXL status is separate from IVs.
  • An XXL Slowpoke can also have another special status.
  • Keep notable XXL Pokémon if you collect size variants.
  • Do not judge size solely from CP.

Slowpoke FAQ

What type is Slowpoke?

Slowpoke is a Water/Psychic-type Pokémon.

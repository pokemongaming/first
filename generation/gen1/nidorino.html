Why Nidorino's Typing Matters

Nidorino's pure Poison typing gives it useful resistances against several common attack types, while its main weaknesses are Ground and Psychic.

Nidorino IV Guide

IVs add individual points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Nidorino is the perfect IV combination.

15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% IV Nidorino = 15/15/15

A perfect Nidorino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A 100% IV Nidorino is mainly valuable for collection, evolution and personal investment rather than high-level raid performance.

100% IV Nidorino

Lucky Nidorino

Lucky Nidorino is a special trade result that has reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Nidorino

A Lucky Nidorino can be particularly attractive for trainers who want to reduce Stardust costs while building a Pokémon.

Lucky Nidorino Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for Stardust-conscious collectors.
  • Can have strong IVs.
  • Can also be Shiny if the traded Pokémon is Shiny.
  • Can potentially be a 100% IV Lucky Nidorino.

Lucky + 100% Nidorino

A Lucky Nidorino with 15/15/15 IVs is an especially nice collection piece because it combines the Lucky status with perfect IVs.

XXL Nidorino

XXL Nidorino refers to an unusually large specimen. Size is separate from IV quality and does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect battle IVs.

📏

XXL Nidorino

A large-size Nidorino suitable for collection and size-based Pokédex goals.

🏆

Why Keep XXL?

Collectors may keep XXL Pokémon for size records, medals and personal collection goals.

Important:

XXL size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Nidorino is not automatically a 100% IV Nidorino.

XXS Nidorino

XXS Nidorino is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of IV quality.

🔹

XXS Nidorino

A very small Nidorino that can be useful for size-based collection goals.

📊

XXS + IVs

An XXS Nidorino can have low, average or perfect IVs. Size does not determine IV quality.

Nidorino for Raids

Nidorino is not generally considered a top-tier Poison raid attacker. Its relatively low maximum CP and overall stat profile make it more useful as a collection or evolution Pokémon than as a serious PvE investment.

PvE Recommendation

Use Nidorino mainly for collection, evolution and special-form goals rather than building a raid team around it.

Nidorino PvP Guide

Nidorino can technically participate in PvP formats, but its relatively modest overall stats limit its competitive relevance compared with stronger options.

Great League

Nidorino can fit within Great League's CP limit, but trainers should compare its performance against the current cup and open Great League meta before investing resources.

Ultra League

Nidorino's relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural for the species.

Master League

Nidorino is not suited to Master League because its maximum CP is far below the level generally required for that format.

Should You Power Up Nidorino?

COLLECTORS

Excellent candidate for Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, Purified, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV collection goals.

Nidorino FAQ

What type is Nidorino?

Nidorino is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Nidorino weak against?

Nidorino is weak to Ground and Psychic-type attacks.

What is Nidorino's Max CP?

Nidorino's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,575.

Is Shiny Nidorino available?

Yes. Shiny Nidorino is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Nidorino be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Nidorino is available as a separate Shadow variant.

Can Shadow Nidorino be purified?

An eligible Shadow Nidorino can be purified, subject to the game's current purification mechanics.

What is a 100% Nidorino?

A 100% Nidorino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Nidorino useful?

Lucky Nidorino has reduced Stardust power-up costs, making it useful for collectors and players who want to minimize Stardust spending.

What is an XXL Nidorino?

An XXL Nidorino is an unusually large specimen. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is an XXS Nidorino?

An XXS Nidorino is an unusually small specimen. Its size does not determine its IVs.

Is Nidorino good for raids?

Nidorino is not generally a top-tier raid attacker. It is more useful for collection, evolution and special-form goals.

Should I evolve a 100% Nidorino?

If your goal is to obtain a perfect Nidoking, a 100% Nidorino is an excellent candidate to evolve. However, keeping it unevolved can also be useful for collection purposes.

Can Nidorino be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky can overlap when the relevant conditions are met.

Can Nidorino be 100% and XXL?

Yes. IV quality and size are separate characteristics, so an XXL Nidorino can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

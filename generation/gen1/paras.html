Why Paras's Typing Matters

Paras combines Bug and Grass typing. This gives it access to useful type coverage but also creates several important weaknesses, particularly against Fire, Flying, Ice, Poison, Bug and Rock attacks.

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Paras simply because the purified version may have higher IVs. The best choice depends on whether you value the Shadow form, collection rarity, evolution plans or purified Pokémon.

Lucky Paras

Lucky Paras is a special trade-related variant. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background in the Pokémon GO storage interface and generally require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Paras

A Lucky Paras can be a fun addition to a collection, especially when combined with a strong IV spread, Shiny status or an interesting size.

Lucky Paras Checklist

  • Keep an eye out for a Lucky Shiny Paras.
  • Consider Lucky Paras for collection goals.
  • Compare its investment cost with stronger Pokémon before powering up.

Paras Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Paras Candy is primarily useful for evolving Paras, powering up Paras or Parasect, and managing collection projects. Because Paras is not normally a top-tier attacker, consider your resource priorities before making a large Stardust investment.

  • Save enough Paras Candy for evolution.
  • Check IVs before investing Stardust.
  • Keep rare variants such as Shiny Paras.
  • Consider keeping interesting Shadow and Purified specimens.
  • Save XXL and XXS specimens for size collections.
  • Do not assume high CP means perfect IVs.

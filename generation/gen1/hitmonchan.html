FIGHTING TYPE

Hitmonchan

Hitmonchan is a Fighting-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its distinctive boxing-inspired design, multiple elemental punching moves and availability in regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky forms.

01 · OVERVIEW

Hitmonchan Overview

Hitmonchan is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. Unlike many Pokémon, Hitmonchan is part of the Hitmon family and is connected to Tyrogue through a branching evolution system. In Pokémon GO, Hitmonchan can be used in certain Fighting-type matchups, although its overall PvE value is generally lower than top-tier Fighting attackers.

10 · SHINY

Shiny Hitmonchan

Shiny Hitmonchan is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a desirable addition for collectors, especially when combined with high IVs, Lucky status or rare size records.

✨ Shiny Hitmonchan

Shiny Hitmonchan has an alternate appearance compared with the standard form. Shiny status does not change Hitmonchan's fundamental Fighting typing.

Shiny Hitmonchan Collector Value

12 · PURIFIED

Purified Hitmonchan

Purified Hitmonchan is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Hitmonchan. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the standard purification benefits.

Purified Hitmonchan

Purified Hitmonchan no longer has Shadow status. Its IVs receive the standard purification adjustment when eligible, and purification also affects its resource requirements and moves.

17 · EVOLUTION

Hitmonchan Evolution

Hitmonchan does not evolve further in Pokémon GO. Instead, it is one of the possible evolutions of Tyrogue. Tyrogue can evolve into Hitmonchan, Hitmonlee or Hitmontop depending on its Attack, Defense and HP relationships when the evolution conditions are met.

Evolution note:

Hitmonchan itself does not evolve into another Pokémon. Its place in the evolution family comes from Tyrogue.

Hitmonchan FAQ

Is Hitmonchan a Fighting-type Pokémon?

Yes. Hitmonchan is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Hitmonchan weak to?

Hitmonchan is weak to Psychic, Flying and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Shiny Hitmonchan available?

Yes. Shiny Hitmonchan is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Hitmonchan available?

Shadow Hitmonchan can be available through Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket-related encounter rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting for one.

What is a 100% Hitmonchan?

What is an XXS Hitmonchan?

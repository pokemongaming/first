Regular Seel

Regular Seel is the standard version without special cosmetic, Shadow, Purified, Lucky or size-related status.

Regular Seel

STANDARD FORM

A regular Seel is the normal version encountered through eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods. IVs, level and moves can differ between individual Seel.

Shiny Seel

Shiny Seel is the alternate-color version of Seel. A Shiny Pokémon has the same base species mechanics but a different appearance.

✨ Shiny Seel

SHINY

Shiny Seel is primarily a collector variant. Its IVs, level and battle usefulness are determined independently from its Shiny status.

Collector note:

A Shiny Seel can also potentially have other eligible attributes, such as Lucky status after trading, depending on current game mechanics.

Shadow Seel

Shadow Seel is the Shadow version of Seel. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon and require separate investment decisions.

🌑 Shadow Seel

SHADOW

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure while having reduced defensive durability. This can make Shadow Pokémon particularly interesting for damage-focused roles.

Shadow Seel Investment

  • Check the Shadow Seel's IVs before transferring it.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve it into Shadow Dewgong.
  • Check its Charged Move availability.
  • Remember that Shadow Pokémon require additional resources.
  • Do not purify automatically just for a small IV increase.
Availability note:

Shadow Seel availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Do not permanently claim that it is currently obtainable unless verified against the live rotation.

Purified Seel

Purified Seel is created when an eligible Shadow Seel is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

✨ Purified Seel

PURIFIED

Purification removes the Shadow battle characteristics. Whether purification is worthwhile depends on your personal goals, including collection, IVs, evolution plans and battle use.

Lucky Seel

Lucky Seel is a special traded version of Seel. Lucky Pokémon are valuable because they require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Seel

LUCKY

A Lucky Seel can be an attractive long-term project if you plan to evolve it and invest Stardust into its evolution.

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean perfect IVs.
  • Check IVs before evolving.
  • A Lucky Seel can be evolved into Dewgong.

XXL Seel

XXL Seel is an unusually large Seel. Size categories are primarily collector and Showcase-related attributes.

🐘 XXL Seel

XXL SIZE

XXL status is determined by the Pokémon's size measurements. It does not automatically mean that Seel has higher IVs or better battle stats.

Why Keep XXL Seel?

  • Useful for Pokémon Showcases when eligible.
  • Rare collector variant.
  • Can be combined with other eligible special attributes.
  • Does not automatically improve battle performance.

XXS Seel

XXS Seel is an unusually small version of Seel. Like XXL Pokémon, its size status is primarily related to collection and eligible Showcase mechanics.

🔹 XXS Seel

XXS SIZE

XXS Seel has a size classification separate from IVs and combat performance. A small Seel can still have high IVs.

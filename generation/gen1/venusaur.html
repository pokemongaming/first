Why Venusaur's Typing Matters

Venusaur's Grass typing gives it strong offensive access to Water, Ground and Rock targets, while its Poison typing adds useful resistances and gives it access to Poison-type attacks.

The combination also creates several important weaknesses, particularly against Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic attacks.

Venusaur PvP Guide

Venusaur has much more PvP identity than its raw raid ranking might suggest. Its Grass / Poison typing, good bulk and access to Frenzy Plant make it a useful option in CP-limited formats.

Great League

Venusaur can perform well in Great League because its CP and bulk fit the format and Frenzy Plant provides strong shield pressure. Its typing can also help against Water, Ground and Fairy-related matchups.

Ultra League

Ultra League Venusaur generally benefits from higher levels and strong IV combinations. A carefully built Venusaur can pressure Water, Ground and Fairy Pokémon.

Master League

Venusaur is less naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP is lower than many top Master League Pokémon. It can still have niche value, but it is not normally the first Venusaur format to prioritize.

PvP Core Moves

Vine Whip + Frenzy Plant

Shadow Venusaur Moves

For a Grass-focused Shadow Venusaur, Vine Whip and Frenzy Plant are the primary moves to consider when available. A newly caught Shadow Pokémon may know Frustration until an eligible event or purification changes its Charged Move situation.

Do not purify automatically.

Purification removes the Shadow battle profile. Compare your goals for raids, PvP and collection before making the decision.

Purified Venusaur

Purified Venusaur is created by purifying a Shadow Bulbasaur, Ivysaur or Venusaur. Purification improves the appraisal, lowers certain resource costs and replaces Frustration with Return.

Lucky Venusaur

Lucky Venusaur is a Venusaur obtained through trading that becomes Lucky. Lucky Pokémon have a special shimmery appearance in the game and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Venusaur

A trade-created special status that reduces Stardust requirements for Power Ups.

🤝

How to Get One

Trade Venusaur or another Pokémon with a friend. The Lucky result is determined by the game's Lucky trade mechanics.

Lucky Venusaur IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. Because trading rerolls the Pokémon's IVs, the Venusaur you send into a trade does not retain its original IVs.

Lucky Hundo Venusaur

A Lucky Venusaur with 15/15/15 IVs is a particularly desirable combination because it combines perfect IVs with the Stardust benefits of Lucky status.

Mega Energy Requirements

Mega Evolving Venusaur requires Mega Energy. The first Mega Evolution costs more Energy than subsequent Mega Evolutions, with the current standard cost structure listed as 200 Mega Energy for the first Mega Evolution and 40 for subsequent evolutions.

Important:

Mega Venusaur is not a permanently evolved replacement for Venusaur. Mega Evolution is a temporary form.

Venusaur Candy, Candy XL & Stardust

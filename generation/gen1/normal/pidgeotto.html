Why Pidgeotto's Typing Matters

Pidgeotto's Normal/Flying combination gives it several useful resistances, while also creating specific weaknesses that trainers should remember when using it in battle. The Flying typing is particularly important because it changes how Pidgeotto interacts with Ground attacks and other common battle types.

09 • PVP

Pidgeotto PvP Guide

Pidgeotto's relatively low CP ceiling limits its standard PvP opportunities, but middle-evolution Pokémon can sometimes become interesting in special cups with restricted rules.

Great League

Great League is the most realistic standard league for Pidgeotto because its CP remains within the lower league range. However, its actual usefulness depends strongly on the current moveset and cup rules.

Ultra League

Pidgeotto is generally not an obvious Ultra League investment because its maximum CP is relatively low.

Master League

Pidgeotto is not suitable for conventional Master League competition because its CP and stats are far below the requirements of the format.

PvP Investment Advice

If you enjoy limited cups or unusual Pokémon, evaluate Pidgeotto using the current cup rules and moveset rather than assuming its standard PvP ranking.

Should You Purify Shadow Pidgeotto?

Purification is permanent, so there is no universal answer. If you value the Shadow damage modifier or want to preserve a Shadow collection, keeping it Shadow may be preferable. If you need the benefits of purification or simply prefer the purified form, purification can be considered.

Purification warning:

Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically. Compare its Shadow value, IVs, evolution potential and your personal collection goals before making the decision.

Shadow Pidgeotto Availability

Shadow Pokémon availability is controlled by Team GO Rocket content, events and current Pokémon GO rotations. Availability can change over time, so historical Shadow encounters should not automatically be presented as currently obtainable.

14 • RESOURCES

Pidgey Candy, Candy XL & Stardust

Pidgeotto belongs to a common three-stage evolution family, making Candy management relatively straightforward. The main resource decision is usually whether to save resources for Pidgeot rather than investing heavily into Pidgeotto itself.

  • Save enough Candy to complete the full Pidgey evolution family.
  • Keep high-IV Pidgeotto specimens if you plan to evolve them.
  • Consider keeping strong Pidgey instead of transferring them.
  • Collect Candy XL if you eventually plan a high-level Pidgeot.
  • Avoid unnecessary Stardust investment into Pidgeotto.
  • Check the current moveset before evolving for PvP.

20 • FAQ

Pidgeotto FAQ

What type is Pidgeotto?

Pidgeotto is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Pidgeotto weak to?

Pidgeotto is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

What is Pidgeotto's Pokédex number?

Pidgeotto is Pokédex number #017.

What generation is Pidgeotto from?

Pidgeotto was introduced in Generation 1 and is part of the original Kanto Pokédex.

What is Pidgeotto's Max CP?

Pidgeotto has a relatively low CP ceiling compared with fully evolved and Legendary Pokémon. Its maximum CP is approximately 1,509 at high level with excellent IVs.

Does Pidgeotto evolve?

Yes. Pidgeotto evolves into Pidgeot.

What does Pidgeotto evolve into?

Pidgeotto evolves into Pidgeot, the final stage of the Pidgey evolutionary family.

Can Pidgeotto be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Pidgeotto is an alternate-color form when the Shiny form is available through the relevant Pokémon GO encounter or evolution mechanics.

Can Shiny Pidgeotto evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Pidgeotto can evolve into Shiny Pidgeot, retaining its Shiny status.

Can Pidgeotto be Shadow?

Shadow Pidgeotto can exist when it is included in the current Team GO Rocket Pokémon lineup.

Is Shadow Pidgeotto stronger than regular Pidgeotto?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive damage but reduced defensive durability. The practical value of Shadow Pidgeotto depends on its moves, IVs and intended use.

Should I purify Shadow Pidgeotto?

There is no universal answer. Compare its Shadow value, IVs, evolution potential and your personal goals before making the permanent decision to purify it.

Is Pidgeotto good for raids?

Pidgeotto is generally not considered a high-end raid attacker. Trainers usually get more value by evolving strong specimens into Pidgeot.

Is Pidgeotto good in PvP?

Pidgeotto has limited standard PvP value, although it can become interesting in certain restricted cups or special formats.

What are Pidgeotto's best counters?

Electric, Ice and Rock-type attackers can exploit Pidgeotto's weaknesses.

Is a Hundo Pidgeotto worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Pidgeotto is worth keeping, particularly if you plan to evolve it into Pidgeot or want a perfect IV collection entry.

Should I power up Pidgeotto?

For most players, heavy Stardust investment is better reserved for stronger Pokémon or for its final evolution, Pidgeot.

Can Shadow Pidgeotto evolve?

Yes. When evolution is permitted by the current game mechanics, Shadow Pidgeotto can evolve while retaining its Shadow status.

Can Shiny Pidgeotto become Shadow?

Shiny and Shadow are separate Pokémon form states. Whether a particular combination can be encountered depends on Pokémon GO's current encounter mechanics.

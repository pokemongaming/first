Why Rattata's Typing Matters

Rattata's Normal typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. It is vulnerable to Fighting-type attacks while resisting Ghost-type attacks.

Because Rattata has relatively modest stats, its type alone does not make it a major raid attacker. Its main value is as a collectible, evolution-family Pokémon and potential early-game option.

09 • PVP

Rattata PvP Guide

Rattata has limited PvP relevance because of its low overall stats. It is generally not a first-choice Pokémon for standard Great, Ultra or Master League play.

Great League

Rattata can technically be used in lower-CP formats, but its performance is generally limited. Special cups, restricted formats or themed challenges can change the value of otherwise uncommon Pokémon.

Ultra League

Rattata's low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment impractical.

Master League

Rattata is not suited to Master League because its stat and CP ceiling are far below what is normally required for the format.

PvP Investment Advice

Do not spend large amounts of Stardust or Candy XL on Rattata specifically for standard PvP unless you have a special limited-format strategy.

13 • EVOLUTION

Rattata Evolution Family

Rattata evolves into Raticate using Rattata Candy. Unlike Pokémon with branching evolution systems, the standard Rattata family has a straightforward two-stage evolution line.

Rattata #019
→
Raticate #020

Evolution Requirement

Rattata → Raticate

Requires 25 Rattata Candy.

Shiny Evolution

A Shiny Rattata can evolve into Shiny Raticate. The Shiny status is retained when the Pokémon evolves.

Shadow Evolution

A Shadow Rattata can evolve into Shadow Raticate. Players should consider whether they want to retain the Shadow Pokémon before evolving.

Rattata FAQ

Is Rattata a Generation 1 Pokémon?

Yes. Rattata was introduced in Generation 1 and is Pokédex number #019.

What type is Rattata?

Rattata is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Rattata weak to?

Rattata is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What types does Rattata resist?

Rattata resists Ghost-type attacks under standard Pokémon GO type interactions.

What is Rattata's Max CP?

Rattata has a relatively low CP ceiling compared with fully evolved and stronger Pokémon. Verify the current maximum against updated Pokémon GO game data.

Is Rattata good for raids?

No. Rattata's low stats make it unsuitable for serious raid attacking compared with stronger Pokémon.

Is Rattata good in PvP?

Rattata has limited standard PvP relevance because of its low stats and CP ceiling, although special cups can change its usefulness.

Does Rattata evolve?

Yes. Rattata evolves into Raticate.

How much Candy does Rattata need to evolve?

Rattata evolves into Raticate using 25 Rattata Candy.

Can Rattata be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Rattata is available in Pokémon GO when offered through eligible encounters.

Does Shiny Rattata have better stats?

No. Shiny status changes appearance, not the Pokémon's base battle statistics.

Can Shiny Rattata evolve?

Yes. Shiny Rattata can evolve into Shiny Raticate.

Is Shadow Rattata available?

Yes. Shadow Rattata can evolve into Shadow Raticate.

What is the best Rattata moveset?

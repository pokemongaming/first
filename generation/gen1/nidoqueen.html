Nidoqueen

Nidoqueen is a Poison- and Ground-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, Nidoqueen is known for its durable defensive profile, dual typing and usefulness in selected PvP formats. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Nidoqueen.

Nidoqueen Overview

Nidoqueen is a Generation 1 Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region. It evolves from Nidorina using a Moon Stone in the main Pokémon series; in Pokémon GO, Nidorina evolves into Nidoqueen using Nidorina Candy.

Why Nidoqueen's Typing Matters

Nidoqueen's Poison/Ground typing gives it a useful collection of resistances, including Electric and Poison attacks. At the same time, the dual typing creates four major weaknesses: Water, Ground, Ice and Psychic.

Nidoqueen PvP Guide

Nidoqueen has historically been more interesting in PvP than as a dedicated raid attacker. Its Poison typing, Ground coverage and charged-move pressure can make it useful in selected formats.

Great League

Nidoqueen can fit Great League teams because its CP can be kept under the league limit. Poison Fang provides shield pressure while Ground coverage can threaten certain Steel, Poison and Electric-type opponents.

Ultra League

Nidoqueen can also be considered in Ultra League teams, although IV distribution, current rankings and the metagame should be checked before investing significant resources.

Master League

Nidoqueen's lower maximum CP makes Master League a less natural environment compared with higher-stat Pokémon. Its usefulness should be evaluated against the current Master League meta.

Shiny Nidoqueen

Shiny Nidoqueen is available in Pokémon GO. Its Shiny appearance uses an alternate coloration compared with standard Nidoqueen.

Is Shiny Nidoqueen Rare?

Purified Nidoqueen

A Purified Nidoqueen is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Nidoqueen. Purification changes its Shadow status and can alter its appraisal and resource requirements.

Nidoqueen FAQ

What type is Nidoqueen?

Nidoqueen is a Poison/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Nidoqueen weak to?

Nidoqueen is weak to Water, Ground, Ice and Psychic-type attacks.

What is Nidoqueen's Max CP?

Nidoqueen's maximum CP is 2,812 at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs. At Level 40, its maximum CP is 2,488.

Is Shiny Nidoqueen available?

Yes. Shiny Nidoqueen is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Nidoqueen available?

Shadow Nidoqueen exists in Pokémon GO. Its current availability should be checked against the active Team GO Rocket rotation.

Can Shadow Nidoqueen be purified?

An eligible Shadow Nidoqueen can be purified.

What is a 100% Nidoqueen?

A 100% Nidoqueen has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Nidoqueen?

Lucky Nidoqueen is a Nidoqueen that has received Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Is XXL Nidoqueen rare?

XXL Nidoqueen is an unusual size variant and can be valuable to collectors, especially when combined with Shiny or high-IV attributes.

What is XXS Nidoqueen?

XXS Nidoqueen is an exceptionally small Nidoqueen identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Nidoqueen good for raids?

Nidoqueen has useful Poison and Ground moves but is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker.

Is Nidoqueen good in PvP?

Nidoqueen can be useful in selected PvP formats, particularly where its Poison typing, Ground coverage and charged-move pressure are valuable.

What is the best Nidoqueen moveset?

A commonly used PvP-oriented combination is Poison Jab with Poison Fang and Earth Power. Current move rankings should be checked before making a competitive recommendation.

Should I purify Shadow Nidoqueen?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your battle goals, IVs, moves and resource budget before making the decision.

Can Nidoqueen be Shiny and Lucky?

A Pokémon can have multiple independent attributes, so a Shiny Nidoqueen can also be a Lucky Pokémon.

Can Nidoqueen be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXL Nidoqueen can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Magnemite Overview

Why Magnemite's Typing Matters

Magnemite's Electric and Steel combination gives it several resistances and makes its defensive profile different from a pure Electric-type Pokémon. Its typing also becomes important when comparing Magnemite with its later evolutions.

Magnemite Best Moves

Recommended Approach

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Magnemite

Purified Magnemite is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Magnemite. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs, level and other mechanics.

✨
Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has the Shadow status after purification.

💎
Purification

Purification can provide specific gameplay benefits and should be considered individually.

Should You Purify Magnemite?

There is no universal answer. If your goal is to keep a Shadow Pokémon for battle or collection purposes, purification may not be desirable. If you want the purified form, lower resource requirements or the associated bonuses, purification can make sense.

Think before purifying:

Once purified, the Pokémon no longer retains its Shadow status. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the change.

Lucky Magnemite

Why Keep a Lucky Magnemite?

  • Useful for collectors.

  • Useful for building a special evolution collection.
12 • 100% IV

100% IV Magnemite

A 100% IV Magnemite has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Magnemite

A 15/15/15 Magnemite is considered a perfect-IV Magnemite. Because Magnemite evolves into Magneton and eventually Magnezone, a perfect specimen can be worth keeping for future evolution.

100% Magnemite Tip

Before transferring a high-IV Magnemite, check its appraisal carefully. A perfect IV specimen can be useful as both a collector's Pokémon and an evolution candidate.

16 • PVP

Magnemite PvP Guide

Magnemite's relatively low maximum CP means it is not normally a primary choice for high-level PvP. Its later evolutions are generally more relevant when building competitive teams.

Great League

Magnemite can technically be used in CP-limited formats depending on the current rules and available moves, but players should compare it against stronger options before spending significant resources.

Ultra League

Magnemite's limited maximum CP makes Ultra League a poor natural fit compared with many fully evolved Pokémon.

Master League

Magnemite is not a realistic Master League investment. If you want to use the Magnemite family competitively, investigate its later evolutions instead.

21 • FAQ

Magnemite FAQ

What type is Magnemite?

Magnemite is an Electric and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Magnemite weak to?

Magnemite is vulnerable to Fire, Fighting and Ground-type attacks.

Does Magnemite evolve?

Yes. Magnemite evolves into Magneton and can eventually evolve into Magnezone.

Is Shiny Magnemite available?

Yes. Shiny Magnemite can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Magnemite available?

Shadow Magnemite can be available through Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket-related mechanics and rotations.

What is a 100% Magnemite?

A 100% Magnemite has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Magnemite?

Lucky Magnemite is a Magnemite that became Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is XXL Magnemite?

XXL Magnemite is a Magnemite classified as an unusually large Pokémon by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Magnemite?

XXS Magnemite is a Magnemite classified as an unusually small Pokémon by Pokémon GO's size system.

Should I evolve my 100% Magnemite?

If you want a perfect member of the Magnemite evolution family, a 100% Magnemite is an excellent candidate to keep for evolution.

Should I purify Shadow Magnemite?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, Shadow status, intended evolution and your collection or battle goals before purifying it.

Is Magnemite good for raids?

Magnemite itself is generally not a top raid attacker. Its greater value is usually as a member of an evolution family leading to Magneton and Magnezone.

Is Magnemite good in PvP?

Magnemite is generally less relevant than its evolved forms. Always check the current format, moves and cup rules before investing in a PvP build.

Can Shiny Magnemite evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Magnemite retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Can Lucky Magnemite evolve?

Yes. A Lucky Magnemite retains its Lucky status when evolved.

Can XXL Magnemite evolve?

Special size classifications should be checked against the current Pokémon GO mechanics when determining how size is represented after evolution.

Cubone

Cubone Overview

Cubone is a Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Marowak when the required Candy is available. Cubone has a relatively low maximum CP compared with many fully evolved Pokémon, so its main appeal is generally collection, evolution, specialty variants and certain limited PvP or themed uses rather than high-level raid damage.

What Does Cubone's Ground Typing Mean?

Cubone's pure Ground typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. Ground-type Pokémon are particularly useful against Electric-, Poison-, Rock-, Steel- and Fire-related matchups, although Cubone itself does not have the offensive stats of the strongest Ground attackers.

Cubone IV Guide

Individual Values add to Cubone's underlying Attack, Defense and HP values. A perfect Cubone has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

⭐ 100% Perfect Cubone

A 100% Cubone has a 15/15/15 IV combination. For collectors, this can be a valuable specimen to keep before evolving into Marowak. A perfect IV Cubone is not necessarily the strongest possible Pokémon for every battle simply because it has perfect IVs.

How to Recognize a 100% Cubone

A 100% Cubone has maximum Attack, Defense and HP IVs. The exact CP of a perfect Cubone depends on its level, so CP alone cannot identify every perfect specimen.

Can Variants Combine?

Several Cubone characteristics can exist together. For example, a Cubone can be Shiny and Lucky, or Shiny and XXS. A Shadow Cubone can also have excellent IVs. The important distinction is that some attributes describe appearance or size while others describe battle status or IV quality.

Great League

Cubone can theoretically fit within the Great League CP limit, but its performance must be evaluated against the current Cup rules and metagame. Its evolved forms and other Ground-type Pokémon can often provide more practical options.

Ultra League

Cubone FAQ

What type is Cubone?

Cubone is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Cubone weak to?

Cubone is weak to Water, Grass and Ice-type attacks.

What is Cubone's Max CP?

Cubone's maximum CP is 1,152 at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Cubone available?

Yes. Shiny Cubone is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Cubone available?

Yes. Shadow Cubone exists as a Shadow variant. Its Ground typing remains unchanged.

Should I purify Shadow Cubone?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your collection goals and intended battle use before purifying.

Can Cubone be Lucky?

Yes. Cubone can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Cubone?

A 100% Cubone has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Cubone be XXL?

Yes. Cubone can have XXL size characteristics.

Can Cubone be XXS?

Yes. Cubone can also have the XXS size category.

Does XXL Cubone have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Cubone can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Does XXS Cubone have lower stats?

Size does not automatically determine IV quality. An XXS Cubone can still have excellent IVs.

Does Cubone evolve?

Yes. Cubone evolves into Marowak.

Is Cubone good for raids?

Cubone is generally not a high-priority raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack and maximum CP. More powerful Ground-type Pokémon are normally preferred.

What is Cubone's best moveset?

Mud-Slap and Bone Club are a recommended Ground-focused moveset for Cubone.

Can Shiny Cubone evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Cubone retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Can Shadow Cubone evolve?

Shadow Cubone can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status, subject to the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Alakazam Overview

Alakazam is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon and the final evolution of Abra. It evolves from Kadabra and represents one of the highest Attack-oriented Psychic Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

Why Psychic Typing Matters

Alakazam's Psychic typing gives it a strong offensive role against Fighting and Poison Pokémon. Its high Attack stat allows it to produce significant damage, although its comparatively low bulk means that battle survival can be an important consideration.

Should You Purify Shadow Alakazam?

Do not purify automatically. Shadow Alakazam may be valuable to players who prioritize damage output. Compare the Shadow version with your intended Purified result before making a permanent decision.

Purified Alakazam

Purified Alakazam is created by purifying an eligible Shadow version. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and may affect its IVs and resource requirements.

  • Compare Shadow performance before purifying.
  • Check whether IV improvement is valuable.
  • Consider your raid or PvP objective.
  • Remember that purification cannot be reversed.

Lucky Alakazam

Lucky Alakazam is obtained through Pokémon trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups, making them attractive long-term investment candidates.

Why Lucky Alakazam Is Valuable

Alakazam can require significant Stardust when powered toward higher levels. Lucky status can make long-term investment more resource-efficient.

06 — SPECIAL FORMS

Nidoran♀ Special Variants

Nidoran♀ can appear in several Pokémon GO variants. These variants can matter for collectors even when the Pokémon itself is not a major raid attacker.

✨

Shiny Nidoran♀

Shiny Nidoran♀ has an alternate coloration and is an important collection variant. Shiny status does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

🌑

Shadow Nidoran♀

Shadow Nidoran♀ has the Shadow battle mechanics. Shadow Pokémon trade some durability for increased offensive pressure.

💎

Purified Nidoran♀

Purified Nidoran♀ is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification changes its Shadow status and affects its IVs and other associated mechanics.

🍀

Lucky Nidoran♀

Lucky Nidoran♀ is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

💯

100% Nidoran♀

A 100% Nidoran♀ has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. Perfect IV specimens are especially attractive to collectors and players building complete Pokédex collections.

📏

XXL Nidoran♀

XXL Nidoran♀ is a size variant that is larger than normal. Size does not directly increase the Pokémon's battle stats.

🔹

XXS Nidoran♀

XXS Nidoran♀ is a smaller-than-normal size variant. It is primarily useful for collection, showcases and size-based goals.

🐭

Regular Nidoran♀

The standard Nidoran♀ form. Its IVs, level, moves and evolution potential determine its practical value.

07 — SHINY

Shiny Nidoran♀

Shiny Nidoran♀ is the alternate-color form of Nidoran♀. For collectors, a Shiny specimen with strong IVs can be particularly desirable.

Shiny Collector Tip

If you encounter a Shiny Nidoran♀, consider checking its IVs, size and appraisal before transferring it. A Shiny Pokémon can be valuable for collection even when its battle stats are not exceptional.

09 — PURIFIED

Purified Nidoran♀

Purified Nidoran♀ is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Nidoran♀. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

💎
Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has Shadow status after purification.

⭐
IV Changes

Purification increases each IV by the applicable purification amount, subject to the 15-point cap.

💰
Investment

Purified Pokémon can have different power-up economics from Shadow Pokémon.

Should you purify Nidoran♀?

Purification is a choice rather than an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before committing.

10 — LUCKY

Lucky Nidoran♀

Lucky Nidoran♀ is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon are useful for players who want to reduce Stardust costs when powering up a Pokémon.

🍀
Lucky Status

The Pokémon receives the Lucky background and Lucky status after a successful qualifying trade.

💰
Lower Power-Up Cost

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when powered up.

⭐
Minimum IV Floor

Lucky trades have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary trades.

11 — 100% IV

A 100% Nidoran♀ has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

💯 Perfect Nidoran♀

A 15/15/15 Nidoran♀ is a perfect-IV specimen. For many players, the main value of a perfect Nidoran♀ is collection, evolution and personal investment rather than high-level raid performance.

How to Identify a 100% Nidoran♀

  • Check the in-game appraisal.
  • Confirm Attack IV is 15.
  • Confirm Defense IV is 15.
  • Confirm HP IV is 15.
  • Consider the Pokémon's level before comparing CP.

16 — INVESTMENT

Should You Power Up Nidoran♀?

Nidoran♀ is generally more interesting as an evolution, collection and research Pokémon than as a primary high-level attacker.

  • Keep a strong IV Nidoran♀ if you plan to evolve it.
  • Check whether the Pokémon is Shiny.
  • Keep unusual XXL and XXS specimens for collection.
  • Consider keeping a 15/15/15 specimen.
  • Compare Shadow and purified versions before purifying.
  • Lucky Nidoran♀ can be useful when minimizing Stardust costs for future evolution projects.

Nidoran♀ FAQ

What type is Nidoran♀?

Nidoran♀ is a Poison-type Pokémon.

Nidoran♀ is weak to Ground and Psychic-type attacks.

Is Nidoran♀ Shiny in Pokémon GO?

Shadow variants can exist depending on the current Pokémon GO Shadow encounter availability.

04 — SIGNATURE MECHANIC

Ditto Transform Mechanics

Transform is the defining mechanic of Ditto in Pokémon GO. Instead of fighting entirely as its original species, Ditto can copy the opposing Pokémon's appearance and battle characteristics.

Transform

Ditto's Fast Move is Transform. Its purpose is fundamentally different from a normal damaging Fast Move because it changes Ditto into the Pokémon it is battling.

Ditto's Standard Moveset

Move Type Category Purpose
Transform Normal Fast Move Copies the opponent
Struggle Normal Charged Move Charged attack

Does Ditto Keep Its Own Moves?

Transform is what makes Ditto different from a conventional Pokémon. Once Ditto transforms, the battle should be evaluated according to the game's Transform mechanics rather than assuming Ditto simply continues using a conventional Ditto moveset.

Important:

Ditto's Transform mechanics should be considered separately from normal Pokémon move rankings. It is therefore misleading to rank Ditto like a conventional raid attacker using ordinary DPS tables.

07 — WILD ENCOUNTERS

How to Find Ditto in Pokémon GO

One of the most important things to understand about Ditto is that it normally does not announce itself as Ditto before the encounter.

The Disguise Mechanic

Ditto can appear in the wild disguised as another Pokémon. After catching the disguised Pokémon, the encounter can reveal that the Pokémon was actually Ditto.

13 — RESOURCES

Ditto Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

16 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Ditto

Before Using Ditto

  • Understand the Transform mechanic.
  • Do not judge Ditto only by its CP.
  • Remember that Ditto is not a conventional raid attacker.
  • Consider the opponent before deciding whether Ditto is interesting for a battle.

For Collectors

Ditto is much more interesting as a collection target. A Shiny Ditto, high-IV Ditto, Shadow Ditto or special costume Ditto can each represent a different collection goal.

For New Players

If you are trying to complete your Pokédex, catching disguised Pokémon is one of the most important ways to find Ditto. Pay attention to the post-catch reveal rather than assuming every encounter is what it appears to be.

18 — FAQ

Ditto FAQ

What type is Ditto?

Ditto is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Ditto weak to?

Ditto's Normal typing makes it weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Ditto's maximum CP?

Ditto's maximum CP is 940 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Ditto's stats?

Ditto has 91 Attack, 91 Defense and 134 HP in Pokémon GO.

Does Ditto evolve?

No. Ditto does not have a normal evolution line.

How do you find Ditto in Pokémon GO?

Ditto can appear disguised as other Pokémon. The disguise pool can change, so players should check the current Ditto encounter information.

Can Ditto be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Ditto is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Ditto good for raids?

Ditto is not considered a conventional raid attacker. Its main value comes from its unusual Transform mechanic and collection status.

What is Ditto's best moveset?

Ditto's standard listed moveset is Transform and Struggle. Transform is the important move because it defines Ditto's battle mechanic.

Can Ditto Dynamax?

Current Pokémon GO data lists Ditto as unable to Dynamax. Always check the current game mechanics if Niantic introduces new Ditto functionality.

Is Shadow Ditto available?

Shadow availability can change through Team GO Rocket rotations and special events. Check the current Pokémon GO schedule rather than relying on a permanent availability claim.

Should I purify Shadow Ditto?

Do not purify automatically. Consider the rarity and collection value of the Shadow specimen and compare its current mechanics with the purified version.

Is Shiny Ditto rare?

Shiny Ditto is a desirable collector Pokémon, particularly because Ditto itself can be hidden behind a disguise when encountered in the wild.

Can Ditto copy another Pokémon?

Yes. Transform is Ditto's defining battle mechanic and allows it to take on the identity of the opposing Pokémon according to the game's rules.

Can Ditto be a good Pokémon to power up?

Usually, Ditto should not be a priority for large Stardust investments. Its value is primarily tied to its unusual mechanics and collection appeal.

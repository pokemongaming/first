Bulbasaur

Bulbasaur is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first member of the Bulbasaur evolutionary family and as a popular collectible with Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

01 · DATABASE OVERVIEW

Bulbasaur Overview

Bulbasaur is a dual-type Grass/Poison Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Ivysaur and eventually Venusaur, making it the first stage of one of Kanto's classic three-stage starter families.

Why Bulbasaur's Typing Matters

Bulbasaur's dual typing gives it useful resistances to several common attack types, but it also creates four important weaknesses: Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic.

09 · PVP

Bulbasaur PvP Guide

Bulbasaur is not a major Great League or Ultra League meta Pokémon, but it can have niche relevance in Little Cup and special formats where eligible.

Great League

Bulbasaur can technically be used under the Great League CP limit, but its low maximum CP and stat profile make it a poor conventional choice compared with stronger Pokémon.

Ultra League

Bulbasaur's maximum CP is too low for serious Ultra League competition.

Master League

Bulbasaur is not suitable for the Master League because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required.

Little Cup

Little Cup and other restricted formats can create niche opportunities for unevolved Pokémon. Always check the current eligibility rules before building a team.

PvP Starting Point

Vine Whip + Power Whip + Seed Bomb can be considered for eligible restricted formats.

No. Shiny Pokémon do not receive an automatic stat increase simply because they are Shiny. IVs determine the individual stat values of a Pokémon.

12 · PURIFIED

Bulbasaur Evolution Family

Evolution Resources

Bulbasaur requires Candy to evolve into Ivysaur, and Ivysaur requires additional Bulbasaur Candy to evolve into Venusaur. Keep high-IV, Shiny, Shadow and other valuable Bulbasaur specimens in mind before spending Candy.

Collection Advice

If you find a Shiny, Hundo, Shadow or Lucky Bulbasaur, consider whether you want to preserve it in its original form or evolve it as part of your collection.

20 · RESOURCES

Bulbasaur Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Bulbasaur Candy is useful for evolving Bulbasaur through the Venusaur family and for powering up Pokémon from the family. Candy XL becomes important at higher levels.

  • Save enough Bulbasaur Candy for the entire evolution family.
  • Keep high-IV specimens before transferring duplicates.
  • Consider preserving Shiny and Shadow Bulbasaur.
  • Lucky Bulbasaur can be attractive for Stardust-efficient investment.
  • Collect Candy XL if you plan to build a high-level Venusaur.

22 · PRACTICAL GUIDE

How to Use Bulbasaur

For Collectors

  • Keep Shiny Bulbasaur specimens.
  • Look for 100% IV Bulbasaur.
  • Keep interesting Lucky trades.
  • Save unusual XXL and XXS specimens.
  • Consider keeping Shadow and Purified examples.

For Evolution Projects

Before evolving a Bulbasaur, check its IVs, appraisal, Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky status and size. Evolution is permanent, so keeping a desirable Bulbasaur in its original form may be preferable for collectors.

For PvP

Check the current Little Cup and other restricted-format rules before investing in a PvP Bulbasaur. Bulbasaur is not a conventional Great, Ultra or Master League staple.

24 · FAQ

Bulbasaur FAQ

What type is Bulbasaur?

Bulbasaur is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Bulbasaur weak to?

Bulbasaur is weak to Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic attacks.

What is Bulbasaur's Max CP?

Bulbasaur's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,260.

What are Bulbasaur's base stats in Pokémon GO?

Bulbasaur has 118 Attack, 111 Defense and 128 HP.

Is Shiny Bulbasaur available?

Yes. Shiny Bulbasaur is available in Pokémon GO.

What is Shadow Bulbasaur?

Shadow Bulbasaur is the Shadow variant of Bulbasaur associated with Team GO Rocket mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Bulbasaur?

Not automatically. Compare its IVs, collection value, evolution plans and intended battle role before purifying.

What is Lucky Bulbasaur?

Lucky Bulbasaur is a Bulbasaur that has received Lucky status through a trade. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Bulbasaur?

A 100% or Hundo Bulbasaur has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a Hundo Bulbasaur?

At Level 50, a 15/15/15 Bulbasaur reaches 1,260 CP. Encounter-level Hundos can have lower CP.

What is XXL Bulbasaur?

XXL Bulbasaur is an unusually large size variant. XXL status does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

What is XXS Bulbasaur?

XXS Bulbasaur is an unusually small size variant. Its size is separate from its IV quality.

Is Lucky Bulbasaur the same as Hundo Bulbasaur?

No. Lucky is a Pokémon status obtained through trading, while Hundo describes a 15/15/15 IV combination. A Bulbasaur can be Lucky without being a Hundo.

Is Bulbasaur good for raids?

Bulbasaur is generally too weak to be a serious conventional raid attacker. Its evolutionary forms are much more suitable for battle investment.

Is Bulbasaur good in PvP?

Bulbasaur is not a major Open Great, Ultra or Master League choice, although restricted formats can create niche uses.

Does Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. Bulbasaur evolves into Ivysaur and then Venusaur.

Can Shiny Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Bulbasaur can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

Can Shadow Bulbasaur evolve?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be evolved while retaining their Shadow status.

Can a Lucky Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. Lucky status is retained when an eligible Pokémon evolves.

Can a Hundo Bulbasaur evolve?

Yes. Its perfect IV combination remains associated with the evolved Pokémon.

Why Marowak's Typing Matters

Regular Marowak is a pure Ground-type Pokémon. Ground attacks are useful against several important Pokémon types, while Marowak itself has useful defensive interactions against Electric, Poison and Rock attacks.

Marowak PvP Guide

Marowak's lower maximum CP makes it more naturally suited to CP-limited formats than Master League.

Great League

Marowak can be considered for Great League and other limited formats where its Ground typing, defensive profile and available moves provide useful matchup opportunities.

Ultra League

Master League

Regular Marowak's CP ceiling makes it a poor fit for unrestricted Master League competition compared with higher-stat Pokémon.

Marowak FAQ

What type is Marowak?

Regular Marowak is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Marowak weak to?

Regular Marowak is weak to Water, Grass and Ice-type attacks.

Does Marowak evolve?

Marowak is the evolved form of Cubone and does not normally evolve further.

Is Shiny Marowak available?

Shiny Marowak can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Marowak available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and encounter rules.

What is a 100% Marowak?

A 100% Marowak has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Marowak?

Lucky Marowak is a Marowak obtained through a trade that results in the Lucky status.

What is XXL Marowak?

XXL Marowak is an extra-large individual that can be relevant for collection and eligible Pokémon GO Showcases.

What is XXS Marowak?

XXS Marowak is an extra-small individual and can be desirable for collectors and size-related features.

What type is Alolan Marowak?

Alolan Marowak is Fire/Ghost type, unlike regular Marowak, which is Ground type.

Is Alolan Marowak good in PvP?

Alolan Marowak can have specialized PvP value because of its Fire/Ghost typing and available moves, depending on the current format and meta.

Should I purify Shadow Marowak?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of keeping the Shadow form with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

Is Hundo Marowak rare?

Why Grimer's Poison Typing Matters

Grimer's pure Poison typing gives it useful resistances against several attack types while leaving it vulnerable to Ground and Psychic attacks. This typing remains important when deciding where Grimer and its evolution Muk may fit into a team.

✨ Shiny Grimer

Shiny Grimer has alternate coloration compared with regular Grimer. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not change its underlying base stats.

✨

Shiny Grimer

A Shiny Grimer is primarily a collector's variant. It can still be evolved when eligible, allowing players to add Shiny Muk to their collection.

Should You Evolve Shiny Grimer?

  • Check whether you already own Shiny Muk.
  • Consider keeping one Shiny Grimer unevolved.
  • Compare IVs before selecting an evolution target.
  • Keep duplicate Shinies for trading if desired.
```
```

🌑 Shadow Grimer

Shadow Grimer is the Shadow version of Grimer. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from standard Pokémon.

🌑

Shadow Grimer

Shadow status does not change Grimer's Poison typing. However, the Shadow battle mechanics make it a separate investment decision.

Shadow Grimer vs Regular Grimer

Feature Regular Shadow
Type Poison Poison
Shadow Status No Yes
Battle Profile Standard Higher offensive pressure
Durability Standard Reduced by Shadow mechanics
Purification Not Applicable Possible when eligible
Shadow warning:

Do not purify a Shadow Grimer automatically. Consider whether you want a Shadow Muk, a Purified Muk or a standard version before making the choice.

```
```

💠 Purified Grimer

Purified Grimer is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Grimer. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

💠

Purified Grimer

Purified Grimer is useful for collectors who want a complete Grimer form collection and players who prefer the standard purified battle profile.

Before Purifying

  • Check your Shadow Grimer's IVs.
  • Decide whether you want Shadow Muk.
  • Compare the IV improvement after purification.
  • Consider collection value.
  • Avoid purifying only because the appraisal bar appears higher.
```
```

🍀 Lucky Grimer

Lucky Grimer is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players planning to power up their Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Grimer

A Lucky Grimer can be an attractive evolution project because Lucky Pokémon generally have reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

Should You Evolve Lucky Grimer?

Lucky Grimer can be a useful candidate for Muk if its IVs fit your intended build. The Lucky status remains important for long-term Stardust efficiency.

XXL Grimer

Grimer FAQ

What type is Grimer?

Grimer is a Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Grimer weak to?

Grimer is weak to Ground and Psychic attacks.

What does Grimer evolve into?

Grimer evolves into Muk.

Is Shiny Grimer available?

Shiny Grimer can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Grimer be Shadow?

Grimer can have a Shadow variant when it is available through eligible Team GO Rocket content.

Should I purify Shadow Grimer?

Not automatically. Consider whether you want Shadow Muk before purifying it.

What is a 100% Grimer?

A 100% Grimer has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Grimer have better stats?

XXL size does not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Does XXS Grimer have weaker stats?

No. XXS size is separate from Grimer's IVs and normal battle stats.

Is Lucky Grimer worth keeping?

Lucky Grimer can be useful because Lucky Pokémon are attractive for Stardust-efficient power-up projects.

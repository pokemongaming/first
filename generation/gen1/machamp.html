Machamp Overview

Machamp is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Machoke and is the final stage of the Machop family.

Machamp has been especially useful as an accessible Fighting attacker because the Machop family has been featured through various events and encounter methods.

Purified Machamp

Purified Machamp is created by purifying a Shadow Machamp. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

PURIFIED

Purified Machamp

A purified Machamp no longer retains the Shadow status.

DECISION

Should You Purify?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Purification Tip

If your main goal is maximum Shadow raid damage, keeping a strong Shadow Machamp may be preferable to purification.

Lucky Machamp

Lucky Machamp is a special status obtained through a Lucky Trade. Its main practical benefit is reduced Stardust cost for powering up compared with a normal Pokémon.

LUCKY

Lucky Machamp

A Machamp with the Lucky status can be an attractive long-term investment because of its reduced power-up Stardust cost.

INVESTMENT

Why Lucky Matters

Lucky status is especially useful when you plan to power a Pokémon to high levels.

Lucky status does not mean the Pokémon automatically has perfect IVs. Always inspect the IVs after a trade.

100% IV Machamp

A 100% IV Machamp has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is the highest possible IV combination for a standard Pokémon.

XXL Machamp

XXL Machamp is an unusually large individual. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Machamp can have any IV combination.

XXL

XXL Machamp

XXL Does Not Mean 100%

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Machamp can be 100% IV, low IV or anything in between.

XXS Machamp

Machamp FAQ

What type is Machamp?

Machamp is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Machamp weak to?

Machamp is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic attacks.

Is Machamp good for raids?

Machamp can be a useful Fighting-type raid attacker, particularly when Fighting damage is super effective.

Is Shiny Machamp available?

Yes. Shiny Machamp is available through eligible Shiny encounters involving the Machop family.

Is Shadow Machamp good?

Shadow Machamp can be a strong damage-focused Fighting attacker. Its increased offensive pressure comes with reduced durability and additional investment considerations.

Should I purify Shadow Machamp?

Not automatically. If your goal is raid damage, compare the Shadow version with the purified version before making the decision.

What is Lucky Machamp?

Lucky Machamp is a Machamp that has received the Lucky status through the trade mechanic. Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is 100% Machamp?

A 100% Machamp has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Machamp rare?

XXL Machamp refers to an unusually large individual. Its rarity depends on encounter distribution and individual size values.

What is XXS Machamp?

XXS Machamp is an unusually small individual. XXS status is separate from IV quality.

Can an XXL Machamp be 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Machamp can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can a Lucky Machamp be 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Machamp can have perfect IVs after the trade, although Lucky status does not guarantee a perfect IV combination.

What is the best Machamp moveset?

Counter and Dynamic Punch are a strong Fighting-focused combination for Machamp. Always verify current move availability before making a time-sensitive recommendation.

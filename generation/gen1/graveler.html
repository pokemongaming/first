Graveler

Graveler is a Rock and Ground-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Geodude and can evolve into Golem. In Pokémon GO, Graveler is a mid-stage evolution with several collectible variants, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Graveler Overview

Graveler is the middle evolution in the Geodude family. It evolves from Geodude and can later evolve into Golem. Its Rock and Ground typing gives it access to several useful offensive matchups while also creating important defensive weaknesses.

Why Graveler's Typing Matters

Rock and Ground is a powerful offensive typing combination, but it also gives Graveler several important weaknesses. Players should consider the raid boss or PvP opponent before using Graveler in battle.

Graveler Forms & Variants

The following variants represent collectible or gameplay states that a Graveler can have in Pokémon GO.

Regular Graveler

The standard version of Graveler with its normal Rock and Ground typing.

✨ Shiny Graveler

Shiny Graveler has alternate coloration and is primarily a collectible version of the standard Pokémon.

🌑 Shadow Graveler

Shadow Graveler has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability compared with the standard version.

✨ Purified Graveler

Purified Graveler is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Graveler.

🍀 Lucky Graveler

Lucky Graveler requires less Stardust for power-ups, making it an attractive long-term investment.

💯 100% Graveler

A perfect Graveler has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

⬆ XXL Graveler

XXL Graveler is a larger size variant useful for collection and size-based showcases.

⬇ XXS Graveler

XXS Graveler is a smaller size variant and is primarily a collectible form.

Shadow Graveler

Shadow Graveler has the Shadow Pokémon battle modifier, which increases offensive potential while reducing defensive durability.

⚔️ Higher Damage

Shadow Pokémon focus more heavily on offensive pressure.

🛡 Lower Durability

Shadow Pokémon take increased damage.

🌑 Shadow Status

The Pokémon retains the Shadow state until purification.

Should You Purify Shadow Graveler?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Graveler simply to increase its IVs. Consider whether you want a damage-focused Shadow version or a lower-cost Purified version.

Purified Graveler

Purified Graveler is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Graveler. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state and can make future power-ups more resource friendly.

  • Purified Pokémon are no longer Shadow Pokémon.
  • Purification can improve IVs.
  • Purified Pokémon have different resource considerations.
  • Think carefully before permanently removing Shadow status.

Lucky Graveler

Lucky Graveler is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. This can make a strong Lucky specimen attractive if you plan to evolve it into Golem.

Should You Evolve a 100% Graveler?

A perfect Graveler can be an attractive candidate for evolution into Golem, especially for collectors who want a perfect-IV final evolution.

XXL Graveler & XXS Graveler

XXL and XXS Graveler are size variants. These forms do not normally represent separate combat species, but they can be valuable for collectors and size-related showcases.

⬆ XXL Graveler

An unusually large Graveler that can be useful for Showcases and collection goals.

⬇ XXS Graveler

An unusually small Graveler that provides a rare size variant for collectors.

Graveler Raid Performance

Graveler is generally an intermediate evolution rather than a top-tier raid attacker. Players looking for maximum Rock or Ground damage will usually consider stronger fully evolved Pokémon.

Rock Role

Useful for some early-game or limited collections.

Ground Role

Long-Term Value

Many players may eventually evolve Graveler into Golem.

Graveler PvP Guide

Graveler's usefulness in PvP depends heavily on league CP limits, moveset availability and the current metagame.

Great League

Graveler may occasionally be used for specific matchups, but its weaknesses should be considered carefully.

Ultra League

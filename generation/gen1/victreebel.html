Victreebel Overview

Victreebel is the final evolution of the Bellsprout family. It evolves from Weepinbell and is a dual Grass / Poison-type Pokémon. Its Pokémon GO profile gives it a relatively strong Attack stat but considerably less bulk than many dedicated defensive Grass Pokémon.

Lucky Victreebel

Lucky Victreebel is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Victreebel

A Lucky Victreebel can be especially attractive to players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of a long-term investment.

Lucky Victreebel & IVs

A Lucky Pokémon is not automatically a hundo. Its IVs are determined by the trade mechanics, so always check the appraisal after the trade.

Collector Combination

Shiny + Lucky + High IV Victreebel is an especially desirable collection combination.

XXL & XXS Victreebel

XXL and XXS describe size variations. They are not separate species, forms or evolutions.

📏 XXL Victreebel Extra-large size classification

XXL Victreebel can be valuable for collectors, showcases and size-related achievements.

📐 XXS Victreebel Extra-small size classification

XXS Victreebel is an unusually small specimen and can be desirable for collectors.

Does Size Change Combat Stats?

XXL and XXS status should not be confused with IVs. A Pokémon's size classification is separate from its Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Collection tip:

A Shiny, Lucky, hundo, XXL or XXS Victreebel can all represent different collection goals. One specimen does not need to satisfy every category.

Victreebel PvP Guide

Victreebel's PvP value is more significant than its general raid value. Its Grass / Poison typing and charged-move options can create useful matchups.

Great League

Victreebel can be considered in Great League teams, particularly when its fast-move pressure and Grass or Poison coverage fit the current metagame.

Ultra League

Victreebel can also be evaluated in Ultra League, although its relatively modest bulk means team composition and shield management become important.

Master League

Standard Victreebel's CP ceiling makes Master League a much less natural environment compared with the lower CP leagues.

How to Use Victreebel

Before PvP

  • Check the league CP limit.
  • Review the current PvP meta.
  • Compare Victreebel's IV spread with the intended league.
  • Select the appropriate fast and charged moves.
  • Consider shield pressure and matchup coverage.

Before Mega Evolution

  • Choose a Victreebel with strong IVs.
  • Check its moveset.
  • Consider whether you want Grass or Poison Mega utility.
  • Save sufficient Mega Energy.
  • Consider Mega Level progression.

Victreebel Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Long-term Victreebel investment requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Mega Evolution adds Mega Energy to the resource calculation.

  • Keep high-IV Victreebel candidates.
  • Save Candy XL for long-term builds.
  • Compare Stardust costs before powering up multiple copies.
  • Keep useful Shadow specimens before purifying.
  • Consider a strong Victreebel for future Mega use.

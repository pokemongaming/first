Shiny Drowzee

Shiny Drowzee is the alternate-colour version of regular Drowzee. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically improve battle stats.

✨ Shiny Drowzee

A Shiny Drowzee can evolve into Shiny Hypno while retaining its Shiny appearance.

```
```

Shadow Drowzee

Shadow Drowzee is a Shadow variation of Drowzee. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability.

🌑

Shadow Drowzee

Shadow Drowzee can be evaluated separately from regular Drowzee, especially if you plan to evolve it into Shadow Hypno.

Shadow Investment Checklist

  • Check the IVs before investing Stardust.
  • Consider evolving Shadow Drowzee into Hypno.
  • Check current Frustration removal opportunities.
  • Compare Shadow Hypno with your existing Pokémon.
  • Do not purify automatically.
```
```

Purified Drowzee

Purified Drowzee is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Drowzee. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and may improve its IVs.

✨ Purified Drowzee

Consider your long-term goal before purifying. A Shadow Pokémon and its Purified version can serve different purposes.

```
```

Lucky Drowzee

Lucky Drowzee is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon generally have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Investment

A Lucky Drowzee can be useful for players who plan to power it up and evolve it into Hypno.

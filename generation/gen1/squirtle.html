Squirtle

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the first stage of the Squirtle evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Squirtle is primarily valued as a collectible, evolution candidate and source of Blastoise Candy, while special variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Squirtle give collectors additional goals.

Squirtle Overview

What Does Squirtle's Water Typing Mean?

Squirtle is a pure Water-type Pokémon, so its type matchups are straightforward. Water attacks are useful against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Squirtle itself must be careful around Electric and Grass attacks.

Squirtle PvP Guide

Great League

Squirtle's low maximum CP means it can fit more naturally into CP-limited formats than many fully evolved Pokémon. However, whether it is competitive depends heavily on the specific cup rules, moveset and current metagame.

Ultra League & Master League

Squirtle is generally not a standard choice for open Ultra League or Master League play because its overall stat profile is much lower than the Pokémon normally used in those formats.

10 • SHADOW

Shadow Squirtle

Shadow Squirtle is a special Shadow variant of Squirtle. Its Shadow status changes its battle characteristics while leaving its basic Water typing unchanged.

SHADOW

Shadow Squirtle & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Attack Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration depends on Pokémon GO events and current Team GO Rocket mechanics.

Do not purify automatically.

If your goal is to eventually use a Shadow Blastoise or preserve the Shadow specimen for collection purposes, compare the Shadow and Purified options before making the purification decision.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Squirtle

🍀
Lucky Squirtle

Lucky Squirtle is a special trade outcome with a distinct Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a good Lucky Squirtle an attractive candidate for collectors or players who plan to evolve and invest.

Why Keep a Lucky Squirtle?

  • Lucky status reduces Stardust costs for power-ups.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor from the Lucky trade mechanic.
  • A Lucky Squirtle can be evolved while retaining Lucky status.
  • Lucky Shiny Squirtle can be particularly desirable to collectors.

Lucky Squirtle Investment

Because Squirtle itself is not normally a major raid attacker, you may get more practical value by evolving a strong Lucky Squirtle into Blastoise if the resulting moveset and current game meta justify the investment.

13 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Squirtle

A 100% Squirtle has perfect Individual Values of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV

What Does 100% Squirtle Mean?

A 100% Squirtle has a total IV combination of 15/15/15. It is a perfect-IV specimen and is especially appealing for collectors and players planning to evolve it.

Should You Keep a 100% Squirtle?

Yes, if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon. Before transferring a Squirtle, check its appraisal because a perfect specimen can be useful as an evolution candidate or as a permanent collection entry.

14 • SIZE

XXL Squirtle & XXS Squirtle

Squirtle can also be collected for its size. XXL and XXS specimens are especially interesting for trainers working on size records, medals or personal collection goals.

XXL SQUIRTLE

Large Squirtle

An XXL Squirtle is a notably large specimen. Its size can make it more interesting to collectors and players who track Pokémon size records.

XXS SQUIRTLE

Small Squirtle

An XXS Squirtle is an unusually small specimen and can be valuable to collectors pursuing both ends of the Pokémon size spectrum.

Do XXL and XXS Squirtle Have Better Battle Stats?

No. Size designation should not be confused with IV quality. An XXL Squirtle is not automatically stronger than an ordinary Squirtle, and an XXS Squirtle is not automatically weaker.

Collection tip:

If you are building a complete Squirtle collection, consider tracking IV quality, Shiny status, Shadow/Purified status, Lucky status and size separately.

21 • FAQ

Squirtle FAQ

What type is Squirtle?

Squirtle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Squirtle weak against?

Squirtle is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Does Squirtle evolve?

Yes. Squirtle evolves into Wartortle and then Blastoise.

Is Shiny Squirtle available?

Yes. Shiny Squirtle is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and events.

Is Shadow Squirtle available?

Shadow Squirtle has been available through Team GO Rocket and related Pokémon GO distributions. Current availability should be checked against the latest game rotation.

What is Purified Squirtle?

Purified Squirtle is a Squirtle that was originally Shadow and was subsequently purified.

What is Lucky Squirtle?

Lucky Squirtle is a Squirtle that became Lucky through the Pokémon GO trade mechanic.

What is a 100% Squirtle?

A 100% Squirtle has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Squirtle?

An XXL Squirtle is an unusually large Squirtle according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Squirtle?

An XXS Squirtle is an unusually small Squirtle according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Are XXL Squirtle stronger?

No. XXL size should not be confused with better IVs or higher battle performance.

Are XXS Squirtle weaker?

No. XXS size does not automatically mean lower IVs or weaker battle performance.

Is Squirtle good for raids?

Regular Squirtle is generally not a top raid attacker. Its main value is its evolution family and collection variants.

Should I evolve my 100% Squirtle?

If your goal is to own a perfect-IV member of the Squirtle evolution family, a 100% Squirtle is an excellent evolution candidate. Check current moves and your intended use first.

Should I purify Shadow Squirtle?

Not automatically. Consider whether you prefer keeping the Shadow status, evolving it later, or having a Purified specimen before making the decision.

Can Lucky Squirtle be evolved?

Yes. Lucky status remains when a Pokémon evolves.

Can Shiny Squirtle evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Squirtle remains Shiny when evolved.

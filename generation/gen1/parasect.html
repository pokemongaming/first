Parasect Overview

Parasect is the evolved form of Paras and is known for its unusual Bug and Grass dual typing. Unlike many Legendary or pseudo-Legendary Pokémon, Parasect is not normally selected as a premier raid attacker. Its value is more strongly connected to collection goals, special forms, Pokédex completion and niche battle situations.

Why Parasect's Typing Matters

Parasect's Bug and Grass combination gives it access to useful offensive coverage, but the dual typing also creates several important weaknesses. In particular, Fire-type attacks are especially dangerous because both Bug and Grass are vulnerable to Fire.

Parasect PvP Guide

Parasect is more of a niche choice than a mainstream PvP Pokémon. Its Bug and Grass typing can create useful matchups, but its overall performance depends heavily on the current league, moveset and opponent lineup.

Great League

Parasect can be considered for limited or themed Great League teams, especially when its typing and moves provide a useful matchup. It should not be treated as a universal meta choice.

Ultra League

Parasect's maximum CP and overall stat profile make it less natural for serious Ultra League investment compared with many dedicated Ultra League Pokémon.

Master League

Parasect's CP ceiling makes Master League an especially difficult environment for it. It is primarily a collection, challenge or specialist choice rather than a standard Master League investment.

Parasect FAQ

What type is Parasect?

Parasect is a Bug and Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Parasect weak to?

Parasect is vulnerable to several attacking types, including Fire, Flying, Ice, Poison, Rock and Bug.

Is Parasect good for raids?

Parasect is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Bug- and Grass-type options are usually preferred for optimized raid teams.

Is Shiny Parasect available?

Shiny Parasect is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can be kept as a separate collection variant.

Can Parasect be Shadow?

Shadow Parasect availability depends on the current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotations.

What is a 100% Parasect?

A 100% Parasect has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Parasect?

Lucky Parasect is a Parasect obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system with Lucky status.

What is XXL Parasect?

XXL Parasect is a Parasect classified as an extra-large Pokémon by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Parasect?

XXS Parasect is a Parasect classified as an extra-small Pokémon by Pokémon GO's size system.

Should I purify Shadow Parasect?

Not automatically. Consider why you want the Pokémon before purification and compare the value of keeping the Shadow status with the purified result.

Does Parasect evolve?

Paras evolves into Parasect. Parasect does not have a further normal evolution.

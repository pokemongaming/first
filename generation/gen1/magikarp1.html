Magikarp Overview

Magikarp is a pure Water-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it has very low battle stats and a low maximum CP, but its real value comes from its evolution into the much stronger Gyarados.

Is Magikarp Good for Raids or PvP?

Raids

Magikarp is not a practical raid attacker. Its very low Attack stat and limited moveset make it dramatically weaker than usable Water-type attackers.

PvP

Magikarp is also not a conventional Great League, Ultra League or Master League Pokémon. Its low CP and limited moveset prevent it from functioning as a normal competitive PvP choice.

Shadow Magikarp

Shadow Magikarp is the Shadow variant of Magikarp. Shadow Pokémon have their own battle mechanics and can later be purified.

😈

Shadow Magikarp

Shadow Magikarp retains its Water typing. It can be purified when eligible, but players should consider their goals before spending resources on purification.

Shadow Water #129

Shadow Magikarp Mechanics

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive power and reduced defensive durability compared with their regular counterparts. For Magikarp, however, this does not overcome its fundamental weakness as a battle Pokémon.

Shadow Magikarp Moves

Shadow Charged Move

Newly caught Shadow Magikarp can have Frustration. Frustration may require a suitable Team GO Rocket event or other eligible mechanic before it can be replaced.

Purified Magikarp

Purified Magikarp is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Magikarp. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the normal purification benefits.

💎

Lucky Magikarp

Lucky Magikarp is a special status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background in the Pokémon storage system and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Magikarp

Lucky status does not change Magikarp's species or Water typing. Its main advantage is reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

Lucky Trade Water

Why Keep a Lucky Magikarp?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collectors completing special-status Pokémon.
  • Can be evolved into a Lucky Gyarados.
  • A Lucky Shiny Magikarp is especially desirable to collectors.

100% IV Magikarp — Hundo Magikarp

A 100% IV Magikarp has perfect individual values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

100% IV Magikarp

15/15/15 Magikarp is considered a perfect-IV or "Hundo" Magikarp. Although Magikarp itself has limited battle value, a Hundo can be an excellent collection target and can become a perfect Gyarados after evolution.

Hundo CP

At Level 50, a 15/15/15 Magikarp reaches a maximum of 310 CP.

Magikarp Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

The most important resource for Magikarp is usually Candy rather than Stardust. You need 400 Magikarp Candy for the standard evolution into Gyarados.

  • Catch Magikarp whenever available.
  • Use Pinap Berries when farming Candy.
  • Transfer unwanted Magikarp for additional Candy.
  • Set Magikarp as your Buddy when you want extra Candy.
  • Save high-IV, Shiny, Shadow and Lucky specimens before transferring.
  • Keep enough Candy for your preferred Gyarados evolution.
Buddy Distance

Magikarp has a 1 km Buddy distance, making Candy collection through walking relatively convenient.

Magikarp FAQ

What type is Magikarp?

Magikarp is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Magikarp weak to?

Magikarp is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What is Magikarp's max CP?

Magikarp reaches 310 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

How much Candy does Magikarp need to evolve?

Magikarp requires 400 Magikarp Candy to evolve into Gyarados.

Is Shiny Magikarp available?

Yes. Shiny Magikarp is available in Pokémon GO and has a distinctive golden appearance.

Is Shadow Magikarp available?

Yes. Shadow Magikarp can be obtained through eligible Shadow encounters and Team GO Rocket-related content.

Can Shadow Magikarp be purified?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Magikarp can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and applies the standard purification mechanics.

What is a 100% Magikarp?

A 100% or Hundo Magikarp has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Magikarp?

An XXL Magikarp is a Magikarp that falls into the XXL size category. Size and IVs are separate properties.

What is an XXS Magikarp?

An XXS Magikarp is an exceptionally small Magikarp that falls into the XXS size category.

Is Lucky Magikarp worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Magikarp can be useful for collectors and can be evolved into a Lucky Gyarados. Lucky Pokémon also require less Stardust to power up.

Is Magikarp good for raids?

No. Magikarp has very low battle performance and is not a practical raid attacker.

Is Magikarp good for PvP?

Magikarp is not normally useful in competitive PvP because of its low CP, stats and limited moveset.

Is a Hundo Magikarp worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Magikarp is a perfect-IV Pokémon and can be evolved into a perfect Gyarados.

Can Shiny Magikarp evolve into Shiny Gyarados?

Yes. A Shiny Magikarp retains its Shiny identity when evolved into Gyarados.

What moves does Magikarp have?

Magikarp's standard moveset is Splash and Struggle.

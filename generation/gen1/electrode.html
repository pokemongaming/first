Electrode Overview

Electrode is the evolved form of Voltorb and was introduced in Generation 1. It is a pure Electric-type Pokémon with balanced Attack and Defense and relatively high Speed in the main Pokémon games.

In Pokémon GO, Electrode has a more specialized role. It is not generally considered one of the strongest Electric raid attackers, but its moveset and typing can give it opportunities in PvP and themed battles.

Electrode PvP Guide

Electrode can be more interesting in PvP than in raids because its moveset, typing and relatively fast energy generation can create opportunities that raw CP rankings do not show.

Great League

Great League is one of the more natural formats for Electrode because the 1,500 CP limit allows it to use its defensive profile without requiring extremely high levels.

Ultra League

Electrode can also be considered for Ultra League depending on the available moves, IV distribution and current meta.

Master League

Electrode's maximum CP is not competitive with the highest-stat Pokémon commonly used in Master League, making this its least natural open-league environment.

Electrode FAQ

What type is Electrode?

Electrode is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Electrode weak against?

Electrode is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Electrode good for raids?

Electrode is generally not considered a top-tier Electric raid attacker. Stronger Electric options are usually preferred for optimized PvE teams.

Is Electrode good in PvP?

Electrode can have niche PvP value, particularly in CP-limited leagues, depending on its moveset, IVs and the current metagame.

Is Shiny Electrode available?

Yes. Shiny Electrode is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and events.

Is Shadow Electrode available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I purify Shadow Electrode?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your battle plans and collection goals before purifying.

What is a 100% Electrode?

A 100% Electrode has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Electrode?

Lucky Electrode is an Electrode that became Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is XXL Electrode?

XXL Electrode is an unusually large Electrode. XXL is a size characteristic rather than a separate species.

What is XXS Electrode?

XXS Electrode is an unusually small Electrode. Like XXL, it is a size category rather than a separate Pokémon species.

Does Electrode evolve?

Electrode is the final standard evolution stage of Voltorb and does not normally evolve further.

Is a Shiny Electrode different in battle?

No. Shiny status is primarily an appearance characteristic and does not create a separate base-stat species.

Is Lucky Electrode better than normal Electrode?

Lucky status provides Stardust-related benefits, but it does not automatically make Electrode a stronger battler than every normal Electrode.

Can an Electrode be Shiny, Lucky and 100% at the same time?

Yes. These characteristics can describe the same individual Pokémon when the relevant conditions are met. Special status, IVs and size are separate characteristics.

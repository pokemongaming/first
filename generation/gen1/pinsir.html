01 • OVERVIEW

Pinsir Overview

Pinsir is a pure Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Unlike many Kanto Pokémon, Pinsir does not evolve into another standard Pokémon. Its major special form in Pokémon GO is Mega Pinsir.

Why Pinsir's Typing Matters

Pinsir's pure Bug typing gives it a focused identity as a Bug attacker. Its weaknesses and resistances are determined by the standard Bug type, while its Mega Evolution adds the Flying type and changes its defensive profile.

09 • PVP

Pinsir PvP Guide

Pinsir is much more naturally suited to offensive PvE use than to the highest levels of Pokémon GO PvP. Its effectiveness depends heavily on league format, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Pinsir can be tested in CP-limited environments, but its overall PvP value should be judged against the current Bug and Fighting-type competition.

Ultra League

Its stats and typing give it some matchup potential, but the effectiveness of Pinsir depends heavily on the available moves and current opponents.

Master League

Pinsir's maximum CP is considerably lower than the Pokémon normally prioritized for Master League, making it a niche option rather than a standard Master League investment.

What Is Mega Evolution?

Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation. Your original Pokémon remains Pinsir, while its Mega form is active for the relevant duration and provides increased battle power plus Mega-related bonuses.

Weather Boost

Regular Pinsir receives a boost in Rainy weather because it is a Bug-type Pokémon. Mega Pinsir can be boosted by Rain and Windy weather because of its Bug / Flying typing.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Pinsir

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Bug damage is useful against the boss.
  • Confirm Pinsir has the intended moveset.
  • Check its level and IVs.
  • Compare regular, Shadow and Mega options.
  • Consider weather and Mega bonuses.

During Team Building

Do not choose Pinsir simply because it has a high Attack stat. The best team depends on the raid boss, boss moveset, weather, available counters and whether a Mega slot is being used.

Long-Term Investment

The strongest reason to keep an excellent Pinsir is the combination of regular, Shadow and Mega possibilities. This makes a high-quality specimen more useful than a simple single-form attacker.

18 • FAQ

Pinsir FAQ

What type is Pinsir?

Regular Pinsir is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Pinsir weak to?

Pinsir is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What is Pinsir's max CP?

Regular Pinsir can reach a maximum CP of 3,345 at level 50.

What are the best moves for Pinsir?

Fury Cutter and X-Scissor are a strong Bug-focused moveset for Pinsir.

Is Shiny Pinsir available?

Yes. Shiny Pinsir is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Pinsir available?

Yes. Shadow Pinsir is an available Shadow form.

Is Shadow Pinsir good for raids?

Shadow Pinsir can be useful for players who prioritize offensive damage, although it should be compared against regular and Mega Bug attackers before investing heavily.

Does Pinsir evolve?

Pinsir does not have a traditional evolution chain. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Pinsir.

What type is Mega Pinsir?

Mega Pinsir is Bug / Flying type.

What is Mega Pinsir's max CP?

Mega Pinsir can reach a maximum CP of 4,728.

What is Mega Pinsir weak to?

Mega Pinsir is especially vulnerable to Rock-type attacks and is also weak to Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice.

Is Mega Pinsir good for raids?

Yes. Mega Pinsir has strong Bug-type raid value and can provide useful Mega-related team bonuses.

Can Shiny Pinsir become Mega Pinsir?

Yes. A Shiny Pinsir can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Can Shadow Pinsir Mega Evolve?

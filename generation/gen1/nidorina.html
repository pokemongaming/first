Why Nidorina's Typing Matters

Nidorina's pure Poison typing gives it a defensive matchup profile that differs from its eventual evolution, Nidoqueen. The Poison type is especially relevant when considering Grass and Fairy matchups.

Lucky Nidorina

Lucky Nidorina is a special trade result with a distinct Lucky background in Pokémon GO. Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust to power up.

LUCKY POKÉMON

⭐ Lucky Nidorina

A Lucky Nidorina can be an appealing collector target and may be useful when you want to reduce Stardust costs for powering up a Pokémon.

100% IV Nidorina

A 100% IV Nidorina has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are the maximum individual values possible for a Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
Perfect IV

15/15/15 is commonly called a hundo or perfect-IV Pokémon. For Nidorina, the collection value can be more important than raw battle performance.

Should You Power Up a 100% Nidorina?

A perfect Nidorina can be worth keeping for collection purposes. If your main goal is battle performance, compare the cost and benefits of evolving it into Nidoqueen before investing heavily.

XXL & XXS Nidorina

Pokémon GO can record size categories such as XXL and XXS. These size characteristics are separate from IVs and should not be confused with a Pokémon's Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

📏 XXL Nidorina

An XXL Nidorina is a particularly large specimen that can be valuable for size-based collection goals and certain game features.

EXTRA LARGE
📐 XXS Nidorina

An XXS Nidorina is a particularly small specimen. Size is independent from the Pokémon's IV percentage.

EXTRA SMALL
Important:

XXL or XXS does not automatically mean high IV. A Pokémon can be XXL, XXS, 100% IV, Shiny or Lucky independently, depending on the individual Pokémon.

Nidorina FAQ

What type is Nidorina?

Nidorina is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Nidorina weak against?

Nidorina is weak to Ground- and Psychic-type attacks.

What does Nidorina evolve into?

Nidorina evolves into Nidoqueen.

Is Shiny Nidorina available?

Shiny Nidorina is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounter sources.

Is Shadow Nidorina available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and encounter rules.

What is a 100% Nidorina?

A 100% Nidorina has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Nidorina?

Lucky Nidorina is a Nidorina obtained as a Lucky Pokémon through trading mechanics.

What is XXL Nidorina?

XXL Nidorina is a particularly large Nidorina based on Pokémon GO's size mechanics.

What is XXS Nidorina?

XXS Nidorina is a particularly small Nidorina based on Pokémon GO's size mechanics.

Is Nidorina good for raids?

Nidorina is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Players should normally compare it with stronger Poison attackers before investing heavily.

Should I evolve Nidorina?

If your goal is battle performance or completing the evolution family, evolving Nidorina into Nidoqueen can be worthwhile.

Should I keep a 100% Nidorina?

Yes. A perfect-IV Nidorina is a valuable collection specimen. You can decide later whether to keep it unevolved or evolve it into Nidoqueen.

Does XXL mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

Does XXS mean low IV?

No. XXS describes size, not IV quality.

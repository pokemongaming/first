Omanyte

Omanyte is a Rock- and Water-type Fossil Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, Omanyte is best known as the first stage of the Omastar evolution family and can also appear in Shiny and Shadow forms when those forms are available through the game.

01 — OVERVIEW

Omanyte Overview

Why Omanyte's Dual Typing Matters

Rock/Water is a distinctive combination. When evaluating Omanyte, you need to consider both types rather than looking at Water or Rock in isolation. The combination determines its final weakness and resistance profile.

08 — IV GUIDE

Omanyte IV Guide

Individual Values add extra points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Omanyte is a perfect-IV specimen, although the ideal IV spread depends on how you intend to use it.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Omanyte

A 15/15/15 Omanyte is considered perfect IV. For collectors, research rewards and evolution projects, a high-IV specimen can be worth keeping.

IV Tip

Do not transfer an Omanyte solely because its CP is low. Check its appraisal and consider its future evolution into Omastar before making the decision.

09 — PVP

Omanyte PvP Guide

Omanyte can be considered for specialized low-CP formats, but it is generally overshadowed by stronger Pokémon and its evolved form.

Great League

Omanyte's maximum CP allows it to enter Great League, but its actual viability depends heavily on its moveset, IV distribution and the current PvP meta.

Ultra League

Omanyte's low maximum CP makes it poorly suited to Ultra League compared with Pokémon that can reach much higher levels.

Master League

Omanyte is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the level generally required for the format.

10 — EVOLUTION

Omanyte Evolution Line

Omanyte is the first stage of a two-stage evolution family. It evolves into Omastar using Omanyte Candy.

Omanyte #138 · Rock / Water
→
Omastar #139 · Rock / Water
Evolution Requirement

Omanyte evolves into Omastar after collecting 50 Omanyte Candy.

Why Evolve Omanyte?

The primary gameplay reason to collect Omanyte Candy is to complete the evolution family and build Omastar. A strong IV Omanyte can therefore be worth saving even if it is not useful in its unevolved form.

18 — FAQ

Omanyte FAQ

What type is Omanyte?

Omanyte is a Rock/Water-type Pokémon.

What is Omanyte weak to?

Omanyte is weak to Grass, Electric, Fighting, Ground and Steel-type attacks.

Does Omanyte evolve?

Yes. Omanyte evolves into Omastar using 50 Omanyte Candy.

Is Shiny Omanyte available?

Shiny Omanyte is an available alternate form when Shiny Omanyte encounters are enabled in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Omanyte available?

Shadow Omanyte availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotations.

Is Omanyte good for raids?

Omanyte is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its greater value is usually as an evolution, collection and Pokédex Pokémon.

Is Omanyte good for PvP?

Omanyte can be used in certain low-CP formats, but its PvP usefulness depends on the current meta and moveset.

How much Candy does Omanyte need to evolve?

Omanyte requires 50 Omanyte Candy to evolve into Omastar.

Can Shiny Omanyte evolve into Shiny Omastar?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Omanyte evolves into Omastar.

Should I purify Shadow Omanyte?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow form's battle, collection and evolution value before deciding whether purification makes sense for your account.

What is the perfect IV Omanyte?

A perfect Omanyte has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Omanyte a Fossil Pokémon?

Yes. Omanyte is a Fossil Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1.

08 · IV GUIDE

Kabuto IV Guide

Kabuto has three Individual Values: Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Kabuto is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Kabuto

A 15/15/15 Kabuto is valuable for collectors and players who want a perfect evolution candidate. However, IV perfection is not required for Kabuto to be useful for evolution or collection.

IV Tip

Before transferring a Kabuto, check its appraisal, shiny status, Shadow status and whether you want to evolve it into Kabutops.

Shadow Kabuto and Frustration

Important Shadow Move Note

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move when obtained. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace it. Check the current event rules before spending resources on a Shadow Kabuto.

Should You Purify Shadow Kabuto?

  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions first.
  • Check the Kabuto IVs before making a decision.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve it into Shadow Kabutops.
  • Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically simply because its purified appraisal improves.
  • Consider keeping a Shadow specimen for collection.

12 · PVP

Kabuto PvP Guide

Kabuto is generally much less prominent in PvP than its evolved form. Its low overall stats and limited CP ceiling make it a niche choice rather than a standard competitive Pokémon.

Great League

Kabuto can technically be used within lower CP formats, but its overall stat profile means that it is normally outclassed by stronger Pokémon. It is more appropriate for themed teams, personal challenges or players who enjoy using unusual Pokémon.

Ultra League

Kabuto's maximum CP makes Ultra League investment impractical for most players.

Master League

Kabuto is not a practical Master League investment because its CP and stats are far below the level normally required for the format.

13 · RESOURCES

Kabuto Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

If your goal is to evolve Kabuto or build a Kabutops, Candy is more immediately important than investing large amounts of Stardust into Kabuto itself.

  • Keep enough Kabuto Candy for evolution.
  • Save high-IV Kabuto before transferring extras.
  • Check Shadow status before transferring.
  • Keep Shiny Kabuto for your collection.
  • Consider Candy XL only if you have a specific high-level investment plan.

15 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Kabuto

Before Investing

  • Check the Kabuto's IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow.
  • Decide whether you want to evolve it.
  • Compare the potential Kabutops with your existing Pokémon before spending Stardust.

Collection Strategy

Kabuto can be particularly valuable to collectors because it represents one of the original Kanto Fossil Pokémon. Keeping regular, Shiny and Shadow examples can help build a more complete Pokédex collection.

Evolution Strategy

If your objective is Kabutops, prioritize Kabuto with strong IVs and desirable form status. A Shiny or Shadow Kabuto may be worth keeping even when another Kabuto has better IVs.

17 · FAQ

Kabuto FAQ

What type is Kabuto?

Kabuto is a Rock / Water-type Pokémon.

What is Kabuto weak to?

Kabuto is vulnerable to several attack types, including Grass, Electric, Fighting and Ground.

Does Kabuto evolve?

Yes. Kabuto evolves into Kabutops.

How much Candy does Kabuto need to evolve?

Kabuto requires 50 Kabuto Candy to evolve into Kabutops.

Is Shiny Kabuto available?

Yes. Shiny Kabuto is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Kabuto available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current in-game encounter schedule.

Is Kabuto good for raids?

Kabuto is generally not considered a top raid attacker. Its lower CP and first-stage status make evolved and stronger Pokémon more practical.

Is Kabuto good for PvP?

Kabuto is generally a niche PvP choice rather than a mainstream competitive Pokémon.

Should I evolve my perfect Kabuto?

If your goal is to obtain a perfect Kabutops, a 15/15/15 Kabuto is an excellent evolution candidate. However, always consider whether you want to preserve it for collection purposes.

Is Shadow Kabuto better than regular Kabuto?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile. Whether a Shadow Kabuto is preferable depends on your collection and battle goals.

Can Shiny Kabuto evolve into Shiny Kabutops?

Yes. A Shiny Kabuto can evolve into Shiny Kabutops.

Can Shadow Kabuto evolve?

Shadow Pokémon can evolve while retaining their Shadow status when the evolution is available.

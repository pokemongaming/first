Omastar

Omastar is a Rock- and Water-type Pokémon from Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it is the evolved form of Omanyte and is known for its unusual dual typing, fossil-themed design and access to Rock- and Water-type attacks.

Omastar Overview

Omastar is the final evolutionary stage of the Omanyte family. It is a dual Rock/Water-type Pokémon and is classified as the Spiral Pokémon.

Shadow Omastar

Shadow Omastar is the Shadow variant of Omastar. Shadow Pokémon deal increased damage but also take increased damage, creating a different battle profile from the standard form.

🌑

Shadow Omastar

Shadow Omastar retains its Rock/Water typing but gains the standard Shadow battle modifier.

⚔️

Shadow Bonus

Shadow Pokémon deal more damage with their attacks while also taking more damage from incoming attacks.

Purified Omastar

A Shadow Omastar can be purified using Stardust and Candy. Purification changes its appraisal and removes the Shadow status.

🌟

Purified Omastar

Purified Omastar returns to a standard Pokémon state and learns Return when purification is completed.

🔮

Purification Benefits

Purification improves appraisal and reduces certain future resource costs for powering up and learning attacks.

Should You Purify Shadow Omastar?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow Omastar with the purified result first. If your priority is keeping the Shadow battle bonus, purification removes that advantage.

  • Compare the Shadow IVs before purifying.
  • Check the current move requirements.
  • Consider whether you want the Shadow bonus.
  • Check Stardust and Candy costs.
  • Keep rare Shadow specimens if you are a collector.

    XXL Omastar

    XXL Omastar is an exceptionally large Omastar. Size variants are separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon does not automatically have better IVs.

    📏 XXL Omastar

    An XXL Omastar has an exceptionally large size measurement. This can be valuable for collectors and Pokémon GO Showcase participation.

    🏆 Showcase Value

    Large Pokémon can be useful for size-based PokéStop Showcases when Omastar or its category is eligible.

    Important:

    XXL size and IVs are independent characteristics. An XXL Omastar can have low, average or perfect IVs.

    XXS Omastar

    XXS Omastar is an exceptionally small Omastar. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic and does not determine the Pokémon's IV percentage.

    🔹 XXS Omastar

    An XXS Omastar has an exceptionally small size measurement compared with standard specimens.

    🎯 Collector Value

    XXS Pokémon can be interesting to collectors who want to complete size-based Pokémon collections.

10 • PVP

Blastoise PvP Guide

Blastoise has historically been more interesting in PvP because of its bulk and access to efficient charged-move pressure than because of raw Attack.

Great League

Blastoise can be considered for Great League builds when the current rules and moveset make its bulk and Water typing useful.

Ultra League

Master League

13 • PURIFIED

Purified Blastoise

Purified Blastoise is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Blastoise. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can alter its IVs and other mechanics.

💠

Purified Blastoise

Purified Blastoise no longer has the Shadow battle modifier. It can also benefit from the mechanics associated with purified Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Blastoise?

  • Compare the Shadow version before purification.
  • Consider whether you need the Shadow offensive bonus.
  • Check the Pokémon's IVs and move requirements.
  • Consider the Candy and Stardust implications.
  • Do not purify purely because the resulting IVs look better.

16 • SIZE FORMS

XXL & XXS Blastoise

Pokémon GO can display size classifications such as XXL and XXS. These classifications are separate from IV quality and should not be confused with a Pokémon's battle stats.

📏 XXL Blastoise

An XXL Blastoise is a particularly large specimen. Its size classification does not automatically make it stronger in battle.

📐 XXS Blastoise

An XXS Blastoise is a particularly small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV percentage.

Important

XXL and XXS classifications should be treated as collection and size characteristics rather than direct indicators of combat quality.

24 • FAQ

Blastoise FAQ

What type is Blastoise?

Blastoise is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Blastoise weak to?

Blastoise is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Is Blastoise a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Blastoise is the final evolution of the Squirtle family and is not a Legendary Pokémon.

Does Blastoise evolve?

Blastoise is the final evolution of Squirtle. It does not evolve into another standard Pokémon, but it can Mega Evolve into Mega Blastoise.

Is Shiny Blastoise available?

Yes. Shiny Blastoise is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution from an eligible Shiny Squirtle family member.

Is Shadow Blastoise good?

Shadow Blastoise can be useful for players who value the increased offensive pressure provided by the Shadow mechanic. Its value depends on moveset, resources and the current Pokémon GO meta.

Should I purify Shadow Blastoise?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

What is Lucky Blastoise?

Lucky Blastoise is a Blastoise obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Blastoise?

A 100% Blastoise has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Blastoise?

XXL Blastoise is a particularly large size classification. It does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better IVs or higher battle performance.

What is XXS Blastoise?

XXS Blastoise is a particularly small size classification. Size and IV percentage are separate characteristics.

Is Mega Blastoise good for raids?

Mega Blastoise can be a very strong Water-type option for raids and also provides Mega-related team utility while active.

Can Shiny Blastoise Mega Evolve?

Yes. An eligible Shiny Blastoise can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Is Blastoise good in PvP?

Blastoise can be useful in PvP formats where its bulk, typing and available moveset fit the current league meta. Its performance can change as moves and formats change.

What is the best Blastoise moveset?

For a Water-focused build, Water Gun and Hydro Cannon are an important combination when Hydro Cannon is available to the Pokémon.

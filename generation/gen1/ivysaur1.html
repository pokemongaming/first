Why Ivysaur's Typing Matters

Ivysaur's Grass and Poison typing gives it useful resistances while also creating several important weaknesses. Its Grass typing is useful against Water, Ground and Rock opponents, while its Poison typing adds additional defensive interactions.

09 • PVP

Ivysaur PvP Guide

Great League

Ivysaur can be more interesting in CP-limited formats than it is in raids. Its Grass / Poison typing gives it useful matchups against certain Water, Ground and Fairy-related opponents.

Ultra League

Ivysaur's relatively low maximum CP makes it less natural for Ultra League compared with Pokémon designed for higher CP environments.

Master League

Ivysaur is generally not suited to Master League because its maximum CP and overall stats are much lower than the Pokémon normally used in that format.

23 • FAQ

Ivysaur FAQ

What type is Ivysaur?

Ivysaur is a Grass / Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Ivysaur weak to?

Ivysaur is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying and Psychic-type attacks.

Does Ivysaur evolve?

Yes. Ivysaur evolves into Venusaur as the final stage of the Bulbasaur family.

Is Shiny Ivysaur available?

Shiny Ivysaur is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution from Shiny Bulbasaur.

Is Shadow Ivysaur available?

Shadow Ivysaur can be available through the Team GO Rocket Shadow Pokémon system when included in the current rotation.

What is a 100% Ivysaur?

A 100% Ivysaur has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Ivysaur?

XXL Ivysaur is an exceptionally large individual. XXL status is a size attribute and does not automatically mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

What is an XXS Ivysaur?

XXS Ivysaur is an exceptionally small individual. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV quality.

What is Lucky Ivysaur?

Lucky Ivysaur is an Ivysaur that became Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading system.

Should I purify Shadow Ivysaur?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your collection, battle and resource goals before purifying.

Is Ivysaur good for raids?

Ivysaur is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Venusaur and stronger Grass-type attackers are usually better choices for serious PvE investment.

Is Ivysaur good in PvP?

Ivysaur can have niche value in CP-limited PvP formats, although its usefulness depends on the current moveset and metagame.

Can a Shiny Ivysaur be Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon GO attributes can overlap, so an individual Pokémon can have multiple special characteristics depending on how it was obtained.

Can a 100% Ivysaur be XXL?

Yes. IV quality and size are separate attributes. An Ivysaur can simultaneously be 100% IV and XXL if both conditions apply.

Can an XXS Ivysaur be Shiny?

Yes. Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so special attributes can overlap.

01 • OVERVIEW

Rhyhorn Overview

Rhyhorn is a dual Ground/Rock-type Pokémon from Generation 1. Although Rhyhorn itself is not normally considered a top-end battle attacker, it is an important Pokémon to collect because its Candy is required to evolve Rhyhorn into Rhydon and then into Rhyperior.

Why Rhyhorn's Typing Matters

Rhyhorn's Ground/Rock typing gives it useful resistances to several common attack types, but it also creates several important weaknesses. Water and Grass are particularly dangerous, while Ice, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks can also exploit Rhyhorn.

20 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Rhyhorn

For New Players

Do not rush to transfer every Rhyhorn with ordinary IVs. First check whether you have better specimens, because Rhyhorn can eventually become Rhyperior.

For Collectors

Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV specimens can all have separate collection value. Keeping one example of each variant can be a useful long-term Pokédex project.

For Raiders

Rhyhorn itself should generally not be the main target for heavy Stardust investment. If your goal is a strong Ground or Rock attacker, consider the eventual Rhyperior build instead.

Before Evolving

  • Check IVs.
  • Check whether the Pokémon is Shiny.
  • Check Shadow or Purified status.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Check XXL or XXS size designation.
  • Compare multiple Rhyhorn before choosing one to evolve.

22 • FAQ

Rhyhorn FAQ

What type is Rhyhorn?

Rhyhorn is a Ground and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Rhyhorn weak to?

Rhyhorn is weak to Water, Grass, Ice, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks. Water and Grass are particularly important weaknesses.

Does Rhyhorn evolve?

Yes. Rhyhorn evolves into Rhydon, and Rhydon can then evolve into Rhyperior.

Is Shiny Rhyhorn available?

Yes. Shiny Rhyhorn is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Rhyhorn be Shadow?

Shadow Rhyhorn can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations.

Can Shadow Rhyhorn be purified?

An eligible Shadow Rhyhorn can be purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and can increase its Individual Values.

What is a Lucky Rhyhorn?

Lucky Rhyhorn is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

What is a 100% Rhyhorn?

A 100% Rhyhorn has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Rhyhorn?

XXL Rhyhorn is an extra-large size variant. Size is separate from IVs and Shiny status.

What is an XXS Rhyhorn?

XXS Rhyhorn is an extra-small size variant. It can be especially interesting to collectors.

Does Mega Rhyhorn exist?

No. Mega Rhyhorn is not an official Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO.

What should I evolve Rhyhorn into?

Rhyhorn evolves into Rhydon, and Rhydon can eventually evolve into Rhyperior. If your goal is long-term battle use, Rhyperior is generally the important final stage to consider.

Should I power up Rhyhorn?

Usually, it is better to consider your eventual evolution before investing heavily in Rhyhorn. Saving resources for a strong Rhyperior can make more sense for many players.

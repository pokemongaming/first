Pidgeotto Overview

Pidgeotto is the evolved form of Pidgey and the pre-evolution of Pidgeot. It is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region.

Why Pidgeotto's Typing Matters

Pidgeotto's Normal/Flying combination gives it a different weakness and resistance profile from pure Flying-type Pokémon. Trainers should pay particular attention to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks when fighting it.

100% IV Pidgeotto

A 100% IV Pidgeotto has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Perfect Pidgeotto can be particularly interesting for collectors and trainers who enjoy building complete Pokédex collections.

Shiny Pidgeotto

Shiny Pidgeotto has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny status does not change its species, typing or base battle role.

✨

Shiny Pidgeotto

A Shiny Pidgeotto is primarily a collector's target. It can also be evolved into Shiny Pidgeot while retaining its Shiny status.

Shiny Pidgeotto Evolution

A Shiny Pidgey can evolve into Shiny Pidgeotto and then Shiny Pidgeot. Shiny status is retained through the evolution process.

XXL Pidgeotto

XXL Pidgeotto is a size variant that is larger than typical specimens. Size does not automatically mean higher IVs or stronger battle performance.

📏

XXL Pidgeotto

Keep XXL Pidgeotto if you enjoy size collection, Pokédex completion or Pokémon GO's size-related features.

XXL vs IVs

A Pokémon's size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Pidgeotto can have excellent IVs, average IVs or poor IVs.

XXS Pidgeotto

XXS Pidgeotto is a smaller-than-usual size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, its size is separate from its IV quality.

📐

XXS Pidgeotto

XXS Pidgeotto can be valuable to collectors who want to record different size variants of the Pidgey family.

XXS and Battle Performance

Being XXS does not automatically make Pidgeotto stronger or weaker in the way IVs affect its battle statistics. Evaluate size and IVs separately.

Pidgeotto PvP Guide

Pidgeotto's middle-stage status means it is not normally the first choice for open-league PvP, but special CP-limited formats can create opportunities for unevolved or middle-stage Pokémon.

Great League

Pidgeotto can potentially fit into certain Great League situations depending on its moveset, IV spread and the current cup rules.

Ultra League

Its maximum CP and overall stats make Ultra League a less natural home for standard Pidgeotto compared with stronger fully evolved Pokémon.

Master League

Pidgeotto is generally not a natural Master League investment because the format favors Pokémon with substantially higher overall stats.

Pidgeotto FAQ

What type is Pidgeotto?

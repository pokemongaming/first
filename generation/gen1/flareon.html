Why Flareon's Fire Typing Matters

Fire-type attacks are especially useful against Grass-, Bug-, Ice- and Steel-type Pokémon. Flareon's single Fire typing gives it a straightforward offensive role without the additional weaknesses or resistances created by a secondary type.

Flareon can work as a Fire-type raid attacker, but it is generally a secondary option when compared with stronger modern Fire attackers. It becomes more useful for newer players who need an accessible Fire-type damage dealer.

10 — SHADOW FLAREON

Shadow Flareon

Shadow Flareon is created by evolving an eligible Shadow Eevee into Flareon. It retains the Fire typing of regular Flareon while benefiting from the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanic.

Should You Purify Shadow Flareon?

Do not automatically purify a useful Shadow Flareon. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before spending resources. If your objective is maximizing Shadow damage, keeping the Shadow form can be more appropriate.

  • Check the Shadow Flareon's IVs.
  • Check its current Charged Move.
  • Consider the Stardust investment.
  • Compare it with your existing Fire attackers.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Shadow Flareon Availability

Shadow Eevee availability depends on Team GO Rocket encounters and current Pokémon GO events. Because Rocket rotations change, availability should be updated whenever the current Shadow roster changes.

13 — PVP

Flareon PvP Guide

Flareon's high Attack stat and relatively modest bulk make it less comfortable in CP-limited PvP than Pokémon specifically designed around efficient bulk and move pressure.

Great League

Flareon can technically be used in Great League under appropriate CP limits, but it is generally not a first-choice Fire-type PvP Pokémon.

Ultra League

Flareon can fit some Fire-focused teams, but its overall ranking depends heavily on the current moveset and metagame.

Master League

Flareon's maximum CP is much lower than the CP commonly associated with top Master League Pokémon, limiting its competitive relevance in this format.

17 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Flareon

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the boss is weak to Fire.
  • Confirm Flareon has Fire Spin and Overheat.
  • Check Flareon's level and IVs.
  • Consider Sunny weather.
  • Compare Flareon with your other Fire attackers.

During Team Building

Flareon should not automatically occupy every Fire slot in a raid party. Compare its damage and durability with your available Fire attackers for the specific raid boss.

Long-Term Investment

Flareon is more attractive as an accessible Fire attacker, collector Pokémon or personal favorite than as a top-end long-term PvE investment.

19 — FAQ

Flareon FAQ

What type is Flareon?

Flareon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Flareon weak to?

Flareon is weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

What is Flareon's max CP?

Flareon's maximum CP is 3,424 at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

What are Flareon's best moves?

Fire Spin and Overheat are the recommended Fire-focused PvE moves.

Is Flareon good for raids?

Flareon can be useful as an accessible Fire-type raid attacker, but it generally ranks below stronger modern Fire options.

Is Shiny Flareon available?

Yes. Shiny Flareon is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Eevee evolve into Flareon?

Yes. Flareon is one of Eevee's original Generation 1 evolutions.

Is Shadow Flareon available?

Shadow Flareon can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow Eevee when the relevant Shadow Pokémon is available.

Is Shadow Flareon better than regular Flareon?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on your resources and battle goal.

Should I purify Shadow Flareon?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Can Flareon Dynamax?

Yes. Flareon can Dynamax in Pokémon GO.

Does Shiny Flareon have better stats?

No. Shiny Flareon has the same underlying battle stats as regular Flareon. The difference is its appearance.

What is the best Flareon IV?

A 15/15/15 Flareon is the perfect-IV specimen. However, IVs should be evaluated alongside level, moveset and intended use.

Can Shadow Flareon be shiny?

A Shiny Shadow Flareon can exist when a Shiny Shadow Eevee is eligible for the corresponding evolution.

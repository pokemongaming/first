01 • OVERVIEW

Tauros Overview

Tauros is a Normal-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and was introduced in the first generation of the Pokémon series. In Pokémon GO, regular Tauros has a different role from its Paldean variants. The regular form remains a Normal-type Pokémon, while the Paldean breeds use Fighting-based typings.

09 • PVP

Tauros PvP Guide

Regular Tauros

Regular Tauros can appear in Great and Ultra League discussions, although it is generally overshadowed by stronger and more flexible options. Its access to Trailblaze gives it some additional coverage and setup potential.

Paldean Tauros in PvP

The Paldean breeds are more interesting individually because their Fighting-based typings create different matchup profiles. Combat Breed can pressure Steel, Rock and Dark Pokémon, while Blaze and Aqua gain additional coverage from their secondary types.

General PvP Reminder

Great League, Ultra League and Master League IV requirements are different. Always test the exact Pokémon rather than treating a 15/15/15 specimen as automatically optimal.

Tauros FAQ

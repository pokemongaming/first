09 • PURIFIED FORM

Purified Aerodactyl

Purified Aerodactyl is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Aerodactyl. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

✨ Purified Status

Shadow status is removed after purification.

📈 IV Increase

Purification can increase Individual Values according to current game mechanics.

💰 Resource Efficiency

Purified Pokémon can have different power-up and move costs.

Should You Purify Shadow Aerodactyl?

Do not purify automatically. Compare your goal first. A Shadow Aerodactyl may be more valuable for damage-focused gameplay, while a purified version may be preferred for collection, IV improvement or resource efficiency.

10 • LUCKY FORM

Lucky Aerodactyl

Lucky Aerodactyl is obtained through the Lucky Trade system. Lucky status is especially valuable for Pokémon that require significant Stardust investment.

🍀 Lucky Pokémon

Obtained through an eligible Lucky Trade.

✨ IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than normal trades.

💰 Stardust Benefit

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

Is Lucky Aerodactyl Worth Powering Up?

A Lucky Aerodactyl can be a good long-term investment, especially if it has strong IVs and you plan to use Mega Aerodactyl.

17 • FAQ

Aerodactyl FAQ

What type is Aerodactyl?

Aerodactyl is a Rock and Flying-type Pokémon.

Does Aerodactyl evolve?

Aerodactyl does not evolve through a normal evolution chain. It can undergo Mega Evolution when eligible.

Is Shiny Aerodactyl available?

Shiny Aerodactyl can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Aerodactyl stronger?

Shadow Aerodactyl has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability. Its value depends on the battle and investment goal.

Should I purify Shadow Aerodactyl?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow form with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

What is a 100% Aerodactyl?

A perfect Aerodactyl has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Aerodactyl?

Lucky Aerodactyl is an Aerodactyl obtained with Lucky status through an eligible trade.

Does XXL Aerodactyl have better battle stats?

No. Size classification alone does not automatically make an Aerodactyl stronger in normal battles.

Is Mega Aerodactyl good for raids?

Mega Aerodactyl can be an excellent Rock-focused raid attacker when used against suitable raid bosses.

Can Shiny Aerodactyl become Mega Aerodactyl?

A Shiny Aerodactyl can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

01 • OVERVIEW

Omastar Overview

Omastar is the evolved form of Omanyte and one of the Fossil Pokémon originally introduced in the Kanto region. Its Water/Rock typing gives it several useful resistances, but also leaves it vulnerable to important attacking types such as Grass and Electric.

Why Omastar's Typing Matters

Omastar's dual Water/Rock typing gives it useful defensive interactions against several common attack types while also giving it access to two distinct offensive identities.

The downside is a collection of important weaknesses, especially to Grass and Electric attacks. Trainers should therefore consider the opponent's moveset before relying on Omastar in battle.

10 • PVP

Omastar PvP Guide

Omastar has access to useful Water- and Rock-type attacks, but its PvP value depends heavily on the format and current Great League or Ultra League metagame.

Great League

Great League Omastar can provide interesting coverage because its dual typing and move pool can pressure selected opponents. Its weaknesses should be carefully considered when building a team around it.

Ultra League

Omastar can be considered in specialist or limited formats, although it is not generally the first choice for a broad Ultra League roster.

Master League

Omastar's maximum CP and overall stat profile make Master League a much less natural environment for it. Trainers looking for high-level Master League Pokémon generally have stronger alternatives.

PvP Tip

Check the current league rules and rankings before spending Elite TMs or large amounts of Stardust specifically for PvP Omastar.

Omastar FAQ

What type is Omastar?

Omastar is a dual Water- and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Omastar weak to?

Omastar is weak to Grass, Electric, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks.

Does Omastar evolve?

Omastar is the final evolution of Omanyte and does not have another standard evolution.

Is Omastar good for raids?

Omastar can work as a niche Water- or Rock-type attacker, but it is generally not a top-tier choice compared with stronger modern attackers.

Shiny Omastar is an obtainable Shiny variant when its evolutionary or encounter availability is active in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Omastar available?

Shadow Omastar availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket encounter rotations and other relevant game events.

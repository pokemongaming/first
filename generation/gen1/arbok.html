Arbok

Arbok is a Poison-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. It evolves from Ekans and is known for its intimidating cobra-like design, flexible move options and multiple collectible forms in Pokémon GO.

Arbok Overview

Arbok is the evolved form of Ekans and belongs to the original Generation 1 Pokémon lineup. Its pure Poison typing gives it useful defensive resistances, while its various moves can make it an interesting option for collectors and some PvP situations.

Shadow Arbok

Shadow Arbok is the Shadow version of Arbok. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile compared with standard Pokémon.

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon gain increased offensive pressure while becoming less durable. This makes Shadow Arbok a separate investment decision from regular Arbok.

Feature Regular Arbok Shadow Arbok
Type Poison Poison
Offensive Profile Standard Higher pressure
Durability Standard Reduced

Shadow Arbok Investment

  • Check its IVs before investing heavily.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare Shadow Arbok with other Poison attackers.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Arbok

Purified Arbok is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Ekans or Shadow Arbok. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and creates a separate collectible form.

Purified Form

Purified Pokémon no longer retain the Shadow battle profile. Purification can also change the Pokémon's IVs according to Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

🍀 Lucky Arbok

Lucky Arbok is obtained when an eligible trade results in a Lucky Pokémon. Lucky status does not change Arbok's type or move pool.

Arbok FAQ

What type is Arbok?

Arbok is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Arbok weak to?

Arbok is weak to Ground and Psychic attacks.

Does Arbok evolve?

Arbok is the final evolution of the Ekans family.

Is Shiny Arbok available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game availability.

Is Shadow Arbok stronger than regular Arbok?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced durability, making them different rather than automatically better in every situation.

Should I purify Shadow Arbok?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow form's battle role and your collection goals before purifying.

What is a 100% IV Arbok?

A 100% IV Arbok has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Arbok?

Lucky Arbok is a traded Lucky Pokémon that requires less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Arbok?

XXL Arbok is a larger size variation primarily useful for collecting and size-related features.

What is XXS Arbok?

XXS Arbok is a smaller collectible size variation.

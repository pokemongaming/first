Why Mewtwo's Typing Matters

Regular Mewtwo is a pure Psychic type. Psychic attacks are especially useful against Fighting and Poison opponents. Its typing also gives it useful resistance to Fighting and Psychic damage while leaving it vulnerable to Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks.

09 — PVP

Mewtwo PvP Guide

Master League

Mewtwo's high CP and enormous Attack make it a natural candidate for Master League play. Psystrike creates strong charged-move pressure, while coverage moves can alter how the Pokémon performs against different opponents.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Mewtwo is generally more naturally suited to high-CP formats. League restrictions, IV spreads and the current metagame should be checked before investing a Mewtwo specifically for a limited league.

PvP reminder

A raid-perfect 15/15/15 Mewtwo is not automatically the ideal specimen for every PvP situation. Compare the specific league rules and IV spread before spending Stardust or Candy XL.

10 — SHADOW FORM

Shadow Mewtwo

Shadow Mewtwo is the Shadow variant of Mewtwo. It retains the Psychic typing of regular Mewtwo while receiving the Shadow battle modifier. Because Shadow Pokémon trade durability for increased offensive pressure, Shadow Mewtwo should be evaluated as a separate investment.

  • Keep strong Shadow Mewtwo specimens rather than judging only by CP.
  • Check whether Frustration has been removed before planning the final moveset.

Shadow Mewtwo Availability

Shadow Legendary availability is controlled by Team GO Rocket storylines and special events. Treat availability as a rotating status rather than a permanently available encounter.

15 — MEGA MECHANICS

Mewtwo Mega Evolution Mechanics

Practical Mega Strategy

  • Choose Mega Mewtwo Y when you want the strongest Psychic-focused Mega role.
  • Choose Mega Mewtwo X when Fighting utility is important to your team.
  • Compare the current raid boss's weaknesses before choosing the form.
  • Save Mega Energy for forms you will actually use.
  • Check current Mega Levels and active bonuses before evolving.

Can Shiny Mewtwo Mega Evolve?

A Shiny Mewtwo can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution. This makes a strong Shiny Mewtwo an attractive long-term collector and Mega candidate.

Mega Energy

Mega Mewtwo requires its own Mega Energy system. Because Mega Energy costs and acquisition methods can change with events and Mega Evolution updates, use your live game data for the current exact cost rather than hard-coding a value into a permanent guide.

Mega Mewtwo Super Mega Raids

Mega Mewtwo X and Mega Mewtwo Y debuted through Pokémon GO's 2026 Mega Mewtwo Super Mega Raid rollout. These raids use special Mega Raid mechanics, so trainers should check the current event rules before entering.

18 — INVESTMENT

Mewtwo Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Mewtwo is an expensive Legendary to build at high levels. Before committing resources, consider whether the Pokémon has the moveset, IVs and role you actually need.

  • Prioritize strong Mewtwo specimens for long-term projects.
  • Save Candy XL for high-level raid or Master League builds.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite resources.
  • Keep useful Shadow Mewtwo instead of purifying automatically.
  • Consider Mega Energy when planning a Mega Mewtwo investment.
19 — AVAILABILITY

Mewtwo Availability

Mewtwo is a Legendary Pokémon whose availability is controlled by Pokémon GO raid rotations, events and special encounters. Shadow Mewtwo follows Team GO Rocket availability, while Mega Mewtwo X and Y follow Mega and Super Mega Raid rotations.

Current-status strategy

Separate historical appearances from current availability on your live website. Update the availability field whenever Niantic changes the Legendary or Mega rotation.

20 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Mewtwo

Before a Raid

  • Check the boss's weaknesses and current moveset.
  • Choose Psychic, Fighting or coverage moves based on the matchup.
  • Check Mewtwo's level, IVs and available resources.
  • Choose the correct Mega form when using Mega Mewtwo.
  • Account for weather and the rest of your raid party.

Long-Term Investment

A high-quality Mewtwo can remain useful for years because its huge Attack stat, flexible movepool and multiple Mega forms give it several possible roles. However, the best investment depends on the current raid and PvP meta.

21 — FAQ

Mewtwo FAQ

Is Mewtwo a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Mewtwo is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 1.

What type is Mewtwo?

Regular Mewtwo is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Mewtwo weak to?

Regular Mewtwo is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks.

What is Mewtwo's max CP?

Regular Mewtwo reaches 4,724 CP at level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Mewtwo good for raids?

Yes. Mewtwo is a powerful Psychic attacker and its movepool gives it additional coverage options.

Is Shadow Mewtwo better than regular Mewtwo?

Shadow Mewtwo has greater offensive pressure but reduced durability. The better choice depends on the role, matchup and resources available.

Is Shiny Mewtwo available?

Yes. Shiny Mewtwo is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and retains its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution.

What is Mega Mewtwo X?

Mega Mewtwo X is the Psychic/Fighting Mega form of Mewtwo. It has 399 Attack, 215 Defense, 228 HP and 6,910 maximum CP at level 50.

What is Mega Mewtwo Y?

Mega Mewtwo Y is the pure Psychic Mega form. It has 413 Attack, 223 Defense, 228 HP and 7,267 maximum CP at level 50.

Which is better: Mega Mewtwo X or Y?

Mega Mewtwo Y is the stronger choice for a Psychic-focused role, while Mega Mewtwo X provides valuable Fighting-type utility in addition to Psychic damage.

Can Shiny Mewtwo become Mega Mewtwo?

Yes. A Shiny Mewtwo can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved into an eligible Mega form.

Does Mewtwo evolve normally?

No. Mewtwo has no conventional evolution chain. Its major Pokémon GO transformations are its Shadow status and two Mega forms.

What is the best Mewtwo moveset?

For a Psychic-focused build, Psystrike is the key charged move. The best fast move depends on whether you prioritize energy generation, damage or the specific battle format.

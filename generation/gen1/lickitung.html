Why Lickitung's Typing Matters

Lickitung has a single Normal typing. This gives it a relatively simple defensive profile, with a major weakness to Fighting-type attacks and an immunity-style interaction with Ghost-type attacks under Pokémon GO's type-effectiveness system.

Lickitung FAQ

What type is Lickitung?

Lickitung is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Lickitung weak to?

Lickitung is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Lickitung good in Pokémon GO?

Lickitung is most notable for PvP, particularly Great League, where its bulk can make it a useful Pokémon with the right moveset and IVs.

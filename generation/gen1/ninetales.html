Ninetales

Ninetales is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is the evolved form of Vulpix and is known for its elegant fox-like design, Fire typing and multiple collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Why Ninetales' Typing Matters

Regular Ninetales is a pure Fire-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it useful offensive matchups against Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel Pokémon. At the same time, Fire-type weaknesses mean Ninetales should be used carefully against Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Important form distinction:

This page describes regular Kanto Ninetales. Alolan Ninetales is a separate regional form with Ice/Fairy typing and should have its own dedicated database information.

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Ninetales

Lucky Ninetales is a special trade variant identified by its Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀
Lucky Ninetales

A Lucky Ninetales can be particularly attractive to collectors because of its special background and reduced power-up Stardust requirement.

Why Keep a Lucky Ninetales?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • A Lucky Ninetales can be useful for a themed collection.
  • Check the IVs after a trade before investing Candy.
  • Lucky status is permanent once obtained.
Trading note:

Lucky status is created through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. A normal Ninetales does not become Lucky simply by powering it up.

23 — FAQ

Ninetales FAQ

What type is Ninetales?

Regular Kanto Ninetales is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Ninetales weak to?

Ninetales is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Does Ninetales evolve?

Ninetales does not evolve further. It evolves from Vulpix.

Is Shiny Ninetales available?

Yes. Shiny Ninetales is an alternate-color variant that can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or by evolving an eligible Shiny Vulpix.

Can Vulpix evolve into Ninetales?

Yes. Regular Vulpix can evolve into regular Ninetales when the required evolution conditions are met.

Is Shadow Ninetales available?

Shadow Ninetales can exist when an eligible Shadow Vulpix is evolved into Ninetales.

Should I purify Shadow Ninetales?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first, especially if you value Shadow mechanics or the Pokémon's current battle role.

What is a Lucky Ninetales?

Lucky Ninetales is a Ninetales with Lucky status obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% IV Ninetales?

A 100% IV Ninetales has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Ninetales?

XXL Ninetales is a Ninetales with a large size classification recorded by Pokémon GO.

What is XXS Ninetales?

XXS Ninetales is a Ninetales with a particularly small size classification recorded by Pokémon GO.

Is XXL Ninetales automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Ninetales can have any IV combination.

Is XXS Ninetales automatically rare?

XXS is a special size classification, but its collection value depends on the player's goals and the circumstances under which it was obtained.

Is Ninetales good for raids?

Ninetales can contribute Fire-type damage, but it is generally not a first-choice Fire raid attacker compared with stronger options.

Is Ninetales good for PvP?

Ninetales can have PvP applications depending on the league, moveset, IV spread and current metagame.

Is Alolan Ninetales the same as regular Ninetales?

No. Alolan Ninetales is a separate regional form with Ice/Fairy typing. Regular Kanto Ninetales is Fire type.

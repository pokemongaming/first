FIRE-TYPE POKÉMON

Magmar

Magmar is a Fire-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as part of the Magby evolutionary family and can evolve into Magmortar using Magmar Candy and an evolution item. Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV Magmar can all be valuable for different collection and gameplay goals.

01 — OVERVIEW

Magmar Overview

Magmar is a Fire-type Pokémon from Generation 1. It evolves from Magby and can evolve further into Magmortar. Magmar is more useful as an evolutionary family member and collector target than as a top-tier standalone raid attacker.

Why Magmar's Fire Typing Matters

Fire attacks are super effective against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel Pokémon. Magmar's Fire typing also gives it useful resistances, although its overall performance depends heavily on its moves, level and the battle format.

08 — SHINY MAGMAR

Shiny Magmar

Shiny Magmar has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny Magmar is particularly popular with collectors because it can also be evolved into Shiny Magmortar.

✨

Shiny Magmar

The Shiny variant has a different color palette from standard Magmar. Its Shiny status is retained when evolved.

🔥

Shiny Evolution

A Shiny Magmar can be evolved into Shiny Magmortar, making a high-IV Shiny Magmar a useful long-term collection target.

Should You Keep Shiny Magmar?

  • Keep your first Shiny Magmar if you are completing your Shiny Pokédex.
  • Check IVs before evolving a Shiny Magmar.
  • Consider keeping duplicate Shinies for trading.
  • A high-IV Shiny Magmar can be a good candidate for evolution.
09 — SHADOW MAGMAR

Shadow Magmar

Shadow Magmar is a Shadow variant of Magmar. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts, making Shadow Magmar worth evaluating separately.

Shadow Magmar

A Shadow Magmar retains its Fire typing but has the Shadow battle modifier. This can increase offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to remove Frustration using a Charged TM.

If your goal is to preserve a Shadow Pokémon's battle characteristics, compare the Shadow and purified versions before using the Purify option.

Should You Keep Shadow Magmar?

  • Check its IVs before transferring it.
  • Compare Shadow Magmar with your existing Fire attackers.
  • Consider its future evolution into Shadow Magmortar where applicable.
  • Do not purify solely because the purified IVs will be higher.
  • Consider keeping unusual Shadow IV combinations for collection or future gameplay.
10 — PURIFIED MAGMAR

Purified Magmar

Purified Magmar is created by purifying a Shadow Magmar. Purification changes its Shadow status and provides the mechanics associated with purified Pokémon.

Purified Magmar

Purified Magmar can be useful for collectors and players who want a purified entry in their collection. However, purification should be considered carefully before removing a Shadow Pokémon's unique battle characteristics.

There is no universal reason to purify every Shadow Magmar. If the Shadow version has strong collection or battle value, keeping it Shadow may be preferable. Purification can make sense when you specifically want a Purified Magmar or its associated benefits.

12 — 100% IV MAGMAR

100% IV Magmar

A 100% IV Magmar has perfect Individual Values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

⭐ Perfect Magmar — 15/15/15

A 15/15/15 Magmar is a perfect-IV specimen. Because Magmar can evolve into Magmortar, a perfect Magmar may be worth keeping for the entire evolutionary line.

Should You Evolve a 100% Magmar?

If you want a perfect Magmortar for your collection, a 100% Magmar is an excellent candidate for evolution. Always check the current evolution requirements and resource costs before committing.

13 — XXL MAGMAR

XXL Magmar

XXL Magmar is an unusually large specimen. XXL is a size designation rather than a different species or elemental form.

XXL XXL Magmar

An exceptionally large Magmar that can be useful for size-focused collections and medal progress.

Size Collection

If you are collecting size variants, keep XXL Magmar separate from ordinary Magmar rather than transferring it solely because its combat IVs are not perfect.

Size vs IV:

XXL status does not mean that a Pokémon has perfect IVs. Size and Individual Values are separate attributes.

14 — XXS MAGMAR

XXS Magmar

XXS Magmar is an unusually small Magmar. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification and does not represent a different Pokémon species.

XXS XXS Magmar

A very small Magmar that can be valuable to collectors interested in size records and Pokémon GO size mechanics.

Keep or Transfer?

Collectors may want to keep an XXS Magmar even when its IVs are not perfect. Decide based on your collection goals rather than IVs alone.

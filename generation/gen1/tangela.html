Tangela

Tangela is a pure Grass-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, Tangela is best known as a collection target, evolution-family Pokémon and potential Grass-type attacker, with multiple special attributes including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS variants.

01 • OVERVIEW

Tangela Overview

Tangela is a pure Grass-type Pokémon from Generation 1. Its tangled-vine appearance makes it one of Kanto's more distinctive Pokémon, while its evolution into Tangrowth gives the species an important place in the wider evolution family.

What Does Tangela's Grass Typing Mean?

Tangela's Grass typing gives it access to useful Grass-type offensive coverage while also defining its defensive matchup profile. Its weaknesses include Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison attacks.

Is 100% Tangela Rare?

A 100% Tangela has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Perfect IV Tangela can be valuable for collectors and for players who want a perfect specimen to evolve into Tangrowth.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Tangela

Purified Tangela is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Tangela. Purification changes its status and can improve its IVs, while also removing the Shadow battle modifier.

💎

PURIFIED

How to Use & Manage Tangela

Before Transferring Tangela

  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow or Purified.
  • Check IVs for a possible 100% Tangela.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Check XXL and XXS size classifications.
  • Check whether you still need Tangela for evolution.

Before Evolving Tangela

Before evolving Tangela into Tangrowth, compare its IVs, moves, special attributes and your collection goals. A Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, Purified or perfect Tangela may be worth preserving in its original form.

Long-Term Collection Strategy

A useful Tangela collection can include a regular Tangela, Shiny Tangela, Shadow Tangela, Purified Tangela, Lucky Tangela, a 100% IV Tangela and size variants such as XXL and XXS.

22 • FAQ

Tangela FAQ

What type is Tangela?

Tangela is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Tangela weak to?

Tangela is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

What is Tangela's Max CP?

Tangela's Level-50 maximum CP is 2,530 with perfect IVs.

What are Tangela's best moves?

Vine Whip with Grass Knot is a strong Grass-focused Tangela moveset.

Is Shiny Tangela available?

Yes. Shiny Tangela is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Tangela available?

Shadow Tangela can exist when Tangela is available through the relevant Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

What is Purified Tangela?

Purified Tangela is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Tangela.

What is Lucky Tangela?

Lucky Tangela is a Tangela that became Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is a 100% Tangela?

A 100% Tangela has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Tangela?

XXL Tangela is an unusually large Tangela that receives the XXL size classification.

What is XXS Tangela?

XXS Tangela is an unusually small Tangela that receives the XXS size classification.

Does Tangela evolve?

Yes. Tangela can evolve into Tangrowth.

Should I evolve a Shiny Tangela?

That depends on your collection goals. Some players keep Shiny Tangela unevolved while others evolve it to complete the Shiny evolution family.

Should I purify Shadow Tangela?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, Shadow value, moves, collection goals and the purified result before making the decision.

Is Tangela good for raids?

Tangela can contribute Grass-type damage, but it is generally not considered a top-tier Grass raid attacker.

Is a Shiny Hundo Tangela rare?

Yes. Combining Shiny status with perfect 15/15/15 IVs makes a Tangela particularly desirable to collectors.

Is XXL Tangela stronger than normal Tangela?

No. XXL is a size classification and does not automatically mean better IVs or better battle performance.

Is XXS Tangela stronger than normal Tangela?

No. XXS describes size rather than combat quality.

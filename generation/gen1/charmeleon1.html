Charmeleon

Charmeleon is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the evolved form of Charmander. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily an evolution-stage Pokémon and can also be collected in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and high-IV forms.

09 • IV GUIDE

Charmeleon IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Charmeleon is considered a perfect IV or 100% IV Pokémon.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

100% Charmeleon

A 100% Charmeleon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. For collectors, a perfect Charmeleon can be valuable on its own. For players focused on battle, consider whether evolving that perfect Charmeleon into Charizard better matches your goals.

10 • PVP

Charmeleon PvP Guide

Great League

Charmeleon can be considered in CP-limited formats when eligible, but its overall PvP value depends heavily on the current moveset, league rules and surrounding metagame.

Ultra League

Charmeleon's maximum CP limits its usefulness compared with many fully evolved Pokémon. Always check current league rules and rankings before investing Stardust specifically for competitive use.

Master League

Charmeleon is generally not a natural Master League choice because its maximum CP and battle statistics are much lower than the Pokémon commonly used in that format.

Shadow Charmeleon IVs

23 • FAQ

Charmeleon FAQ

What type is Charmeleon?

Charmeleon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmeleon weak to?

Charmeleon is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Does Charmeleon evolve?

Yes. Charmeleon evolves into Charizard, the final evolution of the Charmander family.

Is Shiny Charmeleon available?

Shiny Charmeleon can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Charmeleon available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations.

What is a 100% Charmeleon?

A 100% Charmeleon has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Charmeleon?

Lucky Charmeleon is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system.

What is XXL Charmeleon?

XXL Charmeleon is an exceptionally large specimen. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is XXS Charmeleon?

XXS Charmeleon is an exceptionally small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS does not indicate perfect IVs.

Is Charmeleon good for raids?

Charmeleon is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Evolving a strong specimen into Charizard is usually more relevant for battle-focused players.

Should I evolve my 100% Charmeleon?

That depends on your collection goals. If you want a perfect Charizard, evolving a 100% Charmeleon is a natural choice. If you collect perfect Pokémon at every evolution stage, keeping Charmeleon may also be worthwhile.

Should I purify Shadow Charmeleon?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions and consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow status for future evolution or battle use.

Can Shiny Charmeleon become Shiny Charizard?

Yes. A Shiny Charmeleon retains its Shiny identity when evolved into Charizard.

Bellsprout

Why Bellsprout's Typing Matters

08 • PVP

Bellsprout PvP Guide

Bellsprout is generally a niche PvP choice rather than a standard competitive pick. Its usefulness depends heavily on league rules, moveset and the current Pokémon GO metagame.

Great League

Bellsprout can occasionally be considered for specialized limited formats where its typing and moves provide a particular matchup advantage. It should not automatically be treated as a general-purpose Great League Pokémon.

Ultra League

Its low overall CP ceiling makes Ultra League a poor natural environment for standard Bellsprout.

Master League

Bellsprout is not designed for Master League. Players interested in competitive use should generally focus their resources on stronger evolved Pokémon.

Is a 100% Bellsprout Worth Keeping?

Yes. Even though Bellsprout itself is not a premier raid attacker, a perfect specimen can be valuable for collectors and for building a perfect evolution-family collection.

10 • SHINY

✨ Shiny Bellsprout

Shiny Bellsprout is a collectible alternate-color version of the species. A Shiny Bellsprout can be kept as a collector specimen or evolved through its normal evolutionary family.

✨

Shiny Bellsprout

Shiny status does not change Bellsprout's Grass / Poison typing. It is primarily a visual and collection distinction.

SHINY GRASS / POISON #069

Should You Evolve Shiny Bellsprout?

That depends on your collection goals. If you have only one Shiny Bellsprout, keeping it unevolved may make sense for a complete Shiny Pokédex collection. If you want the full Shiny evolutionary family, you may choose to evolve additional Shiny specimens.

11 • SHADOW

🌑 Shadow Bellsprout

Shadow Bellsprout is the Shadow version of the species. Shadow status affects its battle characteristics and creates a separate collection and investment decision.

🌑

Shadow Bellsprout

Shadow Bellsprout retains its Grass / Poison typing. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability compared with their standard counterparts.

SHADOW GRASS / POISON

Should You Keep Shadow Bellsprout?

  • Keep strong Shadow IV specimens for collection.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve it.
  • Compare the Shadow version with the purified version.
  • Check its current moves before spending resources.
  • Do not purify automatically just because the IVs increase.
12 • PURIFIED

💎 Purified Bellsprout

Purified Bellsprout is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Bellsprout. Purification changes its status and can increase its individual values.

💎

Purified Bellsprout

Purified Bellsprout is no longer a Shadow Pokémon. It remains Grass / Poison type and can continue through the normal Bellsprout evolutionary family.

13 • LUCKY

🍀 Lucky Bellsprout

Lucky Bellsprout is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special collection characteristics and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀

Lucky Bellsprout

Lucky status is independent from Shiny status. This means a Bellsprout can be Lucky and Shiny at the same time when the game mechanics allow the relevant combination.

LUCKY TRADE OBTAINED

Why Keep a Lucky Bellsprout?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky Bellsprout can be useful for collection goals.
  • A Lucky Bellsprout can also have high IVs.
  • Lucky and Shiny are separate characteristics.
  • Lucky Bellsprout can be evolved normally.

21 • FAQ

Bellsprout Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Bellsprout?

Bellsprout is a Grass / Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Bellsprout weak to?

Bellsprout is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying and Psychic-type attacks.

Does Bellsprout evolve?

Yes. Bellsprout evolves into Weepinbell and eventually Victreebel.

Is Shiny Bellsprout available?

Yes. Shiny Bellsprout is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is 100% Bellsprout?

A 100% Bellsprout has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Shadow Bellsprout available?

Shadow Bellsprout can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting.

Can Shadow Bellsprout be purified?

An eligible Shadow Bellsprout can be purified, creating a Purified Bellsprout.

Is Lucky Bellsprout available?

Lucky Pokémon can be obtained through trades, so Bellsprout can have Lucky status when obtained through the appropriate trading mechanics.

What is XXL Bellsprout?

XXL Bellsprout is a large-size classification. It is not a separate Pokémon species.

What is XXS Bellsprout?

XXS Bellsprout is a small-size classification. Like XXL status, it is separate from Bellsprout's IVs.

Is Bellsprout good for raids?

Bellsprout itself is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its evolutionary family is more important for battle-focused players.

Is Bellsprout good in PvP?

Bellsprout is generally a niche PvP choice. Its usefulness depends on the current league rules, moveset and limited cup formats.

Should I evolve my Shiny Bellsprout?

That depends on your collection goals. If you want a complete Shiny evolutionary family, you may want multiple Shiny Bellsprout so you can keep one unevolved and evolve others.

Should I keep a 100% Bellsprout?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Bellsprout is a perfect specimen and can be valuable for collectors or for completing a perfect evolutionary family.

Can Bellsprout be Shiny and Lucky?

Shiny and Lucky are separate characteristics, so they can represent overlapping collection attributes when the game's mechanics allow the combination.

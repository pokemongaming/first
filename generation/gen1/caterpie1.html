Caterpie

Lucky Caterpie

Lucky Caterpie is a special trade-result variant. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀

A Lucky Caterpie is obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics and can be particularly useful for collectors.

11 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Caterpie — Hundo Caterpie

A 100% IV Caterpie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are the maximum individual values available for a Pokémon caught through normal appraisal.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% IV Caterpie

A 15/15/15 Caterpie is commonly called a Hundo. Because Caterpie is an evolution-stage Pokémon with relatively low battle relevance, its main value is often collection, evolution and Pokédex completion.

Should You Evolve a Hundo Caterpie?

That depends on your collection goals. A perfect-IV Caterpie can be evolved into a perfect-IV Metapod and eventually a perfect-IV Butterfree. If you enjoy collecting perfect Pokémon, evolving it can be a satisfying long-term project.

12 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Caterpie

Pokémon GO can record size variations for individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS Caterpie are especially interesting to collectors and players who enjoy size-based showcases.

XXL XXL Caterpie

An XXL Caterpie is a particularly large specimen. Size is separate from IV quality and CP.

XXS XXS Caterpie

An XXS Caterpie is a particularly small specimen and can be a fun collection target.

XXL and XXS Collection Tips

  • Check the size before transferring unusual Caterpie.
  • Keep XXL and XXS specimens if you collect size variants.
  • Compare IVs before evolving a rare-size specimen.
  • Size and IVs are separate characteristics.
  • A Caterpie can potentially combine size with Shiny, Lucky or other collection characteristics.

19 • FAQ

Caterpie FAQ

What type is Caterpie?

Caterpie is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Caterpie weak to?

Caterpie is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

Does Caterpie evolve?

Yes. Caterpie evolves into Metapod and then Butterfree.

Is Shiny Caterpie available?

Yes. Shiny Caterpie is an available collectible variant when its encounter is active.

Is Shadow Caterpie available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotation.

What is a 100% Caterpie?

A 100% Caterpie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Hundo Caterpie?

Hundo is the community term for a Pokémon with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is XXL Caterpie?

XXL Caterpie is a particularly large individual Caterpie recorded by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Caterpie?

XXS Caterpie is a particularly small individual Caterpie recorded by the game's size system.

Can Caterpie be Lucky?

Yes. Caterpie can become Lucky through eligible Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

Is Lucky Caterpie worth keeping?

For collectors, yes. Lucky status is a permanent special characteristic and can make a Caterpie interesting even if it is not a major battle Pokémon.

Is Caterpie good for raids?

Caterpie has limited raid value compared with stronger evolved and Legendary Pokémon.

Should I evolve my Shiny Caterpie?

That depends on your collection goals. If you want a Shiny Caterpie specifically, keep one unevolved before evolving additional specimens.

Should I transfer a low-IV Shiny Caterpie?

IVs are only one consideration for a Shiny. Many collectors keep Shiny Pokémon regardless of their IV quality.

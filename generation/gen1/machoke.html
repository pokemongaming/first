Why Machoke's Fighting Typing Matters

Fighting-type attacks are strong against several important Pokémon types, including Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark. Machoke therefore has a straightforward offensive identity, although its middle-stage stats limit its usefulness compared with stronger Fighting-type attackers.

How to Evolve Machoke

Machop evolves into Machoke using Machop Candy. Machoke can then be evolved into Machamp. Depending on the Pokémon GO evolution mechanics currently available, trading an eligible Machoke can remove the usual Candy cost for its final evolution.

  • Catch Machop to collect Candy.
  • Evolve Machop into Machoke.
  • Check Machoke's IVs before final evolution.
  • Trade an eligible Machoke if you want to use the trade-evolution mechanic.
  • Evolve your strongest candidate into Machamp.

    XXL Machoke

    XXL Machoke is a size variant that is larger than the standard size range. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Machoke can have any IV combination.

    XXL Extra-large size

    XXL status is based on the Pokémon's measured size and does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

    Collection Value Size variant

    XXL Pokémon can be useful for size-based collection goals and medals.

    XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

    A common mistake is assuming that an XXL Pokémon is also a high-IV Pokémon. IVs and Pokémon size are separate attributes. Always check the appraisal screen when evaluating an XXL Machoke.

    XXS Machoke

    XXS Machoke is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL, XXS status is independent of IVs and does not automatically indicate a strong or weak Pokémon.

    XXS Extra-small size

    An XXS Machoke is smaller than the normal size range used for the species.

    Collector Target Size-based collection

    An XXS Machoke can be valuable to collectors trying to complete different size categories.

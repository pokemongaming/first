Vileplume

Vileplume is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it evolves from Gloom using a Sun Stone and can appear in several special forms and attributes including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS.

01 — OVERVIEW

Vileplume Overview

Vileplume is the final evolution of one branch of the Oddish evolutionary family. It is a dual Grass / Poison-type Pokémon and was introduced in Generation 1.

Vileplume is not one of the strongest general-purpose raid attackers in Pokémon GO, but it remains useful as a Pokédex entry, collector Pokémon and a source of different variants such as Shiny, Shadow and Lucky Vileplume.

Vileplume belongs to the Oddish evolutionary family. Gloom can evolve into either Vileplume or Bellossom, making the Sun Stone an important resource for players who specifically want Vileplume.

10 — SPECIAL VARIANTS

Vileplume Special Forms & Attributes

Pokémon GO can attach several different properties to Vileplume. Some are actual battle or appearance variants, while others are Pokémon attributes.

🌺

Regular Vileplume

The standard Grass / Poison version of Vileplume. This is the normal form obtained through the Gloom evolution branch.

✨

Shiny Vileplume

Shiny Vileplume has an alternate coloration. Shiny status is retained when a Shiny Gloom evolves into Vileplume.

🌑

Shadow Vileplume

Shadow Vileplume can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow Gloom. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon.

💎

Purified Vileplume

Purified Vileplume is obtained when an eligible Shadow Gloom is purified before or during its evolution path.

🍀

Lucky Vileplume

Lucky Vileplume is created through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

💯

100% Vileplume

A perfect Vileplume has 15/15/15 IVs. This is an IV quality rather than a separate species or form.

📏

XXL Vileplume

XXL is a size classification assigned to individual Pokémon. It does not change Vileplume's species or typing.

🔹

XXS Vileplume

XXS is a size classification representing an especially small individual Vileplume. It is an attribute rather than a separate form.

11 — SHINY

Shiny Vileplume

✨
Shiny Vileplume

Shiny Vileplume has an alternate appearance compared with the standard form. A Shiny Vileplume can be produced by evolving a Shiny Gloom into Vileplume.

Shiny Vileplume Collecting

Shiny Vileplume is primarily a collector target. Players interested in completing their Shiny Pokédex may want to keep both Shiny Vileplume and Shiny Bellossom because Gloom has two possible evolutions.

12 — SHADOW

Shadow Vileplume

Shadow Vileplume is the Shadow version of Vileplume. It retains the Grass / Poison typing but receives the standard Shadow Pokémon battle characteristics.

🌑

Shadow Vileplume

Shadow Vileplume can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow Gloom. Its Shadow status is distinct from ordinary Vileplume and should be evaluated separately when considering investment.

Shadow Vileplume Considerations

  • Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure.
  • Shadow Pokémon also take more damage than normal Pokémon.
  • Check the Charged Move Frustration before investment.
  • Compare the Shadow build with regular Vileplume.
  • Do not purify automatically if you prefer the Shadow bonus.

13 — PURIFIED

Purified Vileplume

Purified Vileplume originates from an eligible Shadow Pokémon that has been purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and moves.

💎

Purified Vileplume

Purified Vileplume remains Grass / Poison type. Purification removes the Shadow status and applies the standard purification mechanics.

Should You Purify Shadow Vileplume?

There is no universal answer. Purification can be attractive for collectors or players who want the purified designation, but players who value the Shadow battle bonus should consider keeping the Pokémon Shadow.

Investment tip:

Before purifying, check the Pokémon's IVs, moves, level, appraisal and your own collection goals. Purification is a meaningful decision, not simply a cosmetic change.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Vileplume

Lucky Vileplume is a special trade outcome. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Vileplume

A Lucky Vileplume is obtained when a trade produces the Lucky Pokémon result. Lucky status is an attribute of the individual Pokémon rather than a new Vileplume species.

Why Keep a Lucky Vileplume?

  • Lower Stardust costs for power-ups.
  • Attractive for collectors.
  • Useful for building a themed Lucky collection.
  • Can be especially interesting when combined with strong IVs.
15 — SIZE

XXL & XXS Vileplume

Pokémon GO tracks individual Pokémon size. Vileplume can therefore be encountered with XXL or XXS size classifications.

📏

XXL Vileplume

XXL Vileplume is an especially large individual. The size classification does not change its Grass / Poison typing or base species.

🔹

XXS Vileplume

XXS Vileplume is an especially small individual. Like XXL status, XXS is an individual Pokémon attribute rather than a separate evolution.

Collector tip:

If you enjoy collecting unusual specimens, consider keeping XXL, XXS, Lucky, Shiny, Shadow and perfect-IV Vileplume separately rather than transferring them solely because their combat stats are average.

16 — PVP

Vileplume PvP Guide

Vileplume's Grass / Poison typing gives it useful matchup opportunities, but its PvP performance depends heavily on the league, moveset and current meta.

Great League

Vileplume can be considered in Great League as a specialist pick when its typing and moveset provide useful matchup coverage. However, it competes with many other Grass and Poison Pokémon.

Ultra League

Vileplume can occasionally fit specialized Ultra League teams, although it is generally less common than many stronger meta options.

Master League

Vileplume's relatively low maximum CP makes Master League a poor environment for a standard Vileplume investment.

PvP Reminder

Do not automatically chase 15/15/15 IVs for every PvP build. Lower Attack IVs can sometimes allow a Pokémon to reach a higher level under a CP cap.

17 — RESOURCES

Vileplume Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Powering up Vileplume requires Stardust and Oddish Candy, with Candy XL becoming important at higher levels.

22 — FAQ

Vileplume FAQ

What type is Vileplume?

Vileplume is a Grass / Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Vileplume weak to?

Vileplume is weak to Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic-type attacks.

Does Vileplume evolve?

Vileplume is the final evolution in its branch. Gloom evolves into Vileplume using a Sun Stone and Candy.

Can Gloom evolve into Vileplume or Bellossom?

Yes. Gloom has two evolutionary paths: Vileplume and Bellossom.

Is Shiny Vileplume available?

Yes. Shiny Vileplume can be obtained through eligible Shiny encounters or by evolving an eligible Shiny Gloom.

Can Vileplume be Shadow?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Gloom can evolve into Shadow Vileplume.

What is Purified Vileplume?

Purified Vileplume comes from an eligible Shadow Pokémon that has been purified.

What is Lucky Vileplume?

Lucky Vileplume is a Vileplume obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% IV Vileplume?

A 100% IV Vileplume has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Vileplume be XXL?

Yes. Vileplume can have the XXL size classification.

Can Vileplume be XXS?

Yes. Vileplume can also have the XXS size classification.

Is Vileplume good for raids?

Vileplume can be used as a Grass or Poison attacker, but it generally sits below the strongest options available for optimized raid teams.

Is Vileplume good in PvP?

Vileplume can have niche PvP applications depending on the league, moveset and current metagame, but it is not generally considered a top Master League option.

Should I keep a 100% Vileplume?

A perfect 15/15/15 Vileplume is a valuable collector specimen and may be worth keeping even if you do not frequently use Vileplume in battle.

Should I purify Shadow Vileplume?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions and consider whether you value the Shadow battle bonus, purification benefits or collection status.

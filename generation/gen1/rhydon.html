Why Rhydon's Typing Matters

Rhydon's Ground/Rock typing gives it access to useful offensive coverage, but it also creates several important weaknesses. Water and Grass are especially dangerous because of the interaction between its two types.

Shadow Rhydon

Lucky Rhydon

Lucky Rhydon is a special status that can result from trading Pokémon. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and have a minimum IV floor.

🍀

Lucky Rhydon

A traded Rhydon can become Lucky, reducing Stardust costs for powering it up.

⭐

Lucky Trade Value

Lucky Rhydon can be particularly useful for players planning to evolve it into Rhyperior.

Lucky Rhydon IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV value for each stat. The exact resulting IV combination should still be checked after the trade.

Best Use

A Lucky Rhydon can be a practical candidate for evolution and long-term Stardust-efficient investment.

Mega Rhydon

There is currently no official Mega Rhydon form in Pokémon GO. Rhydon should therefore not be presented as having a Mega Evolution.

Mega Rhydon Status

No official Mega Rhydon form is available. Rhydon's official evolutionary path instead leads from Rhyhorn → Rhydon → Rhyperior.

What Should You Invest In Instead?

If you are looking for a stronger final-stage Pokémon from the Rhydon family, consider evolving Rhydon into Rhyperior rather than searching for a Mega Rhydon.

Rhydon FAQ

What type is Rhydon?

Rhydon is a Ground and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Rhydon weak to?

Rhydon is vulnerable to Water, Grass, Ice, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks.

What is Rhydon's maximum CP?

Rhydon's maximum CP is approximately 3,300, depending on the Pokémon GO CP calculation and current game data.

Is Shiny Rhydon available?

Yes. Shiny Rhydon is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Rhydon available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and current game events.

Can Shadow Rhydon be purified?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Rhydon can be purified, changing its Shadow status and applying the normal purification mechanics.

What is a 100% Rhydon?

A 100% Rhydon has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Rhydon?

Lucky Rhydon is a Rhydon that has obtained the Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Rhydon?

XXL Rhydon is an unusually large Rhydon that receives the XXL size designation.

What is XXS Rhydon?

XXS Rhydon is an unusually small Rhydon that receives the XXS size designation.

Does Rhydon evolve?

Yes. Rhydon evolves into Rhyperior using 100 Rhyhorn Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is there a Mega Rhydon?

No official Mega Rhydon form currently exists. Rhydon's evolutionary family instead ends with Rhyperior.

Is Rhydon good for raids?

Rhydon can contribute in Ground and Rock roles, but players generally have stronger reasons to evolve a good Rhydon into Rhyperior.

Should I keep a 100% Rhydon?

Yes. A perfect Rhydon is an excellent candidate for evolution into a perfect Rhyperior.

Is Lucky Rhydon worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Rhydon can be particularly attractive for players who intend to evolve and power up the Pokémon while reducing Stardust costs.

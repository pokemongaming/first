Dodrio Overview

Dodrio is the evolved form of Doduo and was introduced in Generation 1. It is a dual Normal/Flying-type Pokémon with three heads and a distinctive high-speed design.

In Pokémon GO, Dodrio is more valuable as a collection, Pokédex and themed Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker. Its special variants, however, can make it interesting for collectors.

Why Dodrio's Typing Matters

Dodrio's Normal/Flying typing gives it access to Flying-oriented offensive options while also determining its defensive matchup against several Pokémon GO attack types.

Its Flying typing makes Dodrio especially vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks, while its Normal typing contributes additional resistances and immunities.

What Is a 100% Dodrio?

A 100% Dodrio, commonly called a Hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is a desirable specimen for collectors and players who enjoy maximizing their Pokémon.

Shiny Dodrio

Shiny Dodrio is the alternate coloration of Dodrio. It is primarily a collector-focused variant and does not receive a separate species identity.

SHINY VARIANT

✨ Shiny Dodrio

Shiny Dodrio has an alternate appearance compared with the standard Dodrio. Its Shiny status does not automatically make it stronger in battle.

Shiny Dodrio IVs

A Shiny Dodrio can also have any IV combination. A Shiny Hundo Dodrio, with 15/15/15 IVs, is considerably more desirable to many collectors than an ordinary Shiny specimen.

Should You Purify Dodrio?

Dodrio FAQ

What type is Dodrio?

Dodrio is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Dodrio weak to?

Dodrio is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Is Shiny Dodrio available?

Shiny Dodrio is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny form is obtainable.

Is Shadow Dodrio available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and encounter mechanics.

What is a 100% Dodrio?

A 100% Dodrio has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo.

What is Lucky Dodrio?

Lucky Dodrio is a special trade-related version that receives the Lucky Pokémon benefits.

What is XXL Dodrio?

XXL Dodrio is a Dodrio with an unusually large recorded size. Size and IVs are separate attributes.

What is XXS Dodrio?

XXS Dodrio is a particularly small Dodrio. Its size does not automatically determine its IV quality.

Is Dodrio good for raids?

Dodrio can participate in battles, but it is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Flying-type Pokémon are usually preferable for optimized raid teams.

Is Dodrio good in PvP?

Dodrio is primarily an off-meta PvP option. Its usefulness depends on league rules, moveset and the current Pokémon GO metagame.

Can Dodrio be Shiny and Lucky?

Special Pokémon attributes can overlap when the relevant mechanics allow them. A Shiny Pokémon can retain its Shiny identity when becoming Lucky through a trade.

Can Dodrio be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so a Dodrio can combine an XXL size with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Dodrio be XXS and Shiny?

When the relevant size and Shiny mechanics are available, these attributes can coexist, making an XXS Shiny Dodrio an interesting collection target.

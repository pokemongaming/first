Geodude

Geodude is a Rock and Ground-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, Geodude evolves into Graveler and eventually Golem, while also being available in several special forms including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS.

Geodude Overview

Geodude is the first Pokémon in the Geodude evolution family. It evolves into Graveler and then Golem. Although Geodude itself is primarily an early-stage Pokémon, its evolution family can be useful for players building Rock and Ground-type teams.

Geodude Type

Geodude has two Pokémon types: Rock and Ground. This combination gives it several useful resistances but also creates important weaknesses.

🪨
Rock Primary Type
🌎

Why Geodude's Typing Matters

Rock and Ground typing gives Geodude useful offensive coverage, but it also means that Water and Grass attacks are especially dangerous. Understanding these weaknesses is important when using Geodude or its evolutions.

Shiny Geodude

Shiny Geodude is an alternate-color version of regular Geodude. It is primarily a collector target and can also be evolved while retaining its Shiny status.

Can Shiny Geodude Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Geodude can evolve into Shiny Graveler and eventually Shiny Golem while retaining its Shiny status.

Shadow Geodude

Shadow Geodude is the Shadow version of Geodude. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon and require additional consideration before purification or heavy investment.

🌑 Shadow Geodude

Damage-focused Shadow variant with reduced defensive durability.

Regular Geodude

Standard version without Shadow mechanics.

Shadow Geodude Type

Shadow status does not change Geodude's normal typing. Shadow Geodude remains a Rock and Ground-type Pokémon.

Purified Geodude

Purified Geodude is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Geodude. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's progression and investment characteristics.

🌑 Shadow Geodude

Shadow mechanics remain active.

✨ Purified Geodude

Shadow status removed after purification.

Should You Purify Shadow Geodude?

Do not purify automatically. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, future evolution plans, collection goals and whether you prefer to keep the Shadow battle profile.

XXL Geodude

XXL Geodude is an unusually large size variant. Size categories are primarily important for collection goals and size-related gameplay systems.

📏 XXL Geodude

Larger-than-usual Geodude size variant. XXL status does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has better IVs or stronger battle stats.

Is XXL Geodude Stronger?

XXL size should not be confused with IV quality, CP or battle strength. A smaller Geodude can still have better IVs than an XXL specimen.

Geodude FAQ

What type is Geodude?

Geodude is a Rock and Ground-type Pokémon.

Does Geodude evolve?

Yes. Geodude evolves into Graveler, which can eventually evolve into Golem.

XXS is a special size category and can be useful for players collecting different Pokémon sizes.

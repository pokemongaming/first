Why Wartortle's Water Typing Matters

Wartortle's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward type matchup profile. Water attacks are useful against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Wartortle itself must watch for Electric and Grass attacks.

Lucky Wartortle

Lucky Wartortle is a Wartortle obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

A Lucky Wartortle is especially attractive to collectors and players who want to reduce the Stardust cost associated with powering up a Pokémon.

Do XXL and XXS Change Battle Stats?

Size variants should not be confused with IVs. A Pokémon's XXL or XXS designation is a size-related characteristic, while IVs determine the additional Attack, Defense and HP values used in Pokémon GO's stat calculation.

Wartortle FAQ

What type is Wartortle?

Wartortle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wartortle weak to?

Wartortle is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Does Wartortle evolve?

Yes. Wartortle evolves into Blastoise as the final stage of the Squirtle evolution family.

Is Shiny Wartortle available?

Yes. Shiny Wartortle is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can retain its Shiny status when evolved.

What is Shadow Wartortle?

Shadow Wartortle is the Shadow variant of Wartortle and retains its Water typing.

What is Purified Wartortle?

Purified Wartortle is a former Shadow Wartortle that has been purified.

What is Lucky Wartortle?

Lucky Wartortle is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic and requires less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Wartortle?

A 100% Wartortle has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is XXL Wartortle?

XXL Wartortle is a size variant classified as extra large. It is mainly valuable to collectors.

What is XXS Wartortle?

XXS Wartortle is an extra-small size variant and can be kept as a special collection specimen.

Is Wartortle good for raids?

Wartortle is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its greater value is as an evolution-stage Pokémon and collector variant.

Should I evolve my Wartortle?

If your goal is battle performance, evolving Wartortle into Blastoise is generally more useful. Collectors may choose to keep a Wartortle unevolved.

Should I keep a 100% Wartortle?

Yes. A perfect 15/15/15 Wartortle is a valuable collection specimen and can also be evolved while retaining its perfect IVs.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Wartortle?

If you collect size variants, both XXL and XXS Wartortle are worth keeping. Their primary appeal is collection rather than a guaranteed battle advantage.

Can Shadow Wartortle be purified?

Eligible Shadow Wartortle can be purified using the Pokémon GO purification mechanic.

Can Shiny Wartortle be evolved?

Yes. Evolving a Shiny Wartortle preserves its Shiny status through the evolution line.

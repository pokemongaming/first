Charmeleon

Charmeleon is the Fire-type middle evolution of the Charmander family. In Pokémon GO, it sits between Charmander and Charizard and can also appear as a Shadow Pokémon. Although it is not a top-tier raid attacker, Charmeleon can have niche value in PvP and is an important step toward building Charizard.

Why Charmeleon's Typing Matters

Charmeleon's pure Fire typing gives it useful offensive coverage against Bug, Grass, Ice and Steel Pokémon. Defensively, Fire typing also provides several useful resistances, although Charmeleon is vulnerable to Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

Should You Power Up Charmeleon for Raids?

Usually no. Charmeleon's main long-term value comes from evolving it into Charizard rather than using the middle-stage Pokémon as a dedicated raid attacker.

Investment advice:

If your goal is PvE performance, save Candy and Stardust for Charizard, Mega Charizard X, Mega Charizard Y or other stronger Fire attackers instead of heavily powering up Charmeleon.

08 • IV GUIDE

Charmeleon IV Guide

The ideal Charmeleon IVs depend on whether you plan to use it directly in PvP or evolve it into Charizard. For evolution projects, prioritize the IV spread that matches the eventual role of Charizard.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect IV Charmeleon

A 15/15/15 Charmeleon is a useful collection specimen and makes an excellent candidate for evolution if you want a perfect Charizard.

PvP IVs

PvP IV priorities can be different from raid IVs. For Great League, lower Attack with higher Defense and HP can sometimes produce a better stat product. Always evaluate the final Charmeleon or Charizard role before transferring a low-Attack specimen.

Evolution tip:

If Charmeleon is being kept specifically for Charizard, evaluate the IVs with Charizard's eventual league or battle role in mind.

09 • PVP

Charmeleon PvP Guide

Charmeleon is not a dominant Pokémon in the main PvP leagues, but it can have niche value in Great League and special cups depending on the current meta.

Great League

Charmeleon's low maximum CP allows it to participate in Great League. Fire Punch provides relatively frequent charged-move pressure, while Flamethrower offers stronger Fire damage.

PvP Moveset Option

Ember + Fire Punch + Flamethrower

Ultra League

Charmeleon's limited bulk and CP ceiling make it much less attractive in Ultra League. Other Fire Pokémon generally provide more competitive value.

Master League

Charmeleon is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the requirements of the format.

PvP note:

PvP rankings change as moves and the metagame change. Check the current cup rules and rankings before spending Elite TMs or Stardust.

14 • RESOURCES

Charmeleon Candy, Stardust & Investment

Charmeleon itself is usually more valuable as an evolution-stage Pokémon than as a heavily powered-up battle attacker. Saving resources for Charizard can provide substantially more long-term value.

  • Save Charmander Candy for Charizard evolution.
  • Keep high-IV Charmander specimens for future Charizard projects.
  • Check PvP IVs before transferring low-Attack specimens.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust on Charmeleon without a specific role.
  • Compare Shadow and regular specimens before purifying.
  • Keep strong Shiny specimens if you plan to complete the evolution family.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Charmeleon

Before Powering Up

  • Decide whether Charmeleon itself has a PvP role.
  • Consider whether evolving it into Charizard provides more value.
  • Check its IV spread.
  • Check whether it is Shadow or regular.
  • Check the current PvP moves and league restrictions.
  • Avoid spending excessive Stardust without a specific purpose.

For Evolution Projects

If your objective is Charizard, the most important decision is usually the quality of the underlying Charmander or Charmeleon. Since IVs are retained through evolution, a strong specimen can become the foundation for a much more useful Charizard.

For PvP

Charmeleon can have niche Great League applications. However, its PvP value is considerably more specialized than that of many stronger Fire Pokémon.

19 • COMMON MISTAKES

Common Charmeleon Mistakes

  • Spending large amounts of Stardust on Charmeleon without a specific PvP goal.
  • Transferring low-Attack Charmeleon without checking PvP IV potential.
  • Purifying Shadow Charmeleon automatically.

Charmeleon FAQ

Is Charmeleon a Fire-type Pokémon?

Yes. Charmeleon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmeleon's Pokédex number?

Charmeleon is National Pokédex #005.

What is Charmeleon's Max CP?

Charmeleon reaches 1,868 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Charmeleon weak to?

Charmeleon is weak to Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

What does Charmeleon resist?

Charmeleon resists Bug, Fairy, Fire, Grass, Ice and Steel attacks.

What is Charmeleon's best moveset?

Fire Fang and Flamethrower are the recommended Fire-focused moves.

Is Charmeleon good for raids?

Charmeleon is not a top-tier raid attacker. It is generally better to evolve it into Charizard for stronger battle performance.

Is Charmeleon good in Great League?

Charmeleon can have niche Great League value, particularly with moves such as Ember, Fire Punch and Flamethrower.

No. Its low maximum CP makes it unsuitable for Master League.

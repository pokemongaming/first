Raichu Forms & Special Variants

Raichu can exist in several collectible or mechanically different forms in Pokémon GO. Some forms change battle mechanics while others mainly affect appearance, IV status, size or resource requirements.

⚡

Regular Raichu

The standard Electric-type form obtained through the Pikachu evolution line.

✨

Shiny Raichu

A rare alternate-color Raichu intended primarily for collectors. Battle performance depends on IVs, level and moves rather than Shiny status.

🌑

Shadow Raichu

Shadow Raichu has the Shadow battle profile, giving it greater offensive pressure while reducing its defensive durability.

✨

Purified Raichu

A Shadow Raichu that has been purified. Purification changes the Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource costs.

🍀

Lucky Raichu

Lucky Raichu is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

💯

100% Raichu

A perfect Raichu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

📈

XXL Raichu

An unusually large Raichu that is primarily relevant to collection goals and size-based showcases.

📉

XXS Raichu

An unusually small Raichu with collectible size characteristics.

Shiny Raichu

Shiny Raichu is an alternate-color version of Raichu. Its rarity makes it valuable for collectors, but Shiny status does not automatically improve battle stats.

Collector Tip

A Shiny Raichu with strong IVs, a useful moveset or an additional special background can be worth keeping as a long-term collection piece.

Shadow Raichu

Shadow Raichu should be evaluated separately from Regular Raichu because Shadow mechanics create a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

Shadow Raichu Investment

  • Check the current moveset before investing resources.

Purified Raichu

Purified Raichu is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Raichu. Purification removes Shadow status and creates a separate investment decision.

Shadow

Higher offensive pressure with Shadow mechanics.

Purified

Standard battle profile after purification.

Decision

Choose based on your collection and battle goals.

Lucky Raichu

Lucky Raichu is especially useful for players who want to build a Raichu while reducing Stardust investment costs.

Why Lucky Raichu Matters

Lucky status does not directly increase Raichu's damage, but it can make powering up a strong specimen more resource-efficient.

100% IV Raichu

A 100% Raichu has the perfect IV combination:

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

A perfect IV Raichu is an excellent collector target, although IVs are only one part of determining practical battle performance.

XXL Raichu

XXL Raichu is an unusually large specimen. Size does not normally increase battle damage or CP, but XXL Pokémon can be valuable for collectors and size-based showcases.

XXS Raichu

XXS Raichu represents an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is mainly a collection and size-category feature.

Raichu FAQ

What type is Raichu?

Regular Raichu is an Electric-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Raichu available?

Shiny Raichu can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Raichu stronger than Regular Raichu?

Shadow mechanics increase offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Raichu?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow battle profile with your collection and investment goals first.

What is a Lucky Raichu?

Lucky Raichu is a Lucky Pokémon and can be cheaper to power up with Stardust.

What is a 100% Raichu?

A 100% Raichu has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Raichu deal more damage?

Size is generally a collection characteristic and does not automatically increase battle damage.

Are Mega Raichu X and Mega Raichu Y available in Pokémon GO?

Yes. Mega Raichu X and Mega Raichu Y are Mega forms present in Pokémon GO and should be tracked separately for current raid and availability rotations.

Which Mega Raichu is better?

The better choice depends on current stats, movesets, raid matchups and Pokémon GO's active Mega mechanics.

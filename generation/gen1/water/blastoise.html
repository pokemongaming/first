Blastoise Overview

Blastoise is a pure Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Wartortle and is the final stage of the Squirtle evolutionary family. Blastoise can also Mega Evolve into Mega Blastoise.

Why Blastoise's Typing Matters

Blastoise's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward battle role. Water attacks are super effective against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Blastoise's defensive typing gives it useful resistance to Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

09 • PVP

Blastoise PvP Guide

Blastoise's excellent bulk and access to Hydro Cannon make it much more interesting in PvP than its raw Attack stat suggests.

Great League

Blastoise can operate as a bulky Water-type option in Great League. Its ideal IV spread is generally different from a raid Blastoise because PvP rewards stat-product optimization.

Ultra League

Ultra League is another important environment for Blastoise. XL Candy investment may be required depending on the target level and build.

Master League

Blastoise is much less attractive in Master League because its maximum CP and overall stats do not compete with the strongest Master League Pokémon.

PvP Investment

For PvP, prioritize a Blastoise with a suitable league IV spread, sufficient Candy XL where necessary and a competitive moveset.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Blastoise

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Water.
  • Confirm Blastoise has a Water-focused moveset.
  • Use Hydro Cannon when appropriate.
  • Check Blastoise's level and IVs.
  • Consider current weather.
  • Compare regular Blastoise with Mega Blastoise.

During Team Building

Regular Blastoise should not automatically occupy every Water-type slot. Compare its performance with stronger Water attackers and consider whether Mega Blastoise provides greater team value.

PvP Team Building

Blastoise's bulk and charged-move pressure make it more interesting in PvP than its raid Attack stat would suggest. Choose IVs based on the league rather than simply searching for a 15/15/15 specimen.

Long-Term Investment

A good Blastoise can serve several purposes: PvP, collection, Water-type teams and Mega Evolution. If you have limited resources, prioritize the role that you actually need.

20 • FAQ

Blastoise FAQ

Is Blastoise a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Blastoise is a non-Legendary Water-type Pokémon from Generation 1.

What type is Blastoise?

Blastoise is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Blastoise weak to?

Blastoise is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

What is Blastoise's Max CP?

Blastoise reaches 2,788 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Mega Blastoise's Max CP?

Mega Blastoise reaches 4,455 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Blastoise's best raid moveset?

Water Gun with Hydro Cannon is the recommended Water-focused combination.

Is Hydro Cannon a legacy move?

Yes. Hydro Cannon is a legacy move and may require an event or Elite Charged TM.

Is Blastoise good for raids?

Blastoise is usable as a Water attacker, but stronger Water attackers and Mega options generally offer greater raid performance.

Is Blastoise good in Great League?

Yes. Blastoise can be useful in Great League because of its strong bulk and access to powerful charged moves.

Is Blastoise good in Ultra League?

Blastoise can be a strong Ultra League option, especially when built with an appropriate PvP IV spread and moveset.

Is Blastoise good in Master League?

Blastoise is generally less competitive in Master League because of its relatively low maximum CP compared with top Master League Pokémon.

Can Blastoise be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Blastoise is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Shadow Blastoise be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Shadow Blastoise is available when the relevant Shadow encounter is offered.

Is Shadow Blastoise Water type?

Yes. Shadow Blastoise remains a pure Water-type Pokémon.

Is Shadow Blastoise stronger than regular Blastoise?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on your goal.

Should I purify Shadow Blastoise?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent decision.

Can Shadow Blastoise Mega Evolve?

No. Shadow Pokémon cannot Mega Evolve. A regular Blastoise is required for Mega Blastoise.

What is Mega Blastoise?

Mega Blastoise is the temporary Mega Evolution of Blastoise.

What type is Mega Blastoise?

Mega Blastoise remains a pure Water-type Pokémon.

Is Mega Blastoise good for raids?

Yes. Mega Blastoise is a strong Water-type Mega raid attacker, especially when using Water Gun and Hydro Cannon.

Can Shiny Blastoise Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Blastoise can Mega Evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.

How does Squirtle evolve into Blastoise?

Squirtle evolves into Wartortle using 25 Candy, and Wartortle evolves into Blastoise using 100 Candy.

What is Blastoise's best IV?

For general collection and raid purposes, 15/15/15 is the perfect IV. For PvP, the best IV spread depends on the league.

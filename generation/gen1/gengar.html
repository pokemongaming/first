Gengar Overview

Gengar is the final evolution of Gastly. Its Ghost / Poison typing gives it a distinctive offensive profile in Pokémon GO. Gengar is generally more focused on dealing damage than absorbing it, which makes moveset selection and matchup knowledge especially important.

Gengar for Raids

Gengar is primarily valued for its offensive power. It can perform well when Ghost-type damage is useful, although its lower durability means it may faint faster than bulkier raid attackers.

Raid Role

High-damage Ghost-type attacker.

  • Use Gengar when Ghost damage is effective.
  • Check the raid boss moveset.
  • Avoid using Gengar against strong Psychic, Dark or Ghost damage.
  • Mega Gengar can provide additional team utility.

Gengar PvP Guide

Gengar can be a dangerous PvP Pokémon because of its offensive pressure, but its relatively low durability means that shield management and matchup awareness are important.

Great League

Gengar can function as an aggressive specialist, but it requires careful positioning.

Ultra League

Gengar can be used in certain team compositions, although its performance depends heavily on the current metagame and available moves.

Master League

Standard Gengar generally faces stronger competition because of its lower maximum CP compared with many Legendary and high-stat Pokémon.

Shadow Gengar for Raids

Shadow Gengar can be especially attractive for players who prioritize maximum offensive output. However, Shadow investment requires more resources and should be planned carefully.

Shadow Investment Tip

Do not automatically purify a strong Shadow Gengar just to improve its IVs. Compare the battle role before making the decision.

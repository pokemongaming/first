01 • OVERVIEW

Metapod Overview

Why Metapod's Typing Matters

Metapod is a pure Bug-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it useful resistance to Fighting, Grass and Ground damage, but Fire, Flying and Rock attacks are super effective against it.

04 • STATS

Metapod Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

Metapod has 45 Attack, 80 Defense and 137 HP. Its Level 50 maximum CP is 509, making it a very low-CP Pokémon compared with its final evolution, Butterfree.

CP note:

07 • EVOLUTION

Metapod Evolution Family

Metapod is the middle stage of the Caterpie evolution family. It evolves into Butterfree using Candy.

→

Evolution tip:

Metapod requires 50 Caterpie Candy to evolve into Butterfree. Because Metapod is already the middle stage, keeping additional Metapod specimens is mostly useful for collection, IV hunting and special-form projects.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Metapod

Purified Metapod is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Metapod. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes its Charged Move from Frustration to Return.

🌟

Purified

The Shadow status is removed after purification.

🔄

Return

Purification replaces Frustration with Return.

💎

Collection

Purified Metapod can be useful for special-form collection goals.

Should You Purify Metapod?

Because Metapod has limited battle utility, the decision is mostly a collection and evolution choice. If you value Shadow Pokémon, you may prefer to keep the Shadow form. If you want a Purified entry or Purified evolution family, purification can be considered.

22 • FAQ

Metapod Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Metapod?

Metapod is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Metapod weak against?

Metapod is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What is Metapod's max CP?

Metapod's maximum CP is 509 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Metapod available?

Yes. Shiny Metapod is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Metapod be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Metapod is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Shadow Metapod be purified?

Eligible Shadow Metapod can be purified. Purification removes the Shadow status and replaces Frustration with Return.

What is a 100% Metapod?

A 100% Metapod has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Metapod?

A Lucky Metapod is obtained through a Lucky Trade and receives the benefits associated with Lucky Pokémon.

Can Metapod be XXL?

Yes. Metapod can have an XXL size classification.

Can Metapod be XXS?

Yes. Metapod can have an XXS size classification.

Does Metapod evolve?

Yes. Metapod evolves into Butterfree using 50 Candy.

Can Metapod Dynamax?

Yes. Dynamax Metapod is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Metapod good for raids?

No. Metapod's low Attack and low maximum CP make it a poor raid attacker.

Is Metapod good for PvP?

Metapod has very limited PvP utility and is generally not a practical competitive choice.

Should I keep a 100% Metapod?

Yes, particularly if you collect perfect-IV Pokémon or want to evolve the specimen into a perfect Butterfree.

Kadabra

Kadabra is a Psychic-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the evolved form of Abra. In Pokémon GO, Kadabra is primarily useful as a Pokédex and evolution target, with additional collector value through Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

01 • OVERVIEW

Kadabra Overview

Kadabra is the second stage of the Abra evolutionary family. It is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon and evolves into Alakazam. Because Kadabra sits between Abra and Alakazam, it is generally more important as an evolution-stage Pokémon than as a long-term raid investment.

Why Kadabra's Typing Matters

Kadabra's pure Psychic typing gives it weaknesses to Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks. Psychic attacks are particularly useful against Fighting- and Poison-type Pokémon.

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Kadabra simply because purification can improve its displayed IVs. Consider its current IVs, moveset, collection value and your intended use before making the decision.

12 • 100% IV

100% IV Kadabra

A 100% IV Kadabra has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a perfect IV or hundo Pokémon.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
💯 Perfect Kadabra

A 15/15/15 Kadabra is a perfect-IV specimen. Even though Kadabra may not be your final battle investment, a hundo can be worth keeping for evolution, collection and completion purposes.

Should You Evolve a 100% Kadabra?

If you want a perfect-IV Alakazam, evolving a 15/15/15 Kadabra is the natural route. Always confirm the IVs before evolving because the IV values remain the same through evolution.

13 • XXL

XXL Kadabra

XXL Kadabra is a particularly large specimen. Pokémon GO's size system allows players to collect Pokémon with different size classifications.

📏 XXL Kadabra Extra-large size classification

XXL Kadabra can be valuable to collectors who want to build a complete size-based Pokémon collection.

🏆 Collector Value Size-focused collection

Size classification is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Kadabra does not automatically have perfect IVs.

14 • XXS

XXS Kadabra

XXS Kadabra is a particularly small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS Pokémon are interesting collectibles because size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

📐 XXS Kadabra Extra-small size classification

An XXS Kadabra can be retained as part of a size collection or Pokédex completion project.

📊 IVs Are Separate Size does not determine IVs

An XXS Kadabra can have low, average or perfect IVs. Check the appraisal independently.

17 • PVP

Kadabra PvP Guide

Kadabra has strong offensive potential but limited bulk. Its low defensive profile makes it difficult to use consistently against stronger PvP options.

Great League

Kadabra can technically fit within lower CP formats, but its lack of durability means it requires careful team building and favorable matchups.

Ultra League

Kadabra's relatively low overall stat product makes Ultra League a difficult environment for the standard form.

Master League

Kadabra is not naturally suited to Master League because its maximum CP and defensive profile are far below the level normally required for the format.

21 • FAQ

Kadabra FAQ

What type is Kadabra?

Kadabra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Kadabra weak against?

Kadabra is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

Does Kadabra evolve?

Yes. Kadabra evolves into Alakazam in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Kadabra available?

Shiny Kadabra is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution from Shiny Abra.

What is a 100% Kadabra?

A 100% Kadabra has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Kadabra?

Shadow Kadabra is the Shadow version of Kadabra. It retains the Psychic type while using Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Kadabra?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen, its IVs, moves and your intended use before purifying it.

What is Purified Kadabra?

Purified Kadabra is created by purifying a Shadow Kadabra.

What is Lucky Kadabra?

Lucky Kadabra is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system and receives the Lucky Pokémon power-up cost benefit.

What is XXL Kadabra?

XXL Kadabra is an extra-large size specimen under Pokémon GO's size classification system.

What is XXS Kadabra?

XXS Kadabra is an extra-small size specimen. Its size classification is independent of its IVs.

Is Kadabra good for raids?

Kadabra is generally not a top long-term raid investment. Players seeking stronger Psychic-type performance should consider evolving it into Alakazam and comparing it with other current Psychic attackers.

Is Kadabra good in PvP?

Kadabra has offensive potential but relatively poor durability, which limits its consistency in competitive PvP formats.

Can Shiny Kadabra evolve into Shiny Alakazam?

Yes. A Shiny Kadabra retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Can a Shadow Kadabra become a Shadow Alakazam?

Yes. Shadow status is retained when the Pokémon evolves.

Do XXL and XXS affect Kadabra IVs?

No. Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics.

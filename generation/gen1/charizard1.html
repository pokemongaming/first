Charizard Overview

Charizard is the final evolution of the Charmander evolution family and is one of the most recognizable Fire-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its Fire/Flying typing gives it useful offensive matchups, while its two Mega Evolutions provide significantly different battle roles.

02 • TYPING

Charizard Type

🔥
Fire / Flying Dual Type Pokémon

Why Charizard's Typing Matters

Charizard combines Fire and Flying typing. This gives it useful resistances to several attack types, but it also creates a major vulnerability to Rock-type attacks. The Flying typing also makes Charizard immune to Ground-type damage in the main Pokémon games, although Pokémon GO uses its own simplified battle system.

🔥 Fire Strong against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel targets.
🪽 Flying Provides additional matchup utility and resistance profile.

Shiny Charizard

Shiny Charizard is one of the most recognizable Shiny forms in Pokémon GO. Its black body coloration makes it visually distinct from standard Charizard.

🌑

Should You Purify Shadow Charizard?

Do not automatically purify a strong Shadow Charizard. Purification removes the Shadow status, so players focused on maximizing Shadow raid damage should compare both options before making a permanent decision.

Shadow investment tip

Consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, resource cost and your existing Fire-type team before deciding whether to power it up or purify it.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Charizard

Purified Charizard is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Charizard. Purification changes its status and provides the normal benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

🌟

Purified Charizard

Purified Charizard can be useful for collectors and players who prefer purified Pokémon, but purification should be considered carefully if the original Shadow Charizard has strong raid value.

Purified Charizard Considerations

  • Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status.
  • Purified Pokémon receive their associated purification benefits.
  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions before purifying.
  • A purified Charizard can still be valuable for collection.

13 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Charizard

Pokémon GO can identify Pokémon with unusually large or small sizes. XXL and XXS are size classifications rather than separate species.

XXL

XXL Charizard

An XXL Charizard is a particularly large specimen. Its size status can make it desirable for collectors and certain size-related gameplay features.

XXS

XXS Charizard

An XXS Charizard is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Charizard, its size status is separate from its species and IV combination.

Important:

XXL or XXS does not automatically mean high IV. A Charizard can be XXL or XXS while having any IV combination.

22 • PRACTICAL GUIDE

How to Use Charizard

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Fire.
  • Check Charizard's moveset.
  • Check its level and IVs.
  • Consider using Mega Charizard Y for Fire-focused teams.
  • Avoid Charizard against bosses with dangerous Rock moves when stronger alternatives are available.

Before Mega Evolving

  • Choose between Mega Charizard X and Y based on the matchup.
  • Check your available Mega Energy.
  • Consider the rest of your raid team's composition.
  • Use your strongest suitable Charizard when resources are limited.

Long-Term Investment

A high-IV Charizard with a strong moveset can be useful for years of Pokémon GO gameplay, particularly when combined with Mega Evolution.

23 • FAQ

Charizard FAQ

What type is Charizard?

Charizard is Fire/Flying type.

What is Charizard weak to?

Charizard is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock is especially important because of Charizard's Fire/Flying typing.

What is Charizard's max CP?

Standard Charizard has a maximum CP of approximately 3,266 under the standard level-cap assumptions used for Pokémon GO databases.

Is Shiny Charizard available?

Yes. Shiny Charizard is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Charizard available?

Shadow Charizard availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I purify Shadow Charizard?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version's raid performance and your investment goals before purifying it.

What is Lucky Charizard?

Lucky Charizard is a Charizard that has obtained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is a 100% Charizard?

A 100% Charizard has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo.

What is an XXL Charizard?

An XXL Charizard is an unusually large specimen. XXL status is independent of its IV combination.

What is an XXS Charizard?

An XXS Charizard is an unusually small specimen. XXS status does not indicate that the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

What type is Mega Charizard X?

Mega Charizard X is Fire/Dragon type.

What type is Mega Charizard Y?

Mega Charizard Y is Fire/Flying type.

Which is better, Mega Charizard X or Y?

For a dedicated Fire-type raid role, Mega Charizard Y is generally the more natural choice because of its extremely high Attack stat. Mega Charizard X provides a different Fire/Dragon typing and matchup profile.

Is Mega Charizard Y good for raids?

Yes. Mega Charizard Y is a powerful Fire-oriented Mega Evolution and can be a valuable choice for raids where Fire damage is super effective.

Can Shiny Charizard Mega Evolve?

Yes. An eligible Shiny Charizard can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Can Shadow Charizard Mega Evolve?

Shadow Pokémon and Mega Evolution have special interaction rules. Check the current Pokémon GO mechanics before planning a Shadow-to-Mega build.

Is Charizard good for raids?

Charizard can perform a useful Fire-type role, while its Mega Evolutions provide significantly stronger specialized options for raid teams.

Is Charizard good in PvP?

Charizard can be useful in selected PvP formats depending on its moveset, IVs, shields and the current metagame. Its exact ranking can change as Pokémon GO updates moves and balance.

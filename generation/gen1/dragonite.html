Dragonite

Dragonite is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon from Generation 1 and the final evolution of the Dratini family. In Pokémon GO, Dragonite is a versatile attacker with strong Dragon and Flying options, major Master League relevance and multiple special forms including Shadow, Shiny and Mega Dragonite.

Why Dragonite's Typing Matters

Dragonite's dual Dragon/Flying typing gives it strong offensive flexibility while also creating a distinctive defensive profile. It resists several common attacking types but has a severe weakness to Ice-type attacks.

10 — PVP

Dragonite PvP Guide

Dragonite has historically been one of the most recognizable Master League Pokémon because its combination of high CP, Dragon typing and efficient charged-move pressure works well in high-level PvP.

Master League

Master League is Dragonite's most natural open PvP environment. Dragon Breath, Dragon Claw and other charged-move options can create consistent pressure, while Dragonite's high overall stats allow it to operate without a CP cap.

Ultra League

Dragonite can be used in Ultra League, although availability, IV optimization and team composition should be considered before investing specifically for the format.

Great League

Standard Dragonite's high CP makes Great League a less natural environment for it. Special formats can change this assessment, so always check the current cup rules.

17 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Dragonite

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Dragon-type or Flying-type damage is needed.
  • Confirm Dragonite's moveset.
  • Check its level and IVs.
  • Consider Windy weather for Dragon-type moves.
  • Compare Dragonite with your other Dragon attackers.

During Team Building

Dragonite should not automatically fill every Dragon slot. Compare the specific boss matchup, move timing, weather boost, survivability and available alternatives.

Long-Term Investment

Dragonite is a strong long-term Pokémon for players who participate in Master League and raids. A good specimen can serve multiple purposes instead of being limited to one battle format.

19 — SPECIAL FORMS

Shadow, Shiny & Mega Dragonite

Dragonite's special forms should be treated as separate investment decisions. Shadow Dragonite focuses on its Shadow battle profile, Shiny Dragonite focuses on its alternate appearance and Mega Dragonite focuses on temporary Mega Evolution and raid utility.

  • Regular Dragonite — flexible Dragon/Flying attacker.
  • Shadow Dragonite — higher offensive pressure with lower durability.
  • Shiny Dragonite — alternate collectible appearance.
  • Mega Dragonite — enhanced temporary Mega form.
  • Shiny Mega Dragonite — Shiny identity retained through Mega Evolution.
20 — FAQ

Dragonite FAQ

What type is Dragonite?

Dragonite is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Dragonite weak to?

Dragonite is weak to Ice, Dragon, Fairy and Rock. Ice is especially dangerous because Dragonite takes double super-effective damage from Ice.

What is Dragonite's Max CP?

A perfect Level 50 regular Dragonite can reach 4,287 CP.

What is Mega Dragonite's Max CP?

Mega Dragonite can reach 5,452 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Dragonite good for raids?

Dragonite can be a strong Dragon-type attacker when equipped with an appropriate Dragon moveset.

Is Dragonite good in Master League?

Yes. Dragonite has strong Master League relevance because of its high CP, stats and Dragon-type moveset options.

Is Shiny Dragonite available?

Yes. Shiny Dragonite is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Dragonite available?

Shadow Dragonite is an available Shadow form, with availability depending on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and events.

Is Shadow Dragonite stronger than regular Dragonite?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on the battle and investment goal.

Should I purify Shadow Dragonite?

Not automatically. If your priority is maximizing Shadow damage, retaining the Shadow form may be preferable.

What is Mega Dragonite?

Mega Dragonite is Dragonite's special Mega Evolution form. It remains Dragon/Flying type and has stronger Attack and Defense than regular Dragonite.

How much Mega Energy does Dragonite need?

Current references list 300 Mega Energy for the first Mega Evolution and 60 Mega Energy for later Mega Evolutions.

Can Shiny Dragonite become Mega Dragonite?

Yes. A Shiny Dragonite can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Can Shadow Dragonite become Mega Dragonite?

Shadow Pokémon should be kept separate from Mega Evolution mechanics. Do not present Shadow Dragonite as a normal Mega Evolution candidate unless current Pokémon GO rules explicitly allow that interaction.

What is Dragonite's best Dragon moveset?

Dragon Tail paired with a strong Dragon Charged Move, such as Outrage or Draco Meteor depending on the intended role and current move availability, is a strong Dragon-focused setup.

What is Mega Dragonite's best moveset?

Dragon Tail and Draco Meteor are a strong Dragon-focused Mega Dragonite combination.

Does Dragonite evolve further?

Dragonite is the final stage of the Dratini evolutionary family. Mega Dragonite is a special Mega form rather than another permanent evolution stage.

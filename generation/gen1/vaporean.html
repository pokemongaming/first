Vaporeon

Vaporeon Overview

Vaporeon is a Water-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. It evolves from Eevee and has a high Stamina stat that helps it survive longer than many similarly powered Water-type Pokémon.

Why Vaporeon's Water Typing Matters

Water is one of Pokémon GO's most useful attacking types. Vaporeon's Water typing gives it strong offensive options against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon while also providing resistance to several common attack types.

Vaporeon Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

When Is Vaporeon Useful in Raids?

Vaporeon can be useful when you need an accessible Water attacker, especially for newer players building their first raid teams. However, experienced players should compare Vaporeon against stronger modern Water attackers before spending premium resources.

What Counters Vaporeon?

Vaporeon PvP Guide

Vaporeon's high HP and Water typing give it some PvP utility, although its overall ranking depends heavily on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Great League

Vaporeon's high CP relative to the Great League limit means it generally requires careful IV selection and does not naturally fit every Great League team.

Ultra League

Vaporeon can be considered in Ultra League formats, particularly when its Water typing and moveset provide useful matchup coverage.

Master League

Vaporeon's maximum CP allows it to enter Master League, but players should compare it against the many stronger Water-type and Legendary options available in the format.

Shiny Vaporeon

Shiny Vaporeon is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collection target, especially for players completing an Eeveelution Shiny collection.

✨
Shiny Vaporeon

Shiny Vaporeon has an alternate coloration compared with the regular form. Shiny status does not provide a direct combat-stat advantage.

Is Shiny Vaporeon Stronger?

No. Shiny Vaporeon has the same fundamental battle stats and typing as regular Vaporeon. The primary difference is its appearance and collector value.

Shadow Vaporeon

Shadow Vaporeon should be treated separately from regular Vaporeon because Shadow Pokémon use different battle mechanics. However, current Pokémon GO data should be checked before presenting Shadow Vaporeon as an obtainable form.

Current Shadow Status

Vaporeon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Long-term Vaporeon investment requires Eevee Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Vaporeon is an evolved Pokémon, planning your Eevee resources is important.

  • Save Eevee Candy for the Eeveelutions you actually need.
  • Keep high-IV Eevee before committing to an evolution.
  • Check the final Vaporeon moveset before heavy investment.
  • Collect Candy XL if you intend to push Vaporeon toward higher levels.
  • Compare Vaporeon against stronger Water attackers before spending large amounts of Stardust.

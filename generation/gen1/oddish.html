Why Oddish's Typing Matters

Oddish's Grass and Poison typing gives it a combination of offensive and defensive matchups. Its eventual evolutions can use this typing in different ways, although Oddish itself has a much lower battle ceiling than its evolved forms.

Shadow Oddish

Shadow Oddish is the Shadow variant of Oddish. Its Shadow status changes its battle characteristics while retaining the same Grass / Poison typing.

SHADOW
🌑

Shadow Oddish

SHADOW EVOLUTION
🖤

Shadow Evolution Project

If you intend to evolve Shadow Oddish, check its IVs, moves and your intended evolution before spending resources.

Shadow Oddish Checklist

  • Check the Shadow Pokémon's IVs before investing.
  • Consider the cost of powering up a Shadow Pokémon.
  • Check whether Frustration is currently removable.
  • Compare the evolved Shadow Pokémon before spending Candy.
  • Do not purify automatically just to increase IVs.

Purified Oddish

Purified Oddish is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Oddish. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and resource costs.

XXL Oddish

XXL Oddish is a size variant with a particularly large size measurement. Size is separate from IV quality, so a large Oddish does not automatically have better IVs.

XXL XXL Oddish

Large-size Oddish can be valuable to collectors and may be useful for completing size-related collection goals.

COLLECTION Why Keep XXL?

Keep an XXL Oddish if you are collecting size records, medals or unusual Pokémon variants.

Size vs IV:

XXL status and IV quality are separate characteristics. An XXL Oddish can have low, average or perfect IVs.

XXS Oddish

XXS Oddish is a particularly small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of the Pokémon's IVs.

Oddish FAQ

Is Oddish a Grass-type Pokémon?

Yes. Oddish is a Grass / Poison dual-type Pokémon.

What is Oddish weak to?

Oddish is vulnerable to several attack types including Fire, Ice, Flying, Psychic and Bug.

Does Oddish evolve?

Yes. Oddish evolves into Gloom, which can eventually lead to Vileplume or Bellossom.

Is Shiny Oddish available?

Yes. Shiny Oddish is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

What is a 100% Oddish?

A 100% Oddish has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Oddish?

Shadow Oddish is the Shadow variant of Oddish and has the battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Oddish?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent decision.

What is Lucky Oddish?

Lucky Oddish is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading mechanics and has special Lucky characteristics.

What is XXL Oddish?

XXL Oddish is a particularly large Oddish based on its size measurement.

What is XXS Oddish?

XXS Oddish is a particularly small Oddish based on its size measurement.

Is Oddish good for raids?

Oddish itself is not normally considered a strong raid attacker because of its relatively low stats and maximum CP.

Should I evolve a perfect Oddish?

A perfect Oddish can be a good evolution candidate, but your preferred evolution branch and intended use should be considered before spending resources.

Golduck

Why Golduck's Water Typing Matters

Golduck's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. Water-type Pokémon are weak to Electric and Grass attacks while resisting Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks.

09 • PVP

Golduck PvP Guide

Golduck can be used in PvP, although it is not normally considered a top-tier choice across the main GO Battle League formats.

Great League

Golduck can fit within the Great League CP limit, but its performance depends heavily on moveset, IV distribution and the current meta.

Ultra League

Golduck can also be built for Ultra League, although players should compare its performance with other Water-type options before committing significant resources.

Master League

Golduck's maximum CP is relatively low for the Master League environment, making it a much less natural choice for this format.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Golduck

Lucky Golduck is not a separate species or evolution. It is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading mechanics.

⭐ Lucky Golduck

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up compared with normal Pokémon. Lucky status is particularly useful when building a Pokémon that you intend to invest in over many levels.

Lucky Golduck Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Guaranteed floor for IVs after becoming Lucky.
  • Useful for collectors.
  • Can be Shiny and Lucky at the same time.
  • Can also have strong or perfect IVs.
Important:

Lucky, Shiny, Shadow and IV status are different characteristics. A Pokémon can combine some of these characteristics depending on the game rules.

💯 Perfect Golduck — 15/15/15

15 • XXL

XXL Golduck

XXL Golduck is an unusually large specimen. XXL status is a size characteristic rather than a new Pokémon species.

📏 XXL Golduck

A very large Golduck that can be useful for size-focused collections and showcases.

Standard Golduck

Normal size range without the XXL designation.

Does XXL Golduck Have Better Stats?

XXL status should not be confused with higher IVs. Size and Individual Values are separate characteristics. An XXL Golduck can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Collector tip:

Keep an XXL Golduck if you collect unusual sizes, showcases or special Pokémon variants.

16 • XXS

XXS Golduck

XXS Golduck is an unusually small Golduck. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than a separate species.

🔹 XXS Golduck

A very small Golduck that can be valuable for collectors interested in unusual sizes.

Standard Golduck

A Golduck without the XXS size designation.

XXS Golduck and IVs

XXS status does not mean the Pokémon has lower IVs. A tiny Golduck can still be a 100% IV specimen.

23 • FAQ

Golduck FAQ

What type is Golduck?

Golduck is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Golduck weak to?

Golduck is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

What is Golduck's max CP?

Golduck's maximum CP is around 2,707 under the standard Pokémon GO level-cap calculation.

Is Golduck good for raids?

Golduck can be used as a Water-type attacker, but it generally falls behind the strongest Water Pokémon for optimized raid teams.

Is Golduck good in PvP?

Golduck can be used in GO Battle League formats, but its effectiveness depends on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Is Shiny Golduck available?

Yes. Shiny Golduck is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Psyduck evolve into Golduck?

Yes. Golduck is the evolution of Psyduck.

What is a 100% Golduck?

A 100% Golduck has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Golduck?

Shadow Golduck is the Shadow variant of Golduck. It retains the Water typing while having the special Shadow battle characteristics.

Can Shadow Psyduck become Shadow Golduck?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Psyduck can evolve into Shadow Golduck.

Should I purify Shadow Golduck?

Purification is a personal investment decision. Compare the Shadow benefits with the advantages of a purified Pokémon before making the choice.

What is Lucky Golduck?

Lucky Golduck is a Golduck that has received the Lucky status through trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Golduck?

XXL Golduck is an unusually large Golduck. XXL status is a size characteristic and does not automatically mean higher IVs.

What is XXS Golduck?

XXS Golduck is an unusually small Golduck. XXS status is independent of IV quality.

Can a Golduck be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can have both statuses.

Can Golduck be 100% IV and XXL?

Yes. IV quality and Pokémon size are separate characteristics, so an XXL Golduck can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Does Golduck have another evolution?

No. Golduck is the final evolution in the Psyduck family.

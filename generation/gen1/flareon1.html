Flareon

Flareon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon and one of Eevee's eight evolutions. In Pokémon GO, Flareon is an accessible Fire attacker with several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Is Flareon Good for Raids?

Flareon can be a useful Fire-type raid attacker, especially for players who need an accessible Pokémon for raids against Bug, Grass, Ice and Steel Pokémon.

  • Useful Fire-type attacker for developing teams.
  • Eevee availability makes Flareon relatively easy to obtain.
  • Shadow Flareon provides a different damage-focused option.
  • Stronger Legendary and Mega alternatives can exist for highly optimized raid teams.

Shiny Flareon

Shiny Flareon is the alternate-color version of regular Flareon. Its battle stats and type remain associated with the Flareon species.

Shiny Collector Tip

A Shiny Eevee can be evolved into Shiny Flareon. Make sure the evolution method is understood before evolving a valuable Shiny Eevee.

Shadow Flareon

Shadow Flareon is a damage-focused variant with a different battle profile from regular Flareon.

Higher Damage

Shadow mechanics increase offensive pressure.

Lower Durability

Shadow Pokémon take increased damage.

Higher Cost

Shadow investments can require additional resources.

Shadow move note:

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. Check current Team GO Rocket events and move rules before planning to change the Charged Move.

Purified Flareon

Purified Flareon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Flareon. Purification removes Shadow status and changes its battle and investment characteristics.

Lucky Flareon

Lucky Flareon can be obtained when a Lucky Eevee is evolved into Flareon or through other eligible Lucky Pokémon mechanics.

Why Lucky Flareon Is Useful

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making Lucky Flareon a resource-efficient option for players building multiple Fire-type Pokémon.

100% Flareon IV Guide

A 100% IV Flareon has the following Individual Values:

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

A perfect IV Flareon is a valuable collector and long-term investment specimen, although lower-IV Flareon can still perform well.

XXL Flareon

XXL Flareon is a larger-than-normal size variant. Size does not normally increase standard battle stats, but XXL Pokémon can be valuable for collectors and eligible PokéStop Showcases.

XXL Collector Value

Keep unusually large Flareon specimens if you participate in size-based Pokémon GO Showcases.

XXS Flareon

XXS Flareon is an unusually small size variant. Its primary value is collection rarity rather than additional battle performance.

Flareon FAQ

What type is Flareon?

Flareon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

Is Flareon good in Pokémon GO?

Flareon can be a useful Fire-type attacker, especially for players building accessible teams.

What is Flareon weak to?

Flareon is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Can Flareon be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Flareon is available through eligible Shiny Eevee evolution and encounter methods.

Can Flareon be Shadow?

Shadow Flareon can exist through eligible Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

What is a 100% Flareon?

A 100% Flareon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Flareon cheaper to power up?

Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up requirements.

Does XXL Flareon have higher stats?

Size variants do not normally increase Flareon's standard battle stats.

Does Flareon evolve further?

No. Flareon is a final evolution and does not evolve further.

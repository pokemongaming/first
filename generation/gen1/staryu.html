Staryu Overview

Staryu is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Starmie using 50 Staryu Candy. Staryu is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker, but it remains useful for collectors, Pokédex completion, evolution projects and special variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS.

Lucky Staryu

Lucky Staryu is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀
Lucky Staryu

A special trade-related variant that can be attractive for collectors and players who want to reduce Stardust costs.

Why Keep Lucky Staryu?

  • Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky Staryu can be evolved into Lucky Starmie.
  • Lucky + Shiny can make an especially desirable collection combination.

XXL Staryu

XXL Staryu is a size-variant Staryu that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

🟣
XXL Staryu

A particularly large Staryu. Its size attribute is independent of its IV percentage.

XXL + 100%

Should You Keep XXL Staryu?

XXL Staryu can be worthwhile for collectors, especially if it also has a desirable IV spread, Shiny status, Lucky status or a useful appraisal.

XXS Staryu

XXS Staryu is an unusually small Staryu recorded by Pokémon GO's size system. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV quality.

🔹
XXS Staryu

A particularly small Staryu that can be valuable as a size-variant collectible.

XXS + Shiny

A Shiny XXS Staryu can be an especially interesting collector combination.

Should You Keep XXS Staryu?

If you collect size records, XXS Pokémon are worth keeping. An XXS Staryu with strong IVs or Shiny status can be an even more desirable specimen.

Staryu PvP Guide

Staryu's low maximum CP and first-stage status generally limit its relevance in standard Great League, Ultra League and Master League competition.

Great League

Staryu can reach the Great League CP range, but it is generally outclassed by stronger Pokémon with better battle profiles.

Ultra League

Staryu's maximum CP makes it a poor fit for the Ultra League compared with Pokémon specifically suited to that format.

Master League

Staryu is not a practical Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the levels normally required for this format.

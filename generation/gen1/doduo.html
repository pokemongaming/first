01 • DATABASE

Doduo Overview

Doduo is a dual-type Normal/Flying Pokémon from Generation 1. It evolves into Dodrio after the required Candy investment. Although Doduo is not normally considered a major raid attacker, collectors may want to keep different variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Doduo.

Doduo Evolution

Shadow Doduo & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration. Always check the current event rules before spending resources.

Should You Purify Shadow Doduo?

Purification is a choice rather than an automatic upgrade. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, appraisal, collection value and whether you specifically want to retain its Shadow status.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Doduo

Purified Doduo is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Doduo. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can improve its appraisal values.

💎

Purified Doduo

Purified Doduo loses its Shadow status and can become a useful collector's variant.

✨

Purification Bonus

Purification increases each IV by the applicable amount, up to the IV maximum.

Purification Tip

Check the Shadow Doduo's IVs before purifying it. Purification is permanent, so make the decision based on whether you value the Shadow or Purified form more.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Doduo

Lucky Doduo is a special trade variant identified by its Lucky background. Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust for power-ups, making them appealing to collectors and players who want to reduce investment costs.

🍀 Lucky Doduo

A Lucky Doduo can be especially interesting when combined with high IVs, Shiny status or a memorable trade.

Why Keep a Lucky Doduo?

  • Lower Stardust investment requirements.
  • Useful for completing a Lucky Pokédex collection.
  • Can be combined with high IVs.
  • Lucky and Shiny combinations are highly collectible.
  • Can be evolved into a Lucky Dodrio.

13 • SIZE

XXL Doduo

XXL Doduo is an unusually large specimen. Size categories are separate from IV quality, meaning an XXL Doduo does not automatically have better IVs.

LARGE SPECIMEN 📏 XXL Doduo

Keep an XXL Doduo if you are collecting size records, medal progress or unusual Pokémon specimens.

IMPORTANT Size ≠ IV

An XXL Doduo can have any IV combination. Check the appraisal separately.

Why Collect XXL Doduo?

  • Useful for size-focused collections.
  • Can be combined with Shiny status.
  • Can also have perfect IVs.
  • Can be evolved into an XXL Dodrio.
14 • SIZE

XXS Doduo

XXS Doduo is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of the Pokémon's IV values.

SMALL SPECIMEN 🔹 XXS Doduo

An XXS Doduo can be valuable to collectors interested in size records and unusual Pokémon variants.

COLLECTION NOTE Size and IVs Are Separate

An XXS Doduo can be low IV, high IV or even a perfect 15/15/15 specimen.

XXS Doduo Collection Ideas

A Shiny XXS Doduo, Lucky XXS Doduo or 100% XXS Doduo can be especially interesting for collectors because multiple independent attributes can overlap on the same Pokémon.

How to Identify a Hundo Doduo

A perfect Doduo has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Third-party IV tools can estimate IV combinations, but the in-game appraisal provides the authoritative appraisal information for your Pokémon.

Remember that CP and level affect the displayed CP. Two Doduo with different IVs can have similar CP values, so CP should not be used as the only method of identifying a perfect Pokémon.

22 • FAQ

Doduo Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Doduo in Pokémon GO?

Doduo is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Doduo weak to?

Doduo is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Does Doduo evolve?

Yes. Doduo evolves into Dodrio.

Is Shiny Doduo available?

Shiny Doduo is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny species is active in the game.

What is a 100% Doduo?

A 100% Doduo, or Hundo Doduo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Doduo?

Shadow Doduo is a Shadow variant of Doduo associated with Team GO Rocket encounters when available.

Should I purify Shadow Doduo?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, collection value and whether you want to preserve the Shadow status before purifying it.

What is Lucky Doduo?

Lucky Doduo is a Doduo that became Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is an XXL Doduo?

XXL Doduo is an unusually large Doduo. Its size classification is independent of its IVs.

What is an XXS Doduo?

XXS Doduo is an unusually small Doduo. Like XXL, XXS status does not determine the Pokémon's IVs.

Can an XXL Doduo be 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Doduo can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can an XXS Doduo be Shiny?

Yes. Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so an eligible Shiny Doduo can also have an XXS size classification.

Is Doduo good for raids?

Doduo is generally not a priority raid attacker. Most players should invest Stardust in stronger Pokémon.

Is Doduo good for PvP?

Doduo has limited competitive relevance in standard PvP leagues. Its role is generally more focused on collection and evolution.

Should I evolve my 100% Doduo?

If you want a perfect Dodrio, evolving a 100% Doduo is a logical choice. If you collect perfect first-stage Pokémon, you may prefer keeping it as Doduo.

Should I keep multiple Doduo?

Collectors may want multiple Doduo because Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect IV, XXL and XXS characteristics can make different specimens valuable for different reasons.

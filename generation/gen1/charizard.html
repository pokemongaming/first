02 • TYPE

Charizard Type

Fire / Flying Dual Type

Why Charizard's Typing Matters

Charizard combines Fire and Flying typing. This gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates a major weakness to Rock-type attacks. Understanding the dual typing is particularly important when selecting Charizard for raids and PvP.

09 • PVP

Charizard PvP Guide

Great League

Charizard can be useful in Great League when built around its shield pressure and appropriate charged moves. League rules and current rankings should always be checked because the PvP meta changes over time.

Ultra League

Charizard has historically been more comfortable in Ultra League than in Great League when properly powered and equipped with the right moveset.

Master League

Regular Charizard generally faces tougher competition in Master League because of its lower maximum stat product compared with many Legendary and high-stat Pokémon.

27 • FAQ

Charizard FAQ

What type is Charizard?

Regular Charizard is Fire/Flying type.

What is Charizard weak to?

Charizard is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock is particularly dangerous because of Charizard's double weakness to Rock.

Is Shiny Charizard available?

Yes. Shiny Charizard is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Charizard good?

Shadow Charizard can be useful for players who prioritize offensive damage, although its reduced durability and resource requirements should be considered.

Should I purify Shadow Charizard?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

What is a 100% Charizard?

A 100% Charizard has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Charizard?

Lucky Charizard is obtained through a Lucky Trade and benefits from the reduced Stardust cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is XXL Charizard?

XXL Charizard is an extra-large size variant. Size is separate from IVs and does not automatically make Charizard stronger in battle.

What is XXS Charizard?

XXS Charizard is an extra-small size variant. Like XXL, its size designation is separate from its battle IVs.

What is Mega Charizard X?

Mega Charizard X is Charizard's Fire/Dragon Mega form.

What is Mega Charizard Y?

Mega Charizard Y is Charizard's Fire/Flying Mega form and is particularly useful for Fire-focused battle roles.

Which is better, Mega Charizard X or Y?

For a Fire-focused raid role, Mega Charizard Y is generally the more natural choice. Mega Charizard X has a different Fire/Dragon identity and can be preferred when that typing fits the matchup.

Can Shiny Charizard Mega Evolve?

Yes. An eligible Shiny Charizard can Mega Evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.

Can Shadow Charizard Mega Evolve?

Special-form interactions should be checked against the current Pokémon GO rules before treating Shadow Charizard as eligible for Mega Evolution.

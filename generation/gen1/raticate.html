09 • IV GUIDE

Raticate IV Guide

IVs add individual Attack, Defense and HP points to Raticate. A 15/15/15 specimen is considered a perfect IV Raticate.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Is a 100% Raticate?

A 100% Raticate has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a perfect or hundo Raticate.

100% Raticate

IV combination: 15 / 15 / 15

Is a 100% Raticate Worth Keeping?

Yes, particularly for collectors, Pokédex completion, perfect-IV collections and personal goals. Its perfect IV status does not automatically make Raticate a top raid attacker.

10 • PVP

Raticate PvP Guide

Raticate's low maximum CP makes Great League the most natural format to consider. However, its current PvP performance is limited compared with stronger options.

Great League

Great League is the format where regular Raticate can be considered most naturally because of the 1,500 CP limit. Its Quick Attack and charged-move options provide some flexibility, but it remains a niche choice.

Ultra League

Raticate's low CP ceiling makes Ultra League a poor natural environment for the standard form.

Master League

Raticate is not a practical Master League Pokémon because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for this format.

Shadow Raticate Moves

Should You Purify Shadow Raticate?

Purification is a personal investment decision. Do not assume that purification is automatically better simply because it increases the appraisal values.

  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions.
  • Consider whether you want a Shadow collection.
  • Check the current Frustration rules.
  • Consider the Candy and Stardust investment.
  • Keep a strong Shadow specimen if you collect Shadow Pokémon.

Raticate FAQ

What type is Raticate?

Regular Kanto Raticate is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

Raticate is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

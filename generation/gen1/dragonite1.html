Dragonite

Dragonite is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, Dragonite is a versatile Pokémon with strong Dragon-type options, significant Master League relevance and several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS.

Why Dragonite's Typing Matters

Dragonite's Dragon/Flying typing gives it access to powerful Dragon-focused attacks while also providing useful resistances. However, the Flying secondary typing creates an important weakness to Ice-type attacks.

09 • PVP

Dragonite PvP Guide

Dragonite has long been an important Pokémon to consider in Master League formats because of its high overall stats, Dragon typing and access to efficient charged-move pressure.

Master League

Master League is Dragonite's most natural traditional GO Battle League environment. Dragon Breath can apply consistent pressure while Dragon Claw can create shield-management situations.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Dragonite is generally more naturally suited to higher-CP formats. Special cups and limited formats can change this recommendation, so always check the current league rules.

  • Check Dragonite's IV spread for the intended league.
  • Consider Candy XL requirements for high-level builds.
  • Compare Dragonite with other current Dragon types.
  • Learn when to shield against Fairy and Ice coverage.

    23 • FAQ

    Dragonite FAQ

    What type is Dragonite?

    Dragonite is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon.

    What is Dragonite weak to?

    Dragonite is weak to Ice, Dragon and Fairy attacks. Its Dragon/Flying typing makes Ice particularly dangerous.

    Is Dragonite good for raids?

    Dragonite can be useful in raids when its Dragon or Flying attacks match the boss's weaknesses, although the current attacker landscape should always be considered.

    Is Dragonite good in Master League?

    Yes. Dragonite has historically been a relevant Master League Pokémon thanks to its high stats, Dragon typing and efficient moves.

    Is Shiny Dragonite available?

    Yes. Shiny Dragonite is an available Shiny variant through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

    What is Shadow Dragonite?

    Shadow Dragonite is Dragonite with the Shadow Pokémon status and associated Shadow battle mechanics.

    Should I purify Shadow Dragonite?

    Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your intended battle role and collection goals before purifying.

    What is Lucky Dragonite?

    Lucky Dragonite is a Dragonite with Lucky status, generally obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

    What is a 100% Dragonite?

    A 100% Dragonite has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

    What is XXL Dragonite?

    XXL Dragonite is an unusually large individual Dragonite recorded as an XXL size variant.

    What is XXS Dragonite?

    XXS Dragonite is an unusually small individual Dragonite recorded as an XXS size variant.

    Does Dragonite evolve?

    Dragonite is the final stage of the Dratini evolutionary family and does not normally evolve into another standard Pokémon.

    What is the Dragonite evolution family?

    The family is Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite.

    Does Dragonite have a Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO?

    Do not treat Mega Dragonite as an active Pokémon GO form unless it has been officially introduced. The Mega section on this page is structured as a future database placeholder.

    Is a hundo Dragonite worth keeping?

    Yes. A 15/15/15 Dragonite is a perfect-IV specimen and can be a strong long-term collection or investment target.

    Should I power up Shadow or regular Dragonite?

    The answer depends on your goal. Shadow Dragonite can be attractive for offensive performance, while regular Dragonite can be easier to invest in and remains highly useful for many trainers.

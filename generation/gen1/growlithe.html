Why Growlithe's Typing Matters

Growlithe's pure Fire typing gives it useful offensive matchups against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel Pokémon. However, it also leaves Growlithe vulnerable to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Shadow Growlithe

Shadow Growlithe is the Shadow variation of the species and should be considered separately from regular Growlithe.

🌑 Shadow Growlithe

Shadow Growlithe retains Growlithe's Fire typing but has the different offensive and defensive characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

  • Fire typing remains unchanged
  • Higher offensive pressure
  • Lower defensive durability
  • Can be purified when eligible
  • Can evolve into Shadow Arcanine

Should You Evolve Shadow Growlithe?

Players interested in Shadow raid attackers may prefer to evaluate the evolved Shadow Arcanine before deciding whether to invest resources.

Purified Growlithe

Purified Growlithe is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Growlithe.

✨ Purified Growlithe

Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state into a Purified Pokémon. This can affect IVs, move access and investment decisions.

Dragonair

Dragonair is a Dragon-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the evolved form of Dratini. In Pokémon GO, Dragonair is best known as an important middle stage in the Dratini evolution family, while its different statuses and sizes make it an interesting collector's Pokémon.

Dragonair Overview

Dragonair is the second stage of the Dratini evolutionary family. It evolves from Dratini and can eventually evolve into Dragonite. Unlike Dragonite, Dragonair has a much lower CP ceiling, so its primary value is usually connected to evolution, collection, special Pokémon challenges and niche PvP situations.

Why Dragon Typing Matters

Dragonair's pure Dragon typing gives it the offensive and defensive characteristics associated with Dragon-type Pokémon. Its main weaknesses are Dragon, Fairy and Ice, while it resists several common elemental attack types.

XXL Dragonair

XXL Dragonair is an exceptionally large individual Dragonair. XXL is a size classification and does not represent a new Pokémon species or evolutionary form.

📏

XXL Dragonair

A Dragonair with an exceptionally large recorded size. Size classification is separate from IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

A Dragonair can be XXL without having perfect IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Dragonair can have any IV combination.

XXS Dragonair

XXS Dragonair is an exceptionally small individual Dragonair. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification rather than a separate form of Dragonair.

📐

XXS Dragonair

A Dragonair with an exceptionally small recorded size. XXS status is independent of IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

XXS and IVs

An XXS Dragonair can have low, average or perfect IVs. Size and IV quality are separate attributes.

08 • IV GUIDE

100% IV Krabby

A perfect Krabby has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These specimens are commonly kept by collectors and players who want a perfect evolution into Kingler.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Krabby

A 15/15/15 Krabby is a 100% IV Pokémon. If you plan to evolve it into Kingler, a perfect Krabby can be a useful long-term collection and evolution target.

100% Krabby Tip

Before transferring a high-IV Krabby, check the appraisal carefully. A perfect specimen can be valuable even when the Pokémon is not currently part of your active battle team.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Krabby

Lucky Krabby is a special trade outcome that has a sparkling background and reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

🍀

Why Keep a Lucky Krabby?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Unique Lucky background and collection status.
  • Can be evolved into a Lucky Kingler.
  • Useful for Lucky Pokédex completion.
  • Can be especially interesting when combined with strong IVs.
13 • XXL

XXL Krabby

XXL Krabby is an unusually large size specimen. Pokémon GO size categories are separate from IV quality, so a large Krabby does not automatically have perfect IVs.

🏆 XXL Krabby

A large Krabby that qualifies for the XXL size category.

Size ≠ IV

XXL status and IV percentage are independent. An XXL Krabby can have any IV combination.

Should You Keep XXL Krabby?

Yes, if you care about size records, showcases, collection goals or simply want a large specimen. Check its IVs separately before transferring it.

14 • XXS

XXS Krabby

XXS Krabby is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS classification is separate from IV quality.

🔹 XXS Krabby

A Krabby that qualifies for the XXS size category.

XXS Krabby IVs

Being XXS does not make Krabby a high-IV Pokémon. Always inspect the appraisal separately when deciding whether to keep or transfer it.

22 • FAQ

Krabby FAQ

What type is Krabby?

Krabby is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Krabby weak to?

Krabby is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Krabby evolve?

Yes. Krabby evolves into Kingler.

Is Shiny Krabby available?

Shiny Krabby is an available special variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Krabby?

A 100% Krabby has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Shadow Krabby?

Shadow Krabby is a Shadow variant of Krabby with Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Krabby?

Not automatically. Consider your collection, battle goals and the value of retaining its Shadow status before purifying.

What is Lucky Krabby?

Lucky Krabby is a Pokémon obtained through a Lucky Trade and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Krabby?

XXL Krabby is a Krabby that qualifies for the game's unusually large size category.

What is XXS Krabby?

XXS Krabby is a Krabby that qualifies for the unusually small size category.

Does XXL Krabby mean perfect IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Krabby can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Krabby mean low IV?

No. XXS status does not determine IV quality. Check the Pokémon's appraisal separately.

Is Krabby good for raids?

Krabby itself is generally less useful for raids than its evolution, Kingler. Players interested in battle performance should evaluate the evolved Pokémon as well.

Is Krabby good in PvP?

Krabby is not generally considered a top competitive PvP choice. Its low overall CP and stat profile limit its usefulness in the higher leagues.

Why Articuno's Typing Matters

Articuno's Ice/Flying combination gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates several important weaknesses. Rock attacks are especially dangerous because the Flying and Ice combination leaves Articuno with a major vulnerability to Rock-type damage.

08 — IV GUIDE

Articuno IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to a Pokémon's base stats. A 15/15/15 Articuno is considered a perfect IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

100% Articuno = 15 / 15 / 15

A perfect Articuno has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Hundo Articuno can be especially desirable for collectors and long-term investment.

Is a 100% Articuno Worth Keeping?

Yes. A perfect Articuno is an excellent collector's Pokémon and gives you the maximum possible IV values. Whether it deserves a large Stardust investment depends on your preferred battle format and available alternatives.

09 — PVP

Articuno PvP Guide

Articuno's defensive profile and access to Ice-type attacks give it potential PvP utility, especially when its typing and moves line up well against the current metagame.

Great League

Standard Articuno's high base stats and Legendary status make Great League use dependent on special availability and CP-limited situations. Check the current format rules before investing.

Ultra League

Master League

11 — SHADOW

Shadow Articuno

Shadow Articuno is a special Shadow variant of Articuno associated with Team GO Rocket content. It should be evaluated separately from standard Articuno because Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics.

🌑

Shadow Articuno

Shadow Articuno retains its Ice/Flying typing, while the Shadow mechanic changes its offensive and defensive battle profile.

Shadow Articuno Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Articuno's basic Ice/Flying typing. Its type-based weaknesses therefore remain Rock, Electric, Fire and Steel.

Shadow Articuno & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Rocket-related events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration, so players should check current event rules before spending resources.

Should You Purify Shadow Articuno?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Articuno simply to increase its IVs. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result and consider your goals before making the decision.

Investment reminder:

Shadow Pokémon generally require careful Stardust and Candy investment. Keep a strong Shadow Articuno if you value its unique battle profile or collection status.

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Articuno

Lucky Articuno is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

🍀

Lucky Articuno

A Lucky Articuno is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Its Lucky status is separate from Shiny, Shadow and Purified status.

🍀 Lucky Status Obtained through trading.
⭐ Minimum IVs Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed IV floor.
💰 Stardust Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Lucky Articuno vs 100% Articuno

A Lucky Articuno is not automatically a 100% Articuno. Lucky status and IV perfection are separate properties. A Lucky Articuno can have excellent IVs, but only 15/15/15 qualifies as a hundo.

22 — FAQ

Articuno FAQ

What type is Articuno?

Articuno is an Ice/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Articuno weak to?

Articuno is weak to Rock, Electric, Fire and Steel-type attacks. Rock is especially effective because Articuno has a double weakness to Rock.

What is Articuno's max CP?

Articuno's maximum CP is 3,452 under the standard Pokémon GO stat and level assumptions.

Is Shiny Articuno available?

Yes. Shiny Articuno is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Articuno available?

Shadow Articuno has appeared through Team GO Rocket content. Current availability depends on Pokémon GO's active Rocket and Legendary rotation.

Can Shadow Articuno be purified?

An eligible Shadow Articuno can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status, so compare both options before making the decision.

What is a 100% Articuno?

A 100% Articuno has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Articuno?

Lucky Articuno is an Articuno that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Is every Lucky Articuno a hundo?

No. Lucky status and IVs are separate properties. Only an Articuno with 15/15/15 IVs is a hundo.

What is an XXL Articuno?

XXL Articuno is an unusually large Articuno according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

What is an XXS Articuno?

XXS Articuno is an unusually small Articuno according to Pokémon GO's size classification system.

Does XXL Articuno have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Articuno can have any IV combination.

Does Articuno evolve?

No. Articuno is a standalone Legendary Pokémon and does not have a normal evolution.

Is Articuno good for raids?

Articuno can be useful in Ice-focused situations, but its relatively low Attack means players should compare it with stronger current Ice attackers.

Is Articuno good in PvP?

Articuno can have PvP utility depending on the league, moveset and current metagame. Its exact ranking can change as Pokémon GO updates moves and battle mechanics.

Can Shiny Articuno be Lucky?

Yes. A Pokémon can have multiple independent traits, so a Shiny Articuno can also become Lucky through trading.

Can Shadow Articuno be Shiny?

Special combinations such as Shadow and Shiny depend on the specific Pokémon GO encounter mechanics and availability at the time. Check the current encounter rules before claiming a particular combination is obtainable.

What is the best Articuno IV?

For a perfect specimen, the best IV combination is 15/15/15. However, the ideal PvP IV distribution can differ depending on the league and battle format.

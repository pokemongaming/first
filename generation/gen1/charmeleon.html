KANTO FIRE TYPE

Charmeleon

Charmeleon is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the middle stage of the Charmander evolutionary family. In Pokémon GO, Charmeleon is mainly useful as an evolution milestone, collector Pokémon and Great League option rather than a top-tier raid attacker. It is also available in special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky.

01 — OVERVIEW

Charmeleon Overview

Charmeleon is the second stage of the Charmander family. It evolves from Charmander and can evolve into Charizard. Unlike Charizard, Charmeleon does not have Mega Evolution of its own.

Charmeleon Type

Why Charmeleon's Typing Matters

Charmeleon's pure Fire typing gives it a straightforward offensive and defensive profile. Fire attacks are useful against Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel Pokémon, while Charmeleon itself takes increased damage from Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

06 — RAID PERFORMANCE

Is Charmeleon Good for Raids?

Charmeleon is not considered a top-tier Fire-type raid attacker. Its relatively modest CP and stats compared with fully evolved Fire attackers make Charizard and other strong Fire Pokémon better choices for serious PvE teams.

08 — IV GUIDE

Charmeleon IV Guide

What IVs Should You Keep?

  • Lucky Charmeleon with strong IVs can be useful for future evolution.
09 — PVP

Charmeleon PvP Guide

Charmeleon has considerably more relevance in Great League than in high-level PvE. Its CP allows it to fit within the 1,500 CP limit when appropriately powered.

Great League

Charmeleon can function as a Fire-type option in Great League. A lower Attack and higher Defense/HP IV distribution can sometimes be preferable because it may allow Charmeleon to reach a higher level while remaining below 1,500 CP.

Great League IV Example

A low-Attack / high-Defense / high-HP spread can be considered instead of automatically using a 15/15/15 Pokémon.

Ultra League

Charmeleon's lower maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural for the species compared with Pokémon specifically built for that format.

Master League

Charmeleon is generally not a natural Master League choice because its maximum CP is substantially lower than the requirements of the format.

11 — SHINY

Shiny Charmeleon

Shiny Charmeleon is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny form has an alternate coloration compared with standard Charmeleon.

✨ Shiny Charmeleon

Shiny Charmeleon has the same underlying species, typing, base stats and CP formula as regular Charmeleon. The primary difference is its alternate appearance.

Shiny Charmeleon for Collectors

  • Keep a Shiny Charmeleon if you like collecting alternate forms.
  • A Shiny Charmeleon can be evolved into Shiny Charizard.
  • Check IVs before evolving if you want a strong Shiny evolution.
  • Shiny status is retained when evolving.
12 — SHADOW

Shadow Charmeleon

Shadow Charmeleon is the Shadow variant of Charmeleon. It retains Charmeleon's Fire typing and underlying species stats, while the Shadow mechanic changes its battle profile.

😈 Shadow Charmeleon

Shadow Charmeleon is a separate investment from regular Charmeleon. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive damage and reduced defensive durability compared with their standard counterparts.

13 — PURIFIED

Purified Charmeleon

Purified Charmeleon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Charmeleon. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and available moves.

✨ Purified Charmeleon

Purified Charmeleon is no longer Shadow. It can therefore be considered separately from both regular and Shadow Charmeleon when deciding which Pokémon to keep.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Charmeleon

Lucky Charmeleon is a special status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for powering up, making Lucky Charmeleon particularly interesting for players planning a future Charizard investment.

🍀
Lucky Charmeleon

The Lucky status does not change Charmeleon's species or typing. Its primary practical benefit is reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

Why Keep a Lucky Charmeleon?

A 100% Charmeleon, commonly called a Hundo, has maximum IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

Is a 100% Charmeleon Rare?

Perfect IV Pokémon are much less common than ordinary specimens. A Hundo Charmeleon can therefore be valuable as a collector Pokémon, even if Charmeleon itself is not a top raid attacker.

Should You Evolve a Hundo Charmeleon?

20 — INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Charmeleon

Before Powering Up

  • Check whether the Charmeleon is regular, Shadow, Lucky or another special variant.
  • Check the IVs.
  • Check whether the Pokémon is Shiny, XXL or XXS.
  • Consider whether you want to evolve it into Charizard.
  • Check the current moveset before using Elite resources.

For Collectors

Collectors may want to keep one or more examples of every major Charmeleon category, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, Hundo, XXL and XXS.

For PvP Players

Do not automatically transfer low-Attack Charmeleon. Some lower-Attack and higher-Defense/HP combinations can be more interesting for CP-limited PvP formats.

For Future Charizard Projects

Because Charmeleon evolves into Charizard, a strong Shiny, Shadow, Lucky or Hundo Charmeleon can be worth keeping even when Charmeleon itself is not the intended final battle investment.

22 — FAQ

Charmeleon FAQ

What type is Charmeleon?

Charmeleon is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmeleon weak to?

Charmeleon is weak to Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

What is Charmeleon's max CP?

Charmeleon's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,868.

What are Charmeleon's base stats?

Charmeleon has 158 Attack, 126 Defense and 151 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Charmeleon available?

Yes. Shiny Charmeleon is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Charmeleon available?

Shadow Charmeleon exists in Pokémon GO, with availability depending on Team GO Rocket content and current rotations.

Can Shadow Charmeleon be purified?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Charmeleon can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and changes its associated mechanics.

Is Lucky Charmeleon useful?

Lucky Charmeleon can be useful because Lucky Pokémon receive a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is a 100% Charmeleon?

A 100% Charmeleon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a Hundo.

What is an XXL Charmeleon?

An XXL Charmeleon is an unusually large individual. XXL is a size classification rather than a separate species.

What is an XXS Charmeleon?

An XXS Charmeleon is an unusually small individual. XXS is a size classification rather than a separate species.

Is Charmeleon good for raids?

Charmeleon is generally not a top-tier Fire raid attacker. Fully evolved Fire Pokémon are normally stronger choices for serious raid teams.

Is Charmeleon good in Great League?

Charmeleon can have some Great League relevance because its maximum CP allows it to fit within the 1,500 CP limit. The best IV spread depends on the specific PvP build.

Should I evolve my Hundo Charmeleon?

If your goal is a perfect Charizard collection or future Charizard investment, evolving a Hundo Charmeleon can be a strong option. Check the current Charizard moves and your intended role first.

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, especially if you value Shadow battle performance or collection rarity.

Can Lucky Charmeleon evolve?

Yes. A Lucky Charmeleon can evolve into a Lucky Charizard.

Can XXL Charmeleon evolve?

Yes. An XXL Charmeleon can be evolved, although the size characteristics of the resulting Pokémon should be checked against the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Can XXS Charmeleon evolve?

Yes. An XXS Charmeleon can be evolved. Size-related characteristics should be verified against current game rules.

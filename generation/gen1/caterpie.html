01 · OVERVIEW

Caterpie Overview

Caterpie is a pure Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It is one of the earliest Pokémon encountered in the Kanto Pokédex and evolves into Metapod before reaching its final evolution, Butterfree.

In Pokémon GO, Caterpie is not considered a major raid attacker or high-level PvP specialist. Its main value comes from evolution, Candy collection, Pokédex completion, Shiny hunting and its place in the classic Kanto evolutionary family.

Why Caterpie's Typing Matters

Caterpie is a pure Bug-type Pokémon, so its type matchups are considerably simpler than those of dual-type Pokémon.

Bug-type Pokémon generally perform well against Psychic, Dark and Grass types, while Caterpie itself is vulnerable to Fire, Flying and Rock attacks.

Perfect Caterpie

A 15/15/15 Caterpie is a perfect-IV specimen. Because Caterpie has limited battle value, a perfect specimen is generally more interesting as a collector's Pokémon or as an evolution project.

Should You Keep High-IV Caterpie?

Keeping a high-IV Caterpie can make sense if you want to evolve it into Metapod and Butterfree or maintain a strong specimen for your collection. Avoid spending significant Stardust on Caterpie unless you have a specific personal or collection goal.

09 · PVP

Caterpie PvP Guide

Caterpie has very limited PvP value because its stats and CP ceiling are far below the requirements normally associated with competitive Pokémon GO battles.

Great League

Caterpie can technically be used below the Great League CP limit, but its low stats make it an extremely unconventional choice. It is generally outclassed by stronger Bug-type and other low-CP Pokémon.

Ultra League

Caterpie's maximum CP is far below the Ultra League limit, making it impractical for serious Ultra League competition.

Master League

Caterpie is not a realistic Master League Pokémon. Its maximum CP and stat profile are nowhere near the requirements of the format.

PvP recommendation:

Caterpie is best treated as a collection and evolution Pokémon rather than a competitive PvP investment.

Should You Purify Shadow Caterpie?

There is usually little reason to purify Shadow Caterpie solely for battle performance because Caterpie itself has limited competitive value. The decision is better based on collection goals, IVs, future evolution plans and whether you want to preserve the Shadow version.

  • Check the Shadow appraisal before purifying.
  • Consider evolving the Shadow Pokémon before transferring it.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Keep a Shadow specimen if you collect Shadow evolutionary families.
  • Do not purify simply because the appraisal improves.

13 · EVOLUTION

Caterpie Evolution Line

Caterpie is the first stage of a three-stage evolutionary family. It evolves into Metapod and then into Butterfree.

Caterpie Bug
→
Metapod Bug
→
Butterfree Bug / Flying

Caterpie Evolution Cost

Caterpie requires 12 Caterpie Candy to evolve into Metapod. Metapod then requires 50 Caterpie Candy to evolve into Butterfree.

Evolution Tip

Caterpie is a useful Pokémon to collect in large numbers when you are farming XP through evolution events or Lucky Egg sessions.

Shiny Evolution

Evolving a Shiny Caterpie produces a Shiny Metapod and eventually a Shiny Butterfree. The Shiny status is preserved through the evolution process.

Shadow Evolution

Eligible Shadow Caterpie can be evolved into Shadow Metapod and eventually Shadow Butterfree. Shadow status remains through evolution unless the Pokémon is purified.

14 · RESOURCES

Caterpie Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Caterpie is inexpensive to evolve compared with many Pokémon, making it useful for Candy farming and evolution-focused XP strategies.

  • Catch Caterpie whenever you are building Candy.
  • Use Pinap Berries when Candy farming.
  • Keep Shiny Caterpie separate from normal specimens.
  • Check IVs before evolving your best specimens.
  • Save strong specimens if you want a Butterfree collection.
  • Transfer low-value duplicates when storage becomes limited.

Caterpie Candy Strategy

Because Caterpie is an early-stage Pokémon with relatively low evolution requirements, it can be an easy target for Candy accumulation. Catching multiple specimens during events can quickly provide enough Candy to complete the entire evolutionary family.

16 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Caterpie

For Raids

Caterpie should generally not be used as a raid attacker. Its low Attack and CP ceiling make it dramatically weaker than evolved Bug-type attackers.

For PvP

Caterpie is primarily a novelty pick rather than a competitive PvP Pokémon. If used in a low-CP challenge, its Bug typing gives it resistance to Grass, Fighting and Ground attacks.

For Evolution

Evolution is Caterpie's most practical gameplay purpose. Build enough Candy to evolve it into Metapod and then Butterfree.

For Collection

Collectors may want normal, Shiny and Shadow versions, as well as their evolved forms. A strong-IV specimen can also be kept as a long-term collection piece.

17 · COLLECTION

Caterpie Collection Guide

Caterpie has more collection value than battle value. A completed Caterpie collection can include different IV combinations, Shiny variants, Shadow variants and the complete evolutionary family.

  • Keep one strong normal Caterpie.
  • Keep a Shiny Caterpie if you obtain one.
  • Keep Shadow Caterpie if you collect Shadow families.
  • Consider evolving one specimen into Metapod.
  • Consider completing the family with Butterfree.
  • Keep special event Caterpie when applicable.
Collector tip:

Do not automatically transfer every low-CP Caterpie. A low-stat specimen can still have collection value if it is Shiny, Shadow, event-related or otherwise unusual.

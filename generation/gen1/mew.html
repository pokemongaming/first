Mew Type

Psychic Primary Type

Why Mew's Typing Matters

Mew's pure Psychic typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile while its unusually broad move pool gives it much more offensive flexibility than a typical Psychic-type Pokémon.

The Psychic typing also gives Mew useful resistance to Fighting- and Psychic-type attacks, while Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks are its primary weaknesses.

Mew can be used in raids because of its broad move selection, but it is generally better known for its versatility and PvP applications than for being a top-tier specialist raid attacker.

08 — IV GUIDE

Mew IV Guide

Individual Values add additional Attack, Defense and HP points to Mew. A 15/15/15 Mew is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV

Perfect Mew

A 15/15/15 Mew is a perfect-IV Mew. However, IV perfection is not automatically the most important factor when choosing a Mew for PvP.

PvP IV Considerations

Mew's ideal IV distribution can depend on the league, CP target and specific moveset. Before investing large amounts of Stardust or Candy XL, evaluate the exact build you want rather than assuming 15/15/15 is always the optimal PvP spread.

09 — PVP

Mew PvP Guide

PvP is one of the most interesting places to use Mew. Its extremely broad move pool makes its moveset difficult for opponents to predict.

Great League

Mew can be interesting in Great League when a player builds around a suitable low-CP specimen. Its value comes primarily from move flexibility and unpredictable coverage.

Ultra League

Mew can also be used in Ultra League. Shadow Claw, Wild Charge and Psyshock are among the combinations that can give Mew significant pressure and coverage.

Master League

Mew can participate in Master League, although its relatively modest maximum CP compared with the highest stat-heavy Master League Pokémon can limit its overall role.

Example Flexible PvP Build

Shadow Claw + Wild Charge + Psyshock

PvP warning:

Mew has many viable move combinations. A "best" moveset can change according to league rules, team composition and the current GO Battle League metagame.

15 — RESOURCES

Mew Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Mew requires long-term resource planning if you want to build it for high-level PvP. Because Mew is a Mythical Pokémon and does not evolve, its Candy is primarily used for powering up and unlocking additional moves.

Mew Investment Checklist

  • Decide whether the Mew is intended for PvP or collection.
  • Check the available moves before investing heavily.
  • Save Stardust for the complete build.
  • Consider Candy XL requirements for high-level PvP.
  • Do not automatically transfer a low-IV Mew.
16 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Mew

Before PvP

  • Decide which league you want to target.
  • Check the CP limit for your intended league.
  • Select a fast move that fits your team.
  • Choose two Charged Moves with complementary coverage.
  • Test the build before spending large amounts of Stardust.

During Team Building

Mew works best when its unusual move coverage fills a specific role. Avoid building Mew simply because one moveset is popular; its enormous move pool means team composition matters.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Mew can remain interesting for a long time because its available move combinations give it flexibility as the PvP environment changes.

19 — FAQ

Mew FAQ

Is Mew a Legendary Pokémon?

Mew is classified as a Mythical Pokémon rather than a Legendary Pokémon.

What type is Mew?

Mew is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Mew weak to?

Mew is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

What does Mew resist?

Mew's Psychic typing gives it resistance to Fighting- and Psychic-type attacks.

What is Mew's max CP?

Mew reaches a maximum CP of 3,691 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Mew's stats?

Mew has 210 Attack, 210 Defense and 225 HP.

Does Mew evolve?

No. Mew does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Mew available?

Yes. Shiny Mew has been made available through eligible Pokémon GO research opportunities.

Is Mew good for PvP?

Mew can be useful in PvP because of its huge move pool and unpredictable coverage. Its effectiveness depends heavily on league, moveset and team composition.

Is Mew good for raids?

Mew is usable in raids but is generally more interesting for its flexibility and PvP applications than as a top-tier specialist raid attacker.

What is Mew's best PvP moveset?

Shadow Claw with Wild Charge and Psyshock is one useful example of a flexible PvP-oriented build, although the best combination depends on the current league and team.

Does Mew have a Mega Evolution?

Mew does not have a standard Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO.

Does Mew have a Primal form?

No. Primal Reversion is not a Mew mechanic.

Does Mew have a Shadow form?

There is no standard Shadow Mew form that should be treated as an ordinary available Pokémon GO build.

How far must I walk Mew for Candy?

Mew has a 20 km Buddy Candy distance.

How much does Mew's second Charged Move cost?

Mew's second Charged Move costs 100 Candy and 100,000 Stardust.

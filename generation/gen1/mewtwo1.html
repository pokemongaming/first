Mewtwo

01 — OVERVIEW

Mewtwo Overview

Mewtwo is a pure Psychic-type Legendary Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. It does not have a normal evolution chain, but Pokémon GO has special forms including Shadow Mewtwo and the Mega Evolutions Mega Mewtwo X and Mega Mewtwo Y.

Why Mewtwo's Typing Matters

Mewtwo's pure Psychic typing gives it a strong offensive identity against Fighting- and Poison-type opponents. At the same time, Dark-, Ghost- and Bug-type attacks can threaten it.

Shadow Mewtwo and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. Whether and when Frustration can be removed depends on Pokémon GO events and current Team GO Rocket mechanics.

Should You Purify Shadow Mewtwo?

Purification is a strategic choice rather than an automatic upgrade. If your primary goal is maximizing Shadow raid damage, carefully compare the Shadow specimen with the purified result before purifying it.

  • Compare the Shadow IVs.
  • Check the current moveset.
  • Consider the Stardust and Candy investment.
  • Think about whether you already have strong Psychic attackers.
  • Do not purify solely because the purified appraisal improves.
12 — PURIFIED

Purified Mewtwo

Purified Mewtwo is created when an eligible Shadow Mewtwo is purified. Purification changes its status and appraisal while removing the Shadow mechanic.

🕊️
Purified Status

Shadow status is removed.

⭐
IV Changes

Purification increases each IV by the applicable amount, up to the IV cap.

⚡
Resource Benefits

Purified Pokémon can have different investment requirements.

Investment warning:

A Shadow Mewtwo and a Purified Mewtwo should not automatically be considered interchangeable. Decide based on your intended raid, PvP or collection goals.

Is Lucky Mewtwo Worth Keeping?

Yes. A Lucky Mewtwo can be an attractive long-term investment because the Lucky status reduces Stardust costs for power-ups. This can make a strong Lucky Mewtwo especially appealing for players building multiple high-level attackers.

20 — RESOURCES

Mewtwo Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

High-level Mewtwo investment can require a significant amount of Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Prioritize specimens that fit your long-term goals.

  • Keep high-IV Mewtwo for long-term investment.
  • Save Candy XL for serious level-up projects.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite TMs.
  • Consider Lucky Mewtwo for Stardust-efficient investment.
  • Compare Shadow and regular Mewtwo before investing heavily.
  • Consider Mega Energy requirements separately.
21 — AVAILABILITY

Mewtwo Availability

Mewtwo availability in Pokémon GO is controlled by events, raids, research and other special rotations. Shadow Mewtwo availability is tied to Team GO Rocket content and related events.

22 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Mewtwo

Before a Raid

  • Check the raid boss's weaknesses.
  • Confirm Mewtwo's moveset.
  • Check its level and IVs.
  • Consider weather and team composition.
  • Compare regular and Shadow Mewtwo.

During Team Building

Mewtwo should be selected according to the matchup rather than simply because it has a high Attack stat. The best build depends on the target's typing, moveset, weather and the role you want Mewtwo to fill.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Mewtwo can remain useful for a long time, particularly when paired with a strong legacy or event moveset.

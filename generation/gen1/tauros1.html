Tauros Overview

Tauros is a pure Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Unlike many Pokémon, Tauros does not evolve into or from another Pokémon in its standard Kanto form.

Its Pokémon GO profile is more interesting than its basic Pokédex entry suggests because players can encounter different ownership and collection states including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky Tauros.

Why Tauros's Typing Matters

Tauros is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Normal-type Pokémon generally have a straightforward defensive profile, with their most important weakness being Fighting-type damage.

Its Normal typing also means Tauros does not receive the same offensive STAB opportunities as a dual-type Pokémon, so its usefulness depends heavily on its available moves and the battle format.

XXL Tauros

XXL Tauros is a size classification rather than a different species or form. Its size can make it valuable for collectors and size-related medals or gameplay systems.

XXL Extra-large size classification
Collection Useful for size-focused collections and personal milestones.

Does XXL Change Tauros's Type?

No. XXL status does not turn Tauros into another type. Tauros remains a Normal-type Pokémon.

Does XXL Mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Tauros can have any IV combination.

Collector Tip

A particularly desirable collection specimen could combine XXL size with Shiny, Lucky or perfect-IV characteristics.

XXS Tauros

XXS Tauros is the opposite size classification to XXL. It represents an exceptionally small specimen rather than a different species or form.

XXS Extra-small size classification
Collector Value Particularly interesting for players collecting unusual size records.

Does XXS Change Tauros's Stats?

XXS is primarily a size classification and should not be treated as an alternative Pokémon form. IVs, species identity and type remain separate from the size label.

XXS + Shiny + Hundo

A Tauros combining XXS size, Shiny status and perfect IVs would be an especially notable collection specimen.

Tauros FAQ

What type is Tauros?

Tauros is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Tauros weak to?

Tauros is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Tauros a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Tauros is a standard Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1.

Does Tauros evolve?

The standard Tauros species does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Tauros available?

Yes. Shiny Tauros is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Tauros?

Shadow Tauros is the Shadow variant of Tauros and retains its Normal typing.

Should I purify Shadow Tauros?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your collection and battle goals.

What is Lucky Tauros?

Lucky Tauros is a Tauros that has received Lucky status through an eligible trade.

What is a 100% Tauros?

A 100% Tauros has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Tauros?

XXL Tauros is an extra-large size classification. It is not a separate species.

What is XXS Tauros?

XXS Tauros is an extra-small size classification rather than a different Pokémon species.

Can Tauros be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes, a Tauros can combine different collection characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky status when the relevant game mechanics allow it.

Can Tauros be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Tauros can also have perfect IVs.

Can Tauros be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size classification and Shiny status are separate characteristics.

Is Tauros good for raids?

Tauros is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker, so expensive investment should be compared with stronger alternatives.

Is Tauros good in PvP?

Its PvP usefulness depends on league, moveset and the current metagame. It is generally a specialty rather than universal PvP choice.

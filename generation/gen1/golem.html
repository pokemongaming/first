Golem Overview

Golem is the final evolution of the Geodude evolutionary family. It is a dual Rock/Ground Pokémon and was introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, Golem can be encountered in several collectible variants, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS versions.

Why Golem's Typing Matters

Golem's Rock/Ground combination gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates several significant weaknesses. Water and Grass are particularly dangerous, while its typing provides useful resistance against several common attack categories.

Lucky Golem

Lucky Golem is a special Pokémon obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀 Lucky Golem

Lucky status is particularly useful for trainers planning to power up Golem because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

Lucky Golem Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term collection projects.
  • Guaranteed minimum IV floor applies to Lucky Pokémon.
  • Can be useful when building a powered-up Pokémon without spending as much Stardust.
  • Lucky status remains separate from Shiny status.

Lucky + Shiny Golem

Does XXL or XXS Change Golem's IVs?

Size classification and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Golem can have poor IVs, while an XXS Golem can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Size ≠ IV

XXL and XXS describe size, while IVs describe additional Attack, Defense and HP values.

Golem FAQ

What type is Golem?

Golem is a Rock/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Golem weak to?

Golem is vulnerable to several attack types, including Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Ice and Steel.

Does Golem evolve?

Golem is the final evolution of Geodude and Graveler.

Is Shiny Golem available?

Yes. Shiny Golem is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution paths.

What is a 100% Golem?

A 100% Golem has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Shadow Golem?

Shadow Golem is the Shadow variant of Golem. It retains the Rock/Ground typing while having the standard Shadow battle characteristics.

Should I purify Shadow Golem?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow Golem's moves, IVs, cost and your intended use before choosing to purify it.

What is a Lucky Golem?

Lucky Golem is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

Can Golem be XXL?

Yes. Individual Golem can have XXL size classifications.

Can Golem be XXS?

Yes. Individual Golem can also have an XXS size classification.

Does XXL Golem have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Golem can have any IV combination.

Is Golem good for raids?

Golem can provide Rock or Ground damage, but trainers should compare it with the current strongest attackers before making major investments.

Is Golem good in PvP?

Golem can have situational PvP use, depending on the league, moveset, IVs and current metagame.

Can a Golem be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Golem can have both statuses.

Can a Golem be 100% and Lucky?

Yes. A Lucky Golem can have perfect IVs, although Lucky Pokémon have their own minimum IV floor.

Can a Golem be Shiny, Lucky and 100%?

Yes. Shiny status, Lucky status and IV perfection are independent characteristics.

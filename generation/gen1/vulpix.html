Vulpix

Vulpix is a Fire-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first stage of the Vulpix evolution family, with regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants possible depending on how it was obtained.

Why Vulpix's Typing Matters

Vulpix's pure Fire typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. Fire attacks are resisted by several common Pokémon types, while Water, Ground and Rock attacks are especially effective against Vulpix.

08 — IV GUIDE

Vulpix IV Guide

Individual Values provide additional Attack, Defense and HP points. A 15/15/15 Vulpix is considered a perfect IV specimen.

Is 100% IV Vulpix Rare?

A 100% IV Vulpix has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. Because Vulpix is not normally a major raid attacker, a perfect specimen is often more interesting to collectors, evolution projects and Pokédex enthusiasts.

Perfect IV Definition

15 Attack + 15 Defense + 15 HP = 100% IV.

09 — PVP

Vulpix PvP Guide

Vulpix has limited overall PvP relevance compared with many other Pokémon, but specialty cups can change the value of individual Pokémon.

Great League

Vulpix can fit within the Great League CP limit, but its overall performance depends heavily on the current cup rules, moveset and opponent pool.

Ultra League

Master League

10 — EVOLUTION

Vulpix evolves into Ninetales. In Pokémon GO, Candy is used for the evolution rather than the traditional main-series Fire Stone method.

Kanto Vulpix vs Alolan Vulpix

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Vulpix

Lucky Vulpix is a special trading outcome in Pokémon GO. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Obtained through trading.
💰 Reduced Cost Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
🎲 Better IV Floor Lucky trades have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary trades.

Lucky Vulpix IVs

A Lucky Vulpix is not automatically a 100% IV Pokémon. However, Lucky trades provide a higher minimum IV floor, making them attractive for players looking for a strong specimen without relying entirely on random wild IVs.

Collector Tip

A Lucky Shiny Vulpix can be a particularly attractive collection piece because it combines two special attributes in one Pokémon.

Does XXL Increase CP?

21 — INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Vulpix

For New Players

Vulpix can be a useful early Fire-type addition when available, especially for players building their first collection. However, its low maximum CP means that players should avoid spending excessive Stardust on it without a specific reason.

For Collectors

Collectors may want to keep Shiny, Lucky, Shadow, Purified, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Vulpix separately. These attributes can make otherwise ordinary catches much more interesting.

Before Transferring Vulpix

  • Check the appraisal.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Check its size classification.
  • Compare it with your existing Vulpix collection.

23 — FAQ

Vulpix FAQ

What type is Vulpix?

Kanto Vulpix is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Vulpix weak to?

Vulpix is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What is Vulpix's Pokédex number?

Vulpix is Pokédex number #037.

What generation is Vulpix from?

Vulpix was introduced in Generation 1 and is associated with the Kanto region.

Does Vulpix evolve?

Yes. Vulpix evolves into Ninetales.

Is Shiny Vulpix available?

Yes. Shiny Vulpix is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Vulpix available?

Shadow Vulpix is a Shadow variant of the species when offered through eligible Pokémon GO mechanics.

Can Shadow Vulpix be purified?

An eligible Shadow Vulpix can be purified. Purification is a permanent decision.

What is a Lucky Vulpix?

Lucky Vulpix is a Vulpix obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic.

What is a 100% IV Vulpix?

What is XXL Vulpix?

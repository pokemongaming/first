13 — SIZE

XXL Sandshrew

XXL Sandshrew is a size variant that records an unusually large specimen. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

📏 XXL Sandshrew

An XXL Sandshrew can be valuable for collectors who track Pokémon size records.

IVs Are Separate

An XXL Sandshrew can have high, low or perfect IVs. Size and IV appraisal are separate characteristics.

Collector Tip

If you collect size records, consider keeping unusual XXL Sandshrew even when the IVs are not particularly strong.

14 — SIZE

XXS Sandshrew

XXS Sandshrew is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than an IV rating.

📏 XXS Sandshrew

An XXS Sandshrew can be kept as a rare size-based collection entry.

Size vs IV

XXS status does not automatically indicate poor or excellent IVs.

21 — FAQ

Sandshrew FAQ

What type is Sandshrew?

Regular Sandshrew is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Sandshrew weak to?

Sandshrew is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Does Sandshrew evolve?

Yes. Sandshrew evolves into Sandslash using Sandshrew Candy.

Is Shiny Sandshrew available?

Shiny Sandshrew is an available Shiny variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Sandshrew?

Shadow Sandshrew is Sandshrew with Shadow status, obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters.

Should I purify Shadow Sandshrew?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's collection and battle value before purifying it.

How do I get Lucky Sandshrew?

Lucky Pokémon are obtained through trading. The exact probability of a Lucky outcome depends on Pokémon GO's current trade mechanics.

What is a 100% IV Sandshrew?

A 100% IV Sandshrew has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Sandshrew better than normal Sandshrew?

XXL status describes size and does not automatically make Sandshrew stronger in battle.

Is XXS Sandshrew rare?

XXS is a size category that can be valuable to collectors who track unusually small Pokémon.

Does Lucky mean 100% IV?

No. Lucky status and IVs are separate properties. A Lucky Sandshrew is not automatically a 100% IV Sandshrew.

Is Sandshrew good for raids?

Sandshrew is generally not considered a top-tier Ground raid attacker. Players looking for stronger PvE performance should consider its evolution and other Ground-type options.

Should I keep a 100% Sandshrew?

Yes, especially if you collect perfect IV Pokémon or want to evolve a perfect Sandshrew into Sandslash.

Can a Shiny Sandshrew be Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon GO statuses can overlap, so a Pokémon can have more than one collection characteristic, subject to the game's current rules.

Can Sandshrew be Shadow and Shiny?

Special Pokémon characteristics should be checked against the current Pokémon GO availability and encounter rules. Do not assume every combination is obtainable simply because the individual characteristics exist.

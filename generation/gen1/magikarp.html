01 • OVERVIEW

Magikarp Overview

Magikarp is a Water-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and Pokédex #129. In Pokémon GO, its main purpose is not direct combat but evolution into Gyarados. Magikarp requires 400 Magikarp Candy to evolve, making Candy collection one of the most important parts of building this Pokémon family.

Why Magikarp's Typing Matters

Magikarp is a pure Water-type Pokémon. Its Water typing determines the attacks that deal increased damage to it and the types it can resist. However, Magikarp's very low Attack and extremely limited moveset make typing much less important for battle performance than it is for its evolved form, Gyarados.

04 • STATS

Magikarp Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

Magikarp has extremely low offensive statistics. Its 29 base Attack is one of the clearest reasons that Magikarp is not intended to be a serious raid attacker. Its 85 Defense and 85 HP provide more durability than its Attack suggests, but the Pokémon remains primarily an evolution project.

05 • MOVES

Magikarp Best Moves

Magikarp has an intentionally unusual moveset. Splash does no damage, while Struggle is its normal Charged Move option.

06 • RAID VALUE

Magikarp Raid Performance

Magikarp is not a meaningful raid attacker. Its extremely low Attack stat and unusual moveset make it unsuitable for normal PvE investment.

⚔️ Raid Attacker

Not recommended.

🧬 Evolution Value

Extremely high because it evolves into Gyarados.

🎯 Collector Value

High for Shiny, Shadow and high-IV specimens.

Should You Power Up Magikarp?

For almost every player, powering up Magikarp before evolution is unnecessary. Save your Stardust and Candy for Gyarados unless you have a specific collection, challenge or Little Cup-related reason to keep a powered-up Magikarp.

07 • COUNTERS

What Counters Magikarp?

Magikarp is weak to Electric and Grass attacks. Because Magikarp has such low battle power, almost any appropriately leveled super-effective attacker can defeat it easily.

⚡

🌿
Grass Attackers

🐟
Practical Tip

There is normally no reason to build a dedicated counter team specifically for Magikarp.

08 • IV GUIDE

Magikarp IV Guide

IVs become more interesting because Magikarp evolves into Gyarados. Instead of judging a Magikarp only by its current CP, consider what you want to do with the eventual Gyarados.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Magikarp

A 15/15/15 Magikarp is a perfect-IV specimen. For many players, a high-IV Magikarp is worth keeping because its eventual Gyarados can become a long-term investment.

IV Tip

Before transferring a high-IV Magikarp, check its appraisal and consider whether you want to evolve it into Gyarados.

09 • EVOLUTION

Magikarp Evolution — Gyarados

Magikarp has one of the most famous evolution requirements in Pokémon GO. It requires 400 Magikarp Candy to evolve into Gyarados.

Best Way to Collect Magikarp Candy

  • Catch Magikarp whenever you encounter them.
  • Use Pinap or Silver Pinap Berries when appropriate.
  • Walk Magikarp as your Buddy when useful.
  • Transfer unwanted Magikarp for additional Candy.
  • Take advantage of events that increase Magikarp spawns.
  • Consider Mega Evolution bonuses when catching Water-type Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Magikarp?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Magikarp simply because its appraisal can improve after purification. If your goal is to eventually build Shadow Gyarados, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be more valuable. Consider the role you want the evolved Gyarados to perform before making the purification decision.

Important:

Shadow Magikarp is much more interesting as an evolution project than as a standalone battle Pokémon. If you obtain a strong Shadow Magikarp, consider its future Shadow Gyarados potential before transferring it.

16 • PVP

Magikarp PvP Guide

Magikarp has very limited PvP value because of its extremely low Attack and unusual moveset.

Great League

Magikarp is generally not a serious Great League investment. Its available moves provide very little offensive pressure.

Ultra League

Magikarp is not suitable for standard Ultra League competition.

Master League

Magikarp's low maximum CP and limited moveset make it unsuitable for normal Master League battles.

Better strategy:

If you want to use this evolutionary family in PvP, evaluate Gyarados rather than Magikarp.

19 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Magikarp

In Normal Gameplay

  • Catch Magikarp primarily for Candy.
  • Appraise high-IV specimens before transferring.
  • Save Shiny Magikarp if you want Shiny Gyarados.
  • Keep strong Shadow Magikarp for Shadow Gyarados.
  • Avoid unnecessary Stardust investment before evolution.

The Real Goal

The most important reason to collect Magikarp is Gyarados. The 400-Candy evolution requirement means that Magikarp encounters can eventually turn into a valuable Water-type Pokémon investment.

Long-Term Planning

If you are building a strong Gyarados collection, consider keeping several candidates rather than immediately evolving the first Magikarp that reaches 400 Candy.

22 • FAQ

Magikarp FAQ

What type is Magikarp?

Magikarp is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Magikarp weak to?

Magikarp is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What is Magikarp's max CP?

Magikarp's maximum CP is 310 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Magikarp's best moves?

Magikarp's standard moveset is Splash and Struggle. Magikarp is not recommended as a serious raid attacker.

How much Candy does Magikarp need to evolve?

Magikarp requires 400 Magikarp Candy to evolve into Gyarados.

Is Shiny Magikarp available?

Yes. Shiny Magikarp is available in Pokémon GO and has a distinctive golden appearance.

Is Shadow Magikarp available?

Shadow Magikarp is available through eligible Shadow Pokémon encounters and Team GO Rocket related rotations.

Can Shadow Magikarp evolve?

Yes. Shadow Magikarp can be evolved into Shadow Gyarados when the evolution requirements are met.

Should I purify Shadow Magikarp?

Not automatically. If your goal is Shadow Gyarados, compare the Shadow and purified options before spending resources on purification.

Is Magikarp good for raids?

No. Magikarp has extremely low Attack and a very limited moveset. Its main value is evolving into Gyarados.

Is Magikarp good in PvP?

Magikarp has very limited PvP usefulness because of its low stats and moveset. Gyarados is the much more relevant battle option.

What is the best Magikarp to keep?

Consider keeping high-IV Magikarp, Shiny Magikarp, Shadow Magikarp and other rare specimens that you may want to evolve into Gyarados.

How far do I need to walk Magikarp as a Buddy?

Magikarp has a 1 km Buddy distance.

Is Shiny Magikarp worth evolving?

If you want Shiny Gyarados, yes. Shiny Magikarp retains its Shiny identity when evolved.

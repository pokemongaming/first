Why Jigglypuff's Typing Matters

Jigglypuff's combination of Normal and Fairy typing gives it a different defensive profile from many other early-generation Pokémon. Its Fairy typing provides useful interactions against Dragon-type Pokémon, while its Normal typing contributes its own set of resistances and weaknesses.

Jigglypuff Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

Jigglypuff IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to an individual Pokémon. A 15/15/15 Jigglypuff is considered a perfect IV specimen.

What Does 100% Jigglypuff Mean?

A 100% Jigglypuff has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. The term "100%" refers to its IV appraisal and does not mean that Jigglypuff has 100% battle performance.

Perfect IV Jigglypuff

Attack 15 • Defense 15 • HP 15

Jigglypuff PvP Guide

Jigglypuff's high HP stat gives it some interesting defensive characteristics, but its low Attack and Defense stats limit its overall battle performance.

Great League

Jigglypuff can technically fit within Great League CP limits, but players should compare it with stronger Fairy and Normal-type options before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Jigglypuff's low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment impractical for most traditional builds.

Master League

Jigglypuff's maximum CP is far below the requirements normally associated with Master League combat.

Jigglypuff Candy & XL Candy

Jigglypuff Candy is useful for evolution and powering up Jigglypuff or Wigglytuff. Candy XL becomes relevant for higher-level investment.

  • Save Jigglypuff Candy for useful evolutions.
  • Transfer unwanted catches when appropriate.
  • Use Pinap Berries when farming Candy.
  • Keep strong IV Jigglypuff specimens.
  • Keep interesting Shiny, Lucky, Shadow and size variants.
  • Consider evolving a strong specimen into Wigglytuff.

How to Use Jigglypuff

Before Battle

  • Check Jigglypuff's current moveset.
  • Check its IVs and level.
  • Compare it with Wigglytuff before evolving.
  • Consider whether the Pokémon is Shadow, Lucky or another special variant.

Long-Term Investment

Jigglypuff is generally more interesting as an evolution, collection and specialty Pokémon than as a high-level raid attacker. A strong specimen can still be worth keeping, especially when it has desirable IVs or a rare combination of status, Shiny appearance or size.

Jigglypuff FAQ

What type is Jigglypuff?

Jigglypuff is a Normal and Fairy-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

What is Jigglypuff weak to?

Jigglypuff is weak to Fighting, Poison and Steel-type attacks.

Does Jigglypuff evolve?

Yes. Jigglypuff evolves into Wigglytuff using Jigglypuff Candy.

Is Shiny Jigglypuff available?

Shiny Jigglypuff is an available Shiny variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Jigglypuff available?

Shadow Jigglypuff availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and game events.

What is 100% Jigglypuff?

A 100% Jigglypuff has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Jigglypuff?

Lucky Jigglypuff is a special Lucky Pokémon status associated with trading mechanics and reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is Purified Jigglypuff?

Purified Jigglypuff is a Jigglypuff that has been purified from its Shadow state.

What is XXL Jigglypuff?

XXL Jigglypuff is an exceptionally large size category of Jigglypuff.

What is XXS Jigglypuff?

XXS Jigglypuff is an exceptionally small size category of Jigglypuff.

Is Jigglypuff good for raids?

Jigglypuff is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker because of its low Attack and maximum CP.

Is Jigglypuff good in PvP?

Jigglypuff can technically be used in lower CP formats, but its overall battle performance should be compared with stronger alternatives.

Can Shiny Jigglypuff evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Jigglypuff can evolve into a Shiny Wigglytuff while retaining its Shiny status.

Does Shadow Jigglypuff keep its Normal and Fairy typing?

Yes. Shadow status does not change Jigglypuff's underlying Normal and Fairy typing.

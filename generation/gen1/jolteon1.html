Shadow Jolteon

Shadow Jolteon is a Shadow variant of Jolteon. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their standard counterparts and should be evaluated separately when considering investment.

🌑 Shadow Jolteon

Shadow Jolteon retains its Electric typing while receiving the standard Shadow battle characteristics. Its increased offensive pressure comes with reduced defensive durability.

Should You Purify Shadow Jolteon?

Purification Tip

Lucky Jolteon

Lucky Jolteon is a special status associated with Pokémon obtained through trades that become Lucky. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering them up.

🍀 Lucky Jolteon

Lucky status does not change Jolteon's species or Electric typing. Its main advantage is the reduced Stardust cost associated with powering up Lucky Pokémon.

Lucky Jolteon for Collectors

A Lucky Jolteon with excellent IVs can be a desirable combination because it joins the Lucky background/status with a strong individual Pokémon.

  • Lucky status is obtained through trading.
  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky Jolteon remains Electric type.
  • Check IVs before committing to expensive investments.

    Jolteon FAQ

    What type is Jolteon?

    Jolteon is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

    What is Jolteon weak to?

    Jolteon is weak to Ground-type attacks.

    Is Jolteon a Legendary Pokémon?

    No. Jolteon is an Eeveelution and is not a Legendary Pokémon.

    What Pokémon evolves into Jolteon?

    Eevee evolves into Jolteon.

    Is Shiny Jolteon available?

    Yes. Shiny Jolteon is available in Pokémon GO.

    Is Shadow Jolteon available?

    Shadow Jolteon can be available through Pokémon GO's Shadow Pokémon mechanics when the relevant species is featured.

    Is Purified Jolteon available?

    Yes. A Shadow Jolteon can become Purified through the Pokémon GO purification system.

    What is a 100% Jolteon?

    A 100% Jolteon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is Lucky Jolteon?

    Lucky Jolteon is a Jolteon that has obtained Lucky status, typically through trading.

    Does Lucky Jolteon have better IVs?

    Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, but Lucky status does not automatically mean a Pokémon has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

    What is XXL Jolteon?

    XXL Jolteon is a Jolteon that receives an exceptionally large size classification.

    What is XXS Jolteon?

    XXS Jolteon is a Jolteon that receives an exceptionally small size classification.

    Is Jolteon good for raids?

    Jolteon can be useful as an Electric attacker, especially for players who need an accessible Electric-type option. Its value should be compared with stronger current Electric attackers.

    Is Jolteon good in PvP?

    Jolteon's PvP usefulness depends on the league, moveset and current metagame. It is generally more important to evaluate it matchup by matchup rather than assuming it is universally strong.

    Can Shadow Jolteon be purified?

    Yes. Shadow Jolteon can be purified when eligible, but players should consider the trade-offs before doing so.

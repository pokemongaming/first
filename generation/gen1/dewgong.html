Dewgong Overview

Dewgong PvP Guide

Dewgong is much more notable as a PvP Pokémon than as a raid attacker. Its combination of Ice-type pressure, debuff potential and coverage can make it useful in Great League environments.

Great League

Great League is generally the format most associated with Dewgong. Its stat profile and moveset can allow it to pressure opponents while using charged moves to control matchups.

Ultra League

Dewgong can be used under certain Ultra League situations, but its relatively limited maximum CP makes it less naturally suited to the format than some other Water and Ice Pokémon.

Master League

Dewgong's maximum CP is far below the level normally expected for competitive Master League teams.

Shadow Dewgong Investment

  • Check IVs before investing Stardust.
  • Check whether the moveset fits your intended role.
  • Consider the additional cost of powering up a Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Compare Shadow Dewgong with your regular Dewgong builds.

Purified Dewgong

Purified Dewgong is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Dewgong. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's investment path.

Lucky Dewgong

Lucky Dewgong is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky status is especially useful for players who plan to power up a Dewgong.

LUCKY POKÉMON

XXL Dewgong

XXL Dewgong is an unusually large size variant. XXL status is primarily connected with size-based collection and Showcase-style gameplay rather than increased battle stats.

EXTRA LARGE

🦭 XXL Dewgong

XXL Dewgong remains the same Pokémon species with the same typing and normal battle mechanics. Its main difference is its recorded size.

XXS Dewgong

XXS Dewgong is an unusually small size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is primarily a collection and size-category characteristic.

EXTRA SMALL

Dewgong FAQ

What type is Dewgong?

Dewgong is a Water and Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Dewgong weak to?

Dewgong is weak to Electric, Fighting, Grass and Rock attacks.

Is Dewgong good in Pokémon GO PvP?

Dewgong is particularly notable for PvP, especially in formats where its moveset and stat profile fit the CP limit.

Is Shiny Dewgong stronger than regular Dewgong?

No. Shiny Dewgong has the same underlying battle stats as regular Dewgong.

Is Shadow Dewgong stronger?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile, so Shadow Dewgong should be evaluated separately from the regular form.

Should I purify Shadow Dewgong?

Not automatically. Compare your Shadow Dewgong's IVs, moveset and intended use before purifying it.

What is a 100% Dewgong?

A 100% Dewgong has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Dewgong do more damage?

XXL status is primarily a size characteristic and does not automatically increase Dewgong's battle damage.

Does XXS Dewgong have different stats?

XXS Dewgong remains the same species and does not automatically receive different base battle stats because of its size.

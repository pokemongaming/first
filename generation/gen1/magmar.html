01 — OVERVIEW

Magmar Overview

Magmar is a pure Fire-type Pokémon from Generation 1. It sits between Magby and Magmortar in its evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Magmar can be useful as a collector Pokémon and has some PvP interest, but it is not a leading Fire-type raid attacker compared with stronger options.

For most players, Magmar should not be a priority Fire-type raid investment. Stronger Fire attackers are generally available. Magmar can still be worth keeping for collection, PvP experimentation, evolution projects and nostalgic Gen 1 value.

09 — PVP

Magmar PvP Guide

Magmar is not a top-tier PvP choice, but its energy generation and access to Fire and coverage moves can make it interesting for limited formats and experimentation.

Great League

Magmar can technically participate in the Great League, but its low durability makes many matchups difficult. Its performance is much more situational than that of established Fire-type PvP specialists.

Ultra League

Magmar can be built for Ultra League, although its stat profile and overall bulk limit its consistency against stronger opponents.

Master League

Magmar's CP ceiling does not make Master League a natural competitive environment for it.

PvP Starting Point

Karate Chop + Fire Punch + Scorching Sands

Magmar to Magmortar

Magmar evolves into Magmortar using Magmar Candy together with a Sinnoh Stone in Pokémon GO. Magmar requires 100 Candy for the evolution.

Magmar's evolution into Magmortar is one of the main reasons to collect Magmar Candy and preserve good Magmar specimens.

18 — FAQ

Magmar FAQ

What type is Magmar?

Magmar is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Magmar weak against?

Magmar is weak to Ground, Rock and Water-type attacks.

What is Magmar's max CP?

Magmar can reach 2,706 CP at Level 50 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Magmar good for raids?

Magmar is not considered a top Fire-type raid attacker. Stronger Fire Pokémon are generally preferred for optimized PvE teams.

Is Magmar good in PvP?

Magmar is mostly a niche or limited-format PvP option. Its low bulk makes its performance inconsistent in open formats.

Does Magmar evolve?

Yes. Magmar evolves into Magmortar in Pokémon GO using Magmar Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Magmar available?

Yes. Shiny Magmar is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Magmar available?

Yes. Shadow Magmar is currently listed as an available Shadow Pokémon, although specific encounter availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations.

What are Magmar's best moves?

For a Fire-focused build, Ember and Flamethrower are a practical recommendation. Karate Chop can also be useful depending on the battle format and desired coverage.

Should I keep a 100% IV Magmar?

Yes. A perfect 15/15/15 Magmar is valuable for collection, evolution and future use.

Should I purify Shadow Magmar?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, especially when the Shadow specimen has strong IVs or personal collection value.

Is Shiny Magmar stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not inherently give Magmar higher battle stats.

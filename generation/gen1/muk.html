Why Muk's Typing Matters

Muk's pure Poison typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. Poison Pokémon are useful against Fairy and Grass targets while needing to watch out for Ground and Psychic attacks.

09 — PVP

Muk PvP Guide

Muk can have a more interesting PvP role than its raid performance suggests. Its Poison typing, bulk and access to different charged-move options can make it useful in selected formats.

Great League

Muk can be considered for Great League teams when its moveset fits the current format. IV optimization can be different from the traditional 15/15/15 approach used for raids.

Ultra League

Muk's relatively high HP and Poison typing can make it useful in selected Ultra League environments. Always check current rankings and moves before building specifically for the format.

Master League

Standard Muk's maximum CP is much lower than the Pokémon commonly optimized for Master League, so it is generally less natural there than in the lower CP leagues.

11 — PURIFIED

Purified Muk

Purified Muk is created by purifying a Shadow Muk. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can alter its IVs and move situation.

✨

Purified Muk

A purified Muk no longer has Shadow status and receives the benefits associated with purification.

🌑

Shadow Muk

Retains the Shadow battle mechanics and increased offensive pressure.

Should You Purify Shadow Muk?

Purification should be treated as a deliberate decision rather than an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before committing, especially if you value the Shadow battle bonus.

A purified Pokémon receives an IV increase as part of the purification process, subject to the game's purification rules.

22 — FAQ

Muk FAQ

What type is Muk?

Regular Muk is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Muk weak to?

Muk is weak to Ground and Psychic attacks.

Is Muk good for raids?

Muk is generally more useful in selected PvP, defensive or collection roles than as a top-tier raid attacker.

Is Muk good in PvP?

Muk can have PvP value in selected Great League and Ultra League situations depending on its moveset and the current metagame.

Is Shiny Muk available?

Yes. Shiny Muk is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Does Muk evolve?

Muk is the final evolutionary stage of Kanto Grimer and does not evolve further.

What is Shadow Muk?

Shadow Muk is the Shadow version of Muk and has the standard Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Muk?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, especially if you value Shadow performance.

What is a 100% Muk?

A 100% Muk has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Muk?

Lucky Muk is a Muk obtained through trading that has the Lucky Pokémon status and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Muk?

XXL Muk is an exceptionally large Muk that can be useful for collection and Pokémon GO Showcase goals.

What is XXS Muk?

XXS Muk is an exceptionally small Muk and can be valuable for collectors and size-based goals.

Is Shadow Muk still Poison type?

Yes. Shadow Muk remains a Poison-type Pokémon.

Can Muk be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon can have multiple applicable collection characteristics, so a Muk can be both Shiny and Lucky when obtained through an eligible trade.

Can Muk be 100% IV and Lucky?

Yes. A Lucky Muk can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs. The Lucky status and IVs are separate attributes.

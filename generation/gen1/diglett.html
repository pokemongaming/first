Diglett

08 · SHADOW

Shadow Diglett

Shadow Diglett is the Shadow version of Diglett associated with Team GO Rocket encounters. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from their regular counterparts.

DARKENED FORM

Shadow Diglett

Shadow Diglett retains its Ground typing. The Shadow mechanic increases offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability, making the decision to keep, purify or evolve one dependent on your goals.

Should You Purify Shadow Diglett?

Do not purify every Shadow Pokémon automatically. Consider its IVs, your collection goals, the evolution you want and whether you prefer to keep the Shadow mechanics before making the decision.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. During eligible events, a Charged TM may be used to replace it. Check the current Team GO Rocket event rules.

09 · PURIFIED

Purified Diglett

Purified Diglett is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Diglett. Purification changes its status and can also affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🌟

Purified Status

The Pokémon is no longer Shadow after purification.

🍬

Resource Benefit

Purified Pokémon can have different power-up and evolution resource requirements.

❤️

Purification

Purification is permanent, so consider the Shadow version before committing.

✨

Collection

Purified Diglett can be useful for completing a special-form collection.

Purified Diglett IVs

Purification increases each IV by 2 points, up to the maximum of 15. This means a Shadow Diglett can become a perfect IV Pokémon after purification if its original IVs meet the required thresholds.

10 · LUCKY

Lucky Diglett

Lucky Diglett is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up.

✨ LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Diglett

Lucky Diglett is primarily a collection and Stardust-efficiency variant. Trading can also make a Lucky Diglett interesting when you want a memorable specimen or a strong IV roll.

Lucky Diglett Benefits

  • Requires less Stardust to power up.
  • Has a distinctive Lucky background.
  • Can be useful for completing a special-form collection.
  • Trading provides a new IV roll.
  • A Lucky Diglett can also have strong IVs.

Is 100% Diglett Worth Keeping?

Yes, if you care about perfect-IV collections, Pokédex completion or building a perfect evolution family. Diglett is not normally a high-priority raid investment, so avoid powering it up solely because it has perfect IVs.

20 · FAQ

Diglett FAQ

What type is Diglett?

Regular Diglett is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Diglett weak to?

Diglett is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Does Diglett evolve?

Yes. Diglett evolves into Dugtrio.

Is Shiny Diglett available?

Shiny Diglett can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Diglett available?

Shadow Diglett can appear through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations.

What is a Purified Diglett?

Purified Diglett is a Diglett that has been purified from its Shadow state.

What is Lucky Diglett?

Lucky Diglett is obtained through Pokémon GO trading and receives the Lucky Pokémon status.

What is a 100% Diglett?

A 100% Diglett has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Diglett?

XXL Diglett is a Diglett classified by Pokémon GO as exceptionally large.

What is an XXS Diglett?

XXS Diglett is a Diglett classified by Pokémon GO as exceptionally small.

Is Diglett good for raids?

Diglett is generally not a strong raid attacker because of its low overall stats.

Is Diglett good in PvP?

Diglett is a highly niche PvP option because of its low stats and low maximum CP.

Should I evolve a 100% Diglett?

If you want a perfect-IV Dugtrio, evolving a 100% Diglett preserves its perfect IV combination. If you are a collector, you may also choose to keep the perfect Diglett unevolved.

Should I purify Shadow Diglett?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow's IVs, your collection goals and whether you want to preserve the Shadow status before purifying.

Can a Diglett be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. A Shiny Pokémon can also have the Lucky status after an eligible trade.

Can Diglett be both XXL and Shiny?

Size and Shiny characteristics are separate attributes, so a Diglett can have multiple desirable characteristics at the same time.

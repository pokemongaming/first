Onix

Onix is a Rock/Ground-type Pokémon from the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the unevolved form of Steelix and can also appear in Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and high-IV forms.

Onix Overview

Onix is a dual Rock/Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Its unusually high Defense compared with its Attack gives it a distinctive stat profile, although its low maximum CP limits its overall battle impact compared with many stronger Pokémon.

Why Onix's Typing Matters

Onix's Rock/Ground typing gives it access to useful offensive coverage but also creates several important weaknesses. In particular, Water- and Grass-type attacks can deal heavily increased damage because both types exploit its dual typing.

Onix Attack, Defense, HP & Max CP

Onix is heavily weighted toward Defense. Its Attack stat is much lower, while its maximum CP remains relatively modest. This makes Onix more notable as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a top general-purpose attacker.

Onix IV Guide

Individual Values add additional Attack, Defense and HP points to an individual Onix. A perfect Onix has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

100% IV Onix

A 100% Onix has perfect IVs across all three categories.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

How to Find a 100% Onix

  • Check appraisal results for maximum IV bars.
  • Compare Attack, Defense and HP IVs.
  • Use an IV checker when appropriate.
  • Keep high-IV Onix if you want a strong Steelix candidate.
  • Remember that IV perfection is different from maximum CP.

Shiny Onix

✨
Shiny Onix

Shiny Onix has an alternate coloration compared with the standard Onix appearance. Shiny status does not change its underlying species or base battle stats.

Shiny Onix IVs

A Shiny Onix can have any IV combination. Shiny status and IV quality are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Onix is not automatically a high-IV or perfect Pokémon.

Collector Tip:

A Shiny Onix with strong IVs can be especially useful as a future Steelix evolution candidate if its moveset and investment goals make sense.

Purified Onix

Purified Onix is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Onix. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and other gameplay characteristics.

Purified Onix

Purification is a strategic choice. If you are considering purifying a Shadow Onix, compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Lucky Onix

Lucky Onix is obtained through trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a special visual status and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

🍀
Lucky Onix

A Lucky Onix can be particularly attractive to collectors or players planning to evolve and power up an Onix while saving Stardust.

Why Keep a Lucky Onix?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collection goals.
  • Can be evolved into Steelix.
  • Can also have excellent IVs.
  • Lucky status and Shiny status can potentially coexist.

XXL Onix

XXL Onix is a size variant representing an unusually large individual. Size is separate from IV quality, Shiny status and battle stats.

📏 XXL Onix Extra-large size category.

An XXL Onix is primarily interesting for collection, size-related achievements and special research or gameplay mechanics that recognize Pokémon size.

XXL + 100% IV Size and IV quality are separate.

An XXL Onix can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs. These characteristics can coexist.

XXS Onix

XXS Onix is an unusually small Onix. Like XXL status, XXS status is independent of IV quality and Shiny status.

📐 XXS Onix Extra-small size category.

An XXS Onix can be kept for collection purposes, size-related achievements and personal goals.

XXS + Shiny Onix Multiple characteristics can coexist.

An Onix can have Shiny status while also belonging to the XXS size category.

Onix Evolution — Steelix

Onix can evolve into Steelix. Unlike many simple Candy-only evolutions, Steelix evolution uses a special evolution requirement.

Onix Rock / Ground
→
Steelix Steel / Ground

Should You Evolve Onix?

If your primary goal is battle performance, Steelix is generally much more relevant than Onix itself. A good Onix can therefore be valuable as an evolution candidate rather than as a final battle investment.

  • Keep high-IV Onix for potential Steelix evolution.
  • Consider a Lucky Onix if you want to reduce Stardust costs.
  • Compare Shadow and purified versions before evolving.
  • A Shiny Onix can be evolved into Shiny Steelix.
  • Check current evolution requirements before spending resources.

Onix PvP Guide

Onix's very high Defense relative to its Attack can make it interesting in certain CP-limited situations, but its overall performance depends heavily on the specific league, moveset and current metagame.

Great League

Onix can be considered for specialized Great League or limited-format strategies, particularly where its typing and defensive profile are useful.

Ultra League

Onix's relatively low maximum CP makes it less natural for Ultra League compared with Pokémon designed to operate at higher CP levels.

Master League

Onix is not a practical standard Master League choice because its maximum CP is far below the format's typical stat requirements.

Onix Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

If you plan to evolve or power up Onix, Candy and Stardust management becomes important.

  • Save Onix Candy for Steelix evolution projects.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for long-term investments.
  • Consider Lucky Onix when Stardust efficiency matters.
  • Compare Shadow and purified investment costs.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust before deciding whether Onix or Steelix better fits your goal.

Onix FAQ

What type is Onix?

Onix is a Rock/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Onix weak to?

Onix is vulnerable to several attack types including Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Ice and Steel.

What is Onix's max CP?

Onix has a relatively low maximum CP compared with many fully evolved Pokémon.

Is Shiny Onix available?

Yes. Shiny Onix is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Onix available?

Shadow Onix can be associated with Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations. Availability can change over time.

Can Shadow Onix be purified?

An eligible Shadow Onix can be purified. Always compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Can Onix be Lucky?

Yes. Onix can become Lucky through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Onix?

A 100% Onix has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Can Onix be XXL?

Yes. Onix can appear in the XXL size category.

Can Onix be XXS?

Yes. Onix can also appear in the XXS size category.

Does Onix evolve?

Yes. Onix evolves into Steelix using the appropriate Pokémon GO evolution requirements.

Is Shiny Onix worth keeping?

Yes. Shiny Onix is valuable for collectors and can also be evolved into Shiny Steelix.

Is 100% Onix good?

A 100% Onix is valuable as a perfect-IV specimen, but its relatively low Attack and CP mean that its main long-term value is often connected to Steelix evolution or collection.

Should I power up Onix?

For most players, Onix is not a priority raid attacker. Consider its evolution into Steelix before investing large amounts of Stardust.

Can a Shiny Onix be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics and can coexist.

Can an Onix be 100% and Shiny?

Yes. An Onix can be Shiny and have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can an Onix be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IV quality are separate characteristics, so an XXL Onix can also have perfect IVs.

Can an Onix be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so an XXS Onix can also be Shiny.

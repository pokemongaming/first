Shiny Gastly

Shiny Gastly is an alternate-color version of Gastly. It keeps the same Pokémon typing, evolution family and base battle role.

✨ Shiny Gastly

Shiny Gastly is primarily a collector variant. Its battle performance is determined by its individual IVs, level and moves rather than its Shiny status.

Shadow Gastly

Shadow Gastly is the Shadow version of Gastly. It retains Gastly's Ghost and Poison typing but has Shadow-specific battle characteristics and resource considerations.

🌑 Shadow Gastly

Shadow Gastly can be especially interesting because its evolution family eventually leads to Shadow Haunter and Shadow Gengar.

Shadow Gastly Investment

  • Check IVs before investing significant resources.
  • Consider whether you plan to evolve it into Shadow Haunter or Shadow Gengar.
  • Shadow Pokémon require additional resources for power-ups.
  • Do not purify automatically only for higher IVs.
  • Check current Frustration removal opportunities.

Purified Gastly

Purified Gastly is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Gastly. Purification changes its Shadow status and may alter its IV values.

✨ Purified Gastly

A Purified Gastly can still evolve normally into Haunter and Gengar.

Lucky Gastly

Lucky Gastly is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for long-term evolution projects because of their reduced Stardust power-up cost.

🍀 Lucky Gastly

A Lucky Gastly can be an excellent candidate for evolving into Haunter and Gengar if it has strong IVs and you plan to power it up.

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Can evolve into Lucky Haunter.
  • Can eventually evolve into Lucky Gengar.
  • Useful for collectors and long-term investment.

XXL Gastly

XXL Gastly is a larger-than-average size variant. Its size status is separate from its IVs, Shiny status and Lucky status.

📈 XXL Gastly

XXL Gastly is primarily valuable for collecting, showcases and size-related game mechanics.

XXS Gastly

XXS Gastly is a smaller-than-average size variant. Like XXL, its size does not automatically determine battle performance.

📉 XXS Gastly

XXS Gastly is mainly a collection variant and can be kept alongside other special Gastly forms.

Gastly FAQ

What type is Gastly?

Gastly is a Ghost and Poison-type Pokémon.

What does Gastly evolve into?

Gastly evolves into Haunter, which can later evolve into Gengar.

Can Gastly be Shiny?

Yes. Shiny Gastly is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Gastly be Shadow?

Yes, Shadow Gastly can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations.

Can Shadow Gastly be purified?

Eligible Shadow Gastly can be purified, creating Purified Gastly.

Can Gastly become Lucky?

Yes. Gastly can become Lucky through eligible Pokémon trades.

What is a 100% Gastly?

A 100% Gastly has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP Individual Values.

Can Gastly be XXL or XXS?

Yes. Gastly can exist as XXL or XXS size variants.

Is Gastly worth evolving?

Gastly is often worth evolving because its evolution family leads to Haunter and Gengar, which can have more significant battle roles.

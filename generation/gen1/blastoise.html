Blastoise

Blastoise is a Water-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the final evolution of Squirtle. In Pokémon GO, Blastoise is available in regular, Shiny, Shadow and Purified forms, while Mega Blastoise provides a significantly stronger special form for raids and other gameplay situations.

Is Shiny Blastoise Stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual and collecting distinction. A Shiny Blastoise does not automatically receive stronger battle stats simply because it is Shiny.

Shadow Blastoise

Shadow Blastoise is the Shadow variant of the species. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from their regular counterparts.

Shadow Blastoise

Blastoise FAQ

What type is Blastoise?

Regular Blastoise is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Blastoise weak to?

Blastoise is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Is Blastoise a starter Pokémon?

Yes. Blastoise is the final evolution of the Kanto Water starter Squirtle.

Is Shiny Blastoise available?

Yes. Shiny Blastoise is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution opportunities.

Is Shadow Blastoise available?

Shadow Blastoise availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket encounters and current rotations.

Should I purify Shadow Blastoise?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your raid, PvP and collection goals.

What is Lucky Blastoise?

What is 100% Blastoise?

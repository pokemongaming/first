01 • DATABASE OVERVIEW

Slowbro Overview

Slowbro is the evolved form of Slowpoke and is one of the original Kanto Pokémon. In Pokémon GO, Slowbro combines Water and Psychic typing with comparatively high stamina and defense.

Slowbro can also Mega Evolve into Mega Slowbro. Special variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Slowbro can have additional collection or investment value.

09 • PVP

Slowbro PvP Guide

Slowbro's high stamina and respectable defensive profile can make it interesting in PvP formats. Its effectiveness depends heavily on the current moveset, league rules and meta.

Slowbro can be considered for limited or specialty Great League formats where its Water/Psychic typing provides useful matchup coverage. Its actual value depends on the current moves and cup rules.

Master League

23 • FAQ

Slowbro FAQ

What type is Slowbro?

Slowbro is a Water/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Slowbro weak to?

Slowbro is vulnerable to Electric, Grass, Bug, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Is Shiny Slowbro available?

Yes. Shiny Slowbro is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Slowbro available?

Shadow Slowbro availability depends on current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

Can Shadow Slowbro be purified?

An eligible Shadow Slowbro can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and changes its associated mechanics.

What is a Lucky Slowbro?

Lucky Slowbro is a Slowbro obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Trade mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Slowbro?

A 100% Slowbro has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Slowbro better?

XXL classification does not automatically mean better IVs or better battle performance. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Is XXS Slowbro rare?

XXS Slowbro is a special size classification and can be desirable for collectors.

Can Slowbro Mega Evolve?

Yes. Slowbro can Mega Evolve into Mega Slowbro when the required Mega Evolution conditions are met.

What type is Mega Slowbro?

Mega Slowbro remains Water/Psychic type.

Is Mega Slowbro permanent?

No. Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation in Pokémon GO.

Is Slowbro good for raids?

Regular Slowbro is not generally a top-tier specialist raid attacker, although Mega Slowbro provides additional Mega Evolution utility.

Is Slowbro good in PvP?

Slowbro can have value in selected PvP formats, particularly where its Water/Psychic typing and bulk provide useful matchup coverage.

Does Slowbro evolve?

Slowbro evolves from Slowpoke. Slowbro itself does not have a conventional permanent evolution; it can instead Mega Evolve.

Can Shiny Slowbro become Mega Slowbro?

Yes. A Shiny Slowbro can be used for Mega Evolution and retains its Shiny identity.

Should I power up a 100% Slowbro?

A perfect Slowbro is a strong collection target, but whether it deserves a major investment depends on your intended PvP, Mega, collection or battle role.

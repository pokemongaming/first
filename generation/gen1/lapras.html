Why Lapras's Typing Matters

Lapras combines Water and Ice typing, giving it access to two useful offensive damage categories. Its dual typing also creates a distinctive defensive profile compared with pure Water or pure Ice Pokémon.

The Ice typing is especially relevant when Lapras is used with Ice-type moves against Pokémon vulnerable to Ice damage, while its Water typing supports Water-oriented builds.

06 • RAID PERFORMANCE

Lapras Raid Performance

Is Lapras Good for Raids?

Lapras can work in raids when a player needs a Water- or Ice-type attacker and does not have stronger alternatives. However, its high HP does not compensate for its comparatively low Attack when the goal is maximizing raid damage.

Perfect Lapras

A 15/15/15 Lapras is considered a perfect IV specimen. However, a perfect IV is not automatically the best possible PvP specimen because different leagues can favor different IV distributions.

PvP IV Reminder

Do not automatically transfer a low-Attack Lapras. Lower Attack IVs can sometimes be desirable in CP-limited PvP formats.

09 • PVP

Lapras PvP Guide

Lapras's high HP and useful charged-move options can make it more interesting in PvP than its raw raid Attack stat might suggest.

Great League

Lapras can have value in Great League environments when its moveset and current cup rules allow it. Its bulk can make it capable of absorbing considerable damage while applying Water- or Ice-type pressure.

Ultra League

Lapras can also be considered for Ultra League strategies. Its performance depends heavily on moveset, IVs, shields and the current metagame.

Master League

Lapras's maximum CP is much lower than the cap reached by many Master League specialists. As a result, it is generally less natural for open Master League play.

PvP note:

PvP rankings change as moves, cups and Pokémon availability change. Treat this section as a strategic overview rather than a permanent ranking.

Should You Purify Shadow Lapras?

Purification should not be treated as an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, especially if the Pokémon has strong PvP IVs or sentimental value.

  • Check the Shadow IVs before transferring.
  • Check whether the moveset fits your intended role.
  • Consider the cost of Shadow power-ups.
  • Compare regular and Shadow performance.
  • Do not purify automatically for higher IVs.

18 • FAQ

Lapras FAQ

What type is Lapras?

Lapras is a Water/Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Lapras weak to?

Lapras is weak to Electric, Grass, Fighting and Rock-type attacks.

What does Lapras resist?

Lapras resists Water- and Ice-type attacks.

Does Lapras evolve?

No. Lapras is a standalone Pokémon and does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Lapras available?

Yes. Shiny Lapras is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Lapras available?

Shadow Lapras can be available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting for it.

Is Lapras good for raids?

Lapras can be used for Water- or Ice-type raid damage, but its relatively low Attack means that dedicated attackers are often stronger choices.

Is Lapras good in PvP?

Lapras can be useful in certain PvP formats because of its high HP and access to useful charged moves. Its ranking depends on the current format and meta.

Is Shadow Lapras better than regular Lapras?

Shadow Lapras has increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. Whether it is better depends on the battle format and intended role.

Should I purify Shadow Lapras?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's IVs, moves and intended role before deciding whether purification makes sense.

Is Shiny Lapras stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual distinction. A Shiny Lapras uses the same species battle system, with its actual performance determined by its stats, IVs, moves and level.

What is Lapras's maximum CP?

Lapras has a maximum CP of approximately 2,985 under the standard maximum-level calculation. Always use current Pokémon GO data if publishing an exact level-specific CP value.

What are the best Lapras moves?

Water Gun and Surf provide a useful Water-oriented combination, while Ice-focused builds can use Ice-oriented moves. The ideal moveset depends on the battle format.

Is 15/15/15 Lapras perfect?

Yes. A Lapras with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs is a perfect IV specimen.

Tentacruel Overview

Tentacruel is the evolved form of Tentacool and is Pokédex #073. It is a dual Water/Poison-type Pokémon with considerably more Defense than Attack, making its battle role different from many offensive Water Pokémon.

09 · GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Tentacruel PvP Guide

Tentacruel's high Defense and useful charged-move options make PvP one of the more interesting reasons to keep a strong Tentacruel.

Great League

Tentacruel can be considered for Great League teams when its moveset, IV spread and the current meta make the Water/Poison typing useful. Because Great League has a strict CP limit, low-Attack IV spreads can sometimes be more desirable than a conventional 15/15/15 specimen.

Ultra League

Tentacruel can also be evaluated in Ultra League, although its relatively low maximum CP means it may require substantial investment to compete near the league's CP ceiling.

Master League

Tentacruel's maximum CP is far below the typical Master League ceiling, making it much less natural for unrestricted Master League play.

PvP Move Concept

Poison Jab combined with Scald and Acid Spray can provide a combination of direct pressure and debuff utility.

Lucky Tentacruel

Lucky Tentacruel is not a separate species or form. It is a special trade status that gives the Pokémon a distinctive Lucky background and reduces the Stardust cost of power-ups.

🍀

Why Keep a Lucky Tentacruel?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have at least a 12/12/12 IV floor.
  • A Lucky Tentacruel can be useful for collectors.
  • Lucky status is retained after powering up.
  • Lucky status is different from Shiny status.

Tentacruel FAQ

What type is Tentacruel?

Tentacruel is Water and Poison type.

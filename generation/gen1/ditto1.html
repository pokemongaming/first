Ditto Overview

Ditto is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, its most important feature is Transform: instead of appearing as Ditto on the map, it can disguise itself as certain other Pokémon until the encounter begins.

What Does Ditto's Typing Mean?

Ditto is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its unusual role in Pokémon GO comes less from its base typing and more from its Transform mechanic.

Normal-type Pokémon have a relatively straightforward defensive profile, with Fighting as the key weakness and Ghost as an important interaction.

Ditto Transform Mechanic

Transform is the defining feature of Ditto. When Ditto enters a battle, its behavior is based on the Pokémon it transforms into rather than simply using Ditto's normal appearance and moves.

Why Ditto Is Different

Ditto is not normally found by seeing a purple Ditto on the overworld map. It can appear disguised as another Pokémon, and the disguise is revealed after the encounter begins.

Ditto Hunting Tip

  • Learn the Pokémon currently capable of being Ditto disguises.
  • Catch potential Ditto disguises when you encounter them.
  • Do not assume that every encounter with a possible disguise will transform into Ditto.
  • Check current Pokémon GO announcements because the disguise pool can change.

Ditto Availability

Ditto availability works differently from ordinary Pokémon because it can disguise itself as selected Pokémon in the wild.

Current Ditto Hunting Strategy

Check the current Pokémon GO Ditto disguise list before hunting. The disguise pool can change through events and game updates.

How to Find Ditto

  • Check which Pokémon can currently be Ditto disguises.
  • Catch those potential disguises.
  • Wait for the encounter to reveal whether it transforms.
  • Repeat the process when hunting additional Ditto.

Ditto FAQ

What type is Ditto?

Ditto is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Ditto's Pokédex number?

What is Ditto's biggest feature?

Ditto is famous for Transform, which allows it to disguise itself as other Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Ditto available?

Is a 100% Ditto rare?

A perfect 15/15/15 Ditto is a special IV specimen. Its rarity depends on encounter availability and the number of Ditto you have checked.

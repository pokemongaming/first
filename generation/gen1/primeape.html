Primeape PvP Guide

Primeape has traditionally been more interesting in PvP than in high-end raids. Its Fighting typing and relatively quick charged-move access can give it useful shield-pressure opportunities.

Great League

Great League is one of the formats where Primeape's CP profile can make it more practical. IV selection, moveset and the current Cup or open-meta environment should be considered before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Primeape can also be considered in Ultra League situations where its Fighting-type pressure fits the team. League-specific metas can significantly change its usefulness.

Master League

Primeape generally has a less natural role in Master League because its maximum CP is much lower than that of the Pokémon commonly optimized for the format.

XXL & XXS Primeape

Primeape can also be encountered with different Pokémon sizes. XXL and XXS status is independent of Shiny, Shadow, Lucky and IV quality.

XXL Primeape Extra-large size

An XXL Primeape is a size-based variant that can be useful for collection goals and size-related game mechanics.

XXS Primeape Extra-small size

An XXS Primeape is an unusually small specimen. Its size does not automatically make its IVs better or worse.

XXL / XXS vs IVs

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. A Primeape can therefore be XXL or XXS while also being a high-IV, low-IV, Shiny, Lucky or Shadow Pokémon.

Collection tip:

If you are building a complete Primeape collection, you can track size, IV, Shiny, Shadow and Lucky status as separate attributes rather than treating them as mutually exclusive forms.

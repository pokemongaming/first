Electabuzz is a pure Electric-type Pokémon first introduced in Generation 1. It has a three-stage evolutionary family in Pokémon GO, progressing from Elekid to Electabuzz and eventually Electivire.

Should You Purify Shadow Electabuzz?

Do not automatically purify every Shadow Electabuzz. If you intend to evolve it into Shadow Electivire, compare the Shadow benefits, IVs, resources and your intended battle role before making the decision.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on special Team GO Rocket events.

💎 Purified Electabuzz

Purified Electabuzz is created by purifying a Shadow Electabuzz. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can provide benefits such as reduced future power-up costs.

🌑

Shadow Electabuzz

Retains Shadow status and its associated battle characteristics.

💎

Purified Electabuzz

Purified from its Shadow state and receives purification-related benefits.

Purified Electabuzz IVs

Purification increases each IV by 2 points, up to the maximum of 15. This means purification can turn some Shadow specimens into higher-IV Pokémon.

Purify Carefully

If your goal is maximum Shadow battle performance, purification is generally a decision that should be made carefully rather than automatically.

Is a Hundo Electabuzz Worth Keeping?

📏 XXL Electabuzz

XXL Electabuzz is a size variant with an unusually large body size. Size does not replace IVs and does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has better battle stats.

📏

XXL Electabuzz

Large-size Electabuzz. XXL status is separate from IV quality, Shiny status, Lucky status and Shadow status.

XXL vs 100% IV Electabuzz

An XXL Electabuzz is not necessarily a hundo. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. You can have an XXL Electabuzz with low IVs, high IVs, or even perfect IVs.

🔬 XXS Electabuzz

XXS Electabuzz is an unusually small-size Electabuzz. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than an IV rating.

🔬

XXS Electabuzz

Small-size Electabuzz that can be kept for collection, showcases and personal milestones.

XXS Electabuzz and IVs

XXS status does not mean perfect IVs. Always check the Pokémon's appraisal separately if IV quality matters to you.

Electabuzz FAQ

What type is Electabuzz?

Electabuzz is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Electabuzz weak against?

Electabuzz is weak to Ground-type attacks.

What is Electabuzz's Pokédex number?

Electabuzz is Pokédex number #125.

Can Electabuzz evolve?

Yes. Electabuzz can evolve into Electivire in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Electabuzz available?

Yes. Shiny Electabuzz is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Electabuzz available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I purify Shadow Electabuzz?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's battle value, IVs, intended evolution and your personal goals before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Electabuzz?

Lucky Electabuzz is obtained through trading and receives the Lucky Pokémon benefits associated with reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Electabuzz?

A 100% Electabuzz has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Electabuzz?

XXL Electabuzz is an unusually large-size Electabuzz. XXL status is independent of IVs.

XXS Electabuzz is an unusually small-size Electabuzz. XXS status does not indicate a particular IV combination.

Is a Lucky Hundo Electabuzz rare?

Is Electabuzz good for raids?

Should I evolve a 100% Electabuzz?

Can Shiny Electabuzz evolve into Shiny Electivire?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when an eligible Shiny Pokémon evolves.

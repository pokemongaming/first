01 • OVERVIEW

Moltres Overview

Moltres is a Legendary Fire/Flying-type Pokémon first introduced in Generation 1. It is one of the three legendary birds of the Kanto region alongside Articuno and Zapdos.

In Pokémon GO, Moltres has value as a Fire-type attacker, especially when equipped with a strong Fire moveset. Its Flying typing also gives it a useful set of resistances and matchup advantages.

Why Moltres's Typing Matters

Moltres combines Fire and Flying typing. This gives it access to Fire-type offensive options while also providing resistances associated with both types.

However, the Flying typing creates a major vulnerability to Rock-type attacks. Rock attacks are especially dangerous against Moltres because Fire/Flying Pokémon take heavily increased damage from Rock-type attacks.

Should You Purify Shadow Moltres?

Purification should not be treated as an automatic upgrade. If your goal is maximum Shadow raid performance, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

  • Keep Shadow Moltres with strong overall IVs.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be replaced.
  • Consider the increased Stardust and Candy investment.
  • Compare it with your existing Fire attackers.
  • Do not purify solely because purification increases IVs.

Shadow Moltres Availability

18 • FAQ

Moltres FAQ

Is Moltres a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Moltres is one of the Legendary birds originally introduced in Generation 1.

What type is Moltres?

Regular Moltres is Fire/Flying type.

What is Moltres weak to?

Regular Moltres is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock attacks are especially dangerous because of the Fire/Flying combination.

Is Moltres good for raids?

Yes. Moltres can be a useful Fire-type raid attacker when equipped with an appropriate Fire moveset.

Is Shadow Moltres good for raids?

Shadow Moltres can be a powerful raid attacker because Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure. Its lower durability and higher investment requirements should also be considered.

Is Shiny Moltres available?

Yes. Shiny Moltres is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What type is Galarian Moltres?

Galarian Moltres is Dark/Flying type.

Is Galarian Moltres the same as regular Moltres?

It is the same species with a regional form, but its typing, appearance and gameplay characteristics differ from the Kanto form.

Does Moltres evolve?

No. Moltres does not have a normal evolutionary chain.

Should I purify Shadow Moltres?

Not automatically. If your goal is maximum Shadow raid performance, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before deciding.

Why is Rock especially strong against Moltres?

Moltres has both Fire and Flying typing. Both typings are weak to Rock, making Rock attacks especially effective against it.

Can Galarian Moltres be found in regular Legendary Raids?

Galarian Moltres has its own encounter mechanics and should not automatically be assumed to follow the same raid rotation as standard Moltres.

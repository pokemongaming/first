Why Exeggutor's Typing Matters

The combination of Grass and Psychic gives Exeggutor access to useful offensive coverage, but it also creates a fairly broad weakness profile. Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks can exploit the regular form.

Exeggutor PvP Guide

Exeggutor's dual typing and offensive stats can provide interesting coverage in certain PvP formats, although its usefulness depends heavily on the current league metagame.

Great League

Regular Exeggutor generally requires careful consideration in Great League because of its CP and vulnerability profile. Specific limited cups can change its viability.

Ultra League

Exeggutor can appear in certain Ultra League situations, but its performance depends on moveset, IV distribution and the current opponent pool.

Master League

Regular Exeggutor is not naturally optimized for the highest CP Master League environment compared with many Legendary and other high-stat Pokémon.

Exeggutor FAQ

What type is Exeggutor?

Regular Exeggutor is Grass/Psychic type.

What is Exeggutor weak to?

Regular Exeggutor is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

Is Shiny Exeggutor available?

Yes. Shiny Exeggutor is available through eligible encounters.

Is Shadow Exeggutor available?

Shadow Exeggutor can be available through Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations when Niantic makes it obtainable.

What is a 100% Exeggutor?

A 100% Exeggutor has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Exeggutor?

A Lucky Exeggutor is obtained through a Lucky Trade and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Does XXL Exeggutor have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Exeggutor can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Exeggutor have lower stats?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not automatically mean lower IVs or battle stats.

What type is Alolan Exeggutor?

Alolan Exeggutor is Grass/Dragon type.

Is Alolan Exeggutor different from regular Exeggutor?

Yes. Alolan Exeggutor is a regional form with a different typing, appearance and battle characteristics.

Should I purify Shadow Exeggutor?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of retaining the Shadow form against the benefits of purification before making the decision.

Is Exeggutor good for raids?

Exeggutor can be useful in Grass-focused situations, but its ranking depends on the current Pokémon GO moveset and the other attackers available to players.

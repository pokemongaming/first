Dugtrio FAQ

What type is Dugtrio?

Regular Dugtrio is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Dugtrio weak to?

Dugtrio is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Is Shiny Dugtrio stronger?

Shiny status is primarily cosmetic. IVs, level and moveset determine battle performance.

Is Shadow Dugtrio stronger?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure with reduced defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Dugtrio?

Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before purifying because the best choice depends on your collection and battle goals.

What is a 100% Dugtrio?

A 100% Dugtrio has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Dugtrio have higher stats?

XXL size does not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Does XXS Dugtrio have lower stats?

No. Size is separate from IVs and standard battle statistics.

Is Lucky Dugtrio stronger?

Lucky status does not directly increase base stats, but Lucky Pokémon have useful investment benefits.

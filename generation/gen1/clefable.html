Clefable

Clefable is a Fairy-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it can be used in PvP and selected PvE situations, while its Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS and high-IV variants give collectors and battlers different reasons to keep multiple Clefable.

100% IV • HUNDO

Perfect Clefable

A 15/15/15 Clefable is a perfect-IV Pokémon. A perfect specimen can be especially attractive to collectors and players building high-level Pokémon.

Should You Keep a 100% Clefable?

Yes, if you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon. A hundo Clefable can also be useful as a long-term investment depending on its moveset and the current Pokémon GO metagame.

09 • PVP

Clefable PvP Guide

Clefable's Fairy typing and access to Fairy pressure can make it interesting in PvP, particularly when facing Dragon, Fighting and Dark-oriented teams.

Great League

Clefable can be considered for Great League depending on the current format, moveset and available alternatives. League-specific IV optimization can be more important than simply looking for 15/15/15.

Ultra League

Clefable can also have applications in Ultra League. A carefully optimized Pokémon can perform differently from a simple high-CP specimen.

Master League

Clefable's maximum CP is significantly lower than many dedicated Master League Pokémon, so it is less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League play.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Clefable

Lucky Clefable is a special status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon require fewer Stardust resources when powered up.

🍀 Lucky Clefable

Lucky status is especially useful for players who want to build a Clefable while reducing Stardust costs.

Why Keep a Lucky Clefable?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky trades can be useful for building collection projects.
  • A Lucky Clefable can be combined with strong IVs.
  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny status.
  • Lucky and Shiny Clefable can potentially be combined when obtained through appropriate mechanics.

15 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Clefable

Clefable can also be encountered with different size classifications. XXL and XXS are separate size attributes and do not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

📏 XXL Clefable

XXL Clefable is a large-sized specimen. Its size is useful for collection goals, showcases and other size-related features.

  • Large size classification.
  • Can have any IV combination.
  • Size does not automatically mean higher CP.
  • Valuable for collectors and showcases.

🔹 XXS Clefable

XXS Clefable is a particularly small-sized specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, size and IVs are separate characteristics.

  • Small size classification.
  • Can have different IV combinations.
  • Size does not guarantee perfect IVs.
  • Useful for collection goals.

22 • FAQ

Clefable FAQ

What type is Clefable?

Clefable is a pure Fairy-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

What is Clefable weak to?

Clefable is weak to Poison and Steel-type attacks.

What is Clefable's max CP?

Clefable's maximum CP is approximately 2,437 under standard maximum-level conditions.

Is Shiny Clefable available?

Yes. Shiny Clefable is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolution paths.

Is Shadow Clefable available?

Shadow Pokémon availability depends on current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I purify Shadow Clefable?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision, particularly if you value Shadow battle performance.

What is a 100% Clefable?

A 100% Clefable has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Trainers commonly call this a hundo.

Is Lucky Clefable worth keeping?

Lucky Clefable can be valuable because Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs. It can be especially attractive when combined with strong IVs.

Is XXL Clefable stronger?

XXL status does not automatically mean Clefable has better IVs or stronger battle performance. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Is XXS Clefable rare?

XXS Pokémon can be desirable for collectors and size-related goals. Its collection value is separate from its IV quality.

Is Clefable good for raids?

Clefable can contribute Fairy-type damage, but players looking for maximum raid efficiency generally have stronger Fairy-type alternatives.

Is Clefable good for PvP?

Clefable can have PvP applications depending on the league, moveset and current metagame. Always evaluate the current format before investing heavily.

Can a Clefable be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can potentially possess both.

Can a Clefable be 100% IV and Shiny?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are independent characteristics, so a Shiny Clefable can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Clefable be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes. Therefore an XXL Clefable can also have perfect IVs.

Can Clefable be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size classification and Shiny status are separate characteristics.

Scyther Overview

06 — SHINY

Shiny Scyther

Shiny Scyther is an alternate-color version of Scyther. It is primarily a collector variant and can be especially valuable when combined with strong IVs or other desirable attributes.

✨ Shiny Scyther

Shiny status does not automatically improve Scyther's stats. A Shiny Scyther can have any IV combination, size category or other eligible attributes.

Should You Evolve Shiny Scyther?

The decision depends on whether you prefer to keep Scyther in your collection or evolve it into Shiny Scizor.

07 — SHADOW

Shadow Scyther

Shadow Scyther is a separate investment option from regular Scyther. The Shadow mechanic changes its battle profile, making it more focused on offensive pressure.

🌑 Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade additional offensive power for reduced durability. Whether Shadow Scyther is worth investing in depends on your resources and intended use.

Shadow Scyther Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Scyther's Bug / Flying typing. Its core type weaknesses remain Fire, Electric, Ice and Rock.

Should You Purify Shadow Scyther?

  • Do not purify automatically for IV improvements.
  • Compare Shadow and Purified investment goals.
  • Consider future evolution plans.
  • Check current move availability.
  • Consider the higher resource cost of Shadow Pokémon.

Why Zapdos's Typing Matters

Electric/Flying is an unusual combination that gives Zapdos useful resistances while leaving it vulnerable to two important attacking types: Rock and Ice. Its Electric typing also makes it naturally useful against many Water- and Flying-type raid bosses.

    15 • SIZE VARIANTS

    XXS & XXL Zapdos

    Pokémon GO can record size categories for Pokémon. XXS and XXL Zapdos are collector-focused variants and do not represent different species.

    🔹 XXS Zapdos

    An XXS Zapdos is a particularly small specimen. Its size classification is separate from its IV quality and does not automatically make it a stronger battle Pokémon.

    🔷 XXL Zapdos

    An XXL Zapdos is a particularly large specimen. XXL status is primarily useful for collection, showcases and size-focused gameplay features.

    XXS/XXL vs IVs

    Size and IVs are separate attributes. A huge Zapdos is not automatically a hundo, and a tiny Zapdos is not automatically a low-IV Pokémon.

    18 • PVP

    Zapdos PvP Guide

    Zapdos can be useful in Trainer Battles because its typing and charged-move pressure allow it to threaten a variety of opponents.

    Great League

    Great League is not the most natural environment for standard Zapdos because of its high CP and Legendary investment requirements. League-specific rules can nevertheless create opportunities.

    Ultra League

    Zapdos has more room to operate in Ultra League. Thunder Shock, Drill Peck and Thunderbolt provide a combination of fast energy generation, Flying pressure and Electric damage.

    Master League

    Master League allows Zapdos to use its full stat potential. A Level 50 15/15/15 Zapdos reaches 3,987 CP.

    Master League Recommendation

    Thunder Shock + Drill Peck + Thunderbolt

    22 • BATTLE GUIDE

    How to Use Zapdos

    Before a Raid

    • Check whether the boss is weak to Electric or Flying attacks.
    • Confirm Zapdos has the correct moveset.
    • Check Zapdos's level and IVs.
    • Consider the raid boss's moveset.
    • Compare Zapdos with stronger alternatives.

    26 • FAQ

    Zapdos FAQ

    What type is Zapdos?

    Regular Zapdos is Electric/Flying type.

    What is Zapdos weak to?

    Zapdos is weak to Rock and Ice attacks.

    What is Zapdos's Max CP?

    A perfect Level 50 regular Zapdos reaches 3,987 CP.

    What are the best Zapdos moves?

    Thunder Shock + Thunderbolt is a strong Electric raid combination, while Thunder Shock + Drill Peck is useful for a Flying-focused build.

    Is Shiny Zapdos available?

    Yes. Shiny Zapdos is available in Pokémon GO.

    Is Shadow Zapdos good?

    Shadow Zapdos can provide increased offensive pressure and can be attractive for raid-focused players.

    Should I purify Shadow Zapdos?

    Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before deciding, especially if your goal is maximizing raid damage.

    What is Lucky Zapdos?

    Lucky Zapdos is a Zapdos with the Lucky status, generally obtained through trading and useful because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

    What is a 100% Zapdos?

    A 100% or hundo Zapdos has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is XXL Zapdos?

    XXL Zapdos is an especially large size specimen. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

    What is XXS Zapdos?

    XXS Zapdos is an especially small size specimen. Like XXL, size is separate from IV quality.

    What type is Galarian Zapdos?

    Galarian Zapdos is Fighting/Flying type.

    What is Galarian Zapdos's Max CP?

    A perfect Level 50 Galarian Zapdos reaches 4,012 CP.

    Is Galarian Zapdos the same as regular Zapdos?

    They share the same Pokédex species number but are distinct regional forms with different typing, stats, weaknesses and battle roles.

    Does Zapdos evolve?

    No. Zapdos does not have a normal evolution.

    Is Zapdos good for raids?

    Zapdos can be a strong Electric attacker when equipped with an appropriate Electric moveset, although stronger alternatives exist.

    Is Zapdos good in PvP?

    Zapdos can be useful in Ultra League and Master League depending on its moveset, IVs and the current metagame.

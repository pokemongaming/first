```
Shiny Seadra

Shiny Seadra is the alternate-colour version of Seadra. A Shiny Horsea can retain its Shiny status when evolved into Shiny Seadra.

✨ Shiny Seadra

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not directly change Seadra's base battle stats. IVs, level and moveset remain more important for battle performance.

Is Shiny Seadra Rare?

Its rarity depends on the encounter method and the event during which Horsea or Seadra is available. Availability and Shiny encounter chances should be checked against current Pokémon GO data.

```
```

Shadow Seadra

Shadow Seadra is the Shadow version of Seadra. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon, making Shadow Seadra a separate investment consideration.

🌑 Shadow Seadra

Shadow Pokémon generally provide increased offensive pressure while receiving reduced defensive durability.

Shadow Seadra Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Seadra's Water typing. Its primary type weaknesses remain Electric and Grass.

Should You Purify Shadow Seadra?

  • Check the Shadow Seadra's IVs.
  • Consider whether you want the Shadow battle profile.
  • Compare it with your regular Seadra and Kingdra.
  • Do not purify automatically just for higher IVs.
```

```
Purified Seadra

Purified Seadra is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Seadra. Purification removes the Shadow status and creates a Purified Pokémon.

✨ Purified Seadra

A Purified Seadra should be evaluated based on its final IVs, moveset, Stardust requirements and your intended use.
```

```
Lucky Seadra

Lucky Seadra is a Lucky Pokémon version of Seadra. Lucky Pokémon are primarily valuable because they can require less Stardust for powering up compared with standard Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Seadra

Lucky status does not directly increase Seadra's base species stats, but reduced Stardust costs can make long-term investment more efficient.

Should You Keep a Lucky Seadra?

  • Keep high-IV Lucky Seadra specimens.
  • Consider evolving strong Lucky Seadra into Kingdra.
  • Compare its IVs with your existing Kingdra projects.
  • Take advantage of reduced Stardust costs.
```

```
XXL Seadra

XXL Seadra refers to a Seadra with an unusually large size measurement. XXL status is separate from IVs, battle stats and Shiny status.

📏 XXL Seadra

XXL Pokémon can be valuable for collectors and size-related gameplay features. Being XXL does not automatically mean that a Seadra has higher battle stats.

XXL Seadra Can Also Be
```

```
Seadra FAQ

What type is Seadra?

Seadra is a Water-type Pokémon.

Seadra is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Seadra evolve?

Yes. Seadra can evolve into Kingdra when the applicable Pokémon GO evolution requirements are met.
```

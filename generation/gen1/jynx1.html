Joltik Overview

Joltik is a Bug/Electric-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 5. Its National Pokédex number is #595 and it evolves into Galvantula. In Pokémon GO, Joltik has a relatively low maximum CP compared with its evolved form, so it is generally more interesting to collectors, evolution projects, Little Cup-style formats where eligible, and Pokédex completion than as a top-level raid attacker.

Joltik PvP Guide

Joltik's low maximum CP makes it much more niche than Galvantula for Pokémon GO PvP.

Great League

Joltik can fit below the Great League CP limit, but its overall battle performance is considerably more limited than its evolved form. If using Joltik in a special format, evaluate its exact IV spread and current moveset rather than assuming a 100% IV is automatically optimal.

Ultra League & Master League

Joltik's low maximum CP makes it an unusual choice for Ultra League and Master League. Galvantula is generally the more natural competitive investment.

XXL Joltik

XXL Joltik is a size variant that is larger than the normal size range for Joltik. XXL status is separate from IV percentage and CP.

XXL

A large-size Joltik that can be valuable for collectors and size-focused Pokédex projects.

IV ≠ Size

A Joltik can be XXL without having perfect IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Why Keep an XXL Joltik?

  • Useful for size collection.
  • Can be evolved into an XXL Galvantula.
  • Adds variety to a complete Joltik collection.
  • Can be combined with other special attributes.

XXS Joltik

XXS Joltik is an unusually small Joltik size variant. Like XXL status, XXS status is separate from IV percentage and CP.

XXS

A very small Joltik that can be useful for collectors building a complete size collection.

Independent Attribute

XXS does not automatically mean low IVs. A tiny Joltik can still have excellent IVs.

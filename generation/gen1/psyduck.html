Why Psyduck's Typing Matters

Psyduck's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward battle profile. Water-type attacks are strong against several common Pokémon types, while Psyduck itself must watch out for Electric and Grass attacks.

Purified Psyduck

Purified Psyduck is created when an eligible Shadow Psyduck is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🌟

Purified Psyduck

A Purified Psyduck no longer has the Shadow status. It can be useful for collectors who want a complete set of Psyduck variants.

💎

Purification Consideration

Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

Purification is a permanent decision. If the Shadow version has collection or battle value for you, consider keeping it before purifying.

Is a Hundo Psyduck Worth Keeping?

Absolutely, if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon. A 100% Psyduck is also useful if you want to evolve it into a perfect-IV Golduck for your collection.

A perfect IV rating does not automatically mean a Pokémon is one of the strongest choices for raids. Species stats, moves and battle roles still matter.

XXL Psyduck

XXL Psyduck is a size classification that indicates a particularly large individual Psyduck. Size is separate from IV quality.

XXL Psyduck Extra-large size classification
Collection Value Useful for size-focused collections and showcases

Does XXL Mean Perfect IV?

No. XXL size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Psyduck can have any IV combination, including low IVs or 100% IVs.

Collector Tip

An XXL 100% Psyduck can be an especially interesting collection piece because it combines a large size classification with perfect IVs.

XXS Psyduck

XXS Psyduck is a size classification for an especially small Psyduck. Like XXL, it is independent of the Pokémon's IV percentage.

XXS Psyduck Extra-small size classification
Collector Value Useful for size-based Pokédex and showcase goals

Can XXS Psyduck Have Perfect IVs?

Yes. Size and IVs are independent characteristics. An XXS Psyduck can theoretically also be a 100% IV Psyduck if its Attack, Defense and HP IVs are all 15.

Psyduck FAQ

What type is Psyduck?

Psyduck is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Psyduck weak to?

Psyduck is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Does Psyduck evolve?

Yes. Psyduck evolves into Golduck.

Is Shiny Psyduck available?

Shiny Psyduck is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Psyduck?

A 100% Psyduck has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Psyduck?

XXL Psyduck is an especially large size classification. Its size is independent of its IVs.

What is XXS Psyduck?

XXS Psyduck is an especially small size classification. Its size is independent of its IVs.

What is Lucky Psyduck?

Lucky Psyduck is a Psyduck with Lucky status, normally obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system.

Is Shadow Psyduck different from regular Psyduck?

Shadow Psyduck has the same Water typing but is affected by Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Psyduck?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow version's collection value and battle characteristics before purifying it.

Is Psyduck good for raids?

Psyduck is generally not a top-tier Water-type raid attacker. Stronger Water Pokémon are usually preferred for serious PvE investment.

Can a Psyduck be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Psyduck can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can a Psyduck be XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. An XXS Psyduck can also have perfect IVs because size does not determine IV quality.

Can Shiny Psyduck be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Psyduck can also become Lucky through eligible trading mechanics.

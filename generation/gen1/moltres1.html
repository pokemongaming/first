Why Moltres's Typing Matters

Moltres combines Fire and Flying typing, giving it useful offensive options against several Pokémon vulnerable to Fire or Flying attacks. Its Flying typing also changes how it interacts with Ground attacks and contributes to its overall resistance profile.

XXL Moltres

XXL Moltres is a size variant with an exceptionally large recorded size. Size is separate from IVs, CP and battle stats.

XXL Moltres EXTRA LARGE

A Moltres with an unusually large recorded height and/or weight.

Collection Value Size-focused collectors may want to keep notable XXL specimens.

XXL Moltres and IVs

XXL size does not mean that a Moltres has perfect IVs. An XXL Moltres can have any IV combination.

Important:

Size, IVs and CP are separate characteristics. An XXL Moltres can also be Shiny, Lucky or have another applicable status.

XXS Moltres

XXS Moltres is a very small size variant. Like XXL status, XXS is independent of the Pokémon's IV combination.

XXS Moltres EXTRA SMALL

A Moltres with an unusually small recorded size.

Collector Value Useful for players collecting unusual size variants.

XXS Moltres and IVs

XXS status does not indicate poor or perfect IVs. An XXS Moltres can have any combination of Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Moltres FAQ

Is Moltres a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Moltres is a Legendary Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1.

What type is Moltres?

Moltres is a Fire and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Moltres weak to?

Moltres is weak to Rock, Water and Electric attacks. Rock attacks are especially effective because of its dual typing.

Does Moltres evolve?

No. Moltres does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Moltres available?

Yes. Shiny Moltres is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Moltres good for raids?

Moltres can be useful as both a Fire and Flying-type raid attacker depending on its moveset and the matchup.

Is Shadow Moltres good?

Shadow Moltres can provide strong offensive pressure, making it attractive for players focused on raid damage.

Should I purify Shadow Moltres?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, particularly if you care about raid performance.

What is a 100% Moltres?

A 100% Moltres has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Moltres?

A Lucky Moltres is obtained through trading and benefits from a reduced Stardust power-up cost.

Can Moltres be XXL?

Yes. Moltres can be encountered with different size characteristics, including XXL and XXS classifications when supported by the game.

Does XXL Moltres have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Moltres can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Moltres have lower IVs?

No. XXS size does not determine IV quality.

Can Shiny Moltres also be Lucky?

Special statuses can overlap when the game allows the relevant encounter and trading mechanics. Always check the current Pokémon GO rules for specific combinations.

What is Moltres's maximum CP?

Standard Moltres has a maximum CP of approximately 3,917 under the standard Pokémon GO CP system.

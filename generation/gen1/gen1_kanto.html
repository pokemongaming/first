Generation 1 Pokémon Overview

What is Generation 1?

Generation 1 is the original Pokémon generation and introduced the first 151 Pokémon. These Pokémon originated from the Kanto region and include many of the most recognizable Pokémon in the franchise.

Original Starters

Kanto's three starter Pokémon are Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle. Each follows a three-stage evolution family.

Legendary Pokémon

Generation 1 introduced Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres and Mewtwo, along with the Mythical Pokémon Mew.

FAQ

Generation 1 Pokémon FAQ

How many Pokémon are in Generation 1?

Generation 1 contains 151 Pokémon, beginning with Bulbasaur at National Pokédex #001 and ending with Mew at #151.

Which region is Generation 1 from?

Generation 1 Pokémon were introduced as Pokémon from the Kanto region.

What are the Gen 1 starter Pokémon?

The original Kanto starter Pokémon are Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle.

What are the Gen 1 Legendary Pokémon?

Generation 1 introduced Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres and Mewtwo. Mew is the Mythical Pokémon associated with the original Pokédex.

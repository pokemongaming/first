Haunter PvP Guide

Haunter can be a high-risk and high-reward PvP Pokémon. Its offensive pressure can be useful, but its low bulk means careful shield and energy management are important.

Great League

Haunter can fit into Great League teams that value fast Ghost-type pressure and aggressive play. However, it can be punished quickly when exposed to Dark, Ghost, Ground or Psychic damage.

Ultra League

Haunter's relatively low maximum CP means that it is generally more naturally suited to lower CP environments than Master League.

PvP Playstyle

High damage potential, fast pressure and low durability. Haunter rewards aggressive and well-timed play.

Shiny Haunter

Shiny Haunter is the alternate-color version of Haunter. It is primarily a collector variant and can be evolved when the relevant evolution requirements are met.

✨ Shiny Haunter

Shiny Haunter has an alternate appearance while retaining the same underlying species, typing and battle mechanics as regular Haunter.

Shiny Haunter Battle Performance

Shiny status does not directly increase Attack, Defense or HP. A Shiny Haunter's usefulness depends on its IVs, level and moveset.

Shadow Haunter

Shadow Haunter is the Shadow variant of Haunter. Shadow status changes its battle profile and creates a separate investment decision compared with regular Haunter.

🌑 Shadow Haunter

Shadow Haunter has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability, making it particularly interesting for damage-focused players.

Purified Haunter

Purified Haunter is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Haunter. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and move considerations.

✨ Purified Haunter

Purified Haunter returns to a standard battle profile while retaining its identity as a specially obtained form.

Should You Purify Shadow Haunter?

  • Compare the Shadow and Purified battle roles.
  • Consider whether you value Shadow damage.
  • Check the IV improvement before purifying.
  • Consider evolution into Gengar.
  • Do not purify automatically without a purpose.

XXL Haunter

XXL Haunter is a size variant of Haunter. Size classification is primarily a collection and showcase feature rather than a direct increase to battle stats.

📈 XXL Haunter

XXL Haunter is larger than a standard Haunter and can be valuable for collectors interested in Pokémon size records and showcases.

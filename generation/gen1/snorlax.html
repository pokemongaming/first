02 • TYPE

Snorlax Type

💤
Normal Primary and only type

Why Snorlax's Typing Matters

Snorlax has a pure Normal typing. This gives it a relatively simple matchup profile: Fighting-type attacks are super effective, while Ghost-type attacks do not affect it.

The simplicity of its typing is balanced by Snorlax's large HP pool and useful defensive characteristics.

09 • PVP

Snorlax PvP Guide

PvP is one of Snorlax's most interesting applications. Its large HP pool, neutral typing and flexible charged-move options can make it difficult to handle.

Great League

Standard Snorlax can be used in CP-limited formats when its CP fits the league restrictions. League rules and cup-specific restrictions should be checked before building a team.

Ultra League

Snorlax can function as a bulky general-purpose Pokémon in Ultra League. Its neutral Normal typing and move coverage can allow it to pressure a variety of opponents.

Master League

Snorlax can also have value in Master League environments, especially when its bulk and charged-move coverage fit the current metagame.

Common PvP Concept

Lick + Body Slam provides Snorlax with consistent fast-move pressure and a relatively accessible charged attack.

19 • FAQ

Snorlax FAQ

What type is Snorlax?

Snorlax is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Snorlax weak to?

Snorlax is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Snorlax good in Pokémon GO?

Snorlax can be useful in PvP, gym gameplay and certain team compositions. It is generally less important as a dedicated raid attacker.

Is Snorlax good for PvP?

Yes. Snorlax can be useful in multiple PvP formats depending on its moveset, IVs, CP and the current metagame.

Is Shadow Snorlax better than regular Snorlax?

Not universally. Shadow Snorlax gains increased offensive pressure but loses defensive durability. The better choice depends on the battle.

Is Shiny Snorlax available?

Yes. Shiny Snorlax is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

Does Snorlax evolve?

Snorlax is the evolved form of Munchlax and does not evolve into another Pokémon.

Is Snorlax good for raids?

Snorlax can participate in raids, but it is generally valued more for its bulk than for high raid damage.

What is the best Snorlax moveset?

The best moveset depends on the role. Lick and Body Slam are commonly associated with Snorlax's PvP-oriented play.

Should I purify Shadow Snorlax?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Does Shiny Snorlax have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily an alternate appearance and does not automatically provide superior battle stats.

Does Snorlax have a Mega Evolution?

Snorlax does not currently have a Mega Evolution or Primal Reversion form in Pokémon GO.

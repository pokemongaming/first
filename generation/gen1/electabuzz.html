A 15/15/15 Electabuzz is perfect from an IV perspective. However, IV perfection is not always necessary. A good Electabuzz with useful stats can still be an excellent evolution candidate.

09 • PVP

Electabuzz PvP Guide

Electabuzz can have some PvP applications, although its role should be evaluated separately from Electivire and other Electric-type Pokémon.

Great League

Electabuzz's lower maximum CP makes it more naturally suited to CP-limited formats than Master League. Its Electric typing can provide useful pressure against Water and Flying Pokémon.

Ultra League

Electabuzz can be considered in certain limited or specialized formats, but it generally faces stronger competition from other Electric Pokémon.

Master League

Electabuzz's maximum CP is considerably lower than the requirements normally associated with top Master League Pokémon, making it a less natural choice for open Master League play.

Frustration & Shadow Moves

Important Shadow mechanic

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. During eligible events, trainers may be able to use a Charged TM to remove it. Check the current Team GO Rocket rules before spending resources.

Should You Purify Shadow Electabuzz?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Electabuzz simply to increase its appraisal. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result and consider whether you intend to evolve it into Shadow Electivire.

Shadow Electabuzz Availability

Shadow Pokémon availability can change through Team GO Rocket events, encounters and research. Current availability should therefore be checked before publishing a permanent claim that Shadow Electabuzz is obtainable.

18 • FAQ

Electabuzz FAQ

What type is Electabuzz?

Electabuzz is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Electabuzz weak against?

Electabuzz is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Electabuzz a Gen 1 Pokémon?

Yes. Electabuzz was introduced in Generation 1 and originates from the Kanto region.

What does Electabuzz evolve into?

Electabuzz evolves into Electivire in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Electabuzz available?

Yes. Shiny Electabuzz is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Electabuzz available?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket encounter rotation and related events.

Is Shadow Electabuzz better than regular Electabuzz?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on your intended role, resources and whether you plan to evolve the Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Electabuzz?

Do not purify automatically. Consider its IVs, moves, future evolution, resource requirements and your battle goals before making the decision.

Can Shiny Electabuzz evolve into Shiny Electivire?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Electabuzz evolves into Electivire.

Is Electabuzz good for raids?

Electabuzz can provide Electric-type damage, but players should compare it with Electivire and other stronger Electric attackers before making a major investment.

Is Electabuzz good in PvP?

Electabuzz can have niche PvP applications, particularly in CP-limited formats, but its usefulness depends heavily on the current format and moveset.

What is the perfect Electabuzz IV?

The perfect IV combination is 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP, commonly written as 15/15/15.

01 • OVERVIEW

Venomoth Overview

Venomoth is the evolved form of Venonat and is a dual Bug / Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 1. It does not evolve further in Pokémon GO. Its relatively modest maximum CP means it is not normally considered a top general-purpose raid attacker, but Venomoth can still have value for collectors, Pokédex completion and specialized PvP or themed teams.

02 • TYPE

Venomoth Type

Venomoth has two types: Bug and Poison. This combination gives it a different defensive profile from pure Bug-type Pokémon and determines which attacks deal super-effective damage.

Why Venomoth's Typing Matters

The Bug typing gives Venomoth access to Bug-type offensive coverage, while Poison provides additional resistances and access to Poison-type attacks. The dual typing also means its weaknesses and resistances should be evaluated differently from a pure Bug Pokémon.

09 • PVP

Venomoth PvP Guide

Venomoth's PvP value is more specialized than that of many commonly used Great League Pokémon. Its Bug / Poison typing can create useful matchup opportunities, but its overall performance depends heavily on the current moveset and format.

Great League

Great League is the most natural standard CP-limited environment to investigate for Venomoth. Before investing, compare the Pokémon's actual PvP moveset, IV spread and current Cup rules.

Ultra League

Venomoth's lower maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural than Great League. It can still be evaluated in specialized formats where its typing and moves are advantageous.

Master League

Venomoth is generally not a conventional Master League investment because its maximum CP is considerably below the level of Pokémon commonly used in the format.

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Venomoth

Lucky Venomoth is a special trade-related version of Venomoth. Lucky status does not create a new species or change Venomoth's typing.

🍀

Lucky Venomoth

Lucky Venomoth has the Lucky status and can be attractive to collectors and trainers looking for a more resource-efficient Pokémon to power up.

Why Collect a Lucky Venomoth?

  • Lucky Pokémon have a special visual background.
  • Lucky status is associated with reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.
  • Lucky trades provide an opportunity to obtain stronger IV spreads.
  • A Lucky Venomoth can be useful as a collection trophy even if it is not a raid priority.
Important:

Lucky status is different from Shiny status. A Venomoth can potentially have multiple desirable attributes depending on how it was obtained.

16 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Venomoth

Pokémon GO can record different Pokémon sizes. XXL and XXS are size classifications and should not be confused with different species or evolutionary forms.

📏
XXL Venomoth

An exceptionally large Venomoth classification. XXL status is primarily useful for collection, showcases and size-related gameplay features.

🔬
XXS Venomoth

An exceptionally small Venomoth classification. XXS Pokémon can be desirable for collectors and size-focused challenges.

Do XXL and XXS Change Battle Stats?

Size classification should not be treated as a replacement for IV evaluation. When comparing two Venomoth, examine their actual IVs, level and intended battle role rather than assuming that XXL automatically means stronger.

Collector Tip

A Shiny, Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL or XXS Venomoth can each serve a different collection purpose. You do not need to treat one attribute as automatically better than all the others.

22 • FAQ

Venomoth FAQ

What type is Venomoth?

Venomoth is a Bug and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Venomoth weak to?

Venomoth is vulnerable to Fire, Flying, Rock, Ice and Psychic-type attacks.

What is Venomoth resistant to?

Its Bug / Poison typing provides useful resistances against several attack types, including Grass, Fighting, Bug and Poison, with additional Poison-related defensive benefits.

Does Venomoth evolve?

Venomoth is the final evolution of Venonat and does not have a standard further evolution.

Is Shiny Venomoth available?

Yes. Shiny Venomoth is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods.

Is Shadow Venomoth available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket and encounter rotations. Check current game data before claiming it is currently obtainable.

What is a Purified Venomoth?

A Purified Venomoth is a Venomoth that was previously Shadow and was subsequently purified.

What is a Lucky Venomoth?

A Lucky Venomoth is a Venomoth that received Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

What is a 100% Venomoth?

A 100% Venomoth has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is XXL Venomoth stronger?

XXL is a size classification and should not be treated as a direct indicator of superior IVs or battle performance.

What is XXS Venomoth?

XXS Venomoth is an exceptionally small size classification that can be desirable for collection and size-related gameplay features.

Is Venomoth good for raids?

Venomoth is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Bug and Poison options are normally preferable for optimized raid teams.

Is Venomoth good in PvP?

Venomoth can have specialized PvP applications, particularly in CP-limited formats, but its value depends heavily on its current moveset and the active metagame.

Should I power up a 100% Venomoth?

A hundo is an excellent collector specimen, but perfect IVs alone do not make Venomoth a top raid attacker. Check its intended role before investing heavily.

Is Shiny Venomoth better than regular Venomoth?

Shiny status is primarily a cosmetic and collection attribute. It does not automatically make Venomoth stronger in battle.

Is Lucky Venomoth worth keeping?

Yes, especially for collectors. Lucky status can also make powering up a Pokémon more resource efficient because of its Stardust-related benefit.

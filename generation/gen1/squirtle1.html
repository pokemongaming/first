Squirtle Overview

Squirtle is a pure Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Wartortle and then Blastoise. Squirtle itself is not normally considered a top raid attacker, but it remains important for collectors, Pokédex completion, evolution projects and special variants.

Why Squirtle's Typing Matters

Squirtle's pure Water typing gives it a simple defensive profile. Water Pokémon are commonly useful against Fire, Ground and Rock opponents, while Water Pokémon must be careful around Electric and Grass attacks.

Squirtle Weaknesses & Resistances

How to Identify a Hundo Squirtle

Use the in-game appraisal system to check Squirtle's Attack, Defense and HP IVs. A Pokémon with all three IV categories at their maximum is a 100% IV Pokémon.

Hundo tip:

Do not transfer a Squirtle solely because its CP appears low. CP depends heavily on the Pokémon's current level, so a low-CP Squirtle can still have excellent IVs.

Squirtle PvP Guide

Squirtle has limited relevance in standard high-level Pokémon GO PvP compared with its evolved forms and stronger alternatives.

Great League

Squirtle's low maximum CP allows it to fit within Great League's CP limit, but its overall performance is generally niche. Cup-specific rules can sometimes make unusual Pokémon more interesting.

Ultra League

Squirtle is generally not a preferred Ultra League Pokémon because its maximum CP is comparatively low.

Master League

Squirtle is not suitable for normal Master League competition because its maximum CP is far below the levels commonly used in that format.

Lucky Squirtle

Lucky Squirtle is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and can be desirable for collectors and future evolution projects.

🍀

Lucky Squirtle

A Squirtle obtained as a Lucky Pokémon through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

Why Lucky Squirtle Is Useful

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky Squirtle can be evolved into Lucky Wartortle.
  • It can eventually become Lucky Blastoise.
  • Lucky Pokémon are popular among collectors.
  • Trading is one way to pursue Lucky Pokémon.

Lucky Squirtle IVs

Lucky Pokémon receive a special minimum IV floor when obtained through a Lucky trade. A Lucky Squirtle can therefore be an attractive candidate for a long-term investment, although IVs are still randomized.

XXL Squirtle

XXL Squirtle is a size variant that can occur when Squirtle is caught or obtained. XXL status does not mean the Pokémon has better IVs or higher base stats.

XXL Squirtle Extra-large size category

A useful collection target for trainers who collect size variants and medals.

Does XXL Squirtle Have Better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Squirtle can have poor IVs, average IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Should You Keep XXL Squirtle?

If you collect size variants, XXL Squirtle is worth keeping. An especially desirable collection specimen could combine XXL size with Shiny, Lucky or perfect IVs.

XXS Squirtle

XXS Squirtle is the extra-small size category. Like XXL status, XXS status is separate from IVs and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger.

XXS Squirtle Extra-small size category

A desirable collection target for trainers working on size-related achievements.

Does XXS Squirtle Have Special Stats?

XXS is a size classification rather than an IV classification. Check the appraisal screen separately when evaluating its IVs.

How to Use Squirtle

For Collection

Squirtle is an excellent collector Pokémon because there are many desirable combinations including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect IV, XXL and XXS.

For Evolution

If you want a strong Blastoise, evaluate Squirtle's IVs before spending Candy on evolution. A high-IV Squirtle can become a valuable long-term project.

For PvP

Squirtle itself has limited competitive relevance, so check the current league and cup rules before investing heavily.

For Raids

Squirtle is generally not the first choice for serious Water-type raid teams. Evolving it and comparing Blastoise with stronger Water attackers is usually more useful.

Squirtle FAQ

What type is Squirtle?

Squirtle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Squirtle weak against?

Squirtle is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

What is Squirtle's max CP?

Squirtle's maximum CP is approximately 1,069 at level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Squirtle available?

Yes. Shiny Squirtle is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and events.

Is Shadow Squirtle available?

Shadow Squirtle has been available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability can change.

What is a 100% Squirtle?

A 100% Squirtle has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Squirtle?

Lucky Squirtle is a Squirtle obtained as a Lucky Pokémon through trading mechanics.

Is XXL Squirtle rare?

XXL Squirtle is a size variant and can be desirable to collectors. Its rarity depends on encounter availability and individual size distribution.

What is XXS Squirtle?

XXS Squirtle is an extra-small Squirtle size variant. XXS status is separate from IVs.

Is Squirtle good for raids?

Squirtle itself is generally not a top raid attacker. Players seeking a stronger Water attacker should consider its evolution into Blastoise or other stronger Water-type Pokémon.

Does Squirtle evolve?

Yes. Squirtle evolves into Wartortle and then Blastoise.

How much Candy does Squirtle need to evolve?

Squirtle requires 25 Squirtle Candy to evolve into Wartortle, while Wartortle requires 100 Squirtle Candy to evolve into Blastoise.

Should I keep a perfect Squirtle?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Squirtle is a perfect IV Pokémon and can be valuable for collection or evolution into a perfect Blastoise.

Does XXL Squirtle have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Squirtle can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Squirtle have better stats?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not automatically provide better IVs or battle stats.

Should I purify Shadow Squirtle?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, Shadow status, collection value and your intended use before deciding whether to purify it.

22 • FAQ

Horsea Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Horsea?

Horsea is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Horsea weak to?

Horsea is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Horsea evolve?

Yes. Horsea evolves into Seadra, which can eventually evolve into Kingdra.

Is Shiny Horsea available?

Yes. Shiny Horsea is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Horsea?

A 100% Horsea has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Horsea?

XXL Horsea is an unusually large individual according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Horsea?

XXS Horsea is an unusually small individual according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Lucky Horsea worth keeping?

Lucky Horsea can be valuable for collectors and for players who want a Pokémon with reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Should I purify Shadow Horsea?

Not automatically. Consider your collection, battle goals and whether you want to preserve the Shadow status before purifying.

Is Horsea good for raids?

Horsea is generally not a top-tier raid attacker. Its greater value is as an evolutionary and collection Pokémon.

Is Horsea good in PvP?

Horsea has limited usefulness in standard PvP formats because of its relatively low stats and maximum CP.

Can Shiny Horsea evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Horsea can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

Can a Lucky Horsea be Shiny?

Yes. A Pokémon's Lucky status and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Pokémon can also become Lucky through trading.

Can Horsea be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Horsea can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Horsea be XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. An XXS Horsea can also have perfect IVs because size and IV statistics are separate attributes.

Machop Overview

Machop is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. It evolves into Machoke using Candy, and Machoke can later evolve into Machamp.

Why Machop's Typing Matters

Machop's Fighting typing gives it a clear offensive identity. Fighting-type attacks are especially useful against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark-type Pokémon.

Because Machop is an early evolutionary stage, its maximum combat potential is much lower than Machoke and Machamp. Most long-term battle investments are therefore better directed toward its evolved forms.

Should You Evolve Machop?

Yes, if your goal is to use the Machop family in battle or complete your Pokédex. Machamp has substantially more battle value than Machop because it is the final evolution.

  • Save high-IV Machop before transferring duplicates.
  • Collect enough Machop Candy for the full evolution line.
  • Keep strong specimens for Machamp projects.
  • Check the current moveset before investing Stardust.

    🍀 Lucky Machop

    Lucky Machop is a special status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

    🍀

    Lucky Pokémon

    A Lucky Machop is identified by its Lucky status and sparkling visual effect in the Pokémon storage interface.

    ⭐

    Minimum IVs

    Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon.

    Lucky Machop Investment

    Machop itself usually is not the final Stardust investment target. A Lucky Machop becomes more interesting when you intend to evolve it into Machoke and eventually Machamp.

    • Keep a Lucky Machop with strong IVs.
    • Consider evolving it into Machamp.
    • Use Lucky status to reduce Stardust costs.
    • Compare its IVs with your existing Machop collection.

    💯 100% IV Machop

    A 100% IV Machop has perfect Attack, Defense and HP Individual Values.

    15 Attack IV

    15 HP IV

    What Does 100% IV Mean?

    100% IV means that Machop has the maximum possible individual values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. This does not mean that every 100% IV Pokémon has the same CP at every level, because CP also depends on level.

    Perfect Machop Collector Tip

    If you find a 15/15/15 Machop, consider saving it as an evolution candidate for a perfect Machamp.

    📏 XXL Machop

    🔬 XXS Machop

    Machop FAQ

    What type is Machop?

    Machop is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

    What is Machop weak to?

    Machop is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic-type attacks.

    What Pokémon does Machop evolve into?

    Machop evolves into Machoke, which can then evolve into Machamp.

    How much Candy does Machop need to evolve?

    Machop requires 25 Machop Candy to evolve into Machoke. Machoke requires 100 Machop Candy to evolve into Machamp.

    Is Shiny Machop available?

    Yes. Shiny Machop is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

    Is Shadow Machop available?

    Shadow Machop can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations.

    Should I purify Shadow Machop?

    Not automatically. If you want to preserve the Shadow battle characteristics for its future evolution, compare the Shadow and Purified options before purifying.

    What is a 100% Machop?

    A 100% Machop has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    Can Machop be Lucky?

    Yes. Machop can become Lucky through eligible Pokémon GO trades.

    Can Machop be XXL?

    Yes. Machop can appear in the XXL size category.

    Can Machop be XXS?

    Yes. Machop can also appear in the XXS size category.

    Is Machop good for raids?

    Machop is primarily valuable as an evolution candidate. Machamp is much more relevant for battle than Machop itself.

    Is Machop good for PvP?

    Machop has limited PvP relevance compared with its evolved forms. Always evaluate the current Cup rules and moveset before investing.

    Should I keep a 100% Machop?

    Yes. A 15/15/15 Machop is an excellent collector's specimen and can be saved as a perfect Machamp evolution candidate.

    Should I keep a Shiny Machop?

    Yes, especially if you collect Shiny Pokémon or want to eventually evolve it into Shiny Machamp.

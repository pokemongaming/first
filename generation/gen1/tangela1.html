Tangela

01 • OVERVIEW

Tangela Overview

Tangela is a pure Grass-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Its distinctive body of blue vines makes it one of the more recognizable Pokémon from the original Kanto Pokédex.

In Pokémon GO, Tangela is more important as an evolution-family Pokémon and collection target than as a top-tier raid attacker. It can evolve into Tangrowth, which has a considerably stronger role in Grass-type battle teams.

Why Tangela's Typing Matters

Tangela's pure Grass typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. It takes super-effective damage from Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug attacks.

Its Grass typing also lets Tangela resist several common attacking types, including Water, Electric, Grass and Ground.

08 • IV GUIDE

Tangela IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to Tangela. A 15/15/15 specimen is considered a perfect IV Tangela.

Is a 100% Tangela Worth Keeping?

A 100% Tangela has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is an excellent collection specimen and can be useful if you want to preserve a perfect member of the Tangela family.

However, perfect IVs do not automatically make Tangela a top raid attacker. Its species and overall battle performance still matter.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Tangela

Purified Tangela is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Tangela. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can improve its IVs while also removing the Shadow battle modifier.

Purified Tangela

Purification adds +2 to each IV that is not already at 15, up to the maximum of 15.

Should You Purify Tangela?

Purification should be considered carefully. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle characteristics, purifying Tangela removes that advantage. If you prefer a purified Pokémon for collection purposes, appraisal, cost or other mechanics, purification can make sense.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Tangela

Lucky Tangela is a special status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups and are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor.

TRADE STATUS
🍀

Lucky Tangela

A Lucky Tangela is especially useful for players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Tangela?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky Tangela can be useful for collectors.
  • Lucky Tangela can be evolved into Tangrowth.
  • A Lucky status does not itself increase base Attack.
Important

Lucky is a Pokémon status rather than a separate species or permanent alternate form.

18 • PVP

Tangela PvP Guide

Tangela can have niche PvP applications, but its overall usefulness depends heavily on league rules, CP limits, moveset and the current Pokémon GO meta.

Great League

Tangela's relatively moderate CP makes the Great League the most natural place to investigate its PvP potential. However, it competes against a very large number of Grass-type and anti-Grass Pokémon.

Ultra League

Tangela can be used experimentally in higher CP formats, although its evolution Tangrowth generally provides a stronger investment path for players specifically interested in the Tangela family.

Master League

Tangela's maximum CP makes Master League a poor fit compared with Pokémon designed for that format.

24 • FAQ

Tangela FAQ

What type is Tangela?

Tangela is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Tangela weak to?

Tangela is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

Is Shiny Tangela available?

Yes. Shiny Tangela is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Tangela available?

Shadow Pokémon availability is controlled by Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check current game data before claiming it is currently obtainable.

Can Shadow Tangela be purified?

Yes, an eligible Shadow Tangela can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and changes its IVs according to the purification mechanic.

What is a Lucky Tangela?

Lucky Tangela is a Tangela that has received Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Tangela?

A 100% Tangela has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Tangela stronger?

No. XXL is a size classification and does not automatically make Tangela stronger in battle.

Is XXS Tangela stronger?

No. XXS describes size and is separate from Tangela's IVs and battle statistics.

Does Tangela evolve?

Yes. Tangela evolves into Tangrowth using the required Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Tangela good for raids?

Tangela can deal Grass-type damage, but it is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Tangrowth and other stronger Grass Pokémon are generally better choices for optimized raid teams.

Is Tangela good in PvP?

Tangela can have niche PvP applications depending on league rules and moveset, but its PvP value is considerably more limited than that of many specialized Pokémon.

Should I keep a 100% Tangela?

Yes. A perfect Tangela is a valuable collection specimen and can also be evolved into a perfect Tangrowth.

Should I transfer my XXL Tangela?

That depends on your collection goals. XXL Pokémon can be useful to collectors who track size variants, even when their IVs are not particularly high.

Should I keep an XXS Tangela?

If you collect size variants, an XXS Tangela is worth keeping. Otherwise, its value depends on its IVs, Shiny status, Shadow status and evolution potential.

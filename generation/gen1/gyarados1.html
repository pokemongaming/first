Gyarados FAQ

What type is Gyarados?

Regular Gyarados is a Water / Flying-type Pokémon.

What type is Mega Gyarados?

Mega Gyarados is Water / Dark type.

Is Shiny Gyarados available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game availability.

Is Shadow Gyarados good?

Shadow Gyarados can be valuable for players prioritizing offensive performance.

Should I purify Shadow Gyarados?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow Pokémon's intended role, IVs and your available resources before purifying.

Does XXL Gyarados have better battle stats?

No. Size and battle IVs are separate characteristics.

What is a 100% Gyarados?

A 100% Gyarados has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Gyarados Mega Evolve?

Yes. Eligible Gyarados can undergo Mega Evolution into Mega Gyarados using Pokémon GO's Mega Evolution mechanics.

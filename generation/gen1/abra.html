Abra Overview

Abra is a Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in the Kanto region. It evolves into Kadabra and eventually Alakazam, making Abra the first stage of one of the best-known Psychic evolution families in Pokémon.

Why Abra's Typing Matters

Abra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon. Psychic typing gives its evolution family a strong offensive identity against certain opponents, although Abra itself is generally a temporary stage for players planning to evolve Kadabra and Alakazam.

Abra Evolution

Abra is the first Pokémon in its evolution family.

Abra

First evolution stage.

Kadabra

Evolves from Abra using Abra Candy.

Alakazam

Final evolution and the primary long-term battle target for most players.

Evolution tip:

Before evolving a high-IV or special Abra, check whether you want to keep it as a collector's Abra or evolve it into Kadabra and Alakazam.

Abra Moves

Abra is usually not a major battle investment. Most players eventually evolve it, so its moves are generally less important than the final moveset of Kadabra or Alakazam.

Long-Term Recommendation

Evaluate Abra primarily as an evolution candidate rather than investing heavily in the unevolved form.

✨ Shiny Abra

Shiny Abra is a special alternate-color version of regular Abra. It is primarily valuable for collectors, although it can also be evolved into Shiny Kadabra and Shiny Alakazam.

Shiny Abra

A Shiny Abra retains its Shiny identity through evolution, making a strong Shiny specimen valuable for players who want a complete Shiny evolution line.

Should You Evolve Shiny Abra?

  • Keep one Shiny Abra if you want a complete collection.
  • Consider evolving duplicate Shiny Abra.
  • Check IVs before choosing a long-term evolution.
  • A Shiny Abra can become Shiny Kadabra and Shiny Alakazam.

Shadow Abra

Shadow Abra is the Shadow variant of Abra. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile compared with their regular versions.

Shadow Abra

Shadow Abra is generally more interesting as a future Shadow Kadabra or Shadow Alakazam evolution candidate than as a Pokémon to keep powered up in its base form.

Purified Abra

Purified Abra is created when an eligible Shadow Abra is purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its long-term collection and investment value.

Purified Abra

A Purified Abra can still evolve normally through Kadabra and Alakazam.

Should You Purify Shadow Abra?

  • Do not purify automatically.
  • Compare the future value of Shadow Alakazam.
  • Consider whether you collect Purified Pokémon.
  • Check IV changes before making the decision.
  • Remember purification cannot be reversed.

Lucky Abra

Lucky Abra is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky trade system. Lucky Pokémon can be particularly useful for players planning to invest Stardust in future evolutions.

🍀 Lucky Abra

A Lucky Abra can be an excellent long-term evolution candidate because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups compared with standard Pokémon.

Best Use for Lucky Abra

Abra itself is generally not the main investment target. The biggest value of a Lucky Abra is usually the ability to evolve it and power up its later evolution stages with reduced Stardust requirements.

XXL Abra

XXL Abra is an oversized specimen within Pokémon GO's size system. Its XXL classification is primarily a collection and showcase characteristic.

📏 XXL Abra

XXL Abra can be valuable for collectors interested in unusually large Pokémon and size-based showcases.

Does XXL Abra Have Better Stats?

XXL status should not automatically be treated as better IVs or a stronger battle Pokémon. Size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

Abra FAQ

What type is Abra?

Abra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Abra weak to?

Abra is weak to Dark, Bug and Ghost-type attacks.

Does Abra evolve?

Yes. Abra evolves into Kadabra and eventually Alakazam.

Is Shiny Abra available?

Shiny Abra can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Abra be Shadow?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation.

Can Shadow Abra be purified?

Eligible Shadow Abra can be purified, but purification is permanent.

What is a Lucky Abra?

Lucky Abra is a trade-based special variant with reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Abra?

A 100% Abra has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Abra stronger?

XXL size does not automatically mean better IVs or stronger battle performance.

Is XXS Abra rare?

XXS Abra is a special size classification and can be valuable for collectors interested in size variants.

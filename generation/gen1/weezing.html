Weezing

11 • SHINY

Shiny Weezing

Shiny Weezing is the alternate-color version of Kanto Weezing. A Shiny encounter does not receive a separate species number; it remains Weezing with the Shiny status attached to the Pokémon.

🟣

Regular Weezing

Standard Kanto Weezing coloration.

✨

Shiny Weezing

Alternate Shiny coloration while retaining Weezing's Poison typing.

Is Shiny Weezing Useful?

Shiny Weezing is primarily valuable for collectors, Living Pokédex projects and players who enjoy collecting alternate-color Pokémon. Shiny status itself does not automatically make Weezing stronger in battle.

Shiny status is separate from IV quality. A Shiny Weezing can have any IV combination, including 0%, 100% or anything between.

Shadow Weezing & Frustration

Newly obtained Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration, so players should check the current event rules before using Charged TMs.

Think Before Purifying

Purification removes the Shadow status. If you specifically want a Shadow collection piece or want to retain the Shadow battle profile, do not purify it automatically.

Purified Weezing

Purified Weezing Benefits

Purification is an irreversible choice. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before committing resources or changing a collectible Pokémon.
14 • LUCKY

Lucky Weezing

Lucky Weezing is a special trade-related status. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor.

🍀

Lucky Weezing

A Lucky Weezing can be especially attractive for players who want to build a collection while reducing the Stardust cost of powering it up.

Why Keep a Lucky Weezing?

Lucky Does Not Mean 100% IV

Lucky status and perfect IV status are different properties. A Lucky Weezing can have IVs below 15/15/15, while a Lucky Weezing can also become a perfect-IV Pokémon after the trade.

Weezing FAQ

What type is Weezing?

Kanto Weezing is a pure Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Weezing weak to?

Kanto Weezing is weak to Ground and Psychic attacks.

Is Weezing a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Weezing is a standard Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1.

Does Weezing evolve?

Weezing is the final evolution of Koffing and does not have another standard evolution.

Is Shiny Weezing available?

Shiny Weezing is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is Shadow Weezing?

Shadow Weezing is a Shadow version of Kanto Weezing. It retains the Poison typing while using Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

What is Purified Weezing?

Purified Weezing is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Weezing.

What is Lucky Weezing?

Lucky Weezing is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is 100% IV Weezing?

A 100% IV Weezing has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Weezing?

XXL Weezing is a Weezing with an exceptionally large size classification.

What is XXS Weezing?

XXS Weezing is a Weezing with an exceptionally small size classification.

Can Weezing be Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate properties, so a Shiny Weezing can also have perfect IVs.

Yes. Lucky status does not prevent a Pokémon from having perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, moves, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

Is Kanto Weezing the same as Galarian Weezing?

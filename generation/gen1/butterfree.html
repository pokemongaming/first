Butterfree Overview

Butterfree is the final evolution of Caterpie. It evolves from Metapod and is a dual Bug and Flying-type Pokémon. While Butterfree is generally more popular as a Pokédex and collection Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker, different versions such as Shadow, Purified, Lucky and Shiny Butterfree can make the species interesting for collectors.

Why Butterfree's Typing Matters

Butterfree's Bug and Flying typing creates a mixed weakness and resistance profile. Its Flying type gives it immunity-style advantages against some Ground interactions in the main series, while Pokémon GO uses its own damage effectiveness system. Players should always consider both of Butterfree's types when selecting counters or evaluating matchups.

Shadow Butterfree Mechanics

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from regular Pokémon. Shadow Butterfree should therefore be evaluated independently when deciding whether to power it up or purify it.

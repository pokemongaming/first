Why Kingler's Water Typing Matters

Kingler's pure Water typing gives it access to Water-type Same Type Attack Bonus when using Water attacks. This makes Crabhammer particularly important when building Kingler for Water-focused battles.

Should You Purify Shadow Kingler?

Purification is not automatically an upgrade. Before purifying a Shadow Kingler, consider whether you want to keep the Shadow battle characteristics or instead prefer the benefits associated with a purified Pokémon.

Shadow move warning:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Attack. Special Team GO Rocket events may provide opportunities to remove it with a Charged TM.

12 • PURIFIED KINGLER

Purified Kingler

Purified Kingler is a Kingler that has been purified after being obtained as a Shadow Pokémon. Purification increases each IV by a fixed amount, up to the maximum of 15, and changes the Pokémon from Shadow to Purified.

13 • LUCKY KINGLER

Lucky Kingler

🍀

Lucky Kingler

A Lucky Kingler is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups, making a good Lucky Kingler attractive for long-term investment.

Lucky Kingler IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, so a Lucky trade cannot produce an arbitrarily low-IV Pokémon. The exact IV combination still varies.

  • Useful for reducing Stardust investment.
  • Can be a good long-term powered-up Kingler.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.
  • A Lucky 15/15/15 Kingler is both Lucky and perfect IV.

15 • SIZE

XXL & XXS Kingler

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS are size categories rather than separate Pokémon forms.

XXL

XXL Kingler

XXL Kingler is an exceptionally large Kingler. Size classification is independent of IV quality.

  • Large size classification
  • Can still have any IV combination
  • Can also be Shiny or Lucky
XXS

XXS Kingler

XXS Kingler is an exceptionally small Kingler. It remains the same species and keeps the same basic battle identity.

  • Small size classification
  • Can have any IV combination
  • Can also be Shiny or Lucky
Important:

XXL, XXS, 100% IV, Shiny and Lucky are not mutually exclusive. A single Kingler could, for example, be Shiny, Lucky, XXL and 100% IV if all of those conditions happen to apply.

21 • FAQ

Kingler FAQ

What type is Kingler?

Kingler is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Kingler weak to?

Kingler is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Is Kingler good for raids?

Kingler can be a useful Water-type attacker, particularly with a strong Crabhammer moveset, although stronger Water attackers may be available.

What is the best moveset for Kingler?

Bubble and Crabhammer are a strong Water-focused combination for Kingler.

Is Shiny Kingler available?

Yes. Shiny Kingler is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Kingler available?

Kingler can exist as a Shadow Pokémon when obtained through the appropriate Shadow Pokémon route.

Should I purify Shadow Kingler?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of keeping the Shadow Pokémon with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

What is a Lucky Kingler?

A Lucky Kingler is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is a 100% Kingler?

A 100% Kingler has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Kingler?

XXL Kingler is an exceptionally large Kingler classified by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Kingler?

XXS Kingler is an exceptionally small Kingler classified by Pokémon GO's size system.

Can Kingler be Shiny and Lucky at the same time?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate properties, so a Kingler can have both.

Can Kingler be 100% IV and XXL?

Yes. IV quality and size are separate properties. A Kingler can be both perfect IV and XXL.

Does Kingler evolve?

Kingler is the evolution of Krabby and does not have a standard further evolution in Pokémon GO.

What generation is Kingler from?

Kingler was introduced in Generation 1 and is originally from the Kanto region.

Why Omanyte's Typing Matters

Omanyte's Water / Rock combination gives it a mixture of offensive and defensive matchups. However, the dual typing also creates several important weaknesses that players should understand before using Omanyte in battle.

Omanyte Weaknesses & Resistances

Major Weaknesses

🌿
Grass

Grass-type attacks are especially dangerous against Omanyte because its Water / Rock typing creates a double weakness to Grass damage.

⚡
Electric

Electric-type attacks deal super-effective damage against Omanyte.

🥊
Fighting

Fighting-type attacks can exploit Omanyte's Rock typing.

🌎
Ground

Ground-type attacks are super effective against Omanyte.

Other Weaknesses

🪨
Rock

Rock attacks deal super-effective damage.

⚙️
Steel

Steel-type attacks can also hit Omanyte super effectively.

Shiny Omanyte

Shiny Omanyte has an alternate coloration and is a popular collection target for Fossil Pokémon collectors.

SHINY FORM

✨ Shiny Omanyte

Shiny Omanyte retains the same Water / Rock typing, base species and evolution path as regular Omanyte. The major difference is its alternate appearance.

  • Shiny status does not change Omanyte's basic typing.
  • Shiny Omanyte can evolve while retaining its Shiny identity.
  • Shiny specimens are useful for collectors and living Pokédex projects.
  • Check the current encounter method before hunting for Shiny Omanyte.

    • Shadow Omanyte

    🌑 Shadow Omanyte

    Shadow Omanyte retains its Water / Rock typing. The Shadow mechanic changes its offensive and defensive battle profile.

    Lucky Omanyte

    Lucky Omanyte is a special trade-related variant with the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon have useful Stardust investment characteristics.

    LUCKY POKÉMON

    🍀 Lucky Omanyte

    Lucky Omanyte has the same Water / Rock typing as regular Omanyte. Its special value comes from the Lucky status rather than a different species or typing.

    🍀 Lucky Status

    Obtained through Pokémon GO's trade mechanics.

    ⭐ Collection Value

    Useful for Lucky Pokédex collections.

    💎 Investment

    Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

    100% IV Omanyte

    A 100% IV Omanyte has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo.

    15 Attack IV
    15 Defense IV
    15 HP IV
    Perfect Omanyte

    A 15/15/15 Omanyte is a perfect-IV specimen. Whether it is worth powering up depends on your intended use, available resources and whether you plan to evolve it into Omastar.

    XXL Omanyte

    XXL Omanyte is a size variant with an unusually large Pokémon size measurement.

    SIZE VARIANT

    📏 XXL Omanyte

    XXL status does not create a new species or change Omanyte's Water / Rock typing. It is primarily a size and collection characteristic.

    • XXL is a size classification.
    • XXL Omanyte remains Water / Rock type.
    • XXL status can be valuable for collectors.
    • XXL Omanyte can be evolved into Omastar.

    XXS Omanyte

    XXS Omanyte is a size variant representing an unusually small Omanyte.

    SIZE VARIANT

    📐 XXS Omanyte

    XXS status does not change Omanyte's species, typing or normal evolution path. Its main importance is its size classification and collector value.

    • XXS represents an unusually small size.
    • XXS Omanyte remains Water / Rock type.
    • XXS specimens can be kept for size collections.
    • XXS Omanyte can evolve into Omastar.

    Omanyte Candy & Evolution Investment

    Omanyte Candy is useful for evolving Omanyte into Omastar and powering up Pokémon from the Omanyte family.

    • Save strong Omanyte specimens before transferring duplicates.
    • Check IVs before spending Candy on evolution.
    • Keep Shiny Omanyte if you are building a complete collection.
    • Consider keeping Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens separately.
    • Save a perfect-IV Omanyte if you value Hundo collections.

    Omanyte is particularly interesting for collectors because of its Fossil identity and its range of possible variants, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS examples.

    Omanyte FAQ

    What type is Omanyte?

    Omanyte is a Water / Rock-type Pokémon.

    What is Omanyte weak to?

    Omanyte is especially vulnerable to Grass and is also weak to Electric, Fighting, Ground, Rock and Steel attacks.

    Does Omanyte evolve?

    Yes. Omanyte evolves into Omastar.

    Is Shiny Omanyte available?

    Shiny Omanyte is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods.

    What is Shadow Omanyte?

    Shadow Omanyte is the Shadow variant of Omanyte and retains its Water / Rock typing.

    Should I purify Shadow Omanyte?

    Do not purify automatically. Consider the purpose of the Pokémon and compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision.

    What is a Lucky Omanyte?

    A Lucky Omanyte is an Omanyte that has received Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trade mechanics.

    What is a 100% IV Omanyte?

    A 100% IV Omanyte has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is XXL Omanyte?

    XXL Omanyte is an Omanyte with a particularly large size classification.

    What is XXS Omanyte?

    XXS Omanyte is an Omanyte with a particularly small size classification.

    Can Shiny Omanyte evolve?

    Yes. Shiny Omanyte can evolve into Shiny Omastar.

    Can Shadow Omanyte evolve?

    Eligible Shadow Omanyte can evolve while retaining its Shadow status.

    Can XXL and XXS Omanyte evolve?

    Yes. Size classifications do not prevent an eligible Omanyte from evolving.

12 • LUCKY

Lucky Starmie

Lucky Starmie is a special trading outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Starmie

A Lucky Starmie can be particularly attractive for players who want to build it while spending less Stardust on power-ups.

Lucky Starmie Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term investment projects.
  • Can be valuable for collectors.
  • Lucky status does not change Starmie's typing.
  • Lucky status does not automatically make Starmie a top-tier raid attacker.
Trading note:

A Lucky Pokémon is created through trading and cannot simply be activated with an item.

18 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Starmie PvP Guide

Starmie's Water/Psychic typing gives it useful resistances, but its five weaknesses and overall battle profile mean it needs careful matchup selection.

Great League

Starmie can be considered in Great League builds where its typing and charged-move options fit the team. CP limits and the current PvP meta should always be checked before investing specifically for this league.

Ultra League

Starmie can also be considered in Ultra League environments. Its effectiveness depends heavily on moves, IV distribution, shields and the current opponent pool.

Master League

Starmie's maximum CP is much lower than the Pokémon commonly built for Master League, making it a less natural choice for unrestricted Master League teams.

23 • FAQ

Starmie FAQ

What type is Starmie?

Starmie is a Water/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Starmie weak to?

Starmie is weak to Bug, Dark, Electric, Ghost and Grass-type attacks.

What is Starmie's maximum CP?

A perfect Level 50 Starmie can reach 2,922 CP.

What are Starmie's best moves?

Water Gun and Surf are a strong Water-focused combination, while Psywave and Psychic can be used for a Psychic-focused build.

Is Shiny Starmie available?

Yes. Shiny Starmie is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Starmie available?

Yes. Shadow Starmie exists as a Shadow variant and can be purified.

What is Purified Starmie?

Purified Starmie is created by purifying Shadow Starmie. Purification removes the Shadow status.

What is Lucky Starmie?

Lucky Starmie is obtained through trading and has the Lucky Pokémon status, including reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Starmie?

A 100% or hundo Starmie has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Starmie?

XXL Starmie is a Starmie that falls into the game's XXL size category.

What is an XXS Starmie?

XXS Starmie is a Starmie that falls into the game's smallest size category.

Does Starmie have a Mega Evolution?

Yes. Mega Starmie is available in Pokémon GO. It is a Water/Psychic Mega Evolution of Starmie.

What is Mega Starmie's maximum CP?

Mega Starmie can reach 4,184 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Mega Starmie's best moves?

Water Gun + Surf is recommended for a Water-focused role, while Psywave + Psychic is useful for a Psychic-focused role.

Can Shiny Starmie become Mega Starmie?

Yes. A Shiny Starmie can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution.

Is Starmie good for raids?

Regular Starmie is generally a niche raid attacker. Mega Starmie is significantly more relevant because it can serve Water and Psychic roles.

Is Starmie good in PvP?

Starmie can be considered in Great and Ultra League environments depending on the current meta, moves, IVs and team composition.

Dratini

Dratini is a Dragon-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first stage of the Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite evolution family and as an important target for Candy, Shiny, Shadow, Lucky and high-IV collectors.

06 • EVOLUTION

Dratini Evolution Family

Should You Evolve Dratini?

Yes, if you have a strong specimen and enough Candy. Before evolving, check the Dratini's IVs, level, appraisal and whether you want to preserve a particular special variant.

  • Check IVs before evolving.
  • Keep strong Attack IV specimens for battle projects.
  • Consider saving a Shiny Dratini.
  • Keep interesting XXL and XXS specimens for your collection.
  • Compare multiple Dratini before spending Candy.

10 • LUCKY

Lucky Dratini

Lucky Dratini is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Dratini potentially useful for long-term investment.

✨ LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Dratini

Lucky status is separate from Shiny status. A Dratini can be Lucky without being Shiny, and a Shiny Dratini can also become Lucky through trading.

Why Keep a Lucky Dratini?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term evolution projects.
  • Valuable for collectors.
  • Can be combined with Shiny status through trading.
  • Useful when building a lower-cost Dragonite project.

Lucky Dratini Collection Value

A Lucky Dratini with strong IVs can be especially interesting because it combines the Lucky status with the potential to evolve into Dragonite.

11 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Dratini

A 100% IV Dratini has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a Hundo and is a desirable specimen for collectors and evolution projects.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% Dratini = 15/15/15

A perfect Dratini can be evolved into a perfect Dragonair and eventually a perfect Dragonite, making it a strong long-term collection target.

Should You Keep a 100% Dratini?

  • Yes — perfect IV Dratini is a valuable specimen.
  • Consider evolving it into Dragonite.
  • Save enough Candy before evolving.
  • Consider its level before spending Stardust.
  • Keep it unevolved if you specifically collect Hundo Dratini.
12 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL Dratini & XXS Dratini

Pokémon GO records size information separately from IVs. XXL and XXS Dratini can therefore be interesting collection targets even when their battle IVs are not perfect.

XXL

XXL Dratini

An XXL Dratini is a particularly large specimen. Size is a separate characteristic from Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

  • Large-size specimen.
  • Useful for collection goals.
  • Can have any IV combination.
  • Can also be Shiny or Lucky when applicable.
XXS

XXS Dratini

An XXS Dratini is an especially small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, its size classification is separate from its IV combination.

  • Small-size specimen.
  • Valuable for size collections.
  • Can have any IV combination.
  • Can be kept unevolved as a collection piece.
Size vs IV:

XXL or XXS does not automatically mean a Pokémon has good IVs. Check the appraisal separately when evaluating a Dratini for battle or evolution.

20 • FAQ

Dratini FAQ

What type is Dratini?

Dratini is a pure Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Dratini weak against?

Dratini is weak to Ice, Dragon and Fairy attacks.

What is Dratini's maximum CP?

Dratini's maximum CP is approximately 1,004 under the standard Pokémon GO level and stat system.

Does Dratini evolve?

Yes. Dratini evolves into Dragonair and then Dragonite.

How much Candy does Dratini need to evolve?

Dratini requires 25 Candy to evolve into Dragonair. Dragonair then requires 100 Candy to evolve into Dragonite.

Is Shiny Dratini available?

Yes. Shiny Dratini is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Dratini available?

Shadow Dratini has appeared through Team GO Rocket related content. Current availability should be checked against the latest Pokémon GO schedule.

Should I purify Shadow Dratini?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen with the purified result before making a permanent decision.

What is a 100% Dratini?

A 100% Dratini has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Dratini?

Lucky Dratini is a Dratini that has received the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Can Dratini be XXL?

Yes. Dratini can have size classifications including XXL and XXS when applicable to the Pokémon GO size system.

Does XXL Dratini have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Dratini can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Does XXS Dratini have better PvP stats?

XXS is a size classification and does not automatically indicate better IVs or battle performance.

Should I evolve a 100% Dratini?

A perfect Dratini is an excellent long-term evolution candidate if you want a perfect Dragonair or Dragonite.

Is Dratini good for raids?

Dratini itself has limited raid value compared with stronger evolved Pokémon. Its main value is as the first stage of the Dragonite evolution family.

Can Shiny Dratini become Shiny Dragonite?

Yes. Shiny Dratini retains its Shiny identity when evolved through Dragonair into Dragonite.

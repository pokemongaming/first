01 — OVERVIEW

Farfetch'd Overview

Farfetch'd is a dual Normal/Flying-type Pokémon from Generation 1. It is a relatively uncommon Pokémon and is especially recognizable because it carries a leek that it uses as part of its signature design.

Unlike many Pokémon, the Kanto form of Farfetch'd does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO. Galarian Farfetch'd is a separate regional form with its own evolution line.

Why Farfetch'd's Typing Matters

Farfetch'd combines Normal and Flying typing. This gives it a useful set of resistances, but it also leaves it vulnerable to several common attack types including Electric, Ice and Rock.

Should You Purify Shadow Farfetch'd?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Farfetch'd. Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow version for collection purposes or potential battle use before making the decision.

Frustration note:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Attack. The ability to replace Frustration depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket events and rules.

22 — FAQ

Farfetch'd FAQ

What type is Farfetch'd?

Kanto Farfetch'd is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Farfetch'd weak to?

Farfetch'd is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Does Farfetch'd evolve?

Kanto Farfetch'd does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO. Galarian Farfetch'd is a separate regional form that can evolve into Sirfetch'd.

Is Shiny Farfetch'd available?

Shiny Farfetch'd can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is 100% Farfetch'd?

A 100% Farfetch'd has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Farfetch'd?

A Lucky Farfetch'd is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trade mechanic and receives the associated Lucky power-up benefits.

What is Shadow Farfetch'd?

Shadow Farfetch'd is a Shadow variant that retains its Normal/Flying typing while using the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Farfetch'd?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, collection value, Shadow status and your intended use before purifying it.

What is XXL Farfetch'd?

XXL Farfetch'd is an unusually large individual Pokémon and can be valuable for collection and Showcase purposes.

What is XXS Farfetch'd?

XXS Farfetch'd is an unusually small individual Pokémon and can also be valuable for collection and Showcase purposes.

Is Farfetch'd good for raids?

Farfetch'd is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Pokémon should normally receive priority for serious PvE investment.

Is Farfetch'd good for PvP?

Farfetch'd has limited relevance in standard PvP, although special cups and future move changes can affect its usefulness.

Are Kanto and Galarian Farfetch'd the same?

No. They are separate regional forms. Kanto Farfetch'd is Normal/Flying, while Galarian Farfetch'd is a Fighting-type regional form.

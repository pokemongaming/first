Blastoise Pokémon GO Overview

08 — IV GUIDE

Blastoise IV Guide

Individual Values add to Blastoise's Attack, Defense and HP. A perfect Blastoise has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% Blastoise — 15/15/15

A 15/15/15 Blastoise is commonly called a "hundo" by Pokémon GO players. It is the maximum IV combination and is especially attractive to collectors and players who want a perfect specimen.

Is a 100% Blastoise Worth Keeping?

Yes. A perfect Blastoise is a valuable collection specimen and can become especially interesting if you intend to use Mega Blastoise. However, IV perfection should be considered alongside moveset, level and your actual gameplay goals.

09 — PVP

Blastoise PvP Guide

Great League

Blastoise can be considered in CP-limited formats when an appropriate moveset and IV spread are available. Its Water typing and access to charged-move pressure can give it useful matchups.

Ultra League

Blastoise can also be considered for Ultra League teams. The ideal IV spread can differ from the traditional 15/15/15 PvE target.

Master League

Regular Blastoise's CP ceiling makes it less natural for open Master League play compared with Pokémon specifically built for that environment.

PvP IV optimization is format-specific. A Pokémon with lower Attack IV can sometimes be preferable in a CP-limited league because of how CP is calculated.

15 — SIZE

XXL & XXS Blastoise

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Blastoise are size variants rather than separate species or evolutions.

XXL Blastoise Extra-large size classification

An XXL Blastoise is a particularly large specimen. Size classification can be useful for collection, showcases and other size-related features.

XXS Blastoise Extra-small size classification

An XXS Blastoise is a particularly small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, its size makes it interesting for collectors and size-related gameplay features.

Important:

XXL and XXS classifications should not be treated as separate Pokémon forms. They are size characteristics of the individual Pokémon.

23 — FAQ

Blastoise FAQ

What type is Blastoise?

Regular Blastoise is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Blastoise weak to?

Blastoise is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

Is Blastoise a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Blastoise is a standard Pokémon and the final evolution of the Kanto starter Squirtle.

Is Shiny Blastoise available?

Yes. Shiny Blastoise is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Blastoise?

Shadow Blastoise is the Shadow variant of Blastoise. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Blastoise?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

What is Lucky Blastoise?

Lucky Blastoise is a Blastoise with Lucky status, obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Blastoise?

A 100% Blastoise has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Players commonly call this a hundo.

What is XXL Blastoise?

XXL Blastoise is an extra-large size classification of an individual Blastoise, not a separate species.

What is XXS Blastoise?

XXS Blastoise is an extra-small size classification of an individual Blastoise.

Is Mega Blastoise good for raids?

Yes. Mega Blastoise can be a strong Water-type option for raids, particularly when Water damage is super effective.

Can Shiny Blastoise Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Blastoise can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Is a 100% Blastoise worth powering up?

A perfect Blastoise is an excellent collection and investment target, especially if you plan to use Mega Blastoise. However, moveset and gameplay role should also be considered.

Is Blastoise good for PvP?

Blastoise can have value in CP-limited PvP formats, depending on its moveset, IV spread and the current metagame.

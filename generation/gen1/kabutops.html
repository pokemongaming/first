Kabutops Overview

Why Kabutops' Typing Matters

Kabutops combines Rock and Water typing, giving it access to useful offensive coverage while also creating a distinctive defensive profile. The combination is particularly important when evaluating its weaknesses because Grass attacks are especially dangerous.

Kabutops PvP Guide

Kabutops has access to useful coverage, but its relatively low maximum CP and current competitive positioning limit its role in the major GO Battle League formats.

Great League

Kabutops can fit within the Great League CP limit, but its overall matchup profile and move options make it a specialist rather than a consistently dominant choice.

Ultra League

Kabutops can be built for Ultra League, although it is generally overshadowed by more established Rock- and Water-type options.

Master League

With a Level 50 maximum CP of only 3,067, Kabutops is poorly suited to the open Master League environment, where many Pokémon reach substantially higher CP values.

Should You Purify Shadow Kabutops?

Purification should be a deliberate choice rather than an automatic upgrade. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle modifier, keep the Pokémon Shadow. If you prefer a purified collection or want the benefits associated with purification, compare both options first.

  • Check the IVs before transferring a Shadow Kabutops.
  • Compare its moveset with your existing Rock and Water Pokémon.
  • Consider the Stardust cost of powering up Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not purify solely because the appraisal becomes better.
  • Keep unusual or high-IV Shadow specimens if you collect Shadow Pokémon.

Kabutops FAQ

What type is Kabutops?

Kabutops is a Rock- and Water-type Pokémon.

What is Kabutops weak to?

Kabutops is weak to Grass, Electric, Fighting and Ground attacks. Grass is the most dangerous because it deals double super-effective damage through the interaction of Kabutops' two types.

What is Kabutops' Max CP?

Kabutops has a Level 50 maximum CP of 3,067.

Is Kabutops good for raids?

Kabutops has usable Rock and Water attacks, but it generally does not compete with the strongest modern raid attackers.

What is Kabutops' best moveset?

Waterfall and Stone Edge is a strong general-purpose combination. Current rankings can change as Pokémon GO updates move values and available attacks.

Is Shiny Kabutops available?

Yes. Shiny Kabutops is available in Pokémon GO through the Shiny Kabuto evolution family when the relevant encounter is available.

Is Shadow Kabutops available?

Yes. Shadow Kabutops is available through the Shadow Kabuto evolution family when the relevant Shadow Pokémon are available.

Is Shadow Kabutops stronger than regular Kabutops?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive damage but reduced defensive durability. Whether Shadow Kabutops is preferable depends on the battle and your investment goals.

Should I purify Shadow Kabutops?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before committing to purification, especially if you value Shadow damage.

How do you evolve Kabuto into Kabutops?

Kabuto evolves into Kabutops using 50 Kabuto Candy.

Is Kabutops good in Master League?

Kabutops has a Level 50 maximum CP of 3,067, which makes it poorly suited to the open Master League compared with Pokémon that can reach much higher CP.

Is Kabutops a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Kabutops is a Fossil Pokémon from Generation 1 and evolves from Kabuto.

What region is Kabutops from?

Kabutops originates from the Kanto region and is Pokédex number 141.

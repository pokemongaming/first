Nidoran♂ FAQ

What type is Nidoran♂?

Nidoran♂ is a Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Nidoran♂ weak to?

Nidoran♂ is weak to Ground and Psychic attacks.

Does Nidoran♂ evolve?

Yes. Nidoran♂ evolves into Nidorino, which can eventually evolve into Nidoking.

Is Shiny Nidoran♂ available?

Yes. Shiny Nidoran♂ is an available special variant when offered through eligible encounters.

What is a 100% Nidoran♂?

A 100% Nidoran♂ has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Nidoran♂?

Shadow Nidoran♂ is the Shadow variant of Nidoran♂. Its battle characteristics differ from the regular form.

Should I purify Shadow Nidoran♂?

Not automatically. Consider whether you prefer retaining its Shadow status or having a Purified specimen before making the decision.

What is Lucky Nidoran♂?

Lucky Nidoran♂ is a special trade-related variant. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is XXL Nidoran♂?

XXL Nidoran♂ has an extra-large size classification. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Nidoran♂?

XXS Nidoran♂ has an extra-small size classification. It can be useful for collectors and size-based achievements.

Is Nidoran♂ good for raids?

Nidoran♂ is generally not considered a top raid attacker. Its evolutionary line is more relevant for players interested in battle performance.

Is Nidoran♂ good in PvP?

Nidoran♂ has limited PvP relevance compared with stronger Pokémon available in Pokémon GO.

Can Lucky Nidoran♂ be evolved?

Yes. A Lucky Nidoran♂ retains its Lucky status when evolved.

Can Shiny Nidoran♂ be evolved?

Yes. Shiny Nidoran♂ can evolve through its male evolutionary line while retaining its Shiny status.

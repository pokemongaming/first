Wartortle

Wartortle is a Water-type Pokémon from the original Kanto Pokédex and the evolved form of Squirtle. In Pokémon GO, Wartortle is primarily a Pokédex and evolution target, while its special variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS make it an interesting collector's Pokémon.

10 — IV

Wartortle IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points. A perfect Wartortle has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
💯 100% Wartortle

A 100% Wartortle has a 15/15/15 IV combination. This is commonly called a Hundo. For collectors, a perfect Wartortle can be especially desirable because it can later be evolved into a perfect Blastoise.

Is a 100% Wartortle Worth Keeping?

Yes. If you find a 15/15/15 Wartortle, it is generally worth keeping. You can also consider evolving it into a perfect Blastoise if you want a Hundo member of the Squirtle family.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Wartortle

Lucky Wartortle is not a separate species or form. Lucky is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have useful Stardust-related investment benefits.

23 — FAQ

Wartortle FAQ

What type is Wartortle?

Wartortle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Wartortle weak to?

Wartortle is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Wartortle evolve?

Yes. Wartortle evolves into Blastoise as the final stage of the Squirtle evolution family.

What is Wartortle's Pokédex number?

Wartortle is #008 in the National Pokédex.

Is Shiny Wartortle available?

Yes. Shiny Wartortle is available as the alternate-color version of Wartortle.

Can Wartortle be Shadow?

Shadow Wartortle can exist when an eligible Shadow Squirtle is evolved into Wartortle.

Can Shadow Wartortle be purified?

Yes. An eligible Shadow Wartortle can be purified, creating a Purified Wartortle.

What is a Lucky Wartortle?

Lucky Wartortle is Wartortle with the Lucky status obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is a 100% Wartortle?

A 100% Wartortle, commonly called a Hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Wartortle?

XXL Wartortle is an extra-large size specimen. XXL is a size characteristic and not a separate species.

What is XXS Wartortle?

XXS Wartortle is an extra-small size specimen. Like XXL, XXS describes size rather than creating a separate species.

Is Wartortle good for raids?

Wartortle is generally not a top-tier Water-type raid attacker. Its final evolution, Blastoise, is more relevant for battle-focused investment.

Should I evolve a 100% Wartortle?

If you want a perfect Blastoise, evolving a 100% Wartortle is a good way to continue the perfect-IV evolution line.

Should I keep Shiny Wartortle?

Yes. Shiny Wartortle can be valuable for collectors, especially if you want a complete Shiny Squirtle family.

Are XXL and XXS Wartortle different Pokémon?

No. XXL and XXS describe size. They are not separate species or separate Pokémon in the Pokédex.

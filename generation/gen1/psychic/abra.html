01 • DATABASE

Abra Overview

Abra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It is the first stage of the Abra family and evolves into Kadabra, which can then evolve into Alakazam. Abra is not normally considered a top-tier standalone raid attacker, but it remains useful for collecting, evolution projects, Pokédex completion and building toward Alakazam.

Why Abra's Typing Matters

Abra's Psychic typing gives it access to Psychic-type attacks and determines its defensive matchups. Psychic Pokémon are strong offensively against Fighting and Poison types. However, Abra must be careful around Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

06 • PVE

Abra Raid & PvE Performance

Abra is not normally a serious standalone raid attacker. Its low Defense, modest HP and relatively low CP make it far less useful in raids than evolved Psychic Pokémon. The primary PvE reason to collect Abra is to evolve it toward Alakazam.

Should You Power Up Abra?

For most players, powering up Abra directly is not an efficient use of Stardust. If you want a Psychic attacker, it is generally better to evolve a strong Abra and invest in its later evolution.

Investment Advice

Keep strong Abra, evolve strategically, and avoid spending large amounts of Stardust on Abra itself unless you specifically want to use it as a challenge Pokémon.

09 • PVP

Abra PvP Guide

Abra is generally a niche PvP choice because its low Defense and limited CP ceiling make it much less reliable than its evolved forms. It can occasionally appear in special cups or themed formats where unevolved Pokémon are allowed.

Great League

Abra can fit within the Great League CP limit, but its very low defensive profile makes it difficult to use effectively against many established competitors.

Ultra League

Abra is not a natural Ultra League Pokémon because its maximum CP is far below the 2,500 CP limit.

Master League

Abra is not suitable for Master League. Its CP ceiling is dramatically below the league's maximum.

PvP Investment Advice

If you enjoy unusual Pokémon or special cups, Abra can be an interesting project. For conventional PvP, focus your resources on more competitive Psychic Pokémon.

14 • RESOURCES

Abra Candy, Candy XL & Stardust

Abra Candy is primarily valuable because it allows you to progress through the Abra evolution family. If your objective is to build Alakazam, collecting a large Candy supply can be more useful than powering up Abra itself.

  • Save high-IV Abra for evolution projects.
  • Keep Shiny Abra if you are building a Shiny evolution family.
  • Compare Shadow and regular Abra before transferring either.
  • Collect Candy during events that increase Abra encounters.
  • Use Candy XL primarily for Pokémon you actually plan to power up.
  • Avoid unnecessary Stardust investment into Abra itself.

19 • FAQ

Abra FAQ

Is Abra a Generation 1 Pokémon?

Yes. Abra was introduced in Generation 1 and is Pokédex #063.

What type is Abra?

Abra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Abra weak to?

Abra is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks.

What does Abra evolve into?

Abra evolves into Kadabra and then into Alakazam.

What is Abra's Max CP?

Abra can reach approximately 1,448 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Abra good for raids?

Abra is generally not considered a strong standalone raid attacker because of its low defensive stats and limited CP. Its main value is as an evolution-stage Pokémon.

Is Abra good in PvP?

Abra is generally a niche PvP option because of its low bulk and limited CP ceiling.

Can Abra be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Abra is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Does Shiny Abra evolve into Shiny Kadabra?

Yes. Shiny status is retained when Abra evolves through its evolution family.

Is Shadow Abra available?

Shadow Abra can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and events. Availability can change over time.

Is Shadow Abra stronger than regular Abra?

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive damage increase but also take increased damage. The best choice depends on your goals and the eventual evolution you want to build.

Should I purify Shadow Abra?

Do not purify automatically. Consider its Shadow value, future evolution and your collection goals before making a permanent decision.

Should I power up Abra?

Usually not. Most players will get more value by saving resources for its evolved forms.

What is Abra's best use?

Abra's primary value is collecting, evolution and building toward Kadabra and Alakazam.

Is a hundo Abra worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Abra is worth keeping, especially if you intend to evolve it or collect perfect IV Pokémon.

Is Shiny Abra rare?

Shiny availability and encounter rates can vary by event and encounter source, so its practical rarity depends on how and when Abra is encountered.

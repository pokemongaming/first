15 — SIZE

XXL Mew

XXL Pokémon are larger-than-normal size variants. Size is separate from IV quality, meaning an XXL Mew does not automatically have better IVs.

XXL Mew Extra-large size classification

An XXL Mew can be an interesting collector's specimen. Its size does not automatically determine its battle IVs.

XXL + 100% Size and IVs are separate

A Mew can be both XXL and perfect IV if the individual Pokémon meets both conditions.

22 — FAQ

Mew FAQ

Is Mew a Legendary Pokémon?

Mew is classified as a Mythical Pokémon rather than a Legendary Pokémon.

What type is Mew?

Mew is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Mew weak to?

Mew is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks.

Does Mew evolve?

No. Mew does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Mew available?

Shiny Mew has been available through special Pokémon GO content. Current availability should be checked before claiming it is obtainable.

Is Mew good for PvP?

Mew can be useful in PvP because of its unusually broad movepool and ability to run unexpected coverage.

Is Mew good for raids?

Mew can provide flexible coverage, but it is not automatically the best choice for every raid role. Compare its current moveset with specialized attackers.

What is 100% Mew?

A 100% Mew has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Mew?

XXL refers to a Mew that receives an extra-large size classification in Pokémon GO.

What is XXS Mew?

XXS refers to a Mew that receives an extra-small size classification.

Can Mew be Lucky?

Mew is subject to Pokémon GO's Mythical Pokémon trading restrictions. Do not assume a Lucky Mew can be created through ordinary trading.

Can Mew be Shadow?

Shadow availability should be based on actual Pokémon GO mechanics and official content rather than treating Shadow Mew as a standard permanent form.

Can Mew be Purified?

Purified status requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Mew should not be presented as Purifiable unless Pokémon GO specifically provides such a mechanic.

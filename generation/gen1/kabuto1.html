Why Kabuto's Typing Matters

Kabuto's combination of Rock and Water typing gives it a different matchup profile from either a pure Rock-type or pure Water-type Pokémon.

The dual typing also means players should consider both type interactions when evaluating Kabuto for battles.

Kabuto Special Variants

Kabuto can be collected in several forms and classifications. Some are cosmetic or collection distinctions, while others affect how the Pokémon is obtained or evaluated.

✨

Shiny Kabuto

Shiny Kabuto has an alternate coloration and is a popular target for collectors. A Shiny Kabuto can also be evolved into Shiny Kabutops.

🌑

Shadow Kabuto

Shadow Kabuto is a Shadow Pokémon variant. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics and should be evaluated separately from regular Kabuto.

💎

Purified Kabuto

Purified Kabuto is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Kabuto. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its appraisal and resource requirements.

🍀

Lucky Kabuto

Lucky Kabuto is a special trading result. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background effect and require less Stardust to power up.

💯

100% Kabuto

A 100% Kabuto has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a hundo and is highly desirable for collectors.

📏

XXL Kabuto

XXL Kabuto is an exceptionally large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size classification. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

🔹

XXS Kabuto

XXS Kabuto is an exceptionally small specimen. Like XXL, XXS size is independent of the Pokémon's IV percentage.

🐚

Regular Kabuto

Regular Kabuto is the standard Rock/Water form and the starting point for the Kabuto evolution line.

Shiny Kabuto

Shiny Kabuto is a collectible alternate-color form. The Shiny status does not change Kabuto's basic Rock/Water typing.

XXL & XXS Kabuto

Pokémon GO records individual Pokémon sizes. XXL and XXS classifications are separate from IVs, meaning a Pokémon can be XXL or XXS regardless of whether it has perfect IVs.

📏

XXL Kabuto

XXL Kabuto is a very large specimen according to the game's size classification. XXL status is mainly valuable for collection, showcases and size-related achievements.

🔹

XXS Kabuto

XXS Kabuto is an exceptionally small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, its size classification is independent of its IV percentage.

Important:

Size and IV quality are different measurements. For example, an XXL Kabuto can also be a hundo, while another XXL Kabuto may have low IVs.

Kabuto FAQ

What type is Kabuto?

Kabuto is a Rock and Water-type Pokémon.

What is Kabuto weak to?

Kabuto is vulnerable to several types including Grass, Electric, Fighting, Ground and Steel.

Does Kabuto evolve?

Yes. Kabuto evolves into Kabutops.

Is Shiny Kabuto available?

Yes. Shiny Kabuto is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Kabuto?

A 100% Kabuto has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a hundo.

What is Shadow Kabuto?

Shadow Kabuto is the Shadow variant of Kabuto. It has the standard Rock/Water typing but uses Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Kabuto?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the permanent decision to purify.

What is Lucky Kabuto?

Lucky Kabuto is obtained through trading and benefits from the reduced Stardust cost associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is XXL Kabuto?

XXL Kabuto is a Kabuto classified as exceptionally large by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Kabuto?

XXS Kabuto is a Kabuto classified as exceptionally small by Pokémon GO's size system.

Can a Shiny Kabuto evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Kabuto can evolve into Shiny Kabutops.

Can a Lucky Kabuto evolve?

Yes. Lucky Kabuto can evolve while retaining its Lucky status.

Can an XXL Kabuto evolve?

Yes. Kabuto can evolve into Kabutops regardless of whether the Kabuto has an XXL classification.

Is Kabuto good for raids?

Kabuto is generally not a high-priority raid attacker. Its main value is collection and evolution into Kabutops.

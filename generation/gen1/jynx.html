15 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Jynx

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Ice damage is super effective.
  • Confirm Jynx has an appropriate Ice moveset.
  • Check its level and IVs.
  • Consider weather and team composition.
  • Compare it with stronger Ice alternatives.

During Team Building

Jynx works best when its Ice typing lines up with the raid boss's vulnerabilities. It should not automatically replace higher-performing Ice attackers.

Long-Term Investment

A Shiny Jynx may be more attractive as a collection project, while a strong regular Jynx can serve as a practical backup Ice attacker when your roster needs more coverage.

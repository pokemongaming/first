08 • IV GUIDE

100% IV Pidgeot

A perfect Pidgeot has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. However, the ideal IV spread depends on the league or role in which you plan to use it.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Perfect Pidgeot

A 15/15/15 Pidgeot is a perfect-IV specimen. For CP-limited PvP formats, however, a lower Attack IV can sometimes be preferable because it allows more investment in Defense and HP before reaching the league's CP limit.

09 • PVP

Pidgeot PvP Guide

Great League

Pidgeot can be useful in CP-limited PvP because its Flying typing, debuff pressure and charged-move options can create difficult matchups for opponents.

Ultra League

Pidgeot can also be considered in Ultra League when built around an appropriate moveset and IV spread. Its performance depends heavily on the current meta.

Master League

Pidgeot is generally less natural for Master League than for lower CP-limited formats because of its maximum stat ceiling.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Pidgeot

Purified Pidgeot is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pidgeot. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🌟
Purified Status Shadow status removed
⬆️
IV Increase Purification can increase each IV by the game's purification amount up to the normal IV cap.
💠
Purified Collection Useful for players collecting different Pidgeot forms.
Important:

Purification is irreversible. If your Shadow Pidgeot has special PvP or collection value, compare the options before purifying it.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Pidgeot

✨ Lucky Pidgeot

Lucky Pokémon have a special background and benefit from reduced Stardust costs for power-ups. A Lucky Pidgeot can therefore be attractive for players who want to build a high-level specimen while reducing the Stardust burden.

Lucky Pidgeot IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor. A Lucky Pidgeot does not automatically have perfect IVs, so check its actual Attack, Defense and HP IVs before investing.

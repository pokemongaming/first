Why Zapdos's Typing Matters

Zapdos combines Electric and Flying typing, giving it a useful offensive identity against Water and Flying targets while also providing several defensive resistances.

Its dual typing is especially valuable in Pokémon GO because Ground-type attacks do not affect Zapdos in the normal way due to its Flying typing.

09 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Zapdos PvP Guide

Zapdos has meaningful PvP potential because of its Electric/Flying typing and access to quick-charging charged attacks.

Great League

Zapdos can appear in Great League formats, although its Legendary status and CP profile mean that availability and specific league rules should be considered before investing a specimen for this format.

Ultra League

Ultra League can provide a more natural environment for Zapdos. Its Electric typing gives it useful matchups against several Flying- and Water-type Pokémon.

Master League

Master League is one of Zapdos's more natural PvP environments because there is no low CP ceiling. Its high Attack and access to Electric and Flying attacks can create useful matchup pressure.

PvP Move Consideration

Drill Peck is useful because it provides a relatively inexpensive Flying-type charged attack, while Thunderbolt or Zap Cannon can provide Electric pressure.

Should You Purify Shadow Zapdos?

Purification should be treated as a strategic decision, not an automatic upgrade. If your objective is maximum Shadow raid damage, keeping the Shadow form can be preferable.

  • Compare Shadow and purified performance.
  • Check the current moveset.
  • Consider Stardust and Candy costs.
  • Keep a strong Shadow specimen for PvE if useful.
  • Do not purify simply to increase IVs.

14 • EVOLUTION

Zapdos Evolution & Forms

Zapdos does not have a normal evolution chain in Pokémon GO. The Kanto and Galarian versions are separate forms of the same Pokédex species rather than evolutionary stages.

Zapdos Kanto Form
↔
Galarian Zapdos Galarian Form
There is no Candy evolution for Zapdos. Galarian Zapdos should therefore be treated as a regional form, not an evolved Zapdos.

21 • FAQ

Zapdos FAQ

What type is Zapdos?

Regular Zapdos is an Electric/Flying-type Legendary Pokémon.

What is Zapdos weak to?

Zapdos is weak to Rock- and Ice-type attacks.

What is Zapdos's Max CP?

A perfect Level 50 regular Zapdos reaches 3,987 CP.

Is Zapdos good for raids?

Yes. Zapdos can be a strong Electric-type raid attacker, particularly with an appropriate Electric moveset.

What is the best Electric moveset for Zapdos?

Thunder Shock and Thunderbolt are a strong Electric-focused combination.

Is Shiny Zapdos available?

Yes. Shiny Zapdos is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Zapdos good?

Shadow Zapdos can be an excellent damage-focused option, especially for players building optimized Electric raid teams.

Should I purify Shadow Zapdos?

Not automatically. Players focused on Shadow raid damage should carefully compare the Shadow and purified versions before purifying.

What type is Galarian Zapdos?

Galarian Zapdos is a Fighting/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Galarian Zapdos weak to?

Galarian Zapdos is weak to Electric, Fairy, Flying, Ice and Psychic attacks.

Is Galarian Zapdos the same as regular Zapdos?

They are different forms of the same Pokédex species, but they have different typing, stats, weaknesses and moves.

Is Shiny Galarian Zapdos available?

Shiny Galarian Zapdos is not currently available.

Does Zapdos evolve?

No. Zapdos does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Galarian Zapdos good in PvP?

Galarian Zapdos can be useful in Great, Ultra and Master League formats, although its effectiveness depends heavily on the current metagame and moveset.

Can Shadow Zapdos be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Shadow Zapdos is available through eligible encounters.

Can Galarian Zapdos evolve?

No. Galarian Zapdos is a regional form and does not evolve.

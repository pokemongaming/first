Ivysaur

Ivysaur is a Grass/Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 1 and the evolved form of Bulbasaur. In Pokémon GO, Ivysaur is primarily useful as a collection, evolution and PvP-oriented Pokémon, while its different variants can have very different investment value.

Ivysaur Overview

Ivysaur is the second stage of the Bulbasaur evolutionary family. It is a dual Grass/Poison Pokémon and evolves into Venusaur after collecting the required Bulbasaur Candy.

Why Ivysaur's Typing Matters

Ivysaur's Grass/Poison typing gives it useful resistances but also creates several important weaknesses. Trainers building PvP teams should consider both its typing and the current moveset before choosing Ivysaur.

11 • SHADOW

Shadow Ivysaur

Shadow Ivysaur is a Shadow variant of Ivysaur. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile because their Attack is increased while Defense is reduced.

🌑 Shadow Ivysaur

A Shadow Ivysaur can be useful for trainers who specifically want the Shadow version for collection, PvP experimentation or evolution into Shadow Venusaur.

Shadow Ivysaur Mechanics

15 • EVOLUTION

Ivysaur Evolution Family

Ivysaur is the middle stage of the Bulbasaur evolutionary family.

STAGE 1

Bulbasaur

Evolves into Ivysaur using 25 Bulbasaur Candy.

STAGE 2

Ivysaur

Evolves into Venusaur using 100 Bulbasaur Candy.

STAGE 3

Venusaur

Fully evolved Grass/Poison Pokémon and the final stage of the Bulbasaur family.

Special Variants Can Be Preserved Through Evolution

Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky characteristics can affect how an Ivysaur is treated when evolved. Before evolving a rare specimen, check its current status, IVs and the moves available to the resulting Venusaur.

16 • PVP

Ivysaur PvP Guide

Ivysaur can be more interesting in CP-limited formats than its relatively low maximum CP might suggest.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to consider Ivysaur because the format has a 1,500 CP limit. IV distribution, moveset and team composition are important when evaluating an individual Ivysaur.

Ultra League

Ivysaur's relatively low maximum CP makes it less natural for Ultra League compared with Pokémon designed for higher CP environments.

Master League

Standard Ivysaur is not generally designed for Master League because the format has no CP ceiling and favors much stronger fully evolved Pokémon.

PvP Tip

Do not automatically transfer a low-Attack, high-Defense/HP Ivysaur. CP-limited PvP can value different IV spreads than raids.

21 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use and Evaluate Ivysaur

Before Transferring

  • Check whether Ivysaur is Shiny.
  • Check whether it is Shadow or Purified.
  • Check the IV percentage.
  • Check whether it is Lucky.
  • Check for XXL or XXS size status.
  • Consider its PvP IV spread.

Before Evolving

  • Check the current Venusaur moveset.
  • Check whether a special event move is available.
  • Confirm the IVs are suitable for your goal.
  • Preserve rare Shiny, Shadow, Lucky or size variants if they matter to your collection.

Long-Term Value

Ivysaur is usually more valuable as part of the Bulbasaur family than as a high-end standalone attacker. For many players, the best Ivysaur to keep is the one that supports a specific goal: PvP, evolution, collection, Shiny hunting, Shadow collecting, Lucky investment or perfect-IV completion.

23 • FAQ

Ivysaur FAQ

What type is Ivysaur?

Ivysaur is a Grass/Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Ivysaur weak against?

Ivysaur is weak to Flying, Fire, Psychic and Ice attacks.

What is Ivysaur's max CP?

A perfect Ivysaur can reach 1,921 CP at Level 50.

Is Shiny Ivysaur available?

Yes. Shiny Ivysaur is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and can also be obtained by evolving a Shiny Bulbasaur.

Is Shadow Ivysaur available?

Shadow Pokémon availability changes with Team GO Rocket rotations and events, so current availability should be checked before publishing a live claim.

What happens when Shadow Ivysaur is purified?

Purification increases each IV by 2, up to a maximum of 15, changes the Pokémon to Purified status and provides Return. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Can Purified Ivysaur be 100% IV?

Yes. For example, a Shadow Pokémon with 13/13/13 IVs can become 15/15/15 after purification.

What is a 100% Ivysaur?

A 100% Ivysaur has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Ivysaur?

Lucky Ivysaur is an Ivysaur obtained through the Lucky Trade mechanic.

Is Lucky Ivysaur worth keeping?

It can be worthwhile, especially if you plan to evolve it and invest in the resulting Venusaur. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for Stardust-conscious investments.

What is XXL Ivysaur?

XXL Ivysaur is an Ivysaur with an extra-large size classification. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Ivysaur?

XXS Ivysaur is an Ivysaur with an extra-small size classification. It is primarily relevant to size collection and related game mechanics.

Is Ivysaur good for raids?

Ivysaur is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main value is usually associated with evolution, PvP-specific use and collection.

Is Ivysaur good for PvP?

Ivysaur can have value in CP-limited PvP formats, particularly when its IV distribution and moveset fit the specific league or cup.

How many Candy does Ivysaur need to evolve?

Ivysaur evolves into Venusaur using 100 Bulbasaur Candy.

Does Shiny Ivysaur stay Shiny when evolved?

Yes. A Shiny Ivysaur retains its Shiny identity when evolved into Shiny Venusaur.

Should I transfer a low-IV Ivysaur?

Check for Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL, XXS, PvP IVs and evolution value before transferring. IV percentage alone should not be the only filter.

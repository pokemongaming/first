Metapod Pokémon GO Overview

Metapod is a Bug-type Pokémon from the original Kanto region. It is the second stage of the Caterpie evolutionary family and sits between Caterpie and Butterfree.

In Pokémon GO, Metapod is primarily an evolutionary and collection Pokémon rather than a strong attacker. Its limited offensive stats and shallow movepool make it uncommon in serious raids and PvP. However, Metapod can still be useful for completing the Pokédex, earning XP through evolution and building a Caterpie family collection.

The three forms covered on this page are Regular Metapod, Shiny Metapod and Shadow Metapod.

Shiny Metapod in Pokémon GO

Shiny Metapod is a collectible alternate-color version of Metapod. Like other shiny Pokémon, its coloration is different from the standard form.

Shiny Evolution Tip

If you evolve a Shiny Caterpie into Shiny Metapod and later into Shiny Butterfree, the shiny status is retained through the entire evolution family.

Shadow Metapod

Shadow Metapod belongs to the Team GO Rocket Shadow Pokémon system. Shadow Pokémon receive a damage bonus while also suffering increased damage from opponents.

Attack Bonus

Shadow Pokémon deal increased damage.

Defense Penalty

Shadow Pokémon also take increased damage.

Evolution

Shadow Pokémon can retain their Shadow status when evolved.

Because Metapod itself has relatively limited battle potential, trainers will generally have more reason to evolve a useful Shadow Caterpie-family Pokémon rather than invest heavily in Metapod itself.

Should You Keep a 100% IV Metapod?

A perfect IV Metapod is primarily a collector's target. If you obtain a perfect Caterpie, Metapod or Butterfree, keeping the entire evolutionary family can be worthwhile.

Metapod in PvP

Metapod is generally not considered a competitive PvP Pokémon. Its low overall combat performance means that trainers normally choose other Bug-type Pokémon for Great League, Ultra League and Master League battles.

Metapod as a Raid Attacker

Metapod is not a recommended raid attacker. Its low Attack stat and limited offensive options make it unsuitable for serious raid teams.

If you are building a Bug-type raid team, focus your resources on stronger Pokémon with significantly higher Attack and better movesets.

Metapod Catch & Farming Guide

Catching Metapod can help trainers accumulate Candy for the Caterpie evolutionary family. However, catching Caterpie directly is usually the more efficient way to build Candy because Caterpie is the base evolution.

  • Catch Caterpie whenever available.
  • Use Pinap Berries when farming Candy.
  • Transfer unwanted Pokémon for additional Candy.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for evolution.
  • Keep Shiny specimens for collection.
  • Consider keeping strong Shadow specimens before evolving.

Metapod Tips for New Players

  1. Don't spend Stardust heavily on Metapod.
  2. Prioritize Candy for evolving into Butterfree.
  3. Keep an eye out for Shiny Caterpie and Shiny Metapod.
  4. Check IVs before transferring rare specimens.
  5. Keep useful Shadow specimens if you intend to build the complete Shadow evolution family.

Metapod Pokémon GO FAQ

What is Metapod's Pokédex number?

Metapod is #011 in the National Pokédex.

What type is Metapod?

Metapod is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What does Metapod evolve into?

Metapod evolves into Butterfree.

How much Candy does Metapod need to evolve?

Metapod requires 50 Caterpie Candy to evolve into Butterfree.

Is Shiny Metapod available?

Shiny Metapod can be obtained through the available shiny Caterpie evolutionary line when the shiny form is obtainable.

Is Metapod good in Pokémon GO?

Metapod has limited battle usefulness. Its main value is as part of the Caterpie evolutionary family, for Pokédex completion, collection and evolution into Butterfree.

Is Shadow Metapod worth powering up?

Shadow Metapod generally has little reason to receive significant Stardust investment because of its limited battle performance. Consider evolving useful specimens instead.

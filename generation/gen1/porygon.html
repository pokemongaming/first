What Does Porygon's Normal Typing Mean?

Porygon's pure Normal typing gives it a relatively simple matchup profile. It does not receive a second typing to add additional weaknesses or resistances. In Pokémon GO, Fighting-type attacks are super effective against Porygon, while Ghost-type attacks deal reduced damage.

Shadow Porygon and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Players should check the current Team GO Rocket event rules before using resources to change a Shadow Pokémon's Charged Move.

Should You Purify Shadow Porygon?

Do not purify automatically. The correct decision depends on your collection goals, IVs, available resources and whether you specifically want to retain the Shadow form.

  • Check the Shadow Porygon's IVs.
  • Consider its long-term collection value.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be replaced.
  • Compare Shadow and purified versions before committing.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust without a clear goal.

Porygon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Porygon Candy becomes especially useful because two evolution stages require Candy and special evolution items.

Porygon FAQ

What type is Porygon?

Porygon is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Porygon weak to?

Porygon is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What does Porygon resist?

Porygon resists Ghost-type attacks.

What is Porygon's Max CP?

Porygon's maximum CP is 1,944 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Porygon's stats?

Porygon has 153 Attack, 136 Defense and 163 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Porygon available?

Yes. Shiny Porygon is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Porygon evolve?

Yes. Porygon evolves into Porygon2 using 25 Candy and an Upgrade. Porygon2 can then evolve into Porygon-Z using 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

What is Porygon's best moveset?

Tackle and Hyper Beam are listed as Porygon's recommended Normal-focused PvE moveset.

Is Porygon good for raids?

Porygon is not considered a top-tier raid attacker. It is generally more valuable for evolution and collection purposes.

Is Porygon good in PvP?

Porygon is a niche PvP choice rather than a dominant meta Pokémon. Its suitability depends on the league, moveset and current metagame.

Is Shadow Porygon available?

Shadow Porygon is part of the Shadow Porygon family, but its current availability can depend on Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Is Shadow Porygon stronger than regular Porygon?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure while sacrificing defensive durability. Whether that trade-off is worthwhile depends on the battle and your investment goals.

Should I purify Shadow Porygon?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen with the purified result and consider whether you value the Shadow form, its IVs and its collection status.

Can Shiny Porygon evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Porygon can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

What items are required to evolve Porygon?

Porygon requires an Upgrade and 25 Candy to evolve into Porygon2. Porygon2 requires a Sinnoh Stone and 100 Candy to evolve into Porygon-Z.

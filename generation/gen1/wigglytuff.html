Wigglytuff Overview

Why Wigglytuff's Typing Matters

Wigglytuff's Normal/Fairy combination gives it useful defensive interactions. In particular, Dragon and Ghost attacks are strongly resisted, while Dark and Bug attacks are also resisted. Poison and Steel attacks are the primary threats.

Wigglytuff PvP Guide

Wigglytuff is much more interesting in PvP than its relatively low maximum CP might suggest. Its enormous HP and Charm pressure can make it especially useful in Great League.

Great League

Great League is Wigglytuff's most natural PvP environment. Its high HP provides substantial bulk, while Charm can put immediate pressure on opponents weak to Fairy damage.

Shiny Wigglytuff

Shiny Wigglytuff is available in Pokémon GO. Its Shiny coloration makes it a popular collector target.

✨

Shiny Wigglytuff

Shiny Wigglytuff retains the same species, typing and underlying battle statistics as regular Wigglytuff. The difference is its alternate appearance.

SHINY AVAILABLE

Is Shiny Wigglytuff Stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic. A Shiny Wigglytuff can still have excellent IVs, but being Shiny does not itself increase its battle stats.

Lucky Wigglytuff

Lucky Wigglytuff is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. A Wigglytuff can become Lucky when obtained through a Lucky Trade.

🍀

Lucky Wigglytuff

Lucky Pokémon have a special background and benefit from reduced Stardust costs when powered up.

Why Keep a Lucky Wigglytuff?

  • Reduced Stardust investment can make powering up more affordable.
  • Lucky Wigglytuff is useful for collectors.
  • A Lucky Pokémon can still have excellent IVs.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.
  • Lucky status is different from Shiny status.

XXL Wigglytuff

XXL Wigglytuff refers to an exceptionally large size record rather than a separate Pokémon species. Size variants are primarily relevant to collection, visual size and certain Pokémon GO features.

📏

XXL Wigglytuff

A Wigglytuff recorded at the XXL size category. Its size does not inherently increase Attack, Defense or HP.

SIZE VARIANT
🏆

XXL Collector Value

XXL Pokémon can be desirable for collectors, showcases and size-related achievements.

Does XXL Wigglytuff Have Higher CP?

No. XXL status should not be interpreted as a direct CP multiplier. CP is determined from the Pokémon's battle stats, IVs and level rather than simply its visual size category.

XXS Wigglytuff

XXS Wigglytuff is the opposite size extreme: an exceptionally small Wigglytuff recorded with an XXS size designation.

🔬

XXS Wigglytuff

An XXS Wigglytuff is primarily a collection and size variant. It remains the same species with the same basic typing.

SIZE VARIANT
⭐

XXS Collector Value

Very small Pokémon can be desirable to collectors looking for unusual size records.

Does XXS Wigglytuff Have Lower CP?

XXS status should not automatically be interpreted as a battle-stat penalty. The size designation and battle CP system should be treated as separate characteristics.

Wigglytuff FAQ

What type is Wigglytuff?

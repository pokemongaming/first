Pidgey Overview

Pidgey is a small Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region. It evolves into Pidgeotto and then Pidgeot, making it a simple three-stage evolution family.

Why Pidgey's Typing Matters

Pidgey's dual typing gives it access to both Normal and Flying characteristics. Its Flying typing also makes it immune to Ground-type attacks in the main series, while Pokémon GO uses its own simplified type-effectiveness system.

100% IV Pidgey

A 100% IV Pidgey has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. Perfect IVs are mainly valuable for collectors and players who want an optimal specimen to evolve.

Should You Keep a 100% Pidgey?

Yes, if you enjoy collecting perfect IV Pokémon or want a perfect Pidgeot evolution project. However, a perfect Pidgey is not automatically a top-tier raiding Pokémon simply because it has perfect IVs.

Purified Pidgey

Purified Pidgey is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pidgey. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🌟
Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has Shadow status.

💎
IV Increase

Purification can increase each IV by the applicable amount, subject to the game's IV limits.

⚡
Return to Standard

The Shadow battle bonus is removed after purification.

Should You Purify Pidgey?

Purification is primarily a personal choice for a Pidgey. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before purifying if you care about preserving the Shadow form or its battle characteristics.

Pidgey PvP Guide

Pidgey is not normally a major open-league PvP Pokémon because of its relatively low CP and stats. Its evolved forms can be more interesting in certain formats.

Great League

Pidgey's low maximum CP means it can theoretically fit within Great League limits, but its overall battle performance is generally much less important than many specialized PvP Pokémon.

Ultra League & Master League

Pidgey is not naturally suited to the higher CP leagues. Pidgeotto and Pidgeot should be considered separately when evaluating the evolution family for PvP.

Pidgey FAQ

What type is Pidgey?

Pidgey is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Pidgey weak to?

Pidgey is vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Does Pidgey evolve?

Yes. Pidgey evolves into Pidgeotto and then Pidgeot.

Is Shiny Pidgey available?

Shiny Pidgey is an available alternate form when featured through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Pidgey be Shadow?

Yes. Shadow Pidgey can exist when it is available through the relevant Team GO Rocket encounters.

Can Shadow Pidgey be purified?

Yes, an eligible Shadow Pidgey can be purified.

What is a Lucky Pidgey?

A Lucky Pidgey is a Pidgey that has received Lucky status through the game's trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Pidgey?

A 100% Pidgey has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Pidgey be XXL?

Yes. Pidgey can be encountered with different size classifications, including XXL specimens.

Can Pidgey be XXS?

Yes. XXS Pidgey can be valuable to players who collect unusually small Pokémon.

Is Pidgey good for raids?

Pidgey is not generally considered a strong raid attacker. Its primary value is as the first stage of the Pidgeot evolution family and as a collection Pokémon.

Should I keep a perfect Pidgey?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Pidgey can be a worthwhile collection specimen and can be evolved into Pidgeotto and Pidgeot.

Should I purify Shadow Pidgey?

There is no universal answer. Consider the Shadow form, IVs, intended use and your collection goals before purifying it.

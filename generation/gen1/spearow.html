Spearow

Spearow is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon from Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily useful as an early Pokédex Pokémon and as the first stage of the Spearow → Fearow evolution family. Collectors can also look for Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS Spearow variants.

01 • OVERVIEW

Spearow Overview

Spearow is a small dual-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. Its Normal/Flying typing gives it access to a combination of Flying and Normal attacks, while its evolution into Fearow makes it part of one of Kanto's early Flying-type evolution families.

Why Spearow's Typing Matters

Spearow's Normal/Flying typing gives it a useful set of resistances, but it also creates several weaknesses that are important to understand when using it in battles. Flying typing gives Spearow an immunity-like interaction with Ground-type damage in the main Pokémon type system, while Electric, Ice and Rock attacks are particularly important weaknesses.

What Is 100% IV Spearow?

A 100% IV Spearow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is commonly called a hundo or perfect-IV Pokémon.

20 • RESOURCES

Spearow Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Spearow Candy is primarily useful for evolving Spearow into Fearow and for powering up or unlocking moves. Because Spearow itself has limited competitive value, avoid spending large amounts of Stardust without a specific reason.

  • Collect enough Spearow Candy to evolve a strong specimen.
  • Keep high-IV and interesting special variants.
  • Check IVs before transferring duplicate catches.
  • Save Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens when they are important to your collection.
  • Use Candy XL only when a specific investment goal justifies the cost.

Spearow FAQ

What type is Spearow?

Spearow is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Spearow weak to?

Spearow is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What does Spearow evolve into?

Spearow evolves into Fearow using Spearow Candy.

How much Candy does Spearow need to evolve?

Spearow requires 50 Spearow Candy to evolve into Fearow.

Is Shiny Spearow available?

Shiny Spearow can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events. Check the current game rotation for active encounter methods.

What is 100% Spearow?

A 100% Spearow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a hundo or perfect-IV Spearow.

What is Shadow Spearow?

Shadow Spearow is the Shadow variant of Spearow. It retains the Normal/Flying typing while receiving the battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Spearow?

Not automatically. Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow version before making a permanent purification decision.

What is Lucky Spearow?

Lucky Spearow is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

What is XXL Spearow?

XXL Spearow is an unusually large Spearow that receives the XXL size designation in Pokémon GO.

What is XXS Spearow?

XXS Spearow is an unusually small Spearow that receives the XXS size designation.

Does XXL Spearow mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Spearow can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Spearow mean low IV?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not determine the Pokémon's IV percentage.

Is Spearow good for raids?

Spearow is generally not considered a strong raid attacker. Its primary value is usually related to collection, evolution and special variants.

Is Spearow good for PvP?

Spearow is not normally considered a top competitive PvP Pokémon. Its low CP ceiling limits its usefulness, although special formats can change individual Pokémon's value.

Can Shiny Spearow evolve into Shiny Fearow?

Yes. A Shiny Spearow retains its Shiny status when evolved into Fearow.

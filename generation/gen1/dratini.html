Dratini

Dratini is a Dragon-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the first stage of the Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Dratini is especially important for players working toward Dragonite and for collectors hunting its Shiny and Shadow forms.

Dratini Type

Why Dratini's Typing Matters

Dratini's pure Dragon typing gives it a relatively simple defensive profile. It is vulnerable to Dragon, Fairy and Ice attacks, while resisting several elemental attack types. Its greatest long-term value comes from its evolution into Dragonair and eventually Dragonite.

08 • IV GUIDE

Dratini IV Guide

IVs add additional Attack, Defense and HP points. For collectors and long-term evolution projects, 15/15/15 is the perfect IV combination.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Should You Keep a 100% Dratini?

Yes. A perfect Dratini can be an excellent long-term candidate for evolution into a perfect Dragonair and Dragonite, provided the resulting Pokémon fits your goals.

What About Low-IV Dratini?

Do not automatically transfer every low-IV Dratini. Shadow, Shiny, PvP-specific and high-level specimens can have value beyond a simple IV percentage.

09 • PVP

Dratini PvP Guide

Dratini is generally more useful as an evolution-family Pokémon than as a primary PvP investment.

Great League

Dratini can occasionally have niche use depending on the available moves, cup rules and current metagame. However, it should not automatically be treated as a top-tier Great League Pokémon.

Ultra League

Its relatively low maximum CP makes Dratini a less natural Ultra League investment compared with its evolved forms.

Master League

Dratini is generally outclassed by stronger evolved Dragon-type Pokémon in Master League environments.

PvP rankings change as moves, move timings, cups and the wider Pokémon GO metagame change. Use current battle data when making competitive recommendations.

Frustration:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace it. Always check current game rules before using Charged TMs on Shadow Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Dratini?

Do not purify a Shadow Dratini automatically. If your objective is to eventually build a powerful Shadow Dragonite, keeping the Shadow form may be more valuable than purification. Consider purification only after comparing the benefits of both options.

Shadow Dratini Availability

17 • FAQ

Dratini FAQ

Is Dratini a Dragon-type Pokémon?

Yes. Dratini is a pure Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Dratini weak to?

Dratini is weak to Dragon, Fairy and Ice attacks.

Does Dratini evolve?

Yes. Dratini evolves into Dragonair and then Dragonite.

Is Shiny Dratini available?

Yes. Shiny Dratini is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Dratini available?

Shadow Dratini can be available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations.

Dratini itself is not generally considered a top raid attacker. Its main value is its evolution into stronger Pokémon, especially Dragonite.

If your goal is to build Dragonair or Dragonite, evolving Dratini is normally the intended use of your Dratini Candy.

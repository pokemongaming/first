10 — LUCKY

Lucky Poliwrath

Lucky Poliwrath is a special status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Poliwrath

Lucky status does not change Poliwrath's species or typing. Its main advantage is reduced Stardust cost for power-ups, making a Lucky Poliwrath attractive for long-term investment.

Why Keep a Lucky Poliwrath?

  • Lower Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Useful for players building multiple PvP Pokémon.
  • Can be a valuable trade outcome for collectors.
  • Lucky status can make high-level investment more affordable.

13 — SIZE

XXS Poliwrath

XXS represents an unusually small Poliwrath. Like XXL, it is a size classification rather than a different Pokémon form.

🔎

XXS Poliwrath

An XXS Poliwrath is a very small specimen. Its size does not change Poliwrath's Water/Fighting typing or automatically determine its battle strength.

Why Keep an XXS Poliwrath?

XXS Pokémon can be interesting for collectors who track unusual size records. If the Pokémon also has high IVs, Shiny status, Lucky status or another desirable attribute, it can become an especially interesting collection specimen.

20 — FAQ

Poliwrath FAQ

What type is Poliwrath?

Poliwrath is a Water/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Poliwrath weak to?

Poliwrath is weak to Electric, Grass, Psychic, Flying and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Shiny Poliwrath available?

Yes. Shiny Poliwrath is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Poliwrath different from regular Poliwrath?

Yes. Shadow Poliwrath has Shadow battle mechanics, although it retains the Water/Fighting typing.

Should I purify Shadow Poliwrath?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your PvP, PvE and collection goals.

What is Lucky Poliwrath?

Lucky Poliwrath is a traded Pokémon that has obtained Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is a 100% IV Poliwrath?

A 100% IV Poliwrath has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Poliwrath?

XXL Poliwrath is an unusually large Poliwrath based on its size classification. XXL status does not make it a separate Pokémon.

What is XXS Poliwrath?

XXS Poliwrath is an unusually small Poliwrath. It remains the same Water/Fighting Pokémon.

Is Poliwrath good for PvP?

Poliwrath can be useful in PvP, particularly in formats where its typing, moves and bulk fit the current meta.

Is Poliwrath good for raids?

Poliwrath can contribute to raid teams, but its PvP utility is generally a more important reason to build one.

Is a 100% Poliwrath always the best PvP Poliwrath?

No. PvP often favors different IV distributions depending on the league and specific stat-product goals.

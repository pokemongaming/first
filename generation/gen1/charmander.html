Why Charmander's Typing Matters

Charmander's Fire typing determines which attacks deal super-effective damage to it and which attack types it can resist. The Fire type also carries into Charmeleon before Charizard eventually becomes a Fire/Flying Pokémon.

Why Keep Strong Charmander?

The main reason to keep a strong Charmander is its future evolution potential. A high-IV Charmander can become a high-IV Charmeleon and eventually a high-IV Charizard.

08 • IV GUIDE

Charmander IV Guide

Charmander has three IV categories: Attack, Defense and HP. Each IV can range from 0 to 15.

What Is a 100% Charmander?

A 100% Charmander is a Charmander with perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs. It is commonly called a hundo by Pokémon GO players.

Perfect IV Combination

Attack 15 • Defense 15 • HP 15

Shadow Charmander Mechanics

Shadow Pokémon receive a damage-related bonus while also suffering reduced defensive durability. This makes Shadow Charmander different from a normal Charmander even though both retain the Fire type.

Shadow Charmander Weaknesses

💧
Water

Water attacks remain super effective.

🪨

🌎

Ground attacks remain super effective.

Shadow Charmander and Frustration

Important:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration. Always check the current event rules before using resources on a Shadow Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Charmander?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Charmander simply because purification can increase its appraisal. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result based on your collection, battle goals and available resources.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Charmander

Purified Charmander is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Charmander. Purification changes several aspects of the Pokémon and removes its Shadow status.

💠 Purified Charmander
PURIFIED

Purified Charmander can be useful for collectors, evolution projects and players who prefer purified Pokémon. Its IV appraisal is increased as part of purification, subject to the game's mechanics.

Feature Shadow Purified
Shadow Status Yes No
Shadow Battle Bonus Retained Removed
Frustration Can be present Removed through purification
Purified Status No Yes

Purified Charmander IV Increase

Purification increases each of the Pokémon's Attack, Defense and HP IV values by a fixed amount, up to the game's maximum of 15. Therefore, the resulting IVs should be checked individually after purification.

Purification Decision

If the Charmander is a valuable Shadow specimen, compare its Shadow utility with the purified form before committing to purification.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Charmander

Lucky Charmander is a special trading result. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive appearance in the appraisal interface and require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Charmander
LUCKY

A Charmander can become Lucky after a successful trade. Lucky status is particularly useful for players who want to reduce Stardust costs when powering up Pokémon.

🍀
Lucky Status

Obtained through eligible trades.

✨
Reduced Stardust

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

Lucky Charmander IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. However, a Lucky Charmander is not automatically a 100% Charmander.

Lucky vs 100% Charmander

"Lucky" describes the Pokémon's special trade status, while "100%" describes its IV combination. A Charmander can therefore be Lucky without being perfect IV.

16 • PVP

Charmander PvP Guide

Charmander is generally overshadowed by its evolved forms in competitive PvP, but it can occasionally become relevant in special cups or restricted formats.

Great League

Charmander's low maximum CP allows it to fit under the Great League CP limit, but its overall performance depends heavily on the current cup rules and available moves.

Ultra League

Charmander is generally not a natural Ultra League investment because of its relatively low stats and maximum CP.

Master League

Charmander is not normally suited to open Master League competition because its maximum CP and battle stats are substantially below the Pokémon commonly used in that format.

PvP rankings can change as moves, cups and the competitive metagame change. Check current rankings before making a format-specific recommendation.

22 • FAQ

Charmander FAQ

What type is Charmander?

Charmander is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmander weak to?

Charmander is weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

What is Charmander's max CP?

Charmander's maximum CP is approximately 1,093 under standard Pokémon GO mechanics.

What are Charmander's base stats?

Charmander has 116 Attack, 93 Defense and 118 HP as its Pokémon GO base stat values.

Does Charmander evolve?

Yes. Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard.

Is Shiny Charmander available?

Yes. Shiny Charmander is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is a 100% Charmander?

A 100% Charmander has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Charmander?

Shadow Charmander is a Shadow version obtained through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters.

Should I purify Shadow Charmander?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's usefulness and your collection goals before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Charmander?

Lucky Charmander is a Charmander that became Lucky through a successful trade. Lucky Pokémon receive a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

Can Lucky Charmander be 100% IV?

Yes. Lucky status and IV percentage are separate characteristics, so a Lucky Charmander can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is XXL Charmander?

XXL Charmander is an unusually large size variant. XXL status does not automatically mean the Pokémon has high IVs.

What is XXS Charmander?

XXS Charmander is an unusually small size variant. Its size is separate from its IV appraisal.

Is Charmander good for raids?

Charmander itself is generally not a strong raid attacker. Its evolved forms, particularly Charizard and its Mega forms, are much more relevant for serious battle investment.

Should I evolve a 100% Charmander?

If you want a perfect-IV Charizard, a 100% Charmander is an excellent candidate. Before evolving during an event, check whether a special evolution move is available or desirable.

Can Shiny Charmander evolve into Shiny Charizard?

Yes. Its Shiny status is retained when it evolves through the Charmander family.

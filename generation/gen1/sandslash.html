13 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL Sandslash & XXS Sandslash

XXL and XXS Sandslash are size variants. Size is mainly a collection and showcase characteristic and does not normally change Sandslash's base combat stats.

📏 XXL Sandslash

An unusually large Sandslash. XXL Pokémon are particularly valuable for players who participate in size-related showcases and enjoy collecting extreme Pokémon sizes.

🔬 XXS Sandslash

An unusually small Sandslash. XXS Pokémon are rare collection variants and can be especially interesting for players building a complete Sandslash collection.

Size values are separate from IV values. An XXL Sandslash can have any IV combination, including 100%, while an XXS Sandslash can also be Shiny, Lucky or have other eligible statuses.

Sandslash FAQ

What type is Sandslash?

Regular Kanto Sandslash is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Sandslash weak to?

Sandslash is weak to Water, Grass and Ice-type attacks.

Is Shiny Sandslash available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and current game availability.

Is Shadow Sandslash stronger than regular Sandslash?

Shadow Sandslash has a different battle profile with greater offensive pressure but reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Sandslash?

Not automatically. The decision depends on whether you value Shadow battle performance, IV improvements or collector value.

What is a Lucky Sandslash?

Size and IVs are separate mechanics. XXL status does not automatically mean higher combat stats.

Can Sandslash be both Lucky and 100%?

Yes, a Lucky Sandslash can have perfect IVs if it receives the appropriate IV combination.

Can Sandslash be Shiny and Lucky?

Eligible Pokémon can potentially combine multiple collection attributes, depending on Pokémon GO trade and availability mechanics.

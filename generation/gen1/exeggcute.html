Exeggcute Overview

Exeggcute is a dual Grass/Psychic Pokémon from Generation 1. It is part of the Exeggcute evolution family and can evolve into Exeggutor using Candy. Although Exeggcute is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker, it can still be useful for collectors, evolution projects and special-form hunting.

Exeggcute FAQ

What type is Exeggcute?

Exeggcute is a Grass/Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Exeggcute weak to?

Exeggcute is vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, Bug and Dark-type attacks.

Does Exeggcute evolve?

Yes. Exeggcute evolves into Exeggutor.

Is Shiny Exeggcute available?

Shiny Exeggcute is an available Shiny variant when offered through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Exeggcute available?

Shadow Exeggcute availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation.

What is a 100% Exeggcute?

A 100% Exeggcute has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Exeggcute?

Lucky Exeggcute is an Exeggcute that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon trading.

What is XXL Exeggcute?

XXL Exeggcute is an unusually large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Exeggcute?

XXS Exeggcute is an unusually small specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Should I evolve my Shiny Exeggcute?

That depends on your collection goals. You may want to keep one Shiny Exeggcute unevolved and evolve another Shiny specimen if you want both forms in your collection.

Is Exeggcute good for raids?

Exeggcute is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Pokémon are normally better choices for serious raid investment.

Is Exeggcute good for PvP?

Exeggcute has limited PvP relevance and should generally be considered a niche or experimental choice rather than a standard meta Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Exeggcute?

Not automatically. Consider the benefits of retaining the Shadow status and compare them with what you want from the purified Pokémon.

Can Exeggcute be Lucky and Shiny?

Pokémon attributes such as Lucky and Shiny can coexist when the Pokémon is obtained through the appropriate mechanics.

Can Exeggcute be 100% IV and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size classifications are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can have perfect IVs while also having an XXL or XXS size classification.

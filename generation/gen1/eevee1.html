Why Eevee's Typing Matters

Regular Eevee is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its Normal typing gives it a relatively simple defensive profile, but Eevee itself is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker.

Do not purify automatically:

Purifying a Shadow Pokémon changes its characteristics. If you plan to evolve the Eevee, decide whether you prefer the Shadow or purified version before committing.

10 — PURIFIED EEVEE

Purified Eevee

Purified Eevee is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Eevee. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

💜

Purified Status

Purified Eevee is no longer a Shadow Pokémon and can be evolved normally into its available Eeveelutions.

⭐

IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by the game's applicable amount, up to the maximum.

Should You Purify Eevee?

There is no universal answer. If the Shadow version has strong battle value or you want to preserve its Shadow status, purification may not be desirable. If you value the purified bonuses or want a specific purified collection specimen, purification can make sense.

11 — LUCKY EEVEE

Lucky Eevee

Lucky Eevee is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Eevee useful for players who want to evolve and build an Eeveelution while reducing Stardust costs.

🍀 Lucky Eevee

Lucky status is particularly useful when you plan to power up the Pokémon or one of its Eeveelutions.

Lucky Eevee Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Guaranteed minimum IV floor from a Lucky Trade.
  • Useful for collectors building special Eeveelutions.
  • Excellent candidate for long-term investment.
12 — 100% IV

100% IV Eevee — Hundo Eevee

A 100% IV Eevee has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a Hundo Eevee by the Pokémon GO community.

Should You Keep a Hundo Eevee?

Yes. A perfect Eevee is an excellent collector specimen and can become a perfect-IV Eeveelution. Before evolving it, consider which Eeveelution best matches your goals.

13 — SIZE VARIANTS

XXL Eevee & XXS Eevee

Pokémon GO can identify individual Pokémon as exceptionally large or exceptionally small. XXL and XXS status is separate from IV quality and does not automatically make a Pokémon stronger.

📏 XXL Eevee

An exceptionally large Eevee. XXL status is primarily useful for collection, showcases and size-related gameplay features.

📐 XXS Eevee

An exceptionally small Eevee. XXS status is independent of IV percentage and can be useful for collectors and showcases.

Size vs IV:

XXL or XXS does not mean 100% IV. Size, IVs, appraisal and CP are separate characteristics.

15 — PVP

Eevee PvP Guide

Eevee itself is generally not the primary reason players build an Eevee for PvP. The more important decision is which Eeveelution to develop for a particular league or role.

Great League

Eevee can occasionally appear in specialty or restricted formats, but its evolved forms are usually more relevant to serious PvP team building.

Ultra League

Standard Eevee has limited relevance in Ultra League. Players interested in this level should generally investigate its Eeveelutions instead.

Master League

Eevee's low maximum CP makes it poorly suited to conventional Master League play.

17 — RESOURCES

Eevee Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Eevee Candy is particularly valuable because the same resource is used across the entire Eeveelution family.

  • Save Eevee Candy before committing to multiple evolutions.
  • Walk Eevee as your Buddy when you need additional Candy.
  • Save strong IV Eevee for specific evolution projects.
  • Keep special variants such as Shiny, Shadow and Lucky.
  • Collect Eevee XL Candy if you plan high-level investment.

19 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Eevee

Before Evolving

  • Check Eevee's IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny, Shadow or Lucky.
  • Check whether it is XXL or XXS.
  • Decide which Eeveelution you actually need.
  • Confirm the evolution method before spending Candy.

Collection Strategy

Eevee is one of the best Pokémon to collect in multiple forms. A long-term collector may want regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, Hundo, XXL and XXS examples.

Investment Strategy

Eevee itself usually should not receive significant Stardust investment unless you have a specific reason. In most cases, resources are better directed toward an Eeveelution or another stronger Pokémon.

22 — FAQ

Eevee FAQ

What type is Eevee?

Regular Eevee is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Eevee weak against?

Eevee is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Does Eevee evolve?

Yes. Eevee can evolve into several different Pokémon in Pokémon GO, including Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon and Sylveon.

Is Shiny Eevee available?

Yes. Shiny Eevee is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Eevee available?

Shadow Eevee can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket encounters and rotations.

Should I purify Shadow Eevee?

Not automatically. Consider the value of the Shadow Pokémon and the Eeveelution you intend to create before purifying it.

What is a Hundo Eevee?

A Hundo Eevee has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Eevee?

Lucky Eevee is an Eevee obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is an XXL Eevee?

An XXL Eevee is an Eevee identified as exceptionally large by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Eevee?

An XXS Eevee is an Eevee identified as exceptionally small by Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Eevee good for raids?

Eevee itself is not a top raid attacker. Its main PvE value comes from its evolved forms.

Is Eevee good in PvP?

Eevee has limited conventional PvP relevance. Its Eeveelutions are generally more important for battle team building.

Should I keep a 100% Eevee?

Yes. A 100% Eevee is an excellent collector specimen and can become a perfect-IV Eeveelution.

Can Shiny Eevee evolve?

Yes. Shiny Eevee can evolve into the available Eeveelutions while retaining its Shiny status.

01 · OVERVIEW

Jolteon Overview

Jolteon is the Electric-type Eeveelution introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Eevee using a Thunder Stone in the main Pokémon games, while Pokémon GO uses its own evolution mechanics.

Jolteon's Electric typing gives it a useful role against Water- and Flying-type opponents. Its relatively high Attack makes it more focused on dealing damage than absorbing hits.

Why Jolteon's Typing Matters

Electric attacks are especially useful against Water- and Flying-type Pokémon. Jolteon therefore has a natural offensive role in battles where Electric damage is super effective.

The main defensive drawback of pure Electric typing is its Ground weakness. This means Ground-type attacks should be avoided whenever possible.

18 · FAQ

Jolteon FAQ

What type is Jolteon?

Jolteon is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Jolteon weak against?

Jolteon is weak to Ground-type attacks.

What is Jolteon resistant to?

Jolteon resists Electric, Flying and Steel-type attacks.

What is Jolteon's Pokédex number?

Jolteon is Pokédex number #135.

What generation is Jolteon from?

Jolteon was introduced in Generation 1 and is part of the original Kanto Pokédex.

Does Jolteon evolve?

Jolteon is an Eevee evolution. Jolteon itself does not have another evolution.

Is Shiny Jolteon available?

Yes. Shiny Jolteon is available as an alternate coloration of Jolteon through eligible Shiny Eevee evolution opportunities.

Is Shadow Jolteon available?

Shadow Jolteon can exist when a Shadow Eevee is evolved into Jolteon under the applicable Pokémon GO mechanics and availability rules.

Is Shadow Jolteon stronger than regular Jolteon?

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive bonus while having reduced defensive durability. Shadow Jolteon can therefore deal more damage, but it may faint sooner.

Should I purify Shadow Jolteon?

Not automatically. If your objective is maximizing Shadow raid damage, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

Is Jolteon good for raids?

Jolteon can be useful as an Electric attacker, particularly for players who need an accessible Electric-type option. Its performance should be compared with the current strongest Electric attackers.

Is Jolteon good in PvP?

Jolteon's PvP usefulness depends on the league, moveset and current metagame. It should be evaluated against the current competitive environment.

What is Jolteon's maximum CP?

Jolteon's maximum CP is around 3,265 under the standard Pokémon GO CP system, although displayed CP depends on IVs and level.

What are Jolteon's base stats?

Jolteon's Pokémon GO base stats are approximately 232 Attack, 182 Defense and 163 HP/Stamina.

Can Shiny Eevee evolve into Shiny Jolteon?

Yes. A Shiny Eevee retains its Shiny status when it evolves into Jolteon.

01 — OVERVIEW

Gyarados Overview

Gyarados is the evolved form of Magikarp and one of the most recognizable Pokémon from the original Kanto Pokédex. In Pokémon GO, Gyarados combines high Attack, useful bulk and Water/Flying typing. It also has access to Shadow, Shiny and Mega forms.

02 — TYPE

Gyarados Type

🌊
Water / Flying Dual Type

Why Gyarados's Typing Matters

Gyarados's Water/Flying combination gives it several useful resistances, while also creating an important double weakness to Electric-type attacks. Its Flying typing also makes Ground-type attacks ineffective against it.

09 — PVP

Gyarados PvP Guide

Gyarados has historically been a useful PvP Pokémon because of its combination of bulk, typing and charged-move pressure.

Master League

Gyarados can be considered in Master League because of its relatively high CP ceiling. Dragon Breath, Water-type attacks and coverage moves can create different tactical options depending on the current metagame.

Ultra League

Gyarados can also be used in Ultra League when built around the appropriate CP limit and IV spread.

Great League

Standard Gyarados is generally less natural for Great League because of its high base stats, although special cups and unusual formats can change its relevance.

19 — FAQ

Gyarados FAQ

What type is Gyarados?

Regular Gyarados is a Water/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Gyarados weak to?

Gyarados is weak to Electric and Grass attacks. Electric attacks are especially effective because of Gyarados's double Electric weakness.

How do you evolve Magikarp into Gyarados?

Magikarp evolves into Gyarados using 400 Magikarp Candy.

Is Shiny Gyarados available?

Yes. Shiny Gyarados is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution.

Is Gyarados good for raids?

Gyarados can be useful as a Water-type attacker, although its exact ranking depends on the current Pokémon GO move pool and competing attackers.

Is Shadow Gyarados good?

Shadow Gyarados can be a strong damage-focused option because Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive power at the cost of additional damage taken.

Should I purify Shadow Gyarados?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before deciding, especially if your goal is raid performance.

What type is Mega Gyarados?

Mega Gyarados is a Water/Dark-type Pokémon.

Is Mega Gyarados good for raids?

Mega Gyarados can be an excellent Mega option for relevant Water- and Dark-type raid roles while also providing Mega-related team and Candy bonuses.

Can Shiny Gyarados become Mega Gyarados?

Yes. A Shiny Gyarados can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution.

Does Mega Evolution permanently change Gyarados?

No. Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation. The underlying Pokémon remains Gyarados.

Is Gyarados good in PvP?

Gyarados can be useful in PvP, particularly in formats where its typing, bulk and charged-move pressure fit the current metagame.

Is Gyarados better than Magikarp?

Gyarados is the evolved form and is substantially stronger in battle. Magikarp is primarily important as the source Pokémon for Gyarados evolution.

What is the best Gyarados moveset?

The preferred moveset depends on the battle format. For a traditional Water-focused PvE build, Waterfall with a strong Water charged move is a natural choice. Always check current move availability.

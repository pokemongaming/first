Koffing

Koffing is a Poison-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Kanto region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the first stage of the Koffing evolution family and for its collection of Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants.

Koffing Overview

Koffing is a Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 1. It evolves into Weezing after receiving enough Koffing Candy. Koffing is not normally considered a top-tier raid attacker, but it can be useful for collectors, evolution projects and special-form hunting.

Why Koffing's Typing Matters

Koffing's pure Poison typing gives it a defensive profile that differs from many other Pokémon. Poison Pokémon are particularly useful when dealing with certain Grass and Fairy matchups, while Ground and Psychic attacks are important threats.

What type is Koffing?

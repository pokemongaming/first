What Does Normal Typing Mean?

Regular Eevee is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it a straightforward weakness profile, but it does not provide the offensive type specialization that many evolved Eeveelutions have.

09 · PVP

Eevee PvP Guide

Great League

Regular Eevee is generally not a major Great League meta choice. Its evolved forms can have considerably different PvP roles.

Ultra League

Eevee itself has limited competitive relevance in Ultra League because of its modest CP and overall battle profile.

Master League

Eevee is not a natural Master League investment. Players interested in competitive Eevee-family Pokémon should instead evaluate the relevant Eeveelution for the format.

PvP rankings can change when Pokémon GO introduces new moves, move updates, balance changes and new Pokémon. Always check current battle data before making a competitive recommendation.
10 · SHINY

Shiny Eevee

Shiny Eevee is available in Pokémon GO and is especially popular with collectors because the Shiny can also be evolved into the Eeveelutions.

✨

Shiny Eevee

Alternate coloration

Shiny Eevee keeps its Shiny identity when evolved. This makes Shiny Eevee a popular starting point for collectors who want a complete collection of Shiny Eeveelutions.

Shiny Eevee Evolution

A Shiny Eevee can be evolved into a Shiny Eeveelution when the required evolution conditions are met. The resulting Pokémon retains its Shiny status.

Collector Tip

Before evolving a valuable Shiny Eevee, decide which Eeveelution you want and verify the current evolution requirements.

Shadow Eevee is a special Shadow variant of Eevee. It retains the Normal typing of regular Eevee while using the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics.

Shadow Eevee and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on Team GO Rocket events and current Pokémon GO rules.

Do Not Purify Automatically

If your goal is to preserve the Shadow mechanics, compare the Shadow Pokémon with its purified result before making a permanent purification decision.

Shadow Eevee Evolution

13 · RESOURCES

Eevee Candy & Stardust

Eevee Candy is particularly valuable because multiple evolution options use the same Candy resource.

  • Save Eevee Candy before committing to an evolution.
  • Keep strong IV Eevee for long-term evolution projects.
  • Consider keeping useful PvP IV spreads separately.
  • Keep Shiny Eevee if you want Shiny Eeveelutions.
  • Evaluate Shadow Eevee before purifying it.
  • Use events with increased Eevee encounters to build a larger Candy reserve.

15 · INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Eevee

Before Evolving

  • Check the Eevee's IVs.
  • Decide which Eeveelution you want.
  • Check the current evolution requirements.
  • Confirm whether the Eevee is Shiny or Shadow.
  • Consider whether you want to keep it as a collector specimen.

For Collectors

Eevee is one of the strongest collection targets because its evolution family covers multiple types and includes several highly recognizable Pokémon.

For PvP Players

Instead of simply choosing the highest CP Eevee, evaluate the IV spread and intended evolution. Different PvP formats can favor different IV distributions.

For Raid Players

Eevee itself should generally not be the first choice for raid investment. Its main battle value comes from the specialized Pokémon available through evolution.

17 · PLAYER TIPS

Common Eevee Mistakes

  • Evolving a valuable Eevee without deciding which Eeveelution you actually want.
  • Assuming a high CP automatically means perfect IVs.
  • Spending large amounts of Stardust on regular Eevee without a clear battle purpose.
  • Purifying a Shadow Eevee without considering its long-term value.
  • Using a naming trick without checking whether that method has already been used for the account.
  • Forgetting to check current evolution requirements.
18 · FAQ

Eevee FAQ

What type is Eevee?

Regular Eevee is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Eevee weak to?

Eevee is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Eevee good for raids?

Regular Eevee is generally not a high-priority raid attacker. Its main value is its evolution family.

How many Eeveelutions are in Pokémon GO?

Eevee currently has eight evolution forms in Pokémon GO: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon and Sylveon.

Is Shiny Eevee available?

Yes. Shiny Eevee is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Shiny Eevee evolve?

Yes. Shiny Eevee can evolve into Shiny Eeveelutions when the applicable evolution requirements are met.

Is Shadow Eevee available?

Shadow Eevee can appear through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before planning a hunt.

Should I purify Shadow Eevee?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, intended evolution, Shadow mechanics and your long-term goals before purifying it.

Is a 100% Eevee worth keeping?

A perfect 15/15/15 Eevee is a valuable collector specimen and can be an attractive candidate for evolution, depending on your goals.

Does Eevee evolve into Sylveon?

Yes. Sylveon is one of Eevee's eight available evolution forms in Pokémon GO.

Can Shadow Eevee evolve?

Shadow evolution mechanics should be checked against the current Pokémon GO rules before evolving a valuable Shadow Eevee.

What is the best Eeveelution?

There is no single best Eeveelution for every situation. The best choice depends on whether you care about raids, PvP, collection or simply filling a particular type role.

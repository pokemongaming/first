Why Kangaskhan's Typing Matters

Kangaskhan's pure Normal typing gives it a relatively simple defensive profile. It is particularly important to remember that Fighting-type attacks are its primary type weakness.

Purified Kangaskhan

Purified Kangaskhan is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Kangaskhan. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and other mechanics.

🌟
Purified Status

Purification removes the Shadow status and provides the standard purification benefits.

❤️
Reduced Purification Cost

Purified Pokémon can have different resource requirements for certain power-up actions.

✨
IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by a fixed amount, up to the normal IV maximum.

Should You Purify Kangaskhan?

Purification should be considered carefully. If you value the Shadow battle profile, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be preferable. If you want a purified collection Pokémon or a specific IV result, purification can make sense.

Lucky Kangaskhan

Lucky Kangaskhan is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Pokémon mechanics, usually involving trades. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive because they require less Stardust to power up.

🍀
Lucky Kangaskhan

A special trade-related status that can reduce Stardust costs for powering up.

💫
Lucky Background

Lucky status is separate from Shiny status. A Pokémon can potentially have multiple collectible characteristics.

⭐
Minimum IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon.

Should You Keep a Lucky Kangaskhan?

A Lucky Kangaskhan can be an efficient candidate for powering up because of its reduced Stardust requirements. It can be especially attractive if you plan to Mega Evolve Kangaskhan.

Kangaskhan PvP Guide

Kangaskhan's PvP usefulness depends heavily on its moveset, CP limits and the current Pokémon GO meta.

Great League

Kangaskhan can be evaluated for Great League when its CP and moveset fit the format, although it is not automatically a top choice simply because it has access to several different attacks.

Ultra League

Ultra League allows higher CP investments, making Kangaskhan's bulk and available moves more relevant. Its performance should be compared against current meta Pokémon before investing heavily.

Master League

Kangaskhan FAQ

What type is Kangaskhan?

Kangaskhan is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Kangaskhan weak against?

Kangaskhan is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Does Kangaskhan evolve?

Kangaskhan does not have a conventional evolution chain. It can Mega Evolve into Mega Kangaskhan.

Is Shiny Kangaskhan available?

Shiny Kangaskhan is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Kangaskhan available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

Should I purify Shadow Kangaskhan?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version's intended role with the purified version before making the decision.

What is a 100% Kangaskhan?

A 100% Kangaskhan has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Kangaskhan?

Lucky Kangaskhan is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky trade mechanics.

What is XXL Kangaskhan?

XXL Kangaskhan is an unusually large individual. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

What is XXS Kangaskhan?

XXS Kangaskhan is an unusually small individual. It can have any IV combination.

Is Mega Kangaskhan good?

Mega Kangaskhan can be useful for Mega-related raid utility, bonuses and collection goals. Its exact usefulness depends on the current game meta and available moves.

Can Shiny Kangaskhan Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Kangaskhan can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Can a Lucky Kangaskhan Mega Evolve?

A Kangaskhan with Lucky status can be used as a Mega Evolution candidate when Mega Evolution is available under the current Pokémon GO mechanics.

Is 100% Kangaskhan worth keeping?

Yes. A perfect 15/15/15 Kangaskhan is an excellent collector specimen and can be a strong candidate for Mega Evolution.

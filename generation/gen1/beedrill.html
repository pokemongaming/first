Shadow Beedrill and Frustration

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration as their Charged Attack when obtained. Rocket-related events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration, so check current event rules before spending resources.

Should You Purify Shadow Beedrill?

Do not purify a Shadow Beedrill automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result first, especially if you value the Shadow battle bonus or want to keep the Pokémon as a Shadow collector piece.

14 • PURIFIED

Purified Beedrill

Purified Beedrill is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Beedrill. Purification changes its status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

💎

Purified Beedrill

No longer Shadow and retains the purified status.

⭐

Purified IV Bonus

Purification increases each IV by the applicable purification bonus, up to the maximum.

⚡

Resource Benefit

Purified Pokémon can have reduced power-up and evolution resource requirements compared with normal Pokémon.

Purification is a strategic decision. A player focused on Shadow performance should compare both versions before purifying a valuable Shadow Beedrill.

What type is Beedrill?

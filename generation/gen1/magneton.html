Magneton Overview

Magneton is the evolved form of Magnemite and the middle stage of its evolutionary family. Its unusual Electric / Steel typing gives it several useful resistances, although Ground-type attacks are a major threat.

Why Magneton's Typing Matters

Magneton's Electric / Steel combination gives it a different defensive profile from many other Electric-type Pokémon. Steel typing provides a large number of resistances, while Electric attacks give Magneton access to useful damage against Water and Flying targets.

Magneton Weaknesses & Resistances

Understanding Magneton's dual typing is important when choosing raid counters or deciding whether to leave it in a particular battle.

Weaknesses

🌎
Ground

Ground-type attacks are especially dangerous to Magneton because of its Electric / Steel combination.

🔥
Fire

Fire-type attacks exploit Magneton's Steel typing.

🥊
Fighting

Fighting-type attacks can also deal super-effective damage against Magneton.

Important Resistances

⚡
Electric

Magneton resists Electric-type attacks.

🪽
Flying

Steel typing gives Magneton useful resistance to Flying.

🧚
Fairy

Steel typing makes Magneton resistant to Fairy attacks.

Magneton's Steel typing also gives it resistance to several additional attack categories, making it a defensively unusual Electric-type Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Magneton?

Purifying a Shadow Magneton is a decision rather than an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before spending Stardust and Candy.

  • Check the Shadow Magneton's IVs.
  • Review its current moves.
  • Consider the cost of powering it up.
  • Compare Shadow and purified performance.
  • Do not purify simply because the purified IVs will increase.

Purified Magneton

Purified Magneton is created when an eligible Shadow Magneton is purified. Purification changes several properties and can make the Pokémon cheaper to power up.

✨
Purified Status

Purified Magneton no longer has its Shadow status and receives the benefits associated with purification.

💎
IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by 2, up to the maximum of 15.

Lucky Magneton

Lucky Magneton is a special status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of power-ups.

🍀
Lucky Magneton

A Lucky Magneton can be identified by its Lucky status after a successful trade.

⭐
Lower Power-Up Cost

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when powering them up than standard Pokémon.

Lucky Magneton IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed minimum IV floor, although the exact IV combination can still vary. A Lucky Magneton can also be a 100% IV Pokémon.

Lucky 100% Magneton

A perfect Lucky Magneton combines the collector value of a 15/15/15 Pokémon with the Stardust benefits of Lucky status.

XXL Magneton

An XXL Magneton is an exceptionally large specimen recognized by Pokémon GO's size system.

📏
XXL Magneton

XXL status is related to a Pokémon's size and is separate from its IV percentage.

💪
Size ≠ IVs

A large Magneton does not automatically have better Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Should You Keep an XXL Magneton?

  • Keep unusual XXL specimens for your collection.
  • Check the IVs separately from size.
  • Consider keeping high-IV XXL Pokémon.
  • Keep especially interesting size and IV combinations.

XXS Magneton

An XXS Magneton is a particularly small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic and should be evaluated separately from IVs.

🔹
XXS Magneton

XXS identifies a very small Magneton and can be useful for collectors building a complete size collection.

📊
IVs Are Separate

XXS Magneton can have poor, average or perfect IVs. Size does not determine IV quality.

Magneton FAQ

What type is Magneton?

Magneton is an Electric / Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Magneton weak to?

Magneton is vulnerable to Ground, Fire and Fighting attacks.

What is Magneton's Pokédex number?

Magneton is #082 in the National Pokédex.

Does Magneton evolve?

Yes. Magneton can evolve into Magnezone when the required Pokémon GO evolution conditions are met.

Is Shiny Magneton available?

Yes. Shiny Magneton is available as an alternate appearance of Magneton.

What is a 100% Magneton?

A 100% Magneton has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Magneton?

Shadow Magneton is the Shadow variant of Magneton and has different battle characteristics from regular Magneton.

Should I purify Shadow Magneton?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent purification decision.

What is Lucky Magneton?

Lucky Magneton is a Magneton that has gained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is XXL Magneton?

XXL Magneton is a particularly large Magneton according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Magneton?

XXS Magneton is a particularly small Magneton according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Does XXL Magneton have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Magneton can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Magneton have better IVs?

No. XXS status does not automatically indicate better IVs.

Is Magneton good for raids?

Magneton can be useful as an Electric-type attacker, especially for players developing their early or mid-game Electric teams, although stronger options can be available.

Is Magneton better than Magnezone?

Magnezone is the final evolution of the family and is generally the more appropriate long-term investment when you want to build around this Pokémon family.

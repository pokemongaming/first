Why Bulbasaur IVs Matter

A strong Bulbasaur can eventually become a stronger Ivysaur and Venusaur. For this reason, many players evaluate IVs before evolving or powering up a specimen.

Bulbasaur Forms & Special Variants

Bulbasaur can exist in several collectible forms and statuses in Pokémon GO. These include Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect IV, XXL and XXS specimens.

🌱 Regular Bulbasaur

Regular Bulbasaur is the standard version of the species. Its main long-term purpose is evolving into Ivysaur and Venusaur.

✨ Shiny Bulbasaur

Shiny Bulbasaur has alternate coloration compared with the standard version. It is primarily a collector variant, although it can still be evolved and used like a normal Bulbasaur.

  • Shiny status does not automatically improve IVs.
  • A Shiny Bulbasaur can evolve into Shiny Ivysaur.
  • It can eventually become Shiny Venusaur.
  • Collectors may prefer keeping one unevolved.

🌑 Shadow Bulbasaur

Shadow Bulbasaur is a Shadow version of the species. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile and generally require additional resources for investment.

SPECIES Bulbasaur
TYPE Grass / Poison

EVOLUTION Ivysaur

Shadow Bulbasaur Investment

  • Consider evolving based on your long-term Venusaur plans.
  • Do not judge a Shadow Pokémon only by IV percentage.
  • Check current Frustration removal opportunities.
  • Compare the Shadow evolution line with your existing Pokémon.

✨ Purified Bulbasaur

Purified Bulbasaur is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Bulbasaur. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

🍀 Lucky Bulbasaur

Lucky Bulbasaur is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon are valuable for players planning to power up and evolve their Pokémon because of their special Stardust mechanics.

  • Useful for long-term evolution projects.
  • Can be evolved into Lucky Ivysaur.
  • Can eventually become Lucky Venusaur.
  • Can be valuable for collectors.

⬆️ XXL Bulbasaur

XXL Bulbasaur is a larger-size specimen. Size is mainly important for collecting and PokéStop Showcase-style competition where size can matter.

  • Larger than standard specimens.
  • Useful for size-based collecting.
  • Can be relevant for Showcases.
  • Does not automatically mean better IVs.

⬇️ XXS Bulbasaur

XXS Bulbasaur is an unusually small-size specimen. It is mainly a collection and rarity variant.

  • Smaller than standard specimens.
  • Primarily valuable for collectors.
  • Does not change normal species typing.
  • Does not automatically affect IV quality.

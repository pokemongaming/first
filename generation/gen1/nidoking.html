01 / OVERVIEW

Nidoking Overview

Nidoking is a Poison/Ground-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 1. It evolves from Nidorino when exposed to a Moon Stone in the main Pokémon series, while Pokémon GO uses Candy for evolution.

Why Nidoking's Typing Matters

Nidoking's dual Poison/Ground typing gives it a useful combination of offensive coverage and defensive resistances. Ground attacks are especially valuable against Electric, Fire, Poison, Rock and Steel targets, while Poison attacks can pressure certain Grass and Fairy opponents.

09 / PVP

Nidoking PvP Guide

Nidoking's Poison/Ground typing gives it interesting coverage, but its overall PvP performance depends heavily on its moveset and the current league meta.

Great League

Nidoking can be used in Great League if its CP and IV combination fits the league limit. Its dual typing gives it useful resistances and offensive coverage.

Ultra League

Ultra League Nidoking can provide a different option for trainers who enjoy experimenting with Poison and Ground coverage. IVs, moves and team composition are important when evaluating it.

Master League

Nidoking's maximum CP is generally not competitive with the highest-stat Master League Pokémon. It is therefore more naturally suited to limited formats and personal challenge teams.

12 / PURIFIED

Purified Nidoking

A Shadow Nidorino can potentially be purified before evolving into Nidoking. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the standard benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

✨
Purified Status

Purification removes the Shadow status.

📈
IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by 2, up to the maximum of 15.

💨
Purified Move

Purification changes the Charged Move associated with the Shadow state.

Should You Purify Nidoking?

Purification is a personal decision. If the Shadow Pokémon has strong combat value or collector value, keeping it Shadow may be preferable. If you prefer the purified form or want the benefits of purification, evolving a purified Nidorino into Nidoking is another option.

Investment Tip

Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before committing to purification.

13 / LUCKY

Lucky Nidoking

Lucky Nidoking is a Nidoking obtained through the Pokémon GO Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Nidoking

Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive to collectors and trainers looking to reduce the Stardust cost of power-ups.

Lucky Nidoking Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Special Lucky status in the Pokémon's appraisal.
  • Useful for collection goals.
  • Can be especially interesting when received through a favorable trade.

Lucky Nidoking IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. However, Lucky status does not automatically mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

24 / FAQ

Nidoking FAQ

What type is Nidoking?

Nidoking is a Poison- and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Nidoking weak to?

Nidoking is weak to Water, Ice, Ground and Psychic attacks.

Is Nidoking a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Nidoking is a non-Legendary Pokémon from the original Kanto Pokédex.

Is Shiny Nidoking available?

Yes. Shiny Nidoking is an alternate-color variant of Nidoking.

Is Shadow Nidoking available?

Shadow Nidoking can exist through the Shadow Pokémon system when its evolutionary family is available through the relevant Pokémon GO mechanics.

Can Shadow Nidorino evolve into Shadow Nidoking?

A Shadow Nidorino can be evolved into Nidoking while retaining its Shadow status when the relevant evolution is available.

What is a 100% Nidoking?

A 100% Nidoking has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs, commonly called a hundo.

What is Lucky Nidoking?

Lucky Nidoking is a Nidoking obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust power-up cost.

What is XXL Nidoking?

XXL Nidoking is a Nidoking with an unusually large size classification.

What is XXS Nidoking?

XXS Nidoking is a Nidoking with an unusually small size classification.

Does Nidoking evolve?

Nidoking is the final evolution of Nidorino in the male Nidoran evolutionary family.

Is Nidoking good for raids?

Nidoking can provide Poison or Ground damage, but it generally faces competition from stronger specialized attackers.

Is Nidoking good for PvP?

Nidoking can be used in PvP, but its usefulness depends strongly on its moveset, IVs, league and current metagame.

Is a 100% Nidoking always the best PvP Nidoking?

No. A 15/15/15 Pokémon is perfect in terms of IVs, but some PvP formats favor different IV distributions because of their CP limits.

Is Shiny Nidoking stronger than regular Nidoking?

No. Shiny status is primarily an appearance variant and does not inherently make Nidoking stronger.

Is XXL Nidoking stronger than normal Nidoking?

XXL size should not be treated as a direct measure of combat strength or IV quality.

Is XXS Nidoking weaker than normal Nidoking?

No. XXS classification is a size characteristic and should not automatically be interpreted as a lower IV or weaker Pokémon.

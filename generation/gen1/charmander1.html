01 — OVERVIEW

Charmander Pokémon GO Overview

Charmander is the Fire-type starter from Generation 1. It evolves into Charmeleon and eventually Charizard, making Charmander Candy useful for building the entire evolution family.

Charmander is also a popular collector Pokémon because Pokémon GO has featured Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS variants through different encounter and game mechanics.

Why Charmander's Fire Typing Matters

Charmander's Fire typing determines which attacks are super effective against it and which attack types it can resist.

Fire Pokémon are especially useful against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel targets. However, Charmander itself is vulnerable to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

Lucky Charmander IV Guide

Lucky + 100% Charmander

11 — PERFECT IV

100% IV Charmander

A 100% IV Charmander has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a hundo in the Pokémon GO community.

15 Attack
15 Defense
15 HP
100% Charmander = 15/15/15

If Charmander has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs, its IV combination is perfect.

Should You Evolve a 100% Charmander?

If your goal is to build the strongest possible evolution-family specimen, a perfect Charmander can be an excellent candidate for evolution. However, the best evolution decision also depends on moves, event-exclusive attacks and your intended use.

22 — FAQ

Charmander Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Charmander?

Charmander is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmander weak to?

Charmander is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What is Charmander's Pokédex number?

Charmander is National Pokédex #004.

Does Charmander evolve?

Yes. Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard.

Is Shiny Charmander available?

Shiny Charmander is an obtainable Shiny variant when the applicable encounter mechanics make it available.

Is Shadow Charmander available?

Shadow availability depends on Team GO Rocket encounters and current Pokémon GO rotations.

What is a 100% Charmander?

A 100% Charmander has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Charmander?

Lucky Charmander is a Charmander that has gained Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is XXL Charmander?

XXL Charmander is a Charmander classified as a large-size Pokémon by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Charmander?

XXS Charmander is a Charmander classified as a particularly small Pokémon by the game's size system.

Is Lucky Charmander always 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have a favorable IV floor, but a Lucky Charmander is not automatically 15/15/15.

Can Charmander be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics and can overlap when the applicable game mechanics allow the combination.

Can Charmander be XXL and 100% IV?

Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Charmander can also have a perfect IV combination.

Should I evolve a 100% Charmander?

If you want to build a high-quality member of the Charmander evolution family, a perfect Charmander can be an excellent evolution candidate. Check current move and event considerations first.

Is Charmander good for raids?

Charmander itself is generally not a top Fire-type raid attacker because of its first-stage stats. Its main battle value comes from its evolution family.

Is Charmander good for PvP?

Charmander is generally limited in standard PvP, although special CP-limited formats can sometimes make unevolved Pokémon more interesting.

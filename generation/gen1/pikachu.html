Pikachu IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Pikachu is considered a perfect-IV Pikachu.

100% IV Pikachu

A 100% Pikachu has a perfect 15/15/15 IV combination. These Pokémon are particularly attractive to collectors, especially when combined with a rare form such as Shiny, Lucky, XXL or XXS.

💯 Perfect Pikachu

15 Attack + 15 Defense + 15 HP = 100% IV.

Pikachu PvP Guide

Pikachu can participate in PvP, but its relatively modest stats mean it is generally more of a niche or themed choice than a standard top-ranked option.

Great League

Pikachu can fit under the Great League CP limit, making this the most natural standard league for experimenting with Pikachu. Its performance depends heavily on the current moveset and cup rules.

Ultra League

Pikachu's low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment less practical for standard competitive play.

Master League

Regular Pikachu is not normally suitable for Master League because its maximum CP and stats are far below the level expected in that format.

Shadow Pikachu IVs

A Shadow Pikachu does not need to have perfect IVs to be an interesting Pokémon. Collectors may value unusual combinations such as high Attack, Shiny status, Lucky status where applicable, or special size.

Frustration reminder:

Lucky Pikachu

Lucky Pokémon are obtained through trading and have special visual effects and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

🍀

Lucky Pikachu

A Lucky Pikachu is a fun collector's target, particularly when it has strong IVs or is combined with another desirable characteristic.

Why Collect Lucky Pikachu?

  • Lucky Pokémon have a special visual background.
  • Powering up Lucky Pokémon requires less Stardust.
  • A Lucky Pikachu with strong IVs can be a great long-term collection piece.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.

XXL Pikachu

XXL Pikachu is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size classification system. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

XXL

📏 XXL Pikachu

A Pikachu classified as XXL is larger than the standard size range used by the game.

COLLECTION

XXL Collector Value

XXL Pikachu can be particularly appealing when combined with Shiny, Lucky, costume or perfect-IV characteristics.

XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Pikachu can have any IV combination, including low IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

XXS Pikachu

XXS Pikachu is the small-size counterpart to XXL. It is another collectible size classification and does not automatically indicate a particular IV combination.

XXS

🔹 XXS Pikachu

An XXS Pikachu is classified by the game as being at the smallest size range.

COLLECTION

XXS Collector Value

XXS Pikachu can be especially interesting when combined with Shiny, Lucky, costume or perfect-IV characteristics.

XXS Does Not Mean Low IV

Size and IVs are independent. An XXS Pikachu can have excellent IVs, poor IVs or a perfect 15/15/15 IV combination.

Pikachu Candy & Stardust

Pikachu Candy is used for evolution and power-ups, while Stardust is required for powering up Pokémon. Candy XL becomes important for higher-level investment.

  • Save Pikachu Candy for evolution and power-ups.
  • Collect Candy XL when building high-level Pokémon.
  • Lucky Pikachu can reduce Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Check IVs before investing large amounts of Stardust.
  • Consider keeping rare costume Pikachu before transferring duplicates.

Pikachu FAQ

What type is Pikachu?

Pikachu is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Pikachu weak to?

Pikachu is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Shiny Pikachu available?

Yes. Shiny Pikachu is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Pikachu available?

Shadow Pikachu can be associated with Team GO Rocket encounters and availability can change with the game's current rotations.

Can Shadow Pikachu be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified. Check the current Pokémon GO mechanics for the specific encounter.

What is a Lucky Pikachu?

Lucky Pikachu is a Pikachu that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

What is a 100% Pikachu?

A 100% Pikachu has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Pikachu?

XXL Pikachu is a Pikachu classified by Pokémon GO as being in the extra-large size category.

What is an XXS Pikachu?

XXS Pikachu is classified by Pokémon GO as being in the extra-small size category.

Does XXL Pikachu have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Pikachu can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Pikachu have worse IVs?

No. XXS status does not determine IV quality.

Is Pikachu good for raids?

Regular Pikachu is generally not a top-tier Electric raid attacker. Stronger Electric Pokémon are normally preferred for optimized PvE teams.

Is Pikachu good in PvP?

Pikachu can be used in Great League and special cups, but it is generally a niche choice rather than a standard meta Pokémon.

Does Pikachu evolve?

Yes. Pikachu can evolve into Raichu using Pikachu Candy.

Can Shiny Pikachu evolve?

Evolution availability can depend on the specific Pikachu costume or special variant. Check the individual event or form rules.

Is Lucky Pikachu automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky status and IV quality are separate. A Lucky Pikachu can have different IV combinations.

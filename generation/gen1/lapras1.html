Why Lapras's Typing Matters

Lapras combines Water and Ice typing, giving it access to two different offensive identities. Its Ice typing is particularly useful when dealing damage to Pokémon vulnerable to Ice attacks, while its Water typing gives it a different set of offensive and defensive matchups.

Shadow Lapras

Should You Purify Shadow Lapras?

Purification is not automatically an upgrade. If you value the Shadow battle modifier, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be preferable. If you want the benefits of purification, compare both options before making the decision.

Purified Lapras

Purified Lapras is created when an eligible Shadow Lapras is purified. Purification is a permanent decision, so players should consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, intended role and collection value first.

  • Check the Shadow Lapras's original IVs.
  • Consider whether you want to retain the Shadow modifier.
  • Check the moves available after purification.
  • Consider whether the purified Pokémon has collector value.
  • Do not purify automatically simply because the IVs increase.

XXL & XXS Lapras

Pokémon GO includes size categories that identify exceptionally large or small individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS status is independent of IV percentage.

📏 XXL Lapras

An XXL Lapras is an exceptionally large specimen. Its size can be useful for collection goals and size-related achievements.

📐 XXS Lapras

An XXS Lapras is an exceptionally small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status does not mean the Pokémon has higher IVs.

Can a Lapras Be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size classification and IVs describe different characteristics, so a Lapras can combine XXL or XXS status with a high-IV or perfect-IV appraisal.

Can Lapras Be Shiny, Lucky and XXL?

Different Pokémon attributes can overlap. This means collectors can pursue unusual combinations such as Shiny, Lucky, high-IV and size variants when the game mechanics allow those attributes to coexist.

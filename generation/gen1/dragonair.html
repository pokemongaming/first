Dragonair

Dragonair is a Dragon-type Pokémon from the Kanto region and the middle stage of the Dratini evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Dragonair is mainly interesting as an evolution-stage Pokémon, a collector target and a niche PvP option.

Dragonair Overview

Dragonair is the evolved form of Dratini and evolves into Dragonite. It is a pure Dragon-type Pokémon and is one of the recognizable members of the original Kanto Dragon evolution family.

Why Dragon Typing Matters

Dragonair's pure Dragon typing gives it access to Dragon-type attacks while also giving it a specific defensive matchup profile. Dragon-type Pokémon are particularly useful against other Dragon types, while Dragonair must be careful around Ice, Fairy and opposing Dragon attacks.

Should You Power Up Dragonair?

For most players, resources are better spent on stronger fully evolved Dragon-type Pokémon. Dragonair can still have value for PvP-specific formats, collectors and players who enjoy using unevolved or middle-stage Pokémon.

Perfect Dragonair

A 15/15/15 Dragonair is a perfect-IV specimen. However, perfect IVs are not automatically the best choice for every CP-limited PvP format. League-specific IV spreads should be evaluated separately.

Dragonair PvP Guide

Dragonair can be more interesting in PvP than its raid performance suggests. Its evolution-stage status allows it to appear in formats where fully evolved Pokémon are not always the only competitive option.

Great League

Dragonair can be considered in Great League situations where its Dragon typing, moveset and stat distribution fit the current format. The ideal IV spread depends on the exact CP limit and the current PvP meta.

Ultra League

Dragonair is less naturally suited to Ultra League compared with many fully evolved Pokémon. Its lower overall stat ceiling can become a significant limitation.

Master League

Dragonair's CP ceiling makes standard Master League play an unfavorable environment for it compared with powerful fully evolved Dragon-type Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Dragonair?

Do not treat purification as an automatic upgrade. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your intended use, IVs, moveset and available resources.

  • Check the IVs before making a decision.
  • Consider whether Dragonair or Dragonite is your intended final use.
  • Check the current Shadow moveset.
  • Consider the cost of powering up a Shadow Pokémon.
  • Do not purify solely because the purified appraisal will increase.

Dragonair FAQ

What type is Dragonair?

Dragonair is a pure Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Dragonair weak to?

Dragonair is weak to Ice, Dragon and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Dragonair evolve?

Yes. Dragonair evolves into Dragonite.

What Pokémon evolves into Dragonair?

Dratini evolves into Dragonair.

Is Shiny Dragonair available?

Yes. Shiny Dragonair can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Dratini.

Is Shadow Dragonair available?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket encounters and Pokémon GO's active rotations.

Is Dragonair good for raids?

Dragonair is generally not a top raid attacker. Fully evolved Dragon-type Pokémon are usually much more appropriate for serious raid investment.

Is Dragonair good for PvP?

Dragonair can have niche PvP value, particularly in CP-limited formats where its moveset and stat distribution fit the current meta.

Should I evolve Dragonair into Dragonite?

If your goal is stronger general battle performance, evolving Dragonair into Dragonite is usually the more useful long-term investment.

Is Shadow Dragonair better than regular Dragonair?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile. Whether Shadow Dragonair is better depends on the battle format, IVs, moveset and intended use.

Should I purify Shadow Dragonair?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Can Shiny Dragonair evolve?

Yes. Shiny Dragonair can evolve into Shiny Dragonite.

Is Dragonair a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Dragonair is a standard Pokémon from the Dratini evolution family.

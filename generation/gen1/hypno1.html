Hypno PvP Guide

Hypno is historically known more for PvP than raid damage. Its bulk and potential charged-move coverage can make it an interesting choice in league formats where its CP fits.

Great League

Great League is generally one of the most natural formats to evaluate Hypno. Its defensive profile allows it to absorb damage while building pressure through its moveset.

Ultra League

Hypno's lower maximum CP makes it less naturally suited to Ultra League than Pokémon capable of approaching the CP cap, but individual team compositions can still influence its use.

Master League

Hypno's maximum CP is comparatively low for Master League, making it a niche option rather than a primary investment for that format.

Shiny Hypno

Shiny Hypno is the alternate-color version of Hypno. Its gameplay stats remain tied to its IVs and level rather than its Shiny status.

✨ Collector Form

Shiny Hypno is primarily a collector-focused version, although a high-IV Shiny can still be powered up and used like any other Hypno.

Shadow Hypno

Shadow Hypno is the Shadow version of Hypno. Shadow status changes its battle profile by increasing offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

Shadow Hypno

Shadow Hypno should be evaluated separately from regular Hypno because its battle mechanics and resource costs can make it a different investment decision.

Purified Hypno

Purified Hypno is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Hypno. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered carefully before making the decision.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Purification changes the Pokémon's battle profile.
  • Consider the IV improvement before deciding.
  • Compare the Shadow and Purified versions for your goal.
  • Do not purify purely because the Pokémon is Shadow.

Lucky Hypno

Lucky Hypno is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon mechanic. The main gameplay advantage is reduced Stardust cost when powering it up.

Why Lucky Hypno Can Be Useful

Lucky status can make a high-level Hypno investment cheaper, particularly for players who want to build and test multiple PvP Pokémon.

100% Hypno

A 100% Hypno has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly referred to by players as a perfect or "hundo" Hypno.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV

A 100% IV Hypno is not automatically the best PvP IV spread for every league. PvP stat optimization can differ depending on CP limits and the target level.

XXL Hypno

XXL Hypno is a size variant representing an unusually large specimen. Size is primarily a collection characteristic and does not automatically increase Hypno's battle stats.

📏 XXL Collector Value

XXL Pokémon can be particularly interesting for players collecting unusual size records and participating in Pokémon GO Showcase-related activities.

XXS Hypno

XXS Hypno represents an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, its size does not automatically change its standard battle stats.

🔹 XXS Collector Value

XXS Hypno is primarily valuable as a rare collection variant for players interested in unusual Pokémon sizes.

Hypno FAQ

What type is Hypno?

Hypno is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Hypno weak to?

Hypno is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Does Hypno evolve?

Hypno evolves from Drowzee and is the final stage of its evolution family.

Is Shiny Hypno available?

Shiny Hypno can be obtained through eligible encounters when its Shiny form is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Hypno stronger?

Shadow Hypno has a different offensive and defensive battle profile. Whether it is better depends on the battle format and your investment goal.

Does XXL Hypno have higher stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase Hypno's standard battle stats.

Does XXS Hypno have lower stats?

No. XXS size does not automatically reduce Hypno's standard battle stats.

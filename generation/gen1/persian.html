12 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Persian

A 100% IV Persian has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is the perfect IV combination commonly referred to as a hundo.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

How to Identify a 100% Persian

Use the in-game Appraisal system to inspect Persian's Attack, Defense and HP IVs. A Pokémon with all three IV categories at 15 is a perfect 100% IV specimen.

Collector Tip

A perfect Persian can be a fun collection piece even if you do not intend to use it as a primary raid attacker.

20 • FAQ

Persian FAQ

What type is Persian?

Persian is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Persian weak to?

Persian is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Persian resistant to?

Persian's Normal typing gives it a strong resistance to Ghost-type attacks.

Does Meowth evolve into Persian?

Yes. The standard Kanto Meowth evolves into Persian.

Is Shiny Persian available?

Shiny Persian can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Meowth.

Is Shadow Persian available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Shadow and Team GO Rocket rotations. Check current game data before claiming that it is obtainable.

What is a 100% IV Persian?

A 100% IV Persian has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Persian?

Lucky Persian is a Persian that has received the Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is XXL Persian?

XXL Persian is an extra-large size variant. Its size classification is separate from its IVs.

What is XXS Persian?

XXS Persian is an extra-small size variant. XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has lower IVs.

Is Persian good for raids?

Persian is generally not a top-priority raid attacker compared with stronger Pokémon available for optimized PvE teams.

Is Persian good for PvP?

Persian's PvP value depends heavily on its current moveset and the format's metagame. Check current battle rankings before investing heavily.

Does XXL Persian have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Persian can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Persian have worse stats?

XXS status is a size classification and should not be confused with IV quality.

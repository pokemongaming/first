Hypno Overview

Hypno is the evolved form of Drowzee and a pure Psychic-type Pokémon. While it is not usually known as a top raid attacker, Hypno has historically been valued for its defensive profile and flexible move coverage in PvP.

Why Hypno's Psychic Typing Matters

Psychic typing gives Hypno useful matchups against Fighting and Poison-type Pokémon. However, Hypno must also be careful around Bug, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Venusaur is a Grass- and Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 1. In Pokémon GO, it is a durable Grass-type attacker, a useful PvP Pokémon and the base species for Mega Venusaur. Shiny and Shadow variants add additional investment options for collectors and battle-focused players.

Why Venusaur's Typing Matters

Venusaur's Grass typing gives it strong offensive potential against Water, Ground and Rock targets. Its Poison typing adds useful resistances and gives Venusaur access to Poison-type attacks for additional coverage.

09 • PVP

Venusaur PvP Guide

Venusaur has historically been a useful PvP Pokémon, especially because of its combination of bulk, Grass typing, Poison typing and access to Frenzy Plant.

Great League

Venusaur can be useful in Great League when its CP and IV spread are optimized. Its Grass attacks threaten Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon while Sludge Bomb provides additional coverage.

Ultra League

Venusaur can also function in Ultra League with sufficient Candy XL investment. Its bulk and typing can make it useful against appropriate opponents.

Master League

Venusaur is less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League battles because its maximum CP is significantly lower than many top Master League Pokémon.

PvP Investment

For PvP, evaluate Venusaur's league-specific IV spread, moveset and current metagame rather than automatically using a 15/15/15 specimen.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Venusaur

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Grass.
  • Confirm Venusaur has a Grass-focused moveset.
  • Check Venusaur's level and IVs.
  • Consider weather effects.
  • Compare Venusaur with other Grass attackers.
  • Consider whether Mega Venusaur would provide more value.

During Team Building

Venusaur should not automatically occupy every Grass-type slot. Compare damage, survivability, moveset, weather, Mega bonuses and the specific raid boss.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Venusaur can serve several purposes: Grass-type raids, PvP battles and Mega Evolution. This versatility can make a good Venusaur a useful long-term investment.

20 • FAQ

Venusaur FAQ

Is Venusaur a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Venusaur is a fully evolved Starter Pokémon from Generation 1.

What type is Venusaur?

Venusaur is a Grass- and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Venusaur weak to?

Venusaur is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying and Psychic attacks.

What is Venusaur's Max CP?

Venusaur reaches 2,720 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What is Venusaur's best raid moveset?

Vine Whip and Frenzy Plant are the recommended Grass-focused moves for Venusaur.

Is Frenzy Plant a legacy move?

Frenzy Plant is an event-associated move and its current availability can depend on Pokémon GO events and Elite TMs.

Is Venusaur good for raids?

Venusaur can be a useful Grass-type raid attacker, especially with Frenzy Plant. Mega Venusaur offers significantly stronger raid utility.

Is Venusaur good in PvP?

Yes. Venusaur can be useful in Great and Ultra League depending on the current metagame and moveset.

Does Venusaur evolve?

Venusaur is the final evolution of Bulbasaur. It can also undergo Mega Evolution.

What is Mega Venusaur?

Mega Venusaur is the temporary Mega Evolution of Venusaur.

What type is Mega Venusaur?

Mega Venusaur remains a Grass- and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Mega Venusaur's Max CP?

Mega Venusaur has a much higher CP ceiling than regular Venusaur and is a powerful raid form.

Is Mega Venusaur good for raids?

Yes. Mega Venusaur is a highly useful Grass-type Mega for appropriate raids.

Can Shiny Venusaur become Mega Venusaur?

Yes. A Shiny Venusaur can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved.

Is Shadow Venusaur still Grass type?

Yes. Shadow Venusaur remains Grass/Poison type.

Is Shadow Venusaur stronger than regular Venusaur?

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure but reduced defensive durability. Whether it is better depends on the player's goals.

Should I purify Shadow Venusaur?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent decision.

Is Shadow Venusaur good for raids?

Shadow Venusaur can be a strong damage-focused Grass attacker when equipped with a suitable moveset.

Can Venusaur be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Venusaur is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Mega Venusaur be shiny?

Yes. A Shiny Venusaur can be Mega Evolved while retaining its Shiny status.

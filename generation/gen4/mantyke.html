Why Mantyke's Typing Matters

Mantyke's combination of Water and Flying typing gives it several useful resistances, including Ground, while also creating a major vulnerability to Electric-type attacks.

Mantyke Weaknesses & Resistances

Mantyke's dual typing creates a very clear matchup profile. Electric attacks are especially dangerous, while Rock-type attacks also deal super-effective damage.

Is Mantyke Good for Raids?

Mantyke is not a top-tier Water or Flying raid attacker. Its low Attack stat means it is generally better viewed as a collection Pokémon or as an evolution project rather than a serious PvE investment.

⚔️ Raid Attacker

Not recommended for serious raid damage.

🛡️ Defensive Profile

High Defense relative to its Attack.

⭐ Collection Value

Shiny, Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens can be interesting collection targets.

Mantyke PvP Guide

Mantyke's low maximum CP allows it to fit naturally into CP-limited formats. Its defensive stat profile can make it more interesting in Little Cup-style environments than its raid performance suggests.

Great League

Mantyke can technically be built for Great League, but its performance is generally limited compared with more established Pokémon.

Little Cup

Mantyke's low CP and relatively high Defense can make it a more interesting option in formats where its evolution is not allowed and its typing has useful matchups.

PvP IV Tip

Do not automatically transfer low-Attack Mantyke. Before doing so, check the rules of the current CP-limited cup and its PvP IV ranking.

Mantyke FAQ

What type is Mantyke?

Mantyke is a Water- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Mantyke weak to?

Mantyke is weak to Electric- and Rock-type attacks. Electric attacks are especially dangerous because of its dual typing.

What is Mantyke's Max CP?

Mantyke's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,411 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Does Mantyke evolve?

Yes. Mantyke evolves into Mantine using 50 Mantyke Candy.

Is Shiny Mantyke available?

Yes. Shiny Mantyke is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Mantyke available?

Mantyke does not currently have a separate Shadow form listed in Pokémon GO's current species/form data.

Can Mantyke be Purified?

Not currently as a separate obtainable form. A Pokémon must first exist as a Shadow Pokémon to have a Shadow-to-Purified path.

Can Mantyke be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a status that can result from trading; it is not a separate Mantyke species or form.

What is a 100% Mantyke?

A 100% Mantyke has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a hundo Mantyke?

A Level 50 100% Mantyke has 1,411 CP. A Level 40 hundo has 1,248 CP.

Can Mantyke be XXL?

Yes. Mantyke can have an XXL size category.

Can Mantyke be XXS?

Yes. Mantyke can have an XXS size category.

Is Mantyke good for raids?

Mantyke is not considered a top raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack stat.

Is Mantyke good for PvP?

Mantyke can have niche value in CP-limited formats, but its usefulness depends heavily on the current cup rules and metagame.

Should I evolve my Shiny Mantyke?

That depends on your collection goals. If you want a Shiny Mantine, evolve it. If you prefer keeping a Shiny Baby Pokémon, you may want to leave it as Mantyke.

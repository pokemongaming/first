Bonsly Overview

Bonsly is a pure Rock-type Baby Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. It is numbered #438 in the National Pokédex and can evolve into Sudowoodo.

Unlike many fully evolved Pokémon, Bonsly has a relatively low maximum CP. Its value is therefore more closely connected to collecting, evolution, special attributes and limited-format PvP than conventional raid attacking.

Bonsly PvP Guide

Bonsly's low maximum CP prevents it from being a conventional Master League Pokémon. Its more interesting competitive use comes from restricted formats such as Little Cup, depending on the current rules and eligibility.

Little Cup

Bonsly can be considered in Little Cup-style formats when it meets the active eligibility rules. Its performance depends on the cup's allowed Pokémon, movesets and current metagame.

Great League

Bonsly can technically be built toward the Great League CP limit, but its low maximum CP means it does not naturally compete with most Pokémon that reach the 1,500 CP limit.

Ultra & Master League

Bonsly's maximum CP is far below the normal power level expected in Ultra and Master League, making it an unusual choice for these formats.

Bonsly Evolution

Bonsly evolves into Sudowoodo. Bonsly is therefore different from a Pokémon with no evolution because it represents the baby stage of the Sudowoodo family.

Shiny Bonsly

XXL Bonsly

XXL Bonsly refers to a Bonsly that receives the XXL size designation. XXL is a size attribute, not a separate Pokémon species.

📏

XXL Bonsly

An unusually large Bonsly. XXL status can make a specimen especially interesting for collectors and size-based medal progress.

Average Bonsly

Standard-size Bonsly sits within the normal size distribution and does not receive the XXL or XXS designation.

Bonsly XXL Size Range

Current Pokémon GO size data places Bonsly's XXL range around 0.75 m to 0.875 m, although the exact displayed size of an individual Pokémon is generated from its encounter data.

Collector Tip

If you find an XXL Bonsly, check its Shiny, Lucky and IV status before transferring it. A combination such as Shiny + XXL + Hundo can be particularly desirable for collectors.

XXS Bonsly

XXS Bonsly refers to a Bonsly with the smallest size classification. Like XXL, XXS is an attribute rather than a different Pokémon form.

🔹

XXS Bonsly

An unusually small Bonsly that receives the XXS designation. These specimens can be useful for collectors and size-based medal progress.

Size Collection

Players who collect size variants may want to keep both XXL and XXS Bonsly specimens, especially when they also have rare attributes.

Bonsly XXS Size Range

Current size data places the XXS Bonsly range approximately around 0.245 m to 0.25 m.

Bonsly FAQ

What type is Bonsly?

Bonsly is a pure Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Bonsly weak against?

Bonsly is weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel-type attacks.

What is Bonsly's max CP?

Bonsly's Level 50 maximum CP is 1,472 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Bonsly available?

Yes. Shiny Bonsly is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Bonsly available?

Current database information lists Shadow Bonsly as unavailable. This can change with future Team GO Rocket updates.

What is Purified Bonsly?

Purified Bonsly would refer to a Bonsly purified from Shadow status. Since Shadow Bonsly is not currently available, there is no normal current acquisition route for Purified Bonsly.

What is a Lucky Bonsly?

Lucky Bonsly is a Bonsly that has received Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is a 100% Bonsly?

A 100% Bonsly, or Hundo, has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is XXL Bonsly?

XXL Bonsly is a Bonsly with the XXL size classification. XXL is a size attribute rather than a separate species.

What is XXS Bonsly?

XXS Bonsly is a Bonsly with the smallest size classification. Like XXL, XXS is a size attribute.

Does Bonsly evolve?

Yes. Bonsly evolves into Sudowoodo.

Is Bonsly good for raids?

No. Bonsly is not a strong raid attacker. Its low maximum CP and overall stats make it much more interesting for collecting and limited PvP formats.

Is Bonsly good for PvP?

Bonsly has limited PvP relevance but can be considered for restricted formats such as Little Cup when eligible.

Can Bonsly be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially have both statuses.

Can Bonsly be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXL Bonsly can also have perfect IVs.

Can Bonsly be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. XXS describes size while Shiny describes coloration, so the attributes can overlap.

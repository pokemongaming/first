Roserade

Roserade is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is an offensive Pokémon with strong Poison and Grass attacking options, while its many collectible variants make it especially useful for a complete Pokédex collection.

01 · OVERVIEW

Roserade Overview

Roserade is a dual Grass / Poison-type Pokémon and the final evolution of the Budew family. It evolves from Roselia using 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Why Roserade's Typing Matters

Roserade's dual typing gives it access to both Grass and Poison offensive roles. This allows it to target different raid bosses depending on the matchup and moveset.

10 · IV GUIDE

Roserade IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Roserade is known as a perfect-IV or 100% Roserade.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV

What Is a 100% Roserade?

A 100% Roserade has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a hundo or perfect-IV Pokémon.

11 · 100% ROSERADE

100% IV Roserade

A perfect Roserade is primarily a collector and investment target. The value of a hundo depends on whether you intend to use it for raids, PvP, collection or simply personal achievement.

Is a 100% Roserade Worth Powering Up?

If you already have a strong Grass or Poison roster, a hundo Roserade can be a long-term collector and secondary attacker. Prioritize the investment when its moveset and your team requirements justify the Stardust and Candy XL cost.

12 · PVP

Roserade PvP Guide

Roserade can be used in Trainer Battles, but its value depends heavily on league format, moveset, IV spread and the current metagame.

Great League

Roserade can operate as a pressure-oriented Pokémon in Great League when the format and moveset favor it. Its offensive stats can create significant pressure, but its relatively limited bulk can punish poor shield management.

Ultra League

Roserade has more room to use its offensive profile in Ultra League. However, its effectiveness depends on the current meta and the exact moveset.

Master League

Roserade's maximum CP is generally less suited to unrestricted Master League competition than many dedicated Legendary and high-stat options.

13 · SHINY

Shiny Roserade

Shiny Roserade is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collector target.

✨

Shiny Roserade

Shiny status is a cosmetic variant and does not create a separate species. A Shiny Roserade uses the same species identity and core battle stats as regular Roserade.

Shiny Roserade Collection Tips

  • Keep a Shiny Roserade with good IVs if you enjoy collecting strong variants.
  • Shiny status does not automatically mean the Pokémon has good IVs.
  • A Shiny Roselia can be evolved into Shiny Roserade.
  • Check IVs before transferring rare Shiny specimens.
14 · SHADOW

Shadow Roserade

Shadow Roserade

Purified Roserade

Purified Roserade remains Grass / Poison type. Purification should be considered a strategic choice rather than an automatic upgrade.

16 · LUCKY

Lucky Roserade

Lucky Roserade is a traded Roserade that has received the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players looking to reduce Stardust costs when powering up.

🍀

Lucky Roserade

Lucky status is not a separate species or form. It is a special trade-related status that can make the Pokémon more attractive for long-term Stardust investment.

Why Keep a Lucky Roserade?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up.
  • A Lucky Roserade with strong IVs can be a useful long-term investment.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.
  • Always inspect the post-trade IVs.
17 · XXL

XXL Roserade

18 · XXS

XXS Roserade

XXS Roserade is the opposite size extreme from XXL. It remains Roserade #407 and should be treated as a size variant rather than a separate Pokémon.

🔬

XXS Roserade

XXS status can make Roserade more interesting for collectors and size-based achievements. Size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

XXS Collection Tips

  • Check the Pokémon's size classification before transferring it.
  • Size status is independent of IV percentage.
  • An XXS hundo can be an especially interesting collector combination.
  • Keep rare size variants if you are building a complete Roserade collection.

22 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Roserade

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Grass or Poison damage is required.
  • Confirm the moveset.
  • Check Roserade's level and IVs.
  • Consider weather and raid-boss moves.
  • Compare Roserade against your other attackers.

Offensive Strategy

Roserade is most useful when its offensive typing directly exploits the raid boss. Avoid choosing it simply because it has a high Attack stat.

Long-Term Investment

A high-IV Roserade can remain useful as a specialist Grass or Poison attacker. However, investment should always be compared with stronger alternatives released in future Pokémon GO updates.

25 · FAQ

Roserade FAQ

What type is Roserade?

Roserade is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Roserade weak to?

Roserade is weak to Fire, Flying, Ice and Psychic-type attacks.

What is Roserade's Max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Roserade reaches 3,359 CP.

What are Roserade's best moves?

Poison Jab with Sludge Bomb is a strong Poison build, while Magical Leaf with Grass Knot can be used for a Grass-focused role.

How do I evolve Roselia into Roserade?

Roselia evolves into Roserade using 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Roserade available?

Yes. Shiny Roserade is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Roserade?

A 100% Roserade has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Shadow Roserade different from regular Roserade?

Yes. Shadow Roserade retains the same Grass / Poison typing but has Shadow-specific battle characteristics.

Should I purify Shadow Roserade?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making an irreversible purification decision.

What is a Lucky Roserade?

A Lucky Roserade is a Roserade that received Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

Is XXL Roserade stronger?

Is XXS Roserade rare?

XXS Roserade can be desirable for collectors because of its size classification. Its size status is separate from IV quality.

Can a Shiny Roselia become Shiny Roserade?

Yes. Evolving a Shiny Roselia results in a Shiny Roserade.

Can a Lucky Roserade be 100% IV?

Is Roserade good for raids?

Roserade can be useful as a Grass or Poison attacker when its moveset matches the raid matchup.

Is Roserade good for PvP?

Roserade can have PvP applications, but its effectiveness depends on the league, moveset, IV spread and current metagame.

01 • OVERVIEW

Drapion Overview

Drapion is a Poison/Dark-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. It evolves from Skorupi and has no further evolution after Drapion. Its dual typing gives it a distinctive defensive profile and makes it particularly useful to consider for Pokémon GO PvP teams.

02 • TYPE

Drapion Type

Drapion is a dual-type Poison and Dark Pokémon. The combination gives it several useful resistances and only a small number of type weaknesses.

☠️
Poison / Dark Dual Type
Poison Dark

Why Drapion's Typing Matters

The Poison/Dark combination removes the normal Psychic weakness associated with many Poison-type Pokémon and gives Drapion useful resistance coverage. Its typing is one of the reasons Drapion can be annoying and flexible in PvP battles.

07 • PVP

Drapion PvP Guide

Drapion's typing, bulk and charged-move flexibility make it a Pokémon worth considering in multiple PvP formats.

Great League

Drapion can be useful in Great League because its typing provides valuable resistances and its charged moves can create shield pressure. League-specific moveset recommendations should always be checked against the current PvP meta.

Ultra League

Drapion can also see use in Ultra League depending on the current cup format, available moves and team composition.

Master League

Drapion's maximum CP is considerably lower than the stat ceiling of many Master League specialists, so it is generally less natural here than in lower leagues.

PvP Tip

Drapion's value often comes from forcing opponents to respect its charged-move options. Avoid treating it only as a straightforward damage dealer.

17 • IV GUIDE

Drapion IV Guide

The best IV spread depends on how you intend to use Drapion. A perfect 15/15/15 specimen is excellent for collection, but PvP builds may prefer different IV combinations because of CP limits.

PvE IVs

For general PvE purposes, higher Attack can be useful, although Drapion is not usually a first-choice raid attacker.

PvP IVs

PvP IV selection is more complicated. Lower Attack combined with higher Defense and HP can sometimes allow a Pokémon to reach a more favorable stat product under a league's CP limit.

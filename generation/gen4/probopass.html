Probopass

Probopass is a Rock and Steel-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its unusual defensive typing, wide resistance profile and relevance in certain PvP formats.

Probopass Overview

Probopass is the evolved form of Nosepass and has a Rock / Steel typing. Its exceptionally high Defense makes it more naturally suited to defensive roles than pure damage-focused PvE roles.

Probopass PvP Guide

Probopass's biggest strength in PvP is its enormous Defense combined with its collection of resistances and useful charged attacks.

Great League

Probopass can be useful in Great League when its typing, bulk and charged-move coverage fit the current format. Its performance is highly dependent on shields and the opponent's team composition.

Ultra League

Probopass can also be considered in Ultra League, although its CP limitations and matchup distribution should be evaluated before investing heavily.

Master League

Standard Probopass generally has less natural relevance in Master League because its overall CP ceiling is much lower than the Pokémon commonly optimized for that league.

Should You Purify Shadow Probopass?

Purification is a permanent decision. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle mechanics, do not purify automatically just because the resulting IVs increase.

Shadow Pokémon can also have Frustration during relevant periods. Check the current Team GO Rocket and move-event rules before planning a Shadow investment.

Purified Probopass

Purified Probopass is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Probopass. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and available mechanics.

🌟

💎

Purified Probopass Investment

A purified Probopass can be useful for trainers who prefer the purified mechanics, but the decision should be made after comparing the original Shadow specimen with the purified result.

Probopass FAQ

What type is Probopass?

Probopass is a Rock / Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Probopass weak to?

Probopass is particularly vulnerable to Fighting, Ground and Water attacks.

Is Probopass good for raids?

Probopass is generally more useful for defensive roles and PvP than as a high-damage raid attacker.

Is Probopass good in PvP?

Probopass can be useful in certain PvP formats because of its high Defense and numerous resistances.

Can Probopass be Shiny?

Can Probopass be Shadow?

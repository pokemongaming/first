Why Skorupi's Typing Matters

Skorupi's dual Poison and Bug typing gives it several resistances, including reduced damage from Fighting, Grass, Poison, Bug and Fairy attacks. It is particularly vulnerable to Fire, Flying, Psychic and Rock attacks.

Skorupi PvP Guide

Great League

Skorupi can be evaluated in Great League-related formats because its maximum CP is below the standard Great League limit. However, its actual ranking can vary with moves, cups and the current metagame.

Ultra League

Skorupi's low maximum CP makes it a poor natural choice for standard Ultra League competition.

Master League

Skorupi's 1,141 Level 50 maximum CP prevents it from functioning as a conventional Master League Pokémon.

PvP IV reminder:

Why Froslass's Typing Matters

Froslass has one of the more unusual combinations of types in Pokémon GO. Its Ice and Ghost typing gives it offensive coverage against several Pokémon categories, but also creates important weaknesses that must be considered in battle.

Froslass PvP Guide

Froslass is particularly interesting in PvP because its typing and charged-move pressure allow it to threaten several common opponents.

Great League

Great League is one of the primary formats where Froslass can be considered. Its effectiveness depends heavily on energy management, shields and team support.

Ultra League

Froslass can also be used in Ultra League, although its relatively limited maximum CP means that investment decisions should be evaluated carefully.

Master League

Froslass is generally less naturally suited to Master League because of its maximum CP compared with many Legendary Pokémon.

Purified Froslass

A Purified Froslass is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Froslass. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource costs.

✨

Purified Status

The Shadow battle bonus is removed.

📈

IV Improvement

Purification can improve the Pokémon's IVs.

💰

Resource Costs

Purified Pokémon can have different power-up costs.

🌟

Collector Form

Purified Pokémon are a separate collection form.

Think carefully before purifying. A strong Shadow Pokémon may have more value for a player who prioritizes maximum damage.

Lucky Froslass

Lucky Froslass is obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have special investment advantages that can make them attractive long-term projects.

Why Lucky Froslass Is Valuable

Lucky status can reduce Stardust requirements when powering up the Pokémon.

  • Useful for Stardust-efficient power-ups.
  • Can be a valuable collector form.
  • IVs should still be checked before investment.

How to Evolve Froslass

Froslass FAQ

What type is Froslass?

Froslass is an Ice and Ghost-type Pokémon.

Is Shiny Froslass available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game availability.

Is Shadow Froslass stronger?

Shadow Froslass has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Froslass?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

What is a 100% Froslass?

A 100% Froslass has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Froslass have stronger stats?

Size variants are generally separate from normal battle IV and moveset mechanics.

Is Lucky Froslass useful?

Lucky Froslass can be useful for players who want to reduce Stardust costs when powering it up.

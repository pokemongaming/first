Leafeon Overview

Leafeon is the Grass-type Eeveelution introduced in Generation 4. It evolves from Eevee and has a defensive stat profile that allows it to function as a reasonably durable Grass-type attacker.

Why Leafeon's Typing Matters

Leafeon's pure Grass typing gives it access to super-effective damage against Water, Ground and Rock-type opponents. However, it must be careful around Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug attacks.

Leafeon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Powering up Leafeon requires Eevee Candy, Candy XL at higher levels and Stardust. Because Leafeon evolves from Eevee, players can build multiple Eeveelutions from the same Candy pool.

  • Save high-IV Eevee before evolving.
  • Keep enough Candy for the evolution you want.
  • Collect Candy XL for high-level investment.

Leafeon FAQ

What type is Leafeon?

Leafeon is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Leafeon weak to?

Leafeon is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

What is Leafeon strong against?

Grass-type attacks are super effective against Water, Ground and Rock-type Pokémon.

Is Leafeon good for raids?

Leafeon can be useful as a Grass-type raid attacker, especially for players who want an accessible non-Legendary option.

Is Shiny Leafeon available?

Yes. Shiny Leafeon is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and Eevee evolution.

Does Leafeon evolve from Eevee?

Staraptor Overview

Staraptor is the final evolution in the Starly evolutionary family. It evolves from Staravia using 100 Candy and is a Normal/Flying-type Pokémon. Unlike Legendary Pokémon, Staraptor can be obtained through its normal evolution family and has several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky forms.

Why Staraptor's Typing Matters

Staraptor's Flying typing gives it useful resistances against Ground, Bug and Grass attacks. Its Normal typing also gives it a strong resistance to Ghost attacks. However, the combination leaves Staraptor vulnerable to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

Purified Staraptor

A Shadow Staraptor can be purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides several practical benefits, including removing Frustration.

Purified Staraptor

Purification removes the Shadow status and changes Frustration into Return.

Purified Staraptor Benefits

  • Shadow status is removed.
  • Frustration is removed.
  • The Pokémon learns Return when purified.
  • Purified Pokémon require fewer resources for certain power-up and evolution actions.
  • Purification improves the appraisal.
Purification decision:

Do not purify a strong Shadow Staraptor automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first, especially if you value the Shadow damage bonus.

XXL Staraptor

XXL Staraptor refers to a Staraptor with an exceptionally large size classification in Pokémon GO. XXL is a size attribute rather than a separate species or evolution.

XXL XXL Staraptor

A large-size Staraptor that can be valuable for collectors, showcases and personal collection goals.

Does XXL Change Staraptor's Battle Stats?

XXL status should not be treated as a direct replacement for IVs. A large Staraptor can have poor IVs, excellent IVs, or anything in between.

XXS Staraptor

XXS Staraptor is the small-size classification of Staraptor. Like XXL, XXS is a size attribute rather than a different Pokémon species.

XXS XXS Staraptor

A small-size Staraptor that can be especially interesting for collectors and showcase goals.

XXS vs IVs

XXS size and IV quality are separate characteristics. An XXS Staraptor can still be a 100% IV Pokémon if its Attack, Defense and HP IVs are all 15.

Staraptor PvP Guide

Staraptor's high Attack and lower defensive stats make it more specialized in PvP than extremely bulky Pokémon.

Great League

Staraptor can technically be used within Great League CP limits, but its overall ranking is limited compared with many specialized PvP Pokémon.

Ultra League

Staraptor can provide offensive Flying pressure in certain matchups, but trainers should compare it against the current Ultra League meta before investing.

Master League

Staraptor's maximum CP is substantially lower than the CP ceilings of many Master League staples, making it a less natural Master League investment.

Staraptor Availability

Starly → 25 Candy → Staravia → 100 Candy → Staraptor

Staraptor can also appear through other encounter methods or events when Niantic includes the species in the current Pokémon GO rotation.

Staraptor Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

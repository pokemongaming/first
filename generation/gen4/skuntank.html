01 • OVERVIEW

Skuntank Overview

Skuntank is the evolved form of Stunky and is a dual Poison / Dark-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. Its combination of Poison and Dark typing gives it a useful defensive profile, although its relatively modest offensive stats limit its importance as a high-end PvE attacker.

02 • TYPING

Skuntank Type

Skuntank has a dual Poison / Dark typing. This combination is important because its weaknesses and resistances are determined by both types.

☠️
Poison / Dark Dual Type
☠️ Poison 🌑 Dark

Why Skuntank's Typing Matters

The Poison / Dark combination gives Skuntank useful defensive qualities. In particular, the combination removes the normal Psychic weakness associated with Poison-type Pokémon. However, Ground-type attacks remain a major threat and should be considered when using Skuntank in battle.

09 • PVP

Skuntank PvP Guide

Skuntank's value is more interesting in PvP than in raids. Its Poison / Dark typing gives it useful defensive matchups, while its charged moves provide coverage.

Great League

Skuntank can be considered in Great League formats where its typing and move coverage fit the current metagame. Its performance can vary considerably depending on moveset, IVs and the opponents being targeted.

Ultra League

Skuntank can also be evaluated in Ultra League environments, although its usefulness depends on the specific cup or metagame.

Master League

Skuntank's maximum CP and overall stat profile make Master League a less natural home for it compared with Pokémon specifically built around the Master League meta.

PvP IV warning

Do not automatically transfer every low-Attack Skuntank. A specimen with low Attack and high Defense/HP can sometimes be more interesting for CP-limited PvP formats.

16 • XXL

📏 XXL Skuntank

XXL Skuntank is a size variant of Skuntank. XXL status does not create a new Pokémon species and does not fundamentally change Skuntank's combat typing or base stats.

📏 XXL SKUNTANK

XXL refers to the Pokémon's size measurement. It is separate from IV quality.

XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

A Pokémon can be XXL without having perfect IVs, and a 100% IV Pokémon does not need to be XXL. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

24 • FAQ

Skuntank FAQ

What type is Skuntank?

Skuntank is a Poison / Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Skuntank weak to?

Skuntank's primary weakness is Ground-type damage.

Is Skuntank good for raids?

Skuntank is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. It is more interesting for PvP and collection purposes.

Is Shiny Skuntank available?

Shiny Skuntank can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods. Current availability should be checked before publishing.

Is Shadow Skuntank available?

Shadow Skuntank availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket encounter rotation.

Is Purified Skuntank different from regular Skuntank?

Purified Skuntank has undergone purification and retains the Poison / Dark typing, but its mechanics and IVs can differ from a normal Skuntank.

What is a Lucky Skuntank?

A Lucky Skuntank is obtained through trading and benefits from reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Skuntank?

A 100% Skuntank has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is XXL Skuntank stronger?

XXL status is a size characteristic and should not be confused with higher IVs or a separate species form.

Is XXS Skuntank rare?

XXS Skuntank is a size variant that can be desirable to collectors. Its size does not automatically mean that its IVs are better.

Is 15/15/15 always the best Skuntank for PvP?

No. In CP-limited PvP formats, different IV distributions can provide more useful bulk. The best spread depends on the league and current PvP meta.

Does Shiny Skuntank have different stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual and collection variant and does not provide a separate base-stat profile.

Can a Lucky Skuntank be 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Pokémon can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs, although the Lucky trade IV floor does not guarantee perfection.

Can Skuntank be XXL and Shiny?

Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can potentially have multiple collection attributes at the same time when supported by the encounter mechanics.

DARK / ICE POKÉMON

Weavile

Weavile is a fast Dark- and Ice-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its high Attack, useful Dark- and Ice-type offensive options, raid utility and access to several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky Weavile.

01 · DATABASE

Weavile Overview

Weavile is the evolved form of Sneasel and was introduced in Generation 4. It combines Dark and Ice typing with a high Attack stat, making it particularly interesting for players who want a strong offensive Pokémon rather than a bulky defender.

Its usefulness changes depending on the moveset, battle format and available alternatives. Weavile can be considered for both raid attacking and selected PvP situations.

Why Weavile's Typing Matters

The combination of Dark and Ice gives Weavile a broad offensive identity. Dark attacks are useful against Psychic- and Ghost-type targets, while Ice attacks can be valuable against Dragon-, Flying- and Grass-type opponents.

09 · PVP

Weavile PvP Guide

Weavile's high Attack and low durability make it a more specialized PvP pick than a general-purpose bulky fighter.

Great League

A standard Weavile is usually difficult to fit naturally under the Great League CP limit because its high offensive stats and comparatively low bulk create a challenging stat profile.

Ultra League

Weavile can be considered in certain Ultra League teams, although its viability depends heavily on the current metagame and available moves.

Master League

Weavile's maximum CP and fragile defensive profile make it more matchup-dependent in Master League than many legendary or bulky alternatives.

11 · SPECIAL VARIANTS

Weavile Special Variants

Weavile can appear in several Pokémon GO collection categories. These variants should be treated as separate collection or investment considerations rather than assuming they are all stronger versions of the standard Pokémon.

✨

Shiny Weavile

Shiny Weavile has an alternate coloration and is highly desirable among collectors. Shiny status does not by itself increase combat stats.

🌑

Shadow Weavile

Shadow Weavile has the Shadow battle modifier, increasing offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability. It can be especially interesting for damage-focused players.

🍀

Lucky Weavile

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and have at least a minimum IV floor. Lucky status is obtained through trading rather than normal catching.

🧪

Purified Weavile

A Purified Weavile is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Weavile. Purification changes its investment and battle characteristics, so compare both versions before purifying.

12 · SHINY

Shiny Weavile

Shiny Weavile is the alternate-color form of Weavile. Its visual difference makes it a popular collection target.

✨ Shiny Weavile

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not provide a direct Attack, Defense or HP bonus. A Shiny Weavile can still be excellent in raids if it has good IVs, level and moves.

Shiny Weavile IVs

A Shiny Weavile can have any legal IV combination. A 15/15/15 Shiny Weavile is both a perfect-IV and Shiny specimen, making it particularly valuable to collectors.

13 · SHADOW

Shadow Weavile

Shadow Weavile is the Shadow version of Weavile. The Shadow modifier changes its battle profile, making it more offensive but less durable than the regular version.

🌑 Shadow Weavile

Shadow Weavile can be attractive for players who prioritize raid damage. Its lower durability means that revives, dodging and team composition can become more important.

15 · LUCKY

Lucky Weavile

Lucky Weavile is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for powering up and receive a minimum IV floor when created.

🍀 Lucky Weavile

Lucky status is particularly useful when you plan to invest heavily in a Pokémon because the reduced Stardust requirement can lower the cost of powering it up.

Is Lucky Weavile Good for Investment?

  • Good option for long-term raid investments.

17 · SIZE

XXL Weavile

XXL Weavile is a size variant representing an exceptionally large individual. Size does not automatically mean higher Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

📏 XXL Weavile

XXL status is primarily a size and collection characteristic. An XXL Weavile can also have strong IVs, be Shiny, Lucky or belong to another eligible collection category.

Does XXL Weavile Have Better Stats?

No. XXL classification should not be interpreted as a direct combat-stat bonus. Battle performance is determined by the relevant Pokémon GO mechanics, including IVs, level, moves and battle modifiers.

22 · PRACTICAL GUIDE

How to Use Weavile

Before a Raid

  • Check the raid boss's weaknesses.
  • Choose Dark or Ice moves according to the matchup.
  • Check Weavile's level and IVs.
  • Consider its lower durability.
  • Compare Weavile with your other attackers.

During Team Building

Weavile can provide high offensive pressure, but its relatively fragile defensive profile means that it may faint faster than bulkier alternatives. Team composition should therefore be based on the specific boss and battle conditions.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Weavile can remain useful when its Dark or Ice offensive role matches the raid matchup. Shadow, Lucky and perfect-IV specimens can each have different reasons for being worth keeping.

24 · FAQ

Weavile FAQ

What type is Weavile?

Weavile is a Dark- and Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Weavile weak to?

Weavile is weak to Fighting, Fire, Bug, Rock, Steel and Fairy-type attacks.

Does Weavile evolve?

Weavile is an evolved Pokémon. It evolves from Sneasel using the required Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

Is Shiny Weavile available?

Yes. Shiny Weavile is available through eligible Shiny Sneasel evolutions and other applicable Pokémon GO mechanics.

Is Shadow Weavile good?

Shadow Weavile can be attractive for damage-focused players because the Shadow modifier increases offensive pressure, although its durability is reduced.

Should I purify Shadow Weavile?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before purifying, especially if you want maximum Shadow raid performance.

Can Weavile be Lucky?

Yes. Weavile can become Lucky through trading when the trade results in a Lucky Pokémon.

What is a 100% Weavile?

A 100% Weavile has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Weavile stronger?

XXL status is a size characteristic and should not be treated as a direct combat-stat bonus.

Is XXS Weavile stronger?

No. XXS status describes size and does not automatically increase Weavile's Attack, Defense or HP.

What is the best Weavile moveset?

For a Dark-focused role, Snarl and Foul Play are useful choices. For an Ice-focused role, Ice Shard and Avalanche are strong options. Always check the current Pokémon GO moveset before making a time-sensitive recommendation.

Is Weavile good for raids?

Weavile can be useful in raids where its Dark or Ice attacks hit the boss for strong damage. Its relatively low durability should also be considered.

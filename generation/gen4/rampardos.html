Rampardos

Rampardos is a powerful Rock-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its extremely high Attack stat and strong raid damage potential, although its lower bulk means it can be a high-risk attacker.

Rampardos Overview

Rampardos is the evolved form of Cranidos and is one of the most offensively focused Rock-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its standout feature is its very high Attack stat, which allows it to produce strong Rock-type raid damage.

✨ Shiny Rampardos

Shiny Rampardos is the alternate-color version of Rampardos. Its battle performance is identical to regular Rampardos with the same IVs, level and moveset.

SHINY FORM

Shiny Rampardos

A Shiny Rampardos is primarily a collector's variant. Shiny status does not increase Attack, Defense, HP or Max CP.

  • Same species stats as regular Rampardos.
  • Same battle performance with identical IVs and level.
  • Can be powered up for raids normally.
  • Useful for collectors and Shiny Pokémon collections.

🌑 Shadow Rampardos

Shadow Rampardos is a Shadow version of Rampardos. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon, making Shadow Rampardos a separate investment decision.

SHADOW FORM

Shadow Rampardos

Because Rampardos already focuses heavily on damage output, the Shadow version can be particularly attractive for players who prioritize raid damage.

Shadow Rampardos is best considered by players who want maximum damage potential and are willing to accept higher resource costs and reduced durability.

Shadow move warning:

✨ Purified Rampardos

Purified Rampardos is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Rampardos. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

PURIFIED FORM

Purified Rampardos

A Purified Rampardos no longer receives Shadow battle mechanics. Whether purification is worthwhile depends on your collection, IVs and raid investment goals.

Should You Purify Shadow Rampardos?

  • Compare Shadow and Purified battle roles first.
  • Do not purify automatically for higher IVs.
  • Consider long-term raid damage goals.
  • Check the Pokémon's current moves.
  • Keep rare high-quality Shadow Pokémon for comparison.

🍀 Lucky Rampardos

Lucky Rampardos is obtained through Pokémon trades that result in a Lucky Pokémon. Lucky status primarily affects investment costs rather than directly increasing battle stats.

LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Rampardos Investment

A Lucky Rampardos can be especially useful for players who want to power up Rampardos while reducing Stardust costs.

  • Lucky status does not change Rampardos's base stats.
  • Lucky Pokémon can have useful IVs from trading.
  • Lower Stardust investment can make high-level builds easier.
  • Good option for raid-focused players.

💯 100% IV Rampardos

Is a 100% Rampardos Worth Powering Up?

A perfect Rampardos can be an excellent long-term investment, particularly for collectors and players building optimized Rock raid teams. However, a non-perfect Rampardos can still perform extremely well when powered up.

Rampardos FAQ

What type is Rampardos?

Rampardos is a pure Rock-type Pokémon.

Rampardos can be an excellent Rock-type raid attacker because of its high Attack stat, although its durability is lower than some alternatives.

Yes, especially if you collect Lucky Pokémon or want to evolve a Lucky Glameow into Purugly. Because the Lucky status is permanent, an interesting Lucky specimen can be a valuable collection piece.

13 · XXL

XXL Glameow

An XXL Glameow is an unusually large specimen. Size variants are separate from IV quality, so a large Glameow does not automatically have better IVs.

📏 XXL Glameow

XXL status is primarily useful for collectors, size-based showcases and players completing their size collection.

XXL Unusually large size category
IVs Independent of size
Important:

XXL is a size characteristic and should not be confused with a 100% IV Pokémon. A Glameow can be XXL without being perfect IV.

21 · FAQ

Glameow FAQ

Is Glameow a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Glameow is a regular Pokémon introduced in Generation 4.

What type is Glameow?

Glameow is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Glameow weak against?

Glameow is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Glameow immune to?

As a Normal-type Pokémon, Glameow is immune to Ghost-type attacks under standard type effectiveness rules.

Does Glameow evolve?

Yes. Glameow evolves into Purugly.

Is Shiny Glameow available?

Shiny availability depends on Pokémon GO's current encounter and event mechanics.

What is a 100% Glameow?

A 100% Glameow has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Glameow?

Shadow Glameow is the Shadow variant of Glameow. It retains its Normal typing while using Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

Should I purify Shadow Glameow?

Not automatically. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, collection value and your personal goals before purifying it.

What is Lucky Glameow?

Lucky Glameow is a Glameow that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon benefit from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Glameow?

XXL Glameow is an unusually large Glameow. Its size is separate from its IV quality.

What is XXS Glameow?

XXS Glameow is an unusually small Glameow. Its size is independent of its IVs.

Is Glameow good for raids?

Glameow is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Stronger Pokémon are usually preferred for serious PvE investment.

Should I keep a perfect Glameow?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Glameow can be a valuable collector's Pokémon and can also be evolved into a perfect Purugly.

Can a Shiny Glameow be Lucky?

Yes. Pokémon characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky status can coexist when the Pokémon is obtained through an eligible trade.

Can Glameow be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Glameow can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Glameow be both XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size and Shiny status are separate Pokémon characteristics.

Purified Drifloon

Purified Drifloon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Drifloon. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and removes its Shadow battle characteristics.

✨ PURIFIED FORM

Purified Drifloon

Purification can also change a Pokémon's IV values. Players should compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making a permanent decision.

  • Check the Shadow Drifloon's IVs first.
  • Consider whether you want a Shadow Drifblim.
  • Remember that purification is permanent.
  • Compare resource requirements.
  • Consider collection goals before purifying.

Lucky Drifloon

Lucky Drifloon is a special trade result. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon, making them attractive for players planning long-term investments.

🍀 LUCKY FORM

Lucky Drifloon

A Lucky Drifloon can be particularly useful if you plan to evolve and power it up while saving Stardust.

Why Lucky Drifloon Can Be Useful

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term evolution projects.
  • Adds a special status to a Pokémon collection.
  • Can evolve into Lucky Drifblim.

XXL Drifloon & XXS Drifloon

XXL and XXS Drifloon are special size variants. Size is separate from IVs, Shiny status and other special Pokémon properties.

XXL & XXS Lopunny

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS Lopunny are useful collection variants and may also matter for size-related medals or showcases.

📏 XXL Lopunny Extra-large size classification

An XXL Lopunny is a particularly large specimen compared with typical Lopunny encounters.

📐 XXS Lopunny Extra-small size classification

An XXS Lopunny is an unusually small specimen and can be desirable for collectors.

Size Does Not Automatically Mean Better IVs

Size classification and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL or XXS Lopunny can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Lopunny FAQ

What type is Lopunny?

Regular Lopunny is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Lopunny weak to?

Regular Lopunny is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Does Lopunny evolve?

Lopunny evolves from Buneary. Lopunny can also undergo Mega Evolution into Mega Lopunny.

Is Shiny Lopunny available?

Yes. Shiny Lopunny is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is Shadow Lopunny?

Shadow Lopunny is the Shadow variant of Lopunny. Its base typing remains Normal.

Should I purify Shadow Lopunny?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your battle and collection goals before purifying.

What is Lucky Lopunny?

Lucky Lopunny is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Lopunny?

A 100% Lopunny has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Lopunny?

XXL Lopunny is an individual Lopunny that receives the XXL size classification.

What is XXS Lopunny?

XXS Lopunny is an individual Lopunny that receives the XXS size classification.

What type is Mega Lopunny?

Mega Lopunny is a Normal- and Fighting-type Pokémon.

Is Mega Lopunny good for raids?

Mega Lopunny can be useful in raids where its Fighting-type damage and Mega team utility are valuable.

Can Shiny Lopunny Mega Evolve?

A Shiny Lopunny can retain its Shiny identity when used for Mega Evolution where the relevant Mega mechanics allow it.

Can a Lucky Lopunny Mega Evolve?

Lucky status and Mega Evolution are separate characteristics. An eligible Lopunny can be Lucky and can also be used for Mega Evolution.

Can a 100% Lopunny Mega Evolve?

Yes. A perfect-IV Lopunny can be an excellent candidate for Mega Evolution if you want to invest in a high-IV specimen.

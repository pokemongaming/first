Hippowdon Overview

Hippowdon is a Ground-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It evolves from Hippopotas and has a bulky stat profile with high stamina.

In Pokémon GO, Hippowdon is available in several collectible versions, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Why Hippowdon's Ground Typing Matters

Hippowdon's pure Ground typing gives it useful matchups against Electric, Poison and Rock-type attacks. Ground-type moves are also super effective against several important Pokémon categories.

Hippowdon PvP Guide

Hippowdon's bulky stats and access to multiple elemental attack types can make it interesting in PvP formats. Its usefulness depends heavily on moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Hippowdon may require a specific IV spread and level investment to fit below the Great League CP limit. Its move selection can provide unexpected coverage.

Ultra League

Hippowdon's bulk can be useful in Ultra League situations, although its overall performance depends on the current meta and available moves.

Master League

Hippowdon can reach a useful CP level but competes against significantly more powerful Legendary and high-stat Pokémon in Master League.

✨ Shiny Hippowdon

Shiny Hippowdon is the alternate-color version of Hippowdon. A Shiny Pokémon has the same species stats as a regular Hippowdon, meaning the Shiny status itself does not increase combat power.

Shiny Hippowdon

Shiny Hippowdon is primarily a collector form. Its battle performance depends on IVs, level and moveset rather than its Shiny appearance.

Should You Power Up Shadow Hippowdon?

  • Compare it with your existing Ground attackers.
  • Check its IVs before making a large investment.
  • Consider the increased resource requirements.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Do not purify automatically.

✨ Purified Hippowdon

Purified Hippowdon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Hippowdon. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow status into a Purified version.

Purified Hippowdon

🍀 Lucky Hippowdon

Lucky Hippowdon is obtained when a Hippowdon becomes Lucky through an eligible Pokémon trade. Lucky status does not change Hippowdon's species typing or moveset.

Why Lucky Hippowdon Is Valuable

Hippowdon FAQ

What type is Hippowdon?

Hippowdon is a Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Hippowdon weak to?

Hippowdon is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Does Hippowdon evolve?

Hippowdon evolves from Hippopotas and is the final stage of its evolution family.

Is Shiny Hippowdon available?

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO encounter pool and eligible sources.

Is Shadow Hippowdon stronger than regular Hippowdon?

Shadow Hippowdon has a different offensive and defensive battle profile. Whether it is better depends on the battle role and investment goal.

Should I purify Shadow Hippowdon?

Not automatically. Compare the benefits of the Shadow version with the potential Purified result before making the decision.

Is Lucky Hippowdon good?

Lucky Hippowdon can be useful because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Hippowdon?

A 100% Hippowdon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Hippowdon do more damage?

XXL status is primarily a size classification. Battle performance depends on species stats, IVs, level and moveset.

Does XXS Hippowdon have lower stats?

XXS size does not automatically mean lower combat stats. IVs and level remain the important factors for battle performance.

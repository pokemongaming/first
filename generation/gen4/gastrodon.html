Why Gastrodon's Typing Matters

The Water and Ground combination gives Gastrodon an important defensive identity. Electric-type attacks do not damage Gastrodon because Ground typing provides immunity to Electric damage.

🌑 Shadow Gastrodon

Shadow Gastrodon is the Shadow version of Gastrodon. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately because their battle characteristics differ from standard forms.

Shadow Gastrodon

Shadow Pokémon trade defensive durability for increased offensive pressure. Whether Shadow Gastrodon is worth investing in depends on its current availability, moveset and your intended battle role.

Higher Damage

Lower Durability

Shadow Pokémon take more damage.

Shadow Gastrodon Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Gastrodon's Water and Ground typing. Grass remains its most important weakness.

Frustration Warning

Check current Team GO Rocket events before attempting to remove Frustration from a Shadow Gastrodon.

✨ Purified Gastrodon

Purified Gastrodon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Gastrodon.

Purified Gastrodon

🍀 Lucky Gastrodon

Lucky Gastrodon is obtained through Pokémon trading when a Lucky Trade occurs.

Lucky Gastrodon Benefits

  • Requires less Stardust to power up.
  • Has a Lucky background in the Pokémon storage system.
  • Can be an attractive long-term investment.
  • Battle typing remains Water / Ground.

Lucky status does not change Gastrodon's species, typing or available moves.

Tangrowth

Tangrowth is a bulky Grass-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its strong bulk, Grass-type moves and flexible collection of special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS examples.

Tangrowth Overview

Tangrowth is a pure Grass-type Pokémon that evolves from Tangela. In Pokémon GO, Tangela evolves into Tangrowth using 100 Tangela Candy and a Sinnoh Stone. Tangrowth has strong bulk but is not generally among the very highest-DPS Grass raid attackers.

Tangrowth IV Guide

IVs determine the additional Attack, Defense and HP values of an individual Tangrowth. The perfect IV combination is 15/15/15.

Perfect / Hundo Tangrowth

A 15/15/15 Tangrowth is a 100% IV or Hundo Tangrowth. For collectors, PvE investment and Master League, a perfect specimen can be particularly desirable.

Is a 100% Tangrowth Worth Keeping?

Yes. A Hundo Tangrowth is an excellent collector's Pokémon and can be useful for high-level investment. However, the decision to power it up should still depend on your preferred battle formats and available Grass attackers.

Tangrowth PvP Guide

Tangrowth can be used in PvP, with Vine Whip, Power Whip and Rock Slide providing its commonly recommended moveset. Its PvP value varies considerably by league and current metagame.

Great League

Tangrowth can participate in Great League but generally requires careful IV optimization because of the 1,500 CP limit.

Ultra League

Tangrowth can reach the Ultra League CP range and can use its Grass typing and charged-move coverage against suitable opponents.

Master League

Tangrowth can technically reach Master League CP levels, although it is not normally considered a top Master League choice.

PvP Moveset

Vine Whip + Power Whip + Rock Slide

Purified Tangrowth

Purified Tangrowth is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Tangrowth. Purification removes its Shadow status and provides the normal benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Tangrowth?

Do not purify automatically. If your main goal is battle performance, compare the Shadow version against the purified result before making the decision. If your goal is collection, cost reduction or obtaining a purified Pokémon, purification can make sense.

Purification Decision

Keep Shadow Tangrowth if you value its Shadow battle performance; purify only when the benefits of purification match your collection or investment goals.

🍀 Lucky Tangrowth

Lucky Tangrowth is a Tangrowth that has received the Lucky status through a Pokémon trade. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making them attractive long-term investment candidates.

Lucky Tangrowth

Lucky status does not change Tangrowth's species or typing. Its main practical benefit is the reduced Stardust cost for powering it up.

Lucky Tangrowth Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term collection projects.
  • Can be a practical way to build an affordable high-level Tangrowth.
  • Lucky status is permanent once obtained.
  • A Lucky Tangrowth can also be Shiny or 100% IV depending on its IVs and appearance.

Is Lucky Tangrowth Worth Powering Up?

A Lucky Tangrowth can be a sensible Stardust-saving project, especially if it already has a good moveset and useful IVs. However, do not invest simply because a Pokémon is Lucky; consider its actual role on your teams.

💯 100% IV Tangrowth

A 100% Tangrowth, commonly called a Hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Hundo Tangrowth CP

At Level 50, a perfect Tangrowth reaches 3,425 CP. A perfect raid encounter reaches 1,731 CP at Level 20, or 2,164 CP when weather boosted at Level 25.

Tangrowth Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Tangrowth requires Tangela/Tangrowth Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Plan your investment around the role you want Tangrowth to perform.

  • Save Tangrowth-family Candy for useful power-up projects.
  • Collect Candy XL if you want to push Tangrowth beyond Level 40.
  • Lucky Tangrowth can reduce Stardust requirements.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite TM resources.
  • Compare Tangrowth with your other Grass attackers.
  • Keep strong IV specimens for future investment.

Full Level 50 Investment

A Level 50 Tangrowth requires substantial Stardust, Candy and Candy XL. A full investment should therefore be reserved for a Tangrowth you genuinely intend to use or keep as a long-term collection project.

Tangrowth FAQ

What type is Tangrowth?

Tangrowth is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Tangrowth weak to?

Tangrowth is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

What is Tangrowth's Max CP?

Tangrowth's Level 50 maximum CP is 3,425.

What are Tangrowth's best moves?

Vine Whip and Power Whip are the recommended Grass-focused moveset.

Is Shiny Tangrowth available?

Yes. Shiny Tangrowth is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Tangrowth good?

Shadow Tangrowth can be attractive for players who prioritize offensive damage, although it also has reduced defensive durability compared with its regular form.

Should I purify Shadow Tangrowth?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your battle and collection goals.

What is a Hundo Tangrowth?

A Hundo Tangrowth has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What CP is a 100% Tangrowth from a raid?

A perfect Level 20 raid Tangrowth is 1,731 CP. If weather boosted at Level 25, a perfect encounter is 2,164 CP.

Is Lucky Tangrowth worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Tangrowth requires less Stardust to power up, making a good Lucky specimen attractive for long-term investment.

Does XXL Tangrowth have higher stats?

Size category itself does not automatically make Tangrowth stronger in battle. XXL status is primarily relevant to size, collection and Showcase-related features.

Does XXS Tangrowth have lower stats?

XXS status is a size category and should not automatically be interpreted as weaker battle performance.

Can Tangrowth be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Lucky status and Shiny status are different properties, so a Tangrowth can have both.

Can Tangrowth be Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. A Tangrowth can simultaneously have Shiny status and perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Tangrowth good for raids?

Tangrowth can be useful as a Grass-type raid attacker, especially when Grass damage is super effective, but its current raid ranking should be compared with other available Grass attackers.

Is Tangrowth good in PvP?

Tangrowth can be used in PvP and has access to useful Grass and coverage moves, but its value depends strongly on the league and current metagame.

How do you evolve Tangela into Tangrowth?

Tangela evolves into Tangrowth using 100 Candy and a Sinnoh Stone.

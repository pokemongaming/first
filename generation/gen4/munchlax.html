Munchlax Overview

Munchlax is a Normal-type baby Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It is the pre-evolution of Snorlax and belongs to a relatively small evolutionary family.

Unlike many Pokémon that are primarily valued for raids, Munchlax is more interesting as a collector, evolution project and PvP-oriented Pokémon depending on the current Pokémon GO formats.

Why Munchlax's Typing Matters

Munchlax's pure Normal typing gives it only one direct type weakness in Pokémon GO: Fighting. This makes its defensive matchup straightforward, although its actual performance depends heavily on stats, moves and the battle format.

Munchlax Special Forms & Variants

Munchlax can be encountered or owned with several collectible characteristics. These should be considered separately because they can affect appearance, appraisal, trading value or size classification.

✨
Shiny Munchlax

Shiny Munchlax has an alternate coloration. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

🌑
Shadow Munchlax

Shadow Munchlax is a Shadow variant with the standard Shadow battle modifier. Its usefulness should be evaluated separately from regular Munchlax.

💎
Purified Munchlax

Purified Munchlax is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification changes its status and can affect its appraisal and move situation.

🍀
Lucky Munchlax

Lucky Munchlax is obtained through trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up, making a good Lucky specimen attractive for investment.

💯
100% IV Munchlax

A 100% Munchlax has perfect 15/15/15 IVs. It is especially desirable for collectors and players planning to evolve it into Snorlax.

📏
XXL Munchlax

XXL Munchlax is a size variant classified as exceptionally large. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

🔹
XXS Munchlax

XXS Munchlax is an exceptionally small size variant. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXS Pokémon can still have excellent IVs.

💤
Regular Munchlax

The standard Munchlax form without Shadow, Purified, Lucky or special size status.

Shiny Munchlax

Shiny Munchlax is the alternate-color version of Munchlax. For collectors, a Shiny Munchlax can be especially valuable when combined with high IVs, Lucky status or an uncommon size.

Shiny Collection Tip

If you obtain a Shiny Munchlax, check its IVs, size and appraisal before transferring or evolving it. Rare combinations can be worth keeping.

Should You Purify Shadow Munchlax?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Munchlax simply to increase its appraisal. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your intended use.

Frustration note:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events may provide opportunities to replace it, so check current event rules before using Charged TMs.

Purified Munchlax

Purified Munchlax is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Munchlax. Purification is a permanent decision, so consider the Pokémon's IVs and intended use first.

💎 Purified Status

Shadow status is removed through purification.

⭐ IV Change

Purification increases each IV by the applicable purification amount, up to 15.

🍬 Resource Consideration

Purification has its own Candy and Stardust requirements.

Purified vs Shadow

A purified Munchlax may be attractive for collectors or for players who prefer the purified status, but purification is not automatically the strongest competitive choice.

Lucky Munchlax

Lucky Munchlax is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon are useful for players looking to reduce the Stardust cost of power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Status

Lucky status comes from Pokémon GO trading.

✨ Reduced Power-Up Cost

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

🎁 Trading

Trading is required to obtain a Lucky Pokémon.

Is Lucky Munchlax Worth Keeping?

Yes, especially if you enjoy collecting unusual Munchlax variants or plan to evolve one into Snorlax. Check its IVs after the trade because traded Pokémon are re-rolled during the trading process.

100% IV Munchlax

A perfect Munchlax has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A perfect specimen is commonly called a 4-star Pokémon in Pokémon GO's appraisal system.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Why Keep a 100% Munchlax?

A perfect Munchlax is an excellent collector's target and can be evolved into a perfect Snorlax. Whether it deserves substantial investment depends on the battle format and the current usefulness of Snorlax.

XXL & XXS Munchlax

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon by size. XXL and XXS are size classifications and should not be confused with IV quality.

📏
XXL Munchlax

XXL Munchlax is an exceptionally large specimen. Its size is separate from Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

🔹
XXS Munchlax

XXS Munchlax is an exceptionally small specimen. A small Pokémon can still have perfect IVs.

Collector Tip

Check the size classification before transferring unusual Munchlax. A combination such as Shiny + 100% IV + XXL or XXS can be particularly interesting to collectors.

Munchlax PvP Guide

Munchlax can be more interesting in PvP than its raid performance suggests. Its high HP and access to useful moves can give it a niche in certain CP-limited formats.

Great League

Munchlax can function as a bulky Pokémon in Great League when its moveset and matchup profile fit the current meta. Its Normal typing provides useful protection against Ghost-type attacks.

Ultra League

Munchlax can be considered in some specialized Ultra League situations, although its effectiveness depends on the current cup rules, available moves and opposing Pokémon.

Master League

Munchlax's maximum CP is much lower than that of many Master League staples, making it less natural for open Master League play.

PvP IVs

Munchlax FAQ

What type is Munchlax?

Munchlax is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Munchlax weak against?

Munchlax is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What is Munchlax resistant to?

Munchlax's Normal typing gives it strong protection against Ghost-type attacks.

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

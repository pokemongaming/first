Lucky Mesprit

Lucky Mesprit is not a separate species or evolution. It is a special status that can result from Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

🍀 Lucky Mesprit

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up. A Lucky Mesprit can therefore be attractive for players planning a long-term investment.

Why Keep a Lucky Mesprit?

15 — XXL

XXL Mesprit

📏 XXL Mesprit

An XXL Mesprit is an unusually large individual Mesprit. XXL status is an individual size attribute and does not create a new species or evolution.

Why Keep an XXL Mesprit?

XXL Pokémon can be attractive to collectors and players who enjoy building a collection around size records. Size status is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Mesprit does not automatically have perfect IVs.

Important:

XXL, XL, regular and XXS size classifications should not be confused with individual values. A Pokémon can have an unusual size and any IV combination.

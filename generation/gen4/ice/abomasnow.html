Why Abomasnow's Typing Matters

The Grass/Ice combination gives Abomasnow useful offensive coverage but also creates a large weakness profile. Fire is especially dangerous, while Bug, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Rock and Steel attacks also deal super-effective damage.

Raid / Mega Investment

For a Mega Abomasnow project, a high-IV Abomasnow is a reasonable long-term investment because the same Pokémon can be reverted into its Mega form when needed.

PvP IVs

PvP IV preferences can differ substantially from the traditional 15/15/15 approach. Always evaluate the specific league, CP limit and current moveset before discarding a lower-Attack Abomasnow.

TRAINER BATTLES

Abomasnow PvP Guide

Abomasnow's access to Powder Snow, Weather Ball and Energy Ball gives it meaningful PvP utility despite its long list of weaknesses.

Great League

Abomasnow can function as a flexible Grass/Ice attacker in Great League. Its inexpensive charged pressure can force shields while its typing gives it useful matchups against Water- and Ground-oriented opponents.

Ultra League

Abomasnow can also be considered in Ultra League, although its effectiveness depends heavily on the current meta and the exact IV distribution.

Master League

Its maximum CP is generally less suited to Master League than many legendary and pseudo-legendary options.

SHADOW FORM

Shadow Abomasnow

Shadow Abomasnow retains its Grass/Ice typing but receives the standard Shadow battle modifier. Its higher offensive pressure comes with reduced defensive durability.

Shadow Abomasnow Moves

For PvP, Powder Snow with Weather Ball and Energy Ball provides useful pressure. For PvE, Shadow Abomasnow is generally not a premier raid attacker and should be judged against stronger Grass and Ice alternatives.

Frustration:

A newly obtained Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. The timing of Rocket events and move-removal opportunities matters when planning a Shadow investment.

Should You Purify Shadow Abomasnow?

Do not purify automatically. First decide whether you want to preserve the Shadow form for its battle characteristics or whether you specifically want the purified Pokémon for Mega Evolution and other investment purposes.

Shadow Abomasnow IV Guide

A perfect 15/15/15 Shadow Abomasnow is a collector's target, but Shadow Pokémon should not automatically be discarded because they lack perfect IVs.

Shadow Abomasnow Availability

Shadow availability can change with Team GO Rocket storylines and rotations. Treat current availability as dynamic rather than permanently listing an old event as the current acquisition method.

SHINY FORM

Shiny Abomasnow

Shiny Abomasnow is available in Pokémon GO. Its alternate coloration makes it a popular collection target, and a Shiny Abomasnow can also be used for Mega Evolution.

Shiny Abomasnow

Shiny Abomasnow & Mega Evolution

A Shiny Abomasnow can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved, making a high-IV Shiny specimen an attractive long-term collector and Mega candidate.

Should You Keep a Shiny Abomasnow?

  • Keep your first Shiny for your collection.
  • Check its IVs before transferring duplicates.
  • Consider keeping a strong Shiny for Mega Evolution.
  • Avoid judging a Shiny only by CP.

FAQ

Abomasnow Frequently Asked Questions

What type is Abomasnow?

Abomasnow is a Grass/Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Abomasnow weak against?

Abomasnow is especially vulnerable to Fire and is also weak to Bug, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Rock and Steel.

What is Abomasnow's max CP?

Regular Abomasnow reaches 2,670 CP at Level 50 under standard maximum-IV conditions.

Is Abomasnow good for raids?

Regular Abomasnow is not generally a top-tier raid attacker. Mega Abomasnow is considerably more useful when Grass or Ice Mega utility is needed.

Is Shadow Abomasnow available?

Yes. Shadow Abomasnow is an available Pokémon GO form, although its current acquisition method can change with Team GO Rocket rotations.

Can Shadow Abomasnow be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Shadow Abomasnow is possible when the relevant Shadow and Shiny availability conditions are active.

Is Shiny Abomasnow available?

Yes. Shiny Abomasnow is available in Pokémon GO.

Can Shiny Abomasnow Mega Evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Abomasnow can be used for Mega Evolution while retaining its Shiny identity.

What is Mega Abomasnow's max CP?

Mega Abomasnow reaches 3,850 CP at Level 50 under maximum-IV conditions.

What are Mega Abomasnow's stats?

Mega Abomasnow has 240 Attack, 191 Defense and 207 HP in Pokémon GO.

How much Mega Energy does Abomasnow need?

The initial Mega Evolution requires Mega Energy, while repeat Mega Evolutions of the same Pokémon require less Energy. Check current in-game requirements before publishing event-specific numbers.

Is Mega Abomasnow good for raids?

Mega Abomasnow can be valuable for Grass- and Ice-focused raid teams, with its strongest role depending on the boss, moveset and available alternatives.

Does Abomasnow evolve?

Yes. Snover evolves into Abomasnow. Abomasnow itself does not have another normal evolution; it can Mega Evolve.

Should I power up Abomasnow or Mega Abomasnow?

If your goal is Mega raid utility, choose a strong Abomasnow that you are comfortable investing in and Mega Evolving. For PvP, evaluate the individual Pokémon using league-specific IV and CP considerations.

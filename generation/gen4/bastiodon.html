Bastiodon Overview

Bastiodon is the evolved form of Shieldon and was introduced in Generation 4. Its defining characteristic in Pokémon GO is its extremely defensive stat profile.

Although Bastiodon is not a conventional raid attacker, it has earned a strong reputation as a defensive Great League Pokémon because its combination of typing, bulk and CP distribution can make it difficult for many opponents to break through.

Bastiodon PvP Guide

Bastiodon is primarily known for its Great League PvP role. Its enormous defensive profile can allow it to absorb considerable neutral damage while applying pressure with Rock-type attacks.

Great League

Great League is Bastiodon's signature format. Trainers often build Bastiodon close to the 1,500 CP limit and take advantage of its exceptional bulk.

Great League Strategy

  • Build Bastiodon around the Great League CP limit.
  • Take advantage of its exceptional Defense.
  • Watch carefully for Fighting and Ground attacks.

  • Use Flamethrower for additional coverage.
  • Consider team composition carefully because Bastiodon has several important counters.

How to Use Bastiodon

Great League

Bastiodon works best when its defensive strengths are supported by teammates that can cover its weaknesses. Avoid exposing it unnecessarily to strong Fighting, Ground or Water pressure.

Team Building

  • Build around Bastiodon's extreme defensive profile.
  • Include teammates that can handle Fighting Pokémon.
  • Prepare for Ground-type threats.
  • Consider Water-type counters when planning the team.
  • Use Bastiodon's Rock pressure to punish appropriate targets.

Raid Battles

Bastiodon is generally not the first choice for maximizing raid damage. Trainers building raid teams should normally prioritize Pokémon with stronger offensive statistics and more efficient PvE movesets.

Bastiodon FAQ

What type is Bastiodon?

Bastiodon is a Rock and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Bastiodon weak to?

Bastiodon is particularly vulnerable to Fighting, Ground and Water attacks.

Is Bastiodon good in Pokémon GO?

Bastiodon is especially notable for Great League PvP because of its very high defensive profile. It is much less impressive as a raid attacker.

Is Bastiodon good in Great League?

Yes. Great League is Bastiodon's most important competitive format because its defensive stats work particularly well under the CP limit.

What is the best Bastiodon moveset?

A commonly recommended Great League build uses Smack Down with Stone Edge and Flamethrower, although moves and PvP rankings can change over time.

Is 100% Bastiodon good?

A 15/15/15 Bastiodon is an excellent hundo and collection target. However, perfect IVs are not necessarily the same as the ideal Great League IV spread.

What is a Lucky Bastiodon?

Lucky Bastiodon is a Bastiodon with Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon can be cheaper to power up with Stardust.

Is Shiny Bastiodon available?

Shiny Bastiodon can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters. Availability should be checked against the current game rotation.

Is Shadow Bastiodon better than regular Bastiodon?

Not automatically. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics, so Shadow Bastiodon should be evaluated according to the specific PvP or collection goal.

Should I purify Shadow Bastiodon?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen's IVs, CP, PvP potential and your personal collection goals before deciding.

What is XXL Bastiodon?

XXL Bastiodon is an unusually large Bastiodon. XXL status is a size characteristic rather than a 100% IV rating.

What is XXS Bastiodon?

XXS Bastiodon is an unusually small Bastiodon. It can be valuable to collectors interested in unusual Pokémon sizes.

Does Bastiodon evolve?

Yes. Shieldon evolves into Bastiodon.

Is Bastiodon good for raids?

Bastiodon is generally not a top raid attacker. Its greatest Pokémon GO value is its defensive Great League PvP role.

Is a Shiny Bastiodon stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily an alternate appearance and collection characteristic. Battle performance depends on the Pokémon's stats, IVs, moves and battle format.

Can Bastiodon be Lucky and Shiny at the same time?

Yes. Lucky and Shiny are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can potentially have multiple special collection attributes.

Can Bastiodon be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Bastiodon can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

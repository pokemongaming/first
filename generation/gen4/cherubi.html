What Does Grass Typing Mean?

Cherubi's pure Grass typing gives it useful resistances against Water, Electric, Grass and Ground attacks. However, it is vulnerable to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

Should You Power Up Cherubi?

Most players should prioritize evolving Cherubi into Cherrim rather than spending large amounts of Stardust on Cherubi itself. A high-IV or unusual variant can be worth keeping for collection purposes.

Purified Cherubi

A Purified Cherubi would be a Cherubi that was previously Shadow and then purified. Because Shadow Cherubi is not currently available, Purified Cherubi should not be advertised as a currently obtainable variant.

💎

Purified Status

Purification removes the Shadow status and provides the standard Purified Pokémon benefits.

🔄

Evolution

If a Purified Cherubi becomes obtainable, it can be evolved according to the normal Cherubi evolution rules.

Purified Pokémon Mechanics

  • Purification improves appraisal values.
  • Purification removes Frustration.
  • Purified Pokémon learn Return when purified.
  • Power-Up and evolution costs are reduced.
  • Purification permanently removes the Shadow status.
Do not automatically purify a Shadow Pokémon simply because its appraisal is lower. Shadow and Purified Pokémon serve different collection and battle goals.

Cherubi FAQ

What type is Cherubi?

Cherubi is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Cherubi weak to?

Cherubi is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

What is Cherubi's maximum CP?

Cherubi reaches 1,074 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Does Cherubi evolve?

Yes. Cherubi evolves into Cherrim for 50 Candy. The resulting Cherrim can be Overcast Form or Sunny Form.

Is Shiny Cherubi available?

Yes. Shiny Cherubi is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Cherubi available?

Shadow Cherubi is recognized in Pokémon GO data, but availability should not be claimed unless the current Team GO Rocket rotation specifically confirms it.

Can Cherubi be Purified?

Purification requires a Shadow Pokémon. Because Shadow Cherubi is not currently available as a standard Shadow encounter, Purified Cherubi should be treated as unavailable unless the game introduces an eligible Shadow encounter.

Can Cherubi be Lucky?

Yes. Cherubi can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Cherubi?

A 100% Cherubi, or Hundo Cherubi, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Cherubi?

XXL Cherubi is an extremely large Cherubi under Pokémon GO's size classification system.

What is XXS Cherubi?

XXS Cherubi is an extremely small Cherubi. XXS is a size attribute rather than a different species.

Is Cherubi good for raids?

Cherubi is not considered a serious high-end raid attacker. Players generally evolve it into Cherrim or keep it for collection purposes.

Is Cherubi good for PvP?

Cherubi has limited PvP relevance and is mainly of interest in niche low-CP formats.

What is the best Cherubi moveset?

Tackle and Seed Bomb are a commonly recommended Cherubi moveset, although its overall battle performance remains limited.

GEN 4 • GRASS TYPE

Tangrowth

Tangrowth is a Grass-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it evolves from Tangela and can appear in several collection variants, including Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky examples. Large and small specimens can also be recorded as XXL or XXS.

Why Tangrowth's Typing Matters

Tangrowth's pure Grass typing gives it strong offensive potential against Water, Ground and Rock targets. However, it also leaves Tangrowth vulnerable to several common attack types, including Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug.

10 • PVP

Tangrowth PvP Guide

Tangrowth's combination of Grass typing, bulk and charged-move options can make it interesting in selected PvP formats.

Great League

A standard Tangrowth is generally not naturally suited to Great League because of its high CP potential, but special cups and restricted formats can change which Pokémon are practical.

Ultra League

Tangrowth can be considered in some Ultra League situations depending on its moveset, IV distribution and the current meta.

Master League

Tangrowth is less naturally positioned for unrestricted Master League competition than the most prominent Legendary and pseudo-Legendary choices. Always evaluate current rankings rather than relying on a permanent tier.

13 • PURIFIED

Should You Purify Shadow Tangela Before Evolution?

If your goal is to keep a Shadow Tangrowth, evolve the Shadow Tangela without purifying it. If your goal is a Purified Tangrowth, purification can be performed before evolution. Compare IVs, move requirements and your collection goals before making the decision.

Important

Purification is not simply a way to create a stronger Shadow Pokémon. It removes the Shadow status, so players interested in Shadow performance should avoid purifying automatically.

Extra-Small Individual

Size vs IV

XXL and XXS are not the same thing as high or low IVs. A Pokémon can be XXL and 100% IV, XXS and 100% IV, or have any other eligible IV combination.

23 • FAQ

Tangrowth FAQ

What type is Tangrowth?

Tangrowth is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Tangrowth weak to?

Tangrowth is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

What is Tangrowth resistant to?

Tangrowth resists Water, Ground, Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Tangrowth evolve?

Tangrowth is the final evolution in the Tangela family. It does not have another normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Tangrowth available?

Shiny Tangrowth can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Tangela.

Can you get Shadow Tangrowth?

Shadow Tangrowth can exist through the evolution of an eligible Shadow Tangela.

Can Shadow Tangrowth be purified?

Shadow Tangela can be purified before evolution, producing a Purified Tangrowth after evolution. Purification permanently removes Shadow status.

Is Lucky Tangrowth possible?

Yes. Tangrowth can have Lucky status when obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system and the relevant Lucky Trade mechanics.

What is a 100% IV Tangrowth?

A 100% IV Tangrowth has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is a Lucky Tangrowth automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky status and IVs are separate characteristics. A Lucky Tangrowth can have different IV combinations.

What is XXL Tangrowth?

XXL Tangrowth is an unusually large individual according to Pokémon GO's size system. XXL is not a separate species.

What is XXS Tangrowth?

XXS Tangrowth is an unusually small individual. Like XXL, it is a size classification rather than a separate species.

Can Tangrowth be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate characteristics, so a Tangrowth can potentially have both statuses.

Can Tangrowth be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, allowing combinations such as XXL hundo Tangrowth.

Is Tangrowth good for raids?

Tangrowth can serve as a Grass-type raid attacker, particularly when its current moveset is effective for the matchup. Its value should be compared with the strongest Grass attackers available.

Is Tangrowth good in PvP?

Tangrowth can have niche PvP applications depending on the league, moveset and current metagame.

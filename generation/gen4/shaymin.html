XXL Shaymin

XXL is a size classification rather than a different Pokémon species or form. An XXL Shaymin remains Shaymin and keeps its normal battle stats.

XXL Shaymin

Shaymin FAQ

What type is Shaymin?

Land Forme Shaymin is Grass type. Sky Forme Shaymin is Grass / Flying type.

What is Shaymin's Pokédex number?

Shaymin is #492 in the National Pokédex.

What is Shaymin's max CP?

Land Forme reaches 3,691 CP at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs. Sky Forme reaches 4,061 CP under the same conditions. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Does Shaymin have two forms?

Yes. Pokémon GO features Land Forme and Sky Forme.

Is Shiny Shaymin available?

Yes. Shiny Shaymin is an established collectible variant, with availability tied to eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Shaymin available?

No current Shadow Shaymin form is available. Do not publish fictional Shadow Shaymin stats.

Is Purified Shaymin available?

No. There is currently no Shadow Shaymin that could be purified.

Can Shaymin be Lucky?

Shaymin is currently not tradable, so a Lucky Shaymin cannot currently be obtained through normal Lucky trading mechanics. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

What is a 100% Shaymin?

A 100% Shaymin has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Shaymin?

XXL Shaymin is an extra-large size classification. It is not a separate species.

What is XXS Shaymin?

XXS Shaymin is an extra-small size classification. It remains the same Shaymin species and form.

Is Shaymin good for raids?

Shaymin can contribute as a Grass-type attacker, but its current raid value should be compared against other available Grass Pokémon and their movesets.

Is Sky Forme stronger than Land Forme?

Sky Forme has higher Attack and a higher maximum CP, while Land Forme has substantially more Defense. The better choice depends on the role and battle format.

Can Shaymin change between Land and Sky Forme?

Shaymin's forms are connected to the game's form change mechanics. The exact form-change availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO implementation.

Rotom

Rotom is a unique Electric-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region whose form changes when it possesses different appliances. Pokémon GO features Base Rotom plus Heat, Wash, Frost, Fan and Mow Rotom forms.

Rotom Overview

Rotom is a one-member Pokémon family and does not evolve into another Pokémon. Instead, Rotom can appear in different appliance-based forms, with each form changing its secondary typing and some of its moves.

Lucky Rotom

Lucky is not a separate Rotom species or form. It is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading.

✨ Lucky Rotom

A Rotom can have the Lucky status if it becomes Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special Stardust investment advantages compared with ordinary Pokémon.

Why Lucky Rotom Can Be Valuable

  • Reduced Stardust requirements for Powering Up.
  • Useful for collectors completing special Rotom variants.
  • Can combine the Lucky status with desirable IVs.
  • Can be especially attractive for collectors who enjoy unusual Pokémon combinations.
Important:

Lucky Rotom should be presented on your site as a status/attribute, not as a separate Rotom form.

100% IV Rotom

A 100% IV Rotom has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. The term "hundo" is commonly used by Pokémon GO players for this perfect-IV combination.

A perfect Rotom is primarily a collection and investment milestone. Its usefulness still depends on the form, moveset and battle format.

XXL & XXS Rotom

XXL and XXS are size classifications rather than different Rotom forms. They can make a Rotom more interesting for collectors and size-related achievements.

📏 XXL Rotom An exceptionally large size classification. Useful as a collection and size milestone.

Rotom FAQ

What type is Rotom?

Base Rotom is Electric / Ghost type.

What generation is Rotom from?

Rotom was introduced in Generation 4 and is associated with the Sinnoh region.

Does Rotom evolve?

No. Rotom does not have a normal evolution chain. Instead, it has multiple appliance-based forms.

How many Rotom forms are in Pokémon GO?

Pokémon GO has Base Rotom plus Heat, Wash, Frost, Fan and Mow Rotom forms.

Is Shiny Rotom available?

Yes. Shiny Rotom has been released in Pokémon GO, although availability can depend on specific releases and events.

Can Rotom be Lucky?

What is 100% Rotom?

What Does Regigigas's Normal Typing Mean?

Regigigas has a single Normal typing. This gives it a relatively simple defensive profile compared with dual-type Pokémon, with Fighting as its primary weakness and Ghost attacks unable to damage it normally.

Its Normal typing also affects the way its available moves are evaluated. A strong individual Regigigas should be judged according to its intended PvE or PvP role rather than its impressive Attack stat alone.

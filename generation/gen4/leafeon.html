05 • EVOLUTION

How to Evolve Eevee into Leafeon

Leafeon requires 25 Eevee Candy and a specific evolution condition. The repeatable method is to evolve Eevee while within range of an active Mossy Lure Module.

Eevee #133 • Normal
→
Leafeon #470 • Grass

Repeatable Evolution Method

  • Have at least 25 Eevee Candy.
  • Find or activate a PokéStop with a Mossy Lure Module.
  • Stay within range of the PokéStop.
  • Open the Eevee you want to evolve.
  • Confirm that the evolution preview shows Leafeon.
  • Spend 25 Eevee Candy to evolve.

Linnea Nickname Trick

The nickname method uses the name Linnea to guarantee Leafeon. This method is intended as a one-time-per-account shortcut, so it is best saved for an Eevee that you particularly want to evolve.

Important Evolution Check

Before spending Candy, make sure the evolution button displays the Leafeon silhouette. Do not rely on a nickname or location without confirming the in-game evolution preview.

13 • PURIFIED

Purified Leafeon

Purified Leafeon can result from purifying an eligible Shadow Eevee and then evolving it into Leafeon.

💠

Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has Shadow status after purification.

⭐

Purification IV Bonus

Purification increases each IV by the game's applicable purification amount, capped at 15.

🟢

Purified Evolution

The purified Eevee can then be evolved using the normal Leafeon evolution method.

Should You Purify Shadow Eevee Before Leafeon?

Purification is a strategic decision. If you want to preserve Shadow performance, do not purify simply because the IVs will increase. If you prefer a purified collection entry or need a specific purified build, purification may make sense.

Think Before Purifying

Once a Shadow Pokémon has been purified, the Shadow battle profile is removed. Compare both options before making the change.

18 • PVP

Leafeon PvP Guide

Leafeon's strong Defense and Grass typing give it some useful matchup potential, but its overall PvP value depends heavily on league, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

A Leafeon built for Great League must satisfy the league's CP limit. PvP IV optimization can therefore differ considerably from the conventional 15/15/15 collection target.

Ultra League

Leafeon's maximum CP makes Ultra League investment more natural than Great League, although trainers should compare it with other Grass options before spending large amounts of Stardust and Candy XL.

Master League

Leafeon is generally less attractive in Master League than Pokémon specifically designed around the format's higher stat requirements.

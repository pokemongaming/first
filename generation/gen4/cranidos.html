01 • OVERVIEW

Cranidos Overview

Cranidos is a Rock-type Fossil Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It evolves into Rampardos after collecting the required Candy. Cranidos itself has a high Attack stat for its stage of evolution, while its relatively low Defense means it is more vulnerable in prolonged battles.

14 • SIZE

XXL & XXS Cranidos

Pokémon GO can identify individual Pokémon with unusually large or unusually small sizes. XXL and XXS status is separate from IV quality and does not mean that the Pokémon has perfect stats.

XXL CRANIDOS

Extra Large

XXL Cranidos is a large-sized Cranidos. Its size classification is independent of its Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

  • Large size classification
  • Can have any IV combination
  • Can be Shiny if obtained as a Shiny
  • Can be Lucky when obtained through trade
XXS CRANIDOS

Extra Small

XXS Cranidos is an unusually small Cranidos. Like XXL status, its size does not determine whether the Pokémon has good IVs.

  • Small size classification
  • Can have any IV combination
  • Valuable for collectors
  • Can coexist with Shiny or Lucky status
Size vs IV reminder:

XXL, XXS and 100% are different characteristics. A Cranidos can be XXL and 100% IV, XXS and 100% IV, or either size with low IVs.

18 • PVP

Cranidos PvP Guide

Great League

Cranidos has a high Attack stat but relatively low Defense. This makes it less naturally bulky than many Pokémon designed around Great League battles. Team composition and moveset therefore matter heavily when considering Cranidos for PvP.

Ultra League

Cranidos is generally less attractive as an Ultra League investment than many Pokémon specifically optimized for that CP range.

Master League

Cranidos' maximum CP is relatively low for the Master League environment. Its evolved form, Rampardos, is the more relevant target for players interested in its offensive potential.

Cranidos FAQ

What type is Cranidos?

Cranidos is a pure Rock-type Pokémon.

Cranidos is weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel-type attacks.

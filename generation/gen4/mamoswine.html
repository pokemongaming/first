Mamoswine Overview

Why Mamoswine's Typing Matters

Mamoswine's dual typing gives it access to two important offensive categories. Ice attacks are particularly useful against Dragon, Flying and Ground-type targets, while Ground attacks can be effective against Fire, Electric, Rock, Steel and Poison-type opponents.

08 — IV GUIDE

Mamoswine IV Guide

For raid attackers, a 15/15/15 Mamoswine is the perfect IV combination. However, IV perfection is not required for a strong raid Pokémon.

15 HP IV

100% IV Mamoswine

A 100% IV Mamoswine has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It reaches the highest CP possible for its level and is an excellent long-term investment for collectors and dedicated raiders.

Perfect IV

15 / 15 / 15 = 100% IV Mamoswine

Is a 100% Mamoswine Necessary?

No. A high-level Mamoswine with slightly lower IVs can still perform extremely well. Moveset, level, resources and the specific raid matchup all matter.

09 — PVP

Mamoswine PvP Guide

Mamoswine's typing and charged-move pressure give it interesting PvP applications, especially in formats where its Ice and Ground coverage matters.

Master League

Mamoswine can apply significant offensive pressure in Master League thanks to its high CP potential and access to useful Ice and Ground attacks. However, its numerous weaknesses mean that matchup knowledge is important.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Mamoswine is generally more naturally suited to higher-CP formats. League-specific cups can change its usefulness, so always check the current rules and available move pool before building a dedicated PvP Mamoswine.

PvP note:

PvP rankings change as moves, Pokémon and formats are updated. Avoid treating a static ranking as permanent.

15 — SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Mamoswine

Pokémon GO can assign size classifications to Pokémon. XXL and XXS Mamoswine are size variants, not separate species or evolutions.

🏆 XXL Mamoswine

An unusually large Mamoswine. XXL status is primarily relevant to collection, showcases and size-based gameplay features.

🔹 XXS Mamoswine

An unusually small Mamoswine. XXS status can be valuable for collectors and size-based gameplay features.

Do XXL and XXS Change Mamoswine's Battle Stats?

Size classifications should not be treated as direct replacements for IVs. A larger or smaller Mamoswine is not automatically a stronger raid attacker.

Collector tip:

Keep interesting XXL and XXS specimens if you are building a complete Pokémon GO collection or participating in size-based features.

17 — RESOURCES

Mamoswine Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Mamoswine requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Mamoswine can be useful in multiple raid roles, choosing which specimens to power up is important.

  • Keep high-IV Mamoswine for long-term investments.
  • Save Candy XL for Pokémon you intend to use at higher levels.
  • Check the moveset before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • Consider keeping both Ice and Ground builds.
  • Lucky Mamoswine can reduce Stardust requirements.
  • Compare Shadow and regular Mamoswine before investing heavily.

19 — BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Mamoswine

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Ice or Ground damage is preferred.
  • Confirm the Mamoswine has the appropriate moveset.
  • Check level and IVs.
  • Consider the raid boss's moveset.
  • Compare regular and Shadow Mamoswine.

Ice-Type Role

Ground-Type Role

A Ground-focused Mamoswine can provide an additional Ground attacker for appropriate raid matchups, particularly for players who have enough Candy and Stardust to build multiple specialized teams.

Long-Term Investment

21 — FAQ

Mamoswine FAQ

What type is Mamoswine?

Mamoswine is an Ice and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Mamoswine weak to?

Mamoswine is weak to Fire, Water, Grass, Fighting and Steel-type attacks.

Is Mamoswine good for raids?

Yes. Mamoswine is particularly useful as an Ice-type raid attacker and can also perform a Ground-type role.

What is the best Mamoswine moveset?

Powder Snow and Avalanche are a strong Ice-focused combination, while Mud Slap and High Horsepower provide a Ground-focused option.

Is Shiny Mamoswine available?

Yes. Shiny Mamoswine is available through the evolution of eligible Shiny Swinub.

Is Shadow Mamoswine good?

Shadow Mamoswine can be a powerful damage-focused option, particularly for raid players who want increased offensive pressure.

Should I purify Shadow Mamoswine?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, especially if you care about raid damage.

Is Lucky Mamoswine worth powering up?

A Lucky Mamoswine with good IVs can be an excellent Stardust-efficient investment.

What is a 100% Mamoswine?

A 100% Mamoswine has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Mamoswine?

XXL Mamoswine is an unusually large Mamoswine size variant.

What is XXS Mamoswine?

XXS Mamoswine is an unusually small Mamoswine size variant.

Does Mamoswine evolve?

Yes. Mamoswine evolves from Piloswine, which evolves from Swinub.

What region is Mamoswine from?

Mamoswine was introduced in Generation 4 and is associated with the Sinnoh region.

Is Mamoswine good in PvP?

Mamoswine can be useful in PvP formats, especially where its Ice and Ground coverage provides valuable matchup pressure.

Is 100% Mamoswine necessary for raids?

No. A perfect IV Mamoswine is desirable, but high-level Pokémon with slightly lower IVs can still perform extremely well.

Do XXL and XXS Mamoswine have different IVs?

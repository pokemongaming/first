13 · LUCKY

Lucky Spiritomb

Lucky Spiritomb is a special trade outcome rather than a separate species or evolution. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups and are particularly popular among collectors.

🍀 Lucky Spiritomb

A Lucky Spiritomb is identified by its Lucky status and special background effect in Pokémon GO.

Lucky Spiritomb Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term collection projects.
  • Can have strong IVs after a Lucky Trade.
  • Can be combined with other desirable attributes depending on the trade and encounter.

Lucky Spiritomb IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor under the game's Lucky Trade mechanics. A Lucky Spiritomb is therefore guaranteed to have a stronger minimum IV profile than an ordinary random trade.

Lucky status is obtained through trading and is not an evolutionary form. The exact probability of a Lucky Trade depends on Pokémon GO's current mechanics and should not be presented as a permanent fixed rate without verification.

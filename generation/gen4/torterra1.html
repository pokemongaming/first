Torterra Overview

Torterra is the final evolution of the Turtwig family. It combines Grass and Ground typing with high HP and solid defensive stats. Its strongest Pokémon GO identity is as a Grass attacker using Razor Leaf and Frenzy Plant.

Why Torterra's Typing Matters

Torterra's Grass/Ground combination gives it useful resistances to Electric, Ground and Rock attacks. However, the combination also creates a major vulnerability to Ice-type attacks.

Shadow Torterra

Shadow Torterra IVs

A Shadow Pokémon does not need to have perfect IVs to be useful. When evaluating Shadow Torterra, consider the Pokémon's IVs, level, moveset, resources and intended role together.

Shadow Torterra Tip

Do not automatically transfer a Shadow Torterra because of imperfect IVs. Shadow performance and IVs should be evaluated separately.

Frustration

Shadow Pokémon normally know Frustration when caught. Special events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration with another Charged Attack.

Purified Torterra

A Shadow Torterra can be purified. Purification changes its status from Shadow to Purified and provides several mechanical benefits.

✨

Purified Status

Purified Torterra is no longer a Shadow Pokémon.

💎

IV Improvement

Purification improves each IV by 2 points, up to the maximum of 15.

🌟

Return

A purified Pokémon learns Return when purified.

💰

Reduced Costs

Purified Pokémon have reduced Candy and Stardust costs for certain investments.

Purification Cost

Standard Torterra Purification

Current database data lists a purification cost of 3 Torterra Candy and 3,000 Stardust.

Should You Purify Shadow Torterra?

Purification is not automatically an upgrade. If your goal is to keep the Shadow damage bonus, purifying removes that Shadow status. Compare the two options before making the decision.

Lucky Torterra

Lucky Torterra is not a separate species or permanent form. It is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading.

🍀

Lucky Status

A Lucky Torterra is obtained through a Lucky Trade.

💫

Lower Power-Up Cost

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up compared with ordinary Pokémon.

Lucky Torterra IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary wild Pokémon. The exact IVs of a Lucky Torterra are determined when the trade becomes Lucky.

Lucky Torterra Investment

A Lucky Torterra can be attractive for trainers who want to reduce Stardust costs while building a high-level Grass/Ground Pokémon.

XXL Torterra

XXL Torterra is a size variant. XXL status does not change Torterra's species or typing, but it can be useful for collection goals and certain size-related game features.

XXL Extra-large Torterra

A Torterra classified in the XXL size category.

Standard Normal size range

Most Torterra encountered fall into ordinary size categories.

Does XXL Torterra Have Higher Stats?

No. XXL classification should not be confused with higher IVs. Size and IVs are separate attributes.

Collector Tip

An XXL Hundo, XXL Shiny or XXL Lucky Torterra can be especially interesting for collectors because multiple desirable attributes can exist on the same Pokémon.

XXS Torterra

XXS Torterra is the extra-small size category. Like XXL status, XXS is a size classification and does not directly increase or decrease Torterra's IV values.

XXS Extra-small Torterra

Torterra classified within the XXS size range.

Size ≠ IV Separate attributes

A tiny Torterra can have poor, average or perfect IVs.

Is XXS Torterra Rare?

XXS Pokémon are primarily interesting to collectors and trainers who care about size-based achievements. Rarity can depend on the encounter method and the Pokémon GO distribution of sizes.

Torterra PvP Guide

Torterra's high HP and Grass/Ground typing give it some interesting matchup opportunities, but its 4× Ice weakness is a major liability.

Great League

Great League Torterra requires careful CP management. Bulk-oriented IV spreads can be more relevant here than simply using a 15/15/15 specimen.

Ultra League

Torterra can be considered in Ultra League when its moveset and IV spread fit the format. Check current PvP rankings before investing heavily.

Master League

Torterra's maximum CP makes it less naturally suited to the highest-level Master League environment than many Legendary and other top-end Pokémon.

PvP IV Tip

A PvP-optimized Torterra does not necessarily need 15/15/15 IVs. League CP limits can favor different IV distributions.

Torterra FAQ

What type is Torterra?

Torterra is a Grass/Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Torterra weak to?

Torterra is weak to Ice, Bug, Fire and Flying. Ice is its most severe weakness.

What is Torterra's biggest weakness?

Ice-type attacks are particularly dangerous because Torterra has a 4× weakness to Ice.

What is Torterra's Max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Torterra can reach 3,318 CP.

Is Torterra good for raids?

Torterra can function as a Grass attacker, especially with Razor Leaf and Frenzy Plant, but trainers should compare it with stronger current Grass options.

Is Shiny Torterra available?

Yes. Shiny Torterra is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Torterra available?

Shadow Torterra exists in Pokémon GO. Its current availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and game events.

Can Shadow Torterra be purified?

Yes. Eligible Shadow Torterra can be purified. Purification removes its Shadow status and provides the standard Purified Pokémon benefits.

What happens when Torterra is purified?

Purification improves its IVs by up to 2 points per stat, removes the Shadow status, and teaches Return.

Is Lucky Torterra a separate form?

No. Lucky is a special Pokémon status obtained through trading rather than a separate species form.

What is a 100% Torterra?

A 100% or Hundo Torterra has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Torterra have higher IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Torterra can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Torterra have lower stats?

XXS is a size classification and should not be interpreted as a reduction in IVs or battle stats.

What is Torterra's best Grass moveset?

Razor Leaf and Frenzy Plant are a recommended Grass-focused moveset.

What is Torterra's evolution family?

Turtwig evolves into Grotle, and Grotle evolves into Torterra.

Can a Shiny Torterra be Lucky?

Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially combine multiple desirable statuses when the game mechanics allow it.

Can a Hundo Torterra be XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate attributes, so a Torterra can combine perfect IVs with an XXL size classification.

Should I keep a 100% Torterra?

Yes, a Hundo is generally worth keeping, especially for collection and long-term investment purposes. Whether it deserves immediate power-up resources depends on its moveset and your team needs.

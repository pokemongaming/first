Glaceon FAQ

What type is Glaceon?

Glaceon is a pure Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Glaceon weak to?

Glaceon is weak to Fire, Fighting, Rock and Steel-type attacks.

Is Glaceon good for raids?

Glaceon can be a useful Ice-type raid attacker, especially for players who need accessible Ice damage.

Is Shiny Glaceon available?

Shiny Glaceon can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution methods.

Does Shiny Glaceon have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual variation. IVs, level and moves determine battle performance.

What is Shadow Glaceon?

Shadow Glaceon is the Shadow variation of Glaceon with a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

Should I purify Shadow Glaceon?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version with your reasons for wanting a Purified Glaceon before deciding.

What is a 100% IV Glaceon?

A 100% IV Glaceon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Glaceon have higher stats?

No. XXL status represents Pokémon size and does not automatically improve combat stats.

Does XXS Glaceon have lower stats?

No. XXS status is primarily a size variation and does not directly reduce Glaceon's battle stats.

Is Lucky Glaceon worth powering up?

Lucky Glaceon can be attractive for long-term investment because Lucky Pokémon have useful resource benefits.

01 • OVERVIEW

Chimchar Overview

Chimchar is a Fire-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4 and is one of the three Sinnoh starter Pokémon. It evolves into Monferno and eventually Infernape.

Chimchar is more important as an evolution-family Pokémon than as a standalone high-level raid attacker. Its main long-term value comes from collecting good IV specimens and evolving strong Chimchar into its later forms.

Chimchar Type

Why Chimchar's Fire Typing Matters

Fire attacks are useful against several common Pokémon types, especially Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel. Chimchar itself has the pure Fire typing, while its final evolution Infernape gains a secondary Fighting type.

    12 • PERFECT IV

    💯 100% IV Chimchar

    A 100% IV Chimchar has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In Pokémon GO terminology, this is often called a hundo.

    15 ATTACK IV
    15 DEFENSE IV
    15 HP IV
    💯 Perfect Chimchar = 15 / 15 / 15

    A perfect Chimchar is particularly desirable for collectors and players who want to evolve the strongest possible specimen.

    Should You Power Up a 100% Chimchar?

    Usually, the better long-term approach is to evolve a perfect Chimchar and evaluate the final evolution before making a large Stardust investment. The usefulness of the final form depends on its current moves and the Pokémon GO metagame.

    13 • SIZE

    🦣 XXL Chimchar

    XXL Chimchar is an unusually large specimen. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Chimchar can have any combination of Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

    🦣
    XXL Chimchar

    Large-size Chimchar. Its size does not directly mean that it has better IVs or higher battle performance.

    📏
    Size Collection

    XXL Pokémon can be useful for collectors and size-related medals or features.

    XXL vs IVs

    Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Chimchar can also be a 100% IV Chimchar, but being XXL alone does not indicate perfect IVs.

    14 • SIZE

    🔹 XXS Chimchar

    XXS Chimchar is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic and does not automatically determine the Pokémon's IVs.

    🔹
    XXS Chimchar

    A very small Chimchar that can be desirable for collectors and size-focused players.

    📏
    Size ≠ IV

    XXS status does not mean that the Pokémon has poor or excellent IVs. Check the appraisal separately.

    XXS Chimchar Collector Value

    A rare combination such as XXS + Shiny, XXS + Lucky, or XXS + 100% IV can be particularly interesting to collectors.

    Chimchar PvP Guide

    Chimchar is not normally a top priority for standard CP-limited PvP formats. Its main PvP relevance comes from the possibility of using its evolutionary family in formats where their CP and moves are appropriate.

    Great League

    Chimchar can fit within lower CP limits, but its overall battle performance means that players should compare it carefully against other available Fire Pokémon before committing resources.

    Ultra League

    Chimchar's stats make Ultra League use relatively niche. Its evolved forms are generally more relevant when their CP and moves fit the format.

    Master League

    Chimchar is not designed as a Master League investment. Players looking for a serious Master League Fire Pokémon should generally consider stronger candidates.

    22 • FAQ

    Chimchar FAQ

    What type is Chimchar?

    Chimchar is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

    What is Chimchar weak to?

    Chimchar is weak to Water, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

    Does Chimchar evolve?

    Yes. Chimchar evolves into Monferno and then Infernape.

    Is Shiny Chimchar available?

    Yes. Shiny Chimchar is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

    Can Shadow Chimchar be purified?

    An eligible Shadow Chimchar can be purified using the Pokémon GO purification system.

    What is a 100% Chimchar?

    A 100% Chimchar has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is Lucky Chimchar?

    Lucky Chimchar is a Chimchar that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

    Is XXL Chimchar better?

    XXL size does not automatically make Chimchar stronger. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

    Is XXS Chimchar rare?

    XXS Chimchar can be desirable for collectors because it represents an unusually small specimen.

    Is Chimchar good for raids?

    Chimchar itself is generally not a priority raid attacker. Its main value comes from its evolution family and collection potential.

    Should I evolve a 100% Chimchar?

    A 100% Chimchar is an excellent candidate for evolution, especially if you want a perfect-IV evolution family.

    Should I purify Shadow Chimchar?

    Not automatically. Compare the value of keeping the Shadow Pokémon with the benefits of purification before making the decision.

    Can Shiny Chimchar be evolved?

    Yes. Shiny Chimchar can evolve while retaining its Shiny status.

    Can Lucky Chimchar evolve?

    Yes. Lucky status can remain associated with the Pokémon as it evolves.

    Can an XXL Chimchar also be Shiny?

    Yes. Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so special combinations can occur.

    Can XXS Chimchar be 100% IV?

    Yes. Size and IVs are independent characteristics, so an XXS Chimchar can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Why Stunky's Typing Matters

Stunky's Poison and Dark typing gives it a useful defensive profile with several resistances and a particularly narrow weakness profile. However, its relatively low overall stats mean its evolution, Skuntank, is generally the more important long-term battle investment.

Stunky Evolution

Stunky evolves into Skuntank using Stunky Candy.

Stunky → Skuntank

Stunky evolves into Skuntank using 50 Candy.

Should You Evolve Stunky?

For most players, Skuntank is the more useful Pokémon for battle-focused projects. However, collectors may want to keep special versions of Stunky before evolving them.

Regular Stunky

Regular Stunky is the standard version of the Pokémon. It is the base form used for evolution, IV evaluation and most collection projects.

Regular Form

The standard version of Stunky with no special Shadow, Purified, Lucky or size classification.

  • Can evolve into Skuntank.
  • Can have different IV combinations.
  • Can be Shiny when available through eligible encounters.
  • Can have XXL or XXS size classification.
  • May be worth keeping for a future evolution project.

Shiny Stunky

Shiny Stunky is the alternate-color version of Stunky. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not automatically improve IVs or battle performance.

✨

Shiny Stunky

A collectible alternate-color version of Stunky. A Shiny Stunky can still have different IVs, sizes and evolution potential.

Should You Evolve Shiny Stunky?

If you do not already own Shiny Skuntank, evolving a Shiny Stunky can be useful for completing your Shiny evolution family collection.

Shadow Stunky is the Shadow version of Stunky. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts and can require more resources to power up.

Purified Stunky

Purified Stunky is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Stunky. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon into its Purified version.

✨

Purified Stunky

A Purified Pokémon no longer receives the Shadow battle characteristics of its previous form.

Should You Purify Shadow Stunky?

  • Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow version.
  • Compare the IV change from purification.
  • Consider whether you prefer collection value.
  • Think about future evolution into Skuntank.
  • Do not purify automatically without considering both versions.

Is a 100% Stunky Worth Keeping?

Yes. A perfect IV Stunky is valuable for collectors and can also be considered for evolution into a high-IV Skuntank. However, the best IV spread for competitive PvP can differ from the standard 15/15/15 combination.

XXL Stunky

XXL Stunky is a larger-than-standard size version of Stunky. Size classification is primarily a collection feature.

📏

XXL Stunky

A large Stunky size classification that can be useful for collection goals and eligible Showcase competitions.

Does XXL Stunky Have Better Stats?

XXL status does not automatically mean the Pokémon has better IVs, higher Attack or stronger battle performance. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Stunky FAQ

What type is Stunky?

Stunky is a Poison and Dark-type Pokémon.

What does Stunky evolve into?

Stunky evolves into Skuntank.

How much Candy does Stunky need to evolve?

Stunky evolves into Skuntank using 50 Candy.

Can Stunky be Shiny?

Yes, Shiny Stunky can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Stunky different from regular Stunky?

Yes. Shadow Stunky has Shadow battle characteristics while retaining Stunky's Poison and Dark typing.

Should I purify Shadow Stunky?

Not automatically. Consider the IV changes, collection value and whether you want to retain the Shadow version.

No. XXL size does not automatically increase Stunky's IVs or battle stats.

Does XXS Stunky have weaker stats?

No. XXS size is separate from IVs and standard battle performance.

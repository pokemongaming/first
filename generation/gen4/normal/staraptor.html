Staraptor

Staraptor is a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It is the final evolution of Starly and Staravia and is known in Pokémon GO for its strong Flying-type attacks, high Attack stat and access to the powerful move Brave Bird.

Staraptor Overview

Staraptor is the final evolutionary stage of the Starly family. It evolves from Staravia and combines Normal and Flying typing with a strong offensive stat profile. In Pokémon GO, Staraptor can be useful as a Flying attacker, particularly when equipped with appropriate Flying-type moves.

Why Staraptor's Typing Matters

Staraptor's Normal/Flying combination gives it useful offensive access to Flying-type damage while also providing resistances to several common attack types. Its Flying typing is especially important when using Staraptor against Fighting-, Bug- and Grass-type targets.

09 • PVP

Staraptor PvP Guide

Staraptor can function as an aggressive PvP Pokémon because of its strong charged attacks. However, its low defensive stat means that shield management and timing are important.

Great League

Staraptor can occasionally appear in limited formats or cups where its typing and moves are useful. Its availability and performance depend heavily on the current cup rules.

Ultra League

Staraptor can be used in some Ultra League situations, but it generally competes with other Flying attackers that may provide different combinations of bulk, typing and moves.

Master League

Staraptor's CP ceiling is much lower than the Pokémon typically optimized for open Master League, so it is not a natural Master League specialist.

PvP Consideration

Brave Bird can create significant offensive pressure, but its self-debuff makes shield timing and battle planning especially important.

16 • RESOURCES

Staraptor Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Staraptor requires Stardust and Candy, while Level 40+ investment requires Candy XL. Because Staraptor evolves from a commonly encountered family in some events, Candy can often be accumulated more easily than for Legendary Pokémon.

  • Save enough Starly Candy for the full evolution family.
  • Keep strong Starly and Staravia candidates before evolving.
  • Collect Candy XL if you plan to build a high-level Staraptor.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite TMs.
  • Compare regular and Shadow investment before powering up.
  • Keep Shiny specimens if you are completing the evolution collection.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Staraptor

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the target is weak to Flying attacks.
  • Confirm Staraptor has a suitable Flying moveset.
  • Check Staraptor's level and IVs.
  • Consider whether a stronger Flying attacker is available.
  • Check current weather and raid conditions.

During Team Building

Staraptor should be compared with other Flying-type attackers before spending significant Stardust. Its strong offensive potential can make it useful, but its relatively low Defense limits its durability.

Long-Term Investment

A high-quality Staraptor can be a practical addition to a Flying-type collection, especially for players who prefer building teams from non-Legendary Pokémon.

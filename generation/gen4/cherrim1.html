01 • OVERVIEW

Cherrim Overview

Cherrim is a Grass-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. It evolves from Cherubi using 50 Cherubi Candy. Pokémon GO recognizes two Cherrim forms: Overcast Form and Sunny Form.

02 • FORMS

Cherrim Forms — Overcast & Sunny

Cherrim has two visual forms in Pokémon GO. Both forms share the same species, typing and base stats, but the Sunny Form has access to Weather Ball Fire.

17 • PVP

Cherrim PvP Guide

Cherrim can be interesting in selected PvP situations, particularly in Great League environments and limited metas.

Great League

Cherrim's lower maximum CP makes Great League a more natural format to consider than Master League. Its Grass typing and access to charged attacks can create interesting matchup opportunities.

Sunny Form PvP

Sunny Form is notable for access to Weather Ball Fire, giving Cherrim coverage beyond its primary Grass typing.

Ultra League

Cherrim can be tested in selected Ultra League environments, but it is generally more niche than many higher-stat Grass Pokémon.

PvP Starting Point

Bullet Seed + Weather Ball + Solar Beam

23 • FAQ

Cherrim FAQ

What type is Cherrim?

Cherrim is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Cherrim weak to?

Cherrim is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

What is Cherrim's max CP?

Cherrim's maximum CP is 2,315 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Does Cherrim have different forms?

Yes. Pokémon GO features Overcast Form and Sunny Form Cherrim.

Is Shiny Cherrim available?

Yes. Shiny Cherrim is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Cherrim good for raids?

Cherrim is generally not considered a high-priority Grass raid attacker because stronger options are available.

Is Cherrim good in PvP?

Cherrim can have niche PvP applications, particularly in Great League and limited metas.

What is a 100% Cherrim?

A 100% Cherrim has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Cherrim?

Lucky Cherrim is a Cherrim obtained through a Lucky Trade and receives the standard benefits associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is Shadow Cherrim?

Shadow Cherrim refers to a Shadow version of Cherrim. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive damage and also take increased damage.

What is Purified Cherrim?

Purified Cherrim refers to a Cherrim that was previously Shadow and then purified.

What is XXL Cherrim?

XXL Cherrim is a Cherrim with an exceptionally large size classification.

What is XXS Cherrim?

XXS Cherrim is a Cherrim with an exceptionally small size classification.

Is XXL Cherrim automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Cherrim can have any IV combination.

Is Lucky Cherrim automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky status does not guarantee perfect IVs.

Does Shiny Cherrim have different stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual variant and does not provide a separate base-stat profile.

Bibarel

Bibarel is a Water and Normal-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the evolved form of Bidoof and can be collected in regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified and Lucky variants, as well as different size classifications.

14 · LUCKY

Lucky Bibarel is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have a special background/effect and require less Stardust to power up.

17 · PVP

Bibarel PvP Guide

Bibarel's relatively low maximum CP makes it more suitable for lower CP formats than Master League. Its Water / Normal typing and available moves can provide some interesting matchup opportunities.

Great League

Great League is the most natural CP-limited environment to consider for Bibarel. Its relatively high HP can provide some useful durability, although its overall performance depends heavily on moves, IVs and the current PvP meta.

Ultra League

Bibarel can technically be used in higher CP formats, but its maximum CP and stat profile make it less naturally suited to Ultra League than many specialized PvP Pokémon.

Master League

Bibarel is generally not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP is substantially lower than the Pokémon commonly used at that level.

18 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Bibarel

Before Battle

  • Check Bibarel's IVs.
  • Review the current moveset.
  • Consider whether Water or Normal coverage is preferred.
  • Check the format's CP limit.
  • Compare Bibarel with stronger alternatives.

For PvP

IV spreads should be evaluated specifically for the PvP league being played. A 15/15/15 Pokémon is not automatically the optimal PvP specimen because CP limits can change which IV combinations provide the best stat product.

For Raids

Bibarel is generally better considered a collection or casual-use Pokémon than a premium raid investment. Save expensive resources for stronger attackers when optimizing raid teams.

Bibarel FAQ

What type is Bibarel?

Bibarel is a Water and Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Bibarel weak to?

Bibarel is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What is Bibarel's maximum CP?

Does Bibarel evolve?

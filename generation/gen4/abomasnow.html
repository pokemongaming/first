Why Abomasnow's Typing Matters

Grass and Ice give Abomasnow access to useful offensive coverage, particularly against Water, Ground and Flying targets depending on the moveset.

The same dual typing also creates several weaknesses. Fire is especially dangerous because Abomasnow takes heavily increased Fire damage.

10 • PVP

Abomasnow PvP Guide

Abomasnow has a more interesting PvP profile than its raw CP might suggest. Its Grass/Ice typing and charged-move options can create useful matchup pressure.

Great League

Abomasnow can be considered for Great League builds where its typing and moveset provide useful coverage. League-specific IV optimization is important because a standard 15/15/15 build is not always the ideal Great League specimen.

Ultra League

Abomasnow can also be used in Ultra League environments. The increased CP limit allows more investment while preserving the same Grass/Ice identity.

Master League

Regular Abomasnow's maximum CP is considerably lower than many Master League specialists. It is therefore generally less natural there than in the lower leagues.

PvP Build

Powder Snow + Weather Ball (Ice) + a suitable second Charged Move can provide useful pressure.

13 • PURIFIED

Purified Abomasnow

Purified Abomasnow is created by purifying Shadow Abomasnow. Purification improves the appraisal, reduces certain resource costs and replaces Frustration with Return.

Why Purified Abomasnow Can Be Interesting

Abomasnow is one of the species with a Mega Evolution. Since Shadow Pokémon cannot Mega Evolve, a trainer interested in Mega Abomasnow may have a reason to consider a purified specimen.

Purification IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by 2, up to the maximum value of 15. This means a Shadow Abomasnow with sufficiently high IVs can become a 100% IV Pokémon after purification.

Purification Decision

Keep a Shadow Abomasnow if you value its Shadow battle bonus. Consider purification if you want the purified form, lower resource costs or an eligible candidate for Mega Evolution.

How Mega Evolution Works

Mega Evolution temporarily transforms an eligible Abomasnow. It is not a permanent evolution. Mega Energy is used to activate the transformation, and repeated Mega Evolutions can reduce the energy cost as the individual Pokémon's Mega Level increases.

Mega Abomasnow Strategy

Mega Evolve a high-quality Abomasnow when you want to provide Grass/Ice Mega utility or strengthen a raid team built around its available type coverage.

Can Shadow Abomasnow Mega Evolve?

No. Shadow Pokémon cannot Mega Evolve. If you want to use a Shadow Abomasnow for its Shadow battle bonus, it must remain Shadow.

Can Purified Abomasnow Mega Evolve?

Yes. Purified Pokémon can be eligible for Mega Evolution. This makes a purified Abomasnow an interesting alternative for trainers who want a Mega Abomasnow without sacrificing a regular Shadow attacker.

Can a Lucky Abomasnow Mega Evolve?

Yes. Lucky status does not prevent an otherwise eligible Abomasnow from Mega Evolving. A Lucky Abomasnow can therefore combine reduced Stardust requirements with Mega Evolution.

Abomasnow FAQ

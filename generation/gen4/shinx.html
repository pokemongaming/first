Shiny Shinx

Shiny Shinx is an alternate-color version of regular Shinx. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically improve the Pokémon's IVs or battle strength.

✨ Shiny Shinx

A Shiny Shinx can evolve into Shiny Luxio and eventually Shiny Luxray.

  • Shiny status is separate from IV quality.
  • A Shiny Pokémon can have low or high IVs.
  • Shiny Shinx can be an excellent evolution project.
  • Keep rare Shiny specimens for your collection.

Purified Shinx

Purified Shinx is created when an eligible Shadow Shinx is purified. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon into a Purified version.

💫
Purified Status

Shadow status is removed.

⚡
Electric Type

Shinx remains an Electric-type Pokémon.

🦁
Evolves

Purified Shinx can continue through its evolution family.

Should You Purify Shadow Shinx?

Purification should depend on your collection and battle goals. Do not assume that purification is automatically better simply because it changes the Pokémon's IVs.

Lucky Shinx

Lucky Shinx is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon are particularly valuable for players planning to invest Stardust into an evolution project.

🍀 Lucky Evolution Project

A Lucky Shinx can be a useful long-term candidate for evolving and powering up toward Luxray.

  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny status.
  • Lucky status is separate from XXL and XXS size.
  • Compare IVs before making a major investment.
  • Consider evolving a strong Lucky Shinx into Luxray.

XXL Shinx & XXS Shinx

XXL and XXS are size variants. Size is separate from IV quality, Shiny status, Lucky status and Shadow status.

📈

XXL Shinx

XXL Shinx is an unusually large specimen and may be useful for size-related collection goals and showcase systems.

📉

XXS Shinx

XXS Shinx is an unusually small specimen and is primarily a special collection variant.

Does Size Affect Battle Strength?

XXL and XXS size should not automatically be treated as an IV or combat-power advantage. Size, IVs and Pokémon status mechanics are separate characteristics.

Shinx Moves

Shinx FAQ

What type is Shinx?

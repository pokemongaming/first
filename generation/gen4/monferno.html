Morelull

Morelull is a small Grass and Fairy-type Pokémon from the Alola region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its distinctive mushroom-like appearance and its evolution into Shiinotic. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, perfect-IV, XXL and XXS Morelull.

100% IV Morelull

A perfect Morelull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What Is a Hundo Morelull?

A 15/15/15 Morelull is commonly called a "hundo" by Pokémon GO players. It has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Should You Keep a 100% Morelull?

Yes if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon. It can also be evolved into a perfect-IV Shiinotic for your Pokédex or collection.

Shiny Morelull

Shiny Morelull is the alternate-color version of Morelull. Its Shiny status is separate from IV quality, size and Lucky status.

✨
Shiny Morelull

A Shiny Morelull can be kept as a collector Pokémon or evolved into Shiny Shiinotic.

Shiny Morelull Checklist

  • Shiny status is independent of IVs.
  • A Shiny Morelull can have any IV combination.
  • A Shiny Morelull can also be Lucky after trading.
  • A Shiny Morelull can have XXL or XXS size.

Shadow Morelull

Shadow Morelull should be treated as a separate collection and battle variant. Shadow availability can depend on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

🌑🍄

Shadow Morelull IVs

Shadow Morelull does not need to have perfect IVs to be useful to a collector. Consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, availability and your own investment goals.

Frustration reminder:

Newly obtained Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. The ability to replace it depends on applicable Pokémon GO events and mechanics.

Should You Purify Shadow Morelull?

Do not purify automatically. If the Shadow Pokémon has collection value or you want to retain its Shadow status, compare the two options before spending resources on purification.

Purified Morelull

Purified Morelull is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Morelull. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the normal benefits associated with purification.

Lucky Morelull

Lucky Morelull is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background effect and receive reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Morelull

A Lucky Morelull can be especially attractive to collectors because Lucky status is separate from Shiny status, IVs and size.

Lucky Morelull Can Also Be

  • Shiny
  • 100% IV
  • XXL
  • XXS
  • An evolved Shiinotic after evolution
Lucky status is created through trading and cannot simply be applied to an existing Morelull with an item.

XXL & XXS Morelull

Pokémon GO can identify individual Pokémon as exceptionally large or exceptionally small. XXL and XXS are size characteristics and are separate from IVs.

🟢 XXL Morelull Extra-large Morelull suitable for collectors who enjoy size records and special Pokédex entries.
🔵 XXS Morelull Extra-small Morelull that can be retained as a special size specimen.

Morelull FAQ

What type is Morelull?

Morelull is a Grass and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Morelull weak to?

Morelull is vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Poison and Flying-type attacks.

Does Morelull evolve?

Yes. Morelull evolves into Shiinotic.

Is Shiny Morelull available?

Shiny Morelull is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when its Shiny variant is active in the game.

What is a 100% Morelull?

A 100% Morelull has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Morelull?

Lucky Morelull is a Morelull that has received the Lucky status through trading.

What is XXL Morelull?

XXL Morelull is an exceptionally large Morelull identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Morelull?

XXS Morelull is an exceptionally small Morelull identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

Is Shadow Morelull available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current game schedule before claiming that Shadow Morelull is currently obtainable.

Should I purify Shadow Morelull?

Purification is a choice rather than an automatic upgrade. Consider the Pokémon's Shadow status, IVs, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

Can Morelull be Shiny and XXL?

Special characteristics such as Shiny status, size and IVs can represent separate properties of an individual Pokémon when supported by the game.

Is Morelull good for raids?

Morelull is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its collection, evolution and special-variant value are more significant for most trainers.

Hippopotas

Hippopotas is a Ground-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It evolves into Hippowdon and is known for its highly recognizable sand-based Pokédex concept. In Pokémon GO, Hippopotas is primarily collected as the first stage of the Hippowdon evolution family.

Why Ground Typing Matters

As a pure Ground-type Pokémon, Hippopotas is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks. Its Ground typing also gives it useful resistance against Electric, Poison and Rock attacks.

Shadow Hippopotas

Purified Hippopotas

Purified Hippopotas is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Hippopotas. Purification changes its status from Shadow to Purified.

💠

Purified Hippopotas

Purified Hippopotas is useful as a separate collection form and should be evaluated independently from Shadow Hippopotas before purification.

  • Compare the Shadow form before purifying.
  • Consider collection goals.
  • Check IV changes after purification.
  • Do not purify automatically without considering evolution plans.

Should You Keep Hippopotas?

  • Keep a strong IV Hippopotas if you plan to evolve it.
  • Consider keeping one regular specimen for your Pokédex.
  • Keep a Shiny Hippopotas for your collection.
  • Evaluate Shadow Hippopotas before purification.
  • Lucky specimens can be useful evolution projects.
  • Keep XXL and XXS versions for size collections.
  • A 100% IV Hippopotas is an excellent collector target.

Hippopotas FAQ

What type is Hippopotas?

Hippopotas is a pure Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Hippopotas weak to?

Hippopotas is weak to Water, Grass and Ice attacks.

Does Hippopotas evolve?

Yes. Hippopotas evolves into Hippowdon.

Can Hippopotas be Shiny?

Shiny Hippopotas can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Hippopotas be Shadow?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and Pokémon GO events.

What is a 100% IV Hippopotas?

A 100% IV Hippopotas has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Hippopotas?

XXL Hippopotas is a larger-than-normal size variant.

What is XXS Hippopotas?

XXS Hippopotas is a smaller-than-normal size variant.

Should I purify Shadow Hippopotas?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow form with your collection and evolution goals before purifying.

What Does Lumineon's Water Typing Mean?

Lumineon's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile. It is weak to Electric and Grass attacks while resisting Fire, Water, Ice and Steel-type damage.

Lucky Lumineon

Lucky Pokémon are special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky status does not create a new species or change Lumineon's type.

🍀 Lucky Lumineon

A Lucky Lumineon can be useful for collectors and players looking for a lower Stardust cost when powering up Pokémon.

Lucky Lumineon IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor from the trade mechanic, but Lucky status does not guarantee perfect IVs. A Lucky Lumineon can still have different IV combinations.

Should You Keep a Lucky Lumineon?

  • Keep it if you collect Lucky Pokémon.
  • Check its IVs after the trade.
  • Consider the reduced Stardust cost for powering it up.
  • Keep a high-IV Lucky specimen for collection purposes.

XXL & XXS Lumineon

Pokémon GO can assign size categories to individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS Lumineon are size variants rather than different species.

🐘 XXL Lumineon Extra-large size category

An XXL Lumineon is a particularly large individual and can be useful for size-related collection goals and showcases.

🐜 XXS Lumineon Extra-small size category

An XXS Lumineon is an unusually small individual and can be kept for collection and showcase purposes.

Do XXL and XXS Change Battle Stats?

Size categories should not be confused with IVs. A Pokémon's XXL or XXS classification does not mean that it has better Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Lumineon PvP Guide

Lumineon's relatively modest Attack and stronger defensive stats make PvP a more interesting area to investigate than high-level raid damage.

Great League

Great League allows Pokémon with CP limits that can make lower-CP Pokémon more practical. Lumineon's performance depends heavily on its moveset, IV spread and the current PvP metagame.

Ultra League

Lumineon's maximum CP can make Ultra League investment possible, although trainers should compare it against stronger Water-type options before spending large amounts of Stardust.

Master League

Lumineon's maximum CP is too low for it to be a natural Master League investment compared with Pokémon designed for that format.

Lumineon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Powering up Lumineon requires Stardust and Candy, while higher-level investments require Candy XL. Before spending resources, decide whether your Lumineon is being built for PvP, collection or personal use.

Lumineon FAQ

What type is Lumineon?

Lumineon is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Lumineon weak to?

Lumineon is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

What is Lumineon resistant to?

Lumineon resists Fire, Water, Ice and Steel-type attacks.

Does Lumineon evolve?

Lumineon is the final evolution of Finneon.

Is Shiny Lumineon available?

Shiny Lumineon can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny form is available.

Is Lumineon good for raids?

Lumineon is generally not considered a top-tier Water-type raid attacker. Stronger Water Pokémon are usually preferred for optimized raid teams.

Is Lumineon good in PvP?

Lumineon can be considered in selected PvP situations, but its usefulness depends on the league, moveset and current metagame.

What is a 100% Lumineon?

A 100% Lumineon has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Lucky Lumineon?

Lucky Lumineon is a Lumineon with the Lucky classification obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

Does XXL Lumineon have better IVs?

No. XXL describes size, not IV quality. An XXL Lumineon can have any valid IV combination.

Does XXS Lumineon have better stats?

XXS is a size classification and should not be interpreted as a higher-IV or stronger-stat variant.

Is Shadow Lumineon different from regular Lumineon?

Shadow Lumineon has the same Water typing but uses Shadow Pokémon mechanics, resulting in a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

Should I purify Shadow Lumineon?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen with its potential purified result and consider your collection and battle goals.

Can a Shiny Lumineon also be Lucky?

Pokémon characteristics such as Shiny and Lucky can represent separate classifications, so collectors may value specimens that combine desirable traits.

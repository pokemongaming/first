11 — SPECIAL VARIANTS

Porygon-Z Special Variants

Porygon-Z can be encountered or owned in different special states and sizes. These variants do not all represent separate species; they can instead reflect Pokémon GO mechanics such as Shiny status, Shadow status, Lucky trades or size records.

✨

Shiny Porygon-Z

Shiny Porygon-Z has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny status is primarily a collector distinction and does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger.

🌑

Shadow Porygon-Z

Shadow Porygon-Z retains its Normal typing. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile, including increased damage dealt and increased damage received.

💎

Purified Porygon-Z

Purified Porygon-Z is created when an eligible Shadow Porygon or evolved Shadow Porygon2 is purified before reaching Porygon-Z.

🍀

Lucky Porygon-Z

Lucky Porygon-Z is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and have a special Lucky background.

💯

100% Porygon-Z

A 100% Porygon-Z has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. This is commonly called a hundo.

📏

XXL Porygon-Z

An XXL Porygon-Z is a Pokémon with an especially large recorded size. XXL status is a size characteristic rather than a separate species or evolution.

🔬

XXS Porygon-Z

An XXS Porygon-Z has an especially small recorded size. Like XXL, this is a size classification and does not create a different Pokémon species.

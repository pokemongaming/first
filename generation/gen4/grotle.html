Grotle

Grotle is a Grass-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region and the evolved form of Turtwig. In Pokémon GO, Grotle is primarily a Pokédex, collection and evolution-family Pokémon, while its Shadow, Shiny, Lucky, XXS and XXL variants provide additional collection goals.

Grotle Overview

Grotle is the middle stage of the Turtwig evolution family. It is a pure Grass-type Pokémon and evolves into Torterra. In Pokémon GO, Grotle has a Level 50 maximum CP of 2,137.

Why Grotle's Typing Matters

Grotle's pure Grass typing gives it useful resistances against Water, Electric, Grass and Ground attacks. However, it is vulnerable to several common attacking types, including Fire, Ice, Flying, Bug and Poison.

Grotle IV Guide

A perfect Grotle has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Because Grotle is an intermediate evolution, IV priorities depend heavily on whether you plan to keep Grotle or evolve it into Torterra.

100% IV Grotle

A 15/15/15 Grotle is a perfect-IV specimen. If you plan to evolve it, the same individual can be developed into a perfect-IV Torterra.

A 100% Grotle has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs. These Pokémon are commonly called "hundos" by the Pokémon GO community.

Grotle PvP Guide

Grotle can technically participate in Pokémon GO's CP-limited leagues, but it is generally a niche choice rather than a standard competitive pick.

Great League

Grotle's CP can fit within lower league limits at suitable levels, but its overall performance is limited compared with stronger Grass-type choices.

Ultra League

Grotle is generally less attractive in Ultra League because its overall stat product and moveset do not give it the same competitive presence as many established Pokémon.

Master League

Grotle's maximum CP of 2,137 makes it poorly suited to Master League compared with Pokémon designed for that format.

Shadow Grotle

Shadow Grotle is available in Pokémon GO. Shadow Grotle retains its Grass typing but has the Shadow battle mechanic, which increases offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

👿 Shadow Grotle

Shadow Grotle is a separate collection and battle variant. Its maximum CP is also 2,137 at Level 50 because the species' underlying base stats remain the same for CP calculation.

Shadow Grotle and Frustration

A Shadow Grotle can have the Charged Attack Frustration. Rocket-related events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration, so check the current event rules before spending resources on its moveset.

Do not purify a Shadow Grotle automatically. If you value the Shadow form, compare its future use with the purified version before making the decision.

Purified Grotle

Purified Grotle is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Grotle. Purification removes its Shadow status and provides the normal purified-Pokémon mechanics.

✨ Purified Grotle

Purified Grotle can have Return as its special Charged Move after purification. The exact costs and mechanics should be checked against the current Pokémon GO system.

Grotle Evolution

In Pokémon GO, Turtwig evolves into Grotle using Candy, and Grotle can then evolve into Torterra using additional Candy. Because Grotle is an intermediate stage, many trainers may prefer to save a strong specimen for the final Torterra evolution.

XXS & XXL Grotle

Pokémon GO supports size categories that allow Grotle to appear in unusually small or unusually large sizes. These size classifications are separate from IVs.

🔹
XXS Grotle Approximate height range: 0.539–0.55 m

XXS Grotle is an unusually small specimen. It can be valuable to collectors and may contribute to size-related Pokédex or collection goals.

🔶
XXL Grotle Approximate height range: 1.65–1.705 m

XXL Grotle is an unusually large specimen and can be especially desirable for collectors who are completing size variants.

Size and IV are independent characteristics. A Grotle can be XXS or XXL while also being a 100% IV Pokémon.

Grotle FAQ

What type is Grotle?

Grotle is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Grotle weak to?

Grotle is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

What does Grotle evolve into?

Grotle evolves into Torterra, completing the final stage of the Turtwig evolutionary family.

What is Grotle's maximum CP?

Grotle's maximum CP is 2,137 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Grotle available?

Yes. Shiny Grotle is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Grotle available?

Yes. Shadow Grotle is available and retains its Grass typing.

Can Shadow Grotle be purified?

Yes. Eligible Shadow Grotle can be purified, creating a Purified Grotle.

What is a Lucky Grotle?

A Lucky Grotle is a Grotle that has obtained Lucky status through trading.

What is a 100% Grotle?

A 100% Grotle has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is XXS Grotle?

XXS Grotle is an unusually small Grotle. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is XXL Grotle?

XXL Grotle is an unusually large Grotle. It can be valuable to collectors completing size variants.

Can a Grotle be both XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXS Grotle can also have perfect IVs.

Can a Grotle be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status can coexist on the same Pokémon.

Is Grotle good for raids?

Grotle is generally not considered a top-tier Grass raid attacker. Trainers looking for stronger PvE performance will usually prefer more powerful evolved or Legendary Grass-type Pokémon.

Should I evolve my perfect Grotle?

If your goal is battle performance, evolving a strong Grotle into Torterra can make better use of the investment. If you collect Grotle specifically, you may prefer to keep the Pokémon unevolved.

Should I purify Shadow Grotle?

Not automatically. Consider whether you value the Shadow form before deciding to purify it.

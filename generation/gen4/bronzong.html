Bronzong PvP Guide

Bronzong's defensive typing and bulk make PvP one of the more interesting areas for the Pokémon. Its usefulness depends strongly on the league, moveset and current meta.

Great League

Bronzong can be considered in Great League formats when its typing provides useful defensive coverage against common opponents.

Ultra League

Bronzong can be used below the Ultra League CP cap, but team composition and matchup coverage should determine whether it is worth building.

Master League

Bronzong's maximum CP and Attack profile generally make it less naturally suited to unrestricted Master League competition compared with major Legendary Pokémon.

Regular Bronzong

Regular Bronzong is the standard form obtained by evolving Bronzor. It has the same species stats and battle mechanics as its cosmetic variants.

🔔 Standard Form

Regular Bronzong is the baseline version used for standard PvP, collection and general gameplay investment.

```
```

Shiny Bronzong

Shiny Bronzong has an alternate appearance but does not receive different battle stats or a special damage bonus.

✨ Shiny Bronzong

Shiny status is primarily cosmetic. A Shiny Bronzong can still be powered up, used in PvP and compared by IVs just like a regular Bronzong.

A Shiny Bronzong is not automatically stronger than a regular Bronzong. IVs, level and moveset determine its practical battle performance.
```
```

Shadow Bronzong

Shadow Bronzong is the Shadow version of Bronzong. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile compared with regular Pokémon.

🌑 Shadow Bronzong

Shadow Bronzong can provide increased offensive pressure while giving up part of its defensive durability.

Purified Bronzong

Purified Bronzong is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Bronzong. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's investment path.

✨ Purified Bronzong

Purified Bronzong no longer has the Shadow battle profile. It should be evaluated as a separate investment decision before purification.

Should You Purify Shadow Bronzong?

  • Compare the Shadow and purified battle roles.
  • Check IV changes before making the decision.
  • Consider whether you want Shadow or standard PvP use.
  • Do not purify solely because a Pokémon has low CP.

Lucky Bronzong

Lucky Bronzong is obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and are valuable because of their Stardust investment advantages.

🍀 Lucky Bronzong

Lucky status does not create a new species or change Bronzong's base typing. Its major value is related to powering up and collection status.

XXL Bronzong

XXL Bronzong is an unusually large specimen. Size categories are primarily important for collection and Pokémon GO Showcase systems.

📏 XXL Bronzong

XXL status does not automatically increase Bronzong's Attack, Defense, HP or Max CP. Its main value is connected to size-based collection and Showcase participation.

XXS Bronzong

XXS Bronzong is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL Pokémon, the XXS category is mainly a collection and size classification.

📐 XXS Bronzong

XXS Bronzong keeps the same underlying species battle mechanics as other Bronzong. Size alone does not make it stronger or weaker in normal Pokémon GO battles.

Bronzong FAQ

What type is Bronzong?

Bronzong is a Steel and Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Bronzong weak to?

Bronzong can be vulnerable to Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Is Bronzong good in PvP?

Bronzong can have PvP usefulness because of its typing and defensive profile. Its value depends on league, moveset and the current meta.

Is Shiny Bronzong stronger?

No. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not automatically increase Bronzong's battle stats.

Is Shadow Bronzong stronger than regular Bronzong?

Shadow Bronzong has a different offensive and defensive battle profile. The better choice depends on your intended use.

What is a 100% IV Bronzong?

A 100% IV Bronzong has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does Lucky Bronzong have better stats?

Lucky status is primarily valuable for trade and power-up investment. Individual IVs should still be checked separately.

Does XXL Bronzong have more HP?

XXL size classification does not automatically give Bronzong higher battle HP.

Does Bronzong evolve?

Bronzong is the evolved form of Bronzor and does not have a further standard evolution.

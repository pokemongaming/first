Luxray

Luxray is a powerful Electric-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, Luxray is known for its strong Attack stat, Electric-type moves and several collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

01 • OVERVIEW

Luxray Overview

Luxray is the final evolution of the Shinx evolutionary family. It is a pure Electric-type Pokémon and is registered as Pokédex #405. Its high Attack stat gives it an offensive role, while its lower Defense means it can be less forgiving in difficult battles.

Why Luxray's Typing Matters

Luxray's pure Electric typing gives it a straightforward offensive identity. Electric attacks are especially useful against Water- and Flying-type opponents. However, Ground-type attacks are particularly dangerous because Electric Pokémon are weak to Ground damage.

14 • XXL

XXL Luxray

XXL Luxray is a Luxray that falls into Pokémon GO's extra-large size category. Size classifications are separate from IVs and do not automatically indicate stronger battle performance.

XXL Luxray can be worth keeping for collection, size-related medals and trading even when its IVs are not perfect.

Luxray FAQ

What type is Luxray?

Luxray is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Luxray weak against?

Luxray is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Luxray a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Luxray is a regular Pokémon from the Sinnoh region.

What is Luxray's maximum CP?

Luxray's maximum CP is approximately 3,265 under standard Pokémon GO stat calculations.

Is Shiny Luxray available?

Yes. Shiny Luxray is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolution.

Is Shadow Luxray available?

Shadow Luxray can be available through eligible Team GO Rocket-related encounters and rotations. Current availability should be checked before hunting.

Is Purified Luxray available?

Yes. A Shadow Luxray can be purified when eligible.

What is a 100% Luxray?

A 100% Luxray has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Luxray?

Lucky Luxray is a Luxray that has obtained Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

No. XXS size does not automatically indicate poor IVs.

Is Luxray good in PvP?

Luxray can have uses in certain PvP formats, particularly where its moveset and Electric typing provide useful matchup pressure.

Does Luxray evolve further?

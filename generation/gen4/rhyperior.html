Rhyperior

Rhyperior is a Ground- and Rock-type Pokémon from Generation 4 and the final evolution of the Rhyhorn family. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its strong raid utility, particularly when equipped with Rock Wrecker or an appropriate Ground-focused moveset.

02 • TYPE

Rhyperior Type

🪨
Ground / Rock Dual Type

Why Rhyperior's Typing Matters

Rhyperior's Ground and Rock combination gives it several useful offensive matchups, but it also creates a significant number of weaknesses. Trainers should pay particular attention to Water and Grass attacks when using Rhyperior defensively.

25 • FAQ

Rhyperior FAQ

What type is Rhyperior?

Rhyperior is a Ground- and Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Rhyperior weak against?

Rhyperior is weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Steel and Ice attacks.

What is Rhyperior's max CP?

Rhyperior's listed maximum CP is approximately 4,221 under the standard maximum-level calculation.

Is Rhyperior good for raids?

Yes. Rhyperior can be an excellent Rock attacker with an appropriate Rock moveset and can also perform a Ground role.

What is the best Rhyperior moveset?

For a Rock-focused raid role, Smack Down with Rock Wrecker is a strong recommendation. Ground builds can use Mud-Slap with a suitable Ground charged move.

Is Shiny Rhyperior available?

Yes. Shiny Rhyperior can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny Rhyhorn or Shiny Rhydon.

Can Rhyperior be Shadow?

Shadow Rhyperior can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shadow member of the Rhyhorn family.

Can Shadow Rhyperior be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified. Compare the Shadow and Purified options before making that decision.

Is Lucky Rhyperior possible?

Yes. Rhyperior can become Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a reduced Stardust cost for powering up.

What is a 100% Rhyperior?

A 100% Rhyperior has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Rhyperior?

XXL Rhyperior is a size classification. XXL does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has perfect IVs or higher battle performance.

What is XXS Rhyperior?

XXS Rhyperior is a very small size classification and is mainly relevant to collecting and size records.

Does Rhyperior have a Mega Evolution?

Rhyperior does not currently have an official Mega Evolution available in Pokémon GO. This should be updated if an official Mega form is introduced.

How do you evolve Rhydon into Rhyperior?

Rhyperior evolves from Rhydon using the required Rhyhorn family Candy and a Sinnoh Stone. Check the current in-game evolution screen for the exact requirement.

Is a 100% Lucky Rhyperior rare?

A Lucky Pokémon has a minimum IV floor when received from a trade, but Lucky status does not guarantee perfect IVs. A Lucky 15/15/15 Rhyperior is therefore a particularly desirable combination.

Is Shadow Rhyperior better than regular Rhyperior?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile. Whether Shadow Rhyperior is better depends on the battle, moveset and your investment goals.

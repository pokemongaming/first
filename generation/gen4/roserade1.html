Roserade

Roserade is a powerful Grass and Poison-type Pokémon introduced in the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its high Attack stat and strong offensive potential as both a Grass-type and Poison-type attacker.

Roserade Overview

Roserade is the final evolution of the Budew evolution family. Budew evolves into Roselia, which can then evolve into Roserade. Roserade combines Grass and Poison typing with a high Attack stat, giving it an aggressive offensive profile.

Why Roserade's Typing Matters

The Grass and Poison combination gives Roserade access to two important offensive roles. Grass attacks allow Roserade to pressure Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Poison attacks can be useful against Fairy and Grass targets.

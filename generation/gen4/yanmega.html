Yanmega

Yanmega is a Bug/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it evolves from Yanma using 100 Yanma Candy and a Sinnoh Stone. Its strongest PvE identity is as a Bug-type attacker using Bug Bite and Bug Buzz.

01 · DATABASE OVERVIEW

Yanmega Overview

Yanmega is the evolved form of Yanma and a Bug/Flying-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh Pokédex. Its 231 base Attack gives it a useful offensive profile, although its relatively modest Defense means it is not one of the game's most durable attackers.

02 · TYPE

Yanmega Type

🐞
Bug / Flying Dual Type

Why Yanmega's Typing Matters

Yanmega's Bug/Flying combination gives it useful offensive access to Bug and Flying attacks while also creating a very specific defensive profile. It takes especially heavy damage from Rock-type attacks and is also weak to Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice.

11 · GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Yanmega PvP Guide

Yanmega is not currently a major open-league staple, but its Wing Attack, Aerial Ace and Ancient Power combination can provide some interesting coverage.

Great League

A low-Attack, high-Defense and high-HP IV spread can maximize Yanmega's stat product under the 1,500 CP limit. Current rankings should be checked before building one.

Ultra League

Yanmega can reach the 2,500 CP limit, but it currently sits well outside the most competitive choices in the format.

Master League

A 15/15/15 Yanmega can reach 3,330 CP at Level 50, but the current Master League environment is extremely demanding and Yanmega is not considered a top choice.

PvP Starting Moveset

Wing Attack + Aerial Ace + Ancient Power

Does XXL Yanmega Have Higher Stats?

No. XXL is primarily a size classification and does not automatically mean the Yanmega has better Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

XXL Collection Tip

Keep an XXL Yanmega if you are working toward size-based Pokédex or collection goals. Its size should not be confused with its IV quality.

25 · PRACTICAL GUIDE

How to Use Yanmega

Before Powering Up

  • Check Yanmega's IVs.
  • Confirm whether you want Bug or Flying damage.
  • Check the current moveset.
  • Compare Yanmega with your existing Bug attackers.
  • Consider whether a Lucky Yanmega could reduce your Stardust cost.

For Raids

Use Yanmega primarily when Bug-type damage is useful. Bug Bite and Bug Buzz give it its clearest PvE identity. It should not automatically replace stronger specialized attackers in every raid.

For Collection

A Shiny, Lucky, hundo, XXL or XXS Yanmega can be worth keeping even if you do not intend to power it up. These attributes are independent of one another and can overlap.

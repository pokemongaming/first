Generation 4 Pokémon Overview

Generation IV expanded the National Pokédex with 107 new Pokémon and introduced many new evolutions for Pokémon from previous generations.

🏔️

Sinnoh Region

Generation 4 introduced the Sinnoh region and Pokémon numbered #387 through #493.

🌿

Sinnoh Starters

Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup form the three Sinnoh starter evolution families.

🧬

New Evolutions

Gen 4 added new evolutions such as Rhyperior, Electivire, Magmortar, Togekiss, Gliscor and Mamoswine.

🌌

Legendary Lore

Sinnoh introduced Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Heatran, Cresselia, Regigigas and several Mythical Pokémon.

FAQ

Generation 4 Pokémon FAQ

How many Pokémon were introduced in Generation 4?

Generation IV introduced 107 new Pokémon, numbered #387 through #493 in the National Pokédex.

Which region is Generation 4 from?

Generation IV introduced the Sinnoh region.

What are the Gen 4 starter Pokémon?

Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup are the three Sinnoh starter Pokémon.

Which Pokémon is #493?

Arceus is National Pokédex #493 and is the final Pokémon introduced in Generation IV.

Which Gen 4 Pokémon have Mega Evolutions?

Sinnoh Pokémon with Mega Evolutions include Garchomp, Lucario, Abomasnow, Lopunny, Gallade and Audino.

Which Gen 4 Pokémon are Legendary?

Sinnoh introduced Legendary Pokémon including Uxie, Mesprit, Azelf, Dialga, Palkia, Heatran, Regigigas, Giratina and Cresselia.

Which Gen 4 Pokémon are Mythical?

Phione, Manaphy, Darkrai, Shaymin and Arceus are associated with the Mythical Pokémon group from Generation IV.

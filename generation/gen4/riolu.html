Riolu Overview

Why Riolu's Type Matters

Riolu is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon. This typing is important because its evolution, Lucario, becomes a Fighting / Steel Pokémon and gains a significantly different battle profile.

Riolu Moves

Riolu is primarily collected as a future Lucario investment. For that reason, its evolution potential is generally more important than its own battle performance.

Main Investment Goal

Evolve a strong Riolu into Lucario when appropriate.

Move availability note:

Pokémon GO move pools can change. Always verify the current Riolu and Lucario movesets before spending valuable resources or recommending a build.

Riolu Evolution

Riolu evolves into Lucario. For many players, the primary reason to collect Riolu is to build a strong Lucario for raids, PvP or Mega Evolution.

🥊 Riolu

Baby Fighting-type Pokémon.

➡ Lucario

Riolu's evolved form and a major Fighting-type Pokémon.

Investment tip:

Before evolving your best Riolu, compare its IVs and intended use. A raid-focused Lucario and a league-focused Lucario may not always require the same IV priorities.

Riolu Forms & Special Variants

Riolu can exist in several collectible Pokémon GO variants. These variants may affect appearance, IV floors, trading value, purification status or collection value.

Regular Riolu

Shiny Riolu

Shiny Riolu is one of the more desirable collectible versions of Riolu. Players seeking a Shiny Riolu should pay attention to eligible event, egg or encounter opportunities when available.

✨ Shiny Status

Shiny Riolu retains its Shiny identity when evolved into Lucario.

Should You Evolve Shiny Riolu?

That depends on your collection goals. Some players prefer keeping a Shiny Riolu unevolved because of its rarity, while others prefer evolving it into Shiny Lucario.

Shadow Riolu

Shadow Riolu should be treated as a separate variant because Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from regular Pokémon. Its long-term value may depend heavily on the performance of the evolved Shadow Lucario.

🌑 Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally trade increased offensive pressure for reduced defensive durability.

Shadow Riolu Investment

  • Do not automatically purify a strong Shadow Riolu.
  • Consider its future evolution into Lucario.
  • Check current Shadow move mechanics.
  • Compare the Shadow evolution with your regular Lucario.
  • Consider the higher resource cost of Shadow power-ups.
Availability note:

Shadow Pokémon availability can change through Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Display current availability separately from historical availability.

Purified Riolu

A Purified Riolu is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Riolu. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

💠 Purified Riolu

Purification removes Shadow status. Whether purification is the right choice depends on your intended long-term use.

Lucky Riolu

Lucky Riolu is obtained through eligible Pokémon trading. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for future power-up projects because they require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon.

✨ Lucky Investment Value

A Lucky Riolu can be especially useful if you intend to evolve it into Lucario and invest significant Stardust.

Why Lucky Riolu Is Valuable

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Useful for long-term Lucario investments.
  • Can combine strong IV potential with lower Stardust costs.
  • High collector value.

100% IV Riolu

A 100% Riolu has perfect Individual Values of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

💯 Perfect Riolu

A 15/15/15 Riolu is an excellent long-term collector and evolution candidate, particularly for players who want a high-IV Lucario.

XXL Riolu & XXS Riolu

XXL and XXS Riolu are size variants. Their primary value is collectibility and potential participation in Pokémon Showcases rather than increased combat power.

⬆ XXL Riolu

An exceptionally large Riolu. XXL Pokémon may be useful for size-based collection goals and eligible Showcases.

⬇ XXS Riolu

An exceptionally small Riolu primarily valued for collection purposes.

Size note:

XXL and XXS size classifications should not be confused with IV quality. A large Riolu is not automatically stronger in battle than a standard-sized Riolu.

Mega Riolu

Riolu FAQ

What type is Riolu?

Riolu is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What does Riolu evolve into?

Riolu evolves into Lucario.

Is Shiny Riolu available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and events.

Can Riolu Mega Evolve?

No. Riolu does not Mega Evolve. It evolves into Lucario, which can Mega Evolve into Mega Lucario.

Is Lucky Riolu good?

Lucky Riolu can be very useful because reduced Stardust power-up costs can make future Lucario investment cheaper.

No. XXL size does not automatically increase battle stats or IV quality.

Should I evolve my best Riolu?

If your goal is to build Lucario, evolving a strong Riolu can be a good long-term investment.

Combee

Combee is a Bug and Flying-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, Combee is best known for its unusual gender-based evolution: only Female Combee can evolve into Vespiquen. Its small CP ceiling also makes it more interesting for collectors and Little Cup players than for raids.

Combee Pokémon GO Overview

Combee is a dual Bug/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. It is a member of the Combee family and can evolve into Vespiquen, but there is an important gender requirement. Only Female Combee can evolve into Vespiquen.

Why Combee's Typing Matters

Combee's Bug/Flying combination gives it useful resistances to several attack types, but it also creates a major vulnerability to Rock-type attacks. In Pokémon GO, Rock attacks deal especially high damage to Combee.

08 • IV GUIDE

Combee IV Guide

IVs add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A perfect Combee has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Perfect Combee

A 15/15/15 Combee is commonly called a 100% IV or Hundo Combee. Its Level 50 maximum CP is 559.

09 • 100% IV

100% IV Combee — Hundo Combee

A 100% Combee has perfect IVs across all three individual stats: Attack, Defense and HP.

Is a Hundo Combee Worth Keeping?

Absolutely, especially for collectors. A perfect Combee can also be interesting if you enjoy Little Cup or want a perfect specimen for your Pokédex collection.

  • Keep a 15/15/15 Combee for your collection.
  • Consider its evolution potential if Female.
  • Check whether the Combee has a useful size tag.
  • Consider keeping a perfect male separately because it cannot evolve into Vespiquen.
10 • SHINY

Shiny Combee

Shiny Combee is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny form has an alternate coloration and is a popular collector target.

✨
Shiny Combee

Available in Pokémon GO through eligible Combee encounters.

Shiny Combee Evolution

A Shiny Female Combee can evolve into Shiny Vespiquen when the evolution requirement is met. Male Combee cannot evolve into Vespiquen.

Collector tip:

Before evolving a Shiny Female Combee, check its IVs, appraisal, size and any special tags you may want to preserve in your collection.

11 • SHADOW

Shadow Combee

Shadow Pokémon are separate variants with the Shadow battle mechanic. This section is designed for your database to distinguish Shadow Combee from standard Combee.

Shadow Combee

Shadow variant information should be displayed only when supported by the current game data.

Shadow Combee Typing

Shadow status does not change Combee's underlying Bug/Flying typing. Its normal type matchup therefore remains associated with Bug/Flying.

Shadow Combee Investment

Because Combee has very low base Attack and a low maximum CP, Shadow Combee is primarily a collection or specialized-format consideration rather than a conventional raid investment.

Availability note:

Do not permanently claim that Shadow Combee is currently obtainable unless your live availability data confirms it.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Combee

Lucky Combee is a traded Combee that has received the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon have a special appearance in storage and require less Stardust to power up.

🍀✨
Lucky Combee

A special traded Combee with Lucky status and reduced Stardust power-up requirements.

Why Keep a Lucky Combee?

  • Useful for collectors.
  • Reduced Stardust cost makes Lucky Pokémon cheaper to power up.
  • Check its IVs after the trade.
  • Female Lucky Combee can still evolve into Vespiquen.
  • A Lucky Shiny Combee can be a particularly desirable collection piece.
Important:

A Pokémon cannot normally be both Shadow and Lucky at the same time because Shadow Pokémon cannot be traded while they remain Shadow.

14 • XXL

XXL Combee

XXL Combee is a size variant that is larger than standard Combee. Size is independent from IV percentage and CP, so an XXL Combee does not automatically have better IVs.

XXL Extra-large Combee
Collection Useful for size-focused collectors

XXL Combee and IVs

XXL status does not mean 100% IV. You can have an XXL Combee with poor IVs, average IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Always inspect the Pokémon's appraisal separately from its size classification.
15 • XXS

XXS Combee

17 • PVP

Combee PvP Guide

Combee's very low CP limits its usefulness in standard Great and Ultra League battles. However, its stat profile and low CP can give it a niche in Little Cup formats.

Great League

21 • PRACTICAL GUIDE

How to Use Combee

Before Transferring Combee

  • Check whether it is Female.
  • Check IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny.
  • Check XXL or XXS size status.
  • Check Lucky or other special collection tags.
  • Consider whether it could be useful for Little Cup.

Before Evolving

If your goal is Vespiquen, confirm that the Combee is Female and that you have the required Candy. This is especially important for Shiny, Hundo, Lucky, XXL and XXS Combee because evolving a collectible specimen permanently changes its form.

Combee Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Combee?

Combee is a Bug and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Combee weak against?

Combee is especially vulnerable to Rock-type attacks and is also weak to Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice attacks.

What is Combee's max CP?

Combee's maximum CP is 559 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Combee's best moves?

Bug Bite and Bug Buzz are Combee's recommended moveset.

Can Combee evolve?

Yes. Only Female Combee can evolve into Vespiquen.

How much Candy does Combee need to evolve?

Female Combee requires 50 Combee Candy to evolve into Vespiquen.

Can Male Combee evolve?

No. Male Combee does not evolve into Vespiquen.

Is Shiny Combee available?

Yes. Shiny Combee is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Combee good for raids?

No. Combee's low Attack and limited moveset make it a poor raid attacker.

Is Combee good in PvP?

Combee is generally too weak for the standard Great and Ultra Leagues, but it can have a niche in Little Cup formats.

What is a 100% Combee?

A 100% Combee has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Combee?

XXL Combee is an extra-large size classification. Size is separate from IVs.

What is XXS Combee?

XXS Combee is an extra-small size classification. It can have any valid IV combination.

Can Combee be Lucky?

A Combee can have the Lucky status after a successful trade.

Can a Shiny Combee evolve?

Yes. A Shiny Female Combee can evolve into Shiny Vespiquen.

Can Shadow Combee be purified?

If Shadow Combee is available through the current game mechanics, an eligible Shadow Pokémon can generally be purified. Confirm the current Combee-specific availability before publishing this as an active encounter.

Can Shadow Combee be Lucky?

Shadow Pokémon cannot be traded while they remain Shadow, so a Pokémon cannot normally become Lucky without first losing its Shadow status.

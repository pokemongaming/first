Grotle

Grotle is a Grass-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region and the middle evolution of Turtwig. It evolves into Torterra and can appear in several special Pokémon GO variations including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

Grotle Forms & Special Variants

Grotle can exist in multiple collectible variants. These forms may differ visually, mechanically or through their IV, weight or purification status.

✨ Shiny Grotle

Shiny Grotle has alternate coloration from the regular version. A Shiny Grotle can also be evolved into Shiny Torterra.

🌑 Shadow Grotle

Shadow Grotle has the Shadow battle profile, providing increased offensive pressure with reduced defensive durability.

💠 Purified Grotle

Purified Grotle is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Grotle.

🍀 Lucky Grotle

Lucky Grotle has the Lucky Pokémon status and is generally cheaper to power up than a standard Grotle.

💯 100% Grotle

A 100% IV Grotle has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

📏 XXL Grotle

XXL Grotle is a larger size variant and can be valuable for collectors and size-based showcases.

🔹 XXS Grotle

XXS Grotle is a smaller size variant primarily valued for collecting and Pokédex completion.

🌿 Regular Grotle

The standard form of Grotle, obtained through normal encounters or by evolving Turtwig.

Purified Grotle

Purified Grotle is created when an eligible Shadow Grotle is purified.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Purified Pokémon can receive IV increases.
  • The Shadow battle profile is removed.
  • Consider your long-term goal before purifying.
  • A Purified Grotle can still evolve into Torterra.

Lucky Grotle

Lucky Grotle is primarily valuable because Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust to power up.

Lucky Investment Benefit

Lucky status can make a high-level Grotle or its future Torterra evolution less expensive to build.

XXL Grotle

XXL Grotle is a larger-than-normal size variant. Its main value is collecting and participation in size-based PokéStop Showcases.

Collector Value

Keep unusually large Grotle specimens if you participate in XXL and size-based showcases.

XXS Grotle

XXS Grotle is a smaller size variant. It is primarily a collector form and can be useful for players completing size-related Pokédex categories.

Grotle PvP Guide

Grotle is generally more of a transitional Pokémon because Torterra provides access to a different final-stage stat profile and typing.

Great League

Grotle can be used in limited situations, but players should compare it with other Grass-type Pokémon before investing heavily.

Ultra League

Its maximum CP and middle-evolution status make Grotle a less natural long-term Ultra League investment.

Grotle Raid Performance

Grotle is generally not a major raid attacker. Most players will eventually evolve it into Torterra for a stronger final-stage Pokémon.

  • Useful primarily as an evolution stage.
  • Consider evolving strong specimens.
  • Keep special forms for your collection.
  • Evaluate Shadow specimens before evolving.
  • Save Candy for high-value evolution projects.

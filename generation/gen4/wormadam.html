Wormadam

Wormadam is a Bug-type Pokémon with three permanent Cloak forms in Pokémon GO: Plant Cloak, Sandy Cloak and Trash Cloak. Its final typing, stats and moves depend on the Cloak carried by the female Burmy before evolution.

01 • OVERVIEW

Wormadam Pokémon GO Overview

Wormadam is the female evolution of Burmy and is one of the more unusual Pokémon in the Sinnoh Pokédex because its form is determined before evolution. Unlike Pokémon with interchangeable battle forms, Wormadam's Plant, Sandy and Trash Cloaks are tied to the Burmy Cloak used during evolution.

08 • GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Wormadam PvP Guide

Wormadam can have some niche value in lower CP formats, particularly because its high Defense provides a different battle profile from many Bug-type Pokémon.

Great League

Plant and Sandy Cloak Wormadam can be considered for specialized Great League teams. However, its current ranking should be checked against live PvP simulations because move balance and the metagame can change.

Ultra League

Wormadam's low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment less natural than Great League use. Even a Level 50 Wormadam remains below the 2,500 CP Ultra League limit.

Master League

Wormadam is generally not a natural Master League investment because its maximum CP is far below the level required to compete effectively with most Master League Pokémon.

PvP note:

IV spreads should be selected based on the specific Cloak and league rather than automatically assuming that 15/15/15 is the best PvP specimen.

18 • RESOURCES

Wormadam Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Wormadam is a relatively inexpensive Pokémon to maintain compared with Legendary Pokémon, but collectors who want multiple Cloaks can still spend significant Candy and Stardust.

  • Collect enough Burmy Candy to evolve all three Cloaks.
  • Keep separate Plant, Sandy and Trash Cloak specimens.
  • Check IVs before transferring duplicate Wormadam.
  • Keep desirable Shiny specimens.
  • Consider Lucky trades for lower Stardust investment.
  • Save XL Candy for Wormadam only if you have a specific PvP or collection goal.

22 • FAQ

Wormadam FAQ

What type is Wormadam?

Wormadam is Bug-type with a second type determined by its Cloak: Grass for Plant Cloak, Ground for Sandy Cloak and Steel for Trash Cloak.

How many Wormadam forms are there in Pokémon GO?

There are three main Cloak forms: Plant Cloak, Sandy Cloak and Trash Cloak.

How do you get Wormadam in Pokémon GO?

Evolve a female Burmy using 50 Burmy Candy. The Burmy's Cloak determines the Wormadam form.

What is Plant Cloak Wormadam's type?

Plant Cloak Wormadam is Bug / Grass.

What is Sandy Cloak Wormadam's type?

Sandy Cloak Wormadam is Bug / Ground.

What is Trash Cloak Wormadam's type?

Trash Cloak Wormadam is Bug / Steel.

What is Wormadam's maximum CP?

Plant and Sandy Cloak Wormadam can reach 2,005 CP at Level 50 with perfect IVs. Trash Cloak Wormadam can reach 1,802 CP.

Is Shiny Wormadam available?

Yes. Shiny Wormadam is available, including the three Cloak forms.

Is Shadow Wormadam available?

Shadow Wormadam is not currently an obtainable standard Pokémon GO variant.

Can you get Purified Wormadam?

Not through the normal current game system because Shadow Wormadam is not currently available as an obtainable Shadow Pokémon.

Can Wormadam be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a trade-related status, not a separate Wormadam species or Cloak.

What is a 100% Wormadam?

A 100% Wormadam has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Wormadam be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification and can coexist with other properties such as Shiny or Lucky.

Can Wormadam be XXS?

Yes. XXS is the extra-small size classification and is separate from the Wormadam Cloak.

Is Mega Wormadam available?

No. There is currently no released Mega Wormadam form in Pokémon GO.

Is Wormadam good for raids?

Wormadam is not currently considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main value is in collection, form completion and niche battle situations.

Which Wormadam form has the highest CP?

Plant Cloak and Sandy Cloak tie for the highest Level 50 maximum CP at 2,005.

Which Wormadam form has the best collection value?

There is no single objectively best collection form. Completing all three Cloaks, especially with Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV specimens, is the larger collection goal.

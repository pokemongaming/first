Why Cherrim's Typing Matters

Cherrim's Grass typing gives it useful resistances against Water, Electric, Grass and Ground attacks. However, it is vulnerable to five common attack categories: Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison.

12 • PURIFIED STATUS

Purified Cherrim

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying Shadow Pokémon. Because Cherrim does not currently have a normal Shadow acquisition route, Purified Cherrim should not be presented as a commonly obtainable current variant.

✨
Purified Cherrim: Not Currently Available Through Normal Shadow Purification A Purified Cherrim would require a Shadow Cherrim source first.

What Happens When a Pokémon Is Purified?

According to Pokémon GO's official help information, purification improves appraisal and reduces Stardust and Candy requirements for certain actions. A purified Pokémon also forgets Frustration and learns Return.

Do not confuse Purified with Lucky:

Purified and Lucky are separate Pokémon statuses. A Pokémon can potentially have different collection attributes, but these terms describe different mechanics.

16 • PVP

Cherrim PvP Guide

Cherrim has some interesting moves, particularly Sunshine Cherrim's Weather Ball, but it is not a consistently strong open-format PvP Pokémon.

Great League

Sunshine Cherrim can be more interesting in selected limited formats because Weather Ball gives it faster charged-move pressure. However, its overall stats and moveset prevent it from being a dependable open-format Great League choice.

Ultra League

Cherrim can technically be used in Ultra League, but it generally faces stronger alternatives. Limited cups can occasionally create more favorable matchups.

Master League

Cherrim's maximum CP is too low for serious Master League competition.

PvP form to watch

Sunshine Cherrim is generally the more interesting form for PvP experimentation because of Weather Ball (Fire).

Cherrim Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Cherrim?

Cherrim is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Cherrim's Max CP?

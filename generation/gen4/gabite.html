DRAGON / GROUND POKÉMON

Gabite

Gabite is a Dragon and Ground-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It evolves from Gible and can evolve into Garchomp. In Pokémon GO, Gabite is primarily an evolution-stage Pokémon that players usually evolve toward Garchomp, but high-quality Gabite can still be important for collectors, PvP projects and evolution planning.

🌑 Shadow Gabite

Shadow Gabite

Shadow Gabite is the Shadow version of Gabite. Players should evaluate it separately from a regular Gabite because Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile and resource cost.

Shadow Gabite Strategy

  • Check its IVs before deciding to evolve.
  • Consider Shadow evolution costs.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare Shadow Garchomp with your existing team.
  • Do not purify automatically.

⬆️ XXL Gabite

XXL Gabite

XXL Gabite is a size variant with a larger recorded size than standard Gabite. Size does not automatically make a Pokémon stronger in raids or PvP.

  • Useful for collectors.
  • Can be relevant for size-based competitions.
  • Does not automatically improve battle stats.
  • Can still evolve.

⬇️ XXS Gabite

XXS Gabite

XXS Gabite is a very small size variant of Gabite. Its value is mainly related to collecting and size-based gameplay.

  • Rare collector variant.
  • Size does not directly increase damage.
  • Can evolve normally.
  • Can be kept for collection purposes.

Gabite FAQ

What type is Gabite?

Gabite is a Dragon and Ground-type Pokémon.

What does Gabite evolve into?

Gabite evolves into Garchomp.

Can Gabite be Shiny?

Yes, Shiny Gabite is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Gabite good?

Shadow Gabite can be valuable primarily because it can evolve into Shadow Garchomp.

Should I purify Shadow Gabite?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the benefits of a Shadow evolution with the purified result before making the decision.

Is Lucky Gabite good?

Lucky Gabite can be an excellent investment because its future evolution can have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% IV Gabite?

A 100% IV Gabite has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Gabite have better stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase Gabite's battle stats.

Can XXS Gabite evolve?

Yes. XXS Gabite can evolve normally.

Torterra Overview

Torterra is the final evolution of Turtwig, the Grass-type starter from Pokémon Diamond, Pearl and Platinum. In Pokémon GO, Torterra combines Grass and Ground typing with high HP and a balanced offensive profile.

Why Torterra's Typing Matters

Torterra's Grass/Ground combination gives it useful offensive coverage but also creates a major vulnerability to Ice-type attacks. Its dual typing also allows it to resist Electric attacks extremely well while reducing damage from Ground and Rock.

🌿
Grass

Provides strong offensive coverage against Water, Ground and Rock targets.

🌎
Ground

Adds Ground-type offensive coverage and powerful resistance to Electric attacks.

⚡
Electric Resistance

Torterra takes greatly reduced damage from Electric-type attacks.

Torterra Weaknesses & Resistances

Torterra's Grass/Ground typing creates four important weakness categories, with Ice being the most dangerous.

Weaknesses

❄️
Ice — 256%

Ice is Torterra's most dangerous weakness. Ice-type attacks deal double super-effective damage because both of Torterra's types are vulnerable to Ice.

🐞

🔥
Fire — 160%

🪽

Torterra PvP Guide

Torterra can function in GO Battle League because its Grass/Ground typing provides useful coverage, although its performance depends strongly on league, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Torterra can be considered for Great League when its CP and moveset fit the format. Its Grass typing gives it useful pressure against Water- and Ground-oriented opponents, while the Ground typing provides additional coverage.

Ultra League

Torterra can also be used in Ultra League with an appropriate CP and IV spread. Before investing, compare its current performance with other Grass-type and Ground-type options.

Master League

Torterra's maximum CP of 3,318 makes Master League less natural than the higher-stat Pokémon that dominate the format. A specialized build can still be evaluated against the current metagame.

Lucky Torterra

Lucky Torterra is a special trade status rather than a separate species or battle form. A Lucky Torterra retains its normal Grass/Ground typing and species characteristics.

🍀

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a good Lucky Torterra attractive for players planning a long-term investment.

Torterra FAQ

What type is Torterra?

Torterra is a Grass and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Torterra weak against?

Torterra is weak to Ice, Bug, Fire and Flying attacks. Ice is its most severe weakness because both of its types are vulnerable to Ice.

What is Torterra's Max CP?

Torterra reaches 3,318 CP at Level 50 with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is the best Torterra moveset?

Razor Leaf and Frenzy Plant are the recommended Grass-focused moveset.

Is Shiny Torterra available?

Yes. Shiny Torterra is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolutions.

Is Shadow Torterra good?

Shadow Torterra can be useful for players looking for additional Grass or Ground damage. Its higher offensive pressure comes with reduced durability.

Should I purify Shadow Torterra?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before purifying, particularly if your goal is raid damage.

What is Lucky Torterra?

Lucky Torterra is Torterra with Lucky status, normally obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Torterra?

A 100% Torterra has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a hundo Torterra?

A Level 50 perfect Torterra reaches 3,318 CP. A Level 40 perfect Torterra reaches 2,934 CP.

What is XXL Torterra?

XXL Torterra is an extremely large size category. It is not a separate species or a separate battle-stat form.

What is XXS Torterra?

XXS Torterra is the smallest size category. It remains the same Grass/Ground Torterra.

Does Torterra evolve?

Yes. Turtwig evolves into Grotle for 25 Candy, and Grotle evolves into Torterra for 100 Candy.

Does Torterra have a Mega Evolution?

Torterra does not currently have a Mega Evolution or Primal Reversion form.

Is Torterra good for raids?

Torterra can be useful as a Grass or Ground attacker depending on its moveset, although stronger specialized attackers may outperform it in particular raid situations.

Is Torterra good in PvP?

Torterra can have PvP applications in the lower leagues, but its ranking depends on its moveset, IVs and the current GO Battle League metagame.

Can a Shiny Torterra be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Torterra can combine multiple desirable traits.

Can a Lucky Torterra be 100% IV?

A Lucky Pokémon has a special IV floor, but not every Lucky Pokémon is perfect. A Lucky Torterra still needs to be checked for its actual IVs.

Can Torterra be Shadow and Shiny?

Special Shadow and Shiny combinations depend on Pokémon GO's encounter availability and current game mechanics. Check the current Shadow encounter pool before making a current-availability claim.

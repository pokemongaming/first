01 • OVERVIEW

Cherubi Overview

Cherubi is a Grass-type Pokémon from Generation 4. It evolves into Cherrim and is one of the more recognizable Sinnoh Pokémon introduced to Pokémon GO. 100% IV Cherubi

A Cherubi with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs is a perfect-IV or "100%" Cherubi.

Is a 100% Cherubi Worth Keeping?

Yes, especially for collectors and players who enjoy completing perfect-IV Pokédex collections. A perfect Cherubi can also be evolved into a perfect-IV Cherrim.

A perfect IV Pokémon is not automatically the strongest Pokémon for every battle format. Always consider the Pokémon's species, level, moveset and league rules.

    23 • FAQ

    Cherubi FAQ

    What type is Cherubi?

    Cherubi is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

    What is Cherubi weak to?

    Cherubi's Grass typing makes it vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Poison, Bug and Flying attacks.

    What does Cherubi evolve into?

    Cherubi evolves into Cherrim after meeting the required Candy cost.

    Is Shiny Cherubi available?

    Shiny Cherubi is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny is active in the game's encounter pool.

    Is Cherubi good for raids?

    Cherubi is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. It is more valuable as a collectible and evolution Pokémon.

    What is a 100% Cherubi?

    A 100% Cherubi has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is a Lucky Cherubi?

    Lucky Cherubi is a Cherubi that has received Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trading system.

    Can Cherubi be XXL?

    Cherubi can be kept as an XXL specimen when an encounter receives the corresponding size classification.

    Can Cherubi be XXS?

    An XXS Cherubi is a particularly small specimen and can be valuable to size collectors.

    Is XXL the same as 100% IV?

    No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Cherubi can have any IV combination.

    Is XXS the same as low IV?

    No. XXS refers to size, not IV quality. An XXS Cherubi can have excellent IVs.

    Is Shadow Cherubi available?

    Shadow Pokémon availability changes over time. Check the current Team GO Rocket lineup before claiming that Shadow Cherubi is currently obtainable.

    Can Shadow Cherubi be purified?

    If Shadow Cherubi is currently obtainable and eligible for purification, it can be purified according to the applicable Pokémon GO mechanics.

    Should I keep a 100% Cherubi?

    Yes. A perfect Cherubi is a useful addition to a perfect-IV Pokédex collection and can also be evolved into a perfect-IV Cherrim.

    Should I keep an XXL Cherubi?

    If you collect Pokémon by size, an XXL Cherubi is worth keeping, especially if it also has another desirable characteristic.

    Should I keep an XXS Cherubi?

    Yes, if you collect XXS Pokémon. An XXS Cherubi can be particularly interesting when combined with Shiny, Lucky or high-IV status.

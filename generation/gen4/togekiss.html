Togekiss

Togekiss is a Fairy and Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO it is best known for its strong Fairy-type damage, excellent Dragon matchups and long-standing relevance in Master League PvP.

Togekiss Overview

Togekiss is the final evolution of Togepi. Its Fairy and Flying typing gives it a unique combination of offensive and defensive strengths. Togekiss is particularly notable for its ability to threaten Dragon-type Pokémon with Fairy damage.

Why Togekiss's Typing Matters

Fairy typing makes Togekiss particularly useful against Dragon, Dark and Fighting Pokémon, while Flying typing provides additional defensive utility. The combination also creates several important weaknesses, especially against Electric, Ice, Poison, Rock and Steel attacks.

Shiny Togekiss

✨ Shiny Togekiss

Shiny Togekiss is the alternate-color version of Togekiss. Its Shiny status is cosmetic and does not directly change its base stats, typing or battle mechanics.

A Shiny Togekiss can still have excellent IVs and can be used for raids or PvP like a standard Togekiss.

Shadow Togekiss

🌑 Shadow Togekiss

Do not judge a Shadow Togekiss only by whether it is a 100% IV Pokémon. A strong Shadow specimen may still be valuable because of its different battle characteristics.

Purified Togekiss

✨ Purified Togekiss

A Purified Togekiss originates from a Shadow Togekiss that has been purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can alter its IVs.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Purified Pokémon may receive IV increases.
  • Purified Togekiss should be evaluated separately from Shadow Togekiss.
  • Consider your long-term raid and PvP goals before purifying.

Should You Purify Shadow Togekiss?

Do not purify automatically. If your goal is to preserve the Shadow battle profile, purification may not be the best choice. If you value IV improvements, collection goals or purified mechanics, the decision can be different.

Lucky Togekiss

🍀 Lucky Togekiss

Lucky Togekiss is a Togekiss obtained through a Lucky trade after evolving from a Lucky Togepi or Lucky Togetic. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than standard Pokémon, making them attractive long-term investment options.

Why Lucky Togekiss Is Valuable

Because Togekiss can require substantial Stardust investment, a Lucky specimen can be particularly useful for players planning to build a high-level Togekiss.

XXL Togekiss & XXS Togekiss

XXL Togekiss

XXL Togekiss is an unusually large size variant. XXL status primarily relates to Pokémon size and collection mechanics.

XXS Togekiss

XXS Togekiss is an unusually small size variant. Like XXL, the XXS designation is primarily related to size and collection features rather than base battle strength.

Does XXL or XXS Change Togekiss Stats?

Size classifications such as XXL and XXS should not be treated as normal IV or base-stat upgrades. A larger Togekiss is not automatically stronger than a smaller Togekiss in standard battle calculations.

Togekiss FAQ

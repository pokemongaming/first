Mismagius FAQ

What type is Mismagius?

Mismagius is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon.

No. Mismagius is the evolved form of Misdreavus.

How do you evolve Misdreavus into Mismagius?

Misdreavus can evolve into Mismagius using Misdreavus Candy and a Sinnoh Stone in Pokémon GO.

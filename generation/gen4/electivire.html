Why Electivire's Typing Matters

Electivire's pure Electric typing gives it a focused offensive role. Electric attacks are especially useful against Water- and Flying-type Pokémon.

Its main defensive concern is Ground-type damage, making matchup selection important when using Electivire in raids or PvP.

Electivire FAQ

What type is Electivire?

Electivire is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Electivire weak to?

Electivire is weak to Ground-type attacks.

Is Electivire good for raids?

Electivire can be a useful Electric-type raid attacker when equipped with an appropriate Electric moveset.

What is the best moveset for Electivire?

Thunder Shock and Wild Charge are a strong Electric-focused moveset for Electivire.

Is Shiny Electivire available?

Yes. Shiny Electivire is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or evolution from an eligible Shiny member of its evolution family.

Is Shadow Electivire good?

Shadow Electivire can be a strong damage-focused Electric attacker because Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure.

Should I purify Shadow Electivire?

Not automatically. If your goal is raid damage, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

What is a Lucky Electivire?

Lucky Electivire is an Electivire obtained through Lucky Trade mechanics and benefits from a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is a 100% Electivire?

A 100% Electivire has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is an XXL Electivire?

XXL Electivire is an unusually large Electivire according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is an XXS Electivire?

XXS Electivire is an unusually small Electivire according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Does XXL Electivire have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Electivire can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Electivire have worse stats?

XXS status is a size characteristic and should not be treated as an IV rating.

What Pokémon evolves into Electivire?

Electivire is the final evolution of Elekid through Electabuzz.

What generation is Electivire from?

Electivire was introduced in Generation 4 and is associated with the Sinnoh region.

Mime Jr.

Mime Jr. is a Psychic and Fairy-type Baby Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, Mime Jr. is best known as the pre-evolution of Mr. Mime and as a collectible Pokémon with Shiny, Lucky and size variants.

Mime Jr. Candy & Stardust

Mime Jr. requires Candy for evolution and power-ups. Because it is primarily a collection and evolution Pokémon, you may want to prioritize Candy for evolution before investing large amounts of Stardust.

  • Lucky Mime Jr. can reduce Stardust costs for power-ups.

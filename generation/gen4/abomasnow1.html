17 · FAQ

Abomasnow FAQ

What type is Abomasnow?

Abomasnow is a Grass and Ice-type Pokémon.

Does Abomasnow have a Mega Evolution?

Yes. Abomasnow can temporarily Mega Evolve into Mega Abomasnow.

Is Shiny Abomasnow different in battle?

Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not normally change standard battle mechanics.

Is Shadow Abomasnow stronger?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile, making Shadow Abomasnow useful for some damage-focused strategies.

Should I purify Shadow Abomasnow?

Not automatically. Consider your long-term battle and collection goals before purifying.

What is a 100% Abomasnow?

A 100% Abomasnow has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

What is XXL Abomasnow?

XXL is a larger-than-normal size classification.

What is XXS Abomasnow?

XXS is a smaller-than-normal size classification.

Is Lucky Abomasnow cheaper to power up?

Lucky Pokémon benefit from reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

Is Mega Abomasnow good for raids?

Mega Abomasnow can be a strong specialist option in raids where Ice or Grass damage is valuable.

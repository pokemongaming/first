Giratina Overview

Giratina is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. Unlike many Pokémon, Giratina does not evolve from or into another Pokémon.

Its main distinction in Pokémon GO is its two separate forms. Altered Forme is generally known for its defensive and PvP-oriented characteristics, while Origin Forme has a more offensive identity.

Giratina Forms

Giratina has two major forms in Pokémon GO. They are separate forms with different battle identities.

Giratina Altered Forme

Altered Forme is generally associated with greater durability and is particularly important in PvP formats.

  • Defensive stat profile
  • Strong PvP identity
  • Ghost / Dragon typing
  • Legendary Pokémon

Giratina Origin Forme

Origin Forme has a more offensive identity and can be particularly valuable for players looking for strong Ghost and Dragon-type damage.

  • More offensive role
  • Strong PvE potential
  • Ghost / Dragon typing
  • Legendary Pokémon
Form note:

Altered Forme and Origin Forme should be treated as separate Pokémon GO builds when planning PvP teams, raid teams and long-term investments.

Giratina PvP Guide

Giratina is one of the most important Legendary Pokémon to understand when discussing high-level Pokémon GO PvP.

Great League

Certain Giratina builds can be used under Great League rules when obtained and powered up appropriately. IV combinations and CP limits are particularly important.

Ultra League

Giratina Altered Forme is especially well known for its PvP relevance because of its bulk, typing and charged-move pressure.

Master League

Both Giratina forms can have Master League relevance, although team composition, current meta trends and movesets strongly influence which form is preferred.

Shadow Giratina

Shadow Giratina is a special Shadow variant of Giratina. Shadow status changes its battle profile and investment requirements.

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally have increased offensive pressure while receiving reduced defensive durability. This makes Shadow Giratina a separate investment decision from a standard Giratina.

Species Giratina
Type Ghost / Dragon
Form Shadow
Evolution None

Shadow Giratina for Raids

Shadow Giratina can be especially interesting for players who prioritize damage output. The higher offensive pressure can make a strong Shadow specimen worth considering for appropriate raid teams.

Frustration warning:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as a Charged Move. The ability to remove Frustration depends on Pokémon GO event mechanics.

Shadow Giratina Investment

  • Do not automatically discard non-perfect Shadow IVs.
  • Consider the higher Stardust and Candy cost.
  • Check move availability before investing.
  • Compare Shadow damage with your existing attackers.
  • Avoid purifying automatically.

Giratina FAQ

Is Giratina a Legendary Pokémon?

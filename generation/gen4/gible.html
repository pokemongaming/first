Gible Evolution

Gible is the first stage of a three-stage evolution family.

Gible

First evolution stage.

Gabite

Evolves from Gible.

Garchomp

Final evolution.

Long-Term Goal

Many players collect and evolve high-quality Gible because Garchomp is the final evolution and can have significant PvE and PvP value.

Gible Moves

Gible's moves are generally less important as a long-term investment compared with the moveset of Gabite and Garchomp.

Investment Tip

Before using significant Stardust or Candy on Gible, consider whether you intend to evolve it into Garchomp.

Shadow Gible

Shadow Gible is the Shadow variant of Gible. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular versions.

🌑 Shadow Gible

Shadow Gible is primarily valuable because it can eventually evolve into Shadow Gabite and Shadow Garchomp.

Shadow Gible vs Regular Gible

Feature Regular Shadow
Typing Dragon / Ground Dragon / Ground
Shadow Bonus No Yes
Offensive Profile Standard Higher
Durability Standard Lower

Should You Evolve Shadow Gible?

Shadow Gible can be an attractive long-term investment because its final evolution may benefit from the Shadow battle mechanic.

Shadow Move Warning:

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. Check current Pokémon GO event rules before using Charged TMs or planning a Shadow evolution.

Purified Gible

Purified Gible is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Gible.

✨ Purified Gible

Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and battle characteristics.

Shadow vs Purified Gible

Feature Shadow Purified
Shadow Status Yes No
Shadow Battle Bonus Retained Removed
IV Adjustment Original Improved on purification
Think Before Purifying:

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Gible simply to improve IVs. Consider the long-term potential of its Shadow evolution first.

Lucky Gible

Lucky Gible is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Pokémon trade mechanic.

🍀 Lucky Gible

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making Lucky Gible potentially attractive for players planning to evolve it.

Why Lucky Gible Can Be Valuable

  • Lower Stardust power-up requirements.
  • Useful for long-term Garchomp projects.
  • Can combine good IVs with cheaper investment.
  • Valuable for collectors.

100% Gible

A 100% IV Gible has the maximum possible Individual Values of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV
Perfect IV Combination

15 / 15 / 15 = 100% IV Gible

A 100% Gible can be an excellent long-term evolution candidate, particularly for players building a high-level Garchomp.

XXL Gible

XXL Gible is a larger-than-normal size variation of Gible.

📏 XXL Gible

XXL Pokémon are primarily collectible size variations. Their size can also be relevant for eligible Pokémon GO showcases and other collection features.

Does XXL Gible Have Better Battle Stats?

XXL status should not automatically be treated as a higher-IV or stronger battle version. Evaluate IVs, level and moves separately.

```
```

XXS Gible

XXS Gible is a smaller-than-normal size variation of Gible.

📐 XXS Gible

Gible FAQ

What type is Gible?

Gible is a Dragon and Ground-type Pokémon.

What does Gible evolve into?

Gible evolves into Gabite, which eventually evolves into Garchomp.

Is Shiny Gible available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game data.

Is Shadow Gible good?

Shadow Gible can be valuable because of the long-term potential of its Shadow evolution line.

Should I purify Shadow Gible?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs and long-term Shadow Garchomp potential first.

What is a 100% Gible?

Does XXL Gible have better stats?

Size should not be confused with IV quality or battle strength.

XXL is larger than normal while XXS is smaller than normal.

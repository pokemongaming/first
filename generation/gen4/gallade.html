Gallade Overview

Gallade is the male evolution associated with the Ralts evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Gallade evolves from a male Kirlia and has a completely different typing from Gardevoir.

Why Gallade's Typing Matters

Gallade's Psychic and Fighting typing gives it access to multiple offensive roles. Its Fighting attacks can be useful against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark Pokémon, while its Psychic typing provides additional coverage options.

Shadow Gallade

Shadow Gallade is the Shadow variant of Gallade. Shadow status changes its battle characteristics and should be considered a separate investment decision.

Shadow Gallade SHADOW FORM

Shadow Gallade provides increased offensive pressure in exchange for reduced defensive durability.

Shadow Gallade Strengths

  • Higher offensive pressure.
  • Can be attractive for damage-focused players.
  • Useful for collectors building different Gallade variants.
  • Can have different PvP and PvE performance from regular Gallade.

Shadow Gallade Investment

Do not judge Shadow Gallade only by its IV percentage. Its Shadow battle characteristics can make a non-perfect specimen worth keeping depending on your goals.

Purified Gallade

Purified Gallade is created when an eligible Shadow Ralts evolution-family Pokémon is purified before reaching Gallade.

Purified Gallade PURIFIED

Purification removes Shadow status and creates a different collection and investment option from Shadow Gallade.

Shiny Gallade

Shiny Gallade is the alternate-colour version of Gallade. Its battle mechanics are generally the same as regular Gallade, with its main difference being its appearance.

Shiny Gallade ✨ SHINY

A Shiny Gallade can also be particularly valuable when it has strong IVs, Lucky status or is suitable for Mega Evolution.

XXL & XXS Gallade

Gallade FAQ

What type is Gallade?

Gallade is a Psychic and Fighting-type Pokémon.

Can Gallade be Shiny?

Yes, Shiny Gallade can be obtained through eligible Shiny Ralts evolution-family encounters.

Can Gallade be Shadow?

Gallade can exist as a Shadow variant when obtained through an eligible Shadow evolution path.

Can Shadow Gallade be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified according to Pokémon GO's current Shadow and purification mechanics.

Can Gallade be Lucky?

Yes. Gallade can have Lucky status when obtained through eligible Lucky Pokémon mechanics.

What is a 100% Gallade?

A 100% Gallade has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Gallade Mega Evolve?

Mega Gallade is Gallade's Mega Evolution form and uses the Mega Evolution mechanics available in Pokémon GO.

Can Gallade be XXL or XXS?

Yes. Gallade can exist in different size categories when obtained from Pokémon with eligible size variations.

Is Gallade good for PvP?

Gallade can be useful in PvP depending on its moveset, league, IV spread and the current metagame.

Should I keep multiple Gallade?

Keeping multiple Gallade can be useful if you collect different forms such as Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and Mega candidates.

Why Piplup's Typing Matters

Piplup's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward weakness and resistance profile. Water-type attacks are useful against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Piplup itself is vulnerable to Electric and Grass attacks.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Piplup

Purified Piplup is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Piplup. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and provides the benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

✨
Purified Status

The Pokémon is no longer Shadow after purification.

❤️
Purification

Purification increases the Pokémon's appraisal values under the game's purification rules.

💠
Special Collection

Purified Piplup can be retained as a separate collection variant.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Piplup

Lucky Piplup is a special trade-related variant. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive appearance in the Pokémon GO collection and require fewer Stardust resources for powering up.

⭐ Lucky Piplup

A Lucky Piplup can be useful as a collection piece or as an evolution project. Before investing, compare its IVs, level and intended evolution.

Lucky Piplup Tips

  • Check the appraisal before evolving.
  • Consider whether you want Lucky Prinplup or Empoleon.
  • Lucky Pokémon can reduce Stardust requirements for power-ups.
  • Keep particularly good Lucky specimens for long-term projects.
12 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Piplup

A 100% IV Piplup has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs. This corresponds to a 15/15/15 IV combination.

15 / 15 / 15 Perfect IV Piplup

A perfect Piplup can be particularly valuable to collectors and players who want a perfect specimen to evolve.

What Does 100% Piplup Mean?

The term "100% IV" refers to the Pokémon having maximum individual values in Attack, Defense and HP. It does not mean that every stat displayed in the game is at its maximum possible value.

  • Attack IV: 15
  • Defense IV: 15
  • HP IV: 15
  • Total IV combination: 45/45
13 • SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Piplup

Piplup can also be encountered with different size classifications. XXL and XXS Pokémon are separate size variants and can be useful for collectors and size-related gameplay features.

📏 XXL Piplup Extra-large size classification

XXL Piplup is a particularly large specimen. Collectors may keep one for size records, showcases or personal collection goals.

📐 XXS Piplup Extra-small size classification

XXS Piplup represents the smaller end of Piplup's size range and can be desirable for players collecting unusual size variants.

Collection tip:

Size, IVs, Shiny status, Shadow status and Lucky status are separate characteristics. A single Piplup can therefore be especially interesting when several desirable traits appear together.

15 • PVP

Piplup PvP Guide

Piplup is generally less relevant in the major Pokémon GO PvP leagues than its evolved form, Empoleon. However, special cups with unusual restrictions can sometimes change which Pokémon are useful.

Great League

Piplup's low maximum CP allows it to remain under the Great League CP limit more naturally than many fully evolved Pokémon, but its actual usefulness depends on the current cup rules, moveset and competition.

Ultra League

Piplup is generally not a natural Ultra League investment. Its evolution line becomes much more relevant after evolving into Prinplup or Empoleon.

Master League

Piplup's stats and maximum CP make it unsuitable for standard Master League competition.

16 • RESOURCES

Piplup Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

If your goal is to evolve or build an Empoleon, Candy management becomes important. Candy XL becomes relevant for high-level investment.

  • Consider event bonuses when farming Piplup Candy.
  • Check the moveset before investing heavily in an evolved form.

How to Use Piplup

21 • FAQ

Piplup FAQ

What type is Piplup?

Piplup is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Piplup weak to?

Piplup is weak to Electric and Grass-type attacks.

Does Piplup evolve?

Yes. Piplup evolves into Prinplup and then Empoleon.

Is Shiny Piplup available?

Yes. Shiny Piplup is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Piplup be Shadow?

Shadow Piplup can exist when it is made available through the relevant Pokémon GO Shadow encounter system.

What is a 100% Piplup?

A 100% Piplup has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Lucky Piplup?

Lucky Piplup is a Piplup that has obtained the Lucky status through Pokémon GO's trade mechanics.

What is XXL Piplup?

XXL Piplup is an extra-large size classification of Piplup.

What is XXS Piplup?

XXS Piplup is an extra-small size classification of Piplup.

Should I evolve a 100% Piplup?

If your goal is collection or maximizing the IV quality of its evolution line, a perfect Piplup can be an excellent candidate for evolution. Consider its intended role before spending Candy.

Is Piplup good for raids?

Piplup itself is generally not considered a top raid attacker. Its evolved form, Empoleon, is considerably more relevant for battle teams.

Is Piplup good in PvP?

Piplup has limited relevance in the standard PvP leagues, although special cups and restricted formats can change which Pokémon are useful.

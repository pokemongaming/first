Purugly Overview

Purugly is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. It evolves from Glameow and does not evolve further. Its Normal typing gives it only one standard weakness while allowing it to resist Ghost-type damage.

Why Purugly's Typing Matters

As a pure Normal-type Pokémon, Purugly has a relatively simple defensive profile. Fighting-type attacks are its primary weakness, while Ghost-type attacks do not damage Normal-type Pokémon.

Shiny Purugly

Shiny Purugly is the alternate-color version of Purugly. A Shiny Glameow can retain its Shiny status when evolved.

✨ Shiny Collection Tip

If you have a Shiny Glameow, evolving it will create a Shiny Purugly. The Shiny status is retained after evolution.

Purified Purugly

Purified Purugly is created when an eligible Shadow Purugly is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its resource costs and IVs.

Purification Decision

Do not purify automatically. Compare your goals for the Pokémon, its IVs, PvP usefulness and collection value before making a permanent purification decision.

  • Compare Shadow and Purified battle roles.
  • Check whether IV changes are important to your build.
  • Consider the removal of Shadow status.
  • Evaluate collection value before purifying.

Lucky Purugly

Lucky Purugly is a special trade-generated version of Purugly. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making them attractive for players planning a long-term investment.

🍀 Lucky Benefit

The biggest advantage of Lucky Purugly is reduced Stardust investment when powering it up.

How to Get Lucky Purugly

A Lucky Purugly can be obtained through an eligible Lucky trade, or by evolving a Lucky Glameow.

Why Magnezone's Typing Matters

Magnezone's dual typing is one of its biggest strengths. Electric gives it access to useful Electric-type damage, while Steel provides a large number of resistances. This makes Magnezone useful in situations where its typing allows it to stay on the battlefield longer than more fragile Electric attackers.

Magnezone IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. For a traditional high-end PvE Magnezone, a 15/15/15 specimen is the perfect IV combination.

What Is a 100% Magnezone?

A 100% Magnezone has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These three values represent the maximum possible IV combination. A perfect Magnezone can be particularly attractive for collectors, long-term investments and players who want to maximize its overall stats.

XXL & XXS Magnezone

XXL and XXS describe size variations of individual Magnezone. They are not separate Pokémon species.

🟣 XXL Magnezone An unusually large Magnezone that can be useful for size-based collection goals and relevant gameplay mechanics.
🔵 XXS Magnezone An unusually small Magnezone that can be collected as a special size variant.

Do XXL and XXS Change Magnezone's IVs?

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Magnezone can have any IV combination, including 15/15/15, and the same is true for an XXS Magnezone.

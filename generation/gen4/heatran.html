Heatran Overview

Heatran is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. Unlike most Pokémon, it has no evolution line. Its Fire and Steel typing gives Heatran an unusual defensive profile while also allowing it to function as a Fire-type attacker.

Shadow Heatran

Shadow Heatran is a Shadow version of Heatran. It retains Heatran's Fire and Steel typing while having a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

SPECIES Heatran
TYPE Fire / Steel
FORM Shadow
EVOLUTION None

Shadow Heatran for Raids

Shadow Heatran should be evaluated separately from regular Heatran. Shadow Pokémon can provide greater offensive pressure while sacrificing durability.

Frustration warning:

Shadow Pokémon may have Frustration. The ability to remove this move depends on current Pokémon GO events and Team GO Rocket mechanics.

Should You Power Up Shadow Heatran?

  • Check its IVs and moveset.
  • Consider increased Shadow power-up costs.
  • Compare it with your existing Fire attackers.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Heatran FAQ

Is Heatran a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Heatran is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 4.

What type is Heatran?

Heatran is a Fire and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Heatran weak to?

Heatran is vulnerable to Ground, Water and Fighting-type attacks.

Does Heatran evolve?

No. Heatran does not have a normal evolution line.

Is Shiny Heatran available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Heatran encounters in Pokémon GO.

Is Heatran good for raids?

Heatran can be a useful Fire-type raid attacker with an appropriate moveset.

What is a 100% Heatran?

A 100% Heatran has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Heatran?

Lucky Heatran is a traded Lucky Pokémon that requires less Stardust to power up.

Does XXL Heatran do more damage?

No. XXL size does not directly increase Heatran's battle stats or damage.

Does XXS Heatran have weaker stats?

No. XXS size classification does not directly reduce Heatran's battle stats.

Should I purify Shadow Heatran?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow battle profile with the purified result before making the decision.

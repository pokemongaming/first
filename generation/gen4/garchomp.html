Garchomp Overview

Garchomp is the final evolution of Gible and Gabite. It is one of Sinnoh's pseudo-Legendary Pokémon and has an offensive typing combination of Dragon and Ground.

Garchomp FAQ

What type is Garchomp?

Garchomp is a Dragon and Ground-type Pokémon.

What is Garchomp weak to?

Garchomp is weak to Ice, Dragon and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Garchomp good for raids?

Yes. Garchomp can be an excellent Ground or Dragon raid attacker depending on its moveset.

Is Shadow Garchomp good?

Shadow Garchomp can be extremely strong for players focused on high raid damage.

Should I purify Shadow Garchomp?

Not automatically. Consider the value of its Shadow offensive performance before purifying.

Is Lucky Garchomp worth powering up?

Yes. The reduced Stardust cost can make Lucky Garchomp an excellent long-term investment.

What is a 100% Garchomp?

A 100% Garchomp has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Garchomp Mega Evolve?

Yes. Garchomp can Mega Evolve into Mega Garchomp when the required Mega Evolution mechanics are available.

Does XXL Garchomp have better stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase standard battle stats.

Does XXS Garchomp have lower stats?

No. XXS size does not automatically reduce normal battle stats.

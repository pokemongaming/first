09 • PVP

Lucario PvP Guide

Great League

Lucario's maximum CP is much higher than the Great League limit, so obtaining a low-level Lucario with a suitable combination of IVs and level can be important when building for limited CP formats.

Ultra League

Lucario can be considered for Ultra League formats, although specific moves, IVs and the current meta should be evaluated before investing significant resources.

Master League

Lucario's Level 50 maximum CP is lower than many dedicated Master League Pokémon. Its PvP value therefore depends heavily on moves, matchup coverage and the current format.

Should You Purify Shadow Lucario?

Do not purify a Shadow Lucario automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result and consider whether you value Shadow battle performance, lower resource requirements or the purified Pokémon's benefits.

Frustration:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. The availability of events that allow Frustration to be replaced can change, so check the current Team GO Rocket schedule before using valuable resources.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Lucario

Purified Lucario is the result of purifying an eligible Shadow Lucario. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's investment profile.

💎

Purified Lucario

Purified status does not change Lucario's basic Fighting / Steel typing.

Purified Lucario Benefits

  • Purification increases each IV by the applicable amount.
  • Purification removes the Shadow status.
  • Frustration is replaced through the purification process.
  • Purified Pokémon can have lower investment requirements than Shadow Pokémon.
  • Compare the purified result with the original Shadow before making the decision.
Important:

If your main objective is maximizing Shadow damage, purifying a strong Shadow Lucario may not be the best choice.

16 • SIZE

XXS Lucario

XXS Lucario is the opposite end of the size spectrum. It is mainly a collector-oriented variation and should not be confused with a low-IV Pokémon.

XXS Lucario Collection Value

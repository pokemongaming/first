Honchkrow

Honchkrow is a Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its strong offensive Attack stat, Dark-type damage potential and its evolution from Murkrow using a Sinnoh Stone.

01 • OVERVIEW

Honchkrow Overview

Honchkrow is the evolved form of Murkrow and becomes a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon after evolution. Its biggest strength is its high Attack, while its relatively low Defense means it can be vulnerable when taking heavy neutral or super-effective damage.

09 • PVP

Honchkrow PvP Guide

Honchkrow has an aggressive stat profile and access to Dark and Flying attacks. Its low Defense can make it difficult to use safely in prolonged PvP battles.

Great League

Honchkrow's maximum CP and stat distribution make it a more specialized choice for Great League. Its performance depends heavily on the current moveset and PvP meta.

Ultra League

Honchkrow can function as a high-pressure attacker in some Ultra League situations, but its limited bulk means shields and matchup selection become particularly important.

Master League

Honchkrow is generally less natural for Master League than Pokémon specifically built around extremely high overall stats and durability.

11 • PURIFIED

Purified Honchkrow

Purified Honchkrow is created by purifying a Shadow Honchkrow. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and provides the standard benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

Should You Purify Shadow Honchkrow?

Purification is a choice rather than an automatic upgrade. Players focused on raid damage should compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision.

💎 Purification Tip

Do not purify a useful Shadow Honchkrow solely because its purified IVs will look better. Consider the battle role and resource cost first.

19 • AVAILABILITY

Honchkrow Availability

Honchkrow is obtained through evolution rather than being treated as a normal wild encounter in the same way as its pre-evolution Murkrow.

Main Acquisition Method

Catch Murkrow, collect the required Candy and use a Sinnoh Stone to evolve Murkrow into Honchkrow.

Shiny Availability

If Shiny Murkrow is available through current Pokémon GO encounters, it can be evolved into Shiny Honchkrow.

Shadow Availability

Shadow availability is controlled by Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Current availability should always be checked separately from historical appearances.

20 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Honchkrow

Before a Raid

  • Check whether Dark or Flying damage is preferred.
  • Confirm Honchkrow has the appropriate moveset.
  • Check the boss's attacks and weaknesses.
  • Consider Honchkrow's relatively low Defense.
  • Compare it with your stronger Dark-type attackers.

Team Building

Honchkrow works best when its high Attack is allowed to shine. Avoid relying on it as a defensive anchor. Instead, use it when fast offensive pressure is more important than long-term durability.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Honchkrow can remain useful for players who need additional Dark-type attackers, especially when the Pokémon has good IVs and a suitable moveset.

22 • FAQ

Honchkrow FAQ

What type is Honchkrow?

Honchkrow is a Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Honchkrow weak to?

Honchkrow is weak to Fairy, Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What Pokémon does Honchkrow evolve from?

Honchkrow evolves from Murkrow.

How do you evolve Murkrow into Honchkrow?

Murkrow requires the appropriate amount of Candy and a Sinnoh Stone to evolve into Honchkrow.

Is Shiny Honchkrow available?

Yes. Shiny Murkrow can be evolved into Shiny Honchkrow when the Shiny form is available.

Is Shadow Honchkrow good?

Shadow Honchkrow can provide stronger offensive pressure than regular Honchkrow, but it also has reduced defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Honchkrow?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your battle goals, IVs and available resources.

What is a 100% Honchkrow?

A 100% Honchkrow has perfect 15/15/15 IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

Is Lucky Honchkrow worth keeping?

Yes. Lucky Honchkrow requires less Stardust for power-ups, making it attractive for long-term investment.

What is XXL Honchkrow?

XXL Honchkrow is an individual classified as extra large. It is a size variant rather than a different species.

What is XXS Honchkrow?

XXS Honchkrow is an individual classified as extra small. It is primarily useful as a collectible size variant.

Is Honchkrow good for raids?

Honchkrow can be useful for Dark-type raid damage because of its high Attack, although its relatively low Defense limits its durability.

Is Honchkrow good in PvP?

Honchkrow is a specialized PvP option. Its offensive pressure can be useful, but its low Defense makes matchup selection important.

Does Honchkrow have a Mega Evolution?

Honchkrow does not have a Mega Evolution in Pokémon GO.

No. Honchkrow does not have a Primal Reversion form.

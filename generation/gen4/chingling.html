Chingling

Chingling is a Psychic-type baby Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a collectible Pokémon that evolves into Chimecho and can also be found in Shiny, Lucky and size variants.

09 · SHINY

Shiny Chingling

Shiny Chingling is available in Pokémon GO. This makes Shiny Chingling an appealing collector target, especially for players completing the Sinnoh Pokédex or baby Pokémon collection.

SHINY AVAILABLE

✨ Shiny Chingling

Shiny Chingling has an alternate coloration from the standard form. Its Shiny status is independent of its IVs, CP, Lucky status and size.

  • Shiny status is separate from IV quality.
  • A Shiny Chingling can also be a Lucky Pokémon after a successful trade.
  • A Shiny Chingling can be evaluated for 100% IV quality separately.
  • Keep an eye on current encounter sources because availability can change.

    11 · PURIFIED

    Purified Chingling

    Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. A Purified Chingling would therefore depend on Shadow Chingling being available through the game's current mechanics.

    SPECIAL STATUS

    Purified Chingling

    Purified status is separate from Shiny, Lucky, perfect IV and size status. Availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO Shadow encounter pool.

    Important:

    Do not treat Purified Chingling as a separate species. It is still Chingling with a Purified status.

    12 · LUCKY

    Lucky Chingling

    Lucky Chingling can be a desirable collector Pokémon because Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

    COLLECTOR VARIANT

    🍀 Lucky Chingling

    A Lucky Chingling is obtained through trading. Its Lucky status does not automatically mean it has perfect IVs.

    Lucky Chingling Checklist

    • Lucky status is obtained through trading.
    • Lucky Pokémon receive a minimum IV floor determined by the game's current Lucky Pokémon mechanics.
    • Lucky does not mean 100% IV.
    • A Lucky Chingling can also be Shiny.
    • A Lucky Chingling can be evaluated for size variants separately.
    13 · PERFECT IV

    100% Chingling — Perfect IV

    A 100% Chingling has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is often called a "hundo" by Pokémon GO players.

    15 Attack IV
    15 Defense IV
    15 HP IV
    Perfect Chingling

    15/15/15 is the perfect IV combination. Because Chingling is not a major raid attacker, a perfect specimen is generally more interesting as a collector piece or evolution candidate than as a PvE investment.

    Can a 100% Chingling be Shiny?

    Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate attributes. Therefore, a Shiny Chingling can theoretically have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

    Can a 100% Chingling be Lucky?

    Yes. A Lucky Pokémon can have perfect IVs. Therefore, a Lucky 15/15/15 Chingling would be an especially desirable collector specimen.

    14 · XXL

    XXL Chingling

    XXL Chingling is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system. Size classification is separate from IVs, CP, Shiny status and Lucky status.

    XXL Chingling Extra-large size classification

    Chingling's XXL height range is approximately 0.30 m to 0.35 m according to current database size information.

    XXL Does Not Mean Perfect IV

    A Pokémon can be XXL without having perfect IVs. If you are hunting for a 100% XXL Chingling, you need to evaluate both its size and its IVs.

  • Check the size classification.
  • Check Attack, Defense and HP IVs separately.
  • Shiny status is independent of size.
  • Lucky status is independent of size.

    XXS Chingling Collector Value

    Rare Combination

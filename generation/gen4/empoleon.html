Empoleon Overview

Empoleon is the final evolution of the Sinnoh Water-type starter family. Its Water/Steel typing gives it a distinctive defensive profile, while its balanced stats allow it to work in several different Pokémon GO formats.

Why Empoleon's Typing Matters

Empoleon's combination of Water and Steel gives it many useful resistances. The Steel typing is particularly valuable because it reduces damage from several common attack types, while Water gives Empoleon a useful offensive identity.

Empoleon IV Guide

IVs add additional Attack, Defense and HP points to an individual Empoleon. A 15/15/15 specimen is considered perfect IV.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

100% Perfect Empoleon

A 100% Empoleon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. For collectors, a perfect Empoleon can be especially valuable. For PvP, however, different IV combinations can sometimes perform better depending on the league.

Empoleon PvP Guide

Empoleon is particularly interesting in PvP because its Water/Steel typing provides a useful combination of resistances and offensive coverage.

Great League & Ultra League

Empoleon can be useful in CP-limited leagues when its specific IV spread and moveset fit the format. PvP IV optimization should therefore be evaluated separately from the simple 15/15/15 perfect-IV approach.

Master League

Empoleon generally has a more limited role in Master League because of its overall stat ceiling compared with the strongest Legendary and high-stat Pokémon.

Shadow Empoleon IVs

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. The ability to remove Frustration depends on eligible Team GO Rocket events and current game mechanics.

Should You Purify Shadow Empoleon?

Purification is a permanent decision. Before purifying, compare the benefits of keeping the Shadow form with the benefits of obtaining the purified version.

Purified Empoleon

Purified Empoleon is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Empoleon. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

💎 Purified Empoleon

A purified Empoleon can be useful for collectors, appraisal purposes and players who specifically want a purified Pokémon. Purification should be considered carefully before committing to it.

Shadow status removed.

Purified Empoleon IVs

Purification increases each IV by the appropriate amount, up to the maximum of 15. This means a Shadow Empoleon with near-perfect IVs can potentially become a 100% IV Pokémon after purification.

Not necessarily. A perfect 15/15/15 Empoleon is excellent for collecting and many PvE uses, but PvP formats can favor different IV distributions because of CP limits.

Empoleon Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a strong Empoleon requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Before spending large amounts of resources, consider the Pokémon's IVs, moveset and intended role.

  • Keep strong IV Empoleon for long-term projects.
  • Save Candy XL for high-level investments.
  • Check whether Hydro Cannon is available before investing.
  • Consider PvP IV spreads separately from perfect IVs.
  • Lucky Empoleon can reduce Stardust power-up costs.

Empoleon Availability

Empoleon can generally be obtained by evolving Prinplup, which itself evolves from Piplup. The availability of Piplup encounters, Community Day opportunities and special events can change over time.

Empoleon FAQ

What type is Empoleon?

Empoleon is a Water- and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Empoleon weak to?

Empoleon is weak to Electric, Grass and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Shiny Empoleon available?

Yes. Shiny Empoleon is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters and evolution opportunities.

Is Shadow Empoleon available?

Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket content and Pokémon GO rotations.

Should I purify Shadow Empoleon?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making a permanent decision.

What is a 100% Empoleon?

A 100% Empoleon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Empoleon?

Lucky Empoleon is an Empoleon obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Empoleon?

XXL Empoleon is an exceptionally large size variant. Its size does not automatically improve its IVs.

What is XXS Empoleon?

XXS Empoleon is an exceptionally small size variant. Like XXL status, it is independent of IV quality.

Is Empoleon good in PvP?

Empoleon can be useful in PvP, particularly in CP-limited formats where its Water/Steel typing and moveset provide useful matchup coverage.

Is Empoleon good for raids?

Empoleon can be useful as a Water-type attacker, although its raid value depends on the current Pokémon GO attacker pool and available moves.

Is a 100% Empoleon always best for PvP?

No. PvP CP limits can make lower-Attack IV combinations more effective in some leagues. Perfect IVs are generally more straightforward for collection and many PvE investments.

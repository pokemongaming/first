Snover

Snover is a Grass and Ice-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. In Pokémon GO, Snover is mainly valued as the first stage of the Snover evolution family, while its different forms and status variants make it an interesting collection target.

01 • OVERVIEW

Snover Pokémon GO Overview

Snover is a dual-type Grass/Ice Pokémon introduced in Generation 4. It evolves into Abomasnow after receiving the required Candy.

Unlike Legendary Pokémon, Snover is a regular species that can be encountered through Pokémon GO events, wild encounters and other rotating encounter methods.

02 • TYPE

Snover Type

Snover is a dual-type Grass and Ice Pokémon. This combination gives it several useful resistances but also creates a relatively large weakness profile.

🌲
Grass / Ice Dual Type
🌿 Grass ❄️ Ice

Why Snover's Typing Matters

The Grass typing provides useful matchups against Water, Ground and Electric attacks, while the Ice typing gives Snover access to the offensive utility associated with Ice-type Pokémon.

The combination also creates several important weaknesses, especially against Fire-type attacks.

08 • IV GUIDE

Snover IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to a Pokémon. A 15/15/15 Snover is considered a perfect IV specimen.

15 Defense IV

100% IV Snover

A 15/15/15 Snover is a perfect-IV Snover. If you are collecting perfect Pokémon, this is the IV combination to look for.

Does a 100% Snover Matter?

A perfect Snover is mainly valuable as a collection milestone or as a candidate for evolution into Abomasnow. A perfect IV does not automatically make Snover a strong raid attacker.

09 • 100% SNOvER

Snover Evolution

14 • LUCKY

Lucky Snover

Lucky Snover is a special trade status rather than a separate species or form. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor.

✨
Lucky Snover

A Lucky Snover can be particularly attractive to collectors and players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Pokémon.

Lucky Snover Benefits

⭐

🎁

Is Lucky Snover Worth Keeping?

Absolutely for collectors. A Lucky Snover with excellent IVs can also be a useful candidate for evolving into Abomasnow while taking advantage of the lower Stardust cost associated with its Lucky status.

15 • XXL

XXL Snover

XXL Snover is a size variant that is larger than normal Snover. Size is separate from IVs, CP and Pokémon quality.

XXL Snover

A large-sized Snover that qualifies for the XXL size category.

Size ≠ IV

A Pokémon can be XXL without having perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Does XXL Snover Have Better Stats?

No. XXL status does not automatically increase the Pokémon's Attack, Defense, HP or CP. It is primarily a size and collection characteristic.

Why Keep XXL Snover?

  • XXL Pokémon are useful for size-focused collections.
  • XXL status can be useful for showcase participation when applicable.
  • An XXL Shiny Snover is an especially attractive collector combination.
  • XXL and Hundo are separate characteristics.
16 • XXS

XXS Snover

XXS Snover is a very small-sized Snover. Like XXL, XXS is a size characteristic and does not directly determine IV quality.

XXS Snover

A Snover that qualifies for the XXS size category.

Independent of IV

XXS Snover can have any combination of IVs.

Should You Keep an XXS Snover?

If you enjoy collecting size variants, an XXS Snover is worth keeping. An XXS Shiny or XXS Hundo can be particularly desirable for collectors.

23 • FAQ

Snover FAQ

What type is Snover?

Snover is a dual-type Grass and Ice Pokémon.

What is Snover weak against?

Snover is weak to Fire, Flying, Poison, Bug, Rock and Steel attacks. Fire is especially dangerous because Snover has a double weakness to Fire.

Does Snover evolve?

Yes. Snover evolves into Abomasnow.

Is Shiny Snover available?

Yes. Shiny Snover is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Snover available?

Shadow Snover availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and Pokémon GO content.

Can Shadow Snover be purified?

Yes. A Shadow Snover can be purified when eligible.

What is a Purified Snover?

Purified Snover is a Snover that has been purified from its Shadow state using Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

What is Lucky Snover?

Lucky Snover is a Snover that has obtained Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Snover?

A 100% Snover has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Snover better than normal Snover?

No. XXL status represents size and does not automatically give Snover better IVs or battle stats.

Is XXS Snover rare?

XXS Snover can be desirable for players who collect size variants. Its value is primarily related to collection and size rather than combat performance.

Is Snover good for raids?

Snover is generally not a top raid attacker. Its evolved form, Abomasnow, is much more relevant for battle purposes.

Should I evolve a Hundo Snover?

If you want to use the perfect specimen in battle or simply complete your Hundo evolution collection, evolving it into Abomasnow can be a good choice.

Should I purify Shadow Snover?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow characteristics with the purified result and decide based on your battle and collection goals.

Can a Shiny Snover be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate characteristics, so a Pokémon can potentially have both.

Can Snover be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Snover can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Snover be both XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size and Shiny status are independent characteristics.

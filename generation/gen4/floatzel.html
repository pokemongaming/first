Floatzel Forms & Special Variants

Floatzel has several collectible and gameplay-related variants in Pokémon GO.

💧 Regular Floatzel

The standard evolution of Buizel. Regular Floatzel is the base version used for general gameplay, raids and collection purposes.

✨ Shiny Floatzel

Shiny Floatzel has alternate coloration and is primarily a collector-focused variant. Its battle potential is based on its IVs and moves rather than its shiny status.

🌑 Shadow Floatzel

Shadow Floatzel has increased offensive pressure but reduced durability. It should be evaluated separately when building raid teams.

✨ Purified Floatzel

Purified Floatzel is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Buizel or Shadow Floatzel.

🍀 Lucky Floatzel

Lucky Floatzel costs less Stardust to power up, making it an attractive option for players who want to invest in a higher-level Floatzel.

💯 100% Floatzel

A 100% Floatzel has perfect 15/15/15 IVs and is often considered a valuable long-term collector and investment target.

📏 XXL Floatzel

XXL Floatzel is a larger size variant. Size does not normally change its battle stats.

🔹 XXS Floatzel

XXS Floatzel is a smaller size variant. It is primarily a collectible form and does not normally receive special battle stats because of its size.

Shadow Floatzel

Shadow Floatzel is the Shadow version of Floatzel. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile compared with regular Pokémon.

Shadow Floatzel Role

Damage-focused Water-type option with increased offensive pressure and lower durability.

Should You Purify Shadow Floatzel?

Do not automatically purify a Shadow Floatzel. Consider whether you value its Shadow damage profile or prefer a Purified Floatzel with lower investment costs.

Purified Floatzel

Purified Floatzel is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow version. Purification removes the Shadow status and creates a separate investment path.

Purification decisions should be made carefully because a Shadow Pokémon cannot return to its Shadow form after purification.

Lucky Floatzel

Lucky Floatzel is obtained through Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for investment because they require less Stardust to power up than normal Pokémon.

Why Lucky Floatzel Can Be Useful

A Lucky Floatzel can be a resource-efficient choice for players who want to power up Floatzel while saving Stardust.

  • Lower Stardust power-up costs.
  • Useful for collectors.
  • Can be valuable if it has strong IVs.
  • Does not automatically guarantee perfect IVs.

XXL Floatzel

XXL Floatzel is an oversized version of Floatzel. Its size classification is primarily relevant for collecting and Pokémon size-related showcases.

XXL Status

Larger size does not normally provide additional Attack, Defense or HP in standard Pokémon GO battles.

XXS Floatzel

XXS Floatzel is a smaller size variant. Like XXL Floatzel, its size is primarily a collectible characteristic.

XXS Status

XXS Floatzel retains the same species and gameplay identity as regular Floatzel.

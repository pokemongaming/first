Shellos

Shellos is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in the Sinnoh region. It is known for having two distinct regional appearances, commonly called West Sea Shellos and East Sea Shellos. In Pokémon GO, Shellos can also be collected in several special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS.

Shellos Overview

Shellos is a Water-type Pokémon from Generation 4. Unlike many Pokémon with regional appearances, both Shellos forms belong to the same Pokédex species but have different visual designs. Shellos evolves into Gastrodon.

Shellos Forms

Why Shellos Being Water Type Matters

As a Water-type Pokémon, Shellos takes super-effective damage from Electric and Grass attacks. It also resists Fire, Water, Ice and Steel attacks. Its typing changes after evolving into Gastrodon, making the evolution line more interesting for battle-focused players.

Purified Shellos

Purified Shellos is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Shellos. This removes the Shadow status and creates a separate collection state.

Purification Consideration

Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically. Consider whether you prefer the Shadow battle profile, the lower power-up costs associated with purification, IV changes or simply collecting the Purified version.

  • Purified Shellos remains the same Pokémon species.
  • Its Water typing does not change.
  • Purification removes the Shadow status.
  • Purification can change IV values.
  • Purified Shellos can remain part of a separate collection.

    • Lucky Shellos

    Lucky Shellos is obtained through the Lucky Trade system. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players planning to invest Stardust into their Pokémon.

    🍀 Lucky Pokémon Advantage

    Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up. This can make a Lucky Shellos or Lucky Gastrodon attractive for collectors who also want to build the Pokémon.

  • Lucky status is obtained through Pokémon trades.
  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky Shellos can evolve into Gastrodon.
  • Lucky status remains part of the Pokémon's collection identity.

    • 100% IV Shellos

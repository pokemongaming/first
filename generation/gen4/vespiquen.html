01 • OVERVIEW

Vespiquen Overview

Vespiquen is the final evolution of female Combee. It is a Bug/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in the Sinnoh region. Only female Combee can evolve into Vespiquen, making the evolution requirement more restrictive than many other Pokémon families.

02 • TYPE

Vespiquen Type

🐝
Bug / Flying Dual Type

Why Vespiquen's Typing Matters

Vespiquen's Bug/Flying typing gives it useful resistances to Ground, Fighting, Grass and Bug attacks. However, the combination also creates a significant weakness to Rock-type attacks.

Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice attacks also deal super-effective damage. Rock is the major danger because Vespiquen takes double super-effective damage from Rock-type attacks.

08 • IV GUIDE

Great League Example

A commonly recommended Great League spread is 0/12/15, which can reach approximately 1,498 CP around Level 29. The ideal spread can change depending on the current PvP meta and specific battle goals.

Perfect 100% Vespiquen

A 100% Vespiquen has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is an excellent collector specimen and can be useful for formats where perfect IVs are preferred, although 15/15/15 is not necessarily the optimal Great League PvP spread.

09 • PVP

Vespiquen PvP Guide

Vespiquen's strongest practical competitive niche is Great League, where its bulk and access to low-cost Bug pressure plus Power Gem coverage can make it interesting in specific matchups.

Great League

Great League is Vespiquen's most natural PvP environment. Its defensive profile allows it to withstand attacks while Fury Cutter can generate energy quickly.

Great League Moveset

Fury Cutter + X-Scissor + Power Gem

Ultra League

Vespiquen can technically reach the Ultra League CP limit, but its overall ranking is considerably less impressive than its Great League performance.

Master League

Vespiquen is generally not a practical Master League choice because its maximum CP is far below the level normally required for competitive Master League teams.

26 • FAQ

Vespiquen FAQ

What type is Vespiquen?

Vespiquen is a Bug/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Vespiquen weak against?

Vespiquen is weak to Rock, Electric, Fire, Flying and Ice attacks. Rock is especially dangerous because Vespiquen has a double weakness to Rock.

What is Vespiquen's max CP?

Vespiquen's maximum CP is approximately 2,267 at Level 50.

How do you evolve Combee into Vespiquen?

Only female Combee can evolve into Vespiquen, and the evolution costs 50 Combee Candy.

Is Shiny Vespiquen available?

Yes. Shiny Vespiquen is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Vespiquen good in Great League?

Great League is Vespiquen's strongest PvP environment. Its bulk and moveset give it useful matchup potential.

Is Vespiquen good in raids?

Vespiquen is generally not a top raid attacker. Its PvP and collection value are more significant than its PvE damage output.

What is a 100% Vespiquen?

A 100% Vespiquen has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Vespiquen?

Lucky Vespiquen is a Vespiquen obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Vespiquen?

XXL Vespiquen is an exceptionally large size specimen. XXL status does not change its species or typing.

What is XXS Vespiquen?

XXS Vespiquen is an exceptionally small size specimen. XXS status is separate from IV quality.

Is Shadow Vespiquen different from regular Vespiquen?

Shadow Pokémon have different battle mechanics and increased offensive pressure with reduced durability. Specific Shadow availability should be checked against current Pokémon GO data.

Should I purify Shadow Vespiquen?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow version, its IVs and its intended use before making a permanent decision.

Is a Hundo Vespiquen better than a Great League Vespiquen?

Not necessarily. A Hundo has perfect IVs, but Great League often rewards a different IV distribution because of the CP limit.

Can Shiny female Combee evolve into Shiny Vespiquen?

Yes. Because Vespiquen requires a female Combee, a Shiny female Combee can be evolved into Shiny Vespiquen.

Magmortar Overview

Magmortar is a pure Fire-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh generation. It evolves from Magmar and represents the final stage of the Magby evolutionary family.

In Pokémon GO, Magmortar has a solid offensive profile but faces strong competition from other Fire-type attackers. Its usefulness depends heavily on its moveset, level and the matchup being played.

Why Magmortar's Typing Matters

Magmortar's pure Fire typing gives it access to super-effective damage against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel-type opponents.

The same typing also creates weaknesses to Water, Ground and Rock attacks, so Magmortar needs careful matchup selection.

Magmortar PvP Guide

Magmortar has access to useful Fire-type attacks but is not naturally suited to every PvP format. Its performance depends heavily on moves, shields and the current meta.

Great League

Magmortar's relatively high Attack and CP profile make it less straightforward to build for Great League. League-specific restrictions should be checked before investing in a low-level specimen.

Ultra League

Magmortar can potentially function as a Fire-type specialist in selected Ultra League situations, although its matchups can be difficult.

Master League

Magmortar is generally less natural for Master League than many Legendary and highly optimized Pokémon. Consider its current moveset and the live metagame before investing heavily.

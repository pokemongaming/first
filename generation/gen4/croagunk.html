Croagunk

Croagunk is a Poison/Fighting-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 4. In Pokémon GO, it is mainly interesting as a collectible, evolution candidate and PvP-related Pokémon, with several special variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS forms.

09 • SHADOW

Shadow Croagunk

Shadow Croagunk is the Shadow variant of Croagunk. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts and require additional consideration before purification or investment.

SHADOW FORM

🌑 Shadow Croagunk

Shadow Croagunk retains its Poison/Fighting typing. The Shadow mechanic affects battle performance rather than changing Croagunk's basic type.

Should You Purify Shadow Croagunk?

Do not purify a Shadow Croagunk automatically simply because purification increases its appraisal values. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result and consider whether you want the Shadow battle profile or the benefits of a purified Pokémon.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Special Team GO Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace Frustration. Check the current event rules before planning your Shadow investment.

Shadow Croagunk IVs

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

A 15/15/15 Shadow Croagunk is a perfect Shadow specimen, but perfect IVs are not required for every use case.

10 • PURIFIED

Purified Croagunk

Purified Croagunk is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Croagunk. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its appraisal, level and resource requirements.

PURIFIED FORM

💠 Purified Croagunk

Purified Croagunk no longer has the Shadow status. It can also display the Purified designation in your collection.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Croagunk

Lucky Croagunk is a special trade-related variant. Lucky Pokémon have a distinctive background in the Pokémon storage screen and require fewer Stardust resources for power-ups than standard Pokémon.

LUCKY POKÉMON

🍀 Lucky Croagunk

Lucky Croagunk can be useful for collectors and trainers who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Pokémon.

Lucky Croagunk Features

  • Lucky status is obtained through trading mechanics.
  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • A Lucky Croagunk can also be Shiny.
  • A Lucky Croagunk can also have strong IVs.
  • Lucky status is different from Shiny, Shadow and Purified status.
Important:

Lucky is not a species or evolution form. It is a special Pokémon status associated with trading.

12 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Croagunk

A 100% IV Croagunk has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Trainers often refer to this combination as a "hundo."

15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
💯 100% Croagunk = 15/15/15

A perfect Croagunk has maximum IVs in all three categories. Whether it should be powered up depends on your intended use, available resources and whether you prefer Croagunk or its evolution.

How to Identify a 100% Croagunk

  • Check the Pokémon appraisal.
  • Confirm Attack reaches 15 IV.
  • Confirm Defense reaches 15 IV.
  • Confirm HP reaches 15 IV.
  • Use an IV calculator if you need additional confirmation.

An XXS Croagunk is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL, XXS status does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.

21 • FAQ

Croagunk FAQ

What type is Croagunk?

Croagunk is a Poison/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Croagunk weak to?

Croagunk is particularly vulnerable to Psychic, Ground and Flying-type attacks.

Does Croagunk evolve?

Yes. Croagunk evolves into Toxicroak.

Is Shiny Croagunk available?

Shiny Croagunk is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Croagunk available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current game schedule before assuming it is obtainable.

What is a 100% Croagunk?

A 100% Croagunk has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Croagunk?

Lucky Croagunk is a special trade-related Pokémon status. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Croagunk?

XXL Croagunk is an exceptionally large Croagunk identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Croagunk?

XXS Croagunk is an exceptionally small Croagunk identified by Pokémon GO's size system.

Can a Shiny Croagunk be evolved?

Yes. Evolving a Shiny Croagunk results in the corresponding Shiny evolution.

Can Shadow Croagunk be purified?

An eligible Shadow Croagunk can be purified. However, purification is a choice rather than an automatic upgrade, so compare the Shadow and Purified versions first.

Is Croagunk good for raids?

Croagunk itself is generally not a top raid attacker. Trainers interested in battle performance should also consider its evolution and stronger Pokémon available for the specific raid matchup.

Should I keep a 100% Croagunk?

Yes, especially if you enjoy collecting perfect-IV Pokémon or intend to evolve it. Its usefulness should still be evaluated against your personal goals.

Should I transfer XXL or XXS Croagunk?

Not automatically. XXL and XXS specimens can be useful for collectors, showcases and completing size-related collections.

Can Croagunk be Lucky and Shiny at the same time?

Yes. Lucky and Shiny are separate Pokémon attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially have both statuses.

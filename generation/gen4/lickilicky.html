Why Lickilicky's Typing Matters

Lickilicky's pure Normal typing gives it a relatively simple weakness and resistance profile. It takes super-effective damage from Fighting-type attacks, while Ghost-type attacks do not damage it normally.

PURIFIED

Purified Lickilicky

Purified Lickilicky is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Lickilicky. Purification changes its status and can alter its IVs.

Purification

Purification removes the Shadow status and applies the standard purification mechanics.

IV Increase

Purification increases each IV by the game's applicable amount, up to the maximum of 15.

Think before purifying:

Purification is not automatically better. Compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision, especially if the Pokémon has battle or collection value as a Shadow.

Lickilicky FAQ

What type is Lickilicky?

Lickilicky is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Lickilicky weak to?

Lickilicky is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Lickilicky good in Pokémon GO?

Lickilicky is primarily interesting for its bulk, PvP potential and collection value rather than being a top-tier raid attacker.

Is Shiny Lickilicky available?

Shiny Lickilicky is available when its corresponding Shiny encounter or evolution availability is active in Pokémon GO.

What is 100% Lickilicky?

A 100% Lickilicky has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Lickilicky?

Shadow Lickilicky is the Shadow variant of Lickilicky and has the battle modifiers associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Lickilicky?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions according to your intended battle and collection goals before purifying.

What is Lucky Lickilicky?

Lucky Lickilicky is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system and receives the benefits associated with Lucky Pokémon.

What is XXL Lickilicky?

What is XXS Lickilicky?

XXS Lickilicky is a particularly small specimen identified by Pokémon GO's size mechanics.

What is a hundo Lickilicky?

Can Lickilicky be Shiny and Lucky?

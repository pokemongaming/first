XXL Shaymin & XXS Shaymin

Size categories are separate from IVs and battle stats. An XXL Shaymin is not automatically stronger, and an XXS Shaymin is not automatically weaker.

Does Size Change Battle Performance?

XXL and XXS size categories should be treated mainly as collection characteristics. Size alone does not mean that a Shaymin has higher IVs or automatically better combat performance.

Shaymin FAQ

What type is Shaymin?

Land Forme Shaymin is Grass type. Sky Forme Shaymin is Grass and Flying type.

Is Shiny Shaymin available?

Yes, Shiny Shaymin has been released in eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Shaymin available?

Shadow Shaymin should not currently be treated as an officially released Shaymin variant.

Can Shaymin be purified?

Purification requires a Shadow Pokémon. Without an official Shadow Shaymin, Purified Shaymin is not a current obtainable variant.

Can Shaymin be Lucky?

Lucky Pokémon are normally created through trading. Shaymin's trade restrictions prevent it from normally becoming Lucky through the standard trade system.

What is a 100% Shaymin?

A 100% Shaymin has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP Individual Values.

Does XXL Shaymin have better stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase IVs, Attack, Defense or HP.

Does XXS Shaymin have lower stats?

No. XXS size is primarily a size characteristic and does not automatically mean weaker battle stats.

Which Shaymin form has higher Attack?

Sky Forme Shaymin has a higher Attack stat than Land Forme Shaymin.

Does Shaymin evolve?

No. Shaymin does not have a normal evolution chain, but it can exist in Land Forme and Sky Forme.

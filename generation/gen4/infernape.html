01 • OVERVIEW

Infernape Overview

Infernape is the final evolution of the Chimchar family and a Fire/Fighting-type Pokémon from Generation 4. Its combination of Fire and Fighting typing gives it access to useful offensive coverage, while its relatively high Attack makes it an interesting Pokémon for players building themed teams, collections and selected battle lineups.

Why Infernape's Typing Matters

Infernape's dual typing gives it offensive access to both Fire- and Fighting-type attacks. This allows it to target several different Pokémon weaknesses, although the combination also gives Infernape four primary weaknesses that should be considered when building a battle team.

08 • IV GUIDE

Infernape IV Guide

Individual Values add additional Attack, Defense and HP points to Infernape. A 15/15/15 Infernape is considered a perfect-IV specimen.

Perfect 100% Infernape

A 100% Infernape has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. A perfect specimen can be particularly attractive to collectors and players who want a maxed Pokémon, although IV perfection is not always required for useful battle performance.

09 • PVP

Infernape PvP Guide

Great League

Infernape can technically be used in Great League if obtained within the appropriate CP limit, but it is not generally considered one of the most consistent choices for the format.

Ultra League

Infernape's offensive typing can create interesting matchups, but its relatively fragile defensive profile means it requires careful shield and move management.

Master League

Infernape's maximum CP is considerably lower than many dedicated Master League Pokémon, limiting its overall role in that format.

PvP rankings can change as moves, Pokémon and the GO Battle League meta evolve. Treat current rankings as time-sensitive data.

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Infernape

Purified Infernape is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Infernape. Purification changes several Shadow Pokémon mechanics and can also increase the Pokémon's individual values.

✨
PURIFIED FORM Purified Infernape

A purified Infernape no longer has the Shadow status and can benefit from the mechanics associated with purification.

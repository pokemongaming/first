Shiny Dialga is a collectible alternate-colour version of regular Dialga. Shiny status does not automatically increase its battle statistics.

Purified Dialga

Purified Dialga is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Dialga. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and may affect its IVs and resource requirements.

Purification Decision

Do not purify automatically. Compare the potential value of keeping the Shadow version with the benefits of a Purified version.

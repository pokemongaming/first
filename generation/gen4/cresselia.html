09 • PVP

Cresselia PvP Guide

Cresselia's combination of high Defense, high HP and useful charged-move coverage makes PvP one of its most important uses in Pokémon GO.

Great League

Cresselia's high CP and Legendary status can make Great League use more restricted than its Ultra and Master League applications. Special formats may occasionally change the answer.

Ultra League

Ultra League is an important environment for evaluating Cresselia. Its bulk allows it to remain on the field while generating charged-move pressure.

Master League

Cresselia can be considered in Master League depending on the current format and moveset, but its relatively low Attack compared with many Legendary Pokémon limits its raw offensive pressure.

PvP Strategy

Use Cresselia's bulk to create shield pressure, manage energy carefully and select coverage moves according to the opponents you expect to face.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Cresselia

Lucky Cresselia is a traded Cresselia that has received the Lucky status. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for players looking to reduce Stardust costs when powering up a Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Cresselia

A Lucky Cresselia can be a useful long-term investment because Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Lucky Cresselia Benefits

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Obtained through Pokémon trading.
  • Guaranteed minimum IVs apply to Lucky Pokémon.
  • Can be valuable for collectors.
  • A Lucky status does not change Cresselia's species.

Lucky Cresselia IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. A Lucky Cresselia can still have IVs below 15/15/15, so check its appraisal rather than assuming every Lucky Pokémon is perfect.

15 • XXL

XXL Cresselia

XXL Cresselia is a size classification rather than a different Pokémon species or form.

📏 XXL Cresselia

An XXL Cresselia is larger than the normal size range for the species. Size can be important for collection goals, medals and visual distinction.

Does XXL change battle stats?

Size classification should not be confused with IVs or base stats. An XXL label does not mean the Pokémon automatically has higher Attack or Defense.

XXL Cresselia Collection Tips

  • Check the size information on the Pokémon's profile.
  • Keep notable XXL specimens if you collect size variants.
  • Compare size separately from IV quality.
  • An XXL Pokémon can also have Shiny, Lucky or other applicable statuses.
16 • XXS

XXS Cresselia

XXS Cresselia represents the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size spectrum. It is a small-sized specimen rather than a different species or form.

🔹 XXS Cresselia

XXS Cresselia is smaller than the normal size range for the species and can be useful for collection and size-related achievements.

Does XXS change IVs?

XXS classification and IV appraisal are separate characteristics. A tiny Cresselia can have high, low or perfect IVs.

XXS Cresselia Collection Tips

  • Check the Pokémon's size classification.
  • Keep an XXS specimen if you collect size variants.
  • Check IVs separately from size.
  • XXS can coexist with other applicable statuses.

23 • FAQ

Cresselia FAQ

What type is Cresselia?

Cresselia is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Cresselia weak to?

Cresselia is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

Is Cresselia a Legendary Pokémon?

Yes. Cresselia is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 4.

Does Cresselia evolve?

No. Cresselia does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Cresselia available?

Yes. Shiny Cresselia has been available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Cresselia good for raids?

Cresselia is generally more notable for its defensive profile and PvP applications than for raw raid damage.

Is Cresselia good for PvP?

Cresselia can be a strong PvP option, particularly because of its high bulk and useful charged-move coverage.

What is a 100% Cresselia?

A 100% Cresselia has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Cresselia?

Lucky Cresselia is a Cresselia that has received the Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is Shadow Cresselia?

Shadow Cresselia is the Shadow version of Cresselia. Shadow Pokémon have different battle mechanics and resource considerations from regular Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Cresselia?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen with the purified result and consider your battle and collection goals first.

What is XXL Cresselia?

XXL Cresselia is a large-size Cresselia. XXL is a size classification rather than a different species.

What is XXS Cresselia?

XXS Cresselia is a small-size Cresselia. XXS is independent of the Pokémon's IVs.

Can a Cresselia be Shiny and Lucky?

Applicable Pokémon GO statuses can coexist in some combinations. Check the game's current trading and encounter mechanics for the specific specimen.

Is a 100% Cresselia always the best PvP Cresselia?

No. A perfect 15/15/15 Cresselia is excellent for collection, but PvP CP limits can make other IV distributions preferable.

Drifblim

Drifblim is a Ghost and Flying-type Pokémon from the Sinnoh region. It evolves from Drifloon and is known in Pokémon GO for its unusual typing, high HP and strong PvP potential.

Drifblim Overview

Drifblim is the evolved form of Drifloon and is classified as a Ghost / Flying-type Pokémon. Its exceptionally high HP makes it particularly interesting for Trainer Battles, while its Ghost typing gives it useful matchups against Psychic and Ghost Pokémon.

Drifblim PvP Guide

Drifblim is primarily interesting as a PvP Pokémon. Its large HP stat allows it to absorb considerable damage, while its Ghost-type moves and coverage options can create pressure in multiple matchups.

Great League

Drifblim can be a useful Great League option because it combines high bulk with unusual typing. Its value depends on the current meta and its available moves.

Ultra League

Drifblim can also be considered for Ultra League teams, especially when players want a Ghost / Flying Pokémon with strong HP and charged-move utility.

Master League

Drifblim's lower maximum CP compared with Legendary Pokémon makes Master League a less natural role.

Drifblim FAQ

What type is Drifblim?

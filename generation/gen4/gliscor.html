Why Gliscor's Typing Matters

Gliscor's Ground and Flying combination gives it useful resistances and an important immunity to Electric-type attacks. However, the dual typing also creates a significant weakness to Ice-type attacks.

21 — FAQ

Gliscor FAQ

What type is Gliscor?

Gliscor is a Ground and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Gliscor weak to?

Gliscor is weak to Ice and Water-type attacks. Ice is particularly important because of its Ground/Flying dual typing.

Is Gliscor good in Pokémon GO?

Gliscor is generally more notable for PvP than for high-end raid damage. Its typing and defensive profile can make it useful in appropriate formats.

Is Shiny Gliscor available?

Yes. Shiny Gliscor is an available alternate coloration when obtained through eligible methods.

Is Shadow Gliscor good?

Shadow Gliscor should be evaluated separately from regular Gliscor because Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

Should I purify Shadow Gliscor?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's battle role, IVs, moves and your collection goals before deciding to purify it.

What is a 100% Gliscor?

A 100% Gliscor has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Gliscor?

Lucky Gliscor is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Gliscor?

XXL Gliscor is an unusually large individual that receives an XXL size classification.

What is XXS Gliscor?

XXS Gliscor is an unusually small individual with an XXS size classification.

Does Gliscor evolve?

Yes. Gliscor evolves from Gligar using the applicable Pokémon GO evolution requirements.

Is Gliscor good for raids?

Gliscor is generally not considered a top-tier Ground raid attacker because of its offensive stat profile. It is usually more interesting for PvP and collection purposes.

Is 15/15/15 Gliscor always best for PvP?

No. PvP often rewards specific IV spreads rather than simply perfect IVs. Check the league-specific rankings before investing.

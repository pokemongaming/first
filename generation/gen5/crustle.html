16 — SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Crustle

Pokémon GO can classify individual Pokémon according to their size. XXL and XXS are separate size characteristics and do not automatically indicate better IVs or stronger battle performance.

XXL Crustle

An exceptionally large Crustle classification. XXL status is primarily a size and collection characteristic.

XXS Crustle

An exceptionally small Crustle classification. XXS status can be valuable for collectors and size-related achievements.

Does XXL Mean Better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Crustle can have poor IVs, while a small Crustle can have excellent IVs.

Does XXS Mean Weak?

No. XXS is a size classification and should not be treated as a direct measure of battle strength.

Crustle FAQ

What type is Crustle?

Crustle is a Bug/Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Crustle weak to?

Crustle's Bug/Rock typing makes it vulnerable to several attacking types, including Water, Rock, Steel and Electric.

What Pokémon does Crustle evolve from?

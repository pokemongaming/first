Simisear Overview

Simisear is the evolved form of Pansear and belongs to the elemental monkey trio introduced in Generation 5. It is a pure Fire-type Pokémon and is known for its flame-producing tail and fiery design.

In Pokémon GO, Simisear is more notable for its regional/event history, Shiny availability, collection value and evolution requirements than for elite raid performance.

Simisear FAQ

What type is Simisear?

Simisear is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Simisear weak to?

Simisear is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What Pokémon evolves into Simisear?

Pansear evolves into Simisear.

Does Pansear require a Unova Stone to evolve?

Simisear's evolution uses the special evolution requirements shown by Pokémon GO. Check the in-game evolution screen for the current cost.

Is Shiny Simisear available?

Shiny Simisear can be part of Pokémon GO's Shiny collection when its evolutionary family is eligible for Shiny encounters.

Is Simisear good for raids?

Simisear can perform a Fire-type role, but it is generally not a top-tier Fire raid attacker.

Is 100% Simisear rare?

A 100% Simisear has 15/15/15 IVs and is desirable for collectors and perfect-IV collections.

What is Lucky Simisear?

Lucky Simisear is a Simisear obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Trade mechanic.

Does Lucky Simisear need less Stardust?

Yes. Lucky Pokémon receive a Stardust discount when powered up.

What is XXL Simisear?

XXL Simisear is a particularly large Simisear according to Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Simisear?

XXS Simisear is a particularly small Simisear according to Pokémon GO's size system.

Does XXL Simisear have higher IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Simisear can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Simisear have lower CP?

Size should not be treated as an automatic replacement for the Pokémon's IV and level calculations.

Is Shadow Simisear available?

Shadow availability should only be listed when Pokémon GO officially makes Shadow Simisear obtainable through its Shadow system.

Can Simisear be purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, Purified Simisear depends on Shadow Simisear being officially obtainable.

Is Simisear good in PvP?

Simisear can be used in CP-limited formats, but its overall PvP value depends on its current moveset and the active metagame.

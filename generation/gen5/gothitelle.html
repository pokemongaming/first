Gothitelle

Gothitelle is a Psychic-type Pokémon from the Unova region. It is the final evolution of the Gothita family and is known for its distinctive appearance, high Special-style attack presence in Pokémon GO battles and usefulness as a Psychic-type option.

Why Gothitelle's Typing Matters

As a pure Psychic-type Pokémon, Gothitelle takes super-effective damage from Bug, Ghost and Dark attacks. Its Psychic typing also gives it useful resistances against Fighting and Psychic-type attacks.

Should You Purify Shadow Gothitelle?

Purification should not automatically be treated as an upgrade. Compare your IVs, intended battle role and resource cost before making the decision.

Purified Gothitelle

Purified Gothitelle is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification changes its status and removes the Shadow battle characteristics.

Purified Gothitelle

A useful choice for collectors and players who prefer the purified version or want to improve IV values through the purification process.

  • Shadow status is removed.
  • Purification can increase IV values.
  • Power-up requirements differ from Shadow Pokémon.
  • Compare the Shadow and Purified roles before deciding.

Lucky Gothitelle

Lucky Gothitelle is obtained through Lucky Trades. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players planning a long-term Stardust investment.

Lucky Investment Advantage

Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust for power-ups, making Lucky Gothitelle cheaper to build than a comparable non-Lucky version.

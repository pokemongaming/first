Frillish Forms & Special Variants

Pokémon GO can display the same Pokémon through different collectible states. These forms do not all change battle stats, but they can affect rarity, appearance, cost or collector value.

Frillish

The standard version of Frillish. It is the normal form used for evolution, collection and battle investment.

Shiny Frillish ✨

Shiny Frillish has alternate coloration. Its battle performance is determined by its level, IVs and moves rather than its Shiny appearance.

Shadow Frillish 🌑

Shadow Frillish is a Shadow variant with a different battle profile and resource considerations from a regular Pokémon.

Purified Frillish ✨

Purified Frillish is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Frillish. Purification should be considered carefully before committing.

Lucky Frillish 🍀

Lucky Frillish has Lucky status and is particularly useful for players planning long-term Stardust investment.

100% Frillish 💯

A 100% Frillish has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

XXL Frillish 📈

XXL Frillish is a larger size variant that may be particularly valuable for collection and size-related challenges.

XXS Frillish 📉

XXS Frillish is an unusually small size variant intended primarily for collection and size-related gameplay features.

Purified Frillish

Purified Frillish is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Frillish. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and should be considered before making a permanent decision.

  • Check IV changes before purifying.
  • Consider whether the Shadow battle profile is useful.
  • Compare future Jellicent plans.
  • Avoid purifying automatically for cosmetic reasons.
```
```

Lucky Frillish

Lucky Frillish is primarily valuable for players who plan to invest Stardust into powering up Frillish or its evolution.

Why Lucky Status Matters

Lucky Pokémon can be particularly attractive long-term investment candidates because of their reduced Stardust power-up requirements.

Frillish PvP Guide

Frillish itself has a limited maximum CP, but its evolution into Jellicent is particularly important when evaluating the family for PvP.

PvP Investment Strategy

  • Check Frillish IVs before evolving.
  • Consider the target league for Jellicent.
  • Avoid assuming a 100% IV spread is always best for PvP.
  • Compare level and CP breakpoints before investment.
  • Check the current PvP moveset and meta.

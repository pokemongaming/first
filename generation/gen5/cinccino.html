XXS Cinccino

Regular Cinccino

The standard non-Shiny, non-Shadow Cinccino is the baseline version for comparison with other variants.

Shiny Cinccino

Shiny Cinccino is a collectible alternate coloration of Cinccino. Its Shiny status does not change the species' underlying base stats.

✨ Shiny Collection Tip

A Shiny Cinccino with strong IVs can be especially attractive to collectors, particularly when combined with another desirable attribute such as Lucky, Hundo or a special size.

Shadow Cinccino

Shadow Cinccino is the Shadow version of Cinccino. Shadow status affects its battle profile and resource requirements.

Purified Cinccino

Purified Cinccino is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Cinccino.

💎

Purified Cinccino

Purification removes Shadow status and applies the game's purification bonuses. A Purified Pokémon can therefore be attractive for collectors and players interested in appraisal improvements.

Should You Purify Shadow Cinccino?

Do not purify automatically. First consider the Pokémon's IVs, moves, collection value and whether you specifically want to keep the Shadow version.

  • Check the current Shadow IVs.
  • Compare the purified appraisal.
  • Consider whether the Shadow version has collection or battle value.
  • Purify only after deciding which form you actually want to keep.

Lucky Cinccino

Lucky Cinccino is obtained through trading. Lucky status is different from Shiny status, IV quality and size.

🍀

Lucky Cinccino

Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust power-up costs. Lucky status can make a Cinccino more attractive to collectors and traders.

Lucky Does Not Mean 100%

A Lucky Cinccino is not automatically a Hundo. Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor, but their final IVs can still vary.

🍀 Lucky Hundo

A Lucky Cinccino can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs. A Lucky Hundo is therefore a particularly desirable combination for collectors.

XXL Cinccino

XXL Cinccino is an exceptionally large individual of the species. Size is recorded separately from IVs, so an XXL Cinccino can have excellent, average or poor IVs.

📏

XXL Cinccino

Large-size Pokémon are primarily useful for collection goals, showcases and personal milestones. XXL status does not automatically make a Cinccino a stronger battler.

XXL + Hundo Combination

An XXL Hundo Cinccino combines two separate attributes: maximum IVs and an exceptionally large size. This can make it a particularly appealing collector's Pokémon.

XXS Cinccino

XXS Cinccino is an exceptionally small individual of the species. Like XXL status, XXS is a size characteristic rather than an IV rating.

🔹

XXS Cinccino

XXS Cinccino can be valuable for collectors who want to complete size records or showcase collections.

XXS + Hundo Cinccino

A perfect-IV XXS Cinccino combines a 15/15/15 IV spread with an exceptionally small size, making it an uncommon combination for collectors.

What type is Cinccino?

Snivy

Snivy is a Grass-type Pokémon from the Unova region and the first stage of the Snivy evolution family. In Pokémon GO, Snivy is mainly valuable as an evolution and collection Pokémon, with additional interest from its Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL, XXS and high-IV variants.

01 • OVERVIEW

Snivy Overview

Snivy is the Grass-type starter Pokémon associated with Unova's starter trio. It evolves into Servine and then Serperior.

Unlike fully evolved Pokémon that are commonly evaluated for raid or PvP performance, Snivy's main value in Pokémon GO is its evolution family, collection potential and special variants.

What Does Snivy's Grass Typing Mean?

Snivy is a pure Grass-type Pokémon. Its typing determines both the attacks it can use effectively and the types of attacks it is vulnerable to.

Because Snivy has relatively low combat power compared with many evolved Pokémon, its Grass typing is generally more important for understanding its evolution family and matchup mechanics than for high-level raid use.

The best IV spread depends on what you plan to do with the Pokémon. For collection purposes, a 15/15/15 Snivy is especially desirable. For PvP, the ideal IV spread can differ because CP limits affect stat combinations.

14 • XXL

XXL Snivy

XXL Snivy is a size variant that is larger than the normal size range. Size status is separate from IV quality, CP and Shiny status.

📏 XXL Snivy

A Snivy identified by Pokémon GO as XXL. This status is primarily useful for collection, showcases and size-based features.

💯 IVs Are Separate

An XXL Snivy can have any IV combination. XXL does not automatically mean 100% IV.

Is XXL Snivy Rare?

Size variants can be desirable to collectors because they are separate from normal Pokémon measurements. Their value depends heavily on the current Pokémon GO showcase and size-related features.

23 • FAQ

Snivy FAQ

What type is Snivy?

Snivy is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What generation is Snivy from?

Snivy was introduced in Generation 5 and is associated with the Unova region.

What is Snivy weak to?

Snivy is weak to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying and Bug-type attacks.

Does Snivy evolve?

Yes. Snivy evolves into Servine and then Serperior.

Is Shiny Snivy available?

Shiny Snivy is an available Shiny variant in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters.

What is a 100% Snivy?

A 100% Snivy has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is Shadow Snivy different from regular Snivy?

Yes. Shadow Snivy has Shadow status and the associated Shadow battle mechanics, while retaining its Grass typing.

Can Shadow Snivy be purified?

An eligible Shadow Snivy can be purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and applies the normal purification mechanics.

Is Lucky Snivy good?

Lucky Snivy can be useful for collectors and players who want a Stardust-efficient Pokémon to evolve or power up.

What does XXL Snivy mean?

XXL Snivy is a Snivy that Pokémon GO identifies as being exceptionally large compared with the normal size range.

What does XXS Snivy mean?

XXS Snivy is a very small Snivy recognized by Pokémon GO's size system.

Does XXL Snivy have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Snivy can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Snivy have better IVs?

No. XXS status does not determine IV quality.

Is Snivy good for raids?

Snivy itself is generally not a high-level raid attacker. Its main value is its evolution family and collection variants.

Should I evolve my Hundo Snivy?

If you want to build a perfect-IV evolution family, evolving a Hundo Snivy can be worthwhile. Check current event moves before evolving.

Should I keep Shiny Snivy unevolved?

That depends on your collection goals. Keeping one Shiny Snivy unevolved allows you to preserve the original form while additional Candy can be used for the evolved forms.

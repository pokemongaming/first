Golett Overview

Golett is a dual Ground/Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves into Golurk after being powered up with Candy. Because Golett is not a Legendary or pseudo-Legendary Pokémon, its primary appeal is collecting, evolution, special forms and completing the Unova Pokédex.

Why Golett's Typing Matters

Golett's Ground/Ghost combination gives it a distinctive matchup profile. Ground attacks are useful against several common Pokémon types, while Ghost typing gives Golett access to Ghost-oriented offensive options.

Should You Power Up Golett?

For most players, it makes more sense to save Stardust and Candy for a strong Golett that you intend to evolve. A high-IV or special-form Golett can be especially valuable for collectors.

Golett Evolution

Golett evolves into Golurk. Unlike Legendary Pokémon, this is a conventional Pokémon GO evolution that uses Candy.

Shiny Golett

Shiny Golett is the alternate-color version of Golett. Shiny Pokémon are primarily collectible, but a Shiny Golett can also be evolved into a Shiny Golurk.

Shadow Golett IVs

Do not automatically transfer a Shadow Golett simply because its IVs are not perfect. Shadow Pokémon can remain interesting even with imperfect IV spreads.

Frustration reminder:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. Check current Team GO Rocket events before planning a TM strategy.

Purified Golett

Purified Golett is created by purifying a Shadow Golett. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs, level and resource requirements.

💎

Purified Golett

A purified Golett no longer has the Shadow status and can be useful for collection or evolution purposes.

✨

Purified Bonus

Purification provides benefits such as a reduced power-up cost compared with Shadow Pokémon.

Lucky Golett

Lucky Golett is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up.

⭐ Lucky Golett

A Lucky Golett is especially attractive for collectors and players planning to evolve it into Golurk.

Why Keep a Lucky Golett?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Guaranteed minimum IV floor after a Lucky Trade.
  • Unique Lucky background.
  • Can be evolved into Lucky Golurk.
  • Useful for collection completion.

XXL & XXS Golett

Pokémon GO can classify Pokémon as XXS or XXL based on their individual size measurements. These size classifications are separate from IVs.

🟣 XXL Golett An exceptionally large Golett. XXL status is a size classification and does not mean the Pokémon has perfect IVs.
🔵 XXS Golett An exceptionally small Golett. XXS status is independent of Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Golett PvP Guide

Golett has a lower CP ceiling than many fully evolved Pokémon, so its PvP relevance is more limited than that of its evolution. Special Cups or restricted formats can change the usefulness of individual Pokémon.

Great League

Golett can be considered for specialized Great League formats, but its performance depends heavily on the current Cup rules, moveset and available opponents.

Ultra League

Golett's relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural for the standard form. Golurk is generally the more relevant member of the evolutionary family.

Master League

Golett is not normally a primary Master League choice because of its relatively low CP potential.

Golett Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Golett FAQ

What type is Golett?

Golett is a Ground/Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Golett weak to?

Golett is weak to Water, Grass, Ice, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

What does Golett evolve into?

Golett evolves into Golurk.

Is Shiny Golett available?

Shiny Golett can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and events.

Is Shadow Golett available?

Shadow Golett availability depends on current Team GO Rocket encounter rotations and events.

What is a 100% Golett?

A 100% Golett has perfect 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Golett?

Lucky Golett is obtained through trading and has the special Lucky status and reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Golett?

XXL Golett is a Golett classified as exceptionally large based on its size measurements.

What is XXS Golett?

XXS Golett is a Golett classified as exceptionally small based on its size measurements.

Should I evolve my Shiny Golett?

If you have more than one Shiny Golett, you may choose to keep one unevolved and evolve another into Shiny Golurk.

Should I purify Shadow Golett?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow's IVs, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

Is a 100% Golett worth keeping?

Yes. A perfect Golett is an excellent collection specimen and can be evolved into a perfect Golurk.

Can Golett be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Golett can be XXL and have perfect IVs.

Can Golett be both XXS and Shiny?

Yes. Size classifications and Shiny status are independent characteristics.

Is Golett good for raids?

Golett itself is generally not a high-priority raid attacker. Players interested in this family should also evaluate Golurk.

Thundurus Overview

08 — PURIFIED

Purified Thundurus

Purified Thundurus can only result from purifying an eligible Shadow Thundurus. Purification removes the Shadow status and creates a different long-term investment profile.

✨

Purification

Removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and move considerations.

⚡

Standard Battle Profile

Purified Thundurus no longer retains the Shadow offensive modifier.

Should You Purify Shadow Thundurus?

  • Compare Shadow raid performance before purifying.
  • Consider IV changes from purification.
  • Check your long-term PvE or PvP goal.
  • Do not purify automatically just for a higher IV.
  • Compare resource costs with your existing team.

09 — LUCKY

Lucky Thundurus

Lucky Thundurus is not a separate battle forme. Lucky status affects the Pokémon's trade and investment characteristics while the underlying Thundurus species and forme remain unchanged.

🍀⚡

Lucky Investment

A Lucky Thundurus can be particularly attractive for long-term power-up projects because Lucky Pokémon have favourable Stardust power-up costs.

A Lucky Thundurus can also independently be Shiny, have high IVs, or be either Incarnate or Therian Forme depending on how it was obtained.

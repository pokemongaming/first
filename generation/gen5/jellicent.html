Jellicent Overview

Jellicent is a Water/Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves from Frillish and has separate male and female appearances. Its combination of high HP, solid Defense and dual typing makes Jellicent more notable for endurance and battle utility than raw raid damage.

Purified Jellicent

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. If Shadow Jellicent becomes available, its purified form should be treated as a separate collection state.

💎

Purified Jellicent

Purified Jellicent is a database form/state that depends on Jellicent first being available as a Shadow Pokémon.

What Happens When a Pokémon Is Purified?

  • The Shadow status is removed.
  • The Pokémon receives appraisal improvements.
  • Power-up costs are reduced.
  • Frustration is replaced with Return when purified.
  • The Pokémon remains the same species.
Purification should be treated as an investment decision, not automatically as an upgrade. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the change.

Jellicent FAQ

What type is Jellicent?

Jellicent is a Water/Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Jellicent weak to?

Jellicent is weak to Electric, Grass, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

What is Jellicent's max CP?

Jellicent reaches 2,644 CP at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Jellicent available?

Yes. Shiny Jellicent is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Jellicent?

A 100% Jellicent has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Jellicent be Lucky?

Yes. Jellicent can become Lucky when obtained through a Lucky trade.

What is XXL Jellicent?

XXL Jellicent is an extra-large specimen. XXL status is independent of IV percentage.

What is XXS Jellicent?

XXS Jellicent is an extra-small specimen. XXS status does not mean the Pokémon has low IVs.

Is Shadow Jellicent available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket rotation rather than permanently assumed.

What is Purified Jellicent?

Purified Jellicent would be a Jellicent that was previously Shadow and then purified, if that Shadow form is available.

What is Jellicent's best moveset?

Hex and Shadow Ball are a strong Ghost-focused moveset. Water-focused builds can use Jellicent's Water moves depending on the battle role.

Is Jellicent good for PvP?

Jellicent can be useful in CP-limited PvP environments because of its bulk and Water/Ghost typing. Current rankings should be checked before building one specifically for a league.

Why Meloetta's Typing Matters

Meloetta FAQ

What type is Meloetta?

Aria Forme Meloetta is Normal/Psychic type. Pirouette Forme is Normal/Fighting type.

What is Meloetta weak to?

Aria Forme Meloetta is weak to Bug and Dark attacks. Pirouette Forme has a different weakness profile because its secondary type changes to Fighting.

What is Meloetta's Pokédex number?

Meloetta is National Pokédex number 648.

Is Meloetta a Legendary Pokémon?

Meloetta is classified as a Mythical Pokémon rather than a Legendary Pokémon.

Does Meloetta evolve?

No. Meloetta does not have a conventional evolution chain.

How many forms does Meloetta have?

Meloetta has two principal forms: Aria Forme and Pirouette Forme.

What is 100% Meloetta?

A 100% Meloetta has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Meloetta?

XXL Meloetta refers to a Meloetta that receives an extra-large size classification.

What is XXS Meloetta?

XXS Meloetta refers to a Meloetta that receives an extra-small size classification.

Is Shadow Meloetta available?

Shadow Meloetta should only be listed as obtainable if Pokémon GO officially introduces it through an eligible Team GO Rocket or other Shadow encounter.

Is Purified Meloetta available?

Purified Meloetta depends on the existence of an eligible Shadow Meloetta. It should not be presented as a normal currently obtainable form without an official mechanic.

Can Meloetta be Lucky?

Meloetta is a Mythical Pokémon and Mythical Pokémon are subject to Pokémon GO trading restrictions. Do not assume that Meloetta can be turned into a Lucky Pokémon through ordinary trading mechanics.

Is Meloetta good in Master League?

Meloetta can be relevant in Master League because of its high overall stats, although its actual performance depends on moveset, team composition and the current metagame.

Is Meloetta good for raids?

Meloetta is not primarily known as a specialized top-tier raid attacker. Its Mythical status, forms and PvP potential are generally more important reasons to keep and invest in one.

Can Meloetta change forms?

Meloetta has Aria and Pirouette forms. The exact Pokémon GO form-change requirements should be checked against the current implementation before publishing exact costs or mechanics.

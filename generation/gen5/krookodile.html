22 · FAQ

Krookodile FAQ

What type is Krookodile?

Krookodile is a Ground and Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Krookodile weak to?

Krookodile is vulnerable to Water, Grass, Ice, Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

What Pokémon does Krookodile evolve from?

Krookodile evolves from Krokorok, which evolves from Sandile.

Is Shiny Krookodile available?

Shiny Krookodile can be obtained by evolving an eligible Shiny member of the Sandile evolutionary family when Shiny availability is active.

What is a 100% Krookodile?

A 100% Krookodile has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is a Lucky Krookodile?

A Lucky Krookodile is a Krookodile that obtained Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system.

What is Shadow Krookodile?

Shadow Krookodile is the Shadow variant of Krookodile and has the battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Krookodile?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow Pokémon's current value, moveset, IVs and your intended use before purifying it.

What is XXL Krookodile?

XXL Krookodile is an exceptionally large individual. XXL status is based on size rather than IV percentage.

What is XXS Krookodile?

XXS Krookodile is an exceptionally small individual. XXS status is separate from its IVs.

Is Krookodile good for raids?

Krookodile can be used in Ground- or Dark-focused teams, but its current raid value should be compared against other available attackers before investing.

Is Krookodile good in PvP?

Krookodile's PvP usefulness depends on its current moveset, CP, league restrictions and the current metagame.

Is a Hundo Krookodile worth keeping?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Krookodile is a perfect-IV specimen and is particularly attractive to collectors and players who want to maximize its individual stats.

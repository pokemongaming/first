Vullaby

Vullaby is a Dark- and Flying-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its Great League potential, its evolution into Mandibuzz, and its range of collectible variants including Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXL and XXS.

01 — OVERVIEW

Vullaby Overview

Vullaby is a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is Pokédex number #629 and evolves into Mandibuzz after 50 Vullaby Candy. Vullaby has relatively low maximum CP compared with many fully evolved Pokémon, but it can be useful in Great League PvP because of its bulky stat distribution.

Vullaby Type

Why Vullaby's Typing Matters

The combination of Dark and Flying gives Vullaby useful resistances to Ground, Psychic, Ghost, Dark and Grass attacks. However, it also leaves Vullaby vulnerable to Electric, Fairy, Ice and Rock attacks.

07 — PVP

Vullaby PvP Guide

Vullaby's biggest competitive identity is associated with Great League. Its relatively bulky stat distribution allows it to function differently from many low-CP attackers.

Great League

Vullaby can be used in Great League, where its bulk and Dark/Flying typing allow it to pressure several Psychic-, Ghost-, Grass- and Dark-related matchups.

Great League
B Tier

Ultra League

Vullaby's maximum CP of 1,437 makes it poorly suited to the standard Ultra League CP environment compared with Pokémon capable of reaching much higher levels.

Master League

Vullaby is generally not a natural Master League choice because its maximum CP is far below the format's usual ceiling.

Great League IV Reminder

Do not automatically transfer a low-Attack Vullaby. PvP IV preferences depend on the specific CP limit, level and matchup goals.

08 — IV GUIDE

Vullaby IV Guide

What Is a 100% Vullaby?

A 100% IV Vullaby has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a hundo.

100% IV Vullaby

15 / 15 / 15 = Perfect IV Vullaby

PvP IVs vs 100% IVs

A perfect 15/15/15 Vullaby is excellent for collection purposes, but it is not automatically the best Great League specimen. PvP builds can prefer different IV distributions depending on level and CP optimization.

10 — SHINY

Shiny Vullaby

Shiny Vullaby is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny version has an alternate coloration while retaining the same Pokémon species, typing and underlying battle mechanics.

✨

Shiny Vullaby

Shiny Vullaby is primarily a collector-focused variant. Its Shiny status does not provide a direct battle-stat bonus.

Shiny Vullaby Checklist

13 — LUCKY

Lucky Vullaby

Lucky Vullaby is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have special visual treatment and require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

🍀

Lucky Vullaby

Lucky status is independent of Shiny status. A Vullaby can therefore be both Lucky and Shiny if the trade results in the appropriate combination.

Why Keep a Lucky Vullaby?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.
  • Lucky Vullaby can be useful for collectors.
  • Lucky and Shiny status can coexist.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor after trading.

Lucky + Shiny Vullaby

One particularly desirable collectible combination is a Lucky Shiny Vullaby. Such a Pokémon combines two separate collection traits: Lucky status and Shiny status.

24 — FAQ

Vullaby FAQ

What type is Vullaby?

Vullaby is a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Vullaby weak to?

Vullaby is weak to Electric, Fairy, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What is Vullaby's max CP?

Vullaby reaches a maximum CP of 1,437 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Does Vullaby evolve?

Yes. Vullaby evolves into Mandibuzz using 50 Vullaby Candy.

Is Shiny Vullaby available?

Yes. Shiny Vullaby is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Vullaby available?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and Pokémon GO events.

What is a 100% Vullaby?

A 100% Vullaby has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Vullaby good for Great League?

Vullaby can be useful in Great League because of its bulky stat distribution and Dark/Flying typing.

Is Vullaby good for raids?

Vullaby is not considered a major raid attacker. Its primary interest is PvP and collection.

What is Lucky Vullaby?

Lucky Vullaby is obtained through trading and receives the special Lucky status, including reduced Stardust requirements for power-ups.

What is XXL Vullaby?

XXL Vullaby is a larger-than-normal size variant. Size does not automatically mean higher IVs.

What is XXS Vullaby?

XXS Vullaby is an exceptionally small size variant. It is mainly relevant to collectors and size-based Pokémon GO mechanics.

Can Vullaby be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Lucky and Shiny are separate characteristics, so a traded Shiny Vullaby can potentially become a Lucky Shiny Vullaby.

Can Vullaby be both 100% and Shiny?

Yes. A Shiny Vullaby can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs, creating a Shiny hundo.

Can Vullaby be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Vullaby can also have perfect IVs.

Can Vullaby be XXS and Shiny?

Yes. XXS and Shiny are separate characteristics, so an XXS Shiny Vullaby is possible.

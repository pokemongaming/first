Lucky Bisharp

Lucky Bisharp is a special trading status rather than a separate species or form. Lucky Pokémon are particularly attractive for players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering them up.

⭐ LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Bisharp

A Bisharp can become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading system. Its Lucky status is shown separately from its species, typing and Shiny status.

Why Keep a Lucky Bisharp?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky status is useful for long-term investments.
  • A Lucky Bisharp can also be Shiny.
  • A Lucky Bisharp can have strong IVs.
  • Lucky status does not automatically mean 100% IV.

Lucky + Shiny + 100% Bisharp

These characteristics can overlap. For example, a single Bisharp can potentially be Shiny, Lucky and have perfect 15/15/15 IVs. These are separate attributes and should be represented independently in your database.

100% IV Bisharp

XXL & XXS Tirtouga

XXL and XXS categories are size-based collectible variations. Size does not automatically change the Pokémon's standard battle stats.

XXL Tirtouga

An unusually large Tirtouga. XXL specimens can have additional collector appeal and may be useful for size-related Pokédex or showcase features.

XXS Tirtouga

An unusually small Tirtouga. XXS specimens are primarily collectible variants and can be valuable for players completing size categories.

Does XXL or XXS Affect Battle Power?

Size classification should not automatically be confused with IVs, level or battle performance. A large Tirtouga is not necessarily stronger than a smaller Tirtouga.

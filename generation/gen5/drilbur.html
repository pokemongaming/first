Purified Drilbur

Purified Drilbur is created when an eligible Shadow Drilbur is purified.

Before Purifying

Compare the potential IV increase, resource savings and the loss of Shadow status before deciding to purify.

A Purified Drilbur can still evolve into Excadrill. The correct choice depends on whether your goal is collecting, PvP or raid performance.

Lucky Drilbur

Lucky Drilbur is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are especially interesting for long-term investments because they have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

Lucky Investment Value

A Lucky Drilbur can be an excellent candidate for evolving into Excadrill when you want to save Stardust.

Is Drilbur Good for Raids?

Drilbur itself is generally more valuable as the first stage of the Drilbur evolution line. Its major long-term raid value comes after evolving into Excadrill.

Long-Term Raid Value

If you catch a strong Drilbur, consider whether evolving it into Excadrill would improve your Ground or Steel raid teams.

Drilbur PvP Guide

Drilbur itself is generally a limited PvP investment compared with its evolved form. Players interested in competitive battles will usually evaluate Drilbur based on its potential evolution into Excadrill.

Evolution for PvP

Before evolving, use an IV ranking tool appropriate for the league you want to play. The ideal IV spread can differ between Great League, Ultra League and Master League.

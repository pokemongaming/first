01 · OVERVIEW

Tornadus Overview

Tornadus is a Legendary Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is part of the Forces of Nature trio alongside Thundurus and Landorus.

Pokémon GO features Tornadus in Incarnate Forme and Therian Forme. The two forms should be treated as separate battle options because their stat profiles and practical performance differ.

02 · TYPE

Tornadus Type

Flying Primary Type

Why Tornadus's Typing Matters

Tornadus is a pure Flying-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it useful offensive opportunities against targets weak to Flying attacks while also providing several important resistances.

The same Flying typing applies to both Incarnate and Therian Forme. Changing between the forms does not turn Tornadus into a dual-type Pokémon.

22 · FAQ

Tornadus FAQ

What type is Tornadus?

Tornadus is a pure Flying-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO.

What is Tornadus weak to?

Tornadus is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock-type attacks.

What is Tornadus's Pokédex number?

Tornadus is Pokédex #641.

What generation is Tornadus from?

Tornadus was introduced in Generation 5 and originates from the Unova region.

Does Tornadus evolve?

Tornadus does not have a normal Pokémon evolution chain. Pokémon GO instead features its different forms.

What is Tornadus Incarnate Forme?

Incarnate Forme is one of Tornadus's two major forms available in Pokémon GO.

What is Tornadus Therian Forme?

Therian Forme is Tornadus's alternate form and has a different Attack and Defense profile from Incarnate Forme.

Is Shiny Tornadus available?

Shiny Tornadus is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Tornadus?

A 100% Tornadus has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is a hundo Tornadus?

"Hundo" is community terminology for a Pokémon with 100% IVs. A hundo Tornadus is therefore a 15/15/15 Tornadus.

Can Tornadus be Lucky?

A Tornadus can have Lucky status when obtained through an eligible trade.

Is Lucky Tornadus automatically 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor, but a Lucky Tornadus is only 100% if all three IVs are 15.

What is XXL Tornadus?

XXL Tornadus is an individual Tornadus classified as unusually large under Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Tornadus?

XXS Tornadus is an individual Tornadus classified as unusually small under Pokémon GO's size system.

Is XXL Tornadus stronger?

XXL status should not be confused with IV quality or a separate species form. Its main importance is related to size and collection features.

Is XXS Tornadus weaker?

XXS status does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has poor IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

Is Shadow Tornadus stronger than regular Tornadus?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on the specific battle and investment goal.

Should I purify Shadow Tornadus?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first, especially if your goal is raid damage.

Is Tornadus good for raids?

Tornadus can provide Flying-type raid damage, although its ranking should be compared against the current Flying-type attacker meta.

Which Tornadus form has higher Attack?

Incarnate Forme has the higher Attack stat, while Therian Forme has higher Defense.

Which Tornadus form has higher Defense?

Therian Forme has higher Defense than Incarnate Forme.

Are Incarnate and Therian Tornadus different Pokémon?

They are different forms of Tornadus rather than separate Pokédex species. Both use Pokédex #641.

Can Shiny Tornadus be Incarnate or Therian?

Shiny status and form are separate characteristics. The availability of a particular Shiny form should be checked against the current Pokémon GO encounter and form rotation.

Can a Lucky Tornadus be 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Tornadus can be a hundo if its Attack, Defense and HP IVs are all 15.

Amoonguss

Amoonguss is a Grass and Poison-type Pokémon from the Unova region. It evolves from Foongus and is known for its unusual Poké Ball-like appearance and access to both Grass and Poison attacks.

Amoonguss Overview

Amoonguss is the evolved form of Foongus and belongs to the Grass / Poison type combination. Its stat distribution gives it considerably more bulk than raw offensive power, making it more interesting for defensive and PvP-oriented roles than for high-end raid damage.

Stunfisk Overview

Stunfisk is a dual Ground/Electric-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. Unlike many Pokémon, Stunfisk does not evolve into another Pokémon.

Its relatively low Attack but strong Defense and HP make it more interesting for PvP than its raw CP might initially suggest.

Why Stunfisk's Typing Matters

Stunfisk's Ground/Electric combination gives it several useful resistances and an unusual offensive profile. Most importantly, its Ground typing makes it immune to Electric-type damage in the standard type-effectiveness system used by Pokémon GO.

What Is a 100% Stunfisk?

A 100% Stunfisk has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is the perfect-IV combination commonly called a "hundo."

Hundo Tip

A 15/15/15 Stunfisk is excellent for collection purposes, but the perfect IV spread is not always the ideal PvP spread. For CP-limited leagues, compare the Pokémon's actual PvP stat product before investing.

Lucky Stunfisk

Lucky Stunfisk is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special characteristics that can make them attractive investment targets.

🍀

Lucky Stunfisk

A traded Stunfisk can become Lucky through the game's Lucky trade mechanics.

Why Keep a Lucky Stunfisk?

Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust to power up. This can make a good Lucky Stunfisk useful for players who want to build a Pokémon while conserving Stardust.

  • Check the IVs immediately after the trade.
  • Keep high-IV Lucky Stunfisk specimens.
  • Consider Lucky trades for PvP collection projects.

Stunfisk FAQ

What type is Stunfisk?

Standard Stunfisk is a Ground/Electric-type Pokémon.

What is Stunfisk weak to?

Standard Stunfisk is vulnerable to Water, Grass and Ice-type attacks.

Does Stunfisk evolve?

No. Standard Stunfisk does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Stunfisk available?

Shiny Stunfisk is an available special appearance when featured through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Stunfisk good in Pokémon GO?

Stunfisk is more notable for PvP and its unusual typing than for high-end raid damage.

Is Stunfisk good in Great League?

Standard Stunfisk can be useful in Great League because its bulk, typing and moves give it useful matchup opportunities.

What is a 100% Stunfisk?

A 100% Stunfisk has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is a hundo Stunfisk always best for PvP?

No. A 15/15/15 Pokémon is perfect in terms of IVs, but many CP-limited PvP builds prefer different IV distributions to maximize bulk.

What is Shadow Stunfisk?

Shadow Stunfisk is the Shadow variant of Stunfisk. It retains the Ground/Electric typing but has the different battle characteristics associated with Shadow Pokémon.

Should I purify Shadow Stunfisk?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow specimen's intended role, IVs and move requirements before deciding to purify it.

What is Lucky Stunfisk?

Lucky Stunfisk is a Stunfisk obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system that has become Lucky.

Is Lucky Stunfisk cheaper to power up?

Lucky Pokémon receive a Stardust discount when powering them up, making good Lucky specimens attractive investment candidates.

What is XXL Stunfisk?

XXL Stunfisk is an exceptionally large Stunfisk recorded by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Stunfisk?

XXS Stunfisk is an exceptionally small specimen recorded by the game's size system.

Does XXL Stunfisk have higher stats?

XXL status itself should not be treated as a direct Attack, Defense or HP bonus.

Can Stunfisk be Shiny and Lucky?

Special attributes such as Shiny and Lucky can combine on the same Pokémon when the applicable game mechanics allow it.

Can Stunfisk be a Shiny hundo?

Yes. A Shiny Stunfisk can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Stunfisk be an XXS hundo?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes, so an XXS Stunfisk can theoretically have perfect IVs.

Is Galarian Stunfisk the same as regular Stunfisk?

No. Galarian Stunfisk is a different regional form with a different typing and battle profile. It should be treated separately from standard Ground/Electric Stunfisk.

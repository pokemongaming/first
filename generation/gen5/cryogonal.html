Why Cryogonal's Ice Typing Matters

Cryogonal's pure Ice typing gives it a focused offensive identity. Ice attacks are particularly useful against Dragon, Flying, Grass and Ground Pokémon. The trade-off is that Ice Pokémon also have several important defensive weaknesses.

13 · LUCKY

Lucky Cryogonal

Lucky Cryogonal is not a different species or form. It is a special status that can result from trading Pokémon.

🍀
Lucky Cryogonal Lucky Pokémon status

Lucky Pokémon require fewer Stardust resources to power up than ordinary Pokémon. A Lucky Cryogonal can therefore be an attractive resource-efficient investment if its IVs and battle role make sense for your collection.

Lucky Cryogonal Benefits

  • Lucky status can reduce Stardust costs for power-ups.
  • Lucky Pokémon are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor.
  • Lucky status does not change Cryogonal's Ice typing.
  • Lucky status is different from Shiny status.
  • A Lucky Pokémon can also be Shiny if obtained through an eligible trade.

20 · FAQ

Cryogonal FAQ

What type is Cryogonal?

Cryogonal is a pure Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Cryogonal weak to?

Cryogonal is weak to Fire, Fighting and Rock-type attacks.

Does Cryogonal evolve?

Cryogonal does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Shiny Cryogonal available?

Shiny Cryogonal can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny is enabled for that encounter.

What is 100% Cryogonal?

A 100% Cryogonal has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Cryogonal?

Lucky Cryogonal is a Cryogonal with Lucky status, normally obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is XXL Cryogonal?

XXL Cryogonal is an unusually large Cryogonal classified as an XXL-size specimen.

What is XXS Cryogonal?

XXS Cryogonal is an unusually small Cryogonal classified as an XXS-size specimen.

Does XXL Cryogonal have higher stats?

Size classification should not automatically be interpreted as a direct combat-stat bonus. XXL is primarily a size-related property.

Does Shiny Cryogonal have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily an appearance variant and does not automatically increase Cryogonal's battle stats.

Is Shadow Cryogonal stronger than regular Cryogonal?

Shadow Pokémon have increased damage output and increased damage received. Whether that makes a particular Shadow Cryogonal more useful depends on its moveset and intended role.

Should I purify Shadow Cryogonal?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions before making the decision, especially if you value the Shadow battle modifier.

Can a Cryogonal be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate properties, so a Pokémon can potentially have both.

Is Cryogonal good for raids?

Cryogonal can provide Ice-type damage, but its raid value should be compared with the strongest current Ice attackers before investing heavily.

Is Cryogonal good in PvP?

Cryogonal's PvP usefulness depends on its moveset, IVs, league, cup rules and the current metagame.

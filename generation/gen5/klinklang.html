Klinklang Overview

Klinklang is the final evolution of Klink and Klang. It is a pure Steel-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5 and is associated with the Unova region.

Unlike Legendary or pseudo-legendary Pokémon, Klinklang is not normally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its value is more closely connected to collecting, completing the Pokédex, hunting special variants and using it in selected battle situations.

Why Klinklang's Steel Typing Matters

Steel is one of the most defensive typings in Pokémon GO. Klinklang therefore has a useful resistance profile even though its overall offensive performance is more limited than many dedicated Steel attackers.

Klinklang PvP Guide

Klinklang's Steel typing provides useful resistances, while its moves can give it interesting coverage in selected PvP formats.

Great League

A Great League Klinklang must meet the 1,500 CP limit. A PvP-focused specimen should be evaluated using its actual IV combination, level and current moveset rather than simply choosing the highest percentage IV.

Ultra League

Ultra League allows a higher CP ceiling, giving Klinklang more room to use its stats. Its success depends heavily on the current meta and available moves.

Master League

Klinklang's maximum CP is much lower than the strongest Master League Pokémon, making it a less natural Master League choice.

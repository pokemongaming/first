Whimsicott

Whimsicott is a Grass- and Fairy-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known for its distinctive dual typing, useful resistances and relevance in selected PvP formats. This guide covers Regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Whimsicott.

01 · OVERVIEW

Whimsicott Overview

Whimsicott is the final evolution of the Cottonee family. It evolves from Cottonee using a Sun Stone and Candy. Its Grass/Fairy typing gives it a very different battle profile from many other Grass-type Pokémon.

Why Whimsicott's Typing Matters

Whimsicott's Grass/Fairy combination gives it several useful resistances while also creating important weaknesses to Poison, Fire, Ice, Flying and Steel attacks. The Fairy typing also gives Whimsicott an important defensive relationship with Dragon-type attacks.

09 · PVP

Whimsicott PvP Guide

Whimsicott's Grass/Fairy typing and access to Fairy and Grass moves can give it useful matchups in CP-limited PvP formats. Its performance depends heavily on the current move pool and metagame.

Great League

Great League is the format where Whimsicott's CP profile is most naturally suited. A lower-Attack, higher-bulk IV spread can sometimes outperform a traditional 15/15/15 build.

Ultra League

Whimsicott can be used in Ultra League under suitable team and ruleset conditions, although its resource requirements and relative bulk should be compared with alternative Fairy and Grass Pokémon.

Master League

Whimsicott's maximum CP is considerably lower than the Pokémon typically favored in open Master League competition. It is therefore generally not a conventional Master League choice.

PvP IV reminder:

Do not automatically transfer a low-Attack Whimsicott simply because it is not a 100% IV Pokémon. Lower Attack can be valuable under CP limits.

16 · XXL

XXL Whimsicott

XXL Whimsicott is a size variation. Size does not inherently increase the Pokémon's Attack, Defense or HP, but XXL status can be important for collection, showcases and size-based gameplay features.

📏 XXL Whimsicott

An XXL Whimsicott is larger than the normal size range. Its size designation is separate from IV quality.

Does XXL Whimsicott Have Better Stats?

No. XXL status should not be confused with high IVs. A Pokémon can be XXL and have poor IVs, or it can be XXL and have excellent IVs.

XXL + 100% IV

An XXL 100% IV Whimsicott combines a large size with perfect IVs and can therefore be particularly desirable to collectors.

17 · XXS

XXS Whimsicott

XXS Whimsicott is the small-size counterpart to XXL Whimsicott. Like XXL status, XXS is primarily a size characteristic and does not automatically indicate better IVs or battle stats.

📐 XXS Whimsicott

An XXS Whimsicott falls into an exceptionally small size category and can be valuable to collectors interested in size records and showcases.

23 · FAQ

Whimsicott FAQ

What type is Whimsicott?

Whimsicott is a Grass- and Fairy-type Pokémon.

What is Whimsicott weak to?

Whimsicott is weak to Poison, Fire, Ice, Flying and Steel attacks.

Does Whimsicott evolve?

Whimsicott is the final evolution of Cottonee. Cottonee evolves into Whimsicott using a Sun Stone and Candy.

Is Shiny Whimsicott available?

Shiny Whimsicott is available through eligible Shiny encounters and evolution from Shiny Cottonee.

Is Shadow Whimsicott available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Shadow Pokémon rotation.

What is Purified Whimsicott?

Purified Whimsicott is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Whimsicott or its evolution-stage predecessor.

What is a Lucky Whimsicott?

A Lucky Whimsicott is a Whimsicott obtained through trading that receives Lucky Pokémon characteristics.

What is a 100% IV Whimsicott?

A 100% IV Whimsicott has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Whimsicott have better stats?

No. XXL status is a size characteristic and does not automatically increase Attack, Defense or HP.

Does XXS Whimsicott have lower stats?

No. XXS is a size characteristic and should not be confused with low IVs or poor battle performance.

Is Whimsicott good for raids?

Whimsicott is generally not a top priority raid attacker. Its value is more specialized toward PvP, collection and typing-based utility.

Is Whimsicott good for PvP?

Whimsicott can have value in selected PvP formats, particularly where its Grass/Fairy typing and available moves provide useful matchups.

Is a 100% IV Whimsicott best for PvP?

Not necessarily. CP-limited leagues can favor lower-Attack, higher-bulk IV distributions instead of 15/15/15.

Can a Shiny Whimsicott be Lucky?

Yes. A Shiny Whimsicott can become Lucky through trading if the trade produces a Lucky Pokémon.

Can a Lucky Whimsicott be 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Whimsicott can have 15/15/15 IVs.

Can a Whimsicott be both XXL and Shiny?

Yes. Size, Shiny status and IVs are separate attributes, so multiple characteristics can occur on the same Pokémon.

Can a Whimsicott be XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are independent characteristics.

Zebstrika Overview

Zebstrika is the evolved form of Blitzle and a pure Electric-type Pokémon from Generation 5. Why Zebstrika's Typing Matters

Zebstrika's pure Electric typing gives it one primary offensive identity. Electric attacks are particularly effective against Water- and Flying-type opponents, while Zebstrika's only standard type weakness is Ground.

09 • PVP

Zebstrika PvP Guide

Zebstrika can be considered for lower-CP formats, but its overall PvP value depends heavily on its current moveset, league rules and the surrounding metagame.

Great League

Great League is the most natural place to experiment with Zebstrika because of its lower CP ceiling. However, its relatively fragile defensive profile means that shield management and matchup selection are important.

Ultra League

Zebstrika can be tested in Ultra League, although its lower overall stats can make it difficult to compete consistently against stronger established options.

Master League

    Zebstrika FAQ

    What type is Zebstrika?

    Zebstrika is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

    What is Zebstrika weak to?

    Zebstrika is weak to Ground-type attacks.

    What Pokémon does Zebstrika evolve from?

    Zebstrika evolves from Blitzle.

    Does Zebstrika evolve again?

    No. Zebstrika is the final stage of the Blitzle evolution family.

    Is Shiny Zebstrika available?

    Shiny availability depends on Pokémon GO's current release and encounter rules. Check the current game data before claiming a live encounter method.

    Is Shadow Zebstrika available?

    Shadow availability is controlled by Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket and encounter rotations. Current availability should be checked before publication.

    Can Shadow Zebstrika be purified?

    An eligible Shadow Zebstrika can be purified. Purification removes the Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's attributes.

    Is Lucky Zebstrika possible?

    Yes. A Zebstrika can become Lucky through an eligible Pokémon GO trading outcome.

    What is a 100% Zebstrika?

    A 100% Zebstrika has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

    What is XXL Zebstrika?

    XXL Zebstrika is an exceptionally large individual of the species. XXL is a size attribute, not a separate Pokémon species.

    What is XXS Zebstrika?

    XXS Zebstrika is an exceptionally small individual. Like XXL, XXS is a size attribute rather than a separate species.

    Is Zebstrika good for raids?

    Zebstrika can be used in raids, but it is generally not a first-choice Electric attacker for players focused purely on maximum PvE performance.

    Is Zebstrika good in PvP?

    Zebstrika can be experimented with in lower-CP leagues, but its usefulness depends on the current moveset and PvP metagame.

    Is a 100% Zebstrika worth keeping?

    Yes. A perfect-IV Zebstrika is a valuable collection piece even if it is not a top-tier attacker.

    Can Shiny Zebstrika also be Lucky?

    Yes. A Pokémon can possess multiple attributes, meaning a Shiny Zebstrika can also have Lucky status after an eligible trade.

    Can Zebstrika be both XXL and 100% IV?

    Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so a Zebstrika can combine an XXL size attribute with perfect IVs.

    Does Shiny Zebstrika have different stats?

    No. Shiny status is primarily a visual variation and does not inherently give Zebstrika different base battle stats.

Karrablast Overview

Karrablast is a Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is part of a unique evolution family and evolves into Escavalier through the special Karrablast–Shelmet evolution mechanic used by Pokémon GO.

Karrablast PvP Guide

Karrablast itself is generally less important in PvP than its evolution, Escavalier. Players interested in PvP should consider whether evolving the Pokémon better fits their intended league.

Great League

Karrablast's relatively low overall CP ceiling makes it more naturally suited to lower-level formats than Master League, although its actual PvP value depends heavily on the current move pool and league meta.

Ultra League

Karrablast is generally not a first-choice Ultra League investment. Escavalier is usually the more relevant target for players who want to build around this evolution family.

Master League

Karrablast's maximum CP makes it a poor fit for the traditional Master League environment compared with much stronger high-CP Pokémon.

Karrablast FAQ

What type is Karrablast?

Karrablast is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Karrablast weak against?

Karrablast is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What does Karrablast evolve into?

Karrablast evolves into Escavalier.

Is Shiny Karrablast available?

Yes. Shiny Karrablast is available as a special alternate-color variant.

Can Karrablast be Lucky?

Yes. Karrablast can become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics.

What is a 100% Karrablast?

A 100% Karrablast has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Karrablast be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification that is separate from IV quality.

Can Karrablast be XXS?

Yes. XXS is an extra-small size classification and does not determine the Pokémon's IVs.

Is Shadow Karrablast available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO game data before being presented as an obtainable form.

Can Karrablast be Purified?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, Purified Karrablast availability depends on whether Shadow Karrablast is officially available.

Is Karrablast good for raids?

Karrablast itself is generally not a priority raid attacker. Its evolution into Escavalier is usually more relevant for battle-focused players.

Should I evolve a 100% Karrablast?

If you want a perfect Escavalier, a 15/15/15 Karrablast is an excellent evolution candidate.

Does Shiny Karrablast evolve into Shiny Escavalier?

Yes. A Shiny Karrablast retains its Shiny identity when evolved.

Are XXL and XXS Karrablast different Pokémon?

No. XXL and XXS are size classifications of the same species rather than separate Pokémon species.

Excadrill for Raids

Excadrill can be a useful Ground-type raid attacker, especially for players building teams to counter Electric, Fire, Rock, Poison and Steel raid bosses.

  • Strong Ground-type offensive role.
  • Useful against Electric raid bosses.
  • Useful against Rock and Steel opponents.
  • Shadow Excadrill can provide a different offensive profile.
  • Move availability should always be checked against current Pokémon GO data.

Shadow Excadrill

Shadow Excadrill is the Shadow version of Excadrill. It should be evaluated separately from the regular version because Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

👤 Shadow Excadrill

Shadow Excadrill is generally more attractive for players focused on maximizing offensive pressure, while accepting reduced durability and higher resource costs.

Excadrill FAQ

What type is Excadrill?

Excadrill is a Ground and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Excadrill weak to?

Excadrill is weak to Fire, Water, Fighting and Ground-type attacks.

Is Excadrill good for raids?

Excadrill can be a useful Ground-type raid attacker depending on the raid boss and your available team.

Is Shiny Excadrill different in battle?

Shiny Excadrill has an alternate appearance but uses the same species battle mechanics as regular Excadrill.

Is Shadow Excadrill stronger?

Shadow Excadrill has a different offensive and defensive battle profile and can be particularly attractive for damage-focused players.

Should I purify Shadow Excadrill?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow battle role, IVs and investment costs before purifying.

What is a 100% Excadrill?

A 100% Excadrill has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Excadrill?

XXL Excadrill is an unusually large size variant that can be relevant for collecting and eligible Showcase events.

What is XXS Excadrill?

XXS Excadrill is an unusually small size variant primarily valued as a collectible.

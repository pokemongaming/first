Serperior Overview

Serperior is a pure Grass-type Pokémon from the Unova region. It is the final evolution in the Snivy evolution family: Snivy → Servine → Serperior. In Pokémon GO, Serperior is especially notable for Trainer Battles. Its defensive-oriented stat distribution makes it more naturally suited to PvP than high-level raid damage.

Serperior PvP Guide

Serperior is primarily known as a PvP Pokémon. Its bulk and charged-move pressure can make it useful in formats where its maximum CP and matchups fit the league.

Great League

Great League is one of Serperior's most important formats. A properly built Serperior can use its defensive profile and Grass damage to pressure Water, Ground and Rock Pokémon.

Ultra League

Serperior can also be considered for Ultra League. However, resource cost, IV spreads and the current meta should be considered before committing Candy XL and Stardust.

Master League

Serperior's maximum CP is relatively low compared with many Legendary and pseudo-Legendary Master League Pokémon, making it a much less natural choice for that format.

Shiny Serperior

✨
SHINY FORM

Shiny Serperior

Shiny Serperior has an alternate appearance compared with the standard version. Shiny status is cosmetic and does not automatically improve Attack, Defense, HP or IV values.

  • Shiny status does not guarantee strong IVs.
  • A Shiny Serperior can still be used in PvP.
  • Shiny and regular Serperior use the same battle mechanics.
  • Collectors may prefer high-IV Shiny specimens.

Purified Serperior

✨
PURIFIED FORM

Purified Serperior

Purified Serperior originates from an eligible Shadow Serperior after purification. Purification should be considered carefully because it removes Shadow status.

  • Purification removes Shadow status.
  • Purified Pokémon can have different investment considerations.
  • Do not purify only because you want a cosmetic change.
  • Compare the PvP and collection value before purification.

Serperior FAQ

What type is Serperior?

Serperior is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

Is Serperior good in Pokémon GO?

Serperior is primarily notable for Trainer Battles and can be particularly useful in PvP formats where its typing, bulk and moveset fit the current meta.

Is Shiny Serperior stronger?

No. Shiny status changes appearance but does not automatically increase battle stats.

What is a 100% Serperior?

A 100% Serperior has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is a 100% IV Serperior always best for PvP?

Not necessarily. Great League and Ultra League builds can use different IV spreads depending on CP limits and stat optimization.

What is Lucky Serperior?

Lucky Serperior is obtained through the Lucky Trade system and requires less Stardust to power up.

Does XXL Serperior have stronger stats?

No. XXL classification does not automatically increase standard battle stats.

Does XXS Serperior have lower stats?

No. XXS size classification is separate from IVs and normal battle stats.

Can Serperior be Shiny and Lucky?

Depending on the current Pokémon GO mechanics and acquisition method, collectible traits can overlap. Always verify the specific in-game interaction.

Should I purify Shadow Serperior?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow battle profile with the benefits of purification before making a permanent decision.

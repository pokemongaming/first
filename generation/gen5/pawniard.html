Pawniard Overview

Pawniard is a dual Dark/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation 5. It has a relatively low maximum CP compared with many fully evolved Pokémon, but it remains useful as an evolutionary and collection target.

Pawniard Type

Pawniard combines Dark and Steel typing, giving it a very different matchup profile from many other Pokémon in its evolutionary family.

Why Pawniard's Typing Matters

The Dark/Steel combination gives Pawniard several useful resistances, including a particularly strong matchup against Psychic-type attacks. However, Fighting-type attacks are especially dangerous.

Pawniard PvP Guide

Pawniard can be more interesting in PvP than its modest maximum CP might suggest. Its Dark/Steel typing gives it a useful defensive profile.

Great League

Pawniard can fit within Great League CP limits, although its performance depends heavily on the current format, moveset and available alternatives.

Ultra League

Pawniard's relatively low maximum CP makes Ultra League less natural for the species itself. Its evolved forms are generally more relevant for higher CP formats.

Master League

Pawniard's maximum CP is far below the typical Master League range, so it is not a natural Master League investment.

Lucky Pawniard

Lucky Pawniard is a special traded version of Pawniard. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up, making a Lucky Pawniard an interesting collection or evolution project.

🍀
Lucky Pawniard

A Lucky Pawniard can be obtained through Pokémon GO trading. Its Lucky status is separate from Shiny status, so a Pokémon can have special combinations depending on how it was obtained.

Why Keep a Lucky Pawniard?

  • Reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.
  • Useful for collectors completing special variants.
  • Can be evolved into Bisharp.
  • Can contribute toward a complete Pawniard family collection.

100% IV Pawniard

A 100% Pawniard has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP. In Pokémon GO, this is commonly called a hundo.

XXL Pawniard

XXL Pawniard is a size variant that is larger than standard Pawniard. Size is separate from IVs, CP and Shiny status.

📏 XXL Pawniard

Keep an XXL Pawniard if you enjoy collecting size variants, completing Pokédex records or building a collection of unusual specimens.

XXL Does Not Mean 100%

An XXL Pawniard does not automatically have perfect IVs. A Pokémon's size and IV appraisal are separate characteristics.

XXS Pawniard

XXS Pawniard is the small-size variant of Pawniard. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is independent of Shiny status, IVs and CP.

📐 XXS Pawniard

An XXS Pawniard can be a valuable collection piece for Trainers interested in size records and unusual Pokémon variants.

XXS + Shiny + 100% Combination

A particularly rare collection target would be a Pawniard combining multiple desirable characteristics, such as Shiny status, perfect IVs and an XXS size. These characteristics are independent of each other.

Pawniard Candy & XL Candy

Pawniard requires Candy for evolution and additional resources for powering up. If you plan to evolve several Pawniard, Candy management becomes important.

  • Keep high-IV Pawniard before transferring extras.
  • Keep a Shiny Pawniard if you want a Shiny evolution.
  • Consider keeping Lucky Pawniard for reduced Stardust investment.
  • Check IVs before transferring rare size variants.
  • Keep XXL and XXS specimens if you collect size records.

Pawniard FAQ

What type is Pawniard?

Pawniard is a Dark- and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Pawniard weak to?

Pawniard is especially vulnerable to Fighting-type attacks and is also weak to Fire and Ground.

What is Pawniard's max CP?

Pawniard's Level 50 maximum CP is approximately 1,620 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Pawniard available?

Yes. Shiny Pawniard is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Pawniard available?

Current database references list Shadow Pawniard as unavailable. This can change if Niantic adds Pawniard to a future Team GO Rocket rotation.

Can Pawniard be Purified?

Purification requires a Shadow Pokémon. Since Shadow Pawniard is not currently listed as available, Purified Pawniard should not currently be presented as an obtainable form.

Can Pawniard be Lucky?

Yes. Pawniard can become Lucky through trading.

What is a 100% Pawniard?

A 100% Pawniard has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Pawniard?

XXL Pawniard is a larger-than-standard size variant. Size is independent of IVs and Shiny status.

What is an XXS Pawniard?

XXS Pawniard is a smaller-than-standard size variant. It can be kept as a collection specimen.

Is Pawniard good for raids?

Pawniard is not a major raid attacker. Its primary value is its evolution family, PvP potential in appropriate formats and collection variants.

What does Pawniard evolve into?

Pawniard evolves into Bisharp. Bisharp is part of the same evolutionary family that leads to Kingambit.

Should I keep a Shiny Pawniard?

Yes, especially if you are collecting Shiny Pokémon or want a Shiny member of the Pawniard evolutionary family.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Pawniard?

If you care about size records or collection completeness, both XXL and XXS Pawniard are worth considering before transferring them.

Golurk Overview

Golurk is a dual Ground/Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves from Golett after the required Candy investment. Its unusual typing gives Golurk a mixture of offensive opportunities and defensive resistances.

Why Golurk's Typing Matters

Golurk's Ground/Ghost typing gives it a very different battle profile from a conventional Ground-type Pokémon. Ground attacks provide super-effective coverage against several important types, while the Ghost typing gives Golurk additional resistances and immunities.

Golurk FAQ

What type is Golurk?

Golurk is a Ground- and Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Golurk weak to?

Golurk is vulnerable to Water, Grass, Ice, Ghost and Dark attacks.

What is Golurk's max CP?

Golurk's maximum CP is around 3,226 under the commonly listed Pokémon GO stat calculations. Actual displayed CP depends on IVs and level.

Is Golurk a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Golurk is a regular Pokémon introduced in Generation 5.

Does Golurk evolve?

Yes. Golurk evolves from Golett.

Is Shiny Golurk available?

Shiny Golurk is available when its Shiny form is obtainable through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Golurk?

A 100% Golurk has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Is Shadow Golurk better than regular Golurk?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. Whether Shadow Golurk is preferable depends on your battle role, moveset and available alternatives.

Should I purify Shadow Golurk?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result before making the decision, especially if the Shadow Golurk has strong IVs.

What is Lucky Golurk?

Lucky Golurk is a Golurk that has become Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is XXL Golurk?

XXL Golurk is an unusually large individual. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Golurk?

XXS Golurk is an unusually small individual. XXS status does not automatically make the Pokémon stronger in battle.

Is Golurk good for raids?

Golurk can be useful in Ground- or Ghost-focused teams, although its ranking depends on the current Pokémon GO moves and the available competition.

Is Golurk good for PvP?

Golurk can have niche PvP applications because of its Ground/Ghost typing, but its effectiveness depends heavily on league, moveset and the current metagame.

Is a Shiny 100% Golurk rare?

A Shiny Golurk with perfect 15/15/15 IVs combines two desirable collection attributes and can be particularly valuable to collectors.

Is a Lucky 100% Golurk possible?

Yes. A Lucky Pokémon can have perfect IVs if the resulting traded Pokémon receives 15/15/15 IVs.

Does XXL or XXS affect Golurk's IVs?

No. Size attributes such as XXL and XXS are separate from the Pokémon's Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

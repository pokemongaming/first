Mega Roggenrola

Mega Evolution Status: Not Available

Roggenrola does not have a Mega Evolution in the Pokémon series. Therefore, Mega Roggenrola is not an available form and should not be listed as a playable Pokémon GO Mega Evolution.

Important:

Roggenrola evolves into Boldore and Gigalith, but neither evolution changes Roggenrola into a Mega Pokémon.

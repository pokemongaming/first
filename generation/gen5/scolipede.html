Scolipede Overview

Scolipede is the final member of the Venipede evolution family. In Pokémon GO, its Bug and Poison typing gives it an unusual combination of offensive and defensive matchups. Scolipede can be evaluated for PvP, collection purposes and special variants such as Shadow, Purified, Lucky, Shiny, XXL and XXS.

Why Scolipede's Typing Matters

Scolipede combines Bug and Poison typing. This gives it access to Poison-oriented matchups while also providing Bug-type coverage. Its dual typing also creates several important weaknesses, so the opponent's moveset should always be considered before using Scolipede in battle.

Perfect Scolipede

A 15/15/15 Scolipede is a strong collector target, but perfect IVs are not automatically the best IV combination for every CP-limited PvP league.

Is a 100% Scolipede Always Best?

Not necessarily. For PvE and high-level collection purposes, perfect IVs are highly desirable. In CP-limited PvP leagues, different IV spreads can sometimes provide more optimized stat distributions.

XXL Scolipede

XXL Scolipede is a larger size variant. Size categories are primarily important for collecting, showcases and size-based Pokémon GO activities.

XXL Scolipede

Larger-than-normal size variant. Useful for collectors and size-focused events or PokéStop Showcases when eligible.

Battle Performance

XXL size does not automatically make a Scolipede stronger. Battle performance is determined by stats, IVs, level, moveset and relevant game mechanics.

XXS Scolipede

XXS Scolipede is a smaller size variant. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is primarily a collection and size-category characteristic.

XXS Scolipede

Smaller-than-normal size variant and a useful collector category for players building a complete Scolipede collection.

Battle Performance

XXS status should not be confused with IV quality. A very small Scolipede can still have excellent IVs, while a large Scolipede can have low IVs.

Genesect Overview

Genesect is a Mythical Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is associated with the Unova region and has the unusual combination of Bug and Steel typing.

Genesect does not evolve. Instead, its most important special variations are its different Drive forms. Each Drive changes the elemental type of Genesect's signature charged move, Techno Blast.

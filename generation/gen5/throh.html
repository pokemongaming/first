Throh Overview

``` Throh is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon from Generation 5. Unlike many Fighting Pokémon, Throh does not evolve from or into another Pokémon. Throh FAQ

What type is Throh?

Throh is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

Does Throh evolve?

No. Throh does not evolve into or from another Pokémon.

What is Throh weak to?

Throh is weak to Flying, Psychic and Fairy-type attacks.

Is Shiny Throh different in battle?

Shiny status changes appearance rather than automatically improving battle performance.

Is Shadow Throh stronger than regular Throh?

Shadow mechanics can increase offensive pressure while reducing defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Throh?

Not automatically. Compare your collection and battle goals before removing Shadow status.

What is a Lucky Throh?

XXL size status should not be treated as a direct battle-stat increase.

What is XXS Throh?

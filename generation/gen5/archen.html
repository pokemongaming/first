Archen

01 · OVERVIEW

Archen Pokémon GO Overview

Archen is a Rock/Flying-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 5. It is #566 in the National Pokédex and evolves into Archeops after spending 50 Archen Candy. Its relatively high Attack is offset by very low Defense, so Archen is generally more interesting as a collectible, evolution target or Pokédex entry than as a top-tier raid attacker.

02 · TYPE

Archen Type

🪨
Rock / Flying Dual Type Pokémon

Why Archen's Typing Matters

Archen combines Rock and Flying typing, giving it useful resistances to several common attack types but also a long list of weaknesses. Water, Electric, Ice, Rock and Steel attacks deal super-effective damage. Its dual typing also means Archen takes reduced damage from Normal, Fire, Flying, Poison and Bug attacks, while Ground attacks are especially favorable because Flying typing makes Archen immune to Ground-type damage.

07 · BATTLE

Is Archen Good for Raids?

Archen is not generally considered a top-tier Rock or Flying raid attacker. Its low Defense and modest CP limit its practical PvE value compared with stronger Pokémon. It can still be useful for early-game players, collection challenges or situations where a player simply wants to use their favorite Pokémon.

12 · SHADOW

Shadow Archen

Shadow Archen is a separate Shadow version of Archen. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from their regular counterparts and can also have Frustration as their Charged Move.

SHADOW FORM

🌑 Shadow Archen

Shadow Archen retains the Rock/Flying typing of Archen. Its Shadow mechanics make it an option that should be evaluated separately from regular Archen.

Shadow Archen Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Archen's basic typing. Therefore its primary weaknesses remain Water, Electric, Ice, Rock and Steel.

13 · PURIFIED

Purified Archen

Purified Archen is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Archen. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and provides the standard benefits associated with purified Pokémon.

PURIFIED FORM

✨ Purified Archen

Purified Archen no longer has Shadow status. It can also receive the purified Pokémon treatment, including the replacement of Frustration with Return during purification.

Should You Purify Shadow Archen?

  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions before committing.
  • Do not purify solely because the IVs increase.
  • Consider whether you value the Shadow collection status.
  • Consider your Candy and Stardust resources.
  • Keep special Shadow specimens if collecting is important to you.
14 · LUCKY

Lucky Archen

Lucky Archen is a special trade result. A Pokémon can become Lucky through trading, and Lucky Pokémon receive the reduced Stardust cost for powering up associated with the Lucky mechanic.

TRADE VARIANT

🍀 Lucky Archen

Lucky Archen is visually marked by its Lucky status in the Pokémon GO collection and can be attractive to collectors because of its lower power-up Stardust requirements.

Why Keep a Lucky Archen?

  • Lower Stardust investment for power-ups.
  • Useful for completing a Lucky Pokédex collection.
  • A Lucky Shiny Archen can be a particularly rare collection piece.
  • Lucky status can be retained after evolving into Archeops.
15 · SIZE

XXL Archen

XXL Archen is a size variant associated with Pokémon GO's height and size system. An XXL Archen can be useful for collection goals, size-related medals and personal collection value.

EXTRA LARGE

📏 XXL Archen

An XXL Archen is larger than the standard size range. The XXL designation is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon is not automatically a high-IV Pokémon.

Important Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Archen can have poor IVs, average IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.
16 · SIZE

XXS Archen

XXS Archen represents the opposite end of the size system. It is smaller than the standard size range and can be kept as a collection specimen.

EXTRA SMALL

🔹 XXS Archen

An XXS Archen is smaller than the normal size range. Like XXL, the XXS classification does not determine IV quality.

Collector Tip

A 100% IV XXS or 100% IV XXL Archen combines two separate collection characteristics and can be especially satisfying to keep.

18 · PVP

Archen PvP Guide

Archen's very low Defense makes it a difficult Pokémon to build around in competitive PvP. Its high Attack does not fully compensate for its poor durability.

Great League

Archen can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but its fragile defensive profile makes it a niche choice rather than a reliable meta option.

Ultra League

Archen's CP ceiling and defensive profile make it an unusual choice for Ultra League teams.

Master League

Archen is not a natural Master League Pokémon because its maximum CP is far below the level of the format's strongest competitors.

PvP advice If you want to experiment with Archen in PvP, focus on specific cup rules and limited metas rather than assuming that its high Attack automatically makes it competitive.

23 · FAQ

Archen FAQ

What type is Archen?

Archen is a Rock/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Archen weak to?

Archen is weak to Water, Electric, Ice, Rock and Steel attacks.

What is Archen's max CP?

Archen reaches roughly 2,083 CP at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Archen available?

Yes. Shiny Archen is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Archen evolve?

Yes. Archen evolves into Archeops using 50 Archen Candy.

What is a 100% Archen?

A 100% Archen has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Archen be Lucky?

Yes. Archen can become Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading system.

Can Archen be XXL?

Yes. Pokémon GO's size system can produce XXL specimens.

Can Archen be XXS?

Yes. XXS is the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size system.

Is Shadow Archen available?

Shadow Archen exists as a separate Shadow variant and should be evaluated independently from regular Archen.

Can Shadow Archen be purified?

Eligible Shadow Pokémon can be purified. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and applies the normal purified Pokémon mechanics.

Is Archen good for raids?

Archen is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its low Defense and relatively low CP limit its PvE usefulness.

Is Archen good in PvP?

Archen is a niche PvP option because its very low Defense makes it difficult to use consistently against stronger opponents.

Should I evolve my Hundo Archen?

If your goal is a perfect Archeops, evolving a 15/15/15 Archen is a sensible choice. If you collect Archen itself, consider keeping a Hundo Archen before evolving it.

Should I keep an XXL or XXS Archen?

Yes, if you care about size collection, medals or rare specimens. Size and IV quality are independent characteristics.

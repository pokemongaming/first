Lampent

Lampent is a Ghost and Fire-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, Lampent is best known as the middle stage of the Litwick evolution family and as the Pokémon that evolves into Chandelure.

01 · OVERVIEW

Lampent Overview

Lampent is the second stage of the Litwick evolutionary family. It evolves from Litwick and can eventually evolve into Chandelure. Lampent has a unique Ghost / Fire typing that gives it a distinctive set of strengths and weaknesses.

Lampent Special Forms & Variants

Pokémon GO can feature different Lampent variants and collectible characteristics. These should not be confused with separate species or evolution stages.

Shiny Lampent has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. A Shiny Lampent can be evolved into a Shiny Chandelure.

Shadow Lampent

Shadow status is a special Pokémon GO mechanic. When available, Shadow Lampent should be evaluated separately from its regular counterpart.

Purified Lampent

Lucky Lampent can result from Pokémon GO's Lucky trade mechanics. Lucky Pokémon have special visual effects and reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

📏

XXL Lampent

XXL Lampent is a size variant. Its large size can make it particularly interesting for collectors and size-focused Pokédex completion.

📐

XXS Lampent

XXS Lampent is a small-size variant that can be collected independently from normal, XL and XXL size specimens.

23 · FAQ

Lampent FAQ

What type is Lampent?

Lampent is a Ghost and Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Lampent weak to?

Lampent is weak to Dark, Ground, Rock, Ghost and Water-type attacks.

Does Lampent evolve?

Yes. Lampent evolves into Chandelure.

What Pokémon evolves into Lampent?

Litwick evolves into Lampent.

Is Shiny Lampent available?

Yes. Shiny Lampent is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and retains its Shiny status when evolved.

Is Shadow Lampent available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and event rotations.

What is a 100% Lampent?

A 100% Lampent has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Lampent?

XXL Lampent is an extra-large size variant. Its size classification is independent of its IVs.

What is XXS Lampent?

XXS Lampent is an extra-small size variant. XXS status does not indicate perfect IVs.

How do I get Lucky Lampent?

Lucky Pokémon are obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. A traded Lampent can become Lucky when the applicable Lucky trade mechanics trigger.

Should I power up Lampent?

For most battle-focused players, it is generally more useful to evolve a strong Lampent into Chandelure rather than heavily investing in Lampent itself.

Is Lampent good for PvP?

Lampent has niche PvP applications, particularly in restricted formats, but its usefulness depends on the current moves and Cup rules.

Is a 100% Lampent worth keeping?

Yes. A 100% Lampent can be an excellent specimen to keep for collection purposes or to evolve into a perfect-IV Chandelure.

Archeops

Archeops is a Rock / Flying-type Pokémon originally discovered in the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its very high Attack stat and distinctive fossil Pokémon design, while its lower defensive profile affects how it performs in battle.

Archeops Overview

Archeops is the evolved form of Archen and was introduced in Generation 5. It is a dual Rock / Flying-type Pokémon with an unusually offensive stat profile.

Archeops does not evolve further. Trainers who collect different Pokémon GO variants may also encounter Shiny, Lucky, XXL and XXS versions, depending on the game's current mechanics and availability.

Purified Archeops

Purified Pokémon are created by purifying eligible Shadow Pokémon. A Purified Archeops would therefore require Archeops to first be available as a Shadow Pokémon.

🌟

Purified Archeops

Treat Purified Archeops as an availability-dependent form rather than claiming permanent availability.

Purification Mechanics

  • Purification applies to eligible Shadow Pokémon.
  • Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status.
  • Purification affects the Pokémon's IVs and level.
  • Purification removes the Shadow battle mechanic.
  • Always compare the Shadow and Purified versions before purifying a valuable Pokémon.
Important:

If Shadow Archeops is not part of the active Pokémon GO ecosystem, Purified Archeops cannot simply be obtained independently.

Archeops FAQ

What type is Archeops?

Archeops is a Rock / Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Archeops weak to?

Archeops is weak to Water, Electric, Ice, Rock and Steel attacks.

What is Archeops' maximum CP?

Archeops can reach approximately 3,144 CP at level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Archeops available?

Shiny Archeops is the alternate-color form of Archeops. Check the current Pokémon GO availability before planning a Shiny hunt.

Is Archeops good for raids?

Archeops has very high Attack, but its low Defense means its practical raid value depends heavily on its current moveset and the specific matchup.

Is Archeops good in PvP?

Archeops can be used in PvP formats under the appropriate CP limits, but its defensive profile makes it more matchup-dependent than many established PvP choices.

What is a 100% Archeops?

A 100% Archeops has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Archeops?

Lucky Archeops is an Archeops obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics and has the Lucky status, including reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Archeops?

XXL Archeops is a larger-than-normal size variant. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

What is XXS Archeops?

XXS Archeops is a very small size variant. XXS status does not automatically mean that the Pokémon has good or bad IVs.

Is Shadow Archeops available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Team GO Rocket rotation. Do not assume that every Pokémon has a currently obtainable Shadow version.

Can I get Purified Archeops?

Purification requires an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, Purified Archeops depends on whether Shadow Archeops is officially available.

Does Archeops evolve?

Archeops is the final evolution of Archen and does not have another standard evolution.

Is 100% Archeops better than Shiny Archeops?

They represent different attributes. A 100% Archeops has perfect IVs, while Shiny Archeops represents an alternate appearance. A Pokémon can potentially be both Shiny and perfect IV.

Can Archeops be Lucky and Shiny?

Lucky and Shiny are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can potentially combine multiple collection characteristics when the relevant game mechanics allow it.

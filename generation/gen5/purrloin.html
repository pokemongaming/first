Why Purrloin's Typing Matters

Purrloin is a pure Dark-type Pokémon. This gives it useful resistance against Psychic-type attacks and makes it completely unaffected by Psychic-type damage in standard Pokémon GO type interactions.

Shiny Purrloin

Shiny Purrloin is the alternate-color version of regular Purrloin. It has the same species, typing and underlying gameplay mechanics as standard Purrloin.

✨ SHINY FORM

Shiny Purrloin

Shiny Purrloin is primarily a collector variant. Its value comes from its alternate appearance and rarity rather than increased battle stats.

Shiny Purrloin Facts

  • Same Pokémon species as regular Purrloin.
  • Same Dark typing.
  • Does not automatically have better IVs.
  • Can be kept as a collector Pokémon.
  • Can evolve into Shiny Liepard.

Shadow Purrloin

Shadow Purrloin is a Shadow version of the regular species. Shadow Pokémon should be evaluated separately because the Shadow battle mechanic changes their offensive and defensive profile.

SHADOW FORM

Shadow Purrloin

Shadow Purrloin retains the Dark typing and species identity of Purrloin while receiving the Shadow battle characteristics available in Pokémon GO.

Purified Purrloin

Purified Purrloin is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Purrloin. Purification changes its status from Shadow to Purified and can alter IVs and resource costs.

✨ PURIFIED FORM

Purified Purrloin

Purified Purrloin remains a Dark-type Purrloin. It can be useful as a collector variant and may also be evolved into Purified Liepard.

Should You Purify Shadow Purrloin?

  • Check the Shadow Pokémon's IVs first.
  • Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow variant.
  • Compare collector value before purifying.
  • Consider the evolution you want to build.
  • Do not purify automatically only for cosmetic reasons.

Lucky Purrloin

Lucky Purrloin is obtained through Pokémon GO's Lucky Trade mechanic. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for collectors and Pokémon intended for future investment.

🍀 LUCKY POKÉMON

Lucky Purrloin

Lucky Purrloin can be an interesting collector variant, especially when it has strong IVs or when the player plans to evolve it into Liepard.

100% Purrloin

A 100% Purrloin has perfect Individual Values of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

15 ATTACK IV
15 DEFENSE IV
15 HP IV
Perfect IV Combination: 15 / 15 / 15

A 100% Purrloin can be a desirable collector specimen. However, perfect IVs do not automatically mean that Purrloin is the strongest Pokémon for every battle format.

Should You Evolve a 100% Purrloin?

Whether to evolve a perfect Purrloin depends on your collection goals. Some players keep the unevolved 100% Pokémon, while others evolve it into a 100% Liepard.

XXL Purrloin

XXL Purrloin is an oversized Purrloin variant. XXL status is based on size and does not automatically change its battle stats or IVs.

XXL SIZE

XXL Purrloin

XXL Purrloin is primarily valuable for collectors, size records and Pokémon GO showcases where size may be relevant.

  • XXL does not automatically mean better IVs.
  • XXL status is separate from Shiny status.
  • An XXL Purrloin can potentially have other special attributes.
  • Size-based collection goals can make XXL Pokémon valuable.

XXS Purrloin

XXS Purrloin is an unusually small Purrloin variant. Like XXL Pokémon, the XXS classification is related to size rather than its IV percentage.

XXS SIZE

XXS Purrloin

XXS Purrloin can be a desirable collection variant for players interested in rare size classifications.

Size note:

XXL and XXS status should not be confused with Pokémon level, IV percentage or battle strength.

08 — IV GUIDE

Reshiram IV Guide

Reshiram's Individual Values determine additional Attack, Defense and HP points. A 15/15/15 Reshiram is considered a perfect IV specimen.

💯 Perfect Reshiram

A 100% Reshiram has maximum IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

Is 100% Reshiram Worth Keeping?

Absolutely. A 15/15/15 Reshiram is a perfect specimen and can be an excellent long-term investment for collectors and raid-focused players.

However, players should not automatically discard Reshiram with lower IVs. Moveset, level, available resources and the role you need can be more important than a small IV difference.

09 — PVP

Reshiram PvP Guide

Master League

Reshiram's high maximum CP makes Master League its most natural PvP environment. Its Dragon/Fire typing and charged-move pressure can make it useful against selected opponents.

Great League & Ultra League

Reshiram's high CP makes it much less natural for the lower CP-limited leagues. Always check the current league rules and permitted formats before investing specifically for PvP.

Reshiram FAQ

What type is Reshiram?

Reshiram is a Dragon/Fire-type Legendary Pokémon.

What is Reshiram weak to?

Reshiram is weak to Dragon, Ground and Rock-type attacks.

Is Reshiram good for raids?

Yes. Reshiram can be a powerful Fire-type raid attacker when equipped with an appropriate Fire-focused moveset.

What is the best Reshiram moveset?

Emolga Overview

Emolga is a dual Electric/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It does not evolve into another Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its relatively low maximum CP means it is not normally used as a top-tier raid attacker, but its typing and moveset can give it a place in Great League PvP.

Why Emolga's Typing Matters

Emolga's Electric/Flying combination gives it useful resistances against several common attack types. It also gives Emolga two important weaknesses: Rock and Ice. The Flying typing additionally means Ground-type attacks normally do not affect Emolga.

100% / Hundo Emolga

A 100% Emolga has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. At Level 50, a perfect Emolga can reach 1,847 CP. For Great League, however, a Hundo is not automatically the ideal IV spread because the CP limit can favor lower Attack and higher bulk combinations.

Emolga PvP Guide

Emolga's most interesting competitive role is in Great League, where its Electric/Flying typing and access to useful charged attacks can create specific matchup advantages.

Great League

Great League is the main format to consider when building an Emolga for PvP. Instead of automatically looking for 15/15/15 IVs, trainers should evaluate PvP-specific IV spreads that maximize stat product while remaining under the CP limit.

PvP IV reminder

A PvP-optimized Emolga may have significantly different IVs from a Hundo Emolga.

Ultra League & Master League

Emolga's relatively low maximum CP makes it much less natural for Ultra League and Master League than for Great League.

XXL Emolga

XXL Emolga is an extra-large size variant that can be particularly valuable for collectors and Pokédex completionists.

📏 XXL Emolga

XXL refers to the Pokémon's size classification, not its IV quality. An XXL Emolga can have excellent, average or poor IVs.

XXL Does Not Mean Hundo

Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Emolga is not automatically a 100% Emolga.

  • Keep an XXL Emolga for collection goals.
  • Check its IVs separately.
  • Do not transfer it simply because its CP is low.
  • Record XXL entries for size-based Pokédex goals.

XXS Emolga

XXS Emolga is the extra-small size variant and is another useful collection category for trainers building a complete size-based Pokédex.

🔬 XXS Emolga

XXS is a size classification. It does not directly determine Emolga's IVs, CP or battle performance.

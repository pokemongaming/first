Shelmet

Shelmet is a Bug-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as the pre-evolution of Accelgor and for its niche usefulness in Little Cup formats. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Shelmet.

01 • OVERVIEW

Shelmet Overview

Shelmet is a Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. Its Pokémon GO Pokédex number is #616 and it evolves into Accelgor. Unlike most evolutions, Shelmet and Karrablast have a special trade-related evolution mechanic.

Why Shelmet's Typing Matters

Shelmet's pure Bug typing gives it three major weaknesses: Fire, Flying and Rock. It also takes reduced damage from Fighting, Grass and Ground-type attacks.

07 • RAID PERFORMANCE

Is Shelmet Good for Raids?

Shelmet is not a practical raid attacker. Its low Attack stat and modest overall offensive output make it unsuitable for serious PvE teams. Its value is much more interesting in limited PvP formats.

Should You Power Up Shelmet for Raids?

Generally, no. Shelmet's low Attack makes it a poor investment for conventional raid attacking. If you are building a Shelmet, Little Cup or collection goals are much more appropriate reasons.

08 • PVP

Shelmet PvP Guide

Shelmet's most interesting Pokémon GO battle role is in Little Cup. Its low maximum CP and relatively bulky stat distribution make it much more relevant there than in Great, Ultra or Master League.

Little Cup

Shelmet can be a strong Little Cup option when the format allows it. Infestation provides fast energy generation while Body Slam gives Shelmet access to relatively frequent Charged Move pressure. Bug Buzz provides stronger Bug-type damage when Shelmet can reach it.

Little Cup Starting Point

Infestation + Body Slam + Bug Buzz

Great League

Shelmet's maximum CP of 942 means it cannot naturally reach the Great League's 1500 CP ceiling. It therefore has no meaningful standard Great League role.

Ultra League

Shelmet is not a practical Ultra League Pokémon because its maximum CP is far below the format's CP limit.

Master League

Shelmet is not suitable for Master League competition. Its CP and offensive stats are far too low for this format.

09 • EVOLUTION

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Shelmet

Lucky Shelmet is not a separate Pokémon form. It is Shelmet that has become Lucky through a trade. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for powering up and have a minimum IV floor.

LUCKY POKÉMON

Should You Power Up Lucky Shelmet?

Usually, Lucky Shelmet is more interesting as a collection or Little Cup project than as a raid investment. Its low Attack prevents it from becoming a serious conventional raid attacker.

14 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Shelmet

A 100% IV Shelmet has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a Hundo or perfect IV Shelmet.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
Perfect Shelmet

Is 100% Shelmet Useful?

A perfect Shelmet is valuable primarily for collectors and players interested in Little Cup or completing their perfect-IV Pokédex. It is not a worthwhile conventional raid attacker simply because it has perfect IVs.

15 • XXL

XXL Shelmet

XXL Shelmet is an exceptionally large size variant of Shelmet. XXL is a size attribute rather than a different species or permanent evolution.

XXL SHELMET Extra Large

XXL Shelmet can be kept as a collection specimen, used for size-related collection goals and displayed as an unusually large Shelmet.

Does XXL Shelmet Have Different Stats?

Size does not turn Shelmet into a different Pokémon species. XXL status is primarily a size classification and should not be confused with IV quality.

XXL vs IV A Pokémon can be XXL and have poor IVs, or XXL and simultaneously have 100% IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.
16 • XXS

XXS Shelmet

XXS Shelmet is an exceptionally small Shelmet. Like XXL, XXS is a size classification rather than a separate Pokémon form.

XXS SHELMET Extra Small

XXS Shelmet is especially useful for collectors trying to build a complete collection of unusual size records.

XXS Shelmet and IVs

XXS size and IV percentage are independent. A tiny Shelmet can have any IV combination, including 0% or 100%.

24 • FAQ

Shelmet FAQ

What type is Shelmet?

Shelmet is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Shelmet weak to?

Shelmet is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What does Shelmet evolve into?

Shelmet evolves into Accelgor.

How much Candy does Shelmet need to evolve?

Shelmet normally requires 200 Candy to evolve. Eligible trade evolution can remove the Candy requirement.

Is Shiny Shelmet available?

Yes. Shiny Shelmet is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Shelmet available?

Shadow Shelmet exists in Pokémon GO. Its current availability should be checked against the latest Team GO Rocket rotation.

Is Purified Shelmet different from regular Shelmet?

Purified Shelmet is a purified Shadow Pokémon. It remains Shelmet and retains its Bug typing.

What is Lucky Shelmet?

Lucky Shelmet is Shelmet that became Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is 100% IV Shelmet?

A 100% IV Shelmet has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shelmet's maximum CP?

Shelmet's maximum CP is 942 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shelmet good for raids?

No. Shelmet has very low Attack and is not a practical raid attacker.

Is Shelmet good in PvP?

Shelmet is most interesting in Little Cup. Its low maximum CP prevents it from being a practical Great, Ultra or Master League Pokémon.

Is XXL Shelmet different from normal Shelmet?

No. XXL is a size attribute rather than a different Pokémon species.

Is XXS Shelmet different from normal Shelmet?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not create a different Shelmet species.

Can a Shelmet be both Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. Shiny status and IVs are separate attributes, so a Shiny Shelmet can theoretically have a perfect 15/15/15 IV combination.

Can Shelmet be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Shelmet can also have perfect IVs.

Can Shelmet be both XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. XXS status does not determine the Pokémon's IV combination.

What is Shelmet's best moveset?

Infestation and Bug Buzz is the recommended general moveset in current Pokémon GO database data.

Can Shadow Shelmet be Shiny?

Current database data lists Shiny Shadow Shelmet as available. Availability and encounter mechanics can change, so check current Pokémon GO information.

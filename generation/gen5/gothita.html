Lucky Gothita

Lucky Gothita is obtained through Pokémon trading when the trade results in Lucky status.

🍀 Lucky Gothita

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than regular Pokémon, making Lucky Gothita potentially useful if you plan to evolve and invest in the Gothita family.

  • Lucky status is obtained through a Lucky Trade result.
  • Lucky Pokémon have special background effects.
  • Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust for power-ups.
  • A Lucky Gothita can still be evolved.

Gothita FAQ

What type is Gothita?

Why Tepig's Typing Matters

Tepig's pure Fire typing gives it the standard strengths and weaknesses associated with Fire-type Pokémon. Its Fire attacks are useful against Pokémon vulnerable to Fire damage, while Water, Ground and Rock attacks can threaten Tepig.

Should You Evolve Tepig?

If you are collecting the Tepig family, it is usually useful to keep several candidates before evolving. This lets you choose between high-IV specimens, Shiny Pokémon, Lucky Pokémon and other special variants.

  • Keep a high-IV Tepig for evolution.
  • Consider saving Shiny Tepig before evolving.
  • Compare CP and IVs before spending Candy.
  • Keep special Shadow or Lucky specimens when useful.
  • Save good candidates if an evolution-focused event may provide an exclusive move.
07 · IV GUIDE

100% IV Tepig

⭐ Perfect Tepig — 15/15/15

A 100% IV Tepig has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Perfect specimens are especially attractive for collectors and players who want to evolve a perfect member of the Tepig family.

Is 100% Tepig Worth Keeping?

Yes. Even though Tepig itself has modest battle stats, a perfect specimen can be valuable as an evolution candidate, collection piece or future investment.

08 · PVP

Tepig PvP Guide

Tepig is generally not the main competitive choice for standard high-level Pokémon GO PvP. Its low maximum CP and first-stage status make its evolved forms more relevant for battle-focused players.

Great League

Tepig's low maximum CP means that it can technically remain below the Great League CP limit, but its battle performance should be evaluated separately from the more powerful Pignite and Emboar.

Ultra League

Tepig is generally not a natural Ultra League investment. Its CP and stat profile make its evolved forms much more appropriate candidates for serious battle consideration.

Master League

Tepig is not designed for Master League competition. The format favors Pokémon with substantially higher overall combat potential.

09 · SHINY

Shiny Tepig Evolution

Shiny Tepig can evolve into Shiny Pignite and eventually Shiny Emboar. The Shiny status is retained throughout the evolutionary line.

✨ Shiny Collection Tip

If you have only one Shiny Tepig, consider checking its IVs and keeping it before evolving. A collector may prefer retaining a Shiny Tepig alongside its evolved forms.

10 · SHADOW

Shadow Tepig

Shadow Tepig is the Shadow variant of Tepig. Shadow Pokémon have different battle characteristics from their regular counterparts and require separate consideration when deciding whether to power up, evolve or purify them.

🌑🔥

Shadow Tepig

Shadow Tepig retains its Fire typing but has the Shadow battle modifier. It can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status.

Shadow Tepig IVs

A Shadow Tepig does not need to be 15/15/15 to be useful. Shadow Pokémon have their own investment considerations, so avoid automatically transferring a Shadow specimen solely because its IVs are imperfect.

⚠️ Check Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on special Team GO Rocket events and current game rules.

12 · LUCKY

Lucky Tepig

Lucky Tepig is a special trade outcome. Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor and require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

✨🍀

Lucky Tepig

A Lucky Tepig is especially useful for collectors and players who want to reduce Stardust costs when investing in a Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Tepig?

  • Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.
  • Lucky trades provide a useful way to obtain special Tepig specimens.
  • Lucky Tepig can be evolved while retaining its Lucky status.
  • A Lucky Tepig with strong IVs can be an attractive evolution candidate.

Lucky Tepig IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary traded Pokémon. The exact IVs of an individual Lucky Tepig should always be checked in-game.

100% Tepig CP

A perfect IV does not mean every Tepig will have the same CP. CP also depends on Pokémon level. A 100% Tepig at a low level can therefore have a much lower CP than the species' maximum CP.

⭐ Hundo Tepig

"Hundo" is a common community term for a Pokémon with perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

14 · XXL TEPIG

XXL Tepig

XXL Tepig is a size variant that is larger than ordinary Tepig. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Tepig can have any combination of IVs.

📏🔥

XXL Tepig

A large-size Tepig identified by its size category. XXL status is independent of whether the Tepig is Shiny, Lucky, Shadow or perfect IV.

Does XXL Affect Battle Stats?

XXL status should not be treated as an automatic improvement to Attack, Defense or HP. It is primarily a size-related characteristic rather than an IV stat.

Should You Keep XXL Tepig?

XXL Tepig can be worth keeping for collectors, especially if it also has another desirable characteristic such as Shiny status, strong IVs or Lucky status.

15 · XXS TEPIG

23 · FAQ

Tepig FAQ

What type is Tepig?

Tepig is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Tepig weak against?

Tepig is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What generation is Tepig from?

Tepig was introduced in Generation 5 and is part of the Unova Pokémon family.

What does Tepig evolve into?

Tepig evolves into Pignite and then Emboar.

How much Candy does Tepig need to evolve?

Tepig requires 25 Candy to evolve into Pignite, while Pignite requires 100 Candy to evolve into Emboar.

Is Shiny Tepig available?

Shiny Tepig is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

Is Shadow Tepig available?

Shadow Pokémon availability can change with Team GO Rocket rotations and events. Check the current in-game availability before hunting for one.

What is a Purified Tepig?

Purified Tepig is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Tepig.

What is a Lucky Tepig?

Lucky Tepig is a Tepig obtained through a Lucky Trade or other eligible Lucky Pokémon mechanic. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Tepig?

A 100% Tepig has perfect 15/15/15 IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

What is XXL Tepig?

XXL Tepig is a very large size variant of Tepig. Its size is separate from its IVs.

What is XXS Tepig?

XXS Tepig is a very small size variant. Like XXL, XXS status is independent of IV quality.

Is Tepig good for raids?

Tepig itself is generally not a major raid attacker. Its primary value is as the first stage of the Tepig evolutionary family.

Should I evolve a 100% Tepig?

If you want a perfect member of the Tepig family, evolving a 100% Tepig is a strong collection choice. Check the current evolution moves before spending resources.

Can Shiny Tepig be evolved?

Yes. Shiny Tepig retains its Shiny status when evolved into Shiny Pignite and Shiny Emboar.

Can Lucky Tepig be evolved?

Yes. Lucky status is retained when Tepig evolves.

Can Shadow Tepig be evolved?

Yes. Shadow Tepig can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status, subject to the current game's evolution rules.

Should I purify Shadow Tepig?

Not automatically. Consider the Shadow battle characteristics, IVs, resources and your collection goals before purifying.

Can XXL Tepig also be Shiny?

Size and Shiny status are separate characteristics, so a Tepig can have multiple desirable attributes.

Can XXS Tepig have perfect IVs?

Yes. XXS size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXS Tepig can also have 15/15/15 IVs.

Minccino Overview

Minccino is a small Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves into Cinccino and is more commonly encountered as a collection, evolution and event Pokémon than as a top-tier battle attacker.

Should You Purify Shadow Minccino?

Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically simply because purification improves its appraisal values. Consider your collection goals, evolution plans and the Shadow mechanics before making the decision.

Shadow Minccino Tip

If you want to keep a Shadow collection, consider saving notable IV, Shiny or other special specimens before deciding whether to purify them.

Lucky Minccino

Minccino PvP Guide

Minccino's relatively low stats and maximum CP make it an uncommon choice for high-level Pokémon GO PvP. Its evolution, Cinccino, is generally the more relevant form to consider when evaluating battle potential.

Great League

A low-CP Minccino can technically fit under the Great League CP limit, but that does not mean it is a strong competitive option. Check current PvP rankings and movesets before investing resources.

Ultra League

Minccino's low maximum CP makes Ultra League investment generally impractical.

Master League

Minccino is not a realistic Master League investment because its maximum CP and stats are far below the requirements of the format.

Minccino FAQ

What type is Minccino?

Minccino is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Minccino weak to?

Minccino is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What does Minccino evolve into?

Minccino evolves into Cinccino.

Is Shiny Minccino available?

Shiny Minccino is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny form is active in the game.

What is a 100% Minccino?

A 100% Minccino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Shadow Minccino?

Shadow Minccino is the Shadow form of Minccino and has the special Shadow battle mechanics.

What is Purified Minccino?

Purified Minccino is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Minccino.

What is Lucky Minccino?

Lucky Minccino is a Minccino that has obtained the Lucky status through Pokémon GO trading mechanics.

What is XXL Minccino?

XXL Minccino is a Minccino whose size qualifies it for the XXL category.

What is XXS Minccino?

XXS Minccino is a Minccino whose size qualifies it for the XXS category.

Is Minccino good for raids?

Minccino is not generally considered a strong raid attacker because of its relatively low stats and CP.

Is Minccino good in Master League?

No. Its low maximum CP and overall stats make Minccino unsuitable for serious Master League investment.

Should I evolve my 100% Minccino?

If your goal is a perfect-IV evolution collection, evolving a 100% Minccino into Cinccino is a reasonable choice. Keeping it unevolved is also valid if you want a perfect Minccino in your collection.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Minccino?

Yes, especially if you are building a size-focused collection or looking for unusual trade candidates.

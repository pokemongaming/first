Simisage

Simisage Overview

11 • PVP

Simisage PvP Guide

Simisage can be used in GO Battle League, although its performance depends heavily on league, IV spread, moveset and the current meta.

Great League

A low-Attack/high-Defense IV spread can sometimes be preferred for CP-limited formats. Simisage's availability and matchup profile should be considered before investing.

Ultra League

Simisage can reach the Ultra League CP range, but it is generally a niche choice rather than a universal meta pick. XL Candy may be necessary for an optimized build.

Master League

With a Level 50 maximum CP of only 2,657, Simisage is significantly below the CP ceiling of many Master League Pokémon. It is therefore a specialist rather than a standard Master League investment.

PvP rankings change as moves, Pokémon and battle mechanics change. Treat current rankings as time-sensitive information.

13 • SHADOW STATUS

Shadow Simisage

Shadow Pokémon are special variants created through Team GO Rocket encounters. However, Shadow Simisage should not be presented as a currently obtainable official form unless Pokémon GO introduces it through the Shadow system.

Current Database Status

Shadow Simisage is not currently listed as an available separate Pokémon GO form in the current reference data. If Shadow Pansage becomes available and can be evolved, this section should be updated accordingly.

If Shadow Simisage Is Introduced

A Shadow Simisage would retain the Grass typing of Simisage. Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive bonus while also taking more damage from attacks. They also require additional Candy and Stardust for powering up and second moves.

16 • XXL

XXL Simisage

XXL Simisage is a size variant. It does not have different base Attack, Defense or HP stats simply because it is XXL. Its primary value is collection, visual size and applicable Pokémon GO size-related mechanics.

XXL

Large Simisage

An XXL Simisage is substantially larger than a standard specimen. Keep one if you are collecting size variants or working toward size-related achievements.

Should You Keep an XXL Simisage?

  • Keep one for size collection purposes.
  • Check its appraisal before transferring duplicates.
  • An XXL Pokémon can be useful for size-related collection goals.
  • XXL status does not automatically mean better battle IVs.
17 • XXS

XXS Simisage

XXS Simisage is the opposite size extreme. It is a particularly small specimen and can be valuable to players who collect size variants.

XXS

Tiny Simisage

An XXS Simisage is smaller than the standard size range. Like XXL status, XXS is a size attribute rather than a different Pokémon species.

XXS Simisage Collection Value

For collectors, finding both XXL and XXS Simisage can be more interesting than simply keeping multiple standard-size copies. The size designation should be evaluated separately from IV quality.

Simisage Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Simisage FAQ

What type is Simisage?

Simisage is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

How Boldore Evolves

Boldore can evolve into Gigalith using Pokémon GO's evolution mechanics. A traded Boldore can also be eligible for a Candy-free evolution, making trading an important option for players who want to complete the Roggenrola family efficiently.

Shiny Boldore

Shiny Boldore has an alternate coloration compared with the standard form. Shiny status does not give Boldore additional combat stats.

Collector tip:

If you are collecting Shiny Pokémon, consider keeping a Shiny Boldore before evolving it, especially if you want one entry for every stage of the family.

Shadow Boldore

Shadow Boldore

Should You Keep Shadow Boldore?

  • Keep strong Shadow specimens before automatically transferring them.
  • Check the current moveset before investing resources.
  • Consider whether you plan to evolve it into Shadow Gigalith.
  • Do not purify solely because the IVs look better afterward.
Frustration note:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket events and rules.

Purified Boldore

Purified Boldore is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Boldore. Purification changes its status and can alter its IVs and resource requirements.

Purified Boldore

Purified Boldore can be useful for collection, evolution and Lucky/Purified-focused collections. Whether purification is worthwhile depends on the individual Pokémon and your goals.

Lucky Boldore

Lucky Boldore is a special Pokémon obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system. Lucky Pokémon have special visual characteristics and reduced Stardust requirements for powering up.

✨ Lucky Boldore

A Lucky Boldore is especially attractive to collectors and players who want to evolve and power up a Lucky Gigalith.

Why Keep a Lucky Boldore?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Lucky status remains when Boldore evolves.
  • A Lucky Boldore can become a Lucky Gigalith.
  • Trading can be useful when completing the evolution family.

XXL Boldore

XXL Boldore is a size-variant Boldore that is larger than typical specimens. Size does not automatically increase its Attack, Defense or HP battle stats.

XXL Boldore Extra-large size variant

Useful for collectors, showcases and completing size-based Pokémon collections.

Battle Stats Not automatically increased

XXL status should not be interpreted as a combat stat bonus.

Collection tip:

If you participate in Pokémon GO Showcases or collect size variants, an XXL Boldore can be worth keeping even when its IVs are not perfect.

XXS Boldore

XXS Boldore is a smaller-than-usual size variant. Like XXL status, XXS is primarily a size and collection attribute rather than a direct combat-stat upgrade.

XXS Boldore Extra-small size variant

An interesting collectible for players completing size-based Pokédex collections.

Combat Impact No automatic battle bonus

XXS status does not by itself make Boldore stronger or weaker in battle.

Boldore FAQ

What type is Boldore?

Boldore is a pure Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Boldore weak to?

Boldore is weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks.

What Pokémon does Boldore evolve into?

Boldore evolves into Gigalith.

What Pokémon evolves into Boldore?

Roggenrola evolves into Boldore.

Can Boldore evolve through trading?

Yes. A traded Boldore can qualify for a Candy-free evolution into Gigalith under Pokémon GO's trade evolution mechanic.

Is Shiny Boldore available?

Shiny Boldore can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny species is active.

Can Shadow Boldore evolve?

An eligible Shadow Boldore can be evolved while retaining its Shadow status.

Should I purify Shadow Boldore?

Not automatically. Consider your collection goals, the Shadow bonus and the Pokémon's IVs before purifying it.

What is a Lucky Boldore?

Lucky Boldore is a Boldore obtained through trading that has the Lucky status and benefits from reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

What is a 100% Boldore?

A 100% Boldore has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Boldore?

XXL Boldore is an extra-large size variant. Its size does not automatically provide higher battle stats.

What is XXS Boldore?

XXS Boldore is an extra-small size variant. Its size does not automatically provide a combat-stat advantage.

Is Boldore good for raids?

Boldore is generally more useful as an evolution-stage Pokémon. Players interested in Rock-type PvE performance should also evaluate Gigalith and other available Rock attackers.

Is Boldore good in PvP?

Boldore is more of a niche PvP option. Its usefulness depends on the league, moveset and current metagame.

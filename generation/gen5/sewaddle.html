Sewaddle Forms & Special Variants

Sewaddle can exist as several collectible variants in Pokémon GO. Some variants affect appearance or collection value, while others affect battle mechanics or resource costs.

🐛

Regular Sewaddle

The standard Sewaddle form. It has normal stats, typing and evolution potential.

✨

Shiny Sewaddle

Shiny Sewaddle has alternate coloration while retaining the same underlying battle mechanics as the regular form.

🌑

Shadow Sewaddle

Shadow Sewaddle is a special Team GO Rocket variant with a different battle profile and higher resource requirements.

✨

Purified Sewaddle

A Shadow Sewaddle that has been purified. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect its investment characteristics.

Lucky Sewaddle

Lucky Sewaddle is obtained through Lucky Trades and requires less Stardust to power up.

100% Sewaddle

A 100% Sewaddle has perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

⬆️

XXL Sewaddle

XXL Sewaddle is an oversized specimen with increased height and weight values for collection and Showcase-related purposes.

⬇️

XXS Sewaddle

Regular Ducklett

Shiny Ducklett

Shiny Ducklett has alternate coloration compared with regular Ducklett and is primarily valuable as a collector variant.

✨ Shiny Ducklett

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not directly increase Attack, Defense, HP or battle damage.

Should You Evolve Shiny Ducklett?

Many collectors keep one Shiny Ducklett unevolved and evolve another into Shiny Swanna if they have multiple specimens available.

Shadow Ducklett

Shadow Ducklett is a Shadow version of Ducklett. Its Shadow status changes its battle profile and makes it a separate collection and investment decision.

🌑 Shadow Ducklett

Shadow Pokémon generally provide increased offensive pressure while sacrificing defensive durability.

Shadow Ducklett Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Ducklett's Water and Flying typing. Its type weaknesses therefore remain associated with the standard Ducklett typing.

XXL Ducklett

XXL Ducklett is a size-based variant that is larger than standard Ducklett.

📏 XXL Ducklett

XXL status is primarily a size and collection characteristic rather than a direct increase to battle stats.

  • Larger than standard Ducklett.
  • Useful for size-based collections.
  • Can be valuable for showcase participation where eligible.
  • Does not automatically mean better IVs.

XXS Ducklett

XXS Ducklett is an unusually small size variant of Ducklett.

🔍 XXS Ducklett

XXS status is a collectible size characteristic. It does not automatically indicate better battle performance.

  • Smaller than standard Ducklett.
  • Rare size-based collector variant.
  • Separate from IV quality.
  • Can be kept alongside XXL and regular versions.

Ducklett FAQ

What type is Ducklett?

Ducklett is a Water and Flying-type Pokémon.

What does Ducklett evolve into?

Is Shiny Ducklett available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and event availability.

What is Ducklett weak to?

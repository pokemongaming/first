Shiny Terrakion

Shiny Terrakion is the alternate-color version of Terrakion. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not automatically improve a Pokémon's stats, IVs or battle performance.

Shiny Terrakion

A Shiny Terrakion can have any IV combination, meaning collectors may want to evaluate both its appearance and battle potential.

  • Shiny status does not guarantee high IVs.
  • A Shiny Terrakion can still be powered up for raids.
  • A Shiny Legendary is generally a valuable collector item.

Shadow Terrakion

Shadow Terrakion should be evaluated separately from regular Terrakion because Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally gain greater offensive pressure in exchange for reduced durability.

Shadow Terrakion Investment

  • Evaluate its IVs without focusing only on perfection.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare the higher resource cost before powering up.
  • Compare Shadow performance with your regular Terrakion.
  • Avoid purifying automatically.

Purified Terrakion

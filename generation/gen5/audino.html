Audino

Audino is a Normal-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 5. In Pokémon GO, Audino is best known as a defensive-oriented Pokémon with an unusually high HP-focused profile and several collectible variants, including Shiny, Lucky, XXL, XXS and perfect-IV specimens.

Why Audino's Typing Matters

Audino's Normal typing gives it a straightforward defensive matchup profile. Its most important weakness is Fighting-type damage, while Ghost-type attacks do not normally damage it.

Shadow Audino

Shadow Audino Availability

Shadow Pokémon availability is controlled by Team GO Rocket content and Niantic's current Pokémon GO rotations. Do not assume that a species has a permanently available Shadow encounter simply because the Shadow concept exists in the game.

Availability note If Shadow Audino is not currently available in Pokémon GO, there is no legitimate way to obtain a new Shadow Audino until the game introduces or rotates the relevant encounter.

If Shadow Audino Becomes Available

Evaluate it separately from regular Audino because Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile. Do not automatically purify a Shadow Pokémon before deciding whether you want to preserve its Shadow characteristics.

11 • PURIFIED

Purified Audino

Purified Audino

A Purified Audino would require Audino to first be obtainable as a Shadow Pokémon. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can affect its IVs and other game mechanics.

Keep this section dynamically connected to current Pokémon GO Shadow availability. If Audino is not obtainable as a Shadow Pokémon, a player cannot newly create a Purified Audino.
12 • LUCKY

Lucky Audino

🍀

Lucky Audino

Lucky Audino is created through Pokémon trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and require less Stardust to power up than ordinary Pokémon.

Why Keep a Lucky Audino?

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Useful for collectors who want rare Pokémon combinations.
  • Can be combined with other attributes such as Shiny status.
  • Check IVs after the trade before deciding whether to invest.
13 • PERFECT IV

100% IV Audino

A 100% IV Audino has the maximum possible individual values: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

Perfect Audino

A 15/15/15 Audino is considered a perfect-IV specimen. Its value is primarily determined by your collection, PvP or personal investment goals rather than raid performance alone.

How to Identify 100% Audino

Use Pokémon GO's appraisal system or an IV calculator to determine whether your Audino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

14 • SIZE

XXL & XXS Audino

Pokémon GO assigns size classifications to individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS are attributes of an individual Pokémon rather than separate Audino forms.

📏 XXL Audino An unusually large Audino with the XXL size classification. Keep one if you are interested in size-based collection goals.
🔬 XXS Audino An unusually small Audino with the XXS size classification.

Should You Transfer XXL or XXS Audino?

If you are a collector, it is usually worth keeping at least one XXL and one XXS example. Size can be useful for collection goals and special showcases, depending on the features currently available in Pokémon GO.

21 • FAQ

Audino FAQ

What type is Audino?

Audino is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Audino weak to?

Audino is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Audino immune to Ghost?

Yes. Normal-type Pokémon are immune to Ghost-type attacks in Pokémon GO.

Does Audino evolve?

Audino does not have a normal evolution in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Audino available?

Shiny Audino can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters. Always check the current encounter method.

What is a 100% Audino?

A 100% Audino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Audino be Lucky?

Yes. Audino can become Lucky through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics when eligible.

What is an XXL Audino?

XXL Audino is an individual Audino with the XXL size classification.

What is an XXS Audino?

XXS Audino is an individual Audino with the XXS size classification.

Is Shadow Audino available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket content. It should not be presented as permanently available unless the current game data confirms it.

Can Audino be Purified?

Purification requires a Shadow Pokémon. Therefore, a Purified Audino would depend on Audino being available as a Shadow Pokémon.

Is Audino good for raids?

Audino is generally not considered a priority offensive raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack stat.

Is Audino good in PvP?

Audino can be evaluated in CP-limited formats, but its usefulness depends heavily on its current moveset and the PvP metagame.

Can Shiny Audino be Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can have both attributes.

Can a 100% Audino be Lucky?

A traded Pokémon's IVs are rerolled during the trade, so a Lucky Pokémon can potentially have perfect IVs after the trade.

09 • PVP

Litwick PvP Guide

Litwick is not a standard Great League or Ultra League choice because of its low overall stats. However, it can become relevant in Little Cup-style formats when eligible.

Little Cup

Little Cup restrictions can make unevolved Pokémon such as Litwick more interesting than they are in standard leagues. Its Ghost and Fire typing can create useful matchup opportunities, although the exact meta depends on the current cup rules.

Great League

Litwick's low overall stats generally make it a poor choice for unrestricted Great League battles.

Master League

Litwick is not appropriate for Master League because its CP ceiling and stats are far below the requirements of that format.

Shadow Litwick for Evolution

A Shadow Litwick can be evolved into Shadow Lampent and eventually Shadow Chandelure. This can make a good Shadow Litwick more valuable as a long-term evolution project than as a standalone battler.

Long-Term Shadow Strategy

Keep a strong Shadow Litwick if your goal is to build a Shadow Chandelure.

Frustration Warning

Important:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. Removing Frustration normally requires a Team GO Rocket event that permits the change.

Should You Purify Shadow Litwick?

Do not purify automatically. If your goal is a Shadow Chandelure, keeping the Shadow status can be valuable. Purification is permanent, so compare the long-term benefits before making the decision.

11 • EVOLUTION

Litwick Evolution Family

Litwick evolves into Lampent and then Chandelure. This three-stage evolution family is one of the main reasons players collect large numbers of Litwick Candy.

Litwick #607
→
Lampent #608

Litwick → Lampent

Litwick evolves into Lampent using Pokémon GO's required Candy cost. Check the current in-game evolution screen for the exact requirement before planning a mass evolution.

Lampent → Chandelure

Lampent evolves into Chandelure using additional Litwick Candy and, depending on the current game mechanics, may require an Unova Stone.

Evolution advice:

Save enough Litwick Candy before evolving. During Candy-focused events, it can be much more efficient to build the resources needed for several evolutions.

16 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Litwick

For New Players

  • Catch as many Litwick as practical during events.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for evolution.
  • Check every Shadow Litwick before transferring it.
  • Save Shiny Litwick for your collection.
  • Build Candy before evolving multiple specimens.

For Raid Players

The primary objective should be evolving strong Litwick into Chandelure rather than investing heavily in Litwick itself. Chandelure can provide substantially more combat value.

For PvP Players

If a Litwick is being considered for a limited cup, check the exact CP restrictions and current meta before investing. IV preferences can differ considerably between limited formats and standard evolution use.

18 • INVESTMENT

Is Litwick Worth Investing In?

For Evolution

Yes. Litwick is worth collecting because its final evolution, Chandelure, can be considerably more useful in battle.

For Raids

Do not normally spend significant Stardust powering up Litwick itself. Evolve a strong specimen first and then evaluate the resulting Chandelure.

For Collectors

Shiny and Shadow Litwick can both be worthwhile collection pieces, especially if you want to complete the entire evolution family.

Best Investment Rule

Catch first, appraise second, evolve third, and power up only after deciding which final role the Pokémon will fill.

Why Chandelure's Typing Matters

08 • IV GUIDE

Chandelure IV Guide

Raid IV Priorities

  • Prioritize high Attack for raid damage.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for long-term investment.
  • Do not transfer a useful Chandelure simply because it is not a hundo.

IV note:

A high-level Chandelure with slightly imperfect IVs can be more immediately useful than a perfect specimen that requires a large Stardust investment.

09 • PVP

Chandelure PvP Guide

Chandelure can appear in PvP discussions because of its unusual typing and offensive pressure. However, its PvP value depends heavily on the current moveset, format and metagame.

Ultra League

Chandelure can be considered for specialized Ultra League teams where its Ghost and Fire typing provides useful matchup coverage.

Master League

Chandelure's CP ceiling allows it to participate in Master League, although its practical value depends on the current meta and available moves.

Great League

Chandelure is not naturally optimized for the low CP environment of Great League and is generally better suited to higher leagues or PvE.

PvP Investment

Before spending XL Candy or large amounts of Stardust, check the current PvP moveset and matchup environment.

13 • EVOLUTION FAMILY

Chandelure Evolution Line

Chandelure is the final evolution in the Litwick family. Unlike Kyogre, Chandelure has a conventional three-stage evolution line.

Litwick #607
→
Lampent #608
→
Chandelure #609

Evolution Requirements

  • Litwick evolves into Lampent using Candy.
  • Lampent evolves into Chandelure using a Unova Stone and Candy.
  • Chandelure is the final standard evolution.
  • Shiny Pokémon retain their Shiny identity after evolution.
  • Shadow status can be retained when a Shadow Pokémon evolves.
Evolution tip:

If you have a strong Shadow or Shiny Litwick, consider the long-term evolution path before spending Candy and other resources.

18 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Chandelure

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Ghost or Fire.
  • Choose the appropriate Chandelure moveset.
  • Check Chandelure's level and IVs.
  • Consider current weather.
  • Compare Chandelure with other Ghost and Fire attackers.
  • Consider Mega or team-wide bonuses available in the current raid.

Ghost Attacker Strategy

Use a Ghost-focused Chandelure when the raid boss is weak to Ghost attacks. Hex and Shadow Ball provide a straightforward Ghost-oriented build.

Fire Attacker Strategy

Use Fire Spin and Overheat when Chandelure is being used specifically as a Fire attacker against suitable targets.

Long-Term Investment

Chandelure is particularly attractive because the same species can be built for two different PvE roles. Players with limited resources can therefore choose the role that fills the biggest gap in their roster.

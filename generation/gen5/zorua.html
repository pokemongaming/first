01 • OVERVIEW

Zorua Pokémon GO Overview

Zorua is a Generation 5 Dark-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is especially memorable because of its illusion-style encounter mechanic: Zorua can appear disguised as another Pokémon until the encounter is completed.

Zorua's low defensive stats and relatively low CP ceiling make it a niche choice rather than a standard Great League recommendation. Specialized cups or limited formats can change the value of individual Pokémon, so current rules should always be checked.

Is a 100% Zorua Worth Keeping?

Absolutely. Even if Zorua itself is not a major raid attacker, a perfect specimen is valuable for collectors and can be evolved into a perfect Zoroark.

19 • SPECIAL MECHANIC

Zorua Disguise Mechanic

One of the most unusual features of Zorua in Pokémon GO is its disguise mechanic. Zorua can appear on the map disguised as another Pokémon associated with the player's collection or encounter context.

Watch the Catch Screen

If an apparently ordinary Pokémon suddenly reveals itself as Zorua after the encounter begins, the disguise mechanic has been triggered.

Why Zorua's Disguise Is Important

  • Zorua may not look like Zorua on the overworld map.
  • Catching disguised Pokémon can reveal Zorua.
  • The mechanic makes Zorua encounters more memorable.
  • A disguised encounter can still be worth checking carefully for IVs and Shiny status.

Zorua Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Zorua?

Regular Zorua is a pure Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Zorua weak against?

Zorua is weak to Fighting, Bug and Fairy-type attacks.

What is Zorua's max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Zorua can reach 1,329 CP.

What are Zorua's base stats?

Zorua has 153 Attack, 78 Defense and 120 HP.

Is Shiny Zorua available?

Yes. Shiny Zorua is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Zorua available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and encounter rotations.

Can Zorua be purified?

An eligible Shadow Zorua can be purified according to the normal Shadow Pokémon mechanics.

What is a Lucky Zorua?

Lucky Zorua is a Zorua that has obtained the Lucky status through Pokémon trading.

A 100% Zorua has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

Is Zorua worth keeping?

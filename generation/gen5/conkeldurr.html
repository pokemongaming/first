01 — OVERVIEW

Conkeldurr Overview

Conkeldurr is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is the final stage of the Timburr evolutionary family and is known for its high Attack and strong Fighting-type offensive role.

Conkeldurr Type

🥊

Why Conkeldurr's Typing Matters

Conkeldurr's pure Fighting typing gives it a clear offensive role. Fighting-type attacks are especially valuable against Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark opponents.

Its typing also determines which attacks deal super-effective damage against Conkeldurr and which attacks it can resist.

XXL & XXS Conkeldurr

XXL and XXS describe size characteristics rather than separate Pokémon species. A Conkeldurr can therefore be regular, Shiny, Lucky or another eligible status while also having an XXL or XXS size designation.

🏆 XXL Conkeldurr

XXL Conkeldurr is an exceptionally large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

XXL status is primarily relevant to collection, showcases and size-based gameplay features rather than directly increasing the Pokémon's combat stats.

📏 XXS Conkeldurr

XXS Conkeldurr is an exceptionally small specimen according to Pokémon GO's size system.

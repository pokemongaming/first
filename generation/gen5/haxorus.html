Shiny Haxorus

Shiny Haxorus is an alternate-colour version of Haxorus. Its battle stats remain the same as regular Haxorus.

✨ Shiny Haxorus

Shiny status changes appearance only. A Shiny Haxorus can have any IV combination and can be powered up and used in battle like a regular Haxorus.

```
```

Shadow Haxorus

Shadow Haxorus has a different battle profile from regular Haxorus because Shadow Pokémon trade defensive durability for increased offensive pressure.

🌑 Shadow Haxorus

Shadow Haxorus can be particularly interesting for players focused on damage output. Shadow investment should be considered separately because of higher resource requirements.

Shadow Haxorus Investment

  • Consider its IVs, but do not automatically discard a useful Shadow specimen.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Compare Shadow Haxorus with your existing Dragon attackers.
  • Remember that Shadow power-ups require additional resources.
  • Avoid purifying automatically only for higher IVs.
```
```

Purified Haxorus

Purified Haxorus is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Haxorus. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon into its Purified form.

💠 Purified Haxorus

A Purified Haxorus should be evaluated based on your long-term goals. Purification can improve IVs, but it also removes the Shadow battle characteristics.

Purification decision:

Compare the Shadow version and Purified result before making a permanent decision.

Lucky Haxorus is a special Lucky Pokémon obtained through eligible Pokémon trades.

🍀 Lucky Haxorus

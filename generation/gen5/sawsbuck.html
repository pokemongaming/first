Sawsbuck Type

Sawsbuck has the dual typing Normal and Grass. This combination gives it a useful set of resistances but also creates several important weaknesses.

🌿

Why Sawsbuck's Typing Matters

The Grass component gives Sawsbuck access to useful resistances against Water, Ground, Electric and Grass attacks, while its Normal component adds a strong resistance to Ghost attacks. The trade-off is a broad weakness profile against Bug, Fighting, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison.

Lucky Sawsbuck

Lucky Sawsbuck is not a separate species or form. A Sawsbuck can become Lucky when obtained through a Lucky Trade or other applicable Lucky Pokémon mechanic.

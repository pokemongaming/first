Braviary PvP Guide

Braviary can be used in PvP formats where its typing, attack pressure and charged move coverage fit the current metagame.

Great League

Braviary can be difficult to optimize under the Great League CP limit and should be evaluated carefully before building one specifically for this format.

Ultra League

Ultra League can provide Braviary with more room to use its stats, although its weaknesses remain important when building a team around it.

Master League

Braviary can reach a relatively high CP but generally faces competition from stronger legendary and specialized Flying attackers.

Shiny Braviary

Shiny Braviary has an alternate appearance compared with the standard form. Shiny status is primarily a collector feature and does not automatically increase battle stats.

✨ Shiny Braviary Guide

A Shiny Braviary can have any IV combination and can still be powered up for battle. A Shiny Pokémon should therefore be evaluated separately for its appearance and IV quality.

Shadow Braviary

Shadow Braviary is the Shadow version of Braviary. Shadow status changes its battle characteristics compared with a regular specimen.

🌑 Shadow Battle Profile

Shadow Pokémon generally provide increased offensive pressure while receiving reduced defensive durability. This makes Shadow Braviary a different investment decision from regular Braviary.

Purified Braviary

Purified Braviary is created when an eligible Shadow Braviary is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow status to its Purified form.

💠 Purified Braviary

Before purifying, compare the potential IV increase, resource cost and loss of Shadow battle characteristics with your long-term goal.

  • Check the Shadow Braviary's IVs.
  • Consider whether you want Shadow raid damage.
  • Compare the purified result before committing.
  • Do not purify automatically just for cosmetic reasons.

Braviary FAQ

What type is Braviary?

Braviary is a Normal and Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Braviary weak to?

Braviary is weak to Electric, Ice and Rock attacks.

What does Rufflet evolve into?

Rufflet evolves into Braviary.

Is Shiny Braviary different in battle?

Shiny status primarily changes appearance. Battle performance depends on IVs, level and moveset.

Is Shadow Braviary stronger?

Shadow status changes Braviary's battle profile, increasing offensive pressure while reducing durability.

Should I purify Shadow Braviary?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version with your intended Purified result.

Is Lucky Braviary good?

Lucky Braviary can be useful because it costs less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Braviary?

A 100% Braviary has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Braviary have higher battle stats?

XXL size is primarily a size and collection variation rather than a direct battle-stat increase.

Does XXS Braviary have lower battle stats?

Size variation does not normally mean lower core battle stats.

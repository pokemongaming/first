Why Zekrom's Typing Matters

PURIFIED STATUS

Purified Zekrom

Purified Zekrom represents the purified state of a Shadow Zekrom. Purification changes the Pokémon's status, appraisal and resource requirements and removes Frustration.

PURIFIED

Purified Zekrom

Purified Zekrom should be evaluated separately from both regular and Shadow Zekrom because purification changes several mechanics.

What Happens When Zekrom Is Purified?

  • Shadow status is removed.
  • Frustration is replaced by Return.
  • The appraisal improves.
  • Power-up and other resource requirements are reduced.
  • The Shadow damage bonus is no longer retained.

Purified Zekrom vs Shadow Zekrom

Purification is not automatically an upgrade. If the Shadow version is valuable for your raid strategy, compare both versions before purifying.
🍀 TRADE STATUS

Lucky Zekrom

Lucky Zekrom is a Zekrom that became Lucky through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and receive a special visual effect.

Lucky Zekrom

A strong Lucky Zekrom can be especially attractive for trainers who want to take a Legendary to a high level while reducing Stardust expenditure.

How to Get Lucky Zekrom

Zekrom can become Lucky when it is traded. Lucky Friends guarantee that the next trade between those Lucky Friends produces Lucky Pokémon.

GO BATTLE LEAGUE

Zekrom PvP Guide

Zekrom's high CP ceiling and Dragon / Electric typing make it particularly relevant to Master League battles.

Master League

Zekrom can be a strong Master League Pokémon because it combines high Attack, useful bulk and powerful charged-move pressure. Its Electric typing is useful against several Water- and Flying-type targets, while Dragon Breath and Dragon coverage provide additional pressure.

Great League

Zekrom's high CP makes it an unnatural fit for standard Great League play.

Ultra League

Zekrom is also difficult to optimize for the standard Ultra League CP cap compared with Pokémon naturally suited to that format.

Master League Starting Point

Dragon Breath + Fusion Bolt + Crunch

INVESTMENT

Zekrom Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

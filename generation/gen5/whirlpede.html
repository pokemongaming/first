Whirlipede

Whirlipede is a Bug and Poison-type Pokémon from Generation 5. In Pokémon GO, it is the middle stage of the Venipede evolution family and can eventually evolve into Scolipede. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Whirlipede.

01 · DATABASE

Whirlipede Overview

Whirlipede is the second stage of the Venipede evolution family. It evolves from Venipede and can later evolve into Scolipede. Its Bug / Poison typing gives it a combination of offensive and defensive matchups that differ from many other Bug Pokémon.

The Bug / Poison combination gives Whirlipede useful resistances, including resistance to Fighting, Poison, Bug, Grass and Fairy attacks. It is also vulnerable to several common attacking types.

11 · SHADOW

Shadow Whirlipede

Shadow Whirlipede is the Shadow version of the species. Shadow Pokémon have a distinct battle profile and are separate investment choices from their regular counterparts.

🌑
Shadow Whirlipede

A Shadow variant with the same Bug / Poison typing.

⚔️
Shadow Bonus

Shadow Pokémon receive increased offensive pressure while taking more damage.

💰

Shadow Whirlipede Weaknesses

Shadow status does not change Whirlipede's Bug / Poison typing. Its primary type weaknesses therefore remain Fire, Flying, Psychic and Rock.

Shadow Whirlipede & Frustration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. The ability to replace Frustration depends on applicable Team GO Rocket events and current game mechanics.

Purification warning

Do not purify a Shadow Whirlipede automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first, especially if the Pokémon has collection value or strong IVs.

12 · PURIFIED

Purified Whirlipede

Purified Whirlipede is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Whirlipede. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and can alter its IVs.

🌟
Purified Status

The Pokémon no longer has Shadow status after purification.

⬆️
IV Increase

Purification can increase each IV by 2, up to a maximum of 15.

💎
Special Value

Purified Pokémon can be useful for collectors and certain evolution projects.

Should You Purify Whirlipede?

There is no universal answer. If you value the Shadow version, keeping it Shadow may be preferable. If you want a Purified specimen or the purification bonuses, purifying can make sense.

16 · PVP

Whirlipede PvP Guide

Whirlipede can be considered for specialized PvP projects, but its usefulness depends heavily on the league, moveset, CP and current metagame.

Great League

Whirlipede's lower CP ceiling makes the Great League the most natural place to investigate its PvP potential. However, its actual performance should be evaluated using the current move pool and battle rankings.

Ultra League

Whirlipede is generally less naturally suited to Ultra League because of its overall stat profile and maximum CP. Specific formats can change this assessment.

Master League

Whirlipede is not a natural Master League investment due to its relatively low CP ceiling.

PvP Tip

Before investing in a PvP Whirlipede, check the exact CP, IV spread, moveset and current league rules rather than assuming a high-IV Pokémon is automatically optimal.

How to Use Whirlipede

Whirlipede FAQ

What type is Whirlipede?

Whirlipede is a Bug / Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Whirlipede weak to?

Whirlipede is weak to Fire, Flying, Psychic and Rock-type attacks.

Is Shiny Whirlipede available?

Shiny Whirlipede can be available through applicable Pokémon GO encounters and events. Availability should be checked against the current game.

Can Whirlipede evolve?

A 100% Whirlipede has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

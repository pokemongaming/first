15 — SIZE VARIANTS

XXL & XXS Carracosta

XXL and XXS are size classifications rather than separate species. A Carracosta can therefore have the Water/Rock typing and simultaneously be an XXL or XXS specimen.

📏 XXL Carracosta An exceptionally large Carracosta relative to the game's size classification system.
📐 XXS Carracosta An exceptionally small Carracosta according to the game's size classification system.

Does XXL Carracosta Have Better Stats?

XXL status should not be treated as a guaranteed combat upgrade. Size classification is separate from the normal Attack, Defense and HP IV system.

Does XXS Carracosta Have Better IVs?

No. XXS status and IVs are separate attributes. An XXS Carracosta can have excellent IVs, poor IVs or perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

21 — FAQ

Carracosta FAQ

What type is Carracosta?

Carracosta is a Water/Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Carracosta weak to?

Carracosta is vulnerable to Grass, Electric, Fighting, Ground and Steel-type attacks.

Does Carracosta evolve?

Carracosta is the final evolution of Tirtouga.

Is Shiny Carracosta available?

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO encounter and event mechanics. A Shiny Tirtouga can be evolved into Shiny Carracosta when eligible.

What is a 100% Carracosta?

A 100% Carracosta has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a Hundo.

What is a Lucky Carracosta?

A Lucky Carracosta is obtained through trading and has the Lucky status, which provides a reduced Stardust cost for power-ups.

What is Shadow Carracosta?

Shadow Carracosta is the Shadow version of Carracosta. It retains the Water/Rock typing while using the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanic.

Should I purify Shadow Carracosta?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your battle goals, IVs, moves and resource requirements.

What is XXL Carracosta?

XXL Carracosta is an unusually large Carracosta according to Pokémon GO's size classification. XXL is an attribute, not a separate species.

What is XXS Carracosta?

XXS Carracosta is an unusually small Carracosta according to Pokémon GO's size classification.

Is a Hundo Carracosta automatically the best Carracosta for PvP?

No. PvP optimization can favor IV spreads other than 15/15/15 depending on the league and CP limit.

Is Carracosta good for raids?

Carracosta can contribute in appropriate situations, but trainers should compare its current moveset and performance with stronger Water and Rock alternatives before investing heavily for raids.

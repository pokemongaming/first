Shadow Heatmor

Shadow Heatmor is the Shadow variant of Heatmor. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular versions, with increased offensive pressure and reduced durability.

👤 Shadow Heatmor

Shadow Heatmor remains a pure Fire-type Pokémon. Its Shadow status does not change its basic typing, weaknesses or resistances.

Shadow Heatmor Strengths

  • Increased offensive pressure.
  • Can be interesting for damage-focused builds.
  • Retains Heatmor's Fire typing.
  • Has collector value as a separate form.

Shadow Heatmor Weaknesses

Shadow Heatmor remains weak to Water, Ground and Rock. Its reduced durability can make these weaknesses even more important in battle.

Purified Heatmor

Purified Heatmor is created when an eligible Shadow Heatmor is purified. Purification removes Shadow status and creates a separate collectible version of Heatmor.

✨ Purified Heatmor

Purified Heatmor remains a Fire-type Pokémon and can be useful for collectors who want to complete multiple Heatmor variants.

Lucky Heatmor

Lucky Heatmor is a special traded version of Heatmor. Lucky Pokémon are especially attractive for players planning to power them up because Lucky status reduces the Stardust required for power-ups.

🍀 Lucky Heatmor

A Lucky Heatmor is primarily valuable as an investment and collection variant. It keeps the same Fire typing and species identity as regular Heatmor.

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Separate Lucky Pokédex collection value.
  • Can be useful for a high-level Heatmor project.
  • Useful when trading for better Heatmor IVs.

XXL Heatmor

XXL Heatmor is an oversized version of Heatmor. XXL status is primarily connected to size collection and showcase-style gameplay rather than directly increasing Heatmor's standard battle stats.

📏 XXL Heatmor

Keep XXL Heatmor if you collect large Pokémon, participate in size-based PokéStop Showcases or want a complete collection of Heatmor variants.

  • Large-size collectible variant.
  • Useful for size-based collections.
  • Can be valuable for PokéStop Showcases.
  • Does not automatically mean better IVs.

XXS Heatmor

XXS Heatmor is an unusually small Heatmor. Like XXL, XXS status is primarily a collection characteristic rather than a direct indicator of battle strength.

🔍 XXS Heatmor

XXS Heatmor is worth keeping for players collecting unusual size variants or trying to build a complete Heatmor collection.

  • Small-size collectible variant.
  • Separate collection appeal.
  • IVs remain more important for battle performance.
  • Can be kept alongside an XXL Heatmor.

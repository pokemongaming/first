Regular Lilligant

The standard form of Lilligant. This is the base reference for comparing Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky and size variants.

✨ Shiny Lilligant

Shiny Lilligant is the alternate-color version of Lilligant. Shiny status does not automatically make a Pokémon stronger in battle, but it can make the Pokémon considerably more desirable to collectors.

🌑 Shadow Lilligant

Shadow Lilligant should be treated as a separate battle variant. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile from their regular counterparts. Always check current Pokémon GO data to confirm whether Shadow Lilligant is currently obtainable.

💎 Purified Lilligant

Purified Lilligant is a Lilligant that has undergone the purification process. Purification changes the Pokémon's attributes and removes its Shadow status. Current availability and purification mechanics should be verified before publication.

🍀 Lucky Lilligant

Lucky Lilligant is a special trading outcome. Lucky Pokémon have a distinct visual background in Pokémon GO and generally require fewer Stardust resources to power up compared with ordinary Pokémon.

💯 100% IV Lilligant

A 100% IV Lilligant has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It represents the maximum IV combination for the species.

XXL Lilligant

XXL Lilligant is a size variant that is larger than the standard size range. XXL status is independent of IV quality, so an XXL Lilligant can have any IV combination.

XXS Lilligant

XXS Lilligant is a size variant that is smaller than the standard size range. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status does not determine the Pokémon's IVs.

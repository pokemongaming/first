Why Keldeo's Typing Matters

The Water/Fighting combination gives Keldeo useful offensive coverage while also creating a broad weakness profile. Electric, Fairy, Flying, Grass and Psychic attacks deal super-effective damage.

Is a 100% Keldeo Worth Keeping?

Absolutely. A perfect Keldeo is a valuable Mythical collection piece and can also be useful for high-level battle and raid investment.

Keldeo PvP Guide

Keldeo's high maximum CP makes Master League the most natural high-level format to consider. Its performance depends heavily on the current moveset and metagame.

Master League

Keldeo's combination of Water/Fighting typing and charged-move pressure can create interesting matchups. However, its weaknesses to Flying, Psychic, Fairy, Grass and Electric attacks must be managed carefully.

Great League & Ultra League

Keldeo's high CP and Mythical availability make it a less natural choice for Great League. Ultra League applications depend on the specific CP and IV combination available to the player.

Keldeo Forms

Keldeo has two forms in Pokémon GO: Ordinary Forme and Resolute Forme. These are forms of the same species rather than separate evolutions.

AVAILABLE

Keldeo Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Keldeo requires significant resources if you want to build a high-level specimen. Its long 20 km Buddy distance makes Candy farming slower than for many common Pokémon.

  • Use Rare Candy carefully when building Keldeo.
  • Save Candy XL for serious Level 50 projects.
  • Check the moveset before investing Stardust.
  • Prioritize high-IV Keldeo for long-term projects.
  • Resolute Forme can be particularly interesting for Fighting-focused investment.

Keldeo Availability

Keldeo is a Mythical Pokémon and its availability is controlled through special Pokémon GO research, events and other limited-time distribution methods.

Current Availability

How to Use Keldeo

Before Investing

  • Check the IV spread.
  • Check whether Secret Sword is available for the Resolute Forme.
  • Decide whether you want a Fighting or Water role.
  • Compare Keldeo with your existing attackers.
  • Keep enough Candy and Stardust for the full investment.

For Raids

Keldeo is most interesting when used as a Fighting-type attacker. Resolute Forme with Secret Sword is particularly important when building around its signature move.

For Collection

Collectors may want to keep Ordinary Forme, Resolute Forme, Shiny Keldeo, 100% Keldeo and unusual size variants such as XXS and XXL.

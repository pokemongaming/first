Musharna

Musharna is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is known for its high HP, bulky profile and unusual dream-themed design. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100%, XXL and XXS Musharna.

Musharna Overview

Musharna is the evolved form of Munna and is Pokédex number #518. It is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. Its biggest strength is its high HP and relatively bulky overall profile rather than exceptional raid damage output.

For most players, Musharna is more interesting as a Pokédex entry, collection Pokémon, Gym defender or specialty PvP project than as a priority raid attacker.

Musharna PvP Guide

Musharna has a high HP stat, but its overall PvP performance remains limited by its available moves and competition from stronger Psychic-type Pokémon.

Great League

Musharna can technically be used under Great League CP restrictions when eligible, but it is not considered a major meta threat.

Ultra League

Its bulk can be interesting, but its performance is limited compared with more efficient Pokémon available for the format.

Master League

Musharna's maximum CP is not high enough to compete naturally with the strongest Master League Pokémon. It is therefore generally a specialty rather than a competitive investment.

🟦 XXL Musharna

XXL Musharna is a size variant. Pokémon GO assigns size categories independently from IVs, so an XXL Musharna does not automatically mean it has perfect IVs.

📏
XXL Musharna Large size category

Musharna can reach the XXL size category, with the game's size system determining the exact classification.

📊
XXL ≠ 100% Size and IVs are separate

An XXL Musharna can have any IV combination. Always inspect its appraisal separately.

XXL Musharna for Collectors

An XXL Musharna with Shiny, Lucky or perfect IV status can become an especially interesting collection piece.

🟪 XXS Musharna

XXS Musharna represents the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size spectrum. Like XXL, XXS classification is independent of IV quality.

📏
XXS Musharna Very small size category

An XXS Musharna is classified by its size, not by its Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

🔬
XXS Collection Value Useful for size collectors

An XXS Shiny, Lucky or perfect-IV Musharna can be an excellent collector target.

Musharna FAQ

What type is Musharna?

Musharna is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Musharna weak to?

Musharna is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

What is Musharna's Max CP?

Musharna's Level 50 maximum CP is 3,079 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Musharna available?

Yes. Shiny Musharna is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Musharna good for raids?

Musharna is not considered a top-tier Psychic raid attacker. Its strengths are more closely associated with its bulk and collection value.

Is Musharna good for PvP?

Musharna can be used in PvP, but it is generally not considered a major meta Pokémon.

How do you evolve Munna into Musharna?

Munna evolves into Musharna using 50 Candy and a Unova Stone.

What is a 100% Musharna?

A 100% Musharna has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Musharna be Lucky?

Yes. Musharna can have the Lucky status after an eligible trade.

Can Musharna be XXL?

Yes. Musharna can have different size classifications, including XXL and XXS.

Does XXL Musharna mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Musharna can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Musharna mean low IV?

No. XXS is a size classification and does not determine Attack, Defense or HP IVs.

Is Lucky Musharna worth powering up?

It can be worthwhile for collectors or players who want to reduce the Stardust cost of powering up a Musharna.

Is Shadow Musharna available?

Shadow availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO game data. This page does not assume that Shadow Musharna is currently obtainable unless officially introduced.

Can Musharna be both Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny status and Lucky status are separate properties, so a Musharna can have both.

Can Musharna be both 100% and XXL?

Yes. IVs and size are separate characteristics, so a perfect Musharna can also be XXL.

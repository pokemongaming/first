Do not automatically purify a Shadow Solosis simply to remove its Shadow status. Consider its IVs, intended evolution, battle role and your collection goals first.

Lucky Solosis

Why Keep a Lucky Solosis?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky trades can be useful for collecting high-IV Pokémon.
  • Lucky Solosis can be evolved into the rest of its family.
  • It is a useful collector variant even when Solosis is not your primary battle Pokémon.

Lucky Solosis IV Floor

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than ordinary trades. The exact IV combination is still determined by the individual trade result.

Solosis PvP Guide

Solosis is generally not a top choice for open Great League, Ultra League or Master League play. Its low overall CP and evolutionary stage limit its role compared with more specialized Pokémon.

Great League

Solosis can technically fit within the Great League CP limit, but its overall battle performance is more limited than many established Psychic-type options.

Ultra League & Master League

Solosis is generally not suited to higher CP formats. Duosion and especially Reuniclus are more relevant if you want to explore the evolutionary family in battle.

PvP Tip

If you are building a Solosis specifically for PvP, check the current moveset, league rankings and CP requirements before spending Stardust or Candy XL.

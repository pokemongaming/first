22 • FAQ

Beheeyem FAQ

What type is Beheeyem?

Beheeyem is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

What is Beheeyem weak against?

Beheeyem is weak to Bug, Dark and Ghost-type attacks.

What is Beheeyem's max CP?

Beheeyem can reach a maximum CP of 3,112 with perfect IVs at level 50.

What are Beheeyem's stats?

Beheeyem has 221 Attack, 163 Defense and 181 HP as its Pokémon GO base stats.

Is Beheeyem good for raids?

Beheeyem can deal Psychic-type raid damage, but it is generally not considered a priority Psychic raid attacker compared with stronger alternatives.

What is Beheeyem's best moveset?

Confusion and Psychic provide Beheeyem with its straightforward Psychic-focused PvE moveset.

Is Beheeyem good in PvP?

Beheeyem is generally a niche PvP choice. Its coverage moves can make it interesting for experimentation, but it is not normally a top meta Pokémon.

Is Shiny Beheeyem available?

Shiny Beheeyem is a collectible alternate-color version of Beheeyem when available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

What is a 100% Beheeyem?

A 100% Beheeyem has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Beheeyem?

Lucky Beheeyem is a Beheeyem that has obtained the Lucky status through the Pokémon GO trading system. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is XXL Beheeyem?

XXL Beheeyem is an individual Beheeyem classified as extra large. XXL is a size category rather than a different species.

What is XXS Beheeyem?

XXS Beheeyem is an individual Beheeyem classified as extra small.

Can Shadow Beheeyem be purified?

An eligible Shadow Beheeyem can be purified. Because purification is permanent, compare the Shadow and purified versions before making the decision.

Does Shiny Beheeyem have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual and collectible distinction and does not inherently provide better battle stats.

Is a Hundo Beheeyem worth keeping?

Yes. A perfect-IV Beheeyem is valuable for collectors and completionists even if it is not a top-tier battle attacker.

What evolves into Beheeyem?

Elgyem evolves into Beheeyem. Beheeyem is the final stage of that evolution family.

Why Eelektross's Typing Matters

Eelektross has a pure Electric typing. Electric-type attacks are especially useful against Water- and Flying-type Pokémon, while Ground-type attacks are the primary offensive threat to Eelektross.

Eelektross IV Guide

100% IV Eelektross

A 100% Eelektross has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These Pokémon are commonly called Hundo Pokémon and are popular among collectors and players who enjoy maximizing their Pokémon.

A lower-IV Eelektross can still be useful. IV quality should be considered together with level, moveset, available resources and intended battle format.

Eelektross FAQ

What type is Eelektross?

Eelektross is a pure Electric-type Pokémon.

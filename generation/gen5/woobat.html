01 • DATABASE

Woobat Overview

Woobat is a small Psychic / Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is known for its heart-shaped nose, unusual appearance and evolution into Swoobat. In Pokémon GO, Woobat is generally more valuable as an evolution, collection and special-form Pokémon than as a top-tier raid attacker.

100% Woobat Collector Tip

09 • PVP

Woobat PvP Guide

Woobat is not generally a major open-format PvP threat, but its evolution and low-CP nature can make it relevant to specialized formats depending on the current rules.

Great League

Woobat can potentially fit into specialized Great League situations because the league has a CP limit. However, its actual viability depends on the current moves, cups, available competitors and format rules.

Ultra League

Woobat's overall stats and CP profile make Ultra League a less natural environment for the unevolved Pokémon. Its evolution may be a more relevant candidate depending on the format.

Master League

Woobat is generally unsuitable for Master League because its maximum battle potential is far below the Pokémon commonly used in that format.

22 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Evaluate an Individual Woobat

Not every Woobat should be judged by the same criteria. A trainer looking for a Shiny collector piece may value a specimen differently from someone searching for a perfect IV evolution.

For Collectors

  • Look for Shiny Woobat.
  • Keep Shadow and Purified specimens.
  • Track Lucky trades.
  • Save XXL and XXS examples.
  • Keep unusual event or research specimens.

For Perfect-IV Hunters

  • Check the appraisal screen.
  • Look for 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.
  • Confirm the Pokémon is genuinely 100% IV.
  • Consider whether to evolve the perfect specimen.

For Battle Players

Before investing Stardust, compare Woobat and its evolution with stronger alternatives for the specific league or battle format. A good IV spread does not automatically make a Pokémon meta-relevant.

23 • FAQ

Woobat FAQ

What type is Woobat?

Woobat is a Psychic / Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Woobat weak to?

Woobat is vulnerable to Electric, Ice, Rock, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

Does Woobat evolve?

Yes. Woobat evolves into Swoobat in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Woobat available?

Shiny availability depends on its official Pokémon GO release and current encounter mechanics. Check the current game data before publishing availability claims.

Is Shadow Woobat available?

Shadow availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations and encounters. It should be checked against the current Pokémon GO Rocket lineup.

What is Purified Woobat?

Purified Woobat is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Woobat.

What is Lucky Woobat?

Lucky Woobat is obtained through trading and has the special Lucky status, including reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Woobat?

A 100% Woobat has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is an XXL Woobat?

An XXL Woobat is a Woobat that receives an XXL size classification. Size and IVs are separate attributes.

What is an XXS Woobat?

An XXS Woobat is a Woobat with an unusually small size classification.

Is Woobat good for raids?

Woobat is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its main value is usually collection, evolution and special-variant hunting.

Should I evolve a Shiny Woobat?

If you have multiple Shiny Woobat, evolving one into Shiny Swoobat can expand your collection. If you have only one, consider whether you prefer keeping the unevolved Shiny.

Should I purify Shadow Woobat?

Do not purify automatically. Consider the Shadow status, IVs, collection goals and potential evolution before making the decision.

Is a 100% Woobat rare?

Perfect IV specimens are much less common than ordinary IV combinations, making a 100% Woobat a desirable collector target.

Zoroark Overview

Zoroark is the evolved form of Zorua and is a pure Dark-type Pokémon. Its identity is strongly connected to the Illusion ability in the mainline Pokémon games, while Pokémon GO handles its battle mechanics through its own rules.

XXL Zoroark and IVs

An XXL Zoroark can simultaneously be a high-IV or 100% specimen, but size itself does not guarantee better IVs.

  • Keep an XXL Zoroark if you enjoy collecting size variants.
  • Check its IVs separately from its size.
  • A 100% XXL Zoroark is an especially notable collection find.
  • Size does not automatically increase Attack or Defense.

XXS Zoroark

Zoroark FAQ

What type is Zoroark?

Zoroark is a pure Dark-type Pokémon.

What is Zoroark weak to?

Zoroark is weak to Fighting, Bug and Fairy attacks.

Does Zoroark evolve?

Yes. Zoroark evolves from Zorua and is the final evolutionary stage of the Zorua family.

Is Shiny Zoroark available?

Yes, Shiny Zoroark can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and evolution methods.

Is Shadow Zoroark better than regular Zoroark?

Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile. Whether Shadow Zoroark is better depends on your battle goal, moveset and available alternatives.

Should I purify Shadow Zoroark?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions first because purification is permanent.

What is Lucky Zoroark?

Lucky Zoroark is a Zoroark obtained through a Lucky Trade. Lucky Pokémon have special visual characteristics and can require less Stardust to power up.

What is a 100% Zoroark?

A 100% Zoroark has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is XXL Zoroark?

XXL Zoroark is a Zoroark with an unusually large size classification. Size is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Zoroark?

XXS Zoroark is an unusually small Zoroark. Its size does not automatically indicate better or worse IVs.

Is Zoroark good for raids?

Zoroark can provide useful Dark-type damage, but its performance should be compared with other available Dark attackers before investing heavily.

Is Zoroark good in PvP?

Zoroark can be useful in selected PvP situations, particularly when its Dark typing and moveset provide good matchup coverage.

Can a Zoroark be Shiny, Lucky and 100% at the same time?

Yes. These characteristics describe different aspects of a Pokémon. A specimen can potentially combine multiple special characteristics, although obtaining specific combinations can be extremely difficult.

Can Zoroark be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Zoroark can also have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Zoroark be XXS and Shiny?

Special characteristics such as Shiny status and size can coexist when the game allows the relevant combination.

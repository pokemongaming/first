Why Shelmet's Typing Matters

Shelmet's pure Bug typing gives it a straightforward offensive and defensive profile. Bug attacks are useful against Grass, Psychic and Dark Pokémon, while Shelmet takes increased damage from Fire, Flying and Rock attacks.

09 • PVP

Shelmet PvP Guide

Shelmet's low CP makes it more naturally suited to lower leagues than Master League. However, its limited Attack stat and move pool mean that it is generally a niche pick rather than a top competitive Pokémon.

Great League

Shelmet can technically be built for Great League, but it generally struggles to compete with stronger Bug-type and other meta Pokémon. Its evolution, Accelgor, is usually the more relevant choice for players interested in the family.

Ultra League

Shelmet's low maximum CP makes Ultra League an impractical environment for it.

Master League

Shelmet is not suitable for standard Master League competition because its maximum CP is far below the league's typical requirements.

PvP Investment Advice

Do not spend large amounts of Stardust on Shelmet unless you have a specific PvP, collection or research goal.

13 • EVOLUTION

Shelmet Evolution

Shelmet evolves into Accelgor. In Pokémon GO, Shelmet requires 200 Candy to evolve. Shelmet and Karrablast also have a special evolution relationship associated with trading.

Shelmet Evolution Strategy

  • Keep high-IV Shelmet specimens for evolution projects.
  • Save enough Candy before evolving.
  • Consider evolving a Shiny Shelmet if completing the Shiny family.
  • Compare regular and Shadow specimens before evolving.
  • Remember that evolution does not improve a Pokémon's IVs.
Evolution note:

Shelmet's evolution family is unusual because Shelmet and Karrablast are linked by a special trade mechanic. Check the current in-game evolution requirements before spending Candy.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Shelmet

Before Powering Up

  • Decide whether the Shelmet is for evolution or PvP.
  • Check the IV spread.
  • Check whether it is Shiny or Shadow.
  • Compare the specimen with your other Shelmet.
  • Save enough Candy for evolution.
  • Avoid unnecessary Stardust spending.

During Team Building

Shelmet should rarely be a first-choice raid attacker. Its relatively low Attack stat limits its PvE performance. For serious Bug-type raid teams, stronger evolved or Legendary options are generally preferable.

Collection Value

Shelmet can have considerable collection value because of its Shiny and Shadow variants and its unusual evolution relationship with Karrablast.

Is Shelmet Worth Investing In?

Raid Investment

Shelmet is generally not recommended as a major raid investment. Its maximum CP and Attack stat are too low to make it a competitive general-purpose PvE attacker.

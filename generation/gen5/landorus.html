Why Landorus's Typing Matters

Landorus's Ground / Flying typing gives it useful immunities and resistances in Pokémon GO while also creating a significant weakness to Ice-type attacks. This combination is particularly important when using Landorus in raids or PvP.

Landorus FAQ

What type is Landorus?

Landorus is a Ground / Flying-type Legendary Pokémon.

How many Landorus formes are in Pokémon GO?

The major Landorus formes featured in Pokémon GO are Incarnate Forme and Therian Forme.

What is Landorus weak to?

Landorus is weak to Water and Ice attacks. Because it is Ground / Flying type, Ice is especially effective.

Is Landorus good for raids?

Yes. Landorus can be a strong Ground-type raid attacker, with Therian Forme being particularly notable for PvE damage.

Is Therian Landorus better than Incarnate Landorus?

For PvE, Therian Forme generally offers the stronger offensive profile. For PvP, the preferred forme can depend on the current league and moveset.

Is Shiny Landorus available?

Yes. Shiny versions can be available through eligible Landorus encounters when supported by Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Landorus?

A 100% or hundo Landorus has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Landorus?

Lucky Landorus is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Landorus?

XXL Landorus is an individual Landorus classified as extra large by Pokémon GO's size system.

What is XXS Landorus?

XXS Landorus is an individual Landorus classified as extra small.

Does XXL or XXS affect Landorus IVs?

No. Size classification and IVs are separate attributes.

Can Landorus be Shadow?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's current Team GO Rocket and Legendary Shadow rotation.

Should I purify Shadow Landorus?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, particularly if you want maximum raid damage.

Is Shiny Therian Landorus available?

When the Shiny Therian Forme is enabled in Pokémon GO, eligible Therian Landorus encounters can provide its Shiny form.

Does Landorus evolve?

Landorus does not have a conventional evolution chain. Incarnate and Therian are alternate formes rather than normal evolutionary stages.

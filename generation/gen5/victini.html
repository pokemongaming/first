What Is a 100% Victini?

A 100% Victini has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. Trainers commonly call this a "hundo." Because Victini is a rare Mythical Pokémon, a perfect specimen can be especially desirable for collectors.

Hundo Victini

Should You Keep Lower-IV Victini?

Yes. IV perfection is not required for a useful Pokémon. A lower-IV Victini can still be valuable because of its rarity, move set, collection value and potential future game relevance.

XXL Victini & XXS Victini

Pokémon GO can assign Pokémon different size categories. XXL and XXS are size classifications, not alternate species and not separate evolutionary forms.

📏

XXL Victini

An XXL Victini is an unusually large specimen according to Pokémon GO's size mechanics. XXL status can be useful for collection goals and size-related features.

📐

XXS Victini

An XXS Victini is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS is a size classification rather than a separate form.

Do XXL and XXS Change Victini's Battle Stats?

Size categories should not be treated as IVs. A Pokémon being XXL or XXS does not mean that it has better IVs, higher CP or automatically superior battle performance.

Collector tip:

A Shiny, Hundo, XXL or XXS Victini can be valuable to collectors for different reasons. These characteristics should be recorded separately in a Pokémon database.

Victini FAQ

What type is Victini?

Victini is a Fire/Psychic-type Pokémon.

Victini's Fire/Psychic typing makes it vulnerable to Water, Ground, Rock, Ghost and Dark attacks.

Purified Hydreigon

PURIFIED FORM

Purified Hydreigon

Should You Purify Shadow Hydreigon?

Hydreigon FAQ

What type is Hydreigon?

Hydreigon is a Dark and Dragon-type Pokémon.

What is Hydreigon weak to?

Hydreigon is weak to Fairy, Ice, Dragon, Bug and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Hydreigon good for raids?

Hydreigon can be a strong Dark-type raid attacker with an appropriate moveset.

Is Shadow Hydreigon stronger?

Shadow Hydreigon has a different offensive and defensive profile and can be attractive for players focused on damage.

Is Shiny Hydreigon stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic.

What is a 100% Hydreigon?

A 100% Hydreigon has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Hydreigon good?

Lucky Hydreigon can be an excellent investment because Lucky Pokémon require reduced Stardust for power-ups.

Does XXL Hydreigon have stronger stats?

No. XXL status should not be treated as a direct combat stat increase.

Does XXS Hydreigon have weaker stats?

No. XXS size does not automatically determine combat strength.

Does Hydreigon evolve?

Hydreigon is the final evolution of Deino and evolves from Zweilous.

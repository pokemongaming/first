Dewott

Dewott is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves from Oshawott and later evolves into Samurott. In Pokémon GO, Dewott is primarily an evolution-stage Pokémon and is also available in several collectible variants.

Dewott Overview

Dewott is the middle stage of the Oshawott evolutionary family. It evolves from Oshawott and can evolve into Samurott. Unlike the final evolution, Dewott is generally more important as a Pokédex, collection and evolution-stage Pokémon than as a high-level raid attacker.

✨ Shiny Dewott

Shiny Dewott is the alternate-color version of regular Dewott. A Shiny Oshawott that is eligible to evolve can retain its Shiny status when evolving into Dewott.

✨

Shiny Dewott

Shiny status is cosmetic and does not provide an automatic combat-stat advantage over a regular Dewott with the same level and IVs.

  • Shiny Dewott is primarily a collector variant.
  • Shiny status is retained through evolution.
  • IVs remain important for combat performance.
  • A Shiny Dewott can evolve into a Shiny Samurott.

🌑 Shadow Dewott

Shadow Dewott is the Shadow version of Dewott. Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile from regular Pokémon and require their own investment decisions.

🌑

Should You Purify Shadow Dewott?

The decision depends on whether you value Shadow mechanics, collection status, IV changes or the purified variant itself. Do not purify automatically without considering the Pokémon's future evolution and intended use.

🍀 Lucky Dewott

Dewott PvP Guide

Dewott's battle usefulness should be evaluated in the context of league CP limits and its moveset. Because it has a final evolution, players should also compare Dewott with Samurott before making a major resource investment.

Great League

Dewott can be considered in limited formats, but specific recommendations depend on current move availability and the active PvP rules.

Ultra League

Samurott is generally the more natural member of the evolutionary family to evaluate for higher-level competitive investment.

Dewott for Raids

Dewott is generally not the primary Water-type raid investment for players with access to strong final-evolution, Legendary or Mega/Primal attackers. Its main value is usually collection and progression toward Samurott.

Long-Term Recommendation

If resources are limited, evaluate your Dewott before evolving and powering up Samurott rather than automatically investing large amounts of Stardust into the middle evolution.

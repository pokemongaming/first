01 • OVERVIEW

Samurott Overview

Samurott is the final evolution of the Oshawott evolutionary family. It was introduced in Generation 5 and is a pure Water-type Pokémon in Pokémon GO. Its combination of respectable Attack, useful HP and access to Water-type attacks makes it a practical option for players building a Water collection.

Why Samurott's Water Typing Matters

Samurott's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. Water attacks are particularly useful against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon. However, Samurott must be careful against Electric and Grass attacks, which can deal super-effective damage.

What Is a 100% Samurott?

A 100% Samurott has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is the highest possible IV combination. A perfect IV does not automatically make Samurott the strongest Pokémon in every battle, but it can be valuable for collectors, PvE investment and long-term ownership.

13 • LUCKY

Lucky Samurott

Lucky Samurott is a special trade-result status. It is not a separate species or evolution.

🍀

Lucky Samurott

A Lucky Samurott has the visual Lucky background and benefits from the lower Stardust cost associated with powering up Lucky Pokémon.

Why Lucky Samurott Can Be Useful

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for long-term investment projects.
  • Attractive addition to a special Pokémon collection.
  • Lucky trades can be useful for obtaining stronger IV floors.

Lucky Samurott IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a minimum IV floor when received through a Lucky Trade. This makes Lucky Samurott particularly interesting for players looking for an inexpensive long-term investment.

Lucky does not mean 100% IV.

A Lucky Samurott can have different IV combinations. A Lucky 15/15/15 Samurott is both Lucky and perfect IV.

24 • FAQ

Samurott FAQ

What type is Samurott?

Samurott is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Samurott weak against?

Samurott is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

What is Samurott's Pokédex number?

Samurott is National Pokédex #503.

What generation is Samurott from?

Samurott was introduced in Generation 5 and is associated with the Unova region.

Does Samurott evolve?

Samurott is the final evolution in the Oshawott evolutionary family. Oshawott evolves into Dewott, which evolves into Samurott.

Is Shiny Samurott available?

Shiny Samurott is an available Shiny species form when obtained through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or by evolving an eligible Shiny Oshawott.

Is Shadow Samurott different from regular Samurott?

Yes. Shadow Samurott has the Shadow status and associated battle mechanics while retaining its Water typing.

Should I purify Shadow Samurott?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow battle benefits with the advantages of purification before making the decision.

What is Lucky Samurott?

Lucky Samurott is Samurott that received the Lucky status through a trade. Lucky Pokémon have reduced Stardust costs for power-ups.

What is a 100% Samurott?

A 100% Samurott has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is a 100% Samurott rare?

Perfect IV Pokémon are uncommon because the three IV values must all reach their maximum of 15.

What is XXL Samurott?

XXL Samurott is an extra-large size classification. It is not a separate Pokémon species.

What is XXS Samurott?

XXS Samurott is an extra-small size classification. Like XXL, it is separate from the Pokémon's IV quality.

Can Samurott be both Lucky and Shiny?

Yes. Lucky and Shiny are separate attributes, so a Samurott can have both statuses.

Can Samurott be both XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate attributes. An XXL Samurott can have a perfect 15/15/15 IV combination.

Can Samurott be XXS and 100% IV?

Yes. An XXS Samurott can also have perfect IVs.

Is Samurott good for raids?

Samurott can contribute to Water-focused raid teams, but its exact value depends on its current moveset and the alternatives available in Pokémon GO.

Is Samurott good in PvP?

Samurott's PvP value depends on its current moveset, league and the surrounding meta. PvP IV requirements can also differ from PvE priorities.

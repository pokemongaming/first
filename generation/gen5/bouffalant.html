What Does Normal Typing Mean?

Bouffalant's Normal typing gives it a simple defensive profile. Fighting-type attacks deal super-effective damage, while Ghost-type attacks are heavily resisted in Pokémon GO.

Battle tip:

If you are battling Bouffalant, Fighting-type attackers are the natural counter category. Avoid relying on Ghost-type attacks for damage.

Bouffalant FAQ

What type is Bouffalant?

Bouffalant is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Bouffalant weak against?

Bouffalant is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

What does Bouffalant resist?

Bouffalant's Normal typing gives it a strong resistance to Ghost-type attacks.

What is Bouffalant's Max CP?

Bouffalant reaches 3,163 CP at Level 50 with 15/15/15 IVs.

Is Shiny Bouffalant available?

Yes. Shiny Bouffalant is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Bouffalant available?

Shadow Bouffalant is currently listed as unavailable. Future Team GO Rocket content could change this.

Is Purified Bouffalant available?

A Purified Bouffalant is currently not obtainable because Shadow Bouffalant is not currently available.

How do I get Lucky Bouffalant?

Lucky status is obtained through Pokémon GO trading mechanics. A traded Bouffalant can become Lucky.

What is a 100% Bouffalant?

A 100% or Hundo Bouffalant has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Bouffalant be XXL?

Yes. Pokémon GO supports size classifications, including XXL and XXS, for eligible Pokémon.

Can Bouffalant be XXS?

Yes. An especially small Bouffalant can receive the XXS size designation.

Does Bouffalant evolve?

No. Bouffalant does not have a normal evolution chain.

Is Bouffalant good for raids?

Bouffalant is not normally considered a top-tier raid attacker. Its greater appeal is generally collection and specific PvP or personal-use builds.

Is a Hundo Bouffalant useful?

A Hundo Bouffalant is valuable for collectors and can be useful depending on the battle format and current moveset.

Is a Shiny Hundo Bouffalant rare?

A Shiny with perfect 15/15/15 IVs combines two desirable characteristics, making it a particularly attractive collection specimen.

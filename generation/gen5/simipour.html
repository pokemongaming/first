Should You Power Up Shadow Simipour?

  • Compare its IVs with your existing Water attackers.
  • Check its current Fast and Charged Moves.

  • Do not purify automatically without considering your goal.

Purified Simipour

Purified Simipour is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Pokémon. Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon's resource and move mechanics.

🤍 PURIFIED FORM

🤍 Purified Simipour

A Purified Simipour no longer retains the Shadow battle profile. Purification can improve IVs within Pokémon GO's purification mechanics.

Lucky Simipour

Lucky Simipour is obtained through the Lucky Pokémon trade mechanic. Lucky status is valuable because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for powering up than standard Pokémon.

🍀 LUCKY FORM

🍀 Lucky Simipour

Is a 100% Simipour Always the Best?

For general collection and maximum-stat investment, a 15/15/15 Simipour is highly desirable. PvP can sometimes favor different IV spreads depending on the CP limit.

XXL Simipour

XXL Simipour is an unusually large individual Simipour. Size can be especially relevant for Pokémon GO Showcases and collection purposes.

📏 XXL SIZE

📏 XXL Simipour

XXL status represents an exceptionally large size variation. Size does not automatically change Simipour's species base stats or provide a direct standard battle advantage.

Best Use

XXL Simipour is primarily valuable for collectors and potential Pokémon Showcase participation when the species or evolution family is eligible.

XXS Simipour

XXS Simipour is an unusually small individual Simipour. It is primarily a collection variation based on Pokémon size.

🔬 XXS SIZE

🔬 XXS Simipour

XXS status identifies a particularly small Simipour. Size variation should be treated separately from IV quality, moveset and Lucky or Shadow status.

Important

XXS does not automatically mean that Simipour has weaker battle stats. Size and IVs are separate properties.

Simipour FAQ

What type is Simipour?

Simipour is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Simipour weak to?

Simipour is weak to Electric-type and Grass-type attacks.

Does Simipour evolve?

Simipour is the final standard evolution in its evolution family.

Can Simipour be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters and current game availability.

Is Shadow Simipour stronger than regular Simipour?

Shadow mechanics provide a different offensive and defensive battle profile, making Shadow Simipour a separate investment choice.

Should I purify Shadow Simipour?

Not automatically. Consider your battle goals, IVs, resource costs and collection preferences before purifying.

What is a Lucky Simipour?

Lucky Simipour is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the trade system and benefits from reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Simipour?

A 100% Simipour has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Simipour have better stats?

XXL size does not automatically give Simipour better battle stats. Size and IVs are separate properties.

Does XXS Simipour have weaker stats?

No. XXS size does not automatically mean weaker battle stats.

Can Shiny Simipour also have perfect IVs?

Yes. Shiny status and IV values are separate properties.

Can Lucky Simipour be 100% IV?

Yes. Lucky status and IV quality are separate Pokémon properties.

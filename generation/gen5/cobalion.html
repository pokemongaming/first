01 • OVERVIEW

Cobalion Overview

Cobalion is a Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is part of the Swords of Justice group alongside Terrakion, Virizion and Keldeo. In Pokémon GO, Cobalion is a Steel/Fighting-type Pokémon with a much more defensive stat profile than many Legendary attackers.

Why Cobalion's Typing Matters

Cobalion's Steel/Fighting combination gives it a large number of resistances. This makes its defensive profile one of its most important characteristics, especially when evaluating Cobalion for PvP. The Fighting typing also gives Cobalion access to Fighting-type offensive options.

09 • PVP

Cobalion PvP Guide

PvP is one of Cobalion's most important roles in Pokémon GO. Its Steel/Fighting typing, solid bulk and access to Fighting-type pressure give it useful matchup potential.

Great League

Cobalion's CP and availability make Great League less natural than higher-level formats. League rules and special cups should always be checked before building a team around Cobalion.

Ultra League

Cobalion can be particularly interesting in Ultra League because its defensive profile and Fighting pressure can create useful matchups against several common Pokémon.

Master League

Master League requires substantial investment. A high-IV Cobalion can be considered for Master League teams, but its performance should be evaluated against the current metagame rather than historical rankings alone.

PvP Tip

Do not judge Cobalion only by its Attack stat. Its value comes from the combination of typing, bulk, fast-move pressure and charged-move access.

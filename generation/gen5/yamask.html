Yamask

Yamask is a Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a collectible Ghost-type Pokémon with Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, XXS and XXL possibilities. Yamask can also evolve into Cofagrigus using Yamask Candy.

01 · OVERVIEW

Yamask Overview

Yamask is a Ghost-type Pokémon from the Unova region. Its relatively low maximum CP means it is not a major general-purpose raid attacker, but it can still be interesting for collectors, special formats and evolution-focused players.

Why Yamask's Typing Matters

Regular Yamask has a pure Ghost typing. This gives it useful interactions with Normal, Fighting, Poison and Bug attacks while making it vulnerable to Ghost and Dark-type damage.

08 · IV GUIDE

Yamask IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Yamask is considered a perfect-IV Yamask.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

⭐ 100% IV Yamask

A 100% Yamask has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is a perfect specimen from an IV perspective and can be especially desirable for collectors.

A perfect IV does not automatically make Yamask a top raid attacker. The value of a 100% Yamask depends on the player's collection goals and intended format.

How to Identify a 100% Yamask

  • Attack IV must be 15.
  • Defense IV must be 15.
  • HP IV must be 15.
  • The appraisal should show maximum bars for all three IV categories.
  • Use an IV calculator when the exact appraisal result is unclear.
09 · PVP

Yamask PvP Guide

Yamask's low maximum CP makes it much more suitable for lower CP formats than Master League. Its stat distribution and Ghost typing can be interesting in specialized cups.

Little League

Yamask can be interesting in Little League-style formats when the species is eligible and the current cup rules favor it. Its Ghost typing, defensive stats and available moves can create useful matchup opportunities.

Great League

Standard Yamask does not naturally reach the CP profile of many established Great League Pokémon. Its usefulness depends heavily on the current format and moveset.

Ultra League & Master League

Yamask's low maximum CP makes it an unconventional choice for Ultra League and Master League.

11 · SHADOW

Shadow Yamask

Shadow Yamask is a separate Shadow variant of Yamask. Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile from their regular counterparts and can require additional resources to build.

😈 Shadow Yamask

Shadow Yamask retains Yamask's Ghost typing. Its Shadow status changes its battle characteristics, while its underlying species and typing remain recognizable as Yamask.

12 · PURIFIED

Purified Yamask

A Shadow Yamask can become a Purified Yamask through purification when the game allows that interaction. Purification changes the Pokémon's status and removes the Shadow state.

😈

Shadow Yamask

Retains Shadow status and its associated battle characteristics.

✨

Purified Yamask

Purification removes Shadow status and provides the standard Purified Pokémon benefits.

Should You Purify Yamask?

  • Check the Yamask's IVs before purifying.
  • Consider whether you want to keep the Shadow form.
  • Check the current cost of purification.
  • Compare the value of the Shadow and Purified versions for your collection.
  • Do not purify a Shadow Pokémon automatically.
Important

Purification is a permanent decision. If you value the Shadow collection entry, consider keeping one Shadow Yamask before purifying another specimen.

13 · LUCKY

Lucky Yamask

Lucky Yamask is created through Pokémon GO's trading system. A Lucky Pokémon receives a special Lucky background and requires less Stardust to power up.

🍀 Lucky Yamask

Lucky Yamask is particularly attractive to collectors because the Lucky status is permanently attached to the traded Pokémon.

Lucky Yamask Benefits

🍀
Lucky Background

Lucky Pokémon receive a distinctive visual background in the Pokémon storage interface.

💎
Reduced Stardust

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

🔄
Trade Origin

Lucky status is obtained through trading rather than normal catching.

Lucky Yamask IVs

Lucky Pokémon have a higher minimum IV floor than many ordinary wild encounters. However, Lucky does not mean automatically 100% IV.

Always check the appraisal after a trade. A Lucky Yamask can still have different IV combinations, so confirm whether the individual specimen is also a 100% IV Pokémon.

16 · XXS

XXS Yamask

XXS Yamask is the smallest size category for Yamask. It is a separate size classification and does not indicate anything about the Pokémon's IV quality.

🔎 XXS Yamask

Current Yamask size data places the XXS category around 0.245 m to 0.25 m. A very small Yamask can therefore be particularly attractive to collectors interested in size variants.

24 · FAQ

Yamask FAQ

What type is Yamask?

Regular Yamask is a pure Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Yamask weak to?

Yamask is weak to Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

What is Yamask's Max CP?

Yamask's listed Level 50 maximum CP is 1,110.

What are Yamask's best moves?

Astonish and Shadow Ball are the recommended Ghost-focused moveset.

Is Shiny Yamask available?

Yes. Shiny Yamask is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Yamask available?

Shadow Yamask can be available through eligible Shadow Pokémon GO encounters and rotations.

Can Shadow Yamask be purified?

A Shadow Yamask can be purified when the Pokémon is eligible for purification.

What is a Lucky Yamask?

A Lucky Yamask is obtained through Pokémon GO's trading system and receives Lucky status.

Does Lucky Yamask mean 100% IV?

No. Lucky Pokémon have a higher IV floor, but a Lucky Yamask is not automatically a 15/15/15 hundo.

What is a 100% Yamask?

A 100% Yamask has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Yamask be XXL?

Yes. Yamask can appear in the XXL size category.

Can Yamask be XXS?

Yes. Yamask can appear in the XXS size category.

Does XXL Yamask have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Yamask can have any valid IV combination.

Does XXS Yamask have lower CP?

Size should not be treated as a direct replacement for IV information. Check the Pokémon's actual CP, level and appraisal separately.

Does Yamask evolve?

Yes. Regular Yamask evolves into Cofagrigus using 50 Yamask Candy.

Is Yamask good for raids?

Yamask is generally not considered a high-tier raid attacker because of its low Attack and low maximum CP.

Is Yamask good in PvP?

Yamask can have value in specialized lower-CP formats, depending on the current cup rules and moveset.

Should I keep a Shiny Yamask?

Yes, especially if you are collecting Shiny Pokémon. Check its IVs and size before transferring it.

Should I keep a Shadow Yamask?

If you collect Shadow Pokémon or want to preserve the Shadow form, keeping at least one can be useful.

Should I purify my Shadow Yamask?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions and consider your collection goals before making a permanent decision.

What is the best Yamask variant?

There is no single best variant for every player. A 100% Yamask is desirable for IV collectors, Shiny Yamask for Shiny collectors, Lucky Yamask for Stardust-efficient investment and XXL or XXS Yamask for size collectors.

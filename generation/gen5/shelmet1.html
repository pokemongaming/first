Shelmet Overview

Shelmet is a Bug-type Pokémon from Generation 5. It is part of a two-stage evolution family and evolves into Accelgor in Pokémon GO. Shelmet itself is generally more important as a collection and evolution Pokémon than as a high-level raid attacker.

✨ Shiny Shelmet

✨

Shiny Shelmet

Shiny Shelmet has alternate coloration compared with the standard version. It is primarily a collector variant and can be evolved if the player wants a Shiny Accelgor.

Should You Evolve Shiny Shelmet?

  • Keep at least one Shiny Shelmet for your collection.
  • Consider evolving an extra Shiny into Accelgor.
  • Check the IVs before choosing your best evolution candidate.
  • A Shiny status does not automatically improve battle stats.

🌑 Shadow Shelmet

🌑

Shadow Shelmet

Shadow Shelmet is a special Shadow variant obtained only when it is available through relevant Team GO Rocket encounters or event rotations. Shadow status changes the Pokémon's battle profile while retaining Shelmet's Bug typing.

Shadow Shelmet Considerations

  • Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive profile.
  • Shadow Shelmet retains Bug typing.
  • Availability depends on Team GO Rocket rotations.
  • Do not purify automatically without considering your goal.
  • Keep collectible Shadow variants when possible.
Availability note:

Do not permanently claim Shadow Shelmet is currently available unless it is confirmed by the current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket rotation.

✨ Purified Shelmet

✨

Purified Shelmet

Purified Shelmet is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Shelmet. Purification removes Shadow status and creates a separate collectible version.

Shadow Form

Special battle profile with Shadow status.

Purified Form

Shadow status removed after purification.

Before purifying:

Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow version for collection purposes. Once purified, the Pokémon no longer remains a Shadow Pokémon.

🍀 Lucky Shelmet

🍀

Lucky Shelmet

Lucky Shelmet is a special trade-based variant. Lucky Pokémon are valuable for players who want to power up Pokémon with reduced Stardust costs.

Why Lucky Shelmet Is Useful

  • Reduced Stardust cost for powering up.
  • Special Lucky background in your Pokémon collection.
  • Useful for collectors completing Shelmet variants.
  • Can be evolved into a Lucky Accelgor.

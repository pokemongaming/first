01 • OVERVIEW

Virizion Overview

Virizion is one of the Swords of Justice and was introduced in Generation 5. It is a dual Grass/Fighting-type Legendary Pokémon and does not evolve through a conventional evolution chain.

In Pokémon GO, Virizion has a particularly interesting role because its typing and move pool allow it to function very differently in PvE and PvP. Sacred Sword is especially important when evaluating its battle performance.

Why Virizion's Typing Matters

Virizion's dual typing gives it access to both Grass- and Fighting-type offensive coverage. Defensively, the combination creates several useful resistances, but it also leaves Virizion especially vulnerable to Flying-type attacks.

10 • PVP

Virizion PvP Guide

Virizion is considerably more interesting in PvP than its raid performance might suggest. Its bulk, typing and access to moves such as Sacred Sword and Leaf Blade give it useful shield-pressure options.

Master League

Virizion can be considered in Master League formats when its current moveset and the surrounding metagame favor its Grass/Fighting coverage. Its bulk can help it stay in battles, although its Flying weakness must always be respected.

Ultra League

Virizion can be more naturally suited to CP-limited PvP environments than to raw PvE damage. League-specific IV optimization should be evaluated separately from a traditional 15/15/15 collection target.

Great League

Whether Virizion is eligible for a specific Great League format depends on the Pokémon's CP and the format's rules. Check the current cup or league restrictions before building a dedicated Virizion.

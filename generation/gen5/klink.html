Klink Overview

Klink is a Steel-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It is part of an evolutionary family that continues into Klang and Klinklang. Although Klink is not generally considered a top-tier raid attacker, it can be useful for Pokédex completion, evolution, shiny collecting and building specialized collections.

Why Klink's Steel Typing Matters

Steel is one of the most defensively useful Pokémon types in the game. Klink benefits from a broad set of resistances, although its low overall combat stats limit its usefulness compared with stronger Steel-type Pokémon.

Klink FAQ

What type is Klink?

Klink is a pure Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Klink weak to?

Klink is weak to Fire, Fighting and Ground attacks.

Is Shiny Klink available?

Shiny Klink can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is a 100% Klink?

A 100% Klink has perfect IVs: 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Shadow Klink?

Shadow Klink is a Shadow variant of Klink that retains its Steel typing.

Should I purify Shadow Klink?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, collection value and your intended use before purifying it.

What is Lucky Klink?

Lucky Klink is a Klink that has received the Lucky status through a qualifying trade.

Is XXL Klink rare?

XXL Klink is a large-size specimen and can be desirable for collectors.

What is XXS Klink?

XXS Klink is a particularly small Klink based on Pokémon GO's size system.

Does Klink evolve?

Yes. Klink evolves into Klang and eventually Klinklang.

Is Klink good for raids?

Klink is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. It is more valuable for collecting, evolving and hunting special variants.

Is a Shiny 100% Klink rare?

A Shiny Klink with perfect 15/15/15 IVs combines two desirable traits and is particularly valuable to collectors.

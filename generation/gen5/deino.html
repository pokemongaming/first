Should You Evolve Shadow Deino?

  • Evaluate its IVs before making a major investment.
  • Check its available Charged Move.
  • Consider the future value of Shadow Hydreigon.
  • Do not purify automatically for higher IVs.
  • Compare resource costs with your regular Deino.

Purified Deino

Purified Deino is created when an eligible Shadow Deino is purified. Purification changes the Pokémon from its Shadow state and may change IV values.

Before Purifying

Consider whether you want to preserve the Shadow version for its different battle profile before committing to purification.

Deino FAQ

What type is Deino?

Deino is a Dark and Dragon-type Pokémon.

Does Deino evolve?

Yes. Deino evolves into Zweilous, which eventually evolves into Hydreigon.

Is Shiny Deino available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and current game content.

Can Deino be Shadow?

Shadow availability depends on current Team GO Rocket rotations and Pokémon GO events.

Should I purify Shadow Deino?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version with the potential Purified version before making the decision.

What is a 100% Deino?

A 100% Deino has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Deino good?

Lucky Deino can be useful because it requires less Stardust to power up, making it attractive for future Hydreigon investment.

Does XXL Deino have better stats?

No. Size classification and IVs are separate mechanics. An XXL Deino does not automatically have better battle stats.

What is XXS Deino?

XXS Deino is a smaller-than-average size variant, primarily valued as a collectible form.

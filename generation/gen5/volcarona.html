15 • SIZE

XXL Volcarona

XXL Volcarona is a size variant. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have low, average or perfect IVs.

📏 XXL Volcarona Extra-large size classification
💯 XXL Hundo XXL size + 100% IV

XXL Does Not Mean 100% IV

A common mistake is assuming that a larger Pokémon must have better IVs. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Volcarona can be valuable for collection purposes even when its IVs are not perfect.

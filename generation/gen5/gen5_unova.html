🌍 Gen 5 / Unova Overview

Everything you need to navigate the fifth generation Pokémon database.

What is Generation 5?

Generation 5 introduced the Unova region and a large new group of Pokémon. The generation begins with Victini at National Pokédex #494 and ends with Genesect at #649.

01 Complete National Pokédex range from #494 to #649.
02 Multiple three-stage evolution families including Snivy, Tepig and Oshawott.
03 Legendary and Mythical Pokémon include Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem, Victini and Genesect.
04 This database can be expanded with Pokémon GO moves, raid roles, PvP information and availability.

❓ Gen 5 Pokémon FAQ

Common questions about the Unova Pokédex.

How many Pokémon are in Generation 5?

Generation 5 contains 156 Pokémon in the National Pokédex, numbered #494 through #649.

What region is Generation 5 based on?

Generation 5 introduced the Unova region.

Who is the first Gen 5 Pokémon?

Victini is National Pokédex #494 and is the first Pokémon in the Generation 5 National Pokédex range.

Who is the last Gen 5 Pokémon?

Genesect is National Pokédex #649 and is the final Pokémon in the Generation 5 range.

Which Gen 5 Pokémon are legendary?

Important Unova Legendary Pokémon include Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion, Tornadus, Thundurus, Landorus, Reshiram, Zekrom and Kyurem.

Which Gen 5 Pokémon are Mythical?

Victini, Keldeo, Meloetta and Genesect are among the Mythical Pokémon introduced in Generation 5.

Can this page be used as a Pokémon GO database?

Yes. The structure is designed so Pokémon GO-specific information such as Max CP, movesets, raid performance, PvP rankings, Shadow forms, shiny availability and event history can be added to individual entries.

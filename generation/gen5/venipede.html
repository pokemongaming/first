Should You Purify Shadow Venipede?

Purification is not automatically an upgrade. Before purifying a Shadow Pokémon, consider its IVs, moves, evolution plans, collection goals and the value of retaining the Shadow status.

Frustration note:

Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration as their Charged Move. The ability to replace Frustration can depend on eligible Team GO Rocket events.

22 • FAQ

Venipede FAQ

What type is Venipede?

Venipede is a Bug and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Venipede weak against?

Venipede is vulnerable to Fire, Flying, Psychic and Rock-type attacks.

Does Venipede evolve?

Yes. Venipede evolves into Whirlipede and eventually Scolipede.

Is Shiny Venipede available?

Shiny Venipede is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the Shiny form is obtainable.

Is Shadow Venipede available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket rotations and events.

What is a 100% Venipede?

A 100% Venipede has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Venipede?

Lucky Venipede is a Venipede that received the Lucky status through trading.

What is XXL Venipede?

XXL Venipede is an unusually large Venipede that receives the XXL size classification.

What is XXS Venipede?

XXS Venipede is an unusually small Venipede that receives the XXS size classification.

Is Venipede good for raids?

Venipede is generally not considered a strong raid attacker because of its relatively low offensive stats.

Is Venipede good in PvP?

Venipede itself is generally a low-priority PvP Pokémon. Its evolved forms may have more relevance depending on the current format.

Should I evolve my perfect Venipede?

If you value the evolution family, evolving a 100% Venipede can be a good collection project. Keep in mind that the IVs remain the same through evolution.

Does evolving Venipede change its IVs?

No. Evolution does not reroll the Pokémon's Individual Values.

Can a Shiny Venipede evolve into Shiny Scolipede?

Yes. Shiny Pokémon retain their Shiny status when evolved.

Does XXL Venipede have better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Venipede can have any IV combination.

Does XXS Venipede have lower IVs?

No. XXS classification does not determine the Pokémon's IV quality.

Why Oshawott's Typing Matters

Oshawott's pure Water typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. Water-type Pokémon are useful against Fire, Ground and Rock opponents, while Oshawott must be careful around Electric and Grass attacks.

Lucky Oshawott

Lucky Oshawott is a special trade-related status. The Lucky status affects Stardust costs for powering up the Pokémon and is separate from Shiny, Shadow and IV status.

LUCKY POKÉMON

⭐ Lucky Oshawott

A Lucky Oshawott can be particularly useful for collectors and players looking to reduce the Stardust cost of power-ups.

Lucky Oshawott Facts

  • Lucky status is obtained through eligible trades.
  • Lucky Pokémon generally require less Stardust for power-ups.
  • Lucky status is separate from Shiny status.
  • A Lucky Pokémon can also have other applicable characteristics depending on the encounter.

100% IV Oshawott

A perfect Oshawott has 15 Attack IV, 15 Defense IV and 15 HP IV. This is commonly described as a 15/15/15 or 100% IV Pokémon.

Perfect Oshawott

A 15/15/15 Oshawott is a perfect-IV specimen. Whether it is worth powering up depends on its evolution, moveset, intended use and your available resources.

XXL & XXS Oshawott

Pokémon GO can assign size measurements to individual Pokémon. XXL and XXS are size classifications and are separate from IV percentage.

XXL 📏 XXL Oshawott

An XXL Oshawott is a particularly large individual. Its size classification does not automatically mean that it has high IVs.

XXS 📐 XXS Oshawott

An XXS Oshawott is a particularly small individual. Size and IV percentage are independent characteristics.

Size vs IV:

A Pokémon can be XXL or XXS without being a 100% IV Pokémon. Check the appraisal screen when evaluating IVs.

Oshawott FAQ

What type is Oshawott?

Oshawott is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What region is Oshawott from?

Oshawott was introduced in Generation 5 and originates from the Unova region.

What is Oshawott weak to?

Oshawott is weak to Electric- and Grass-type attacks.

Does Oshawott evolve?

Yes. Oshawott evolves into Dewott and then Samurott.

Is Shiny Oshawott available?

Shiny Oshawott is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters and events.

What is a 100% Oshawott?

A 100% Oshawott has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Oshawott?

Lucky Oshawott is an Oshawott that has received the Lucky status through eligible trade mechanics.

What is Shadow Oshawott?

Shadow Oshawott is Oshawott with the Shadow Pokémon status and associated battle mechanics.

What is Purified Oshawott?

Purified Oshawott is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Oshawott.

What is XXL Oshawott?

XXL Oshawott is an Oshawott with an especially large size classification.

What is XXS Oshawott?

XXS Oshawott is an Oshawott with an especially small size classification.

Is XXL Oshawott automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Oshawott can have any IV combination.

Is Shiny Oshawott automatically rare?

Shiny status makes Oshawott a special collection variant, but its encounter availability and rarity can depend on Pokémon GO events and encounter rates.

Should I keep a 100% Oshawott?

A perfect Oshawott is generally worth keeping, especially if you intend to evolve it or collect perfect-IV Pokémon.

Should I keep XXL and XXS Oshawott?

If you collect size variants, both XXL and XXS Oshawott can be worth keeping. Their size does not determine their IV percentage.

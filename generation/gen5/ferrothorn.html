100% IV Ferrothorn

A perfect Ferrothorn has a 15/15/15 IV combination. This is commonly referred to by players as a 100% IV or "hundo" Ferrothorn.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% IV = 15/15/15

IV perfection is valuable for collecting, but the best IV spread for PvP can differ depending on the league and CP limit.

XXL Ferrothorn

XXL Ferrothorn is a larger size variant. Size classification is separate from IV quality and battle performance.

XXL Collection Value

XXL Ferrothorn can be useful for collectors and may be relevant for eligible PokéStop Showcase competitions.

XXS Ferrothorn

XXS Ferrothorn is a smaller size variant. Like XXL, size does not automatically indicate better or worse IVs or battle performance.

XXS Collection Value

XXS Ferrothorn is mainly a special collection variant for players who collect unusual size forms.

Ferrothorn FAQ

What type is Ferrothorn?

Ferrothorn is a Grass and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Ferrothorn weak to?

Ferrothorn is particularly vulnerable to Fire-type attacks and also has weaknesses that depend on its dual Grass and Steel typing.

Is Shiny Ferrothorn available?

Shiny availability depends on the Pokémon GO form and encounter pool currently active.

What is a 100% Ferrothorn?

A 100% IV Ferrothorn has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Ferrothorn?

Lucky Ferrothorn is a Lucky Pokémon that requires less Stardust for power-ups.

Should I purify Shadow Ferrothorn?

Not automatically. Compare the value of the Shadow version with the Purified result before deciding.

Does XXL Ferrothorn have better stats?

No. Size classification does not automatically mean better IVs or battle performance.

Does Ferrothorn evolve?

Ferrothorn is the final evolution of Ferroseed.

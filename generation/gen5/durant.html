Why Durant's Typing Matters

Bug and Steel typing provides Durant with resistance to many attack types. However, Fire attacks are especially dangerous because both of Durant's types are weak to Fire.

Shiny Durant

Shiny Durant is an alternate-color version of regular Durant and is primarily valuable for collectors.

✨ Shiny Durant

Shiny status does not change Durant's base typing, battle stats or evolution status. Its main difference is its alternate appearance.

Shiny Durant Investment

  • Keep a Shiny Durant with strong IVs.
  • Consider keeping at least one for your collection.
  • Check its moves before investing Stardust.
  • Compare its IVs with your strongest regular Durant.

Shadow Durant

Shadow Durant should be evaluated separately from regular Durant because Shadow Pokémon have a different offensive and defensive battle profile.

🌑 Shadow Durant

Shadow Durant retains Durant's Bug / Steel typing while gaining the Shadow battle characteristics.

Shadow Durant Weakness

Shadow status does not change Durant's typing. Fire remains its major weakness.

Fire

Should You Power Up Shadow Durant?

  • Check its IVs before making a major investment.
  • Compare it with your existing Bug attackers.
  • Consider the increased Stardust requirement.
  • Check whether Frustration can currently be removed.
  • Do not purify automatically.

Purified Durant

Purified Durant is obtained by purifying an eligible Shadow Durant.

💠 Purified Durant

Purification removes Shadow status and changes the Pokémon back into a standard battle profile.

Lucky Durant

Lucky Durant is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades.

🍀 Lucky Durant

Durant FAQ

What type is Durant?

Durant is a Bug and Steel-type Pokémon.

What is Durant weak to?

Fire-type attacks are Durant's major weakness.

Does Durant evolve?

No. Durant does not have a normal evolution line.

Is Shiny Durant available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Is Shadow Durant different from regular Durant?

Shadow Durant has the same Bug / Steel typing but a different Shadow battle profile and resource cost.

Should I purify Shadow Durant?

Not automatically. Consider whether you value the Shadow battle characteristics or a purified version with improved IVs.

What is a 100% Durant?

A 100% Durant has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is Lucky Durant good?

Lucky Durant can be useful because Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

Does XXL Durant have higher battle stats?

XXL status is primarily a size characteristic and should not be confused with IVs or battle strength.

What is XXS Durant?

XXS Durant is a smaller-than-average size variant.

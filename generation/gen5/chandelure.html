Chandelure

Chandelure is a Ghost and Fire-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it is particularly valuable as a Ghost-type attacker and can also perform well in a Fire-type role. This guide covers regular, Shiny, Shadow, Purified, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL and XXS Chandelure.

01 • OVERVIEW

Chandelure Overview

Chandelure is a dual Ghost/Fire-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. It evolves from Lampent after the required evolution conditions are met. Its high Attack makes it particularly interesting for Pokémon GO PvE teams, especially when using Ghost-focused moves.

Why Chandelure's Typing Matters

Chandelure's Ghost/Fire combination gives it offensive access to two useful attacking roles. Ghost moves are especially useful against Psychic and Ghost targets, while Fire moves provide strong coverage against several Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel opponents.

09 • IV GUIDE

Chandelure IV Guide

Individual Values add points to Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 Chandelure is considered a perfect-IV or "100%" Chandelure.

What Is a 100% Chandelure?

A 100% Chandelure has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly called a hundo by Pokémon GO players.

10 • PVP

Chandelure PvP Guide

Chandelure can be used in PvP, although its very high maximum CP makes it more naturally suited to Master League than the lower leagues.

Master League

Chandelure's high Attack and Ghost/Fire typing give it interesting offensive pressure in Master League. However, its performance depends heavily on the current metagame and the opponents it encounters.

Great League & Ultra League

Standard Chandelure is less naturally suited to the CP limits of Great League and Ultra League. Specialized cups or limited formats can change its usefulness.

11 • SHINY

Shiny Chandelure

Shiny Chandelure is available in Pokémon GO. The Shiny form has an alternate coloration and can be evolved from an eligible Shiny Litwick through the evolution family.

✨

Shiny Chandelure

A collectible alternate-color form that retains Chandelure's Ghost/Fire typing and battle identity.

🕯️

Shiny Evolution

Shiny Litwick can be evolved through Lampent into Shiny Chandelure.

Is Shiny Chandelure Stronger?

No. Shiny status is primarily cosmetic and does not automatically increase Chandelure's battle stats.

16 • XXL

XXL Chandelure

XXL Chandelure is a size variant associated with Chandelure's size classification in Pokémon GO. Size is separate from IVs and does not automatically indicate stronger battle performance.

📏

XXL Chandelure

A larger-than-standard size specimen that can be valuable for collectors and size-based Pokédex completion.

🏆

Collector Value

An XXL Chandelure can be especially interesting when combined with Shiny, Lucky or high-IV status.

XXL vs IV

Size and IV are separate characteristics. An XXL Chandelure is not automatically a 100% Chandelure, and a hundo is not automatically XXL.

17 • XXS

XXS Chandelure

XXS Chandelure represents a very small size variant. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from the Pokémon's IV appraisal.

🔎

XXS Chandelure

A small-size Chandelure that can be valuable for collectors and players completing size variants.

📊

Size Is Not IV

XXS status does not mean poor IVs, just as XXL status does not mean perfect IVs.

Ideal Collector Combination

A Shiny, Lucky, 100% IV, XXL or XXS Chandelure can each represent different collection goals. A specimen that combines multiple rare characteristics can be especially desirable to collectors.

23 • FAQ

Chandelure FAQ

What type is Chandelure?

Chandelure is a Ghost and Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Chandelure weak to?

Chandelure is weak to Dark, Ghost, Ground, Rock and Water-type attacks.

What is Chandelure's max CP?

Chandelure's maximum CP at Level 50 is 3,695. Its Level 40 maximum is 3,268.

Is Chandelure good for raids?

Yes. Chandelure is particularly useful as a Ghost-type raid attacker and can also perform well in a Fire-focused role.

What is the best Ghost moveset for Chandelure?

Hex and Shadow Ball are a strong Ghost-focused moveset for Chandelure.

What is the best Fire moveset for Chandelure?

Fire Spin and Overheat are a strong Fire-focused moveset.

Is Shiny Chandelure available?

Yes. Shiny Chandelure is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Chandelure available?

Yes. Shadow Chandelure is available in Pokémon GO.

What is a 100% Chandelure?

A 100% Chandelure has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Can Chandelure be Lucky?

Yes. Chandelure can have Lucky status after the applicable trading mechanics.

Can Chandelure be XXL?

Yes. XXL is a size classification separate from IVs.

Can Chandelure be XXS?

Yes. XXS is a size classification separate from IVs.

Does XXL mean Chandelure has perfect IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Chandelure can have any eligible IV combination.

Does XXS mean Chandelure is weak?

No. XXS is a size characteristic and does not automatically determine battle strength.

Should I purify Shadow Chandelure?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow version with the purified result, particularly if you care about raid performance.

Is Chandelure good in Master League?

Chandelure can have Master League relevance, but its performance depends on the current metagame, moveset, IVs and battle format.

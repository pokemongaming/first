Why Munna's Typing Matters

Munna's pure Psychic typing gives it a straightforward matchup profile. It takes increased damage from Bug, Dark and Ghost attacks while resisting Fighting and Psychic-type damage.

What Is a 100% Munna?

A 100% Munna has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is also called a hundo. A perfect Munna reaches 1,294 CP at Level 50.

XXL Munna

XXL Munna is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system. Size does not automatically make Munna stronger in battle.

Does XXL Munna Have Higher Stats?

No. XXL status should not be confused with IV quality. A large Munna can have poor IVs, while a normal-sized Munna can have perfect IVs.

XXS Munna

XXS Munna represents the opposite end of the Pokémon GO size system and can be valuable to collectors who track size variants.

🔎
XXS Munna Extra-small size classification.
📖
Size Collection Useful for completing size-related collection goals.

Does XXS Munna Have Better IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXS Munna can have any valid IV combination.

Munna Evolution

Munna evolves into Musharna. In Pokémon GO, the evolution requires 50 Munna Candy and a Unova Stone.

Should You Evolve a 100% Munna?

If you own a perfect Munna, evolving it into Musharna preserves its IVs. For collectors, a hundo Munna can therefore make an excellent evolution candidate.

Munna FAQ

What type is Munna?

Munna is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon.

Purified Foongus

Purified Foongus is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Foongus. Purification changes its status and removes the Shadow battle modifier.

  • Purified Pokémon are separate collection variations.
  • Purification can change IV values according to Pokémon GO mechanics.
  • Purified Pokémon have different resource considerations.
  • Consider the Shadow version before deciding to purify.

Lucky Foongus

Lucky Foongus can be obtained through eligible Pokémon GO trades. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust when powered up, making them attractive long-term investment options.

Lucky Benefit

The main advantage of a Lucky Pokémon is reduced Stardust cost when powering it up.

Collector note:

Lucky status is independent from size, IV quality and shiny status, allowing some combinations to be especially rare for collectors.

Watchog Overview

Why Watchog's Typing Matters

Watchog is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. Its typing gives it a straightforward defensive profile, but it does not provide a large offensive advantage against many raid bosses. Normal-type attacks are generally most useful against targets vulnerable to Normal damage.

09 • IV GUIDE

100% IV Watchog

A 100% IV Watchog has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. For collectors, a perfect Watchog can be a worthwhile specimen even when the Pokémon is not a major raid attacker.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What Does 100% Watchog Mean?

100% IV Watchog means that the Pokémon has the maximum possible individual values in Attack, Defense and HP. The term "hundo" is commonly used by Pokémon GO players for a perfect-IV Pokémon.

Collector Tip

Do not transfer a perfect Watchog simply because it is not a top raid attacker. Hundo Pokémon can have long-term collection value.

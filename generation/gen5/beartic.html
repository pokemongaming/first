Beartic

Beartic is an Ice-type Pokémon from the Unova region. In Pokémon GO, it has a high Attack stat for its species and can be used as an Ice-type attacker, while its different variants such as Shiny, Shadow, Lucky, XXL and XXS make it useful for both collectors and battle-focused players.

08 — IV GUIDE

Beartic IV Guide

Individual Values add extra Attack, Defense and HP points to the Pokémon's underlying stats. The maximum IV combination is 15/15/15.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
💯 100% Beartic

A 100% Beartic has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. In the Pokémon GO community, this is commonly called a "hundo."

A perfect Beartic is particularly attractive to collectors, PvE investors and players who enjoy completing their Pokédex with high-IV specimens.

Is 100% Beartic Worth Powering Up?

A perfect IV does not automatically make a Pokémon a top meta attacker. Evaluate the moveset, current Ice-type alternatives and your own resource budget before investing heavily.

09 — PVP

Beartic PvP Guide

Beartic's Ice typing can give it useful offensive coverage, but its performance depends heavily on the league, moveset and current PvP metagame.

Great League

Beartic's CP and stat distribution can make it situational in Great League. League eligibility and the current moveset should be checked before building a dedicated Great League specimen.

Ultra League

Beartic can be considered for specialist Ultra League builds when its Ice coverage fits the current format. However, its defensive profile means it requires careful matchup selection.

Master League

Beartic's maximum CP is substantially lower than that of many traditional Master League staples, limiting its general usefulness in open Master League play.

Frustration Consideration

Shadow Pokémon can have the Charged Move Frustration. Because Rocket events can affect when Frustration can be removed, players should check the current event rules before using Charged TMs on a Shadow Beartic.

Should You Power Up Shadow Beartic?

  • Check its current Charged Move situation.

16 — SIZE VARIANT

XXL Beartic

XXL Beartic is an unusually large individual. Size is a separate characteristic from IVs, so an XXL Beartic can have any IV combination.

🐻‍❄️

XXL Beartic

An extra-large Beartic that can be valuable to collectors interested in size records, showcases and medals.

📊

XXL + 100%

A Beartic can theoretically combine an XXL size with perfect 15/15/15 IVs, making it a particularly attractive collector specimen.

Does XXL Increase Beartic's Stats?

No. XXL size does not automatically increase Beartic's Attack, Defense, HP or CP. Size and IVs are separate characteristics.

17 — SIZE VARIANT

XXS Beartic

XXS Beartic is an unusually small individual. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS Pokémon are primarily interesting from a size and collection perspective.

🐻‍❄️

XXS Beartic

A very small Beartic that can be kept as a special size specimen.

📏

XXS + Perfect IV

Size and IV characteristics are independent, allowing a Beartic to be both XXS and 100% IV.

Does XXS Reduce Beartic's Stats?

No. XXS size does not inherently reduce Beartic's Attack, Defense, HP or CP.

22 — FAQ

Beartic FAQ

What type is Beartic?

Beartic is a pure Ice-type Pokémon.

What is Beartic weak to?

Beartic is weak to Fire, Fighting, Rock and Steel-type attacks.

What is Beartic's Max CP?

Beartic has a Level 50 maximum CP of 3,439 with perfect IVs.

What are Beartic's stats?

Beartic has 233 Attack, 152 Defense and 216 HP in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Beartic available?

Yes. Shiny Beartic is available as an alternate coloration.

Is Shadow Beartic available?

Shadow availability depends on Pokémon GO's Team GO Rocket and event rotations. Check the current game schedule for the latest encounter method.

Can Shadow Beartic be purified?

An eligible Shadow Beartic can be purified. However, players should compare the Shadow benefits with the purified result before making that decision.

What is Lucky Beartic?

Lucky Beartic is a Beartic that has received Lucky status through trading. Lucky Pokémon have useful Stardust-related power-up benefits.

What is a 100% Beartic?

A 100% Beartic has perfect 15/15/15 IVs in Attack, Defense and HP.

What is XXL Beartic?

XXL Beartic is an unusually large individual. Size does not automatically increase its IVs or combat stats.

What is XXS Beartic?

XXS Beartic is an unusually small individual. Like XXL status, XXS size is separate from IVs.

Is Beartic good for raids?

Beartic can provide Ice-type raid damage, but its usefulness should be compared with stronger Ice attackers and the current Pokémon GO meta.

Is Beartic good in PvP?

Beartic can have situational PvP value depending on the league and current moveset, but it is not generally considered a universal PvP staple.

Does XXL Beartic have higher CP?

No. XXL status itself does not directly increase Beartic's CP. CP is determined by its stats, IVs and level.

Does Shiny Beartic have better stats?

No. Shiny status is primarily a visual variant and does not inherently give Beartic higher combat stats.

Is a 100% Beartic always the best Beartic?

Not necessarily. IVs are only one part of a Pokémon's usefulness. Moveset, Shadow status, level, role and current meta relevance also matter.

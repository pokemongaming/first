Regular Darumaka

🔥 Standard Darumaka

Regular Darumaka is the standard version of the species. It retains its normal appearance and can be evolved into Darmanitan when the required evolution conditions are met.

Regular Darumaka Guide

  • Check IVs before committing major resources.
  • Consider whether you plan to evolve it.
  • Keep high-IV specimens for long-term projects.
  • Compare the evolved form with your existing Fire attackers.

Shiny Darumaka

✨ Shiny Darumaka

Shiny Darumaka has an alternate appearance compared with the standard version. Its battle stats and base gameplay role remain tied to the same species.

Should You Evolve Shiny Darumaka?

Collectors may choose to keep one Shiny Darumaka unevolved while evolving another specimen into Shiny Darmanitan.

Shadow Darumaka

🌑 Shadow Darumaka

Shadow Darumaka has the Shadow battle profile, giving it different offensive and defensive characteristics from a standard specimen.

Purified Darumaka

💎 Purified Darumaka

Purified Darumaka is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Darumaka. Purification changes its Shadow status and can affect IVs and resource requirements.

Purified vs Shadow Darumaka

  • Purification removes the Shadow status.
  • IVs may increase after purification.
  • The Shadow battle profile is removed.
  • Purified Pokémon have different investment considerations.

Lucky Darumaka

🍀 Lucky Darumaka

Lucky Darumaka is obtained through eligible Pokémon trades. Lucky Pokémon are especially useful for players planning long-term power-up projects.

Why Lucky Darumaka Can Be Valuable

  • Useful for Stardust-efficient power-up projects.
  • Can be valuable when evolving into Darmanitan.
  • A good candidate for collectors.
  • Consider IVs before investing heavily.

XXL Darumaka

📏 XXL Darumaka

XXL Darumaka is an unusually large specimen. Size variants are primarily collectible distinctions and can also be relevant to Pokémon GO size-based systems and showcases.

XXL Darumaka Collection Value

  • Rare size variation for collectors.
  • Can be useful in eligible Showcase systems.
  • Battle stats should not be confused with visual size.
  • Keep unusually large specimens if you collect size variants.

XXS Darumaka

🔍 XXS Darumaka

XXS Darumaka is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL, the XXS designation is primarily related to size and collection rather than standard battle strength.

XXS Darumaka Collection Value

  • Rare small-size collectible.
  • Useful for players collecting every size variant.
  • May be relevant to future or eligible size systems.
  • Does not automatically mean stronger or weaker battle stats.

Darumaka Evolution

Purified Gigalith

Purified Gigalith is created by purifying an eligible Shadow member of the Roggenrola evolution family. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can affect its IVs and resource requirements.

✨ Purified Gigalith

21 — FAQ

Gigalith FAQ

What type is Gigalith?

Gigalith is a pure Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Gigalith weak to?

Gigalith is weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel attacks.

What does Gigalith evolve from?

Gigalith evolves from Boldore and is the final evolution of Roggenrola.

Is Shiny Gigalith available?

Shiny Gigalith can be obtained by evolving eligible Shiny members of its evolution family.

Is Gigalith good for raids?

Gigalith can be a useful Rock-type raid attacker, particularly with a strong Rock-focused moveset.

What is the best Gigalith moveset?

For a Rock-focused role, Smack Down and Meteor Beam are commonly associated with Gigalith's strongest Rock-oriented performance.

What is a 100% Gigalith?

A 100% Gigalith has perfect 15/15/15 Individual Values.

Is Shadow Gigalith stronger than regular Gigalith?

Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile with increased offensive pressure and reduced durability, so the better choice depends on the battle role.

Should I purify Shadow Gigalith?

Not automatically. Consider your long-term goal before removing the Shadow status.

What is Lucky Gigalith?

Lucky Gigalith is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through the Pokémon GO trading system and requires less Stardust to power up.

Does XXL Gigalith have stronger stats?

XXL classification is based on size and does not automatically provide stronger battle stats.

Does XXS Gigalith have weaker stats?

No. XXS status is primarily a size classification and does not automatically reduce Gigalith's battle stats.

Mandibuzz PvP Guide

Mandibuzz is primarily valued for PvP rather than raid damage. Its combination of high bulk, useful typing and charged-move pressure makes it a notable option in CP-limited formats.

Great League

Mandibuzz can be useful in Great League when its CP, IV spread and moveset are optimized for the format. Its bulk allows it to remain on the field for long periods and absorb substantial damage.

Ultra League

Mandibuzz can also be considered for Ultra League teams. The increased CP limit allows trainers to invest more heavily in its defensive profile.

Master League

Mandibuzz is generally less suited to unrestricted Master League battles because its maximum CP is much lower than many Legendary and high-CP Pokémon.

Mandibuzz IV Guide

100% IV Mandibuzz

A 100% IV Mandibuzz has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is an excellent collector specimen and can be useful in formats where a high-Attack IV spread is desirable.

Important PvP IV Note

For CP-limited PvP, a lower Attack IV with higher Defense and HP can sometimes provide a better stat product. Always evaluate the exact league before transferring a low-Attack Mandibuzz.

Shadow Mandibuzz and Frustration

Newly obtained Shadow Pokémon can have Frustration. Special Rocket events can provide opportunities to replace it with another Charged Move. Always check the current event rules before spending resources.

Purification warning:

Do not purify a Shadow Mandibuzz automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, especially if the Pokémon has desirable IVs or collector value.

Mandibuzz Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Mandibuzz investment can require significant Candy, Candy XL and Stardust, particularly when building a high-level PvP specimen.

Mandibuzz FAQ

What type is Mandibuzz?

Mandibuzz is a Dark/Flying-type Pokémon.

What is Mandibuzz weak to?

Mandibuzz is weak to Electric, Ice, Rock and Fairy attacks.

Is Mandibuzz good in Pokémon GO?

Mandibuzz is particularly valuable in PvP because of its high HP and Defense and its useful Dark/Flying typing.

Is Mandibuzz good for raids?

Mandibuzz is generally not considered a top raid attacker because of its relatively low Attack stat.

Is Shiny Mandibuzz available?

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO release and encounter rules. Check current game data before publishing availability claims.

What is Shadow Mandibuzz?

Shadow Mandibuzz is the Shadow variant of Mandibuzz. It retains Dark/Flying typing but has the standard Shadow battle modifier.

Should I purify Shadow Mandibuzz?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, especially when the Shadow Mandibuzz has desirable IVs.

What is a Lucky Mandibuzz?

Lucky Mandibuzz is a Mandibuzz that became Lucky through the Pokémon GO trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon receive reduced Stardust power-up costs.

What is a 100% Mandibuzz?

A 100% Mandibuzz has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is 100% Mandibuzz best for PvP?

Not necessarily. CP-limited PvP often favors lower Attack and higher Defense and HP IV combinations.

What is XXL Mandibuzz?

XXL Mandibuzz is an extra-large size variant. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Mandibuzz?

XXS Mandibuzz is an extra-small size variant. XXS status does not automatically indicate better or worse IVs.

Does Mandibuzz evolve?

Yes. Mandibuzz evolves from Vullaby and is the final evolutionary stage of that family.

What is Mandibuzz's maximum CP?

Mandibuzz has a maximum CP of approximately 2,271 under standard maximum-level conditions. Always keep database values synchronized with current Pokémon GO data.

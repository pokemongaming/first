Purified Mienshao

Purified Mienshao is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Mienshao. Purification changes the Pokémon's Shadow status and can alter its IVs and other Pokémon GO attributes.

Lucky Mienshao

Lucky Mienshao is a special Pokémon GO status that can result from trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up and are guaranteed to have a minimum IV floor.

LUCKY

Lucky Mienshao

A Lucky Mienshao can be useful as a lower-Stardust investment project, particularly when the Pokémon has strong IVs and a moveset suited to your goals.

Why Keep a Lucky Mienshao?

  • Lower Stardust cost for power-ups.
  • Useful for collectors building Lucky Pokédex entries.
  • Can combine Lucky status with strong IVs.
  • Useful as a long-term collection project.

XXL Mienshao

XXL Mienshao is an unusually large individual Mienshao. Size is separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

XXL XXL Mienshao

XXL status identifies Mienshao as an unusually large specimen. It is primarily a collection and size distinction rather than a direct increase to combat stats.

XXL vs IVs

XXL size and IV quality are independent. An XXL Mienshao is not automatically a 100% IV Pokémon.

XXS Mienshao

XXS Mienshao is an unusually small individual. Like XXL status, XXS is separate from IV quality and does not automatically indicate better battle statistics.

XXS XXS Mienshao

An XXS Mienshao can be valuable to collectors who want to document the full range of Pokémon GO size variations.

Mienshao FAQ

What type is Mienshao?

Mienshao is a pure Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Mienshao weak to?

Mienshao is weak to Fairy, Flying and Psychic attacks.

Does Mienshao evolve?

Mienshao evolves from Mienfoo and does not have a standard evolution after Mienshao.

Is Shiny Mienshao available?

If Shiny Mienshao is available in the current Pokémon GO game data and encounter pool, eligible encounters can produce its Shiny version.

What is 100% Mienshao?

A 100% Mienshao has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

What is Shadow Mienshao?

Shadow Mienshao is the Shadow version of Mienshao. Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability.

Should I purify Shadow Mienshao?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions according to your battle and collection goals before purifying.

What is Lucky Mienshao?

Lucky Mienshao is a Lucky Pokémon obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

What is XXL Mienshao?

XXL Mienshao is an unusually large individual. XXL status is independent of IV quality.

What is XXS Mienshao?

XXS Mienshao is an unusually small individual. XXS status is independent of IV quality.

Is XXL Mienshao automatically 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Mienshao can have any IV combination.

Is XXS Mienshao automatically 100% IV?

No. XXS status does not mean that Mienshao has perfect IVs.

Is Mienshao good for raids?

Mienshao can provide Fighting-type damage, but its raid value should be compared with the stronger Fighting attackers available in the current Pokémon GO meta.

Is Mienshao good for PvP?

Its PvP value depends on its current moveset, stats, league CP limits and the current metagame. Check current rankings before investing heavily.

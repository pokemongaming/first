Stoutland

01 • OVERVIEW

Stoutland Overview

Stoutland is the final evolutionary stage of Lillipup and Herdier. It was introduced in Generation 5 and is a pure Normal-type Pokémon. In Pokémon GO, Stoutland has a much more modest PvE role than many Legendary or pseudo-Legendary Pokémon, but it can still be useful for collection, special-form hunting and certain PvP formats.

Why Stoutland's Typing Matters

Stoutland has a pure Normal typing, meaning it does not gain a secondary type to create additional offensive coverage. Normal-type Pokémon are generally straightforward to understand defensively: Fighting-type attacks are their primary weakness, while Ghost-type attacks cannot normally damage them.

08 • IV GUIDE

100% IV Stoutland

A 100% IV Stoutland has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. This is commonly called a "Hundo" by Pokémon GO players.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV
100% / Hundo Stoutland

A 15/15/15 Stoutland is a perfect-IV specimen. Its perfect IV status increases its collector value, although perfect IVs do not automatically make a Pokémon the strongest attacker.

Should You Keep a 100% Stoutland?

  • Keep it if you collect perfect IV Pokémon.
  • Consider its PvP suitability before transferring it.
  • Check its level and Stardust cost before powering it up.
  • Consider keeping a Hundo for future move updates.

14 • SIZE

XXL & XXS Stoutland

XXL and XXS describe Pokémon size categories. They are separate from IV quality and do not mean that the Pokémon has stronger battle statistics.

XXL Stoutland Extra-large size category

An XXL Stoutland is a larger-than-normal specimen and can be useful for size collections, showcases and other size-related features.

XXS Stoutland Extra-small size category

An XXS Stoutland is an unusually small specimen. Its size is independent of whether it is Shiny, Lucky, Shadow or a perfect IV Pokémon.

Can Size Combine With Other Attributes?

Yes. A Stoutland can potentially be valuable for multiple reasons at the same time. For example, a player may encounter a Shiny XXL Stoutland, a Lucky XXS Stoutland or another combination of collection attributes.

20 • FAQ

Stoutland FAQ

What type is Stoutland?

Stoutland is a pure Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Stoutland weak against?

Stoutland is weak to Fighting-type attacks.

Is Stoutland a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Stoutland is a regular Pokémon from Generation 5.

What Pokémon evolve into Stoutland?

Stoutland evolves from Herdier, which evolves from Lillipup.

Is Shiny Stoutland available?

Shiny Stoutland can be available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters when the relevant Shiny forms are enabled.

Is Shadow Stoutland different from regular Stoutland?

Yes. Shadow Stoutland has the Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics and should be evaluated separately from regular Stoutland.

Should I purify Shadow Stoutland?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions based on your intended use, IVs, moves and available resources.

What is a 100% Stoutland?

A 100% or Hundo Stoutland has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is Lucky Stoutland?

Lucky Stoutland is a Stoutland that has gained Lucky status through the trading mechanic. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups.

What is XXL Stoutland?

XXL Stoutland is a Stoutland that falls into the game's extra-large size category.

What is XXS Stoutland?

XXS Stoutland is an extra-small Stoutland. Size is separate from IVs and does not mean the Pokémon is weaker or stronger in battle.

Can Stoutland be Shiny and Lucky?

A Pokémon can have multiple collection attributes. A Stoutland can therefore be particularly valuable when several desirable attributes occur together.

Is Stoutland good for raids?

Stoutland is generally not considered a top-tier raid attacker. Players should compare it with current meta attackers before investing heavily for PvE.

Is Stoutland good for PvP?

Stoutland can be tested in PvP depending on the league, moveset and current metagame. Its usefulness can change as Pokémon GO receives balance updates.

Does Stoutland evolve further?

No. Stoutland is the final stage of the Lillipup evolutionary family.

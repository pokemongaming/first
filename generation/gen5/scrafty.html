Scrafty Overview

Scrafty is the evolved form of Scraggy and is a dual Dark/Fighting-type Pokémon. Its unusual typing gives it a distinctive defensive and offensive profile. In Pokémon GO, Scrafty is generally more interesting as a PvP-oriented Pokémon than as a dedicated raid attacker.

Shadow Scrafty Battle Profile

The Shadow mechanic generally increases offensive pressure while reducing durability. Whether Shadow Scrafty is preferable depends on the format and the player's investment goals.

Scrafty FAQ

What type is Scrafty?

Scrafty is a Dark and Fighting-type Pokémon.

Does Scrafty evolve?

Scrafty is the evolved form of Scraggy and does not evolve further.

Is Shiny Scrafty available?

Shiny availability depends on eligible Pokémon GO encounter methods and current game availability.

What is Shadow Scrafty?

Shadow Scrafty is a Shadow variant with different battle characteristics and resource considerations.

Should I purify Shadow Scrafty?

Not automatically. Compare the Shadow and Purified versions based on your intended use before deciding.

Is Lucky Scrafty good?

Lucky status can make a Scrafty attractive for long-term investment, but its IVs and intended PvP league should still be considered.

What is a 100% Scrafty?

A 100% Scrafty has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Is XXL Scrafty stronger?

XXL status refers primarily to Pokémon size and should not be confused with IV quality or battle strength.

Is XXS Scrafty weaker?

No. XXS refers to size and does not automatically determine IVs or battle performance.

Is Scrafty good for PvP?

Scrafty can have significant PvP utility, particularly in suitable CP-limited formats, depending on the current metagame and moveset.

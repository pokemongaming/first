Why Emboar's Typing Matters

Emboar combines Fire and Fighting typing, giving it useful offensive coverage against Steel, Ice, Bug, Grass, Rock and Dark-related matchups. Its dual typing also creates several weaknesses that trainers should consider when using it defensively.

Purified Emboar

Purified Emboar is created by purifying an eligible Shadow Emboar. Purification changes its Shadow status and can also modify its IVs.

Should You Purify Shadow Emboar?

Purification should be a deliberate decision. If your goal is maximizing Shadow raid damage, keeping the Shadow form may be preferable. If you value a purified specimen, lower costs or a potential Hundo, purification may be worth considering.

XXL & XXS Emboar

Pokémon GO can assign different size classifications to Pokémon. XXL and XXS Emboar are special size variants rather than separate species or forms.

XXL

XXL Emboar

An XXL Emboar is significantly larger than the normal size range. XXL status is useful for collectors and size-related medals.

  • Large size classification.
  • Same Fire/Fighting typing.
  • Does not automatically mean higher IVs.
  • Useful for size collections.
XXS

XXS Emboar

An XXS Emboar is significantly smaller than the standard size range. Like XXL Pokémon, XXS status is mainly a size classification.

  • Small size classification.
  • Same Fire/Fighting typing.
  • Does not guarantee perfect IVs.
  • Useful for collector goals.
Important:

XXL and XXS size classifications should not be confused with IV quality. A Pokémon can be XXL, XXS, Hundo, Shiny or Lucky independently, depending on its individual characteristics.

Emboar FAQ

What type is Emboar?

Emboar is a Fire/Fighting-type Pokémon.

What is Emboar weak to?

Emboar is weak to Water, Ground, Flying and Psychic-type attacks.

Is Shiny Emboar available in Pokémon GO?

Yes. Shiny Emboar can be obtained when its Shiny form is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters or evolution.

Is Shadow Emboar available?

Shadow availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket and Pokémon GO rotation.

What is a 100% Emboar?

A 100% Emboar, also called a Hundo, has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What is a Lucky Emboar?

Lucky Emboar is obtained through trading and has reduced Stardust power-up costs compared with standard Pokémon.

What is XXL Emboar?

XXL Emboar is an Emboar with a particularly large size classification. It is not a different species.

What is XXS Emboar?

XXS Emboar is an unusually small Emboar. The classification is separate from IV quality.

Is Shadow Emboar stronger than regular Emboar?

Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability. The better choice depends on the battle and investment goal.

Should I purify Shadow Emboar?

Do not purify automatically. Compare the Shadow and purified versions first, especially if your goal is raid damage or collecting.

Is Emboar good for raids?

Emboar can be useful as a Fire-type raid attacker with an appropriate Fire moveset, although its ranking can change as new Pokémon and moves are introduced.

Is Emboar good in PvP?

Emboar can have niche PvP applications, but its performance depends heavily on the current moveset, league and metagame.

Does Emboar evolve?

Emboar is the final evolution of Tepig. It does not evolve into another Pokémon.

Can a Shiny Emboar be Lucky?

A Shiny Pokémon can also become Lucky through the trading system when the Lucky condition is triggered.

Can Emboar be both Hundo and Lucky?

Yes. A Lucky Emboar can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs, although this combination depends on the IV result of the trade.

Can Emboar be Shiny and XXL?

Yes. Shiny status and size classification are separate characteristics, so a Shiny Emboar can also have an XXL or XXS size.

Can Shadow Emboar be Hundo?

Yes. A Shadow Emboar can have 15/15/15 IVs and would then be considered a Shadow Hundo.

Why Charmander's Typing Matters

Charmander's pure Fire typing gives it a straightforward offensive identity. Fire attacks are particularly useful against Grass, Bug, Ice and Steel Pokémon.

Its defensive weaknesses include Water, Ground and Rock, which means Charmander generally needs to avoid these attack types in battle.

09 • PVP

Charmander PvP Guide

Charmander is not generally a major open-league PvP threat because of its relatively low stats. However, it can become relevant in special cups or formats with restricted Pokémon selections.

Great League

Charmander can technically fit below the Great League CP limit, but its overall performance is generally inferior to stronger Fire-type options.

Ultra League

Charmander is generally not a preferred Ultra League investment because its CP ceiling and stats are too low.

Master League

Charmander is not suitable for conventional Master League competition. Its evolved forms are much more relevant for higher-CP combat.

PvP Investment Advice

If you enjoy Little Cup or special cup formats, check the current rules before investing in Charmander specifically for PvP.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Charmander

For New Players

Charmander is best viewed as an investment into its evolution family rather than as a final combat Pokémon. A strong Charmander can eventually become a useful Charizard.

Before Evolving

  • Check the Charmander's IVs.
  • Check whether it is Shiny or Shadow.
  • Check current and upcoming evolution-exclusive moves.
  • Consider whether you want Charizard for raids or PvP.
  • Keep enough Candy for the complete evolution line.

Before Transferring

Avoid transferring Charmander immediately after catching it. Check its appraisal, Shiny status, Shadow status, event background or special move first.

Long-Term Investment

For long-term value, prioritize specimens that can become strong Charizard builds. A high-quality Charmander can eventually provide access to Charizard and its Mega Evolution forms.

20 • FAQ

Charmander FAQ

Is Charmander a Starter Pokémon?

Yes. Charmander is one of the three original Kanto Starter Pokémon.

What type is Charmander?

Charmander is a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

What is Charmander weak to?

Charmander is weak to Water, Ground and Rock attacks.

What is Charmander's Max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Charmander reaches roughly 1,106 CP.

Is Charmander good for raids?

Charmander itself is not a top raid attacker. Its greatest PvE value comes from evolving it into Charizard.

Does Charmander evolve?

Yes. Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard.

What does Charmander evolve into?

Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and eventually Charizard.

How many stages are in the Charmander family?

The family has three stages: Charmander, Charmeleon and Charizard.

Can Charmander be shiny?

Yes. Shiny Charmander is available through eligible Pokémon GO encounters.

Can Shiny Charmander evolve into Shiny Charizard?

Yes. A Shiny Charmander retains its Shiny identity when evolved through Charmeleon into Charizard.

Is Shadow Charmander available?

Shadow Charmander can be available through Team GO Rocket-related encounters and special Pokémon GO rotations. Availability can change over time.

Does Shadow Charmander have different typing?

No. Shadow Charmander remains a pure Fire-type Pokémon.

Is Shadow Charmander stronger than regular Charmander?

Shadow Pokémon receive an offensive bonus and a defensive penalty, so Shadow Charmander has a different battle profile from regular Charmander.

Should I purify Shadow Charmander?

Do not automatically purify it. Consider whether you want to eventually build a Shadow Charizard before making a permanent decision.

Can Shadow Charmander evolve into Shadow Charizard?

Yes. A Shadow Charmander can be evolved through its evolution line while retaining its Shadow status.

Is Charmander good for PvP?

Charmander is generally limited in standard PvP because of its low stats, although special cups can create different opportunities.

What is the best Charmander moveset?

For a Fire-focused build, Ember paired with a suitable Fire-type Charged Move is the natural approach.

Should I keep a 100% IV Charmander?

Yes. A perfect Charmander can be valuable as a collection piece and as the foundation for a perfect Charizard.

Is Charmander worth powering up?

Usually, the better long-term approach is to evolve Charmander into Charizard before making major Stardust investments.

Can Charmander become Mega Charizard?

Charmander itself cannot Mega Evolve. It must first be evolved into Charizard, which can then access its Mega Evolution forms when the relevant mechanics are available.

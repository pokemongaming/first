Raticate Overview

Raticate is the final evolution of the Rattata family. It is a pure Normal-type Pokémon and is registered as Pokédex #020. Its role in Pokémon GO is more limited than many stronger evolved Pokémon, but it remains useful for collection goals, evolution-family completion and certain PvP situations.

Why Raticate's Typing Matters

Normal typing gives Raticate a simple offensive identity. Its Normal-type attacks benefit from STAB, while its defensive profile gives it resistance to Ghost attacks. However, Fighting-type attacks are super effective against Raticate.

07 • COUNTERS

09 • PVP

Raticate PvP Guide

Raticate is not a major PvP powerhouse, but its low maximum CP makes it much more relevant to Great League than to Master League.

Great League

Raticate can be considered for Great League or limited cups where its moveset and typing fit the format. However, it is generally a niche choice rather than a universal meta Pokémon.

Ultra League

Raticate's relatively low maximum CP makes it difficult to justify in Ultra League compared with Pokémon that can reach the league's CP ceiling more efficiently.

Master League

Raticate is not a practical Master League investment. Its 1,956 Level-50 maximum CP is far below the CP levels of typical Master League Pokémon.

PvP Investment Advice

If you want to experiment with Raticate in PvP, evaluate individual IVs and the current move balance rather than simply choosing the highest CP specimen.

How to Use Raticate

Great League Strategy

Long-Term Investment

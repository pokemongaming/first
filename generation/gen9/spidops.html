Should You Power Up Spidops for Raids?

For most players, no. If your primary goal is raid damage, there are substantially stronger Bug, Fire and Flying attackers. Spidops is better evaluated as a collection or PvP project.

Spidops PvP Guide

PvP is where Spidops becomes much more interesting. Its defensive profile, flexible Fast Moves and debuff-oriented Charged Moves give it meaningful utility in Great League formats.

Great League

Great League is the most natural competitive environment for Spidops. A low Attack IV combined with high Defense and suitable HP can allow a Spidops to remain under the 1,500 CP limit while maximizing its overall battle stats.

Ultra League

Spidops can be used in higher CP formats, but its relatively low maximum CP limits its ceiling compared with Pokémon specifically suited to Ultra League.

Master League

Master League is generally not the format where Spidops shines. Its maximum CP of 1,907 is far below the level commonly required for competitive Master League teams.

PvP Build to Consider

Bug Bite + Lunge + Rock Tomb

PvP rankings change with cups, move updates and the current metagame. Treat rankings as format-dependent rather than permanent.

What Is a Hundo Spidops?

A Hundo Spidops has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. It is commonly represented by the in-game 4-star appraisal and the search term 4*.

100% IV Spidops — Hundo Guide

A 100% IV Spidops has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs. While it is not a major raid attacker, a Hundo can be a satisfying collection piece and a useful fully optimized specimen for certain PvP or personal projects.

Spidops Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Investment decisions should be based on how you actually plan to use Spidops. For most players, Candy is more useful for evolution and unlocking additional moves than for high-level raid investment.

  • Save enough Tarountula Candy for evolution.
  • Consider the second Charged Move before powering up.
  • Build a PvP IV candidate if you enjoy Great League.
  • Keep a Hundo for collection purposes.
  • Keep interesting Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens.
  • Do not spend large amounts of Stardust solely because a Spidops has perfect IVs.

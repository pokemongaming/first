Why Skeledirge's Typing Matters

Skeledirge's Fire/Ghost combination gives it a broad resistance profile while also creating five major weaknesses. It resists or heavily resists several common attack types, including Normal, Fighting and Bug, while taking increased damage from Dark, Ghost, Ground, Rock and Water attacks.

09 • PVP

Skeledirge PvP Guide

Skeledirge can be relevant in GO Battle League, particularly because its Fire/Ghost typing gives it useful matchup opportunities and access to strong charged attacks.

Great League

Skeledirge is naturally above the Great League CP limit when fully powered, so Great League performance requires a specifically low-level specimen and should be evaluated using PvP IV rankings rather than the standard 15/15/15 raid-oriented build.

Ultra League

Skeledirge has considerably more room to operate in the Ultra League. A properly selected IV spread and moveset can make it an interesting option depending on the current meta.

Master League

Its maximum CP of 3,422 allows Skeledirge to enter the Master League, although its lower overall ceiling means it faces significant competition from higher-stat Pokémon.

PvP Build to Test

Incinerate + Torch Song + Shadow Ball

12 • PURIFIED

Purified Skeledirge

A Purified Skeledirge would be the result of purifying an eligible Shadow Skeledirge. Purification changes the Pokémon's status, IVs and resource requirements.

✨ PURIFIED SKELEDIRGE

Purified Pokémon receive improvements to their IV values and receive the Purified status.

What Happens When a Shadow Pokémon Is Purified?

  • Each IV receives a guaranteed +2 increase, capped at 15.
  • The Pokémon becomes Purified.
  • Purification changes the Shadow battle modifier.
  • Purified Pokémon receive Return as their special charged move when applicable.
  • Purified Pokémon receive discounted resource costs.

Should You Purify Skeledirge?

Purification should be a deliberate decision rather than an automatic upgrade. If you value the Shadow damage profile, keeping the Pokémon Shadow may be preferable. If you want the Purified status, IV boost and discounted investment costs, purification can make sense.

Purification Decision

Compare Shadow damage, IV improvement, move availability and your collection goals before purifying.

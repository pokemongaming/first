Glimmet FAQ

What type is Glimmet?

Glimmet is a Rock- and Poison-type Pokémon.

What is Glimmet weak to?

Glimmet has several weaknesses due to its Rock/Poison typing, including Ground, Steel, Water, Psychic and Grass attacks.

Does Glimmet evolve?

Yes. Glimmet evolves into Glimmora.

Is Shiny Glimmet available?

Shiny availability should be checked against the current Pokémon GO encounter and event data.

What is a 100% Glimmet?

A 100% Glimmet has perfect Attack, Defense and HP IVs: 15/15/15.

What is XXL Glimmet?

XXL Glimmet is an unusually large specimen. XXL status is separate from IV quality.

What is XXS Glimmet?

XXS Glimmet is an unusually small specimen. Like XXL status, XXS status is separate from IVs.

Can Glimmet be Lucky?

Lucky status is obtained through Pokémon GO trading when the relevant Pokémon can be traded.

Is Glimmet good for raids?

Glimmet is generally not a priority raid attacker. Its evolutionary and collection value is more important for most players.

Is Glimmet good for PvP?

Glimmet has more limited PvP potential than many fully evolved Pokémon. Its usefulness depends on its current moveset and the specific format.

Should I evolve my 100% Glimmet?

If you want a perfect Glimmora, a 15/15/15 Glimmet is an excellent candidate for evolution.

Should I keep a Shiny Glimmet?

Yes, especially if you collect Shiny Pokémon. You may also want to keep one for evolving into Shiny Glimmora.

Is Shadow Glimmet available?

Shadow availability changes over time. Check current Pokémon GO Team GO Rocket data before claiming that Shadow Glimmet is currently obtainable.

Can Shadow Glimmet become Purified Glimmet?

If Shadow Glimmet is available and eligible for purification, it can be purified according to Pokémon GO's current purification mechanics.

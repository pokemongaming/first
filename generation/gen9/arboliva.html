Arboliva FAQ

What type is Arboliva?

Arboliva is a Grass / Normal-type Pokémon.

What is Arboliva weak against?

Arboliva's Grass / Normal typing makes it vulnerable to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, Bug and Fighting-type attacks.

Is Arboliva good for raids?

Arboliva can be used as a Grass-type attacker, but its value should be compared with the stronger Grass attackers currently available.

What is 100% Arboliva?

A 100% Arboliva has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO release status of the Smoliv evolutionary family. Check current game data before claiming an active encounter method.

What is Shadow Arboliva?

How do I evolve Smoliv into Arboliva?

Is Arboliva good in PvP?

Can one Arboliva have multiple special attributes?

Different Pokémon GO attributes describe different properties. Depending on the game's mechanics and eligibility, collectors may pursue combinations such as Shiny, Lucky, perfect IV and size characteristics.

Why Sinistcha's Typing Matters

The Grass/Ghost combination gives Sinistcha a mixture of useful resistances and several important weaknesses. Its Ghost typing also gives it access to Ghost-type offensive pressure through moves such as Shadow Ball and Poltergeist.

10 • PVP

Sinistcha PvP Guide

Sinistcha can be used in PvP, although its usefulness depends strongly on league, IVs, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Great League Sinistcha generally favors low Attack IVs and higher defensive IVs when the individual Pokémon can fit under the CP cap.

Ultra League

Sinistcha has a more natural CP range for Ultra League than Great League, and its Grass/Ghost typing gives it interesting matchup potential.

Master League

Sinistcha can reach 3,343 CP at Level 50. However, its overall Master League role is much more specialized than that of the strongest meta Pokémon.

Recommended PvP Moveset

Astonish + Shadow Ball + Energy Ball

11 • EVOLUTION

Sinistcha Evolution & Forms

Sinistcha does not evolve further in Pokémon GO. Instead, it is the final stage of Poltchageist and can appear in different forms depending on the Poltchageist form used for evolution.

Counterfeit Poltchageist 50 Candy
→
Unremarkable Sinistcha Final form
Artisan Poltchageist 400 Candy
→
Masterpiece Sinistcha Final form
Form reminder

The two Sinistcha forms are connected to different Poltchageist forms. They should be treated as separate collection targets rather than cosmetic changes that can simply be switched at will.

16 • LUCKY SINISTCHA

Lucky Sinistcha

Lucky is a trade-related status rather than a separate species or permanent form. A Sinistcha received through a successful Lucky Trade can become a Lucky Pokémon.

✨
Lucky Sinistcha

A Lucky Sinistcha can be obtained through trading when the trade results in the Lucky status.

Why Lucky Sinistcha Matters

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • A Lucky Sinistcha can be useful for collection goals.
  • Lucky status can make a high-IV Sinistcha especially attractive as a long-term investment.
  • Lucky and 100% are different properties.
Important:

A Lucky Sinistcha is not automatically a 100% IV Sinistcha. Its IVs are rerolled as part of the trade, so check the resulting Pokémon after the trade.

22 • RESOURCES

Sinistcha Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a high-level Sinistcha requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Sinistcha is not among the game's strongest general raid attackers, consider your intended use before committing significant resources.

  • Save Candy for the Sinistcha form you actually want.
  • Remember that Masterpiece evolution requires much more Candy than Unremarkable evolution.
  • Collect Candy XL before planning a Level 50 build.
  • Check the moveset before spending Elite resources.
  • Consider keeping unusual IV, Lucky, XXL and XXS specimens for your collection.

How to Use Sinistcha

26 • FAQ

Sinistcha FAQ

What type is Sinistcha?

Sinistcha is a Grass/Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Sinistcha weak to?

Sinistcha is weak to Dark, Fire, Flying, Ghost and Ice-type attacks.

What is Sinistcha's max CP?

Sinistcha reaches a maximum CP of 3,343 at Level 50.

Is Sinistcha a Legendary Pokémon?

No. Sinistcha is a standard Generation 9 Pokémon.

Does Sinistcha evolve?

Sinistcha is the final evolutionary stage of Poltchageist and does not evolve further.

What are Sinistcha's forms in Pokémon GO?

Pokémon GO features Unremarkable Sinistcha and Masterpiece Sinistcha, associated with Counterfeit and Artisan Poltchageist respectively.

How do I get Masterpiece Sinistcha?

Masterpiece Sinistcha evolves from Artisan Poltchageist and currently requires 400 Candy.

How do I get Unremarkable Sinistcha?

Unremarkable Sinistcha evolves from Counterfeit Poltchageist and currently requires 50 Candy.

Is Shiny Sinistcha available?

No. Shiny Sinistcha is not currently released in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Sinistcha available?

Shadow Sinistcha is not currently an available Pokémon GO form.

Is Purified Sinistcha available?

Not currently. Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon, and Shadow Sinistcha has not been released.

Can Sinistcha be Lucky?

Yes. Sinistcha can have Lucky status when obtained through a successful Lucky Trade.

What is a 100% Sinistcha?

A 100% Sinistcha has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 Stamina IVs.

Is XXL Sinistcha stronger?

No. XXL is a size classification and should not be interpreted as a higher-IV or automatically stronger Sinistcha.

Is XXS Sinistcha rare?

XXS Sinistcha can be desirable for collectors because of its very small size classification, but size and IV quality are separate characteristics.

Is Sinistcha good for raids?

Sinistcha has usable Grass and Ghost moves, but it is generally a niche raid attacker rather than a top-tier choice.

Is Sinistcha good in PvP?

Sinistcha can have niche PvP applications, especially when its Grass/Ghost typing and charged moves create useful matchup opportunities.

Can a Lucky Sinistcha also be 100% IV?

Yes. A Lucky Sinistcha can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs after the trade.

Can an XXL Sinistcha also be 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are independent properties, so an XXL Sinistcha can also be a perfect 100% IV Pokémon.

Can an XXS Sinistcha also be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky status and size are separate Pokémon characteristics.

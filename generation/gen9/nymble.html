15 — XXL

XXL Nymble

XXL Pokémon are unusually large specimens. Nymble can be encountered with different size characteristics, making XXL specimens interesting collection targets.

XXL

XXL Nymble

Extra Large

An XXL Nymble is a particularly large specimen and can be kept as a size-based collection entry.

Why Keep XXL Nymble?

XXL Pokémon can be useful for collectors, showcases and size-focused Pokédex goals. Size does not automatically mean better battle performance.

XXL classification is based on the Pokémon's size characteristics rather than its IV percentage. A 0% IV Pokémon can still be XXL, and an XXL Pokémon does not have to be 100% IV.
16 — XXS

XXS Nymble

XXS Nymble represents the opposite end of the size spectrum. These unusually small specimens are useful collection targets for trainers completing size-based Pokémon collections.

XXS

XXS Nymble

Extra Small

An XXS Nymble is a particularly small specimen. Its size is separate from its IV quality.

XXS Collection Goal

Trainers interested in completing size variants can keep XXS Nymble separately from their normal and XXL specimens.

XXS and XXL are size classifications. They should not be confused with 0% IV, 100% IV, CP or Pokémon level.

24 — FAQ

Nymble FAQ

What type is Nymble?

Nymble is a pure Bug-type Pokémon.

What is Nymble weak to?

Nymble is weak to Fire, Flying and Rock-type attacks.

What region is Nymble from?

Nymble was introduced in Generation 9 and is associated with the Paldea region.

What is Nymble's Pokédex number?

Nymble is National Pokédex number #919.

Does Nymble evolve?

Yes. Nymble evolves into Lokix.

Is Nymble good for raids?

Nymble is generally not considered a strong raid attacker because of its low overall stats.

What is a 100% Nymble?

A 100% Nymble has perfect IVs of 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP.

What is Lucky Nymble?

Lucky Nymble is a Nymble that has become Lucky through the trading system.

What is XXL Nymble?

XXL Nymble is an unusually large Nymble based on its size characteristics.

What is XXS Nymble?

XXS Nymble is an unusually small Nymble based on its size characteristics.

Does XXL mean 100% IV?

No. Size and IVs are separate characteristics. An XXL Nymble can have any IV combination.

Does XXS mean low IV?

No. XXS refers to size and does not determine whether a Nymble has good or bad IVs.

Can Nymble be Lucky?

Lucky status is obtained through trading. A Nymble can be a Lucky Pokémon when the trading mechanics allow the traded Nymble to become Lucky.

Should I keep a perfect Nymble?

Yes. A 15/15/15 Nymble is a perfect-IV specimen and can be useful for collection and evolution purposes even if it is not a major battle Pokémon.

Should I transfer XXL or XXS Nymble?

If you are interested in size collections, it can be worthwhile to keep XXL and XXS specimens separately from your normal Nymble.

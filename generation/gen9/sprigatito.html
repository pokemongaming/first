Why Sprigatito's Typing Matters

Grass typing gives Sprigatito useful resistances against Water, Ground, Electric and Grass attacks. At the same time, it leaves Sprigatito vulnerable to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

07 • PVP

Sprigatito PvP Guide

Sprigatito has limited competitive relevance. Its low maximum CP means it is more naturally associated with restricted formats such as Little Cup when eligible.

Little Cup

Sprigatito can be considered for Little Cup-style formats when the species is eligible and the rules allow it. Always check the current cup rules before investing in a dedicated PvP specimen.

Great League

Sprigatito's low CP ceiling makes it a poor choice for a conventional Great League build compared with Pokémon specifically suited to the format.

Ultra & Master League

Its low maximum CP makes Sprigatito unsuitable for conventional Ultra League and Master League competition.

14 • XXL SPRIGATITO

XXL Sprigatito

XXL is a size category assigned to individual Pokémon. It does not represent a separate Sprigatito form.

📏

XXL Size

SIZE ATTRIBUTE

Sprigatito's normal average height is around 0.4 m, while XXL specimens can reach the game's XXL size range.

🏆

XXL Collector Value

XXL Pokémon can be useful for collection challenges, showcases and personal size collections when those features are active.

XXL does not mean higher IVs

Size and IVs are separate properties. An XXL Sprigatito can have low, average or perfect IVs.

15 • XXS SPRIGATITO

XXS Sprigatito

XXS is the opposite end of Pokémon GO's size system. Like XXL, it is an individual Pokémon attribute rather than a different species.

🔬

XXS Size

SIZE ATTRIBUTE

Very small Sprigatito can receive the XXS designation. The size system is independent of the Pokémon's IV percentage.

🏆

XXS Collector Value

XXS Pokémon can be attractive to collectors who maintain complete size collections or search for rare combinations.

XXS does not mean weaker

The XXS label is a size classification. It should not be interpreted as an IV rating or automatically as a weaker competitive Pokémon.

XXS Combinations Worth Keeping

  • XXS + 100% IV
  • XXS + Shiny
  • XXS + Lucky
  • XXS + Shiny + 100% IV
  • XXS + Shiny + Lucky

22 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Sprigatito

For Raids

Sprigatito should generally not be selected when building an optimized Grass raid team. Its role is better suited to collection and evolution purposes.

For PvP

Check restricted cups before investing in Sprigatito for PvP. Its low CP ceiling makes Little Cup-style formats much more relevant than higher CP leagues.

For Collectors

Shiny, Lucky, hundo, XXL and XXS combinations are much more meaningful collection targets.

  • Keep Shiny Sprigatito.
  • Keep 100% IV Sprigatito.
  • Consider keeping XXL and XXS specimens.
  • Lucky Sprigatito can be valuable for collection and lower-cost powering up.
  • Do not treat Shadow or Purified Sprigatito as currently obtainable forms.

Sprigatito Pokémon GO FAQ

What type is Sprigatito?

Sprigatito is a pure Grass-type Pokémon.

What is Sprigatito's Pokédex number?

Sprigatito is #906 in the National Pokédex.

What is Sprigatito's max CP?

Sprigatito's maximum CP is 1,147 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Sprigatito available?

Yes. Shiny Sprigatito is available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Sprigatito available?

No. Shadow Sprigatito is not currently released as an obtainable Pokémon GO form.

Can I get Purified Sprigatito?

Not currently. Purified Pokémon originate from Shadow Pokémon, and Shadow Sprigatito has not been released.

How do I get Lucky Sprigatito?

Lucky status can be obtained through trading. Lucky Sprigatito is still the same species, but it receives the Lucky designation.

What is a 100% IV Sprigatito?

A 100% IV Sprigatito has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a hundo Sprigatito?

A Level 50 100% IV Sprigatito has 1,147 CP.

What is XXL Sprigatito?

XXL Sprigatito is an individual Sprigatito that has received the XXL size classification.

What is XXS Sprigatito?

XXS Sprigatito is an individual Sprigatito with the XXS size classification.

Can a Sprigatito be Shiny and Lucky?

Yes. Shiny and Lucky are separate attributes, so a Pokémon can possess both.

Can Sprigatito be Shiny and 100% IV?

Yes. A Shiny Sprigatito can have perfect 15/15/15 IVs.

Can Sprigatito be XXL and 100% IV?

Yes. Size and IVs are separate properties.

Is Sprigatito good for raids?

Sprigatito is not currently a significant raid attacker. Its evolved forms are more relevant for battle purposes.

How does Sprigatito evolve?

Sprigatito evolves into Floragato for 25 Candy, then Floragato evolves into Meowscarada for 100 Candy.

What is Sprigatito weak against?

Sprigatito is weak to Bug, Fire, Flying, Ice and Poison-type attacks.

Pawmo

Pawmo is an Electric/Fighting-type Pokémon from the Paldea region. In Pokémon GO, it sits between Pawmi and Pawmot in its evolution family and has a relatively low maximum CP compared with fully evolved Pokémon.

Pawmo Overview

Pawmo is the middle evolution of the Pawmi family. It combines Electric and Fighting typing and can eventually evolve into Pawmot after meeting the required Candy and Buddy walking condition.

Why Pawmo's Typing Matters

Pawmo's dual Electric/Fighting typing gives it different matchups from its earlier Pawmi form. Its Electric typing provides access to Electric-type damage, while Fighting typing adds another offensive and defensive dimension.

Shiny Pawmo

✨
Shiny Pawmo

Shiny Pawmo is available in Pokémon GO. Its Shiny appearance uses an alternate coloration while retaining Pawmo's Electric/Fighting typing.

Shiny Pawmo for Collectors

A Shiny Pawmo can be kept as a collection piece or evolved into Shiny Pawmot. If you prefer collecting different evolutionary stages, keeping one Pawmo unevolved can also be worthwhile.

Shadow Pawmo

Shadow Pawmo should be treated as a separate variant from standard Pawmo because Shadow Pokémon have a different battle profile.

👿

Shadow Pawmo

Shadow status does not change Pawmo's basic Electric/Fighting typing. However, Shadow Pokémon have increased offensive pressure and reduced defensive durability.

Should You Power Up Shadow Pawmo?

  • Check the available moves before investing.

  • Do not automatically purify it just to increase its IVs.

Lucky Pawmo

🍀

Lucky Pawmo

A Lucky Pawmo is obtained through trading. Lucky Pokémon have a special background and reduced Stardust costs for powering up.

Why Keep a Lucky Pawmo?

  • Lower Stardust requirements can make investment cheaper.
  • Lucky Pokémon are useful collection targets.
  • Trading can produce interesting IV combinations.
  • A Lucky Pawmo can later be evolved into Pawmot.

XXL Pawmo

XXL Pawmo is a size variant that can be identified through Pokémon GO's size system.

📏
XXL Pawmo Large-size Pawmo variant

XXL Pawmo can be valuable for collectors and size-focused Pokédex completion. Size and IVs are separate characteristics, so an XXL Pawmo can also have any IV combination.

XXL Pawmo Collection Tips

  • Keep an XXL Pawmo if you collect size variants.
  • Check its IVs separately from its size.
  • Consider keeping an XXL Shiny if you find one.
  • Do not transfer a rare size variant accidentally.

XXS Pawmo

XXS Pawmo represents the small end of Pawmo's Pokémon GO size range.

🔹
XXS Pawmo Small-size Pawmo variant

XXS Pawmo can be useful for collectors completing size-based Pokémon collections. Its size does not determine its IV percentage.

XXS Pawmo Collection Tips

  • Keep an XXS Pawmo if you collect size variants.
  • Compare size and IVs independently.
  • A Shiny XXS Pawmo can be an especially unusual collection combination.

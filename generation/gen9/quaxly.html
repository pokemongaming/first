Quaxly FAQ

What type is Quaxly?

Quaxly is a Water-type Pokémon.

What generation is Quaxly from?

Quaxly is a Generation 9 Pokémon from the Paldea region.

Does Quaxly evolve?

Yes. Quaxly evolves into Quaxwell, which later evolves into Quaquaval.

What is Quaxly weak to?

As a Water-type Pokémon, Quaxly is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

Can Quaxly be Shiny?

Shiny availability depends on the current Pokémon GO release and encounter availability.

What is a 100% Quaxly?

A 100% Quaxly has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

Does XXL Quaxly have better stats?

No. Size and IV quality are separate mechanics.

Does XXS Quaxly have lower IVs?

No. XXS size does not determine a Pokémon's IV quality.

Can Quaxly become Lucky?

Eligible Pokémon can receive Lucky status through Pokémon trading mechanics.

Is Shadow Quaxly always available?

No. Shadow Pokémon availability depends on the current Team GO Rocket rotation and other active game mechanics.

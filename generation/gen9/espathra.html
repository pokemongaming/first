Espathra Overview

Espathra is a pure Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 9. It evolves from Flittle and has a distinctive ostrich-inspired design. Its relatively high Attack gives it offensive potential, while its lower Defense means that it can require careful use in battles.

XXL Espathra

XXL Espathra is a size variant representing an exceptionally large individual. Size variants are separate from IV quality, so an XXL Pokémon can have any IV combination.

📏 XXL Espathra

Very large size classification. Useful for collectors, showcases and size-based goals.

IVs Are Separate

Being XXL does not automatically mean the Pokémon has high or perfect IVs.

An XXL Espathra can simultaneously be Shiny, Lucky, Hundo, Shadow or another eligible variant when the game's mechanics allow those characteristics.

XXS Espathra

XXS Espathra represents an exceptionally small individual. Like XXL, the XXS classification is independent of IV quality.

📐 XXS Espathra

Very small size classification that can be valuable for collectors and showcases.

Not an IV Rating

XXS size does not mean poor IVs. An XXS Espathra can still be a Hundo.

Why Orthworm's Steel Typing Matters

Steel is one of Pokémon GO's most defensive typings. Orthworm therefore has a useful collection of resistances, although it has important weaknesses to Fighting, Fire and Ground attacks.

A 100% Orthworm has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. For collectors, a perfect specimen is an excellent long-term addition even if Orthworm is not a major raid attacker.

Orthworm PvP Guide

Orthworm's high Defense and Steel typing can make it more interesting in PvP than its raid performance suggests.

Great League

Orthworm can function as a bulky Steel-type option in Great League. Its resistances allow it to handle certain matchups effectively, while Mud-Slap and its Charged Moves provide useful coverage.

Ultra League

Orthworm can also be considered for Ultra League teams, although its effectiveness depends heavily on the current metagame, move balance and team composition.

Master League

Orthworm's maximum CP is relatively low for the Master League, making it a much less natural choice for open Master League compared with Pokémon built around much higher maximum CP.

Lucky Orthworm

Lucky Orthworm is a traded Orthworm that has received Lucky Pokémon status. Lucky status is separate from Shiny status and does not change Orthworm's species or typing.

🍀

Lucky Orthworm

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up than normal Pokémon, making a good Lucky Orthworm potentially useful for players who want to build one economically.

Lucky Orthworm IVs

Lucky Pokémon receive a minimum IV floor when obtained through a Lucky Trade. A Lucky Orthworm can therefore be especially attractive if you want both reduced power-up costs and reasonably strong IVs.

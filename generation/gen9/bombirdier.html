Bombirdier is a Flying/Dark-type Pokémon from the Paldea region. In Pokémon GO, it is best known as a raid and collection Pokémon with access to Flying, Dark and Rock-type attacks. Its Shiny form is available, while Shadow Bombirdier is not currently available.

What Is a Perfect Bombirdier?

A Bombirdier with 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP is known as a 100% IV or Hundo Bombirdier. A perfect specimen is particularly desirable for collectors and players who want the highest possible IV combination.

17 — PVP

Bombirdier PvP Guide

Bombirdier's dual typing and access to Fly and Rock Tomb give it some interesting PvP options. Its overall ranking can change as the Great League, Ultra League and move meta evolve.

Great League

Bombirdier can be considered for Great League teams where its Flying/Dark typing and charged-move options fit a specific team strategy. IV selection is more important here than simply searching for a 15/15/15 specimen.

Ultra League

Ultra League Bombirdier generally requires additional investment and careful matchup selection. XL Candy may become relevant depending on the target level.

Master League

Bombirdier's 2,812 Level-50 maximum CP makes it a comparatively poor fit for the Master League, where much higher-CP Pokémon are commonly used.

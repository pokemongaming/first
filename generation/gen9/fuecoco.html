Fuecoco FAQ

What type is Fuecoco?

Fuecoco is a Fire-type Pokémon.

What generation is Fuecoco from?

Fuecoco was introduced in Generation 9 as one of the Paldea starter Pokémon.

What is Fuecoco weak to?

Does Fuecoco evolve?

Yes. Fuecoco evolves into Crocalor and eventually Skeledirge.

What is a 100% Fuecoco?

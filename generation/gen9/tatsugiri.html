09 — IV GUIDE

Tatsugiri IV Guide

Individual Values add to Tatsugiri's Attack, Defense and HP. A 15/15/15 specimen is known as a perfect IV or 100% Tatsugiri.

15 Attack IV
15 Defense IV
15 HP IV

What IVs Should You Keep?

  • Keep 15/15/15 specimens for your 100% collection.
  • Keep high-Attack specimens for collection or battle projects.
  • Keep rare regional forms even when their IVs are average.
  • Consider Lucky Tatsugiri before spending large amounts of Stardust.
10 — 100% TATSUGIRI

100% Tatsugiri — Hundo Guide

A 100% Tatsugiri has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs. These are commonly called perfect IVs or a Hundo.

15 / 15 / 15
Perfect IV Tatsugiri

A perfect Tatsugiri has the maximum possible IV contribution in all three stat categories.

Is a Hundo Tatsugiri Rare?

A perfect specimen is much less common than an ordinary catch. The exact odds depend on the encounter method and its applicable IV floor.

Should You Max a Hundo Tatsugiri?

For most players, a Hundo Tatsugiri is more valuable as a collection achievement than as a priority Level 50 battle investment.

14 — LUCKY

Lucky Tatsugiri

Lucky Tatsugiri is a special trade outcome rather than a separate Tatsugiri species or form.

🍀 Lucky Tatsugiri

A Tatsugiri received through a Lucky Trade can become a Lucky Pokémon. Lucky Pokémon generally require fewer Stardust resources to power up.

Lucky Tatsugiri IV Floor

Lucky Trades provide a minimum IV floor. This makes Lucky Tatsugiri especially interesting for collectors looking for a high-IV version that is cheaper to power up than an ordinary specimen.

Should You Trade for a Lucky Tatsugiri?

  • Trade regional forms that you already own duplicates of.
  • Target Lucky Trades if you want a cheaper investment.
  • Keep Lucky Tatsugiri for your special collection.
  • Consider chasing a Lucky Hundo as a long-term collection goal.

17 — PVP

Tatsugiri PvP Guide

Tatsugiri's CP ceiling technically allows it to participate in multiple GO Battle League formats, but its low bulk and current moveset make it a weak competitive choice.

Great League

Tatsugiri is not a strong Great League choice. Its low overall bulk makes it difficult to compete consistently against stronger meta Pokémon.

Ultra League

Tatsugiri remains a niche option in Ultra League. Trainers looking for a Water/Dragon role generally have more effective alternatives.

Master League

Its maximum CP of 3,105 is far below the typical stat ceiling expected from serious Master League Pokémon, making Tatsugiri a poor Master League investment.

Recommended PvP Moves

Water Gun + Surf + Outrage

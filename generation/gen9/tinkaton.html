Tinkaton

Tinkaton is a Fairy and Steel-type Pokémon from the Paldea region. Its unusual defensive typing, access to Fairy Wind and powerful moves such as Gigaton Hammer make it an interesting Pokémon for PvP, collecting and long-term Pokémon GO investment.

11 • LUCKY

Lucky Tinkaton

🍀 Why Lucky Tinkaton Is Valuable

Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust for power-ups than regular Pokémon. A Lucky Tinkaton can therefore be an attractive long-term investment for players building high-level PvP or collection projects.

Lucky Tinkaton Benefits

  • Lower Stardust cost when powering up.
  • Useful for Candy XL investment projects.
  • Can be valuable for Ultra League preparation.
  • Retains normal Tinkaton typing and moves.
  • Lucky status does not directly increase battle stats.

Pawmot Overview

Pawmot is the final stage of the Pawmi evolutionary family. It combines Electric and Fighting typing, giving it an unusual offensive identity compared with many other Electric-type Pokémon.

Why Pawmot's Typing Matters

Pawmot's Electric/Fighting combination gives it access to two useful offensive categories. Electric attacks are valuable against Water and Flying targets, while Fighting attacks can pressure Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark Pokémon.

XXL Pawmot

XXL Pawmot is a size variation where the Pokémon's measured height and weight qualify it for the XXL category.

📏

XXL Pawmot

Extra-Large Size

XXL status is separate from IV quality. An XXL Pawmot can have low, average or perfect IVs.

Important:

Size and IVs are separate attributes. A 100% Pawmot is not automatically XXL, and an XXL Pawmot is not automatically 100%.

XXS Pawmot

XXS Pawmot represents an unusually small measured size. Like XXL status, XXS is independent from IV quality.

📐

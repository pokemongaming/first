Why Revavroom's Typing Matters

The Steel and Poison combination provides Revavroom with a highly specialized defensive typing. It has numerous resistances but must be protected carefully from Ground attacks, which are particularly dangerous.

Revavroom Weaknesses & Resistances

Weaknesses

🌎
Ground

Ground attacks are Revavroom's most dangerous weakness because its Steel and Poison typing creates a double Ground weakness.

🔥

Revavroom PvP Guide

Great League

Revavroom's Steel and Poison typing can create useful matchups against Fairy, Grass and Poison-focused teams. However, its extreme Ground weakness is a major factor when building around it.

Ultra League

Ultra League gives Revavroom more room to use its stat profile, but its effectiveness depends heavily on the current meta and the Ground and Fire threats that are present.

Master League

With a lower maximum CP than many major Master League Pokémon, Revavroom is generally more specialized here than the highest-stat Legendary options.

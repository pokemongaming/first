Clodsire Overview

Clodsire is the evolved form of Paldean Wooper and is a dual Poison and Ground-type Pokémon. Unlike many Pokémon with high PvE attack stats, Clodsire's main strengths are its bulk, unusual defensive typing and potential utility in PvP formats.

Clodsire Type

Clodsire combines Poison and Ground typing. This gives it a useful collection of resistances while also creating four major weaknesses that players should consider when building teams.

☠️ POISON 🌎 GROUND

Why Clodsire's Typing Matters

Poison and Ground is one of Clodsire's most important strengths. The combination gives it extremely strong resistance to Electric and Poison attacks while also resisting Fighting, Rock, Bug and Fairy damage.

Clodsire's relatively low maximum CP and high bulk make it a more natural candidate for Great League than Master League. Its dual typing can also provide useful defensive matchups against several common attack types.

Ultra League

Clodsire can be used in Ultra League, but powering it up and choosing an appropriate moveset should be considered carefully because the format contains many Pokémon that can exploit its weaknesses.

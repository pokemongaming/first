01 • OVERVIEW

Glimmora Overview

Glimmora is a Rock / Poison-type Pokémon from the Paldea region. It is the evolved form of Glimmet and is known for its crystal-like body and flower-shaped appearance.

In Pokémon GO, Glimmora is an interesting option for trainers who want a Pokémon with access to both Rock and Poison offensive roles.

    Glimmora FAQ

    What type is Glimmora?

    Glimmora is a Rock and Poison-type Pokémon.

    What is Glimmora weak to?

    Glimmora is vulnerable to several attacking types, including Ground, Water, Steel and Psychic.

    What Pokémon does Glimmora evolve from?

    Glimmora evolves from Glimmet and is the final stage of the Glimmet evolution family.

    Is Shiny Glimmora available?

    Shiny Glimmora availability depends on the current Pokémon GO release and encounter rules. Check current game data before claiming it is obtainable.

    Is Glimmora good for raids?

    Glimmora can be useful in specific Rock or Poison matchups, but it should be compared with stronger specialists before making a major raid investment.

    Is Glimmora good in PvP?

    Glimmora can have interesting PvP applications, particularly because of its typing and move coverage. Its ranking depends on the current league meta.

    What is a 100% Glimmora?

    A 100% Glimmora has perfect 15/15/15 Attack, Defense and HP IVs.

    What is Lucky Glimmora?

    Lucky Glimmora is Glimmora with the Lucky status obtained through Pokémon GO's trading mechanics. Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.

    What is XXL Glimmora?

    XXL Glimmora is an unusually large Glimmora. XXL is a size attribute rather than a separate Pokémon species.

    What is XXS Glimmora?

    XXS Glimmora is an unusually small Glimmora. XXS is a size attribute rather than a separate species.

    Shadow Glimmora has the Shadow status and therefore follows Shadow Pokémon battle mechanics. Its basic Rock / Poison typing remains the same.

    Should I purify Shadow Glimmora?

    Is a Shiny Glimmora worth keeping?

Why Poltchageist's Typing Matters

The Grass/Ghost combination gives Poltchageist useful resistances against several attack types, while also creating a five-type weakness profile. Its typing gives it access to both Grass and Ghost STAB moves.

Poltchageist PvP Guide

Poltchageist's low maximum CP prevents it from being a natural Ultra League or Master League Pokémon. Its most interesting PvP environment is the Little Cup when eligible.

Little Cup

Poltchageist can make use of its Grass/Ghost typing and Ghost-type coverage in Little Cup formats. Its usefulness is highly dependent on the current cup rules and available Pokémon.

Great League

With a Level 50 maximum CP of only 1,287, Poltchageist cannot naturally reach the Great League CP cap. Its low Defense also limits its general PvP performance.

Ultra & Master League

Poltchageist is not suitable for standard Ultra League or Master League investment because of its low CP ceiling and modest battle stats.

Lucky Poltchageist

Lucky is not a separate species or form. A Poltchageist can become Lucky through Pokémon trading when the Lucky Trade mechanic applies.

🍀
Lucky Poltchageist

Possible through trading.

Why Keep a Lucky Poltchageist?

  • Lucky Pokémon require less Stardust to power up.
  • Trading can produce a Lucky Pokémon.
  • Lucky Pokémon have a guaranteed IV floor once the Lucky status is obtained.
  • A Lucky Poltchageist can be useful for collectors.
  • Consider evolving a Lucky specimen if you want a Lucky Sinistcha.
Collection Tip

If you are collecting special variants, a Lucky Poltchageist can be kept alongside Hundo, XXL and XXS specimens.

XXL Poltchageist

XXL is a size classification rather than a different Pokémon species. An XXL Poltchageist can be especially desirable for collectors.

🏆 XXL Poltchageist

An exceptionally large Poltchageist based on its individual height and weight measurements.

XXL vs Normal Size

Size is an individual characteristic. Two Poltchageist with identical species, IVs and CP can still have different size classifications.

Collector tip:

Keep an XXL specimen if you are building a size collection. Its XXL status is independent of its IVs.

XXS Poltchageist

XXS Poltchageist is an exceptionally small individual based on its recorded size. Like XXL, XXS is an individual attribute.

🔎 XXS Poltchageist

A very small Poltchageist that can be kept as a special collection specimen.

Does XXS Change Battle Stats?

XXS classification should not be confused with IVs. A tiny Pokémon can still have poor, average or perfect IVs.

Poltchageist FAQ

What type is Poltchageist?

Poltchageist is a Grass/Ghost-type Pokémon.

What is Poltchageist weak to?

Poltchageist is weak to Fire, Ice, Flying, Ghost and Dark-type attacks.

What is Poltchageist's max CP?

A perfect Level 50 Poltchageist can reach 1,287 CP.

Is Poltchageist good for raids?

No. Poltchageist has relatively low battle stats and is not considered a strong raid attacker.

Is Poltchageist good in PvP?

Its most relevant PvP opportunity is in Little Cup formats. Its low CP makes it unsuitable for Ultra and Master League.

How does Poltchageist evolve?

Counterfeit Poltchageist evolves into Unremarkable Sinistcha, while Artisan Poltchageist evolves into Masterpiece Sinistcha.

How much Candy does Poltchageist need to evolve?

Counterfeit Poltchageist requires 50 Candy, while Artisan Poltchageist requires 400 Candy.

Is Shiny Poltchageist available?

Shiny Poltchageist is not currently available in Pokémon GO.

Is Shadow Poltchageist available?

Shadow Poltchageist is not currently listed as available.

Can I get a Purified Poltchageist?

Not currently. Purification requires a Shadow Poltchageist, which is not currently available.

Can Poltchageist be Lucky?

Yes. Lucky is a Pokémon status obtained through trading rather than a separate Poltchageist form.

What is a 100% Poltchageist?

A 100% or Hundo Poltchageist has 15 Attack, 15 Defense and 15 HP IVs.

What CP is a Hundo Poltchageist?

A Level 20 Hundo is 650 CP, a Level 25 weather-boosted Hundo is 813 CP, and a Level 50 Hundo reaches 1,287 CP.

What is an XXL Poltchageist?

XXL is a size classification for an unusually large individual Poltchageist.

What is an XXS Poltchageist?

XXS is a size classification for an unusually small individual Poltchageist.

Does XXL or XXS affect IVs?

No. Size and IVs are separate individual characteristics.

Can an Artisan Poltchageist evolve into Masterpiece Sinistcha?

Yes. Artisan Poltchageist follows the Masterpiece Sinistcha evolution path.

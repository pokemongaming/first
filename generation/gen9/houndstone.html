Why Houndstone's Typing Matters

Houndstone's pure Ghost typing gives it useful defensive properties and allows it to deal super-effective damage against Psychic and Ghost opponents.

XXL Houndstone

XXL Houndstone is an exceptionally large individual of the species. Size is separate from IVs, so an XXL Pokémon does not automatically have perfect IVs.

📏
XXL Houndstone Extra-large size category Approximately 3.0 m to 3.1 m height range

XXL vs 100% Houndstone

An XXL Houndstone and a 100% Houndstone are not the same thing. XXL describes the Pokémon's size, while 100% describes its IVs. A single Houndstone can be both XXL and 100% if it meets both conditions.

XXS Houndstone

XXS Houndstone is the smallest size category for Houndstone. Like XXL status, XXS status is separate from IV quality.

Houndstone PvP Guide

Houndstone has more PvP relevance than its raid ranking might suggest. Its Ghost typing and relatively bulky profile make it an interesting option in CP-limited formats.

Great League

Houndstone can be considered for Great League teams, particularly when its Ghost typing and move pressure fit the current format.

Ultra League

Houndstone can also be evaluated in Ultra League. XL Candy and the correct IV distribution become more important at higher levels.

Master League

Its relatively low maximum CP makes Master League a less natural home for Houndstone compared with higher-stat Legendary Pokémon.

PvP IV reminder:

A 100% IV Pokémon is not automatically the best PvP Pokémon. CP-limited leagues often favor different IV distributions that allow a Pokémon to reach a higher level under the league's CP cap.

Houndstone Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building a strong Houndstone requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust, especially if you plan to use it at higher levels.

  • Save Greavard Candy for strong Houndstone candidates.
  • Keep high-IV Houndstone for long-term projects.
  • Collect XL Candy if building Houndstone for higher-level PvP.
  • Check the moveset before spending large amounts of Stardust.
  • Keep interesting XXL and XXS specimens for your collection.
  • Consider Lucky Houndstone for cheaper power-ups.

Accelgor

Accelgor is a pure Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation 5. Known for its extremely fast design, Accelgor evolves from Shelmet after a special trade interaction with Karrablast. In Pokémon GO, it is primarily a Pokédex and collection target rather than a top-tier raid or PvP investment.

Why Accelgor's Typing Matters

Bug is an offensive type that can hit Psychic, Dark and Grass Pokémon super effectively. However, Bug-type Pokémon must also contend with several common weaknesses, including Fire, Flying and Rock attacks.

Because Accelgor is a pure Bug type, it does not receive a secondary typing that would alter these matchups.

09 • PVP

Accelgor PvP Guide

Accelgor has an offensive stat distribution and a relatively low maximum CP compared with Legendary Pokémon. Its PvP value depends heavily on its available moves and the current cup or metagame.

Great League

Accelgor's CP ceiling makes Great League the most natural standard league to investigate. However, its relatively fragile defensive profile means it can struggle against strong neutral attacks and common Bug counters.

Ultra League

Accelgor can be tested in Ultra League formats when its CP and moveset fit the rules, although it is not normally considered a premier open-league investment.

Master League

Accelgor is generally not a natural Master League choice because its maximum CP is much lower than the Pokémon normally competing at the top level of the league.

PvP Investment Advice

Before spending Stardust, check the current PvP rankings and evaluate the exact IV spread for the league you plan to enter.

Absol

Absol is a Dark-type Pokémon from the Hoenn region. In Pokémon GO, Absol is known for its high offensive potential but relatively low bulk. It is available in regular, Shiny and Shadow forms and can undergo Mega Evolution into Mega Absol.

Why Absol's Typing Matters

Dark typing gives Absol an offensive role against Psychic- and Ghost-type opponents. It also provides useful defensive interactions against Psychic, Ghost and Dark attacks.

Dark-Type Offensive Role

Absol can use Dark-type moves such as Snarl and Brutal Swing to deal super-effective damage against Psychic- and Ghost-type targets.

09 • PVP

Absol PvP Guide

Absol's high Attack and low bulk make it a challenging PvP Pokémon. It can apply pressure when its moves are used effectively, but it generally prefers specialized cups or situations where its offensive profile is useful.

Great League

Absol can technically be used in Great League when its CP and IV combination fit the league, but its low bulk makes it a niche choice.

Ultra League

Absol's stat distribution is still relatively fragile in Ultra League. It can surprise opponents but is not normally a first-choice generalist.

Master League

Absol's maximum CP is considerably lower than the requirements of the modern Master League meta, making it a niche option rather than a standard Master League investment.

PvP Investment Advice

Before investing Stardust into Absol for PvP, check the current cup rules and moveset. Its value can vary dramatically depending on the format.

10 • SHINY FORM

Shiny Absol

Shiny Absol is an alternate-color version of the standard Absol. It is a collector-focused variation and retains the same species, typing and core stats.

✨
Shiny Absol

Shiny Absol is available in Pokémon GO through eligible encounters. Its alternate coloration makes it one of the more desirable Shiny Hoenn Pokémon for collectors.

Shiny Absol Gameplay

Shiny status does not inherently increase Absol's battle statistics. A Shiny Absol has the same species and battle mechanics as the corresponding regular Absol.

Shiny Mega Absol

A Shiny Absol can retain its Shiny identity when Mega Evolved, allowing trainers to use Shiny Mega Absol when the Mega form is available.

Should You Purify Shadow Absol?

Purifying Shadow Absol is a permanent decision. If your goal is maximum Shadow raid damage, keeping the Shadow form can be preferable. Purification may make sense for other collection, appraisal or gameplay goals.

15 • RESOURCES

Absol Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Building Absol for raids or Mega Evolution requires Candy, Candy XL and Stardust. Because Absol has a specialized role, investment should be based on your actual raid teams and collection goals.

  • Keep high-Attack Absol specimens for offensive projects.
  • Collect Absol Candy before committing to large power-ups.
  • Save Candy XL for Level 40+ investments.
  • Check the moveset before using Elite TMs.
  • Compare regular and Shadow Absol before purifying.
  • Keep strong Shiny specimens if collecting is important.
  • Consider Mega Energy before planning Mega Absol usage.
  • Prioritize a strong Absol if you intend to use Mega Absol.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Absol

Before a Raid

  • Check whether the raid boss is weak to Dark.
  • Confirm Absol has a Dark-focused moveset.
  • Check Absol's level and IVs.
  • Consider whether Shadow Absol provides more damage.
  • Consider Mega Absol if Mega Energy is available.
  • Check the boss's moves before committing a fragile attacker.

During Team Building

Absol should not automatically occupy every Dark-type slot. Compare its damage, durability and moveset with other available Dark attackers.

When Mega Absol Makes Sense

Mega Absol is especially interesting when you want a high-offense Dark Mega for a raid against a Psychic- or Ghost-type boss. Its high Attack can produce excellent damage, but its relatively fragile profile means that dodging and team composition matter.

Long-Term Investment

For most players, a strong Absol is more attractive as a specialized Dark attacker, Mega Evolution project or collection Pokémon than as a universal PvP investment.

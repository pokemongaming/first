Lairon Type

⚙️
Steel / Rock Dual Type Pokémon

Why Lairon's Typing Matters

Lairon's Steel/Rock combination gives it a wide resistance profile, but it also creates two major weaknesses. Fighting- and Ground-type attacks are especially dangerous because both types exploit Lairon particularly hard.

⚙️ Steel Provides many useful resistances and supports Lairon's defensive identity.
🪨 Rock Adds Rock typing but also contributes to Lairon's Fighting, Ground and Water weaknesses.

08 • IV GUIDE

Lairon IV Guide

Lairon's IV value depends heavily on what you want to do with it. A 15/15/15 specimen is perfect, but perfection is not necessary for every use case.

Perfect Lairon

A 15/15/15 Lairon is a perfect-IV specimen. For collectors, evolution projects and players interested in Great or Ultra League experiments, keeping a range of IV spreads can be more useful than keeping only perfect-IV Pokémon.

PvP IV reminder:

The best PvP IV spread is not necessarily 15/15/15. League CP limits can favor different Attack, Defense and HP combinations.

09 • PVP

Lairon PvP Guide

Lairon can technically be used in Trainer Battles, but it is generally an underpowered choice compared with stronger Steel and Rock Pokémon.

Great League

Lairon can fit below the Great League CP limit, but its performance is limited by its weaknesses and competition from more specialized Pokémon.

Ultra League

Lairon can also be built for Ultra League, although investing heavily in it is usually difficult to justify when stronger options are available.

Master League

Lairon's maximum CP is not high enough to make it a natural Master League investment.

PvP Build

Metal Sound + Rock Tomb + Body Slam can be considered for PvP experimentation, but Lairon is not a top-tier competitive choice.

Should You Purify Shadow Lairon?

Do not treat purification as an automatic upgrade. If you are keeping Shadow Lairon for collection, evolution or personal PvP experimentation, compare the Shadow version with the purified result before committing resources.

  • Check its IVs before transferring it.
  • Check whether Frustration is currently removable.
  • Consider whether you plan to evolve it into Aggron.
  • Compare the Shadow and purified versions before spending Stardust.
  • Keep interesting Shadow specimens if you collect Shadow Pokémon.

16 • INDIVIDUAL GUIDE

How to Use Lairon

Lairon's best use depends on whether you are collecting it, preparing an evolution or testing it in Trainer Battles.

For Collectors

  • Keep a high-IV regular Lairon.
  • Consider keeping a Shiny Lairon.
  • Keep interesting Shadow specimens.
  • Consider saving one for the complete Aron family.

For Evolution Projects

If your ultimate goal is Aggron, check Lairon's IVs and moves before evolving. A good Lairon candidate can become a useful part of your Aggron collection.

For PvP

Check the specific league's CP limit and use an appropriate IV spread rather than automatically choosing a 15/15/15 specimen.

For Raids

Lairon should generally not be a priority raid investment. Its offensive stats are not competitive with the stronger Steel and Rock attackers available in Pokémon GO.

18 • FAQ

Lairon FAQ

What type is Lairon?

Lairon is a dual Steel/Rock-type Pokémon.

What is Lairon weak to?

Lairon is especially vulnerable to Fighting and Ground attacks, and it is also weak to Water.

What is Lairon's max CP?

Lairon's maximum CP is 2,324 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

What are Lairon's best moves?

Metal Claw and Heavy Slam are the recommended Steel-oriented moveset. Metal Claw and Rock Slide can be used for a Rock-oriented build.

Is Lairon good for raids?

Lairon is generally not a strong raid attacker. There are many stronger Steel and Rock attackers available in Pokémon GO.

Is Lairon good in PvP?

Lairon can be used in Great and Ultra League, but it is generally an underperforming choice compared with stronger alternatives.

How do I evolve Aron into Lairon?

In Pokémon GO, Aron evolves into Lairon using 25 Aron Candy.

How do I evolve Lairon into Aggron?

Lairon evolves into Aggron using 100 Lairon Candy in Pokémon GO.

Is Shiny Lairon available?

Yes. Shiny Lairon is available in Pokémon GO.

Does Shiny Lairon evolve into Shiny Aggron?

Yes. A Shiny Lairon retains its Shiny status when evolved into Aggron.

Is Shadow Lairon available?

Shadow Lairon is a Shadow variant of Lairon. Its current acquisition method should be checked against the active Team GO Rocket rotation.

Should I purify Shadow Lairon?

Not automatically. Consider its IVs, intended use, evolution plans and the benefits of keeping the Shadow status before purifying it.

Can Shadow Lairon evolve into Shadow Aggron?

Shadow Pokémon that are eligible for evolution can retain their Shadow status through evolution.

Is Lairon better than Aggron?

Aggron is the final evolution and has a substantially higher overall combat profile. Lairon is primarily valuable as the middle stage of the Aron family or for collection and niche use.

What weather boosts Lairon?

Lairon's Steel/Rock typing is associated with Snow and Partly Cloudy weather boosts.

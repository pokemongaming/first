FAQ

Generation 3 Pokémon FAQ

How many Pokémon were introduced in Generation 3?

Generation III introduced 135 new Pokémon, numbered #252 through #386 in the National Pokédex.

Which region is Generation 3 from?

Generation III introduced the Hoenn region.

What are the Gen 3 starter Pokémon?

Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip are the three Hoenn starter Pokémon.

Which Pokémon are the main Hoenn Legendary Pokémon?

Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Latias, Latios, Kyogre, Groudon and Rayquaza are among the major Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation III.

Which Pokémon is #386?

Deoxys is National Pokédex #386 and is the final Pokémon introduced in Generation III.

Which Gen 3 Pokémon have Primal forms?

Kyogre and Groudon have Primal forms known as Primal Kyogre and Primal Groudon.

Which Gen 3 Pokémon has a Mega Evolution?

Several Hoenn Pokémon have Mega Evolutions, including Sceptile, Blaziken, Swampert, Gardevoir, Manectric, Salamence, Metagross and Rayquaza.

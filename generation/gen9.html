🌎 Gen 9 Overview — Paldea

Generation 9 introduced the Paldea region and the Terastal phenomenon.

What makes Gen 9 different?

Generation 9 introduced a large collection of new Pokémon, including three new starter families, new regional evolutions, Paradox Pokémon, several Legendary Pokémon and the mysterious Mythical Pokémon Pecharunt.

01 Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly are the Paldea starters.
02 Terastalization became the generation's signature battle mechanic.
03 Paradox Pokémon introduced unusual ancient and futuristic designs.
04 Koraidon and Miraidon are the central Legendary Pokémon.
05 Terapagos is closely connected to the Terastal phenomenon.

⏳ Paradox Pokémon

Gen 9 introduced mysterious ancient and futuristic Pokémon.

🦖

Ancient Paradox Pokémon

Ancient Paradox Pokémon are associated primarily with Pokémon Scarlet and have prehistoric-inspired designs, such as Great Tusk, Scream Tail, Brute Bonnet and Roaring Moon.

🤖

Future Paradox Pokémon

Future Paradox Pokémon are associated primarily with Pokémon Violet and feature futuristic mechanical designs, including Iron Treads, Iron Bundle and Iron Valiant.

💎 Terastal Phenomenon

Terastalization is the signature battle mechanic of Gen 9.

💎

Terastallization

A Pokémon can transform into its Tera form during battle, changing the presentation of its typing and enabling strategic use of a Tera Type.

✨

Tera Type

Every Pokémon can have a Tera Type. Strategic Tera Types can change offensive and defensive matchups.

🌟

Tera Raid Battles

Tera Raid Battles became a major part of the Gen 9 experience and are an important subject for individual Pokémon and event guides.

❓ Gen 9 Pokémon FAQ

Common questions about Paldea and Generation 9.

How many Pokémon were introduced in Generation 9?

Generation 9 introduced 120 new species, numbered from National Pokédex #906 through #1025.

What region is Generation 9 based on?

Generation 9 is primarily associated with the Paldea region. The generation was expanded through The Teal Mask and The Indigo Disk.

Who are the Gen 9 starters?

The three Paldea starters are Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly.

What is Terastalization?

Terastalization is Generation 9's signature battle mechanic. It allows Pokémon to adopt a Tera Type during battle.

What are Paradox Pokémon?

Paradox Pokémon are unusual Pokémon associated with past or future versions of Pokémon concepts. They include Ancient Paradox and Future Paradox groups.

Who are the main Gen 9 Legendary Pokémon?

Major Gen 9 Legendary Pokémon include Koraidon, Miraidon, the Treasures of Ruin, the Loyal Three, Ogerpon and Terapagos.

Is Pecharunt Legendary?

No. Pecharunt is classified as a Mythical Pokémon. It is National Pokédex #1025.

What is the last Gen 9 Pokémon?

Pecharunt is #1025, the final Pokémon introduced in Generation 9.

Are Pokémon from the Gen 9 DLC part of Gen 9?

Yes. The Pokémon introduced through The Teal Mask and The Indigo Disk are part of the Generation 9 Pokédex expansion.

Should Pokémon GO and main-series information be separated?

Yes. A strong database should clearly label information as Pokémon GO, main-series, or both. This prevents different CP, stats, moves and availability systems from being confused.

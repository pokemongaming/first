🌍 Gen 6 / Kalos Overview

The sixth generation introduced the Kalos region, Fairy typing and Mega Evolution.

About Generation 6

Generation 6 introduced 72 new Pokémon, beginning with Chespin at National Pokédex #650 and ending with Volcanion at #721. The generation is associated with Pokémon X and Pokémon Y and the Kalos region.

01 Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie form the Kalos starter trio.
02 Fairy became a new Pokémon type in this generation.
03 Mega Evolution became a major battle mechanic.
04 Xerneas, Yveltal and Zygarde are the primary Legendary Pokémon of Kalos.

❓ Gen 6 Pokémon FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Kalos generation.

How many Pokémon are in Gen 6?

There are 72 Pokémon introduced in Generation 6, covering National Pokédex numbers #650 through #721.

What region is Gen 6 from?

Generation 6 introduced the Kalos region.

Who is the first Gen 6 Pokémon?

Chespin is National Pokédex #650 and begins the Generation 6 Pokémon range.

Who is the final Gen 6 Pokémon?

Volcanion is National Pokédex #721 and is the final Pokémon in the Generation 6 range.

What are the Gen 6 starter Pokémon?

The Kalos starter trio consists of Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie.

What Legendary Pokémon were introduced in Gen 6?

Xerneas, Yveltal and Zygarde are the principal Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation 6.

Which Mythical Pokémon were introduced in Gen 6?

Diancie, Hoopa and Volcanion are the Mythical Pokémon introduced during Generation 6.

Why is Gen 6 important for Pokémon GO?

Gen 6 introduced Pokémon that can occupy important roles in raids, PvP and Mega Evolution. Individual Pokémon GO availability can change over time, so the availability section of each guide should be maintained separately.

01 · OVERVIEW

Aggron Overview

Aggron is a Steel / Rock-type Pokémon originally introduced in Generation 3. It evolves from Lairon and is the final stage of the Aron evolutionary family.

Pokémon GO also features Mega Aggron. Unlike regular Aggron, Mega Aggron becomes a pure Steel-type Pokémon, giving it a different set of type matchups and a specialized defensive Mega role.

02 · TYPE

Aggron Type

🛡️
Steel / Rock Dual Type

Why Aggron's Typing Matters

Aggron's Steel / Rock combination gives it numerous resistances, but the combination also creates major vulnerabilities to Fighting and Ground attacks. Water is another important weakness because of its Rock typing.

Mega Aggron is different because Mega Evolution changes its typing from Steel / Rock to pure Steel. This removes its Rock-type weaknesses and changes several of its resistances.

09 · PVP

Aggron PvP Guide

Aggron's high Defense gives it some interesting matchup potential, but its typing creates important vulnerabilities. Its PvP value depends strongly on league rules, moveset and the current metagame.

Great League

Aggron can be considered in specialized Great League teams, particularly where its resistances and available charged moves create useful matchup opportunities. However, Fighting- and Ground-type pressure can be a major problem.

Ultra League

Aggron can reach a more relevant CP range for Ultra League, but its effectiveness depends on the current meta and moveset. It should not be selected simply because of its high Defense.

Master League

Standard Aggron faces significant competition from Pokémon with stronger overall Master League stat profiles and more favorable matchup distributions.

PvP IV note:

The ideal PvP IV spread is not automatically 15/15/15. League CP limits can make low-Attack, higher-Defense spreads preferable for some Pokémon and formats.

Purification should not automatically be considered an upgrade. Keep the Shadow form if its battle or collection value matters to you, and compare the purified result before making a permanent decision.

17 · BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Aggron

Before a Raid

  • Check the raid boss's weaknesses.
  • Check whether Aggron's current moveset is suitable.
  • Check Aggron's level and IVs.
  • Consider Mega Evolution.
  • Compare Aggron with other available attackers.

During Team Building

Aggron's enormous Defense can make it look attractive on paper, but raid performance is determined by damage output as well as survival. A Pokémon that survives longer but deals substantially less damage may not outperform a stronger attacker.

Long-Term Investment

A strong Aggron can be worth keeping because it provides access to Mega Aggron and can serve different collection and gameplay purposes. Players should nevertheless compare the cost and performance with other Steel Pokémon before investing heavily.

Why Squirtle's Typing Matters

Squirtle has a straightforward Water typing. Water attacks are super effective against Fire, Ground and Rock Pokémon, while Water Pokémon resist several common attack types.

Squirtle's Role in the Evolution Family

Squirtle itself is not normally used as a top-end raid attacker. Its greater long-term value comes from evolving it into Wartortle and eventually Blastoise. Collectors may also want to preserve strong Shiny or Shadow specimens.

Should You Power Up Squirtle?

Usually, no. Stardust and Candy are generally better spent on evolving Squirtle into Blastoise first. A strong Squirtle can be worth keeping if it has excellent IVs, is Shiny, has special event significance or is intended for a specific limited-format league.

09 • PVP

Squirtle PvP Guide

Squirtle is not normally a major open-format PvP threat. Its low CP ceiling and first-stage status make Wartortle and Blastoise more interesting choices for competitive projects.

Great League

Squirtle can technically fit within the Great League CP limit, but its performance is generally limited compared with more optimized Water-type Pokémon.

Ultra League

Squirtle's low maximum CP makes it an impractical Ultra League investment in most circumstances.

Master League

Squirtle is not suitable for Master League because its maximum CP is far below the league's requirements.

PvP Investment Advice

If your goal is competitive PvP, consider saving the best Squirtle for evolution into Wartortle or Blastoise rather than investing heavily in Squirtle itself.

12 • EVOLUTION FAMILY

Squirtle Evolution — Wartortle & Blastoise

Squirtle is the first stage of a three-step Water-type evolution family. It evolves into Wartortle and then Blastoise.

Squirtle #007
→
Wartortle #008
→
Blastoise #009

Squirtle → Wartortle

Squirtle evolves into Wartortle using Pokémon GO Candy. Wartortle is the middle stage and has greater combat potential than Squirtle.

Wartortle → Blastoise

Wartortle evolves into Blastoise using additional Squirtle Candy. Blastoise is the final conventional evolution and is considerably stronger than both previous stages.

Evolution Strategy

Before evolving a rare Shiny or Shadow Squirtle, decide whether you want to preserve the original form, create Wartortle, or complete the line with Blastoise.

13 • FINAL EVOLUTION

Blastoise — Squirtle's Final Evolution

Blastoise is the final evolution of Squirtle and is considerably more useful in Pokémon GO than Squirtle itself. It is a bulky Water-type Pokémon with access to powerful Water attacks.

POKÉDEX #009
TYPE Water
ROLE Water Tank
MEGA FORM Yes

Why Blastoise Matters

Blastoise has substantially better combat stats than Squirtle. Players who are collecting Squirtle Candy should generally think about the eventual Blastoise investment rather than treating Squirtle as the final destination.

Mega Blastoise

Blastoise can undergo Mega Evolution to become Mega Blastoise. Mega Blastoise is a significantly more powerful Water-type form and can provide useful team-wide Mega benefits during appropriate battles.

Long-Term Goal

A strong Squirtle can become a strong Blastoise and, where available under current game mechanics, support a Mega Blastoise investment.

15 • RESOURCES

Squirtle Candy, XL Candy & Stardust

Squirtle Candy is useful for completing the entire Squirtle evolution family and powering up Blastoise. Because Stardust is limited, consider the final purpose of each specimen before investing heavily.

  • Save enough Squirtle Candy to complete the evolution family.
  • Keep strong IV specimens for future Blastoise projects.
  • Preserve rare Shiny Squirtle if you are building a collection.
  • Compare Shadow and purified specimens before purification.
  • Check event moves before evolving special-event Squirtle.
  • Consider Mega Blastoise when planning long-term Water investments.

17 • BATTLE GUIDE

How to Use Squirtle

Before Investing

  • Check the IVs of the Squirtle.
  • Decide whether you want Regular, Shiny or Shadow.
  • Check whether the Pokémon has an event-exclusive move history.
  • Consider evolving it into Wartortle or Blastoise.
  • Compare it with your existing Water-type attackers.
  • Save Stardust for the final evolution when possible.

For Collectors

Collectors may want to keep Squirtle in its original form, especially if it is Shiny, Shadow or associated with a special event. Having separate Squirtle, Wartortle and Blastoise specimens can also make a complete collection more satisfying.

For Competitive Players

Competitive players should generally focus on the evolutionary line rather than Squirtle itself. Wartortle and Blastoise have significantly greater battle potential.

Squirtle FAQ

Is Squirtle a Water-type Pokémon?

Yes. Squirtle is a pure Water-type Pokémon.

What is Squirtle's Pokédex number?

Squirtle is #007 in the National Pokédex.

What is Squirtle weak to?

Squirtle is weak to Electric and Grass attacks.

What is Squirtle's Max CP?

Squirtle's maximum CP is approximately 1,000 at Level 50 with perfect IVs.

Is Shiny Squirtle available in Pokémon GO?

Yes. Shiny Squirtle has been released in Pokémon GO.

⚔️ Mega Raid Shinies in Pokémon GO

Mega Raids are one of the best ways to hunt shiny Pokémon while also collecting valuable Mega Energy. After defeating a Mega Raid Boss, trainers have a chance to encounter its regular form, which may be shiny if the shiny version has been released in Pokémon GO.

This guide tracks Mega Raid bosses that can reward shiny encounters and helps trainers decide which Mega Raids are worth prioritizing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can Mega Raid Pokémon be shiny?

Yes. After defeating a Mega Raid Boss, the encounter Pokémon can be shiny if its shiny version has been released in Pokémon GO.

Do Mega Raids have higher shiny odds?

Raid-exclusive Pokémon often have better shiny odds than many wild Pokémon, making Mega Raids a popular shiny hunting method.

Do shiny Mega Pokémon stay shiny after Mega Evolution?

Yes. A shiny Pokémon remains shiny when Mega Evolved.

Can I trade shiny Pokémon obtained from Mega Raids?

Yes. Shiny Pokémon caught after Mega Raids can be traded following normal Pokémon GO trading rules.

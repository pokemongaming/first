Beginner Guide

If you're new to Pokémon GO raids, here are a few basics to get started: Raids are cooperative battles where players team up to defeat powerful bosses within a time limit. Each boss has specific weaknesses, and choosing the right Pokémon is key to victory. Using type advantages, dodging charged moves, and coordinating with other players can significantly improve your chances. Even beginners can win tough raids by using the right strategy.

Tips & Strategy

Winning raids consistently requires smart gameplay, not just strong Pokémon. One key strategy is to build a balanced team with high DPS attackers rather than relying on a single powerful Pokémon. Dodging charged moves at the right time can help your team survive longer, especially in solo or duo raids. Weather conditions also play an important role, as they can boost both your attacks and the raid boss’s moves. Planning ahead and understanding the boss mechanics can make even difficult raids manageable.

Tips for Successful Raiding

Always check the raid boss’s moveset before the battle so you can prepare the best counters and avoid dangerous attacks. Use weather-boosted Pokémon whenever possible because they deal extra damage and improve your raid performance. Coordinate with friends for high-level raids to maximize efficiency, complete battles faster, and save resources. Bring a mix of Meta, Budget, and Free-to-Play counters for flexibility in different raid situations. Learning type weaknesses and resistances is also important because it helps you avoid wasted damage and survive longer in battle.
